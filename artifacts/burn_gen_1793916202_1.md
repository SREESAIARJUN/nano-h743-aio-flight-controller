## 1. Field‑Oriented Control Equations

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

$$\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0})\big)$$

`θ_m,0` is the encoder offset found during alignment: inject `V_d` with `θ_e = 0` and record `θ_m`.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform (Amplitude‑Invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park Transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 PMSM Model in the d‑q Frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\big(\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q\big) \;\approx\; K_t i_q,\quad K_t = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m$$

### 1.5 Current PI with Decoupling and Back‑EMF Feedforward

Pole‑zero cancellation tuning gives a first‑order closed loop with bandwidth `ω_c`:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L_q i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)$$

Set `i_d* = 0`; a surface PM motor has no reluctance torque to exploit.
