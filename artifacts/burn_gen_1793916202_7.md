# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control, Anti-Cogging Compensation, and Dual ICM-42688-P Vibration Rejection

**Scope note:** I don't have the Job #17 specification. This design uses the typical gimbal assumptions listed in §0. Every numeric value is centralized in config structs, so you can substitute your real motor, encoder and mechanical parameters there.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class Cortex-M4F/M7, hardware FPU |
| PWM | 20 kHz, center-aligned, ADC triggered at the PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera frame, SPI, 8 kHz ODR, FIFO plus timestamps |
