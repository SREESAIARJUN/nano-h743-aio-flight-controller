# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack (Job #19): System Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlist

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three constraints shape the rest of this document:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 is the flight controller. It sends DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs, which run the 48 kHz PWM and commutation. Suitable ESC firmware is AM32 on AT32F421/STM32G071-class parts, or Bluejay on EFM8. The 48 kHz switching model therefore applies to the ESC side, not the H743.
2. **80 A per channel cannot be sustained on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board.** The calculations in §3 show roughly 1–1.5 s of adiabatic burst before the junctions exceed their limits. The 80 A figure is treated here as a ≤1 s burst rating. A 30.5×30.5 mm ESC is recommended.
3. **Component values are representative, not vendor quotes.** I use a typical 30–40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET class. Replace every parameter in the table below with datasheet values from your actual BOM before sign-off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

Six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During each 60° sector, two phases carry current:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

Conduction loss per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{hot}\cdot 1 = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot}$$

This result is independent of duty cycle because synchronous rectification removes the D dependence.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0022 = \mathbf{28.2\ W}$$

Copper (PCB trace) loss, with two paths in series:

$$P_{Cu} = 2 I^2 R_{Cu} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0005 = \mathbf{6.4\ W}$$

Peak dissipation in a single FET occurs in the return-leg low side while it conducts:

$$P_{FET,pk} = I^2 R_{hot} = 14.1\ \text{W}$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of an electrical cycle. The average per FET is therefore about 4.7 W, but the instantaneous 14.1 W value drives the junction transient analysed in §3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM-leg FET pair switches hard.

**V–I overlap loss**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 600\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.84\ W}$$

This term is significant. Reducing dead time to 150 ns would halve it, at the cost of shoot-through risk; validate with a double-pulse test.

**Reverse recovery.** Body-diode Q_rr is largely suppressed by synchronous switching. If the datasheet Q_rr is ~50 nC: Q_rr·V·f ≈ 0.06 W.

**Output capacitance loss**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V f = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

**Gate drive loss** (two switching FETs)

$$P_{g} = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

This power is mostly dissipated in the driver and gate resistors.

### Per-channel and board totals at 80 A

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction | 28.2 |
| Copper | 6.4 |
| V–I switching | 1.94 |
| Dead time | 1.84 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + Q_g | ~0.13 |
| **Per ESC channel** | **≈ 38.5 W** |
| **4 channels (simultaneous punch-out)** | **≈ 154 W** |

Switching losses are only about 10% of the total, so 48 kHz is not the problem. Conduction loss dominates, and it scales with I².

**Efficiency check.** At 80 A and ~90% duty, output power is ≈ 1.8 kW per channel, so η ≈ 98%. The efficiency is fine; the issue is the heat density on the board.

## 3. Thermal Calculations

### 3.1 Steady state (why 80 A continuous is impossible)

For a 20×20 mm board in prop-wash airflow (~50 W/m²K effective, both sides):

- Wetted area A ≈ 2 × 4 cm² = 8 cm² = 8×10⁻⁴ m²
- R_θ,board-amb ≈ 1/(hA) = 1/(50 × 8×10⁻⁴) = **25 K/W**

At 154 W this implies a ΔT of about 3,850 K, which is physically impossible. The sustainable continuous load at ΔT = 60 K is about 2.4 W total. Even with 200 W/m²K forced air it is only about 10 W total. Therefore **continuous rating ≈ 20–25 A per channel** on this footprint, depending on airflow.
