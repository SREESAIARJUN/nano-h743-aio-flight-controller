## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low.

- **ON interval (D):** current flows through HS_X → motor → LS_Y.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** current flows through LS_X (sync. rectifier) → motor → LS_Y.

So exactly **two FETs conduct I_ph at every instant**, whatever the duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_J)
$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Inductor ripple adds to RMS current:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH (typical 5″ motor phase-to-phase / 2), D = 0.5, V_bemf ≈ 12.6 V.

- ΔI ≈ 12.6·0.5/(2·15e-6·48e3) ≈ **4.4 A**
- ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.6 A², which is under 0.03 % of 6400 A². Ripple is negligible at 80 A.
