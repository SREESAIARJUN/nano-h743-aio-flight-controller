# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture (Job #9)

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two issues with the specification need to be stated before the math. Ignoring them would invalidate everything that follows.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. The correct architecture is:
   - The **H743** runs ArduPilot and outputs bi-directional DShot600.
   - **Four dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g. AT32F421K8U7 running AM32) handle commutation, each driving 6 MOSFETs.
   - Everything below assumes this split.
2. **"80 A" on a nano (20×20 mm mount, ~30×30 mm PCB) cannot be continuous.** The thermal section quantifies this. It is a burst rating of seconds, not minutes. The output of this analysis is a time limit, not a pass/fail.

**Representative MOSFET** (40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 class). Substitute your actual datasheet values into the equations.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_ds25 | 1.1 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient of R_ds | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 35 nC |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd (switching charge) | Q_sw | 10 nC |
| Plateau voltage | V_pl | 3.5 V |
| Output charge @ 25 V | Q_oss | 25 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Junction-to-case resistance | R_θJC | 1.2 K/W |
| Internal gate resistance | R_g,int | 1.5 Ω |

**Operating point:**

- 6S full charge: V_bus = 25.2 V
- PWM frequency: f_sw = 48 kHz
- Dead time: t_dt = 100 ns
- Gate drive: V_drv = 10 V, R_g,ext = 10 Ω
- Driver output resistance: 5 Ω source, 2 Ω sink
- Worst-case phase current: I_ph = 80 A
