## 0. Architectural Reality Check

Three points shape everything that follows:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any credible 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) and a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. The "48 kHz" figure is the ESC PWM frequency, set in AM32 firmware, not on the H743.
2. **80A is a burst rating only.** The math below shows roughly 30 W per ESC channel at 80A phase current. On any nano-class board that allows about **1 second** of burst before Tj reaches 150 °C.
3. **Form factor.** A 20×20 mm AIO with 80A per channel is not physically credible. I model a **30.5×30.5 mm** board and show the 20×20 scaling. I recommend a stacked FC/ESC (H743 board on top, ESC below) to isolate the IMU from roughly 100 W of heat and switching noise.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, exactly two MOSFETs carry the full phase current at every instant:
- **PWM on (duty D):** HS(A) and LS(B).
- **PWM off (1−D):** LS(A), acting as synchronous rectifier, and LS(B).

So instantaneous conduction loss is independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| Condition | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| Tj = 25 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W** |
| Tj = 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 2.25 mΩ = **28.8 W** |

**Positive feedback:** loss rises with temperature, which raises temperature further. Solve iteratively, or use the hot value for design.
