## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant the motor current I_ph flows through exactly **two** conducting switch positions: one in the sourcing leg and one in the sinking leg.

- During PWM-on, the high-side (HS) of the PWM leg and the low-side (LS) of the return leg conduct.
- During PWM-off, the LS of the PWM leg (synchronous rectifier) and the LS of the return leg conduct.

**Loss per channel, independent of duty:**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Temperature dependence of R_DS:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Electrothermal fixed point

Loss depends on R_DS, which depends on T_j, which depends on loss. So T_j must be solved self-consistently:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta,eff}\cdot P(T_j)$$

$$\Rightarrow\quad T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,(2I^2R_{25})(1-25\alpha)}{1-R_{\theta}\,2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha}$$

**Thermal runaway condition.** The denominator must stay positive:

$$R_\theta < \frac{1}{2I^2R_{25}\alpha}$$

At 80A this gives:

$$R_\theta < \frac{1}{2\cdot6400\cdot0.0007\cdot0.0045} = 24.8\ \text{K/W}$$

This criterion is per-channel lumped, and the margin is comfortable.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80A

At a design point of T_j = 110°C:

$$R_{DS} = 0.70\,(1+0.0045\cdot85) = 0.968\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\cdot80^2\cdot0.968\times10^{-3} = 12.4\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Distribution across the six FETs

Each phase leg carries current for 2/3 of the electrical cycle.

- **HS FET** conducts for D·(1/3) of the cycle.
- **LS FET** conducts for (1−D)·(1/3) + 1/3 of the cycle, i.e. it acts as rectifier and return path.

The worst-case FET is therefore the low-side at low duty:

$$P_{LS,max} \approx I^2R\cdot\tfrac{2}{3} = 6400\cdot0.968\text{m}\cdot0.667 = 4.13\ \text{W}$$

The electrical period at 30k eRPM (≈2 ms) is far shorter than the FET-plus-copper thermal time constant (~100 ms). Time-averaged per-FET power is therefore valid for computing T_j.

### 1.5 Bus current versus phase current

Battery current is not the same as phase current:

$$I_{bat} = D\cdot I_{ph}$$

At full throttle (D ≈ 1), 4×80A = **320A from the battery**. This sizes the XT90, the pads and the battery copper (§4.3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches hard. Each PWM period contains one HS turn-on/off pair, with the LS acting as synchronous rectifier.

### 2.1 V-I overlap loss

**Gate drive current during the Miller plateau** (FD6288-class driver, R_g = 4.7Ω, V_drv = 12V):

$$I_{g,on} \approx \frac{12-3.0}{4.7+1.0} = 1.58\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} \approx \frac{3.0}{4.7+1.0} = 0.53\ \text{A}$$

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = 8.9\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}} = 26.4\ \text{ns}$$
