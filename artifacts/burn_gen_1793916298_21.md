## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 and handle commutation. The H743 does **not** commute the motors. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back.

> **Feasibility flag.** 80 A per motor on a "nano" footprint (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is only achievable as a **short burst**. Section 2 shows that sustained 4×80 A is thermally impossible on this footprint without forced cooling or a heatsink. Treat 80 A as a time-limited rating, and enforce that limit in firmware.

## 1. Loss Model (per motor, i.e. per 3-phase bridge)

### 1.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step operation, two FETs are always in series with the motor current: the high side of one leg and the low side of another. With synchronous rectification, the complementary low side carries the freewheeling current during PWM-off. So, at any instant, **two channels of R_ds(on)** conduct I, independent of duty.

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Per-FET split. Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical period, at duty D:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2R_{ds}, \qquad P_{cond,LS,sync} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2R_{ds} + \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2R_{ds}$$

The second LS term is the conducting low side of the opposite leg.
