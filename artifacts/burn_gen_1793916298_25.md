## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Red Flags

These assumptions drive every number below, so they come first.

| Item | Assumption | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | H743 runs ArduPilot only. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32) plus a 3-phase gate driver. | The H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. It sends DShot600 only. The 48 kHz figure is an ESC firmware setting. |
| Bus | 6S, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | |
| MOSFETs | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN, R_DS(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ, Q_g ≈ 50 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, t_r + t_f ≈ 35 ns | These are typical for nano 4-in-1 boards. Substitute your datasheet values. |
| Form factor | 30.5 mm mounting pattern (~36×36 mm board). "Nano" 20×20 is far worse. | |
| "80 A burst" | Per motor, phase-RMS ≈ DC-link-equivalent | With all 4 motors at 80 A, **pack current is about 320 A**. No nano board survives that for more than a few seconds. Treat 80 A as a per-channel transient rating only. |

**Bottom line up front:** At 80 A per motor, total board dissipation is about 120–135 W. Burst capability is **about 2 s from hot cruise and about 4 s from cold**. Conduction losses dominate switching losses by roughly 11:1.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /^\circ\text{C}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.5 × 1.425 = **2.14 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-step BLDC with complementary (synchronous) PWM

At any instant, two phases conduct. On the PWM'd leg, the high side conducts for duty D and the low side for 1−D, so that leg always presents one R_DS in the path. The return leg presents one more.

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The electrical period at flight eRPM is in the millisecond range, which is far below the die thermal τ, so averaging is valid:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
