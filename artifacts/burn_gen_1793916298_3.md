# Job #31: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two issues in the brief would invalidate the design if left unaddressed.

1. **The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot sends bidirectional DShot to ESCs. It does not run four sensorless bridge controllers. The correct architecture is:
   - STM32H743 (ArduPilot) sends DShot600 to 4× ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32, for example).
   - Each ESC MCU drives a gate driver, which drives a 3-phase bridge.
   
   The switching-loss model below therefore applies to the ESC bridges, with PWM at 48 kHz set by the ESC firmware.

2. **An 80 A burst on all four channels exceeds the thermal capacity of a "nano" (20×20 mm) board.** Section 3 quantifies this. The realistic outcome is either:
   - 80 A for about 1–2 s on one motor, or
   - a 30.5×30.5 mm board with derated sustained current.

**Assumed parameters.** These are representative values. Replace them with your selected MOSFET's datasheet values.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S, fully charged | 25.2 V |
| I_burst | per-phase current | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds(on),25 | per MOSFET (40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3) | 1.0 mΩ |
| k_T | R_ds(on) multiplier at T_j = 110 °C | 1.5 |
| t_r, t_f | at R_g = 2.2 Ω | 12 ns, 15 ns |
| Q_rr | body diode reverse recovery | 45 nC |
| Q_oss | output charge | 40 nC |
| Q_g | total gate charge at 10 V | 45 nC |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous commutation

During each 60° sector, current I flows through two phases: one high-side leg (PWM at duty D) and one low-side FET held fully on. With synchronous rectification, the PWM leg's complementary low-side FET carries current during (1 − D).

- R_hot = k_T · R_ds(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ
- PWM leg: P_HS = D·I²·R_hot and P_LS,sync = (1 − D − 2·t_dead·f_sw)·I²·R_hot
- Static low-side leg: P_LS,static = I²·R_hot
- Per-ESC silicon conduction loss: **P_cond,Si ≈ 2·I²·R_hot**. This is nearly independent of D because the PWM leg always has one FET conducting.
- Copper loss: P_cond,Cu = I²·(2·R_cu) for the two phase paths, plus the bus planes.

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

- P_cond,Si = 2 × 6400 × 1.5e-3 = **19.2 W per ESC**
- P_cond,Cu = 6400 × 0.8e-3 = **5.1 W per ESC**
- Worst single FET (static low side): 6400 × 1.5e-3 = **9.6 W**

Note the thermal runaway coupling: R_ds(on)(T) ≈ R_25·[1 + α(T − 25)] with α ≈ 0.006/K. Solve this iteratively with the thermal network in Section 3.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

**Hard-switching overlap (on the PWM leg only):**
P_sw = ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 27e-9 × 48e3 = **1.31 W**

**Reverse recovery of the body diode:**
P_rr = Q_rr·V·f_sw = 45e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.054 W**

**Output capacitance:**
P_oss ≈ Q_oss·V·f_sw = 40e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.048 W**

**Dead-time diode conduction:**
P_dt = V_F·I·2·t_dead·f_sw = 0.85 × 80 × 400e-9 × 48e3 = **1.31 W**
This is significant. Reducing t_dead to 100 ns halves it. Doing so requires driver propagation-delay matching better than 30 ns.

**Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FETs):**
P_g = 6·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw = 6 × 45e-9 × 10 × 48e3 = **0.13 W**
Only the commutating pair switches at f_sw, so this is a conservative upper bound.

**Per-ESC total at 80 A:**

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (Si) | 19.2 |
| Conduction (Cu) | 5.1 |
| Switching overlap | 1.31 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.10 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.31 |
| Gate drive | 0.13 |
| **Total** | **≈ 27.2 W** |

- **All four ESCs at burst: ≈ 109 W.**
- Electrical power delivered is about 25.2 V × 80 A × 4 ≈ 8 kW, so the board efficiency of ~98.6% looks good. The problem is the absolute heat in a tiny volume.

**Frequency sensitivity.** Only P_sw, P_rr, P_oss and P_dt scale with f_sw: dP/df ≈ 2.7 W per 48 kHz. Moving to 24 kHz saves about 1.35 W per ESC. Conduction losses dominate, so **choose the MOSFET by R_ds(on), not by switching speed.**

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (per hottest FET)

T_j = T_amb + P_FET·(R_θJC + R_θCB) + P_board·R_θBA, with transient response governed by the board heat capacity C_board.

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| R_θJC (PDFN 3.3) | 1.2 K/W |
| R_θCB (solder + via array, 2 oz copper) | 1.5 K/W |
| Board area 20×20 mm, two sides | 8 cm² |
| h_conv with prop wash | ≈ 100 W/m²K |
| R_θBA = 1/(h·A) | **12.5 K/W** |
| C_board (≈ 2.5 g FR4/Cu + 24 FETs, c ≈ 0.9 J/gK) | **≈ 3 J/K** |

### 3.2 Steady state at 80 A on all four channels

ΔT = 109 W × 12.5 K/W ≈ **1360 K**. This is physically impossible. **Continuous 80 A operation is not viable at any size near nano.**

- Sustainable dissipation for ΔT = 60 K is 60 / 12.5 = 4.8 W total, about 1.2 W per ESC.
- Since P ≈ 3.8e-3·I² W per ESC at hot R_ds(on), this gives **I_cont ≈ 18 A per motor** on 20×20 mm.

### 3.3 Burst (adiabatic) limit

τ = R_θBA·C = 12.5 × 3 ≈ 37 s, so for bursts of a few seconds the board behaves adiabatically: dT/dt ≈ P/C.

Allowed board rise from 45 °C to 95 °C, keeping T_j < 125 °C. The local FET rise is 9.6 W × 2.7 K/W ≈ 26 K on top of the board temperature.

| Burst case | dT/dt | Max duration |
|---|---|---|
| All 4 at 80 A | 109/3 ≈ 36 K/s | **≈ 1.4 s** |
| 1 motor at 80 A | 27/3 ≈ 9 K/s | **≈ 5.5 s** |

Note that on a single ESC, the bridge region heats faster than the board average. Use ≈ 3 s with a hot-spot margin.

### 3.4 Recommendations

- Make the "80 A" rating explicitly a **single-motor burst of 1 s or less**.
- Implement ESC-side temperature foldback: an NTC per bridge, with AM32 current limiting.
- Set ArduPilot `BATT_*` current limits and `MOT_BAT_CURR_MAX` accordingly.
- For a real 4 × 80 A burst, move to 30.5×30.5 mm with 3 oz copper and FETs of ≤ 0.7 mΩ. Treat it as roughly 45 A continuous / 80 A for 2 s.

## 4. ArduPilot Bidirectional DShot600: Timing and DMA Allocation

### 4.1 Timing

**Outbound frame:**
- 600 kbit/s gives a bit period T_bit of 1.667 µs.
- A frame is 16 bits: 11-bit throttle, 1 telemetry bit, 4-bit CRC. That is 26.7 µs.
- Bidirectional mode uses inverted signalling (idle low) with an inverted CRC.

**Duty encoding (in timer ticks):**
- ArduPilot uses a bit width of 8 ticks: "0" = 3 ticks (37.5%) and "1" = 6 ticks (75%).
- With a 240 MHz timer clock (APB at 120 MHz × 2): PSC = 240e6 / (600e3 × 8) − 1 = **49**, and ARR = 7.

**ESC telemetry reply:**
- Arrives about 30 µs after the frame.
- 21-bit GCR at 5/4 × 600 kHz = 750 kbit/s, so about 1.33 µs per bit and 28 µs total.
- Payload is eRPM: a 12-bit mantissa/exponent value plus CRC (and EDT if enabled).

**Total pin occupancy:** about 26.7 + 30 + 28 + guard ≈ 100 µs. This is fine at a 1–8 kHz loop rate.
