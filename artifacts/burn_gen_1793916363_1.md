# Job #63: STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC
## System Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Allocation, and Netlists

**Revision:** A (design-intent baseline). Every number here is a modeled value, not a measured one. Values marked ⚠ must be verified on hardware before release.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs on the same board. The ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32 is assumed) generate the 48 kHz PWM and drive the gate drivers and MOSFETs.

```
VBAT ─┬─► 4× [ESC MCU (AT32F421/AM32) ─► 3× half-bridge driver ─► 6× MOSFET] ─► Motors
      ├─► 5V/10V/3V3 regulators
      └─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► ESC MCU ×4
```

**Feasibility finding:** an "80 A burst" on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting, typically about 30×30 mm PCB) with one MOSFET per switch position is **thermally marginal**. The analysis below shows it is achievable only as a **short burst (about 2–5 s) with prop-wash airflow**. It is not a continuous rating. Section 3 derives the limits.

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step complementary PWM, two phases carry current at any instant. In the PWM'd leg, either the high-side or the low-side FET conducts. In the static leg, one FET is on continuously. The current always flows through exactly two FETs:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Temperature-corrected resistance:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond @ 80 A | P_cond @ 45 A |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.10 mΩ | 14.1 W | 4.5 W |
| 100 °C | 1.47 mΩ | 18.8 W | 6.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.60 mΩ | **20.5 W** | 6.5 W |

Add parasitic copper and solder resistance. With 2 oz outer, 2 oz inner, and about 0.3 mΩ per current path at 80 °C:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{path} = 6400 \times 0.0003 \approx 1.9\ \text{W}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each phase leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical period. Averaged across six FETs:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx \frac{20.5}{6} \approx 3.4\ \text{W}$$

The FETs on the static leg see peak instantaneous dissipation of I²R = 10.2 W while conducting. That matters for transient Z_th.

### 2.2 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg's high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side FET switches at about zero voltage after the dead time (body diode clamped).

**(a) V–I overlap, hard-switched device:**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$
$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 22\times10^{-9} \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{1.06\ W}$$

**(b) Output capacitance (C_oss) loss:**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \approx 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery of the complementary body diode:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.04\ \text{W}$$

(Q_rr rises with di/dt and current. Apply a 2–3× derating, about 0.1 W.)

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt} = V_F I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive (two FETs switching per ESC; dissipated mainly in the driver and gate resistors):**

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 45\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.043\ \text{W}$$

**Switching subtotal at 80 A:** about 1.06 + 0.05 + 0.1 + 0.61 + 0.04 ≈ **1.9 W per ESC**

### 2.3 Total Board Dissipation

| Contributor | Per ESC @ 80 A | ×4 ESCs @ 80 A | ×4 @ 45 A |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conduction (T_j = 125 °C) | 20.5 W | 82.0 W | 26.0 W |
| Copper/solder path | 1.9 W | 7.7 W | 2.4 W |
| Switching + dead time | 1.9 W | 7.6 W | 4.3 W |
| Driver + ESC MCU | 0.15 W | 0.6 W | 0.6 W |
| FC side (H743 + regulators) | — | 1.5 W | 1.5 W |
| **Total** | **≈24.5 W** | **≈99 W** | **≈35 W** |

**Key insight:** at 48 kHz the design is conduction-dominated, roughly 85–90% of losses. Going from 24 to 48 kHz costs only about 1.3 W per ESC. Cutting R_DS(on) by paralleling FETs or using a lower-R_DS(on) part would reduce losses far more.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (Foster/Cauer Approximation)

```
P_FET ─► R_jc (1.2 K/W) ─► C_die/pkg (~0.05 J/K)
        ─► R_c-pcb (local spreading, thermal vias) ≈ 8 K/W ⚠
        ─► C_pcb+Cu (board mass) 
        ─► R_pcb-amb (convection: still air ≈ 6 K/W board-level; prop wash ≈ 2 K/W)
```

### 3.2 Board Heat Capacity (Burst Buffer)

Board stack-up: 30×30 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers, 1.6 mm thick.

- Copper: 8 × 900 mm² × 70 µm ≈ 0.50 cm³ → 4.5 g × 0.385 J/g·K = 1.73 J/K
- FR4: ≈1.4 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ = 2.6 g × 1.1 J/g·K = 2.86 J/K
- Components and solder: ≈0.8 J/K

$$C_{th,board} \approx \mathbf{5.4\ J/K}$$
