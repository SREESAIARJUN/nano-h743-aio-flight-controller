# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #59)

## 0. Scope Corrections

Three points in the request need clarifying before the model is useful:

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and does commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. ArduPilot has no ESC firmware. A single H743 doing both jobs would be a custom firmware project, not an ArduPilot configuration.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is a short transient, not a rating.** Section 3 shows the board heats at roughly 40 K/s at 4×80 A. "Burst" must therefore mean well under one second.
3. **All component values below are representative.** They are marked *[ASSUMED]*. Substitute datasheet values for your actual BOM before committing the layout. The netlist in Section 5 is a reference topology, not a DRC-verified production netlist.
