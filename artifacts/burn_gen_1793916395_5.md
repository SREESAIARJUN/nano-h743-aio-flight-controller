| Loop | Rate | Trigger | Sensors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (Tₛ = 50 µs) | PWM center-aligned ADC trigger | shunts, encoder |
| Camera-rate (velocity) | 4 kHz | every 5th FOC tick / IMU data-ready | dual gyro |
| Attitude (angle) | 1 kHz | every 4th rate tick | fused AHRS |

Bandwidth separation rule: each outer loop bandwidth should be at most 1/5 to 1/10 of the inner loop bandwidth.
ω_c (current) ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s, ω_v ≈ 2π·60–120, ω_θ ≈ 2π·8–15.

## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 2.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 3 shunts; for 2 shunts use $i_c = -i_a - i_b$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 2.2 Plant (PMSM, surface magnets, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 2.3 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \hat\omega_e \hat L i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$. Pole-zero cancellation gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c \quad\Big(\text{PI: } K_p + \tfrac{K_i}{s}\Big)$$

Constraint (accounts for the 1.5-sample delay of ZOH plus computation): $\omega_c \le \dfrac{1}{3\,T_s\cdot 1.5}\approx 2\pi\cdot 2\text{ kHz}$ at 20 kHz. Use about 1.
