# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the original spec for Job #54, so this design rests on the assumptions listed in §0. Every constant is a named parameter, so you can substitute your real motor, inertia and IMU-mounting data. ICM-42688-P register values come from datasheet DS-000347. Verify them against your silicon revision before flashing.

---

## 0. Assumptions & Timing Architecture

| Item | Value (default) |
|---|---|
| MCU | Cortex-M7/M4F with FPU (e.g. STM32G4/H7) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Position sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder per joint (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera payload, ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps, SPI + DRDY |
| Current loop | 20 kHz (center-aligned PWM, ADC
