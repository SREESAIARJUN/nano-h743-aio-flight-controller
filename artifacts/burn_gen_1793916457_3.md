## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

Each phase leg spends one third of the electrical period in each of three states:

- **"High":** the high-side (HS) FET chops at duty D. The low-side (LS) FET of the same leg is complementary, with duty 1−D.
- **"Low":** the LS FET is fully on.
- **"Floating":** both FETs are off, and back-EMF is sensed.

With synchronous rectification, motor current always flows through exactly two FETs. These are either HS(x) + LS(y) during the on-time, or LS(x) + LS(y) during freewheel.

### 1.2 Equations

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha\approx0.0059\ \text{K}^{-1}\Rightarrow k_T\approx1.5\text{ at }110^\circ\text{C}$$

Total conduction loss per motor (independent of D):

$$P_{cond,motor}=I^2\cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per-FET average loss:

$$P_{HS}=\frac{D}{3}I^2R_{DS},\qquad P_{LS}=\frac{(1-D)+1}{3}I^2R_{DS}$$
