# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #38): Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, BDShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Feasibility Findings

I have no Job #38 history, so every assumption below is stated explicitly and parametrized. Change the inputs and the equations still hold. Three findings change the architecture, so they come first.

1. **The H743 cannot be the ESC controller under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot outputs DShot. It does not commutate motors. The correct architecture is H743 (ArduPilot) → 4× BDShot600 → 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) → gate drivers → 24 MOSFETs. The 48 kHz PWM lives on the ESC MCUs.
