# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #42 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly in §0. Every motor-, encoder- and mounting-specific number is a named parameter, so you can swap in your real values without touching the structure.

---

## 0. Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 170+ MHz |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω (phase), L = 1.8 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), read at 20 kHz |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on SPI at 8 kHz ODR, mounted on the camera plate, point-symmetric about the plate's rotation centre |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz, velocity 4 kHz, angle/attitude 1 kHz |
| Bus | 12–16 V, 3-shunt low-side current sensing |

**Timing hierarchy and bandwidth ratios:**

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current | 20 kHz | ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·120 Hz |
| Angle | 1 kHz | ω_θ ≈ 2π·25 Hz |

Each loop has at least 5× bandwidth separation from the one inside it, and each sample rate is at least 10× its bandwidth.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform

Amplitude-invariant form. With three shunts, enforce $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$ by dropping the phase with the highest duty, since its low-side window is too short to sample cleanly.

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant model (SPM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.5 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

Pole-zero cancellation of the R-L plant gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p^{(i)} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{(i)} = R\,\omega_c
$$

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Reference values: $i_d^* = 0$ (SPM, no field weakening) and $\lambda_m = K_t/(1.5\,p)$.

### 1.6 Voltage limiting (circular)

$$
|v|_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

Saturate the vector with **q-priority**:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{clamp}(v_d, \pm|v|_{max}),\qquad
v_q \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{|v|_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)
$$
