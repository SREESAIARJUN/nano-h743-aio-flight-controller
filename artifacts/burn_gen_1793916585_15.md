# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Feasibility Finding

**An 80 A per-channel burst rating on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) 4-in-1 is thermally implausible for anything beyond about 2–4 s.** The model below shows why. I've modeled the 80 A burst as specified, but the continuous rating I can defend is much lower (§3.5). Two points to settle before layout:

1. **Topology.** The H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC PWM frequency. Bidirectional DShot600 is the H743 ↔ ESC-MCU link.
2. **Bus voltage.** I assume 6S (25.2 V full charge) and 40 V MOSFETs. On 4S, switching loss scales by roughly 0.67.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd (high side ↔ low side complementary) and one phase's low side is held on. Current I flows through:

- the PWM-phase HS for fraction D, or the PWM-phase LS (synchronous rectification) for fraction 1−D;
- the static LS for the full period.

Per-sector loss:

$$P_{sector} = I^2R\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2R = 2I^2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

**Result: total bridge conduction loss is independent of duty when synchronous rectification is used.**

### 2.2 Per-FET distribution

Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}I^2R \qquad P_{LS} = \frac{(1-D)+1}{3}I^2R = \frac{2-D}{3}I^2R$$

Check: 3·(P_HS + P_LS) = 2I²R ✓.

The low side is always the hotter device. Its worst case approaches 2/3 · I²R as D → 0, but high current does not occur at low duty on a prop load, so the realistic worst case is mid-to-high D.

### 2.3 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \quad T_j = T_{ref} + P(T_j)\cdot Z_{th}$$

Iterate to convergence. With the values here, it converges near R ≈ 2.25 mΩ.
