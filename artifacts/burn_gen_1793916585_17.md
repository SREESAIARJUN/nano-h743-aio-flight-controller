# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #49)

## 0. Architectural Ground Truth and Assumptions

**ArduPilot does not commutate motors.** In this stack:

- The **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot. It generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **Four ESC MCUs** run AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each handles commutation, the 48 kHz PWM, dead time and the DShot response. A typical part is the AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor.
- The power stage is **4 × 3-phase half-bridges**: 24 MOSFETs and 4 gate drivers.

No MOSFET or board size was specified. All numbers below use the following baseline. Swap in your actual datasheet values and the equations still hold.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_pk | 80 A |

| R_ds tempco | α | ≈0.0045 /K (→ ×1.45 at 125 °C) |

| Output charge | Q_oss | 60 nC |
| Total gate charge / drive voltage | Q_g / V_gs | 60 nC / 10 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |

| DC-link shunt | R_sh | 0.25 mΩ |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag:** "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm board. 80 A burst on 20×20 is not thermally credible; the analysis below shows why. I've modelled 30.5×30.5 mm as the minimum viable footprint. Also, 4 × 80 A is 320 A peak battery current, which exceeds what an XT60 and a 12 AWG lead can carry. The burst rating must be understood as **per-motor and non-simultaneous**, or the battery interface must be upsized (XT90, 10 AWG).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During any 60° sector:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_s and the low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) for (1−D)·T_s.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET conducts for 100 % of T_s.
- **Leg C:** floating (BEMF sense).

The instantaneous conduction loss is:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{eff}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{eff} = 2\,I^2 R_{eff}
$$

**Key result:** with synchronous rectification, conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**. This holds only if the ESC firmware runs complementary PWM, which AM32 and BLHeli_32 do by default.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 0.70 × 1.45 = 1.015 mΩ. The 1.0 mΩ R_eff (R_ds,hot plus copper) is therefore slightly optimistic. At high current it should be iterated with the thermal result in §4; that loop converges at about 1.05–1.1 mΩ.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{12.8\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

### 1.4 Shunt and trace loss

The DC-link current is I_dc ≈ D·I_ph. The worst case is D → 1:

$$
P_{sh} = (D\,I)^2 R_{sh} \le 80^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ W
$$

Use about 1.0 W at a realistic D ≈ 0.8.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

There is one hard-switched commutation (turn-on plus turn-off) per PWM period on the PWM leg. The return leg does not switch at f_sw.

### 2.1 V-I overlap loss

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The low-side body diode recovers when the high-side turns on.

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ W
$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 60\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ W
$$
