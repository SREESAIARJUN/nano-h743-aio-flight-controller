## 0. Architectural Clarifications

Three points shape the rest of the design:

1. **The STM32H743 cannot run the ESCs.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The correct architecture is:
   - **STM32H743VIT6** runs ArduPilot as the flight controller.
   - **4 × AT32F421K8U7** (or STM32G071) run AM32 firmware as the ESC MCUs, one per motor.
   - The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to each ESC MCU over on-board traces.
   - **Recommendation:** split into a stacked FC (20×20) and ESC (30.5×30.5 or 20×20) pair, or build a single "AIO" board. This document assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm stacked pair, because 20×20 cannot survive the 80A burst numbers below.

2. **"80A burst" must be defined.** In this document it means **80 A RMS phase current per motor for ≤ 2 s, with all 4 motors simultaneously, from 6S (25.2 V max).** The rest of the design depends on this definition.

3. **Battery current reality check.** Four motors at 80 A phase with duty D ≈ 0.85 draws I_bat ≈ 4·80·0.85 ≈ **272 A**.
   - This exceeds XT60 (60 A continuous) and even XT90 ratings.
   - The battery leads must be 10 AWG minimum.
   - Bulk capacitance must handle large ripple current (see §5.4).
   - If the actual requirement is "80A on one motor transiently", every thermal number below drops by about 4×.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any 60° sector, phase A is high-side PWM, phase B is low-side ON, and phase C floats.

- **PWM-on interval (D):** current flows from the bus through Q_AH, the motor, and Q_BL.
- **PWM-off interval (1−D):** current freewheels through Q_AL (synchronous rectifier), the motor, and Q_BL.

So at every instant, exactly **two switch positions** carry I_ph, independent of D. This is the key simplification of synchronous rectification:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 \cdot \frac{R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}{N_p}$$

### 2.2 Numerical values (per ESC, I_ph = 80 A)

| T_j | R_DS(on) per FET | R_eff per switch (N_p=2) | P_cond per ESC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 0.75 mΩ | 2·6400·0.75 m = **9.6 W** |
| 100 °C | 2.20 mΩ | 1.10 mΩ | **14.1 W** |
| 125 °C | 2.43 mΩ | 1.215 mΩ | **15.6 W** |

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each switch position conducts for 120° (1/3 of the time). The high-side position splits its conduction with the same-leg low-side according to D.

- **Average per position (125 °C):** P_cond,ESC / 6 = **2.6 W** → **1.3 W per FET**.
- **Peak instantaneous (low-side in continuous 60° ON):** I²·R/N_p = 6400·1.215 m = 7.8 W per position → 3.9 W per FET. The electrical period at typical eRPM is about 1–5 ms, far below FET thermal time constants (~100 ms+ to board). The **average** value therefore governs.
