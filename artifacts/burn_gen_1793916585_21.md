# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

**Document:** Job #8, Rev A, engineering baseline

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU running ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 class firmware (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071) and its own 3-phase gate driver. The H743 talks to those ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. Everything below uses this split.

**Baseline assumptions.** Change these and the equations still hold.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Operating mode.** The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) rectification, which is the AM32/BLHeli default. At any instant two phase legs carry current:

- one leg is PWM-switching (high side for duty D, low side for 1−D);
- one leg is the low-side return, on 100% of that 60° sector.

**PWM leg, high-side FET:**
$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\,D$$

**PWM leg, low-side synchronous FET:**
$$P_{LS,sync} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\,(1-D-2t_{dt}f_{sw})$$

**Return leg, low-side FET:**
$$P_{ret} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}$$

**Total bridge conduction** (the PWM leg always has exactly one FET on, ignoring dead time):
$$P_{cond} \approx 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot} + I^2 R_{cu}$$

**At 80 A:**
- FET term: P = 2 · 6400 · 1.12 mΩ = **14.3 W**
- Copper term: P_cu = 6400 · 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**
- **Total: 17.5 W per channel**

**Per-FET distribution over one electrical revolution.** Each FET conducts during 1/3 of the cycle as switch or return. Averaged, the 14.3 W spreads across 6 FETs, but the instantaneous peak per FET is ~7.2 W.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

**Gate currents during the Miller plateau:**
$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g}+R_{drv,src}} = \frac{7}{5.3} = 1.32\text{ A}$$
$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g+R_{drv,snk}} = \frac{3}{4.3} = 0.70\text{ A}$$

**Transition times**, with Q_sw = Q_gd + Q_gs2 = 17 nC:
$$t_r = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 12.9\text{ ns}, \qquad t_f = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 24.3\text{ ns}$$
