**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class firmware), which generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The two halves are modeled separately below.

**Feasibility flag.** 4 × 80 A on a nano-format board is a **burst** rating only, measured in seconds. Section 3 derives the actual allowable duration. Do not let marketing turn this into a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° electrical sector, one phase is PWM-switched (high side), one is held on (low side), and one floats. With synchronous rectification, phase current I_ph always flows through exactly two FETs: a high-side or its complementary low-side on the PWM leg, plus the static low side.

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)] \approx R_{25}\cdot k_T$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts during 2 of 6 sectors (120°):

| FET role in sector | Conduction loss |
|---|---|
| PWM leg, high side | D · I²R |
| PWM leg, low side (sync rectifier) | (1−D) · I²R |
| Static low side | I²R |

Averaged over one electrical revolution:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}}{3}$$
