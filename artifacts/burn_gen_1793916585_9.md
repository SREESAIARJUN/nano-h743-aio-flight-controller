## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate 4 motors itself. ArduPilot does not include motor-commutation firmware, so the system splits into two parts:

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz rev V / 400 MHz in ArduPilot default clock tree) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, emits bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 4× STM32G071 / AT32F421 class (one per motor) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM, returns eRPM telemetry |

This document models both halves.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.6\,\text{mΩ}\times(1 + 0.006\times85) = 2.42\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 2.2 Conduction topology (6-step, complementary PWM)

AM32 and BLHeli_32 use synchronous rectification. In every commutation sector, two phases are active:

- **PWM-on interval:** current flows through HS(A) and LS(B).
- **PWM-off interval:** current flows through LS(A) and LS(B). The low-side FET of phase A conducts synchronously, not through its body diode.

So two FETs are always in series with the motor current, regardless of duty D (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 2.3 Per-FET conduction loss

Each phase spends 120° high-side active, 120° low-side active, and 120° floating. The time fractions are:

| FET | Conduction fraction |
|---|---|
| High-side | $\frac{D}{3}$ |
| Low-side | $\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}$ |

At burst (D → 1):

$$P_{FET,HS} = P_{FET,LS} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3}$$

### 2.4 Numerical results at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

$$P_{cond,motor} = 6400 \times 2 \times 2.42\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{30.98\ W}$$

$$P_{FET} = 6400 \times 2.42\text{ mΩ}/3 = \mathbf{5.16\ W\ (average)}$$

Note that the instantaneous dissipation while a FET conducts is 15.5 W. This matters for the junction ripple calculation in §4.4.

**Copper loss:** Assume a 0.5 mΩ path through the phase trace, battery pad, and vias:

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\text{ mΩ} = 3.2\ \text{W per motor}$$

### 2.5 Battery vs. phase current

$$I_{phase} \approx \frac{I_{batt}}{D}$$

At part throttle, the phase current exceeds the battery current. An "80A" burst rating should be checked against **phase** current. This is the stress the FETs actually see.
