## 0. Architectural Correction and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does not commutate the MOSFETs. In every production AIO and 4-in-1 design:

- **The H743 (flight controller)** runs ArduPilot. It generates DShot600 frames and decodes the bidirectional eRPM telemetry.
- **Four ESC MCUs**, one per motor (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), run 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM.

The 48 kHz switching-loss model therefore applies to the ESC power stage
