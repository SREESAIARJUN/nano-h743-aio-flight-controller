## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

The ESC uses trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant within a 60° sector:

- **Leg A (switching leg):** high-side conducts for duty D, low-side for (1−D) (synchronous rectification).
- **Leg B (return leg):** low-side is on continuously for the sector.
- **Leg C:** floating (BEMF sensing).

Each FET serves as switching high-side, switching low-side, and static return for one-third of an electrical period on average.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss (80 A Burst)

The phase current is the DC current plus PWM ripple:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}}$$

Worst case is D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\times10^{-6}\times 48\times10^{3}} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$

The RMS of the current through the conducting path is:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.59 = 6403.6\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple contributes 0.06%, so it is negligible at 48 kHz. This is the main justification for 48 kHz over 24 kHz, where ripple would be 4× larger and audible.

**Instantaneous bridge conduction loss** has two FETs in series at all times, because the switching leg always has either the high side or the low side conducting:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{ret} \right] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The temperature-dependent resistance is:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per motor @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 80 °C | 1.25 mΩ | 16.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W |

**Electrothermal feedback.** Conduction loss rises with junction temperature. The stable operating point is found by iterating with the thermal model in §3. Use **125 °C: 18.6 W/motor** as the design-limit value.

**Per-FET average** (over a full electrical cycle, 6 FETs): 18.6 / 6 = **3.1 W**. The **peak instantaneous per-FET** loss (one FET in return mode for a full sector) is 80² × 1.45 mΩ = **9.3 W**.

At low eRPM (<~200 Hz electrical, e.g., a punch-out from idle), the sector dwell exceeds the FET thermal time constant (~ms). Design to the 9.3 W instantaneous figure for die-level checks.

**Copper and shunt add-ons (per motor):**

- Phase/bus copper, 2 oz × 4 layers paralleled, ~0.4 mΩ effective: 80² × 0.4 m = **2.6 W**.
- Current shunt. This applies only if per-motor sensing is used; a common bus shunt is assumed here (0.25 mΩ, 4 motors): P = (ΣI)² R. At 4 × 45 A = 180 A this gives **8.1 W**
