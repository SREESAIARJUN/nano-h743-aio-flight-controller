# Job #80: STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Nano FC
## Power-Stage Mathematical Model, DShot DMA Architecture, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Architecture Clarification

On every production 4-in-1 AIO board, the **STM32H743 runs ArduPilot (flight control)**. Commutation is handled by **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, one per motor, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 cannot commutate four sensorless BLDC motors at 48 kHz while also running EKF3. The model below therefore uses this split:

```
VBAT(6S) ─┬─► 4× [ESC MCU (AT32F421/STM32G071, AM32) → 3-ph gate driver → 6× N-FET bridge] → M1..M4
          └─► BEC 5V/10V ─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (1-wire/motor)──► ESC MCUs
```

**Key finding.** "80 A burst" is thermally achievable on a nano (25.5×25.5 mm) board **only for seconds**. With prop-wash cooling, the continuous rating is about 40 A per channel. The derivation is in §3.

## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

With synchronous (complementary) PWM in 6-step trapezoidal drive, two phases are energised at all times. Current flows through exactly **two FETs** (HS+LS during on-time, LS+LS during synchronous freewheel). The loss is therefore duty-independent:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.51 mΩ**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.51\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.3\ W}$$

**Electro-thermal coupling.** Because R_ds rises with T_j, solve iteratively, or use the closed form:

$$T_j = \frac{T_c + R_{\theta JC}\,I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_{\theta JC}\,I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}$$

Thermal runaway occurs if R_θJC · I² · R₂₅ · α ≥ 1. At 80 A this term is 1.5 × 6400 × 0.001 × 0.006 = **0.058**, so the design is stable.

**Board copper.** Add copper conduction from the battery pads to the FETs. With R_copper ≈ 0.3 mΩ shared bus resistance, the loss is (4 × I_avg)² × R. At 4 × 80 A this is about 31 W, distributed across the board. **This is why ≥ 2 oz inner power planes are mandatory.**

### 2.2 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Leg Only)

**Overlap loss:**
$$P_{ov} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ W$$

**C_oss loss (both FETs in the leg):**
$$P_{oss} = 2\times\tfrac12 C_{oss,eff}V^2 f_{sw} = 0.046\ W$$

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I \cdot 2t_{dead}\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
