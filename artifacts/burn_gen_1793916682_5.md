## 1. Inner Loop: Field-Oriented Current Control (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle (including an encoder offset found at alignment):

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off} \pmod{2\pi}$$

Clarke transform, amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

**Angle latency compensation.** The voltage computed in cycle k is applied over roughly 1.5 T_s later on average (computation delay plus ZOH). Use a predicted angle for the inverse Park:

$$\theta_e^{\,inv} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e$$

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

where $\lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$ (amplitude-invariant convention).

### 1.3 Current PI by Pole-Zero Cancellation

Choose $K_p = L\,\omega_{c}$ and $K_i = R\,\omega_{c}$. The PI zero at R/L then cancels the electrical pole, and the closed loop becomes first order:

$$\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Delay bound: with a total loop delay of about 1.5 T_s, keep $\omega_c \le \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{1.5T_s}$ to retain at least 60° of phase margin.

At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}$, so $K_p = 20.7$ V/A and $K_i = 51.8\times10^3$ V/(A·s).

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
