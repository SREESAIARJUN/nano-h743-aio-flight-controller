## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

**Architectural note.** ArduPilot running on the STM32H743 does not commutate motors directly. The H743 is the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs that run AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay. Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The design is therefore:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 x4──► 4× ESC MCU ──► 4× 3-ph gate driver ──► 24× N-MOSFET ──► 4 motors
```

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst case for switching) |
| Burst phase current (per motor) | I_ph | 80 A (defined as motor/phase current, not battery current) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET | — | [40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R_ds(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ @ V_gs=10 V, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_gd = 6 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, E_oss = 0.5 µJ, V_SD = 0.8 V, R_θJC = 1.2 K/W] |
| R_ds(on) tempco | α | 0.005 /K (linear approximation) |
| Rise/fall time (with driver + R_g) | t_r, t_f | 20 ns each |
| Dead time | t_dt | 150 ns per edge |
| Commutation | — | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM |
| Board | — | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz Cu (≈70 µm) |
| Ambient / start temp | T_a | 40 °C |
| Junction limit (design) | T_j,max | 125 °C (MOSFETs rated 150–175 °C, derated) |

> **Feasibility flag up front.** At a 20×20 "nano" footprint, 80 A per motor cannot be sustained. Even at 30×30 it is a short-burst rating only. The thermal model in §3 quantifies exactly how short.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

Because conduction loss raises T_j, which raises R_ds, the loss must be solved self-consistently (§3.4).

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct:
- The **PWM leg** has its high side on for duty D and its low side (synchronous rectifier) on for 1−D. One FET always carries I_ph.
- The **return leg** has its low side on continuously and carries I_ph.

So at every instant two channels carry I_ph, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.3 PCB copper conduction

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,L}{w\,t}, \quad \rho_{Cu}(T) = 1.72\times10^{-8}[1+0.0039(T-20)]$$

Take a phase path of L ≈ 15 mm, w = 4 mm, and 4 paralleled 2 oz layers (t_eff = 280 µm) at 100 °C:
R_Cu ≈ 2.26e-8 × 0.015 / (0.004 × 280e-6) ≈ **0.30 mΩ**. The current crosses two such paths, so:

$$P_{Cu,motor} = 2 I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu} \approx 2 \times 6400 \times 0.30\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.8\ W}$$

Add battery-side bus copper separately. That loss scales with I_batt = D·I_ph, not I_ph.
