# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #17): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** On every practical 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller only. Each ESC channel runs its own motor-control MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 on an STM32G071 or AT32F421 class part). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and answers bidirectional DShot600. The H743 does not drive the gate drivers directly. All three sections below are partitioned that way.

**MOSFET parameters.** No part number was specified, so the analysis uses a representative 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3 MOSFET class. Substitute your datasheet values. The equations are general.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 115 °C this gives 1.2 mΩ × 1.585 = **1.90 mΩ**. Use T_j ≈ 115 to 125 °C for burst design.

### 1.2 Six-step current path

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is the source, one is the sink, and one floats.

- The source-phase high-side (HS) FET conducts for a fraction D of each PWM period.
- The source-phase low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1−D), as the synchronous freewheel.
- The sink-phase LS FET conducts 100% of the sector.

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as source and 1/3 as sink. That gives these per-FET averages:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,D}{3}$$

$$P_{LS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[(1-D)+1\big]}{3}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 3(P_{HS}+P_{LS}) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The per-channel conduction loss is independent of duty cycle.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 115 °C, D = 0.9

The common term is I²R = 6400 × 1.90 mΩ = 12.2 W.

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P_HS per FET | 12.2 × 0.9/3 | **3.66 W** |
| P_LS per FET | 12.2 × 1.1/3 | **4.47 W** |
| P_cond per channel | 2 × 12.2 | **24.4 W** |
| P_cond, all four channels | 4 × 24.4 | **97.6 W** |
