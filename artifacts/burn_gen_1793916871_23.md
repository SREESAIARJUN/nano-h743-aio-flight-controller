**Architecture assumption.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware. A typical choice is an AT32F421 or STM32G071 per channel, driving a 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-FETs. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly.
