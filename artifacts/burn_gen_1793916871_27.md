| Parameter | Value assumed |

### Feasibility warning
At "nano" scale (20×20 mm), 80 A per channel is not physically sustainable. Even at 30.5×30.5 mm it is a **transient rating of a few seconds** (see §2.4). The result in §2 shows roughly 90 W of dissipation with all four motors at burst. That much heat cannot be removed from a board this size in steady state. I recommend specifying:

- **80 A burst, ≤ 3 s, 50% duty-cycle recovery**
- **40–45 A continuous with airflow**

If the spec truly calls for 20×20 mm, re-rate to roughly 35–40 A burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds ≈ 1.55 mΩ. I use **1.5 mΩ hot** below.

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° commutation step, two phases carry current I. One is the PWM'd phase; the other is the phase held on.

- With synchronous rectification, each active phase always has exactly one FET conducting: the high-side (HS) during the on-time and the low-side (LS) during the off-time.
- Each phase leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle.

Per-leg conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,leg} = \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This is split between the two FETs by duty cycle D:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{2}{3}\, D\, I^2 R_{ds}, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{2}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R_{ds}$$

Per channel (3 legs):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Quantity | 25 °C (1.0 mΩ) | 125 °C (1.5 mΩ) |
|---|---|---|
| Per leg | 4.27 W | 6.40 W |
| Worst single FET (HS at D→1) | 4.27 W | **6.40 W** |
| Per channel | 12.8 W | **19.2 W** |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad/trace, 2 oz, ~5 mm × 3 mm, ~0.15 mΩ, ×2 conducting | 0.15 mΩ each | 2·I²·0.15 mΩ = **1.9 W** |
| Shared current shunt per 4-in-1 (0.25 mΩ), carrying total battery current | 0.25 mΩ | Duty-dependent: I_bat ≈ D·ΣI_phase. At D = 0.9 with 4×80 A, I_bat ≈ 288 A → **20.7 W** |

The shunt result matters. Do not use a single 0.25 mΩ shunt at this current. Use either 0.1 mΩ split across two parallel parts (~8 W, still hot), or sense current per phase from the gate-driver / LS FET instead.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches hard in each step, so one hard-switching leg is active at any time per channel.

### 2.1 Loss terms

**Hard-switching overlap (V–I crossover):**
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12 (25.2)(80)(40\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = V_{bus} Q_{oss} f_{sw} = 25.2 \cdot 40\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Body-diode conduction during dead time (2 edges per period):**
$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**
$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \cdot 30\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

**Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors):**
$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 60\text{n} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Summary per channel at 80 A, 25.2 V

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| Crossover | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_oss + Q_rr | 0.08 |
| Gate | 0.06 |
| **Switching total** | **≈ 2.7 W** |
| Conduction (hot) | 19.2 |
| Copper | 1.9 |
| **Channel total (excl. shunt)** | **≈ 23.8 W** |
| **4 channels** | **≈ 95 W** (+ shunt) |

**Sensitivity:** Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. Moving from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs about 1.35 W per channel. Conduction dominates by roughly 7:1, so **R_ds(on) and copper are the levers, not frequency**.

### 2.3 Thermal network

Use a Foster/Cauer approximation:

```
P_FET ──[R_jc 1.2 K/W]──●──[R_cs 2.5 K/W, pad+vias]──● Board node (C_board) ──[R_ba]── T_amb
                       C_die≈0.02 J/K                  C_board≈4 J/K
```

- **R_ba (whole board to ambient):** about 12 K/W in still air, about 3.5 K/W under prop-wash.
- **Board heat capacity:** ~3.5 g Cu × 0.385 + ~3 g FR4/components × 0.9 ≈ **4 J/K**.
