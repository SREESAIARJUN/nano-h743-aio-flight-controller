## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 0.80\,\text{mΩ}\times(1+0.007\times100)=1.36\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Current path in six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, current flows through exactly two half-bridge positions:

- **Driven phase:** The high side conducts for duty D. The low-side synchronous FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return phase:** The low side is continuously on.

So at every instant **two FETs are in series with I_ph, independent of D**:

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\times80^2\times1.36\times10^{-3}=17.4\ \text{W}$$

**Per-FET split.** Each phase is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. Over a full revolution:

$$P_{FET,avg}\approx\frac{17.4}{6}=2.9\ \text{W}$$
