## 0. Feasibility Issues to Resolve First

These issues change the design, so they come before the math.

| # | Issue | Consequence | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The H743 cannot run ArduPilot and also commutate four motors at 48 kHz. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation code. | A single-MCU design is not viable. | **H743 runs ArduPilot.** Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, AM32 firmware) generate the 48 kHz PWM. | 2 | "Nano" (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm) with 4 × 80 A means 320 A of battery current. | Exceeds XT60/XT90 ratings, PCB copper limits, and the battery C-rating of any nano-class airframe. | 80 A is treated as a **per-channel, single-motor, ≤ 2 s burst**. ## 1. Electrical Operating Point and Assumptions

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V<sub>bus</sub> | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst case for switching) |
| Burst phase current | I | 80 A (DC-equivalent during a 6-step conduction interval) |
| PWM frequency | f<sub>sw</sub> | 48 kHz |
| R<sub>DS(on)</sub> at 25 °C | R<sub>25</sub> | 1.0 mΩ |
| R<sub>DS(on)</sub> temperature coefficient | k(T<sub>j</sub>) | ≈ 1 + 0.0055·(T<sub>j</sub> − 25) → 1.55× at 125 °C |
| Current rise + fall time | t<sub>r</sub> + t<sub>f</sub> | 20 ns + 20 ns at 80 A (gate driver ~2 A peak, R<sub>g,ext</sub> ≈ 2.2 Ω) |
| Dead time | t<sub>d</sub> | 200 ns per edge |
| Body diode forward voltage | V<sub>F</sub> | 0.8 V at 80 A |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q<sub>rr</sub> | 60 nC |
| Output charge | Q<sub>oss</sub> | 50 nC at 25 V |
| Total gate charge | Q<sub>g</sub> | 100 nC at V<sub>GS</sub> = 10 V |
| Copper path resistance per channel | R<sub>cu</sub> | 0.5 mΩ (2 oz outer + 2 oz inner, bus bars soldered) |

**Commutation scheme:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During any sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty fraction D. The low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for 1 − D, minus dead time.
- **Static leg:** the LS FET conducts 100 %.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

Conduction loss per channel, summed over the conducting FETs:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D) + 1\,] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of D, because synchronous rectification shifts the loss between the HS and LS FETs rather than removing it.

Commutation rotates every 60° electrical, so each FET carries current for ⅓ of the electrical period on average. **Within a burst sector, however, the instantaneous worst FET is the static LS FET.** That is the one used for the junction calculation.

$$P_{FET,max} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Numbers at 80 A:**

| T<sub>j</sub> | R<sub>DS</sub> | P<sub>cond</sub> per channel | Hottest FET (instantaneous) | Rotation-averaged per FET |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W | 2.13 W |
| 125 °C | 1.55 mΩ | **19.8 W** | **9.9 W** | 3.3 W |

**Copper loss:**

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

per channel. This heat is distributed in the board, not in the junctions.

**Electrothermal coupling.** Conduction loss feeds back into temperature through R<sub>DS(on)</sub>:

$$P_{cond}(T_j) = 2I^2R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad T_j = T_{ref} + P_{FET}\,Z_{th}$$

The loop is stable if:

$$\alpha\,I^2 R_{25}\,Z_{th} < 1$$

With α = 0.0055 and I²R₂₅ = 6.4 W, this holds for Z<sub>th</sub> < 28 K/W. The condition is satisfied, so there is no thermal runaway at the junction level.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Hard-switched inductive load, evaluated per channel. Only the PWM leg switches. There are two transitions per PWM period: one turn-on and one turn-off.

**(a) V·I overlap loss (HS FET):**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery** (LS body diode, injected into the HS FET at turn-on):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 60\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.073\ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

This is a conservative bound. It counts both the HS and LS C<sub>oss</sub> charge exchange.

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (LS FET, two edges per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_F I \cdot 2t_d \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

This term is significant. Trimming dead time to 100 ns saves 0.6 W per channel. Do this only after confirming shoot-through margin with the selected driver's propagation-delay mismatch.

**(e) Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and R<sub>g</sub>, not in the FET):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 100\text{ nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.096\ W$$

**Switching subtotal (FET-dissipated):** 1.94 + 0.07 + 0.06 + 1.23 = **3.30 W per channel.**
