# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack: Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Model and DShot DMA Plan

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Three architectural points shape the rest of the document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, ArduPilot runs on the H743. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743. The H743's only motor-related job is bidirectional DShot600 (Section 4).
2. **"80 A burst" is a thermal-time problem, not a silicon problem.** The math below shows a nano board cannot hold 80 A on all four channels for more than about 2–4 s without a heatsink. Ratings must state burst duration and how many channels are loaded simultaneously.
