## 1. System Architecture

```
          6S ──┬── TVS + 4×470µF low-ESR + 16×10µF/50V MLCC ── VBAT bus (per-channel local bulk)
               │
   ┌───────────┴───────────┐        BDShot600 (bidirectional, 1-wire, 3.3 V)
   │ STM32H743VIH6 (FC)    │──M1──► AT32F421 #1 ─► FD6288Q ─► 6× FET ─► Motor 1
   │ ArduPilot             │──M2──► AT32F421 #2 ─► FD6288Q ─► 6× FET ─► Motor 2
   │ 400/480 MHz           │──M3──► AT32F421 #3 ─► ...
   │                       │──M4──► AT32F421 #4 ─► ...
   │                       │◄─ CURR (INA/shunt amp), VBAT divider
   └───────────────────────┘
```

The H743 should sit on its own copper island, connected to the power section only through a stitched GND and a slotted thermal break. Its Tj limit depends on the part suffix: 125 °C, or 140 °C for the high-temperature "3" grade. Verify the suffix on the BOM.
