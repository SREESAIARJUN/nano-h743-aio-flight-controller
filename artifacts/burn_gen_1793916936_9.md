## 0. Architectural Ground Truth and Scope Corrections

Three assumptions need to be fixed before the math, because each one changes the model.

| Assumption in brief | Engineering reality | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" | The H743 runs ArduPilot (FC). Commutation runs on **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 cannot also close four 48 kHz commutation loops alongside ArduPilot. | Two-tier architecture: FC → DShot → 4× ESC MCU → gate drivers → 12 half-bridges. |
| "Nano" form factor | 80 A/motor burst is physically impossible on 20×20 mm. Twelve FETs, bulk capacitance and 2 oz+ power copper need **≥ 30.5×30.5 mm**, 6–8 layers. | The board is sized 30.5×30.5 mm. "Nano" here means low profile, not 20×20. |
| "80 A burst" | Thermal mass, not FET rating, sets the limit. Section 3 shows the realistic burst window is **~2 s**. | Firmware current limit plus a thermal model are required. |

**Design point:**
- 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal.
- 48 kHz complementary PWM, 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation.
- 40 V N-ch MOSFETs in PDFN 3.3×3.3.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Reference FET class (40 V, PDFN3.3, e.g. ~1.0 mΩ typ / 1.3 mΩ max @ 10 V V_GS):

| T_j (°C) | R_DS (max), mΩ |
|---|---|
| 25 | 1.30 |
| 100 | 1.89 |
| 125 | 2.08 |
| 150 | 2.28 |

Design value: **R_DS = 2.0 mΩ** (hot, max).

### 1.2 Conduction topology

With complementary (synchronous) PWM in 6-step:
- During the **ON interval** (D): current flows through HS(phase A) and LS(phase B).
- During the **freewheel interval** (1−D): current flows through LS(phase A) (synchronous rectification) and LS(phase B).

Two FETs are always in series with the phase current, so:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

**FET conduction at 80 A:**

$$P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.002 = 25.6\ \text{W per ESC channel}$$

**Copper and shunt contribution** (R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ for a 2 oz × 4 parallel-layer path plus a 0.2 mΩ shunt):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.0005 = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution (worst-case device)

Each phase conducts 120° of every 360°. The hottest FET is the PWM-switching high-side:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{1}{3}\left[ D \cdot I^2 R_{DS} \right] + P_{sw}$$

$$P_{LS} = \frac{1}{3}\left[ I^2 R_{DS} \right] + \frac{1}{3}\left[(1-D) I^2 R_{DS}\right]$$

At full burst (D → 0.95):
- P_LS,cond ≈ (1/3)(80²)(0.002)(1.05) ≈ **4.5 W**
- P_HS,cond ≈ (1/3)(80²)(0.002)(0.95) ≈ **4.05 W**, plus switching loss (Section 2)
