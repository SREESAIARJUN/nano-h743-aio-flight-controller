## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Electrical angle.** θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset) mod 2π

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

  i_α = i_a
  i_β = (i_a + 2·i_b)/√3

**Park transform:**

  i_d = i_α·cos θₑ + i_β·sin θₑ
  i_q = −i_α·sin θₑ + i_β·cos θₑ

**Inverse Park transform:**

  v_α = v_d·cos θₑ − v_q·sin θₑ
  v_β = v_d·sin θₑ + v_q·cos θₑ

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

The dq-frame voltage equations are:

  v_d = R·i_d + L·(di_d/dt) − ωₑ·L·i_q
  v_q = R·i_q + L·(di_q/dt) + ωₑ·L·i_d + ωₑ·λ_m

Adding decoupling feedforward leaves two identical first-order plants, G(s) = 1/(L·s + R):

  v_d* = PI_d(i_d* − i_d) − ωₑ·L·i_q
  v_q* = PI_q(i_q* − i_q) + ωₑ·L·i_d + ωₑ·λ_m
