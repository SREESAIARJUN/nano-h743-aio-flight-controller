# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

## 0. Assumptions

I don't have the original Job #3 specification. The design below uses the typical brushless-gimbal assumptions listed here. Every value marked **[tune]** or **[verify]** should be checked against your hardware.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class with single-precision FPU |

## 1. Control Architecture

```
            ┌─────────────── IMU B (frame) ──► dynamic notch freq estimate (ANF)
            │                     │
            │                     ▼ ω_frame (filtered)
 q_ref ──► [Attitude P] ─► ω_cmd ─►[Inverse Jacobian + frame FF]─► θ̇_ref (per joint)
   ▲                                                                    │
   │ q̂ (Mahony)                                                        ▼
 IMU A (payload) ─► AAF ─► dyn. notch ─► LPF ─► ω_axis ──►[Rate PI + anti-windup]
                                                                        │ τ_ref
                                                                        ▼
                                  encoder ─► θ_e ─► [Cogging FF] ─► + ─► i_q,ref
                                                                        ▼
                                                    [FOC current PI 20 kHz] ─► SVPWM
```

**Key design choice.** For stabilization, the rate loop is closed on the **inertial rate about each motor axis**, measured by the payload gyro. It is not closed on encoder velocity, which is rate relative to the frame. The encoder is used for three things only:

- Commutation.
- Cogging compensation.
- The joint-angle Jacobian.

The frame IMU removes base motion *before* it becomes error, by acting as feedforward.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Clarke / Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},
\qquad \theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}})
$$

### 2.2 Motor model (d/q frame, surface PMSM)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L \dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 2.3 PI with pole-zero cancellation

The plant is $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$. Choose:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

This places the zero at $R/L$ and gives the closed-loop transfer $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi \cdot 1\text{ kHz}$.
