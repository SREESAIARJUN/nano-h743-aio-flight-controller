# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #11): Loss, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

Two points need to be settled before the model will hold up in design review.

**1. The H743 is not the ESC controller.** In a stacked or AIO design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The DShot link runs from H743 timers to the ESC MCUs. Commutation and the 48 kHz PWM happen on the ESC MCUs. The model below follows this split.

**2. "80A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is not credible without a strict time limit.** The numbers in §3 show that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 85–90 W. A 20×20 board cannot sustain that for more than a fraction of a second. I model a **30.5×30.5 mm** board, give the 20×20 result for comparison, and specify burst limits.

All component values are **example parameters**. They must be replaced with datasheet values for the actual FETs, driver, and stackup before sign-off.

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

The on-resistance rises with temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega\times1.5=1.5\,\text{m}\Omega$$

In trapezoidal commutation, two phases conduct at any instant. With synchronous rectification, the current always flows through exactly one FET in each active half-bridge, either the high-side FET or the complementary low-side FET. The conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond}=I^2\cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)=80^2\times 3.0\,\text{m}\Omega=\mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Notes:
- Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs averages about 19.2/6 = 3.2 W of conduction loss.
- The instantaneous loss in a conducting FET is 9.6 W. This value sets the per-device junction rise.
- Copper loss is significant and must be added: 2 oz copper has a sheet resistance of about 0.25 mΩ/□. Four squares of pour per path is about 1 mΩ, which adds **about 6.4 W per channel** at 80 A. **This is often underestimated.**

### 2.2 Hard-Switching Loss

Only one half-bridge is PWMing at a time, with one hard edge pair per period:

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=0.5(25.2)(80)(40\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz})=\mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.3 Secondary Switching Terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Dead-time diode conduction | $V_f I\,(2t_{dead}) f_{sw}$ | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | $Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}$ | **0.06 W** |
| C_oss discharge | $\tfrac12 C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}$ | **0.02 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | $2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}$ | **0.05 W** (mostly in the driver and R_g) |

### 2.4 Channel and Board Totals at 80 A, 48 kHz, T_j = 125 °C

| | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching (all terms) | 2.7 W | 10.7 W |
| Copper (estimate) | 6.4 W | 25.6 W |
| **Total** | **≈28.3 W** | **≈113 W** |

**Sensitivity:**
- Conduction loss dominates at 48 kHz, so FET R_ds selection matters far more than switching speed.
- Lowering f_sw to 24 kHz saves only about 1.3 W per channel.
- Paralleling FETs (2 per switch, 12 per channel) halves P_cond to 9.6 W per channel. This is the standard approach for true 80 A-class ESCs.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (Cauer, Simplified)

```
P_FET ─[Rth_jc 1.0 K/W]─┬─[Rth_spread 2–4 K/W]─┬─[Rth_board-air]── T_amb
                        Cth_FET ≈0.05 J/K       Cth_board
```

**Board-to-air resistance:**
- 30.5×30.5 mm, 6-layer 2 oz, in prop wash: about 4–6 K/W.
- Still air: about 15–20 K/W.
- 20×20 mm: about 1.8× worse.

**Board heat capacity:**
- 30.5 mm board: copper about 4 g, FR4 about 4 g, FETs and capacitors about 3 g, giving **C_th ≈ 3–4 J/K**.
- 20×20 mm board: about 1.5 J/K.

### 3.2 Steady State Is Impossible at 80 A

$$\Delta T_{ss}=P\cdot R_{th,ba}=113\,\text{W}\times5\,\text{K/W}\approx565\ \text{K}$$

80 A is therefore a **burst-only** rating, governed by board thermal mass.

### 3.3 Burst Duration (Near-Adiabatic, First Order)

$$\Delta T_{board}(t)=P R_{th}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right),\quad \tau=R_{th}C_{th}\approx 5\times3.5=17.5\ \text{s}$$

For t ≪ τ, the board heats at approximately $dT/dt \approx P/C_{th}$.

| Board | dT/dt (4 ch × 80 A) | Time from 50 °C to 105 °C board |
|---|---|---|
| 30.5×30.5 | 113/3.5 ≈ 32 K/s | **≈1.7 s** |
| 20×20 nano | 113/1.5 ≈ 75 K/s | **≈0.7 s** |

### 3.4 Junction Rise Above the Board

Each conducting FET dissipates 9.6 W of conduction loss. The FET that is PWMing also carries about 2.7 W of switching loss, for about 12.3 W worst case:

$$\Delta T_{j-b}\approx 12.3\,\text{W}\times(1.0+2.5)\,\text{K/W}\approx 43\ \text{K}$$
