# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #31)

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three findings change the scope before any math:

1. **The H743 cannot also be the ESC.** ArduPilot on the H743 does not commutate motors. A 4-in-1 needs four dedicated ESC MCUs, typically AM32 on STM32G071 or AT32F421, or BLHeli_32. Bi-directional DShot is the link between the H743 and those four MCUs. The design below uses this split.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is thermally limited to seconds, not a continuous rating.** Section 3 quantifies this. Treat 80 A as a ~3–5 s per-motor rating with all four motors loaded.
3. **The device values below are representative, not vendor-verified.** They are typical of 40 V-class 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs used in 6S ESCs. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values. The model is written so you only re-plug numbers.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Instantaneous Path

In 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, current always flows through exactly two FETs per motor:

- During the ON portion (D): one high-side FET and one low-side FET.
- During the OFF portion (1−D): the complementary low-side FET plus the static low-side FET.

So the series resistance is always 2·R_DS(on), independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Hot-Die Resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) ≈ R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.006/K, giving k_T ≈ 1.6 at 125 °C.

R_hot = 1.0 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**

### 1.3 Loss per Motor and Board

P_cond,motor = I_ph² · 2 · R_hot = 80² × 2 × 1.6×10⁻³ = **20.48 W**

P_cond,board = 4 × 20.48 = **81.9 W**

### 1.4 Distribution per FET

Each FET carries current for 120° electrical out of 360° (1/3 of the time). Two FETs conduct at any instant, so:

P_cond,FET,avg = 20.48 / 6 = **3.41 W**

Above roughly 200 Hz electrical frequency, the die's thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) smooths the 120° pulses. Below that, for example at startup or stall, the effective per-FET loss approaches 6.8 W during its conduction window.
