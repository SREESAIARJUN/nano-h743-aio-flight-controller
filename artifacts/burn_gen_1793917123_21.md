The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running 6-step BLDC at f_sw = 48 kHz. The H743 generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. This separation shapes the whole design: the power model belongs to the ESC, and the timer/DMA model belongs to the FC.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, exactly two phases carry current: one leg is PWM-switched and one leg's low side is held on.

- **During D·T_sw:** HS(A) is on and LS(B) is on. Two FETs are in series.
- **During (1−D)·T_sw:** LS(A) is on (synchronous freewheel) and LS(B) is on. Again two FETs are in series.

So the conduction path always contains **2 × R_DS(on)**, independent of duty cycle. Assuming continuous, low-ripple phase current (I_rms ≈ I_phase):

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, \frac{R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}{N_{par}} + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

with

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction

Phase inductor ripple adds to the RMS current:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph,LL}\, f_{sw}}
$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_LL ≈ 20 µH), at 48 kHz and D ≈ 0.5, ΔI ≈ 6–13 A. The ripple term adds less than 1 % to P_cond at 80 A, so it is neglected below.
