## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 is a flight controller MCU. It does not commutate motors. The board therefore splits into two domains:

| Domain | Device | Role |

| Power stage ×4 | 6× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN3.3×3.3 | 3 half-bridges per motor |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (trapezoidal, complementary PWM)

During any 60° sector, one high-side FET (phase X) and one low-side FET (phase Y) form the path.

- With synchronous rectification, during PWM-off time the current freewheels through the low-side of phase X instead of its body diode.
- At any instant, therefore, **exactly two channels carry the motor current I_m**.

The battery current is:

$$I_{bat} = D \cdot I_m$$

### 1.2 Per-FET RMS currents

Each phase leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical period: 1/3 sourcing and 1/3 sinking.

**High-side FET of a leg:**
$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I_m^2$$

**Low-side FET of the same leg** (its own 1/3 sink plus synchronous freewheel during the HS off-time):
$$I_{LS,rms}^2 = \tfrac{1}{3} I_m^2 + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D) I_m^2$$

**Total conduction loss for one ESC (6 FETs):**
$$P_{cond} = 3\,R_{ds,hot}\left[\tfrac{1}{3}D + \tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\right] I_m^2 = 2\,R_{ds,hot}\, I_m^2$$

This is independent of D, which is consistent with two channels always being in the path.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$R_{ds,hot} = 1.6 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega$$
$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 2.4\text{m} \times 80^2 = \mathbf{30.7\ W\ per\ ESC}$$
