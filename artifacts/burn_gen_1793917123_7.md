## 0. System Architecture & Assumptions

```
 6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──► 4× 3-phase bridges (24 MOSFETs) ──► 4 motors
                              ▲ gate drivers ×4
                              ▲ ESC MCU ×4 (AM32 / BLHeli_32 / Bluejay – must support bidir DShot)
                              ▲ DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor)
 STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ───────┘    TIM1 CH1–CH4, ESC telem UART, current/voltage ADC
```

**Important clarification:** the H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only generates DShot frames and decodes eRPM telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.004\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_hot = 1.0 mΩ × 1.40–1.6 ≈ **1.6 mΩ** (use the datasheet's normalized curve; 1.6 is conservative).

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM-driven (high side for D, synchronous low side for 1−D), phase B low side is held ON, and phase C floats.

- PWM leg: $I^2 R \cdot D + I^2 R\,(1-D) = I^2 R$
- Static low side: $I^2 R$

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}\quad\text{(independent of duty under sync rectification)}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{20.5\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-MOSFET distribution

Each FET conducts approximately 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx \mathbf{3.4\ W\ average}$$

The instantaneous peak is 10.2 W. At burst eRPM, electrical frequency is about 1–3 kHz, which is fast compared with the ~ms die/package thermal time constant, so the average value governs junction temperature.

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 \cdot R_{trace},\qquad R_{trace} = \frac{\rho\,L}{w\,t\,n_{layers}}$$

| Element | Assumption | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper | 4 × 2 oz layers, ~0.15 mΩ effective path | ~1.0 W |
| Current shunt (if per-channel) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |

## 2. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg's FETs switch hard; the static low side does not.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching, high side)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery (low-side body diode → high-side turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver/R_g)

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} = 2\times50\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total

$$P_{sw,ch} \approx 0.97 + 0.036 + 0.048 + 0.61 + 0.048 \approx \mathbf{1.71\ W}$$

The dead-time term is the second-largest loss. AM32 and BLHeli_32 dead-time tuning is therefore worth real watts.
