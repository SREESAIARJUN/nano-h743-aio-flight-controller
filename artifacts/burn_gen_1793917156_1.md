## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

At any instant exactly **two switch positions carry the phase current I** (one high-side path, one low-side path). Each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the electrical period. With synchronous rectification, the PWM-off interval routes current through the complementary FET's channel rather than its body diode, so conduction is continuous apart from dead time.

**Per channel:**
$$P_{cond,ch}=2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Per FET (time-averaged, D → 1 at burst):**
$$P_{cond,FET}\approx \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{3}$$

### 1.3 Electrothermal fixed-point solution

Junction temperature depends on loss, and loss depends on R_DS(T_j). Solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{board} + R_{th,jb}\,P_{FET}(T_j),\qquad R_{th,jb}\approx 4\ \text{K/W}$$

The R_th,jb value assumes the exposed pad sits on a via-stitched 2 oz plane.
