**Architectural note.** The H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no sensorless FOC/6-step ESC firmware for the H7, and 4×48 kHz commutation with BEMF zero-crossing would compete with the EKF and scheduler. The standard partition is used here:

- The **H743** runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESCs.
- **Four small ESC MCUs** (AM32) on the same PCB drive the gate drivers.

This partition is also what makes bidirectional DShot meaningful.
