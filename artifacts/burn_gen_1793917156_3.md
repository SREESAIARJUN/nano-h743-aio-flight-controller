## 0. Corrections and Feasibility Flags

**F1. Architecture partition.**
- The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot.
- It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, typically:
  - 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or
  - 1× quad-capable MCU running dedicated firmware.
- The H743 cannot run ESC firmware alongside ArduPilot.
- The model below follows this partition.

**F2. "80 A burst" on a nano board.**
- 80 A per channel × 4 channels on 6S is about 8 kW peak.
- The board-level loss at that point is about 75 W (derived below).
- A 20×20 mm board cannot sink this. Even a 30.5×30.5 mm board survives it only adiabatically, for a few seconds.
- 80 A is therefore treated strictly as a **time-limited burst rating**. The burst duration is derived, not assumed.

**F3. Datasheet values.**
- All MOSFET and driver parameters below are representative for a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm or 5×6 mm PQFN part.
- Before release, replace them with the chosen part's datasheet values, including the R_DS(on)(T_j) curve, Q_gd, Q_rr and R_θJC.
- The equations are general. The numbers are a worked instance.
