## 0. Architecture Decision and Stated Assumptions

### 0.1 The H743 should not commutate the motors directly

Driving four sensorless BLDC motors needs 4 × 3 = 12 complementary pairs, which is 24 dead-time-controlled outputs. It also needs 12 BEMF comparator paths and per-motor zero-cross timing. The H743 cannot provide this:

- **TIM1/TIM8** provide only 6 complementary pairs between them.
- **HRTIM** provides 10 outputs, with no BEMF comparators mapped to it.

The H743 would also have to run the 400 Hz–8 kHz flight loop on the same core.
