## 0. Scope, Architecture Clarification, and Stated Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In a 4-in-1 AIO the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs commutate the FET bridges at 48 kHz PWM. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The analysis therefore splits into two independent domains:

| Domain | Owner | Analyzed in |
|---|---|---|
| Power stage losses and thermal | ESC MCU + gate driver + 24 MOSFETs | §1–§4 |
| DShot600 bidir, timers, DMA | STM32H743 / ArduPilot | §5 |
| Interconnect | Netlist | §6 |
