## 1. Conduction Model

### 1.1 Current Paths in 6-Step Commutation

In each 60° electrical sector, exactly two phases carry current I<sub>ph</sub>:

- **PWM phase:**
  - High side (HS) conducts during D·T<sub>sw</sub>.
  - Low side (LS) conducts synchronously during (1−D)·T<sub>sw</sub>.
- **Return phase:** the LS is on for the whole sector.

Each switch position is active for 1/3 of the electrical period. The per-FET RMS currents are:

$$
I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{D}{3} I_{ph}^2 ,\qquad
I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{1}{3}\Big[(1-D) + 1\Big]\frac{I_{ph}^2}{2}\cdot 2
$$

Averaged across the six FETs of one channel, there are always exactly two R<sub>DS(on)</sub> drops in the current path. That gives the compact result:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}
$$

The temperature dependence of R<sub>DS(on)</sub> is:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

### 1.2 Numerical Result at 80 A

| T<sub>j</sub> | R<sub>DS(on)</sub> | P<sub>cond,ch</sub> |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 0.0010 = **12.8 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 0.0016 = **20.5 W** |

The average per FET at 125 °C is about 3.4 W. At high duty, the worst-case HS position carries close to 1/3 of the I²R product.

### 1.3 Copper / Shunt Parasitics (Per Channel)

**Phase pad + via + trace resistance (~0.25 mΩ effective, 2 oz outer + 2 oz inner pours):**

$$
P_{Cu} = I_{ph}^2 \times 0.25\text{ m}\Omega \approx 1.6\text{ W}
$$

**Shunt (0.25 mΩ, in the low-side return), if it is in the path:**

$$
P_{shunt} = (D\cdot I_{ph})^2 \cdot R_{sh} \approx 1.6\text{ W at } D\approx 1
$$

**Electrothermal stability check.** R<sub>DS(on)</sub> rises with T<sub>j</sub>, so the loop gain is:

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\cdot R_{th} = 2 I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\, R_{th}
$$

This must stay below 1 to avoid thermal runaway. At 80 A with R<sub>th,ja,ch</sub> = 8 K/W:

$$
2 \times 6400 \times 0.001 \times 0.006 \times 8 = 0.61
$$

The loop is stable, but with little margin. This is another reason 80 A must be a burst rating only.
