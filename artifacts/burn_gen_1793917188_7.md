# STM32H743 Nano AIO + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #65): Power-Stage, Thermal and Bidirectional DShot600 Architecture Model

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility

This document sets out the equations, parameters and allocations as a reviewable design baseline. Treat every component value as a placeholder until you substitute the selected MOSFET datasheet, PCB stack-up and measured data. **All results must be validated by bench measurement** (Section 7).

**Feasibility finding:** sustaining 80 A on all four channels at once is not physically achievable on a 20×20 mm (nano) board.
- On 6S that is 4 × 80 A ≈ 320 A of phase current, roughly 8 kW.
- The model below shows about 135 W of loss into roughly 2 J/K of thermal mass.
- That gives a usable burst window of about 1 s, after which the board exceeds safe temperatures.

The defensible rating is either:
- 80 A per channel on a 30×30 board, or
- about 40 A continuous and 55 A burst (≤2 s) on 20×20.

The model is parameterized, so you can re-run it for either target.

**Architectural note:** the H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. Each ESC channel still needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071). The H743 cannot also commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz under ArduPilot.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step drive with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two FETs in series:
- the high-side FET of one phase, conducting for duty D, or its complementary low-side, conducting for 1−D; and
- the low-side FET of the return phase.

The duty-cycle terms therefore cancel at channel level:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2\left[2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{cu}\right]$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + k(T_j - 25)\right], \quad k \approx 0.0045\ \mathrm{K^{-1}} \Rightarrow 1.5 \times 1.45 = 2.18\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$
