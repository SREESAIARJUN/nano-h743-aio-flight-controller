## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

These points determine everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot does not run ESC firmware. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs** on the same PCB. Each ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6) runs AM32 or BLHeli_32. Section 4 covers the H743 timer and DMA side. Sections 1–3 cover the power stage driven by the ESC MCUs.

2. **"80A burst" on a nano form factor is a transient rating, not a thermal steady state.** Section 3 shows the board cannot reject the heat continuously. The deliverable is a burst-duration limit derived from thermal capacitance.

3. **The MOSFET part has not been selected.** All parameters below are representative values for a 40 V, ≤1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 / 5×6 DFN N-channel device. **Substitute the datasheet values of the selected part before release.** The model is fully parametric.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Motor current from bus current

In 6-step synchronous PWM, energy balance gives:

$$
I_m \approx \frac{I_{bus}}{D} = \frac{80}{0.95} = 84.2\ \text{A}
$$

PWM ripple, worst case at D = 0.5, is evaluated here at D = 0.95:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.0475}{15\mu \cdot 48k} = 1.66\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_m^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 7090 + 0.23 \approx 7090\ \text{A}^2
$$

Ripple is negligible at burst. It matters at part throttle with low-inductance motors.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance (self-consistent)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

Assume T_j = 110 °C as the iteration seed (verified in §3):

$$
R_{ds}(110) = 0.80 \cdot (1 + 0.0047 \cdot 85) = 1.12\ \text{m}\Omega
$$
