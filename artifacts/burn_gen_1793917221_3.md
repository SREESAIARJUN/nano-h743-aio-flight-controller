## 0. Design Assumptions and Constraints

Three constraints shape the rest of this design.

1. **ArduPilot does not commutate motors.** Bi-directional DShot600 is a protocol between a flight controller and ESC MCUs, so the architecture is:
   - **FC MCU:** STM32H743VIT6 running ArduPilot. It generates DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
   - **ESC MCUs:** 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32. These handle commutation, the 48 kHz complementary PWM, and dead-time.
   - **Gate drivers:** 4× three-phase drivers (FD6288Q class), driving 24 MOSFETs.

2. **80 A is a burst rating, not continuous.** Section 4 shows that on a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board, four channels at 80 A dissipate about 85–100 W. Without a heatsink this is thermally sustainable for **about 3–5 s**. A true "nano" (20 × 20 mm) footprint cannot support 4 × 80 A at all, so this design uses the 30.5 mm stack pattern with a 36 × 36 mm outline.

3. **Device parameters are representative.** They describe a 40 V, PQFN 5×6 / 3.3×3.3, roughly 1 mΩ MOSFET class. Before tape-out, substitute the actual datasheet values into the equations. The structure of the equations stays the same.
