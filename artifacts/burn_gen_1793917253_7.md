**Scope:** Field-oriented control (FOC) current loop, cascaded angle/velocity control, anti-cogging feedforward, and dual ICM-42688-P sensor fusion with vibration rejection.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with two shunts (using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p(θ_m − θ_0) and c = cos θ_e, s = sin θ_e:

$$
i_d = i_\alpha c + i_\beta s,\qquad i_q = -i_\alpha s + i_\beta c
$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d c - v_q s,\qquad v_\beta = v_d s + v_q c
$$

### 1.2 Motor model (SPM machine, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda
$$

$$
\tau_m = \tfrac32 p \lambda\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$
