## 1. Control Architecture

```
            ┌──────── 1 kHz ────────┐  ┌────── 4 kHz ──────┐  ┌───── 20 kHz ─────┐
θ_ref ──►(+)─► Angle P(+I) ──(+)──► (+)─► Velocity PI ──(+)─► (+)─► Current PI ─► SVPWM ─► Inverter
          ▲-               ω_ff  ▲-                  i_cog ▲-         ▲ dq-decoupling
          │                      │                    ▲    │
     θ̂ (fused attitude      ω̂ (filtered gyro    anti-cogging  i_dq (Clarke/Park)
      → joint space)         → joint axis)      FF(θ_m)       θ_e (encoder)
```

The design rests on a few principles:

- **Bandwidth separation.** Each loop should be at least 5× slower than the loop inside it. The targets are:
  - ω_c,I ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz (current)
  - ω_c,ω ≈ 2π·150 Hz (velocity)
  - ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·25 Hz (angle)
- **Velocity feedback comes from the gyro**, rotated into the joint axis. This is what stabilizes the camera inertially. The encoder is used only for commutation and the cogging phase.
- **Angle feedback comes from the fused attitude**, mapped into joint coordinates.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 Plant (SPM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda\, i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 2.3 PI with pole–zero cancellation and decoupling

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth ω_c,I:

$$
K_{p,I} = L\,\omega_{c,I},\qquad K_{i,I} = R\,\omega_{c,I}
$$

Discrete form (forward Euler on the integrator, T_I = 1/f_I), shown for the q-axis:

$$
v_q[k] = K_{p,I}\,e_q[k] + x_q[k] + \underbrace{\omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda}_{\text{feedforward}}
$$

$$
x_q[k+1] = x_q[k] + K_{i,I}T_I\,e_q[k] + K_{aw}\big(v_q^{sat}-v_q\big)
$$

- The d-axis is analogous, with feedforward −ω_e L i_q.
- The anti-windup gain is K_aw = 1/K_p,I (back-calculation).

**Voltage limit.** Saturate the voltage vector as a circle, giving priority to d:

$$
|v_d| \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95,\qquad
|v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

**Delay compensation.** Advance the inverse-Park angle by 1.5 PWM periods to cover computation plus ZOH delay:

$$
\theta_e^{out} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_I
$$
