## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. Each ESC channel runs its own commutation MCU. I assume AM32 firmware on an STM32G071 or AT32F421, which is the usual nano 4-in-1 topology. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not by the H743.

### 0.2 Credibility check on "80 A burst" in a nano form factor

Section 2 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 120 W. On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 board, that is survivable only for seconds, not tens of seconds. I treat 80 A as a **per-channel, ≤2 s, single-or-dual-motor burst rating**. That is consistent with how the market rates these boards, but it is not a continuous capability.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation, two half-bridges carry phase current at any instant. In each active leg, exactly one MOSFET (or its synchronous complement) conducts at any time. Each MOSFET is active for 1/3 of the electrical period.

For complementary PWM at duty cycle D, the active high-side FET carries I for D·T. The low-side synchronous FET of the same leg carries I for (1−D)·T. The leg therefore always presents one R_ds(on) in the path, independent of D, apart from dead time.

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{sh} + I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

with

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]
$$

Including ripple, the RMS current is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}
$$
