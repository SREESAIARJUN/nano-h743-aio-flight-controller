# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Design Reference

**Job #18, Rev A (engineering baseline)**

## 0. Architecture Clarification

A few corrections shape everything that follows:

1. **ArduPilot runs on the H743 and does not commutate motors.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own small MCU (e.g., AT32F421, STM32G071, or EFM8BB21) running AM32 or BLHeli. Bidirectional DShot600 is the link between the H743 timers and those four ESC MCUs. Driving 24 FETs directly from the H743 under ArduPilot is not supported and would not close timing.
2. **"80A burst" is defined here as 80 A phase current per channel for ≤10 s.** Section 3 shows this is a transient rating, not a continuous one. No nano-format board survives it continuously.
3. **Form factor.** 80A/channel needs at least a 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern (~36×36 mm outline). On a 20×20 board, the thermal mass cannot support the burst (see §3.4).

All device parameters are **placeholders typical of 40V, 5×6 mm, ~0.7 mΩ trench MOSFETs**. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values and re-run the equations.

| Symbol | Value | Note |

| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (worst case for switching loss) |

| Q_gd | 10 nC | |
| C_oss,er | 2 nF | |

Commutation is six-step trapezoidal. At any instant one high-side FET and one low-side FET carry I_ph. The PWM leg alternates between its HS FET (D) and its LS FET via sync-rect (1−D).

## 2. Loss Model (per channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

The two current-carrying FET positions are always occupied, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot (0.70\text{m}\Omega \cdot 1.6) = \mathbf{14.3\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,Cu} = I_{ph}^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \cdot 0.6\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.8\ W}$$

R_ds(on) depends on temperature, so solve self-consistently:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006/\text{K}$$

Iterate with T_j from §3.5. The 125 °C assumption is the upper bound.
