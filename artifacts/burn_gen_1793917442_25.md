# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #7)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Scope

Three of the brief's premises need correcting before any numbers mean anything.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** An STM32H743 cannot run four sensorless FOC/six-step loops at 48 kHz alongside ArduPilot. The correct architecture has two parts:
   - an **H743 flight controller** running ArduPilot, which outputs bi-directional DShot600;
   - a **4-in-1 ESC** with four independent ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM).

   The 48 kHz switching analysis below applies to the ESC. The DShot/DMA analysis applies to the FC.
2. **"Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot carry 4 × 80 A.** Section 2 shows roughly 100 W of dissipation at full burst. I size the ESC on a 30.5×30.5 mm pattern with a ~36×36 mm outline. A 20×20 mm board is physically viable only up to about 35–45 A burst.
3. **"80 A burst" is interpreted as the worst case:** 80 A motor/phase current at near-100 % duty (so I_batt ≈ I_phase), for up to 10 s.

All device parameters below are representative values for the 40 V, ~1 mΩ PDFN class. **Re-run the model with the exact datasheet values of the parts you select.**

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes trapezoidal six-step drive with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM on the high side. In each 60° sector:

| Device | Conduction fraction |
|---|---|
| HS FET, PWM leg | D |
| LS FET, PWM leg (sync rect.) | 1 − D |
| LS FET, return leg | 1 |

This gives:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is **independent of duty**. This is a key property of synchronous six-step.

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{2.3}$$

With R_DS,25 = 1.0 mΩ:

| T_j | 25 °C | 100 °C | 125 °C | 150 °C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R_DS (mΩ) | 1.00 | 1.43 | 1.57 | 1.72 |

Add parasitic path resistance R_par ≈ 0.25 mΩ per conducting path (solder, vias, pours) and a 0.25 mΩ shunt.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.57 mΩ | 20.1 W |
| Copper/solder | 2 × 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 3.2 W (heats PCB, not junction) |
| Shunt | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |

Instantaneous dissipation in a single fully-on FET is 6400 × 1.57 mΩ = **10.0 W**.
