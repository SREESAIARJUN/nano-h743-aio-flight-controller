## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partition

The H743 should not commutate the motors directly. Four sensorless BLDC drives need 12 complementary half-bridges (24 gate signals), synchronized ADC/comparator zero-cross detection, and µs-level commutation timing. That conflicts with ArduPilot's scheduler and uses up the H743's advanced timers (TIM1/TIM8 provide only 3 complementary pairs each).

The adopted partition is:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 with Extended DShot Telemetry (EDT) |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, 250 V) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | n/a |
| FC↔ESC link | Bidirectional DShot600, 4 lines | ArduPilot `bdshot` |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

In 6-step complementary PWM, during each 60° sector:

- Phase A high-side (Q_AH) switches at duty D, and its complementary low-side (Q_AL) conducts during (1−D).
- Phase B low-side (Q_BL) is held fully on.
- Phase C floats (BEMF sensing).

The winding current always passes through **exactly two MOSFET channels in series**, except during dead time, when it flows through one channel plus one body diode.

### 1.2 RMS Current With Ripple

Phase current ripple at 48 kHz, assuming a motor with L_ph-ph = 15 µH at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\times10^{-6} \times 48\,000} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}} = \sqrt{6400 + 6.4} = 80.04\ \text{A}
$$

The ripple contribution is negligible (<0.1 %) at 80 A. It becomes significant only for low-inductance motors (<5 µH).
