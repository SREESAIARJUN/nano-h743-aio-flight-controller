## 0. Architecture Decisions and Design-Review Flags

### 0.1 System Partition

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32, which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

```
VBAT(6S) ──┬── 4× [ESC MCU (AT32F421/STM32G071, AM32) → 3-ph gate driver → 6× N-MOSFET] → Motors 1-4
           ├── Bulk caps + TVS
           └── Buck 5V/3A → LDO 3.3V → STM32H743 (ArduPilot) + IMU/Baro/OSD/etc.
STM32H743 TIM1 CH1–4 ──(bidir DShot600, 1 wire/motor)──► ESC MCU input-capture pins
```

### 0.2 Review Flags

| # | Issue | Consequence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Package area.** 24 FETs in PDFN 5×6 need about 720 mm². A 20×20 board has only 400 mm² *total*. | "Nano 20×20" plus 80 A per channel is not physically compatible with 5×6 FETs. | Use 3.3×3.3 FETs (about 260 mm² of FETs alone) on a 30.5×30.5 board, or split into a 20×20 FC stacked on a larger ESC. The model below assumes 3.3×3.3 FETs. |
| F2 | **Input current.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery current. | XT60 (about 60 A continuous) and typical 10–12 AWG leads cannot carry it. | Rate 80 A as *per-channel, non-simultaneous burst*. Specify a total-board burst limit (about 160–200 A) and use XT90 or solder pads. |
| F3 | **Burst duration.** The adiabatic analysis (§3) gives about 1 s at full 4 × 80 A. | An "80 A burst" label without a time qualifier is misleading. | Specify the rating as "80 A / 1 s, 1 channel or 2 channels" and enforce it with AM32 current limiting plus temperature foldback. |

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC, trapezoidal 6-step, complementary PWM)

In any commutation sector, two phase legs carry the current:

- **Active (PWM) leg:** the high side conducts for duty D, and the synchronous low side conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts I continuously.

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

$$P_{cond} = \underbrace{I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)]}_{\text{active leg}} + \underbrace{I^2 R_{DS}}_{\text{return leg}} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of D, to first order with ripple neglected. Including inductor ripple ΔI gives:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \Delta I^2/12$$

**At 80 A and T_j = 125 °C:**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0016 = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$
