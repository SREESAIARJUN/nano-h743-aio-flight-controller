## 1. Electrical Operating Model

AM32 uses six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active phase pair. Let D be the PWM duty and I the phase current. At burst near full throttle, I_DC ≈ D·I_phase.

In each 60° commutation sector:
- **High side (HS) of phase A:** PWM-switched with duty D.
- **Low side (LS) of phase A:** conducts the complement, (1−D), as the synchronous rectifier. It also carries the body-diode current during dead-time.
- **LS of phase B:** continuously ON for the sector.
- **Phase C:** floating.

Each FET is active for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle, so the per-FET duty share is 1/3.

## 2. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 2.1 Temperature-corrected resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0059\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \Rightarrow\ R(110^\circ C) \approx 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Add the PCB copper path per phase. This includes the FET-to-pad spreading resistance and the phase-pad trace, on the order of 0.2 mΩ:

$$R_{path} = R_{DS(on)} + R_{Cu} \approx 1.7\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Bridge conduction loss per ESC channel

At any instant, exactly one switch position of the PWM'd phase plus one LS are in the current path:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS}\right] + I^2 R_{LS,B} = 2 I^2 R_{DS(on)}\ \ (\text{since } R_{HS}=R_{LS})$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}\quad(+\ 2.6\ \text{W in Cu} \Rightarrow 21.8\ \text{W incl. traces})$$

### 2.3 Per-FET average (for junction rise)

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS(on)} = \frac{1}{3}(80^2)(1.5\text{m}) = \mathbf{3.2\ W\ average}\quad(9.6\ \text{W instantaneous while conducting})$$

The instantaneous figure matters. The FET's thermal time constant (about 1–10 ms) is comparable to the commutation period at low eRPM, so the junction ripples. Use the instantaneous 9.6 W for transient Z_th checks below about 3000 eRPM/s commutation.

## 3. Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only one FET per channel hard-switches at a time: the HS of the PWM'd phase. The LS commutates softly, ZVS via the body diode.

### 3.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{DS} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12 (25.2)(80)(40\times10^{-9})(48\times10^{3}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

A more rigorous edge estimate uses the Miller plateau:

$$t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}} = \frac{10\text{nC}\cdot(3.3+1.5)\Omega}{10 - 4.5} \approx 8.7\ \text{ns (Miller only)}$$

The 20 ns figure retained above includes the current-rise interval and common-source inductance degradation, which is about 0.5 nH in a 5×6 package. Keep 20 ns as the conservative design value.
