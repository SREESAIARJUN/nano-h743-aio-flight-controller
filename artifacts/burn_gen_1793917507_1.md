# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #61): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture Model

## 0. Design Basis and Feasibility Flags

**Architecture assumption.** On every production 4-in-1 design, the H743 does not drive the MOSFET gates directly. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model below follows this split.

**Feasibility flags (read these first).**

1. **80 A on four channels simultaneously is thermally burst-only on a nano (20×20 or 30×30) board.** The model below shows about 100 W of bridge dissipation, which limits the burst to roughly 2–3 s from a warm start. Treat 80 A as a per-channel burst rating, not a simultaneous continuous one.
2. **Device parameters are representative placeholders.** All MOSFET values are typical for a 40 V, PDFN 5×6, ~1 mΩ class part. Before sign-off, re-run every equation with your selected part's datasheet values (R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, Q_gs2, Q_rr, Q_oss, V_plateau).
3. **The netlist is a reference topology, not a fab-ready netlist.** It must be captured in your ECAD tool, ERC/DRC-checked, and reviewed against the vendor reference designs.

**Operating point.** 6S pack, V_bus,max = 25.2 V, f_sw = 48 kHz, I_phase = 80 A, T_ambient = 40 °C.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

At any instant, current flows through:
- one high-side FET or its complementary low-side FET (the PWM leg), and
- one low-side FET (the return leg, fully on for 120°).

The current therefore always passes through exactly **two R_DS(on) in series**, regardless of duty cycle D.

Bridge conduction loss per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \left[\,2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\,\right]$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

With R_25 = 1.0 mΩ and T_j = 113 °C:
- R_DS(on) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 88) = **1.53 mΩ**

### 1.3 Per-FET RMS currents

For a phase current I over one electrical cycle:

| Device | RMS current |
|---|---|
| High-side FET of a phase | $I_{HS,rms} = I\sqrt{D/3}$ |
| Low-side FET of a phase | $I_{LS,rms} = I\sqrt{(1-D)/3 + 1/3}$ |

The low-side FET carries more RMS current at low D. For hotspot placement, it is the worst-case device.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

Assuming R_pcb ≈ 0.3 mΩ (pads, vias, phase pour):

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| 2 · I² · R_DS(on) = 2 · 6400 · 1.53 mΩ | 19.6 W |
| I² · R_pcb = 6400 · 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **P_cond per channel** | **21.5 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-leg transistor hard-switches. The synchronous FET switches at near-zero voltage after dead-time diode conduction.

### 2.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

Assumed values:
- Gate drive: V_drv = 10 V (FD6288-class driver)
- Gate resistance: R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω, R_g,int = 1.5 Ω, driver source resistance ≈ 3 Ω, driver sink resistance ≈ 1 Ω
- Plateau voltage: V_pl = 3.5 V
- Switching charge: Q_sw = Q_gs2 + Q_gd ≈ 6 + 10 = 16 nC

Turn-on:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{6.5}{9.2} = 0.71\text{ A} \;\Rightarrow\; t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 22.6\text{ ns}$$

Turn-off:

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{sink}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{3.5}{7.2} = 0.49\text{ A} \;\Rightarrow\; t_{off} = 32.9\text{ ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss terms (per channel)

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_on + t_off) · f = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 55.5 ns · 48 kHz | 2.69 W |
| Reverse recovery (Q_rr ≈ 60 nC at 80 A) | Q_rr · V · f | 0.07 W |
| Output capacitance (Q_oss ≈ 25 nC) | Q_oss · V · f | 0.03
