**Job #62, Rev A (analytical baseline)**

## 0. Scope, Architecture Correction, and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In any production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel runs on a dedicated ESC MCU executing BLHeli_32, AM32, or Bluejay. The AT32F421 running AM32 is the reference here.

The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The model is therefore split into two domains:

1. **Power stage:** 4 × 3-phase bridges, 24 MOSFETs, driven by the ESC MCUs.
2. **Control link:** H743 timer, DMA, and input-capture allocation for DShot600 with eRPM telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM-switched, one is held low (or high), and one floats.

- During the PWM on-time, current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- During the off-time, current freewheels through LS(A) (synchronous) → motor → LS(B).

So **exactly two MOSFETs conduct the phase current at every instant**, apart from dead time.

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$

For a typical nano motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-to-line), at D = 0.5 with V_bemf ≈ 0.5·V_bus:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{12.6 \times 0.5}{15\times10^{-6}\cdot 48\times10^{3}} \approx 8.75\ \text{A}
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6.4\ \text{A}^2
$$

This is negligible against 6400 A², so I_rms ≈ 80.04 A.

### 1.3 Hot on-resistance

$$
R_{ds,hot} = k_T \cdot R_{ds,25} = 1.6 \times 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega = 1.92\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Add PCB and solder path resistance. For a 2 oz outer layer, 5 mm × 3 mm pour, plus vias, this is about 0.3 mΩ per conduction path.

$$
R_{path} = 2(R_{ds,hot}) + R_{cu} = 3.84 + 0.3 = 4.14\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.4 Conduction loss per channel

$$
\boxed{P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{path} = 6406 \times 4.14\times10^{-3} \approx 26.5\ \text{W per channel}}
$$

Per MOSFET, averaged over a full electrical cycle (each device carries current for one third of the cycle as active or static switch):

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{2\, I^2 R_{ds,hot}}{6} = \frac{6400 \times 3.84\times10^{-3}}{6} \approx 4.1\ \text{W}
$$

### 1.5 Current-sense shunt (if fitted)

A 0.25 mΩ shunt adds I²R = 6400 × 0.25 mΩ ≈ 1.6 W. That burst exceeds the rating of a 1206 shunt, so either use a 2512 / 3 W part or sense across R_ds(on).
