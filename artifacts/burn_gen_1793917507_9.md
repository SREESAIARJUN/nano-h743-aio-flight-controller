## 0. Architecture Assumptions

**Topology.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors, so the H743 does not drive gates. The architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot)  --4x bidirectional DShot600-->  4x ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class)
                                                          --> 4x 3-phase gate driver --> 24 MOSFETs
```

The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.

**Physical limit on 80 A.** On a nano (20×20 mm) board, 80 A per motor can only be a **burst** rating, lasting about 1 s. Section 3 shows that steady state is thermally unstable at this current. Treat "80 A" as a transient specification.
