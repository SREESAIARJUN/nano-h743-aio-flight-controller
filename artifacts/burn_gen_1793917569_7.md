## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|

|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |

The loops follow a bandwidth-separation rule of roughly 5–10× between nested stages. The current-loop bandwidth must also stay well below the PWM Nyquist limit, ω_c < ω_PWM / 10.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$
