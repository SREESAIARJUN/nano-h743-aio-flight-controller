## 0. Design Assumptions

I don't have the Job #60 specification, so the design rests on the assumptions below. Every value is a single `constexpr` or config field, so you can change it in one place.

| Item | Assumed value |

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The amplitude-invariant Clarke transform:
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

The Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform:
$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The PMSM dq model (surface magnets, $L_d=L_q=L$):
$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward.** These cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:
$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

With the feedforward in place, each axis reduces to $G(s)=\frac{1}{Ls+R}$.

**PI gains by pole–zero cancellation.** The PI zero cancels the electrical pole, giving a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_c$:
$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1\text{ kHz}$.

**Discretization.** Use backward Euler with back-calculation anti-windup:
$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(\text{sat}(u_k)-u_k)
$$

A good choice for the anti-windup gain is $K_{aw} = 1/K_p \cdot K_i T_s$, i.e. tracking time constant $T_t=\sqrt{T_i}$, simplified.

**Voltage limit.** Limit the output vector to the inscribed circle of the SVPWM hexagon:
$$
|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$
Give $v_d$ priority, then $v_q \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.
