**Architecture choice:** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to **four ESC MCUs** (AM32 on STM32G071/AT32F421 class). Each ESC MCU drives one 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q-class) and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz PWM. Keeping that loop local to each ESC MCU is the standard, robust partitioning.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through:
- **one high-side switch** (phase A, PWM'd at duty D), and
- **one low-side switch** (phase B, held on for the 60° sector).

During PWM-off, current freewheels through the complementary low-side of phase A (the synchronous rectifier). So **two R_DS(on) are always in series** with the motor current, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,A} + R_{LS,B} \right] = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds,hot}
$$

(when R_HS = R_LS).

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$
R_{ds,hot} = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)] \approx 0.75\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.13\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$
P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.13\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 2.26\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{14.5\ W\ per\ motor}
$$

$$
P_{cond,Cu} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{6.4\ W\ per\ motor}
$$

### 1.4 Per-device distribution
Averaged over a full electrical revolution, each of the 6 switches carries current for 1/3 of the time (120° conduction):

$$
P_{cond,per\,FET} \approx \frac{14.5}{6} = 2.4\,\text{W (average)}
$$

Within a commutation sector, though, a single FET carries the full 80 A:

$$
P_{inst} = 80^2 \times 1.13\,\text{m}\Omega = 7.2\,\text{W}
$$

At low eRPM the sector is long, so use 7.2 W for junction-to-case transient checks (§4.2).
