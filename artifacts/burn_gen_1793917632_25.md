# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #24): Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Dir DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

An STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges directly. ArduPilot cannot run the BLDC commutation loop. The stack therefore has two parts:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz (ArduPilot default) | ArduPilot, bi-dir DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 running AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 mA/600 mA bootstrap) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power stage | 24 × 40 V N-FET, DFN3.3×3.3 | 6 FETs per motor |
