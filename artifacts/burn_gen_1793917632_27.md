# Job #33: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80A ESC
## Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, Bi-directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Plan, and Netlists

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Two findings shape the rest of this document.

**Finding 1: The H743 cannot commutate the motors.**
In every production "4-in-1 ESC + FC" design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and *commands* the ESC. Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). A single H743 cannot run ArduPilot's scheduler and also do four sensorless six-step commutation loops with sub-µs zero-cross timing. The architecture below therefore uses the H743 as the flight controller, connected over bi-directional DShot600 to 4× AT32F421 ESC cores on the same PCB.

**Finding 2: "80A burst" and "nano" are thermally incompatible.**
- A 20×20 mm board cannot place 24 power MOSFETs, gate drivers, bulk capacitance and an H743.
- Even on a 36×36 mm (30.5 mm mounting) AIO, the model below shows that 4×80A is a **~3 s** event, not a sustained rating.

All numbers below are computed for the 36×36 mm board. The 20×20 case is noted where it matters.
