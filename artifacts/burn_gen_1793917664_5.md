## 0. Architecture Correction and Executive Verdict

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every viable stack of this class, the H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example, AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), and those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model below follows that partition.
