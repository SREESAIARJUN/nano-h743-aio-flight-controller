## 0. Scope, System Partition and Assumptions

### 0.1 Who does what

The STM32H743 **does not drive the MOSFETs**. The architecture has two separate controllers:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (ArduPilot) | Attitude control, sends DShot600, receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4 channels) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32) | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, current sense |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is an **ESC firmware parameter** (AM32 `PWM_FREQUENCY`). It is not an ArduPilot setting.
