## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At any commutation step, two legs are active:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** current flows through the high-side FET during t_on. It flows through the synchronous low-side FET during t_off, except during dead time.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET is continuously on.

The motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two R_ds(on) elements in series**, independent of duty cycle. This excludes the dead-time diode intervals, which are handled in §2.

### 1.2 Equations

Junction-temperature-corrected resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\cdot\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right] \approx R_{ds,25}\cdot k_T$$

Per-channel conduction loss, using a flat-top trapezoidal current approximation so that I_rms ≈ I_pk:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Per-FET average conduction loss. Each FET conducts for 120° of 360° electrical, so the total is shared over 6 FETs:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot\frac{6}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2}= \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}$$

The high-side/low-side split within a leg depends on duty D. The high side carries ≈ D, and the low side carries the remainder plus the full return-leg interval. **The low-side FETs run hotter at low duty.** Size the layout for the low side.
