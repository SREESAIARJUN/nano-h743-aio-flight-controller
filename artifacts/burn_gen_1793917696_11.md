### 0.1 Critical findings

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---------|--------|
| F1 | The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate 4 motors itself. Commutation needs 24 complementary PWM outputs, back-EMF comparators, and per-motor timing. The H743 runs **ArduPilot** and sends DShot600 to **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (AM32/BLHeli_32 class). | Defines the partition below. |
| F2 | The **48 kHz PWM frequency is an ESC firmware setting**, not an H743 setting. | Switching-loss model applies to the ESC side. |
| F3 | At 80 A phase current per channel, total board dissipation is about **85–90 W**. A nano (20×20 or 30.5×30.5 mm) board cannot sustain this. The rating is **thermal-capacitance limited to roughly 5–7 s**. | "80 A" must be specified as burst with a defined duty cycle (§3.5). |
| F4 | Copper interconnect losses are the same order as the switching losses. They are routinely omitted from marketing ratings. | Included explicitly below. |
