## 0. Architectural Decisions and Up-Front Corrections

Three points in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful.

| # | Assumption in brief | Engineering reality | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The H743 runs the ESC | The H743 runs ArduPilot (FC). Commutation at 48 kHz × 4 motors with BEMF zero-cross detection is done by **dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600. | Two-domain design: H743 FC + 4× AT32F421 ESC MCUs |
| 2 | "Nano" (20×20 mm) at 80 A per motor | 24 power FETs plus drivers will not fit on one 20×20 board that also carries the FC. Thermally, 80 A per channel is a **burst-only (~1–2 s)** rating at this size (§5). | Stacked 20×20 pair (FC board + ESC board), 8-layer, 2 oz |
| 3 | 80 A continuous | Sustainable continuous current is roughly **20–28 A per motor**, airflow-dependent (§5.4). | Label as "80 A burst / 25 A cont." |

## 3. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 3.1 Commutation topology
In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, at any instant one high-side and one low-side FET carry phase current I. The current path therefore always sees **2 × R_DS(on)** in series.

### 3.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 3.3 Instantaneous bridge conduction loss per ESC channel

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.6\,\text{m} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

At a cold start (1.2 mΩ): 15.4 W. Because R_DS rises with temperature, the loss climbs during the burst. This is a positive-feedback term, and §5.3 checks that it stays bounded.
