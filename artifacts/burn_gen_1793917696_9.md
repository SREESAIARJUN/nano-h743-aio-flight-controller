## 0. Architecture Decision

An STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly in this design. The industry-standard and ArduPilot-compatible partition is:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz max, ArduPilot runs 400 MHz) | EKF3, attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071KBU6 running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class firmware) | 6-step trapezoidal commutation, 48 kHz PWM, bidirectional DShot slave (eRPM telemetry) |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver, 20 V class (FD6288Q-class) | Bootstrap high-side drive, ~1.5 A sink / 1 A source |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN | Inverter |

**Form factor:** 25.5 × 25.5 mm board, 20 × 20 mm mounting pattern, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes.
