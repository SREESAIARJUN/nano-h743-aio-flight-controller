# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Cascaded Stabilization, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion

I don't have the Job #40 specification, so the design is built on the assumptions below. Every parameter that depends on them is isolated in a config struct, so you can change it in one place.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, single-precision FPU, 3× advanced timers, center-aligned PWM |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs `p = 7`), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A/MT6701) per axis |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the **camera payload**, SPI @ 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, FIFO off, data-ready IRQ |
| Kinematics | Outer yaw (z) → middle roll (x) → inner pitch (y) |

| Loop | Rate | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Current loop (FOC) | 20 kHz | ADC injected end-of-conversion, PWM center |
| Rate (velocity) loop | 4 kHz | Every 5th FOC tick, after the IMU sample |
| Attitude (angle) loop | 1 kHz | Every 4th rate tick |
| IMU acquisition and filtering | 8 kHz → decimated to 4 kHz | IMU DRDY interrupt |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

Park transform, using the electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The dq-frame plant is

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

A PI controller with pole–zero cancellation gives first-order closed-loop dynamics with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Choose ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz (below f_s/10 at 20 kHz).

Add decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = PI_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Saturation uses circular voltage limiting. The available voltage is |v| ≤ V_bus/√3 (linear SVPWM region). The d-axis has priority:

$$
v_d^{sat} = \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(v_d^*,\ \pm V_{max}\right),\qquad
v_q^{sat} = \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*,\ \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)
$$

Anti-windup uses back-calculation, with K_aw = 1/K_p:

$$
x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + T_s\left(K_i\,e[k] + K_{aw}\,(v^{sat}-v^{*})\right)
$$

### 1.3 SVPWM

Apply the inverse Park transform to get v_α and v_β. Then use min-max zero-sequence injection:

$$
v_{abc} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\qquad
v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max v_{abc} + \min v_{abc}\right)
$$

$$
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Gimbal Kinematics (Joint ↔ Body Rates)

Camera orientation is R = R_z(ψ) R_x(φ) R_y(θ). The camera body rate is

$$
\boldsymbol\omega_b = \dot\psi\begin{bmatrix}-s_\theta c_\phi\\ s_\phi\\ c_\theta c_\phi\end{bmatrix}
+\dot\phi\begin{bmatrix}c_\theta\\0\\ s_\theta\end{bmatrix}
+\dot\theta\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\\0\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse, used to map gyro rates and rate commands into joint space (singular at φ = ±90°, so guard c_φ), is:

$$
\dot\phi = c_\theta\,\omega_x + s_\theta\,\omega_z,\qquad
\dot\psi = \frac{c_\theta\,\omega_z - s_\theta\,\omega_x}{c_\phi},\qquad
\dot\theta = \omega_y - s_\phi\,\dot\psi
$$
