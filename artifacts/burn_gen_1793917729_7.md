# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Cascaded Stabilization, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

I don't have the Job #1 spec sheet, so the design below is parameterized. Defaults are typical for a camera gimbal. Replace them with your measured values.

| Item | Default assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs $p=7$), $R_s \approx 5\,\Omega$, $L_s \approx 1.5$ mH, $K_t \approx 0.08$ Nm/A |
| Commutation sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| Current sensing | 2 or 3 low-side shunts per motor. Voltage-mode fallback is provided. |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera stage, mounted ideally symmetric about the stage's rotation centre ($\mathbf r_2 \approx -\mathbf r_1$) |
| MCU | Cortex-M7/M4F with single-precision FPU (STM32H7/G4 class) |
