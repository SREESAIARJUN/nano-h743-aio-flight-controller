## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park transform** at electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and current PI design

The dq-frame model of a surface-mounted PMSM (L_d = L_q = L) is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

After decoupling feedforward, each axis reduces to the first-order plant G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choosing the PI zero to cancel the plant pole, K_i/K_p = R/L, gives an open loop of ω_c/s. The closed loop is then first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

With ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s, this gives K_p = 20.7 V/A and K_i = 52 800 V/(A·s). The ω_c ≤ f_pwm/10 rule keeps the phase loss from the 1.5-sample transport delay below ~27°.

**Voltage commands.** Feedforward compensates the cross-coupling and back-EMF:

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m),\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}
$$

**Voltage limiting.** The vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon's inscribed circle, |v| ≤ V_dc/√3. Priority goes to v_d, then v_q:

$$
v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}}
$$
