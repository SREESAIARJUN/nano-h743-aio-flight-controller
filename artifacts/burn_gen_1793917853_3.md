## 1. Timing Architecture

```
PWM center ISR (20 kHz) ── ADC currents ── encoder ── Current PI ── SVPWM
      │ every 5th tick
      ▼
Velocity loop (4 kHz) ◄── fused gyro rate (8 kHz → decimated) ◄── IMU DRDY ISR
      │ every 4th tick                                               │
      ▼                                                              ▼
Angle loop (1 kHz) ◄─────────────── Mahony attitude (1 kHz) ◄─ dual-IMU fusion
```

The rule is that each loop has at least 4–5× more bandwidth than the loop outside it:

ω_i ≥ 4ω_v ≥ 16ω_θ

## 2. FOC Current Loop

### 2.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform, using two measured phases and i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
