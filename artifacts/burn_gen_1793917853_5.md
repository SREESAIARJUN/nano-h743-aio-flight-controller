| Loop | Rate | Target closed-loop bandwidth |

| IMU sample + filtering | 8 kHz | — |
| Velocity (rate) | 4 kHz | ~150–250 Hz (mechanically limited) |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | ~20–40 Hz |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θₑ = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The **inverse Park** transform is the transpose of this matrix. Apply it to (v_d, v_q).
