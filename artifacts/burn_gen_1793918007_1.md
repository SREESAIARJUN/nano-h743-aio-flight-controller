# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #19): Engineering Model

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architectural correction:** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each motor channel on the 4-in-1 needs its own ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or STM32G071 running BLHeli_32/AM32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. An H743 cannot run ArduPilot and drive 4× sensorless FOC/trapezoidal commutation at the same time with acceptable latency.

The device parameters below are **representative values for a 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 MOSFET**. Replace them with the datasheet of the part you actually select, then rerun the equations.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching) |
| I_ph | 80 A | burst phase current, per motor |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | ESC PWM frequency |
| R_ds,25 | 1.0 mΩ | R_ds(on) at 25 °C, V_gs = 10 V |
| α | 0.005 /K | R_ds(on) temperature coefficient (≈1.5× at 125 °C) |
| Q_g / Q_gd / Q_gs2 | 60 / 10 / 5 nC | gate charges |
| V_pl | 3.0 V | Miller plateau |
| C_oss(er) | 1.5 nF | energy-related output capacitance |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 · (1 + 0.5) = **1.5 mΩ**

### 1.2 Trapezoidal 120° commutation with synchronous rectification

At every instant, the current path is: high-side FET of phase A (PWM, duty D) → motor → low-side FET of phase B (100 % on). During PWM off-time, the complementary low-side of phase A carries the freewheel current. So **exactly two FETs carry I_ph at every instant**, independent of D:

P_cond,motor = 2 · I_ph² · R_ds(T_j)

= 2 · 80² · 1.5×10⁻³ = **19.2 W per motor**

Each of the 6 FETs is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the average per FET is:

P_cond,FET = P_cond,motor / 6 = **3.2 W**

**Peak-instant hot spot:** The low-side FET of the sinking phase sees 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W** for 120° of the cycle. Junction ripple matters at low eRPM (see §3.4).

### 1.3 Copper and shunt conduction

Assume a 2 oz (70 µm) outer layer with 3 paralleled inner planes. The phase path is about 15 mm long and 6 mm wide, giving roughly 0.25 mΩ effective, plus a 0.25 mΩ shunt:

P_Cu,motor = 80² · (0.25 + 0.25)×10⁻³ = **3.2 W per motor**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches. It commutates one hard-switched transition pair per period.

### 2.1 Gate current and transition times

I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl) / R_g,on = 7/4 = 1.75 A

I_g,off = V_pl / R_g,off = 3/3 = 1.0 A

t_on ≈ (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) / I_g,on = 15 nC / 1.75 A = 8.6 ns

t_off ≈ 15 nC / 1.0 A = 15 ns

These are idealized. Common-source and loop inductance (≈1–3 nH in a 30×30 layout) typically double them, so **use t_on + t_off ≈ 47 ns as the design value**.

### 2.2 Loss components

| Term | Equation | Ideal (23.6 ns) | Derated (47 ns) |
|---|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on + t_off)·f_sw | 1.14 W | 2.27 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 0.06 W | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.023 W | 0.023 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·(2·t_dt)·f_sw | 0.61 W | 0.61 W |
| **Σ P_sw,motor** | | **1.83 W** | **2.96 W** |

Gate-drive power is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET junction:

P_gate = Q_g · V_drv · f_sw = 60 nC × 10 V × 48 kHz = **29 mW per switching FET**

**Dead-time lever:** With good matching, dead time can be cut from 100 ns to 50 ns. That saves about 0.3 W per motor.

### 2.3 Total per motor and per board (derated)

| | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 3.0 W | 12.0 W |
| Copper + shunt | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| **Total** | **25.4 W** | **≈ 102 W** |

**Average per FET:** (19.2 + 3.0) / 6 ≈ **3.7 W**

**Battery current at full burst:** I_bat ≈ ΣI_ph · D. At D = 0.9: 4 × 80 × 0.9 ≈ **288 A** from a 6S pack (~7 kW). Size XT90/AS150 connectors, 10 AWG leads, and bulk capacitance accordingly.
