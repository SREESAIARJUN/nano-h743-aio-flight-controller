## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Sensorless BEMF commutation of four motors needs four independent 3-phase complementary PWM sets with zero-cross comparators, and the H743 cannot provide that cleanly. The design therefore has two tiers:

| Tier | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot. Sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4) | AM32-class MCU (STM32G071 / AT32F421) per motor | 6-step sensorless commutation, 48 kHz PWM. |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× N-MOSFET + 3-phase gate driver per motor | 80 A burst per motor. |

The 48 kHz PWM frequency is therefore an **ESC firmware parameter** (the AM32 PWM frequency setting). It is not an H743 timer setting.

A 20 × 20 mm board using 3.3 × 3.3 mm FETs (R_DS ≈ 2.5–3 mΩ) roughly **doubles** the conduction loss and is not viable at 80 A. Specify **30.5 × 30.5 mm with 5 × 6 mm FETs** as the minimum.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha_R (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C (design point):

$$R_{DS} = 1.5\,(1 + 0.0048 \times 85) = 2.11\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-Step Topology with Synchronous Rectification

At any instant two phases conduct: one high-side leg and one low-side leg.

- In the PWM'd leg, current alternates between the HS FET (fraction D) and the LS FET (fraction 1−D).
- Both FETs have the same R_DS, so the leg dissipates **I²·R_DS** regardless of D.

Per-motor conduction loss:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Ripple Correction

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

At worst-case D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\ \text{A}^2 \quad (\text{negligible at 80 A})$$

### 1.4 Result

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6403.6 \times 2.11\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{27.0\ W\ per\ motor}$$

- **Per leg:** each leg carries current 2/3 of the electrical period, giving 9.0 W average per leg.
- **Per FET:** 4.5 W average.
- **Peak FET instantaneous:** the HS FET at D → 1 carries the full 13.5 W (80² × 2.11 mΩ) during its 120° window.
