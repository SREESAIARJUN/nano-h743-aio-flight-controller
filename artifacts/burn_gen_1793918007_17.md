## 0. Architecture Interpretation and Design Assumptions

**Topology.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, one per motor channel. Each ESC MCU drives its own 3-phase bridge. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back on the same wire.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths per channel

In 6-step operation with complementary PWM, two phases conduct in every 60° sector. One phase leg is PWM-switched and the other is held with its low-side FET on.

- **PWM on-time (duty D):** current flows through HS_x and LS_y, so two R_DS are in series.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_x (synchronous) and LS_y, again two R_DS in series.

The loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, apart from dead time:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Ripple correction

Phase current ripple adds a small RMS term:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}} \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}$$

The worst case is near D = 0.5, at low speed and high current.

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2\left(1+\frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I_{dc}}\right)^2\right)$$

For a typical 5" motor:
- L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-to-line ≈ 30 µH)
- ΔI ≈ (25.2 × 0.5)/(30 µH × 48 kHz) ≈ 8.75 A peak-to-peak
- ΔI/I ≈ 0.11
- Correction ≈ +0.1 %, which is negligible at 80 A.

### 1.4 Result at 80 A burst

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Per-FET averaging over an electrical cycle.** Each FET conducts for about 120° of every 360°.
- Instantaneous loss in the held-on FET: I²R = **9.6 W**.
- Cycle-averaged loss across the six FETs: ≈ 3.2 W each.

At low eRPM, the 60° sector can last longer than the die thermal time constant (≈1–5 ms). The junction then follows the 9.6 W peak, not the average. **The thermal worst case is therefore stall or low-RPM high-throttle, for example a punch-out from hover with a prop strike.**
