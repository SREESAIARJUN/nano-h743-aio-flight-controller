# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### 0.1 System partitioning

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 to four independent ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071). Each ESC MCU drives its own three-phase bridge. Every model below follows from this split.

### 0.2 Assumed operating point

The board-level parts are illustrative. Verify each one against the current datasheet before layout.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max |
| Burst current, per motor (DC link) | I_b | 80 A, ≤ 5 s |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3) | R_ds(on),25 | 1.4 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Gate charge / switching charge | Q_g / Q_sw | 40 nC / 12 nC |
| Output capacitance / reverse recovery charge | C_oss / Q_rr | 1.2 nF / 35 nC |

| Dead time | t_dt | 200 ns |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Commutation | — | Trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM |

### 0.3 Feasibility flag (read first)

80 A per motor on a true "nano" AIO (20×20 mm mounting) is not physically closable:

- 24 FETs in 5×6 packages need about 720 mm² against 400 mm² of board.
- 3×3 FETs fit, but they carry higher R_ds(on) and higher R_θ.

The math below is therefore done on a **30.5×30.5 mm board**. Even there, it shows that 4 × 80 A is a **2–3 s burst rating, not a continuous one**. Section 3 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current waveforms (6-step)

In trapezoidal commutation, each phase conducts for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle, alternately high side and low side.

| Quantity | Expression |
|---|---|
| Phase RMS current | I_ph,rms = I_b · √(2/3) |
| At I_b = 80 A | I_ph,rms = 65.3 A |
| Per-FET active window | 1/3 of the electrical period |

Within that window, at PWM duty D with synchronous rectification:

- the high-side FET of the active pair carries I_b for fraction D;
- the complementary low-side FET (same leg) carries I_b for fraction (1 − D);
- the static low-side FET of the return phase carries I_b continuously.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.4 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.03 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-motor conduction loss

At any instant, current flows through two FET channels in series (one high, one low), so:

$$P_{cond,motor}=I_b^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big]\cdot\tfrac{?}{}\;\Rightarrow\; P_{cond,motor}=2\,I_b^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

(The interval split only changes *which* FET dissipates. The sum is always two channel resistances in series.)

**Per-FET, worst case** (static low side, or high side at D → 1):

$$P_{cond,FET}=\tfrac{1}{3}\,I_b^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$
