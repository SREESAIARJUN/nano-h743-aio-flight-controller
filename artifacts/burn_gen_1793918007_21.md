# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Design Analysis

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

This design rests on four points that shape the rest of the document.

1. **ArduPilot does not run on the ESC.** The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each motor channel is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 originates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. This document treats the system as an AIO stack: H743 FC plus 4× ESC MCU.
2. **80 A is treated as a burst rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal model below shows that a nano-class 4-in-1 cannot sustain 80 A on all four channels without active cooling. Burst duration is part of the specification.
3. **Device parameters are representative placeholders.** They describe a 40 V N-ch MOSFET in a 3.3×3.3 mm package. Replace them with datasheet values for the device you select, and keep the equations.
4. **Netlists are schematic-level reference topologies.** They are not a validated, DRC-clean layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation

In each commutation sector, two phase legs carry current I:

- One leg is PWM'd: the high-side (HS) conducts for D, and the low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D) because of synchronous rectification.
- One leg is held LS-on (or HS-on, depending on scheme) for the whole sector.

So at any instant, current flows through exactly **two MOSFET channels in series**.

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0059\ \text{/K}\ \ (\Rightarrow k_T = 1.5 \text{ at } 110^\circ\text{C})$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 1.2 Per-device distribution (hot-spot FET)

Each phase leg is active for 2/3 of an electrical revolution. Within its PWM'd 1/3, the HS conducts for D. At D = 0.9 (near full throttle):

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R \left[\tfrac{1}{3}D + \tfrac{1}{3}\right] = 9.6\,(0.30 + 0.33) \approx 6.1\ \text{W (worst-case per-FET, scheme-dependent)}$$

Design to **≈ 6 W in the hottest FET** during burst.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses (often underestimated)

| Element | Calculation | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase trace, 4 oz (140 µm), 3 mm × 15 mm | R = ρL/(wt) = 1.72e-8·0.015/(0.003·140e-6) = 0.61 mΩ | 3.9 W |
| Bus/GND planes (estimate) | ≈ 0.3 mΩ effective | 1.9 W |
| Current shunt 0.25 mΩ (if per-ESC) | I²R | 1.6 W |

Copper loss rivals switching loss. Mitigations:

- Use 3–4 inner layers in parallel for the phase and bus paths.
- Stitch with via arrays at about 1 A per 0.3 mm via, or more for burst.
