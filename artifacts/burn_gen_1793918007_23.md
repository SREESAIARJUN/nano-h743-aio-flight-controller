## 0. Architecture Assumptions and an Early Red Flag

**System partition.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs.

**Red flag.** 80 A per motor on a nano-class board (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm) is a seconds-scale burst rating, never a continuous one. Section 3 quantifies this. Market the spec as "80 A burst for X seconds" with a defined duty cycle, or the field-return rate will show it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{2.3}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives a factor of (398/298)^2.3 ≈ 1.95. Datasheet curves for trench FETs typically show about 1.6 to 1.8, so 1.6 is used as the nominal design value:

$$R_{DS,hot} = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega$$

The fully coupled form is solved iteratively in §3.4.
