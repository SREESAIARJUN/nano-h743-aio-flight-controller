## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 commands. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, and that MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The loss model below applies to the ESC power stage. The DShot model applies to the H743-to-ESC-MCU link.
