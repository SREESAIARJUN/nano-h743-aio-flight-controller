## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

The design splits into two control domains:

| Domain | Silicon | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **FC** | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz under ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 out, eRPM in via bi-directional DShot |
| **ESC** (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 per channel, running AM32 | 6-step trapezoidal commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, GCR eRPM telemetry |

The **48 kHz switching** belongs to the ESC MCUs. The **DShot/DMA allocation** belongs to the H743.
