## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

**Architecture correction:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot, which is a flight controller stack, not a BLDC commutation stack. A 4-in-1 design therefore uses this split:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──bidir DShot600 ×4──► 4× AT32F421/STM32G071 (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class)
                                              └─► 4× FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver
                                                  └─► 4× 6 N-MOSFET bridges (24 FETs)
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path model
For 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM):
- At every instant, exactly two switch positions carry I: one high-side path and one low-side path.
- During the PWM off-time, the PWM'd high-side current transfers to the same leg's low-side FET, so the series resistance stays at 2·R_ds(on).
- Each leg conducts for 240° of 360° electrical. Over a full cycle, the conduction duty is shared across the 6 FETs.

### 1.2 Equations

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2\,\big(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{pcb} + R_{shunt}\big)$$

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}$$

The per-FET average is correct over a full electrical cycle. However, the instantaneous FET dissipation while conducting is I²·R_ds. The instantaneous value governs junction ripple at low eRPM; see §3.4.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C
- R_ds(110 °C) = 1.5 × 1.425 = **2.14 mΩ**
- FETs: 6400 × 2 × 2.14 m = **27.4 W**
- PCB copper: 6400 × 0.5 m = **3.2 W**
- Shunt: 6400 × 0.25 m = **1.6 W**
- **P_cond,ESC = 32.2 W**
- Per-FET average: 27.4 / 6 = **4.56 W**
- Instantaneous per conducting FET: 6400 × 2.14 m = **13.7 W**

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point
R_ds depends on T_j, so solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_a + R_{th,ja}\cdot P(T_j) \;\Rightarrow\; T_j^* = \frac{T_a + R_{th}\,I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)/3}{1 - R_{th}\,I^2 R_{25}\alpha/3}$$

Thermal runaway occurs if the denominator reaches 0, that is, if R_th·I²·R_25·α/3 ≥ 1:
- At 80 A: R_th,crit = 3 / (6400 × 1.5e-3 × 0.005) = **62.5 K/W per FET**.
- A soldered DFN on heavy copper is roughly 15–30 K/W to ambient, so steady state stays stable, but it is hot. See §3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. Its complementary low-side FET soft-switches (ZVS, turning on after dead time while the body diode conducts).

### 2.1 Components

**V-I overlap (hard-switched high-side):**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g},\qquad I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int} + R_{drv}}$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_pl = 3 V, R_total = 6.5 Ω:
- I_g ≈ 1.08 A, so t ≈ 8.3 ns per edge.
- Use 10 ns per edge, 20 ns total, to allow for di/dt limiting by the common-source inductance.
