## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two premises in the brief need correcting before the math is meaningful.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** Nearly every 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated low-cost MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), one per motor. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to those MCUs. Running 4× sensorless FOC/6-step at 48 kHz *plus* ArduPilot on one H743 is not supported by ArduPilot. This design therefore uses **H743 = FC** and **4× ESC MCUs = commutation**.
2. **"80A burst" and "nano" conflict thermally.** Section 4 shows that a 20×20 mm or 30×30 mm board cannot sustain 4 × 80 A for more than about 1–2 s. Treat 80 A as a **≤1 s per-motor burst rating**. The realistic continuous rating is about 25–35 A per motor. The model makes this explicit so the marketing number can be defended or revised.
