# STM32H743 AIO "Nano" Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #56)

## 0. Scope and Feasibility Findings

This package rests on four assumptions and produces two findings that change the requirements.

**Assumptions:**
- **Topology.** ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC bridges. The STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each of the four ESC channels has its own commutation MCU running AM32 (e.g. AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071), controlled over bidirectional DShot600.
- **Battery.** 6S LiPo: 25.2 V maximum, 22.2 V nominal, 30 V transient design limit (60 V-rated parts are not required; 40 V FETs are used).
- **Form factor.** "Nano" means a 20×20 mm (M2) footprint. I also evaluate 30.5×30.5 mm because the numbers force the comparison.
- **Burst definition.** 80 A per channel, all four channels simultaneously, at 100% duty (worst case).

**Finding 1: 80 A × 4 cannot be sustained on any nano-class PCB.** At 80 A burst, total ESC dissipation is about 130–150 W. Board heat capacity is about 2–5 J/K. The burst capability is therefore time-limited:

| Board | Time from 40 °C to 110 °C board temperature at 4×80 A |
|---|---|
| 20×20 | ~1.0 s |
| 30.5×30.5 | ~2.5 s |

The specification should read "80 A for ≤ 1 s (20×20) / ≤ 2 s (30.5)". Alternatively, change it to 80 A on a single channel.

**Finding 2: at 100% duty there are no switching losses.** Switching losses peak at partial duty (≈ 85–95%). The worst-case operating point therefore occurs below the 80 A peak, and both cases are calculated below.

All component values are design-intent. Verify every FET parameter against the selected part's datasheet; the symbols are kept explicit so they can be substituted directly.

## 1. Power Stage Mathematical Model

### 1.1 Commutation conduction pattern (AM32 six-step, complementary PWM)

Each electrical period is split into three roles per phase leg, each lasting ⅓ of the period:

| Leg role | High-side FET | Low-side FET |
|---|---|---|
| PWM leg | conducts for fraction D | conducts (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier |
| Low-on leg | off | conducts 100% |
| Floating leg (BEMF sense) | off | off |

The per-FET RMS-equivalent conduction duty over a full electrical period is:

$$\delta_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}, \qquad \delta_{LS} = \frac{(1-D)+1}{3} = \frac{2-D}{3}$$

The conduction loss per FET is:

$$P_{cond,HS} = I_\phi^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,\frac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{cond,LS} = I_\phi^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,\frac{2-D}{3}$$
