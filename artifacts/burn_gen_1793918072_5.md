## 0. Architecture Clarification and Risk Flags

1. **Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which handles commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 does not drive the gates. The models below are split along this boundary.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano board is thermally borderline.** Section 3 shows roughly 100 W of total dissipation when all four channels are at 80A. On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board, this is sustainable for seconds, not minutes. Define "burst" explicitly in the spec. I recommend ≤2 s at a 10% duty cycle.
3. **80A through PCB copper** needs ≥2 oz (preferably 3 oz) copper on multiple layers plus stitched planes. Copper I²R loss is the same order of magnitude as the MOSFET conduction loss and is often ignored.

## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

The commutation scheme is six-step trapezoidal with synchronous (complementary) PWM, which is standard for AM32 and BLHeli_32.

During any commutation sector, the current path always includes **two conducting FETs in series**:
- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T, and the synchronous low side conducts for (1−D)·T. The leg always has one FET on, except during dead time.
- **Sink leg:** the low side is on for the full sector.

This gives:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.51 mΩ**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.51\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.3\ W}$$

Copper loss: P_cu = I² · R_cu,total ≈ 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**.

Note that R_cu also rises at about 0.39 %/K.

### 2.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches.

**Linear V–I overlap:**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**
$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_f\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2 \times 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**
$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\text{nC} \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ W$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.036\ W$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in R_g and the driver, not the FET):
$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 0.058\ W$$

**Switching subtotal: about 3.4 W.**

Dead time is the second-largest switching term. Reducing t_dt from 200 to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. Only do this if the driver's propagation-delay mismatch allows it without shoot-through.

### 2.3 Per-Channel and Total Burst Loss

| Term | W / channel |
|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 19.3 |
| Copper | 3.2 |
| Switching + dead time + Q_rr + C_oss | 3.3 |
| Gate drive | 0.06 |
| **Total** | **≈ 25.9 W** |
| **4 channels at 80A** | **≈ 104 W** |

Conduction dominates at about 75%. **R_DS(on) and copper weight are the levers that matter.** Changing f_sw from 48 to 24 kHz saves only about 1.7 W per channel, and it costs current ripple and acoustic performance.

### 2.4 Bus Capacitor Ripple

The DC-link ripple current is:

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

This peaks at D = 0.5, giving **40 A_rms per channel** at 80A.

The four ESC MCUs' PWM is not phase-interleaved, so worst-case ripple adds. Size the capacitors as follows:
- **Ceramics** at each half-bridge: ≥4 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210, placed as close as possible to the high-side drain and low-side source loop.
- **Bulk:** a low-ESR electrolytic or polymer (≥1000 µF/35 V) on the pigtail.

The ceramics carry the 48 kHz harmonics. Check their rms rating, and check that the effective capacitance at 25 V DC bias is only about 30–40% of nominal.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Per-FET Steady-Equivalent Temperature Rise

Six FETs share the channel's FET loss of about 22.6 W. Each FET conducts about one-third of each electrical revolution. The electrical period at high eRPM (≈ 0.1–1 ms) is much shorter than the die thermal time constant, so averaging is valid:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx 22.6/6 \approx 3.8\ W, \qquad \Delta T_{jc} = 3.8 \times 1.5 = 5.7\ K$$
