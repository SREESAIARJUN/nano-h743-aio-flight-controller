## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

A "4-in-1 ESC" does not run its commutation on the H743. The production architecture is:

- **Flight controller (FC):** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and reads eRPM back.
- **ESC:** four independent 32-bit ESC MCUs, one per motor. Examples are AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. Each MCU produces the 48 kHz complementary PWM that drives its three half-bridges.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM phase):** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty D. The synchronous low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Phase B (static phase):** the LS is on continuously for the whole sector.
- **Phase C:** floating. Its back-EMF is sampled for zero-crossing detection.

The current I flows through exactly two channels at all times:

$$
P_{cond}(t) = I^2 R_{HS} D + I^2 R_{LS}(1-D) + I^2 R_{LS}
$$

With matched FETs (R_HS = R_LS = R):

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}
$$

The result is **independent of duty**. This is the key reason synchronous rectification is used.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on) and the coupled solution

$$
R(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0053\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

$$
R(110^\circ\text{C}) = 1.1\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

At 80 A:

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W\ per\ motor}
$$
