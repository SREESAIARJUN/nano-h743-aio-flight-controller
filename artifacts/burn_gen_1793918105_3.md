**Architecture.**
- The inner current loop uses the encoder angle for commutation.
- The velocity loop is closed on the **inertial gyro rate**, mapped into each joint. This lets base vibration be rejected by feedback, not merely tracked.
- The angle loop is closed on the fused attitude.
- Anti-cogging torque is injected as a current feedforward.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Use amplitude-invariant Clarke. With balanced currents, `i_a + i_b + i_c = 0`, only two shunts are needed:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, using the electrical angle `θ_e = p(θ_m − θ_0)`:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Delay compensation.** Currents are sampled at time k, but the resulting voltage applies on average 1.5 periods later. Use separate angles for each transform:
- Park: θ_e,k
- Inverse Park: θ̂_e = θ_e,k + 1.5·ω_e·T_c

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

In rotor coordinates the stator equations are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Decoupling.** Add the cross terms back as feedforward:
- v_d,ff = −ω_e·L·i_q
- v_q,ff = ω_e·L·i_d + ω_e·λ_m, where λ_m = 2K_t/(3p)

After decoupling, each axis is a first-order plant, G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

**Pole–zero cancellation.** The PI controller is C(s) = K_p + K_i/s. Set its zero at R/L. The closed loop then becomes 1/(s/ω_c + 1), with:

$$
K_p^{i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i}=R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c\le \frac{2\pi f_{PWM}}{10\text{–}20}
$$

For 20 kHz, choose ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s. This gives K_p = 28.3 V/A and K_i = 94 250 V/(A·s).

### 1.3 Discrete PI with Back-Calculation Anti-Windup

Forward Euler integration is acceptable because ω_c·T ≪ 1:

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_k &= r_k - y_k\\
u^{\text{raw}}_k &= K_p e_k + I_{k} + u_{ff,k}\\
u_k &= \operatorname{sat}(u^{\text{raw}}_k)\\
I_{k+1} &= I_k + T\big[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u_k - u^{\text{raw}}_k)\big],\qquad K_{aw}=K_i/K_p
\end{aligned}
$$

### 1.4 Voltage Limiting and SVPWM

**Circular limit, with d-axis priority:**
- V_max = V_bus/√3 (the linear SVPWM region)
- v_d is clamped first to ±V_max
- v_q is then clamped to ±√(V_max² − v_d²)

**Min-max zero-sequence injection** (equivalent to SVPWM):

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v + \min v),\quad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$
