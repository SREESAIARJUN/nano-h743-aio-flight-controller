# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the Job #46 hardware specification, so every hardware-dependent value below sits in one `Config` block that you can replace:

- **Motors:** 12N14P gimbal motors (7 pole pairs, GBM2804-class)
- **Motor encoders:** 14-bit magnetic encoders (AS5048A, MT6701, or similar)
- **Controller:** STM32G4-class MCU with an FPU, running at 170 MHz
- **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted symmetrically about the payload's rotational center

The equations in Parts 1–5 do not depend on those values.
