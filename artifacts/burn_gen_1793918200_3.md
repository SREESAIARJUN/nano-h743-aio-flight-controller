## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (e.g. GBM2804-class): pole pairs p = 7, slots Ns = 12 |
| Phase parameters | R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH, λ (flux linkage) from Kt = 1.5·p·λ |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A-class), SPI |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (e.g. STM32G474 @ 170 MHz) |
| Current sensing | Inline or low-side shunts, ADC triggered at the PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera platform, mounted symmetrically about the platform's center of rotation (r₂ = −r₁) |

**Loop rates (each loop has at least 5–10× bandwidth separation from the next):**

```
PWM 40 kHz center-aligned ─► Current loop (FOC)      20 kHz   BW ≈ 1.5 kHz
                             Velocity loop (gyro)     4 kHz    BW ≈ 150 Hz
IMU ODR 8 kHz ─────────────► Filters + fusion         4 kHz (decimated)
                             Angle/attitude loop      1 kHz    BW ≈ 25 Hz
                             Cogging LMS adaptation   4 kHz    (slow, μ small)
```

**Gimbal-specific topology.** The angle and velocity loops close on the **camera IMU**, which gives inertial stabilization. The encoder is used for commutation, cogging compensation, and the velocity PLL that serves as a fallback and blend source.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here θ_off is measured at startup by locking the d-axis (apply V_d, wait, then read the encoder).

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant)

Three-shunt sensing, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

$$\tau = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$
