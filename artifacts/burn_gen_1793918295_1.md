# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #36): Systems Architecture & Mathematical Model

**Classification:** Engineering design basis, Rev A
**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, schematic netlists, and thermal budget.

---

## 0. Architectural Clarification and Feasibility Notes

The spec needs three corrections before the model can be trusted.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs vehicle control. It does not implement 4-channel BLDC commutation or FOC. The architecture is therefore:
- **FC domain:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot, which outputs bi-directional DShot600.
- **ESC domain:** 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

The 48 kHz figure is an ESC-firmware parameter, not an H743 timer.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano-format board is a thermal-mass rating, not a capability.** The math below shows that 80 A on all four channels gives about 80 W of dissipation into roughly 5 J/K of board thermal mass. That is a window of a few seconds. The rating must be specified with a duration (I recommend **80 A / 1 s per channel, 45 A continuous with prop-wash airflow**).

**3. Component values are representative.** MOSFET parameters below are typical of a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3×3 or 5×6 mm power-FET class. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values. The equations hold; the numbers must be re-run.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path topology

In 6-step commutation (and approximately in sinusoidal drive at peak), current flows through one high-side leg and one low-side leg simultaneously. With synchronous (complementary) PWM at duty D:

- Active high-side FET conducts for D·T.
- Complementary low-side FET in the same phase conducts for (1 − D)·T.
- The opposite phase's low-side FET conducts for the full period.

So the conduction loss for one channel is:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds,hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot}
$$

Synchronous rectification makes the total independent of D. Only the distribution between FETs shifts.

### 2.2 Hot R_DS(on)

$$
R_{ds,hot} = k_T \cdot R_{ds,25} = 1.5 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

For accuracy, iterate with $R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$, where α ≈ 0.0059 /K. This gives 1.5 mΩ at about 110 °C.

### 2.3 Numbers

| Term | Expression | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| Copper conduction | 2 × 80² × 0.4 mΩ | **5.1 W** |
| **Total conduction per channel** | | **24.3 W** |

**Per-FET worst case** (D = 0.5, the opposite-phase low-side FET conducting 100%):

$$
P_{FET,max} = 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 9.6\,\text{W}
$$

This is the hot spot that drives the thermal design.

### 2.4 RMS vs. peak caveat

80 A here is the phase current. Battery (DC) current is about D·I_phase, so at D = 0.5 the battery current is only about 40 A per channel. Telemetry and current-sense scaling must state which current is reported.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per switching leg (one active half-bridge per channel per PWM period):

### 3.1 V–I overlap (hard switching)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
$$

### 3.2 Reverse recovery of the complementary body diode

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}
$$

### 3.3 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} \times 2 = 2.0\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.061\ W}
$$

The factor of 2 accounts for both FETs in the leg.

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.307\ W}
$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (6 FETs per channel)

$$
P_{gate} = 6 \cdot Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 6 \times 50\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.144\ W}
$$

This power is dissipated mostly in the gate driver and gate resistors, not the FETs.

### 3.6 Switching summary per channel

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.968 |
| Q_rr | 0.060 |
| C_oss | 0.061 |
| Dead time | 0.307 |
| Gate | 0.144 |
| **Total switching** | **1.54 W** |
