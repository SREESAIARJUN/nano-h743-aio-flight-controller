**Architectural flags. Review these before layout.**

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production AIO design, the H743 sends DShot commands. Each motor runs on its own small ESC MCU that does 6-step/FOC at 48 kHz. Driving 24 FETs directly from the H743 is not supported by ArduPilot.
2. **"Nano" plus 80 A burst is physically marginal.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A per channel cannot be sustained for more than a few seconds (see §3). 80 A burst ratings are normally 30.5×30.5 mm designs with 8 FETs per phase leg or larger packages. I model 30.5×30.5 mm as the baseline and give the 20×20 derating.
3. Vendor "burst" ratings are usually ~10 s at full throttle with prop wash. This model shows that rating is only credible with a strong thermal path.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With complementary PWM in 6-step commutation, at any instant two phases conduct:

- **Phase X (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw. One switch of this leg is always conducting.
- **Phase Y (return leg):** the low-side FET is on for the full commutation sector.

The motor current I_m therefore always flows through two R_ds(on) in series, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Phase inductor ripple:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

With D(1−D) maximized, V_bus = 25.2 V, L_ph = 10 µH (typical 5″–7″ motor), f_sw = 48 kHz:

$$\Delta I_{max} \approx \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{10\times10^{-6}\cdot 48\times10^{3}} = 13.1\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_m^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 14.3 = 6414\ \text{A}^2$$

At 80 A the ripple term adds only 0.2%, so it is negligible.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\,/\text{K}$$

| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.80 mΩ | 23.1 W | 92.4 W |
