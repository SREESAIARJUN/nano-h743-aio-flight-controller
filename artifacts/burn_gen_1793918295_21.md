| A2 | "80A burst" is ambiguous (per motor or total, battery or phase current). | **80 A RMS phase current per motor, all 4 motors simultaneously** (worst case = 320 A total) |
| A3 | No MOSFET or gate driver was specified. | Generic 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN device (parameters in §1). **Replace with datasheet values before release.** |
| A4 | "Nano" form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm board, 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner copper |
| A5 | Pack voltage | 6S, worst case 25.2 V (4.2 V/cell) |

**Headline conclusion:** 4×80 A on a 30.5 mm board is **transient only**. The thermal model (§3) gives about **3 seconds** of burst from a 45 °C board before the junction limit. Steady-state 4×80 A is physically impossible in this form factor. Market it as "80 A burst (≤2 s)" and plan for about 4×40–45 A continuous.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction losses (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous/complementary PWM)

Per 120° sector, three switches carry current. The PWM high-side conducts for duty D, its complementary low-side conducts for (1−D), and the opposite-phase low-side conducts 100% of the time.

Each leg spends ⅓ of the electrical cycle "high", ⅓ "low" and ⅓ floating. Per leg:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad P_{LS} = \left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}\right) I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{leg} = P_{HS}+P_{LS} = \frac{2}{3} I^2 R_{DS} \quad\Rightarrow\quad P_{cond,motor} = 3 \cdot \frac{2}{3} I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of D, which makes physical sense: there are always exactly two R_DS in the series path.

Temperature dependence, solved iteratively with the thermal model:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$
