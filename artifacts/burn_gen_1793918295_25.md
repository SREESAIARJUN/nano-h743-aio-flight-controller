## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

The prompt bundles two different control domains, so I'll separate them first.

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage.** ArduPilot outputs DShot600 to ESC MCUs. A separate ESC MCU (one per motor, or a dual-core arrangement) runs 6-step commutation and generates the 48 kHz PWM. AM32 on STM32G071 or AT32F421 is typical. ArduPilot has no native motor-commutation firmware, so an "H743 drives the gates directly" topology is not supportable.
2. **No MOSFET part number was specified.** All loss math below uses a parameterised, representative 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 device. Substitute your actual datasheet values into the equations. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are a worked instance.
3. **"80 A burst" is interpreted as 80 A per motor, all four simultaneously**, which is the worst case. This is extremely aggressive for a "nano" (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board. Section 4 shows it is thermally a seconds-scale rating, not a continuous one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path topology
In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) rectification, at any instant the motor current flows through exactly **one FET in each of two legs**. During the PWM ON interval it flows through the high side of leg A and the low side of leg B. During the OFF interval it freewheels through the sync-rectifier low side of leg A and the low side of leg B. Either way, two channels conduct.

So the total conduction loss per motor is:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$
