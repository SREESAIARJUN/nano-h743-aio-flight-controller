**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller only. Each of the four ESC channels has its own ESC MCU (e.g., STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32), driving a 3-phase gate driver (e.g., FD6288Q) and 6 MOSFETs. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the **ESC MCU**. The H743 talks to each ESC over a single-wire **bidirectional DShot600** line.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 100 °C and α = 0.005: R_ds = 0.70 × 1.375 = **0.96 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, one phase's high side is PWM'd, its low-side partner conducts complementarily, and one other phase's low side is held fully on. The motor current therefore always flows through exactly **two** MOSFET channels in series. This holds during t_on (HS_a + LS_b) and during t_off (LS_a + LS_b), ignoring dead time.

**Per-motor conduction loss:**

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

**Per-FET RMS current.** Each phase leg is active for 1/3 of the electrical period in each polarity:

$$
I_{HS,rms} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}}, \qquad I_{LS,rms} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{1-D}{3} + \tfrac{1}{3}}
$$

The second term under the LS root covers the "held-on" sector. The low-side FETs therefore run hotter at low duty. **Use the LS FET as the thermal worst case.**

### 1.3 Numerical result at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 0.96 mΩ | **12.3 W** |
| Copper/via path (R_Cu ≈ 0.15 mΩ ⚠) | 80² × 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| **P_cond per motor** | | **≈ 13.3 W** |
| Worst LS FET at D = 0.5 | 80² × (0.5/3 + 1/3) × 0.96 mΩ | **3.07 W** |
| HS FET at D = 0.5 | 80² × (0.5/3) × 0.96 mΩ | 1.02 W |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Per motor:

| Loss mechanism | Equation | Value @ 80 A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48k = **0.97 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | 2 · V_f · I · t_dt · f_sw | 2·0.8·80·100 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V_bus · Q_rr · f_sw | 25.2·50 nC·48k = 0.06 W |
| C_oss charge | ½ · C_oss · V_bus² · f_sw (HS+LS) | 2·½·2 nF·635·48k = 0.06 W |
| Gate drive (driver loss) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 2·50 nC·10 V·48k = 0.05 W |
| **P_sw per motor** | | **≈ 1.75 W** |
