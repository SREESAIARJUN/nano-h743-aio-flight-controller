# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #30): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware) that speaks DShot to the H743. All numbers below follow from the stated assumptions. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values before release.

**Reality check up front:** 80 A per channel on a 30×30 board is a thermally transient rating. The derivation below shows it is sustainable for seconds, not continuously. The realistic continuous rating is about 40 A per channel.
