# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #17): Electrical, Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and the 6-step commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The math below follows that split.

No datasheet set was supplied for Job #17, so these baseline parameters are assumptions. Replace them with your BOM values. All equations are written so you can substitute directly.

|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst case) |
