# Job #31: STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC

## Engineering Model, DShot Timer/DMA Allocation, Netlists and Thermal Analysis

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation MCUs, either 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32, or one 4-channel ESC MCU. | Architecture below uses H743 (FC) plus 4× AT32F421 (ESC). |
| F2 | **80 A per channel is only a burst rating on a nano (20×20 / 25×25 mm) board.** Calculated losses are about 80–100 W at 4×80 A. Board heat capacity supports this for about 2–3 s. | Continuous rating is about 30–35 A per channel with prop-wash cooling. |
| F3 | 4×80 A phase current at high duty means more than 250 A of battery current. Pads, XT60 and leads cannot carry that. | Specify burst as **per-channel, not simultaneous**, or derate. |
| F4 | Input-capacitor ripple reaches I/2 per channel (40 A RMS). On-board MLCC/polymer capacitors cannot absorb that alone. | A mandatory external low-ESR bulk capacitor (≥1000 µF / 35 V) at the battery pads. |
| F5 | The H743 is LQFP100 (14×14 mm) or TFBGA100 (8×8 mm). | **TFBGA100 required** for nano form factor, which means an HDI or 8-layer stack-up. |

All device parameters below are **design assumptions**. Replace them with your selected MOSFET and driver datasheet values; the equations stay valid.
