## 0. Scope and Ground Rules

Three architectural facts constrain everything below.

1. **The H743 does not drive the gates.** ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware. The board is two systems on one PCB:
   - **FC domain:** STM32H743, running ArduPilot, emitting bidirectional DShot600.
   - **ESC domain:** four independent ESC MCUs, assumed to be AT32F421 running AM32 (one per motor). Each runs 6-step commutation at 48 kHz complementary PWM.
2. **"80A burst" is a transient rating, not a thermal steady state.** On a 30×30 mm board this cannot be sustained. Section 3 derives the allowable burst duration.
3. **Component values are representative and must be checked.** Where I assume a component value, it is stated explicitly. Before release, re-run the model with the datasheet maximum values for your chosen parts.
