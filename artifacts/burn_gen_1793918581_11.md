# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Engineering Model (Job #25)

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

Four issues bound this design before any modeling begins.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The H743 should not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no 48 kHz field-oriented/trapezoidal motor control, and bidirectional DShot (BDShot) only makes sense between the FC and separate ESC MCUs. | Use 4× AT32F421 running AM32 (one per motor) to drive the FETs at 48 kHz. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In synchronous trapezoidal drive, exactly two phases are active in each 60° sector, and current always flows through two FETs:

- **PWM on-time (fraction D):** high side of phase X, then low side of phase Y.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** synchronous rectification through the low side of phase X, then the low side of phase Y.

So, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The high side carries the current for D·(1/3) of the cycle; the low side carries it for (1/3)(1−D) + 1/3 (sync rectification plus return).

The low side therefore runs hotter at low throttle. At burst (D → 0.9–1), the losses are roughly balanced.

### 1.2 Temperature-coupled solution

Because R_ds depends on T_j, solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta C\text{-}PCB})$$

At T_j ≈ 100 °C: R_ds = 1.3 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) ≈ **1.74 mΩ**. Use 1.95 mΩ as a conservative design value that includes the solder joint and the spread across parts.

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.95\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.9\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{24.96\ W/motor}$$

**Copper loss:** 2 oz, 8-layer, with phase pours that are effectively 4 layers in parallel, gives R_Cu ≈ 0.15 mΩ per path.

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.15\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 0.96\ W/motor$$

**Current-sense shunt** (0.25 mΩ, single DC-link shunt per ESC): 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = 1.6 W per motor, or about 0.6 W if Kelvin-sensed at 0.1 mΩ.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWMed phase hard-switches: one FET pair per sector.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching)

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} + Q_{gs2}}{I_g}, \qquad I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_g} = \frac{10 - 3.5}{6.8\,\Omega} \approx 1\ \text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; t_r \approx t_f \approx 10\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Other switching terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.048 W |
| Coss | ½ Q_oss · V · f (approx.) | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f = 0.8 × 80 × 400 ns × 48 k | **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (driver IC) | Q_g · V_drv · f × 2 switching FETs = 50 n × 10 × 48 k × 2 | 0.048
