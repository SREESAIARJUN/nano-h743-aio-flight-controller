### Architecture Decision

The H743 does not drive gates directly. Each motor gets a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) that performs commutation at 48 kHz. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to these ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back. This is how virtually all production AIO boards are built, and it is what ArduPilot supports.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification, exactly two legs conduct at any instant. In each conducting leg, either the high-side (HS) or the low-side (LS) FET carries the current. The current therefore always passes through two R_ds(on) in series.

**Hot on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds,hot} = k_T \cdot R_{ds(on),25} = 1.5 \times 0.75\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.125\,\text{m}\Omega$$

**Conduction loss per motor:**

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I^2 R_{ds,hot} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.125\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{14.4\ W}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each leg conducts for 240° of the 360° electrical cycle. HS and LS share that time according to duty D:

$$P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot D,\qquad P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot(1-D) + I^2 R_{ds,hot}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}$$

The exact split depends on the firmware's commutation scheme. A design-safe bound is that the worst FET carries about I²R_ds,hot × 1/3 ≈ **2.4 W**.

**Copper and interconnect loss:**

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.2\ W/motor}$$

This term is often overlooked. On a nano board it is about 20 % of the total loss, so prioritize stitched pours, multiple vias, and direct motor pads.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-modulated FET (one per motor at a time) hard-switches.

### 2.1 Overlap (V·I) loss

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance loss

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 60\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.073\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery loss

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive loss

This loss is dissipated mostly in the gate driver and R_G, not in the FET.

$$P_g = Q_g V_{DRV} f_{sw}\times 2 = 100\text{nC}\times10\times48\text{k}\times2 = \mathbf{0.096\ W}$$
