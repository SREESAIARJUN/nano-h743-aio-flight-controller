## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous rectification

In any 60° commutation sector, one leg (A) is PWM'd high-side, one leg (B) is held low-side ON, and one leg (C) floats.

- **PWM-on (duty D):** current flows Q_AH → motor → Q_BL
- **PWM-off (1−D):** current flows Q_AL (sync-rect) → motor → Q_BL

Each switch is "active" for 1/3 of the electrical cycle in each role. Per leg over one electrical cycle:

$$
P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R_{ds,T}\cdot\frac{D}{3}
$$

$$
P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R_{ds,T}\left[\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right]
$$

$$
P_{cond,leg} = I^2 R_{ds,T}\cdot\frac{2}{3} \quad\text{(independent of } D\text{)}
$$

Summed over three legs, two switches are always in series:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}
$$

The temperature dependence is approximately:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}
$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond / channel | P_cond / board (×4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** | **76.8 W** |

### 1.3 Copper / interconnect loss

The power path is battery pad → bus planes → FET → phase pad. Assume about 0.3 mΩ effective per channel (plane spreading plus vias plus solder):

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\text{ m}\Omega = 1.9\text{ W / channel}
$$

Shunt (if per-channel 0.5 mΩ sensing): 3.2 W at 80A. **Recommendation:** use one bus shunt at 0.25 mΩ (1.6 W at 320A total is not credible). Use a Hall sensor on the battery lead instead, and estimate per-channel current in ESC firmware.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches in each sector. Hard switching happens on the HS turn-on (LS body diode recovers) and the HS turn-off.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

The overlap time can be derived from the gate drive:

$$
t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g},\qquad I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}}
$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_pl ≈ 3.2 V, and R_g total = 3.5 Ω, this gives I_g ≈ 1.9 A and t ≈ 6 ns intrinsic. The 15 ns per edge figure is deliberately slowed with R_g,ext for dV/dt and EMI control (about 1.7 V/ns).
