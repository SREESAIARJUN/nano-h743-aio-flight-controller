# STM32H743 AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #31): Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

### 0.1 Architecture

The H743 does not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no in-tree FOC or 6-step gate-drive path for H7 boards. The practical architecture is:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) ──4× bi-dir DShot600──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32-class)
                                          └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6 MOSFETs per motor (24 total)
```

The 48 kHz figure is the ESC firmware PWM frequency (set in AM32/BLHeli). It is not an H743 output.

### 0.2 Interpretation of "80A burst"

**80A burst = 80A per-motor phase current at 100% duty for ≤1 s.** This interpretation drives the following consequences:
- At 100% duty, battery current is 4 × 80 × D = **320A** across all four motors.
- No nano (20×20 mm) or XT30/XT60 power path can carry 320A.
- §3 shows the board is thermally limited to **sub-second bursts** at this level.

**Treat "80A" as a marketing-class burst number. Do not use it as a design point.**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation model

The model assumes 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32 default.

Over one electrical cycle, phase A is:
- **High-side (A+) for 2 of 6 steps.** HS_A is on for D; LS_A is on for (1−D) as synchronous freewheel.
- **Low-side (A−) for 2 of 6 steps.** LS_A is on 100%.
- **Floating for 2 of 6 steps.**

Conduction duty fractions per device:

$$\delta_{HS}=\frac{D}{3},\qquad \delta_{LS}=\frac{(1-D)+1}{3}=\frac{2-D}{3}$$

Summed over the three phases: Σδ = D + (2 − D) = 2. **Exactly two FETs carry the full current at every instant, independent of duty.** Therefore:

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j),\qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Temperature self-consistency

Conduction loss depends on R_ds, which depends on T_j, which depends on loss:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,R_{th,eff}$$

Solving for T_j gives a closed form with a runaway condition:

$$T_j=\frac{T_{ref}+ \delta I^2R_{25}R_{th}(1-25\alpha)}{1-\delta I^2R_{25}\alpha R_{th}}$$

**Thermal runaway occurs when δI²R₂₅·α·R_th ≥ 1.**

### 1.3 Numbers at I = 80A, D = 1, T_j ≈ 115 °C

- R_ds = 1.5 × (1 + 0.0045 × 90) = **2.11 mΩ**
- Per-FET loss (δ = 1/3): 6400 × 0.00211 / 3 = **4.50 W**
- Per motor (6 FETs): 2 × 6400 × 0.00211 = **27.0 W**
- Copper per motor: 2 × 6400 × 0.0005 = **6.4 W**
- **Four motors, conduction plus copper: ≈ 134 W**
