# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack: Electrical, Thermal, and DShot Design Package (Job #32)

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three issues change how the requirements must be interpreted.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and sends bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an STM32G071, AT32F421, or GD32E230. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those MCUs, not by ArduPilot. The model below is partitioned this way.
2. **80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally sustainable.** The math in §2 shows this. 80 A is achievable only as a burst of about 1 s. The design target should be 80 A for ≤1 s, 35–40 A continuous, on a 30.5×30.5 mm board.
3. **"80 A" is defined here as peak phase current.** Battery current is lower by the duty factor. At full throttle (D → 1) they converge, giving 4 × 80 A = 320 A from the pack. That figure drives connector, trace, and capacitor sizing.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel, six-step with synchronous rectification)

### 2.1 Conduction

In six-step commutation with complementary PWM, current flows through exactly two FETs at all times:
- **PWM on:** high-side of phase A plus low-side of phase B.
- **PWM off:** low-side of phase A plus low-side of phase B.

Therefore:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2\,(2\,k_T R_{DS,25} + R_{Cu})$$
