**Key finding.** At 80 A per motor, the board dissipates about **130 W** across four channels. A 30.5 mm board can sustain that only as a short thermal-capacitance burst of about 2–3 s from a warm start. The honest continuous rating is about **35–40 A per motor** with prop-wash cooling. Derivations follow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During any commutation sector, one phase is PWM-switched (HS/LS complementary), one phase has its LS held on, and one phase floats. The motor current I<sub>m</sub> therefore always flows through **exactly two conducting MOSFET channels**:

- **PWM on-time (D):** HS<sub>A</sub> → motor → LS<sub>B</sub>
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** LS<sub>A</sub> (sync rectifier) → motor → LS<sub>B</sub>

$$
P_{cond,FET} = I_m^2\left[D\cdot R_{HS} + (1-D)\cdot R_{LS} + R_{LS}\right] \;\xrightarrow{R_{HS}=R_{LS}=R}\; 2\,I_m^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle**. This is an important design property of synchronous 6-step drive.

### 1.2 Temperature coefficient

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.70 = **2.04 mΩ**.
