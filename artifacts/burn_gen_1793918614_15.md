# Job #33: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst, 48 kHz PWM, Bidirectional DShot600)

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four 3-phase bridges itself. It has only two advanced timers (TIM1/TIM8) with complementary outputs, which covers two bridges. HRTIM does not cleanly cover the rest. The architecture is therefore:

- **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC:** four bridge MCUs running AM32 at 48 kHz. Options are 4× STM32G071/AT32F421, or 2× dual-motor MCUs.

**Form-factor warning.** Sustained 4 × 80 A will not fit a true 20×20 mm "nano" board. The thermal model below uses a 36×36 mm ESC (30.5 mm mounting) with 8-layer, 2 oz copper. Section 3.5 shows how the numbers scale for 20×20.
