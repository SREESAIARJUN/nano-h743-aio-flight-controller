## 0. Architecture Baseline and One Critical Finding

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Flight controller MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz, ArduPilot ChibiOS |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32. The H743 sends DShot; it does not commutate. |
| Gate driver | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) or equivalent |
| Bus | 6S LiPo: V_bus,max = 25.2 V, nominal 22.2 V |
| MOSFETs | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3. R_DS(on),25°C,max = 1.2 mΩ. Q_g = 60 nC, Q_gd ≈ 10 nC, Q_gs2 ≈ 5 nC, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, C_oss,eff ≈ 1.5 nF |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), dead-time 200 ns |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm mount, ~36×36 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz on all power layers |

**Critical finding: the stated ratings and the stated form factor conflict.**

- An 80 A *continuous* rating per channel is not physically achievable on a nano (20×20) or 30×30 board.
- 80 A is achievable only as a time-limited burst, on the order of 1–2 s with all four motors loaded.
- The burst rating must therefore be specified with a duration and a starting temperature (Section 3).
- Using 40 V FETs on 6S leaves about 15 V of margin for switching-node overshoot. This requires a tight commutation loop and adequate bulk and ceramic capacitance **[VERIFY ringing on scope at 80 A turn-off]**.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 135 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.495 ≈ **1.8 mΩ**. This hot-max value is the design point.

### 1.2 Current path in trapezoidal six-step with complementary PWM

Take phase A as the PWM'd high side and phase B as the low side held on:

| Interval | Conducting devices |
|---|---|
| D·T (on) | HS_A + LS_B |
| (1−D)·T (off) | LS_A (synchronous rectification) + LS_B |

At every instant, exactly two FETs carry the phase current I. This makes conduction loss independent of duty cycle, apart from the dead-time interval:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,\frac{R_{DS}(T_j)}{N_p}$$

N_p is the number of paralleled FETs per switch position.

### 1.3 Results at I = 80 A

| Configuration | P_cond per channel | Per-FET dissipation |
|---|---|---|
| N_p = 1 (6 FETs/channel) | 2 × 6400 × 1.8 m = **23.0 W** | 11.5 W |
| N_p = 2 (12 FETs/channel) | **11.5 W** | 2.9 W |

**Recommendation:** use N_p = 2 for an 80 A burst target. At N_p = 1, an 11.5 W single-FET hotspot produces the junction-to-board gradient shown in Section 3.4.
