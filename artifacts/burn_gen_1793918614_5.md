# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Math Model (Job #15)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and an Upfront Feasibility Flag

The requested numbers cannot all hold at once:

> **80 A burst per channel on a nano-class (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is not thermally achievable for more than about 1–2 s with all four channels loaded.**
>
> Section 2.6 shows this quantitatively. The design below targets a **30.5×30.5 mm AIO footprint** (~36×36 mm outline), the smallest size where 80 A *burst* (≤2 s) is defensible.
>
> If a true 20×20 mm board is mandatory, plan for **≈35–40 A burst per channel**.

## 2. Power Stage Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At $T_j = 125\,^\circ$C: $R_{ds} = 1.0\times1.55 = 1.55$ mΩ.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

In 6-step complementary PWM, current at any instant flows through:

- the PWM'd leg, where the high-side conducts for $D$ and the low-side synchronously rectifies for $1-D$, and
- one statically-on low-side FET.

The PWM'd leg therefore contributes one $R_{ds}$ at all times, and the static leg contributes another. Including inductor ripple:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\left[1+\frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I}\right)^2\right]$$

At 80 A burst, with ripple ≤ 20 % (correction factor 1.003, negligible):

$$P_{cond} = 2\times 80^2\times1.55\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.8\ W}$$
