## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 cannot drive four 3-phase bridges with sensorless commutation while also running ArduPilot. The architecture is therefore split:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 at 480 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32, bidirectional DShot, 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, integrated dead time) | — |
| Power FETs ×24 | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | — |

> **Feasibility flag (read first):** 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm board is a **transient rating only**. Section 3 shows it is limited to about 1.3 s with all four channels saturated. Any marketing figure must be stated as a burst with a duration, never as continuous.
