## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** ArduPilot does not run motor commutation. The architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──bi-dir DShot600 ×4──► 4 × ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class,
                                                e.g. STM32G071 / AT32F421)
                                              ──► 3-ph gate driver ──► 6 × N-MOSFET per motor
```

Every number below is derived from the **example component values stated in each section**. Replace them with your selected parts' datasheet values. The equations are general.

### Findings that affect the spec

1. **An 80 A burst on a 20×20 / 25.5 mm nano board is a sub-second rating.** The model gives about 0.4 to 0.7 s at 80 A on all four channels before the junctions approach their limit.
2. **Continuous capability is about 15 A per channel**, limited by board-to-air convection, not by the silicon.
3. **"80 A" must be specified as per-motor, ≤0.5 s.** Four simultaneous channels at 80 A means 320 A from the pack. That is beyond any XT30/XT60 lead set or a nano board's power plane.
4. **Conduction loss dominates.** It is about 28 W per channel at 80 A, versus about 2.7 W of switching loss at 48 kHz.
