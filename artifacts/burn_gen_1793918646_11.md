## 0. Feasibility Findings

These results come from the derivations in Sections 2–5. Review them before committing to layout.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | 80 A on all four channels at 6S dissipates **about 127 W** in the power stage. A 20×20 mm board has roughly 2 J/K of thermal mass, so it heats at about 60 K/s. | An all-motor 80 A burst is limited to about **1 s**. A single-channel burst can last about 4–5 s. |
| F2 | Battery input of 4 × 80 A = 320 A through a 20×20 board's pads and planes is not physically credible. Pad I²R alone is several watts, and the XT60 connector is rated at 60 A. | Re-rate to **"80 A single-channel burst / 45 A continuous"**, or move to a 30×30 board with 5×6 mm FETs. |
