# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #57): Design Model and Reference Netlists

## 0. Scope, Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. In this document that MCU is an AT32F421 / STM32G071-class part. Its PWM carrier runs at 48 kHz.

The design therefore has three coupled domains:

1. **FC domain:** H743, timers, DMA, DShot.
2. **ESC control:** per-motor MCU at 48 kHz PWM.
3. **Power stage:** 24 MOSFETs, gate drivers, bulk capacitance, thermal path.

**Baseline assumptions.** Everything below is parameterized, so substitute your datasheet values and re-run.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S, worst-case (full charge) | 25.2 V |
