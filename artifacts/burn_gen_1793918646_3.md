## 0. Architectural Corrections (Read First)

Three issues in the brief must be resolved before any of the math is meaningful.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted in this document |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot outputs DShot commands. It does not run 6-step or FOC commutation for BLDC motors. A "4-in-1 ESC" on an H743 board therefore needs dedicated ESC MCUs. | Topology: the H743 runs ArduPilot. Four ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those ESC MCUs, not the H743. |
| 2 | **"80 A burst" is undefined.** It could mean per motor or total. | The model uses **80 A per channel**, the worst case. Section 2.6 gives scaling for total-current interpretations. |

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction topology

In 6-step synchronous drive, phase current I flows through exactly two conducting paths at any instant:

- The PWM leg: either the high-side FET (during on-time D) or its complementary low-side FET (during off-time 1 − D).
- The static low-side FET of the return phase.

The total conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

With temperature dependence included:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

**At 80 A and T_j = 125 °C:**

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\,\text{mΩ} = \mathbf{30.7\ W}
$$

This splits as about 15.4 W in the static low-side FET and 15.4 W shared between the PWM-leg pair.

### 2.2 Switching loss (hard-switched leg, 48 kHz)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery, Coss, and dead-time losses

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 40n × 25.2 × 48k | 0.048 W |
| Coss | 2 × ½ Q_oss V_bus f_sw = 30n × 25.2 × 48k | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | 2 · V_SD · I · t_dt · f_sw = 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 200n × 48k | **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in the driver) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 × 40n × 10 × 48k | 0.038 W |

> **Design lever:** Dead-time loss exceeds hard-switching loss here. Reducing t_dt from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. The minimum dead time is set by the gate driver's propagation-delay mismatch, so the gate driver choice matters.

### 2.4 Shunt and copper losses

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt | I² R_sh = 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| PCB copper | I² R_cu, with R_cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ (per-channel path, 2 oz, 4 internal layers paralleled) | 1.9 W |

### 2.5 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A

| Loss | W per channel | W per board (×4) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 30.7 | 122.8 |
| Switching + Q_rr + Coss | 1.05 | 4.2 |
| Dead time | 1.23 | 4.9 |
| Shunt + copper | 3.5 | 14.0 |
| **Total** | **≈ 36.5 W** | **≈ 146 W** |

**Efficiency check:** Output power is about 25.2 × 80 × 0.95 ≈ 1.9 kW per channel at full duty, so electrical efficiency is about 98 %. The board may be efficient, but the absolute heat on a tiny PCB is the real problem.

### 2.6 Scaling law

Conduction dominates, so per-channel loss scales roughly as I²:

$$
P(I) \approx 36.5 \times \left(\frac{I}{80}\right)^2\ \text{W}
$$

| Current per channel | Loss per channel |
|---|---|
| 60 A | 20.5 W |
| 45 A | 11.5 W |
| 20 A (i.e., 80 A total across four motors) | 2.3 W, a trivial case |

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (per hot FET, Foster approximation)

$$
T_j(t) = T_{amb} + P_{FET}\sum_{i=1}^{n} R_i\left(1-e^{-t/\tau_i}\right) + \Delta T_{board}(t)
$$

The board is treated as a lumped thermal mass shared by all channels:

$$
C_{board}\frac{dT_b}{dt} = P_{total} - \frac{T_b - T_{amb}}{R_{conv}}
$$

### 3.2 Board parameters (25.5 × 25.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz, about 1.6 mm thick)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Mass (PCB + FETs + components) | ≈ 4.5 g |
| Effective specific heat c_p | ≈ 0.65 J/g·K |
| Thermal capacitance C_board | ≈ **2.9 J/K** |
| R_conv (prop-wash airflow ~5 m/s, both faces, h ≈ 40 W/m²K, area ≈ 1.3 × 10⁻³ m²) | ≈ **19 K/W** |
| Board thermal time constant τ_b = R·C | ≈ 55 s |

### 3.3 Steady state (proves 80 A cannot be continuous)

$$
\Delta T_{board,ss} = 146 \times 19 \approx 2800\ \text{K}
$$
