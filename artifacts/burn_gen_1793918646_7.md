## 0. Architecture Clarification and Stated Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction

**An STM32H743 cannot be the ESC commutation controller in an ArduPilot system.** ArduPilot does not run motor commutation or FOC.

The correct architecture, used by every production AIO, has two controller domains:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, bdshot eRPM consumer |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 (supports 48 kHz PWM and bdshot) | 6-step commutation, gate control, eRPM telemetry |

Every figure below is derived for this split.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology

Each ESC is a 3-phase bridge with 6 FETs, giving 24 FETs in total. With 6-step commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for duty D, and the synchronous low-side conducts for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side conducts for 100 % of the 60° sector.

Phase current is continuous (inductive motor, f_sw ≫ L/R corner), so I_rms ≈ I_ph.

### 1.2 Equation

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS,hot}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS,hot} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS,hot}
$$

The result is independent of D. This is a key property of synchronous rectification: two FET resistances are always in series with the phase current.

$$
R_{DS,hot} = k_T R_{25} = 1.6 \times 1.0\ \text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}
$$

### 1.3 Copper and interconnect loss

Do not omit this term. It is often comparable to FET loss on nano boards.

$$
R_{cu} = \frac{\rho_{cu}\,(1+\alpha\Delta T)\,L}{w\,t\,n_{layers}}
$$

Inputs: ρ = 1.72×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, α = 0.00393/K, ΔT = 60 K, L = 12 mm effective phase path, w = 4 mm, t = 70 µm, 4 paralleled layers.

$$
R_{cu} \approx \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times1.236\times0.012}{0.004\times70\times10^{-6}\times4} \approx 0.23\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Two path segments carry current, so P_cu ≈ 2 × 80² × 0.23 mΩ ≈ **2.9 W per ESC**.

The shared battery bus carries the sum of the battery currents, I_batt = Σ D·I_ph. It must be sized separately using the same equation.

### 1.4 Per-device distribution

Each FET conducts for roughly one third of the electrical cycle. Average loss per FET:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ESC}}{6} \approx 3.4\ \text{W}
$$

The peak instantaneous loss is I²R = **10.2 W**, rippling at the electrical frequency (kHz range). The die and package thermal mass (τ ≈ ms) partially filter this ripple.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Two FETs per ESC hard-switch per PWM period: the PWM leg pair.

### 2.1 Overlap (V–I crossover) loss

$$
P_{ov} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

###
