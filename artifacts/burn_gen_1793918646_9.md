## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

This section states what this document assumes, because the results depend directly on these choices.

### 0.1 Topology

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs (TIM1 and TIM8). That is enough for two 3-phase bridges, not four. The standard topology is therefore used:

```
                      DShot600 (bidir, 1 wire/motor)
  STM32H743 (FC) ──────────────────────────────────►  4 × ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli32)
   ArduPilot                                              │  6 × PWM (complementary, 48 kHz)
                                                          ▼
                                                    Gate driver (FD6288Q class, 3 half-bridges)
                                                          ▼
                                                    6 × N-MOSFET, 3-phase bridge per motor
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

Assume trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, which is the AM32/BLHeli32 default:

- At any instant, two phases carry current I.
- The PWM'd leg alternates the current between its high-side FET (duty D) and its low-side FET (1−D) via synchronous rectification.
- The non-PWM'd leg holds one FET fully on.

So there are always exactly **two R_ds(on) in series** with the load current, independent of D (dead time excluded):

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]
$$

| T_j | R_ds(on) | P_cond per ESC (80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 85 °C | 1.27 mΩ | 16.3 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W |
| 150 °C | 1.56 mΩ | 20.0 W |

**Design value:** use 125 °C, giving **P_cond = 18.6 W per ESC**.

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each FET position carries current for 1/3 of the time on average. That is 2/3 conduction per phase, split between the HS and LS FETs.

$$
\bar P_{FET,cond} = \frac{P_{cond,ESC}}{6} \approx 3.1\ \text{W}
$$

The **instantaneous** peak is ½·P_cond ≈ 9.3 W in one FET during its 120° conduction window. At high eRPM (>1 kHz electrical) the junction thermal time constant averages this out. At **low-RPM / high-torque (stall, punch-out from hover)** it does not, which makes stall the worst case.

### 1.4 Thermal-Runaway Criterion

Conduction loss rises with temperature, so a stable operating point requires:

$$

\;\;\Rightarrow\;\;
2 I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{th,ja} < 1
$$
