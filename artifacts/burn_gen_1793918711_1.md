## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park uses a delay-compensated angle. One sample of computation plus half a PWM period of ZOH gives:

$$
\theta_e^{*} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupled PI

The PMSM d-q model:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Feedforward decoupling:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m),\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}
$$

The plant then reduces to $1/(Ls+R)$. Pole-zero cancellation places the closed loop at a first-order bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_s}{15}\;(\approx 2\pi\cdot1.3\text{ kHz})
$$

Discretization uses backward Euler: $I_k = I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k$.

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

The linear SVPWM limit is $V_{max} = V_{bus}/\sqrt3$. The vector is limited with d-axis priority:

$$
v_d^{sat}=\text{clip}(v_d,\pm V_{max}),\quad
v_q^{sat}=\text{clip}\!\left(v_q,\pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^{sat\,2}}\right)
