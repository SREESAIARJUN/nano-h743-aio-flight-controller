## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, two phases are active: one high-side FET (HS) on one leg and one low-side FET (LS) on another.

- **ON interval (fraction D):** HS_a and LS_b conduct the phase current I_ph.
- **OFF interval (1 − D):** the synchronous rectifier LS_a (complementary) and LS_b conduct I_ph.

In either interval, exactly **two FET channels carry I_ph**. Bus current and phase current are related by:

$$I_{bus} = D \cdot I_{ph}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

With α = 0.0067 K⁻¹ and T_j = 100 °C:

R_DS = 1.2 × 1.50 = **1.80 mΩ**
