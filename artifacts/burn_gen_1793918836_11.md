# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual ICM-42688-P Vibration Rejection

I don't have the specification for Job #55, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute your real motor and frame values. The ICM-42688-P register values are quoted from the datasheet, but you should verify them against your silicon revision before flashing.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, single-precision FPU, center-aligned PWM |
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701) |
| Current sense | 2- or 3-shunt low-side, sampled at the PWM center |
| IMU-A | ICM-42688-P on the **payload** (camera). Used for attitude feedback. |
| IMU-B | ICM-42688-P on the **base/handle**. Used for disturbance feedforward and as the vibration-cancellation reference. |
| Gimbal order | Yaw (outer, base) → Roll → Pitch (inner, payload) |

### Loop Rates and Bandwidth Separation

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz |
| Joint velocity | 4 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·120 Hz |
| Attitude (inertial) | 1 kHz (IMU at 8 kHz, decimated) | ω_θ ≈ 2π·25 Hz |

Each outer loop's bandwidth stays at least 4–5× below the loop inside it, and at least 5× below that loop's sample rate.
