## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here θ_off is found at startup by aligning the d-axis (inject V_d, lock the rotor, then read the encoder).

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, balanced phases)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 PMSM model and decoupling

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

The PI controllers are applied together with cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

with λ_m = Kt / (1.5 p).

### 1.5 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

Placing the PI zero on the electrical pole (Ki/Kp = R/L) makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{PWM}}{10}$$

With f_PWM = 20 kHz, ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s. This gives Kp = 20.7 V/A and Ki = 52.8 kV/(A·s).

### 1.6 Voltage limit

The (v_d, v_q) vector is circle-limited:

$$|v| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}$$

The d-axis gets priority, and the q-axis receives the remainder.

### 1.7 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min/max zero-sequence injection)

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

$$v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\right),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.8 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup

Let T_s be the sample period:

$$u_k^{unsat} = K_p e_k + I_k + u^{ff}_k,\qquad u_k = \mathrm{sat}(u_k^{unsat})$$

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw} T_s\,(u_k - u_k^{unsat}),\qquad K_{aw}\approx \frac{K_i}{K_p}$$
