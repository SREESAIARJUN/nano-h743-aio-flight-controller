# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Mathematical Model and Reference Design

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I have no access to the Job #43 requirements package, so every input below is a stated assumption. Replace the values in **Table 0** with your actual BOM datasheet values (max ratings, not typical). The equations hold regardless. The netlists are reference-level designs to start schematic capture from. They have not been ERC/DRC-verified or built.

**Architectural clarification:** the STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. In a FC + 4-in-1 stack:

- The **H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bi-directional DShot600 commands and captures the eRPM telemetry replies.
- **Each ESC channel has its own MCU** (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). This MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM to a 3-phase gate driver.

If Job #43 truly intends the H743 to drive 12 FETs directly (4 motors × 6), that is a different architecture. It is not supported by ArduPilot and needs 24 complementary PWM outputs with dead-time. Flag it if so.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance
$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha_R (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.66 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step commutation
During each 60° sector, phase A is high (PWM'd) and phase B is low (held on):

- **PWM on (duty D):** current flows through HS_A and LS_B.
- **PWM off (1−D):** current flows through LS_A (synchronous rectifier) and LS_B.

So **exactly two FETs carry the full current at all times**:
$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Per-FET average over one electrical cycle (each phase is "high" for 120° and "low" for 120°):
$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}\,D \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}\,[1 + (1-D)]$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

| Quantity | D = 1.0 (burst full throttle) | D = 0.5 |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond per motor | 2 × 6400 × 1.66 m = **21.2 W** | 21.2 W |
| P_HS per FET | 3.54 W | 1.77 W |
| P_LS per FET | 3.54 W | 5.31 W |

The worst-case low-side FET is at mid-duty, high current. That is a reverse-thrust or braking corner case.

**PCB copper:** the phase path is about 0.25 mΩ (2 oz, ~15 mm × 3 mm × multiple layers plus vias), and the shared bus is about 0.3 mΩ. This adds:
$$P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 0.25\text{ m} \approx 1.6\text{ W/motor}$$

### 1.4 Ripple correction
For phase inductance L_ph ≈ 15 µH and back-EMF E:
$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$
$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

At D = 0.5 with E ≈ 12 V: ΔI ≈ 9.2 A, so the correction is +0.11 %. This is negligible at 80 A.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches (one HS/LS pair per motor at any instant).

| Loss term | Equation | Value @ 25.2 V, 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 24 ns × 48 k = **1.16 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.048 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw (hard-switched HS edge) | 60 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.073 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dead·f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 n × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 2 × 50 n × 10 × 48 k = **0.048 W** (dissipated in driver/R_g) |
| **Total P_sw per motor** | | **≈ 1.94 W** |

Switching loss scales with f_sw and goes to zero at D = 1. At 24 kHz, P_sw ≈ 0.97 W. 48 kHz buys lower acoustic noise and current ripple for about 1 W per motor.

**Recommendation:** cut dead time to 50 ns if the driver supports it. This saves about 0.3 W per motor.

## 3. Total Loss Budget

$$P_{motor} = P_{cond} + P_{sw} + P_{Cu} \approx 21.2 + 1.9 + 1.6 = 24.7\text{ W}$$
$$P_{ESC,total} = 4 \times 24.7 + P_{aux} \approx 99\text{ W} + \sim1\text{ W (LDOs, MCUs)} \approx \mathbf{100\text{ W}}$$

**Efficiency at burst:**
$$\eta = 1 - \frac{100}{4 \times 80 \times 25.2 \times D_{eff}} \approx 98.8\,\%$$
The efficiency is good. The absolute heat is the problem.
