# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #31): Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Dir DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Corrections

These issues affect every number below, so they come first.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors. Bi-directional DShot assumes a separate ESC MCU that decodes DShot and returns eRPM. | H743 is the **flight controller only**, running ArduPilot. The ESC uses **4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32** (or BLHeli_32/Bluejay), one per motor. |
| 2 | **80 A × 4 on a 20×20 mm (nano) board is not thermally survivable as a continuous rating.** | Section 3 shows ~145 W of dissipation at 4×80 A. It is a **~1 s burst** figure. A realistic continuous rating is **~28–32 A/channel** with prop-wash cooling. |
| 3 | 4×80 A at full throttle draws about **320 A from the battery**. XT30 and XT60 connectors and nano pads cannot carry that. | Spec the 80 A burst as **per-channel, non-simultaneous**, or move to a 30×30 form factor. |

All device parameters below are **representative assumptions**. Substitute the datasheet values of your chosen MOSFET and driver and re-run the equations.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Per-FET RMS current

Over one electrical cycle, each phase is driven high for 120°, low for 120°, and floats for 120°. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **High-side FET:** conducts for D of its 1/3 interval.
  I²_rms,HS = I² · D/3
- **Low-side FET:** conducts as the return path for 1/3 of the cycle, plus as the synchronous rectifier during the (1−D) freewheel interval.
  I²_rms,LS = I² · [1/3 + (1−D)/3]

### 2.2 Total per motor channel

Summing over three phases (three high-side plus three low-side FETs):

$$P_{cond,ch} = R_{ds}(T_j)\,I^2\left[D + 1 + (1-D)\right] = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of D. Two FETs are always in the current path.

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 2.7\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per channel | 2 × 80² × 2.7 mΩ = **34.6 W** |
| Per FET, high side at D≈1 | 80² × 2.7 m / 3 = **5.76 W** |
| Per FET, low side at D≈1 | **5.76 W** |
| All four channels | **138 W** |

### 2.4 PCB copper conduction loss

Example: a phase trace 10 mm long and 5 mm wide in 2 oz copper (70 µm):

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{w t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.01}{0.005\cdot 70\times10^{-6}} = 0.49\,\text{m}\Omega$$
