# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #10): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Model, DShot/DMA Allocation, and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 should not drive the power stage directly.** ArduPilot is flight-controller firmware and does not commutate motors. The standard, supportable architecture has two parts:

- **Flight controller:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to the ESC section.
- **4-in-1 ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs, one per motor. Suitable parts are AT32F421K8 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each MCU drives a three-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. AM32 supports 48 kHz PWM natively.

This document uses that architecture. The baseline assumptions are below. Replace them with your bill-of-materials (BOM) datasheet values, because every result scales with them.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C, R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.47 = **1.47 mΩ**. Use 1.5 mΩ as the design value. The thermal loop is closed in §4.4.

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, current I flows through two phases:

- **PWM'd phase:**
  - The high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw.
  - The complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET conducts 100 % of the time.

There are always exactly two R_ds in the current path, independent of D. The per-motor conduction loss is therefore:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each FET is active for 120° of the electrical cycle. Averaged over the cycle, the per-FET loss is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3}$$

Per-FET RMS current, used for checking FET ratings:

- Return-phase low-side FET: $I_{rms} = I\sqrt{1/3}$ = **46.2 A**.
- PWM'd high-side FET: $I\sqrt{D/3}$.
