# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #14): Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The 48 kHz PWM, BEMF sensing and commutation all run on those ESC MCUs.

This document models both sides of that split:

| Domain | Device | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, DShot600 TX/RX via TIM+DMA |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), AM32 | 48 kHz PWM, six-step commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class) | Bootstrapped HS drive, 12 V gate rail |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN | Inverter |
