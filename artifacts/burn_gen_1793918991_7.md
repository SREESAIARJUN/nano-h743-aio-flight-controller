# Job #48: STM32H743 Flight Controller + 80 A Burst 4-in-1 ESC

**Architecture Specification, Loss Models, DShot/DMA Allocation, Netlists**

---

## 0. Feasibility Verdict

Three findings change the brief before any detailed design.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The H743 cannot commutate the motors. Twelve-plus half-bridges at 48 kHz with BEMF sensing, plus the ArduPilot EKF, is not a credible partition. | The H743 is the **flight controller only**. Each motor gets a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). The H743 drives them over **bidirectional DShot600**. |
| F2 | A "nano" (20×20) form factor cannot hold an H743 (LQFP100 is 14×14 mm), 24 power FETs, gate drivers and bulk capacitance. | The design is a **30.5×30.5 two-board stack**: FC on top, 4-in-1 ESC below. |
| F3 | "80 A burst" on 30×30 copper is **thermally limited to roughly 1–2 s** with all four channels loaded. Section 2.6 derives this. | The spec must state burst duration and duty. The recommended label is **"80 A / 1 s, ~25–30 A continuous with prop-wash airflow."** |

All numbers below use a **representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN MOSFET**. They are design-intent values. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values before release.
