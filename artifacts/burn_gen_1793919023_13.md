## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, exactly two phase legs carry current I_ph:
- One leg is PWM-switched (HS on for D, LS synchronous for 1−D).
- One leg is held with its LS (or HS) continuously on.

With synchronous rectification, **exactly one FET per active leg conducts at every instant**. The instantaneous conduction loss is therefore duty-independent:

$$
P_{cond}(t) = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \quad\text{(ripple neglected)}
$$

### 1.2 Including PWM current ripple

Motor phase inductance [A] is L_ph = 15 µH line-to-line (typical 2207–2808 class, 1700–2500 KV). Ripple is worst case at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L_{LL} f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\,\mu H \cdot 48\,kHz} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 6.38 = 6406.4\ \text{A}^2
$$

At 80 A the ripple contribution is only 0.1 %, so it can be neglected at burst current. It matters at light load.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

Model R_DS(on) as a power law:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j+273}{298}\right)^{\alpha}
$$

Solving with R(125 °C)/R(25 °C) = 1.55 gives α = ln(1.55)/ln(398/298) = 1.51.

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.95 mΩ | 12.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.33 mΩ | 17.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.47 mΩ | 18.8 W |
