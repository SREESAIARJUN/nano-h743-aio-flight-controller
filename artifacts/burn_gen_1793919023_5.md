## 0. Architectural Clarification

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no in-tree sensorless FOC/6-step ESC firmware for running four motors from the FC MCU. The architecture therefore splits into two tiers:

| Tier | Device | Firmware | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (custom `hwdef.dat`) | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, current sense |
| Gate drive (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, 6 outputs) | — | High/low-side drive with bootstrap |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 × N-MOSFET, 3 half-bridges | — | 24 FETs total |

> **Feasibility warning.** "80 A burst" on a nano-format board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting) cannot be sustained thermally. The analysis below shows a credible burst duration of about 1–2 s at full load. Continuous rating is closer to 20–30 A per channel even with good prop-wash airflow. The model is derived so that you can set firmware current limits and marketing claims honestly.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous PWM)

### 2.1 Current Path

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM'd phase:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for fraction D and the complementary low-side (LS) FET conducts for 1−D (synchronous rectification).
- **Return phase:** the LS FET conducts continuously.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{on}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{on} = 2\,I^2 R_{on}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 At 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.1\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{26.9\ W}$$

Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the per-FET average is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{26.9}{6} \approx \mathbf{4.5\ W}$$

Peak instantaneous dissipation in a single FET (return LS or HS at D→1) is 80² × 2.1 mΩ = **13.4 W**. This matters at low eRPM, where the sector dwell time approaches the thermal time constant of the die.

### 2.3 Copper / Trace Loss (Often Ignored, Not Negligible)

Assume a phase path of about 15 mm of 4-layer, 2 oz parallel copper, 3 mm wide:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times 0.015}{4\times(70\,\mu m \times 3\,mm)} \approx 0.31\ m\Omega$$

Two paths × 80² × 0.31 mΩ ≈ **4.0 W per motor** in copper. This heat lands directly in the board.

### 2.4 Shunt Loss

With a 0.25 mΩ low-side common shunt per ESC:

$$80^2 \times 0.25\ m\Omega = \mathbf{1.6\ W}$$
