# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #60): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot/DMA Design

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Three framing issues change the math, so I'm addressing them first.

**1. The H743 should not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs, which run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The DShot protocol has no purpose if the H743 drives the bridges directly, and ArduPilot has no FOC/6-step commutation layer. The H743 also lacks enough complementary PWM: TIM1 and TIM8 give two 3-phase sets, and HRTIM gives about 10 outputs, while four motors need 24 gate signals. The 48 kHz switching therefore lives on the ESC MCUs.

**2. 80 A per motor is a burst rating on a nano form factor.** The model below shows that a 20×20 mm board cannot sustain it. It is time-limited to roughly 1–3 s. I treat that as a design constraint rather than ignoring it.

**3. MOSFET parameters are representative placeholders.** They describe a 40 V, ~0.7 mΩ PDFN5×6-class part. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values: R_DS(on)(T), Q_g, Q_gd, Q_rr, C_oss, Z_thJC curve. The equations stay valid.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_ds |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.70 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 0.94 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.02 mΩ |

Add **PCB copper and solder resistance**, which matters at this scale:

$$R_{path} \approx 2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{solder}$$

R_Cu for a short 2 oz, 4-layer stitched phase pour is about 0.15–0.3 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous conduction

In each 60° commutation sector, current flows through one high-side (HS) leg (PWM'd at duty D) and one low-side (LS) leg. With synchronous rectification, the freewheel current goes through the complementary FET, not the body diode.

- HS active FET: $P_{HS} = I^2 R_{ds}\,D$
- Complementary sync FET: $P_{sync} = I^2 R_{ds}\,(1-D)$
- LS FET (on for the full sector): $P_{LS} = I^2 R_{ds}$

The per-motor total is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2I^2R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

At 80 A and T_j = 100 °C:

$$P_{cond} = 2(6400)(0.94\times10^{-3}) + 6400(0.2\times10^{-3}) = 12.03 + 1.28 = \mathbf{13.3\ W/motor}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET hot spot

Each phase leg conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle (120° of 360°), and the LS role rotates. The worst single FET, averaged over a cycle at D → 1, sees:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot I^2R_{ds} = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(0.94\text{ m}) \approx 2.0\ \text{W}$$

During a 120° interval it carries the full 6.0 W. At low eRPM (e.g. a punch-out from hover), the electrical period becomes comparable to the FET's junction thermal time constant (~1–5 ms). The junction then follows the instantaneous 6 W, not the 2 W average. **Design to 6 W peak per FET for Z_thJC ripple.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches hard in each sector, so per motor there is **one hard-switched FET pair** at a time.

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard turn-on and turn-off)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}V_{bus}I(t_r+t_f)f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(22\times10^{-9})(48\,000) = \mathbf{1.06\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = (45\text{n})(25.2)(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.054\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery (body diode of the sync FET during dead time)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = (40\text{n})(25.2)(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (2 edges per cycle)

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(300\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.92\ W}$$

This is the **largest non-obvious loss**. Cutting dead time to 80 ns saves about 0.43 W/motor. Do this only after verifying shoot-through margin with the actual driver propagation-delay matching.

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FET)

$$P_{gate} = Q_gV_{gs}f_{sw}\times 2 = (60\text{n})(10)(48\text{k})(2) = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Per-motor and board totals at an 80 A burst

| Loss | W/motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET + Cu) | 13.30 | 53.2 |
| V-I switching | 1.06 | 4.2 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.92 | 3.7 |
| Q_oss + Q_rr | 0.10 | 0.4 |
| Gate + driver quiescent | 0.10 | 0.4 |
| Bulk cap ESR (see §2.7) | ~0.5 | 2.0 |
| **Total** | **≈16.0 W** | **≈64 W** |

Efficiency check: at D = 0.9, P_out ≈ 25.2 × 0.9 × 80 × 4 ≈ 7.26 kW, so the stage is about 99.1% efficient. The problem is not efficiency but **power density**: 64 W in under 4 cm².

### 2.7 DC-link capacitor ripple

The worst case is at D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80\times0.5 = 40\ \text{A}$$
