## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count, and a calibration offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Motor Model and Current PI with Decoupling

The surface-mount PMSM model is (L_d = L_q = L):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The PI controllers use cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d = PI_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = PI_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

**Tuning by pole-zero cancellation.** With crossover frequency ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

This makes the closed loop first order: i/i* = ω_c/(s + ω_c).

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/20. At f_s = 20 kHz, that gives f_c ≈ 1 kHz.

**Discretization** (forward Euler on the integrator, sample time T_s):

$$x_k = x_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k$$

**Torque production.** Torque is k_t·i_q with k_t = (3/2)·p·λ_m.

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon's inscribed circle, V_max = V_bus/√3:

$$(v_d, v_q) \leftarrow (v_d, v_q)\cdot\min\!\left(1,\ \tfrac{V_{max}}{\|v\|}\right)$$

Anti-windup uses **back-calculation** with gain K_aw ≈ 1/K_p:

$$x_k = x_{k-1} + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(v_{sat} - v_{unsat})\right]$$
