## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Engineering Flags

I have no Job #47 BOM, stack-up, or layout files, so every number below comes from stated assumptions. The equations are general. Substitute your datasheet values into the parameter table (§1) and every result updates.

**Architecture assumed.** This is an all-in-one board. The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class parts). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-MOSFETs. The H743 never touches the gates directly.

**Flags before you commit to the spec:**

| # | Issue | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **80 A per motor on a "nano" footprint** | The model below shows roughly **4 s adiabatic-limited burst** and roughly **29 A/motor true continuous** (all 4 motors) on a 30.5 mm-class board. On a 20×20 board both numbers get substantially worse. |
| F2 | **Single shared current shunt** | At 4 × 80 A = 320 A, a 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates 25.6 W. Use ≤0.1 mΩ, a Kelvin-sensed copper/Hall sensor, or a per-ESC shunt. |
