## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7 class (single-precision FPU), 170+ MHz |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Driver | 3-phase half-bridge with 2–3 low-side shunts, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) on each axis |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separate SPI buses, shared FSYNC |
| Bus voltage | 12–16 V |

**Loop rates (each loop ≥ 5× the bandwidth of the loop outside it):**

```
 20 kHz  Current (FOC) loop      BW_i ≈ 1.5–2 kHz      ADC-triggered ISR
  8 kHz  IMU sampling (both)     ODR = 8 kHz, AAF ≈ 258 Hz
  4 kHz  Rate (velocity) loop    BW_ω ≈ 60–120 Hz      inertial gyro feedback
  1 kHz  Attitude (angle) loop   BW_θ ≈ 10–20 Hz       fused attitude
```

**Signal flow per axis:**

```
θ_ref ─►(+)─► P/PI_θ ─►(+)─► PI_ω ─►(+)─► i_q* ─► FOC PI ─► SVPWM ─► motor
        ▲-            ▲ω_ff  ▲-         ▲ i_cog_ff
        │                    │          │
   fused attitude    filtered gyro   anti-cogging(θ_m)
   (dual IMU)        (dual IMU)      (encoder)
```

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

Let θ_m be the mechanical encoder angle, θ_off the calibrated electrical offset, and p the number of pole pairs.

$$
\theta_e = \big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off})\big) \bmod 2\pi
$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant)

With three shunts, enforce i_a + i_b + i_c = 0 by subtracting the mean.

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant in the rotor frame (SPMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Here ω_e = p·ω_m is the electrical speed. Electromagnetic torque is:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32\, p\, \lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

Choosing the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c.

$$
C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c
\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

**Choosing ω_c.** Pick ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10, accounting for the 1.5-sample transport delay. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s.

**Discretization (forward Euler on the integrator, sample time T_s).**

$$
e_k = i^*_k - i_k,\qquad I_k = I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k,\qquad u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + u_{\text{dec},k}
$$
