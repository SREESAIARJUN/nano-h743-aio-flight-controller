## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step synchronous commutation, current always flows through **exactly two FETs in series**:
- During the ON interval (duty D): HS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B)
- During the freewheel interval (1 − D): LS(phase A) (synchronous rectification) → winding → LS(phase B)

So, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature correction

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha_R (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha_R \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 1.4 Copper loss (often ignored, comparable to the FETs)
Sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) Cu at 25 °C:

$$R_\square = \frac{\rho}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}}{70\times10^{-6}} = 0.246\ \text{m}\Omega/\square$$

Hot copper (100 °C) adds about 30 %, giving 0.32 mΩ/□.

A battery → HS → phase pad → LS → GND path of about 5 squares on a single layer gives 1.6 mΩ. With 4 paralleled layers (stitched with ≥ 40 vias per transition) it gives 0.4 mΩ.

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.4\,\text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{2.6\ W}$$

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution (worst-case device)
A given HS FET conducts for 120° of each electrical cycle (1/3 of the time) at D → 1:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(0.0015) = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-current-limited transition times

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_g} = \frac{10-3.5}{3+2.2} = 1.25\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{sink}+R_g} = \frac{3.5}{1.5+2.2} = 0.95\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{15\,\text{nC}}{1.25} = 12\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{15\,\text{nC}}{0.95} = 15.8\ \text{ns}$$
