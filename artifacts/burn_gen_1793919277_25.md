## 0. Architecture Clarifications

These points bound the model, so they come first.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** No production firmware runs four 6-step BLDC channels at 48 kHz on the H743 while also running ArduPilot. The architecture is:
   - **H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bidirectional DShot600 over PCB traces to
   - **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). These generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.
