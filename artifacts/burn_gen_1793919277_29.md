# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Design Package Rev A

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Running four sensorless BLDC/FOC loops at 48 kHz PWM alongside ArduPilot is not viable on one H743. This design therefore uses the standard architecture:

- **Flight controller:** STM32H743VIT6 running ArduPilot. It produces 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- **ESC:** 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 firmware. Each drives one FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-channel MOSFETs.

### 0.2 Assumptions

Change any of these and the numbers below scale accordingly.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_max = 25.2 V, V_nom = 22.2 V |
| Burst rating | 80 A phase current **per ESC**, 4 ESCs simultaneously (worst case) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, synchronous rectification (complementary), 6-step trapezoidal |
| Dead time | 100 ns |
| MOSFET (generic 40 V DFN3.3×3.3) | R_ds(on) = 0.9 mΩ @25 °C (V_GS = 10 V), Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gs2 = 3 nC, Q_gd = 6 nC, Q_rr = 20 nC, Q_oss = 25 nC, R_θJC = 1.5 K/W |
| Gate drive | 10 V rail; R_g,total(on) = 5 Ω, R_g,total(off) = 3.5 Ω; Miller plateau ≈ 3 V |
| Board | 36 × 36 mm (30.5 mm mount), 8-layer: 2 oz outer, 1 oz inner (2 inner planes at 2 oz) |

### 0.3 Board size

"Nano" here means a 30.5 mm hole pattern. Twenty-four DFN3.3 FETs (≈ 261 mm² of footprint) **cannot** fit on a 20×20 board with an 80 A rating. That rating is physically unrealistic at 20 mm.
