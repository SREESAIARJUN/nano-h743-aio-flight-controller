## 0. Architecture Clarifications

These points constrain everything that follows.

1. **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 commands to those MCUs. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation/FOC firmware for the H743. The 48 kHz switching analysis therefore applies to the ESC MCU and bridge domain.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is thermally marginal.** The model in §3 shows it is a time-limited rating, in the range of 1–3 s depending on starting temperature and airflow. It is not a continuous rating.
3. **All numbers use a declared parameter set (§1).** Replace them with your chosen MOSFET's datasheet values. The equations are general.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Path

In 6-step complementary PWM, current at every instant flows through exactly **two** MOSFET channels in series:

- the PWM leg (high side during D, low side during 1−D), and
- the fixed low-side return leg.

Because the PWM leg's FETs share the current between them over the period, total conduction loss is independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 RMS Current Including Ripple

The PWM current ripple is:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

At the worst case, D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.56\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

The RMS current is then:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple contributes less than 0.1 %, so it is negligible at 48 kHz. This is one argument for 48 kHz over 24 kHz, where ΔI doubles.

### 2.3 Temperature-Dependent Resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × 1.425 = **2.14 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6403.6 \times 2.14\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{27.4\ W}$$

This is about 13.7 W per conducting FET.

### 2.4 Switching Losses (One Hard-Switched Leg per Motor)

**V–I overlap loss:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V f = 60\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.073\ W}$$

This is an upper bound. The more exact form is $E_{oss} f$.

$$P_{dt} = V_F I\,(2t_{dt}) f = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$
