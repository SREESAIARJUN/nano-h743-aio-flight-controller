## 0. Baseline Assumptions and Architecture Correction

### Architecture
The STM32H743 is the **flight controller MCU**. It does **not** drive the power stage. A 4-in-1 ESC that switches at 48 kHz uses **per-motor ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32, with 48 kHz center-aligned complementary PWM. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600 (one wire per motor).

### Form factor
An 80 A burst per motor on a 20×20 mm board is not credible. The FET area and the copper thermal mass are both insufficient (see §3.4). The baseline is therefore a **30.5×30.5 mm stack**: a flight controller board plus an ESC board. "Nano" here means a low-profile FC.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds}=1.2\,\text{m}\Omega\,(1+0.006\cdot100)=1.92\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In any 60° commutation sector, exactly two legs are active. The current path always contains **two conducting channels**:

- **During PWM on-time (D):** the high-side FET of leg A and the low-side FET of leg B conduct.
- **During off-time (1−D):** the low-side FET of leg A (synchronous freewheel) and the low-side FET of leg B conduct.

Total instantaneous conduction loss per motor is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\,I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Per-FET average loss over an electrical revolution (6 positions, symmetric rotation):

$$\bar P_{cond,FET}=\frac{2I^2R_{ds}}{6}=\frac{I^2R_{ds}}{3}$$

**Worst-case per-FET loss** occurs at low D with a high-current stall-like event. The freewheeling low-side of leg A then carries current for (1−D)·T over its 120° window, while the leg-B low-side carries 100 %. Within its active window, the leg-B low-side FET dissipates:

$$P_{LS,B}=I^2R_{ds}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}\ \text{(window-averaged)},\quad P_{inst}=I^2R_{ds}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per motor | Avg per FET | Instantaneous per FET |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 2.56 W | 7.7 W |
| 100 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 22.3 W | 3.71 W | 11.1 W |
| 125 °C | 1.92 mΩ | 24.6 W | 4.10 W | 12.3 W |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and connection losses (per motor)

Assumed resistances:
- Phase trace: ~0.25 mΩ (2 oz, 4 parallel layers)
- Solder pad / wire joint: ~0.15 mΩ
- Share of the shared bus planes: 0.1 mΩ

$$P_{cu}=I^2(2R_{trace}+R_{bus})\approx 6400\cdot0.6\,\text{m}\Omega\approx 3.8\ \text{W}$$

**This loss is not negligible.** It lands directly in the PCB thermal mass.
