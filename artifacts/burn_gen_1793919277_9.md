# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Thermal, Switching, and DShot Design Package (Job #24)

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not drive the MOSFETs. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071), which generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

**Assumptions, all to be replaced with your BOM data:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max |
| MOSFET | 40 V, R_ds(on) = 1.0 mΩ at 25 °C, 5×6 DFN |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | ×1.5 at T_j = 100 °C |
| Q_g | 60 nC |
| Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Q_oss | 60 nC |
| t_r + t_f | 30 ns (with a ~1.5 A gate driver such as the FD6288Q) |
| Dead time | 100 ns |
| Body-diode forward voltage V_f | 0.8 V |
| Commutation / PWM | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** A "nano" (20×20 mm) board cannot fit 12× 5×6 MOSFETs plus bulk capacitance. It also cannot reject the heat calculated below. The numbers support a **30.5×30.5 mm minimum**. Treat "80 A" strictly as a **per-channel burst rating of about 2 s or less**.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two FETs are always in the current path:
- **PWM on:** HS_A + LS_B
- **PWM off:** LS_A + LS_B

So the conduction loss is independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

The on-resistance follows the temperature as:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0067/\text{K}$$

**At 80 A, T_j = 100 °C, with ripple neglected:**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W/channel}$$

**Copper loss** adds to this, with sheet resistance R_□ = ρ/t:
- 2 oz copper: 1.72e-8 / 70e-6 = 0.246 mΩ/□
- Four paralleled 2 oz layers: ≈ 0.06 mΩ/□
- At about 3 effective squares per path: P_Cu ≈ 80² × 0.18 mΩ ≈ **1.2 W/channel**

## 2. Switching Loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWMing leg hard-switches.

| Term | Formula | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48k = **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f · I · 2 · t_dt · f | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f | **0.06 W** |
| Coss | ½ · Q_oss · V · f | **0.04 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in the driver) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f | **0.06 W** |
| **P_sw total** | | **≈ 2.2 W/channel** |

**Per-channel total at 80 A burst:** 19.2 + 2.2 + 1.2 ≈ **22.6 W**.

**All four channels:** ≈ **90 W**.
