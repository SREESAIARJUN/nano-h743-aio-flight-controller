## 0. Corrections to the Brief and Baseline Assumptions

Three points in the brief would cause design errors if taken literally.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware and generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 cannot run ArduPilot plus four sensorless 6-step FOC/trapezoidal loops with deterministic timing.
2. **"80 A" is only valid as a short burst on a nano form factor.** The analysis in §3 shows about 37 A continuous per channel and an 80 A burst of about 2–3 s. Any marketing rating must state the burst duration.
3. **Board stack recommendation.** I recommend a 30.5 × 30.5 mm two-board stack (FC over ESC) rather than a single-PCB AIO. A single board puts the IMU on a 100 W+ heat source and injects switching noise into the gyro.
