# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #68): Power-Stage Thermal, Switching, and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Warning

I don't have access to Job #68's internal design files. Everything below is built from stated, editable assumptions. Replace the parameters marked ⚙ with your BOM datasheet values and the equations will carry through.

**Architecture clarification.** On a 4-in-1 ESC, the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. Each of the four channels has its own ESC MCU (typically AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. The model is split accordingly:

| Domain | Device | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (400 MHz) | DShot600 TX, eRPM telemetry RX, DMA/timers |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 + 3-phase gate driver | 48 kHz 6-step PWM, commutation, DShot decode |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× N-MOSFET per channel (24 total) | Conduction and switching losses |

**Baseline parameters ⚙**

| Symbol | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current, per channel |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | ESC PWM |
| R_DS(on),25 | 0.90 mΩ | 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 class, V_GS = 10 V |
| k_T | 1.5 | R_DS(on) multiplier at T_j ≈ 125 °C |
| R_Cu | 0.60 mΩ | PCB copper + pad loop resistance, per conduction path |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd | 12 nC | Switching charge (above threshold) |
| Q_g,tot | 40 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | Body-diode reverse recovery at the operating di/dt |
| Q_oss | 30 nC | Output charge at V_bus |
| V_plateau | 3.5 V | Miller plateau |
| V_drv | 10 V | Gate driver supply |
| R_g,on / R_g,off | 10 Ω / 5 Ω | External + internal gate resistance |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| V_f | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward voltage at 80 A |

> ⚠ **Feasibility flag.** 80 A on a 20×20 mm nano board is only achievable as a burst of roughly 1–2 s (derived in §3). Continuous capability converges to about 25–30 A per channel even with prop-wash cooling. Any spec sheet claiming more than that continuous on 20×20 should be challenged at design review.
