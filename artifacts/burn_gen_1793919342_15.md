# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #54): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

**Topology.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller only. Each of the four ESC channels has its own MCU running AM32: an AT32F421K8U7 driving an FD6288Q gate driver and six N-FETs. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.

One H743 cannot run four sensorless 6-step commutation loops at 48 kHz while also flying, and ArduPilot does not support that. "48 kHz switching" therefore refers to the ESC MCU's PWM.

**Form factor.** At 80A burst, 24 FETs need roughly 5×6 mm or 3.3×3.3 mm dual-cooled packages. A 20×20 board cannot carry this. Plan on a 30.5×30.5 mm stack: an FC board plus a 6-layer, 2 oz ESC board. If "nano" means 20×20, the burst rating must drop to about 35–45A.
