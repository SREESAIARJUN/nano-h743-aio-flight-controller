# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control & Dual-IMU Fusion Design

I don't have the specifics of Job #44, so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your actual motor, encoder and MCU values.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, e.g. GBM2804-class: 12 slots, 14 poles (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (MT6701 / AS5048A) |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class with FPU, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, 8 kHz ODR, mounted symmetrically about the camera-plate rotation center |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz · Velocity 4 kHz · Angle/attitude 1 kHz · IMU pre-filter 8 kHz |

The structure is:

```
                1 kHz                      4 kHz                     20 kHz
 q_ref ─► [Attitude / Angle P] ─ω_ref─► [Velocity PI] ─iq_ref─► [Current PI dq] ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
            ▲          (+ ω_ff)          ▲      ▲  (+ iq_cog_ff + iq_fric)  ▲
            │                           │      │                          │
     Dual-IMU fusion ◄── notch/LPF ◄── 2×ICM-42688-P      Encoder PLL ─────┘ (θe for Park)
```

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset), where θ_offset is found at calibration.

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The dq voltage equations for a surface PMSM (L_d = L_q = L) are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The control law adds decoupling and back-EMF feedforward to a PI per axis:

$$
v_d = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** The PI zero cancels the electrical pole R/L, so the closed loop becomes first order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{PWM}}{10}
$$

At 20 kHz PWM, choose ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s.

### 1.3 Voltage limiting and SVPWM

- Limit the voltage vector circularly: |v| ≤ V_dc/√3, which is the linear SVPWM region.
- Give d-axis priority: v_q,max = √(V_max² − v_d²).
- SVPWM uses min-max zero-sequence injection:

$$
v_{x}' = v_x - \tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad
\text{duty}_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{dc}}
$$
