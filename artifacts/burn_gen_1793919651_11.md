# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Design Package (Job #15)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

ArduPilot runs only on the flight controller MCU. It does not generate MOSFET gate signals. The architecture below is the only practical one.

| Block | Device / Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (QFN32), AM32 | 48 kHz six-step commutation and synchronous rectification |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge, bootstrap) | High-side/low-side gate drive |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm package | Three half-bridges per motor |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max | — |
| Rating target | 80 A per motor, burst | — |

**Representative MOSFET parameters.** Substitute values from your actual datasheet; every equation below is parametric.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance, 25 °C, max | R_ds25 | 1.0 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 30 nC |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd (switching charge) | Q_sw | 7 nC |
| Miller plateau | V_pl | 3.0 V |
| Internal gate resistance | R_g,int | 1.0 Ω |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 20 nC |
| Output charge at 25 V | Q_oss | 25 nC |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |

**Feasibility warning.** Four channels at 80 A on a nano footprint (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is a burst-only rating, limited to roughly 2 seconds. Section 3 derives this. Any marketing figure of "80 A continuous" on this footprint is not physically supportable without a heatsink and forced airflow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-Step Current Paths

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg.** The high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D), with body-diode conduction during dead time.
- **Return leg.** The low-side FET is statically on.

The phase current I_ph is approximately flat within the sector, and the battery current is I_bat = D·I_ph. At full throttle (D → ~0.95–1), I_ph ≈ I_bat = 80 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

The PWM leg contributes D·I²R_HS + (1−D)·I²R_LS = I²R, assuming R_HS = R_LS. The return leg contributes I²R. Therefore:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.56\ W\ per\ motor\ channel}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET conducts one-third of the electrical cycle, so 18.56 / 6 ≈ 3.1 W per FET.

The peak instantaneous power in a single FET is 6400 × 1.45 mΩ = 9.3 W. At electrical frequencies above about 500 Hz, the die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) averages this out.
