# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Loss, Thermal and DShot Timer Design Package

I have no project history for "Job #48", so this package starts from an explicit assumption set (§0). Every equation is parameterised, so you can substitute your actual BOM values.

## Key Findings

1. **80 A per channel is a burst rating only, about 3–5 s, on a nano-class board.** All four channels at 80 A dissipate about **105–110 W** on a PCB with roughly 6 J/K of thermal mass. A sustained rating needs about 35–40 A per channel, or a larger board or heatsink.
2. **About 93% of the loss is conduction (I²R), not switching.** At 48 kHz, switching loss is only about 1.4 W per channel. Spend effort on R_DS(on), copper and the shunt, not on gate speed.
3. **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** Each ESC channel's own MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class) does. The H743 only sources bidirectional DShot600 on four timer channels with DMA. This partitioning matters for both firmware and netlist ownership.
