# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan

## 0. Corrections and Assumptions

Some issues in the brief change the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production AIO/4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to four separate ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). "48 kHz" is the ESC firmware's PWM frequency, not an H743 timer. Running 4× sensorless FOC/6-step on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not supported by any existing firmware.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is thermally a transient rating, not a sustained one.** Section 2 shows that the PCB, not the MOSFETs, is the limit. 80 A on all four motors at once is survivable for about 1–2 s.
3. I have no prior documents for "Job #35." The parameters below are assumptions you should replace with your BOM values:

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm mount (≈36×36 mm outline), 6-layer, 2 oz outer/inner power |
| MOSFET | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 PQFN-class, R_ds(on) = 0.9 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gd = 5 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, Q_oss = 30 nC, R_θJC = 1.2 K/W |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM |
| Gate driver | ≈1 A source/sink, giving t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 12 ns |
| Dead time | t_dt = 200 ns |
| f_sw | 48 kHz |

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

In 6-step with synchronous PWM, two phase legs carry current at any instant. One is the PWM leg (high side and low side alternate). The other is the static leg (one FET fully on). So the current always flows through exactly **two FETs in series**, regardless of duty cycle.

### 1.1 Conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\ \alpha\approx 0.005/\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R ≈ 1.5 × 0.9 = 1.35 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.35\text{ m}\Omega) = \mathbf{17.3\ W}$$

### 1.2 PCB copper and solder loss

Assume an effective path resistance of R_cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ (battery pad → FETs → phase pad, 2 oz):

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\text{ m} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 1.3 Switching (V–I overlap) loss

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} = \frac{5\text{ nC}}{0.42\text{ A (effective at plateau)}} \approx 12\text{ ns}$$

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(24\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.16\ W}$$

### 1.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

### 1.5 Reverse recovery, C_oss, and gate drive

$$P_{rr} = V Q_{rr} f = 25.2 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$
$$P_{oss} = V Q_{oss} f = 0.036\text{ W}$$
$$P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{gs} f = 2 \times 30\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.029\text{ W}$$

### 1.6 Totals at 80 A

| Loss | W per ESC |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 17.3 |
| PCB copper | 3.2 |
| Switching | 1.16 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.11 |
| **Total per ESC** | **≈ 23.0 W** |
| **All four ESCs at 80 A** | **≈ 92 W** |

Conduction dominates at about 75%. Raising f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz costs only about 1.2 W per ESC, so 48 kHz is acceptable. Cutting dead time to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per ESC.

### 1.7 DC-link capacitor ripple

Per ESC:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)}, \text{ which peaks at } D = 0.5: \quad I_{C,rms} = 0.5 \times 80 = 40\text{ A}$$

On-board MLCCs cannot carry this. **An external low-ESR electrolytic of at least 1000 µF / 35 V on short leads is mandatory.** Add on-board MLCCs of about 4 × 10 µF X7R 50 V per ESC (derated to roughly 30% capacitance at 25 V) placed right at each half-bridge.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Junction rise above the PCB

Worst-case single FET (static-leg FET carrying 100% duty):

$$P_{FET} \approx I^2 R = 6400 \times 1.35\text{ m} = 8.6\text{ W}$$
$$\Delta T_{JC} = 8.6 \times 1.2 = 10\text{ K}$$

This is small. **The die is not the bottleneck; the board is.**

### 2.2 Board thermal capacitance (adiabatic transient)

| Element | Mass | c_p | C_th |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR4 (13 cm² × 1.6 mm) | ≈ 3.8 g | 1.1 J/g·K | 4.2 J/K |
| Cu (6 layers, mixed 1–2 oz, about 60% fill) | ≈ 3.0 g | 0.385 J/g·K | 1.2 J/K |
| Components and solder | — | — | ≈ 1.0 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 6.4 J/K** |

Heating rate:

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = \frac{P}{C_{th}}$$

| Scenario | P | dT/dt | Time from 40 °C to 110 °C PCB |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 × 80 A | 92 W | 14.4 K/s | **≈ 4.9 s** |
| 1 × 80 A + 3 × 25 A | ≈ 32 W | 5 K/s | ≈ 14 s |

### 2.3 Steady state (convective)

Assume prop-wash forced convection, h ≈ 80 W/m²·K, area A = 2 × 13 cm² = 2.6×10⁻³ m²:

$$R_{\theta,BA} = \frac{1}{hA} = 4.8\text{ K/W}$$
$$\tau = R C = 4.8 \times 6.4 \approx 31\text{ s}$$

The allowable board rise is 110 − 40 = 70 K, so P_max ≈ 14.6 W total.
