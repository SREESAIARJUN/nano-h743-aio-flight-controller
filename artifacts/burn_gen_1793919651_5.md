## 0. Architecture Decision (read first)

Two findings change the design before the math begins.

1. **The H743 should not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.**
   - ArduPilot does not run a motor-commutation/FOC loop on the FC MCU.
   - The correct partition is:
     - **H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bidirectional DShot600 to four independent ESC MCUs.
     - Each **ESC MCU** (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32) generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and returns eRPM via GCR telemetry.
2. **"Nano" form factor and 4×80 A burst conflict.**
   - Section 3 shows that a 20×20 mm board absorbs the 4-motor 80 A burst for only about 1–2 s before reaching T_j limits.
   - Recommendations:
     - **30.5×30.5 mm** board minimum for a single-board design, or
     - a **two-board stack** (20×20 FC + 30×30 ESC).
   - Specify 80 A as a **≤1 s burst** rating, not a continuous rating.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per motor, 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step commutation two phase legs conduct at once:

- **Leg A (PWM'd):** current flows through the high-side during D·T and the complementary low-side during (1−D)·T. Exactly one FET of this leg conducts at any instant, apart from dead time.
- **Leg B (static):** the low-side is fully on.

The current path therefore always crosses **two R_DS(on)**, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Temperature dependence, evaluated at T_j = 100 °C (373 K):

$$R_{DS(on)}(373) = 0.85\,\text{mΩ}\cdot(373/298)^{2.3} = 0.85 \times 1.677 \approx 1.43\,\text{mΩ}$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.43\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.3\ W}$$

Copper loss in the phase and bus path, with R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ round trip [A] (2 oz, 8 layers, 6 mm pour):

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

> **Thermal runaway check:** P_cond depends on T_j through R_DS(on), so the thermal solution must be iterated (§3.4).
