## 0. Architecture Correction

The STM32H743 cannot practically run the four ESC motor-control loops itself. Commutation, the 48 kHz PWM and BEMF zero-cross detection belong to dedicated ESC MCUs. The architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight Controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, bidirectional eRPM capture |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 | 6-step sensorless commutation, 48 kHz PWM, DShot slave + GCR telemetry |
| Gate drive | FD6288Q (3-phase, 1 per ESC) | none | Bootstrap high-side/low-side drive |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | none | 6 FETs per motor |

The math below follows this split. Section 1 covers the power stage, Section 3 covers the H743.

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During each 60° sector, three FETs carry current:

- **PWM-leg high-side**, which conducts for duty D
- **PWM-leg low-side**, which acts as a synchronous rectifier for (1 − D)
- **Static-leg low-side**, which conducts 100% of the time

Current through phase windings with ripple ΔI has an RMS value of

$$I_{rms} = I\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I}\right)^2}$$

Motor inductance is roughly 10–30 µH and f_sw is 48 kHz, so ΔI/I < 0.2 at full load. That makes I_rms ≈ 1.002·I, which is negligible.

The conduction loss per channel is

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle**. With R_DS(T_j = 110 °C) = 0.9 × 1.5 = 1.35 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.35\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.28\ W\ per\ channel}$$

This loss is concentrated in two FETs per sector:

- The static low-side FET carries 8.64 W.
- The PWM-leg pair shares the other 8.64 W.

Commutation rotates the role among the six FETs. Each FET is active 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the per-FET average is 17.28/3 ≈ 5.8 W. The instantaneous peak per FET is 8.64 W, but junction thermal time constants are far longer than the electrical period at flight RPM, so averaging is valid above ~100 Hz electrical.

### 1.3 Switching Loss (hard-switched PWM leg only)

**(a) V–I overlap:**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\times25.2\times80\times40\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.048\ W$$

**(c) Reverse recovery** (body diode conducts during dead time, then is hard-commutated):
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.036\ W$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction:**
$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dead})\,f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times400\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, 2 switching FETs):
$$P_g = 2\,Q_gV_{drv}f_{sw} = 2\times50\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.048\ W$$

Total switching-related loss per channel:

$$P_{sw,total} = 1.94+0.048+0.036+1.23+0.048 \approx \mathbf{3.30\ W}$$

**Design note:** Dead-time loss is about 2/3 of the overlap loss. Lowering AM32 dead time to ~100 ns (if the FD6288 and FET t_d(off) allow it) saves ~0.6 W per channel. Going from 24 kHz to 48 kHz doubles P_sw, which costs about 1.65 W per channel in exchange for quieter, smoother low-throttle behavior.

### 1.4 Board Copper and Shunt Loss

Assume the following path:

- Phase-pad to FET drain path: 2 oz outer layers plus 2 oz inner layers in parallel, ~0.25 mΩ effective per channel path.
- Battery bus to 4 half-bridges: ~0.15 mΩ.
- Current shunt: 0.1 mΩ (2512 or 3920 size) on the battery return.

Per channel:
$$P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2\times0.25\text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ W$$

The battery current depends on duty cycle. At the 80 A phase burst, typical D ≈ 0.85 gives I_batt ≈ 4 × 80 × 0.85 = 272 A peak demand. That is unrealistic as a sustained 6S pack draw but is the correct worst case for bus copper:
$$P_{bus} = 272^2\times(0.15+0.1)\text{m}\Omega = 18.5\ W$$

### 1.5 Loss Summary (all 4 channels at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C)

| Loss | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 17.28 W | 69.1 W |
| Switching + dead time + Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 3.30 W | 13.2 W |
| Phase copper | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Bus copper + shunt | none | 18.5 W |
| **Total** | **≈22.2 W** | **≈107 W** |

At P_out ≈ 25.2 × 272 ≈ 6.85 kW, the efficiency is about 98.5%. That looks excellent, but **107 W into a ~30×30 mm board is the real constraint.**
