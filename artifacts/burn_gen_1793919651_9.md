### Architecture

ArduPilot cannot commutate BLDC motors from the H743. A "4-in-1 ESC + FC" therefore means this split:

```
                    ┌──────────────────────── AIO board ─────────────────────────┐
 6S Batt ──┬──► VBAT bus ──► 4× [ESC MCU → 3-ph gate driver → 6 MOSFETs] ──► M1..M4
           │                       ▲  DShot600 bidirectional (1 wire per motor)
           └──► Buck 5V/3V3 ──► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──┘
```

- **Per-channel ESC MCU:** AT32F421 running AM32, or an equivalent BLHeli_32-class part. It generates the **48 kHz** PWM.
- **H743:** generates DShot600 and captures the bidirectional eRPM telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

With six-step trapezoidal drive and synchronous PWM, exactly **two switch positions carry phase current in series** at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** current alternates between the high-side (fraction D) and the synchronous low-side (fraction 1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side is held ON continuously.

So, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, \frac{R_{DS}(T_j)}{n_p}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

$$R_{DS}(125^\circ\text{C}) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.55 = 1.55\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Ripple contribution

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_b^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{\phi\phi}\, f_{sw}}$$

Worst case is D = 0.5. For a typical 5" motor, L_φφ ≈ 20 µH:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.6\,\text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 3.6\,\text{A}^2$$

This is negligible against 6400 A².

### 1.4 Result at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6403.6 \times 1.55\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.9\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution

Over an electrical revolution, each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the period. Average per FET is therefore about 19.9 W / 6 ≈ 3.3 W. The instantaneous peak in the always-on low-side is I²R = 9.9 W.

This peak only matters when the electrical period is long compared with the die thermal time constant (τ_die ≈ 1–5 ms), i.e. at **stall/low eRPM**. Section 3.4 covers that case.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. That is one hard-switched half-bridge per channel.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard turn-on and turn-off of the active switch)

$$t_{r} \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g} = \frac{8\,\text{nC}}{1\,\text{A}} = 8\,\text{ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_b (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\times25.2\times80\times16\,\text{ns}\times48\,\text{kHz} = 0.77\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The body diode conducts during dead time, then the opposite FET hard-commutates it:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\,\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\,\text{kHz} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

At 80 A with high di/dt, Q_rr rises above the datasheet test condition. Budget 2–3× this value, about 0.1 W.

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5\times1\,\text{nF}\times635\times48\,\text{kHz} = 0.015\ \text{W}$$
