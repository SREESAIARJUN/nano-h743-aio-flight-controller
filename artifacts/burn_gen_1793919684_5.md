## 0. Architecture Correction and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning: the H743 cannot commutate the motors

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC at 48 kHz PWM with sensorless BEMF zero-cross detection needs a dedicated MCU per motor (or a 4-motor ESC MCU architecture) running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay.

- **H743:** generates bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 class running AM32): 3-phase complementary PWM at 48 kHz, BEMF comparator, current-sense ADC.

All math below follows this split.
