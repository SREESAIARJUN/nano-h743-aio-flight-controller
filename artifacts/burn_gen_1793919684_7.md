## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three constraints shape the rest of the document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071). That MCU handles commutation, BEMF sensing, and the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 commands to the four ESC MCUs. The derivations below follow that split.
2. **"80 A burst" means phase current per channel, for a limited duration.** The math in §3 shows a nano-format board (about 36×36 mm, 20×20 mounting) cannot sustain 80 A continuously on any channel. The result is a burst *duration limit*, not a thermal rating.
3. **Design assumptions:** 6S LiPo (25.2 V full, 30 V transient design limit), 2-layer-equivalent heat spreading on a 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner stackup, and synchronous (complementary) PWM with 6-step trapezoidal commutation.
