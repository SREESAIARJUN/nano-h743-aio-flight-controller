# STM32H743 AIO FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #66): Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot/DMA Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

Two points shape the rest of this package.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware, not ESC firmware. The architecture is therefore:

- **STM32H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32).
- **Each ESC MCU** drives a 3-phase gate driver, which drives six MOSFETs at f_sw = 48 kHz.

The 48 kHz figure is the ESC MCU's PWM frequency. It is unrelated to the H743's DShot timers.

**2. "Nano" plus 80 A is a contradiction to resolve now.** On 20×20 mm boards, 3×3 mm FETs cannot shed 80 A/motor for more than a few seconds (see §3.4). This package assumes:

- 30.5×30.5 mm mounting
- 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner copper
- 5×6 mm DFN FETs
- "80 A burst" means ≤10 s per motor

If the 20×20 form factor is mandatory, derate to about 35 A burst.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous rectification, the current path in every 60° sector is:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET for D·T_sw, then the complementary low-side FET for (1−D)·T_sw. Current is continuous in the leg.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET at 100%.

At every instant, exactly **two** channels carry I_ph. Ripple is neglected because I_ripple ≪ I_ph at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance of about 10–30 µH.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)
$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.3 Per-motor conduction loss
$$P_{cond,motor}=2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 mΩ × 1.55 = 1.55 mΩ.

$$P_{cond,motor}=2(80)^2(1.55\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.84\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution
Over one electrical revolution, each phase spends 120° as the PWM or return leg and 60° floating.

Average loss per FET for a high-side FET with duty D (using RMS current):
$$P_{HS}=\tfrac{1}{3}I_{ph}^2R_{DS}\cdot D\quad(\text{PWM sector})\;+\;\text{return-leg share}$$

Summed symmetrically, the total divides evenly:
$$\overline{P}_{cond,FET}=\frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6}=3.31\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous peak while a FET is conducting is much higher:
$$I^2R = 80^2 \times 1.55\,\text{m} = \mathbf{9.92\ W}$$

This instantaneous figure, applied over the 60–120° sector duration, matters for Z_θ at low eRPM. At low speed, sectors last milliseconds and junction temperature tracks the peak, not the average.

### 1.5 Phase vs. battery current
$$I_{bat}\approx D\cdot I_{ph}$$

An 80 A phase current at D = 0.6 means 48 A from the battery per motor. Size the bus caps and shunt for I_ph and the XT60 for I_bat.

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Hard-switching losses occur only in the PWM leg, on its high-side FET. The low-side FET switches with near-ZVS because its body diode conducts during dead time.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·22n·48k | **1.064 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40n·25.2·48k | 0.048 W |
| C_oss | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw = 0.5·45n·25.2·48k | 0.027 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2·V_SD·I·t_dt·f_sw = 2·0.8·80·50n·48k | 0.307 W |
| Gate drive (driver side) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2·55n·10·48k | 0.053 W |
| **Total per motor** | | **≈1.50 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** P_sw ∝ f_sw. At 24 kHz this term falls to 0.75 W. At 96 kHz it rises to 3.0 W.

At 80 A, conduction dominates by about 13:1. 48 kHz is therefore a reasonable choice for smoothness and acoustic performance.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total dissipation (all four motors at 80 A burst)
$$P_{motor}=19.84+1.50 = 21.3\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{board}=4\times21.3 + P_{misc}\ (\text{shunts } 4\times80^2\times0.25\text{m}=6.4\text{W, LDO/BEC}\approx1.5\text{W}) \approx \mathbf{93\ W}$$

### 3.2 Electrothermal fixed point (R_DS feedback)
Let the following be defined:

- P₀ = per-FET loss evaluated at R_DS,25
- R_θ = R_θJC + R_θCA,eff

Solving T_j = T_a + P(T_j)·R_θ with P = P₀[1+α(T_j−25)] gives:

$$T_j=\frac{T_a+P_0R_\theta(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha P_0R_\theta}$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion:**
$$\alpha P_0 R_\theta<1$$

**Worked example:**

- P₀ per FET (average) = (2·6400·1.0m)/6 + 1.5/2 ≈ 2.13 + 0.25 = 2.38 W
- Board-level R_θCA,eff per FET, for a 30×30 board in prop wash at about 8 m/s: ≈ 9 K/W
- R_θ = 1.2 + 9 = 10.2 K/W
- αP₀R_θ = 0.0055·2.38·10.2 = 0.134, so the design is stable.
