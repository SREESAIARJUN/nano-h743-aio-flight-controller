# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #62): Loss Model, Thermal Model, Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation, and Netlists

## 0. Scope, Corrections, and Assumptions

Three architectural points need settling before the math, because they change the results.

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** In a standard ArduPilot stack, the H743 flight controller sends DShot600 to the 4-in-1 ESC. The ESC has its own MCU per motor, or one MCU per two motors. Typical choices are AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU runs the 48 kHz PWM and the commutation. The H743's job is the DShot output and eRPM capture (Section 6). Everything in Sections 2–5 applies to the ESC board.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A" conflict.** At 20×20 mm, 80 A per channel is not physically viable (Section 5 shows why). I model a **30.5×30.5 mm stack** and still conclude that **80 A is a 2–3 s burst rating with all four motors loaded**, not a continuous rating.
3. **No MOSFET was specified.** I use a representative 40 V, 5×6 mm (DFN) device. Every number below is a parameter. Re-run the model with the real datasheet values.
