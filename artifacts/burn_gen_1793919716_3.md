# Job #59: STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC Stack. Electro-Thermal Model and DShot DMA Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** In production stacks the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. The H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. The 4-in-1 ESC carries its own MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8/ARM running BLHeli_32/Bluejay). 48 kHz is the ESC-side PWM frequency. DShot600 is the FC→ESC command link. The model below follows that split.

**Feasibility flag.** 4 × 80 A on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not thermally credible. The math is in §3.4. I model a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 80 A as a **per-motor, short-duration burst**. Sustained current is derated.

**Assumed parameters.** Replace these with datasheet values for your BOM.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, full charge |
| I_pk | 80 A | per-motor burst phase current |
| R_ds(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ | per FET (e.g., 40 V, 5×6 mm DFN class) |
| k_T | 1.5 | R_ds multiplier at T_j ≈ 100–110 °C |
| t_r + t_f | 20 ns | effective switching transition time |
| t_dead | 100 ns | per edge |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | body diode forward voltage |
| Q_g | 50 nC @ V_GS = 10 V | gate charge |
| Q_oss | ~25 nC @ 25 V | output charge |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | ESC PWM frequency |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant two phase legs conduct (one high-side path, one low-side path). The commutation sequence is 120° trapezoidal.

- During the PWM on-time D, current flows HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- During the off-time (1–D), the active-freewheel path is the synchronous LS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- Either way, the current always passes through **exactly 2 FETs in series**.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor:

$$P_{cond}(t) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\text{K}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: 1.0 mΩ × 1.51 ≈ **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each leg conducts 2/3 of the electrical cycle. Within that, HS carries D and LS carries (1–D), plus the 1/3 of the cycle when the leg is the "low" return.

The per-leg RMS current is:

$$I_{leg,rms} = I_{pk}\sqrt{2/3} = 65.3\ \text{A}$$

At D ≈ 0.9 (burst): P_HS ≈ 6.4 W and P_LS ≈ 7.4 W per leg pair, averaged over the cycle. That is roughly **3.2 W average per FET** across the 6 FETs, with peaks near 9.6 W while a FET is conducting.

### 1.5 Copper and shunt add-ons

- **Shunt** (0.25 mΩ, one per ESC on the battery line): P = I_bus² R. At ΣI_bus ≈ 4 × 80 × D ≈ 290 A, that is ≈ **21 W**. This is why burst-rated 4-in-1s use 0.1–0.2 mΩ shunts or Hall sensors.
- **Phase traces** (2 oz Cu, ~0.3 mΩ per path): ≈ 1.9 W per motor.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switching leg (one per motor at any instant) incurs hard-switching losses.

| Loss term | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ V_bus I (t_r+t_f) f_sw | ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48 k = **0.97 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD · I · 2t_dead · f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr V_bus f_sw (Q_rr ≈ 60 nC) | **0.07 W** |
| C_oss | Q_oss V_bus f_sw | 25 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.03 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 Q_g V_GS f_sw | 2·50 nC·10·48 k = **0.05 W** (dissipated in driver) |
| **Σ switching** | | **≈ 1.7 W** |

### 2.1 Frequency sensitivity

$$\frac{\partial P_{sw}}{\partial f_{sw}} \approx 35\ \mu\text{W/Hz}$$

- Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 0.85 W per motor.
- The cost is higher current ripple, ΔI = V/(4 L f) for 50 % duty.
- For a low-inductance motor (L ≈ 15 µH phase-phase) at 24 kHz, ripple ≈ 17 A p-p. At 48 kHz it is ≈ 9 A p-p.
- Ripple adds to RMS conduction loss: ΔP = 2R·ΔI²/12.
- 48 kHz is near-optimal for low-L racing motors.

### 2.2 Total burst dissipation

| | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 1.7 W | 6.8 W |
| Traces | 1.9 W | 7.6 W |
| **Total (excl. shunt)** | **22.8 W** | **≈ 91 W** |

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (Cauer, per FET → board → air)

```
P_FET ──[Rθjc 0.8 K/W]──┬──[Rθ_spread 1.5 K/W]──┬──[Rθ_ba]── T_amb
                       C_j (≈0.05 J/K)          C_board (≈7 J/K total)
```

### 3.2 Board thermal mass (30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer 2 oz, ~1.6 mm)

- FR4: 1.5 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ × 1.1 J/gK ≈ 3.0 J/K
- Copper: ~4 g × 0.385 J/gK ≈ 1.5 J/K
- FETs, caps, solder: ≈ 2.5 J/K
- **C_board ≈ 7 J/K**

### 3.3 Board-to-air resistance

- Still air (both faces, ~19 cm²): h ≈ 10 W/m²K, giving Rθ_ba ≈ 50 K/W.
- Prop-wash at 10–15 m/s: h ≈ 80–120 W/m²K, giving **Rθ_ba ≈ 5 K/W**.
- Thermal time constant: τ = R·C ≈ 35 s in flight.

### 3.4 Burst response (all 4 motors at 80 A)

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) = P\,R_{\theta ba}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right)$$

| Burst duration | ΔT_board (P = 91 W, R = 5 K/W, τ = 35 s) |
|---|---|
| 1 s | 12.8 K |
| 3 s | 37.4 K |
| 5 s | 60.6 K |
| 10 s | 113 K → **failure** |

Junction rise above board: per-FET peak ≈ 9.6 W × (0.8 + 1.5) K/W ≈ **22 K**.

**Limit.** With T_amb = 35 °C and T_j,max = 150 °C minus 25 K margin, the allowed board rise is about 68 K. The usable burst is therefore **≈ 5–6 s with all four motors saturated**. The steady-state continuous capability is P_max = 68/5 ≈ 13.6 W total, i.e., about **27–30 A continuous per motor**. This matches real "60–80A burst" product ratings.

**Nano (20×20) check.** C_board ≈ 3 J/K and Rθ_ba ≈ 11 K/W. Steady-state rise at 91 W would be about 1000 K, so the burst limit is about 2 s. Do not ship 80 A on a 20×20.

### 3.5 ESC firmware protection

- NTC on the ESC near FETs, with temperature limit ≈ 110 °C.
- Current limit via shunt + AM32 `CURRENT_LIMIT`.
- Both should report via EDT/BLHeli telemetry to ArduPilot (`ESC_TLM`).

## 4. ArduPilot Bidirectional DShot600 Timing and DMA Allocation

### 4.1 Link timing

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| DShot600 bit time | 1.667 µs (T0H ≈ 625 ns, T1H ≈ 1250 ns) |
| Frame (16 bit) | 26.7 µs |
| ESC turnaround | ~30 µs |
| Telemetry reply | 21-bit GCR at 5/4 × 600 kbit = 750 kbit/s → 28 µs |
| Total transaction | ≈ 90 µs → max ≈ 10 kHz loop. ArduPilot runs at the loop rate (typically 400 Hz–2 kHz, `SCHED_LOOP_RATE`) |

The DShot line is inverted for bidirectional mode (idle high). The same pin flips from timer output-compare to input-capture.
