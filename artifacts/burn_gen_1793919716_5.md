# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #50): Engineering Model

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not run the motor commutation. It runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 frames to four ESC MCUs on the same board. Each ESC MCU (assumed AT32F421 or similar running AM32 or BLHeli_32) generates the 48 kHz PWM and returns eRPM telemetry. The two domains are modelled separately:

```
         ┌──────────────── FC domain (3V3) ────────────────┐   ┌──── ESC domain (VBAT) ────┐
LiPo ──► │ STM32H743 ── TIM1 CH1..4 ──(DShot600 bidir)──────┼──►│ 4× ESC MCU → gate drv →   │
         │   ArduPilot, SPI IMU, baro, UARTs, ADC           │   │ 24× MOSFET (4×3 half-bridges)
         └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘   └───────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path
With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous rectification, two switches carry the phase current at every instant:
- During the ON interval: one high-side FET and one low-side FET.
- During the OFF interval: two low-side FETs, because the high-side FET hands its current to its complementary low-side FET.

The current therefore always sees 2·R_DS(on), regardless of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\left(I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}\right)$$

### 1.2 Ripple term
$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}}\Big|_{D=0.5} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{20\,\mu H \cdot 48\,kHz} = 6.56\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

$$\frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 3.6\ \text{A}^2 \;\ll\; I^2 = 6400\ \text{A}^2 \quad(\text{0.06 \%, negligible})$$

### 1.3 Temperature coupling
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 80 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.7 W | 66.8 W |
| 110 °C | 1.47 mΩ | **18.8 W** | **75.2 W** |

**Per FET.** Each FET conducts for about one third of the electrical cycle, so:
- Average loss ≈ 18.8 W / 6 ≈ 3.1 W.
- Instantaneous loss while conducting = I²R ≈ 9.4 W.

At high eRPM (above ~1 kHz electrical) the die thermal mass averages this out. At low RPM under high load (stall or punch-out from hover), plan for the 9.4 W peak.
