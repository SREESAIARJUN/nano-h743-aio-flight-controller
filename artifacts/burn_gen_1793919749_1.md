## 1. FOC Current Loop (Inner, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose. The angle used for the inverse transform is advanced by 1.5 sample periods to compensate the ZOH and computation delay:

$$\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

### 1.2 PMSM Model and PI Design

The motor model in the dq frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

The PI uses cross-coupling and back-EMF decoupling feedforward, and its zero cancels the electrical pole $R/L$. This gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_s}{10\text{–}20}$$

With $f_s$ = 20 kHz, choose $\omega_c = 2\pi \cdot 1.5\,\text{kHz}$.

The discrete control law (forward Euler on the integrator), per axis $x\in\{d,q\}$:

$$\begin{aligned}
e_x[k] &= i_x^*[k]-i_x[k] \\
v_x^{\text{ff}} &: \quad v_d^{\text{ff}}=-\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{\text{ff}}=\omega_e L i_d+\omega_e\lambda_m \\
v_x[k] &= K_p e_x + I_x[k] + v_x^{\text{ff}}
\end{aligned}$$

**Voltage limiting (circle limit, d-axis priority):**

$$V_{\max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{\max}, \qquad |v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}$$

**Anti-windup (back-calculation),** with $\bar v_x$ the saturated output and $K_{aw}=1/K_p$:

$$I_x[k+1] = I_x[k] + K_i T_s e_x + K_{aw}\,(\bar v_x - v_x)$$

Gimbal motors run with $i_d^*=0$.

### 1.3 SVPWM (Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection)

$$v_{abc} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\qquad v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max v_{abc} + \min v_{abc}\right)$$

$$D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}},\qquad D_x \in [D_{\min}, D_{\max}]$$

Clamp the duty to leave room for the shunt sampling window.
