# FOC Control Loop & Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the original specification for Job #50, so the design below rests on stated assumptions (Section 0). Every equation is written in terms of symbolic parameters, so you can substitute your actual motor, encoder and MCU values. Items marked **[VERIFY]** need to be checked against your hardware or datasheet before flight.

---

## 0. Assumed System Baseline

| Item | Assumption | Symbol |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (e.g., GBM2804-class) | p = 7 pole pairs, N_s = 12 slots |
| Phase resistance / inductance | ~5–10 Ω, ~2–4 mH (line-to-neutral) | R, L |
| Flux linkage | from Kv or a back-EMF measurement | λ |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic on each motor shaft (AS5048A / MT6701) | θ_m |
| MCU | STM32G4 @ 170 MHz with FPU, center-aligned PWM | — |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separate SPI buses | — |
| Loop rates | current 20 kHz, rate 4 kHz, attitude 1 kHz, IMU ODR 8 kHz | T_i, T_v, T_θ |
| Gimbal order | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner, camera) | ψ, φ, ϑ |

**Bandwidth separation rule.** Each outer loop should be about 5–10× slower than the loop inside it:

ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz  ≫  ω_c,v ≈ 2π·100–150 Hz  ≫  ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·10–20 H
