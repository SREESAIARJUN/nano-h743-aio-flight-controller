## 0. Assumed Architecture and Timing

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (e.g. STM32G474), single-precision float |
| Motors | Gimbal PMSM, 7 pole pairs (14p), 12 slots, phase R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, hollow shaft |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) per axis |
| Current sense | 2- or 3-shunt, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz, gyro ODR 8 kHz |
