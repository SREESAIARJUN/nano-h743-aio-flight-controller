## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

AM32 and BLHeli_32 use 6-step commutation with synchronous (complementary) rectification. Over one electrical cycle, each leg spends:

- 120° as the **PWM leg**: the high side conducts for fraction D, and the low side synchronously rectifies for fraction (1 − D).
- 120° as the **sink leg**: the low side is fully on.
- 120° **floating**.

Energy per leg, averaged over the cycle:

$$P_{leg} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[D + (1-D) + 1\right] I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Summing the three legs gives the total conduction loss per motor. It is independent of duty cycle:

$$\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

The terms are defined as:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.2 Phase-Current Ripple

The peak-to-peak ripple is:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\, D}{2 L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Assume L_ph = 15 µH and near full throttle, where V_bemf ≈ 0.9·V_bus and D ≈ 0.95:

- ΔI ≈ 1.7 A.
- The ripple term is negligible: 80² + 0.24 ≈ 80².
