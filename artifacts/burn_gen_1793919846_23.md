### 0.2 Feasibility findings (resolve these at the design review)

**1. "Nano" form factor vs. 80 A burst.**
- A 20×20 mm AIO board physically cannot hold 24× PDFN5×6 FETs plus four driver/MCU sets.
- Production 20×20 AIO boards use PDFN3.3×3 FETs at 2.5–4 mΩ and are rated 12–25 A continuous / 35 A burst.
- The 80 A burst numbers below assume a **30.5×30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz minimum** board.
- On a true nano board, expect about 3× the conduction loss calculated here.

**2. Single-MCU commutation is not recommended.**
- The H743 could in theory commutate four motors with TIM1/TIM8 complementary outputs.
- In practice it lacks enough advanced timers for four 3-phase bridges and would starve the ArduPilot scheduler.
- The plan therefore uses per-motor ESC MCUs, linked by bidirectional DShot600.

**3. Simultaneous 4×80 A burst is thermally a ~2-second event** (see §3). Specify the burst rating as "single-channel, ≤ 5 s" or "all channels, ≤ 1.5 s".

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in six-step with complementary PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct. Take phase A as the PWM leg and phase B as the low-side return:

- High-side A (Q_AH) conducts for a fraction D of each period.
- Low-side A (Q_AL, synchronous rectification) conducts for the remaining (1 − D).
- Low-side B (Q_BL) conducts continuously.
