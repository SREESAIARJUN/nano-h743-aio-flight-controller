# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #26): Power-Stage, Thermal and Bi-Directional DShot Design Model

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

I have no Job #26 specification package (BOM, stack-up, MOSFET part number, board outline), so this model is **parametric**. Every derived number comes from the assumed values below. To finalize the analysis, replace them with the selected parts.

### 0.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600.

The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the **ESC MCUs**, not the H743. These are typically AM32 or BLHeli_32 targets such as the STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32E230, one per channel or one per pair. The model below treats them as two separate subsystems joined by the 8-pin FC–ESC harness.
