## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any 60° commutation sector, phase X is PWM'd high and phase Y is held low.

| PWM state | Duration | Conducting FETs |
|---|---|---|
| ON | D·T_sw | HS_X + LS_Y |
| OFF | (1−D)·T_sw | LS_X (synchronous freewheel) + LS_Y |

Two R_DS(on) are always in series, so bridge conduction loss is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

Silicon trench MOSFETs follow a near power-law:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{2.3}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives a ratio of (398/298)^2.3 = **1.95**, slightly more conservative than the usual ×1.5–1.7 datasheet curves. For design, use the datasheet normalized curve **×1.6**:

$$R_{DS,hot} = 1.2 \times 1.6 = 1.92\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss at 80 A

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W}\ \text{per ESC channel}$$

**Per-FET distribution** (each FET conducts 120° of every 360°):

| FET role | Duty share | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Static low side (LS_Y) | 1/3 of the time | 80²·1.92 mΩ·(1/3) = **4.10 W** |
| PWM high side (at D = 0.9) | 0.9/3 | **3.69 W** |
| Freewheel low side (at D = 0.9) | 0.1/3 | **0.41 W** |

High-side and low-side losses both average about 4.1 W per FET.

**Worst FET, instantaneous:** 80²·1.92 mΩ = **12.3 W** during its active sector. This value drives the junction-ripple analysis in §3.3.

### 1.4 PCB copper conduction loss

Phase and DC-bus traces are 2 oz copper (ρ = 1.72×10⁻⁸ Ω·m at 20 °C, α = 0.0039/K), roughly 15 mm long and 4 mm wide, on 4 paralleled layers with an effective thickness of 140 µm:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot0.015}{0.004\cdot140\times10^{-6}} = 0.46\ \text{m}\Omega\ (\to 0.58\ \text{m}\Omega\ @\ 85^\circ C)$$

$$P_{Cu} \approx 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 0.58\ \text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{7.4\ W}\ \text{per channel}$$

Copper loss is a large term at this scale. It is why phase-pad layer stitching (§5.3) is mandatory.

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge (phase X) hard-switches. Phase Y is static.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched turn-on and turn-off)

Miller-plateau times, with V_pl = 3.5 V and R_g,total = 5 Ω:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd} R_g}{V_{drv} - V_{pl}} = \frac{8\,\text{nC}\cdot 5}{10-3.5} = 6.2\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd} R_g}{V_{pl}} = 11.4\ \text{ns}$$

> **Driver-limited check:** an 0.8 A source current gives 8 nC / 0.8 A = 10 ns, so use **t_r ≈ 10 ns** and **t_f ≈ 11 ns**.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot21\times10^{-9}\cdot48\,000 = \mathbf{1.02\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\,\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\,000 = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\times10^{-9}\cdot48\,000 = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.4 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\,000 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Q_rr increases with di/dt and current, so add a ×2 margin: **0.10 W**.

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET die)

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot45\,\text{nC}\cdot10\cdot48\,000 = 0.043\ W$$

### 2.6 Switching-loss summary per channel

| Term | Loss (W) |
|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 1.02 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr (with margin) | 0.10
