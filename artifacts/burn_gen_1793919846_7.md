# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Thermal Model and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan

## 0. Scope, Corrections, and Assumptions

Three points shape the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8) running AM32 or BLHeli. The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and back-EMF sensing live there. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs. The architecture below follows that split. Driving 24 gates from the H743 directly is not viable under ArduPilot.
2. **80 A burst on a nano (20×20) footprint is not thermally credible.** Even on 30.5×30.5 the board survives only seconds. The math below quantifies this.
3. **The netlist is a reference topology, not a fab-ready design.** It is illustrative and must be validated against real datasheets, DRC/ERC and bench testing before release.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

**Electrothermal R_DS(on):**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 75) ≈ **1.41 mΩ (typ)**. At max R₂₅ this becomes 1.98 mΩ.

**Topology (6-step trapezoidal with complementary PWM).** At any instant, current flows through exactly two FETs in series:
- the HS or LS FET of the PWM leg, and
- the LS FET of the return leg.

Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical period. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**At 80 A, T_j = 100 °C:**
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.41 mΩ = **18.0 W per channel** (25.3 W with max R_DS)
- P_cond,FET ≈ **3.0 W average**

**Ripple correction.** I_rms² = I_dc² + ΔI²/12. With motor inductance L = 20 µH at 48 kHz, worst-case D = 0.5:
- ΔI = V·D(1−D)/(L·f) ≈ 25.2 × 0.25 / (20e-6 × 48e3) ≈ 6.6 A p-p
- Correction is +0.06 %, so negligible.

> **Thermal-runaway check.** dP/dT = 2I²R₂₅α = 2 × 6400 × 1e-3 × 0.0055 = 0.070 W/K per channel.
> Stable if dP/dT × R_θ < 1.
> With R_θ,ch ≈ 4 × 5.6 = 22 K/W (see §3), the loop gain is ≈ 1.5 > 1.
> **The 80 A steady state is unconditionally unstable on this board. Burst operation is mandatory.**
