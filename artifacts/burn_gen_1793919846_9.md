## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Several points in the brief need correcting before any of the numbers below are useful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1/AIO design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32/S. The 48 kHz PWM and the switching losses belong to that MCU's bridge. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.
   - This document assumes 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is thermally marginal.** Section 3 shows that the board's thermal mass limits an all-four-motors 80 A burst to roughly one second, not the 10 s typically implied by a "burst" rating. Treat 80 A as a per-channel peak with limited duty, not a design point.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous PWM)

### 1.1 Which FETs conduct

In any commutation sector, current flows through two phases:

- **PWM phase (A):**
  - High-side Q_AH conducts for D·T
  - Low-side Q_AL (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D)·T
- **Return phase (B):**
  - Low-side Q_BL conducts for the full period

### 1.2 Loss per sector

$$P_{cond,sector} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of D, which is why synchronous rectification is used. Every sector has the same structure, so this is also the channel average.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /K$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.6 × 1.5 = 2.4 mΩ.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A (phase current ≈ bus current at D ≈ 1)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per channel @125 °C | 2 · 80² · 0.0024 = **30.7 W** |
| P_cond per channel @25 °C | 20.5 W |
| Per conducting FET (instantaneous) | I²R = 15.4 W |

Because of the temperature coefficient, the model must be iterated with T_j. There is positive feedback: hotter FETs have higher R_DS(on), which raises loss and temperature further (see §3.4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per channel, only the PWM-phase half-bridge switches.

### 2.1 Hard-switching (V–I overlap)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 1.94\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode conduction during dead time (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 1.23\ \text{W}$$

This term is significant. Reducing dead time to 100 ns halves it, but this must be validated against driver propagation skew.

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Output-capacitance loss

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.018\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET die.

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 40\text{n} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Totals per channel at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss term | Power |
|---|---|
| Conduction | 30.7 W |
| V–I switching | 1.94 W |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.07 W |
| **Channel total** | **≈ 33.9 W** |
| **4 channels at 80 A simultaneously** | **≈ 136 W** |

Add about 0.5 W for shunts/copper per channel if 0.25 mΩ shunts and trace resistance are included: I²R ≈ 1.6 W at 80 A for shunt plus traces.

### 2.7 Design levers

Conduction loss is about 90 % of the total. That means:

- R_DS(on) and copper area dominate the design.
- Moving from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only about 1.6 W per channel.
- Reducing f_sw degrades current ripple and acoustic behavior with low-inductance motors.
