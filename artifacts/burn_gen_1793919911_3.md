# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #64): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture note.** In a stacked FC plus 4-in-1 ESC system, the STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU commutates the bridge and sets PWM frequency (48 kHz is a firmware setting). The H743's job is to:

1. Emit DShot600 frames.
2. Capture bi-directional eRPM telemetry.
3. Read current and voltage.

The model below therefore splits into the power stage (ESC) and the command and telemetry path (FC).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation, one phase leg is PWM-switched, one leg is held low, and one leg floats.

- **Synchronous rectification:** during PWM off-time, current freewheels through the complementary FET of the switched leg rather than its body diode, except during dead time.
- **Conduction count:** at every instant, exactly two MOSFETs carry I_ph.
