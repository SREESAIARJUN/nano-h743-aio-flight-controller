# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #54): Loss Models, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #54 BOM or stack-up, so every number below comes from the stated assumptions. Swap in your real datasheet values; the equations stay the same.

**Architecture.** In any 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The ESC has its own MCUs (4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), and those MCUs generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. The loss model applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model applies to the H743.
