## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Commutation is not done by the H743.** In an ArduPilot AIO board, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 commands to four separate ESC microcontrollers. Those ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generate the 48 kHz PWM. The loss model applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model applies to the H743.

| Parameter | Symbol | Baseline value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst current per motor | I_b | 80 A (phase DC-equivalent, trapezoidal) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) |
| MOSFET class | — | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 |

> ⚠️ **Format risk.** 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm board is far beyond what the copper area can hold continuously. Typical 20×20 4-in-1 ESCs carry honest ratings of 35 to 55 A. The math below quantifies how long 80 A can actually last.
