| Item | Assumed value |

| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |

| Angle | 1 kHz | ω_θ ≈ 2π·20 rad/s | fused attitude (Mahony) |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform**, with a balanced 3-phase assumption (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The **inverse Park transform** is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The dq motor model is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The control law is a PI controller plus feedforward decoupling and back-EMF compensation:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation.** Choose K_i/K_p = R/L so the PI zero cancels the electrical pole. The closed loop then becomes a first-order system with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

Keep ω_c below about f_PWM/10, because of the one-sample computation delay plus the 0.5-sample PWM delay.
