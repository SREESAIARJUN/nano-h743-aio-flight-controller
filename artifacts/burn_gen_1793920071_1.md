## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R_φ ≈ 10 Ω, L_φ ≈ 2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (e.g. AS5048A/MT6835) on each joint |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (STM32G4/H7), center-aligned PWM at 40 kHz |
| Current sensing | 2- or 3-shunt inline. Voltage-mode fallback is given in §1.4 |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, each on its own SPI bus with DMA, gyro ODR 8 kHz |
