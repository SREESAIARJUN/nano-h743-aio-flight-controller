# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: System Model

This document gives the electrical, thermal and timer-allocation model for Job #23. Values marked **[A]** are assumptions to replace with your actual BOM datasheet values. The equations are general; the numbers are only as good as those inputs.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, which run AM32 or BLHeli_32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071 chips. Each ESC MCU drives its own gate driver and six MOSFETs.

```
VBAT(6S) ─┬─ Bulk caps ─┬─ 4× [ESC MCU → 3-ph gate driver → 6× N-FET] → Motor 1..4
          │             └─ Current shunt / INA → H743 ADC
          └─ Buck 5V/3.3V → H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──> ESC MCUs
```

**Feasibility flag:** "80 A burst" on a 20×20 or 30×30 nano layout is **burst-only (seconds)**. Section 3 shows this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Six-step, synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases are active. The PWM phase alternates between its high-side FET and its complementary low-side FET, while the opposite phase's low-side FET stays on. Current therefore always flows through **two series FETs**, regardless of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Temperature dependence of R_DS(on):

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Including phase-current ripple for a winding inductance L at switching frequency f_sw:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L\, f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

### 1.2 Numbers

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full) |
| I_ph | 80 A |
| R_25 | 1.2 mΩ [A] (40 V, PDFN 5×6 or 3.3×3.3) |
| α | 0.0045 /K [A] |
| T_j | 110 °C (design point) |

- R_DS(on)(110 °C) = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.66 mΩ**
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 80² × 1.66 mΩ = **21.2 W per channel**
- Ripple is negligible: with L = 15 µH, D = 0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 20 V, ΔI ≈ 3.6 A, which adds only 0.03 %.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only one FET pair (the PWM phase) switches per period.

| Term | Equation | Inputs [A] | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f | t_r + t_f = 35 ns | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 35n × 48k = **1.69 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | 2·V_F·I·t_dt·f | V_F = 0.8 V, t_dt = 200 ns | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | Q_rr = 40 nC | **0.05 W** |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f | Q_oss = 50 nC | **0.03 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f | Q_g = 60 nC, V_gs = 10 V | **0.06 W** (dissipated in the driver) |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 3.0 W per channel** |

Design levers:

- **Dead time is the second-largest loss.** Trim it to about 100 ns if the gate driver and layout allow; this saves about 0.6 W per channel.
- **48 kHz is a trade-off.** Overlap and dead-time losses scale linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz they would be about 1.5 W, at the cost of more current ripple and audible noise.

### Per-channel and board totals at 80 A

| | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 21.2 W | 84.8 W |
| Switching | 3.0 W | 12.0 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, total current 320 A) | — | ~25 W if a single shunt; use per-ESC sense or a smaller R_shunt |
| **Total (excluding shunt)** | **24.2 W** | **≈ 97 W** |

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Junction rise (fast)

Electrical commutation runs at kHz rates, so loss averages across the six FETs: each FET carries about 1/3 duty high and 1/3 low.

- Loss per FET: P_FET ≈ 24.2 / 6 ≈ **4.0 W**
- ΔT_jc = P_FET × R_th,jc = 4.0 × 1.2 K/W [A] = **4.8 K**
- The FET-to-board via array (R_th ≈ 3 K/W [A]) adds about 12 K.
- **Junction-to-board ΔT ≈ 17 K.**

### 3.2 Board lumped thermal capacitance (slow)

30×30 mm board, 6 layers of 2 oz copper at 80 % fill, plus a 5 g aluminium heatsink:

| Mass | Calculation | C (J/K) |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | 9e-4 m² × 6 × 70 µm × 0.8 × 8960 kg/m³ = 2.7 g × 0.385 | 1.04 |
| FR4 (1.6 mm) | 2.7 g × 1.1 | 2.97 |
| Aluminium heatsink | 5 g × 0.9 | 4.5 |
| **C_board** | | **≈ 8.5 J/K** |

- Convective resistance in propwash: R_th,ba ≈ 8 K/W [A].
- Time constant: τ = R·C ≈ **68 s**.

The board temperature follows a first-order response:

$$T_{board}(t) = T_{amb} + P R_{th,ba}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right)$$

For t ≪ τ this reduces to dT/dt ≈ P/C = 97 / 8.5 ≈ **11.4 K/s**.

### 3.3 Burst budget

Limits used: T_j,max = 150 °C with 25 K derating, so T_j ≤ 125 °C. Ambient is 35 °C. The board limit is 125 − 17 = **108 °C**, giving a 73 K allowable rise.

$$t_{burst} = -\tau \ln\left(1 - \frac{73}{97 \times 8}\right) \approx \mathbf{6.7\ s}$$

At **all four motors** at 80 A, from cold.

Continuous rating, solved from P × 8 K/W = 73 K, gives P ≈ 9 W total. At that loss level, I²R scaling gives **≈ 25–30 A per channel continuous**. Without the heatsink, C drops to about 4 J/K and the burst time halves.

### 3.4 Bus capacitor ripple

- Per channel: I_cap,rms = I × √(D(1 − D)), which peaks at **40 A** at D = 0.5.
- With four channels and non-correlated PWM phases, the combined ripple is about 80 A_rms.
- Design: 4× 35 V polymer capacitors on the board (e.g. 220 µF, roughly 5 A_rms each) plus an **external low-ESR 1000 µF/35 V on the pigtail.** Loop inductance limits how much ripple the on-board capacitors can absorb, so the external capacitor is mandatory.
