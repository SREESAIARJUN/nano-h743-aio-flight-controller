## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors. A "4-in-1 ESC" stage needs its own MCU(s) running AM32 or BLHeli_32, and these must close the commutation loop in real time. The architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device (baseline) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, DShot600 bidir master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 firmware, 6-step/sinusoidal commutation |
| Gate driver ×4 | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, ~1.5 A/1.8 A) | High/low-side drive |
| MOSFETs ×24 | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3 (e.g. 1.4 mΩ class) | Half-bridges |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm (M2 mount), 2-layer stack or AIO 25.5 mm | "Nano" |

**Assumed parameters.** Replace these with your selected datasheet values. All equations below are parametric.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (worst case for switching) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current per channel |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_ds,25 | 1.4 mΩ | At V_GS = 10 V, 25 °C |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_ds(on) temperature coefficient |

| R_cu | 0.4 mΩ | Copper path per channel (traces + vias + pads) |

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.4 × 1.3375 = **1.87 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path

With 6-step commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, which AM32 uses, the phase current always flows through exactly **two** channels in series:

- one high-side FET (or its sync-rectifier complement)
- one low-side FET

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \,\big(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{cu}\big)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 6400 \times (3.75 + 0.4)\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{26.6\ W}$$

### 1.3 Distribution across the six FETs

Each FET carries current for a 120° electrical window. The electrical frequency is high relative to the junction thermal time constant (for example, 30 krpm with 14 poles gives f_e = 3.5 kHz, while τ_j ≈ 1–5 ms). The loss therefore averages as follows:

- **Average per FET:** P_cond / 6 ≈ 4.4 W (FET share only, ≈ 4.0 W).
- **Worst-case FET:** the FET on the PWM-active leg also takes switching loss (§2).
- **Low speed / stall-like loads:** commutation slows and the averaging assumption breaks. One FET can then see I²R_ds ≈ 12 W for extended windows. This is the real burst killer at punch-out from hover.

### 1.4 Iterative T_j solution

R_ds depends on T_j, so solve the following to convergence (2–3 iterations is enough):

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot R_{\theta,jb}$$

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

The PWM leg hard-switches once on and once off per cycle. The complementary FET switches with ZVS-like behavior through the dead time but contributes diode and Q_rr loss.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·40n·48k = **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2·t_dead·f_sw | 0.8·80·400n·48k = **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40n·25.2·48k = **0.05 W** |
| C_oss | C_oss·V²·f_sw | 1n·635·48k = **0.03 W** |
| Gate drive (driver-side) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·50n·10·48k = **0.05 W** |
| **Total P_sw,ch** | | **≈ 3.3 W** |

**Design levers.**

- **Dead time dominates the "hidden" loss.** Cutting t_dead from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. AM32 exposes this setting.
- **Diode loss scales with frequency.** At 24 kHz, the overlap and diode losses halve (about −1.6 W), at the cost of more current ripple and motor iron/copper loss.

## 3. Total Burst Power Budget

| Item | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 26.6 W | 106 W |
| Switching | 3.3 W | 13 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if used) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **~31.5 W** | **~126 W** |

Efficiency at burst: P_out ≈ 25.2 V × 80 A × D. At full throttle that is ≈ 2 kW per channel, so η ≈ 98.4%. The efficiency looks excellent, but the absolute heat on a 20×20 board is the problem.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Network (Cauer-equivalent, simplified)

```
P_FET ──[Rθjc 1.5 K/W]──[Rθ via/TIM 1.5 K/W]── T_board ──[Rθ ba]── T_amb
          (Cj ≈ 0.02 J/K)                        (C_board)      ↑ convection + wire/stack conduction
```

### 4.2 Board heat capacity

A 20×20 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz board with FETs and components weighs about 5 g. With c ≈ 0.8 J/(g·K):

$$C_{board} \approx 4\ \text{J/K}$$

### 4.3 Adiabatic burst (dominant for durations under ~5 s)

$$\frac{dT_{board}}{dt} = \frac{P_{tot}}{C_{board}} = \frac{126}{4} \approx 31\ \text{K/s}$$
