## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three assumptions in the request need fixing before any math is valid.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The H743 cannot commutate 4 motors at 48 kHz.** It has only two advanced-control timers with complementary outputs and dead-time (TIM1, TIM8). Four 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary pairs. | Use a split architecture. The H743 is the FC running ArduPilot. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071) run AM32 and generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. |
| 2 | **80 A burst per channel on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally viable** (proven in §2.5). 4 × 80 A = 320 A at 6S is about 8 kW. | Either define 80 A as **per-channel, ≤1 s burst, 30.5×30.5 mm board**, or derate to 45 A per channel burst on 20×20. Both cases are modelled below. |
| 3 | **"80A" is ambiguous.** | Assumed: 80 A per motor phase-current peak, 6S (25.2 V max), burst ≤ 1 s, duty-cycled with ≥10 s recovery. |

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

AM32 uses 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, current *I* flows through one high-side and one low-side path. With synchronous rectification, the conduction path is always two MOSFET channels regardless of duty *D*:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j)\cdot\left[D + (1-D)\right]_{HS/LS\,sync} + I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)_{LS,return} = 2\,I^2\,\alpha\,R_{ds,25}$$

Temperature-dependent resistance (linearised):

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + k\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad k \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At 80 A, hot:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Add copper and shunt losses (0.3 mΩ shunt plus about 0.4 mΩ trace per path):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.7\times10^{-3} \approx \mathbf{4.5\ W}$$

### 2.3 Switching Losses at 48 kHz (per channel; one switching leg active per PWM period)

**Hard-switching overlap:**
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot40\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = 0.018\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (2 edges):**
$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive (6 FETs per channel; dissipated in driver and R_g, not the FETs):**
$$P_g = 6\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}_{(commutation\ share)} \approx 0.05\ \text{W}$$
