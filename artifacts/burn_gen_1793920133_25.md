# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Electro-Thermal Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlist

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three assumptions in the brief need correcting before any numbers are meaningful.

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage.** In every production 4-in-1 design, the H743 is the flight controller MCU. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071), which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 only sends DShot600 frames and captures the bidirectional eRPM replies. The model is partitioned accordingly.
2. **"Nano" plus "80A burst" is thermally incompatible with sustained operation.** Section 3 shows that a 20×20 mm board reaches thermal runaway limits in about 1.5 s with all four channels at 80A. The 80A figure is only defensible as a short (≤1–2 s) single- or dual-channel burst rating. I recommend 30.5×30.5 mm, or derating to 55A burst / 40A continuous at 20×20.
3. **All component values below are design-class assumptions, not quotes from a specific datasheet.** Final numbers must be re-run with the selected MOSFET's max-spec values at Tj = 125 °C.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Topology.** The ESC uses 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM on the active phase. At any instant, current I flows through exactly two FETs:
- the high-side FET (duty D), or the same leg's low-side FET as synchronous rectifier (duty 1−D), and
- the low-side FET of the return phase (duty 1).

**RMS correction for ripple.** The phase current ripple at worst-case duty (D = 0.5) is:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\times0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\times48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1+\frac{r^2}{12}\right),\quad r=\frac{\Delta I}{I}=0.082 \;\Rightarrow\; +0.06\%$$

The ripple correction is negligible, so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

**Per-channel conduction loss.**

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS(on),25}\,k_T = 2(80)^2(1.3\text{m}\Omega)(1.5) = 24.96\ \text{W}$$

**Copper loss.**

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400\times0.5\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W}$$
