## 0. Scope, Architecture Correction, and Assumptions

**Architecture clarification.** ArduPilot does not run motor commutation. The STM32H743 is the flight controller. A 4-in-1 ESC needs commutation MCUs, normally four independent ones (one per motor) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This package therefore splits the design into two parts:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071KBU6 | AM32, 48 kHz PWM, commutation |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | 6 gates per motor |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN5×6 | Three half-bridges per motor |

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Six-step conduction topology

AM32 uses trapezoidal six-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM. For each leg over one electrical period:

| Interval | High-side (HS) | Low-side (LS) |
|---|---|---|
| 120° "PWM-high" | on for D | on for (1−D), synchronous |
| 120° "low" | off | on 100% |
| 120° float | off | off |
