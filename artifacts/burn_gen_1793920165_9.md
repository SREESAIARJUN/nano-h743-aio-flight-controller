## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Feasibility

These four points shape every number that follows. Review them before reading the rest of the package.

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production-grade 4-in-1 design, each motor (or the pack of four) has a dedicated ESC MCU. The ESC MCU runs commutation, the 48 kHz PWM and the GCR telemetry encoder. This package assumes 4× AT32F421 running AM32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and issues bi-directional DShot600 to those ESC MCUs. The 48 kHz switching analysis therefore applies to the ESC side, and the DMA analysis applies to the H743 side.

2. **The "nano" form factor and 80 A burst per motor are incompatible.** At 80 A, 24 MOSFETs in DFN5×6 packages, the shunt and the DC-link capacitance do not fit on 20×20 mm. The model below uses a **30.5×30.5 mm mount, 36×36 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** board.
   - On 20×20 mm, the 3.3×3.3 mm FETs required by the footprint have roughly 2× the Rds(on), which roughly doubles conduction loss.
   - In that case, derate the burst rating to about 45–50 A.
