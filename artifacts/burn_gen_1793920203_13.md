# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Design Reference

I don't have any prior context for Job #67, so I've stated every assumption explicitly. All numbers are parametric, so you can substitute datasheet values for your chosen MOSFETs and re-run the math.

---

## 0. Design Assumptions and an Early Warning

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (design at max, worst case for switching) |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor phase current, burst (≤ 5 s), all four channels |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), dead time 50 ns |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, AM32-class firmware on per-channel ESC MCUs |
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm), 400 MHz (480 MHz on rev V) |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071-class running AM32 with bidirectional DShot |
| MOSFET (per switch) | 40 V, R_DS(on),25 = 1.0 mΩ max, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_gs2 = 5 nC, V_pl = 3.0 V, R_g,int = 1 Ω, C_oss ≈ 1.5 nF, Q_rr = 50 nC, V_SD = 0.8 V |
| R_DS(on) tempco | α ≈ 0.005 /K (≈1.5× at 125 °C) |
| Gate driver | 3-phase, 10 V drive, R_source ≈ 2 Ω, R_sink ≈ 1 Ω, R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power layers |

**Warning.** The 80A × 4 thermal math (Section 3) shows that a nano or 30×30 AIO **cannot sustain 80A per motor**. At roughly 85 W of dissipation it survives seconds, not minutes. "80A burst" must be specified with a duration and duty cycle. A true 20×20 "nano" form factor makes the problem worse. I recommend specifying something like **"80 A, 2 s, single motor; 45 A continuous with airflow"**, and having the ESC firmware enforce it.
