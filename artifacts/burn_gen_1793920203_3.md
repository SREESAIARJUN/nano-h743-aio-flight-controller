## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Flags

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs on the same PCB. The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. Each ESC MCU is, for example, an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. Each ESC channel has one 3-phase gate driver and six N-channel MOSFETs.

**Baseline parameters.** These are representative values. Replace them with datasheet values for your chosen parts.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S, fully charged | 25.2 V |
| I_burst | Phase current per motor | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_DS(on),25 | 40 V MOSFET, 5×6 DFN, V_GS = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | R_DS temperature coefficient | 0.006 /K |
| Q_g / Q_gd / Q_oss / Q_rr | Gate / Miller / output / recovery charge | 50 / 10 / 50 / 40 nC |
| V_SD | Body-diode forward voltage at 80 A | 0.8 V |
| t_r / t_f | Switching edges (driver ≈ 1 A source / 1.5 A sink, R_g = 4.7 Ω) | 15 / 10 ns |
| t_dead | Dead time per edge | 50 ns |
| V_GS | Gate drive voltage | 10 V |

**Design flags. Read these first.**
1. **"Nano" and 80 A are in conflict.** Four channels at 80 A dissipate about 85 W. A 30×30 mm board cannot sustain that. 80 A must be treated as a time-limited burst rating. The model below derives that time limit (§3.3).
2. **Gate driver choice.** Ensure the bootstrap supply suits 6S. The common FD6288 class is rated about 20 V V_CC, so it needs a 10–12 V V_CC rail, and the bootstrap diode must withstand V_bus plus transients.
3. **Verify DMA mapping against the ArduPilot hwdef tooling.** The STM32H7 DMAMUX allows any request to use any stream, but ArduPilot's DMA resolver still enforces sharing rules (§5).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.51 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases conduct:
- **PWM leg.** The high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Return leg.** The low-side FET is fully on.

Instantaneous conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore **independent of duty**:

$$P_{cond,ch}=I^2R_{DS}\,[D+(1-D)] + I^2R_{DS} = 2I^2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Over one electrical cycle, each phase conducts 2/3 of the time and sits in each role symmetrically. The average loss per MOSFET is therefore:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET}=\frac{2I^2R_{DS}}{6}=\frac{I^2R_{DS}}{3}$$

### 1.3 Results at 80 A

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per channel | 2 × 6400 × 1.51 m = **19.3 W** |
| Average per FET | 3.22 W |
| Peak per FET (return-leg low side, 1/3 of the cycle) | 9.7 W |
| Copper and shunt (R_cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ path) | I²R_cu = 1.9 W |
| **Total conduction, 4 channels** | **≈ 85 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. With synchronous rectification, the high-side FET is hard-switched and the low-side FET switches with near-zero voltage (ZVS) after its body diode conducts.

| Term | Formula | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 25 ns × 48 k = **1.21 W** |
| C_oss charge | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 50 n × 25.2 × 48 k = 0.06 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 n × 25.2 × 48 k = 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dead·f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k = 0.31 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2 × 50 n × 10 × 48 k = 0.05 W (in the driver) |
| **P_sw per channel** | | **≈ 1.68 W** |

**Edge times from gate charge.** The overlap term depends on the edge times. They come from the gate charge and driver current:

$$t_{sw}\approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g},\qquad I_g=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv}}$$

**Linearized form for control-loop use:**

$$P_{sw}(I)\approx 0.0190\,I + 0.16\ \text{W}$$

**Total per channel at 80 A:** 19.3 + 1.9 + 1.7 = **22.9 W**. For 4 channels: **≈ 92 W**.

**Lowering f_sw.** Dropping to 24 kHz saves only about 0.8 W per channel. Conduction loss dominates, so R_DS(on) and copper are the levers that matter.

## 3. Thermal Model (Burst and Continuous)

### 3.1 Two-node lumped network

The model has two nodes: junction and board.

- **Junction node:**
  $$T_j = T_b + P_{FET,peak}\,R_{\theta jb}$$
  where R_θjb = R_θjc + solder + via array ≈ 1.2 + 0.5 + 1.3 = **3.0 K/W**.

- **Board node:**
  $$C_b\frac{dT_b}{dt}=P_{tot}-hA(T_b-T_a)$$

**Board parameters:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Board | 30×30 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz |
| Board mass | ≈ 8 g |
| Effective heat capacity C_b | ≈ 7 J/K (FR-4 plus copper) |
| Thermal pad / heatsink | Optional; adds roughly 3 J/K
