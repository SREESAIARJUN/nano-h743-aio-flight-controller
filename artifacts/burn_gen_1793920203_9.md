## 0. Baseline Assumptions & Architectural Correction

**Architecture note.** The STM32H743 should run the flight controller (ArduPilot), not the ESC. Each motor channel needs its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. A single H743 cannot run four sensorless commutation loops and ArduPilot at the same time with acceptable timing.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **Phase A (PWM phase):** the high-side FET conducts for D. Its complementary low-side FET conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Phase B (return phase):** the low-side FET is held on for the whole sector.

There are always exactly two R_ds(on) in series with the motor current, apart from dead-time intervals (§2.3).

### 1.2 Equations

Temperature-adjusted resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx0.006\,/^\circ\text{C} \Rightarrow R_{ds}(125^\circ\text{C})\approx1.6\text{ m}\Omega$$

Bridge conduction loss per motor channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2\big[D\,R_{ds}+(1-D)R_{ds}\big] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.6\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

Each FET conducts for one-third of the electrical cycle in its role. Per-FET average losses are:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R_{ds} = 3.24\text{ W},\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\big[(1-D)+1\big]I^2R_{ds} = 3.58\text{ W}$$

The worst-case FET is a low side at about 3.6 W average. Its instantaneous peak is I²R = 10.2 W while conducting.

### 1.3 Copper loss (do not ignore at 80 A)

$$R_{trace}=\frac{\rho L}{w\,t},\quad \rho_{Cu}(100^\circ\text{C})\approx2.2\times10^{-8}\,\Omega\text{m}$$

Example: a 2 oz (70 µm) layer, 4 mm wide, 8 mm long, with 3 paralleled layers gives R ≈ 0.21 mΩ. That adds 80² × 0.21 mΩ × 2 paths ≈ **2.7 W per channel**. Plan on ≥4 paralleled power layers and short bus-to-FET-to-pad paths.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-phase FET pair hard-switches in each sector.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching)

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g}} = \frac{12\text{ nC}}{0.6\text{ A}} = 20\text{ ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{ns})(48\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery and output capacitance

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

Q_rr scales with di/dt and current. Use 2–3× (≈0.15 W) at 80 A unless datasheet curves say otherwise.

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = \tfrac12(1.2\text{nF})(25.2)^2(48\text{k}) = 0.018\text{ W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.4 Gate drive

This loss lands in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET.

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_gV_{gs}f_{sw} = 2(45\text{nC})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.043\text{ W}$$
