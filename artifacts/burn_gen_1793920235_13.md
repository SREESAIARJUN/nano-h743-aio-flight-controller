## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning by applying v_d with v_q = 0 at θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, balanced phases, two shunts)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

with ω_e = p ω_m and λ_m = 2K_t / (3p).

### 1.5 PI controller with pole-zero cancellation

$$G_{PI}(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_{c},\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

With these gains, the closed-loop transfer function is first order, $i/i^* = \omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$. Choose ω_c = 2π·1.5 kHz, which is below f_PWM/10 and leaves margin for the 1.5-sample computation and PWM delay.

### 1.6 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)$$

Set i_d* = 0 (surface PMSM, no field weakening needed).
