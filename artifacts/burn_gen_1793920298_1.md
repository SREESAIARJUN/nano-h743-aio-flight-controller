### Architectural correction

ArduPilot does not contain ESC commutation firmware. An "H743 4-in-1" therefore needs:

- **STM32H743**: runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **ESC MCUs**: four small MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. A single dual-core ESC MCU is also possible but uncommon.

The rest of this document assumes the standard 1+4 topology.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, motor current flows through exactly **two** conducting devices:

- **PWM on-state:** HS(A) → winding → LS(B)
- **PWM off-state:** LS(A) (synchronous rectification) → winding → LS(B)

Assuming equal HS and LS R_ds(on), the instantaneous channel conduction loss is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.49 mΩ | 19.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.65 mΩ | 21.1 W |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical period, each phase spends roughly 1/3 driven high (PWM), 1/3 held low, and 1/3 floating. With electrical frequency ≫ 1/τ_thermal, time-average the loss:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,D \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,[(1-D) + 1]$$

- **At full throttle (D → 1):** HS ≈ LS ≈ I²R/3 ≈ **6.3 W per FET** at 100 °C.
- **Worst case at partial throttle with high current (D ≈ 0.5, e.g., hard braking or a heavy prop):** the low-side FET carries I²R × 0.5, about **9.5 W**.

**Design rule:** size thermals on the low-side FETs.
