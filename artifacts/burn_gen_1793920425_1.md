# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #47): Mathematical Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists

The brief left the MOSFET part, ESC MCU, battery voltage, burst duration and PCB stackup unspecified. I've fixed each one as an explicit assumption so you can substitute real datasheet values. The key finding comes first because it affects everything else.

> **Headline result:** A 4 × 80 A simultaneous burst on a 20×20 / 30×30 nano stack produces about **84 W of bridge dissipation**. With realistic board thermal mass, the junctions reach their limit in **about 3–4 s**, even with prop-wash cooling. "80 A burst" is achievable only as a **≤2–3 s, per-motor (not all four at once)** rating. Section 2 derives this.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor, one 3-phase bridge)

### 1.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly **two channels in series**: one high-side path and one low-side path. During PWM-off time, the complementary FET carries the current instead of a diode. This gives:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Hot-junction worst case (T_j = 125 °C):

$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.55\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.84\ W}$$

Averaged over the 6 FETs, that is ≈ 3.3 W per FET. The instantaneous peak in the conducting pair is about 9.9 W per FET during its 1/3 electrical-cycle window. This matters for thermal ripple at low eRPM.

**PCB copper loss (add separately):** A 2 oz inner trace of 10 mm length × 3 mm width has about 0.17 mΩ. That adds:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 0.17\text{ m}\Omega \approx 2.2\ \text{W per motor}$$

### 1.2 Switching Loss (hard-switched leg, one PWM'd leg active at a time)

**Overlap (V–I crossover) loss:**

$$P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot20\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (body diode of the sync FET conducts during dead time, so Q_rr applies):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot \tfrac12 \approx 0.024\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per PWM period):

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.614\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (two FETs toggling per period; dissipated mostly in R_G and the driver):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Totals

| Loss | Per motor (W) | ×4 motors (W) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (125 °C) | 19.84 | 79.4 |
| V–I switching | 0.97 | 3.9 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 | 2.5 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.13 | 0.5 |
| **Bridge total** | **21.55** | **86.2** |
| PCB copper (estimate) | 2.2 | 8.8 |
| **Board total** | **≈23.8** | **≈95 W** |

**Efficiency check.** Mechanical-side power at 80 A per motor and roughly 90% duty is about 1.8 kW per motor, so bridge efficiency is about 98.8%. The losses are small relative to throughput, but the absolute watts are what break a nano PCB.

**Switching-frequency sensitivity.** Since ∂P/∂f_sw ≈ (P_sw,VI + P_rr + P_oss + P_dt + P_g)/f_sw ≈ 36 µW/Hz per motor, moving from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only about 0.9 W per motor. **Conduction dominates at about 92%.** The design lever is R_DS(on) and copper, not PWM frequency.

**Battery-side shunt warning.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt at a 320 A total burst dissipates I²R = 25.6 W. Use a per-motor low-side sense or a Hall sensor (e.g. ACS7xx class), or a ≤0.1 mΩ shunt.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Network (Cauer-equivalent, simplified to 3 nodes)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC 1.5 K/W]──(T_case)──[R_θCB 0.5 K/W: solder + via array]──(T_board)──[R_θBA]──T_amb
         C_j ≈ 0.02 J/K                C_case ≈ 0.15 J/K                       C_board ≈ 4 J/K
```

- **C_board estimate (30.5×30.5 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz):** about 4.5 g of copper × 0.385 J/g·K, plus about 2.5 g of FR-4 × 1.1 J/g·K, plus the FETs and capacitors. This gives about 4–5 J/K. Use 4 J/K for a conservative result.
- **R_θBA under prop wash:** h ≈ 100 W/m²·K, A ≈ 2 × 0.0305² = 1.86×10⁻³ m². This gives R_θBA ≈ 5.4 K/W, i.e. a conductance of only about 0.19 W/K.

### 2.2 Steady State (impossible)

$$\Delta T_{board,ss} = P \cdot R_{\theta BA} = 86\cdot5.4 \approx 465\ \text{K}$$
