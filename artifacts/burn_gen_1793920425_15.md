## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

| Issue | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Who commutates the motors? | **4× dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32. The H743 runs ArduPilot only. | ArduPilot does not perform sensorless 6-step or FOC commutation. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. |
| "80 A burst" on a nano form factor | Treat as a **≤2 s transient, per channel, one or two channels at a time**. It is not a continuous rating. | Section 2 shows ~90 W of total dissipation at 4×80 A. A 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 board cannot remove that in steady state. |
| 48 kHz PWM | Generated by each ESC MCU (center-aligned complementary PWM, AM32 `PWM_FREQ=48`). | It is independent of the H743 timers. |
| Bus voltage | 6S: V_bus,max = 25.2 V; MOSFET V_DS rating 40 V. | Gives ~37% margin for ringing and regen spikes. TVS clamping is required. |

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel, 6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step commutation, **two switch positions conduct at every instant**:

- the high side of the PWM'd phase (D·T) or its synchronous low side ((1−D)·T), and
- the low side of the return phase (continuously during the 60° sector).

The conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ 2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu} \right], \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

**Self-consistent junction temperature.** R_DS rises with temperature, which raises loss, which raises temperature. Iterating at T_j ≈ 115 °C:

- R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0065 × 90) = **1.585 mΩ**
- P_cond = 6400 × (3.17 + 0.3) mΩ = **22.2 W/channel**
  - MOSFETs: 20.3 W
  - Copper: 1.9 W

**Per-device average.** Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The average loss per FET is therefore 20.3 / 6 ≈ **3.4 W**. The instantaneous peak, which is what drives junction ripple, is 10.1 W.

### 2.2 Switching Loss (hard-switched leg, inductive load)

$$P_{sw} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f}_{V\text{-}I\ overlap} + \underbrace{Q_{rr} V f}_{recovery} + \underbrace{C_{oss,er} V^2 f}_{C_{oss}} + \underbrace{2 V_{SD} I\, t_{dt} f}_{dead\text{-}time}$$

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k | 1.452 W |
| Q_rr | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.060 W |
| C_oss | 1.5 nF × 635 × 48 k | 0.046 W |
| Dead time | 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k | 0.614 W |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈2.17 W/channel** |

**Gate drive loss** is dissipated in the driver, not the FETs:

P_g = Q_g · V_GS · f · 2 = 50 nC × 10 × 48 k × 2 = 0.048 W/channel.

**Sensitivity to switching frequency:**

$$\frac{\partial P_{sw}}{\partial f} \approx 45\ \mu\text{W/Hz}$$

Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.1 W per channel, at the cost of current ripple and audible noise.

### 2.3 Total Burst Dissipation

| Quantity | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET + copper) | 22.2 W | 88.8 W |
| Switching + dead time | 2.17 W | 8.7 W |
| Driver and logic | ~0.15 W | 0.6 W |
| **Total** | **≈24.5 W** | **≈98 W** |

**Efficiency.** At D = 0.9, battery current ≈ D·I = 72 A, so P_in ≈ 1.81 kW per channel. The power stage efficiency is then ~98.6%. The problem is the absolute heat flux concentrated in roughly 6 cm², not the efficiency.

### 2.4 Bulk Capacitor Ripple

$$I_{C,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

- Maximum at D = 0.5: **40 A rms per channel**.
- With four unsynchronized channels, the incoherent sum is ≈ 80 A rms worst case.
- Mitigation: per-channel 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R MLCCs placed on the half-bridge, plus a shared 470–1000 µF 35 V low-ESR electrolytic on the pigtail.
- Resulting loss with ESR ≈ 2 mΩ effective: about 1–3 W, located at the input pad.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Foster/Cauer Lumped Network

```
P_FET ─► [Zθ_JC: R=1.2 K/W, τ≈2 ms] ─► [Rθ_CB: 0.5 K/W via array] ─► Board node
Board node: C_board ≈ 4.5 J/K
  (≈3.5 g FR4/Cu at ~0.6 J/g·K + 6 FETs + caps)
Board ─► Rθ_BA ≈ 12 K/W (still air), ≈4 K/W (prop-wash, ~10 m/s)
```
