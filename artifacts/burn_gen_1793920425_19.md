# Job #41: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC
## Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

I have no prior files for Job #41, so every component value below is a stated assumption. Swap in your actual MOSFET datasheet values and the equations still apply. Two findings change the scope, so they come first.

**Finding 1: Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation MCU per channel, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The H743 is the flight controller and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those four MCUs. The thermal and switching math below therefore applies to the ESC board, and the DMA math applies to the H743.

**Finding 2: Thermal feasibility.** At 80 A per channel on a nano (≈30×30 mm) 4-in-1, the board dissipates about 100 W. In free prop-wash it cannot reach steady state. The model in §3 shows the realistic burst is **about 3–4 s without a heatsink and about 7–8 s with a 5 g aluminium spreader**, not the 10 s many vendors quote. Write the spec around that, or derate.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two phases carry I_ph at any instant:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side conducts for D, the complementary low-side for (1−D). The current always flows through exactly one FET of that leg.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side conducts the full 120° interval.

Conduction loss is therefore independent of D:

```
P_cond = I_ph² · R_DS(T_j) · [D + (1−D)] + I_ph² · R_DS(T_j)
       = 2 · I_ph² · R_DS(T_j)
```

The temperature-dependent resistance is:

```
R_DS(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]
```

Note the positive feedback: R rises with T, which raises P, which raises T. You must iterate, or use the hot value, which is the conservative choice.

Results at 80 A:

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.2 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.8 mΩ | **23.0 W** | **92.2 W** |

Add PCB copper and solder losses. Assuming an effective path resistance of R_pcb ≈ 0.3 mΩ:

```
P_pcb = 2 · 80² · 0.3m ≈ 3.8 W per channel
```

### 2.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-leg high-side FET is hard-switched. The low-side commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

**(a) V–I overlap in the hard-switched FET:**
```
P_VI = ½ · V_bus · I_ph · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
     = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 30e-9 · 48e3 = 1.45 W
```

**(b) Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode:**
```
P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 40e-9 · 25.2 · 48e3 = 0.048 W
```

```
E_oss ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V_bus = 0.76 µJ
P_oss = 2 · E_oss · f_sw = 0.073 W
