## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, synchronous rectification)

AM32 and BLHeli_32 run trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary PWM. In each 60° sector, one phase's high side (HS) PWMs at duty D. Its low side (LS) conducts during (1−D). The return phase's LS is held fully on.

**Temperature-dependent resistance**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Instantaneous bridge conduction loss** (independent of D, which is a key result):

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I^2R_{ds}\,D + I^2R_{ds}(1-D) + I^2R_{ds} = 2I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET is active in 2 of 6 sectors per electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}D \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}\cdot\tfrac{1}{1}\Big|_{\text{return}}\times\tfrac12$$

This simplifies to ≈ ⅓·I²R per FET at the high duty typical of a burst (D → 1).

**Numbers at T_j = 110 °C:**

$$R_{ds} = 1.0(1+0.007\cdot85) = 1.595\ \text{m}\Omega$$
$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2(80)^2(1.595\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{20.4\ W/channel}$$
$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.30\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.92\ W/channel}$$
$$P_{FET,avg} \approx 20.4/6 \approx \mathbf{3.4\ W}\ \text{(peak 10.2 W during its conduction sector)}$$
