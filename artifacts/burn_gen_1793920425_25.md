## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate 4 BLDC motors with sensorless BEMF at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. The standard architecture applies:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot, DShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 firmware, 6-step sensorless, 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge, 20 V) | High-side bootstrap drive |
| MOSFETs (×24) | 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.4 mΩ @ 10 V, 25 °C | Bridge |
| IMU | ICM-42688-P | SPI1 |

**Feasibility constraint (read first).** 80 A per channel on a nano (≤30×30 mm) board is a **burst rating only**, on the order of 2–3 s with all four channels loaded. The math in §3 shows why. Any datasheet or marketing claim of "80 A continuous" on this form factor is physically unsupportable. Specify as **"35 A continuous / 80 A burst (≤2 s, 25 °C ambient, prop-wash airflow)."**

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

AM32 runs 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, exactly two legs conduct:

- **PWM leg:** HS on for duty D, LS (sync rectifier) on for (1 − D − 2t_d·f).
- **Return leg:** LS on 100 %.

The current path therefore always crosses **two MOSFET channels** in series, apart from dead-time intervals.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,(1 - 2t_d f_{sw})$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

- R_DS = 1.4 × 1.3375 = 1.873 mΩ
- P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.873e-3 × 0.981 = **23.5 W**

Per-FET instantaneous loss while conducting is 80² × 1.873e-3 = 12.0 W. Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical period (the HS split by D), so the **time-averaged loss per FET ≈ 3.9 W**.
