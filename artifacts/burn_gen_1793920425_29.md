## 0. Assumptions and Critical Scoping Notes

Before deriving anything, three architectural facts constrain the model.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 firmware, which supports bidirectional DShot and 48 kHz PWM. The 48 kHz switching is generated by those ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421), not the H743.
2. **"80A burst" is interpreted as 80 A phase current per channel, for a few seconds.** A nano-format board (30.5×30.5 mm mounting, about 36×36 mm outline) cannot sustain 4×80 A continuously. Section 3 shows that the burst duration is set by the board's thermal capacitance, not by the MOSFET ratings.
3. **The reference netlists are an architecture-level starting point, not fab-ready.** Verify every pin and alternate-function mapping against ST DS12110 / RM0433, the ESC MCU datasheet, and the chosen MOSFET and driver datasheets before layout.
