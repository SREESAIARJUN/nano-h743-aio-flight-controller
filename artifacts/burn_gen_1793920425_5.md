# STM32H743 AIO with 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst: Reference Design Model (Job #29)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors with sensorless BEMF at 48 kHz in ArduPilot. Production 4-in-1 boards use **one ESC MCU per channel**, and the H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot. This model therefore assumes:

| Block | Assumed part (placeholder; verify against your BOM) |
|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6, 480 MHz, ArduPilot |
| ESC MCU (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 running AM32 (PWM frequency set to 48 kHz) |
| Gate driver (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, 250 V/ ~1.5 A) |
| MOSFETs (×24) | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on) = 1.2 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g = 60 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, C_oss ≈ 1 nF |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm "nano", 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner copper |

**Design warning up front:** the thermal model in §3 shows that "80 A burst" on a 20×20 board is a **seconds-scale** rating limited by board heat capacity, not by the silicon. Treat it as such in firmware current limits.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32 uses six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During each 60° sector:
- The **PWM leg** conducts through the high-side FET for fraction D of the period and through the low-side FET (synchronous rectification) for 1−D.
- The **return leg** low-side FET conducts continuously.

The phase current I therefore always flows through exactly two channels:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\quad(\text{dead time excluded})$$

Conduction loss is **duty-independent** with synchronous rectification. Note that the motor phase current (not the battery current) sets it.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Copper path

Add the PCB and solder-path resistance from the phase pad to the switch node to the bus plane. For a 20×20 board with 2 oz copper, assume R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ per conducting path, giving 2 × 0.4 = 0.8 mΩ.

### 1.4 Numbers per channel at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction, T_j = 125 °C | 2·80²·1.8 mΩ | **23.0 W** |
| FET conduction, T_j = 25 °C | 2·80²·1.2 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| Copper | 80²·0.8 mΩ | **5.1 W** |
| **Conduction total (hot)** | | **≈ 28.1 W** |

The PWM ripple correction factor (1 + r²/12, where r is the peak-to-peak ripple divided by I) is under 1 % for typical motor inductance at 48 kHz, so it is neglected.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches, so there is one turn-on and one turn-off per period.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r = t_f = 15 ns (FD6288 into a 60 nC gate, with gate resistor):

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\,\text{ns} \cdot 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (low-side body diode into high-side turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\,\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr rises with di/dt and temperature. Use 2× margin, giving **≈ 0.10 W**.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 (2C_{oss}) V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot 2\,\text{nF}\cdot 635\cdot 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.03\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time events per period:

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot 80\cdot 2\cdot 100\,\text{ns}\cdot 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

AM32 dead time is configurable. Every 50 ns removed saves about 0.3 W per channel at 80 A, but you need at least t_f plus driver propagation skew plus margin to avoid shoot-through.

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and gate resistors, not the FETs)

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\cdot 60\,\text{nC}\cdot 10\,\text{V}\cdot 48\,\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

The FD6288 V_CC for this is supplied by the ESC 12 V or 10 V LDO, and bootstrap refresh is guaranteed by the low-side on-time at D_max < ~98 %.

### 2.6 Channel loss summary at 80 A burst, 6S, 48 kHz

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FET, hot) | 23.0 |
| Copper | 5.1 |
| V–I switching | 1.45 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.13 |
| Gate (driver) | 0.06 |
| **Per channel** | **≈ 30.4 W** |
| **4 channels** | **≈ 122 W** |

Conduction is about 92 % of the total. Moving from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs only about 1 W per channel, so **R_DS(on) and copper dominate, not f_sw**.

Efficiency check: 80 A × 25.2 V × D. At D = 0.8, motor power is about 1.6 kW and the ESC loss of 30 W is about 1.9 %. That is plausible.
