# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: 80A Burst Thermal, Switching Loss, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Package

**Job #5: Design Basis Document, Revision A**

---

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Stated Assumptions

Three findings shape the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a production 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. These can be one quad-capable MCU or four AT32F421/STM32G071-class parts running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Driving 12 half-bridges directly from the H743 would require four independent sensorless commutation loops. That conflicts with ArduPilot's scheduler, and ArduPilot does not support it. The 48 kHz PWM is therefore an **ESC-MCU parameter**, not an H743 parameter.
2. **"80A" is treated as a per-channel burst rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal analysis in §3 shows that a nano-class board (≈25×25 mm copper area, 20×20 mounting) cannot sustain 4×80 A. The physics limits it to roughly **2 seconds**. The deliverable is the burst envelope.
3. **MOSFET parameters are representative assumptions.** They describe a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 PDFN class part. They are *not* quoted from a specific datasheet. Substitute your selected part's values into the equations before release.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per channel | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_25 | 1.0 mΩ |

**Commutation scheme:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. This is the AM32/BLHeli_32 default.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant, two phases are energized: phase A sources and phase B sinks.

- **PWM-on interval (D):** current flows through high-side Q_AH and low-side Q_BL.
- **PWM-off interval (1−D):** current flows through synchronous low-side Q_AL and low-side Q_BL.

In both intervals, the current crosses exactly **two channels in series**. Conduction loss is therefore duty-independent:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Temperature dependence is modeled as:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This gives a factor of ≈1.6 at 125 °C.

### 1.2 Evaluation at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Cold (25 °C) | Hot (T_j = 125 °C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 2·I²·R_DS | 2·6400·0.001 = **12.8 W** | 2·6400·0.0016 = **20.5 W** |
| Shunt | I²·R_sh | 3.2 W | 3.2 W |
| PCB copper (incl. pads, vias) | I²·R_cu | 1.9 W | ≈2.6 W (Cu α = 0.0039) |
| **Total conduction, per channel** | | **17.9 W** | **26.3 W** |

### 1.3 Per-device distribution (worst device)

Over one electrical revolution, each leg is a "sink" for 1/3 of the time and a "PWM leg" for 1/3 of the time. The low-side FET of a leg sees:

$$
\overline{P}_{QL} = \tfrac13 I^2R + \tfrac13(1-D)I^2R
$$

Its instantaneous peak is I²R = **10.2 W** (hot). Peak instantaneous dissipation, not the average, sets the Z_th(j-pcb) transient in §3.3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The sink leg's low-side FET stays on for the full 120° segment. All terms below are per channel.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss (high-side, hard-switched)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}
= 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}
$$

The overlap time is set by the gate driver current and the Miller plateau:

$$
t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{G,tot}}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}
$$

For example, with Q_gd = 8 nC, R_G = 4.7 Ω, V_drv = 10 V, and V_pl = 3.5 V, each edge takes ≈5.8 ns. Allowing for di/dt and package inductance gives the 20 ns total.

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (body diode of synchronous FET)

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 30\text{nC}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}
$$
