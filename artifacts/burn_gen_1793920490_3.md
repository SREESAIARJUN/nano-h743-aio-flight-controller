## 0. Architectural Reality Check (Read First)

Three findings shape the design and should be settled before schematic capture.

**1. Two MCUs, not one.** A 4-in-1 ESC does not run its commutation from the H743.

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 | ArduPilot | Sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC ×4 | STM32G071 / AT32F421 (one per motor) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | Commutation and 48 kHz PWM |

The H743 never sees 48 kHz PWM. It only produces DShot frames and decodes bidirectional replies. The loss model in §2–3 lives entirely on the ESC side.

**2. 80A burst on a nano footprint (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is thermally marginal.** §4 shows the board absorbs roughly a 3–5 s burst before the FETs approach Tj limits, even with forced airflow. Treat "80A" as a short burst rating (≤3 s at full throttle with prop-wash cooling), not continuous. Continuous rating will land around 35–45A per channel.

**3. Bus voltage assumption.** The design uses 6S nominal (22.2 V; 25.2 V full charge) with 40 V MOSFETs. For 8S, use 60 V parts with roughly 2× higher R_DS(on), which invalidates the 80A claim at this size.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in synchronous 6-step commutation

In any 60° sector, one phase is driven high, one is held low, and one floats. With complementary PWM on the switched leg:

- During D·T_sw: current flows through HS_a → motor → LS_b
- During (1–D)·T_sw: current freewheels through LS_a (synchronous) → motor → LS_b

In both intervals, exactly **two MOSFET channels** carry the full phase current I. So for the bridge:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period (120° conduction). Averaged over a rotation:

$$
P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,bridge}}{6}\ \text{(average)}, \qquad P_{cond,FET,peak} = I^2 R_{DS(on)}
$$

The **average** sets steady temperature. The **peak** matters for FET-level transient Z_th when the electrical frequency is low (at startup or stall).

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\,/\mathrm{K}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 0.70 × 1.60 = **1.12 mΩ**.

### 2.3 Parasitic copper and interconnect resistance (often forgotten)

| Element | Estimate |
|---|---|
| FET package + bond/clip | Included in R_DS(on) |
| Phase pad / motor wire solder joint | 0.15 mΩ |
| PCB copper, per current path (2 oz outer + 2 oz inner, ~4 mm wide, ~10 mm) | 0.25 mΩ |
| Shunt (if per-channel current sense) | 0.5 mΩ (often omitted on 4-in-1; use a single bus shunt instead) |

$$
R_{path}(125^\circ C) = 2(1.12) + 0.25 + 0.15 = 2.64\ \mathrm{m\Omega}\ \ (\text{no per-channel shunt})
$$

### 2.4 Numerical results at 80A

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2.64\times10^{-3} = 6400 \times 2.64\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{16.9\ W}
$$

Breakdown:

| Component | Power per channel |
|---|---|
| MOSFETs (2 × 1.12 mΩ) | 14.3 W |
| Copper + joints | 2.6 W |
| **Average per FET** | 14.3 / 6 = **2.39 W** |
| **Instantaneous per conducting FET** | 80² × 1.12 mΩ = **7.17 W** |

For the 4-channel board at simultaneous 80A burst: **P_cond,total = 67.6 W**.

> Iterative note: T_j depends on P, which depends on R(T_j). Section 4.4 closes this loop.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg incurs switching loss: one HS/LS pair per channel at any time. The held-low FET is static.

### 3.1 Hard-switching V-I overlap

Switching-interval times from the gate driver:

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{source}}, \qquad t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{sink}}
$$

Compute the transition times:

- t_r = 18 nC / 1.0 A = 18 ns
- t_f = 18 nC / 1.5 A = 12 ns

Gate resistor and Miller plateau dynamics stretch this in practice. Use an effective total t_r + t_f ≈ 35 ns (bench-validate with a 200 MHz+ probe):

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 35\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.69\ W}
$$

### 3.2 Output capacitance loss (hard turn-on of HS discharges LS C_oss)

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 45\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.054\ W}
$$

### 3.3 Body-diode reverse recovery

The LS body diode conducts during dead time and recovers when HS turns on:

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}
$$

Q_rr grows with di/dt and temperature, so double this for margin: 0.12 W.

### 3.4 Dead-time diode conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dead} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g, not the FET)

$$
P_{gate} = Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} \times 2 = 60\text{n} \times 12 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = \mathbf{0.069\ W}
$$

### 3.6 Switching summary per channel

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| V-I overlap | 1.69 |
| C_oss | 0.05 |
| Q_rr (with margin) | 0.12 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Gate (in driver) | 0.07 |
| **Total** | **2.54 W** |

×4 channels: **10.2 W**.
