## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous commutation

During any 60° sector with complementary PWM:
- **ON interval (D):** high-side of phase A and low-side of phase B conduct.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** low-side of phase A (synchronous rectification) and low-side of phase B conduct.

Two MOSFET channels therefore conduct at all times, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R = 0.8\,(1+0.0045\cdot100) = 1.16 \approx 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Burst result (per motor)

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.2\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{15.36\ W}$$

Copper term: assume R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ for the phase path plus bus planes.

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2(0.3\times10^{-3}) = 1.92\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Per-device averaging

The electrical period is under 1 ms, far shorter than the junction thermal time constant (tens of ms or more). Losses therefore average across all six FETs:

$$\bar P_{FET,cond} \approx \frac{15.36}{6} = 2.56\ \text{W}$$

### 1.5 Electrothermal stability

Combining R_ds(on)(T) with T_j = T_b + P·R_th gives a fixed-point equation:

$$T_j = T_b + R_{th,jb}\cdot\frac{2I^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]}{6}$$

This has a stable solution only if:

$$R_{th,jb}\,\frac{2I^2R_{25}\alpha}{6} < 1$$

At 80 A with R_th,jb = 4 K/W: 4 · 2 · 6400 · 0.8 mΩ · 0.0045 / 6 = 0.061. This is far below 1, so there is **no thermal runaway at the device level**. Board-level heating (Section 3) is the binding constraint.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The synchronous low-side body diode conducts during dead time, then recovers when the high-side turns on.
