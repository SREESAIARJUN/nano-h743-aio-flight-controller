## 0. Baseline Assumptions

Job #75 has no released spec, so this design is built on the assumptions below. Change any of them and the numbers in Sections 1–3 change with it. Every equation is written so you can substitute your own values.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Switching loss scales linearly with V |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor, ≤ 5 s | Continuous rating is derived in §3 |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) 6-step | AM32-style trapezoidal drive |
| MOSFET | 40 V, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C (V_GS = 10 V), Q_g = 60 nC, PQFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | Representative of current-generation 40 V trench parts |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver, one per motor | Integrated bootstrap diodes |
| ESC MCUs | 4 × AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 | See architecture note below |
| Current sense | Low-side shunt, 0.25 mΩ, per motor | |
| PCB | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8 layers, 2 oz outer and inner power planes | |
| FC MCU | STM32H743 @ 400 MHz, timer kernel clock 200 MHz | ArduPilot ChibiOS HAL |

**Architecture note.** The H743 does not commutate the motors itself. Running four sensorless 6-step or FOC loops plus ArduPilot on a single H7 is not a supported ArduPilot configuration. The correct design is:

- The H743 acts as the FC and sends bidirectional DShot600 commands.
- Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32) are co-located on the same PCB.
- Each ESC MCU drives its own gate driver.

Everything below follows that partition.

**Feasibility flag.** 4 × 80 A means 320 A of total burst current on a "nano" footprint. This is achievable only as a short burst rating. Section 3 shows that the board's thermal mass, not the FETs, is the limiting factor.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 135 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} \approx 1.0 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 110) \approx 1.5\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, current flows through one phase in and one phase out.

- **Driven leg (PWM'd):**
  - the high side conducts for fraction D;
  - the synchronous low side conducts for fraction (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the low side is held on for the entire sector.

At any instant, exactly two channels carry the full phase current I. Ignoring the dead-time fraction, which is treated separately below, the per-motor conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

**Per-FET distribution.**

- Each FET position is active for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle.
- Average per FET = P_cond,motor / 6.
- The worst case is a low-side FET. It sees its own 120° return conduction plus synchronous freewheel when its leg is driven at low duty, but the sum across all FETs is invariant.
