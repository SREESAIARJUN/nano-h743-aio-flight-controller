# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC Stack (Job #63): Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, DShot DMA Plan and Netlists

## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

**The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU produces the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 flight controller sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The two domains are modeled separately below.

| Parameter | Value / Assumption |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Per-channel burst current | 80 A (≤ 5 s); continuous rating derived in §3 |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal |
| MOSFET (per switch, 1 device) | 40 V N-ch, 5×6 PQFN, R_DS(on),25 = 0.9 mΩ @ 10 V, Q_g = 60 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_oss ≈ 40 nC @ 25 V, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, V_SD ≈ 0.85 V @ 80 A, R_θJC = 1.2 K/W |
| Dead time | 150 ns per edge |
| Gate driver | 3-phase bootstrap driver, ~1 A source/sink effective, 10 V gate drive |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power planes |

**Form factor flag:** if "nano" means 20×20 mm, 80 A burst is not physically supportable. The heat-capacity analysis in §3 shows why. 30.5×30.5 mm is the minimum credible footprint.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous operation

At any instant, two phase legs carry the motor current I:
- **Active leg:** high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) alternate at 48 kHz with duty D. Current flows through one of them at every instant.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is continuously ON.

Because of synchronous rectification, the current always flows through exactly two channels' worth of R_DS(on) (the body diode conducts only during dead time).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.3 RMS current including ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2 L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH line-to-line (7.5 µH per phase in series = 15 µH loop), D = 0.5, V_bus − V_bemf = 12 V.

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{12 \times 0.5}{15\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 8.3\,A$$

The ripple term ΔI²/12 = 5.8 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². So I_rms ≈ I.

### 1.4 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu,ch}$$

Here R_Cu,ch is the PCB plus solder path from the bus through two legs to the motor pads. For a 30.5 mm board with 2 oz planes, a typical value is about 0.35 mΩ.
