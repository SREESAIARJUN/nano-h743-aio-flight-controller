# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Nano: 80 A Burst Power-Stage and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

**Job #23 · Rev A (pre-layout analysis)**

---

## 0. Executive Finding

**A 20×20 mm AIO cannot sustain 80 A per channel.** It can survive an 80 A *burst* of roughly **1–2 s**, starting from a 40 °C board, before FET junctions approach 150 °C. The model below derives this.

The defensible ratings are:
- **80 A for ≤1 s** on 20×20
- **≈35–40 A continuous** under prop wash

If marketing requires "80 A burst" with any margin, go to 25.5×25.5 or 30.5×30.5 and use dual FETs per switch.

Every number in this document derives from the stated assumptions in §1. Replace them with your chosen MOSFET and driver datasheet values before tape-out.
