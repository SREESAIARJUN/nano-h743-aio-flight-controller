# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

> **Assumptions.** I don't have the Job #11 spec sheet, so this design uses typical gimbal values. Replace every constant marked `// TUNE` or `// HW` with your actual values.
>
> - **MCU:** STM32G4 / H7 class with FPU, CORDIC optional.
> - **Motor:** 12N14P gimbal BLDC (pole pairs p = 7), roughly 10 Ω and 2 mH phase-to-neutral.
> - **Encoder:** 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701).
> - **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P on SPI, both on the camera plate.
> - **Current sensing:** low-side shunts.

## 1. Architecture and Timing

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth target | Feedback source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q) PI | 20 kHz (PWM-synchronous) | ~1.5 kHz | Shunts + encoder θₑ |
| Velocity PI + cogging FF | 4 kHz | ~150 Hz | Fused, notched gyro rate (inertial) |
| Angle P (+ rate FF) | 1 kHz | ~25 Hz | Attitude estimator (Mahony on fused IMU) |
| IMU sampling | 8 kHz ODR, both IMUs | — | — |

Each loop should have roughly 5–10× less bandwidth than the loop inside it. This lets each inner loop be treated as unity gain when tuning the next loop out.

Signal flow per axis:

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle P]─ω_ref─►[Vel PI]─i_q,fb─►(+)─i_q,ref─►[Current PI d/q]─►[SVPWM]─►Motor
             ▲               ▲              ▲
       θ̂ (Mahony)     ω_gyro (notched)   i_q,cog(θ_m)  ◄─ anti-cogging (LMS)
```

## 2. FOC Current Loop Equations

### 2.1 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

### 2.2 Park transform (θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset)

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.3 Plant model in the rotor frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 2.4 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$
$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Pole-zero cancellation tuning gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

where the controller is C(s) = K_p + K_i/s. Keep ω_c ≤ 2π·f_PWM/10 to stay clear of the PWM and sampling delay.

### 2.5 Voltage limit and anti-windup

Saturate the voltage vector magnitude:

$$
|v^*| \le \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}
$$

This is the SVPWM linear-region limit. Give d-axis priority, then let q use the remaining headroom:

$$
v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

Anti-windup uses back-calculation. The integrator update is:

$$
x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + T_s\big(K_i e[k] + K_{aw}(u_{sat}-u)\big)
$$

with K_aw ≈ K_i/K_p.

### 2.6 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

Phase voltages:

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad
v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad
v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

Inject the zero-sequence term and compute duty cycles:

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$

### 2.7 Delay compensation

Advance the angle used for the inverse Park by 1.5 sample periods to cover computation plus PWM delay:

$$
\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

## 3. Cascaded Velocity and Angle Control

### 3.1 Mechanical plant (per axis)

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}
$$

$$
K_t = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m
$$

### 3.2 Velocity PI

Use set-point weighting (β) to reduce overshoot, and a first-order low-pass on the output to attenuate residual gyro noise:

$$
i_{q,fb}^* = K_{pv}\,(\beta\,\omega^* - \omega) + K_{iv}\!\int(\omega^*-\omega)\,dt
$$

Tuning for crossover ω_v (with the current loop ≈ 1):

$$
K_{pv} = \frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_{iv} = K_{pv}\,\frac{\omega_v}{4}
$$

This places the PI zero a factor of 4 below crossover, giving about 76° phase margin before delays.
