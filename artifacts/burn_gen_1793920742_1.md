# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

I don't have earlier context for "Job #2", so this document states every assumption explicitly. Replace the bracketed parameters with your actual BOM values and the equations still hold.

An STM32H743 is not normally the ESC controller. The standard architecture, assumed here, is:

- **FC board:** STM32H743, runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **ESC board:** four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware). Each MCU drives three half-bridges.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.2 × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

**Current path.** With synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two FETs in series. During the PWM on-time the path is HS(phase A) and LS(phase B). During the off-time it is LS(phase A) and LS(phase B).

P_cond,motor = 2 · I_rms² · R_ds(T_j)

The ripple contribution is ΔI²/12, which is negligible when L_motor·f_sw ≫ R. So I_rms ≈ I_pk = 80 A:

P_cond,motor = 2 × 6400 × 1.74 mΩ = **22.3 W**

**Per-FET distribution.** Each phase is high for 120° and low for 120°. Over a 360° electrical cycle:

- HS conducts D/3 of the time.
- LS conducts 1/3 + (1−D)/3 of the time.

At burst (D ≈ 1), each of the 6 FETs conducts about 1/3 of the time:

P_cond,FET ≈ I²R/3 = **3.7 W per FET**

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only one leg hard-switches at a time (the PWM'd phase).

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 24 · 80 · 35 ns · 48 kHz | 1.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 0.046 W |
| C_oss | ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw | 0.014 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 0.038 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.307 W |
| **Total P_sw, motor** | | **≈ 2.0 W** |

Two notes on this result:

- Switching loss scales roughly linearly with I and f_sw. Moving from 24 kHz to 48 kHz roughly doubles it, but conduction dominates (about 11:1).
- Switching loss rotates among the three HS FETs. Each therefore sees about 1.61/3 ≈ 0.54 W average, plus diode loss on its complementary LS.

**Per-motor total:** 22.3 + 2.0 ≈ **24.3 W**
**Hottest FET:** 3.7 + 0.54 + ~0.1 ≈ **4.4 W**

---

## 3. Board-Level Losses

**Power stage, four motors:** 4 × 24.3 = **97 W**

**Bus copper.** At full throttle, I_batt ≈ D·ΣI ≈ 320 A burst.

- With R_bus ≈ 0.5 mΩ (pads, planes, connector): P = 320² × 0.5 mΩ ≈ **51 W**.
- **Design flag:** 320 A through a nano-format XT60 and 12 AWG lead is beyond rating. Realistic simultaneous 4×80 A is rare (80 A is per-motor transient), but the bus must be designed for it, or ArduPilot must cap it (BATT_*_CURR limits, `MOT_BAT_CURR_MAX`).

**Shunt (if 0.25 mΩ per motor):** 4 × 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = 6.4 W.

**Worst-case total:** P_tot ≈ 97 + 6 (+ up to 51 bus) ≈ **103–154 W**
