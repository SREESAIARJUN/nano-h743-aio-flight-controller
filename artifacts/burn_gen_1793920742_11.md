## 0. Architecture Assumptions

These assumptions are stated up front because the requested scope ("H743 4-in-1 ESC") is ambiguous. ArduPilot does not commutate motors, so the H743 cannot drive gates directly. The only workable partition is:

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐      4× bidir DShot600       ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
│  FC: STM32H743VIH6 @ 480MHz │ ───────────────────────────► │ 4-in-1 ESC: 4× STM32G071 (AM32)   │
│  ArduPilot (ChibiOS)        │ ◄─────── eRPM (GCR) ──────── │ 4× FD6288Q gate driver            │
│  30.5×30.5 or 20×20 stack   │      VBAT / CURR / GND       │ 24× N-MOSFET, 6S (25.2V max)      │
└─────────────────────────────┘                               └───────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | 4S-6S design |
| Burst current | 80 A phase current per motor, ≤ 5 s | Worst case for FETs; I_phase ≥ I_bus |

| Parameter | Value |

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (Six-Step, Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Current path

In trapezoidal commutation, two phases are active and one floats in each 60° sector. With synchronous rectification the motor current always flows through **exactly two channels**:

- the high-side (HS) FET of phase A for fraction D, or
- the low-side (LS) FET of phase A for fraction (1–D) during synchronous rectification,
- in series with the LS FET of phase B, which is fully on.

This gives an instantaneous loop resistance of 2·R_DS(on),hot, independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R_{DS} = 0.75 \times 1.6 = 1.20\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Per-motor and per-FET conduction loss

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.2\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.36\ W}
$$

Averaged over the six FETs of one bridge across an electrical revolution:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \mathbf{2.56\ W}
$$

**Worst-case device.** The LS FET in its unswitched 120° sector dissipates I²R = 7.68 W instantaneously. Electrical frequency is several hundred Hz to kHz, which is much faster than the junction thermal time constant (~5–20 ms). The average value is therefore the correct number for T_j, but with a ripple of a few kelvin.

### 1.4 PCB copper loss (frequently ignored, not negligible)

Assume a 2 oz (70 µm) internal layer, 6 mm wide, 15 mm effective length per phase path, ×2 in parallel layers:

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{wt} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.015}{0.006\cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 2} \approx 0.31\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Doubling for the current path and correcting to +40 % at 100 °C gives about 0.87 mΩ:

$$
P_{Cu,motor} \approx 80^2 \times 0.87\ \text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{5.6\ W}
$$

There is also the current shunt (0.25 mΩ, carrying bus current, which is ≤ phase current). At the 80 A worst case:

$$
P_{shunt} = 80^2 \times 0.25\ \text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{1.6\ W}
$$
