## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step bridge with synchronous (complementary) PWM

At every instant, exactly one high-side and one low-side path conduct. During PWM-off, current freewheels through the complementary FET rather than the body diode, so the current path always contains two R_DS(on):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2(80)^2(1.6\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. The duty is split between the high-side and low-side FETs according to D. Averaged over a full electrical revolution:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6} = \mathbf{3.4\ W}$$

The worst-case instantaneous FET is the high side at D → 1, which carries the full 1/3 window at 100 %. Use **≈ 6.8 W peak-window** for junction ripple checks.

### 1.4 RMS sanity check

$$I_{FET,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{1/3} = 46.2\ \text{A}$$

$$P = I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS} = 3.4\ \text{W}$$

This matches section 1.3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches hard. The other active leg is static for that 60° sector.

### 2.1 Gate drive currents (Miller plateau)

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{DRV}-V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{10-3}{2+2.2+1}=1.35\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{sink}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{3}{4.2}=0.71\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{on}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}}=\frac{13\text{ nC}}{1.35}=9.6\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{off}=\frac{13\text{ nC}}{0.71}=18.3\ \text{ns}$$
