## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture corrections

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The design is therefore a stack: an FC board (H743) plus a 4-in-1 ESC board with 4 ESC MCUs.
2. **"Nano" and 80 A burst conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A through one FET per switch with acceptable copper loss. This design assumes:
   - a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board,
   - 8-layer construction with 2 oz copper,
   - a bonded aluminium heat spreader.

   The FC board can stay 20×20 mm.
