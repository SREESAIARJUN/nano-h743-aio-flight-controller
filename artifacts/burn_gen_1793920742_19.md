## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Several premises in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful.

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| The STM32H743 cannot directly run 4× sensored or sensorless FOC/6-step commutation and also run ArduPilot. | **Two-tier architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32) handle commutation. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600. |
| 80A *continuous* on a "nano" (20×20 mm) footprint is physically impossible. | The ESC stage is specified on a **30.5×30.5 mm** mounting pattern. 80A is a **burst** rating, valid for about 2 s as derived in §3.5. The continuous rating is derived, not marketed. |
| MOSFET parameters are not given. | A representative 40V DFN5×6 part is assumed (parameters in §1). **Substitute your selected part's datasheet values into every equation.** |
| Bus voltage | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (4.2 V/cell). Derating is evaluated at 25.2 V. |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_J) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_J - 25)]$$

At the T_J = 125 °C design point:

$$R_{DS}(125) = 0.90 \times (1 + 0.006\times100) = 1.44\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd high, one phase is held low, and one floats. AM32 uses complementary PWM, so the low-side of the PWM'd leg conducts during the off-time instead of its body diode.

At any instant the motor current flows through **exactly two FETs**:

- **PWM on:** HS(A) and LS(B)
- **PWM off:** LS(A) and LS(B)

The total channel conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_J)}$$

Per-device split over one electrical revolution (each leg is active for 2/3 of the period, 1/3 as the high side and 1/3 as the low side):

$$P_{HS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}\,D}{3},\qquad P_{LS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}\,(1 + (1-D))}{3}$$

Check: 3(P_HS + P_LS) = I²R(D + 2 − D) = 2I²R ✓

### 2.3 Numerical evaluation at 80A

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond,ch | 2 × 80² × 1.44 mΩ | **18.43 W** |
| Average per FET | 18.43 / 6 | 3.07 W |
| Worst LS FET (D → 0.5) | 80² × 1.44m × 1.5/3 | **4.61 W** |
| Worst HS FET (D = 1) | 80² × 1.44m × 1/3 | 3.07 W |

**Note on the D-dependence.** At part throttle the low-side FETs carry more loss. A worst-case burst at D ≈ 0.5 with 80A requires a stalled or heavily loaded rotor. This is plausible during punch-outs at low airspeed.
