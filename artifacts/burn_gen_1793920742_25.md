## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

**The H743 is the flight controller, not the motor commutator.** In a conventional 4-in-1 design, the ESC board carries four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The 48 kHz PWM and the six-step commutation run on those ESC MCUs. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to them.

Driving 24 FETs directly from the H743 is not practical. It has only two advanced timers with dead-time (TIM1 and TIM8) plus HRTIM, and it would starve ArduPilot of CPU. The model below therefore assumes the conventional split.
