## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET of the same leg conducts (synchronous rectification) for (1−D) minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the sector.

The instantaneous conduction loss per motor is therefore:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{HS}(T_j)\,D + I^2 R_{LS}(T_j)\,(1-D-2t_{dt}f_{sw}) + I^2 R_{LS,ret}(T_j)
$$

With matched FETs and t_dt·f_sw ≪ 1:

$$
P_{cond} \approx 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Numbers

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per motor | P_cond × 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.1 W | 68.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.2 W |

Add copper and shunt losses:

- **Phase/bus copper:** about 0.3 mΩ of path per motor on 2 oz 8-layer. At 80², this gives about 1.9 W per motor.
- **Shunt:** a 0.25 mΩ shunt per motor gives 1.6 W. Remove this term if the design uses R_DS sensing.

### 1.3 Distribution per FET

Over one electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries current for about 1/3 of the time. At burst RPM (e.g. 14-pole motor at 30k RPM, f_e = 3.5 kHz, period 286 µs), the die thermal time constant (~ms) averages these pulses:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} \approx 6.2\ \text{W at } 125^\circ\text{C}
$$

The worst-case instantaneous value is 9.3 W, for the LS FET in its return-leg sector.

### 1.4 Thermal-runaway stability criterion

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_{th,ja,eff} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 2I^2R_0\alpha\,R_{th,eff} < 1
$$

At 80 A: 2·6400·1e-3·0.0045 = 0.0576 W/K per motor. Stability therefore requires R_th,eff < 17 K/W per motor-equivalent node. This is easily met on copper. **Runaway is not the limit. Total energy into the board's heat capacity is.**
