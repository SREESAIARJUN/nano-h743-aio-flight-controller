## 1. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly two legs carry the phase current at any instant:

- **During the ON interval (D·T):** one high-side FET (leg A) plus one low-side FET (leg B).
- **During the OFF interval ((1−D)·T):** the leg-A low-side FET (synchronous freewheel) plus the leg-B low-side FET.

Current therefore always flows through **two** R_DS(on) in series, independent of duty cycle (neglecting dead-time intervals).

### 1.2 Conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on),hot}$$

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{20.5\ W/channel}$$

Per-FET distribution (6 FETs, each conducting roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle):

- Instantaneous loss while conducting: 80² × 1.6 mΩ = **10.2 W**
- Time-averaged loss: about **3.4 W per FET**

### 1.3 Switching loss (hard-switched leg only)

Only one leg is PWM-switched at a time.

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

From first principles, the plateau gate current is:

$$I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{10 - 3.2}{2.2 + 1.0} \approx 2.1\,\text{A}$$

This gives:

$$t_{Miller} = \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} \approx 3.8\,\text{ns}$$

That is the ideal value. 15 ns is a realistic derating once you include common-source inductance and the driver's current limits. **Your switching loss scales linearly with this number.** Layout is the lever.

### 1.4 Dead-time (body diode) conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
