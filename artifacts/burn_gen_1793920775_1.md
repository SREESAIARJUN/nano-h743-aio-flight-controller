## 1. Power-Stage Topology Recap

Each motor uses one three-phase bridge: 3 half-bridges, 6 FETs. The board has 4 motors, so 24 FETs.

In 6-step synchronous mode, during any 60° electrical sector:

- **Phase X (PWM leg):** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The complementary low side (LS) conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous freewheel.
- **Phase Y (return leg):** the LS is held on for 100% of the sector.
- **Phase Z:** floating, used for back-EMF sensing.

**Key result:** with synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** channel resistances. The duty cycle D does not change the conduction loss, only which FET carries it.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

| T_j | R_ds(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.65 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.80 mΩ |

Because R_ds(on) rises with T_j, the loss and temperature equations are coupled. Iterate (§4.4).

### 2.2 Per-motor conduction loss

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

- **Copper path (R_Cu):** the phase trace plus the bus plane share per motor. At 2 oz and 8 layers this is about 0.4 mΩ.
- **Ripple correction:** at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance (~20–50 µH), the ripple ΔI/I is under 10%. The RMS correction factor, 1 + (ΔI/I)²/12, is below 1.001 and can be ignored.

At I = 80 A and T_j = 100 °C:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.65\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = \mathbf{21.1\ W}$$

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.4\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = \mathbf{2.6\ W}$$

### 2.3 Distribution across the six FETs

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the return leg, and 1/3 floating.

- **HS FET:** conducts D/3 of the time.
- **LS FET:** conducts 1/3 (return) + (1−D)/3 (freewheel).

**Electrical vs. thermal timescales.** A 14-pole motor at 25 krpm has an electrical frequency of about 2.9 kHz, a period of about 0.34 ms. The FET junction-to-case thermal time constant is several ms or more. So each FET sees its time-averaged loss:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3} I^2 R,\qquad P_{LS} = \frac{2-D}{3} I^2 R$$

At full burst (D → 1), and with T_j = 100 °C so that I²R = 6400 × 1.65 mΩ = 10.56 W:

| FET | Conduction loss |
|---|---|
| HS | 10.56 / 3 = 3.5 W |
| LS | 10.56 / 3 = 3.5 W |

Losses are balanced at full throttle.

**Partial throttle (D ≈ 0.5):**

| FET | Conduction loss |
|---|---|
| HS | 0.5 / 3 × 10.56 = 1.8 W |
| LS | 1.5 / 3 × 10.56 = 5.
