## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

ArduPilot on the STM32H743 does not commutate motors. The H743 is the flight controller. It emits bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (or one quad-ESC MCU) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, and those MCUs drive the gate drivers and the 24 MOSFETs. All power-stage math below therefore applies to the ESC side, and all DMA/timer math applies to the H743 side.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path under 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant two phases conduct:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET conducts synchronously for (1 − D).
- **Leg B:** the low-side FET is continuously on.

The current therefore always flows through exactly two channel resistances plus copper, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2\left(2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{cu}\right)$$

Temperature dependence of on-resistance:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0063\,/^\circ\text{C}$$

This gives roughly 1.47× at 100 °C. Because hotter FETs have higher resistance and dissipate more, the thermal solution must be iterated. The model below is evaluated directly at the hot value.

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{FET,cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.10\text{ m}\Omega = 14.08\text{ W}$$

$$P_{cu} = 80^2 \times 0.4\text{ m}\Omega = 2.56\text{ W}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} \approx 16.6\text{ W per channel}$$

### 1.3 Distribution across the six FETs of one channel

Over one electrical cycle each leg spends 1/3 of the time as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the constant-on low side, and 1/3 floating.

| FET | Conduction fraction | Average power at D = 0.8 |
|---|---|---|
| High side | D/3 | 0.267 · I²R = 1.88 W |
| Low side | (1−D)/3 + 1/3 | 0.400 · I²R = 2.82 W ← hottest |

The electrical frequency (kHz range) is far above the junction thermal time constant (ms), so these averages are valid for the thermal analysis.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The high-side FET takes the V·I overlap loss. The synchronous low-side FET switches at near-zero voltage (body-diode clamped), but it incurs Q_rr and dead-time losses.

### 2.1 Gate-current-limited transition times

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_g} = \frac{10-3}{6.2} \approx 1.13\text{ A},\qquad t_{on}=\frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}}\approx 11.5\text{ ns}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{plateau}}{R_g}\approx 0.48\text{ A}\;(\text{or }\sim 1\text{ A with a turn-off diode or low-}R\text{ sink}),\quad t_{off}\approx 13\text{ ns (with sink-assist)}$$

Without the turn-off diode, t_off rises to about 27 ns. **Fit an anti-parallel Schottky across R_g (or use a split R_g,on / R_g,off).**

### 2.2 Loss terms

| Term | Formula | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V_bus·I·(t_on + t_off)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·24.5 ns·48 k = **1.19 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V_b
