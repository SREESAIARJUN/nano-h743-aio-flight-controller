## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In every ArduPilot-compatible AIO (all-in-one) design, the work is split this way:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot. Emits bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4 or 1×quad) | e.g. AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, or EFM8/BB51 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay | Commutation. Generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. |
