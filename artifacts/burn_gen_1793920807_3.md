# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #77): Power-Stage and Bidirectional DShot Design Model

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. Trying to commutate four motors from the H743 while it also runs the EKF is not viable on ArduPilot.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step commutation, two half-bridges carry the phase current at any moment:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET conducts for 1−D through synchronous rectification.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is on continuously.

The current therefore always flows through exactly two FETs in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Add the ripple component to the RMS current:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

Here ΔI_pp is the phase current ripple, set by motor inductance at 48 kHz. It is typically less than 5% of I at 80 A, so this term is under 0.1% and is dropped.

**At T_j = 110 °C:** R_ds(on) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **1.47 mΩ**

$$P_{cond}= 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.47\text{m} = \mathbf{18.8\ W}\ \text{per ESC channel}$$

**PCB copper loss.** Assume about 0.5 mΩ total current path per channel (pads, vias, phase pads, shared bus):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.5\text{m} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

**Electrothermal feedback.** Because R_ds(on) depends on T_j, iterate until T_j converges:

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}\,Z_{th,jb}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches.

### 2.1 Voltage-current overlap

Turn-on gate current:

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{drv,on}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{10-3}{3+2.2+1}\approx1.13\ \text{A}$$

Turn-off gate current:

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv,off}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{3}{1.5+3.2}\approx0.64\ \text{A}$$

Switching times:

$$t_{on}=\frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}}\approx10.6\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off}=\frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}}\approx18.8\ \text{ns}$$

Overlap loss:

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I\,(t_{on}+t_{off})\,f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(29.4\text{n})(48\text{k})=\mathbf{1.42\ W}$$
