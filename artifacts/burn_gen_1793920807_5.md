# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #73): Power Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Plan

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points affect everything downstream:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC microcontrollers, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC firmware parameter, not an H743 timer.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is a time-limited rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal model in §3 shows a realistic continuous rating of about 25–30 A per channel under prop-wash cooling. 80 A is sustainable for only a few seconds.
3. **All MOSFET and driver parameters below are representative placeholders.** They are typical of a 40 V, 3×3 mm PDFN device. Substitute datasheet values for the selected part before any release. The equations are exact; the numbers are only as good as the inputs.

## 2. Loss Model (per channel)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

With complementary PWM, current flows continuously through either the high-side (HS) or low-side (LS) FET of each active leg. Two legs are active at any instant.

For one leg:

$$P_{leg,inst} = D\,I^2R_{ds} + (1-D)\,I^2R_{ds} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

For one motor channel:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2(80)^2(2.2\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{28.2\ W}$$

Each phase leg is active for 120° of every 180° (2/3 of the time), so:

- Average per leg: (2/3)(14.1) = **9.4 W**
- Split within the leg at full throttle (D ≈ 0.95): the HS FET takes about 8.9 W and the LS FET about 0.5 W

Temperature dependence, with α ≈ 0.005 K⁻¹:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

This creates a positive feedback loop: higher temperature raises R_DS(on), which raises loss, which raises temperature. That is why the thermal solution in §3 must be iterated. The value of 2.2 mΩ is the converged result.

### 2.2 Dead-time (body-diode) conduction

Only the PWMed leg incurs this loss. There are two dead-time intervals per switching period:

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_f\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(100\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
