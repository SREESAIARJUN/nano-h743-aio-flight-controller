## 0. Architecture Reality Check (read first)

| Issue | Finding | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| "Nano" form factor vs 80 A × 4 | Twenty‑four 5×6 mm MOSFETs plus a driver and MCU do not fit on 20×20 mm. | **FC: 20×20 mm (M3 holes). ESC: 30.5×30.5 mm stacked**, or a 25.5 mm whoop stack with 3×3 mm FETs at a reduced rating. |
| 80 A rating | Thermally achievable only as a **burst** (seconds). The derivation below gives about **25–30 A continuous** per channel with prop‑wash cooling. | Rate as "80 A burst (≤5 s) / 30 A continuous". |
| Who switches the FETs | The H743 running ArduPilot does **not** drive the bridge. Each ESC has its own MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class) that does the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. | Two separate firmware domains, one 8‑pin interface. |
| Pack current | 4 × 80 A = 320 A from 6S, which exceeds XT60 (≈60 A continuous) and most packs. | All four channels at 80 A at once is a worst case for thermal sizing, not a normal operating point. |
