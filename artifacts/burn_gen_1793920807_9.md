# Job #54: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst) Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In an ArduPilot system the partition is:

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, sends bidirectional DShot600 commands |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 class MCUs | AM32 / BLHeli_32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Power stage | 24 N-ch MOSFETs, 4 three-phase gate drivers | n/a | 80 A burst per motor |
