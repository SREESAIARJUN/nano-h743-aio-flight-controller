## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 PMSM Model in the dq Frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Electromagnetic torque (SPM machine, L_d = L_q):

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

Apply the decoupling feedforward terms:

$$
v_d^* = u_d - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = u_q + \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

With these terms, each axis reduces to the plant 1/(Ls + R).

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole:

$$
C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\quad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

The closed loop then becomes first order:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

**Rules for ω_c**

- Keep ω_c ≤ 2π f_pwm / 10.
- The 1.5-sample computation and PWM delay adds a phase lag of approximately −1.5·ω_c·T_s.

\sqrt{v_d^2+v_q^2} \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}
$$
