# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the Job #52 spec sheet, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every hardware-dependent constant is a named parameter, so you can substitute your real values without changing the structure.

---

## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class: Cortex-M4F/M7, single-precision FPU, about 170 MHz or more |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH, inline low-side current sensing on 2 phases |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701) on each joint |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps, ±16 g |
| Joint order | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |

### Loop rates

| Loop | Rate | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ADC injected-conversion ISR, center-aligned PWM |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | Every 5th FOC tick |
| IMU acquisition and filtering | 8 kHz | IMU DRDY (data ready), via DMA |
| Attitude and angle loop | 1 kHz | Decimated from the IMU path |

Bandwidth targets, each loop ≥ 4× slower than the one inside it:

- Current loop: ω_c = 2π·2000 rad/s
- Velocity loop: ω_v = 2π·150 rad/s
- Angle loop: ω_θ = 2π·30 rad/s

## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})\big)
$$

θ_off is found at calibration by applying a d-axis voltage V_d with θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Assuming balanced phases (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Torque:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.4 PI current control with decoupling

Pole-zero cancellation gives first-order closed-loop dynamics with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Here i_d* = 0. The decoupling terms are small at gimbal speeds but are cheap to include.
