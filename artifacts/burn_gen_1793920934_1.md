## 1. Field-Oriented Control (innermost loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ₀ is the encoder offset, found by aligning the rotor with a d-axis voltage vector at startup.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform, assuming balanced currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant and current PI design

The dq-frame model is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Tuning by pole-zero cancellation.** Choose the target current bandwidth ω_c, typically ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, which must be no more than about f_PWM/10. Then:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

This makes the closed loop i/i* = ω_c/(s+ω_c).
