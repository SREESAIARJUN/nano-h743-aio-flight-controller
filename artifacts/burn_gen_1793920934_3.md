## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R_s ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 3 mH, low-Kv |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), read over SPI each current cycle |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7 class with FPU (and CORDIC on the G4) |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, on separate SPI buses, FIFO high-res mode |
| Bus voltage | 12–16 V |

The control loops run at different rates:

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ~1.5 kHz |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | 250 µs | ~150 Hz |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | 1 ms | ~25 Hz |
| IMU sampling | 8 kHz ODR, decimated ×2 to 4 kHz | — | — |

Two rules govern this layout:

- **Bandwidth separation:** keep a ratio of at least 5–10× between nested loops.
- **Notch placement:** every notch must sit at least 3× above the velocity crossover, or its phase lag will erode phase margin.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

The Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0) is:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

The Park transform uses the electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_0) mod 2π:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 PMSM Model and Torque

$$
v_d = R_s i_d + L_d\dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R_s i_q + L_q\dot i_q + \omega_e(L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q)i_d i_q\right] \;\xrightarrow{\,L_d\approx L_q\,}\; K_t\, i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling

Placing the PI zero on the electrical pole R/L makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

The decoupled voltage commands are:

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L_d i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

Discretize with forward Euler, using integrator gain K_i·T_s. Set i_d* = 0. Gimbal motors are often run in voltage mode when no current shunts are fitted; §6 supports both modes.
