# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the original specification for Job #42, so this design is built on the assumptions listed in §0. The equations are written so that changing a parameter does not change the structure. Any value marked **[VERIFY]** must be checked against your motor or IMU datasheet before you flash hardware.

## 0. Assumptions and System Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs *p* = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (e.g. AS5048A / MT6701) |
| Bridge | 3-phase, center-aligned PWM, 2 low-side shunts, V<sub>dc</sub> = 12–16 V |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, gyro + accel ODR = 8 kHz |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (single-precision FPU) |

### Loop rates

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Closed-loop bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| IMU acquisition + filtering | 8 kHz | 125 µs | — |
| Velocity (rate) | 4 kHz | 250 µs | ~100–150 Hz |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | 1 ms | ~25–40 Hz |

## 1. FOC Current Loop (innermost)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform** (with i<sub>a</sub> + i<sub>b</sub> + i<sub>c</sub> = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ<sub>e</sub> = p·θ<sub>m</sub> − θ<sub>offset</sub>:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this rotation matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and torque

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\l
