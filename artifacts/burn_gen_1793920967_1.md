| Item | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 170+ MHz | Single-precision float throughout |

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 1.5–2.5 kHz |

**Bandwidth separation rule:** keep each outer loop at least 4–5× slower than the loop inside it. Each outer loop must also stay below the dominant structural resonance unless that resonance is notched.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

$$
\theta_e = \mathrm{wrap}_{2\pi}\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})\big)
$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: apply v_d > 0 with v_q = 0, let the rotor align, and record θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform (Amplitude-Invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}} \qquad (\text{assumes } i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)
$$

### 1.3 Park Transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 dq Plant Model (Surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with Decoupling and Pole-Zero Cancellation

Choose the gains so the PI zero cancels the electrical pole R/L. This gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

The closed loop is i/i* = ω_c/(s + ω_c).

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
