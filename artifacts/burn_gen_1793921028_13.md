## 1. Power-Stage Assumptions

The supply is 6S LiPo, using a design voltage of V_bus = 25.2 V (fully charged). This requires **40 V MOSFETs**, because 30 V parts lack ringing margin on 6S.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value (representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN) |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance @25 °C, V_GS=10 V | R_ds25 | 1.5 mΩ |
| R_ds temp coefficient | α | 0.005 /K |
| Gate charge total | Q_g | 30 nC |
| Switching charge (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) | Q_sw | 8 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Junction–case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |
| Gate driver effective current (with R_g) | I_g | 0.5 A |
| Dead time | t_d | 200 ns |
| Gate drive voltage | V_GS | 10 V |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |

The commutation scheme is 6-step trapezoidal with complementary PWM (synchronous rectification), as in AM32. At any instant two phase legs carry current:
- one low-side FET is held ON;
- one leg is PWM-switching, with the high side on for D and the low side for (1−D).

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Per-motor conduction loss

Ignoring dead time, the current always passes through two FET channels in series, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

PCB copper path resistance (2 oz, nano layout, bus to phase pad and back) is taken as R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ.

At I_ph = 80 A:
- FET portion: 2 × 6400 × 2.25 mΩ = **28.8 W**
- Copper portion: 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**
- Total ≈ **32.0 W per motor**

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, either as the held low side or as the PWM leg. The average per FET is about P_FET ≈ 28.8/6 ≈ **4.8 W**, with peaks while conducting of I²R = 14.4 W.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg switches per commutation step.

### 3.1 V–I overlap

$$t_{sw} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g} = \frac{8\,\text{nC}}{0.5\,\text{A}} = 16\,\text{ns per edge}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 32\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 1.55\ \text{W}$$

### 3.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two transitions per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I_{ph}\,(2t_d)\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 1.23\ \text{W}$$

This is almost as large as the overlap loss. **Trimming dead time to about 100 ns, if the driver allows it, saves about 0.6 W per motor.**

### 3.3 Reverse recovery and Coss

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.018\ \text{W}$$
