# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Power-Stage and DShot Design

**Scope note.** The request doesn't specify the MOSFET, gate driver, board size, cell count, or cooling. I have chosen representative parts and stated every assumption, so all numbers can be recomputed when you substitute real datasheet values. Treat the outputs as a **design model to be validated by thermal imaging and a burst-duration test**, not as certified ratings.

---

## 0. Architecture Sanity Checks

These are the risks that most change the design.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every practical 4-in-1 stack, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs. The model below follows that partition.

2. **"80 A burst" must be qualified as per-channel, non-simultaneous.**
   - Four channels at 80 A is 320 A from the pack.
   - An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous, and a 30×30 board's battery pads and polygon cannot carry 320 A.
   - I model **80 A on one channel**, with **all four channels at 80 A only as a sub-second worst case**.

3. **Form factor.** 80 A per channel is not credible on a 20×20 board. I assume **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz copper**, with 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 DFN FETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step with Complementary PWM

At any commutation step, exactly two phases are active:
- the PWM'd phase, where the HS FET conducts for D and its LS FET conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier;
- the return phase, where the LS FET is fully on.

So the motor current always flows through **exactly two FETs**, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 0.75 × 1.45 ≈ **1.09 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.09\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.9\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution (Worst Device)

Over one electrical revolution, each phase is:
- PWM'd for 1/3 of the time,
- the return path for 1/3,
- floating for 1/3.

**LS FET:**
$$\bar P_{LS} = I^2R\left[\tfrac13 + \tfrac{1-D}{3}\right]$$

**HS FET:**
$$\bar P_{HS} = I^2R\,\tfrac{D}{3}$$

The worst case is an LS FET at low duty (D → 0), giving (2/3)·I²R:

$$P_{LS,max} = \tfrac23 \times 6400 \times 1.09\text{ m} = \mathbf{4.65\ W}$$

At realistic burst duty (D ≈ 0.9), the HS and LS losses are about 2.1 W and 2.6 W respectively.

### 1.4 Copper and Shunt Losses

- Phase pad, polygon, and via path: about 0.4 mΩ round trip, giving 80² × 0.4 m = **2.6 W** per channel.
- Bus shunt (0.2 mΩ, shared): **1.3 W** at 80 A total.
