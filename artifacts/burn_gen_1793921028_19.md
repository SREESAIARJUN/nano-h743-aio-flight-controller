## 0. System Architecture and Assumptions

**Architectural note.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class) running a 48 kHz PWM.

The losses in Sections 1–3 occur in the **ESC power stage**. The DMA allocation in Section 5 belongs to the **H743**. The two are coupled only by four signal lines, telemetry, current sense and the shared power and thermal envelope.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two FETs are always in the current path:

- During PWM-ON, current flows through HS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B).
- During PWM-OFF, it freewheels through LS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B).

The series resistance is therefore always 2·R_DS(on), independent of duty cycle (dead time excluded):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot \left(2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\right)$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\mathrm{K} \;\Rightarrow\; k_T(125^\circ\mathrm{C}) = 1.6$$

### 1.2 Numerical results at 80 A

| Condition | R_DS(on) each | 2·R_DS | P_FET per motor |
|---|---|---|---|
| T_j = 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2.0 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| T_j = 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 2.9 mΩ | 18.6 W |
| T_j = 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | 3.2 mΩ | **20.5 W** |

PCB copper adds more. Assuming R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ per phase path (2 oz outer, 4 parallel planes), the copper loss is 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W per motor**. This loss lands in the board, not in the FETs.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

- Instantaneous peak for the "static" low-side FET: I²·R = 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = **10.2 W**.
- Electrical-cycle average per FET ≈ 20.5 W / 6 ≈ **3.4 W**.

The electrical period is ≤ 1 ms at flight RPM, and the die thermal time constant is several ms. The junction therefore sees close to the average, plus a ripple of about 10.2 W × Z_th(≈0.3 ms) ≈ 10.2 × 0.15 K/W ≈ 1.5 K.

### 1.4 Thermal runaway check

Positive feedback is stable if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{th,ja} < 1$$

With ∂P/∂T = I²·2·R₂₅·α = 6400 × 2 × 1e-3 × 0.006 = 0.077 W/K per motor, stability requires R_th(board→amb) < 13 K/W per motor. This is satisfied with airflow. Under a still-air bench test it is marginal, so **never run an 80 A static bench test without prop wash.**
