# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #4): System Design Model

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (80 A burst, 48 kHz), transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA plan, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Ground Rules and Design Flags

Before the math, three items need program-level decisions. The numbers below show that the requirement as written is at the edge of physical feasibility.

| # | Flag | Consequence |
|---|------|-------------|
| F1 | **80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 or 30×30) 4-in-1** means 320 A of aggregate battery current if all four motors burst together. | The battery pads, bulk capacitors and power planes become the limiting factor, not the FETs. "80 A burst" must be defined as a duration and duty cycle (I propose **≤1 s, ≤10 % duty, single-motor or diagonal-pair**). |
| F2 | The H743 does **not** switch the FETs. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32-class). The H743 only generates DShot and decodes eRPM telemetry. | The loss and thermal model applies to the ESC board. The DMA model applies to the FC board. |
| F3 | Every loss number depends on the MOSFET datasheet. | All equations are parameterised. The worked numbers use an **assumed** 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm FET (R_DS(on) = 1.2 mΩ at 25 °C). Re-run the equations with the selected part before freezing the BOM. |

**Operating point assumptions**

- 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge, worst case for switching loss).
- I_phase = 80 A burst.
- f_sw = 48 kHz.
- Trapezoidal six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM.
- T_amb = 40 °C.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ |
| α | 0.0065 K⁻¹ (gives ×1.65 at 125 °C) |
| R_DS(on) at T_j = 125 °C | 1.98 mΩ ≈ **2.0 mΩ** |

### 1.2 Six-step with synchronous rectification

During any commutation sector, one phase is PWM-switched and one phase is held low. The current therefore flows through:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET for duty D, and the low-side (synchronous) FET for 1−D. The combined conduction time is 100 %.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET for 100 % of the time.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,D + I^2 R_{DS}(1-D) + I^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This total is independent of duty cycle. Duty only redistributes heat between the HS and LS FETs of the PWM leg.

Worked values at 80 A:

| Condition | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|
| T_j = 125 °C (R = 2.0 mΩ) | 2 × 6400 × 0.0020 = **25.6 W** |
| T_j = 25 °C (cold start) | 2 × 6400 × 0.0012 = **15.4 W** |

Per FET at worst case (125 °C):

- Return-leg LS FET: I²R = **12.8 W**. This is the hottest device.
- PWM-leg HS FET: 12.8·D W.
- PWM-leg LS FET: 12.8·(1−D) W.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt conduction (frequently forgotten)

| Path | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace, 2 oz, ~4 mm wide equivalent, 8 mm long | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Per-channel shunt (if used) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W (×D_eff) |
| Battery bus (shared, at 320 A aggregate) | 0.1 mΩ | **10.2 W** |

The 10.2 W battery-bus loss is Flag F1 quantified: bus copper loss scales with I²_total.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (HS FET of the PWM leg)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

The current rise/fall times are set by the gate driver and R_G. With FD6288-class drive (≈1.0 A source / 1.5 A sink) and Q_gd ≈ 8 nC:

| Edge | Time |
|---|---|
| t_r | Q_gd / I_g,on ≈ 8 nC / 0.8 A ≈ 10 ns |
| t_f | ≈ 8 ns |

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 18\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.87\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (body diode of the sync FET)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Q_rr increases with di/dt and temperature. Use 1.5× margin, giving **0.07 W**.
