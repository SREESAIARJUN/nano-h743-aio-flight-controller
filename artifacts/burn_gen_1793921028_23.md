## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

### 0.1 Architecture correction

ArduPilot runs on the **flight controller (FC)**. It does not commutate motors. A production 4-in-1 ESC uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32 or Bluejay/BLHeli, typically AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8. The architecture is therefore:

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x bidirectional DShot600--> [4-in-1 ESC: 4x MCU + 4x 3-phase driver + 24 MOSFETs]
```

Having one H743 commutate four sensorless BLDCs while also running ArduPilot is not supported and is not recommended.

### 0.2 Form-factor correction

An 80 A burst per channel is not credible on a 20×20 mm "nano" board. Copper area and thermal mass cannot support it (see §3). This analysis assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC**. On 20×20 mm, plan for roughly 35–45 A burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction path

The ESC uses 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification. At every instant, current flows through exactly **two MOSFET channels in series**: one high-side and one low-side. During PWM-off, the complementary FET of the PWM'd leg carries the current instead.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \quad\Rightarrow\quad R_{DS}(110^\circ C)=1.2\,\text{m}\Omega\times1.383 = 1.66\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss per ESC

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2(80)^2(1.66\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{21.2\ W}$$

### 1.4 Loss per FET

Each of the 6 FETs conducts on average one third of the electrical period (120°), so:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx P_{cond,ESC}/6 = 3.5\ \text{W}$$
