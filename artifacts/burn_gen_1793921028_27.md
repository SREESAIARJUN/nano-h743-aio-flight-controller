## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (AM32 complementary PWM). At every instant, exactly **one high-side and one low-side path** carry the phase current I_ph. The PWM-phase's HS and LS FETs share time D and (1−D), so the series path always contains two R_DS(on) elements.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

The ripple-corrected RMS current is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$

Take a typical 2207-class motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-line ≈ 30 µH) and worst-case ripple at D = 0.5 near stall:

ΔI_pp ≈ (25.2·0.5·0.5)/(30 µH·48 kHz) ≈ 4.4 A → ripple term = 1.6 A².

Against 6400 A², this is 0.025 %, so it is negligible.
