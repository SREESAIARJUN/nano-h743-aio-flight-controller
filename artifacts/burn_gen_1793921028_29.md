# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): System Architecture & Derivations

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. The 4-in-1 ESC section uses four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. They drive the gate drivers at 48 kHz and answer bidirectional DShot600. Every equation below is parametric, so you can substitute your actual MOSFET and board data.
