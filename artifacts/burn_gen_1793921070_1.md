## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors by itself. It lacks enough advanced-control timers for four independent 3-phase bridges, and running 4× sensorless commutation alongside the ArduPilot scheduler is not viable. The architecture assumed here is the standard AIO topology:

```
           ┌────────────────────────── FC domain ───────────────────────────┐
 6S ──┬──► │ STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) ── TIM1 CH1..4 ── bidir DShot600 ──┐  │
      │    └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼──┘
      │    ┌────────────────────────── ESC domain (×4) ────────────────────▼──┐
      └──► │ AT32F421 (AM32) ── 3-ph gate driver ── 6× N-MOSFET ── motor     │
           └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Feasibility flag.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) and "80 A burst per channel" are physically incompatible. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32/BLHeli_32 default. At any instant two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) device conducts for D·T_sw and the synchronous low-side (LS) device for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the LS device conducts 100 % of the time.

The motor current therefore always flows through exactly **two** channels:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$
