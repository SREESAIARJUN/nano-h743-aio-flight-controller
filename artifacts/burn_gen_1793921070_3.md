## 0. Architectural Clarification (read first)

ArduPilot running on the STM32H743 **does not commutate motors**. It is a flight-control stack that emits DShot frames. The 4-in-1 power stage therefore needs **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, or one MCU per pair. The usual choice is the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, which performs 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM.

| Domain | Silicon | Firmware | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master, RPM-based harmonic notch |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase
