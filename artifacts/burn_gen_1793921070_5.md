**Document scope:** This covers electrothermal modelling of the power stage (80 A burst per channel, 48 kHz PWM), the ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA plan on the STM32H743, and a reference netlist with a thermal budget.

## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Design Assumptions

Two points need to be settled before the math, because they change the results.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each motor is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 (assumed here to be an AT32F421 per channel). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM.
