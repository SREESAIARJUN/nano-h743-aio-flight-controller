# STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #60): Reference Design Analysis

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Key Finding

**Architecture:**

- **Flight controller:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600 on 4 outputs.
- **ESC section:** Four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32). The H743 does not commutate the motors. Running four 6-step or FOC loops on the FC MCU is possible but is not supported by ArduPilot.
- **Power stage per motor:** A 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q class) driving 6 N-channel MOSFETs in 3.3×3.3 mm DFN packages.
- **Board:** 20×20 mm mounting pattern (≈25×25 mm outline), 8-layer, 2 oz copper on the power layers.
