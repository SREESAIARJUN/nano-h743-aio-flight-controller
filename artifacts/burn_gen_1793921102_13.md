# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Directional DShot Design (Job #66)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Risks

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) handle commutation and receive bi-directional DShot600 from the H743. Running 4× sensorless commutation plus ArduPilot on one H743 is not a supported ArduPilot configuration.

### 0.2 Form-factor warning

"Nano" (20×20 mm) and 80 A per channel are physically incompatible. As Section 3 shows, the stage dissipates about 117 W when all four channels burst together. This document assumes:

- a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board** (approximately 36×36 mm outline),
- with a stacked 20×20 or 30.
