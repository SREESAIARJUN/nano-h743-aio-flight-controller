## 0. Executive Findings

| Item | Result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Loss per ESC channel at 80 A, 6S, 48 kHz | ≈ 22.9 W (incl. copper) | Burst only |
| Total 4-in-1 loss, all motors at 80 A | ≈ 92 W | Not sustainable |
| Burst limit from 40 °C to 110 °C board temperature (no airflow) | ≈ 6 s | Must be enforced in firmware |
| Burst limit, same range, with propwash (≈ 4 °C/W) | ≈ 9 s | Then thermal-limit |
| Continuous rating at ΔT = 70 K, propwash | ≈ 35–40 A/ch | Realistic spec |
| DMA streams needed for bidirectional DShot600 (4 motors) | 4 (2 UP + 2 input capture) | Fits DMAMUX easily |
| Timer clock and prescaler for DShot600 | 12 MHz (240 MHz ÷ 20) | Exact, 0 % error |

**Design risk:** On a nano-class (20×20 mm) footprint, 80 A per channel is physically marginal. Industry "80 A" ratings are 10-second bursts on 30.5×30.5 mm boards with 2–3 oz copper. Recommendations:
- Use a 30.5 mm ESC board, or re-rate the ESC to 80 A at ≤ 5 s.
- Implement temperature-based current derating in the ESC firmware (AM32/BLHeli_32 temperature limit).
