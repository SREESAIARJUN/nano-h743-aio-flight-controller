# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture Reference

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

This is a reference design, not a production release. Every number below depends on the parameter assumptions in §0.2. Replace them with your selected MOSFET datasheet values before committing layout.

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A reliable AIO splits the work across two processor domains:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot | Attitude control; emits bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels |
| ESC (×4 channels) | AT32F421K8 or STM32G071 (one MCU per channel) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (or equivalent 3-phase bootstrap driver) | — | 12 V gate drive |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET (6 per channel) | — | 3-phase bridges |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Switch conduction fractions (6-step, complementary PWM)

In each 60° sector, one phase's high-side (HS) switch PWMs at duty D with its low-side (LS) switch synchronously rectifying, and a second phase's LS switch is held fully on. Each phase spends 120° as the PWM leg and 120° as the static-low leg. Per electrical period, the conduction fractions are:

$$k_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}, \qquad k_{LS} = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}$$

Treating phase current as flat-topped at I_ph (ripple negligible at 48 kHz for typical motor inductance):

$$P_{cond,HS} = k_{HS}\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad P_{cond,LS} = k_{LS}\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Exactly two switches conduct at every instant, so the per-channel total is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Numbers at T_j = 125 °C

- R_ds = 1.2 × 1.6 = 1.92 mΩ
- I²R = 6400 × 1.92×10⁻³ = 12.29 W (for a single switch at 100% conduction)

| Condition | HS switch | LS switch | Channel total |
|---|---|---|---|
| D = 1.0 (full-throttle burst) | 4.10 W | 4.10 W | **24.6 W** |
| D = 0.5 | 2.05 W | 6.14 W | 24.6 W |

The LS switch is the thermal hotspot at partial throttle. Place it closest to the ground pour and thermal vias.
