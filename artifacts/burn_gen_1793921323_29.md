# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

**Document scope.** This is a parametric reference design. No MOSFET, gate driver, stack-up, or board outline was specified, so every assumption is stated explicitly below. Replace the parameter table with datasheet values for your BOM and the equations remain valid. The netlist is a **reference topology** and must go through schematic review, ERC/DRC, and bench validation before fabrication.

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Up-Front Engineering Flags

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | AIO: STM32H743 flight controller + 4-in-1 ESC on one stack/board. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32). The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to the 4 ESC MCUs. It does **not** commutate motors itself. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal (120° conduction), complementary/synchronous PWM on the active leg |
| f_sw | 48 kHz (AM32 selectable) |
| Burst current | 80 A per phase-pair, per channel, all four channels simultaneously (worst case) |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm ESC board, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner |

**Flag 1: "80 A burst" on a nano board is a seconds-scale rating, not a continuous rating.** Section 3 shows the board can sustain roughly 2–3 s at 4×80 A before the FETs approach Tj = 125 °C. Your marketing spec must state the burst duration and ambient conditions.

**Flag 2: 40 V MOSFETs on 6S leave about 15 V of margin for ringing.** At 80 A switched current with nH-scale loop inductance, V = L·di/dt overshoot is real. The layout loop-inductance budget is given in §2.6.
