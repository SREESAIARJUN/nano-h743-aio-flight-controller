# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #38)

## 0. Scope, Architecture Correction, and Assumptions

**Architectural point.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. Each ESC channel runs its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8/BLHeli_S). The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC over bidirectional DShot600. Running 4× sensorless FOC/6-step plus ArduPilot's scheduler on one H743 is possible in principle but is not supported by ArduPilot. This package assumes:

- **FC:** STM32H743VIH6 (LQFP100/TFBGA100), 480 MHz, ArduPilot ChibiOS.
- **ESC:** 4× AM32 MCUs, 24 MOSFETs (3 half-bridges × 4), 6S (25.2 V max), 48 kHz PWM, 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) freewheeling.
- **Form factor:** 30.5 × 30.5 mm ("nano" / 30×30 stack). A 20×20 board cannot thermally support 80 A bursts; the numbers below show 30×30 is already marginal.

**MOSFET parameters are representative placeholders** for a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN class part. Replace them with your chosen part's datasheet values; every equation is written symbolically for that reason.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 | On-resistance @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient (normalized) | ≈ 0.0067 /K (gives ×1.5 at 100 °C) |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 60 nC |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification
At any commutation sector, exactly two phases conduct. One high-side FET (HS) is PWM'd at duty D, and one low-side FET (LS) is held on.

- **PWM on-time:** current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **PWM off-time (complementary):** current freewheels through LS(A) → motor → LS(B).

In both states, motor current I passes through **exactly two channels**. The instantaneous conduction loss per motor is therefore duty-independent:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0067 × 75) ≈ **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0015 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

The loss rotates through the 6 FETs over each electrical cycle (each FET conducts 1/3 of the cycle on average, in HS or LS role):

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses (often ignored, and they matter)

- **Phase/bus copper:** 2 oz (70 µm), roughly 5 mm effective width × 15 mm path, on 4 paralleled layers.

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{wt\,n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8} \times 0.015}{0.005 \times 70\times10^{-6} \times 4} \approx 0.18\ \text{m}\Omega$$

  At 80 A: P ≈ 80² × 0.18 mΩ × 2 paths ≈ **2.3 W per motor**. At 100 °C, copper resistance rises about 30 %, giving about 3 W.

- **Common current shunt** (0.25 mΩ, total current ≈ 4 × 80 × D; at D ≈ 0.8, I_bus ≈ 256 A): P ≈ 256² × 0.25 mΩ ≈ **16 W**. This is a real hotspot. Use a 0.1–0.15 mΩ shunt with a high-gain amplifier (INA186-class or equivalent), or Hall sensing.

## 2. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches. Per motor, per PWM period, there is one hard-switched turn-on and one turn-off on the HS. The LS acts as a synchronous rectifier (soft, after dead time).

### 2.1 Components

**(a) V-I overlap (HS hard switching)**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**(b) Body-diode reverse recovery (LS diode, recovered by HS turn-on)**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance**
$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

**(d) Dead-time diode conduction (2 edges per period)**
$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive (HS + LS of active leg; dissipated mainly in driver and R_g)**
$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}
