# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #36): Design Reference

## 0. Feasibility Constraints

These constraints shape the rest of the design. Each one changes a number that follows.

| Constraint | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| **"Nano" (20×20 mm) + 80 A** | 16 MOSFETs rated for 80 A burst do not fit on a 20×20 board. Usable copper area can't spread ~90 W. | Stacked design: FC on 20×20 (M2), ESC on **30.5×30.5 (M3)**. Single-board 20×20 limits realistic burst to ~35–45 A/ch. |
| **H743 driving the bridges** | ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 *sends* DShot. | Each phase bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071), one per motor. |
| **48 kHz switching** | This is an ESC firmware setting (AM32 `PWM_FREQ`), not an FC setting. | Switching loss is modelled at 48 kHz below. |
| **80 A "burst"** | Must be defined in time. | Burst = **80 A/ch, 4 channels, 1 s**, from a 40 °C board at 6S (25.2 V full charge). Continuous rating is derived separately. |

All component values are representative. Re-derive with the datasheets of the parts you actually procure before layout release.

## 1. Power Stage Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology and assumptions
- 6-step trapezoidal (AM32), complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active high side.
- At any instant, current flows through 2 MOSFETs in series: the high side of phase A (PWM) and the low side of phase B. During PWM-off, current freewheels through the low side of A (synchronous).
- Therefore the DC-link current I sees ~2·R_DS(on) in the conduction path at all times, regardless of duty.

**Reference MOSFET** (40 V, 5×6 DFN class, e.g. a 0.9–1.2 mΩ part):

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.0055 /K |
| Rise + fall (2 A driver, R_g = 2.2 Ω) | t_r + t_f | 20 ns |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 50 nC |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC |
| Body diode V_f | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.0 K/W |

Other inputs:
- Dead time t_dt = 100 ns per edge
- V_bus = 25.2 V
- f_sw = 48 kHz

### 1.2 Conduction loss (temperature-coupled)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

R_Cu is the trace/plane/solder-joint resistance in the current path. For 2 oz outer + 2 oz inner paralleled planes, R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ per channel.

At 80 A, T_j = 115 °C:
- R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 90) = **1.50 mΩ**
- P_FET,cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.50 m = **19.2 W**
- P_Cu = 6400 × 0.5 m = **3.2 W**

### 1.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz (per channel, one PWM'd leg)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·20n·48k | 0.97 W |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw = 50n·25.2·48k | 0.06 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40n·25.2·48k | 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200n·48k | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2·60n·10·48k | 0.06 W |
| **P_sw,ch** | | **≈ 1.75 W** |

At 48 kHz, dead-time diode loss is the second-largest switching term. AM32 dead-time tuning matters. Halving t_dt saves ~0.3 W per channel.

**Frequency scaling:** P_sw ≈ 36.5 µJ × f_sw.
- 24 kHz: 0.88 W
- 96 kHz: 3.5 W

### 1.4 Totals at the 80 A burst point

| | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching | 1.75 W | 7.0 W |
| **Total** | **24.2 W** | **≈ 97 W** |

- Input power: 4 × 80 × 25.2 = 8064 W, so stage efficiency ≈ 98.8%.
- Per hot FET (worst case, conducting the high side with the full switching share): ≈ 9.6 + 1.75 ≈ **11.4 W**.
