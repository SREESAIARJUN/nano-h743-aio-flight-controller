## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

The H743 does not drive the MOSFET gates. ArduPilot has no field-oriented control or 6-step commutation stack for direct inverter control. The correct topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC) ──DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor)──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32)
                                                              └─► 3-ph gate driver ─► 6× N-MOSFET ─► motor
```

The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 handles DShot frame generation and eRPM telemetry capture. The rest of this document follows that partition.

**Form-factor note.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot thermally support 80 A/channel in any meaningful duration (see §3). This model assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm power board** with 20×20 mm-class density on the FC. The numbers below show why.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step complementary PWM, three switches carry current at any instant:

- **PWM'd phase, high side:** conducts for duty D.
- **PWM'd phase, low side:** acts as a synchronous rectifier and conducts for 1−D.
- **Static low side of the return phase:** conducts 100%.

The two devices in the PWM'd phase together carry current 100% of the time. The total is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The on-resistance at temperature is:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$
