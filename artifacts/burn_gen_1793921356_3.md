## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Several decisions shape every number below. Verify them before using this package.

| # | Decision / Assumption | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 running AM32) that drives an FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600. | Industry-standard partitioning. ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware, and the H743 lacks 4×3 complementary advanced-timer pairs with dead-time. |
| A2 | **Board size: 36×36 mm (30.5 mm mount), not 20×20 mm.** | A 20×20 "nano" board cannot physically hold 24 power MOSFETs rated for 80 A burst. Section 3 shows even 36×36 mm is burst-limited. If the 20×20 mm form factor is a hard requirement, the current spec must drop to about 35–40 A burst. |
| A3 | Bus: 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge), design abs-max 30 V | |
| A4 | MOSFET (representative 40 V, 5×6 DFN class): R_DS(on),25°C = 1.0 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V, k_T(125°C) = 1.5, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_gs2 = 6 nC, V_plateau = 3.5 V, R_g,int = 1.0 Ω, Q_rr = 60 nC (at high di/dt), C_o(er) = 1.5 nF, V_SD = 0.8 V, R_θJB = 1.5 K/W | Replace these with your selected part's datasheet values. All equations are parametric. |
