## 0. Assumptions That Change the Design

Two premises in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful.

### 0.1 The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors

ArduPilot is a flight-control stack. It outputs DShot and does not run sensorless BLDC commutation, BEMF zero-cross detection, or FOC. A working AIO architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32-class)
                                              └─► gate driver ─► 3 half-bridges ─► motor
```

The rest of this document assumes one ESC MCU per motor, which is the standard 4-in-1 topology.

### 0.2 "80 A burst" on a nano form factor is thermally bounded to seconds

The model below shows this quantitatively. 80 A per motor, ×4, at near-full duty also implies about 320 A battery current. That is unrealistic for XT30/XT60 and nano copper. I treat 80 A as a per-motor short burst rating and derive the maximum burst duration from it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM. In each 60° sector:

- The PWM leg alternates current between its high-side FET (duty D) and its low-side FET (1−D).
- The low-side FET of the sink leg conducts the full sector.

Current therefore always passes through exactly two R_DS(on) in series, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]
$$

This is a fixed-point problem, because T_j depends on P, which depends on R_DS(T_j). Iterate to convergence. In practice 3–4 iterations is enough.

### 1.2 Numerical evaluation (T_j = 110 °C first pass)

$$
R_{DS}(110) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \cdot (1 + 0.0045\cdot 85) = 1.38\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1.38\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.7\ W}
$$

### 1.3 Distribution per FET

Each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The electrical period at burst speed (around 1–2 ms) is far shorter than the package thermal time constant (around 10–50 ms). The average is therefore valid:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{17.7}{6} = 2.95\ \text{W}
$$

The instantaneous peak in the sink-leg sector is I²R = 8.8 W. This is only relevant for very low eRPM, such as startup or stall. **Stall at 80 A is a destructive case and must be blocked by ESC current limiting.**
