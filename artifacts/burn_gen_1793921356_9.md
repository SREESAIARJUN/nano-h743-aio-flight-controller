## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

Some assumptions below change the answers, so they come first.

| Item | Assumption | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | STM32H743 FC running ArduPilot. Four separate ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. | The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own MCU. |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm (nano-AIO class), 8-layer, 2 oz internal / 1 oz outer | A 20×20 mm board cannot physically fit 24 power FETs plus thermal copper for this load. |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | 40 V FETs. Peak V_DS with ringing must stay below 32 V. |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM (synchronous rectification) at f_sw = 48 kHz | AM32 default style |
| MOSFET (per switch) | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R_ds,on(25 °C) = 1.2 mΩ typ / 1.5 mΩ max, Q_g = 45 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, Q_oss = 50 nC, R_θJC = 1.5 K/W | These are example values. Substitute your datasheet numbers into the equations below. |
| "80 A burst" | 80 A **phase** current, all 4 channels at once (worst case) | Battery current = D·I_ph per motor. |

**Key finding:** 80 A per channel on this board is a **thermally time-limited burst**, about 3–4 s from cold with all four channels loaded. Sustained capability is roughly 25–35 A per channel, set by prop-wash airflow. The derivation follows.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM
At every instant, two phase legs carry the phase current I:
- **Leg A (PWM'd):** the high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D of each PWM cycle. The synchronous low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D).
- **Leg B (return):** the LS is held fully ON.

So exactly **two FETs in series** carry I at all times, ignoring dead time. The instantaneous bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$
