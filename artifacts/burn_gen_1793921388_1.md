## 0. Architectural Correction: The H743 Cannot Commutate the ESC Directly

The H743 should not drive the power stage itself. A 4-motor, 6-step synchronous-rectified bridge needs **12 complementary PWM pairs** with dead-time insertion. The H743 falls short:

| Resource | Complementary pairs available |
|---|---|
| TIM1 (advanced) | 3 |
| TIM8 (advanced) | 3 |
| HRTIM (5 units × 2 outputs) | Possible in principle, but dead-time, BEMF zero-cross, and comparator blanking on top of ArduPilot's scheduler is not viable |

**Correct partition (industry-standard AIO topology):**

```
           ┌──────────────── STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ────────────────┐
           │ IMU(SPI1)  Baro(I2C)  OSD(SPI2)  SD(SDMMC)  UARTs     │
           │ TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM4_CH1/CH2  → Bi-dir DShot600 ×4      │
           └────┬─────────┬─────────┬─────────┬────────────────────┘
                │ M1      │ M2      │ M3      │ M4   (33Ω series, 3.3V)
           ┌────▼───┐ ┌───▼────┐ ┌──▼─────┐ ┌─▼──────┐
           │AT32F421│ │AT32F421│ │AT32F421│ │AT32F421│  AM32 firmware, 48 kHz
           │+FD6288Q│ │+FD6288Q│ │+FD6288Q│ │+FD6288Q│  3-ph gate driver
           │ 6×FET  │ │ 6×FET  │ │ 6×FET  │ │ 6×FET  │  24 FETs total
           └────────┘ └────────┘ └────────┘ └────────┘
```

AM32 supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot natively. Each ESC MCU owns one bridge with TIM1 CH1–3 / CH1N–3N plus a comparator for BEMF sensing.

**Commutation model:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant the motor current flows through **two conducting FETs in series**:

- **ON interval:** high side (PWM phase) + low side (return phase).
- **OFF interval:** synchronous low side (PWM phase) + low side (return phase).

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Hot R_DS(on)

$$R_{hot} = k_T \cdot R_{25} = 1.5 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Per-FET loss within one 60° sector, at duty D

| FET | Loss expression |
|---|---|
| High side, PWM phase | $P_{HS} = D \cdot I^2 R_{hot}$ |
| Low side, sync-rect, PWM phase | $P_{LS,sr} = (1-D)\cdot I^2 R_{hot}$ |
| Low side, return phase | $P_{LS,ret} = I^2 R_{hot}$ (100% of the sector) |

### 2.3 Per-motor total

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \cdot I^2 R_{hot} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

This total is **independent of D**. The return-leg FET carries **9.6 W** while it conducts.

### 2.4 Time-averaged per-FET loss

Each phase spends 120° as the high side, 120° as the low side, and 120° floating. Averaged over one electrical cycle, the loss spreads roughly evenly over the 6 FETs:

$$\bar P_{FET,cond} \approx 19.2/6 = 3.2\,\text{W}$$

The return low-side FET still sees **9.6 W pulses** at the commutation rate. Commutation is fast relative to the die thermal time constant (eRPM > 10k → sector < 1 ms), so the junction tracks the average plus a small ripple.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Hard switching occurs only on the PWM leg: high side on/off, with its synchronous partner commutating.

### 3.1 V–I overlap loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 3.2 Output capacitance loss

$$P_{Coss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} \times 2 = 1.5\text{n}\times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.046\,\text{W}$$

### 3.3 Reverse recovery loss

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.060\,\text{W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction loss (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{n}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 3.5 Gate drive loss (two FETs switching, dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g)

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\times60\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.058\,\text{W}$$

### 3.6 Per-motor
