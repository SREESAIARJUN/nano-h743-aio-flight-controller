## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Findings

These issues affect the whole design, so they come first.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The H743 cannot commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation or FOC. | Each ESC channel needs its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071). The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. |
| F2 | A "nano" 20×20 mm board cannot carry 80 A per motor. | This model uses a **30.5×30.5 mm mount on a ~37×37 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz**. Even that is burst-only (see §3). |
| F3 | 4 × 80 A means **320 A drawn from the pack** if all motors burst together. | XT60 (~60 A continuous) and 12 AWG leads are inadequate. You need XT90/AS150 and 8 AWG, or you must derate the burst spec to "per-motor, non-simultaneous." |
| F4 | Bus capacitor ripple current reaches tens of amps RMS. | The board needs polymer caps plus an external low-ESR bulk cap (≥1000 µF, 35 V for 6S) at the pads. |

**Recommendation:** Rewrite the spec as **"80 A per channel, ≤ 5 s, ≤ 2 channels simultaneous; 4×45 A ≤ 10 s."** The math below shows why.

## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM):

- Two phases conduct at any instant: one leg is PWM'd, one leg is held low.
- During PWM-on, current flows through HS(A) and LS(B).
- During PWM-off, current freewheels through LS(A) (sync rectified) and LS(B).

So the current always passes through exactly **two R_DS(on) in series**, independent of duty D:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Including PWM ripple ΔI (motor L ≈ 10 µH, worst case D = 0.5):

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{10\,\mu\text{H} \cdot 48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 13.1\,\text{A}_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 14.3 = 6414\,\text{A}^2$$

The ripple contributes about 0.2%, which is negligible.

### 2.2 Temperature-Coupled Resistance (Iterative Solution)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

| Iteration | T_j assumed | R_DS | P_cond (2 FETs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 1 | 90 °C | 1.36 mΩ | 17.4 W |
| 2 (converged, end of burst) | 115 °C | 1.50 mΩ | **19.2 W** |

Add the copper path from battery pad → bus plane → FET → phase pad. At ~0.15 mΩ per channel with a 2 oz, 8-layer stack:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.15\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 1.0\,\text{W}$$
