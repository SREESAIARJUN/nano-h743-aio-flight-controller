## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC/PMSM, e.g. 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), driven through a 3-phase inverter |
| Position sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder per axis (e.g. AS5048A/MT6701), SPI |
| Current sensing | 2- or 3-shunt, ADC sampling synchronized to PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, 8 kHz ODR, SPI, FIFO + timestamps |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G4/H7) with single-precision FPU |
| Gimbal order | Yaw (base) → Roll → Pitch (camera) |

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, balanced currents with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, using electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}})$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Machine Model (Surface PM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$
