## 0. Architecture Correction and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot does not run BLDC commutation, and bidirectional DShot is a protocol between the flight controller and dedicated ESC MCUs. The architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM notch filtering |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrapped) | — | HS/LS gate drive |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | — | 3 half-bridges per motor
