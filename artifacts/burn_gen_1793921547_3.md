# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the Job #48 specification, so the parameters below are stated assumptions. Every gain in the code is computed from these parameters, so replacing them with your motor and payload values re-derives the whole controller.

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC/PMSM, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.0 mH, Kt = 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (e.g., AS5048A/MT6835), read at the current-loop rate |
| Payload inertia | J ≈ 2×10⁻³ kg·m² per axis (worst case pitch) |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU (STM32G4/H7 class) |
| Bus | 12–16 V, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| IMU A | ICM-42688-P on the camera/payload frame (attitude and inertial rate feedback) |
| IMU B | ICM-42688-P on the base/handle (base-motion feedforward and vibration spectrum reference) |

The loop structure, with rate and nominal bandwidth for each stage:

```
                         ┌──────────── 1 kHz ───────────┐ ┌──── 4 kHz ─────┐ ┌──── 20 kHz ────┐
θ_ref ─►(+)─► Angle P+FF ─►(+)─► Velocity PI+FF ─►(+)─► iq_ref ─► Current PI (dq) ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
         ▲ θ̂ (Mahony, IMU A)  ▲ ω̂ (notched gyro A)      ▲ iq_cog(θ_e)  ▲ i_abc, θ_m (encoder)
                              └── base-rate FF (IMU B) ───┘
Bandwidths:   ~8 Hz                 ~40 Hz                     ~1.5 kHz
```

Each loop is placed at least 5× below its inner neighbor in bandwidth. Each loop also runs at least 20× its own bandwidth in sample rate, keeping ZOH phase loss under 10°.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}
$$

The Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

where $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}K_t/p$ for the amplitude-invariant transform.

The controller adds decoupling and back-EMF feedforward terms:

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = PI_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 PI Tuning by Pole-Zero Cancellation

After decoupling, each axis reduces to $G(s)=\dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choosing

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

makes the closed loop first-order, $T(s)=\dfrac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$.

The bandwidth limit comes from the PWM and computation delay, $T_d \approx 1.5\,T_s$. Keeping $\omega_c T_d \le 0.3$ rad gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot1500$ rad/s at $T_s = 50\,\mu s$.

### 1.4 Discretization with Back-Calculation Anti-Windup

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_k &= K_p e_k + I_k\\
\bar u_k &= \mathrm{sat}(u_k)\\
I_{k+1} &= I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(\bar u_k - u_k), \qquad K_{aw}=K_i/K_p\cdot T_s
\end{aligned}
$$

### 1.5 Voltage Limiting and SVPWM

The voltage vector is circle-limited, with d-axis priority:

$$
|v| \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt 3}\cdot m_{max}, \quad m_{max} \approx 0.95
$$

SVPWM is implemented as min-max zero-sequence injection, which is equivalent to classic sector SVPWM:

$$
v_{0} = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\right),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$
