## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$
