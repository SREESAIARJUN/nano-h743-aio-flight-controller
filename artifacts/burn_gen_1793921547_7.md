# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion

This is a complete reference design: the equations, the tuning rules, and a self-contained C++17 implementation for embedded targets (no heap, no exceptions, float math).

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

I don't have the Job #30 specification, so I've assumed typical parameters. Every constant is a `constexpr` or a config field, so you can substitute your own values.

### Assumed hardware

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5
