# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #27): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, DShot DMA Plan

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These results drive the rest of the document:

1. **80 A per channel on a nano/30.5 mm board is a burst-only rating.** At four channels × 80 A the board dissipates about 70 W. Its lumped thermal time constant is about 20 s, so the safe burst is roughly 2–3 s. The realistic continuous rating is about 30–35 A per channel with prop-wash airflow.
2. **The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** Use the H743 as the flight controller (ArduPilot) and put a dedicated ESC MCU on each channel, or one 4-channel ESC MCU, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Examples are AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. This is how every production AIO board is built.
3. **All device parameters below are assumptions.** Substitute datasheet values for your selected MOSFET and driver before sign-off. The equations are exact for the stated model; the numbers are only as good as the inputs.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path
During any 60° commutation sector, current flows through two devices in series:
- The PWM leg contributes either HS (on-time, duty D) or LS-synchronous (off-time, 1 − D).
- The static low-side of the return leg conducts for 100 % of the sector.

### 2.2 Equations
Instantaneous conduction loss per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{HS} D + I_{ph}^2 R_{LS}(1-D) + I_{ph}^2 R_{LS,static} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This assumes equal devices for HS and LS. The result is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Battery and phase current are related by:

$$I_{batt} \approx D \cdot I_{ph} \quad\Rightarrow\quad I_{ph} = I_{batt}/D$$

If "80 A" is a battery-side rating at D < 1, the phase current, and therefore the loss, is higher. Specify which current the rating refers to.

Per-device average over one electrical revolution. Each leg is "active" for 120°, i.e. 1/3 of the time:

$$P_{HS,cond} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_{ph}^2 R D, \qquad P_{LS,cond} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_{ph}^2 R (1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3} I_{ph}^2 R$$

### 2.3 Numbers (I_ph = 80 A, R = 1.2 mΩ hot)
- Per channel: P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.2×10⁻³ = **15.36 W**
- At D = 0.9, per device:
  - HS = 2.30 W
  - LS = 2.82 W (worst-case device)
- Four channels: **61.4 W**

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard. With complementary PWM, the HS sees hard turn-on and turn-off, while the LS switches at near-zero V_ds.

| Term | Equation | Value per channel |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap (HS) | ½ · V_bus · I_ph · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k = **1.452 W** |
| Body-diode dead time (LS) | V_f · I_ph · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.614 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 50 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.060 W** |
| C_oss | ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | **0.024 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 2 × 60 n × 10 × 48 k = **0.058 W** |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.21 W** |

**Sensitivities:**
- Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. Moving to 24 kHz saves about 1.1 W per channel.
- Overlap loss scales with (t_r + t_f), so gate-driver sink/source current is the biggest lever.
- Shortening dead time to 50 ns saves 0.3 W but needs a verified shoot-through margin.

### 5.2 Board-to-air resistance

$$R_{ba} = \frac{1}{h A}, \quad A = 2 \times 1.296\times10^{-3} = 2.59\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^2$$

| Condition | h (W/m²·K) | R_ba |
|---|---|---|
| Still air | 15 | 25.7 K/W |
| Prop wash | ~100 | 3.86 K/W |

Time constant with airflow: τ = R·C = 3.86 × 6.1 ≈ **23.5 s**.
