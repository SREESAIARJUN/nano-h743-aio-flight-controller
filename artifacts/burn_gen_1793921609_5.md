# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #46): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Trapezoidal six-step with synchronous rectification.** During each 60° electrical sector, current flows through exactly one high-side and one low-side path:

- The PWM leg alternates HS (for duty D) and LS (for 1−D).
- The opposite leg's LS conducts continuously.

So the current always sees **two R_ds in series**, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2\left[2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\right], \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**At T_j = 100 °C:**

- R_ds = 1.5 × (1 + 0.0056·75) = **2.13 mΩ**
- MOSFET loss: P = 6400 × 2·2.13 m = **27.3 W**
- PCB copper loss: 6400 × 0.5 m = **3.2 W**
- **P_cond ≈ 30.5 W per motor, ≈ 122 W for all four at 80 A**

**Electrothermal feedback.** R_ds rises with T_j, so loss and temperature must be solved together:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th}\cdot I^2\cdot 2R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

- The solution is stable only while R_th · I² · 2R_ds,25 · α < 1.
- At steady state this condition fails for 80 A on a nano board. **Thermal runaway is avoided only because 80 A is a burst.**

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches.

| Term | Equation | Per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f = 0.5·25.2·80·27n·48k | 1.31 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_d·f = 0.8·80·200n·48k | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 0.05 W |
| C_oss (2 FETs) | 2·½Q_oss·V·f | 0.06 W |
| Gate (mostly in driver) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f | 0.05 W |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.1 W** |

**Key insight.** At 80 A, conduction loss dominates (~94 % of the total). Implications:

- Lowering f_sw to 24 kHz saves only about 1 W per motor.
- **Lowering R_ds through paralleled FETs or a lower-R part is the only lever that matters.**
- Dead-time diode loss is the largest switching term, so keep t_d at 50 ns or less if the driver allows.

**Total burst dissipation:** 4 × (30.5 + 2.1) ≈ **130 W**.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Junction to case

Conduction rotates among the 6 FETs, and the electrical frequency (hundreds of Hz to kHz) is much faster than the FET thermal time constants. Each FET therefore averages roughly P/3 ≈ 10 W.

- ΔT_jc = 10 W × 1.2 K/W ≈ **12 K**
- Add ≈ 15 K for spreading into the copper planes.

### 3.2 Board as a lumped capacitance

For a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board (FR4 ≈ 4 g, Cu ≈ 1.5 g):

$$C_{board} \approx 4\text{ g}\cdot1.1 + 1.5\text{ g}\cdot0.385 \approx 5\ \text{J/K}$$

The board-to-air thermal resistance in propwash is about 8 K/W, giving τ = RC ≈ 40 s. A burst lasting a few seconds is therefore essentially **adiabatic**.

### 3.3 Burst time budget

The temperature budget is T_j,max = 125 °C from a 40 °C ambient:

- ΔT available to the board = 125 − 40 − 12 − 15 = **58 K**

| Scenario | P | dT/dt | Burst limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| All 4 motors at 80 A | 130 W | 26 K/s | **≈ 2.2 s** |
| 1 motor at 80 A (local hotspot, ~1/4 of C) | 32.6 W | ~26 K/s locally | ≈ 2–3 s |
| Continuous at 35 A per motor | ~25 W total | → steady 40 + 25·8 = 240 °C | ✗ |

**Continuous rating.** Steady state solves to about **≤ 20 A per motor at 8 K/W**. The "80 A" rating is honest only as a ≤ 2 s burst rating.

**Required protection.** Use an NTC on the ESC board and ESC firmware current limiting. Set an I²t limit on the H7 side as well, using ESC telemetry (see Section 5).

### 3.4 Bulk capacitor ripple

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \text{A at } D=0.5 \text{ per motor}$$

- The four motors are not phase-interleaved, so worst-case ripple can approach 80 A_rms.
- Mitigation: 4–8 × 10 µF X7R per motor at the bridge, plus a low-ESR bulk capacitor of ≥ 1000 µF / 35 V at the battery pads.

## 5. ArduPilot Timer and DMA Allocation (H743)

**How ArduPilot uses DMA for bidirectional DShot:**

- **Output** uses the timer's UP DMA request in burst mode (DMAR), writing all CCRs of that timer at once.
- **Telemetry input** uses input capture with one CHx DMA request per capture channel.
- **Channel pairing:** in ArduPilot, channels pair up as CH1/CH2 and CH3/CH4.
  - Put the `BIDIR` tag on one channel of each pair.
  - The paired channel's DMA request is used for capture.
- **Stream budget:** the H7 DMAMUX allows any request on any of the 16 streams. This layout needs 2 UP + 4 capture = **6 streams**, leaving 10 for SPI IMU, UARTs, and SDMMC (MDMA).
