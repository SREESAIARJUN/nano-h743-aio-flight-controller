# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Design Model, Job #7

## 0. Architecture Corrections Before Any Math

Three assumptions in the brief need correcting:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production "H743 + 4-in-1" AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four (or two dual-channel) ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32, and they generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model below follows that partition.
2. **"80 A burst" must be defined.** I take it as **80 A per motor phase current, 4 motors simultaneously (≈320 A peak bus)**, for a short burst. Section 3 shows this is thermally achievable only for roughly 0.3–1.5 s on a nano-class board. It is impossible continuously.
3. **"Nano" form factor.** 24 MOSFETs, 4 ESC MCUs, gate drivers and an H743 (LQFP100 or TFBGA) do not fit realistically on a 20×20 mm board. This design assumes a **25.5 × 25.5 mm (whoop) single-board AIO** or a **20×20 two-board stack**. The thermal model is parameterised so either can be plugged in.

All component parameters below are **stated assumptions** for a representative 30 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 MOSFET. Replace them with datasheet values for the selected part before sign-off.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

With synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** FET channels: either the PWM-phase HS or LS, plus the return-phase LS. The total is therefore duty-independent:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,(R_{ds,hot} + R_{cu})\cdot\left(1 - \frac{2t_{dt}f_{sw}}{1}\right)$$

The dead-time correction factor is (1 − 2·200 ns·48 kHz) = 0.981.

$$P_{cond} = 6400 \cdot 2(2.2+0.4)\times10^{-3}\cdot 0.981 = \mathbf{32.6\ W}$$

Of this, FET-only loss is 27.6 W and copper loss is 5.0 W.

**Per-FET split at duty D.** Burst at full throttle gives D ≈ 0.9.

| Device | Instantaneous loss | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PWM-phase HS | D·I²·R = 0.9·6400·2.2 m = **12.7 W** | |
| PWM-phase LS (sync rect.) | (1−D)·I²·R = **1.4 W** | |
| Return-phase LS | I²·R = **14.1 W** | conducts the full 120° sector |

Each FET holds a given role for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Averaged over a revolution (e-freq ≫ 1/τ_die is not guaranteed, see §3.3), per-FET mean dissipation is:

- HS: 12.7/3 = 4.2 W
- LS: (1.4 + 14.1)/3 = 5.2 W

### 2.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz (only the PWM-phase leg switches)

**(a) V–I overlap, hard-switched HS.**

Transition times:
- t_r ≈ Q_sw / I_g,on, with I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl)/R_g = 6.5/6 = 1.08 A, so t_r ≈ 11 ns.
- t_f ≈ Q_sw / I_g,off, with I_g,off = V_pl/R_g = 0.58 A, so t_f ≈ 21 ns.
- Add about 8 ns for layout and ringing, giving t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns.

$$P_{ov} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot40\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$
