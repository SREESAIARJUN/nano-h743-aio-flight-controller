## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.007\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.7 = **1.7 mΩ**. Use this hot value; the cold value under-predicts loss by about 40%.

### 1.2 Six-step current paths

At any instant, two legs conduct:

- **Commutating leg (PWM'd, duty D):**
  - The high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw.
  - The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T_sw − 2t_d.
  - The body diode conducts for 2t_d.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is fully on for the entire 120° sector.

So current always flows through exactly two channels in series:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) = 2(80)^2(1.7\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{21.8\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET is active for one-third of the electrical cycle.

- **High-side FET:**
$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,D$$
- **Low-side FET (synchronous plus return-leg duty):**
$$P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R$$

At D = 0.9 (full-throttle burst):

| FET | Loss |
|---|---|
| High side | 3.26 W |
| Low side | 3.99 W |

The **low-side FET is the hottest device.** Place it nearest the thermal vias and the frame/heat path.
