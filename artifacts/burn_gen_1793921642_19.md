# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #39 requirements, BOM, or stack-up, so every result below is parametric. Each equation is written so you can substitute datasheet values. The numbers I plug in are representative of current 40 V PDFN3.3 ESC MOSFETs. They are not a specific vendor's part. Treat every **[A]** tag as an assumption to verify.

**Architectural correction to the brief.** In a "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" product, the H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. ArduPilot has no in-tree FOC/6-step driver for the H7. Each ESC channel needs its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421K8 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. The design below follows that architecture.
