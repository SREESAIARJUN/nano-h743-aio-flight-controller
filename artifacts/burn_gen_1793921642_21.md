# STM32H743 Nano FC + Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #16)

## 0. Design Review Findings and Assumptions

Four findings shape the rest of this package and should be resolved before layout.

| # | Finding | Severity | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **80 A burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally viable.** It is marginal even on 30.5×30.5 mm. | Critical | This package assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** stack, a 2 s burst definition, and **2 FETs in parallel per switch position**. |
| F2 | **4 × 80 A = 320 A from the battery is not a real operating point.** XT60/XT90 connectors, leads, and the pack cannot support it. | Critical | "80 A burst" is defined as **per-channel, non-simultaneous**. The board-level burst budget is 4 × 45 A = 180 A for 2 s. |
| F3 | **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay. The H743 only talks bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. | Architecture | The architecture below uses an AT32F421 per channel, with a 3-phase gate driver per channel. |
| F4 | **6S with 40 V FETs leaves less than 40 % margin** against ringing spikes. | High | 40 V parts are acceptable only with a tight loop layout and verified spike data. A 60 V option is noted. |

The baseline design parameters used throughout are:

- V_bus = 25.2 V (6S full charge, worst case for switching), 22.2 V nominal.
- f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, 6-step trapezoidal commutation.
- Dead time t_dt = 100 ns per edge.
- **MOSFET model:** a generic 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN part. These are representative values; substitute your selected part's datasheet values.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S ──XT60──┬── TVS + bulk caps ──┬── ESC ch1..4 power stages (6 FETs × 2 parallel each)
            │                     │
            │                     └── per-channel: AT32F421 (AM32) + FD6288-class 3φ driver
            │
            ├── 12 V buck (driver VCC, VTX)
            ├── 5 V buck  (FC, RX)
            └── 3.3 V LDO (MCU, IMU — separate analog LDO for IMU)

 STM32H743 ──TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM5_CH1/CH2 (bidir DShot600, 1 wire each)──► ESC MCUs
           ──ADC: VBAT divider, ESC total current shunt amp
           ──SPI1: IMU1, SPI4: IMU2, SPI2: OSD, I2C: baro/mag, SDMMC1
```

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly two phases are active at any instant.

- During PWM-on, current flows through HS(phase A) and LS(phase B).
- During PWM-off, current freewheels through LS(phase A), the complementary FET, and LS(phase B).
