# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Design Model, Job #32

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and *emits* bidirectional DShot600. Each motor channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz PWM, gate drive, and back-EMF sensing all live on those MCUs. The model below is therefore split into two parts:

- **Power stage (×4):** ESC MCU, 3-phase gate driver, 6 N-FETs, shunt, bulk capacitance
- **FC (H743):** 4 DShot lines, eRPM telemetry decode, DMA allocation

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, as AM32 does by default. During any commutation sector:

- The high-side FET of phase A conducts I for fraction D.
- The low-side FET of phase A conducts I for fraction (1−D), as synchronous rectification.
- The low-side FET of phase B conducts I continuously.

Summing the three:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{path}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{path}(T_j)$$

Duty cycle cancels out. Conduction loss depends only on phase current, not throttle.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{path}(T_j) = R_{DS25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + R_{cu}$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

With line-line motor inductance L_m (worst case D = 0.5):

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_m f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

For L_m = 15 µH: ΔI_pp = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15 µ × 48 k) = **8.75 A**.

The ripple term adds 6.4 A², which is under 0.1% of I² = 6400 A². Ignore it at 80 A. It does matter for low-inductance motors at low current, where it sets idle heating.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_path | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.6 W | 66.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.675 mΩ | 21.4 W | 85.8 W |

### 1.5 Electrothermal stability criterion

Thermal runaway is avoided if the loop gain is below 1:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = 2I^2R_{DS25}\,\alpha\,R_{th,j\text{-}amb} < 1$$

At 80 A this is 0.064 W/K × R_th. It is stable only if R_th(j-amb) < 15.6 K/W per channel. That threshold is easily met in airflow and **not met in still air on a nano board**. Never bench-test at 80 A without prop wash.
