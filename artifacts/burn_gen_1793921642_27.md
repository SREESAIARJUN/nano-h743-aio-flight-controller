## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flag

**What the H743 does and does not do.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU. This design uses 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, which supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot natively.

**Topology:**

```
6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──┬── 4× 3-phase bridge (24 MOSFETs total)
                       ├── 10 V gate-drive buck ── 4× FD6288Q drivers
                       ├── 5 V / 3.3 V buck ── H743 + peripherals
                       └── shunt + amplifier ── H743 ADC (BATT_CURR)

H743 TIM1 CH1–4 ──(DShot600 bidir, single wire each)── 4× AT32F421 (AM32)
```

**Feasibility flag (read first).** 80 A per channel on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5 mm) is only a **burst rating, measured in seconds**. Section 3 shows that full 4-channel 80 A dissipation is about 100 W. A nano PCB has roughly 3–5 J/K of heat capacity, so it heats at about 20–30 K/s. My recommendations:

- Rate the board at **80 A for ≤2 s (single channel) / ≤1 s (all four)**, with **~35–45 A continuous**.
