## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production "H743 AIO / stack" design:

- The **H743** runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and emits DShot600 frames.
- **Four separate ESC MCUs** close the commutation loop. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8BB21 running BLHeli_S.
  - The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC MCU's** PWM frequency.
  - The H743 never generates the 48 kHz.

All numbers below are parametric. Substitute your BOM values into the equations.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S full charge | 25.2 V |
| I_ph | Burst phase current per motor | 80 A |
| D | PWM duty at burst | 0.95 |
| f_sw | ESC PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds(25) | MOSFET R_DS(on) at V_GS = 10 V, 25 °C (40 V, DFN5x6-class) | 1.2 mΩ |
| α | R_DS(on) tempco factor at T_j = 125 °C | 1.5, so R_ds(hot) = 1.8 mΩ |
| Q_g / Q_gd / Q_oss / Q_rr | Gate and charge parameters | 50 / 10 / 40 / 30 nC |
| t_r, t_f | Rise and fall times (FD6288-class driver, ~1 A) | 15 ns each |
| t_dt | Dead time per edge | 100 ns |
| V_SD | Body-diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz power layers | — |
| T_amb | Ambient (in-frame) | 40 °C |

> **Reality flag:** 80 A is achievable only as a **burst** rating on a nano/AIO footprint. As Section 2 shows, the steady state is physically impossible on this board area. The real deliverable is therefore *how long* the burst can last.

## 1. Power-Stage Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

Six-step trapezoidal control with complementary ("damped-light") PWM is used. In each 60° electrical sector:

- **Phase A high-side (HS):** PWM at duty D.
- **Phase A low-side (LS):** synchronous rectifier during (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Phase B low-side:** held fully ON.
- **Phase C:** floating, used for BEMF zero-cross sensing.

### 1.2 Conduction losses

The resistance is temperature dependent:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + k_T\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad k_T \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

The loss in each conducting element is:

$$P_{HS,cond} = I^2 R_{ds}\,D,\qquad P_{LS,sync} = I^2 R_{ds}\,(1-D-2t_{dt}f_{sw}),\qquad P_{LS,on} = I^2 R_{ds}$$

The sector total is approximately:

$$P_{cond,sector} \approx 2\,I^2 R_{ds}$$

The approximation holds because the active phase always has exactly one switch carrying I, and the return leg carries I continuously.

**Numerical results at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:**

| Element | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| HS (PWM) | 80² × 1.8 mΩ × 0.95 | 10.94 W |
| LS (sync) | 80² × 1.8 mΩ × (0.05 − 0.0096) | 0.47 W |
| LS (return, fully on) | 80² × 1.8 mΩ | 11.52 W |
| **P_cond** | | **≈ 22.9 W per channel** |

**Copper term (not negligible).** Assume 6 mm of 2 oz trace-equivalent path per phase, about 0.15 mΩ. Two phases in series give:

80² × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **1.9 W**
