## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 runs **ArduPilot as the flight controller**. ArduPilot has no on-board ESC commutation firmware. The 4-in-1 ESC section therefore needs its own MCU(s) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Typical choices are 4× STM32G071 or 4× AT32F421. Commutation is either per motor or two motors per MCU.

```
[STM32H743 / ArduPilot] --4x bidir DShot600--> [4x ESC MCU (AM32)] --> [4x FD6288-class driver] --> [4x 3-phase MOSFET bridge]
```

The model below covers:
- **(A)** ESC power-stage losses.
- **(B)** Thermal behavior.
- **(C)** H743 timer and DMA allocation.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Trapezoidal Commutation

- In each 60° electrical sector, one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) device carry the phase current.
- With complementary PWM, during PWM-off time the complementary FET of the switching leg conducts instead of its body diode.
- So at any instant, **two MOSFET channels** are in series with the motor current.

Junction-temperature-dependent resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Per-motor conduction loss (flat-top current approximation):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{pk}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{pk}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Here R_Cu is the PCB copper plus solder-joint resistance in the current path.

### 1.2 Per-Device Loss

- Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 120° of 360°, so its average share is 1/3 of the time.
- During PWM-off, the conduction share moves from the PWM-ing HS to its LS complement.

Per-FET average conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx \frac{2 I_{pk}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{6}$$

The worst-case device is the LS FET at low duty: it carries both its own sector and the freewheel current. Bound it at:

$$P_{cond,FET,max} \approx \frac{I_{pk}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{3}\,(2 - D)$$

### 1.3 Numbers (T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 2.18 mΩ)

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| I² | 80² | 6400 A² |
| P_cond, motor (FETs only) | 2 × 6400 × 2.18 mΩ | **27.9 W** |
| Copper (R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ, 2 oz + vias) | 6400 × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **Per-motor total** | | **≈ 29.8 W** |
| Per-FET average | 27.9 / 6 | 4.65 W |
| Worst-case LS FET (D = 0.5) | 6400 × 2.18 m / 3 × 1.5 | 7.0 W |

At T_j = 25 °C the per-motor FET conduction loss is 19.2 W. The positive feedback between temperature and R_DS(on) is handled in §3.4.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches. That is one complementary pair per motor at any time, with hard switching on the HS turn-on edge.

### 2.1 Loss Components

| # | Mechanism | Equation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | V-I overlap | P_ov = ½ · V_bus · I_pk · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw |
| 2 | Reverse recovery | P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw |
| 3 | Output capacitance | P_oss = Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw (≈ ½·C_oss,eq·V²·f_sw, charge-based form) |
| 4 | Dead-time diode conduction | P_dt = 2 · V_f · I_pk · t_d · f_sw |
| 5 | Gate drive (taken by driver/LDO, not the FETs) | P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw |

### 2.2 Numbers (V_bus = 25.2 V, I = 80 A)

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P_ov | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| P_rr | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| P_oss | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.05 W |
| P_dt | 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | **1.23 W** |
| **P_sw, FET-side** | | **≈ 3.28 W / motor** |
| P_g (driver side) | 2 × 45 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.043 W / motor |

### 2.3 Design Levers

- **Dead time is a significant term at 48 kHz.** Cutting t_d from 200 ns to 80 ns saves about 0.75 W per motor. Validate shoot-through margin first, including the driver's propagation-delay mismatch.
- **P_ov scales linearly with f_sw.** At 24 kHz it falls to about 0.97 W, at the cost of more current ripple and audible noise.

### 3.2 Network (Cauer-Approximated Foster Ladder)

```
P_FET → [R_θJC 1.5 K/W | C_j ≈ 0.02 J/K] → T_case
```
