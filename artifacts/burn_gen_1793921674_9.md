## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_DS25 · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.6 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.32 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases carry I_m:
- **Active high-side leg:** the HS FET conducts for D, and the complementary LS FET conducts for (1 − D).
- **Return phase:** the LS FET conducts continuously.

With synchronous rectification, two FETs are always in the current path, so the loss per channel is independent of D:

**P_cond,ch = 2 · I_m² · R_DS(on)(T_j)**
