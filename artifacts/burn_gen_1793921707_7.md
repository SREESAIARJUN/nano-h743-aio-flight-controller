# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and One Key Finding

I don't have the Job #61 design files, BOM or stack-up. Every numeric parameter below is therefore a stated, representative assumption, and the model is built so you can substitute your datasheet values directly. Items marked **[VERIFY]** must be checked against real silicon and hardware before release.

**Architecture assumed:** the H743 is the flight controller. It sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (an AM32 or BLHeli_32-class part such as an AT32F421). Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz gate PWM. The H743 never drives the gates. This split is what makes the DShot and switching-loss sections consistent.
