## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction states (six-step, complementary PWM)

In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM-driven high, one phase is held low, and one floats for back-EMF sensing.

- **PWM on (fraction D):** current flows through HS_x and LS_y.
- **PWM off (fraction 1−D):** current flows through LS_x (synchronous) and LS_y.

Exactly two R_DS(on) are in the current path at all times (ignoring dead time). This gives the per-channel conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{cu}$$

### 1.2 Per-device split over one electrical revolution

Each phase spends 120° driven high (PWM), 120° held low, and 120° floating.

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3} I^2 R_{DS}, \qquad P_{LS} = \frac{(1-D) + 1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

The low-side FETs are the hot devices at low duty. This matters for hover-current asymmetry.

### 1.3 Temperature coupling and runaway criterion

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \qquad T_j = T_{ref} + P_{dev}(T_j)\, R_{th,j\text{-}ref}$$

Solving the fixed point for a device with conduction share k:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_{th}\,(kI^2R_{25})(1 - 25\alpha) + R_{th} P_{sw}}{1 - \alpha\, R_{th}\, k I^2 R_{25}}$$

**Stability requires** $\alpha R_{th} k I^2 R_{25} < 1$.

For the worst-case LS device at 80 A (k ≈ 0.37, R_th ≈ 3 K/W):
0.0045 × 3 × 0.37 × 6400 × 0.9e-3 = **0.029**. This is far from runaway at the device level. The real limit is board-level heat accumulation, covered in Section 3.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j ≈ 125 °C (R_DS = 1.35 mΩ)

- I²R_DS = 6400 × 1.35e-3 = **8.64 W**
- P_cond,FETs = 2 × 8.64 = **17.28 W per channel**
- P_cu = 6400 × 0.3e-3 = **1.92 W per channel**

Per-device split at D = 0.9:
- P_HS = 0.30 × 8.64 = 2.59 W
- P_LS = 0.367 × 8.64 = 3.17 W
- Check: 3 × (2.59 + 3.17) = 17.28 W ✓

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the high-side FET hard-switches. The low side commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching transition (HS)

Switching charge:
$$Q_{sw} = Q_{gd} + \tfrac{1}{2}Q_{gs} = 16.5\ \text{nC}$$

Gate currents during the plateau:
- Turn-on: $I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = 1.3$ A
- Turn-off: $I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.7$ A

Transition times:
- $t_{on} = Q_{sw}/I_{g,on} = 12.7$ ns
- $t_{off} = Q_{sw}/I_{g,off} = 23.6$ ns

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 36.3\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.76\ W}$$
