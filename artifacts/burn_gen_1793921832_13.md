## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C:
- R_ds = 0.70 mΩ × 1.3375 = **0.936 mΩ**

Because R_ds depends on T_j, the loss and temperature must be solved together (iterated). See §3.4.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM

In any 60° sector, two phases conduct:
- **Active leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D. The synchronous low side conducts for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low side is fully on.

The current path always contains exactly two R_ds in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.936\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{11.98\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution over one electrical cycle

| FET | Conduction fraction of cycle | Avg loss at D = 0.5 |
|---|---|---|
| High side | (1/3)·D | 6.0 W × 0.167 = 1.0 W |
| Low side | 1/3 (return) + (1/3)(1−D) (sync) | 6.0 W × 0.5 = **3.0 W** (worst) |
