## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Red Flags

These constraints shape every number below.

| Item | Assumption / Finding |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Topology | H743 FC drives **four separate ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) over bidirectional DShot600. The H743 does not commutate motors directly. |

| MOSFET class | 40 V, R_DS(on),25 ≈ 1.0 mΩ, 5×6 PQFN, Q_g ≈ 60 nC, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, Q_oss ≈ 50 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC. Replace these with values from the datasheet of the part you select. |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility warning:** 4 × 80 A = **320 A battery current** at full throttle. That exceeds XT60 (60 A continuous) and XT90 ratings. It also exceeds the copper capacity of any nano-format PCB for more than a few seconds. The design below treats 80 A as a **thermally limited burst**, not a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.0 mΩ × 1.45 ≈ **1.45 mΩ** (1.5 mΩ is used below for margin).

### 1.2 Bridge conduction (6-step with synchronous rectification)

Two FETs always carry the full phase current I:
- the high-side FET, or its complementary low-side during the PWM-off time, on the PWM'd phase;
- the low-side FET on the return phase.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical period on average:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{3}$$

**At 80 A, T_j ≈ 100 °C:**

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ ESC}$$
$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Current-shunt loss (per-ESC low-side DC shunt, 0.2 mΩ)

$$P_{shunt} = (I\,D)^2 R_{sh} = 80^2 \times 0.2\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.28\ \text{W at } D = 1$$

### 1.4 Copper trace loss (phase and bus, estimated 0.3 mΩ effective per ESC)

$$P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 1.9\ \text{W}$$
