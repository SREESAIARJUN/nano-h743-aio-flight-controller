| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |

| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm ESC, 20×20 mm FC (or a 25.5 mm AIO variant) | See Flag 1 |

| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase, 3 half-bridges) | One per motor |
| PCB | 6-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes | |

**Design flags**

1. **80 A per motor × 4 = 320 A battery current.** That exceeds a single XT60 connector (about 60 A continuous) and the copper of any 20×20 board. Treat "80 A" as a **per-channel burst rating of about 1–3 s**, which is the industry convention. Continuous rating should be about 45–55 A per channel. The thermal model in §4 quantifies this.
2. **Bidirectional DShot is protocol-sensitive.** The ESC firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32) must have EDT/bidir enabled. Ground return and signal integrity at 750 kbit/s GCR telemetry matter (see §5.5).
3. **H7 D-cache.** DShot DMA buffers must live in non-cacheable, DMA-reachable SRAM. They cannot be in DTCM. §5.4 covers this.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model at 80 A Burst

### 2.1 Bridge topology during 6-step commutation

In each 60° sector:

- **Switching leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for fraction D. Its complementary low side (LS) conducts for 1 − D (synchronous rectification).
- **Static leg:** one LS FET conducts 100% of the sector.
- **Floating leg:** off (used for BEMF sensing).

The phase current flows through two FET channels at every instant, whatever the duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge}=I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[D+(1-D)+1\big]=2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each of the 6 FETs serves the switching role for 1/3 of the electrical cycle and the static role for 1/3, on average. So:

$$\overline{P}_{cond,FET}=\frac{I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}{3}$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction

Current ripple at 48 kHz with a low-inductance motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH):

$$\Delta I_{pp}=\frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}\approx\frac{(25.2-20)\cdot0.85}{2\cdot15\mu\cdot48k}\approx3.1\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}=\sqrt{I_{dc}^2+\frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}}=\sqrt{80^2+0.8}\approx80.005\ \text{A}$$

The correction is negligible at 80 A. It matters only at light load.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Quantity | T_j = 25 °C | T_j = 125 °C |
|---|---|---|
| I²R per FET instant | 6.40 W | 10.24 W |
| **Bridge conduction** (2I²R) | 12.8 W | **20.5 W** |
| **Average per FET** | 2.13 W | **3.41 W** |

### 2.4 Copper and interconnect conduction

The current path is battery pad → planes → FET → phase pad → motor wire stub. Assume 0.25 mΩ per channel (2 oz, ~15 mm effective path, vias in parallel):

$$P_{Cu}=I^2R_{Cu}=80^2\cdot0.25\text{ m}\Omega=1.6\ \text{W/channel}$$

Copper resistance rises about 0.39%/K. At +80 K it reaches about 2.1 W.
