# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Reference Architecture Model (Job #1)

## 0. Scope and Two Corrections to the Brief

1. **The 48 kHz PWM is not generated by the H743.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 / GD32E230) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM and six-step commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to them only over bi-directional DShot600. The model below keeps those two domains separate.

2. **"Nano" and "80 A" conflict thermally.** The math in §3 shows that 4 × 80 A on a 20×20 or 30×30 board is a **≤1–2 second transient rating**, not a continuous one. The realistic continuous rating is about 30–35 A per motor with prop-wash airflow. If you need more, plan for a bigger board or a heatsink. Do not lean on the marketing number.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

Six-step trapezoidal drive uses synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At any instant, exactly two phases carry the motor current I:

- **Commutating leg:** the high-side FET conducts during D·T_sw and the low-side FET during (1 − D)·T_sw. Current is always flowing through one of the two.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is continuously on.

So, ignoring dead time, two FET channel resistances are always in series with I.
