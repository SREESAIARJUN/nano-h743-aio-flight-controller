## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The standard partition for this class of product is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM telemetry consumer |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class MCU (one per channel) | AM32 (or BLHeli_32) | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM, bidir DShot slave |
| Power stage ×4 | 3-phase gate driver + 6 N-MOSFETs | none | Inverter |

So:
- The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC MCUs.
- The DShot/DMA analysis applies to the H743.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd high-side, one is held low-side ON, and one floats. With complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T and the low side for (1−D)·T. One device of the leg always carries I_ph.
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts for 100% of T.

So at any instant **exactly two channels' worth of R_DS(on) are in the current path**, independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot[\,D + (1-D)\,] + I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Per individual MOSFET, averaged over the electrical cycle (each device is active for 1/3 of the 6 sectors on average):

$$
P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{6}
$$

The **instantaneous** dissipation in the return-leg low side, however, is the full $I^2R$. This matters thermally at low eRPM, where a sector can last longer than the die's thermal time constant (a few ms).

### 2.2 Temperature-coupled solution

R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. The steady-state solution of the coupled system is:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_\theta \cdot k\,R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\;
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta k R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_\theta k R_{25}\alpha}, \quad k = I^2
$$

**Stability criterion (thermal runaway):** $R_\theta\, I^2 R_{25}\, \alpha < 1$.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

- R_DS(110 °C) = 1.0 mΩ × [1 + 0.006 × 85] = **1.51 mΩ**
- Single device fully on: 80² × 1.51 mΩ = **9.66 W**
- Per channel: 2 × 9.66 = **19.3 W**

| R_25 | R_DS(110 °C) | P_cond / channel | P_cond, 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 mΩ | 1.51 mΩ | 19.3 W | 77.3 W |
| 1.5 mΩ | 2.27 mΩ | 29.0 W | 116 W |

### 2.4 Copper conduction

**Phase trace**, 2 oz (70 µm) copper, 5 mm wide, 10 mm long, two layers in parallel:

$$
R = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.01}{2\cdot(5\times10^{-3}\cdot 70\times10^{-6})} = 0.25\ \text{m}\Omega \;(25\,°C) \rightarrow \approx 0.33\ \text{m}\Omega\ (100\,°C)
$$

- Two phase traces in the path: 2 × 80² × 0.33 mΩ ≈ **4.2 W per channel**.
- **Recommendation:** use 3 oz or 4-layer stitched copper. Copper loss is ~20% of FET conduction loss and is frequently ignored.

**Bus current:** I_bus,avg ≈ D · I_ph. At D = 0.9, all channels at 80 A gives 288 A from the battery, which is unrealistic for a nano class pack. In practice, real full-throttle phase current is limited by motor KV and prop load; 80 A is a transient value.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-leg high side switches hard. The low side commutates "soft" via its body diode, but contributes Q_rr and dead-time losses.

### 3.1 Hard-switching overlap (V–I crossover)

$$
E_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_{on}+t_{off}), \quad P_{sw} = E_{sw} f_{sw}
$$

- E_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 27 ns = **27.2 µJ**
- P_sw = 27.2 µJ × 48 kHz = **1.31 W**
