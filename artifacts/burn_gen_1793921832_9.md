## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM:
- In each 60° sector, one high-side (HS) FET is PWM-switched at duty *D*.
- Its own low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies during (1 − D).
- The return-leg LS FET is held fully on.
- Each leg spends 120° (⅓ of the electrical cycle) as the PWM leg and 120° as the return leg.

### 1.2 Per-Device RMS Duty

Averaged over one electrical revolution:

$$
P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,\frac{D}{3}
\qquad
P_{LS} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\left[\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right] = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,\frac{2-D}{3}
$$

Summing per leg gives ⅔·I²R. Summing per motor (3 legs) gives:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of D: two FETs are always in series with the winding)}
$$

Adding PCB copper:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2\,[\,2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu}\,]
$$

### 1.3 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

This makes the problem a fixed-point problem, because T_j depends on P, which depends on R_DS(T_j). Iterate as follows:

```
Tj ← T_ambient + 30
repeat:
    R  = R25·(1 + α(Tj − 25))
    P  = losses(R)                  # §1–§2
    Tj = T_board(t) + P_fet·(RθJC + RθCB)
until |ΔTj| < 0.5 K
```

Runaway occurs if ∂P/∂T_j · R_θ ≥ 1, i.e. 2I²R₂₅α·R_θ,total ≥ 1. That reduces to a closed-form check on the steady-state thermal path.
