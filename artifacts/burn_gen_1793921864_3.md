## 0. Feasibility Findings (read first)

Three findings from the analysis below affect the product definition. They should be resolved before layout.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** ArduPilot runs on the H743. Each motor channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. This document assumes 4× AT32F421K8U7 with AM32.
2. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm nano board is not thermally credible as a continuous-adjacent rating.** At 80 A per channel, the stage dissipates about **93 W** (§2.6).
   - On a 30.5×30.5 mm board with prop-wash cooling, sustainable continuous current is about **28 A per channel** (§3.4).
   - An 80 A burst is survivable for about **1–2 s** from a cool start (§3.3).
   - On 20×20 mm with 3×3 mm DFN FETs, both figures roughly halve.
   - Recommendation: either market the rating as "80 A / 1 s burst, 30 A continuous" on a 30.5 mm board, or reduce the burst claim.
3. **The 48 kHz PWM frequency costs about 1.6 W per channel** at 80 A, versus about 0.8 W at 24 kHz. It is worth keeping for low-KV/low-inductance motors (smoother current, lower acoustic noise), but it should be a tunable AM32 parameter.
