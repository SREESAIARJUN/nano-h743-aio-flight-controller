## 0. Architecture Corrections

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. A "4-in-1 ESC" on an H743 AIO is therefore:
   - the **H743 flight controller**, which sends DShot600 and receives eRPM, plus
   - **4 × ESC MCUs** running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM. The AT32F421 is recommended; the STM32G071 is an alternative.
   - Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six N-MOSFETs.

   The 48 kHz switching analysis therefore applies to the ESC MCU domain, not to the H743 timers.

2. **"Nano" form factor vs. 80 A.** 80 A burst per motor on a 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 AIO is achievable only as a short burst of seconds, as Section 3 shows. Continuous capability is roughly 25–35 A per motor with prop-wash airflow. The rating should be published as "80 A burst (≤ 5 s), 35 A continuous."

3. **Recommendation: split stack.** Put ESC heat (≈ 90 W at burst) on a separate board from the H743 and IMU. On a single PCB, IMU temperature drift and the H743 junction temperature (Tj ≤ 125 °C for the industrial grade) become limiting. If a single PCB is mandated, see Section 5.4.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant. With complementary (synchronous) PWM, the switching leg alternates HS↔LS, but **current always flows through exactly two channel resistances**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

PWM ripple adds to the RMS current:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH line-to-line, D = 0.5.

- ΔI_pp = 25.2·0.25 / (15e-6·48e3) = **8.75 A**
- ΔI²/12 = 6.4 A², which is negligible against 6400 A² (+0.1 %).

Temperature-dependent resistance (using R25 max):

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = 1.3\,\text{m}\Omega\,[1 + 0.0055(T_j - 25)]$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond (80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.84 mΩ | 23.5 W |
| 125 °C | 2.02 mΩ | 25.9 W |

**Per-FET distribution.** Each of the six FETs carries the phase current for 120° of 360° in the conducting role. With synchronous PWM, conduction splits between HS and LS of the switching leg by D and (1−D). On average:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6}$$

The worst-case FET is the LS FET at low duty in the freewheel role:

$$P_{cond,FET,max} \approx I^2 R\,\left(\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\right)$$

At D → 0.5 and Tj = 100 °C: 6400·0.00184·0.5 = **5.9 W** worst FET, versus 3.9 W average.
