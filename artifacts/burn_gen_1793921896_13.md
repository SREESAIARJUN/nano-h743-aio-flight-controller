# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #80): Electro-Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage. In a 4-in-1 design the H743 runs ArduPilot and commands four independent ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) generates the 48 kHz PWM.
