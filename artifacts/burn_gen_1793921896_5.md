## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility

**Architecture.** I assume the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, one per channel, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. These ESC MCUs (for example, AT32F421 or STM32G071) generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 cannot run ArduPilot and also commutate four BLDC motors with sensorless BEMF timing. If Job #53 assumed otherwise, that assumption needs to be corrected before layout.

**Operating point.**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (worst case, fully charged) |
| Burst current | 80 A per channel, phase/motor current |
| Switching frequency | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, trapezoidal 6-step |
| MOSFETs | 40 V, ~0.65 mΩ R_DS(on) at 25 °C, dual-cool 3.3×3.3 mm or 5×6 mm. Parameters below are representative. Substitute your chosen part's datasheet values. |

**Feasibility verdict.** 80 A per channel on a "nano" footprint (≤ 30.5 mm mount, roughly 36×36 mm board) is achievable only as a **thermally time-limited burst of a few seconds**. It is not a continuous rating. The model below quantifies that limit.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through **two conducting channels**:
- During the PWM on-time: the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- During the PWM off-time: the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous freewheel) and the low-side FET of phase B.

Therefore, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph}^2\,R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond (FETs only) at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.65 mΩ | 8.3 W |
| 100 °C | 0.94 mΩ | 12.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.04 mΩ | 13.3 W |

### 1.3 Copper resistance

R_Cu covers the phase pad, bus, and vias. A 2 oz, 8-layer stack with about 6 paralleled layers in the bus gives R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ, which adds ≈ 1.9 W.

> **Design rule:** copper loss is about 15% of FET conduction loss at this current. Via count and the solder-pad-to-wire joint matter as much as FET selection.

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point

Because R_DS(on) rises with temperature, the junction temperature must be solved iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + R_{\theta,j\text{-}pcb}\cdot P_{FET}(T_j)$$

Solve iteratively (it converges in 3–4 steps). **Thermal runaway** occurs if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_\theta \ge 1,$$

where

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T} = 2 I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha.$$

At 80 A this is ≈ 0.05 W/K per channel. Runaway is therefore not a concern for the local junction (R_θ ≈ 8 K/W gives a loop gain of 0.4). It **is** a concern for whole-board steady state, which has no equilibrium below T_j,max. See Section 3.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches. The low-side FET of the other phase is static for the whole 60° sector.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

The transition times are set by the gate driver current through the Miller plateau:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}},\qquad t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}}$$

Assumptions: Q_gs2 + Q_gd ≈ 14 nC. An FD6288-class driver delivers I_g,on ≈ 0.7 A and I_g,off ≈ 1.2 A through 3.3 Ω gate resistors. This gives t_r ≈ 20 ns and t_f ≈ 12 ns.

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 32\text{ns} \times 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.55\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{DT} = V_F\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$
