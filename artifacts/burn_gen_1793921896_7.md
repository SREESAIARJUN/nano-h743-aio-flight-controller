## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Corrections

Four points need to be clear before any math, because they change the design:

1. **The H743 does not drive MOSFETs directly.** In this topology the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each of the four motor channels is a separate ESC MCU (for example, AT32F421 running AM32, or an EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay) driving a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. This "FC + 4-in-1" stack is the only practical arrangement.
2. **"48 kHz" is the ESC PWM frequency.** It is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743, so it does not consume H743 timers.
3. **80 A on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a short-burst rating only.** The model below quantifies this. The realistic outcome is about 1 s at 80 A on all four motors simultaneously, and roughly 25–30 A continuous per motor. Any datasheet claim beyond that is marketing.
4. **MOSFET parameters are representative placeholders.** They describe a 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3, ~1.2 mΩ class device. Re-run the numbers with the datasheet of the part you actually select.
