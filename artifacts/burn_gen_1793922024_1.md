# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Thermal, Switching, and Bi-directional DShot600 Design

**Scope and assumptions.** I don't have any prior "Job #20" specification, so every parameter below is stated explicitly and kept symbolic until the final numbers. Replace the placeholder values with your MOSFET datasheet values and the formulas still hold.

**One architectural point first.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. The 48 kHz commutation and PWM run on per-motor ESC MCUs, such as AM32 on an AT32F421 or STM32G071 (or BLHeli_32-class firmware). The H743 only emits DShot600 frames and decodes eRPM telemetry. The design therefore splits into three domains:

1. **Power stage:** 4 × 3-phase bridges, 24 MOSFETs, gate drivers, bulk capacitance.
2. **ESC control:** 4 small MCUs running sensorless FOC or 6-step at f_sw = 48 kHz.
3. **FC:** H743, ArduPilot, bi-directional DShot600 over timer + DMA.
