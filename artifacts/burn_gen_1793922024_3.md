# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Allocation and Netlist

**Job #30, Rev A: Engineering Derivation Package**

---

## 0. Executive Summary

This package derives the conduction, switching and thermal models for the 80A burst rating, and the ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan.

The main finding is that **the physics does not support 80A per channel as a sustained or multi-second rating on a 30.5 mm stack-format board.**

| Result | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical loss per channel at 80A, 6S, 48 kHz | **≈ 27 W** | Sections 2–3 |
| Board total at 4 × 80A, MOSFET loss only | ≈ 109 W | Section 4 |
| Board total including copper and shunt loss | ≈ 140–150 W | Section 4 |
| Adiabatic board heating rate at 4 × 80A | **≈ 35 K/s** | Section 4.2 |
| Burst survivable from 50 °C (+55 K budget, 4 × 80A) | **≈ 1.5 s** | Section 4.2 |
| Burst survivable, single channel at 80A | ≈ 4–5 s | Section 4.2 |
| Continuous rating, 30×30 board in prop wash | **≈ 25–30 A per channel** | Section 4.3 |
| Battery current at 4 × 80A, full duty | 320 A | Exceeds any XT60 / XT90 / 10 AWG lead |

**Recommendation:** publish the rating as **"30A continuous / 80A burst (≤ 1 s, single-motor transient)."** Otherwise, move to a 36×36 or larger format with 5×6 mm FETs and a bonded heatsink.

Architecturally, the H743 does **not** commutate motors. Each channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32, and the H743 commands them over bidirectional DShot600. Direct H743 commutation of 12 half-bridges would conflict with the flight-control loop and break ArduPilot compatibility.

### Assumptions

All component parameters are representative 40 V-class trench MOSFET values.

> **Required step:** replace every parameter in Section 1 with your selected part's datasheet values and re-run the equations. The structure of the equations stays valid.
