## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle θ_m:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform, assuming balanced currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda\, i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.3 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda$$

**Gain design by pole-zero cancellation.** With decoupling, the plant is $\frac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choosing $K_p = L\,\omega_{ci}$ and $K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}$ gives a closed loop of $\frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}$.
