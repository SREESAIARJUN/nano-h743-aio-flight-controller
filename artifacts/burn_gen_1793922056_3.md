## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at startup by aligning the rotor: apply v_d > 0 with v_q = 0, wait for it to settle, then read θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant)

With i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Motor model in the dq frame (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI tuned by pole-zero cancellation

After decoupling, the plant is G_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose the controller

$$C_i(s) = K_{p,i} + \frac{K_{i,i}}{s},\qquad K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\quad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The PI zero K_i/K_p = R/L cancels the electrical pole, so the open loop becomes ω_ci/s and the closed loop is first-order with bandwidth ω_ci.

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e \lambda_m$$
