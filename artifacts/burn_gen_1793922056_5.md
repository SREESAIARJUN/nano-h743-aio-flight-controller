## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle with latency compensation

The encoder sample is stale by the SPI transfer time plus the PWM half-period. The angle is therefore extrapolated:

$$
\theta_e = p\left(\theta_m + \hat\omega_m T_d\right) - \theta_{off}, \qquad T_d \approx T_{spi} + \tfrac{1.5}{f_{pwm}}
$$
