### Architectural clarification
In a 4-in-1 ESC the H743 does **not** drive the gates. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. That MCU runs commutation, the 48 kHz PWM and dead-time insertion. The H743 sends only bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The loss model (§1–§3) therefore applies to the ESC power stage, and the timer/DMA plan (§5) applies to the H743.

## 1. Current Waveform Model (6-step, complementary PWM)

During any 60° commutation sector, two phase legs carry current:
- **PWM leg:** HS and LS alternate at f_sw with duty D (synchronous rectification).
- **Return leg:** the LS is held on for the whole sector.

At any instant, exactly **two MOSFET channels are in the current path**, except during dead time, when one path element is a body diode.

Battery current relates to phase current by:

$$I_{bat} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

At 80 A phase and D ≈ 0.85, I_bat ≈ 68 A per motor. With four motors bursting together, that is ~272 A from the pack. Size the XT60/XT90, bulk capacitance and the pack C-rating accordingly.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ |

### 2.2 Bridge conduction loss per motor

Two channels are always in series with the phase current. Dead-time intervals are removed here and counted in §3.3.

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot\left(1 - 2 t_{dt} f_{sw}\right) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

With 2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.0192 and T_j = 125 °C (design worst case):

$$P_{cond,FET} = 6400 \times 2.9\times10^{-3} \times 0.981 = \mathbf{18.2\ W}$$

### 2.3 Copper loss

For a 20×20 board, assume a phase path of ~15 mm through 2× 2 oz internal layers plus vias, giving R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ round-trip:

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.4\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{2.6\ W}$$

### 2.4 Per-FET distribution

Over a full electrical cycle, the conduction loss spreads approximately evenly across the 6 switches, about 3.0 W each. The PWM-side HS/LS pair also carries the switching loss (§3), so it is the hottest pair.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{sink}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{4.5}{4.2} = 1.07\ \text{A}$$
