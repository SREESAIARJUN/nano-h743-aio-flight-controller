# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-directional DShot Allocation and Netlist

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three points need settling before any math, because they change the numbers.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production AIO/4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot (attitude control, DShot generation, telemetry decode). Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which does the six-step commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. This document assumes 4× AT32F421 (QFN32) running AM32, each driving one FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-MOSFETs. A single-chip "H743 drives 24 FETs" design is possible in principle but has no ArduPilot support, so it is out of scope.
2. **80 A is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows that a ~25×25 mm nano board can sustain roughly 20–25 A per channel continuously in propwash. 80 A per channel is only survivable for about 1–2 s. Any datasheet claim of "80 A" on this form factor must be written as "80 A burst (≤1 s)".
3. **All device parameters are parameterized placeholders.** They are representative of the 40 V PDFN3.3 class. Replace them with the selected MOSFET's datasheet values and re-run the equations; the structure of the model does not change.
