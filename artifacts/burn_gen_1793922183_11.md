## 0. Scope and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage. It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and talks to four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC MCU runs AM32, generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, and does sensorless commutation. Every downstream number depends on the assumptions below. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths under six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, phase X is PWM-driven and phase Y is held low:

- **PWM on (duty D):** HS_X → motor → LS_Y. Two FETs conduct current I_ph.
- **PWM off (1 − D):** LS_X (synchronous freewheel) → motor → LS_Y. Two FETs conduct current I_ph.

Because complementary switching is used, **exactly two FETs carry I_ph at all times**, independent of duty cycle. Dead-time intervals are the only exception; they are treated in §2.3.
