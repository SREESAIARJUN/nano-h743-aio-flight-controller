# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Job #65: Mathematical Model, Allocation and Netlists

**Document status:** Engineering analysis, revision A

## Executive Verdict

The model below shows that **80 A per motor on a nano (20×20 mm mount, ~25×25 mm board) 4-in-1 is a transient rating of about 2 seconds**, not a sustained one. With 4 × 80 A, the board dissipates about 88 W. The steady-state electro-thermal loop is unstable, with a gain of about 1.8, which exceeds the stability limit of 1. The deliverables below are complete. Section 6 gives the architectural changes required to make the rating honest.

**Architecture clarification.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 as the flight controller. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The 4-in-1 ESC therefore uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32. This design uses the AT32F421K8U7, and each MCU drives one 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. "48 kHz" is the AM32 PWM frequency.
