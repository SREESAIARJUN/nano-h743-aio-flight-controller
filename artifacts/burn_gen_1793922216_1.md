## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Phase currents are measured by low-side shunts, with i_c = −i_a − i_b.

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park,** with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI
The dq model of a surface PMSM is:
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Apply cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:
$$v_d = PI_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

With these terms the plant reduces to 1/(Ls+R). Choosing pole-zero cancellation gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:
$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \quad(\text{so } K_i/K_p = R/L)$$

Constraint: ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/20. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1000 rad/s.

- With L = 2 mH, K_p ≈ 12.6 V/A.
- With R = 5.6 Ω, K_i ≈ 35 200 V/(A·s).
- λ_m = Kt/(1.5·p).

### 1.3 Voltage limiting and SVPWM
The vector magnitude is clamped to the linear SVPWM range: |v| ≤ V_dc/√3.

Priority goes to v_d, then v_q:
$$v_q^{\max} = \sqrt{(V_{dc}/\sqrt3)^2 - v_d^2}$$

**SVPWM via min-max zero-sequence injection** (mathematically equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM):
$$v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$
$$v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$

### 1.4 Anti-windup (back-calculation)
$$\dot x_I = K_i e + K_{aw}(u_{sat} - u_{unsat}),\qquad K_{aw}\approx K_i/K_p$$
