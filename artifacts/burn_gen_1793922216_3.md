- Current loop: ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s
- Velocity loop: ω_v ≈ 2π·150 rad/s
- Angle loop: ω_θ ≈ 2π·25 rad/s

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform**:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Decoupling

The dq-frame plant (surface-mounted PM, L_d = L_q = L):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

The controller output, with feedforward cross-coupling cancellation:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

### 1.3 PI Gains by Pole-Zero Cancellation

After decoupling, each axis is a first-order plant 1/(R + Ls). Placing the PI zero on the plant pole gives:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{I}{I^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

**Discretization** (forward Euler on the integrator) with sample period T_s = 1/f_PWM, plus back-calculation anti-windup:

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad u^{sat}_k=\mathrm{sat}(u_k),\quad
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(u^{sat}_k - u_k),\quad K_{aw}\approx \frac{K_i T_s}{K_p}$$

**Voltage limiting.** The dq voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon-inscribed circle, |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3. v_d is given priority; v_q receives the remainder.

### 1.4 SVPWM by Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection

This method is equivalent to classical 7-segment SVPWM.

Phase voltages:

$$v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

Zero-sequence offset:

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big)$$

Duty cycles:

$$d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.5 Delay Compensation

Transport delay is 1.5·T_s: one sample of computation plus half a sample for PWM hold. Advance the angle used in the inverse Park transform accordingly:

$$\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

## 2. Cascaded Velocity and Angle Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant per Axis

$$J\dot\omega = K_t\,i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{d}(t)$$

The disturbance τ_d includes cable drag, imbalance m·g·r·sin φ, and base-motion coupling.

For a stabilized gimbal, the controlled variable is the **inertial** rate or angle of the camera, measured by the IMU. The **encoder** provides commutation and the cogging angle, and serves as a fallback.
