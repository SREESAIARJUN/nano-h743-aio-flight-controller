# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Vibration Rejection

I have no project files for Job #30, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every parameter that depends on your hardware is a named constant. Change those and the equations still hold.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC (e.g., GBM2804), 7 pole pairs, 12 slots, R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Current sensing | 2-phase inline or low-side shunts (voltage-mode fallback in §2.6) |
| Position | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per joint (AS5048A/MT6701 class) |
| IMU-P | ICM-42688-P on the **payload** (camera plate). Used for stabilization feedback. |
| IMU-B | ICM-42688-P on the **base/frame
