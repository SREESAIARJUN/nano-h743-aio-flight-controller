## 1. System Architecture

```
            ┌──────────── 1 kHz ────────────┐ ┌──── 4 kHz ────┐ ┌──────── 20 kHz ────────┐
 θ_ref ──►(+)─► Angle P(+I) ──► ω_ref ──►(+)─► Velocity PI ──►(+)─► i_q* ─► Current PI d/q ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
           ▲  + ω_ref,ff                 ▲  + τ_ff/Kt        ▲ + i_cog(θ_m)        ▲
           │                             │                   │                     │
     θ̂ (Mahony, dual IMU)       ω̂ = blend(gyro, encoder PLL) │       i_abc, θ_e (encoder)
                                                    Anti-cogging Fourier LMS
```

Two principles drive the design:

- **Bandwidth separation.** Each loop runs about 4–10× slower than the loop inside it: ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1500, ω_c,v ≈ 2π·150, ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·30 rad/s.
- **Gyro-based rate feedback.** The velocity loop uses the gyro (projected onto the joint axis) as its primary rate signal, because it has low latency and measures inertial rate directly. The encoder rate is used at low frequency and for commutation.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here p is the number of pole pairs and θ₀ is the electrical zero offset found at calibration.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform uses the transpose of this matrix.

### 2.2 Plant and PI Design

The machine equations in the dq frame are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

With decoupling feedforward, each axis reduces to the first-order plant $\frac{1}{Ls+R}$. Placing the PI zero on the plant pole (pole-zero cancellation) gives a closed loop that is first order with bandwidth ω_c,i:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{c,i}$$

The voltage feedforward terms are:

$$v_{d,ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_{q,ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$
