# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Stabilization, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

**Job #44 design document, Rev A**

> **Assumptions.** I don't have the Job #44 spec sheet, so motor and inertia values are placeholders in `GimbalConfig`. I assumed:
> - Three hollow-shaft gimbal BLDCs (12N14P, ~10 Ω phase, 14-bit absolute encoder per axis).
> - Yaw→Roll→Pitch mechanical order.
> - Two ICM-42688-P on the camera stage over SPI.
> - STM32G4/H7-class MCU with FPU.
>
> Every equation is parametric, so you can drop in your measured values.
