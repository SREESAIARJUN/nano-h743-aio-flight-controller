## 0. Critical Scoping Findings

Three issues affect the architecture, so they come first.

| # | Finding | Impact | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 cannot be the commutation controller for 4 motors and also run ArduPilot. ArduPilot's scheduler is not designed for hard-real-time 6-step/FOC commutation of 12 half-bridges. | Architectural | **Two-tier design.** The H743 is the flight controller and emits bidirectional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) perform commutation. Both tiers share one PCB stack. |
| F2 | 80 A burst per motor in a "nano" (20×20 mm mount) footprint is not thermally credible. The derivation below shows ≈86 W total dissipation at full burst on all four channels. | Feasibility | Either (a) define the burst as **≤5 s, single-digit duty**, or (b) move to a 30.5×30.5 mm power board with the FC stacked at 20×20. This document models both. |
| F3 | All device parameters below are **assumed representative values**, not datasheet-verified for a specific part number. | Accuracy | Re-run the model with the selected MOSFET's datasheet values before layout freeze. Every equation is parameterized for this. |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\bigl[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\bigr], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1} \;\Rightarrow\; R_{ds}(125^\circ\text{C}) = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Conduction path in 6-step operation

In any commutation sector, two phase legs are active:

- The **PWM leg** has its high side on for duration D and its synchronous low side on for (1−D).
- The **return leg** has its low side on continuously.

Phase current flows through exactly **two** MOSFET channels at every instant. Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

At 80 A and 125 °C:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$

### 2.3 Per-device loss (needed for junction temperature)

Each MOSFET conducts for about 120° of every 360° electrical cycle. The instantaneous loss in a conducting device is I²R = 9.6 W. Averaged over the electrical cycle:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\times 9.6 = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

The switching node adds to this, as shown in Section 3.

### 2.4 Parasitic conduction

| Element | Expression | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt | I²R_shunt | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W** |
| PCB copper (2 oz inner/outer, ≈0.25 mΩ/sq, ≈3 squares per phase path) | I²R_Cu | 6400 × 0.75 mΩ ≈ **4.8 W** |
| Solder joints and pads (estimate) | | ≈0.5 W |

**Recommendation:** use 3 oz outer and 2 oz × 4 inner layers with paralleled phase pours to bring R_Cu below 0.3 mΩ, which reduces copper loss to about 1.9 W.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. In synchronous mode, hard switching occurs on the high side, and the low side commutates through its body diode during dead time.

### 3.1 V–I overlap loss (hard switching, high side)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 35\times10^{-9} \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{1.69\ W}$$

### 3.2 Output-capacitance loss (both devices in the leg)

$$P_{oss} = 2 \times \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss(er)} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.2\times10^{-9} \times 635 \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{0.037\ W}$$

### 3.3 Reverse-recovery loss

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\times10^{-9} \times 25.2 \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\times10^{-9} \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 3.5 Gate-drive loss (dissipated in the gate driver, not the FET)

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\times10^{-9} \times 10 \times 48\times10^{3} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$
