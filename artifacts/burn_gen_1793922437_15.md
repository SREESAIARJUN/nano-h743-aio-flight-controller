## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal with complementary PWM

At any instant, two phases carry current I:

- The PWM'd leg: high-side conducts for D, low-side (synchronous rectification) for 1 − D.
- The return leg: its low-side conducts for 100% of the interval.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I^2 R_{ds}\cdot 1 = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Synchronous rectification makes conduction loss **independent of duty cycle**.
