# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC Stack (Job #46): Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-DShot Timer/DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Three issues in the brief affect the whole design, so I'm addressing them first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot sends DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM, commutation and gate drive run on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32 on AT32F421, or BLHeli_32-class hardware). The H743 cannot run six-step or FOC on four motors under ArduPilot. The architecture is therefore **H743 FC board + 4× (ESC MCU + 3 half-bridges) on a stacked 4-in-1 ESC board**.
2. **"Nano" and 80 A burst conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A, mechanically (FET footprint, 2×12 FETs) or thermally (§3). The minimum credible format is **30.5×30.5 mm mounting (≈36×38 mm PCB)**. All calculations below use that size. If 20×20 is a hard requirement, the realistic rating is about 35–45 A burst.
3. **No MOSFET was specified.** I parametrized everything and used a representative 40 V, 5×6 mm trench FET. Swap in datasheet values to rerun the numbers.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha=\frac{k_T-1}{100}=0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.44 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous bridge
In trapezoidal (six-step) drive with complementary/synchronous PWM, the current flows through exactly two FETs at every instant:

- **PWM on (D):** HS_x and LS_y conduct.
- **PWM off (1−D):** LS_x (synchronous freewheel) and LS_y conduct.

Conduction loss per bridge is therefore independent of duty (dead time is handled separately):

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,bridge}=2(80)^2(1.44\,\text{m}\Omega)=\mathbf{18.4\ W}\ \ (\text{at }T_j=125^\circ C)$$

At T_j = 25 °C the same calculation gives 11.5 W.

**Per-FET average.** Each FET carries current for ⅓ of an electrical revolution, so:

$$P_{cond,FET}\approx \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{DS} = 3.1\ \text{W}$$

The low-side FETs of the PWM leg carry an extra (1−D) share. Worst case at low duty, the low-side gets roughly ⅔ of I²R, which is **≈6.1 W**.

**Stall/low-eRPM caveat.** At very low eRPM the commutation period exceeds the FET thermal time constant (~10–50 ms). One FET pair then sees the full I²R_DS = 9.2 W. The startup ramp must current-limit accordingly.

### 1.3 PCB copper loss (not negligible at 80 A)
$$R_{Cu}=\frac{\rho\,L}{w\,t}\,(1+0.0039\,\Delta T)$$

Take 3 oz outer plus 2 oz inner layers in parallel, with an effective path of L ≈ 15 mm, w ≈ 6 mm, t_eff ≈ 175 µm. This gives R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ per conductor, or ~0.5 mΩ round trip.

$$P_{Cu}= I^2 R_{Cu} \approx 80^2(0.5\,\text{m}\Omega)= \mathbf{3.2\ W}\ \text{per motor}$$

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg's high-side hard-switches. The synchronous low-side switches at near-zero V_DS after the body diode clamps (ZVS-like).

### 2.1 Switching transition times
$$Q_{sw}=Q_{gd}+\tfrac12 Q_{gs}=17.5\ \text{nC}$$

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{7}{6}=1.17\text{ A}\Rightarrow t_{on}=15.0\text{ ns}$$

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{snk}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{3}{3}=1.0\text{ A}\Rightarrow t_{off}=17.5\text{ ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss components (per motor bridge)

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V_bus·I·(t_on+t_off)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·32.5 ns·48 k = **1.57 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V_bus·Q_rr·f_sw | 25.2·40 nC·48 k = **0.05 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss
