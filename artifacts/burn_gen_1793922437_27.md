# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #15): Design Model

## 0. Architecture and Feasibility

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 frames. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 (AT32F421 or STM32G071), which generates the 48 kHz PWM.

```
 ┌──────────── FC board (stack layer 1) ─────────────┐
 │ STM32H743 (TFBGA100) ── IMU/Baro/OSD/Flash/UARTs  │
 │   M1..M4 DShot600 bidir ──────────────┐           │
 └───────────────────────────────────────┼───────────┘
 ┌──────────── ESC board (stack layer 2) ┼───────────┐
 │ 4× AM32 MCU ── 4× 3φ gate driver ── 24× MOSFET     │
 │ shunt + amp, VBAT bulk caps, BEC                  │
 └────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Feasibility verdict.** "80A burst" per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) board is not thermally sustainable. The model below shows that the whole board absorbs roughly 130 W during a 4×80 A burst. Adiabatically, that limits bursts to about 1–2 s. Treat 80 A as a short transient rating. The continuous rating for this size is about 25–35 A per channel. I give the equations in parameterized form, so you can substitute your selected FET's datasheet values.

**Baseline assumptions**

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge |
| I | 80 A | motor phase current per channel (burst) |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | AM32 PWM frequency |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, complementary PWM)

In each 60° sector:
- One leg's high-side (HS) FET PWMs at duty D, with its synchronous low-side (LS) FET conducting during (1−D).
- Another leg's LS FET is on continuously.

Over one electrical cycle, each leg spends 120° as the PWM leg and 120° as the low-on leg.

**Per-FET average loss**

$$P_{HS}=\tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{LS}=\tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2R_{ds}+\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}=\tfrac{2-D}{3}I^2R_{ds}$$

**Per bridge (3 legs)**

$$P_{cond}=3\left[\tfrac{D}{3}+\tfrac{2-D}{3}\right]I^2R_{ds}=\boxed{2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}$$

This result is independent of D, because two FETs are always in series with the phase current.

**Temperature dependence**

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × 1.45 = 2.18 mΩ.

**Numbers (80 A, T_j = 125 °C)**
- P_cond,FET = 2 × 80² × 2.18 mΩ = **27.9 W per ESC**
- Copper: 2 × 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **3.8 W per ESC**
- Per-FET loss at D ≈ 1 (full-throttle burst): P_HS = P_LS = 6400 × 2.18 mΩ / 3 ≈ **4.65 W per FET**
