# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #31): Loss, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Package

---

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

A "nano" form factor and 80 A burst per channel pull against each other. This package therefore states every assumption explicitly, so the numbers can be re-run with your actual BOM.

### 0.1 Architecture

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421). Each ESC MCU runs 48 kHz complementary PWM into a three-phase gate driver and six N-FETs.

```
 6S LiPo ──┬── Bulk C ── 4× [ESC MCU → 3φ gate driver → 6× N-FET bridge] → Motors 1-4
           │                      ▲ BDShot600 (1 wire/motor, 3.3 V)
           └── Buck 5V/3V3 ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── TIM1 CH1-4 + DMA
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path

With six-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous rectification, two FETs carry phase current at every instant:

- the PWM'ing leg's high-side FET (during D·T_sw) or its complementary low-side FET (during (1−D)·T_sw), and
- the return leg's low-side FET, which is held on for the full 60° sector.

Both paths have the same R_ds(on), so the duty cycle cancels out:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

Each FET conducts during roughly one-third of the electrical cycle, so the average loss per FET is:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot k_{imb},\quad k_{imb}\approx1.3
$$

The imbalance factor k_imb covers the case where the low-side FET carries more current at low duty cycle.

### 1.2 Electrothermal Fixed Point

Losses depend on temperature, and temperature depends on losses. Write the loss as P(T) = P₀[1 + α(T − 25)], with P₀ = 2I²R₂₅, and T = T_ref + P(T)·R_th. This gives a closed-form solution:

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + P_0 R_{th}(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha P_0 R_{th}}
$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:** α·P₀·R_th ≥ 1. Design to keep this below 0.3.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | P per FET (with k_imb) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 4.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.60 mΩ | **20.5 W** | **4.4 W** |

At 35 A continuous and 100 °C: P_cond = 35² × 2 × 1.45 mΩ = **3.55 W per channel**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'ing half-bridge switches.

### 2.1 V-I Overlap Loss

$$
t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} = \frac{8\,\text{nC}}{1\,\text{A}} = 8\,\text{ns per edge}
$$

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(16\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{0.77\,W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse Recovery Loss

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\,\text{W}
$$

The added current overshoot (I_rr) increases turn-on loss by roughly 10%, which is included in the total below.

### 2.3 Output Capacitance Loss

$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = 0.036\,\text{W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot100\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = 0.31\,\text{W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate Drive Loss

Six FETs are present, but only two toggle in any sector. The loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors.

$$
P_g = Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}\cdot 2 = 45\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}\cdot2 = 0.043\,\text{W}
$$

### 2.6 Channel Loss Budget at 80 A and T_j = 125 °C

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction | 20.5 |
| V-I overlap | 0.85 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 |
| Gate | 0.04 |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ) | 1.6 |
| PCB copper (~0.4 mΩ path) | 2.6 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 26 W** |
| **4 channels** | **≈ 104 W** |

**Key insight:** at 80 A, switching loss is only about 5% of the total. Conduction and copper dominate. Raising f_sw
