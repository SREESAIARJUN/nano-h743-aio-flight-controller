# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Model

> **Scope and assumptions.** I don't have the Job #27 requirements docs, BOM, or layout, so this model is fully **parametric**. Every worked number uses a stated representative component, and you should substitute datasheet values for your selected parts. Assumptions:
> - **Pack:** 6S LiPo, 25.2 V maximum.
> - **Form factor:** 30.5 × 30.5 mm stack.
> - **ESC firmware:** AM32 or BLHeli_32-class, running on per-ESC MCUs.
> - **H743 role:** the H743 is the flight controller and drives DShot to the ESC MCUs. It does not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot does not support H7-native motor commutation.

---

## 1. Electrical Operating Model

**Commutation.** The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM at f_sw = 48 kHz. At any instant, two phase legs conduct:
- **Leg A (PWM):** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty D and the low-side (LS) for (1−D), synchronous rectification.
- **Leg B (static):** the LS is fully on.
- Each leg holds each role for 1/3 of the electrical cycle.

**Representative MOSFET** (40 V, 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 class):

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 0.90 mΩ |
| Tempco | α | 0.006 /K |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Body diode V_f @ 80 A | V_f | 0.80 V |
| Junction–case | R_θJC | 0.5 K/W |
| Rise/fall with 2 A driver | t_r, t_f | 20 ns, 20 ns |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Instantaneous per-motor conduction loss.** In Leg A, either the HS or the LS carries I at every instant, so its duty terms sum to 1. Leg B's LS is always on. This gives:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2I^2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Including copper.** The PCB copper path from the battery pad to the phase pad adds R_Cu:

$$P_{cond,total} = 2I^2R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2R_{Cu}$$

**Per-device average over an electrical cycle.**
- **HS device:** I²R·D/3
- **LS device:** I²R·[(1−D)/3 + 1/3]

Low-side FETs therefore run hotter at low duty. Use this result to place the LS devices on the best copper.

**Worked example at T_j = 110 °C:**
- R_DS = 0.90 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.36 mΩ**
- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.36 mΩ = **17.4 W per motor**
- R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ (2 oz, 6-layer, paralleled pours) adds +1.9 W
- **Total: ≈ 19.3 W per motor**

Note that R_DS depends on T_j, so the loss and temperature must be solved together (see §5.4).

## 3. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Switching losses occur only in Leg A, the PWM leg.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value @ 25.2 V, 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | 0.5·25.2·80·40 ns·48k = **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48k = **0.048 W** |
| Coss (E_oss ≈ ½Q_oss·V) | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | **0.024 W** |
| Body-diode dead time | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (HS + LS) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·50 nC·10·48k = **0.048 W** (lost in the driver, not the FETs) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.67 W per motor** |

**Optimization notes:**
- **Overlap loss** scales as V·I·f. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1 W per motor, but increases current ripple and acoustic noise.
- **Dead-time loss** is the second-largest term. Trim t_dt to the minimum safe value. Each 50 ns removed saves about 0.15 W.
- **Ripple check:** ΔI = V(1−D)D / (L_phase·f_sw). With L = 20 µH and D = 0.5, ΔI ≈ 6.6 A pk-pk. This is acceptable, and the RMS correction to conduction loss is under 0.3 %.

## 4. Total Power Budget

| Item | Per motor | 4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET + Cu) | 19.3 W | 77.2 W |
| Switching | 2.7 W | 10.7 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-motor) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **ESC total** | **23.6 W** | **≈ 94 W** |
| FC (H743 at 480 MHz, regulators, OSD) | – | ≈ 1.5 W |

**Efficiency check:** output power ≈ 25.2 V × 80 A × 4 × D_avg. At D ≈ 0.85, this is about 6.9 kW, so ESC efficiency is about 98.6 %. The efficiency is fine; **heat density is the real constraint.**

## 5. Thermal Model

### 5.1 Network (per-motor quadrant, lumped Cauer/Foster hybrid)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC 0.5K/W]── T_case ──[R_pad→pour ~2.5K/W per FET]── T_pcb ──[R_conv]── T_amb
          C_j ≈ 0.05 J/K            C_pcb (shared)
```

### 5.2 Board-Level Transient

**Heat capacity.**
- Board mass: 30.5² mm, 6L, 2 oz, with FETs ≈ 9 g.
- Specific heat: c_eff ≈ 0.75 J/g·K.
- Board total: C ≈ 6.8 J/K.
- Adding a capacitor bank and connectors gives **C ≈ 8 J/K**.

**Convection in prop wash.**
- h ≈ 75 W/m²K
- A ≈ 2 × 0.0305² ≈ 1.86×10⁻³ m²
- R_conv = 1/(hA) ≈ **7.2 K/W**

**Time constant:** τ = R_conv·C ≈ 58 s

**Transient response:**

$$\Delta T_{pcb}(t) = P\,R_{conv}\,(1-e^{-t/\tau}) \approx \frac{P\,t}{C}\quad(t\ll\tau)$$

### 5.3 Burst Limits (P = 94 W, T_amb = 35 °C)

| Burst | ΔT_pcb | T_pcb | T_j (adds ≈ 3.9 W/FET × 3 K/W ≈ 12 K) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 s | 23 K | 58 °C | 70 °C |
| 5 s | 55 K | 90 °C | 102 °C |
| 8 s | 85 K | 120 °C | 132 °C |
| Steady state | 677 K | n/a | **not survivable** |

**Conclusion:** 80 A is valid only as a **≤ 5 s burst** from a ≤ 50 °C start. The sustainable continuous rating, solving P·R_conv ≤ 70 K, is about 9.7 W total. Back-solving through the 2I²R model gives about **≈ 22–25 A continuous per motor** without a heatsink. Specify the product as "25 A continuous / 80 A 5 s burst." Otherwise, add a heatsink to lower R_conv, or add a ~1.5 mm aluminum spreader, which adds roughly 4 J/K and extends the burst by about 50 %.

### 5.4 Electrothermal Convergence

R_DS(on) depends on T_j and T_j depends on loss, so iterate:

T_j(n+1) = T_amb + P(T_j(n))·R_θ,total
