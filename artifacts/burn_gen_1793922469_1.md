## 0. Architecture Decisions and Constraints

Several points in the brief need to be settled before the math, because they change the results.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate 4 BLDC motors (24 gates at 48 kHz with BEMF sensing) while also running ArduPilot. | **Two-tier architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7, AM32 firmware) each drive one motor. DShot600 runs H743 → ESC MCU. This is the only arrangement in which "bidirectional DShot" is meaningful. |
| 2 | A 20×20 mm board cannot hold an H743, the IMU, 24 MOSFETs, and 4 gate drivers. | **Two-board 20×20 stack.** The FC board (H743) sits over the ESC board (24× 3.3×3.3 mm DFN). The ESC board uses 216 of its 400 mm² for FETs. |
| 3 | "80 A" is ambiguous. | Defined here as **80 A motor phase current per channel**, short burst. Section 3 shows that the burst must be time-limited to roughly 1–3 s. |
| 4 | Supply voltage | 6S LiPo: 22.2 V nominal, **25.2 V worst case** for switching loss. |
