### 0.1 Architectural facts that constrain the design

1. **Two-MCU architecture.** In an "H743 4-in-1" AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. Each ESC channel, or one quad-ESC MCU, runs AM32 or BLHeli and generates the 48 kHz PWM.
   - The 48 kHz switching model belongs to the ESC firmware domain.
   - The DShot/DMA model belongs to the H743/ArduPilot domain.
2. **80 A on a nano footprint is a short-burst rating, not a continuous one.** The math in §4 shows a few seconds of 4 × 80 A drives the board past safe temperature. The rating must be defined with a time limit and a current definition.
   - Battery current equals the duty cycle D times phase current.
   - Most marketing figures do not state which current they mean.
3. **Thermal isolation of the H743.** The H743 (T_A max 85 °C for industrial grade) should sit on a separate stacked board or behind a thermal break. It should not share copper pours with the power stage.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

| T_j | R_ds(on) (mΩ) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 |
| 100 °C | 1.49 |
| 125 °C | 1.65 |

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step with complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, current flows from the PWM leg to the return leg:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) switch conducts for D·T. The synchronous low-side (LS) switch conducts for (1−D)·T, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the LS switch conducts 100 % of the sector.

So, ignoring dead time, **exactly two channels carry I_ph at every instant**:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of D)}$$

Per-switch time-averaged loss over one electrical revolution:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R,\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2R + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R$$

The worst case is the LS switch at low D: up to (2/3)·I²R.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

Assume T_j = 110 °C, which gives R = 1.55 mΩ.

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2(80)^2(1.55\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.8\ W}$$

$$P_{LS,max} = \tfrac{2}{3}(6400)(1.55\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{6.6\ W}\ \text{per FET (worst)}$$

### 1.4 Additional conduction terms

| Element | Model | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Copper/pad/via path (≈0.3 mΩ per ESC) | I²R_cu | 1.9 W |
| Current shunt (if per-ESC, 0.25 mΩ) | I²R_sh | 1.6 W |
| DC-link capacitor ESR | I_C,rms² · ESR | see below |

**DC-link capacitor ripple.** The input ripple current is

$$I_{C,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

This reaches **40 A rms at D = 0.5**.

- With 10 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 capacitors (ESR ≈ 3 mΩ each, 0.3 mΩ in parallel), the loss is 0.5 W.
- This also explains why an external low-ESR electrolytic (≥ 470 µF/35 V) at the battery pads is mandatory.
