# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #19): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlist

## 0. Architecture and Feasibility

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware). Each ESC MCU drives one 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. This split is how every production "FC + 4-in-1" stack works. It keeps the H743's timers free for DShot and its CPU free for EKF3 and the rate loops.

**Feasibility verdict.** The model below shows that 80 A per motor on all four motors simultaneously is possible on a 20×20 mm board for only about 1.2 s. The thermally sustainable continuous rating with prop-wash cooling is about 35–40 A per motor. The datasheet should state "80 A burst (≤1 s, single-pulse, T_amb = 40 °C)" and "35 A continuous". Claiming more is a field-failure liability.

**Package.** Use the STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm). LQFP100 (16×16 mm with leads) does not fit a 20×20 board alongside the gyro and power stage.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous PWM

During each 60° electrical sector, phase A (PWM leg) and phase B (return leg) conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The low-side FET conducts the freewheel current synchronously for (1 − D)·T_sw.
