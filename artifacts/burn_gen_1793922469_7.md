### 0.1 Feasibility Flags

| # | Issue | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | "Nano" (20×20 mm) and 80 A/channel are physically incompatible. A 20×20 board cannot dissipate ~85 W, and cannot carry 320 A of combined phase current through its copper. | Baseline is a **30.5×30.5 mm stack-compatible AIO** with a separate 4-in-1 power stage. A 20×20 variant is derated in §3.7. |
| F2 | The H743 does not commutate motors directly in standard ArduPilot designs. | Architecture: **H743 runs ArduPilot** and sends bidirectional DShot600 to **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-compatible firmware. |
| F3 | "80 A burst" is interpreted as **80 A per channel for ≤10 s**, with 4 channels simultaneously as the worst case. | Burst duration limit is derived in §3.6. |
