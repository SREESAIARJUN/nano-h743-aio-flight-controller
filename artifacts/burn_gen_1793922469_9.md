# Job #11: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), System Architecture and Mathematical Model

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Gate

Three constraints shape everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 ESC each channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends **bidirectional DShot600** to those four ESC MCUs. The 48 kHz switching is an ESC-firmware parameter (AM32 `PWM frequency`), not an H743 timer.

2. **80 A on a nano board (20×20 or 30.5×30.5 mm) is a burst rating only.** The model below shows that:
   - continuous capability is about 45 A per channel, even with good propwash;
   - an 80 A burst on all four channels at once is thermally limited to about **6–7 s**.
