# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #77): Reference Architecture and Analysis

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have Job #77's actual BOM, stackup, or mechanical drawings, so this is a **parametric reference design**. Every equation is general. The worked numbers use the assumptions below, and the parameter table (§5) lets you substitute your real values.

**Architectural requirement: the design needs separate ESC MCUs.** ArduPilot on the H743 does not commutate motors. It outputs DShot. A "4-in-1 ESC" on the same board therefore needs **four ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071. Bi-directional DShot runs over on-board traces between the H743 and those MCUs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly **two MOSFET channels in series**:
- the PWM'd leg, where current alternates between the high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) FETs according to duty D, and
- the LS FET of the return phase, which stays on for the whole 60° sector.

Body-diode conduction only occurs during dead time and is counted under switching (§1.3).
