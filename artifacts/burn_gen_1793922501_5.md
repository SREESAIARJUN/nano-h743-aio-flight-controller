## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Some assumptions behind the numbers need to be stated explicitly, because a few of them change the answer materially.

| Item | Decision / Assumption |
|---|---|
| Partitioning | The **STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only**. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32). | Bus voltage | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. Calculations use **25.2 V** (worst case for switching loss). |

| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM, 48 kHz, dead time 200 ns. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path topology

In 6-step commutation with synchronous PWM, phase current at any instant flows through exactly **two** conducting devices:

- **Switched leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts during D·T_s, and the low-side (LS) synchronous rectifier conducts during (1−D)·T_s.
- **Fixed leg:** the LS stays on for the full 60° commutation sector.

Total conduction loss per channel, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS,fixed} \right] = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS(on),T_j}
$$

(This assumes matched HS and LS devices.)

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Phase inductance L_ph ≈ 10 µH (typical 2207–2810 class motor) gives a peak-to-peak ripple of:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}} \Big|_{D=0.5} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{10^{-5}\cdot 48000} = 13.1\ \text{A}
$$

The RMS current then becomes:

$$
I_{rms} = I_{avg}\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I_{avg}}\right)^2} = 80\sqrt{1 + \frac{(0.164)^2}{12}} = 80.09\ \text{A}
$$

The correction is negligible at 80 A. It matters only at light load.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 115 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 90) = **1.50 mΩ**.

### 1.4 Result

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \cdot (80.09)^2 \cdot 1.50\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.24\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

### 1.5 PCB copper loss (frequently ignored, not negligible)

2 oz copper has a sheet resistance of R_□ ≈ 0.245 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, rising to about 0.31 mΩ/□ at 90 °C.

- Assume the phase path is ~1.5 squares effective per layer.
- Assume 4 layers are paralleled for power.

This gives R_Cu ≈ 1.5 × 0.31 / 4 ≈ 0.116 mΩ in each of the out and return paths:

$$
P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 0.116\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.49\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Transition times

Miller plateau drive current: I_g ≈ (V_drv − V_plateau)/(R_g,ext + R_g,int) = (12 − 4.5)/(4.7 + 1.0) ≈ 1.3 A, which the driver limits to ~1.0 A source.

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,src}} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{1.0\,\text{A}} = 10\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,snk}} \approx 7\ \text{ns}
$$

Add V–I overlap from Q_gs2 and layout inductance (L_loop ≈ 3 nH). The realistic total is **t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns**. Use measured values once you have double-pulse test data.
