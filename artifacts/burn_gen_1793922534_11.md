## 1. Plant Models

**Electrical model (rotor dq frame, surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L):**

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_m = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q,\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}
