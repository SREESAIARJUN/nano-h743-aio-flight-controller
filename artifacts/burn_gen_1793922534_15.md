**Bandwidth separation.** Target closed-loop bandwidths:

| Loop | Bandwidth |
|---|---|

That gives roughly a 10× separation per stage. The velocity bandwidth is capped by:
- the gyro filter group delay (§4.6), and
- the first structural resonance of the gimbal arms, which must be measured.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: apply V_d with θ_e = 0, wait for the rotor to settle, and read the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Motor model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

Each axis has plant $G_i(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$. Choose the PI zero at $R/L$:

$$C_i(s) = K_{p,i} + \frac{K_{i,i}}{s},\qquad K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

The open loop then becomes $\omega_c/s$, so the closed loop is a first-order lag $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

**Delay limit.** Computation plus PWM delay is about $1.5T_s$. To keep the phase margin above 45°:

$$\omega_c \lesssim \frac{\pi/4}{1.5\,T_s} = 2\pi\cdot 1667\text{ Hz at }20\text{ kHz}$$
