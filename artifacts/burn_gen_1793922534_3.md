## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, using electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park is the transpose.

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

The surface-PMSM d/q voltage equations are:

$$
v_d = R_s i_d + L_s\dot i_d - \omega_e L_s i_q,\qquad
v_q = R_s i_q + L_s\dot i_q + \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

with $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}K_t/p$.

The feed-forward decoupling terms $v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L_s i_q$ and $v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L_s i_d + \lambda_m)$ reduce each axis to $G(s)=1/(L_s s + R_s)$.
