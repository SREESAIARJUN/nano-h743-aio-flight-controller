# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #28 requirements, so this model rests on the stated assumptions below. Every result is given as a formula first, so you can substitute real datasheet values.

**Architecture clarification.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In an AIO board:
- The **H743 runs ArduPilot** and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (or one quad-ESC MCU).
- **ESC MCUs** (assumed AT32F421 running AM32, one per channel) generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.
- The switching-loss model applies to the ESC side. The DMA and timer model applies to the H743 side.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
With complementary PWM in 6-step commutation, current at every instant flows through exactly **two conducting MOSFETs**:
- the PWM leg's high side (duty D) or its synchronous low side (1−D), and
- the steady low side of the return leg.

Body-diode conduction occurs only during dead time (see §2.4).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx \frac{k_T-1}{100} = 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.3 Channel conduction loss (independent of D under synchronous rectification)

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

**Copper path resistance.** For 2 oz copper (ρ_□ ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□) and roughly 2 squares effective per phase path, plus vias and the pad: R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ.

**At 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:**

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 2 × 6400 × 2.4 mΩ | **30.7 W** |
| Copper | 6400 × 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |
| **Channel conduction total** | | **33.9 W** |

### 1.4 Per-device RMS current
Each phase conducts 240° of 360°, so the phase RMS current is:

$$I_{ph,rms} = I\sqrt{2/3} = 65.3\ \text{A}$$

Averaged over an electrical revolution, the loss is distributed across the 6 FETs. The average per FET is ≈ P_cond/6 ≈ 5.1 W.
