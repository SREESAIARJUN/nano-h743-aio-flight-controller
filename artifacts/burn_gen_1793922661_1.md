# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #44)

I don't have the Job #44 specification, so this design states its hardware assumptions explicitly in §0. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your real motor and frame values. Items marked **[VERIFY]** must be checked against datasheets or measured on your hardware.

---

## 0. Assumed Platform

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz (FPU, CORDIC, 3× ADC), one MCU per axis or one MCU for all three |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), e.g. GM4108-class. R_s ≈ 5.6 Ω, L_s ≈ 2.5 mH, K_t ≈ 0.20 N·m/A **[VERIFY by measurement]** |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A) over SPI, read at 20 kHz |
| Current sense | 2-shunt or 3-shunt low-side, sampled at PWM center |
| IMU A | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate (feedback), SPI @ 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, DRDY interrupt |
| IMU B | ICM-42688-P on the base/handle (disturbance feed-forward), ODR 8 kHz |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer, z) → Roll (middle, x) → Pitch (inner, y) |

### Loop rate hierarchy

| Loop | Rate | T_s | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) + SVPWM | 20 kHz | 50 µs | 1.5 kHz |
| Encoder PLL, anti-cogging FF | 20 kHz | 50 µs | – |
| IMU read + filter chain | 8 kHz | 125 µs | – |
| Velocity (rate) PI | 4 kHz | 250 µs | 60–120 Hz |
| Attitude (angle) P/PI | 1 kHz | 1 ms | 15–25 Hz |
| Attitude estimator (Mahony) | 1 kH
