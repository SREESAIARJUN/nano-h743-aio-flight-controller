## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Phase currents i_a and i_b are measured; i_c = −i_a − i_b.

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$
