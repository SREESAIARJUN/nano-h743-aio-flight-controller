# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC (Job #42): System Architecture & Mathematical Model

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Assumptions

### Partitioning decision

The STM32H743 does **not** switch the MOSFETs directly. The architecture is:

- **H743 flight controller (ArduPilot)** → 4× bidirectional DShot600 → **4× ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, AM32 firmware)** → 4× 3-phase gate drivers → 24 MOSFETs.
- The **48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-firmware parameter** (AM32 `PWM_FREQ`). It is unrelated to H743 timers.
- The two clock domains must be modelled separately (Sections 1–3 and Section 4).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

At every instant, exactly **two** FETs carry the phase current:

- one device in the PWM leg (high side during t_on, low side during t_off via synchronous rectification), and
- the low-side FET held on in the return leg.

The total conduction loss per motor channel is therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At 80 A:

P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.74 × 10⁻³ = **22.3 W per channel**

### 1.3 Per-device distribution

Each phase is driven for 120° positive and 120° negative out of 360°. At D → 1, each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{3} = 3.71\ \text{W}$$

Check: 6 FETs × 3.71 W = 22.3 W ✓

### 1.4 Thermal runaway stability criterion

The loop gain must satisfy:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_{\theta,JA,eff} = P_{cond,FET}\,\frac{\alpha}{1+\alpha(T_j-25)}\,R_{\theta} < 1$$

Substituting: 3.71 × 0.0031 × R_θ < 1, so **R_θ < 87 K/W**. This is satisfied, so the design is stable against runaway. The binding constraint is absolute temperature, not runaway.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. All terms below are per channel.

### 2.1 Switching-loss terms

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 kHz | 1.45 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 kHz | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 kHz | 0.05 W |
| C_oss (2 devices per leg) | 2 × ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.05 W |
| Gate drive (driver dissipation) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 0.04 W |
| **Total switching loss** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |
