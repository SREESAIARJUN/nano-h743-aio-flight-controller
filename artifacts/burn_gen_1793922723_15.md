# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #26)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Reality Check

I don't have prior project documentation for Job #26, so this model rests on the assumptions below. Every parameter tagged **[P]** is a placeholder. Replace it with datasheet values for your selected parts before freezing the design. The equations are structured so you can substitute values directly.

### Architectural clarification

The STM32H743 runs **ArduPilot as the flight controller**. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. "48 kHz" is the **ESC MCU's PWM frequency** (an AM32 option), not anything the H743 generates.

### Reality check (read this first)

**80 A per channel on a nano-format board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is a burst rating only.** The math below shows roughly 28 W of dissipation per channel at 80 A. All four channels at full burst means about 110 W into roughly 2 J/K of board thermal mass. That gives you on the order of one second before you hit limits. Treat the 80 A figure as a ≤1 s transient rating, and expect a continuous rating closer to 25–35 A per channel with airflow.
