## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is driven (PWM on the high side, with the complementary low side synchronously rectifying). A second phase is held low (low side continuously on). The third phase floats for back-EMF ZC sensing.

With motor phase current I_ph and duty D:

- **Active leg:** high side conducts for fraction D, low side for fraction 1 − D (synchronous rectification).
- **Return leg:** low side conducts 100% of the sector.
- **Path:** at every instant, exactly two R_DS(on) are in series with the motor current.

Battery current relates to phase current as I_batt ≈ D·I_ph (ripple neglected).

### 1.2 Equations

**Instantaneous conduction loss per ESC channel:**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

**Per-device average** (each of the six FETs carries current for 120° of 360°):

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}}{3}$$

**Temperature dependence** (self-consistent; solve iteratively with §3):

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0053\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

At D → 1 (full throttle), I_ph ≈ 80 A and R = 1.45 mΩ:

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond, channel | 2 × 80² × 1.45 mΩ | **18.56 W** |
| P_cond, per FET | 80² × 1.45 mΩ / 3 | **3.09 W** |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt conduction (often forgotten)

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt (if per-channel, 0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ | **1.60 W**, carried in the battery current path |
| Phase pad → FET copper (2 oz, ~4 mm wide, 6 mm long, ×2 paths) | ≈ 0.4 mΩ effective | 2.56 W |
| Bus planes (shared, per channel share) | ≈ 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |

> **Design decision:** use **one 0.2 mΩ shunt on the total battery return** (current sense for ArduPilot BATT_MONITOR), not per-channel shunts. Use **ESC-telemetry current per channel** instead. This saves ~4.8 W at full burst.

**Copper subtotal per channel ≈ 3.5 W at 80 A.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

The worst case is at partial duty (D ≈ 0.85–0.95), where PWM edges exist and the current is still near the limit. Use V_bus = 25.2 V and I = 80 A.

### 2.1 Gate drive currents (FD6288Q-class driver)

- V_drv = 10 V (driver VCC from a 10–12 V rail)
- R_g,ext = 3.3 Ω, so R_g,total = 4.3 Ω

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{10-3.5}{4.3} = 1.51\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = 0.81\ \text{A}$$

The driver's own source/sink limits are about 1.5/1.8 A, so the R_g-limited values above apply.

### 2.2 V–I overlap times

$$t_{r} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{1.51} = 7.9\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{f} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{0.81} = 14.8\ \text{ns}$$
