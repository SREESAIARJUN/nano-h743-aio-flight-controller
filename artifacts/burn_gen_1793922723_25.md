# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture

**Key finding up front:** The math below shows that **80 A per channel is not thermally sustainable on a nano (20×20 mm mount, ~30×30 mm) board for more than about one second** when all four channels load at once. The board's thermal mass and its convective path to air are the bottleneck, not the MOSFET junctions. The realistic continuous rating is roughly 25 A per channel. Section 4 has the derivation. I recommend redefining the spec as *"80 A, ≤1 s, single-event, from ≤60 °C board"* or moving to a 30×30 mm mount.

The netlist is reference-level. Pin numbers and part values must be checked against the chosen parts' datasheets before layout.

**Architecture:** The H743 is the flight controller. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071-class), each driving its own bridge. The H743 does not commutate the motors. This partitioning is standard, and it is what ArduPilot supports.
