## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The architecture splits into two domains:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot. Generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 firmware. Runs 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection, and current sensing. |
| Power stage (×4) | FD6288Q driver + 6× N-MOSFET | 3-phase half-bridges. |

The 48 kHz switching frequency belongs to the ESC MCUs. The DShot timer/DMA allocation belongs to the H743. They are analyzed separately below.
