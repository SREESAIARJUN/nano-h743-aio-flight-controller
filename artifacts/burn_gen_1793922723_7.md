# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): 80A Burst Design Package, Job #9

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

**Topology.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. A production-viable architecture splits the work as follows:

- **FC domain:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC.
- **ESC domain:** Four ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32) commutate the motors. Each ESC MCU generates its own 48 kHz complementary PWM to its gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.

So the "48 kHz switching" in this package belongs to the ESC firmware, and the "DMA timers" belong to the H743. The two domains are modeled separately below.

**Red flag: 80A on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board.** Section 2 shows that 4×80A simultaneous is about 125 W of dissipation on roughly 4 cm² of PCB. This is only survivable as a **time-limited thermal-capacitance event (about 1 s)**, not a rating. I recommend publishing the spec as "80A burst, ≤1 s, single-channel" or moving to a 30×30 mm form factor.

## 1. Loss Model for One ESC Channel (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Conduction Path

In any 60° commutation sector, two phases are active: phase A is PWM'd and phase B is held low. Phase C floats.

- **PWM-ON (duration D):** current flows through Q_AH, the motor, and Q_BL.
- **PWM-OFF (duration 1−D):** current flows through Q_AL (sync rectifier), the motor, and Q_BL.

The current therefore always passes through exactly **two R_ds(on)** in series, independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

The general expression is:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.5 × 1.45 ≈ **2.18 mΩ**, so:

$$P_{cond}=2(80)^2(2.18\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{27.9\ W}$$

Per-FET distribution over one electrical cycle (each leg spends 1/3 of the time PWM'ing, 1/3 low, 1/3 floating):

$$P_{HS}=\tfrac13 D\,I^2R,\quad P_{LS}=\tfrac13\big[(1-D)+1\big]I^2R$$

The worst FET is the low-side at low duty:

$$P_{LS,max}\approx \tfrac23(80^2)(2.18\,\text{m}) = \mathbf{9.3\ W}$$

### 1.3 Switching Losses at 48 kHz (Hard-Switched High-Side Only)

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48k | **0.97 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50 nC·25.2·48k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw·2 legs | 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.85·80·200 ns·48k | **0.65 W** |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 0.04 W |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 1.77 W** |

Observations:

- Dead-time loss is about 2/3 of the overlap loss. Cutting t_dt from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.33 W per channel.
- Going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz halves P_sw, but this is minor next to conduction loss.
- At 80A, **conduction loss dominates by roughly 16:1**.

### 1.4 Other Losses Per Channel

| Item | Assumption | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Bulk capacitor ESR | I_rms ≈ I·√(D(1−D)) = 40 A at D = 0.5; ESR ≈ 2 mΩ effective (onboard MLCC + external low-ESR electrolytic) | **3.2 W** if all on board, so push ripple to the external cap |
| Copper planes | 2 oz, ~0.4 mΩ effective path → 80²·0.4 m | 2.6 W |
| Shared current shunt | 0.25 mΩ at 4×80 A (amortized) | ~6.4 W total |

### 1.5 Total Board Dissipation

$$P_{ch} \approx 27.9 + 1.8 + 2.6 \approx \mathbf{32\ W/channel}$$

$$P_{board,4ch} \approx 4(32) + 6.4 \approx \mathbf{135\ W}$$

The ESR term is excluded from these totals on the assumption that ripple is pushed to the external capacitor.

### 1.6 Self-Consistent T_j Iteration

R_ds depends on T_j, which depends on loss. Iterate:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_a + P(T_j^{(k)})\cdot R_{\theta,JA}$$

With R_θJA(steady, per FET on a 20×20 board in prop wash) ≈ 25 K/W:

- 9.3 W × 25 K/W ≈ 230 K rise.
- There is **no steady-state solution below T_j,max**. The iteration diverges (thermal runaway).
- **Conclusion: 80A is transient-only.**
