## 0. Architecture Baseline & Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning
The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This is the standard 4-in-1 topology.

```
 6S LiPo ──┬── 4-in-1 ESC board (4× MCU + 4× 3-ph driver + 24 MOSFETs)
           │        ▲ DShot600 bidir (×4, 3.3V, single-wire)
           │        │
           └── 5V/10V BEC ──► FC board: STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
```

The two boards are either stacked or combined on one PCB. The math below covers both cases.
