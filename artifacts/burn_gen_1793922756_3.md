# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: System Design Package (Job #14)

**Architecture:** STM32H743 FC running ArduPilot, plus four independent ESC MCUs (AM32-class, e.g. AT32F421) on a single 30.5 × 30.5 mm board.

---

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Up-Front Engineering Flags

**Architecture clarification.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. This is the standard AIO topology. Making the H743 do FOC/6-step for 4 motors while also running ArduPilot is not viable.

**Engineering flag.** As the derivation shows, 80 A per motor (≈134 W of dissipation across the board) is a **1–2 second burst rating** on a 30.5 mm board. It is not a continuous rating. Marketing it otherwise will produce field failures. Section 3 quantifies this.
