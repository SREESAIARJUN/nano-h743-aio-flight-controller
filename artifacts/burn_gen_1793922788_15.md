## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to each of them. This partition is standard practice, and the rest of this document assumes it.
