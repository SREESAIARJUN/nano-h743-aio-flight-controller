## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, generates the 48 kHz PWM, and drives the MOSFETs through a 3-phase gate driver. This is how every production 4-in-1 stack works. A single H743 cannot also do sensorless FOC/6-step on 4 motors under ArduPilot.

**Baseline assumptions.** Replace these with your actual BOM values. All equations are parametric.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full) | DC link |
| I_burst | 80 A per motor | Phase/DC current, burst |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_ds(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ (max at V_GS=10 V) | 40 V MOSFET, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 DFN |
| α_R | 0.0065 /K | R_ds temperature coefficient |
| t_r + t_f | 18 ns | With ~1 A gate drive, R_g ≈ 2 Ω |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha_R (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0065 × 100) = **1.65 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

At any instant two phases conduct. With complementary (synchronous) PWM, the current path always contains exactly one high-side-equivalent and one low-side FET in series:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.65\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{21.1\ W}$$

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 120° electrical. The average per FET is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3} \approx 3.5\ \text{W}$$

Peak instantaneous per-FET dissipation is I²R_ds ≈ **10.6 W**. This is the value that matters for the junction's short-τ thermal response.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses (often forgotten)

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Phase pad / pour (2 oz, ~0.2 mΩ effective) | 0.2 mΩ × 2 paths | 2.6 W |
| Battery pad → DC-link pour (shared, 320 A total) | 0.05 mΩ | 5.1 W (board total) |

### 1.4 Thermal runaway criterion

Self-consistent junction temperature:

$$T_j = T_c + R_{\theta JC}\,I^2R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha_R(T_j-25)]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j = \frac{T_c + R_{\theta JC} I^2 R_{ds,25}(1-25\alpha_R)}{1 - R_{\theta JC} I^2 R_{ds,25}\alpha_R}$$

The solution is stable only if the loop gain G = R_θJC · I² · R_ds,25 · α_R < 1.

At 80 A peak per FET: G = 1.5 × 6400 × 0.001 × 0.0065 = **0.062**.

The junction-level loop is stable. The real runaway loop is the **board-level** one (R_θ,board-ambient is roughly 10× larger). That loop is evaluated in §3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step with complementary PWM, only **one leg per motor** is PWM-switching at any time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 18\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.871\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_f I \cdot 2t_{dead} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times100\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.307\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

Gate drive power is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET body.

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2 = 50\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k}\times2 = 0.048\ \text{W per leg}$$

### 2.6 Switching total per motor

$$P_{sw,total} \approx 0.871 + 0.036 + 0.024 + 0.307 = \mathbf{1.24\ W}$$

**Key insight:** at 80 A, conduction loss (21.1 W) dominates switching loss (1.24 W) by about 17:1. Going to 24 kHz saves only about 0.6 W per motor. Spend effort on R_ds(on) and copper, not on f_sw.
