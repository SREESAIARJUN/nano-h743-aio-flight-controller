## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° electrical sector, exactly two half-bridge legs carry the phase current I:

- **Active leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is held on for the full sector.

Because synchronous rectification keeps the freewheel path through a FET channel and not a diode, the active leg contributes `I²R` whether D is high or low. Ignoring dead time, the total is therefore independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] \approx 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

R_DS(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This creates a positive feedback loop: more loss raises T_j, which raises R_DS(on), which raises loss. To find the operating point, solve the following equations together:

$$
T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta JC}
$$

$$
P_{FET} = I^2\,R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

The closed form is:

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{pcb} + I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC}\,(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha\,I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC}}
$$

**Stability criterion.** A thermal-runaway asymptote exists when `α·I²·R₂₅·R_θJC → 1`. At 80 A this product is `0.005 × 6400 × 0.001 × 1.5 = 0.048`, far below 1. The device is stable at this current.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

Assume a PCB temperature of T_pcb = 85 °C near the end of the burst.

1. **Junction temperature (closed form above):**
   T_j = [85 + 6400 × 0.001 × 1.5 × (1 − 0.125)] / (1 − 0.048) = (85 + 8.4) / 0.952 ≈ **98 °C**
2. **Hot resistance:**
   R_DS(on) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 73) ≈ **1.37 mΩ**
3. **Per-FET loss** (the full-sector LS FET is the worst case):
   P = 80² × 1.37 mΩ ≈ **8.8 W**
4. **FET conduction loss per channel:**
   2 × 8.8 W = **17.5 W**

### 2.4 Copper loss (not negligible at this current)

2 oz copper has a sheet resistance of ρ_□ ≈ 0.245 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, and about 0.31 mΩ/□ at 90 °C. Assume the phase path is about 3 squares effective, with two layers in parallel, per leg:

$$
R_{Cu} \approx \frac{3 \times 0.31}{2} \approx 0.47\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ per leg}
$$

$$
P_{Cu,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.47\ \text{m}\Omega \approx 6.0\ \text{W}
$$

Add the motor pad and solder joint, about 0.2 mΩ each, which contributes about 2.6 W more. Total board copper loss is therefore **about 8.6 W per channel** at 80 A.

> **Design rule:** At 80 A, the copper dissipates about 50 % of what the silicon does. Stitch the phase pours with at least 4 layers and at least 30 vias of 0.3 mm per phase node.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the active leg switches, so one HS/LS pair commutates per PWM cycle.

### 3.1 Turn-on and turn-off (V-I overlap)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}
$$

The transition times follow from the gate drive. Driver source and sink resistance are R_drv ≈ 15 Ω and 8 Ω, plus R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω. V_plateau ≈ 3.2 V.

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_{drv,src} + R_g)}{V_{drv} - V_{pl}} = \frac{12\text{n} \times 19.7}{10 - 3.2} \approx 35\ \text{ns}
$$

$$
t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_{drv,snk} + R_g)}{V_{pl}} = \frac{12\text{n} \times 12.7}{3.2} \approx 48\ \text{ns}
$$

With the HS hard-switched and the LS soft-switched (ZVS in sync-rect), only the HS incurs overlap loss:

$$
P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 83\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} \approx \mathbf{4.0\ W}
$$
