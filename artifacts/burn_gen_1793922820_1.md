## 1. Timing Architecture and Bandwidth Separation

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth | Input → output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (T_c = 50 µs) | ω_ci ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s | i_q* → v_αβ (SVPWM) |
| Rate (gyro) | 4 kHz (T_v = 250 µs) | ω_cv ≈ 2π·150 rad/s | ω* → i_q* |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz (T_θ = 1 ms) | ω_cθ ≈ 2π·25 rad/s | q_ref, q̂ → ω* |
| IMU fusion | 8 kHz in, decimated to 4 kHz | — | raw gyro/accel → filtered ω, q̂ |

Each outer loop must be at least 5–10× slower than the loop inside it, so that the inner loop can be treated as unity gain when tuning the outer one:

$$\omega_{ci} \ge 10\,\omega_{cv},\qquad \omega_{cv}\ge 5\,\omega_{c\theta}$$

## 2. FOC Current Loop

### 2.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count, and the encoder zero offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

**Clarke transform** (assumes balanced phases, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.2 Plant Model and Decoupling

The PMSM voltage equations in the rotating frame are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda)$$

The controller adds back the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms, so each axis becomes a simple first-order RL plant:

$$v_d^* = u_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad v_q^* = u_q + \omega_e(L_d i_d + \lambda)$$

### 2.3 PI Tuning by Pole-Zero Cancellation

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The resulting closed loop is first-order:

$$\frac{i(s)}{i^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}$$

Two constraints apply:

- **Delay limit.** Keep ω_ci ≤ 1/(10·T_c) to stay robust to the 1.5-sample PWM and computation delay.
- **Voltage limit.** Apply a circular limit |v_dq| ≤ V_dc/√3 (the linear SVPWM range). The q-axis gets whatever voltage remains after the d-axis:

$$v_{q,max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$$
