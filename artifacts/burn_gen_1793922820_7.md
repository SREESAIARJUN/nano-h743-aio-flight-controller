# High-Frequency FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #11)

I don't have the Job #11 specification, so this design rests on the explicit assumptions in §0. Every parameter that depends on them is isolated in a config struct, so you can substitute the actual values.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Rate Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, FPU, CORDIC), or equivalent |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs N_p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701), one per axis |
| Current sensing | 2- or 3-shunt, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, gyro/accel ODR 8 kHz |
| Kinematics | Yaw → Roll → Pitch (outer to inner) |

**Loop rates:**

| Loop | Rate | Period | Bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|---|
| PWM / current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| IMU sample, filters, velocity PI | 4 kHz (IMU decimated 8→4 kHz) | 250 µs | ω_v ≈ 2π·60–120 rad/s |
| Attitude estimate, angle loop | 1 kHz | 1 ms | ω_a ≈ ω_v / 5 |
| Adaptive notch retune, cogging LMS | 1 kHz | 1 ms | — |

The bandwidths must separate by at least 4–5× per cascade stage. Each inner loop can then be modeled as unity gain plus a small delay when designing the outer loop.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Phase currents i_a, i_b, i_c, with i_c = −i_a − i_b for 2 shunts.

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park**, with electrical angle θ_e = N_p(θ_m − θ_{off}):
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$
