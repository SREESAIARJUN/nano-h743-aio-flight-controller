# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Fusion

This document covers the full control stack, from phase currents to camera attitude. Section 1 lists the hardware assumptions. Sections 2–6 give the equations. Sections 7–8 give a complete, allocation-free C++17 implementation and a commissioning order.

**Assumptions to replace with Job #59 actuals:**
- **Motor:** GBM2804-class gimbal motor, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH.
- **Encoder:** 14-bit magnetic encoder per axis (MT6701 / AS5048A).
- **MCU:** STM32G4/H7-class (FPU, 3 timers with center-aligned PWM).
- **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P on SPI, mounted on the camera plate.

## 1. Architecture and Timing

| Loop | Rate | Feedback | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) ×3 | 20 kHz (PWM ISR) | Phase currents, encoder θ | SVPWM duties |
| IMU sampling ×2 | 8 kHz ODR (data-ready/FIFO) | Raw gyro/accel | Filtered body rates/accel |
| Velocity ×3 | 4 kHz | Inertial joint rate (gyro→joint Jacobian) | i_q* |
| Attitude estimator | 1 kHz | Fused gyro + gated accel | Quaternion q |
| Angle ×3 | 1 kHz | Attitude error → joint space | ω* |
| Cogging LMS adaptation | 4 kHz | Velocity-loop feedback effort | Fourier coefficients |
| Notch retuning | 1 kHz | Motor speeds, config | Biquad coefficients |

**Bandwidth separation rule:** each outer loop should be at least 5× slower than the loop inside it.
- ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s
- ω_c,v ≈ 2π·150 rad/s
- ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·25 rad/s
