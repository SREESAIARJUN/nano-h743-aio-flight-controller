# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

Three issues must be resolved before the models below are useful.

**1. Who switches at 48 kHz?**
ArduPilot runs on the H743 and only *commands* the ESCs over DShot. It cannot generate commutation PWM. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own motor-control MCU(s). This document assumes the standard topology:

- **H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bidirectional DShot600 to **4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32** at 48 kHz PWM.
- The ESC MCUs drive **4× FD6288Q gate drivers**, which drive **24× N-MOSFETs**.

**2. 80 A on a nano board is a burst rating, not a continuous one.**
Section 5 shows a 30.5 mm board survives all four motors at 80 A for only about 2–3 s, adiabatically. The continuous capability is about 25–30 A per motor with prop-wash cooling. Market the rating as "80 A burst, ≤2 s, ≥10 s recovery".

**3. All device parameters are placeholders.**
The values in §1 represent typical 40 V PDFN3.3×3 MOSFETs. Replace them with the selected part's datasheet values and re-run the equations. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers illustrate the method.

**Commutation scheme:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, one leg is PWM-switching (HS/LS alternating) and one leg's LS is held on.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-corrected on-resistance:**

R_ds,hot = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)] ≈ R_ds,25 · k_T = 1.45 mΩ

**Per-motor conduction loss.** In the PWM leg, the HS conducts for D·T_sw and the LS for (1−D)·T_sw. The return-leg LS conducts 100% of the time. The series path therefore always contains exactly two channels, independent of duty:

P_cond,FET = I² · 2R_ds,hot = 80² × 2 × 1.45 mΩ = **18.56 W**
