# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #25)

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, reference netlists, and the thermal budget.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

These points change the design, so they come first.

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel (or a quad-channel ESC MCU) runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware and generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. | Switching-loss math applies to the ESC power stage. Timer/DMA math applies to the H743. Keep them in separate design domains. |
| F2 | **4 × 80 A simultaneously equals 320 A of battery current.** No nano (20×20) or even 30×30 board, XT60/XT90 lead, or 2 oz copper pour sustains that. | "80 A burst" must be specified as **per channel, non-simultaneous, ≤ ~1–2 s**, with a total-stack limit of roughly 120–160 A. This is derived in §3.4. |
| F3 | A 20×20 board cannot dissipate ~90 W, even transiently, without exceeding Tj limits within about 1–2 s. | Either reduce the rating, move to a 30×30 or larger ESC with 5×6 mm FETs, or define the burst duration explicitly. |
| F4 | **Component parameters below are representative placeholders, not a verified BOM.** | Replace every Rds(on), Qg, Qrr, tr/tf, and Rθ value with datasheet values for the selected parts before sign-off. |

**Design-basis assumptions:**
- 6S LiPo: V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal.
- 40 V N-channel MOSFETs, 3.3×3.3 mm package.
- Rds(on) = 1.0 mΩ at 25 °C, Qg = 45 nC, Qrr = 50 nC, tr + tf ≈ 20 ns at the chosen gate resistance.
- Dead time 200 ns. Body-diode Vf = 0.8 V.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent Rds(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C with α = 0.005: R_DS ≈ 1.5 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM

At any instant, one phase is high-side PWM'd, one is low-side ON, and one floats.

- High-side FET (switching leg): $P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS} D$
- Synchronous low-side of the same leg: $P_{LS,sync} = I^2 R_{DS}(1-D)$
- Low-side FET of the return leg (fully on): $P_{LS,ret} = I^2 R_{DS}$

Summing for one ESC channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This total is independent of duty cycle, but it is distributed over 6 FETs. Each FET carries current for about one third of the electrical period. With sinusoidal FOC, the equivalent result is $P = 3 I_{rms,ph}^2 R_{DS}$ using phase RMS current.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

Per channel:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Per FET, averaged over a commutation cycle (2 of 6 FETs conducting at any time): ≈ 6.4 W.

**PCB copper loss** for a phase trace of 2 oz (70 µm), 5 mm wide, 15 mm long:

$$R = \rho \frac{L}{wt} = 1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot\frac{0.015}{0.005 \times 70\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

At 80 A that is **4.7 W per phase path**. Copper is a first-order loss term. Use multi-layer stitched pours, ideally 3 oz on L1, L2, and L4.

**Shunt (current sense):** 0.5 mΩ × 80² = **3.2 W** during burst.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only one leg switches at a time in six-step operation (2 FETs: the active device plus its synchronous partner).

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (high-side FET)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

Recovery current also adds roughly $I_{rr} V t_{rr}$ of extra turn-on loss in the high side. Budget about 0.1 W.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss,eff} V^2 f_{sw}$$

With C_oss,eff ≈ 1.5 nF: 0.5 × 1.5 nF × 25.2² × 48 k = **0.023 W**

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f I (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

This is the largest switching-related term. Cutting dead time to 100 ns halves it, but you must verify shoot-through margin against gate-driver propagation-delay skew.

### 2.5 Gate drive

The loss lands in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET:

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times N_{switching} = 45\text{nC}\times 10\text{V}\times 48\text{k}\times 2 \approx 0.043\ \text{W}$$

Across all 6 FETs with commutation overhead: < 0.15 W.

### 2.6 Per-channel loss summary at 80 A burst

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| FET conduction (Tj = 125 °C) | 19.2 |
| V·I switching | 0.97 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 |
| Qrr + Coss | ~0.2 |
| Gate drive | ~0.15 |
| Shunt | 3.2 |
| Copper (3 phase paths, ~2 active) | ~6–9 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 31–34 W** |
| **4 channels simultaneous** | **≈ 125–135 W** |

**Efficiency check:** 80 A × 22 V ≈ 1.76 kW per channel, so ≈ 1.9 % loss. That looks small, but the absolute watts are what kill a nano board.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Steady-state network (per FET)

$$T_j = T_a + P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta jc} + R_{\theta cb} + R_{\theta ba})$$

| Element | 3.3×3.3 FET on 20×20 board |
|---|---|
| Rθ_jc (bottom) | ~1.5 K/W |
| Rθ_c-board (solder + via array) | ~3–5 K/W |
| Rθ_board-ambient, whole board, 10 m/s prop-wash | ~4–6 K/W (shared by all FETs) |

**Steady-state result:** with 130 W into a board-to-ambient resistance of ~5 K/W, the board rise would be 650 K. The 80 A rating is therefore **strictly transient**.

### 3.2 Transient (lumped thermal capacitance)

Board thermal mass for a 20×20 mm, 1.6 mm board, 6-layer, with 2–3 oz copper plus FETs:

| Component | Calculation | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Cu | ~2.5 g × 0.385 J/g·K | ≈ 0.96 J/K |
| FR4 | ~1.0 g × 1.1 J/g·K | ≈ 1.1 J/K |
| Components | | ≈ 0.4 J/K |
| **Total C_th** | | **≈ 2.5 J/K** |

Time constant: τ = R_ba · C_th ≈ 5 × 2.5 = 12.5 s.

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) = P\,R_{ba}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right) \approx \frac{P}{C_{th}}\,t \quad (t \ll \tau)$$

| Scenario | P (W) | dT/dt (K/s) | Time to +60 K (40 °C → 100 °C board) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 ch × 80 A | 130 | 52 | **1.2 s** |
| 1 ch × 80 A + 3 ch × 30 A | ~32 + 3×5 = 47 | 19 | 3.2 s
