## 0. Architecture Decisions and Stated Assumptions

Several of these choices reshape the brief. They are stated first so every downstream number can be traced to an input.

### 0.1 The H743 does not run the motors

ArduPilot on the H743 generates **bidirectional DShot600**. Commutation and the 48 kHz PWM run on four dedicated ESC MCUs, which is industry-standard practice.

| Block | Part (reference) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, DShot600 BDShot TX/RX |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF commutation |
| Gate driver ×4 | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | 6 gate outputs per motor |
| MOSFET ×24 | 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 class | Inverter bridges |

> **Feasibility flag.** 4 × 80 A simultaneous is 320 A, about 8 kW at 25 V. On a 20×20 or 30×30 nano footprint this is a **transient-only** rating. The board cannot reach thermal equilibrium at that load (see §3). Battery leads, XT60/XT90, and capacitor ripple also become limiting. Advertise it as "80 A burst (≤5 s), 45–55 A continuous with airflow," and validate on the bench.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Derivation for six-step commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM)

In each 60° sector, phase A is PWM'd (high-side duty D, low-side complementary 1−D) and phase B's low-side is held ON. Phase C floats for BEMF sensing. The current path is always:

```
V+ → Q_AH → motor A → motor B → Q_BL → GND        (during D)
GND → Q_AL → motor A → motor B → Q_BL → GND       (during 1−D, sync-rect freewheel)
```

Instantaneous conduction is therefore always **exactly two FETs in series**:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[\underbrace{D + (1-D)}_{\text{PWM leg}} + \underbrace{1}_{\text{held leg}}\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The result is **independent of duty**. That is a useful property: burst conduction loss depends only on current and temperature.

Temperature dependence:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Over a full electrical cycle, each leg spends 1/3 of the time PWM'd, 1/3 held low or high, and 1/3 floating. Averaged over all 6 FETs:

$$
\bar P_{FET} = \frac{2I^2R_{DS}}{6} = \frac{I^2R_{DS}}{3}
$$

The **peak sector** FET (held-ON switch) dissipates the full I²R_DS. The electrical period at burst is

$$
T_e = \frac{60}{RPM \cdot pp}
$$

For example, 30 kRPM with 7 pole pairs gives T_e ≈ 0.29 ms. This is far below the die thermal time constant (~ms), so the **average** governs T_j. Ripple is under 1 K.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt conduction (frequently ignored, and significant)

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 (R_{trace} + R_{shunt}) \qquad R_{Cu}(T)=R_{Cu,20}[1+0.00393(T-20)]
$$

The reference build is 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner, with power planes stacked on 4 layers. Effective loop resistance per motor (V+ plane → bridge → phase pad, plus return) is about 0.35 mΩ at 20 °C, or 0.45 mΩ at 85 °C.

- P_Cu ≈ 80² × 0.45 mΩ = **2.9 W per motor**
- Shunt: a single common 0.25 mΩ shunt at 320 A gives 25.6 W. **This is unacceptable.**

**Decision:** use per-motor 0.5 mΩ 2512 shunts (3.2 W each at 80 A, exceeding the 3 W 2512 rating at temperature). Alternatively, use **2 × 1 mΩ paralleled** (1.6 W each). Another option is a bus-current sensing IC (e.g., hall-effect, ~0.1 mΩ).
