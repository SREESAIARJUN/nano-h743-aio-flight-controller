## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Red Flags

**Architecture assumed:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, which run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware on AT32F421 or STM32G071 parts. The H743 never drives gates directly. ArduPilot has no on-FC six-step or FOC commutation, so this split is mandatory.

> ⚠️ **Red flag (design review):** 80 A × 4 on a nano AIO is a thermally transient-only rating. Section 3 shows it is sustainable for roughly 2–4 s, not continuously. Market this as "80 A burst (≤2 s)", not as a continuous rating. Also confirm the 80 A figure applies per motor, not to the board total.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current-path topology

In six-step commutation with complementary PWM, two phases conduct at any instant:
- **PWM on (D):** the high-side FET of phase X and the low-side FET of phase Y conduct.
- **PWM off (1−D):** the synchronous low-side FET of phase X and the low-side FET of phase Y conduct.

Therefore exactly **two FET channels carry I_ph at all times**, independent of D, as long as dead time is neglected (dead time is treated in §2.4).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_ds = 1.5·R_ds,25.

### 1.3 RMS correction for ripple

With peak-to-peak ripple ΔI = r·I:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right)$$

For low-inductance racing motors at 48 kHz, r ≈ 0.3, so the correction factor is 1.0075. This is negligible but retained in the model.
