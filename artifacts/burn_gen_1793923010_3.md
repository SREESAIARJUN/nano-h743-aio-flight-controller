## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Flags

The analysis is only as good as its inputs, so every parameter is stated explicitly. Replace the placeholder values with datasheet values for your chosen parts before you freeze the design.

### 0.1 Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In any production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 acts as the flight controller running ArduPilot, and it sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (e.g., an AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware) generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM for its own channel. This document models both halves:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz in ArduPilot builds) | Attitude control, DShot600 + eRPM telemetry decode |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or equivalent) | 6-step / FOC commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead time |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (e.g., FD6288Q class) | 8–20 V gate drive, bootstrap high side |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | Half-bridges, 6S max |

### ⚠️ Feasibility Flag

80 A per channel on a 30×30 mm board is a **burst-only** rating. The math below shows it is thermally sustainable for only about 2–3 seconds, and only if prop-wash airflow is present. Treat any marketing "80 A burst" figure as transient-limited and enforce it in firmware (Section 2.6).

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 1.1 Conduction Path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification (active freewheeling, which is standard on AM32 and BLHeli_32), current always flows through exactly **two conducting MOSFETs** in series:

- one high-side FET in phase A plus one low-side FET in phase B, or
- two low-side FETs during freewheeling.

The phase current is therefore continuously in a path of 2 × R_DS(on). This holds regardless of duty cycle D.

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{Cu}
$$

Include current ripple in the RMS value:

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}}
$$

**Ripple estimate.** For a typical motor phase-to-phase inductance L_pp = 15 µH at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L_{pp} f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\times10^{-6} \cdot 48\times10^3} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{80^2 + 8.75^2/12} = 80.04\ \text{A}
$$

The ripple contribution is negligible at 80 A.

**MOSFET term (hot):**

$$
R_{DS(on),hot} = 0.90 \cdot 1.5 = 1.35\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,FET} = 80.04^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1.35\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.30\ W}
$$

**Copper term.** Assume 0.25 mΩ of effective PCB trace/plane resistance per current path, including the via fields to the motor pads:

$$
P_{Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.25\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.60\ W}
$$

### 1.3 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Edge, Linear Crossover)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\times10^{-9} \cdot 48\times10^3 = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
$$

### 1.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\times10^{-9} \cdot 48\times10^3 = \mathbf{0.614\ W}
$$

### 1.5 Reverse Recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\times10^{-9} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\times10^3 = \mathbf{0.060\ W}
$$

Note that Q_rr scales with di/dt and current. At 80 A, the datasheet Q_rr (usually specified at a lower I_F) may understate the true value by 1.5–2×.

### 1.6 Output-Capacitance Loss

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\times10^{-9} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\times10^3 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

### 1.7 Gate-Drive Loss

Only the PWM-switching pair toggles at f_sw. The commutation-rate toggling of the other FETs is negligible.

$$
P_{gate} = 2 \cdot Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 60\times10^{-9} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\times10^3 = \mathbf{0.058\ W}
$$

Most of this is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET die.

### 1.8 Per-Channel and Total Burst Loss

| Loss term | W / channel |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs, hot) | 17.30 |
| Copper | 1.60 |
| Switching crossover | 0.97 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr | 0.06 |
| Q_oss | 0.05 |
| Gate drive | 0.06 |
| **Total** | **≈ 20.65 W** |

$$
P_{total,4ch} \approx \mathbf{82.6\ W}
$$
