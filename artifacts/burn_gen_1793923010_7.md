# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #44): Power, Thermal and DShot Architecture Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Corrections

**Architecture.** On a production AIO board the STM32H743 does not run motor commutation. It is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each ESC channel, or the 4-in-1 block, has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 generates bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The model below is split accordingly.

## 1. Loss Model, per ESC Channel

### 1.1 Conduction Loss

With synchronous-rectified 6-step commutation, phase current always flows through two FETs in series:
- one FET of the PWM'd leg (high-side for duration D, low-side for 1−D), and
- the static low-side FET of the return phase.

**RMS current including ripple:**

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

With a 15 µH phase inductance at 48 kHz and D ≈ 0.5, ΔI ≈ 9 A. This adds only about 1% to I², so it is negligible at 80 A.

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Conduction loss per channel:**

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 110 °C | 1.55 mΩ | 19.9 W |
| 150 °C | 1.81 mΩ | 23.2 W |

**Thermal runaway check.** Loss rises with temperature, so the junction temperature must be solved self-consistently:

$$T_j = T_a + R_{\theta}\,P(T_j)$$

This converges only if

$$R_\theta \cdot 2I^2R_{ds,25}\,\alpha < 1$$

At 80 A that gives R_θ < 12 K/W. This is satisfied for burst operation, but the margin is thin.

### 1.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48k | 1.94 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2 · t_dead · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 400 ns × 48k | 1.23 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | 0.02 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw | 0.04 W (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g) |
| **Total switching loss** | | **≈3.3 W** |

**Optimisation levers:**
- **Dead time.** Reducing dead time from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. AM32 exposes a dead-time setting, but validate it against shoot-through with a scope.
- **Switching frequency.** f_sw is a direct lever. At 24 kHz the switching loss halves, but current ripple doubles and motor iron loss rises.

### 1.3 Bus Capacitor Ripple

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \le 0.5\,I = 40\ \text{A per channel (worst case at } D = 0.5)$$

- With four channels whose PWM is phase-interleaved, the aggregate drops to roughly 40–55 A RMS. Without interleaving it approaches 80 A RMS.
- Ceramics alone cannot carry this. Specify a low-ESR electrolytic or polymer capacitor of at least 1000 µF, 35 V, mounted at the pads.
- Keep 4–6 × 10 µF X7R 50 V ceramics per channel at the half-bridge.

### 1.4 Board Totals at 80 A Burst (T_j ≈ 110 °C)

| Item | Loss |
|---|---|
| Conduction + switching, per channel | ≈23.2 W |
| All four channels | ≈93 W |
| Copper and shunt I²R (≈0.15 mΩ × 80² × 4) | ≈4 W |
| **Board total** | **≈97 W** |

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Two-Node Network

Node 1 is the junctions. Node 2 is the board copper plus FET packages.

$$C_j\frac{dT_j}{dt} = P(T_j) - \frac{T_j - T_b}{R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta CB}}$$

$$C_b\frac{dT_b}{dt} = \sum P - \frac{T_b - T_a}{R_{\theta BA}}$$

**Heat capacity estimates:**
- **Copper:** 400 mm² × 0.42 mm = 168 mm³, giving 1.5 g and 0.58 J/K.
- **FR-4:** about 1.3 g, giving roughly 1.4 J/K. It is weakly coupled.
- **Effective C_b:** ≈1.5 J/K. This rises to 4–6 J/K with a bonded aluminium heat-spreader of 3–5 g.

**Board-to-ambient resistance
