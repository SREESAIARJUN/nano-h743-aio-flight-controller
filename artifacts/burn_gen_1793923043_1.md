## 0. Two Problems to Resolve Before Design

### 0.1 The H743 cannot commutate four motors directly

A 4-in-1 ESC needs 12 half-bridges, which means 24 gate signals. Each bridge also needs dead-time insertion, BEMF zero-crossing detection and per-motor commutation timing.

The STM32H743 has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs and dead time (TIM1 and TIM8), plus HRTIM. That covers about two motors at best, and the H743 would also lose its role as the flight controller.

**Architecture decision:**

| Function | Device |
|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot master | STM32H743VIT6 (400 MHz) |
| ESC MCUs (×4, or 1 per motor) | STM32G071 / AT32F421, running AM32 firmware |
| 48 kHz PWM | Generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743 |
| FC ↔ ESC link | Bidirectional DShot600, one wire per motor |

The switching-loss model in §3 applies to the ESC MCU's PWM. The DMA model in §5 applies to the H743.
