## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These points shape all of the analysis below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each channel has its own ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8 running Bluejay). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 only produces DShot600 frames and decodes eRPM telemetry. Switching losses are therefore an ESC-side problem, and DMA allocation is an FC-side problem.
2. **"Nano" form factor vs. 80 A burst.** On 20×20 mm, 80 A per channel is not thermally credible. The thermal model below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board (36×36 mm outline)**. Section 3 quantifies the limits, and the result is that 80 A is a seconds-scale burst rating, not a continuous one.
3. **All device parameters are stated assumptions** representative of 40 V PDFN5×6 MOSFETs. Substitute datasheet values for your chosen part and re-run the equations. Every equation is parametric.
