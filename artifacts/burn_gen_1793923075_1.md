# Job #51: STM32H743 + 4-in-1 ESC "Nano" AIO
## Architecture, Loss Model, DShot/DMA Allocation, Netlist, Thermal Budget

## 0. Architectural Constraints

Three constraints shape the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no ESC firmware, and 48 kHz six-step or FOC for 4 motors is not done on the FC MCU. The design is therefore:
   - an **STM32H743VIT6** running ArduPilot, plus
   - **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32), linked by bidirectional DShot600.

   The switching-loss model below belongs to the ESC MCUs and gate drivers. The DMA model belongs to the H743.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano footprint is a thermal-capacitance problem, not a steady-state rating.** The math in §2 shows an adiabatic burst limit of about 1.5 to 2 s at 4×80 A. That number belongs on the datasheet rather than an unqualified "80 A."
3. **Form factor.** LQFP100 (14×14 mm) plus 24 power FETs will not fit a 20×20 mm board. I have assumed **25.5×25.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers**. A 20×20 mm variant requires a two-board stack (FC over ESC).

All component parameters are **design assumptions** for a representative 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 MOSFET class. Substitute final datasheet values before release.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

The junction-temperature-corrected resistance is:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 0.9 × 1.45 = **1.31 mΩ**.

In six-step with synchronous rectification, exactly **two FET channels carry I at every instant**:
- one device in the PWM leg (high side for D, low side for 1−D), and
- the low side of the sinking leg.

Therefore:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.31\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{16.7\ W}$$

Per-FET values:
- Instantaneous peak: 80² × 1.31 mΩ = 8.4 W.
- Averaged across the 6 FETs (each conducts about 1/3 of an electrical cycle): ≈2.8 W.

**PCB copper.** Approximately 0.3 mΩ of effective path resistance (phase pour plus bus) adds:

$$80^2 \times 0.3\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

### 2.2 Hard-Switching Loss (one leg switching at f_sw)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$
