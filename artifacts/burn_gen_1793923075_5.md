# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #65 requirements, BOM, or layout files. Everything below uses stated, typical assumptions. Each one is parameterized so you can substitute datasheet values.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU running ArduPilot. ArduPilot does not implement motor commutation. The 4-in-1 ESC therefore uses four per-motor MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) at a 48 kHz PWM carrier. The H743 drives four bi-directional DShot600 lines to those MCUs. If Job #65 assumed the H743 drives the gates directly, that is a custom-firmware project outside ArduPilot, and it should be flagged now.

| Parameter | Assumed value |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology insight

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two legs carry current at any instant:

- **Chopping leg:** current flows through the high-side FET for duty D and through its complementary low-side FET for (1 − D). There is always exactly one R_ds(on) in the path.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is continuously on, adding a second R_ds(on).

So per motor:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \qquad \text{(independent of duty)}$$

### 1.2 Ripple contribution

Worst-case peak-to-peak ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp,max} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.56\,A$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\,A^2$$

The ripple term is under 0.06% of the total, so it can be neglected at 48 kHz.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = **1.34 mΩ**.

### 1.4 Results at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds(on) | P_cond per motor | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.2 W | 68.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.2 W |

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET conducts for 120° electrical (a third of each cycle). At 1 kHz+ electrical frequency this is far faster than the die thermal time constant, so use the average:

$$\bar P_{FET,cond} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 2.9\,W \;(T_j = 100\,°C)$$

**PCB copper loss (add to the budget).** Assume about 0.15 mΩ of effective trace/plane resistance per phase path with 2 oz copper on 8 layers:

$$2 \times 80^2 \times 0.15\,m\Omega \approx 1.9\,W \text{ per motor}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the chopping leg switches: one hard-switched transition at turn-on and one at turn-off per PWM period.

### 2.1 Gate current and transition times

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10 - 3}{5} = 1.4\,A \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.6\,A$$

$$t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{17\,nC}{1.4\,A} \approx 12\,ns \qquad t_{off} \approx \frac{17\,nC}{0.6\,A} \approx 28\,ns$$
