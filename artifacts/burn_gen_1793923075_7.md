## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

| T_j | R_DS |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.41 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.55 mΩ |

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During any commutation sector, phase A (PWM leg) and phase B (low-side held ON) conduct:

- High-side A conducts for duty **D**.
- Low-side A (synchronous rectifier) conducts for **(1 − D − 2t_dt·f_sw)**.
- Low-side B conducts **100%** of the sector.

The motor current is continuous (L/R ≫ T_sw), so I_ph flows through exactly two channels at almost every instant:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)\,\left[D + (1 - D - 2t_{dt}f_{sw}) + 1\right] \approx 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This total does not depend on duty cycle. Duty only redistributes the loss between the high-side and low-side FETs.

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.41\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.0\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

The worst case is at low duty: the low-side FETs carry (1−D) of each PWM-leg sector plus the full-sector "held" role. Averaged over the six sectors, each FET is active in 4/6 of them:

$$P_{LS} \approx \frac{I^2 R}{3}\left[(1-D) + 1\right],\qquad P_{HS} \approx \frac{I^2 R}{3}\,D \cdot 2$$

At D = 0.5 (typical at 80 A burst on high-pitch props):
- P_HS ≈ 3.0 W
- P_LS ≈ 4.5 W

### 1.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2(0.85)(80)(200\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.31\ W}$$

This is significant. Reducing dead time to 100 ns, which the driver's propagation matching allows, halves this term.

### 1.5 Copper (PCB) conduction loss

Per-motor phase path: ~12 mm long, 4 mm wide, 4 paralleled 2 oz layers.

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t\,n}\,[1+0.0039(T-20)] = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.012}{0.004\cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 4}\times1.31 = 0.24\ \text{m}\Omega$$

- Two phase paths conduct, so P_Cu,phase = 2·80²·0.24 m ≈ **3.1 W per motor.**
- The battery bus is separate. I_bat ≈ D·ΣI_ph = 0.5·320 = 160 A. With R_bus ≈ 0.10 mΩ, P_bus ≈ **2.6 W.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The low-side transitions are ZVS-like because the body diode is already conducting.

### 2.1 Voltage-current overlap (high-side, hard switched)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(35\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.69\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (low-side body diode, absorbed by the high-side)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr rises with di/dt and T_j. Use ×2 margin: **0.10 W.**

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,C_{oss,er}\,V_{bus}^2\,f_{sw} = 0.5(1.8\text{n})(635)(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.027\ W}$$

### 2.4 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors)

$$P_g = Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw}\cdot 2 = 45\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}\cdot2 = 0.043\ \text{W per leg}$$
