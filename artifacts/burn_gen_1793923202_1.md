## 0. Architecture Decision (read first)

The STM32H743 should **not** drive the 24 power MOSFETs directly. The reasons:

- The H743 has only two advanced-control timers with complementary outputs and hardware dead-time (TIM1, TIM8). That supports only 2 motors × 3 phases.
- HRTIM gives 10 outputs, which still falls short of 12 complementary pairs (24 gates) and has no BLDC commutation support in ArduPilot.
- ArduPilot is a flight stack, not a sensorless-FOC/6-step commutation firmware.

**Adopted architecture:**

```
                 ┌────────────────────────── 20×20 / 25.5×25.5 FC stack ───────────┐
 Battery 6S ──►  │  STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot)                                       │
                 │   TIM1_CH1/CH2, TIM8_CH3/CH4 ──bidir DShot600──►  4× ESC MCU     │
                 │                                   (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32)   │
                 │                                    each ►FD6288Q ►6× N-MOSFET    │
                 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM with dead-time. The H743 handles control, DShot, and telemetry.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds}(125) = 0.70 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.015\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

Two phases conduct at any instant:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** high side on for duty D, complementary low side on for (1−D).
- **Leg B (return leg):** low side fully on.

Current always flows through exactly two channel resistances:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot \left[D R_{HS} + (1-D) R_{LS}\right] + I^2 R_{LS} = 2 I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This is **independent of duty** with synchronous rectification.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.015\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{12.99\ W\ per\ motor}$$
