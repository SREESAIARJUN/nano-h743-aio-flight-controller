# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #35): Electrothermal Model, DShot DMA Allocation and Reference Netlist

---

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Check

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In an AIO (all-in-one) design:

- The **H743 runs ArduPilot**. It produces bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **Four ESC MCUs commutate the motors.** These are e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. Each generates 48 kHz complementary PWM through a 3-phase gate driver into 6 MOSFETs.

The loss model below therefore applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model applies to the H743.

**Feasibility check.** The figures below show that 80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mm mounting) form factor is a **~1 second** rating at best. Simultaneous 4×80 A is not a credible design point:

- 320 A from a 6S pack is about 7 kW.
- The connector, battery leads and DC-link capacitors cannot support it.

I've built the model so you can see exactly where the limits come from. Section 3.4 gives a defensible rating.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel, 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 2.1 Conduction loss

In any 60° commutation sector, current flows through two conducting paths:

- **PWM leg:** either the high-side FET (during D) or the synchronous low-side FET (during 1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET, on for the whole sector.

So the conducting path is always 2·R_ds, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

- R_ds = 1.5 mΩ × 1.47 = 2.2 mΩ
- P_cond = 80² × 4.4 mΩ = **28.2 W**

Add the PCB copper and solder path. A 2 oz copper trace plus vias is roughly 0.3 mΩ per phase path (2 paths):

- P_Cu = 6400 × 0.6 mΩ ≈ **3.8 W**

### 2.2 Switching loss (only the PWM leg switches)

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 kHz | 1.45 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 0.05 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw × 2 FETs | 0.03 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 kHz | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | Q_g·V_gs·f_sw × 2 | 0.
