# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Thermal, Switching Loss, and Bidirectional DShot600 Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and One Architecture Correction

I have no record of earlier deliverables for this project, so every design input is stated explicitly below. Replace them with your BOM values; all equations are parametric.

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 should not commutate the MOSFET bridges directly. The standard and recommended split is:

- **FC (STM32H743, ArduPilot):** sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **4-in-1 ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class), each with its own 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.

The losses in Sections 1–3 are therefore ESC-board losses. The DMA and timer model in Section 4 is on the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.55) = **1.55 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, exactly two devices carry the full phase current at any instant:

- **PWM-on interval (duty D):** high side of phase A, plus the static low side of phase B.
- **PWM-off interval (1 − D):** low side of phase A (synchronous rectification), plus the static low side of phase B.
