## 1. FOC Current (Torque) Loop: 20 kHz

### 1.1 Transforms
Phase currents i_a, i_b, i_c, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0 enforced in software.

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

**Park**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design
The PMSM dq model is:
$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

With cross-coupling and back-EMF cancelled by feedforward, each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole at R/L. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth ω_c:
$$
K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}\;\Rightarrow\; \omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}
$$

**Decoupling feedforward:**
$$
v_d^{ff} = -\hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$
