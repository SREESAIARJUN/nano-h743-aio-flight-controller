## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two architectural points need settling before any math.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
A 4-in-1 ESC needs one dedicated ESC MCU per motor, or a multi-motor ESC MCU, to do sensorless commutation at 48 kHz PWM. Running six-step BEMF commutation for four motors on the flight-control MCU is not supported by ArduPilot.

The architecture assumed throughout:

```
STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot)  ── DShot600 bidir (×4, 1-wire each) ──►  4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32 firmware)
                                                                      └► 4× FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver
                                                                         └► 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor)
```

**2. 80 A burst on a nano footprint is thermally transient-only.**
The math below shows a burst limit of a few seconds, not a continuous rating. Treat "80 A" as a short burst spec.

**Working parameters.** Every assumption is explicit. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching loss) |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in synchronous six-step commutation

During any commutation sector, exactly two phases carry current:

- **PWM leg:** current flows in the high-side FET during D·T and in the complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) during (1−D)·T.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is held on for the whole sector.

So at every instant the current flows through exactly **two** channel resistances, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu} \;+\; I_{ph}^2 R_{shunt}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{2.3}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad k_T(125^\circ\text{C}) \approx 1.6
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(125^\circ\text{C}) = 1.2 \times 1.6 = 1.92\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

This creates positive feedback: higher T_j raises R_DS(on), which raises loss, which raises T_j. The fixed point exists only if

$$
\frac{dP}{dT_j}\cdot R_{\theta,total} < 1 .
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

- **FET conduction:** 2 × 80² × 1.92 mΩ = **24.6 W**
- **Copper path** (R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ, pads plus 2 oz pours, phase-to-phase): 80² × 0.4 mΩ = **2.6 W**
- **Shunt:** AM32 typically uses one current sense per ESC; assume 0.5 mΩ on the common return, shared:
  80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W per motor when sensed**. If there is no shunt, set this to 0.

$$
P_{cond,motor} \approx 24.6 + 2.6 + 3.2 = 30.4\ \text{W}
$$

At 25 °C junction temperature, the FET term drops to 15.4 W. The temperature coefficient alone is worth about 9 W per motor.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. That is one high-side/low-side pair per motor at any time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 27\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.31\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

The recovery current also adds to turn-on overlap. In practice, scale P_VI by about 1.1–1.3. **Use 1.6 W** for P_VI + P_rr combined.

### 2.3 Output capacitance (C_oss)

$$
P_{oss} = C_{oss,eff}\,V_{bus}^2\,f_{sw} = 1.5\,\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.046\ W}
$$

This counts both FETs in the leg.

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

The 100 ns dead time is the single biggest tunable switching-related loss
