## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In any ArduPilot AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and returns eRPM telemetry.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge, worst case for switching loss) |
| Burst phase current | I = 80 A per ESC channel, 10 s rating target |
| f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) 6-step trapezoidal |
| MOSFET (per switch position, 1×) | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 PQFN: R_DS(on),max = 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V, α = 0.0045 /K |
| Gate charges | Q_g = 60 nC, Q_gs = 15 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, V_plateau = 3.5 V |
| Other FET data | Q_rr = 80 nC (high di/dt), Q_oss = 40 nC, V_SD = 0.8 V |
| Gate drive | 10 V driver. R_g,on = 10 Ω total (ext + int + driver). R_g,off = 6 Ω (diode-bypassed) |
| Dead time | t_dt = 150 ns per edge |
| PCB | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz all layers, 1.6 mm FR4 |
| Junction-to-case | R_θJC = 1.2 K/W |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**. Use T_j = 125 °C as the design point, then iterate (§3.4).

### 1.2 Per-switch conduction (6-step, complementary PWM, duty D)

Each phase spends one third of the electrical period in each state: PWM-high, solid-low, and floating.

| Device | Conduction interval | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| High-side (HS), PWM leg | D·(1/3) | $I^2 R \cdot D/3$ |
| Low-side (LS), sync-rect on PWM leg | (1−D)·(1/3) | $I^2 R (1-D)/3$ |
| LS, return leg | 1/3 | $I^2 R/3$ |

Per phase: $\tfrac{2}{3}I^2R$. Summed over three phases, the total is independent of D:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

At 80 A and 1.45 mΩ: P = 2 × 6400 × 1.45×10⁻³ = **18.6 W per ESC channel**.

### 1.3 Copper and interconnect conduction

Approximate path resistances: 0.15 mΩ phase pad to motor pad, and 0.15 mΩ bus distribution share.

$$P_{Cu} \approx I^2 R_{phase} + I_{bat}^2 R_{bus} \approx 6400(0.15\text{m}) + (D\cdot80)^2(0.15\text{m}) \approx 0.96 + 0.96 = \mathbf{\sim1.9\ W}\ (D=1)$$

### 1.4 Shunt (if per-ESC current sense is fitted)

With R_sh = 0.25 mΩ: $P_{sh} = I_{bat}^2 R_{sh}$ = 1.6 W at D = 1, I_bat = 80 A.
