## 0. Architecture Decisions and Red Flags

| # | Issue | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The H743 runs ArduPilot. It cannot also run four sensorless six-step/FOC commutation loops with the required determinism, and ArduPilot has no in-tree ESC commutation. | Use a **split architecture**: H743 FC plus 4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071) ESC MCUs running **AM32**. The H743 drives them with bidirectional DShot600. |
| 2 | 80 A per motor on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board is a **burst** rating only. It is thermally limited to about 1–2 s with all four motors loaded (see §3). | Rate it as **80 A burst (≤1 s), ~35–40 A continuous per motor, airflow-dependent**. |
| 3 | 4 × 80 A = 320 A of bus current at 6S. Connector, battery leads and bulk capacitors dominate the risk. | XT60 is undersized for this. Use XT90 or direct-solder pads, plus a large low-ESR bulk cap at the pads. |
| 4 | At 48 kHz, switching losses are small compared with conduction losses at 80 A. | 48 kHz is acceptable. Its main costs are gate-driver power and dead-time diode loss (§2). |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Parameters (illustrative 40 V DFN3.3 MOSFET)

| Symbol | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 | 1.6 mΩ | V_GS = 10 V |
| α (temperature coefficient) | ≈ 0.0045 /K | Typical for trench Si |
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, full charge |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst |

**Temperature dependence**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.6 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **2.21 mΩ**. Use **2.4 mΩ** for margin, which also covers a phase-trace and solder contribution of about 0.2 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step (AM32) commutation with complementary PWM

At any instant, two phases conduct.
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is fully on.

$$P_{cond,PWM\,leg} = I^2 R\,D + I^2 R\,(1-D) = I^2 R$$
$$P_{cond,static\,leg} = I^2 R$$
$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

This ignores dead time. Diode conduction during dead time is counted under switching losses in §2.

**At 80 A:**

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs conducts one third of the electrical cycle in a symmetric rotation:

$$P_{FET,avg} = 30.7/6 = \mathbf{5.1\ W}, \qquad P_{FET,peak\ (instantaneous)} \approx I^2R = 15.4\ W$$

The electrical period at typical eRPM (≥ 10 kHz electrical, i.e. ≤ 100 µs) is far below the die thermal time constant (~1–10 ms). The die therefore sees the average plus a small ripple of a few kelvin.

### 1.3 RMS correction

Six-step phase current is quasi-trapezoidal. With PWM ripple ΔI_pp:

$$I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}}$$

For low-inductance racing motors at 48 kHz, ΔI_pp is typically about 10–20 A, which raises I²R by only about 0.5–1.5 %. It can be neglected at burst, but include it for low-kV, low-L motors.
