# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot/DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

**Job #25 · Architecture Rev A (preliminary)**

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Before the math, three conclusions from it:

1. **"80 A burst" on a nano-class board lasts about 1 second, not 10.** Total stage dissipation at 4×80 A is about 117 W. A 20×20 mm board has roughly 2 J/K of thermal mass. That gives a junction-safe burst of about 1.2 s, assuming no heatsink and no realistic airflow model (§2.6).
2. **The STM32H743 cannot drive the bridge directly under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot outputs DShot. It does not do field-oriented or 6-step commutation. The 48 kHz switching therefore lives in per-motor ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32, and the H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600.
3. **Package choice matters.** The LQFP100 H743 (14×14 mm) cannot coexist with 24 power FETs on 20×20. This design assumes **STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm)**. The FC portion likely needs to sit on a stacked board or a 25.5/30.5 mm format.

All parametric values below are design assumptions. Verify them against the final datasheets before layout.
