| Function | Device | Firmware |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM:
- During the ON interval (D), current flows through HS(phase A) → motor → LS(phase B).
- During the OFF interval (1−D), current flows through LS(phase A) → motor → LS(phase B), freewheeling via the channel rather than the body diode.

Two MOSFET channels therefore always carry the phase current, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \quad\text{(synchronous rectification)}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (positive feedback)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]
$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(100) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045\times75) = 1.34\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

Because loss raises T_j and T_j raises R_DS(on), the solution must be iterated. The solver is shown in §4.4.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

$$
P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \times 1.34\,\text{m}\Omega = 8.58\ \text{W per conducting FET}
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 8.58 = 17.2\ \text{W per channel}
$$

### 1.4 PCB copper loss

Assume 0.45 mΩ total path resistance (phase pad → FET → shunt → bus) on 2 oz copper with via arrays. Copper's α is 0.0039 /K, giving 0.53 mΩ at 70 °C board temperature.

$$
P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.53\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.4\ \text{W}
$$

### 1.5 Current-sense shunt (one shared on DC bus)

Use 0.25 mΩ shared across the 4-in-1 bus. If total burst current is 4 × 80 = 320 A (this is not realistic simultaneously; see §4):

$$
P_{shunt} = 320^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 25.6\ \text{W}
$$

This exceeds any 2512 shunt. **Requirement:** total-current sensing must use a lower-value shunt (≤ 0.1 mΩ, or a Hall sensor). Otherwise ArduPilot current monitoring is limited to a realistic total of about 150 A. With 0.1 mΩ at 150 A, P_shunt = 2.25 W.
