**Architectural flags you should resolve before layout:**

1. **The H743 does not run the motors.** Each ESC channel group is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 is the DShot *master*. Section 3 covers the H743 side and Section 4 covers the ESC side.
2. **80 A × 4 at full duty means roughly 320 A of battery current.** A "nano" form factor with XT60 or XT90 leads cannot carry that continuously. Treat "80 A burst" as **per-channel, seconds-scale, and not all four channels simultaneously at 100 % duty**. Section 2.5 quantifies this.
3. **"80 A burst" on a 36 mm board is thermally bounded to roughly 5–10 s** from a 40 °C start, even with prop-wash airflow (Section 2.4).

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Conduction topology

In 6-step synchronous operation, at any instant one phase is high-side PWM, one phase is low-side ON, and one phase floats. The winding current I_ph flows through:

- During the PWM ON-time (D): HS FET of the PWM leg, then the LS FET of the sink leg.
- During the PWM OFF-time (1−D): the LS FET of the PWM leg (synchronous freewheel), then the LS FET of the sink leg.
