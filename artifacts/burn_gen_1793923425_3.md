# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #70): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility

**Topology.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. The 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071). The ESC MCUs drive three-phase gate drivers and six N-MOSFETs per channel. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600.

**Feasibility.** A nano (20×20 mm) stack cannot dissipate 4 × 80 A continuously. The model below shows that 80 A is a **time-limited burst rating**: about 3–4 s with all four channels loaded simultaneously. It must be specified, firmware-limited, and labeled that way. All numbers assume the parameter set below. Replace them with datasheet values once the MOSFET and driver are frozen.
