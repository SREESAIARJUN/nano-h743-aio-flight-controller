## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform**, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Delay-compensated inverse Park.** The voltage is applied about 1.5·T_i after sampling, so evaluate the inverse Park at the advanced angle:

$$\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_i$$

### 1.2 Plant model and PI design

The d/q plant is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

After feed-forward decoupling, each axis reduces to 1/(Ls+R). Choose the PI zero to cancel the pole at R/L:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\Rightarrow\; G_{cl}(s)=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Bandwidth limit: ω_c ≤ 1/(10·1.5·T_i). This gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1 kHz at 20 kHz PWM. With L = 2.4 mH and R = 5.6 Ω: K_p ≈ 15 V/A and K_i ≈ 35 000 V/(A·s).
