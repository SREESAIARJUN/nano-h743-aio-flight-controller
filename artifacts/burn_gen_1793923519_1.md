## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Scope Corrections

Three assumptions change the math, so I am stating them up front.

| # | Issue | Resolution used in this document |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. On every 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical parts are the AT32F421, STM32G071 and GD32E230. | **H743 = flight controller only.** The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the 4 ESC MCUs. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to them. |
| A2 | "Nano" (20×20 mm) with 80A burst per channel is not physically credible. That is 320A total and ≈120W of loss in ≈4 cm² of board. | Modeled as a **30.5×30.5 mm stack** (ESC board ≈ 36×36 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz). I show the 20×20 numbers so the gap is visible. |
| A3 | No MOSFET part number was specified. | I use a **placeholder 40V DFN3.3×3.3 FET** with representative parameters. Substitute real datasheet values; every equation is parametric. |
