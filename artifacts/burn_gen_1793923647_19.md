# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #1)

**Scope:** 80 A burst thermal and loss model, 48 kHz switching, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 DMA plan, reference netlists

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation or FOC stack. The architecture is therefore:

- **Flight controller:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It outputs bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.
- **ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs (reference: AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, or an equivalent BLHeli_32 target). Each drives one 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs at 48 kHz.

**The 80 A rating is a per-channel burst rating, not continuous.** Section 3 shows that the realistic continuous rating is about 25 A per channel. 80 A × 4 continuous is physically impossible on a nano-class PCB.
