# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

**Status:** Engineering reference. All component values are stated assumptions. Validate them against your chosen MOSFET and driver datasheets before layout.

---

## 0. Architecture Decisions

Three issues determine the rest of the design.

1. **The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** The H743 has only two advanced-control timers with complementary outputs and dead-time (TIM1, TIM8). Four 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary pairs. HRTIM could cover part of this, but running 4× 48 kHz commutation alongside the ArduPilot scheduler, EKF, and logging is an unnecessary real-time risk. Use this split instead:
   - **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC.
   - **ESC:** 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421 class, running AM32) driving FD6288-class 3-phase gate drivers.
2. **"80 A burst" means 80 A per motor.** At the battery pads this is 320 A, which is about 8 kW at 25.2 V. Section 2 shows that a nano-size board (≤30.5 × 30.5 mm) can survive this only for a few seconds. It is a burst rating, not a continuous one.
3. **The 48 kHz PWM frequency drives the switching-loss terms.** The model below keeps fsw as a variable, so you can trade frequency against efficiency.

## 1. Loss Model (per motor, 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 1.1 Assumptions

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (worst case) |
| I | 80 A | Burst phase current |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_ds,25 | 0.9 mΩ | 40 V PDFN 5×6 class, V_gs = 10 V |
| α | 0.006 /K | R_ds temperature coefficient (≈1.6× at 125 °C) |
| t_r + t_f | 40 ns | With ~1 A gate drive and R_g ≈ 4.7 Ω |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| V_f | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward drop |
| Q_rr | 50 nC | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge |
| Q_oss | 40 nC | Output charge |
| Q_g | 60 nC | Total gate charge |
| V_gs | 10 V | Gate drive voltage |

### 1.2 Conduction loss

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 0.9 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.3 mΩ**

In 6-step operation, the current always flows through exactly two device positions: the high-side of phase X (or the low-side of X while freewheeling synchronously) plus the low-side of phase Y.

P_cond = 2 · I² · R_ds(T_j) = 2 × 6400 × 0.0013 = **16.6 W per motor**

**Peak per-FET dissipation while conducting:** I² · R_ds = **8.3 W**.

**Time-averaged per-FET dissipation:** 16.6 / 6 ≈ **2.8 W**. Each device carries current for 120° of each 360° electrical cycle.

**Thermal-runaway check:** Conduction loss rises with temperature at roughly d(P_cond)/dT ≈ 2I²·R_ds,25·α ≈ 0.069 W/K. This is stable as long as Rth_total × 0.069 < 1. It is satisfied on any board with a heatsink.

### 1.3 Switching loss (only the PWM-ing leg switches)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Hard-switching overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 kHz | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 kHz | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | **0.06 W** |
| Coss charging | Q_oss · V · f_sw | **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | **0.06 W** (dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

### 1.4 Totals at 80 A, T_j ≈ 100 °C

| Item | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 16.6 W | 66.4 W |
| Switching + dead time | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| Copper (≈0.25 mΩ effective per motor path) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, total bus) | — | 0.25 mΩ × 320² = **25.6 W** |
| **Total** | | **≈ 109 W** |

**The shunt is a significant loss term.** At 320 A, even a 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates more than either motor-group switching loss. Use a 0.1 mΩ shunt or Hall-effect sensing. A 0.1 mΩ shunt dissipates 10.2 W, bringing the total to **≈ 94 W**.

**Sensitivity to f_sw:** Each 10 kHz costs about 0.56 W per motor (2.7 W × 10/48). Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 5.4 W across the board.
