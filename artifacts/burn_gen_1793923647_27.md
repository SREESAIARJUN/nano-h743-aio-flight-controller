## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current waveform

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs carry current in each 60° electrical sector. One leg is PWM-switched; the other has its low-side FET held on.

The inductor current is the battery current reflected through the duty cycle D:

$$I_{ph} = \frac{I_{batt}}{D}$$

Within a sector the phase current is quasi-DC with PWM ripple:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

The RMS current, including ripple, is:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

Example: a typical 2207 motor has L_ph ≈ 15 µH (phase-to-phase ≈ 30 µH). At D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{30\times10^{-6}\times 48000} = 4.4\ \text{A}$$

The ripple term is 1.6 A², which is negligible against 80² = 6400 A². Set I_rms ≈ I_ph = 80 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**

### 1.3 Per-motor conduction loss

With synchronous rectification, at every instant one FET conducts in the switching leg (HS during D, LS during 1−D) and one FET conducts in the static leg. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.74\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.3\ W}\quad(T_j=125°C)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.2\times10^{-3} = 15.4\ \text{W}\quad(T_j=25°C)$$
