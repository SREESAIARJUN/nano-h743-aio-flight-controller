## 0. Architectural Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four 3-phase bridges itself. It has only TIM1 and TIM8 as advanced complementary timers, plus HRTIM, and ArduPilot has no 6-step or FOC ESC firmware. This design therefore uses the standard AIO partition.

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz | ArduPilot, sends BDShot600 to the ESCs |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 | 6-step sensorless commutation, 48 kHz PWM, returns eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers | 4× 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class) | 12 V gate drive with bootstrap high side |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 4 channels × 3 legs × (HS + LS) |
| Supply | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max | 2S–6S input range |

**Form factor.** A 20×20 mm "nano" board cannot thermally sustain 4×80 A. The numbers below show 80 A works only as a burst rating, nominally 1–3 s. A continuous rating of about 35–45 A per channel is what the copper can support. I recommend a 25.5×25.5 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm board if 80 A burst is a marketed spec.

**Parameter values.** All device parameters are design-class values. Replace them with the datasheet values of your selected MOSFET and driver before sign-off.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current Path in 6-Step Commutation with Synchronous Rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM-switched high, one phase is held low, and one phase floats. With complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- **On-time (D):** current flows through HS_a, the motor, then LS_b. That is 2 MOSFETs in series.
- **Off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_a (synchronous), the motor, then LS_b. That is also 2 MOSFETs in series.
