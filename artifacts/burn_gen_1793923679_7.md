# STM32H743 Nano AIO (4-in-1 ESC + FC): Systems Architecture Model for Job #2

## 0. Scope and Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own small MCU. This model assumes AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The H743 is the flight controller and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

All component values below are **representative placeholders**. Replace them with your selected MOSFET and driver datasheet values before release. The equations are general and hold regardless.
