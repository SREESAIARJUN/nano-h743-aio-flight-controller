## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Two points need settling before the math.

**1. The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly.** Running 4 × 3-phase sensorless BEMF commutation at 48 kHz PWM on the same core that runs the ArduPilot EKF/scheduler is not supported by ArduPilot, and it is architecturally fragile. The standard topology, used here, is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir ×4──► 4 × AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM)
                                                └─► FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver ─► 6 × N-MOSFET per motor
```
