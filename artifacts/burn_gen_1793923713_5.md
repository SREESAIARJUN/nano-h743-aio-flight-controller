## 0. Architecture Clarification and Early Risk Flag

### Control partitioning

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate motors. The "48 kHz" figure refers to the ESC-side PWM frequency.

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (4 lines)──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32 on STM32G071 / AT32F421)
                                                         │ 6× PWM @ 48 kHz (complementary)
                                                         ▼
                                              3-phase gate driver ×4
                                                         ▼
                                             24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor)
```

### Critical finding: 80 A burst on a nano board

The analysis in §3 shows that 80 A on all four motors at once is **not thermally sustainable** on a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board. The adiabatic heating rate is about 30–40 K/s, so the burst window is about **1.5–3 s** before the FETs approach their limit.

The model below quantifies this. §5 gives options to close the gap.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per Motor)

### 2.1 Conduction topology (trapezoidal / 6-step, synchronous rectification)

In each 60° commutation sector, current flows from bus to phase A (high-side PWM'd) and returns through phase B's low side, which is held on.

- High-side Q_AH conducts for duty fraction D.
- Synchronous low-side Q_AL conducts for (1−D), carrying the freewheel current.
- Return low-side Q_BL conducts 100% of the sector.

So, ignoring dead-time intervals, current is **always flowing through exactly two FET channels in series**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Including inductor ripple Δi (peak-to-peak):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta i^2}{12}$$

With Δi ≈ 4 A (typical low-inductance quad motor at 48 kHz), the ripple term is 1.3 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond/motor = 2·6400·R |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.30 mΩ | **16.6 W** |
| 75 °C | 1.69 mΩ | **21.6 W** |
| 125 °C | 2.08 mΩ | **26.6 W** |

The coupling to temperature is a positive-feedback loop. It is solved iteratively in §3.4.

### 2.3 Switching loss (hard-switched edges)

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. Per PWM period there is one turn-on and one turn-off transition at full I, V_bus.

**Gate current during the Miller plateau:**

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{src} + R_g} = \frac{10 - 3.5}{6.5} = 1.0\text{ A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{snk} + R_g} = \frac{3.5}{2.5} = 1.4\text{ A}$$

**Transition times:**

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{16\text{ nC}}{1.0} = 16\text{ ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{16\text{ nC}}{1.4} = 11.4\text{ ns}$$

**V–I overlap loss:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_{on} + t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 27.4\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.33\ W}$$
