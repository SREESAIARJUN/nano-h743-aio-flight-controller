# Job #22: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC

**Design Package Rev A: Loss Model, Thermal Model, Bi-Directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation, Netlists**

> **Scope note.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 running AM32 in this design). The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry back. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC PWM frequency, configured in AM32, not on the H743.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS}(100) = 0.80 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 75) = 1.07\ \text{m}\Omega \quad(\text{use } 1.12\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ with margin})$$

### 2.2 Six-step bridge with synchronous rectification

At every instant, two phases conduct in series:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The complementary low-side FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

Because synchronous rectification is used, conduction loss is independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,leg} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] = I^2 R_{DS}$$

$$\boxed{P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.12\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{14.3\ W}$$

### 2.3 Copper loss

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.30\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.92\ W}$$

### 2.4 Effective RMS current

The phase current is approximately trapezoidal. Each FET conducts for about 1/3 of an electrical period:

$$I_{FET,rms} \approx I_{pk}\sqrt{1/3} = 46.2\ \text{A}$$

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx I_{FET,rms}^2\,R_{DS} = 2.39\ \text{W per FET} \quad (\times 6 = 14.3\ \text{W, consistent with 2.2})$$

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In each commutation step, only one leg is hard-switched per PWM edge.

### 3.1 V-I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 35\times10^{-9} \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{1.69\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\times10^{-9} \times 25.2 \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 3.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 50\times10^{-9} \times 25.2 \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{0.030\ W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\times10^{-9} \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 3.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET:

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 60\times10^{-9} \times 10 \times 48\,000 = 0.029\ \text{W per switching FET}$$

### 3.6 Frequency sensitivity

| Frequency | P_VI + P_rr + P_oss + P_dt |
|---|---|
| 24 kHz | 1.19 W |
| 48 kHz | 2.38 W |
| 96 kHz | 4.76 W |

The 48 kHz setting gives smoother current and quieter motors, at a cost of about 1.2 W per channel compared with 24 kHz at 80 A.
