## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.41 = **1.70 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

At any instant, one phase is sourcing and one is sinking current, so two half-bridges are active.

- During on-time D, the high-side FET of phase X and the low-side FET of phase Y conduct.
- During off-time (1−D), synchronous rectification puts the low-side FET of phase X and the low-side FET of phase Y in the path.

The current path therefore always contains exactly two FETs, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The loss distributes between the active high-side FET ($D\,I^2R$) and the synchronous low-side FET ($(1-D)I^2R$). Each leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle, so the per-FET average is lower than the per-channel figure suggests.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.70\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.8\ W}$$

### 1.3 Parasitic copper loss (frequently omitted)

Assume a battery pad-to-FET plus phase-trace path of 0.25 mΩ effective per channel:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.6\ W}$$

The shared battery bus also loses $(4 \times 80)^2 \times R_{bus}$. At R_bus = 0.1 mΩ that is **10 W total**. This is why battery pads must be as close to the FET bank as possible.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the high-side FET of the PWM'd phase hard-switches. The synchronous low side switches at near-zero V_DS (ZVS-like) after dead time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

Gate plateau currents are estimated from the driver strength and the plateau voltage (V_pl ≈ 4 V, V_drv = 10–12 V, R_g,total ≈ 6 Ω):

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{(V_{drv}-V_{pl})/R_g} = \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{1.0\,\text{A}} \approx 9\,\text{ns} \;\;(\text{use } 15\,\text{ns with } Q_{gs2})$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V\,f_{sw} = 30\,\text{nC}\times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

Add roughly 0.1 W for the I_rr-induced overlap in the high-side FET.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss}\,V\,f_{sw} = 0.5\times 40\,\text{nC}\times 25.2\times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.024\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

With t_dt = 100 ns, V_F = 0.8 V, and two dead-time intervals per cycle:

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8\times 80\times 200\,\text{ns}\times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

Dead time is your single biggest tuning lever after FET selection. Reducing it to 50 ns saves 0.3 W per channel.

### 2.5 Gate charge

This loss lands in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET:

$$P_g = Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw}\times 2 = 40\,\text{nC}\times 10\times 48\text{k}\times 2 = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

Bootstrap capacitor sizing:

$$C_{boot} \ge \frac{10\,Q_g}{\Delta V} = \frac{400\,\text{nC}}{0.5\,\text{V}} = 0.8\ \mu\text{F} \;\Rightarrow\; 1\,\mu\text{F X7R 25 V}$$

### 2.6 Per-channel loss budget at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

| Component | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (2 FETs) | 21.8 |
| Copper traces | 1.6 |
| V–I switching | 1.45 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.16 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 25.6 W** |
| **Four channels + bus** | **≈ 112 W** |

At 25.2 V × 80 A = 2016 W per channel, efficiency is about 98.7%. The board still has to absorb about 112 W, which it cannot reject at steady state.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network

Per channel, the thermal path is a Foster/Cauer hybrid:

```
P_FET ──[R_jc 1.5 K/W]──┬──[R_cb 0.8 K/W via array]──┬──[R_ba]── T_amb
                       C_j (~0.05 J/K)               C_board (~1.0 J/K)
```

### 3.2 Board heat capacity per channel (~2 cm² zone)

| Item | Calculation | J/K |
|---|---|---|
| Copper (6 × 70 µm) | 0.084 cm³ × 3.45 J/cm³K | 0.29 |
| FR-4 (1.6 mm) | 0.32 cm³ × 1.9 J/cm³K | 0.61 |
| 6 FET packages + solder | | ≈ 0.10 |
| **C_ch** | | **≈ 1.0 J/K** |
