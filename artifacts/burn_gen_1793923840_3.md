# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion

**Scope and assumptions.** I don't have the Job #38 specification, so this design uses typical brushless gimbal parameters. Every parameter is in a config struct, so you can replace the defaults with your actual hardware values.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Rotor angle sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per motor (AS5048/MT6701 class) |
| Current sensing | 2-phase low-side shunts |
| Attitude sensors | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 8 kHz output data rate (ODR) |
| Mechanical order | Yaw → Roll → Pitch (outer to inner) |
| Current loop (FOC) | 20 kHz, ADC-synchronous ISR |
| Velocity loop | 4 kHz |
| Angle/attitude loop | 1 kHz |
| IMU filtering | 8 kHz, decimated to 4 kHz |

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

With two phase currents measured and the third reconstructed as $i_c = -(i_a + i_b)$:

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):**
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, where $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$, $p$ is the number of pole pairs, $\theta_m$ is the mechanical angle, and $\theta_{\text{off}}$ is the encoder-to-electrical offset:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Current PI Design

The dq-frame voltage equations are:
$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward.** Add these terms to the PI outputs:
$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Gain selection.** After decoupling, each axis reduces to $G(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls + R}$. A PI controller $C(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with the zero placed to cancel the electrical pole gives a closed loop that is a first-order system with bandwidth $\omega_c$:
$$
K_p^{(i)} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{(i)} = R\,\omega_c
$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{PWM}/20$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi \cdot 1\,\text{kHz}$.

**Discrete form with back-calculation anti-windup:**
$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad \bar u_k = \text{sat}(u_k),\qquad
I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\big(K_i e_k + K_{aw}(\bar u_k - u_k)\big),\quad K_{aw} \approx K_i/K_p
$$

**Voltage vector limiting.** The circular SVPWM limit is $V_{max} = V_{bus}/\sqrt3$. Prioritise the d-axis, then give the q-axis the remainder:
$$
|v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

> **Note:** Gimbal motors with $R \gtrsim 10\,\Omega$ are often run in voltage mode ($v_q \approx R\,i_q^* + \omega_e\lambda_m$). The code supports both modes via `use_current_loop`.

### 1.3 Inverse Park and SVPWM (Min/Max Injection)

**Inverse Park:**
$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

**Phase voltages:**
$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

**Common-mode injection and duty cycle:**
$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle / Velocity Control with Inertial Stabilization

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant

For each joint, with joint inertia $J$, viscous friction $B$, torque constant $K_t = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m$ (amplitude-invariant), cogging torque $\tau_{cog}$, and disturbance torque $\tau_d$:
$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_d
$$

### 2.2 Control Structure

The angle loop runs on IMU-measured camera attitude. The velocity loop runs on gyro-measured inertial rate mapped into joint space. The reference flows through the cascade as follows:

```
θ*_cam ─►[Angle P(I)]─► ω*_cam ─► J⁻¹(q) ─► q̇* ─►[Velocity PI + LPF]─► i_q* ─►(+ i_ff_cog + i_ff_accel)─► FOC
              ▲                                     ▲
       attitude (fusion)                 q̇ = J⁻¹(q)·ω_gyro − (base rate from encoder diff)
```

### 2.3 Gimbal Jacobian (Yaw–Roll–Pitch)

Let $q = [\psi, \phi, \vartheta]$ be the yaw, roll, and pitch joint angles. The camera angular velocity, expressed in the camera frame, is the sum of the base rotation and the three joint rates:
$$
\boldsymbol\omega_{cam} = R^\top_{cam/base}\,\boldsymbol\omega_{base} + J(q)\,\dot q
$$

The Jacobian $J(q)$ maps joint rates to camera-frame angular velocity:
$$
J(q) = \begin{bmatrix}
-\cos\phi\sin\vartheta & \cos\vartheta & 0\\
\sin\phi & 0 & 1\\
\cos\phi\cos\vartheta & \sin\vartheta & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$
