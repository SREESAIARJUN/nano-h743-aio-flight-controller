# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC "Nano" Stack: Design Model (Job #15)

## 0. Scope and Feasibility Findings

Several issues in the request need flagging before anyone commits to layout.

| # | Issue | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU, not the ESC MCU.** 4-in-1 ESCs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 on a small MCU per board (STM32G071, AT32F421, STM32F051). ArduPilot runs on the H743 and only *talks* DShot to the ESC. | Architecture is a 2-board stack (FC + ESC) or a combined AIO board with 1 + 4 MCUs. Both are modeled below. |
| F2 | **80A burst per motor on a nano (20×20 mm) footprint is not thermally credible** beyond about 1 s with all four motors loaded. See §2.4. | Re-rate to 40–45A burst on 20×20, or move to 30×30 with 5×6 FETs. |
| F3 | 48 kHz PWM at high current roughly doubles switching loss versus 24 kHz. AM32/BLHeli_32 support it, but it is a choice, not a free lunch. | Included in the model; 24 kHz is shown for comparison. |
| F4 | All device parameters below are **representative assumptions**, not a qualified BOM. | Replace them with datasheet values from the selected MOSFET and driver, then re-run the model. |

## 2. Mathematical Model

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, at any instant the current path is exactly **two FETs in series**: one high-side and one low-side, or the sync-rectifying FET of the PWM leg. This holds regardless of duty cycle.

**Temperature-dependent resistance:**

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **2.20 mΩ**

**Per-motor conduction loss:**

P_cond = 2 · I² · R_ds(T_j) = 2 × 80² × 2.20 mΩ = **28.2 W**

**Per-motor copper loss:**

P_Cu = I² · R_pcb = 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

**Per-FET power:**
- Instantaneous while conducting: 80² × 2.2 mΩ = **14.1 W**
- Averaged (each FET conducts about 1/3 of an electrical cycle): ≈ **4.7 W**

**Electrothermal coupling.** Because R_ds depends on T_j, solve the fixed point:

T_j = T_board + P_FET(T_j) · Z_th,jc(t)

This converges in 2–3 iterations for Z_th < 2 K/W. If α · P · Z_th > 1, thermal runaway occurs. With the numbers above, the loop gain is ≈ 0.0055 × 14 × 1.5 ≈ 0.12, so the system is stable.

### 2.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz (per Motor, One Hard-Switched Leg)

| Loss term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k = **1.94 W** |
| C_oss | Q_oss · V · f | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.06 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.05 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD · I · 2t_dead · f | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f | 2 × 40 nC × 10 × 48 k = **0.04 W** (mostly in the driver) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

At 24 kHz the switching total is ≈ 1.35 W, saving about 1.35 W per motor.

### 2.3 Totals

| | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 28.2 W | 112.8 W |
| Copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching (48 kHz) | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| **Total** | **34.1 W** | **≈ 136 W** |

Efficiency check: with phase power ≈ 80 A × ~22 V × duty, at 100% duty the ESC efficiency is about 1 − 34/1760 ≈ 98%. The percentage looks fine; the absolute heat in a 20×20 mm area is the problem.

### 2.4 Thermal Model

**Two-node RC network:** junction → board (thermal mass) → ambient (prop-wash convection).

**Node 1: Board bulk (the limiting node)**
- Thermal capacitance: 20×20×1.6 mm board with heavy copper and 24 FETs ≈ 2–3 g, so C_th ≈ 2.5 J/K
- Thermal resistance to ambient with prop wash: R_th,ba ≈ 8 K/W (no heatsink)
- Time constant: τ = R·C ≈ 20 s

Board temperature response:

ΔT_board(t) = P · R_th · (1 − e^(−t/τ))

**Initial rate of rise:**

dT/dt ≈ P / C_th = 136 / 2.5 ≈ **54 K/s**

| Burst duration | ΔT_board | T_board (from 40 °C) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 s | ≈ 27 K | 67 °C |
| 1.0 s | ≈ 53 K | 93 °C ← practical limit |
| 2.0 s | ≈ 104 K | **144 °C ✗** (exceeds FR4 Tg and capacitor ratings) |
| Steady state | ≈ 1090 K | Physically impossible: it will fail |

**Node 2: Junction above board**

ΔT_jb = P_FET · Z_th,jb(t_pulse) ≈ 14.1 W × ~0.5 K/W (ms-scale pulse) ≈ **7 K**

The FET die is not the bottleneck. **The board's heat capacity and its path to air are the bottleneck.**

**Allowable burst duration** (T_max = 125 °C, T_0 = 40 °C):

t_max = −τ · ln(1 − ΔT_allow / (P · R_th)) ≈ **1.6 s** (all four motors at 80 A)

**Recommendation:**
- Specify **40A continuous / 55A 1 s burst** for 20×20, or
- Move to a **30.5×30.5 board with 5×6 FETs** (R_ds ≈ 0.8 mΩ, C_th ≈ 8 J/K) to reach a credible "80A burst, 2–3 s".
- Add firmware current limiting in AM32 and ArduPilot `MOT_BAT_CURR_MAX`, plus ESC temperature telemetry.
