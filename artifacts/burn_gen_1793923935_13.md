## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Check

**Architectural clarification.** The STM32H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors itself. That would need 24 complementary PWM outputs with hardware dead-time and four independent sensorless back-EMF observers. The production-viable topology is therefore:

| Board | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| FC (top) | STM32H743VIH6, 480 MHz | ArduPilot | Attitude control. Emits bidirectional DShot600 on 4 lines. |
| ESC (bottom) | 4× STM32G071CBU6 (or AT32F421) | AM32 | Per-motor 6-step commutation. Returns GCR eRPM telemetry. |

**Assumed operating point.** All derived numbers depend on these. Change the inputs and re-run the equations.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max / 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN (e.g. BSZ010NE2LS5-class or NCEP40T15-class) |
| On-resistance at 25 °C | R_ds(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | k_T | 1.6 at T_j = 125 °C |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 45 nC @ 10 V |
| Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 35 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Gate driver | — | FD6288Q (1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink), R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω |
| Board | — | 20×20 mm mounting (≈ 26×26 mm outline), 6-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility verdict, stated up front.** 80 A per motor on a 20×20 nano stack is only a **short-burst rating, on the order of 1–2 s**, and only if not all four motors burst simultaneously. Simultaneous 4×80 A means 320 A from the battery, which no nano-class XT60 / 14 AWG lead set can carry. Section 3 derives this limit quantitatively. Any "80 A" marketing figure for this form factor must be qualified with a duration and a duty cycle.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Per Motor, 6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Current path

In trapezoidal 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:

- one leg is PWM'd with complementary high-side/low-side switching;
- the return leg's low-side FET is held fully ON.

With synchronous rectification, the current therefore always flows through **exactly two channels in series**:

- **PWM on-time:** HS(A) → winding → LS(B)
- **PWM off-time:** LS(A) → winding → LS(B), freewheeling

Over a full PWM period, ignoring dead time:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

Under a burst at stall-like loading, phase current is approximately flat, so I_rms ≈ I_pk.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

Add PCB copper and solder resistance in the loop: about **0.3 mΩ** per FET-to-phase-pad path, giving 2 × 0.3 = 0.6 mΩ series.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Cold (25 °C) | Hot (125 °C) |
|---|---|---|
| FET channels, 2 × I² × R | 2 × 6400 × 1.0 mΩ = 12.8 W | 2 × 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = **20.5 W** |
| Copper path, I² × 0.6 mΩ | 3.8 W | 4.6 W (Cu α = 0.0039) |
| **Conduction subtotal** | **16.6 W** | **25.1 W** |

> **Thermal runaway check.** Conduction loss rises with T_j, and T_j rises with loss. The stable operating point exists only if
>
> $$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{th,ja} < 1$$
>
> With ∂P/∂T ≈ 2I²R₂₅α = 0.077 W/K and R_th,ja ≈ 40 K/W for a DFN3333 on a dense board, the product is ≈ 3.1. That exceeds 1, so **80 A is NOT thermally stable in steady state**. It is a transient rating only, which confirms Section 0.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Each PWM period contains one turn-on and one turn-off hard transition (on the HS), plus dead-time diode conduction and Q_rr in the LS.

### 2.1 Gate-drive-limited transition time

The Miller plateau determines the V-I overlap:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_{drv} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int})}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}$$

Inputs: Q_gd ≈ 10 nC, R_total ≈ 4.7 + 1.0 + 6 (driver output R) ≈ 11.7 Ω, V_drv − V_pl ≈ 10 − 3 = 7 V.

This gives t_r ≈ 16.7 ns. The sink path is faster, so t_f ≈ 12 ns. Including Q_gs2 commutation, use **t_r + t_f ≈ 35 ns**.

### 2.2 Loss components
