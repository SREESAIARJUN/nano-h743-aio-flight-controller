## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology

The ESC uses trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D. The synchronous low-side (LS) conducts for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the LS conducts 100 % of the sector.
