# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #31)

**Scope:** 80 A burst electrothermal model, 48 kHz switching losses, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

**Status:** These are engineering models and reference topology. They are not a production release. Every device parameter marked **[VERIFY]** is an illustrative value that must be replaced with the datasheet value of the part you actually select, and the result must be validated on hardware with a thermocouple or IR camera.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFET bridges. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks bidirectional DShot600 to **four independent ESC MCUs**, which generate the 48 kHz PWM and do commutation.

Typical ESC MCUs are the STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32E230, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. A single H743 cannot also run four sensorless FOC/6-step loops alongside the flight stack in ArduPilot.

```
VBAT ─┬─► 4x [ESC MCU → 3-ph gate driver → 6x N-MOSFET] → Motors 1–4
      │            ▲ DShot600 bidir (1 wire each)
      └─► Buck 5V/3V3 ─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
```

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current Path

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector, current flows through two legs:

- **The PWM leg.** Either the high-side (HS) FET conducts (for duty D) or the low-side (LS) FET in synchronous rectification conducts (for 1−D). One FET of this leg is therefore always in the path.
- **The commutated leg.** Its LS FET is held on for the whole sector.

So two FET channel resistances are in series at all times, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Temperature and Ripple Terms

R_ds(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx0.006\,\text{K}^{-1}\;(\Rightarrow k(125^\circ\text{C})=1.6)$$

Ripple adds to RMS current:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\cdot0.25}{30\,\mu\text{H}\cdot48\,\text{kHz}} = 4.4\,\text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2+\frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400+1.6 \approx 6402\ \text{A}^2 \quad(\text{negligible})$$

### 2.3 Results at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P per conducting FET | P_cond per ESC channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 6.4 W | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 9.3 W | 18.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | 10.2 W | **20.5 W** |

### 2.4 Copper and Shunt Losses

These terms are often forgotten.

- **Phase trace / pour:** 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ effective → 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W/ch**
- **Current shunt:** a 0.25 mΩ shunt carries the summed DC bus current, not 4 × 80 A peak simultaneously in phase. At 4 × 80 A = 320 A it dissipates 25.6 W. Use **≤0.1 mΩ, or a Hall sensor**.

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g} = 14\,\text{ns}$$
