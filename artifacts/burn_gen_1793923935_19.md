## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Sanity Checks

Three assumptions shape everything below, so they come first.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
- The STM32H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1 and TIM8), each with three complementary pairs. That is enough for 2 motors, not 4.
- It also has no fast analog comparators for back-EMF zero-cross on 12 phases.
- The architecture is therefore an **AIO (all-in-one) board**:
  - The H743 runs ArduPilot and drives **4 independent ESC MCUs** (Cortex-M0+/M4 class, AM32 or BLHeli_32-compatible) over on-board bidirectional DShot600 traces.
  - Each ESC MCU drives one 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.

**2. "80 A burst" means 80 A per channel, transient.**
- Four motors at 80 A on 6S is about 300 A of battery current at full duty, roughly 7.5 kW.
- No nano-class board (20×20 mm mounting, about 26×26 mm outline) can sustain that.
- The thermal model in §4 quantifies how long it can last.

**3. Representative component parameters are design variables, not a datasheet.**
- They are listed in Table 1.
- Substitute your selected FET's values. The equations are the deliverable.
