## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate motors directly. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 (AT32F421‑class) or BLHeli_32. The H743 sends DShot600 to the ESC MCUs and receives bi‑directional eRPM telemetry back. This is the only architecture ArduPilot supports, and it keeps the 48 kHz commutation loop off the FC.
