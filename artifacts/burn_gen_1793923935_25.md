# STM32H743 FC + Nano 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Allocation

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each motor on the 4-in-1 ESC is commutated by its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 does not commutate motors. It issues bidirectional DShot600 commands and decodes the eRPM telemetry that comes back.
