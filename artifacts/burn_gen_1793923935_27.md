# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #37)

## 0. Scope, Architecture and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 generates bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs.

Running 4× FOC/6-step plus ArduPilot on one H743 is possible in principle. It is not supportable with stock ArduPilot, and it couples a firmware fault to all four motors.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Current path.** In 6-step trapezoidal drive, current flows through two phase legs at any instant. With synchronous rectification, each active leg always has exactly one FET on (high side for D, low side for 1−D). The conducting resistance is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Temperature dependence of R_ds:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 0.90\,(1+0.0045\cdot85) = 1.244\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2\cdot80^2\cdot1.244\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.9\ W\ per\ channel}$$
