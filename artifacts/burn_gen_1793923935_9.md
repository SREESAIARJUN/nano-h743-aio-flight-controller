## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths

With trapezoidal (6-step) commutation and synchronous rectification, at any instant the phase current flows through exactly **two MOSFET channels**:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D·T. The low-side (sync rect) conducts for (1−D)·T.
- **Return leg:** the low-side is on for the full 60° sector.

So the current always traverses one PWM-leg device plus one return-leg device:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple-corrected RMS current

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor, L_ph ≈ 15 µH and V_bus−V_bemf ≈ 8 V at burst with D ≈ 0.7:

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{8\times0.7}{2\times15\mu\times48k} = 3.9\,\text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; \Delta I^2/12 \approx 1.3\,\text{A}^2$$

This is negligible against 6400 A², so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel (2 devices) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.40 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.40 m = **17.9 W** |
| 100 °C | 1.87 mΩ | **24.0 W** |
| 125 °C | 2.03 mΩ | **26.0 W** |
