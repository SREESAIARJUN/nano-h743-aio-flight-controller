## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Key Findings

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot (flight control). It does not commutate the motors. Each motor is driven by its own ESC MCU running AM32 at f_sw = 48 kHz. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.

```
 6S LiPo ─┬─ Bulk/ceramic bank ─┬─ 4× (3-phase bridge: 6× N-FET + FD6288Q driver + ESC MCU)
          │                     └─ Current shunt / INA → H743 ADC
          └─ 12 V / 5 V / 3.3 V BECs → STM32H743 + IMU + baro + OSD + Rx
 STM32H743 TIM1 CH1..CH4 ──(bidir DShot600, 1 wire per motor)──► ESC MCU 1..4
```
