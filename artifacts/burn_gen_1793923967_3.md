# STM32H743 + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack (Job #8): Thermal, Switching, and DShot DMA Model

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

A design review needs explicit assumptions first, because one of them changes the problem.

| Item | Assumption | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Control partition | The **H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller**. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which generates the 48 kHz PWM. | ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 timers only produce DShot600 and capture bidirectional DShot (BDShot) telemetry. Switching-loss math lives on the ESC side. |
| Bus | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. Worst case uses 25.2 V. | Switching loss scales with V. |
| "80 A burst" | 80 A RMS phase current per channel, ≤10 s, with 4 channels simultaneous as the worst case. | Determines whether the problem is steady-state or transient. |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner copper, 6–8 layers. | **80 A per channel on a 20×20 board is not credible.** I would reject that requirement at review. |
| MOSFET | Representative 40 V N-channel, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 package, one per switch. | Parameters in Section 1. **Replace with your actual datasheet values.** |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver, 12 V gate supply, ~200 ns internal dead time. | Sets gate current and dead-time loss. |
