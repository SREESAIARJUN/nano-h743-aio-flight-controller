# Job #63: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Systems Architecture Package

**Scope:** 80 A burst power-stage loss model, switching losses at 48 kHz, thermal budget, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

A nano 4-in-1 cannot be driven directly by the H743 *and* use DShot, since DShot exists to talk to separate ESC MCUs. The baseline is therefore:

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (VI/VT grade preferred for thermal margin), 400–480 MHz, timer clock 200–240 MHz |
| ESC MCUs | 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32, one per motor |
| Gate drivers | 4 × FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, about 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink) |
| MOSFETs | 24 × 40 V N-ch, 3.3 × 3.3 mm DFN (single FET per switch) |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| PCB | Stacked or single-board, 30 × 30 mm outline with 20 × 20 M3 mounting, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), dead time about 100 ns |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal (AM32/BLHeli_32 default) |

**MOSFET model parameters
