## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.5 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ**. Use this hot value, because burst events start from an already-warm board.

### 1.2 Trapezoidal six-step with complementary PWM

At any instant, two phases carry the full phase current *I*. In the active leg, the high-side (HS) conducts for *D* of the PWM period and the low-side (LS) synchronously rectifies for *(1 − D)*. The opposite leg's LS conducts continuously.

Over an electrical cycle, each leg conducts for 2/3 of the time:

$$P_{cond,leg} = \tfrac{2}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 3 \cdot P_{cond,leg} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**At 80 A:**

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0024 = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

Average per FET (six per ESC) is **5.1 W**, but the split depends on D:

| FET | Share of conduction | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| HS, PWM'ing leg | (1/3)·D | 5.1·D W |
| LS, PWM'ing leg (sync rect.) | (1/3)·(1−D) | 5.1·(1−D) W |
| LS, return leg | 1/3 | 5.1 W |

At 80 A burst, throttle is near full (D → 0.95+), so HS FETs carry nearly all of the leg loss.
