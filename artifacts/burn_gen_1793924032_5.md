## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Decoupled PI

The motor's dq-frame electrical model is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Apply the control law with feedforward decoupling and back-EMF compensation:

$$v_d = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to the first-order plant 1/(Ls + R). **Pole-zero cancellation tuning** with a target closed-loop bandwidth ω_c gives:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_PWM/20. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

$$\|v_{dq}\| \le V_{\max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt{3}}$$
