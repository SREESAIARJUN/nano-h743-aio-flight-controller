# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #23)

## 0. Assumptions

I don't have the Job #23 specification sheet, so the design below rests on the assumptions in this table. All gains are derived symbolically, so you can substitute your real numbers.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (e.g. GM4108), p = 7 pole pairs, R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH (L_d ≈ L_q) |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), read every current cycle |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class: FPU, 3-shunt or 2-shunt inline current sense |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz, velocity 4 kHz, angle 1 kHz, IMU ODR 8 kHz, attitude 1 kHz |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera-side frame, SPI at 24 MHz, data-ready interrupts |
| Supply | V_bus = 12–16 V |

## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(\text{using } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θₑ = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.
