# FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specification for "Job #60", so the design below uses explicit, typical assumptions. Every place that depends on your hardware is a named parameter. Replace the values in Section 0 with your motor datasheet and mechanical CAD numbers.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7 class, FPU, 3× independent PWM timers |
| Motor | 12N14P gimbal BLDC, pole pairs $p = 7$, $R_s \approx 5\,\Omega$, $L_s \approx 2\,\text{mH}$ |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (MT6701 / AS5048A) via SPI |
| Current sense | 2- or 3-shunt, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, gyro/accel ODR = 8 kHz |

**Loop rates.** Each inner loop runs at least 5× faster than the loop around it, and each inner bandwidth is at least 4× the outer bandwidth.

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ~1.5 kHz |
| Rate (velocity) | 4 kHz | ~80–150 Hz |
| Attitude (angle) | 1 kHz | ~15–30 Hz |
| IMU acquisition | 8 kHz | filters run here, then decimated to 4 kHz |

**Control topology per axis.**

$$
\theta_{ref} \xrightarrow{\text{Attitude PI}} \omega_{ref} \xrightarrow{\text{Rate PI} + \text{FF}} i_{q,ref} + i_{q,cog} \xrightarrow{\text{FOC PI}} v_{dq} \to \text{SVPWM}
$$

- The **attitude** feedback is the camera orientation from the fused IMU.
- The **rate** feedback is the fused gyro rate projected onto the joint axis, blended with the encoder rate.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle (with sensor offset $\theta_{off}$ found at calibration):

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

Park transform:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque for a surface PMSM:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 PI with Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Pole-zero cancellation tuning, with target bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$ in rad/s:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

This makes the closed-loop current response $\frac{\omega_{ci}}{s + \omega_{ci}}$. Choose $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/15$ to stay clear of the PWM delay ($1.5\,T_s$).

### 1.4 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

Apply a circular limit $|v_{dq}| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$ (linear SVPWM range). The d-axis has priority:

$$v_d \leftarrow \text{sat}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{sat}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)$$

Use back-calculation anti-windup with $K_{aw} \approx 1/K_p$:

$$x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + T_s\left(K_i e[k] + K_{aw}(u_{sat}[k] - u[k])\right)$$

### 1.5 SVPWM (Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection)

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{InvClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\qquad
v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\right)
$$

$$d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Rate and Attitude Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant per Joint

$$J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - b\,\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}$$

Because the current loop is fast, it is treated as unity gain at rate-loop frequencies.

### 2.2 Encoder Velocity Observer (PLL)

Differencing 14-bit angles at 4 kHz gives roughly 1.5 rad/s of quantization noise. A type-II PLL avoids this:

$$
e = \text{wrap}_\pi(\theta_{enc} - \hat\theta),\qquad
\hat\theta \mathrel{+}= T_s(\hat\omega + K_{p}e),\qquad
\hat\omega \mathrel{+}= T_s K_{i} e
$$
