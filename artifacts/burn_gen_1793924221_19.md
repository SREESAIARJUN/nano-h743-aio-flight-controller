## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600 on four timer channels. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q-class) and six N-channel MOSFETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**. This hot value is used throughout.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

In every 60° sector, current flows through exactly two FETs:
- the high-side FET of the active phase, conducting for D, or its synchronous low-side partner, conducting for 1−D;
- the low-side FET of the return phase, conducting continuously.

Total conduction loss per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$
