# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #1)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly.** Production AIO and 4-in-1 designs use a dedicated ESC MCU per motor, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or Bluejay-class firmware. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. Driving 12 half-bridges with BEMF sensing from the H743 while it also runs the EKF is not supported by ArduPilot and is architecturally unsound. The rest of this package assumes the split architecture.

**80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally marginal.** The model below shows it survives only a few seconds with all four channels loaded. This is a key design-review finding, not a footnote.

All component values below are **assumed reference parameters**. Substitute datasheet values for your selected parts before sign-off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.5 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.18 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

Assume high-side PWM with complementary synchronous rectification. Each phase is driven high for 120°, low for 120°, and floats for 2 × 60°.

Per-FET conduction fractions over one electrical period, with duty cycle D:

| Device | Conduction fraction |
|---|---|
| High-side | D/3 |
| Low-side | 1/3 (static on) + (1−D)/3 (synchronous rectification) |

Losses per leg and per channel:

$$P_{cond,leg} = \tfrac{2}{3}\,I_m^2 R_{DS}, \qquad P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_m^2 R_{DS}$$

The per-channel result follows because two FETs are always in the current path.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| I_m² R | 6400 × 0.00218 | 13.95 W |
| Per FET (worst case) | 13.95 / 3 | **4.65 W** |
| Per channel | 2 × 13.95 | **27.9 W** |
| 4 channels | 4 × 27.9 | **111.6 W** |

Note that I_bus = D·I_m. "80 A" here means **phase current**, so verify which definition your marketing spec uses.

### 1.4 Electro-thermal coupling (runaway check)

The junction temperature depends on the loss, and the loss depends on the junction temperature:

$$T_j = T_c + R_{\theta JC}\,P_{FET}(T_j), \qquad P_{FET}=\tfrac{1}{3}I_m^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_c + R_{\theta JC}\,k\,(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_{\theta JC}\,k\,\alpha}, \qquad k=\tfrac{1}{3}I_m^2R_{25}=3.2\text{ W}$$

- Loop gain: R_θJC·k·α = 1.5 × 3.2 × 0.006 = **0.029**. This is much less than 1, so the system is stable locally (no junction-level runaway).
- At T_c = 100 °C: T_j ≈ (100 + 4.08)/0.971 ≈ **107 °C**.

The real limit is the **board** temperature, not the junction-to-case drop (see Section 3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In six-step operation, only one leg per channel switches at any instant.

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48k | 1.94 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | 2·V_F·I·t_dt·f_sw = 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48k | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 0.036 W |
| C_oss (2 FETs) | 2 × ½·C·V²·f_sw | 0.030 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 0.038 W |
| **Total per channel** | | **≈ 2.65 W** |

**Duty-cycle caveat:** at D → 1 the switching losses vanish. The worst case for combined loss is around D ≈ 0.85–0.95.

**Design lever:** dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per channel. The cost is higher ripple current and audible noise on low-inductance motors.

### Per-channel and board totals (80 A, D ≈ 0.9)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Per channel | 27.9 + 2.65 ≈ **30.5 W** |
| Board (4 channels) | **≈ 122 W** |
| Plus FC, regulators, and shunts | ≈ 3–5 W |

### DC-link capacitor ripple (often the hidden failure)

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I_m\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

This peaks at **40 A rms per channel** at D = 0.5. The four channels interleave only partially. Required measures:

- Bulk low-ESR polymer capacitor plus ≥ 4 × 10 µF X7R per channel, placed adjacent to the bridges.
- An external 35 V, ≥ 1000 µF capacitor at the pigtail is **mandatory**.
