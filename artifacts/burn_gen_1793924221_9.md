**Feasibility flag 1: form factor.** A true "nano" board (16×16 or 20×20 mm) cannot physically hold 24 power MOSFETs, 4 gate drivers, 4 ESC MCUs, an H743 (LQFP100 at minimum) and bus capacitance while carrying 80 A per channel. This design assumes a stacked 30.5×30.5 mm footprint: an FC board on top of an ESC board. If 20×20 is a hard requirement, the realistic burst rating is about 35–45 A per channel.

**Feasibility flag 2: ESC architecture.** Bidirectional DShot implies a separate MCU on each ESC channel. The H743 does not drive gates directly. The architecture is therefore:

- **H743:** ArduPilot. Sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071):** run AM32, one per motor.
- **4× FD6288Q:** 3‑phase gate driver, one per motor.
- **24× MOSFETs.**

The 48 kHz PWM frequency is therefore an AM32 parameter, not an H743 timer setting.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature‑dependent on‑resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At the design limit T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.6 = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path topology

In 6‑step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary PWM, motor current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels:

- During the PWM on‑time: high‑side of phase A, then low‑side of phase B.
- During the off‑time: synchronous low‑side of phase A, then low‑side of phase B.

Body‑diode conduction during dead time is handled separately in §2.4. Because of this, conduction loss is independent of duty cycle to first order:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses

Assume R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ per phase path (2 oz internal planes, vias) and R_shunt = 0.25 mΩ on the bus.

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times (2 \times 0.4\,\text{m}\Omega) = 5.1\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{shunt} = 80^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Per‑FET distribution

Each phase's high‑side FET is in the active path for 120° electrical (1/3 of the period). The low‑side FET carries both its own 120° interval and the freewheel current.

- Average per FET: P_cond / 6 ≈ 3.4 W.
- Worst low‑side FET at low duty: up to about 2/3 × I²R ≈ 6.8 W.

The worst low‑side value is used as the hot‑spot input in §4.

## 2. Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM‑switched leg hard‑switches per period: one turn‑on and one turn‑off.

### 2.1 V‑I overlap (hard switching)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 30\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2}Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead‑time body‑diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
