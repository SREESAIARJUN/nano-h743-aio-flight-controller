# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #23): Power-Stage, Thermal and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32) that commutate at 48 kHz. The H743 does not drive gates directly; doing that while running ArduPilot is not viable.

**These are worked numbers, not verified hardware values.** The device parameters below are representative of 40 V, 5×6 mm PQFN MOSFETs. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values before release; every equation is written so you can substitute.
