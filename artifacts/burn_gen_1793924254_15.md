# Job #37: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst Design Package

## 0. Executive Summary

Before the derivations, here are three findings that should change the specification.

1. **80 A burst per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) 4-in-1 is thermally limited to about 1 s with all four motors loaded.** The derivation (§2.4) shows roughly 85 W dissipated into about 1.2 J/K of board heat capacity, so the board heats at about 70 K/s. The rating is defensible only as a per-motor transient (<1 s) or on a 30.5×30.5 mm board with a heatsink.
2. **4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery current is not a valid system condition.** XT60 connectors, 12 AWG leads, the input bulk capacitor, and a single summing shunt cannot carry it (§2.6). Specify 80 A as a single-channel burst with a total-board limit of about 160 A.
