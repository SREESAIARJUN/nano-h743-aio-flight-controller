# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Design Flags

I don't have the Job #2 requirements document, so this derivation uses the baseline parameters below. Every equation is parametric: substitute your actual BOM values and the numbers update directly.

**Architecture clarification.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and talks bidirectional DShot to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), which drive the gate drivers. The netlist reflects this split.
