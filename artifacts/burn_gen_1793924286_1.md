# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design-Risk Flag

These items determine everything that follows.

1. **Two MCU domains.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It generates DShot600 and decodes the bidirectional telemetry. It does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. Each ESC channel, or a shared ESC MCU, runs its own commutation firmware: AM32 on an AT32F421 or STM32G071, or BLHeli_S/Bluejay on EFM8. That firmware produces the 48 kHz PWM. The two domains are coupled only through the DShot line.
2. **80 A per motor on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is not thermally sustainable.** The math in §3 shows about 100 W of dissipation at full burst. Junction temperature reaches 150 °C in roughly 1.5–2 s even with prop-wash cooling. Treat "80 A" as a **≤1 s burst rating**. Enforce it in firmware with current and temperature limiting. Otherwise, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm or larger board.
3. **All device parameters below are representative assumptions.** They are not a specific part's datasheet values. Replace them with your selected MOSFET's datasheet curves and re-run the equations. Netlists are reference topologies and require ERC/DRC and bench validation.

## 2. Loss Model (per Motor, per Phase-Leg Set)

In 6-step complementary mode, the current path at any instant passes through **two FETs in series**:
- one leg that is PWM-switching, where current alternates between the high-side FET and its synchronous low-side FET, and
- one leg whose low-side FET is held on.

The third leg floats for BEMF sensing.

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{path}$$

The ripple current adds to the RMS value:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH at D = 0.5:
ΔI_pp ≈ 25.2·0.25 / (15 µH·48 kHz) ≈ 8.8 A.
The correction is ΔI²/12 ≈ 6.4 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². It is dropped below.

**At T_j = 125 °C (R_ds = 1.6 mΩ):**

$$P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1.6\text{ m}\Omega = 20.5\text{ W}$$
$$P_{cond,Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.5\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\text{ W}$$

### 2.2 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Edges, One Switching Leg)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 1.94\text{ W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-Time (Body-Diode) Loss

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I_{ph} \cdot 2 t_{dead} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 1.23\text{ W}$$
