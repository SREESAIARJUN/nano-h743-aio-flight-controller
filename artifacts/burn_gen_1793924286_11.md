## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Design Basis

Two corrections shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 commands. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, for example an STM32G071, AT32F421 or EFM8BB51 running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay. That MCU drives the gate driver at 48 kHz. Switching losses are therefore an ESC-board problem, and DMA allocation is an FC-board problem.
2. **80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** Section 4 quantifies this. Expect roughly 1 s with all four channels at 80 A, or a few seconds with a single channel. Market "80 A nano" claims are almost always single-channel, sub-second bursts.
