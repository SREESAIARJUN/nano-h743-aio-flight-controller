# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Design Package (Job #51)

**Scope:** Electro-thermal model at 80 A burst, 48 kHz switching loss model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, schematic netlists, and verification plan.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Flag

The numbers below are worked out in full, and they lead to an uncomfortable conclusion: **80 A per channel × 4 on a 20 × 20 mm nano board is a ~1 second burst rating, not a sustainable rating.** That is not a schematic problem. It is a copper-mass and surface-area problem, so the burst rating must be defined with an explicit duration. Section 4 derives the burst duration and the levers for extending it.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | 40 V MOSFETs required for margin |
| Board | 20 × 20 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz on power layers | "Nano" form factor |
| FC MCU | STM32H743 @ 400 MHz, timer clock 200 MHz | APB1/APB2 timer kernels ×2 |
| ESC MCUs | 4 × AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | The H743 does **not** commutate motors; it sends DShot to per-channel ESC MCUs |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) per channel | Typical 4-in-1 topology |

| Switching | 48 kHz, complementary PWM, dead time 200 ns | Hard-switched, 6-step trapezoidal |
