**Design Package, Job #64, Rev A**

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 cannot practically run the motors and the flight stack at once. ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation firmware. This package therefore uses the industry-standard partition:

| Domain | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP-100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 4× AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6, one MCU per motor | AM32 (BLHeli_32 is EOL) |
| Link | 4× bidirectional DShot600, single wire per motor | — |

### 0.2 Form-Factor Warning

"Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot carry 80 A per motor even as a burst. The PQFN footprints, the copper cross-section, and the thermal mass (§3.4) all fall short.

This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board stacked under a 20×20 or 30×30 FC**. If 20×20 is a hard requirement, derate to roughly 35–40 A burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Waveform

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM. At any instant, two phase legs carry the phase current I:

- One high-side (HS) FET of phase A, and
- One low-side (LS) FET of phase B.

During the PWM off-time, current freewheels through the complementary FET of the PWM'd leg. Either way, **exactly two R_DS paths are always in series**.

For flat-top current, the RMS value equals the DC value: I_rms ≈ I_pk = 80 A.
