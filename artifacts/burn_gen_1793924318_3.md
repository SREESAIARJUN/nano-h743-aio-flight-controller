## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. In a standard AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends **bi-directional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs (e.g., 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32). Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, and the eRPM
