## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The amplitude-invariant **Clarke transform** converts phase currents to the stationary frame:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

The **Park transform** rotates them into the rotor frame, using the electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant in the dq Frame (Surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

$$
\tau_m = \tfrac32 p\lambda\, i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI Controller with Decoupling Feedforward

The control law cancels the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

**Gain selection.** Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole at R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad
G_{cl}(s)=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

- Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. Here $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1000$ rad/s.
- Discrete form (backward Euler): $x_i[k] = x_i[k-1] + K_i T_s e[k]$.
