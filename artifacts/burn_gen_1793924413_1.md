# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #22): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Timers, Netlist

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### Architecture
- **STM32H743 role.** The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors.
- **ESC MCUs.** Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32. I assume an AT32F421 at 120 MHz; an STM32G071 also works.
- **48 kHz PWM.** This is an ESC-side parameter (AM32 `PWM frequency`), not an H743 parameter.
- **H743 to ESC link.** The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry back on the same wire.

### Feasibility warning
80 A per motor on a nano-format board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is only survivable as a short burst lasting a few seconds. Section 3 quantifies this. Treat "80 A" as a transient rating, not a continuous one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path
AM32 uses 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant the current flows through two conducting FETs:
- the PWM leg, where the high side (HS) and low side (LS) alternate as D / (1−D), and
- the low-side-on leg.

Total conducting resistance is therefore always ≈ 2·R_ds, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple contribution
$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{22.2 \cdot 0.25}{30\,\mu H \cdot 48\,kHz} = 3.85\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 1.24 \approx 6401\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple is negligible at 48 kHz. This is one of the benefits of a high PWM frequency.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence
$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per motor | Per FET (two conducting) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W |
| 80 °C | 1.36 mΩ | 17.4 W | 8.7 W |
| 110 °C | 1.55 mΩ | 19.9 W | 9.9 W |
| 125 °C | 1.65 mΩ | 21.1 W | 10.6 W |

### 1.4 Copper loss
Phase pads and the bus plane add roughly 0.3 mΩ per channel at 2 oz:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \cdot 0.3\,m\Omega = 1.9\ \text{W per motor}$$

A bus-side current shunt carrying 4×80 = 320 A would dissipate I²R ≈ 25 W even at 0.25 mΩ. This is unacceptable, so use **per-channel phase sensing or a ≤0.1 mΩ shunt**. Alternatively, accept that ESC current sensing reports only the bus total with a very low-value shunt.
