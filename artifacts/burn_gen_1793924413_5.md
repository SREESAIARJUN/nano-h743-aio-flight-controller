## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Red Flags

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32. The H743 has no spare high-resolution complementary timers for 4×3 phases with sensorless BEMF zero-cross. Industry-standard AIO boards partition the work this way:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 x4──► 4x AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) ──► 4x FD6288Q ──► 24x N-MOSFET ──► 4 motors
```

### 0.3 Red Flags (Read First)

1. **"Nano" vs 80 A.** A 20×20 mm board cannot physically carry 4×80 A. The numbers below show it is thermally limited to about 1–1.5 s of simultaneous 4-motor burst even on a 25×25 mm board. **Recommendation: a 30.5×30.5 board, or an 80 A rating that applies to a single motor transient only.**
2. **Total-current shunt.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt at 320 A dissipates 25.6 W. Use ≤0.1 mΩ, or a Hall sensor (e.g., a TMCS1100-class part is too small; use a CC6920-class or a custom busbar Hall sensor).
3. **Every MOSFET parameter above is a placeholder.** Re-run Section 1 with the chosen part's R_DS(on)(T_j), Q_gd and Q_rr curves.
