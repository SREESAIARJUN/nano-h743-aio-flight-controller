## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology
With 6-step trapezoidal BLDC control and synchronous rectification (complementary PWM), exactly **one high-side and one low-side path carry I_ph at every instant**:
- During the ON portion of PWM, the chopping leg's high-side (HS) conducts.
- During the OFF portion, the same leg's low-side (LS) freewheels synchronously.

The return leg's LS conducts continuously for the 60° sector. Total conduction loss per motor is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The per-device average comes from each of the 6 FETs owning 1/3 of the electrical cycle, split by D/(1−D) on the chopping leg:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6}\quad\text{(time-averaged)},\qquad P_{cond,FET,inst}=I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}
$$

Electrical frequency at a typical 30k eRPM is about 500 Hz, which is far faster than the package thermal time constant. The average is therefore valid for the junction-to-board path. The instantaneous value matters only for Z_θJC at ms scales (§3.3).

### 1.2 Temperature-coupled solution
R_ds rises with T_j, and T_j rises with loss. This gives a fixed-point problem:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_\theta\,P(T_j),\qquad P(T_j)=2I^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

$$
\Rightarrow\quad T_j = \frac{T_{ref}+R_\theta\,2I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1-R_\theta\,2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha}
$$

**Stability criterion (thermal runaway):** R_θ · 2I²R₂₅α < 1.

For burst analysis, evaluate at a design-point T_j = 100 °C:

$$
R_{ds}(100)=0.90\,(1+0.006\cdot75)=1.305\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,motor}=2(80)^2(1.305\text{m})=\mathbf{16.70\ W}
$$

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET}=2.78\ \text{W},\qquad P_{cond,FET,inst}=8.35\ \text{W}
$$

### 1.3 Copper / interconnect loss (often ignored, not negligible)
Phase trace: 2 oz (70 µm), 4 paralleled layers, 4 mm wide, 8 mm long:

$$
R_{Cu}=\frac{\rho L}{wtn}=\frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 8\times10^{-3}}{4\times10^{-3}\cdot70\times10^{-6}\cdot4}\cdot(1+0.0039\cdot60)=0.152\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

The current path per motor is roughly 2 phase segments plus a bus segment, so about 0.4 mΩ:

$$
P_{Cu,motor}\approx 80^2\cdot0.4\text{m}= \mathbf{2.56\ W}
$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the chopping leg switches. Per PWM period there is one HS turn-on and one HS turn-off, plus dead-time diode conduction on the LS.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V-I overlap (HS)

$$
P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(35\text{n})(48\text{k})=\mathbf{1.69\ W}
$$

The effective t_r and t_f are bounded by Miller-plateau charge. For a better estimate, use:

$$
t_{r}\approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}},\qquad t_f\approx\frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{plateau}}
$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS)

$$
P_{dt}=V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2t_{dead})\,f_{sw}=0.85\cdot80\cdot300\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.98\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=45\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=0.054\ \text{W}
$$

Q_rr also adds turn-on loss in the HS. A conservative extra term is about 0.5·Q_rr·V·f ≈ 0.03 W.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss}=\tfrac12 C_{oss(er)}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}\cdot 2 = 1.6\text{n}\cdot635\cdot48\text{k}=0.049\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g, not in the FET)

$$
P_g = Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw}\cdot 2 = 55\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}\cdot2=0.053\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.6 Loss budget at 80A burst, T_j=100 °C

| Term | Per motor (W) | ×4 motors (W) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 16.70 | 66.8 |
| Copper | 2.56 | 10.2 |
| Switching overlap | 1.69 | 6.8 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.98 | 3.9 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.13 | 0.5 |
| Gate | 0.05 | 0.2 |
| **Total** | **22.1** | **88.4** |

Electrical output is roughly 22.2 V × 80 A × 4 ≈ 7.1 kW, so η_stage ≈ 98.8%. **Conduction dominates (~76%)**, so the 48 kHz choice is thermally cheap. Going from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs only about +5.4 W board-wide.
