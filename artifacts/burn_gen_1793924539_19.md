## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths with synchronous rectification

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two switches:

- **PWM on-time (duty D):** high side of phase X, then low side of phase Y.
- **PWM off-time (1 − D):** low side of phase X, then low side of phase Y.

Therefore, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{shunt} + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 75) = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Ripple contribution to RMS current

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} \;\xrightarrow{D=0.5}\; \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.56\,A$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\,A^2$$

The ripple term is negligible (0.06 %), so I_rms ≈ 80 A.
