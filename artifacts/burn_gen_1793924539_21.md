## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° electrical sector, two phases conduct. Take phase A as PWM-high and phase B as low:

- **During D·T_sw:** current flows through Q_AH, the motor, then Q_BL.
- **During (1−D)·T_sw:** current freewheels through Q_AL (synchronous), the motor, then Q_BL.

At every instant, exactly **two MOSFET channels** carry I_ph, regardless of duty cycle. The phase-current ripple at 48 kHz is small compared with 80 A for typical motor inductance (around 10–30 µH), so treat I_ph as flat. The ripple correction is:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{on}(T_j) = R_{on,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \textbf{[A]}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_on = 1.2 × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{on}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2\,R_{Cu,path}$$

| T_j | R_on | 2·I²·R_on (80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | 20.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | **22.3 W** |

### 1.4 Copper loss

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t},\quad \rho_{Cu}(T)=1.72\times10^{-8}[1+0.00393(T-20)]$$

Example: a phase pour 20 mm long × 4 mm wide in 2 oz copper (70 µm), paralleled across two layers, at 100 °C gives about 0.66 mΩ ÷ 2 = 0.33 mΩ.

The conducting loop includes two phase pours plus the bus. Take R_Cu,path ≈ 0.6 mΩ **[A]**, which adds 80² × 0.6 mΩ = **3.8 W per channel**. On a nano board, copper loss is not negligible.

### 1.5 Average loss per FET (for junction calculations)

Over one electrical revolution, each leg's high-side and low-side FETs share the conduction duty:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch,FET}}{6}\ \ (\text{average}),\qquad \hat P_{FET} = I_{ph}^2 R_{on}\ \ (\text{instantaneous peak})$$

At 125 °C: average = 22.3 / 6 ≈ **3.7 W**, peak = **11.1 W**.
