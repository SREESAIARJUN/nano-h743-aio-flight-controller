# STM32H743 + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Flight Controller (Job #46): Power-Stage Model, DShot Timing, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Feasibility Warning

**80 A burst on all four channels of a nano-class stack exceeds what the board can sustain thermally.** The model in §3 shows about 107 W of total loss at 4 × 80 A. With realistic prop-wash cooling, the board reaches a 110 °C limit in about **3 seconds**. Its continuous rating is closer to **35–40 A per channel**.

I treat 80 A as a **≤3 s burst** rating and size everything to that.

- **Board format:** I assume a 30.5 × 30.5 mm (M3) footprint with 5×6 mm DFN FETs. A true 20×20 "nano" cannot carry 80 A per phase through its copper.
- **Placeholder parameters:** All device values marked ⟨P⟩ are typical figures for the 40 V, ~1 mΩ class. Replace them with datasheet values from your chosen parts before you sign anything off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

- R₂₅ = 1.0 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V ⟨P⟩
- α ≈ 0.005 K⁻¹ ⟨P⟩. Fit this from the normalized R_DS(on)-vs-T_j curve in the datasheet.
- At the T_j = 100 °C design point: **R = 1.375 mΩ**

### 1.2 Bridge conduction in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, phase A is PWM'd high, phase B is held low, and phase C floats. With duty D and phase current I:

| Device | Conducts for | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| A-high | fraction D | I²·R·D |
| A-low (sync rect) | fraction (1−D) | I²·R·(1−D) |
| B-low | 100 % of the sector | I²·R |

The D terms cancel, so the loss is independent of duty:

**P_cond,bridge = I²·R·D + I²·R·(1−D) + I²·R = 2·I²·R_DS(on)(T_j)**

At I = 80 A and R = 1.375 mΩ:

P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.375×10⁻³ = **17.6 W per motor**

**Per-FET loss.** Each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the average per FET is about P_cond / 6 ≈ 2.9 W. The instantaneous worst case, the low-side FET during its full-on sector, is I²R = **8.8 W**.

### 1.3 Copper (PCB) conduction

Assume each path has an effective series resistance R_Cu ≈ 1.0 mΩ, covering the bus planes, the phase pour, and the two pads in the loop. This assumes 8 layers with 2 oz on the four power layers.

ρ_Cu(100 °C) ≈ 2.2×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, so:

P_Cu = I²·R_Cu = 6400 × 1.0×10⁻³ = **6.4 W per motor**

This term is frequently underestimated. Verify R_Cu with a DC-IR simulation (Altium PDN, KiCad + Elmer, or similar).
