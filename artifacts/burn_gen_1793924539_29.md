## 0. Architecture Clarifications

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack. It outputs DShot commands and receives eRPM telemetry. Commutation runs on dedicated ESC MCUs with AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware. This design uses one ESC MCU per motor, which is standard for AM32. The "48 kHz" figure is therefore the ESC-side PWM frequency, and DShot600 is the FC-to-ESC link.

**2. "Nano" vs. "80A burst" is the governing constraint.** 4 × 80A on a 20×20 mm board is not thermally feasible beyond sub-second events. This analysis assumes:
- a **30.5×30.5 mm stack**: FC board on top, ESC board on the bottom, joined by board-to-board header;
- **6S operation** at 25.2 V maximum.

The math in §2 shows that even this layout supports 80A for only **about 3 s when all four motors burst simultaneously**.

## 2. Loss Model: One ESC Channel at 80A

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, each 60° electrical interval has:
- **Phase A high side:** PWM'd at duty D.
- **Phase A low side:** complementary at (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Phase B low side:** continuously ON.
- **Phase C:** floating, used for BEMF sensing.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

Instantaneous channel conduction loss during the interval:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 R_{HS}(T_j)\,D + I_{rms}^2 R_{LS}(T_j)(1-D) + I_{rms}^2 R_{LS}(T_j) \approx 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R(T_j)
$$

This is independent of D, assuming matched FETs.

$$
R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
