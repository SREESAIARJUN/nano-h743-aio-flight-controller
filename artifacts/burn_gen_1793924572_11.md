# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC: Electrothermal, Switching, and Bidirectional DShot Architecture (Job #7)

## 0. Architecture and Feasibility Notes

Three points shape the rest of this design.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 48 kHz, 6-step or FOC bridge with dead-time insertion needs a dedicated ESC MCU per motor. The architecture is:
- **FC domain:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC domain:** four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Each generates 48 kHz complementary PWM into a 3-phase gate driver.

**2. "80 A" on a nano (20×20 mm) footprint is only a transient rating.** The math below shows this explicitly: all four motors can sustain 80 A for about 0.6 s, and the continuous rating per motor is about 16–20 A without forced cooling. The spec should state the burst duration and duty cycle, not a bare "80 A".

**3. Parameters are representative, not vendor-verified.** The MOSFET and driver values below are typical of 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3 parts. Re-run every equation with your selected part's datasheet at the actual Tj, gate voltage (VGS), and di/dt.
