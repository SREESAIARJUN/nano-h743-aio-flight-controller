**Architecture assumption.** The STM32H743 cannot practically run ArduPilot and also commutate four motors. Its timers and ADCs are not the problem; the real-time load and failure isolation are. The standard and recommended split is:

- **ESC MCUs:** 4× Cortex-M0+/M4 parts (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-compatible firmware. Each uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM at 48 kHz.

> **Headline finding.** On a nano footprint, 80 A per channel is a **~2-second burst rating**, not a continuous one. Four channels at 80 A dissipate about **135 W**. Continuous capability in forced prop-wash is about **25–30 A per channel**. Section 3 derives this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, synchronous rectification)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(125^\circ\text{C}) = 1.5\,\text{mΩ}\times 1.6 = 2.4\,\text{mΩ}$$

Use the hot value, because the loss and the temperature are coupled. Section 3.4 iterates them together.

### 1.2 Current path

In each 60° commutation step, current flows through one high-side FET (phase A) and one low-side FET (phase B).

- With complementary PWM, during the off-time the current freewheels through the **synchronous** FET of the PWM leg. It does not use the body diode, except during dead time.
- So at almost every instant, **two channels' worth of R_DS(on)** sit in series with the phase current, regardless of duty cycle.
- Each FET carries current for 120° of each 360° electrical cycle.

**Per FET:**
$$P_{cond,FET} = I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}\,\tfrac{1}{3} = 80^2 \times 2.4\,\text{mΩ}/3 = \mathbf{5.12\ W}$$

**Per channel (6 FETs):**
$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)} = 6400 \times 4.8\,\text{mΩ} = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

### 1.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W/ch}$$
