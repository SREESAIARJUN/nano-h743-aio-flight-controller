# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

## 0. Architecture Correction

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no on-FC 6-step or FOC motor driver for multirotor ESCs. A working "4-in-1 FC/ESC" uses two tiers of processor:

| Tier | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control and DShot600 master with bidirectional eRPM |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, back-EMF sensing |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is an **AM32 parameter on the ESC MCUs**. The H743 only sees the DShot link. Everything below follows that split.
