# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points shape everything below:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 frames. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor or a shared quad-capable part. The "48 kHz" PWM is a property of that ESC firmware, not of the H743.
2. **"80 A burst" is treated as per channel.** The model shows that 4 × 80 A simultaneously on a 20×20 mm board is a sub-second event. The numbers below quantify this rather than assume it away.
3. **Device parameters are representative.** Every MOSFET and driver value is a typical value for a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN part. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values; the equations hold unchanged.
