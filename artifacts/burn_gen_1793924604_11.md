## 1. Conduction-Path Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two phases are energized in each 60° sector:

- During the PWM on-time D, current flows through the HS FET of phase A, the motor, then the LS FET of phase B.
- During the off-time (1−D), synchronous rectification moves it to the LS FET of phase A, the motor, then the LS FET of phase B.

So **at every instant, exactly two FETs carry I_ph in series, independent of D** (dead time ignored here; it is treated in §1.4). Over a full electrical cycle, each phase is active 2/3 of the time. Per FET:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The channel total is what matters thermally:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}(T_{pcb}) + I^2 R_{shunt}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (closed-loop)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives about 1.69× at 150 °C, typical for 40 V trench FETs. Because R depends on T_j and T_j depends on P, solve by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{ref} + Z_{th}(t)\cdot P\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right)$$

Thermal runaway is excluded if $\; I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS,25}\,\alpha \cdot Z_{th} < 1$.
