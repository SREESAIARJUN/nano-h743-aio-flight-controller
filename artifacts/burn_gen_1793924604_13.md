# Job #71: STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Power-Stage and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.**
- The H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot.
- Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU does the commutation and the 48 kHz PWM.
- The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs.
- The model below follows that split.

**All numbers are parametric.** No MOSFET part number was fixed for Job #71, so I use a representative 40 V, 5×6 mm-class FET. Replace the values in the table with your selected part's datasheet values and re-run the equations.

## 1. Loss Model (per motor channel, 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

During each 60° commutation sector, one high-side FET is PWM'd at duty D. Its complementary low-side FET synchronously rectifies, and the opposite phase's low-side FET is fully on. Each of the six FETs conducts for one third of the electrical cycle.
