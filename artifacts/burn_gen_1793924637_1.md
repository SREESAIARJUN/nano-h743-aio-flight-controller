## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Electrical angle: θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset).

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design
The PMSM dq model is:

$$v_d = Ri_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = Ri_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

After decoupling feedforward (v_d^ff = −ω_e L i_q, v_q^ff = ω_e(L i_d + λ_m)), each axis becomes G(s) = 1/(Ls+R).

Use a PI controller C(s) = K_p + K_i/s, with the zero placed on the electrical pole (pole-zero cancellation):

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{c},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{c}\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; T(s)=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose ω_c ≈ 2π·f_pwm/20, which gives 2π·1 kHz at 20 kHz PWM. This keeps more than 10× separation from the sampling delay of 1.5 T_s (compute plus PWM update).

### 1.3 Discretization and anti-windup
Use forward-Euler integration with back-calculation anti-windup (gain K_aw = K_i/K_p):

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad \hat u_k=\mathrm{sat}(u_k),\quad I_{k+1}=I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(\hat u_k-u_k)\right]$$

The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon-inscribed circle, |v| ≤ V_bus/√3. The q-axis gets priority:

$$v_{q,\max} = \sqrt{(V_{bus}/\sqrt3)^2 - v_d^2}$$

### 1.4 SVPWM (min-max injection, equivalent to sector SVM)

$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{inverse Clarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max+\min),\quad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity Control

### 2.1 Mechanical plant (per axis)

$$J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - \tau_{dist},\qquad K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m$$

### 2.2 Stabilization architecture (key design decision)
For camera stabilization, the outer loops act on **inertial** (IMU) quantities. The encoder is used for commutation and joint limits.

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle P + FF]─► ω_ref ─►[Velocity PI + D_filt]─► i_q,ref ─►(+ i_cog_ff)─►[FOC PI]─► PWM
           ▲ θ_IMU (attitude)          ▲ ω_IMU (fused gyro, axis-projected)
```

Gyro rate is projected into each motor axis through the joint-angle Jacobian. For yaw-roll-pitch (outer → inner), with camera body rates ω_b = [ω_x, ω_y, ω_z] and joint angles φ (roll) and ϑ (pitch):

$$\omega_{pitch}=\omega_y,\quad \omega_{roll}= \omega_x\cos\vartheta - \omega_z\sin\vartheta,\quad \omega_{yaw}=\frac{\omega_x\sin\vartheta+\omega_z\cos\vartheta}{\cos\varphi}\;(\text{guard }|\cos\varphi|>\epsilon)$$

### 2.3 Velocity loop (4 kHz)

$$i_{q,ref} = K_{pv}e_\omega + K_{iv}\!\int\! e_\omega\,dt + \frac{\hat J}{K_t}\dot\omega_{ref}$$

**Gain design (PI, phase-margin placement).** Let J' = J/K_t, the target crossover be ω_v (about ω_c/8), and the PI zero sit at ω_v/4:

$$K_{pv} = J'\omega_v,\qquad K_{iv} = K_{pv}\,\omega_v/4$$

This gives roughly 60° phase margin, including the ~1.5 T_s delay and the IMU filter group delay (see §4.6).
