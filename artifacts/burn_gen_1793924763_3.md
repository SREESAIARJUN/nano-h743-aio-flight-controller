# FOC Control Loop & Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #32)

## 0. Assumptions

Job #32's specific hardware isn't stated, so this design assumes the configuration below. Every assumption is parameterized in the code.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC (GBM2804-class), 12N14P (12 slots, 14 poles, so pole pairs p = 7) |
| Winding | R_s ≈ 5–10 Ω, L_s ≈ 1–3 mH, K_t ≈ 0.05–0.1 N·m/A |

|---|---|---|

## 1. Plant Model

**Electrical (d-q frame, surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L):**

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Here ω_e = p·ω_m, the electrical angle is θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset, and λ_m = (2/3)·K_t/p.

**Mechanical (one joint, decoupled approximation):**

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - B\omega_m - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{fric}(\omega_m) - \tau_{d}
$$

**Cogging torque.** Cogging is periodic in mechanical angle with fundamental order N_c = lcm(N_slots, N_poles). For 12N14P, N_c = lcm(12, 14) = 84. Manufacturing asymmetries add lower orders: N_slots = 12, 2p = 14, and also order 1 (eccentricity). The model is therefore a sparse Fourier series:

$$
\tau_{cog}(\theta_m) = \sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}} \big[a_k\cos(k\theta_m) + b_k\sin(k\theta_m)\big],\quad \mathcal{K}=\{1,12,14,24,28,84,168\}
$$

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

**2.1 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**2.2 Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**2.3 Delay-compensated electrical angle.** The PWM update is applied one period after sampling, and the average voltage lands half a period later. The angle used for the inverse Park transform is therefore advanced by 1.5 sample periods:

$$
\theta_e^{inv} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

**2.4 PI controllers with cross-coupling decoupling.** Pole-zero cancellation tuning makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_ci:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}\quad(\text{continuous: } C(s)=K_p + K_i/s)
$$

Choose ω_ci = 2π · 1.5 kHz, below f_s/10.

Discrete PI (backward Euler) with back-calculation anti-windup, gain K_aw = K_i/K_p:

$$
\begin{aligned}
u^* &= K_p e_k + I_{k}\\
u &= \text{sat}(u^*)\\
I_{k+1} &= I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u - u^*)\right]
\end{aligned}
$$
