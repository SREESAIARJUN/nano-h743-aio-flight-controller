## 0. Scope and Assumptions

No MOSFET part number, board stackup, battery voltage or thermal environment was given for Job #28, so this document uses a **parameterized model** with representative values. Every assumed value is marked **[A]**. Substitute datasheet values before release. Pinouts and ArduPilot conventions are marked **[verify]** where they must be checked against the RM0433 reference manual, the target datasheets and current ArduPilot master.

**Architecture clarification.** On a commercial AIO, the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, one per motor (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32). Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step / synchronous PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, one phase is driven by PWM and one phase is sunk by a low-side switch held on:

- **PWM phase:** the high side conducts for duty D, the synchronous low side for (1 − D).
- **Return phase:** the low side conducts for 100% of the sector.

At any instant, exactly **two MOSFETs carry the full phase current I**. Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \textbf{[A]}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.60 = **1.60 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Additional series resistances (per channel)

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt resistor **[A]** | 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| Phase and bus copper, ~15 mm of 2 oz, multilayer stitched **[A]** | ~0.3 mΩ effective | 1.92 W |
| Solder joints and pads | ~0.1 mΩ | 0.64 W |

### 1.4 Results at 80 A

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.60\,\text{m}\Omega = 20.5\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 20.5 + 1.60 + 1.92 + 0.64 \approx 24.6\ \text{W per channel}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only one leg switches per sector. Each period contains one turn-on and one turn-off hard transition.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus}\, I\,(t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 24\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = 1.16\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1\text{ nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.015\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Body-diode conduction during dead time

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2 t_{dead} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.31\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr rises with di/dt and temperature, so derate ×2 for the worst case: **0.12 W**.

### 2.5 Gate drive

Two gates (HS and LS of the PWM leg) are charged each cycle:

$$P_{g} = 2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors.

### 2.6 Switching total per channel

| Term | Loss |
|---|---|
| Overlap | 1.16 W |
| C_oss | 0.02 W |
| Dead time | 0.31 W |
| Q_rr (derated) | 0.12 W |
| Gate | 0.04 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 1.65 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** ∂P/∂f_sw ≈ 34 µW/Hz. Going from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs about **0.8 W per channel at 80 A**. This is small next to conduction loss. At 80 A the design is **conduction-dominated (~94%)**.
