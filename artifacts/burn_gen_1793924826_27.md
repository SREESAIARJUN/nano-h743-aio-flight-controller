## 0. Architecture Review: Read This First

Before the math, there are three findings the program needs to address. The numbers below support each one.

| # | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **24 × PDFN5x6 MOSFETs (~30 mm² each, ~720 mm² total) cannot fit on a 20×20 mm "nano" board (400 mm² per side).** A nano board forces PDFN3x3 parts at roughly 2× the R_DS(on), which doubles conduction loss. | Blocking |
| F2 | **80 A on all four channels at once is thermally transient-only.** With realistic board heat capacity, junctions reach their limit in about 2–4 s (§3.3). "80 A burst" must be specified as a duration and duty, not a rating. | Spec change |
| F3 | **The STM32H743 does not drive the gates.** Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class). The H743 only emits bi-directional DShot600 (§4). | Clarification |

**Recommendation:** use a 30.5×30.5 mm stack (H743 FC + separate 4-in-1 ESC) with PDFN5x6 FETs. Specify the rating as **"80 A / 5 s burst per channel, 2 channels concurrent, 45 A continuous at 40 °C ambient with prop wash."** All calculations below assume this 30.5 mm ESC board unless noted otherwise.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per channel)

### 2.1 Conduction topology

In 6-step drive, each 60° electrical sector has current flowing through exactly **two conducting switch positions**: one high-side and one low-side. With complementary PWM on the active leg, the high-side FET conducts for D and its low-side partner for (1 − D). The total conduction path still contains two R_DS(on) in series at all times.

Total conduction loss for the channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of } D\text{)}$$

Each phase leg carries current for 240° of 360° (120° high-side, 120° low-side). The average per-FET conduction loss is therefore:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}$$

The hottest device is the PWM'd high-side FET at high duty. Its sector loss is D·I²R over its 120° window:

$$P_{cond,HS,max} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\, I^2 R_{DS}$$

### 2.2 Conduction numbers (T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 0.90 × 1.55 = 1.395 mΩ)

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.395\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.86\ W}$$

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = 2.98\text{ W}$$

**Copper term (2 oz × 6 layers, phase path ≈ 12 mm × 6 mm):**

- Single layer: R_layer = ρ·L/(t·w) = 1.72e-8 × 0.012 / (70e-6 × 6e-3) = 0.49 mΩ
- Six layers in parallel: 0.082 mΩ
- At 100 °C (×1.31): 0.107 mΩ

Each conducting phase path sees this resistance. Adding about 0.15 mΩ of pad/via spreading per path:

$$P_{Cu,ch} \approx 2 \times 6400 \times 0.26\text{ m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{3.3\ W}$$
