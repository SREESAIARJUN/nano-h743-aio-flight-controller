# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #32): Design Model

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the power stage directly. In this design class (and in ArduPilot), the H743 is the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32). Each ESC MCU:

- generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM,
- drives a 3-phase gate driver, and
- switches 6 MOSFETs.

The model below covers both halves.

### 0.2 Feasibility Warning (Read First)

"Nano" plus "80 A" is thermally inconsistent for anything except a few-second burst. The math in §3 shows that a 30×30-mount board sustains roughly **27 A/channel continuous**. The 80 A rating is a **~4–5 s transient**, limited by board heat capacity. Specify it on the datasheet that way, or the product will fail in the field.
