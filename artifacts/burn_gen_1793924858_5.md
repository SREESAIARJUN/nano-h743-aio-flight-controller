# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #8)

## 0. Feasibility Finding

**4 × 80 A burst on a nano (20 × 20 mm) board is not thermally achievable for any useful burst duration.** The model in Section 3 shows the board reaching its 105 °C limit in about 2 seconds. With realistic per-MOSFET spreading resistance, junction temperatures can exceed Tj,max even sooner.

The two achievable options are:

| Option | Form factor | Rating |
|---|---|---|
| A (recommended) | 20 × 20 nano AIO | 4 × 35 A burst (≤5 s), 4 × 25 A continuous |
| B | 30.5 × 30.5 split stack: H743 FC + separate ESC, 8-layer 2 oz, clip-on heatsink | 4 × 80 A burst (≤10 s), 4 × 50 A continuous |
