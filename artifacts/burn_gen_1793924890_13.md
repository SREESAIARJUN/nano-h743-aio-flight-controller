## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0055 K⁻¹ for 40 V trench FETs.

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **1.47 mΩ** per FET.

With 2 FETs in parallel per switch: **R_sw = 0.735 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Commutation-state current paths (6-step, complementary PWM)

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct and the third floats. AM32 uses complementary (synchronous) PWM on the switching leg:

- **Switching leg:** high side conducts for duty D; the synchronous low side conducts for (1 − D).
- **Static return leg:** low side is ON for the full sector.

Conduction loss per motor channel:

P_cond = I² R_sw [D + (1 − D)] + I² R_sw = **2 I² R_sw**

This is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

Adding PWM ripple, ΔI_pp = V_bus D(1 − D) / (L_ph · f_sw):

- With L_ph = 20 µH, D = 0.5, f = 48 kHz: ΔI_pp = 25.2 × 0.25 / (20e-6 × 48e3) = **6.56 A**
- I²_rms,eff = I² + ΔI²/12 = 6400 + 3.6, which is a 0.06 % correction and negligible.

**Per channel at 80 A:**

- P_cond,FET = 2 × 6400 × 0.735e-3 = **9.41 W**
- Copper and bus loss (phase pad, internal planes, solder joints), R_cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ round-trip: P_cu = 6400 × 0.4e-3 = **2.56 W**

**Per FET, worst case** (static low side, 100 % of sector): (80/2)² × 1.47e-3 = **2.35 W**
