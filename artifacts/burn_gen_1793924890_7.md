## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, phase current flows through exactly **two** FETs in series:

- the PWM'd high-side (on for D) or its synchronous low-side (on for 1−D), and
- the commutated phase's low-side (on for 100%).

The time-averaged conducting resistance is therefore always 2·R_ds(on), independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \big[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,sync} + R_{LS,return} \big] \approx 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence
$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Ripple contribution
Phase-current ripple at 48 kHz, with motor phase-pair inductance L ≈ 20 µH (typical 5" 2306 motor) and D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.6\ A_{pp}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \Delta I^2/12} = \sqrt{6400 + 3.6} \approx 80.02\ A
$$

The ripple contribution is negligible (< 0.1 %).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM'd high-side switches hard. The synchronous low-side switches at near-zero voltage (ZVS) but incurs dead-time diode conduction and reverse recovery.

### 2.1 Equations
$$
P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
$$
$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}
\qquad
P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}
$$
$$
P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw}
\qquad
P_{gate} = Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}\ \text{per switching FET (mostly dissipated in driver/R}_g)
$$
