## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Conduction topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During each 60° electrical sector:

- **PWM phase:** the high-side FET (HS) conducts for duty D. The low-side sync FET (LS) conducts for (1 − D).
- **Sink phase:** the LS FET is on continuously.

So the phase current always flows through exactly **two** R_DS(on) in series:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Temperature dependence:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

**Phase versus battery current.** For 6-step, I_bat ≈ D · I_ph. The worst case for phase current at a given battery draw is low D. The 80 A burst is a full-throttle punch, so D → ~0.95 and I_ph ≈ I_bat ≈ 80 A. Ripple at 48 kHz with a typical 20 µH phase inductance is

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{V}{2L}\cdot\frac{D(1-D)}{f_{sw}} < 1\ \text{A},$$

so I_rms ≈ I_dc.

### 1.2 Conduction loss with electro-thermal feedback

Because R_DS depends on T_j and T_j depends on P, solve self-consistently:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\cdot P(T_j)
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\;
P_{cond} = \frac{2I^2R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_{ref}-25)]}{1 - 2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha\, R_\theta}$$

(Use the per-FET effective R_θ. The stability condition is a positive denominator, i.e. no thermal runaway.)

**Design value:** evaluate at T_j = 105 °C, giving R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 80) = **1.40 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.40\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.9\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the one PWM'd half-bridge switches at any instant.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard switching)**

Switching time is gate-current-limited:

$$t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g}, \qquad I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int} + R_{drv}}$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_pl = 3 V, R_g,ext = 3.3 Ω and ~1 Ω internal:

$$I_g \approx \frac{7}{4.3} = 1.6\ \text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; t_{sw} \approx 7.5\ \text{ns (ideal)}$$
