# 3-Axis Gimbal Motor Control & Dual-IMU Fusion: Design Specification and Reference Implementation

> **Context note:** I have no record of a "Job #0" specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly (§0). Every constant that depends on your hardware is a named parameter. Change it in one place and the derived gains recompute.

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 (Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, single-precision FPU) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (MT6701 / AS5048A class) |
| Current sensing | 2-phase in-line or low-side shunts, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, gyro/accel ODR 8 kHz |
| Kinematics | Yaw (base) → Roll → Pitch (camera) |

**Loop hierarchy** (bandwidth ratio ≥ 5× between adjacent loops):

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop BW | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz | ~1.5 kHz | shunts + encoder angle |
| Velocity (PI + cogging FF) | 4 kHz | ~150–250 Hz | fused gyro (inertial rate) |
| Angle (P/PI + rate FF) | 1 kHz | ~20–40 Hz | AHRS attitude |
| AHRS / IMU fusion | 4 kHz | — | 2 × ICM-42688-P |

**Why the velocity loop closes on gyro rather than encoder.** Stabilization must cancel inertial motion of the camera. The encoder measures only relative joint motion. The encoder is still used for commutation, for the cogging table index, and as a fallback velocity source.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.5 PI with pole-zero cancellation

Per axis the plant is $G(s) = \frac{1}{Ls + R}$. Choose the controller

$$C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}, \qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The controller zero $K_i/K_p = R/L$ cancels the plant pole, giving

$$T(s) = \frac{\omega_c}{s + \omega_c}$$

With ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s:
- K_p = 19.8 V/A
- K_i = 52 778 V/(A·s)

Keep $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{\text{PWM}}/10$ to absorb the 1.5-sample transport delay (ADC + computation + PWM update).

### 1.6 Decoupling feedforward

$$v_d^{*} = \text{PI}_d - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{*} = \text{PI}_q + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e\lambda_m$$
