## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}\big(p\,\theta_m - \theta_{e,0}\big)$$

Here θ_e0 is found at calibration by applying a d-axis voltage and reading θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant in the dq frame (surface PM, $L_d=L_q=L$)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Torque is

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$
