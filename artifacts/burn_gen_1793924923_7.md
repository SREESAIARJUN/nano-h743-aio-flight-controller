# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specification for "Job #7," so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every parameter that depends on them is a named constant. If your hardware differs, change those constants, not the structure.

## 0. Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class, single-precision FPU, about 170 MHz or more |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–4 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| Current sensing | 2 or 3 low-side shunts, sampled at the PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera stage, SPI, ODR = 8 kHz |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz · Velocity 5 kHz · Angle/attitude 1 kHz · IMU filtering 8 kHz |
| Gimbal order | Base → Yaw → Roll → Pitch → camera |

## 1. FOC Current Loop (innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset), wrapped to [0, 2π).

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(\text{assuming } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park:** apply the transpose. Use the delay-compensated angle

$$
\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e
$$

The 1.5 Tₛ term covers one sample of computation delay plus half a sample of PWM zero-order hold.

### 1.2 Plant and PI design

PMSM d–q model:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
