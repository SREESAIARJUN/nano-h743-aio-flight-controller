## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Flight MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm) | LQFP100 (14×14 mm) cannot coexist with 24 MOSFETs on 20×20 mm |
| ESC commutation | 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071, AM32 firmware) | The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs. ArduPilot has no in-FC BLDC commutation. |
| Bus voltage | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | 40 V MOSFETs (≈1.6× derating with ringing margin) |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz, set in the AM32 ESC MCU timer | Not generated by the H743 |
| Board stack | 20×20 mm (M2 mounting), 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power layers | |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag (stated up front):** The model below shows that "80 A burst" on all four channels simultaneously on a 20×20 mm board is limited to about **1–2 seconds** by board thermal mass. It is **not** a sustained rating. Section 2.6 quantifies this. I recommend marketing it as "80 A per channel, 1 s peak, single channel" or moving to a 30.5×30.5 mm board.
