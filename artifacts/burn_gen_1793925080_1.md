## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \pmod{2\pi}$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: inject V_d along α at standstill, let the rotor align, and record θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.5 Current PI by pole-zero cancellation

The plant per axis is $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$. Choose:

$$C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

Then $C(s)G(s) = \omega_c/s$, so the closed loop is first order: $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

Stability constraint with one-sample computation delay plus ZOH (≈1.5 T_s): keep $\omega_c \le 0.1\cdot 2\pi f_s$ (about 2π·2 kHz at 20 kHz).

### 1.6 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

where $\lambda_m = K_t/(1.5p)$ and $K_t$ is in N·m/A.
