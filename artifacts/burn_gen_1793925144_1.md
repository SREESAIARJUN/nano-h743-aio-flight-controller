## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flag

This analysis assumes the standard partitioning:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 at 480 MHz | Runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs (×4, or ×1 quad) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32 | Commutation and the 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate drivers | FD6288Q (3-phase, 1 per motor) | Bootstrap high/low-side drive |
| Power stage | 24 × 40 V N-MOSFETs in 5×6 / 3.3×3.3 packages | 6 per motor |

The H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. It only sends DShot commands and receives eRPM telemetry.

**Feasibility warning.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) and "80 A burst per motor" are physically incompatible as a sustained rating. Section 2.6 shows this quantitatively. The model below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board stacked under a 20×20 or 30.5 FC**. A burst rating is defined here as **≤ 5 s at 25 °C ambient with propwash cooling**.

## 2. Power-Stage Loss Model (per motor)

### 2.1 Conduction topology

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:
- one high-side leg is chopped at duty D, with its complementary low-side FET synchronously rectifying;
- one low-side FET is held on.

Current path resistance is therefore always **two FETs in series**, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,sync} + R_{LS,on} \right] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Conduction loss at burst

Take T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS}(100) = 0.80 \cdot (1 + 0.004 \cdot 75) = 1.04\ \text{mΩ}$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.04\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.3\ W}$$

This splits into 6.66 W in the steady-on FET, and 6.66 W shared between the chopped HS/LS pair according to D.

**Copper and shunt losses.** Assume phase and bus copper of 0.35 mΩ plus a 0.25 mΩ shunt share:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.60\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.84\ W}$$

### 2.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz

**Gate currents during the Miller plateau:**

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{drv} + R_g} = \frac{5.5}{5} = 1.10\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv} + R_g} = \frac{4.5}{5} = 0.90\ \text{A}$$

**Transition times** (Q_gs2 + Q_gd):

$$t_r = \frac{6+12\ \text{nC}}{1.10} = 16.4\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_f = \frac{18\ \text{nC}}{0.90} = 20.0\ \text{ns}$$

**V–I overlap loss** (one switching leg per motor):

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 36.4\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.76\ W}$$

**Capacitive, recovery, dead-time and gate losses:**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\cdot60\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

P_gate is dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g, not in the FET.
