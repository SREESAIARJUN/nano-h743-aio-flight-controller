## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{2.3}$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (383/298)^{2.3} = 1.2 \times 1.78 \approx 2.13\,\text{m}\Omega$$

The linear approximation, R₂₅(1 + 0.0055·ΔT), gives about 1.76 mΩ. **This design uses the more conservative power-law value of 2.13 mΩ.**

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

At any instant, two phases conduct:

- **ON interval (D):** high-side FET (phase A) → motor → low-side FET (phase B).
- **OFF interval (1−D):** synchronous rectification. Current recirculates through the low-side FETs (phase A low-side, phase B low-side).

In both intervals, **two FETs are in series with the motor current**, so:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) \quad \text{(independent of D under sync-rect)}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.13\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 4.26\,\text{m} = \mathbf{27.3\ W}$$

Per conducting FET, worst case: **≈13.6 W** during the burst.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses

| Element | R | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper (2 oz, path est.) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Current shunt (shared bus, 0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W (if at 80 A) |

Copper resistance with temperature: R_Cu(T) = R₂₀[1 + 0.00393(T − 20)].

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Each PWM cycle has one hard-switched leg, which incurs both turn-on and turn-off transitions. The complementary FET switches with near-zero voltage (ZVS-like) after dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance (C_oss,er ≈ 1 nF)

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.015\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F I_{ph} (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g, not in the FETs)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2 = 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Per-channel loss summary at 80 A burst

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (2 FETs) | 27.3 |
| V·I switching | 0.97 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.09 |
| Copper | 1.6 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈30.6 W** |
| **All four channels at 80 A** | **≈122 W** (+ shunt) |
