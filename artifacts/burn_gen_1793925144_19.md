## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 100 °C this gives R_DS ≈ 1.45 mΩ. Use 1.5 mΩ for the worst case.

### 1.2 Conduction loss

In six-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D, and its synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is held on.

$$P_{cond} = \underbrace{D I^2 R + (1-D) I^2 R}_{\text{PWM leg}} + \underbrace{I^2 R}_{\text{return leg}} = 2I^2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of duty cycle.

At 80 A: P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W**

Per-FET instantaneous worst case: return-leg low side, I²R = 9.6 W. It is active for 1/3 of the electrical period, so its average is about 3.2 W.

### 1.3 PCB and interconnect conduction

The current path includes the battery pad, planes, phase pad, and vias. Estimate R_pcb ≈ 0.5 mΩ.

P_pcb = I²R_pcb = 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

This heat is distributed across the board, not concentrated in the FETs.

### 1.4 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The terms are:

**Overlap (V-I crossing)**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 1.94\ \text{W}$$

**Reverse recovery of the synchronous body diode**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance**
$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = 0.5(40\text{n})(25.2)(48\text{k}) = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction**
$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(200\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

**Gate charge** (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors). The two PWM-leg switches transition at f_sw:
$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2(50\text{n})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

Total switching-related loss: P_sw,tot ≈ **2.63 W**

### 1.5 Channel and board totals at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

| Loss | W per channel | × 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 19.2 | 76.8 |
| PCB copper | 3.2 | 12.8 |
| Switching + dead time + Q_rr + Q_oss | 2.63 | 10.5 |
| Driver / gate | 0.05 | 0.2 |
| **Total** | **≈25.1** | **≈100 W** |

Efficiency at 80 A × 25.2 V = 2016 W per channel: η ≈ 98.8%. The losses are small relative to throughput but very large relative to a nano board's thermal mass.

**Sensitivity:**
- Conduction scales with I², so 60 A gives 0.56× the conduction loss.
- Switching scales linearly with f_sw. Moving to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per channel at 80 A, at the cost of more motor current ripple and audible noise.
