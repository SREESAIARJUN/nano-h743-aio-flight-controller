# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #17): Loss Models, DShot DMA Allocation, Netlist, Thermal Limits

## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

**ArduPilot does not commutate motors.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 commands. Each motor needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM for its three half-bridges. This document therefore models two separate domains:

- **Power stage:** 4× ESC MCU, 3-phase driver, and 6× MOSFETs per motor, switching at 48 kHz.
- **Control link:** H743 timers and DMA producing DShot600 and capturing the eRPM telemetry replies.

**Thermal findings:**

- **The board cannot sustain 80 A on all four motors at once.** That load is about 127 W. On a 30.5 mm board it is limited to about 1–2 s, and a 20×20 nano board is worse.
- **Realistic continuous rating is about 27 A per motor.** A single-motor 80 A burst is viable for a few seconds.
- **The full numbers are in §4.** Treat "80 A" as a short-burst, single- or dual-motor rating.

All component parameters below are stated assumptions for a representative 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 MOSFET class. Substitute your actual datasheet values. The equations are general; the numbers are only as good as those inputs.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Six-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM'd complementarily (HS for D, LS for 1−D). The return phase holds its low-side on. The third phase floats.

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2R_{hot} = 2I^2R_{hot}$$

This loss is **independent of duty**, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2(80)^2(2.21\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{28.3\ W}$$
