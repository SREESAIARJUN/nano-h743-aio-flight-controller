**Headline result:** 80 A is a **time-limited burst rating** on a 20 × 20 mm board. With all four motors at 80 A, the board heats adiabatically at about 40 K/s, which allows roughly 1–1.5 s of burst. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C the multiplier is about 1.5, so R_DS,hot = 1.8 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step current paths

At any instant one high-side (HS) leg and one low-side (LS) leg carry the phase current I.

- **PWM on-time:** HS_a and LS_b conduct.
- **PWM off-time:** sync rectification means LS_a and LS_b conduct (or HS_a and HS_b, depending on the PWM scheme).

So the conduction loss per bridge is:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS,hot}$$

Each FET conducts during 120° of the electrical cycle. Averaged over the cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS,hot}}{3}$$

The split between HS and LS depends on duty D:
- HS: $D\cdot I^2R/3$
- LS: $(1-D)\cdot I^2R/3 + I^2R/3$ (the latter term from its own 120° LS interval)

The worst case is the LS FET at low duty.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

- $I^2 = 6400\ \text{A}^2$
- $P_{cond,bridge} = 2(6400)(0.0018) = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$
- Per-FET average = 3.84 W
- Instantaneous per-FET during conduction = 11.5 W

### 1.4 PCB copper conduction (do not omit)

A 2 oz layer is about 0.25 mΩ/□. A realistic phase path has a few squares, paralleled across layers, giving R_Cu ≈ 0.3–0.5 mΩ per current loop.

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.4\ \text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{2.6\ W}\ \text{per motor}$$

Battery-input copper carries $I_{bat} = \sum D\cdot I_{phase}$. At 4 × 80 A and D = 0.8, I_bat ≈ 256 A. Through 0.15 mΩ of input copper this is **≈ 9.8 W**. That figure is an argument for a short, wide pad-to-pour path and capping the burst.
