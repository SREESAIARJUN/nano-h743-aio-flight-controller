## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

### Partitioning

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by **four dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 drives them over bi-directional DShot600. This is the only practical partitioning: ArduPilot is not a motor-commutation firmware.

```
                 ┌───────────────── STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ─────────────────┐
 IMU (SPI1) ─────┤ TIM1 CH1..CH4 ── BDShot600 ──┬──► ESC MCU A ─► Bridge A ─► M1
 Baro (I2C) ─────┤                              ├──► ESC MCU B ─► Bridge B ─► M2
 OSD (SPI2) ─────┤                              ├──► ESC MCU C ─► Bridge C ─► M3
 RX/GPS (UART) ──┤                              └──► ESC MCU D ─► Bridge D ─► M4
 VBAT/CURR (ADC)─┤◄── shunt amp ◄── common negative bus shunt
                 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path Topology

Assume 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In any 60° sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low:

- **On-interval (D):** current flows through HS_X, then the motor, then LS_Y.
- **Off-interval (1−D):** current flows through LS_X (synchronous freewheel), then the motor, then LS_Y.

In both intervals, **exactly two MOSFET channels** carry the full phase current. Duty cycle therefore does not appear in the conduction loss term (outside of dead-time):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T R_{ds,25}$$

$$R_{ds}(100^\circ C) = 0.9 \times 1.45 = 1.31\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.31\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{16.7\ W}$$

### 1.2 Copper and Interconnect Loss

The current path is: bus, HS, phase pad, motor, phase pad, LS, ground.

$$P_{cu,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{cu} \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\ \text{(shared bus planes)} \approx 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 1.3 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

Each PWM period has two dead-time windows. During these, the body diode of the freewheel FET conducts:

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dead})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

> **Dead-time tuning:** At 48 kHz, every 100 ns of dead time costs about 0.6 W per channel at 80 A. Tune dead time down to the minimum shoot-through-safe value. This typically lands at 100–150 ns with an FD6288-class driver.

### 1.4 Temperature Coupling (Self-Consistent Solution)

Because R_ds(on) rises with T_j, solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\, R_{th,jc}, \qquad P_{cond}(T_j) = 2I^2R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j - 25)\right],\ \alpha \approx 0.006/\text{K}$$

At 80A this converges in 2–3 iterations. Thermal runaway is not a concern on a burst timescale, but it becomes one under continuous operation (see §5).
