# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Stack (Job #23): Loss Model, DShot DMA Allocation, Netlist and Thermal Budget

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 is not the ESC MCU.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each motor channel is commutated by its own ESC MCU (assumed 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) at 48 kHz PWM. The loss model applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model applies to the H743.

**Feasibility warning.** A 20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm board cannot sustain 4×80 A. As derived in Section 3, 80 A is a burst rating of about 1–2 s, gated by board heat capacity. The governing limit is copper and thermal mass, not the MOSFET datasheet.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Per-switch RMS current

Each phase leg spends 120° sourcing, 120° sinking, and 120° floating. With active freewheeling at duty D, during a leg's 120° high window:

- High side (HS) conducts for fraction D.
- Its own low side (LS) carries the freewheel current for fraction (1−D).

During the leg's 120° low window, the LS conducts 100%.

Per-switch RMS current:

$$I_{HS,rms}=I\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}},\qquad I_{LS,rms}=I\sqrt{\tfrac{(1-D)}{3}+\tfrac{1}{3}}=I\sqrt{\tfrac{2-D}{3}}$$

### 1.2 Per-motor conduction loss

Sum over three legs with R = k_T · R_DS(on):

$$P_{cond}=3R\left[\tfrac{I^2D}{3}+\tfrac{I^2(2-D)}{3}\right]=2I^2R$$

This result is independent of D. At every instant, exactly two channels are in the current path.

$$P_{cond}=2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3})=\mathbf{19.2\ W/motor}$$

### 1.3 Worst single device

The worst device is the LS at D → 0 (low throttle with high current, e.g., a hard braking or reversal transient):

$$P_{LS,max}=\tfrac{2}{3}I^2R=\mathbf{6.4\ W}$$

At D = 1, both HS and LS dissipate I²R/3 = 3.2 W each.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often larger than designers expect)

Assume a phase path of approximately 0.25 mΩ (2 oz inner and outer planes, short pours) plus a 0.5 mΩ shunt on one leg. Two path segments carry current at any instant:

$$P_{Cu}\approx 2I^2R_{path}+I^2R_{shunt}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}\approx 3.2+1.07=\mathbf{4.3\ W/motor}$$

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

In 6-step operation only one leg hard-switches at any time. The HS takes the hard transition, and the LS commutates softly through its body diode.

| Mechanism | Formula | Result (per motor) |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·40n·48k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time diode | 2·V_SD·I·t_dt·f_sw = 2·0.8·80·100n·48k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2·50n·48k | **0.06 W** |
| C_oss | V·Q_oss·f_sw = 25.2·40n·48k | **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (driver side) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2·60n·10·48k | **0.06 W** |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. Moving to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per motor, at the cost of current ripple and acoustic noise.

### 2.1 Totals at the 80 A burst

| | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| Copper + shunt | 4.3 W | 17.2 W |
| **Total** | **26.2 W** | **≈ 105 W** |

Efficiency check: 4·80 A·25.2 V·D. At D = 0.8, P_out ≈ 6.45 kW, so efficiency ≈ 98.4%. Efficiency is not the problem; heat density is.

### 2.2 Input capacitor ripple

Per motor, input capacitor ripple is:

$$I_{C,rms}=I\sqrt{D(1-D)}=80\cdot0.5=\mathbf{40\ A}\ \text{(at } D=0.5)$$

With interleaving across four motors (asynchronous PWMs), roughly √4 × 40 ≈ 80 A_rms is a worst-case bound.
