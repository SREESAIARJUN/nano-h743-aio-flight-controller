# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #19): Power-Stage, Thermal & Bidirectional DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** ArduPilot does not perform motor commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits **bidirectional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs, one per channel (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM to the gate drivers. The math below follows that split.
