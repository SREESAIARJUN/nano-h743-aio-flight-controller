**Architecture note.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. A real AIO uses the H743 as the flight controller plus a separate ESC MCU (for example STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32). Bidirectional DShot600 runs between the two. The power-stage model below applies to the ESC side; the DMA model applies to the H743.

⚠ **Form-factor reality check.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot physically or thermally support 24 × DFN5x6 FETs at 80 A/motor. This analysis assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm AIO with 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner copper (6-layer). Section 3 shows that even this is strictly burst-limited.
