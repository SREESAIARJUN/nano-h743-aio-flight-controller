# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Model

**Assumptions:**
- I don't have the Job #53 requirements, BOM, or stackup. Every component value below is a stated assumption. Substitute datasheet values before release.
- The netlists are reference topology only. They are not fab-ready and need ERC/DRC, SI review, and bench validation.
- **Architecture:** The H743 is the flight controller. It runs ArduPilot, which does not commutate motors. Each motor needs its own ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), driven by the H743 over DShot. If Job #53 assumes the H743 drives gates directly, that architecture has no ArduPilot or AM32 support path and should be revisited.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Complementary PWM)

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical period PWMing high, 1/3 held low, and 1/3 floating. With synchronous rectification at duty D:

$$
P_{HS} = I^2 R_{hot}\,\frac{D}{3}, \qquad
P_{LS} = I^2 R_{hot}\left[\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}\right]
$$

At any instant, exactly two FETs carry I_ph, so the per-motor total is independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

Here R_hot = k_T·R₂₅ = 1.6 × 1.2 mΩ = **1.92 mΩ**.

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per motor | 2 × 80² × 1.92 mΩ | **24.6 W** |
| Per FET, worst case (LS at D → 0) | 80² × 1.92 m × 2/3 | 8.2 W |
| Per FET at D ≈ 1 | 80² × 1.92 m / 3 | 4.1 W |
| Shunt, per motor | 80² × 0.2 mΩ | 1.28 W |
| PCB copper, per motor (est. 0.3 mΩ path) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |

The worst-case per-FET dissipation occurs at low duty with high current. This is the stall/punch-out-from-low-throttle case, not full throttle.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per motor hard-switches at a time. The low side commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

| Mechanism | Equation | Result per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | 1.94 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss (2 FETs) | 2 × ½·C·V²·f = 1 nF × 25.2² × 48 k | 0.03 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_F·I·2t_d·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive (driver-side) | 2 × Q_g·V_gs·f = 2 × 60 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.4 W** |

**Sensitivity:** P_overlap scales linearly with f_sw. Moving 24 → 48 kHz costs about 1.2 W per motor at 80 A. In return, current ripple drops by half and low-inductance nano motors run smoother. At 80 A, switching loss is under 10 % of conduction loss. **R_DS(on) dominates, not f_sw.**
