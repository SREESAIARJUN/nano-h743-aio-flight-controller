## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform (with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, using electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

Stator dynamics in the rotating frame:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The controller adds the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms as feedforward. This leaves a pure first-order plant per axis:

$$
\frac{I(s)}{V'(s)} = \frac{1}{Ls+R}
$$

### 1.3 PI Design by Pole–Zero Cancellation

Choose the current-loop bandwidth ω_i:

$$
K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_i,\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_i
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{I}{I^*}=\frac{\omega_i}{s+\omega_i}
$$

Take ω_i ≤ 2π·f_pwm/20. At 20 kHz this gives ω_i ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

Discrete form (backward Euler, sample time T_c):

$$
x_k = x_{k-1}+K_iT_c e_k,\qquad
v_k=\operatorname{sat}(K_p e_k + x_k + v_{ff})
$$

Voltage saturation is applied to the circle |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3, with d-axis priority.
