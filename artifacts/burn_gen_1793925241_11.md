The loop hierarchy runs from the innermost loop outward:

- **Current loop:** current → voltage, at 20 kHz.
- **Velocity loop:** rate → iq*, at 5 kHz. Feedback comes from the gyro for stabilization, or from the encoder for joint-position mode.
- **Angle loop:** angle → rate*, at 1 kHz. It also adds a rate feedforward.
- **Anti-cogging:** an iq_ff term injected at the velocity-loop output.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical angle and the calibrated electrical offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{e,0}$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI design

The dq-frame motor model (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L, ω_e = p ω_m) is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

The controller is a PI per axis plus decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda$$
