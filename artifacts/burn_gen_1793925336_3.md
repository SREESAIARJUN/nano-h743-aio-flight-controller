## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | 12N14P gimbal BLDC, pole pairs $p=7$, $R_s=5\,\Omega$, $L_s=2\,$mH, $K_t\approx0.05$ N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic encoder, e.g. AS5048/MT6701, sampled at 20 kHz |
| Inverter | 3-phase, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz, low-side shunt current sensing |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, ODR 8 kHz, SPI at 24 MHz |
| MCU | STM32H7 or G4 class, single-precision FPU |

**Loop rates** (timescale separation of at least 5×):

```
 20 kHz  ── Current loop (FOC: Clarke/Park, PI d/q, SVPWM)       ωc ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz
  4 kHz  ── Velocity loop (inertial axis rate, PI + cogging FF)  ωv ≈ 2π·150 Hz
  8 kHz  ── IMU sampling + vibration filters (per-sensor)
  1 kHz  ── Attitude estimator (Mahony) + angle loop (P + rate FF) ωθ ≈ 2π·25 Hz
```

**Signal chain:**

```
 θ_ref ─►[Angle P + FF]─ω_ref─►[Velocity PI]─iq_fb─►(+)─iq_ref─►[Current PI d/q]─►SVPWM─►Motor

             │ attitude            │ ω_axis         │                  │

                                                    [Cogging LMS]◄─θm (encoder PLL)
```

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}})$.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupled PI Control

The surface-mount PMSM voltage equations are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The controller is a PI plus feedforward decoupling:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choose the integral time $K_i/K_p = R/L$. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{PWM}}{10}
$$

**Discretization** (forward Euler on the integrator, with sample time $T_s$):

$$
I[k] = I[k-1] + K_i T_s e[k],\qquad u[k] = K_p e[k] + I[k]
$$
