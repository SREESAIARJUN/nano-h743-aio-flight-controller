# Job #49: STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 80A ESC
## Thermal, Switching-Loss, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Design Package

> **Scope and assumptions.** No MOSFET part number, board stack-up, or airflow figure was specified, so this package uses representative values. These are marked **[A]**. Replace them with datasheet values before release. All equations stay valid when you substitute your own numbers.
>
> **Architecture.** On an AIO board the STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class) with AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware. Two consequences follow:
> - The 48 kHz PWM frequency is a setting in the ESC firmware.
> - The H743 only generates DShot600 frames and decodes bidirectional (eRPM) telemetry.

## 2. Conduction-Loss Model (Six-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

### 2.1 Current path

In any commutation state, two phases conduct:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D) in synchronous rectification, apart from dead time.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the LS is on for the full 60° sector.

The instantaneous conduction power per channel is:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

This result does not depend on duty, which is why synchronous rectification is used.

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + k(T_j - 25)], \quad k = \frac{\alpha_R - 1}{100} = 4.5\times10^{-3}\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.305 mΩ.

### 2.3 Values at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0009 = **11.52 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.305 mΩ | 2·6400·0.001305 = **16.70 W** |

### 2.4 Copper loss

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu}(T) = 6400 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times [1 + 0.00393(T-25)] \approx 6.4\text{–}8.3\ \text{W}
$$

Copper loss is often 30–40 % of FET conduction loss on nano boards. Do not omit it.

### 2.5 Electrothermal feedback

Conduction loss raises T_j, which raises R_DS, which raises loss. The fixed point is:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,2I^2R_{DS,25}[1+k(T_j-25)]
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta P_0 (1 - 25k)}{1 - R_\theta P_0 k}, \quad P_0 = 2I^2R_{DS,25}
$$

**Stability criterion (no thermal runaway):** R_θ · P_0 · k < 1.

For P_0 = 11.52 W and k = 0.0045, this gives R_θ < 19.3 K/W. Section 4 shows the design is stable for continuous operation.

## 3. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per commutation state, only the PWM leg switches: one HS turn-on/off and its complementary LS edge.

### 3.1 V–I overlap (hard switching, inductive load)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.452\ W}
$$

### 3.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead times per period)

$$
P_{DT} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2t_{dead}\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.614\ W}
$$

### 3.3 Reverse recovery of the LS body diode

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}
$$

### 3.4 Output-capacitance loss

$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 60\text{nC}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.073\ W}
$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (two FETs, mostly dissipated in the driver and R_g)

$$
P_{g} = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot 60\text{nC}\cdot 10\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}
$$

### 3.6 Switching subtotal and frequency trade-off

**P_sw ≈ 2.26 W per channel at 80 A.**
