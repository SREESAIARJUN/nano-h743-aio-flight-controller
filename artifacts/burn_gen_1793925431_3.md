# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #34): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly in a typical 4-in-1 design. The H743 is the **flight controller**, running ArduPilot. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) that generates the **48 kHz** complementary PWM. The design is split accordingly:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master, RPM-based harmonic notch |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | 6-step / sync-rect PWM at 48 kHz, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | 2 A gate drive, 250–500 ns built-in dead time |
| Power ×24 | 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, 10 V V_GS |

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two phase legs are active at any instant: one PWM leg and one sink leg.

- **PWM-ON:** current flows through HS(PWM leg), the motor, and LS(sink leg).
- **PWM-OFF:** current freewheels through LS(PWM leg), the motor, and LS(sink leg).

So the current always flows through **exactly two MOSFETs**, regardless of duty cycle D. This holds outside the dead-time window, which is accounted for in §2.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per motor channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |

### 1.4 Per-device distribution

Each phase's HS conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle at duty D. Each LS conducts for 1/3 of the cycle as a sink, plus D′ = (1−D) of another 1/3 as the freewheel path. Averaged per device:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{DS}, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1 + 1 - D)\, I^2 R_{DS}$$

At D = 0.8 and T_j = 125 °C (I²R = 9.6 W):

- P_HS = 2.56 W
- P_LS = 3.84 W

The **low-side devices are the hot spot**. This argues for LS devices with lower R_DS(on), or for more copper under them.

### 1.5 Shunt loss (low-side return shunt, carries current during PWM-ON)

$$P_{sh} = D\,I^2 R_{sh} = 0.8 \times 6400 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 2.56\ \text{W}$$

### 1.6 Copper trace loss (2 oz Cu, ~0.3 mΩ effective path)

$$P_{Cu} \approx 6400 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 1.9\ \text{W per channel}$$
