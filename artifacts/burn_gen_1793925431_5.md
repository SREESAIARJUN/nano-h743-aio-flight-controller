## 0. Architecture Correction

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz PWM.

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (400 MHz in ArduPilot) | ArduPilot | Generates bidirectional DShot600, decodes eRPM telemetry |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 / STM32G071 (one MCU per motor) | AM32 / BLHeli_32 | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead-time |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | — | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power stage | 24 N-FETs (6 per motor) | — | Inverter |

The thermal and switching model (§1–3) applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model (§4) applies to the H743.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation-state current paths

The model assumes trapezoidal (6-step) drive with complementary synchronous rectification. Two phases are active at any instant.

| Interval | High-side (PWM phase) | Low-side (same phase) | Low-side (return phase) |
|---|---|---|---|
| On (D) | conducts I | off | conducts I |
| Freewheel (1−D) | off | conducts I (sync rect.) | conducts I |

Time-averaged conduction loss for one motor channel:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot}
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle** with synchronous rectification.

With n FETs in parallel per position, replace R_ds with R_ds / n.

### 2.2 Temperature dependence (closed-loop form)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha\approx 0.005\,/\mathrm{K}
$$

This creates positive feedback, so solve iteratively:

$$
T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}\big(T_j^{(k)}\big)\cdot(R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta,via})
$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

- R_ds,hot = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**
- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.5 × 10⁻³ = **19.2 W per motor** (~9.6 W per conducting FET-position)

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard: one high-side turn-on and turn-off per period. The synchronous low-side switches softly, at near-zero V_DS after dead-time.

| Loss | Equation | Value @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·40n·48k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200n·48k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2·50n·48k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | V·Q_oss·f_sw (≈ ½CV² charged/discharged) | 0.04 W |
| Gate drive (in driver, not FETs) | 6·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 0.14 W |
| **P_sw,total (FETs)** | | **≈ 2.65 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** P_sw ∝ f_sw, so ∂P/∂f ≈ 55 µW/Hz per motor. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per motor. That is minor compared with conduction loss, so 48 kHz is not your thermal problem.

### 3.1 Total dissipation

| Scope | Power |
|---|---|
| Per motor | 19.2 + 2.65 ≈ **21.9 W** |
| Hottest single FET (low-side, ~100% conduction + diode share) | ≈ 9.6 + 0.3 ≈ **~10 W** |
| Whole board, all 4 motors at 80 A | **≈ 87.5 W** |
| Copper/trace I²R (est. 0.3 mΩ path × 4) | +7.7 W → **~95 W** |
