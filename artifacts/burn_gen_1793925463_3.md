## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Six assumptions shape every number below:

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller MCU | STM32H743VIT6 at 480 MHz (rev V), running ArduPilot | Timer kernel clock is 240 MHz with APB = 120 MHz and the ×2 timer multiplier |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32 | ArduPilot does not run field-oriented control or 6-step commutation on the H7. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU. |
| Gate driver | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, integrated bootstrap diodes) | Common in 30×30 builds |
| MOSFETs | 24× 40 V N-channel, DFN 3.3×3.3 | Typical: R_DS(on) = 1.2 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V and 25 °C; Q_g = 40 nC; Q_gd = 9 nC; Q_gs2 = 4 nC; V_plateau = 3.0 V; R_g,int = 1.0 Ω; Q_rr = 50 nC at high di/dt |
| Bus | 6S LiPo: V_bus = 25.2 V maximum, 22.2 V nominal | Calculations use 25.2 V as the worst case for switching loss |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm mounting, 6-layer, 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner | "Nano" class |

> **Engineering flag (read before proceeding).** "80 A burst" on 3.3×3.3 DFN FETs in a 30×30 footprint is only achievable as a **short-duration, single-channel or low-duty transient**. The model in §3 shows that four simultaneous 80 A channels exceed ~100 W. That load saturates the board's thermal mass in seconds. The spec must be expressed as **"80 A for t ≤ t_burst at T_amb, with airflow v"**, and §3.5 derives t_burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0048\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This value of α reproduces the typical datasheet ratio R(150 °C)/R(25 °C) ≈ 1.6.
