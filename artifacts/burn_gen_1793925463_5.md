## 0. Feasibility Findings

Three findings shape everything below.

1. **DShot requires a separate ESC MCU per motor.** The H743 cannot sensibly drive 4 × 3-phase bridges directly. It has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs (TIM1 and TIM8), which covers 2 motors. HRTIM adds 10 outputs, but the result is not a supportable firmware target. The architecture is therefore:
   - H743 runs ArduPilot.
   - It sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4 × AM32-class ESC MCUs.
   - Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM for its own motor.
2. **"80A burst" must be defined.** On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm nano board, 80 A *per motor* at 6S is about 2 kW per channel and about 8 kW total. That is not thermally credible for more than about 1–2 s (see §3.4). I model both cases:
   - **Case A:** 80 A per channel.
   - **Case B:** 80 A aggregate, i.e. 20 A per channel.
   
   Recommendation: rate the product on Case B with a per-channel burst of around 35–45 A. Treat Case A as a 1 s transient for fault and robustness validation only.
3. **Copper is a loss term, not a rounding error.** At 80 A, 0.3 mΩ of trace or plane resistance adds about 2 W per channel. It is included in the model.

Rds(on) temperature dependence, valid from 25 to 150 °C:

R_ds(Tj) = R_ds25 · [1 + α(Tj − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0055 /K, so k_T = 1.55 at 125 °C.

### 2.2 Commutation model

AM32 runs trapezoidal 6-step with synchronous complementary PWM:

- At any instant, one phase-leg is PWM'd: the high side for D, its low side complementarily for 1−D.
- One other leg's low side is held fully ON.
- The current path therefore always passes through exactly two FET channels plus copper.
- Each of the 6 FETs carries current for about 1/3 of the electrical period.

## 3. Mathematical Model

### 3.1 Conduction loss

Per channel (bridge):

P_cond = I² · [ 2·R_ds(Tj) + R_cu ]

Each FET averages one-sixth of the device term:

P_cond,FET = (I² · 2·R_ds(Tj)) / 6

RMS refinement. The PWM ripple adds ΔI_pp²/12. With motor L ≈ 15 µH phase-to-phase and f_sw = 48 kHz:

ΔI_pp ≈ V_bus·D(1−D) / (L·f_sw) = 25.2 · 0.09 / (15e-6 · 48e3) ≈ 3.15 A

The added term is 3.15²/12 = 0.83 A², which is negligible at 80 A (6400 A²).

| | Case A (80 A) | Case B (20 A) |
|---|---|---|
| R_ds(125 °C) | 1.55 mΩ | 1.55 mΩ (Tj will be lower; conservative) |
| FET conduction, 2·I²·R_ds | 19.84 W | 1.24 W |
| Copper, I²·R_cu | 1.60 W | 0.10 W |
| **P_cond per channel** | **21.44 W** | **1.34 W** |
