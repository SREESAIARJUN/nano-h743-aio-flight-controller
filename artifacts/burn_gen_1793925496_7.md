# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture (Job #78)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Critical Findings

I don't have the Job #78 requirements document, so this model rests on the assumptions below. Change any of them and the equations still hold, but the numbers must be recomputed.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (worst case for switching loss) |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm AIO, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner copper |
| MOSFETs | 40 V, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ at 25 °C, 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 clip package |
| Commutation | Trapezoidal 120°, synchronous complementary PWM |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| ESC firmware | Per-channel ESC MCUs (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class) |
| Flight controller | H743 running ArduPilot, sending bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs |
| "80 A burst" | Phase RMS current per channel |

Three findings should be raised at design review before any layout work:

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** It lives in the per-channel ESC MCUs. The H743 only sends DShot frames to them and receives eRPM telemetry back.
2. **80 A × 4 channels cannot be sustained on a nano or 30.5 mm board.** The model below shows roughly 88 W of loss at full burst. The board can sustain about 39 A per channel continuously with propwash cooling, and the 80 A rating is a burst of about 2 seconds.
3. **"80 A" must be defined explicitly.** It could mean phase current or battery current. These differ by the duty cycle D, which changes the conduction loss by as much as a factor of 1/D².

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 120° trapezoidal drive, two phases conduct at any time:
- The **PWM leg** alternates between its high side (during the on-time D·T) and its low side (during the synchronous freewheel interval).
- The **static leg's** low-side FET conducts for the whole commutation step.

So the phase current always flows through exactly two channels in series. Instantaneous conduction loss per motor channel is:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature correction

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0058\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 × 1.49 ≈ **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| Copper (phase plus bus, about 0.3 mΩ effective) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | **1.9 W** |

Commutation rotates this loss among the six FETs. The average per FET is about P_ch/3, but the instantaneous peak per FET is about 9.6 W during its 120° conduction window.

### 1.4 Battery current and input-capacitor ripple

- Battery current: I_bat = D · I_ph
- Input-capacitor ripple (worst case at D = 0.5):

$$I_{C,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}\;\rightarrow\;40\ \mathrm{A_{rms}}\ \text{per channel at }80\ \mathrm{A}$$

This drives the bulk capacitor requirement (Section 4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per channel, the loss terms are:

| Term | Equation | Values | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·(20 + 20 ns)·48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·2·100 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.05 W |
| Gate drive | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·60 nC·10 V·48 k | 0.06 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

Rise and fall times scale roughly with Q_gd / I_gate. With a 1 A driver, Q_gd ≈ 15 nC gives about 15 to 20 ns. If you use a weak driver or high gate resistance, t_r/t_f will double, and so will the overlap term. Increasing the dead time beyond about 100 ns makes the body-diode term dominate.

Switching loss is approximately linear in current (≈ 0.034·I W). Conduction loss is quadratic.

### 2.1 Total channel and board loss

$$P_{ch}(I)\approx 0.0033\,I^2 + 0.034\,I\quad[\mathrm{W}]$$

(The quadratic coefficient includes copper loss.)

| I_ph (A) | P_ch (W) | P_board, 4 channels (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | 4.0 | 16 |
| 40 | 6.6 | 27 |
| 60 | 13.9 | 56 |
| **80** | **23.8** | **≈ 95** |

The ≈ 95 W at 80 A includes copper loss. FET-only loss is about 88 W.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network

```
Tj --[Rth_j-pcb ≈ 2 K/W per FET]-- T_pcb --[θ_pcb-amb]-- T_amb
                                     |
                               C_th,board
```

- θ_pcb-amb is about 15 K/W in still air and about 3 K/W in propwash. Measure this on a prototype.
- C_th,board: approximately 6 g of FR4 + copper + FETs at c ≈ 0.65 J/g·K gives about **4 J/K**.

### 3.2 Junction rise above the PCB

Average per-FET dissipation is 23.8 / 6 ≈ 4 W, so:

ΔT_j-pcb ≈ 4 W × 2 K/W ≈ **8 K**

This is small. **The board, not the junction, is the limiting element.**
