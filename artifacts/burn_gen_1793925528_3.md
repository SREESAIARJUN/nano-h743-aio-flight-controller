## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset), wrapped to [0, 2π).

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant (PMSM in the d-q frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Torque (surface PM, so L_d = L_q):

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI Design (Pole–Zero Cancellation)

With feed-forward decoupling, the plant becomes G_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Placing the PI zero on the plant pole gives:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i_q}{i_q^*} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}
$$

Choose ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_pwm/20. At 20 kHz this gives ω_ci ≈ 2π·1000 rad/s.

Decoupling feed-forward terms:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
