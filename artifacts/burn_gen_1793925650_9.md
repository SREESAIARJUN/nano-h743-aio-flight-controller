## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Design Flags

### 0.1 Architectural clarification

The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs. In a 4-in-1 stack:

- **Flight controller (FC):** The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600 on four timer channels.
- **ESC:** Four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8/BB51 running Bluejay/BLHeli) generate 48 kHz complementary PWM through a 3-phase gate driver into 6 MOSFETs per motor.

The loss and thermal model below therefore applies to the ESC board. The DMA model applies to the FC board.

### 0.3 Design flags

- **Flag 1: The full specification is not physically achievable.** Four motors at 80 A simultaneously, sustained, on a 20×20 mm "nano" ESC cannot be met. Section 4 shows that adiabatic heating limits a full 4×80 A burst to about 2–4 s. Commercial 20×20 4-in-1 ESCs are honestly rated about 35–45 A continuous. Recommendation: specify **80 A as a ≤1–2 s single-motor transient** and around 40 A continuous, or move to a 30×30 mm format.
- **Flag 2: Modulation scheme.** The model assumes six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary PWM (synchronous rectification), which is the AM32/BLHeli default.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo ──┬── XT60 ── ESC VBAT plane ──┬── Bulk C (ext. 1000 µF low-ESR + 8×10 µF X7R/ch)
           │                            ├── 4 × [ AT32F421 → FD6288Q → 6× N-FET → Motor ]
           │                            └── 0.25 mΩ shunt + INA → CURR pin
           │
 8-pin JST-SH (VBAT, GND, CURR, TELEM, M1..M4)
           │
 FC: VBAT → 5V/3A buck → 3V3 LDO → STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz)
          TIM3 CH1..CH4 → M1..M4 (bi-dir DShot600, 33 Ω series + ESD)
```
