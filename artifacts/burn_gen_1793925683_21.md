# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Systems Architecture Model (Job #19)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors itself. ArduPilot does not run a BLDC commutation loop. The board therefore has two domains:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 400 MHz) | ArduPilot, bi-directional DShot600 master |
| ESC | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, 2-ch bootstrap) | Drives 24 N-MOSFETs |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | 6 FETs per motor |

**Baseline parameters.** These are explicit so you can substitute your BOM values.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full) | Worst-case switching voltage |
| I_ph | 80 A burst | Per-motor phase current |
| R_ds,on(25 °C) | 1.5 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V | Typical 40 V 3.3×3.3 FET |
| α | 0.006 /K | R_ds,on temperature coefficient |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| t_r, t_f | 15 ns each | With FD6288 at about 1 A and 4.7 Ω gate resistor |
| t_dead | 100 ns | Per edge |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward drop |
| Q_rr, Q_oss | 40 nC, 40 nC | Reverse recovery and output charge |
| Q_g | 30 nC | Gate charge |

> **Key finding up front:** a nano-format (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board cannot *sustain* 80 A on four motors simultaneously. The model below shows about a **3 s** simultaneous 4×80 A burst and about **20 A/motor continuous**. The 80 A rating must be specified as a time-limited burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Derivation
AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). During any commutation sector:

- The PWM leg's high-side conducts for duty D and its low-side for (1 − D).
- The return leg's low-side conducts continuously.
- One phase floats for BEMF sensing.

So the current always flows through exactly **two** channels in series:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T)
$$

The result is independent of duty cycle. The temperature dependence is:

$$
R_{ds}(T) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$
