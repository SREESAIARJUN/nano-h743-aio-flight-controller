## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

I am raising these before the math because they change the design.

**1. Two different jobs, two different processors.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot, which does not commutate brushless motors. A "4-in-1 ESC" AIO board therefore needs:
- **FC domain:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It generates bi-directional DShot600.
- **ESC domain:** four commutation MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, typically STM32G071 or AT32F421
