# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #9)

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AM32-class, e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071). Those MCUs drive the three-phase gate drivers and FET bridges and set the 48 kHz PWM. The two domains share one PCB, ground system and thermal mass. This is why the thermal model and the DShot allocation belong in one document.

**Reference values used below.** Replace them with datasheet values for your chosen parts before release.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full), 22 V under sag | Worst case for switching uses 25.2 V |
| I_burst | 80 A per motor (phase-current equivalent) | |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | R_ds(on) = 1.2 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_gs = 10 V |
| R_ds tempco | α ≈ 0.0045 /K | Gives R_ds ≈ 1.7 mΩ @ 110 °C |

**Form-factor warning.** A 20×20 "nano" footprint cannot credibly support 80 A per motor even as a burst. The figures below assume a 30.5 mm board. They show 80 A is a single-motor, seconds-long burst rating, not a 4×80 A rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phase legs carry the motor current I:
- **ON interval (duty D):** high-side of phase A and low-side of phase B conduct.
- **OFF interval (1−D), complementary PWM:** low-side of A and low-side of B conduct.

Two R_ds(on) are in the current path at all times, independent of D:

```
P_cond,FET = 2 · I² · R_ds(T_j)
R_ds(T_j)  = R_ds,25 · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]
```

Add the copper and solder path, R_pcb ≈ 0.25 mΩ per motor path (pads, vias, phase pads):

```
P_cond = I² · (2·R_ds(T_j) + R_pcb)
```

### 1.2 Results at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_FET | P_pcb | P_cond / motor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 1.6 W | 17.0 W |
| 110 °C | 1.66 mΩ | 21.2 W | 1.6 W | **22.8 W** |

Per FET this is roughly 10.6 W in the hottest device at the 110 °C row. Commutation spreads the load over 6 FETs, but the average per-FET dissipation is 22.8 W ÷ 6 ≈ 3.8 W.

### 1.3 Electrothermal stability (runaway check)

Rising temperature raises R_ds, which raises loss:

```
dP/dT = 2 I² R_ds,25 · α = 2 · 6400 · 1.2e-3 · 0.0045 = 0.069 W/K
```

The loop is stable when the per-motor-region thermal resistance satisfies R_th · dP/dT < 1, i.e., R_th < 14.5 K/W. A 30.5 mm board in prop wash is about 3–6 K/W system-level, so it is stable, but gain is about 0.3. Expect about 1.4× amplification of any temperature excursion.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Per motor, one leg is hard-switched per PWM cycle in complementary mode.

| Term | Formula | Value @ 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f | ½·25.2·80·30e-9·48e3 = **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V · Q_rr · f | 25.2·40e-9·48e3 = **0.05 W** |
| Output capacitance | ½ · Q_oss · V · f (×2 FETs) | **0.05 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | 2 · t_dt · f · V_f · I | 2·150e-9·48e3·0.8·80 = **0.92 W** |
| Gate drive (driver IC, not FET) | Q_g · V_drv · f · 2 | 60e-9·10·48e3·2 = 0.06 W |

**Total switching ≈ 2.5 W per motor.**

### 2.1 Sensitivity

- **Overlap loss** scales linearly with f. Going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves about 0.7 W but adds audible noise and ripple.
- **Dead time** is the cheapest lever. At t_dt = 300 ns the diode term doubles to 1.84 W.
- **Gate resistor:** t_sw ≈ Q_gd · R_g,total ÷ (V_drv − V_plateau). With Q_gd = 10 nC, R_g = 4.7 Ω, and (10 − 3.5) V, each edge takes about 7–8 ns. Do not go below about 3.3 Ω without checking ringing on the phase node.

### 2.2 Total burst budget

```
P_motor (80 A, T_j = 110 °C) ≈ 22.8 + 2.5 + 0.4 (shunt, 0.25 mΩ³) ≈ 25.7 W
```

³ Low-side or bus shunt, if fitted.

| Scenario | P_total |
|---|---|
| 1 motor @ 80 A, 3 motors @ 30 A | 25.7 + 3·(30²·0.0039 + 0.9) ≈ **38 W** |
| 4 motors @ 80 A (unrealistic) | ≈ **103 W** |
| Hover (4 × 15 A) | ≈ 4 × 1.4 W ≈ 5.6 W |

The 3.9 mΩ effective path in the 30 A rows is 2·R_ds(T_j) + R_pcb + R_shunt at about 25 °C. The 0.9 W switching term at 30 A is the 2.5 W figure scaled roughly linearly with current.
