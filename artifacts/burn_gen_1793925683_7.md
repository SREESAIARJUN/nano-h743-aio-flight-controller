## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A production-grade 4-in-1 ESC on an H743 flight controller is partitioned as follows:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400/480 MHz | ArduPilot, sends bi-directional DShot600 to each ESC MCU, receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (QFN32) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF commutation |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver (bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power FETs ×24 | 30–40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | 3-phase inverter |

All loss math below is for the **ESC power stage**. The H743 contributes only about 0.4–0.6 W and is thermally negligible next to the inverter.

> **Critical design-review flag:** "80 A per channel" on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting) board is a **transient, airflow-dependent burst rating**, not a continuous rating. The math in §3 shows the burst is limited to roughly 2–4 s by board heat capacity. Marketing numbers in this class are generally of this nature. Document the rating as *"80 A burst, ≤ 2 s, ≥ 10 m/s prop wash, T_amb ≤ 35 °C"*.

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Topology (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In any commutation sector, current flows through two legs:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):**
  - High-side FET conducts for duty D.
  - Low-side FET conducts for (1−D), because synchronous rectification is used instead of the body diode.
- **Leg B (return leg):** low-side FET on for 100 % of the sector.

The conduction energy per PWM period, normalised to I²R, is therefore:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{ds,hot}\,[\,D + (1-D) + 1\,] = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{ds,hot}
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**, which is a useful property for burst modelling.

#### Ripple correction

Phase-current ripple adds to the RMS value:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$

Typical drone motor parameters are L_ph ≈ 15–30 µH and f_sw = 48 kHz. The worst case (D = 0.5) gives:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2L f_{sw}} \approx \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{2 \times 20\mu \times 48k} \approx 3.3\,A
$$

This ripple adds less than 0.01 % to I_rms² at 80 A, so it is negligible.

#### Result

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}
$$
