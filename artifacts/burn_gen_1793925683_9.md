## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}\Rightarrow R_{DS}(125^\circ\text{C}) \approx 1.45\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one leg PWMs its high-side (HS) switch at duty D, with the complementary low-side (LS) switch freewheeling. Another leg's LS switch is held fully on. Averaged over one electrical revolution, each leg sees:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}\,D}{3},\qquad P_{LS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3}\Big[1 + (1-D)\Big]\cdot\tfrac{1}{1}\;\;\text{(split across its sectors)}$$

Two FETs carry I at every instant, so per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
