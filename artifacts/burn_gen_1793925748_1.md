## 0. Architectural Constraints and Feasibility Findings

These items determine whether the design is viable at all, so they come first.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors directly. | The ESC side uses **4 × dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, the reference choice). The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. Single-MCU 4-in-1 designs exist (e.g. AT32F415/F421 driving 4 channels), but they constrain timer resources and are not assumed here. |
| F2 | "Nano" usually means a 20 × 20 mm mount. | 80 A per motor on 20 × 20 is not physically credible. This document assumes a **30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner** board. Even so, **80 A is a burst rating measured in seconds, not a continuous rating** (see §5). |
| F3 | 4 × 80 A simultaneously = 320 A from the battery (≈ 8 kW at 6S). | The XT60 connector, battery leads and bus capacitors are the true limits. The realistic burst case is **one or two motors at 80 A**, or all four at roughly 45–55 A. |
| F4 | All component values below are representative. | Every R_DS(on), Q_gd, Z_th and similar value **must be replaced with datasheet values of the selected parts** before release. The math is structured so that you substitute and recompute. |

## 1. System Block Architecture

```
 XT60 ─┬─ TVS(SMDJ33A) ─┬─ Cbulk (4×470µF low-ESR external + 8×10µF/50V X7R per bridge)
       │                 ├─ Shunt 0.1mΩ (battery current) ─ INA → H743 ADC
       │                 ├─ 4 × 3-phase bridges (6×FET each) ← FD6288Q ← AT32F421 (AM32) ×4
       │                 └─ Buck 25V→10V (gate drive) → LDO 3.3V (ESC MCUs)
       └─ Buck 25V→5V (FC) → LDO 3.3V (H743, sensors)

 STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz
   ├─ TIM3 CH3/CH4, TIM5 CH1/CH2 → 4 × DShot600 bidir → AT32F421 PA2 (TIM15/AM32 input)
   ├─ SPI1 → ICM-42688-P   (IMU)
   ├─ SPI2 → AT7456E       (OSD) / I2C → DPS310 (baro)
   ├─ UART1..UART8         (GPS, RX, VTX, telemetry)
   └─ SDMMC1               (logging)
```
