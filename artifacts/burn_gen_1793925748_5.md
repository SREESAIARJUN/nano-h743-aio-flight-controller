## 0. Scope, Architecture Assumptions and Ground Rules

I don't have the Job #51 requirements baseline, so every parameter is stated explicitly. Swap in your BOM values and the equations carry through unchanged.

**Architecture assumption.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) driving a 3-phase gate driver and six N-FETs. The H743 talks to each ESC over one bi-directional DShot600 wire. If Job #51 intends the H743 to drive gates directly, Section 4 changes completely; flag it.
