# Job #66: STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC
## Design Model: Power Stage Losses, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan

---

## 0. Architectural Decision and Feasibility Finding

### 0.1 Partitioning

An STM32H743 can't sensibly run four sensorless FOC/6-step motor loops while also running ArduPilot. Twelve half-bridges need 24 gated outputs plus 12 back-EMF comparator or ADC channels with microsecond-level commutation latency. The H743's TIM1, TIM8 and HRTIM could cover the PWM count, but ArduPilot's scheduler would make commutation timing nondeterministic.

**Adopted architecture:**

```
STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot FC)
   │  4× bidirectional DShot600 (TIM1 CH1–CH4)
   ▼
4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 ESC MCUs (AM32 firmware, bidir DShot + eRPM telemetry)
   │  3-phase PWM @ 48 kHz
   ▼
4× FD6288Q-class 3-phase gate drivers → 24× 40 V N-MOSFETs
```

### 0.2 Feasibility Finding

**"80 A burst" is only defensible as a short-duration rating (≈1–2 s) on a nano (≈25×25 mm) board.** The math below shows this. The continuous rating lands around **20–25 A per channel**. That is consistent with real-world nano 4-in-1 ESCs, and it is what the spec sheet should state.

Also note: 4 × 80 A = **320 A at 25.2 V ≈ 8 kW**. An XT60, a nano battery lead (14–16 AWG), and typical nano-class packs cannot deliver this. Simultaneous 80 A on all four channels is not physically achievable in the system. 80 A is a per-channel transient limit only.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.51 mΩ**

### 2.2 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

In 6-step operation, two phase legs conduct at every instant.

- **PWM leg:**
  - The HS FET conducts during D·T.
  - The LS FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts during (1−D)·T, excluding dead time.
  - Either way, one FET carries I.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is on continuously and carries I.

So exactly two FET channels carry I at all times, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2 R_{pcb}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.51\times10^{-3}) + (80)^2(0.5\times10^{-3}) = 19.3 + 3.2 = \mathbf{22.5\ W}$$

### 2.3 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET is active for 120° electrical, so its average share is ≈ 1/3.

The worst case is low eRPM or stall, where commutation is quasi-DC. A single FET then sees the full instantaneous loss:

$$P_{FET,inst} = I^2 R_{ds} = 6400 \times 1.51\,\text{m} = \mathbf{9.7\ W}$$

The HS FET also carries the switching loss from §3, so the worst-case FET dissipates about **11.6 W**.
