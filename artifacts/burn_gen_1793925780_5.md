## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b) \quad (\text{with } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (Surface PMSM)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI with Decoupling and Pole–Zero Cancellation

After decoupling, the plant per axis is $G(s)=\dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choose:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;(\text{so } K_i/K_p = R/L)
$$

This cancels the electrical pole, giving closed loop $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$. Pick $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$ to leave margin for the 1.5-sample PWM/ADC delay.

The control law is:

$$
v_d^* = K_p e_d + K_i\!\int e_d - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q^* = K_p e_q + K_i\!\int e_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

**Discrete form** (backward Euler, $T_s=1/f_{pwm}$), with back-calculation anti-windup gain $K_{aw}\approx 1/K_p$:

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad u^{sat}_k=\text{sat}(u_k),\quad
I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\big[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u_k^{sat}-u_k)\big]
$$
