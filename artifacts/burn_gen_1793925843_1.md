## 0. Architectural Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. An H743 running ArduPilot cannot also host 4× closed-loop sensorless commutation at 48 kHz without compromising scheduler determinism. The baseline topology is:

| Block | Part (baseline) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, DShot600 bidir master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 **[A]** (AM32 firmware) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q **[A]** (3-phase, bootstrap) | HS/LS drive |
| MOSFETs ×24 | 40 V, ≤1.0 mΩ, PDFN5×6 (BSC010N04LS-class) **[A]** | Bridge |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max / 22.2 V nom | |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm mount, ~36×36 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz all layers **[A]** | |

**Program-level warning:** "Nano" and "80 A burst per channel" are in tension. The model below quantifies that tension, and the answer is that 80 A is a **seconds-scale** rating, not a continuous one. Section 3 gives the numbers. Marketing must not publish 80 A without a duration qualifier.
