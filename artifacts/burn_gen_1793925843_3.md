## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate the motors.** Driving 12 half-bridges with BEMF zero-cross detection would consume its timers and ADCs and conflict with ArduPilot's scheduler. The architecture is therefore:

```
┌─────────────────────────── FC board (20×20 mm, M3/M2 holes) ───────────────────────────┐
│ STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) ── ArduPilot ── TIM1 CH1-4 ── bidirectional DShot600 ──────────┼──┐
└─────────────────────────────────
