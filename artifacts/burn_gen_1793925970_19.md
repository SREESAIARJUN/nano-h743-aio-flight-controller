## 0. Architecture and Baseline Parameters

**Architecture:** The H743 cannot practically commutate 4 sensorless BLDC motors while also running ArduPilot. The standard and recommended topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware)
                                               └─► 3-phase gate driver ──► 6× N-MOSFET per motor (24 total)
```

The 48 kHz PWM is set in the ESC firmware, not in ArduPilot.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

At any instant two phase legs carry the current:

- **PWM leg:** The high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D. The low-side conducts the remaining (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** The LS is fully on.

Therefore exactly **two R_ds(on) are always in series with the motor current**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.6\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-device split

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The average loss per FET is:

$$P_{FET,avg} = 18.6/6 = 3.1\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous loss in the conducting FET is 80² × 1.45 mΩ = 9.3 W. This value drives the transient Z_θJC check in §3.3.

### 1.4 Copper (PCB) conduction loss

Assume 2 oz copper (≈0.25 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, ≈0.32 mΩ/□ at 100 °C) and about 3 squares of effective path per phase across 4 paralleled inner layers:

$$R_{Cu} \approx 3 \times 0.32/4 = 0.24\ \text{mΩ per phase} \Rightarrow P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.24\text{m} = 3.1\ \text{W per motor}$$

**This is not negligible.** It is about 17% of FET conduction loss. Stitch power layers with at least 0.3 mm vias at no more than 1 mm pitch under the FET drain/source pads.
