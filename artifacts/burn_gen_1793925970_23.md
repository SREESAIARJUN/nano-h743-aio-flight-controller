## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step commutation

In any 60° sector, one phase is PWM-driven and a second phase's low-side FET is held on as the return path. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **Active leg:** the high-side FET conducts for fraction D and the low-side FET (synchronous rectification) for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the sector.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

This is **independent of duty**, which makes it a useful worst-case invariant.

Average loss per FET over an electrical revolution (each FET carries 1/6 of the total share):

$$
\bar P_{FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence and self-consistent junction temperature

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

With $T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta} P(T_j)$ and $P(T_j) = P_0[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$, where $P_0$ is the loss at 25 °C:

$$
\boxed{T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta P_0 (1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - R_\theta P_0 \alpha}}
$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion:** $R_\theta P_0 \alpha \ge 1$. This is a hard design check.
