# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Flight Controller (Job #31): Engineering Model

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot do the motor commutation.** In every production 4-in-1 design the jobs split like this:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) | ArduPilot, generates bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (QFN32), running AM32 | 6-step commutation, **48 kHz PWM**, BEMF sensing, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver, bootstrapped) | HS/LS gate drive |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 6 per motor |

The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC MCUs. The DShot/DMA analysis applies to the H743.

**Design assumptions.** All results scale from these. Replace them with your actual BOM values.
