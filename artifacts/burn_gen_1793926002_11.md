# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #28): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Budget & DShot Allocation

## 0. Executive Findings

These findings should drive design reviews before layout begins.

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| 80 A burst, all 4 channels, nano form factor | **Not thermally sustainable beyond about 1 s** from a 50 °C start. The power stage dissipates about 120 W, and a 30×30 mm board has about 4 J/K of thermal mass. A single-channel 80 A transient is survivable. Specify the rating as "80 A / 1 s, single channel" or move to a larger board. |
| Realistic continuous rating | About **20–28 A per channel** in prop wash at 40 °C ambient (derived in §3.4). |
| 48 kHz switching | Switching is executed by the **ESC MCUs** (e.g., 4× AT32F421 running AM32), not the H743. At 48 kHz the switching loss is about 1.7 W per channel at 80 A, which is small compared with conduction loss. Dead-time body-diode loss dominates the switching-related terms. |
| Bidirectional DShot600 on H743 | Feasible on a single timer. It needs **3 DMA streams for 4 motors** (1× TIMx_UP plus 2× input-capture channels using the BIDIR pairing). DMAMUX1 removes the usual F4/F7 stream conflicts. |
