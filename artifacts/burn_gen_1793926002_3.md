## 0. Executive Summary and Design-Review Flags

Read these before the math. They change the requirements.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---------|--------|
| F1 | **The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU, not the ESC MCU.** Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESCs over bi-directional DShot600. | Architecture is defined in §1. |
| F2 | **80 A per motor cannot be sustained on a nano-class (≤37×37 mm) board.** The model gives about 78 W of power-stage dissipation at 4×80 A. With prop-wash cooling, steady-state ΔT would be about 290 K. | **Rating must be stated as about 35 A continuous / 80 A for about 5 s (all four motors) or about 10 s (single motor).** See §4. |
| F3 | A single battery-current shunt of 0.1 mΩ dissipates about 10 W at 320 A total. | Use the per-ESC current-sense method or a Hall sensor. See §3.6. |
| F4 | At 48 kHz, hard-switching loss is small (about 1 W per motor). **Conduction loss dominates (about 92%).** | Spend money on R_DS(on) and copper, not on faster switching. |
| F5 | A 48 kHz PWM frequency is an ESC-firmware setting (AM32 supports it). It is independent of the DShot600 command rate. | No conflict. |

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──┬── Bulk caps ── VBAT bus (4 oz equivalent copper pour)
                        │
   ┌────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  ESC SECTION (×4 identical channels, n = 1..4)                  │
   │  ESCn MCU (AT32F421) ── 3-ph gate driver ── 6× N-MOSFET         │
   │      ▲  DShot600 bidir (single wire, open-drain-like,            │
   │      │  pulled up)                                              │
   └──────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
          │ MOTOR1..4 signal nets (TIM1_CH1..CH4)
   ┌──────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  FC SECTION: STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz (ArduPilot default)        │
   │  SPI1: IMU · SPI2: baro/flash · SPI4: OSD · SDMMC1 · UARTs      │
   │  5 V / 10 V BECs, 3.3 V LDO (separate analog rail for IMU)      │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
