# Job #35: STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80A-Burst ESC

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Several items in the brief conflict with each other or with physical limits. Here is how I resolved each one. **Every number below depends on these assumptions**, so review them first.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The STM32H743 does not commutate motors. ArduPilot runs on the FC, and each ESC needs its own commutation MCU. | Two-tier design: **H743 FC** sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4× **AT32F421 ESC MCUs running AM32**. AM32 supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot. |
| A2 | "Nano" (20×20 mm mounting) cannot hold 24 power FETs plus enough copper for 80 A. | Power stage sized for a **30.5×30.5 mount (~41×41 mm board)** using 3.3×3.3 mm FETs. The analysis in §3 shows 80 A is a **burst-only** rating, even at this size. |
| A3 | Battery voltage | 6S. V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge), used as the worst case for switching loss. |
| A4 | MOSFET parameters (representative 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3) | R_ds,25 = 1.8 mΩ at 10 V; α = 0.006 /K; Q_g = 35 nC; Q_gs2+Q_gd = 10 nC; V_plateau ≈ 3.0 V; Q_rr ≈ 60 nC at 80 A; C_oss,eff ≈ 600 pF; R_g,int = 1 Ω; R_θJC = 1.5 K/W. **Replace these with your selected part's datasheet values.** |
| A5 | Gate drive | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver. V_DRV = 10 V, R_out ≈ 1.5 Ω, R_g,ext = 3.3 Ω. |
| A6 | Modulation | AM32 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. Dead time t_d = 200 ns. |
| A7 | Definition of "80 A" | Phase (motor) current per ESC. Burst = all four motors at the same time (worst case). |
