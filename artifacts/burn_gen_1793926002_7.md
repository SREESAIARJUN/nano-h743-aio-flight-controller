# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack (Job #38): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal and DShot Allocation

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

These points shape every number in this document:

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 design the H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8BB51 running Bluejay), which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. This split is used throughout.
2. **The MOSFET part is a placeholder.** The parameters are representative of a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN part that fits a 20×20 mm nano board. Before release, replace them with values from your chosen datasheet. The equations stay valid.
3. **Operating point:** 6S LiPo (25.2 V full, 22.2 V nominal), 80 A burst per motor, 48 kHz PWM, synchronous rectification, 6-step trapezoidal commutation.
4. **Headline result:** a 20×20 nano 4-in-1 cannot sustain 80 A per channel. It can survive 80 A only as a short burst, quantified in §3. A product sheet should state "80 A burst" with a duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is sourcing current (its high-side FET is being PWMed) and one phase is sinking it (its low-side FET is held on).

During the PWM on-time (fraction D of the period):
- Current flows: high-side FET of phase A → motor winding → low-side FET of phase B.

During the off-time (fraction 1−D), with synchronous rectification:
- The low-side FET of phase A is switched on and carries the freewheeling current.
- The current then loops back through the low-side FET of phase B.

So at every instant **exactly two FETs carry the full phase current I**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

with the temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$
