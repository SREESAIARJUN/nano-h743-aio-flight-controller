## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32/BLHeli use six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM. At any instant exactly two FETs carry phase current:

- One leg's high-side or low-side FET, alternating at f_sw.
- The opposite leg's low-side FET, on continuously for 120° electrical.

Total conducted path resistance is therefore always **2·R_DS(on)**, independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = **1.34 mΩ**

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} \;\xrightarrow{D=0.5}\; \frac{24 \times 0.25}{30\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 4.17\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 1.45 \approx 6401\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple contribution is negligible (<0.03 %). At 48 kHz the motor inductance keeps phase current essentially DC.

### 1.4 Conduction loss per motor

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \times 6401 \times 1.34\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.2\ W}$$
