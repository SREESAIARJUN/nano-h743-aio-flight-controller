# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #63): Architecture, Loss Models, DShot DMA and Thermal Budget

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**Architecture.** In a 4-in-1 design the **STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM**. The partitioning is:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot, sends bi-directional DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs, receives eRPM |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (120 MHz), running AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF sensing, DShot decode and GCR eRPM reply |
| Gate drive (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V) | Gate drive with interlock |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET, DFN3.3×3.3 | Three half-bridges |

The H743-side deliverable is therefore the **DShot timer/DMA allocation**. The 48 kHz switching model applies to the ESC MCU and bridge.

**Key findings:**

1. **80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is a sub-second burst rating, not a continuous one.**
   - Conduction loss alone is about 48 W per channel when hot, about 190 W for all four.
   - The board heats adiabatically at roughly 90–100 K/s.
   - The derivation is in sections 1–4.
2. **Recommendation:** spec the board as "80 A burst ≤ 0.5 s, single channel" and "35–40 A continuous with airflow". Otherwise, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 5×6 mm FETs.
3. **Parameter assumptions:** all device parameters below are representative datasheet-class values. Replace them with the selected part's datasheet figures before sign-off.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current Path in 6-Step with Complementary (Synchronous) PWM

During any 60° commutation sector:
- Phase X (PWM leg): high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D; low-side (LS) synchronous-rectifies for 1−D.
- Phase Y (return leg): LS conducts continuously.
- Phase Z: floating, used for BEMF sensing.

Conduction loss per bridge (instantaneous, any sector):

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

**This is independent of duty cycle.** That is a key property of synchronous 6-step: at burst current the loss does not drop at partial throttle if the motor is current-limited.

### 2.2 Temperature Dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per bridge @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 2.00 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0020 = **25.6 W** |
| 80 °C | 2.61 mΩ | **33.4 W** |
| 110 °C | 2.94 mΩ | **37.6 W** |
| 125 °C | 3.10 mΩ | **39.7 W** |

**Copper and interconnect, per motor:**
- Pad, motor-lead and bus-plane resistance is about 1.5 mΩ in the current path.
- This adds I²R = 6400 × 0.0015 = **9.6 W**.
- About half of it is dissipated on-board and half in the leads.

**Shunt (if per-channel 0.25 mΩ):** 6400 × 0.00025 = **1.6 W**.
