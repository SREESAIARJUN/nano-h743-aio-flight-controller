## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate 12 half-bridges while also running ArduPilot. The architecture is therefore split into two domains:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot, sends BDShot600 to 4 ESCs, receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4 channels) | AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (or BLHeli_32) | 6-step / FOC commutation at 48 kHz |
| Power stage (per channel) | 3-phase gate driver plus 6 N-MOSFETs | Inverter |

Most of this document models the ESC power stage. Section 4 covers the H743 timer and DMA design.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-Step Synchronous (Complementary) PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, one leg is PWM-switched with complementary sync rectification, and a second leg's low-side FET is held on. The phase current I flows through:

- the high-side FET of the PWM leg for duty D,
- the low-side FET of the same leg (sync) for 1 − D,
- the low-side FET of the return leg for 100% of the sector.

The per-channel conduction loss is therefore:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{hot}\left[D + (1-D) + 1\right] = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

This result is independent of duty, apart from dead-time effects (Section 2.4).

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{hot} = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R_{hot} = 1.4 \times 1.5 = 2.10\ \text{m}\Omega \quad (\text{using } \alpha = 0.005)
$$

Many datasheets show a factor closer to 1.6–1.7 at 125 °C. The conservative design value is therefore R_hot = 2.24 mΩ (factor 1.6).

### 1.3 Numbers

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.24\ \text{m}\Omega = 28.7\ \text{W per channel}
$$

### 1.4 Copper and Interconnect Loss

Assume 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner layers, with an effective phase path plus bus share of about 0.3 mΩ:

$$
P_{Cu} \approx I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\ \text{m}\Omega = 1.9\ \text{W}
$$

Add a shunt if one is fitted. A 0.5 mΩ shunt carrying the bus current adds about I²_bus · R_sh, which is 0.5–3 W depending on duty.

### 1.5 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET conducts in 2 of 6 sectors as the held low-side, or with D or 1 − D shares when it is the PWM switch. Averaged over an electrical revolution:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond}}{6} \approx 4.8\ \text{W}
$$

The instantaneous peak is I²R_hot = 14.3 W while conducting full current. This peak matters for the transient thermal impedance Z_θ at low eRPM, where sector times reach milliseconds.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches in a given sector, so there is one hard-switched commutation pair per PWM period.

### 2.1 V–I Overlap (Hard-Switched Edge)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 35\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = 1.69\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse Recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

Q_rr rises with di/dt and temperature, so derate by 2–3× to obtain a realistic value of about 0.12 W.

### 2.3 Output Capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = C_{oss,eff} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1\,\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.030\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}
$$

This is the largest secondary term. Reducing dead time to 50 ns halves it, provided the driver propagation-delay mismatch allows it.

### 2.5 Gate Drive (Dissipated in Driver and R_G, Not in the FET)

$$
P_{gate} = Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} \times 2 = 40\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} \times 2 = 0.038\ \text{W}
$$

This is per leg switching. All 6 FETs per channel are refreshed over the revolution, so per channel this stays below 0.1 W.

### 2.6 Total Per-Channel Loss at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction | 28.7 |
| Copper and shunt | ~2.5 |
| V–I overlap | 1.69 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.15 |
| Gate (driver) | 0.04 |
| **Total** | **≈ 33.7 W** |
