## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Critical Findings

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. It cannot also commutate the motors in real time. The standard and only practical topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──bidir DShot600 ×4──► 4 × ESC MCUs (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class,
                                               e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071)
                                               └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6 × N-FET per motor
```

Everything below assumes this topology.

### 0.2 Design basis

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max / 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst current | **80 A per motor (phase/DC-link)**, 10 s target (see §3 for the alternative interpretation) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, synchronous rectification (complementary), 6-step trapezoidal |
| FET (reference class) | 40 V, R_DS(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ, Q_g = 60 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, Q_oss = 50 nC, t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns |
| Board | 20 × 20 mm mounting (≈ 26 × 26 mm outline), 6-layer, 2 oz outer copper |
| Ambient | 40 °C |

### 0.3 Critical finding

**80 A continuous-burst per motor on a nano (20 × 20) board is not thermally feasible for 10 s.** §3 shows the board survives about 2 s. You must either redefine the rating or move to a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board with 5 × 6 mm FETs and a heatsink.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly **two FETs carry phase current I at every instant** per motor:

- During on-time (duty D): the high-side of phase A and the low-side of phase B conduct.
- During off-time (1−D): the two low-sides conduct (synchronous freewheel), or the high-sides in alternate modulation schemes.

Therefore, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{shunt} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{\beta}, \quad \beta \approx 2.0\text{–}2.3$$

Linearised, this is about +0.6 %/K. At T_j = 125 °C the multiplier is **k_T ≈ 1.6**, so R_DS(on) = 1.92 mΩ.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Per motor |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.92 mΩ | **24.6 W** |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-motor) | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| PCB copper (≈0.3 mΩ path, 2 oz, short) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **Conduction subtotal** | | **≈ 28.1 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical period (120° commutation), but carries the full current when it does.

- Instantaneous dissipation: 80² × 1.92 mΩ = **12.3 W**
- Average dissipation: about 4.1 W conduction-only

The instantaneous figure matters for junction ripple (§3.3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only one leg hard-switches per PWM period (the PWM-modulated half-bridge).

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 40 ns · 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 40 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | 0.05 W |
| Output capacitance | ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V · f_sw | 0.03 W |
| Dead-time body-diode | V_F · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 · 80 · 100 ns · 48 k | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 · 60 nC · 10 V · 48 k | 0.06 W (dissipated mostly in driver/R_g) |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 2.4 W** |
