## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings constrain everything below.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | An H743 cannot run the motors directly. The ESC needs its own MCU per channel (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class). | Architecture is H743 FC → DShot → 4× ESC MCU → gate driver → FETs. |
| F2 | 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is thermally a **burst-only** rating of about 1–2 s, not continuous (proven in §3). | Specify "80 A burst (≤1 s), ~25–30 A continuous with airflow". |
| F3 | Four channels at 80 A means 320 A of pack current. | An XT60 (~60 A continuous) and 12–14 AWG leads cannot support it. Firmware current limits must cap total current. |
| F4 | Firmware details below (ArduPilot hwdef rules, AM32 PWM options) reflect current upstream behaviour as I know it. | Verify against the ArduPilot `hwdef` README and AM32 release notes for your target versions before freezing. |

**Assumptions** (change them and re-run the equations):
- 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max.
- 40 V N-ch MOSFETs, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN, R_ds(on) = 1.5 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g ≈ 50 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, Q_oss ≈ 30 nC.
- t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns at the chosen gate resistance.
- Board 30.5×30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz on all layers.
- 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, f_sw = 48 kHz, dead time 100 ns.

## 2. Loss Model: One ESC Channel

### 2.1 Current definitions

In 6-step with complementary PWM, at any instant exactly one high-side FET and one low-side FET carry the phase current I_ph.
- During the on-time, current flows through HS(a) and LS(b).
- During the off-time, the synchronous rectifier LS(a) replaces HS(a).

So two FETs are always in the conduction path, independent of duty cycle D:

$$I_{bat} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

The burst rating is taken as **I_ph = 80 A** at D → 1, so the battery current is about 80 A per channel.

### 2.2 Conduction losses

Temperature-corrected on-resistance (typical trench-FET coefficient α ≈ 0.4 %/K, normalized ~1.5× at 125 °C):

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)] = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 2.25\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 (80)^2 (2.25\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{28.8\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Per-FET average: each FET is in the path for 1/3 of the electrical cycle (120°). Averaged over the 6 FETs this is 28.8/6 = **4.8 W per FET**, with 14.4 W instantaneous while it conducts.

### 2.3 Copper and shunt losses (per channel)

| Element | R | P = I²R |
|---|---|---|
| Low-side shunt 0.25 mΩ (if per-channel) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Phase and bus copper (6L 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ path) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |

### 2.4 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches (HS(a) / LS(a) pair). LS(b) is static within the sector.

**Hard-switching overlap (V-I crossover):**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (2 transitions per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(200\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Body-diode reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}(25.2)(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5(30\text{nC})(25.2)(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.018\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (2 switching FETs, V_dr = 10 V). This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors:

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{dr} f_{sw} = 2(50\text{nC})(10)(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$
