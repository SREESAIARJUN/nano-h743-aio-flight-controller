## 0. Feasibility Findings

Four issues need resolution before layout begins.

| # | Finding | Impact | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | The H743 cannot commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 needs four ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (e.g., AT32F421). The H743 only sources DShot and sinks eRPM telemetry. | Architecture | Adopted (§1) |
| F2 | **80 A per motor on a nano board (≤ 25×25 mm) is not thermally sustainable.** The model in §3 shows about 66 W of stage dissipation with all four motors at 80 A. A board this size has roughly 3–4 J/K of heat capacity, so it heats at about 20 K/s. | Spec | 80 A is a **≤ 1.5 s burst rating**. Continuous rating is about 35 A per motor (§3.5). |
| F3 | Twelve PDFN5×6 FETs do not fit on 20×20 mm. | Form factor | **25.5×25.5 mm board (20×20 M2 pattern)** with PDFN3.3×3.3 FETs, or a 30.5 mm pattern with PDFN5×6. Model uses PDFN3.3 as worst case. |

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per motor, 6-step synchronous, 6S)

### 2.1 Definitions

- V_bus = 25.2 V (6S full charge, worst case for switching loss)
- I = 80 A (phase current during burst, assumed DC-like over the PWM period)
- f_sw = 48 kHz
- D = PWM duty cycle

In each commutation sector, one high-side (HS) FET of leg A is PWMed. Its complementary low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies. One LS FET of leg B is held on for the whole sector.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

The current path at any instant is:

- PWM-on: HS_A, then LS_B
- PWM-off: LS_A (synchronous rectification), then LS_B

So exactly two FET channels carry I at all times, independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

With α ≈ 0.0055 /K, so k(125 °C) = 1.55.

**Worst-case design point (T_j = 125 °C):**

$$R_{ds} = 0.95 \times 1.55 = 1.47\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.47\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.8\ W}$$

**Cold start (T_j = 25 °C):** P_cond = 12.2 W

**Per-device split:** P_HS = I²R·D and P_LS,A = I²R(1−D). LS_B carries I²R for the full sector, then rotates. Averaged over six sectors, each of the six FETs carries about 1/6 of the total, but the instantaneous peak per die is a full I²R ≈ 9.4 W. **That instantaneous value sizes the junction transient (§3.3).**

### 2.3 Switching Loss (hard-switched HS_A only)

Gate drive: FD6288Q, about 1.0 A source / 1.5 A sink at V_gs ≈ 12 V, plus R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω. The Miller-plateau current is approximately (V_drv − V_plateau)/R_g,total ≈ (12 − 3.5)/(2.2 + 1.5 + driver R) ≈ 1 A, current-limited.

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,src}} = \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{1.0\,\text{A}} = 9\,\text{ns} \quad (+\ \text{current rise} \approx 4\,\text{ns}) \Rightarrow 13\,\text{ns}$$

$$t_f \approx \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{1.5\,\text{A}} + 3\,\text{ns} = 9\,\text{ns}$$

**V–I overlap loss:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 22\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.06\ W}$$

Add 30% for loop inductance (L_loop ≈ 1.5 nH gives V_ov ≈ L·di/dt ≈ 1.5 nH × 80 A/4 ns ≈ 30 V): **P_VI ≈ 1.38 W**.

> **Warning:** 25.2 V + 30 V overshoot exceeds a 40 V rating. **The input MLCC must sit ≤ 2 mm from each HS drain.** The netlist below specifies 4 × 10 µF 0805 50 V per leg pair plus a 100 nF 0402 snubber cap.

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 38\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (the LS body diode conducts during dead time and then recovers):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 45\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.054\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (AM32 dead time ≈ 100 ns per edge, scaled to the gate driver):

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET):

$$P_g = Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} \times 2\ (\text{HS + LS switched}) = 45\text{n} \times 12 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = 0.052\ \text{W}$$
