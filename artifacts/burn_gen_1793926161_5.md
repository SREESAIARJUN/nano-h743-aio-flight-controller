## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Design-Risk Flags

Three points need to be settled first, because they change the model.

1. **The H743 does not run the 48 kHz commutation PWM.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU drives the gate drivers at 48 kHz. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 frames to those MCUs, then receives eRPM telemetry back. The model below is partitioned this way:
   - Sections 1–4 cover the ESC power stage.
   - Sections 5–6 cover the H743 timer and DMA allocation.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is thermally marginal.** The math below shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate roughly 90 W in about 4–6 g of copper, FR4 and silicon. That is a burst rating of about 2–3 s from a warm start. It is a single-channel or short-transient rating, not a sustained one. The datasheet should state the burst duration and initial temperature explicitly.
3. **All component parameters are representative assumptions.** They describe a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 5×6 mm-class MOSFET. Substitute the values from your selected part's datasheet into the equations. Every equation is kept in symbolic form for that purpose.
