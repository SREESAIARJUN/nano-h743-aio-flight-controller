## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step current paths with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, exactly two phase legs carry the motor current I_ph:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for fraction D and the low side (LS) for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** LS is on for 100% of the sector.
- **Third leg:** floating (used for BEMF sensing).

At every instant (ignoring dead time) exactly **two FETs** carry I_ph. The instantaneous bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch}=2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At a design junction temperature of T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}=1.2\,(1+0.0045\cdot85)=1.66\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction at 80 A

$$P_{cond,FET}=2(80^2)(1.66\times10^{-3})=21.2\ \text{W}$$

PCB copper and phase-pad path (≈0.30 mΩ total, 2 oz, short pours) adds:

$$P_{Cu}=80^2\cdot0.30\times10^{-3}=1.9\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution (worst case, D→1 at burst)

Each leg spends one third of the electrical cycle as the PWM leg and one third as the return leg.

| FET | Conduction fraction | Average conduction power |
|---|---|---|
| HS | D/3 ≈ 1/3 | 6400·1.66 mΩ/3 = **3.54 W** |
| LS | (1−D)/3 + 1/3 ≈ 1/3 | **3.54 W** |

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point

R_ds rises with T_j, which raises loss and therefore T_j. Iterate:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{board}+P(T_j^{(k)})\,R_{\theta JC}$$

This converges in 3–4 iterations. It runs away if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_\theta \ge 1$$

Here ∂P/∂T ≈ 2I²R₂₅α = 0.069 W/K per channel. Per FET (1/6 share) this is about 0.0115 W/K. Multiplied by 2 K/W this gives a loop gain of 0.023, so the junction-to-board loop is **stable**.

The board-to-air loop is where runaway actually occurs; see §3.

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (HS FET, inductive load)

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f)f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz})=1.94\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=40\text{ nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{ k}=0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss}\approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}=0.018\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt}=V_{SD}I_{ph}(2t_d)f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ ns}\cdot48\text{ k}=0.61\ \text{W}$$
