# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

**Job #20, Revision A (engineering derivation)**

> **Scope and assumptions.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to a 4-in-1 ESC. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 running AM32) driving a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 does not commutate the motors. All component parameters below are representative values for 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN-class MOSFETs. Before release, replace them with the datasheet values for the parts you actually select. The equations are general; the numbers are only as good as their inputs.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path (6-step with synchronous/complementary PWM)

During any 60° commutation sector, phase X is PWM'd high and phase Y is held low:

- **ON interval (D):** current flows through HS_X, the motor, then LS_Y.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_X, the motor, then LS_Y. This is synchronous rectification, so the FET channel conducts, not the body diode (except during dead time).

The current therefore always flows through exactly two FET channels, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction

Phase current ripple (peak-to-peak) with motor inductance L:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L\,f_{sw}}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

Worked example: L = 15 µH (typical 5" motor line-to-line), V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 3 V at high load, D ≈ 0.9.

- ΔI = 3.75 A
- ΔI²/12 = 1.2 A²
- This is negligible against 6400 A². Ignore it at burst current; keep it for low-L, low-kV motors.

### 2.3 Electro-thermal fixed point

Since R depends on T_j and T_j depends on P, solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{ref} + P_{FET}(T_j^{(k)})\cdot R_{\theta,j\text{-}ref}$$

Results at 80 A per channel:

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel = 6400 × 2R |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 22.3 W |
| 125 °C | 1.92 mΩ | **24.6 W** |

Use 125 °C as the design point: **24.6 W per channel, 98 W for all four.**

**PCB copper.** Phase-path copper adds roughly 0.3 mΩ total per current path (2 oz, short pours plus pad/via interfaces). At 80 A this is 6400 × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **1.9 W per channel**. This loss is distributed into the board rather than the FETs.
