## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Two points shape every calculation below.

1. **Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600 commands. It does **not** drive the power stage. The 48 kHz commutation PWM is generated by the ESC-side MCUs. The baseline here is AM32 firmware on 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421, one per motor. Switching-loss math therefore applies to the ESC stage, and DMA/timer math applies to the H743.
2. **"Nano" plus 80A burst is thermally marginal.** On a 20×20 mm ESC, 80A per channel is achievable only as a **seconds-scale transient**. Section 3 quantifies this. If the requirement is "80A for 10 s or more," the board must grow to 30×30 mm or use a heatsink.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, at any instant the phase current flows through exactly **two** conducting devices in series:

- The PWM leg: the high side conducts for D, the low side (synchronous) for 1 − D.
- The return leg: one low side is on for the full 60° sector.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + k(T_j - 25)],\quad k \approx 0.006\,\text{K}^{-1}$$

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.6 = 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \left(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{pcb} + R_{shunt}\right)$$

- **MOSFETs:** 80² × 2 × 1.6 mΩ = **20.5 W**
- **PCB copper** (estimated R_pcb ≈ 0.3 mΩ, 3 oz, 6–8 layer): 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W**
