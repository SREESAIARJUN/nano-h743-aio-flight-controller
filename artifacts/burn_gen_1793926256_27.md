## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot, closes the attitude loop, and emits bi-directional DShot600 frames. Each of the four ESC channels has its own motor-control MCU running AM32 (or BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and handles 6-step commutation. The math below follows that split.
