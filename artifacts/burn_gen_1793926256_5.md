### Architecture

The H743 does not commutate the motors. A 48 kHz FET PWM and BEMF zero-cross loop needs dedicated ESC MCUs. The partitioning is:

```
          Bidir DShot600 (1-wire, open-drain-ish, pulled up)
 STM32H743 ──┬── ESC MCU #1 (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32) ── FD6288Q ── 6× MOSFET ── M1
 (ArduPilot) ├── ESC MCU #2 ── ... ── M2
             ├── ESC MCU #3 ── ... ── M3
             └── ESC MCU #4 ── ... ── M4
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, phase A is PWM'd (HS/LS alternate) and phase B's low side is held ON. Phase C floats for BEMF sensing. The load current therefore always flows through exactly **two** FETs in series:

- during D: HS_A → motor → LS_B
- during (1−D): LS_A (sync rectifier) → motor → LS_B

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K} \Rightarrow R(125\,^\circ\text{C}) = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Per-bridge conduction loss

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I_b^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per motor |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 80² × 2.0 m = **12.8 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 80² × 3.0 m = **19.2 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Averaged over one electrical revolution, each leg sources current for 1/3 and sinks for 1/3:

- **Each low-side FET** conducts the full current as the return leg (1/3), plus (1−D)/3 as sync rectifier.
- **Each high-side FET** conducts D/3.

At high throttle (D → 0.95), the high-side and low-side loads are nearly balanced. The average is 19.2 W / 6 ≈ **3.2 W per FET**.

Instantaneous heating matters more than the average at low eRPM. A FET dwells in the 80 A state for a full sector. At 80 A, a single FET dissipates I²R = **9.6 W** during its conduction window.

### 1.5 Thermal runaway check (R_DS–T_j coupling)

$$P(T_j) = I^2 R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right],\qquad T_j = T_a + R_{\theta JA} \cdot P$$

$$\Rightarrow\ T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{\theta JA} I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_{\theta JA} I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}$$

The system is stable only if R_θJA · I² · R₂₅ · α < 1. Per FET at the 3.2 W-equivalent average, I_eff² ≈ 3.2/1.5m:

$$R_{\theta JA} < \frac{1}{(3.2/1.5\text{m})\cdot 1\text{m}\cdot 0.005} \approx 94\ \text{K/W}$$

This criterion is easily met. Runaway is **not** the limiting mechanism; thermal capacity is (see §3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. The low-side return FET is static during the sector.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap (high-side FET)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_b (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (low-side FET of PWM leg)

$$P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I_b \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery (charged into HS turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

Q_rr scales with di/dt and current. Take this figure from the datasheet at 80 A rather than relying on it as given.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

The full ½CV² is lost per HS turn-on for the switching device plus the charge exchange with its complement.

### 2.5 Gate drive (per switching leg, two FETs)

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$

Most of this is dissipated in the FD6288Q and R_g, not the FETs. The FD6288Q bootstrap and VCC regulator budget must cover 4 × 0.058 W plus quiescent current.
