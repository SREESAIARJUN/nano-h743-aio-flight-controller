## 0. Architecture Clarification and Governing Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the power stage directly. In this AIO architecture:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (×4)──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32)
                                                    │ 48 kHz complementary PWM
                                                    ▼
                                    4× 3-phase gate driver ──► 24× N-MOSFET ──► motors
```

The 48 kHz switching and 80 A conduction losses are therefore properties of the ESC half. The H743 owns the DShot timing and DMA allocation.

**Form-factor warning.** Sustaining 80 A per channel (≈320 A aggregate) on a true nano (20×20 mm) board is not physically credible. The copper cross-section and thermal mass are insufficient. This model assumes:

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.60 = **1.92 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-device conduction (six-step, complementary PWM)

Each phase leg spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the PWM'd high-side leg, 1/3 as the active low-side leg, and 1/3 floating. With duty D:

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS}\,\frac{D}{3}$$

$$P_{LS} = I^2 R_{DS}\left[\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right]$$

Summing one leg:

$$P_{leg} = I^2R_{DS}\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}(D + 1 - D + 1) = \tfrac{2}{3}I^2R_{DS}$$

Summing three legs gives the per-ESC total:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$
