# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation (Job #64)

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Several premises need correcting before any math is meaningful.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel runs its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32), which generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743's job is to emit bi-directional DShot600 and decode the eRPM telemetry. This document models that two-tier architecture.

**2. "80A burst" on a nano board is thermally marginal.** Read it as a per-channel rating lasting a few seconds, not a sustained capability. Four channels at 80A simultaneously means about 320A from the battery. That exceeds the connector rating (XT60 ≈ 60A continuous) and the pack's sustainable current long before the FETs fail. Section 3 quantifies the burst limit.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.92 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

In each 6-step sector, current flows through two "on" switch positions: the PWM'd phase (high side for duty D, low side via synchronous rectification for 1−D) and the low-side return phase.

With complementary PWM, the PWM'd phase always has exactly one FET conducting. The current path therefore always contains two R_DS in series, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At 80 A and 125 °C:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 6400 \times 2 \times 1.92\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each phase is driven for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. Near full throttle (D → 1), each FET conducts about 1/3 of the time:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = \mathbf{4.1\ W\ per\ FET}$$

The total is 6 × 4.1 W = 24.6 W, which checks.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often larger than designers expect)

| Element | Assumed R | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad/trace (2 oz, 4 layers in parallel) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| DC bus planes, per channel share | 0.2 mΩ | 1.3 W |
| Current shunt (if used) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W (DC current) |

Budget roughly **+4–5 W per channel** for copper and shunt.
