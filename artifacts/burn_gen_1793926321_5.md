**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 does not drive the gates directly. In a 4-in-1 AIO design:

- The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- Each motor has a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) that generates the 48 kHz 3-phase PWM, performs BEMF zero-cross detection, and answers DShot with eRPM telemetry.

**Feasibility flag.** A "nano" footprint (20×20 mm) cannot carry 4 × 80 A. Even the 30.5 × 30.5 mm footprint can only carry 80 A per motor as a short burst, with all four motors limited to a few seconds. Section 3 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in synchronous 6-step drive

At any instant, two phases conduct:

- **PWM-ON interval (duty D):** current flows through the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- **PWM-OFF interval (1−D):** current flows through the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous freewheel) and the low-side FET of phase B.

In both intervals, exactly **two FET channels** carry the full phase current I. The conduction loss is therefore independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 0.70 × (1 + 0.0055 × 75) = **0.99 mΩ**

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

Assume a per-motor phase loop (bus, FET pads, phase pad) of R_Cu ≈ 0.30 mΩ.

### 1.4 Results at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS(on) | 2·I²·R_DS | I²·R_Cu | P_cond per motor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.70 mΩ | 8.96 W | 1.92 W | 10.9 W |
| 100 °C | 0.99 mΩ | 12.67 W | 1.92 W | **14.6 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.09 mΩ | 13.95 W | 1.92 W | 15.9 W |

Because loss rises with temperature, the system has positive feedback. It converges if

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,\theta < 1 .
$$

Here ∂P/∂T = 2 × 6400 × 0.70e-3 × 0.0055 = 0.049 W/K per motor, so the loop is stable for θ < 20 K/W. This holds easily.

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution

Averaged over an electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs conducts:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = \frac{I^2R}{3}\approx 2.1\ \text{W}\ (@100^\circ C)
$$

During the 60° sector when a FET carries current, its instantaneous dissipation is up to 6.3 W. This matters for Z_th at low eRPM (motor startup or stall).
