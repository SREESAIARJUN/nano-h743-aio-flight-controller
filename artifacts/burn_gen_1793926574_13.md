```
     │  └───┬────┘  └───┬────┘  └───┬────┘  └───┬────┘     │
     │   3-ph driver + 6× N-FET per motor (24 FETs total)  │
     └──────┬───────────┬───────────┬───────────┬──────────┘
            │ DShot600 bidirectional (single wire, open-drain-like, pulled up)
     ┌──────┴───────────┴───────────┴───────────┴──────────┐
     │  FC: STM32H743 @ 400/480 MHz, ArduPilot (ChibiOS)   │

     └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Architectural clarification.** The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Commutation at 48 kHz runs on per-motor ESC MCUs (AM32). The H743's job is DShot generation, eRPM telemetry capture, and the flight stack.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, two phases carry current I_ph:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T. The low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is held ON for the full 120° sector.
- **Floating leg:** both FETs are off and the phase is used for BEMF sensing.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor:

$$
P_{cond}(t) = I_{ph}^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS}\right] + I_{ph}^2 R_{LS} + 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{cu}
$$

With matched FETs (R_HS = R_LS = R_ds(Tj)), this simplifies to:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,\big(R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{cu}\big)}, \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

This expression is independent of D. At an 80 A burst the throttle is near 100 %, so I_batt ≈ I_ph · D ≈ I_ph.

### 2.2 Numerical evaluation (80 A)

I_ph² = 6400 A².

| Tj | R_ds | FET conduction (2·I²·R_ds) | Copper (2·I²·R_cu) | Total per motor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W | 19.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 6.4 W* | 25.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.60 mΩ | 20.5 W | 6.4 W* | 26.9 W |

\*Copper also has a tempco (+0.39 %/K). At ΔT = 75 K it rises to about 8.3 W. That is included in the worst case in §3.

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET is active for one 120° sector per electrical cycle, i.e. 1/3 duty. Averaged over the electrical cycle at Tj = 100 °C:

$$
\bar P_{FET} = \frac{18.6\ \text{W}}{6} \approx 3.1\ \text{W}
$$

The instantaneous peak while conducting is I²·R_ds = 9.3 W. This peak matters for junction ripple, which is evaluated in §3.2.
