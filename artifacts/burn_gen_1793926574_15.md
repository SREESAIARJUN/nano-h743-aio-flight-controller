| Function | Silicon | Firmware |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005·100) = **1.50 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Bridge conduction topology

AM32 uses 6-step (120°) commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, current flows through one high-side and one low-side path in series. During the PWM off-time the PWM'd leg's complementary FET carries the current, so the path still contains two R_DS elements:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, so:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3}$$

### 1.3 Numerical results at I_ph = 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond / motor | P_cond / FET (avg) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.13 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W | 2.93 W |
| 125 °C | 1.50 mΩ | **19.2 W** | **3.20 W** |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt

**Phase copper.** Trace/plane plus via resistance ≈ 0.3 mΩ per current path (bus + phase):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 \cdot R_{Cu} \approx 6400 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.9\ \text{W/motor}$$

**Shared current-sense shunt.** 0.25 mΩ, carrying battery current, not phase current. Under PWM, I_bat ≈ D·I_ph. At D = 0.85 with 4 motors:

$$I_{bat} \approx 272\ \text{A}$$
$$P_{shunt} = 272^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 18.5\ \text{W}$$

> A 272 A all-motor burst is not physically sustainable on a nano board (connector and battery lead limits apply). The realistic design case is **asymmetric bursts**: one or two motors at 80 A during yaw or punch-out.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches hard in each commutation sector.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching, inductive clamp)

Gate driver: FD6288Q, ≈ 1.0 A source / 1.5 A sink, with R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω. The Miller plateau sets the transition times:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,src}} \approx \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{0.6\,\text{A}} = 15\ \text{ns}$$
$$t_f \approx \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{0.75\,\text{A}} = 12\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 27\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.31\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (complementary body diode)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 35\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.042\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and R_g, not in the FET)

$$P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 45\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.043\ \text{W/motor}$$
