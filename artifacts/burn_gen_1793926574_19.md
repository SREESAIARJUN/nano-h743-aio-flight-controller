**Rev A: Engineering Baseline**

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

A design review would flag these first, so they are stated up front.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC commutation MCU.** Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class, AM32 firmware). The H743 drives them with bi-directional DShot600 under ArduPilot. One H743 cannot perform sensorless commutation and run ArduPilot for four motors at 48 kHz. That topology is not credible.
2. **Electrical envelope:** 6S (25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal). The bus is rated for a 30 V transient clamp.
3. **Nano form factor:** 20×20 mm mounting, about 26×26 mm board, 8-layer stack with 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power layers.
4. **MOSFET:** a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN, single device per switch, 24 FETs total. The reference parameters in §1 are representative placeholders. Substitute the actual datasheet values.
5. **"80 A burst" is per motor and transient.** The thermal model in §4 shows why this is a sub-second to few-second rating, not a continuous one. This is the most important finding in this document.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × 1.375 = **2.06 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

During each 60° step, current flows through the high side (HS) of phase X and the low side (LS) of phase Y. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **HS(X)** conducts for a fraction D.
- **LS(X)** (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1 − D), excluding dead time.
- **LS(Y)** (return path) conducts continuously.

Instantaneous loss in the bridge is:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2I^2R_{ds}$$

This loss is independent of D.

Each leg is active as the PWM leg for 120° and as the return leg for 120°. Per-FET averages are therefore:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{ds}, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\, I^2 R_{ds}$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C, D = 0.95

- I²R = 6400 × 2.06 mΩ = **13.18 W**

| Loss | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge conduction | 2 × 13.18 | **26.4 W** |
| Per HS FET | 13.18 × 0.95 / 3 | **4.17 W** |
| Per LS FET | 13.18 × 1.05 / 3 | **4.61 W** |
| Copper (traces, pads, vias) | 6400 × 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |

The LS is the conduction-dominant device at high throttle. At low D it approaches (2/3)·I²R.

### 2.4 Self-consistent junction temperature

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j) \cdot R_{\theta JC}$$

This is solved by fixed-point iteration, which converges in 3 to 4 steps because α·I²R·R_θJC ≪ 1.
