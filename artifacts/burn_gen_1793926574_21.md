## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 0.90 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = **1.20 mΩ**

### 1.2 Bridge current path (6-step, synchronous rectification)

In any commutation sector, two phases conduct.

- **PWM-on:** HS(a) → motor → LS(b)
- **PWM-off (sync. rect.):** LS(a) → motor → LS(b)

Exactly **two FETs carry I_ph at all times**, ignoring dead time. The bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2\,R_{Cu}
$$

where R_Cu is the phase trace, solder and bus copper resistance (budget 0.3 mΩ round-trip for a well-poured 8-layer board).

### 1.3 Per-FET loss

Average over an electrical revolution:

$$
\bar P_{FET} = \frac{P_{cond,bridge}}{6} \approx \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}}{3}
$$

The worst-case instantaneous loss in a single FET while it is conducting is:

$$
\hat P_{FET} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}
$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

| Term | Equation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction, per bridge | 2 · 80² · 1.20 mΩ | **15.4 W** |
| Copper, per bridge | 80² · 0.3 mΩ | **1.9 W** |
| Instantaneous worst FET | 80² · 1.20 mΩ | **7.7 W** |
| Average per FET | 15.4 / 6 | **2.6 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. Per bridge per PWM period there is one turn-on and one turn-off hard transition. The sync-rect FET soft-switches with its body diode conducting.

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard switching)

Transition times are dominated by the Miller plateau:

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,src}},\qquad
t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,snk}}
$$

With the reference values, t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 10 ns.

$$
P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}
$$
