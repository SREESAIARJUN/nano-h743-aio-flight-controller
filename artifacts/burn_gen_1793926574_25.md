## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and an Architectural Clarification

I don't have the Job #44 BOM, so every numeric result below uses **stated, representative parameters**. All equations are parametric. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values and the numbers will regenerate.

**Architectural note.** In a standard FC + 4-in-1 stack, the **STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM**. Four ESC MCUs run the bridges and generate it, under AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware on parts such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 does the following:

- Emits bidirectional DShot600 frames.
- Captures the GCR eRPM telemetry.
- Runs ArduPilot's harmonic notch from that telemetry.

The loss model (Sections 1–3) therefore applies to the ESC board. The DMA model (Section 4) applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous rectification, the current at any instant flows through exactly **two** switch positions:

- **PWM ON:** HS(A) → winding → LS(B)
- **PWM OFF (synchronous freewheel):** LS(A) → winding → LS(B)

The total bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 0.70 × 1.45 = **1.015 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each phase leg is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle (120°), at duty D:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R, \quad P_{LS} = \frac{1}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R + \frac{1}{3} I^2 R$$

The second LS term is the "return" low side, which is on for the full 120° segment.

At burst (D ≈ 0.95, full throttle):

- P_HS ≈ ⅓ · 0.95 · 6400 · 1.015 mΩ = **2.06 W**
- P_LS ≈ ⅓ · 0.05 · 6.50 + ⅓ · 6.50 = **2.27 W**

### 1.4 Result per motor channel at 80 A

| Term | Value |
