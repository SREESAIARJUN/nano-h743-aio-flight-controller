## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0056 K⁻¹ (from typical R(150)/R(25) ≈ 1.7).

At the design point T_j = 100 °C:
R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0056 × 75) = **1.42 mΩ**

### 1.2 Conduction loss

In 6‑step operation, two FETs are always in the current path: a high-side FET of one phase (or its synchronous partner) and a low-side FET of another phase. For a phase current I_ph with PWM duty D:

- PWM-ing high-side FET: P = I_rms² · R_DS · D/3
- Its synchronous low-side partner: P = I_rms² · R_DS · (1−D)/3
- Static low-side FET of the return phase: P = I_rms² · R_DS · 1/3

The duty terms cancel for the leg pair, so:

**P_cond,ch = 2 · I_rms² · R_DS(T_j)**

Including current ripple ΔI (peak‑to‑peak, triangular): I_rms² = I_dc² + ΔI²/12.

With motor L ≈ 15 µH (phase‑phase), the worst-case ripple occurs at D = 0.5:
ΔI = V·D(1−D)/(L·f) = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15e‑6 × 48e3) ≈ 8.75 A

That gives ΔI²/12 ≈ 6.4 A², which is negligible compared with 6400 A².

**At 80 A:**
P_cond,ch = 2 × 6406 × 1.42e‑3 = **18.2 W**, which averages to **3.03 W per FET** (6 FETs).

### 1.3 Switching loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches hard.

**Transition times from the Miller plateau** (V_pl ≈ 3.0 V):
- Turn-on gate current: I_g,on = (10 − 3)/4 = 1.75 A, so t_on ≈ Q_gd/I_g,on + Q_gs2 term ≈ 3.4 + 2.5 ≈ **6 ns**
- Turn-off gate current: I_g,off = 3/4 = 0.75 A, so t_off ≈ 8 + 4
