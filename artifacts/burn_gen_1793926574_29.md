## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two phases conduct at any time:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side FET conducts for fraction D and the low-side FET for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** one low-side FET is on continuously.

Conduction loss per motor is therefore:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous drive.

**Temperature correction**, at a design junction temperature of T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110^\circ C) = 1.2\,\text{mΩ}\,[1 + 0.005(110-25)] = 1.71\,\text{mΩ}$$
