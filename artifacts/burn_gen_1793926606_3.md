## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, current enters through one high-side FET (HS_a) and returns through one low-side FET (LS_b, held on). During the PWM off-time, HS_a turns off and its complementary low-side FET LS_a takes the freewheel current.

Resistive conduction losses for that sector:

- HS_a: $D\,I^2 R$
- LS_a (sync rectifier): $(1-D)\,I
