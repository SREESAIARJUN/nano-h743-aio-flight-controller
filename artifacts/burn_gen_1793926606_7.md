# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #20): Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot Allocation

**Classification:** Design baseline, Rev A
**Scope:** 30.5×30.5 mm board (M3, 25.5 mm hole pitch variant optional). STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. Four independent ESC MCUs run AM32 at 48 kHz PWM. Motors are driven over bi-directional DShot600.

---

## 0. Executive Summary (Read First)

| Quantity | Result |
|---|---|
| Loss per motor at 80 A phase current, Tj ≈ 125 °C | **≈ 25.7 W** (23.0 W conduction + 2.7 W switching/diode/gate) |
| Board total at 4 × 80 A | **≈ 103 W** |
| Continuous capability (ΔT_board = 60 K, prop-wash cooling) | **≈ 20 A per motor** |
| Permissible 80 A burst, starting from a 15 A hover equilibrium | **≈ 1 s** |
| DMA streams required (ArduPilot, bdshot on 4 channels) | 5 of 16 for motors; 15 of 16 total with all peripherals |

**Engineering position.** On a nano footprint, "80 A burst" is achievable only as a sub-2-second transient. The board's thermal mass is the limiting element, not the MOSFET junctions. The ESC firmware's current limit and the FC's `BATT_*` and `MOT_*` limits must enforce this. Section 2.5 gives the derating curve. If the requirement is 80 A for 10 s, the design must move to a 30×30 ESC with 5×6 mm FETs and a separate FC stack.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 VBAT (2–6S, 25.2 V max)
   │
   ├─► TVS + bulk caps ─► 3-phase bridges ×4 (24 × N-MOSFET, 3.3×3.3 PDFN)
   │                         ▲  gate drive: FD6288Q ×4 (bootstrap, 3-phase)
   │                         │
   │                ESC MCU ×4 (STM32G071 / AT32F421, AM32, 48 kHz)
   │                         ▲  DShot600 bidir, 1 wire per motor
   │                         │
   ├─► Buck 5 V / 2 A ─► LDO 3.3 V ─► STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) @ 480 MHz
   │                                     ├ SPI1: IMU#1 (ICM-42688-P)
   │                                     ├ SPI4: IMU#2 (BMI270) (optional)
   │                                     ├ SPI2: AT7456E OSD / W25N01G flash
   │                                     ├ I2C1: baro (DPS310 / SPL06), ext mag
   │                                     ├ UART×6, USB-FS (PA11/PA12)
   │                                     └ ADC: VBAT divider, CURR (shunt amp)
   └─► Shunt 0.25 mΩ ─► INA186 (gain 200) ─► ADC
```

**Why separate ESC MCUs instead of driving bridges from the H743?** ArduPilot does not implement sensorless FOC or 6-step commutation on the flight MCU. Isolating commutation also contains the timing-critical ISR load and the EMI-coupled zero-cross comparators.
