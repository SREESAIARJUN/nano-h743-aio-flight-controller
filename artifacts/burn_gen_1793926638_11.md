## 0. Feasibility Findings

Three findings shape everything below.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot. Each power stage needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. | The architecture is H743 → 4× ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class) → 4× gate drivers → 24 MOSFETs. |
| F2 | 80 A × 4 on a nano footprint (≤30.5 mm) is physically a short burst rating, not continuous. Section 3 shows roughly 80–90 W of dissipation into a board with about 5–8 J/K heat capacity. | 80 A is specified as a per-motor burst of about 2–4 s. Continuous rating should be about 20–30 A per motor with airflow. |
| F3 | All device parameters below are design-target values, not a specific datasheet. | Re-run the model with the selected MOSFET's R_DS(on)(T), Q_g, Q_rr, Q_oss and Z_th curves before freeze. |

## 1. System Architecture

```
 VBAT (6S, 25.2 V max) ──┬── Bulk cap (ext. ≥1000 µF low-ESR) + on-board MLCC bank
                         ├── 4× 3-phase bridges (6 FETs each) ── Motors M1..M4
                         ├── 4× Gate drivers (3-half-bridge, bootstrap)
                         ├── 4× ESC MCU (AM32) ── NTC, shunt amp, BEMF dividers
                         ├── BEC 10 V / 5 V buck  ── VTX/servo/5 V rail
                         └── VBAT divider + total current shunt ── H743 ADC
 H743 ─(4× bi-dir DShot600, single wire each)─ ESC MCU1..4
```

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel, per motor)

### 2.1 Operating Assumptions

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (worst case for switching) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current (DC-equivalent in 6-step) |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_DS(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 class |
| α | 0.006 /K | Linearised R_on tempco (≈1.5× at 110 °C) |
| t_r + t_f | 20 ns | At V_GS = 10 V, ~1.5 A driver, R_g ≈ 3 Ω |
| Q_g | 60 nC | Total gate charge at 10 V |
| Q_rr | 50 nC | Body diode reverse recovery charge |
| Q_oss | 40 nC | Output charge at V_bus |
| V_F | 0.8 V | Body diode forward voltage at 80 A |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| R_Cu | 0.3 mΩ | PCB copper path per channel (pad → plane → motor pad) |

### 2.2 Temperature-Dependent R_on

$$R_{on}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_on = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006·85) = **1.51 mΩ**.

### 2.3 Conduction Loss

In 6-step (trapezoidal) drive with complementary/synchronous PWM, phase current always flows through exactly two FETs:

- the active high-side plus the low-side of the return phase during D, or
- the synchronous low-side plus the return low-side during (1−D).

Therefore:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{on}(T_j) \quad (\text{independent of } D \text{ to first order})$$

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.51\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.3\ W}$$

Copper loss:

$$P_{Cu}=I^2R_{Cu}=6400\times0.3\times10^{-3}=\mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

### 2.4 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd FET hard-switches. The synchronous FET soft-switches (ZVS via the body diode).

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·20 ns·48 k | **0.97 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2·50 nC·48 k | **0.06 W** |
| Output charge | V·Q_oss·f_sw = 25.2·40 nC·48 k | **0.05 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (6 FETs) | 6·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 6·60 nC·10·48 k | **0.17 W** (mostly in driver/R_g) |
| **Σ switching** | | **≈1.86 W** |

### 2.5 Totals

| | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 19.3 W | 77.2 W |
| Copper | 1.9 W | 7.6 W |
| Switching + gate | 1.9 W | 7.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈23.1 W** | **≈92 W** |

Efficiency at burst: P_out ≈ 4 × 25.2 V × 80 A × D. Losses are dominated by I²R. Switching is only about 8% of loss at 48 kHz, so 48 kHz is affordable. Raising to 96 kHz adds only about 7.4 W total.

**Sensitivity:** ∂P/∂R_on = 2I² = 12.8 W per mΩ per channel. The R_on choice dominates everything.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Steady-State Per-FET Junction Rise

Each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical period, carrying the share of P_cond held by that FET pair. Average per-FET dissipation is about 23.1 W / 6 ≈ 3.9 W, with an instantaneous peak of about 9.7 W during its 120° window.

The electrical period at typical ERPM is a few ms or less, which is shorter than the die thermal time constant, so use the average.
