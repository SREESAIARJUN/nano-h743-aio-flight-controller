# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #59): Loss, Thermal, DShot, and Netlist Model

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

I've stated these explicitly because the numbers below depend on them. Change any of them and the math shifts.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | H743 flight controller board stacked over a 4-in-1 ESC. **Each ESC channel runs its own MCU** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 on AM32). The H743 does *not* commutate motors. It only emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (worst case for switching loss) |
| "80 A burst" | **80 A phase current per channel**, all 4 channels simultaneously (worst case). Battery current I_bat ≈ D·I_ph. |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, 120° conduction, complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active high side, low side of return phase held on |
| PWM | f_sw = 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (reference) | 40 V, DFN5×6, R_ds(on),max = 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g ≈ 90 nC, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, Q_oss ≈ 60 nC, R_θJC ≈ 1.2 K/W. This is representative of the 1 mΩ / 40 V class (e.g. Infineon OptiMOS 5). **Substitute your actual datasheet values.** |
| Gate drive | 3-phase bootstrap driver (FD6288Q class), V_GS = 10–12 V, effective t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns at 80 A, dead time 200 ns |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm ESC, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner copper |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag:** "Nano" (20×20) and 80 A × 4 are not physically compatible for more than about 1 s. DFN5×6 FETs at 6 per channel (24 total) do not fit on 20×20.
