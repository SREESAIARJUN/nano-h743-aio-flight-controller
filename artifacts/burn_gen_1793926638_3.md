## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path (6-step, synchronous rectification)

In any commutation sector, current always flows through two series switch positions:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100 % of the sector.

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent Resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds}=1.0\,(1+0.0045\cdot100)=1.45\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Conduction Loss per Motor Channel

$$P_{cond}=I^2 R_{ds}\,[D+(1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}\cdot 1 = 2I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of D because of synchronous rectification.

$$P_{cond}=2(80^2)(1.45\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{18.56\ W}$$

### 1.4 Copper Loss

$$P_{cu}=I^2R_{cu}=6400\times0.5\times10^{-3}=\mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 1.5 Self-Consistency

R_ds depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. Iterate the pair below; it converges in 2–3 iterations:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{amb} + P(T_j^{(n)})\cdot Z_{th}(t)$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard. Its commutation partner is the synchronous FET.

### 2.1 V·I Overlap Loss

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\text{ ns})(48\text{ k}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse Recovery

During deadtime the low-side body diode conducts, so Q_rr is incurred at high-side turn-on:

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output Capacitance

$$P_{oss}=\tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}=0.5\cdot40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.024\ W}$$

### 2.4 Deadtime Body-Diode Conduction

There are two deadtime intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt}=V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot400\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

> **Design lever:** Deadtime loss is about 63 % of the V·I loss. Reducing t_dt from 200 ns to 100 ns, after verifying shoot-through margin on the bench, saves about 0.6 W per channel.

### 2.5 Gate Drive Loss

This loss is dissipated in the driver, not the FETs:

$$P_g = N_{sw}\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}$$

Worst case is all 6 FETs at full rate: 6 × 40 nC × 10 V × 48 kHz = **0.115 W**.

### 2.6 Loss Budget at 80 A Burst, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss term | Per channel (W) | 4 channels (W) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 18.56 | 74.2 |
| Copper | 3.20 | 12.8 |
| V·I switching | 1.94 | 7.8 |
| Deadtime diode | 1.23 | 4.9 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 | 0.3 |
| Gate drive | 0.12 | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **25.1** | **100.5** |

At 80 A per motor, total phase power is roughly 4 × 25.2 V × 80 A × D. At D ≈ 0.9 that is about 7.3 kW of input, so stage efficiency is about 98.6 %.

### 2.7 Per-FET Average Loss

The conducting role rotates through all 6 FETs over one electrical cycle. Average dissipation per FET:

$$P_{FET}\approx\frac{P_{cond}+P_{VI}+P_{dt}+P_{rr}}{6}\cdot 2 \approx 7.3\ \text{W}$$
