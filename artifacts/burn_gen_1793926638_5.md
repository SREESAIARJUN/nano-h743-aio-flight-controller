**Architecture assumption.** ArduPilot runs on the H743, and each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (AT32F421 or STM32G071). The H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors and run ArduPilot's scheduler simultaneously. The DShot section therefore covers the H743 → ESC-MCU link.

**Feasibility verdict.**
- 80 A burst on all four channels simultaneously is not thermally sustainable on a nano board for more than about 3–5 s without a heatsink (see §3).
- 80 A on one or two channels transiently is achievable.
- The rating must be specified as **"80 A, X seconds, single channel, at T_a"** or it is a marketing number.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
With trapezoidal commutation and complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two conducting devices:
- one high-side (HS) or its leg's low-side (LS) on the PWM'd phase, and
- one LS on the return phase.

Because sync rectification makes the PWM'd leg conduct either HS (duty D) or LS (1−D), conduction loss is to first order independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \big(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}\big)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence
$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.45) = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Ripple correction
Phase current ripple is ΔI = V_bus·D(1−D)/(L_ph·f_sw). For a typical 5 mH-class motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH):
- ΔI ≈ 25.2·0.25/(15e-6·48e3) ≈ 8.75 A pk-pk
- I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12 = 6400 + 6.4 → negligible (<0.1 %)

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, per channel

| Component | Value | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| 2 × R_ds @125 °C | 2.90 mΩ | 18.6 W |
| Copper path (pads, planes, vias) | 0.30 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Current shunt (if used) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| **P_cond (125 °C)** | | **22.1 W** |
| P_cond (25 °C, cold start) | | 16.1 W |

### 1.5 Electro-thermal fixed point
Loss raises T_j, which raises R_ds, which raises loss. Solve:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th}\,I^2\big(2R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + R_{ext}\big)$$

$$\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_{th}I^2\big(2R_{ds,25}(1-25\alpha)+R_{ext}\big)}{1 - 2R_{th}I^2R_{ds,25}\,\alpha}$$

**Thermal runaway condition.** The denominator goes to zero when

$$R_{th} \ge \frac{1}{2I^2R_{ds,25}\alpha} = \frac{1}{2\cdot6400\cdot0.001\cdot0.0045} = 17.4\ \text{K/W}$$

Steady-state R_th(j→air) for one FET pair on a nano board without a heatsink is about 15–25 K/W. **At 80 A continuous you are at or past runaway, which is why this must be a burst rating.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In trapezoidal mode only one leg PWMs at a time. Its HS is hard-switched and its LS sees a dead-time diode plus reverse recovery.

### 2.1 Gate-drive transition time
$$t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g},\quad I_g = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_g+R_{g,int}}$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_pl = 3.5 V, R_g total = 5 Ω:
- I_g ≈ 1.3 A
- t_sw ≈ (5 + 10) nC / 1.3 A ≈ **11.5 ns**

To cover driver limits and layout inductance, use **t_r = t_f = 20 ns** as the design figure.
