**Architectural clarification.** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. The H743 only emits DShot600 commands and decodes the bi-directional eRPM/EDT telemetry. The model below respects that split.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology Insight

The ESC uses six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification and complementary PWM on the active phase. At any instant exactly two MOSFET positions carry the phase current I:

- **Static leg:** the low-side FET of the "sink" phase conducts 100% of the 60° sector.
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.

The PWM leg therefore always has exactly one channel conducting, whatever the duty cycle. The total conduction loss per motor channel is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \;-\; \underbrace{2\,t_{dt} f_{sw} I^2 R_{DS(on)}}_{\text{dead-time window, moved to diode loss}}
$$

The dead-time correction is 2·200 ns·48 kHz = 1.9%. It is small but is kept for consistency with §2.3.

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{\kappa}, \qquad \kappa \approx 2.3 \text{ (40 V trench Si)}
$$

| T_j | Multiplier | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.000 | 1.50 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.677 | 2.52 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.946 | 2.92 mΩ |
| 150 °C | 2.236 | 3.35 mΩ |

### 1.3 80 A Burst Numbers (T_j = 125 °C design point)

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(2.92\times10^{-3})(1-0.019) = 36.7\ \text{W per channel}
$$

**Per-FET loss.** Over one electrical revolution each of the 6 FETs takes an equal share in steady rotation:

- Average per FET: 36.7 / 6 ≈ **6.1 W**.
- Instantaneous peak while a FET is the static low-side: 80²·2.92 mΩ = **18.7 W**.

### 1.4 PCB Copper Conduction (Frequently Forgotten)

Phase and bus path resistance (pads, vias, 2 oz pours, ~12 mm effective path) is assumed at R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ round trip per channel, with α_Cu = 0.00393 /K.

$$
P_{Cu,ch} = I^2 R_{Cu}(T) = 6400 \times 0.4\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1+0.00393\cdot 75) \approx 3.3\ \text{W}
$$

The current shunt (0.25 mΩ, on the common bus) carries the battery current I_bat = D·I_ph, not the phase current. Shunt loss is covered in §4.
