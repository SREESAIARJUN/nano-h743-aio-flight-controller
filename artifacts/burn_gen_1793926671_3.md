## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz, FPU, CORDIC |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs $p=7$), $R_s\approx 5.6\,\Omega$, $L_s\approx 2.1$ mH, $K_t\approx 0.09$ N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701/AS5048A) on each axis, SPI |
| IMU0 | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate. This is the control IMU. |
| IMU1 | ICM-42688-P on the base/handle arm. This is the disturbance reference IMU. |
| Kinematic chain | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |
| Current loop | 20 kHz, ISR synchronized to the PWM center |
| Velocity loop | 4 kHz |
| Angle loop + attitude filter | 1 kHz |
| IMU ODR | 8 kHz gyro, 1 kHz accel. Read from the FIFO with timestamps. |

**Rationale for two IMUs.** IMU0 measures what you want to stabilize. IMU1 measures the disturbance before it passes through the joints. IMU1 serves three purposes:
- It provides a clean reference for **identifying vibration frequencies**. The base side is not attenuated by the control loop.
- It provides **base-rate feedforward** into the velocity loops.
- It allows a **consistency/fault check** between the two IMUs and the encoders.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

$\theta_{\text{off}}$ is found at commissioning by applying $V_d$ at $\theta_e=0$ and recording $\theta_m$. To compensate for SPI latency $T_{lat}$:

$$
\theta_e \leftarrow \theta_e + p\,\hat\omega_m\,(T_{lat} + 1.5\,T_{pwm})
$$

The $1.5\,T_{pwm}$ term covers one sample of computation delay plus the half-period PWM averaging delay.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Phase currents are measured with two shunts, and $i_c=-i_a-i_b$.

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant and current PI with decoupling

The PMSM model in the dq frame is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The PI controller cancels the motor's electrical pole $R/L$ and sets the closed-loop bandwidth to $\omega_c$, a first-order response:

$$
K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}
$$

The total controller output is the PI term plus decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Here $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}K_t/p$, using the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform. The reference $i_d^*=0$ for this surface PMSM.

### 1.4 Voltage limiting

The voltage vector is limited to a circle so that q-axis torque is prioritized:

$$
V_{max} = \tfrac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95
$$

$$
v_d^* \leftarrow \text{sat}(v_d^*, V_{max}),\qquad
v_q^* \leftarrow \text{sat}\!\left(v_q^*, \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}}\right)
$$
