## 1. Mathematical Design

### 1.1 FOC Current Loop (innermost, 20 kHz)

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**PMSM dq model:**

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Gain selection by pole-zero cancellation.** Choose Ki/Kp = R/L so the PI zero cancels the electrical pole. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{PWM}}{10}
$$

At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s.

**Voltage limiting and anti-windup.** The voltage vector is limited to the circle |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3 (the SVPWM linear region). The q-axis receives priority after d. The excess is fed back to the integrator using back-calculation (Sec. 1.3).

**SVPWM via min-max zero-sequence injection:**

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{inverse Clarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\qquad
v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max v_x + \min v_x\right),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

**Voltage mode (no shunts).** Set i_d* = 0 and use:

$$
v_q = R\,i_q^* + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
