## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R_s ≈ 5–12 Ω, L_s ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Driver | 3-phase, inline or low-side current sense, V_bus = 12–16 V |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic absolute (AS5048A / MT6701 class), used for commutation |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G4/H7 class), FPU, single precision |

**Loop rates and target bandwidths** (each loop is spaced ≥ 5× from the next):

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop BW | Feedback source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q) | 20 kHz | ~1.5–2 kHz | Phase currents |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | 60–120 Hz | **Gyro** (inertial rate), encoder PLL as fallback |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | 10–20 Hz | Fused attitude (stabilization) or encoder (follow/lock) |
| IMU fusion | 4 kHz (8 kHz raw, decimated by 2) | — | Dual ICM-42688-P |

Key design point: the encoder is used only for commutation and the cogging map. Stabilization uses inertial rate and attitude. As a result, base and frame motion appear as disturbances that the loops reject, rather than as references they track.
