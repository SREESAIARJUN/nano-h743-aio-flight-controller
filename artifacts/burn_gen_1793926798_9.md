# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #15 specification, so the numbers below are **explicit, configurable assumptions**. The derivations are parametric: substitute your measured motor and inertia values and the gains follow from the formulas.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 (Cortex-M4F, FPU, 170 MHz) |
| Motors | Hollow-shaft gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs) |
| Commutation encoder | 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class), per axis |
| Inverter | 3-shunt or 2-shunt low-side current sense |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mirror-mounted (§4.2), SPI at 24 MHz |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz · IMU and filters 8 kHz · velocity 4 kHz · angle 1 kHz |
