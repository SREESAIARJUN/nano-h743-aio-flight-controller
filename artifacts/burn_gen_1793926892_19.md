## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

| Parameter | Assumed value | Note |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At a design point of T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS}(100) = 1.2 \times [1 + 0.0065 \times 75] = 1.79\ \text{m}\Omega$$
