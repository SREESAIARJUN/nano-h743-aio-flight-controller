## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not run the motors directly.** In any ArduPilot-compatible design, the work is split between two processors:

| Domain | Processor | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, rev V) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Sends bi-directional DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 1× MCU per channel (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, BEMF sensing |

All numbers below are parametric. The example part values are representative of 40 V PDFN3.3 ESC MOSFETs. **Substitute your actual datasheet values before signoff.**

| Symbol | Meaning | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Bus voltage, 6S fully charged | 25.2 V |
| I_ph | Phase burst current, per channel | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds(on),25 | MOSFET on-resistance at V_GS = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ |
| k_T | R_ds(on) multiplier at T_j = 125 °C | 1.6 |
| t_r, t_f | Effective V–I overlap times | 15 ns each |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 60 nC |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse recovery charge (at 80 A, high di/dt) | 40 nC |
| Q_oss | Output charge | 40 nC |
| V_SD | Body-diode forward voltage at 80 A | 0.8 V |
| t_dt | Dead time per edge | 100 ns |
| D | PWM duty at burst | 0.9 |

**Package-level warning.** 80 A per channel × 4 channels on a 20×20 or 30×30 mm board is a **transient (burst) rating only**. Section 3 shows it is limited to roughly 2–3 s by board heat capacity, not by the silicon.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \Rightarrow\ k_T(125^\circ\text{C}) \approx 1.6$$

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ\text{C}) = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current-sharing topology (6-step, synchronous/complementary PWM, AM32-style)

Within each 60° commutation step:

- One leg is PWM'd: its high-side (HS) conducts for D, its low-side (LS) synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- One leg is held LS-on (the current sink).
- One leg floats and is used for BEMF zero-cross sensing.

Each leg spends 120° in each role. Over one electrical cycle, the fraction of time each FET conducts is:

$$\delta_{HS} = \frac{D}{3},\qquad \delta_{LS} = \frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}$$

At every instant, exactly **two** FETs carry I_ph. Ignoring dead time, the per-channel total is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$
