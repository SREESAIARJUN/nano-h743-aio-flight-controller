# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architecture Notes

### 0.1 System partition

ArduPilot on the H743 **does not drive the power MOSFETs directly**. The stack is two subsystems:

| Subsystem | Silicon | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, DShot600 bi-dir master |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 + 4× 3-phase gate drivers + 24 MOSFETs | AM32 (or BLHeli_32) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |

Sections 1–3 model the ESC power stage. Section 4 covers the H743 timer and DMA plan. Section 5 provides the netlists.
