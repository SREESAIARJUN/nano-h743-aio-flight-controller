## 1. System Architecture

### 1.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four motors while also running ArduPilot, because ArduPilot has no in-process ESC firmware. The correct partition is:

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or STM32H743VIT6 | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | EKF, control loops, DShot600 output |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), one per motor | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers ×4 | FD6288Q-class 3-phase, 250 V† | n/a | High- and low-side drive, bootstrap |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN | n/a | 6 FETs per motor |

## 3. Thermal and Loss Model

### 3.1 Conduction losses (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs conduct at any instant.

- During the PWM on-time, current flows through the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- During the off-time with synchronous rectification, current flows through the low-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.

So in both states the current path contains **two R_DS(on) in series**, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_J) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

The hot on-resistance is:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_J) = R_{DS(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_J - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /\text{K}$$

At T_J = 125 °C, R_DS(on) = 1.45 mΩ, so:

$$P_{FET,cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.6\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**PCB copper resistance** (2 oz inner and outer layers, 4 power layers in parallel, phase path ≈ 15 mm long × 5 mm wide):

$$R_{Cu,layer} = \frac{\rho\, l}{w\, t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8} \times 0.015}{0.005 \times 70\times10^{-6}} = 0.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{0.74}{4} \times 1.3_{(hot)} \approx 0.24\ \text{m}\Omega \;\Rightarrow\; P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.24\text{m} = \mathbf{1.5\ W}$$

### 3.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg (the PWM'd phase) hard-switches per commutation interval.

**Overlap (V-I crossover) loss**, worst case at V_bus = 25.2 V:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction:**

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_d \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

**C_oss discharge:**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss} V^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.023\ \text{W}$$

**Gate-drive loss** (two FETs switching per channel, dissipated in the driver and gate resistors):

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 3.3 Loss budget at 80 A burst

| Loss term | Per channel (W) | ×4 channels (W) |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction (hot) | 18.6 | 74.4 |
| PCB copper | 1.5 | 6.0 |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 | 7.8 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 | 2.4 |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.11 | 0.4 |
| **Total** | **22.8** | **≈ 91 W** |
