## 0. Architecture and Engineering Caveats

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** An STM32H743 flight controller drives a 4-in-1 ESC whose motor control runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs. This design assumes AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry.
