# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Model

## 0. Architecture Corrections Before the Math

Two premises in the brief need correcting first, because the rest of the model depends on them.

**1. The H743 should not drive the power stage directly.** ArduPilot has no firmware path for an H7 commutating four motors. The production-viable architecture is:
- **STM32H743VIT6** as the flight controller (ArduPilot), sending **bidirectional DShot600**.
- **Four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32) doing commutation at 48 kHz PWM.
- These can share one PCB ("AIO") or sit in a stack. Either way, the models below assume this split.

**2. 80 A burst per channel on a nano footprint is a short-burst rating only.** The thermal model in §3 shows it is roughly a 3–5 second capability. Continuous operation is in the single-digit-amps-per-channel range. Ratings like "80 A" on small boards are usually peak figures measured on a cold board in strong airflow. I recommend specifying the product as **80 A / 2 s burst, ~25–30 A per-channel short sustained (with prop wash), derated continuous**.

**Assumptions used throughout** (change them and re-run the equations):

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V |
| Board | 36 × 36 mm outline (30.5 mm mounting), 8-layer, 2 oz copper |
| MOSFETs | 40 V N-ch, 5×6 mm (BSC007N04LS6-class): R_DS(on),max = 0.9 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g ≈ 60 nC, Q_gs2 + Q_gd ≈ 12 nC, Q_oss ≈ 60 nC, Q_rr ≈ 80 nC, R_th,JC ≈ 0.9 K/W |
| Gate driver | 3-phase, 12 V gate supply (FD6288-class), R_g,ext = 10 Ω, R_g,int ≈ 1 Ω, V_plateau ≈ 3 V |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous (complementary) PWM, f_sw = 48 kHz, dead time 150 ns |

All component values are representative. Verify every one against the actual datasheets before layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design-point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 0.9 \times (1 + 0.005 \times 85) = 1.28\ \text{m}\Omega$$
