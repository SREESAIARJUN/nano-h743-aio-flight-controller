## 0. Architecture Correction and Design Basis

**The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller.** It is the flight controller MCU. Each of the four motor channels needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. This document therefore covers:

| Block | Device (reference class) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 | ArduPilot, emits bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q-class three-phase bootstrap driver | ~1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink |
| MOSFETs ×24 | 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 PDFN (or 12× dual) | 3 half-bridges per channel |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm holes, ~26×26 mm outline, 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner | Nano class |

**Electrical design point:** 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V, V_bus,nom = 22.2 V. 40 V FETs leave only about 1.6× margin, so the snubber and decoupling analysis in §5 is mandatory.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM. Within any 60° electrical sector:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high side conducts for fraction D. The low side conducts for (1 − D) as the synchronous rectifier. The body diode conducts only during dead time.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low side is on for 100% of the sector.
- **Leg C:** floating (BEMF sensing).

Phase current I_ph is approximately constant over a PWM period, because L_motor/R_motor ≫ 1/f_sw.
