## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight-controller MCU running ArduPilot. It does not drive the power stage. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU, for example AM32 or BLHeli_32 on an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and decodes bidirectional DShot600 from the H743. The design splits cleanly into three parts:

1. **H743 domain:** DShot600 generation, eRPM capture, DMA, and timer allocation.
2. **ESC domain:** 48 kHz six-step synchronous PWM, conduction and switching losses.
3. **Board domain:** thermal mass, heat spreading, and burst duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current distribution in six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

Over one electrical cycle, each phase conducts for 2/3 of the time and floats for 1/3.

- **High-side FET:** conducts for 1/3 of the cycle at duty D.
- **Low-side FET of the same leg:**
  - conducts for 1/3 of the cycle as the return path, at 100%;
  - conducts for 1/3 × (1 − D) during synchronous freewheeling.

The RMS currents per FET are:

$$I_{rms,HS} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}}, \qquad I_{rms,LS} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1-D}{3}}$$

At burst, D → 1. Both FETs then carry:

$$I_{rms} = 80\sqrt{1/3} = 46.2\ \text{A}$$

The low-side FET is the worst case at partial throttle. At D = 0.5 it reaches I_rms,LS = 80·√0.5 = 56.6 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.
