## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical model

The electrical angle is derived from the mechanical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0})$$

where *p* is the number of pole pairs and θ₀ is the encoder-to-rotor alignment offset.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Machine model in the dq frame.** Here ω_e = p·ω_m, and the surface PM motor has L_d = L_q = L:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda$$

Torque is linear in i_q:

$$\tau_m = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.2 Current PI controller with decoupling

Cancel the R/L pole with the PI zero (pole-zero cancellation). This yields first-order closed-loop dynamics with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

Here ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s. This requires ω_c·T_s ≪ 1 (here 0.47).

The control law adds decoupling and back-EMF feedforward to the PI output:

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

The current setpoints are i_d* = 0 and i_q* from the velocity loop plus the cogging feedforward.
