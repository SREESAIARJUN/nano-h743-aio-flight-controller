> - **Hardware:** STM32G4-class MCU with FPU, 3× gimbal BLDC (12N14P, e.g. GBM2804H-100T), 14-bit absolute magnetic encoders (MT6701/AS5048A), inline/low-side shunts on 2 phases, and 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate.
