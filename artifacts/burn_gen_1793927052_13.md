## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step synchronous operation, two phases carry current at any instant.
- **On-time (D):** High-side FET of phase A, then the motor, then the low-side FET of phase B.
- **Off-time (1−D):** Synchronous freewheel through the low-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B. Phase A's high side is complementary-switched, so the low side conducts instead of the body diode.

The current therefore always flows through **exactly two channels in series**, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Per individual FET, averaged over a full electrical cycle, each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the period:

$$
P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot D\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2, \quad
P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\left[(1-D)\tfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2 + \tfrac{1}{3}\right]
$$

For hotspot sizing, use the **instantaneous worst-case FET**: one device carrying the full 80 A within its 120° window. Its time constant (ms range) is longer than one commutation interval at low eRPM.
