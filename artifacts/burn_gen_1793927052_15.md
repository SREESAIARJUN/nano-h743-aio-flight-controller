# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #44): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Allocation, and Thermal Analysis

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three points change the shape of the design, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 generates DShot. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz PWM frequency is an ESC-firmware setting, not an H743 timer setting. This document treats the board as an **AIO: H743 FC plus 4× ESC MCUs plus 24 MOSFETs on one PCB.**
2. **80 A on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not physically achievable** except for sub-second bursts. Section 4 quantifies this. I recommend a 30.5×30.5 mm board as a minimum, with 80 A defined as a ≤1 s per-motor burst.
3. **All component parameters below are design assumptions.** Replace them with values from the specific datasheets you select before releasing the design.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step trapezoidal drive, two phases conduct at any instant:
- **PWM leg:** current alternates between the high side (duty D) and the low side (1−D) under synchronous rectification.
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts continuously.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

**PCB copper loss.** A 2 oz (70 µm) layer has a sheet resistance of ρ/t = 1.72×10⁻⁸ / 70×10⁻⁶ = 0.246 mΩ/□.
- Assume a phase path of about 3 squares, paralleled on 4 layers: 0.18 mΩ per path, two paths active.
- P_Cu = 2 × 6400 × 0.18 mΩ ≈ **2.3 W/channel**.
- Add about 0.5 W for the shared battery-bus share.

Because R_ds rises with temperature, loss rises with temperature. This positive feedback is closed in Section 4.
