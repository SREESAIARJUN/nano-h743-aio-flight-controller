# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #33): Engineering Model

> **Scope note, read first.** I don't have Job #33's BOM, stack-up, or mechanical drawing, so every number below comes from a **stated assumption** (Section 0). Each one is a parameter you can replace with your actual values; the equations stay the same.
>
> **Two findings change the design direction:**
> 1. **The H743 should not drive the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. The correct architecture is H743 (ArduPilot) → bidirectional DShot600 → 4× dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Everything below assumes this.
> 2. **"80A burst" and "nano" form factor conflict thermally.** The model shows a 20×20 mm board survives 80A on all four channels for only about 1–2 s. The numbers are in Section 3.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step synchronous PWM, two phases are active at any instant:
- The **PWM leg** alternates HS (duty D) and LS (1 − D, synchronous rectification). One FET is always conducting.
- The **return leg** has its LS on continuously.

So **exactly two MOSFETs carry the full phase current at all times**, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$
