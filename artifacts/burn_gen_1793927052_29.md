# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #5)

## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

**Architecture.** I don't have the Job #5 requirement docs, so this derivation rests on stated assumptions. The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. In every practical 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 that performs the 6-step commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. If Job #5 actually calls for the H743 to commutate four motors itself, the timer section changes completely, because that would need 24 complementary PWM outputs. Tell me and I'll redo it.
