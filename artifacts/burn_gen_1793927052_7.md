# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture (Job #15)

## 0. Architecture Note and Assumptions

**The H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges.** ArduPilot on the H743 is the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM and handle commutation. The netlist and timer allocation below follow that partitioning.

**All MOSFET parameters are representative values for a 40 V, 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 class device.** Substitute the datasheet values of the selected part before release. Every equation is written so you can do this directly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths
In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM, as AM32 and BLHeli_32 use), current at any instant flows through exactly two MOSFET channels:

- the PWM'd leg: high side during D, low side during (1–D);
- the static low-side leg of the return phase.

The duty cycle therefore cancels out of total conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2 \, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow k_T \approx 1.5\ \text{at}\ 100\,°\text{C}$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction
For PWM ripple ΔI_pp:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

The ripple is:

$$\Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{2 L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

where L_ph is phase inductance and the factor 2 covers two windings in series. A typical 2207 motor has L_ph ≈ 15 µH. At D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{2 \times 15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 4.4\ \text{A}$$

The added term is 4.4²/12 = 1.6 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². **The 48 kHz frequency is well chosen for ripple.**

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times (0.70 \times 1.5)\,\text{m}\Omega = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.44\ W\ per\ motor}$$

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution
Each FET conducts for roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The average conduction loss per FET is:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = \frac{I^2 R}{3} = 2.24\ \text{W}$$

While a FET conducts, its instantaneous loss is 6.72 W. This peak matters for the junction ripple at low eRPM; see Section 3.4.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Each PWM period contains one turn-on and one turn-off of the hard-switched FET.

| Loss term | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r+t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
