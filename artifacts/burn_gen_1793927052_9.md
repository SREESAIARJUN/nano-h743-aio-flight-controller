## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)}(110) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045\times85) = 1.38\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Conduction topology (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

During any commutation step, two phases carry current. Phase A is PWM-switched (HS_A on for D, LS_A on for 1−D) and phase B has LS_B held on for the full period.

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[\underbrace{D}_{HS_A} + \underbrace{(1-D)}_{LS_A\ \text{sync}} + \underbrace{1}_{LS_B}] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle** because synchronous rectification means some MOSFET is always carrying the current.

### 1.3 Results at 80 A

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.38\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.7\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 4 \times 17.7 = \mathbf{70.7\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-device averaging

Commutation rotates through 6 steps per electrical cycle. At flight RPM the electrical frequency is far above the thermal bandwidth of the die, so the loss averages across all six devices:

$$P_{FET,avg} = 17.7 / 6 \approx 2.95\ \text{W}$$

### 1.5 Copper (PCB) loss, not optional at 80 A

Assume a 2 oz copper path of 10 mm length, 6 mm width, 2 layers in parallel:

- R_sheet(2 oz) ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, rising to 0.33 mΩ/□ hot.
- Squares = 10/6 = 1.67 → R ≈ 0.55 mΩ per layer → 0.275 mΩ for 2 layers.
- Two conducting paths per motor: P_Cu = 2 × 80² × 0.275 m ≈ **3.5 W per motor**.

This is comparable to one MOSFET's loss, so the power stage needs stitched multi-layer pours and the minimum possible phase-path length.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg (phase A) switches. Phase B is static, so it contributes only conduction loss.

### 2.1 Transition time from gate charge

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,src}},\quad I_{g,src}\approx \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_g}$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_plateau ≈ 4 V, R_g = 4.7 Ω → I_g ≈ 1.27 A:
- t_r ≈ 8 nC / 1.27
