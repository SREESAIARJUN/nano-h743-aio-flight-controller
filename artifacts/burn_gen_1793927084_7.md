**Scope and honesty note:** I don't have access to the Job #46 requirements package, BOM, or layout. Everything below is derived from first principles using **stated, typical-class parameters**. ## 0. Architecture Decision (Read First)

A single STM32H743 **cannot cleanly drive four 6-PWM inverter bridges by itself**:

| Resource | Complementary output pairs |
|---|---|
| TIM1 (CH1–3 + CH1N–3N) | 3 |
| TIM8 (CH1–3 + CH1N–3N) | 3 |
| HRTIM (5 timer units × 2 outputs) | 5 (with dead-time) |
| **Total available** | ~11 |
| **Required (4 motors × 3 phases)** | **12, plus 4 independent ADC trigger domains** |

The timer budget falls short, and the H743 would also be carrying real-time commutation for four motors on top of ArduPilot. The adopted architecture is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC) ──DShot600 bidir ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class)
                                                    └─► 3-ph gate driver ─► 6 MOSFETs ─► motor
```

All power-stage math below applies per ESC channel. All DMA math applies to the H743.
