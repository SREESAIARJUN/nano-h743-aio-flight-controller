## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

These points shape every number below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel runs its own MCU (this design uses AT32F421 running AM32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600. The loss model applies to the ESC board, and the DMA plan applies to the FC.
2. **"80 A burst" must be defined.** 80 A per channel on all four channels at once means about 320 A of battery current. That is far beyond what a nano-format board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm), an XT60 connector, or 14 AWG leads can carry. I model two cases:
   - **Case A:** one channel at 80 A, the other three at 20 A. This is the realistic burst.
   - **Case B:** all four at 80 A. This is the pathological limit, used to prove the board cannot sustain it.
