## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Per-commutation-state analysis
At any instant in 6-step operation, one phase is PWM-high, one is held low, and one floats. With synchronous rectification at duty D:

- **PWM'd phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The low-side FET conducts for (1−D), via synchronous rectification.
- **Low-held phase:** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the time.

The instantaneous conduction loss of the bridge is:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = I^2R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**. Adding the copper path:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2I^2\left[R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu}\right]
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (fixed-point iteration)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,(R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta CB})
$$

Iterate the two equations until T_j changes by less than 1 K. Convergence fails (thermal runaway) when:

$$
\frac{\partial P_{FET}}{\partial T_j}\,R_{\theta} \geq 1
\quad\Leftrightarrow\quad
\bar{I}_{FET}^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_\theta \geq 1
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, with T_j ≈ 100 °C

R_DS(100 °C) = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.0065 × 75) = **1.79 mΩ**

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction, per motor | 2 × 80² × 1.79 mΩ | **22.9 W** |
| Copper, per motor | 2 × 80² × 0.5 mΩ | **6.4 W** |
| Instantaneous loss in one FET (low-held phase) | 80² × 1.79 mΩ | **11.5 W** |
| Electrical-cycle average per FET (6 FETs share the load) | 22.9 / 6 | **3.8 W** |

The averaging is only valid when the electrical period is much shorter than the die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms).
