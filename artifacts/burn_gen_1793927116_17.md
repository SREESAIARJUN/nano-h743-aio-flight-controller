## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Several requirements constrain each other, so these points are fixed up front.

### 0.1 The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors

ArduPilot runs on the H743 and outputs DShot. It has no sensorless 6-step or FOC commutation layer for 12 half-bridges. The architecture is therefore:

- **H743:** ArduPilot. Generates bi-directional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **4 × ESC MCUs:** AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071), running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM.
- **4 × three-phase gate drivers and 24 MOSFETs:** 2 FETs in parallel per switch position.

### 0.2 An "80 A burst" on a nano-format board is a transient rating only

As derived in §3, 80 A on all four channels at once dissipates about 48–88 W. Copper at nano scale (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) cannot sink that in steady state. The 80 A rating is valid as follows:

| Condition | Validity |
|---|---|
| Per channel | Valid |
| Duration | ≤ about 2–5 s |
| Thermal state | Starting from a thermally soaked board at ≤ 60 °C |
| Airflow | Requires prop-wash airflow |

Four channels at 80 A means **320 A from the battery**. That requires:

- XT60 at its limit (XT90 preferred)
- 10 AWG leads
- A battery capable of 320 A

This is a **system-level** constraint, not just a board constraint.

### 0.3 Operating point used throughout

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 24 V (25.2 V max) |
| f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, 6-step trapezoidal |
| MOSFET (per switch position) | 2× 40 V DFN3.3×3.3, R_DS(on),25 = 1.0 mΩ each @ V_GS = 10 V |
| Q_g = 50 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V | |
| Gate drive | 10 V, R_g,total ≈ 5 Ω (driver 2 Ω + external 2.2 Ω + internal ~1 Ω) |
| Dead time | t_dt = 100 ns |
| Body-diode V_F | 0.8 V |
