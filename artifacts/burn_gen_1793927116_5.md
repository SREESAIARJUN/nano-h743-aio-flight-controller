## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any 60° sector, two phases are active (for example A+ and B−):

- **Leg B:** the low-side FET is on continuously. It carries I.
- **Leg A:** it PWMs at duty D.
  - The high-side FET carries I for a fraction D of the period.
  - The synchronous low-side FET carries I for (1 − D) of the period, less dead time.

So at every instant, motor current flows through exactly **two channels** (MOSFETs or diodes):

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

| T_j | Factor | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.60 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.45 | 2.32 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.60 | 2.56 mΩ |

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

The path runs from the bus pad, through the planes, to the motor pad.

- R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ total for 2 oz and short paths.
- Copper heats at about 0.39 %/K, giving roughly 0.5 mΩ at 100 °C.
