> **Engineering flag (read before layout):** On a 20×20 board, 4 × 80 A simultaneous is a **~1-second adiabatic event**, not a rating. It also implies **320 A at the battery**, which exceeds XT60 (60 A continuous) and practical shunt and lead budgets. Section 2.6 derives this. Recommendation: either market it as "80 A per-motor single-channel burst ≤ 1 s, 4×45 A continuous-with-airflow," or move to 30.5×30.5 mm with 5×6 mm FETs.

## 1. Inverter Topology and Current Paths

Each motor runs a 3-phase bridge (6 FETs). With trapezoidal 6-step commutation and complementary PWM, at any instant during a commutation step:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** HS on for D·T_sw, LS on for (1−D)·T_sw. Synchronous rectification means the current always flows through exactly one FET of this leg, except during dead time.
- **Phase B (return leg):** LS static ON.
- **Phase C:** floating (BEMF sense).

**Result: phase current I flows through exactly 2 channel resistances at all times, independent of D.** The body diode carries current only during dead time.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor, at 80 A)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.5 mΩ × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ**

### 2.2 Conduction loss

Include the inductor ripple (ΔI ≈ 8 A pk-pk is typical for low-L racing motors at 48 kHz):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2+\frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 5.3 \approx 6405\ \text{A}^2$$

$$P_{cond}=2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)=2(6405)(2.25\text{ m}\Omega)= \mathbf{28.8\ W}$$

(At a cold start, T_j = 25 °C: 19.2 W. The model is electrothermally coupled; see §3.4.)

### 2.3 Switching loss (hard-switched PWM leg)

Edge time is dominated by the Miller plateau: t ≈ Q_gd / I_g ≈ 8 nC / 1.2 A ≈ 7 ns. Adding the V_th traverse and loop inductance, a realistic value is t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 15 ns.

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(30\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.4 Reverse recovery, C_oss, dead time, gate drive

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | P_rr = Q_rr · V · f = 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.036 W |
| Output capacitance | P_oss ≈ Q_oss · V · f (both FETs of leg) | 0.030 W |
| Dead time (body diode) | P_dt = V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f = 0.8 × 80 × 400 ns × 48 k | **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (in driver/LDO) | P_g = 2 Q_g V_GS f = 2 × 40 n × 10 × 48 k | 0.038 W |

> **Design lever:** Dead-time loss is about the same as switching loss. Dropping dead time to 100 ns (AM32 allows tuning; verify shoot-through margin on a scope) saves ~0.6 W per motor.
