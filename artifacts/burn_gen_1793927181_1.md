## 0. Assumed System Architecture

| Item | Assumption (change as needed) |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 170+ MHz (CORDIC optional) |
| Motors | Hollow-shaft gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–4 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (MT6701 / AS5048A) per axis, SPI |
| Current sense | 2- or 3-shunt low-side, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, gyro + accel ODR 8 kHz, on the camera plate |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz → Velocity 4 kHz → Angle 1 kHz → Attitude 1 kHz |

**Control topology per axis:**

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle P + FF]──ω_ref──►[Velocity PI]──iq_ref──►[+ cogging FF]──►[Current PI + decoupling]──►SVPWM
              ▲ attitude (fused IMU)        ▲ inertial rate (fused gyro)            ▲ id,iq (shunts)
                                                                                    ▲ θ_e (encoder)
```

The encoder is used only for commutation and the cogging-map phase. Stabilization feedback comes from the IMUs, because the camera's inertial rate is what you want to hold at zero.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle with latency compensation

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) + p\,\hat\omega_m T_d, \qquad T_d \approx T_{\text{SPI}} + \tfrac{1}{2}T_{\text{PWM}}
$$

Here θ_off is found at calibration by applying a d-axis voltage and reading the rest angle.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q,\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{K_t}{1.5\,p}
$$

### 1.4 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

Choose the current bandwidth ω_c (for example 2π·1.5 kHz, at most about f_PWM/10):

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

Pole-zero cancellation of R/L then gives the closed loop $i/i^* = \omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

**Voltage-mode fallback.** High-R gimbal motors often lack good current sensing. In that case, use $v_q = R\,i_q^* + \omega_e\lambda_m$, which is open-loop current control. The rest of the cascade is unchanged.

### 1.5 Voltage limit and SVPWM (min-max injection)

**Step 1: circular limit.**

$$
|v| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{\max}
$$

Keep v_d and scale v_q (d-axis priority).

**Step 2: inverse Park and inverse Clarke.**

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

**Step 3: zero-sequence injection.**

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$
