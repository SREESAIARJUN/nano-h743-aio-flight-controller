## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

With electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

**Clarke (power-invariant form not needed; amplitude-invariant used):**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park:**
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

The decoupling and back-EMF feedforward terms are:
$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$$

### 1.3 PI design by pole-zero cancellation

Choose the current-loop bandwidth ω_c (here 2π·1.5 kHz, which is below f_PWM/10). The PI zero cancels the electrical pole R/L:
$$K_p^{i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i}=R\,\omega_c$$

The closed loop is then first order, i/i* = ω_c/(s+ω_c).

**Discrete form (Tustin integrator) with back-calculation anti-windup:**
$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad u^{sat}_k=\mathrm{sat}(u_k),\quad I_{k+1}=I_k + K_iT_s e_k + K_{aw}(u^{sat}_k-u_k)$$

with K_aw = 1/K_p.

**Voltage vector limit (circular):**
$$|v_{dq}|\le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$$
The d-axis has priority.

### 1.4 SVPWM by min-max injection

$$v_{a,b,c}=\text{inv-Clarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad v_0=-\tfrac12(\max+\min),\quad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.5 Delay compensation

The computation plus PWM update delay is about 1.5·T_s. Advance the angle used in the inverse Park transform:
$$\theta_e^{inv}=\theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle/Velocity Control

### 2.1 Mechanical plant (per axis)

$$J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - B\omega - \tau_c\,\mathrm{sgn}(\omega) - \tau_{dist}$$

### 2.2 Velocity loop (PI with setpoint weighting and 2nd-order output LPF)

**Feedback signal.** For stabilization, the velocity feedback is the inertial camera rate projected onto the motor axis (Section 4.6), ω_fb = **a**_jᵀ **ω**_cam. This is not the encoder derivative. The encoder rate is only used during calibration and when the IMU has faulted.

**Gains for target bandwidth ω_v (≈ 2π·80 Hz, i.e. 1/15–1/20 of ω_c):**
$$K_p^{v}=\frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_i^{v}=K_p^{v}\frac{\omega_v}{4}$$
The integral zero sits at ω_v/4, which gives about 60° phase margin.

**Control law:**
$$i_q^* = K_p^{v}(\beta\,\omega^* - \omega_{fb}) + K_i^{v}\!\int(\omega^*-\omega_{fb})\,dt + \underbrace{\tfrac{J}{K_t}\dot\omega^*}_{\text{accel FF}} + \underbrace{i_{cog}(\theta)}_{\text{§3}} + \underbrace{\tfrac{B\omega^*+\tau_c\,\mathrm{sgn}_\epsilon(\omega^*)}{K_t}}_{\text{friction FF}}$$

- β ∈ [0.6, 1] is the setpoint weight. It reduces overshoot without changing disturbance
