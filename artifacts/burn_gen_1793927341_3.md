# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #41): Engineering Model

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points change the design, so they come first:

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production AIO design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU that runs commutation and drives the gate drivers. Typical ESC MCUs are the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not by the H743.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano footprint is limited by thermal mass and duration, not by FET current rating.** The model below shows that 4 × 80 A simultaneously lasts about 2–3 s at most.
3. **The netlist below is a reference template, not a verified production netlist.** Pin assignments must be checked against the H743 datasheet (DS12110), the chosen FET and gate-driver datasheets, and the ArduPilot `hwdef` DMA resolver output.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.2 \times (1 + 0.005 \times 75) = 1.65\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

With trapezoidal commutation and synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two FET positions in series: one high-side leg and one low-side leg. Synchronous rectification means the complementary FET carries current during the PWM off-time, so the duty cycle cancels out:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.65\times10^{-3} = 21.1\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET loss

Each FET conducts for 120° of each 360° electrical cycle. The thermally relevant figures are:

- **Average per FET:** P ≈ 21.1 / 6 ≈ 3.5 W.
- **Instantaneous during conduction:** P ≈ 10.5 W.

At high eRPM the conduction window is much shorter than the FET's thermal time constant (milliseconds versus tens of milliseconds). The junction therefore follows the *average* power, with a small ripple on top.

### 1.4 Electrothermal feedback

R_ds rises with T_j, so solve for equilibrium:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + R_{\theta,jp}\,\frac{2 I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]}{6}$$

Runaway is impossible when:

$$R_{\theta,jp}\,\frac{I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}{3} < 1$$

At 80 A: 1.5 × 6400 × 1.2e-3 × 0.005 / 3 = 0.019, far below 1. The FET junctions are stable. The board is the limiting element (Section 4).

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-active leg switches hard. The synchronous FET soft-switches because its body diode is already conducting.

| Loss term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 400 ns × 48 k | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.036 W |
| C_oss (C_oss,er ≈ 1 nF) | ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw | 0.015 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw | 0.029 W (dissipated in the driver) |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 3.25 W per channel** |

**Design levers:**

- Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.6 W per channel but increases current ripple and motor noise.
- Dead-time loss is the second-largest term. Trimming t_dt from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. This is only safe if the gate driver's propagation-delay mismatch allows it.

## 3. Total Loss Budget at Burst

| Source | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 21.1 W | 84.4 W |
| FET switching and dead time | 3.25 W | 13.0 W |
| Current shunt (0.5 mΩ × 80²) | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| PCB copper (≈ 0.3 mΩ effective path) | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 29.5 W** | **≈ 118 W** |

Efficiency check: input power = 4 × 80 A × 25.2 V × D. At D = 1, output ≈ 8 kW, so the ESC is about 98.5% efficient. This is efficient electrically, but 118 W is enormous for a roughly 25 × 25 mm board.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Lumped RC network

```
P_total ──► [Rθ_jp ≈ 1.5 K/W per FET] ──► PCB node (C_th) ──► [Rθ_sa] ──► T_amb
                                                 │
                                                 └──► [Rθ_frame] ──► stack/frame (via standoffs)
```

### 4.2 Parameters

- **Board area (both sides):** ≈ 2 × 25 × 25 mm = 1.25 × 10⁻³ m².
- **Propwash convection coefficient:** h ≈ 80 W/m²K.
- **Board-to-air thermal resistance:** R_θ,sa = 1/(hA) ≈ 10 K/W.
- **Heat capacity:** an 8-layer, 2 oz board plus FETs at ≈ 4 g gives C_th ≈ 4 J/K.
- **Thermal time constant:** τ = R·C ≈ 40 s.

### 4.3 Steady state

ΔT = 118 × 10 = 1180 K. Continuous operation at 80 A on all four channels is physically impossible on this board.

### 4.4 Burst (adiabatic regime, t ≪ τ)

$$\frac{dT}{dt} \approx \frac{P}{C_{th}} = \frac{118}{4} \approx 30\ \text{K/s}$$

**Time from 45 °C to a 110 °C PCB limit (T_j ≈ 115 °C):**

| Load case | Power | Time to limit |
|---|---|---|
| All 4 channels at 80 A | 118 W | **≈ 2.2 s** |
| 1 channel at 80 A, others at 20 A | ≈ 37 W | ≈ 7 s |

### 4.5 Full transient solution (for firmware derating)

$$T_{pcb}(t) = T_{amb} + P\,R_{\theta,sa}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right) + \left(T_0 - T_{amb}\right)e^{-t/\tau}$$
