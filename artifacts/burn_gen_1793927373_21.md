# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Electrothermal Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlist

This is an engineering baseline, not a release package. Every device parameter is a stated placeholder. Re-run the numbers with the datasheet values of the parts you actually select. The two most important findings come first.

---

## 0. Findings and Architecture

1. **80 A per motor on a nano board is a burst rating of about 2 s, not a continuous rating.** The thermal model in Section 3 shows that all four channels at 80 A dissipate about 140 W. A board of about 30×30 mm has about 5 J/K of heat capacity, so it heats at roughly 25–30 K/s. Sustainable continuous current is about 20–25 A per motor in prop-wash airflow.
2. **The H743 should not drive the bridges directly.** Twelve half-bridges need 12 complementary PWM pairs with dead time. The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 (3 complementary pairs each) plus HRTIM. That cannot cleanly cover 12 legs at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot.
   - **Baseline:** the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600.
   - **ESC side:** four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071, running AM32) each drive one FD6288Q-class 3-phase gate driver.
   - Everything below assumes this split.
