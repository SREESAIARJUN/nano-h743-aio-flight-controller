# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture (Job #49)

## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

The H743 should not commutate the motors. ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four independent ESC MCUs that run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Making the H743 do 4-motor sensorless commutation is outside ArduPilot's scope and would wreck timing determinism.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path

With synchronous rectification and 6-step drive, two phases conduct at any instant:

- **PWM on-time:** HS(A) + LS(B)
- **PWM off-time:** LS(A) + LS(B), freewheeling

The series path is always two FETs, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 3.0\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET split

Each FET position conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period. At burst throttle (D → 1), the high-side FET carries nearly all of its sector's on-time:

$$P_{cond,HS} \approx I^2 R_{DS}\cdot \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot D + \ldots \approx 80^2(1.5\text{m})(\tfrac13) = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

The low-side FET of the active return phase carries the same current for the full sector. Its average is also about 3.2 W.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (not negligible)

**Board bus.** For 2 oz copper, R_sq ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□ at 25 °C. A 6 mm × 10 mm path is about 1.7 squares, or about 0.42 mΩ per layer. With 4 parallel layers, R ≈ 0.11 mΩ, so P ≈ 80² × 0.11 m ≈ **0.7 W per channel**.

**Single battery shunt** (0.1 mΩ) at 4 × 80 A = 320 A:

$$P = 320^2 \times 10^{-4} = \mathbf{10.2\ W}$$

Recommendations:
- Use 0.05–0.1 mΩ with Kelvin sensing, or
- Use per-channel low-side sense, or
- Use a Hall sensor such as the ACS772 class.
