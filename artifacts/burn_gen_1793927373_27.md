## 0. Architecture Decisions and a Feasibility Flag

### 0.1 Partitioning
The H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel gets its own ESC MCU running AM32 (STM32G071GBU6). This is required for bi-directional DShot, because the eRPM telemetry is generated ESC-side.

| Block | Part (assumed) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | STM32H743VIH6 (VFBGA100, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot FC |
| U2–U5 | STM32G071GBU6 (UFQFPN28) | AM32 ESC MCU ×4 |
| U6–U9 | FD6288Q (QFN24) | 3-phase gate driver ×4 |
| Q1–Q24 | 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 6 per channel |
| U10 | ICM-42688-P | IMU, SPI1 |
| U11 | DPS310 | Baro, I²C2 |
| U12 | W25N01G | Flash, SPI4 / QSPI |

### 0.2 Feasibility flag: read before using the numbers
On a 20×20 mm board, **80 A per motor on all four channels simultaneously is not sustainable**. That condition dissipates over 130 W into roughly 2 J/K of board thermal mass (derived in §3).

The defensible rating is:
- **80 A single-channel burst, ≤1 s**, or
- **4 × 80 A for ≤ ~0.5 s**, with a firmware current limit enforcing it.

The marketing spec should state burst duration and duty cycle explicitly.

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step drive, each electrical sector (60°) has one high-side FET of phase *x* and one low-side FET of phase *y* carrying I_ph. Each FET conducts for 120° of 360°, so the conduction fraction is k = 1/3.

With complementary PWM at duty D on the active high side:
- The active high-side FET conducts for D.
- Its synchronous low-side partner conducts for (1 − D).
- The static low-side FET on the return phase conducts 100% of the sector.

Per-FET averages over one electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
$$P_{cond,LS,sync} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
$$P_{cond,LS,static} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Because each low-side FET serves both roles in different sectors, both terms apply to it.

**Instantaneous conduction loss per channel** (always two FET-equivalents in the path):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}$$

At 80 A and 2.4 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0024 = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

**Hottest single FET** (at D → 1):

$$P = \tfrac{1}{3}\times 15.36 = \mathbf{5.1\ W}$$

**Copper path loss.** Assume phase/bus pours of 3 mm × 10 mm on four 2 oz (70 µm) layers in parallel.

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{wt\,n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times0.01}{0.003\times70\times10^{-6}\times4} \approx 0.20\ \text{m}\Omega$$

The battery path is in series with the phase path, so count roughly 2× this resistance:

$$P_{Cu,ch} \approx 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0002 \approx \mathbf{2.6\ W}$$

This term is significant and is often forgotten.
