## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Feasibility Verdict

**Topology clarification.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel uses its own small motor-control MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware). Bidirectional DShot exists only because a separate MCU decodes the command and returns eRPM. Running four sensorless commutation loops on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not a supported configuration.

**Verdict on 80 A burst in a nano footprint.** The model below shows that 80 A per channel (phase current, all four motors simultaneously) is **not thermally sustainable** on a 20×20 mm board under any MOSFET choice. On a 30.5×30.5 mm board it is achievable only as a **time-limited burst of about 1–3 s**, and only with paralleled FETs. The numbers are carried through so the trade space is explicit. Section 3.5 gives the design changes required to make the spec honest.
