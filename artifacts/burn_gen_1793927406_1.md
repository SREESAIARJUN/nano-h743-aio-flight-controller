# STM32H743 Nano AIO (4-in-1 ESC + FC): Power-Stage Thermal Model and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Architecture

**Design review document, Job #21.** Status: preliminary architecture. All component parameters are stated assumptions. Recheck them against the final BOM datasheets before layout freeze.

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Headline Finding

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. A dedicated ESC MCU drives the power stage. The baseline is an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32.

- 48 kHz PWM is an ESC-firmware setting.
- Bi-directional DShot600 is the link between the H743 and the ESC MCUs.

**Headline finding (read this first).** On a nano form factor (~26 × 26 mm, 20 × 20 mounting), an "80 A" per-motor rating is thermally achievable only as a burst of about 1–2 seconds. The model in §2 gives:

| Condition | Total power-stage dissipation | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 80 A × 4 motors | ≈ 130 W | Board heats at ≈ 25 K/s |
| Continuous, prop-wash cooled | — | ≈ 20 A per motor |

**Recommended label:** "20 A continuous / 80 A burst (≤1 s, Tboard,start ≤ 50 °C)". Do not market 80 A without the burst qualifier.

## 2. Mathematical Model: Power Stage Losses

### 2.1 Conduction Losses (6-step, synchronous rectification)

**Which FETs conduct.** In each 60° sector, phase A (for example) is PWM'd high and phase B is held low:

- Leg A: the high side conducts for duty D. The complementary low side carries the freewheel current for (1 − D).
- Leg B: the low side conducts 100% of the time.

So at any instant, exactly two channels carry the phase current I:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

Temperature-dependent resistance:

$$
R_{hot} = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow R_{125} \approx 2.3\ \text{m}\Omega
$$
