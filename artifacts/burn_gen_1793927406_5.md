**Status:** Engineering model and reference design, not a release-ready design.

**How to use this document.** I don't have the Job #51 BOM, stackup, or layout files. Every device parameter below is therefore a declared assumption. The equations are general. Items that must be confirmed on the bench or against source code are marked **⚠ VERIFY**.

**Architecture assumption.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It drives a 4-in-1 ESC whose per-channel MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class) handle commutation. An H743 does not normally commutate motors itself. If Job #51 truly intends the H743 to commutate, Section 3 changes entirely; tell me and I'll redo it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At 110 °C: R_ds = 1.1 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.52 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step current paths

During any sector, two legs conduct:

- **Phase A, the PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D·T. The complementary low-side conducts for (1−D)·T, minus dead time.
- **Phase B, the return leg:** the low-side is on 100 % of the sector.

With synchronous rectification, the duty cycle cancels out:

$$P_{cond}=I^2R_{ds}\,[D+(1-D)]+I^2R_{ds}=2I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond}=2(80)^2(1.52\text{ m}\Omega)=\mathbf{19.5\ W\ per\ motor\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Hottest single device

The return-leg low-side carries the full current continuously during its sector:

$$P_{FET,max}=I^2R_{ds}=6400\times1.52\text{ m}\Omega=\mathbf{9.7\ W}$$

Averaged over the six sectors, each FET conducts about 1/3 of the time. During a burst shorter than the commutation period at low eRPM, however, treat the instantaneous 9.7 W as the design case.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses

Assume 0.3 mΩ of effective path resistance per channel, covering shunt, vias, and pours:

$$P_{Cu}=I^2R_{path}=6400\times0.3\text{ m}\Omega\approx\mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

### 1.5 Electrothermal stability criterion

Junction temperature and conduction loss feed back on each other:

$$T_j=T_{ref}+R_{th}\,P_0[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j=\frac{T_{ref}+R_{th}P_0(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha R_{th}P_0}$$

Runaway occurs when **α·R_th·P_0 ≥ 1**. For P_0 = 7 W (the 25 °C loss) and α = 0.0045, the limit is R_th < 32 °C/W. This is safe at the device level, but it shows why board-level heating dominates (Section 4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate currents during the Miller plateau

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{on}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{10-3}{9}=0.78\text{ A}$$

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{off}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{3}{7}=0.43\text{ A}$$

### 2.2 Transition times

$$t_{on}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}}=\frac{12\text{ nC}}{0.78}=15.4\text{ ns}$$

$$t_{off}=\frac{12\text{ nC}}{0.43}=27.9\text{ ns}$$
