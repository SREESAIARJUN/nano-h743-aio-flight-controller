**Architectural note:** The H743 does not drive the gates. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases are active: phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low.

- **High-side on (fraction D):** current flows through Q_XH and then Q_YL.
- **Low-side sync-rectifying (fraction 1−D):** current flows through Q_XL and then Q_YL.

So at every instant (ignoring dead time), the motor current flows through **exactly two conducting FETs**. This holds regardless of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

with temperature dependence:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]
$$

Phase current ripple adds a small rms correction:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2 L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$
