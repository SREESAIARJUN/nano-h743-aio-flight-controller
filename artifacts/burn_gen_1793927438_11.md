## 0. Architecture Review: Three Corrections Before Any Math

### 0.1 The H743 cannot drive four ESCs directly
- Four sensorless 3-phase bridges need four timers with complementary outputs and dead-time.
- The H743 has only two full advanced timers (TIM1 and TIM8). TIM15, TIM16 and TIM17 offer only one complementary channel each.
- **Decision:** The H743 runs ArduPilot only. Each motor gets a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071, running AM32) driven by bidirectional DShot600.
- This is also the only configuration where ArduPilot's bdshot path is supported.
