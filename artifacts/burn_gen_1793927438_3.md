## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T<sub>j</sub> (°C) | Factor | R<sub>DS(on)</sub> (mΩ) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.000 | 1.20 |
| 100 | 1.338 | 1.61 |
| 125 | 1.450 | 1.74 |

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

In any 60° commutation sector:

- The **PWM leg** conducts through the high-side FET for duty D and through the complementary low-side FET for 1–D.
- The **static leg** low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

With complementary switching, motor current I<sub>m</sub> therefore always flows through exactly **two channels**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_m^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle. At a typical electrical frequency (2207/1900 KV, 14 poles, 6S gives roughly 5.5 kHz) the commutation period is far shorter than the FET thermal time constant (ms range). Losses can therefore be averaged:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\quad(\text{balanced; the static low side carries slightly more at low } D)$$

### 1.3 Numerical result at 80 A

| T<sub>j</sub> | P<sub>cond,ch</sub> = 2·80²·R |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 15.36 W |
| 100 °C | **20.5 W** |
| 125 °C | 22.3 W |

### 1.4 PCB copper conduction

Sheet resistance for 2 oz (70 µm) copper:

$$R_\square = \frac{\rho_{Cu}}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}}{70\times10^{-6}} = 0.246\ \text{m}\Omega/\square \;(25\,°\text{C}),\quad \times1.39 \text{ at } 125\,°\text{C}$$

Assumed current path per channel: battery pad → FET drains → phase pad. This is about 6 squares, paralleled across 4 power layers, giving roughly 0.37 mΩ cold and 0.5 mΩ hot.

$$P_{Cu,ch} = I_m^2 R_{path} \approx 80^2 \times 0.3\ \text{m}\Omega \approx 1.9\ \text{W}$$
