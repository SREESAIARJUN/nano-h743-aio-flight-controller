# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Flight Controller: 80A Burst, 48 kHz Switching, Bidirectional DShot600 Design Model

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two architectural points shape the rest of the analysis.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any 4-in-1 design the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each motor channel (or a shared MCU on some designs) has its own ESC MCU running the commutation firmware, for example AM32 on an AT32F421 or STM32G071, or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those MCUs, not by the H743. I model it that way below.
2. **80A on a "nano" board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The math in Section 4 shows that a 20–30 mm board survives roughly 3–4 s with all four channels at 80A. Any marketing claim beyond that is thermally false.

**Baseline parameters.** These are assumptions; substitute values from your actual MOSFET datasheet.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V max (6S) | DC link |
| I_pk | 80 A | Burst phase current per channel |

| Q_g / Q_gs2 / Q_gd | 40 / 3 / 8 nC | Gate charges |

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

The switching scheme is 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, which is the AM32 default.

Each phase leg spends one-third of the electrical cycle in each of three states:
- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for a fraction D of each PWM period, the low side (LS) for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the LS is held fully on.
- **Floating:** the leg is off, and the back-EMF is sensed.

Average loss per leg:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D I^2 R, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D) I^2 R + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R$$

Summing over the three legs:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)}$$

This result is independent of D, because exactly two MOSFETs carry the current at every instant.

R_ds(on) rises with junction temperature, which creates a positive feedback loop:

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Numbers at 80A:
- MOSFET conduction: P = 2 × 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **23.0 W per channel**.
- Copper: P_cu = 2 × 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **6.4 W worst case**. About 3.2 W of this lands on the board as a distributed load; the rest is in wires and pads.

## 2. Switching Loss at 48 kHz

Switching loss occurs only on the PWM leg's hard-switched device, one at a time per channel.

**Gate currents during the transitions:**
- Turn-on: I_g,on = (10 − 3)/5 = 1.4 A, so t_on = (Q_gs2 + Q_gd)/I_g = 11 nC / 1.4 A = 7.9 ns.
- Turn-off: I_g,off = 3/5 = 0.6 A, so t_off = 11 nC / 0.6 A = 18
