## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical model (PMSM, dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q ,\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off},\quad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m
$$

### 1.2 Transforms

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant, 2-shunt, using i_c = −i_a − i_b):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

**Delay compensation.** The PWM update lags the current sample by about 1.5 T_s. Advance the angle used for inverse Park accordingly:

$$
\theta_e^{*} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.3 PI with pole-zero cancellation and decoupling

Each axis has the plant G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose a PI controller whose zero cancels the plant pole (K_i/K_p = R/L). The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{s}}{10}
$$

For f_s = 20 kHz, take ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s. With the assumed motor this gives K_p ≈ 17 V/A and K_i ≈ 5.2e4 V/(A·s).

The control law, with feed-forward decoupling:

$$
v_d = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^{*}-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^{*}-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

**Voltage limiting** (circular, with d-priority):

$$
|v_d| \le V_{max},\qquad |v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2},\qquad V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95
$$

### 1.4 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

This is equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM.

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$
