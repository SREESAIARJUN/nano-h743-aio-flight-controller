### Loop rate plan

| Loop | Rate | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC d/q PI + SVPWM) | 20 kHz | ADC injected-conversion end-of-conversion ISR |
| Velocity PI + anti-cogging feedforward | 4 kHz | Every 5th current ISR |
| IMU acquisition, filtering, dual fusion | 8 kHz (sample), 4 kHz (fused output) | IMU data-ready interrupt → DMA |
| Attitude estimator + angle loop | 1 kHz | Every 4th velocity tick |

Each inner loop runs 4–5× faster than its outer loop. Each inner bandwidth is ≥ 4× its outer bandwidth, which keeps the cascade approximately decoupled.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, using `i_a + i_b + i_c = 0`:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$
