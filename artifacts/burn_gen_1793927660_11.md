## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in six-step synchronous PWM

In each 60° sector, two phases are active:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET of the same leg conducts for (1 − D) as the synchronous freewheel path.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET conducts continuously for the whole sector.

So at every instant the motor current I flows through exactly two FET channels, regardless of D (ignoring the dead-time intervals, handled in §2.4).

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big]$$

Add the copper path (bus planes, phase pads, shunt if fitted):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu}, \qquad R_{Cu}\approx 0.5\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ (2 oz, short paths, no shunt)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond = 2I²R_ds | P_Cu |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 3.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.56 mΩ | 20.0 W | 3.2 W |
| 140 °C | 1.75 mΩ | 22.4 W | 3.2 W |

**Ripple correction.** The phase current is not pure DC. With inductor ripple ΔI:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

With L_phase ≈ 15 µH, ΔI ≈ V_bus·D(1−D)/(L·f_sw) ≈ 8.7 A at D = 0.5. This adds about 0.1 % to the loss, so it is negligible at 80 A.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches. The return-leg low-side FET is static within a sector.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching, inductive load)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$
