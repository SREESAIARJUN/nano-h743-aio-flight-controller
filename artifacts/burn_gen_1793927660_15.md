# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Architecture

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and a reference netlist.

> **Note on numbers:** I don't have the Job #4 BOM, so every device parameter below is an explicit, labelled assumption. The equations are general. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values (R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, Q_gs2, Q_rr, Q_oss, R_θJC) and board stack-up, then recompute. The worked numbers show magnitude and where the design breaks.

## 0. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──┬── Bulk caps ──┬── 4× 3-phase bridges (24 MOSFETs)
                        │               │      ▲ gate drive
                        │               │   4× 3-ph gate drivers
                        │               │      ▲ 6× PWM each
                        │               └── 4× ESC MCUs (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class)
                        │                      ▲ bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire each)
                        └── Buck 5V/3.3V ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot) TIM1 CH1–4
```

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by each ESC MCU. The H743 only produces DShot frames and decodes eRPM telemetry. This split determines the DMA analysis in §4.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation geometry (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In each 60° electrical sector, two phases conduct: one high-side (HS) leg and one low-side (LS) leg. Each MOSFET is assigned to 120° of 360°, so its sector duty is 1/3.

With PWM duty D applied to the switching leg and complementary synchronous rectification:

- **Switching-leg HS:** conducts D of its 120°
- **Switching-leg LS (sync rect):** conducts (1−D) of the same 120°
- **Static-leg FET:** conducts 100% of its 120°

The current always flows through exactly **two** MOSFET channels in series (ignoring dead time). This gives a compact per-channel result:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Per-FET average, balanced over rotation:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}{3}$$

### 2.2 Battery vs. phase current

$$I_{bat} \approx D\cdot I_{ph}\quad\Rightarrow\quad I_{ph} = I_{bat}/D$$

At part-throttle, phase current exceeds battery current. **If "80 A" is a battery-side spec at D = 0.6, phase current is 133 A**, and conduction loss rises by 2.8×. Confirm which current the 80 A burst rating refers to.

### 2.3 Self-consistent junction temperature

R_DS(on) depends on T_j, which depends on loss. Solve by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{ref} + P_{FET}\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right)\cdot R_{\theta,j\text{-}ref}$$

Converged at T_j ≈ 110 °C (see §3), R_DS(on) ≈ 1.47 mΩ:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| I²R per FET (full on) | 80² × 1.47 mΩ = 9.41 W |
| **P_cond per FET (÷3)** | **3.14 W** |
| **P_cond per channel** | **18.8 W** |
| **P_cond, 4 channels** | **75.3 W** |

### 2.4 Copper path loss (often larger than expected)

$$R_{trace} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\, L}{w \cdot t \cdot N_{layers}}$$

Example: 8 mm path, 4 mm wide, 4 layers × 70 µm (2 oz), ρ at 80 °C ≈ 2.1×10⁻⁸ Ω·m:

- R ≈ 0.15 mΩ per segment
- 80² × 0.15 mΩ ≈ 1.0 W per segment

With 2–3 high-current segments per channel (bus to HS, LS to GND return, phase pad), add **≈2–3 W per channel**. On a nano board this copper loss is comparable to one FET's loss. Do not omit it.
