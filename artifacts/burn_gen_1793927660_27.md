## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Findings

These findings constrain everything that follows.

**F1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no field-oriented or six-step ESC firmware for the H7. This design uses the standard AIO topology:
- STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.
- Four ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7, 120 MHz, running AM32) generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.

**F2. "80A burst" is a time-limited rating, not an operating point.** At 80 A on all four channels, the power stage dissipates about 120 W (derived in §1–§3).
- A nano board (20×20 mm) has roughly 2 J/K of thermal mass and 8–15 K/W of board-to-air thermal resistance.
- The analysis in §4 shows 4×80 A is survivable for well under 1 s.
- A defensible rating on 20×20 mm is about **35 A continuous / 55 A for 5 s per channel**.
- A true 80 A, 10 s burst needs a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board with larger FETs.
