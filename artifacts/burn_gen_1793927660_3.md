# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Flight Controller: Architecture, Loss Model, and Timer/DMA Plan

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three assumptions in the brief need correcting before any modelling:

1. **The H743 cannot be the ESC.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation MCU(s), for example 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, or 1× MCU per motor with BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs. The **48 kHz is the ESC MCU's PWM frequency**, set in AM32 config, not in ArduPilot.
2. **80 A per motor on a 20×20 "nano" board is not thermally sustainable.** The model below shows it is a sub-second transient at best. Realistic 20×20 boards are rated 25–45 A continuous. 80 A burst belongs on 30.5×30.5 or larger with 5×6 mm FETs.
3. **"Burst" must be defined as a duration.** I model it as a function of time t_b, and solve for the allowable t_b.

The numbers below use stated assumptions. Replace them with your chosen FET's datasheet values. The equations are the deliverable.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path
The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM. At any instant the motor current flows through exactly **two FET channels in series**: the high or low FET of the PWM'd phase, plus the low-side FET of the return phase. Over a PWM period, duty D splits the PWM-leg conduction between its high and low FETs, but the **total** is always 2 channels:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[\,2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\right]
$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

### 2.3 Self-consistent junction temperature
R_DS rises with T_j, and T_j rises with loss. The two must be iterated together:

$$
T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{case} + R_{\theta JC}\cdot P_{FET}\!\left(R_{DS}(T_j^{(n)})\right)
$$

There is a **thermal-runaway criterion** for the loop to converge:

$$
\frac{\partial T_j}{\partial T_j} = R_{\theta JC}\, I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha < 1
$$

At 80 A: 1.5 × 6400 × 0.001 × 0.007 = **0.067**, so it converges.

### 2.4 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond (2 FETs) | P_pcb | Total per motor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 1.9 W | 14.7 W |
| 100 °C | 1.53 mΩ | 19.5 W | 1.9 W | 21.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.70 mΩ | 21.8 W | 1.9 W | **23.7 W** |

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches; the return low-side is static during a commutation step.

### 3.1 Transition time

$$
t_{r} \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} \approx 10\ \text{ns}; \quad
\text{include } Q_{gs2}, \text{ layout } L: \; t_r + t_f \approx 40\ \text{ns (realistic)}
$$

### 3.2 Loss components

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·40n·48k | **1.94 W** |
| Body-diode dead time | V_f·I·2t_d·f_sw = 0.8·80·400n·48k | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50n·25.2·48k | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | 0.04 W |
| Gate drive (in driver, not FET) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2·60n·10·48k | 0.06 W |
| **Total switching per motor** | | **≈3.3 W** |

### 3.3 Design sensitivity
- P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it is about 1.65 W, at the cost of more current ripple and acoustic noise.
- Dead-time diode loss is about 37 % of switching loss. Trimming t_d from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per motor, but shoot-through margin must then be verified on the bench.

## 4. Total Burst Loss and Transient Thermal Model

### 4.1 Total dissipation

$$
P_{motor} \approx 23.7 + 3.3 = 27\ \text{W} \qquad
P_{board} = 4 \times 27 \approx \mathbf{108\ W}\ \text{(all 4 motors at 80 A)}
$$

### 4.2 Lumped thermal capacitance of a 20×20 board
Assume 6 layers of 2 oz copper on 1.6 mm FR4.

| Element | Mass | c_p | C_th |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper (6 × 70 µm × 4 cm²) | 1.50 g | 0.385 J/gK | 0.58 J/K |
| FR4 | 1.20 g | 1.1 J/gK | 1.32 J/K |
| 12 FETs + solder | ~0.6 g | ~0.7 | 0.42 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **≈2.3 J/K** |

### 4.3 Steady-state sink capability
With forced convection under props, h ≈ 100 W/m²K over A ≈ 8 cm² of both faces:

$$
R_{\theta,board\text{-}air} = \frac{1}{hA} \approx 12.5\ \text{K/W}
$$
