# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Reference Design Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Caveats

I have no prior project record for "Job #0," so this document is a self-contained reference design. Every number below follows from the assumptions in this section. If your actual MOSFETs, gate drivers, or stack-up differ, substitute their datasheet values into the equations. The structure of the model does not change.

**System partition.** These are two compute domains:

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (480 MHz max; ArduPilot clocks it at 400 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4 × ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is set by the ESC firmware, not ArduPilot. ArduPilot only issues DShot commands and reads eRPM telemetry.

**Electrical assumptions:**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst-case switching) |
| Burst phase current per motor | I | 80 A (DC-equivalent during conduction) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET | Q | Generic 40 V, 5×6 mm. R_ds(on),25 = 0.9 mΩ max @ V_gs = 10 V |
| Gate charges | Q_g / Q_gs2 / Q_gd | 60 nC / 8 nC / 10 nC |
| Other device values | Q_oss / Q_rr / V_plateau | 60 nC / 50 nC / 4.5 V |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K (R_ds(on) temperature coefficient) |
| Gate drive | V_drv / R_g,total | 10 V / 5 Ω (driver + external + internal) |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns per edge (2 edges per PWM period) |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| PCB copper resistance per commutation path | R_pcb | 0.3 mΩ |
| Board | — | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 1.6 mm, 6 layers × 2 oz Cu, 24 FETs |

> **Engineering flag:** 4 × 80 A on a 30.5 mm board is a burst-only rating. Section 3 shows the board is thermally capacitance-limited to a few seconds. Treat "80A" as a marketing-style burst figure and set continuous current around 35 to 45 A, depending on airflow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 0.9 × 1.45 = **1.305 mΩ**. Use **1.35 mΩ** for margin.

### 1.2 Six-step current path

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation, two phase legs conduct at any instant:
- one high-side FET (switching PWM), and
- one low-side FET (held on).

With synchronous rectification, the switching leg's current alternates between its high-side FET (duty D) and low-side FET (1 − D). Either way, exactly two channels plus PCB copper are in series. Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2\left(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\right)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2\,(2.70 + 0.30)\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 0.0030 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Split between silicon and copper:
- Silicon: 6400 × 2.70 mΩ = **17.28 W**
- Copper: 6400 × 0.30 mΩ = **1.92 W**

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET is in the conduction path for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle. The phase's high-side and low-side FETs split that window by D and 1 − D.

Averaged over a full electrical cycle:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{17.28}{6} = \mathbf{2.88\ W}$$

The worst-case low-side FET, at low duty during a punch-out from low throttle, approaches:

$$\frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3} = 2.88\ \text{W}$$

with instantaneous peaks of **8.64 W** during its conduction window. Instantaneous peaks are filtered by Z_th at eRPM above about 1 kHz.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switching leg incurs switching loss at any instant.

### 2.1 Gate current during the Miller plateau

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10-4.5}{5} = 1.10\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{4.5}{5} = 0.90\ \text{A}$$

### 2.2 Transition times

The switching charge is Q_sw = Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 18 nC.

$$t_r = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 16.4\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_f = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 20.0\ \text{ns}$$

### 2.3 V–I overlap loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 36.4\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.76\ W}$$

### 2.4 Parasitic terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Output capacitance | Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | 60n × 25.2 × 48k = 0.073 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 50n × 25.2 × 48k = 0.060 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200n × 48k = **0.614 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | Q_g · V_drv · f_sw per FET | 60n × 10 × 48k = 0.029 W |

### 2.5 Total switching loss per motor

$$P_{sw,motor} \approx 1.76 + 0.073 + 0.060 + 0.614 = \mathbf{2.51\ W}$$
