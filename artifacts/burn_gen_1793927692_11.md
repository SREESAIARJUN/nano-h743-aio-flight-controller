## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges. ArduPilot has no native 6-step or FOC motor-control path for gate drivers.

- **FC domain:** H743 running ArduPilot. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4× ESC MCUs. AT32F421K8U7 running AM32 is assumed.
- **ESC domain:** Each ESC MCU generates **48 kHz complementary PWM** into a 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q class) and a 6× N-FET bridge.
- The **switching-loss model applies to the ESC domain**. The **DMA model applies to the H743**.

**Headline findings:**

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Conduction loss per channel @ 80 A, hot | ≈ 28 W |
| Switching + dead-time loss per channel @ 48 kHz | ≈ 1.5 W |
| Total, all 4 channels @ 80 A burst | ≈ 130 W |
| Board adiabatic heating rate, 4 × 80 A | ≈ 43 K/s |
| **Safe burst duration, 4 × 80 A simultaneous** | **≈ 1.7 s** |
| Safe burst duration, 1 × 80 A (others idle) | ≈ 7.7 s |
