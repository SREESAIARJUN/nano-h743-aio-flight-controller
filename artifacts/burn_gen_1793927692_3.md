## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture decision

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The architecture is:

| Block | Function | Where the 48 kHz PWM lives |
|---|---|---|
| **FC** (STM32H743, ArduPilot) | Attitude control. Sends DShot600 to the ESCs and receives eRPM telemetry back on the same wire (bi-directional DShot). | — |
| **4-in-1 ESC** (4 × AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class) | 6-step or FOC commutation | 48 kHz complementary PWM on each ESC MCU |

Putting 80 A switching nodes and an H743 on one 20×20 or 25×25 mm board is thermally and EMI-hostile (see §3). The recommended build is a **two-board stack** joined by an 8-pin JST-SH or board-to-board connector.

### 0.2 Assumptions

No MOSFET was specified, so representative values for a 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 device are used. All equations are parametric, so you can substitute the datasheet numbers for your chosen part.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full), 22.2 V nominal | DC link |
| I_burst | 80 A per ESC channel | Phase current during the burst, D→1 |
| R_ds(25 °C) | 1.0 mΩ | V_GS = 10 V |
| α | 1.5 at T_j = 100 °C (≈ +0.4 %/K) | R_ds tempco multiplier |
| Q_g / Q_gd | 40 nC / 8 nC | Total gate charge / Miller charge |
| C_oss(er) | 1.5 nF | Energy-related output capacitance |

| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward voltage at high current |

| T_amb | 35 °C | Air temperature |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

**6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification.** In any 60° sector:

- One leg PWMs: the high-side (HS) conducts for D·T, and the complementary low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D)·T.
- One leg has its LS held on.
- The phase current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly **two channels** in series.

**Phase current versus battery current.** For continuous conduction:

$$I_{ph} \approx \frac{I_{batt}}{D}$$

"80 A burst" is interpreted here as I_ph = 80 A at D ≈ 1, which is the worst case for heating.

### 1.2 Conduction loss equation

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{cu} + I_{ph}^2 R_{shunt}$$

The tempco can be modelled as either:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\kappa\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \kappa\approx0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

or as a power law, $R_{ds,25}(T_j/298)^{2.3}$.

### 1.3 Numbers per channel at 80 A

| Term | 25 °C | 100 °C |
|---|---|---|
| 2·I²·R_ds | 2·6400·1.0 mΩ = **12.8 W** | 2·6400·1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W** |
| Copper (R_cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ phase + bus path, 2 oz) | 1.9 W | 2.3 W (Cu +0.39 %/K) |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-channel) | 1.6 W | 1.6 W |
| **Total conduction** | **16.3 W** | **23.1 W** |

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{th,ja} < 1$$
