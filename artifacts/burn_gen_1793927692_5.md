# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #20): Power-Stage, Thermal & Bidirectional DShot Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarification

**The STM32H743 does not perform the 48 kHz power switching.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack and does not commutate motors. A workable architecture is:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 master, eRPM telemetry capture | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| 4× six-step / sinusoidal commutation at 48 kHz PWM | 4× ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class |
| Gate drive | 4× 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q-class) | — |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | — |

Section 1 models the ESC power stage. Section 4 models the H743 DShot/DMA subsystem.

### 1.1 Conduction losses

- **PWM leg:** the high-side switch conducts for D, the low-side conducts synchronously for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side switch is held on.
