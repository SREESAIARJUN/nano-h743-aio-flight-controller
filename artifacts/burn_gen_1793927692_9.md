## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. The partitioning is as follows:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 generation, bidirectional eRPM capture | STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, back-EMF sensing | 4 × ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071) | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class) |
| Gate drive | 4 × 3-phase driver (FD6288Q-class) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET (6 per channel) | n/a |
