# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #57)

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

I don't have the Job #57 design package, so every numeric input below is an assumption. Parameterized formulas come first, then a worked example. Replace the assumed values with your BOM datasheet values before release.

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. A shippable 4-in-1 AIO therefore uses:

- **H743**: runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600 on timer pins.
- **4× ESC MCUs**: AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These handle 48 kHz PWM, commutation, and bidirectional DShot replies.

The model below assumes this split.

### 0.2 The 80A requirement

The math in §1–§3 shows that "80A per motor" on a nano-format board (20×20 or 30×30 mm) is only achievable as a short burst of about 1–2 s, thermally capacitance-limited. Continuous capability comes out near 25A per motor. That matches what shipping nano AIOs are rated for. Treat 80A as a peak-current spec (OC trip, FET SOA), not a thermal rating.
