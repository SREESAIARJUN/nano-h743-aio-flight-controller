# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Allocation

## 0. Executive Summary

The headline finding is that **80 A continuous per motor is not physically achievable on a 20×20 mm board.** An 80 A *burst* is achievable only for about 0.5–1.5 s, and only with MOSFETs of ≤1.6 mΩ and aggressive copper.

Key figures from the model:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Loss per channel at 80 A, 48 kHz, 6S, T_j = 125 °C | ≈ 33 W (conduction dominates, ~93%) |
| Four channels simultaneously at 80 A | ≈ 130 W into a ~2.2 J/K board, so ≈ 60 K/s temperature rise |
| Junction temperature, single FET, board at 80 °C, electrothermally solved | ≈ 156 °C (exceeds 150 °C design limit) |

Recommendation: **specify 45 A continuous / 65 A burst (≤2 s) per motor** for a 20×20 board, or move to a 30×30 board if 80 A burst is a hard requirement.

The DShot600 bidirectional allocation fits on a single advanced timer (TIM1) with 2–3 DMA streams via DMAMUX. Details are in Section 5.
