# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Loss, Thermal, and DShot DMA Model

**Job #64 Architecture Note, Rev A**

> **Finding:** The thermal model shows that an "80A burst" rating on a nano-class (≤30×30 mm) board is a **time-limited** rating of roughly **3–5 seconds** without a heatsink. It is not a sustainable operating point. Sustained capability lands around **28–35A per channel** with propwash cooling. I recommend we specify burst as *"80A / 3 s, T_board,start ≤ 40 °C"* and enforce it in firmware with the RC thermal model in §4. Without that limit, field returns are likely.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary PWM, two phases conduct during every commutation sector:

- **High-side PWM phase:** HS conducts for fraction D. The complementary LS conducts (synchronous rectification) for 1−D−2t_dt·f_sw.
- **Low-side static phase:** LS is on for the full sector.

The motor current I therefore always flows through **two channels in series**, regardless of D (neglecting dead time):

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

with

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Phase current ripple at 48 kHz with motor inductance L_ph (line-to-line L_LL = 2·L_ph):

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D\,(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}}, \qquad
I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$
