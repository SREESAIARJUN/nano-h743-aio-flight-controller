## 1. Plant Model

For each axis (mechanical angle θ, velocity ω = θ̇):

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t\, i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - \tau_{fric}(\omega) - \tau_d
$$

Electrical model in the rotor dq frame (θ_e = pθ, ω_e = pω):

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q \\
v_q &= R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
\end{aligned}
$$

The torque constant is K_t = (3/2) p λ_m.
