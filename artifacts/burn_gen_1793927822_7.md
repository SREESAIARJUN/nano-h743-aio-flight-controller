# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #46): Design Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and One Architectural Correction

I have no prior documentation for Job #46, so I have stated every assumption. Replace them with your BOM values and the equations will carry through.

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 should not drive the power stages directly.

- It has only two advanced-control timers (TIM1, TIM8) with complementary outputs and hardware dead-time. Four 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary pairs.
- It is also the wrong place to run 4× 48 kHz commutation alongside the ArduPilot EKF.

The standard partition, used below, is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, generates 4× bidirectional DShot600, receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC MCU (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 | AM32 firmware, 6-step/sinusoidal commutation, 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate driver (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per channel |
| Power FETs (×24) | 40 V, ~1.0 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 mm | Phase bridges |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths
In 6-step commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, the phase current always flows through exactly two FETs:

- **ON interval (duty D):** HS FET of phase A and LS FET of phase B.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** LS FET of phase A (synchronous freewheel) and LS FET of phase B.

So, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

with the temperature-dependent resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

### 1.2 Distribution per FET
Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period. Its share depends on D: the HS gets D, and the LS gets (1−D) on the chopped leg plus 1 on the return leg. The worst-case per-FET dissipation is the low-side at low duty (heavy freewheel):

$$P_{LS,max}\approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2R_{DS}\,(1+1) = \tfrac{2}{3}I^2R_{DS}$$

For thermal sizing, use the channel total spread across the 6-FET cluster, plus this hotspot check.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond/channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 80 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.7 W | 66.6 W |
| 110 °C | 1.47 mΩ | 18.8 W | 75.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.55 mΩ | 19.8 W | 79.4 W |

The low-side hotspot at 110 °C is (2/3)(6400)(1.47 mΩ) ≈ **6.3 W in a single package**. This value sets the layout constraint.

### 1.4 Additional I²R terms (do not ignore)
- **Phase copper.** A 4 mm-wide, 2 oz (70 µm) trace that is 10 mm long gives R = ρL/A = 1.72e-8·0.01/(4e-3·70e-6) ≈ 0.61 mΩ. That is comparable to a FET. Use stitched multi-layer pours. Six layers of 2 oz in parallel gives ≈0.1 mΩ, adding ≈0.65 W per channel at 80 A.
- **Shunt.** If a 0.25 mΩ DC-bus shunt is used, the per-channel average current I_dc ≈ D·I_ph. At D=0.8 that is 64 A, giving 1.0 W.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the chopped leg switches, so there is one hard-switched commutation pair per PWM cycle.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard switching, inductive clamp):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = 1.94\text{ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery** of the freewheeling LS body diode at HS turn-on:
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.060\text{ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (2 edges per cycle):
$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph}\,(2t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot100\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = 0.31\text{ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (2 switching FETs, mostly dissipated in R_g and the driver):
$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

**Switching total:** ≈ **2.4 W per channel**. This is about 11% of conduction loss at 80 A. At this current, R_DS(on) dominates, so do not trade R_DS(on) for lower Q_g.

**Sensitivity of t_r + t_f.** With R_g = 10 Ω and a Miller plateau of ~4 V, the plateau gate current is ≈(10−4)/10 = 0.6 A. With Q_gd ≈ 10 nC, the transition takes ≈17 ns, which is consistent with the 20 ns assumption.

- Halving R_g roughly halves P_VI.
- The cost is higher dV/dt, ringing, and EMI into the gyro.
- Recommendation: 4.7–10 Ω turn-on, plus a diode-bypassed ~2.2 Ω turn-off.

## 3. Total Burst Power

At T_j ≈ 110 °C:

| Term | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 18.8 W | 75.2 W |
| Switching (all) | 2.4 W | 9.6 W |
| Phase copper | 0.65 W | 2.6 W |
| Shunt | 1.0 W | 4.0 W |
| **Total** | **≈22.9 W** | **≈91 W** |

Out of ≈4×25.2×64 A ≈ 6.4 kW delivered, this is about 98.6% efficiency. The absolute heat is still severe for a nano board.
