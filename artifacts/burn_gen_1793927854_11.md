Some details were not specified, so the following are baseline assumptions. Change any of them and re-run the equations in §2 to §4.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | 4S variant scales directly |
| Board format | 30.5 × 30.5 mm stack (FC and ESC on separate boards, "nano AIO" mezzanine) | A single 20×20 board cannot survive 4×80 A. See §3.6 |
| ESC MCUs | 4 × AT32F421 running AM32 firmware | The H743 does **not** commutate the bridges. It sends DShot only |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q, 3-phase, V_DRV ≈ 10 V | |
| MOSFET (reference) | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | Parameters in §1. Substitute your actual part |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) | |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, 120° conduction | |
| Burst definition | 80 A per channel for t_b ≤ 2 s, all four channels simultaneously (worst case) | |
| Ambient | T_a = 40 °C | |
| FC clocking | SYSCLK 400 MHz, APB timer clock f_TIM = 200 MHz | ArduPilot H743 default |

**Why the H743 does not drive the bridges:** commutation, BEMF zero-cross detection, and 48 kHz complementary PWM with dead time need roughly 12 advanced-timer outputs plus 4 independent comparator and ADC chains. That would collide directly with ArduPilot's scheduler and DMA budget. The industry-standard and reliable partition is therefore: the FC sends DShot, and each ESC MCU closes its own commutation loop.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path topology

In 6-step operation, two phase legs conduct at any instant:

- **Leg A** (PWM leg): the high-side switch is ON for duty D. During (1−D) the complementary low-side carries the freewheel current.
- **Leg B** (static leg): the low-side switch is ON for the full 60° sector.

With synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through **exactly two channels in series** (excluding dead time). Total conduction loss per ESC is therefore independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = I_{ph}^{2}\cdot 2\,R_{DS}(T_j) \qquad (2.1)
$$

The temperature dependence of R_DS is:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha_R (T_j - 25)\big] \qquad (2.2)
$$

### 2.2 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each phase conducts 240° (120° positive, 120° negative). Within its 120° high-side window, the high-side FET carries current for D of the time when its leg is the PWM leg, and 100% otherwise, depending on the AM32 PWM scheme. Averaged over the cycle, each of the 6 FETs sees:

$$
P_{FET,avg} = \frac{P_{cond,ESC}}{6} \qquad (2.3)
$$

The worst-case **instantaneous** FET carries the full I_ph for a 60° sector. Its local junction ripple at commutation frequency is small, because the die thermal time constant (~ms) is far longer than the commutation period. Example: 1500 rad/s mechanical × 7 pole pairs gives ~1.7 kHz electrical.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

The first-pass estimate assumes T_j ≈ 115 °C:

- R_DS = 1.0 × [1 + 0.0045 × 90] = 1.405 mΩ
- P_cond,ESC = 80² × 2 × 1.405 m = **17.98 W**

### 2.4 Copper and shunt losses (often ignored, should not be)

| Element | Resistance in path | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad to motor wire, 2 oz, 4-layer paralleled | 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| Bus planes, V+ and GND share per channel | 0.10 mΩ | 0.64 W |
| Current shunt (shared bus shunt, 0.25 mΩ, sees I_bus ≈ D·I_ph) | 0.25 mΩ | ≤ 1.6 W at D = 1 |

Taking a mid-throttle average gives **P_Cu ≈ 2.4 W per ESC**.

### 2.5 Thermal-runaway stability criterion

The design is stable if the loop gain of the conduction-loss/temperature feedback is below 1:

$$
G = \frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j} R_{\theta,total} = I^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS,25}\,\alpha_R \cdot R_{\theta,JA,eff} < 1 \qquad (2.4)
$$
