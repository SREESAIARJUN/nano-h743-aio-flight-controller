## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate motors directly. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class) generates the 48 kHz complementary 6-step PWM. This split is what makes the switching-loss and DMA analyses separable.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current path
In 6-step trapezoidal commutation, two phases conduct at any instant: one high-side (HS) leg and one low-side (LS) leg. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- Active phase pair, PWM leg: HS conducts for D, the synchronous LS conducts for (1−D).
- Opposite leg: LS stays on continuously for the 60° sector.

The PWM leg's total conduction is therefore I²R·D + I²R·(1−D) = I²R, independent of duty. This holds as long as the dead-time fraction is small.

**Per-channel conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,(1 - 2t_{dt}f_{sw})$$

**R_DS(on) temperature dependence** (linearized; α ≈ 0.0045 K⁻¹ for 40 V trench parts):

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) ≈ 1.34 mΩ. Use the datasheet curve value of 1.45 mΩ for conservatism.

### 1.2 80 A burst result
With t_dt = 100 ns, the dead-time correction is 1 − 2(100 ns)(48 kHz) = 0.9904.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} \times 0.9904 = \mathbf{18.4\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Electrothermal feedback (iterative fixed point)
Conduction loss raises T_j, which raises R_DS(on), which raises loss. The fixed point is:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,P(T_j)
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta P_0 (1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - R_\theta P_0 \alpha},\quad P_0 = 2I^2R_{25}$$

Thermal runaway occurs when R_θ·P₀·α ≥ 1. At 80 A, P₀ = 12.8 W per channel. Runaway therefore requires an effective R_θ ≥ 17 K/W per channel. A nano board approaches that value at steady state with poor airflow, which is the formal reason the 80 A rating must be time-limited.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-drive transition times
Miller-plateau charge-time approximation:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}},\quad I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{DRV}-V_{pl}}{R_{G,ext}+R_{G,int}+R_{drv,src}}$$

With V_DRV = 10 V, R_G,ext = 4.7 Ω, and R_drv,src ≈ 5 Ω:

- Turn-on: I_g,on = 5.5 / 10.9 = 0.50 A. Using the effective Q_gs2 + Q_gd ≈ 6 + 12 = 18 nC gives t_r ≈ 36 ns.
