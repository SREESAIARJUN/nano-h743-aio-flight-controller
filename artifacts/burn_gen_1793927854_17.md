# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power Stage, Thermal Model, and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Executive Verdict

I have no prior specification for "Job #14," so this design is built from the stated requirements plus the explicit assumptions below. Any assumption can be changed and the model re-run.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Drives the 40 V FET choice |
| Board | 20×20 mm "nano" AIO, 8-layer, 2 oz Cu all layers | 1.6 mm FR4 |
| Burst current | 80 A per channel | Defined as a ≤ 2–5 s burst, not continuous |
| ESC MCU | 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 | Each ESC channel has its own MCU. The H743 only sends DShot. |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step | AM32 default family |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap), about 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink | Typical 4-in-1 choice |
| MOSFET | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN class | R_DS(on),25 = 0.9 mΩ @ 10 V, Q_g ≈ 40 nC, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, C_oss,tr ≈ 1.5 nF, R_θJC ≈ 1.5 K/W |
| Ambient | 40 °C | Inside the frame, sun-loaded |
| FC firmware | ArduPilot, BIDIR DShot600 | |

**Executive verdict:**
- An 80 A per-channel burst on a 20×20 board is achievable **only as a short transient of roughly 1–3 s with all four channels loaded**.
- The sustainable continuous rating with prop-wash cooling is about **18–25 A per channel**.
- The limit is the board's thermal mass and its convection path. The FET junctions are not the bottleneck.
- The detailed math follows. If the requirement is "80 A continuous," the form factor has to change; see §6.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives roughly 1.6× at 125 °C, which is typical for trench 40 V parts.

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.305 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.44 mΩ |

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any commutation step, current I flows through exactly **two FETs**:

- **PWM on-time (D):** HS of phase A, then LS of phase B.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** LS of phase A (synchronous freewheel), then LS of phase B.

Total bridge conduction loss per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph-ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Typical drone motor values: L_ph-ph ≈ 15 µH, V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 4 V at high load, D ≈ 0.9.

ΔI ≈ 4 × 0.9 / (15 µH × 48 kHz) ≈ 5 A. The ripple term is 25/12 ≈ 2.1 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². I_rms ≈ I_avg.

### 1.4 Copper path

Each phase current traverses about 15 mm of pour, roughly 3 mm wide, across 4 paralleled 2 oz layers.

$$R_{Cu} = \rho \frac{l}{w\,t\,n} = 1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot\frac{0.015}{0.003 \cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 4} \approx 0.31\ \text{m}\Omega$$

This applies per path. Two phase paths plus the bus give about 0.8 mΩ effective, and copper heats with α_Cu = 0.0039/K.
