# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

**Document:** Job #33, Design Basis Rev A
**Scope:** Conduction, switching, and thermal model at an 80 A burst; ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation; reference netlists.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Key Findings

### 0.1 Architecture

ArduPilot cannot commutate BLDC motors directly from the H743. The only viable architecture is:

**H743 (ArduPilot)** → bidirectional DShot600 → **4 × ESC MCUs** (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421) → 3-phase gate driver → 6 MOSFETs per channel.

### 0.3 Headline Finding

> **An 80 A per-channel rating is achievable only as a burst of roughly 1–2 s on a nano form factor. It is not a continuous rating.** Steady-state physics limits the board to roughly 25–35 A per channel with propwash cooling. Section 3 derives this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

During each commutation sector:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw, and the low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET conducts continuously.
- **Leg C:** floating, used for BEMF sensing.

The phase current therefore always flows through exactly **two channels**, apart from dead-time intervals:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{DS,hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{DS,hot} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS,hot}
$$

The result is independent of duty cycle. Only the *split* between the high-side and low-side devices depends on D.

### 1.2 Temperature-Corrected R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS,hot} = R_{DS,25}\cdot k_T = 0.70 \times 1.5 = 1.05\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Per-Channel and Board Totals at 80 A

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.44\ W}
$$

### 1.4 Copper and Interconnect Loss

Assume 0.4 mΩ effective per phase path, covering traces, vias, and pads, with two phases in the current path:

$$
P_{Cu,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.4\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{5.12\ W}
$$

> **Layout implication:** copper loss is about 38 % of MOSFET conduction loss. On a nano board, **copper is a first-order loss term**. Mitigations:
> - stack the phase pours on at least 4 layers;
> - via-stitch at ≥ 40 vias per phase pad;
> - use 2 oz copper minimum.

### 1.5 Battery-Side Current

Battery current relates to phase current through duty cycle:

$$
I_{bat} = D \cdot I_{ph}
$$

At full throttle (D ≈ 0.95), four channels at 80 A draw:

$$
I_{bat,total} \approx 4 \times 76 = 304\ \text{A}
$$

The XT60 connector, the battery pads, and the bulk capacitor ripple current must all be checked against this figure. In practice, an 80 A rating is never coincident across all four channels for long.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Its two FETs hard-switch once per period each way.

### 2.1 V·I Overlap (Hard Switching)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
