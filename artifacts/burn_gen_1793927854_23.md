## 0. Architecture Correction

**The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC commutation controller for four motors.** Hardware dead-time complementary PWM exists only on the advanced timers TIM1 and TIM8. Each provides 3 complementary pairs, so the H743 could drive at most 2 motors with hardware dead-time. Doing 48 kHz 6-step commutation for four motors alongside EKF3 in firmware is also not a sane design.

The architecture used in this package is the industry-standard split:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot | Sensors, EKF, mixer, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6
