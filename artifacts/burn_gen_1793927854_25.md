### 0.1 Architecture Flags

1. **Two-MCU architecture.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. The motors are commutated by ESC MCUs (typically 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32, one per motor). The H743 does **not** commutate. The 48 kHz PWM is the ESC firmware's carrier, not an H743 timer.
