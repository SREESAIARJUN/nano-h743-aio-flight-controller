# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Electro-Thermal Model, DShot600 Bidirectional DMA Map, and Netlists

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I have no access to a "Job #20" design package, so the baseline parameters below are explicit assumptions. Every equation is parameterized. Replace the bracketed values with your actual BOM datasheet numbers and re-run the arithmetic.

### Architecture
- **Stack layout.** A 30.5×30.5 mm H743 flight controller sits on top of a 4-in-1 ESC. They connect through an 8-pin JST-SH.
- **Why not an AIO board.** A true 20×20 "nano" all-in-one cannot dissipate 80A-class burst losses (see §3).
- **ESC firmware.** The ESC MCU is an AT32F421 or STM32G071 class part. It runs AM32 or BLHeli_32, which supports bidirectional DShot.
