## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.5) = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In any 60° sector, two legs are active:
- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for duty D, and the low side conducts (synchronous rectification) for 1 − D.
- **Static leg:** the low side is on for the full sector.

$$P_{cond,PWM\,leg} = I^2 R\,D + I^2 R\,(1-D) = I^2 R$$
$$P_{cond,static} = I^2 R$$
$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

Ripple correction: $I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \Delta I_{pp}^2/12$. At 48 kHz with typical 5–15 µH motor inductance, ΔI_pp is a few amps, so the correction is under 1 %. It is neglected here.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond / motor |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2·80²·1.0m = **12.8 W** |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | **17.6 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |

### 1.4 Electrothermal feedback (stability check)

Because P depends on T_j and T_j depends on P, the self-consistent junction temperature is:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_\theta\, 2I^2 R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right] \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + K(1-25\alpha)}{1 - K\alpha},\quad K = 2I^2R_{25}R_\theta$$
