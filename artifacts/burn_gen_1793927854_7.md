## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

```
 6S LiPo (22.2V nom / 25.2V max)
   │
   ├─► 4-in-1 ESC: 4 × [ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class) + 3-ph gate driver + 6 MOSFETs]
   │        ▲  bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire per motor, half-duplex)
   │        │
   └─► BEC ─► STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot) — motor outputs M1..M4
```

**Correction to the premise:** the H743 does not switch the MOSFETs. Each ESC channel has its own MCU that generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only issues DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry. The loss model (§1–3) belongs to the ESC board, and the timer/DMA model (§4) belongs to the FC.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

In 6-step complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two conducting channels:
- During PWM-on, current flows through the high-side (HS) of phase A and the low-side (LS) of phase B.
- During PWM-off, current freewheels through the synchronous LS of phase A and the LS of phase B.

Because of synchronous rectification, the conduction path always contains two R_DS(on) regardless of duty cycle.

**Hot on-resistance:**

$$R_{DS,hot} = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T R_{25} = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

**Per-motor conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS,hot} = 2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs is in the path for 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{19.2}{6}\approx 3.2\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous loss while a FET conducts is 9.6 W, which matters for the transient Z_th analysis.

**Copper/trace loss** (per motor, path ≈ 2 phase traces + bus return):

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho\,\ell}{w\,t},\quad \rho_{Cu}=1.72\times10^{-8}\,\Omega\text{m}\,(1+0.0039\,\Delta T)$$

As an example: 2 oz (70 µm), 3 layers paralleled, 4 mm wide, 15 mm path gives R ≈ 0.31 mΩ, so P ≈ 80² × 0.31 mΩ ≈ 2.0 W.

**Shunt loss** (if a low-side or bus shunt is used): P = I_rms² R_shunt. A 0.25 mΩ shunt gives ≈ 1.6 W per motor at 80 A. Consider Hall/integrated sensing instead.

**Total conduction loss, 4 motors at burst:** 4 × (19.2 + 2.0) ≈ **85 W**, or about 91 W with shunts.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Switching occurs only on the PWM-modulated phase leg. Its HS FET takes hard-switching loss, and its LS FET takes dead-time/body-diode and Q_rr loss.

**Hard-switching overlap (HS FET):**

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Output-capacitance loss:**

$$P_{oss}= Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS FET):**

$$P_{dt}=V_f I_{ph}(2t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot400\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=0.06\ \text{W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}=2\cdot50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}=0.048\ \text{W per motor}$$

**Per-motor switching subtotal:** ≈ **3.3 W**.

**Duty-cycle nuance.** At true 100% throttle (D → 1), the bridge stops switching and P_sw → 0. A sensible worst case is D ≈ 0.9 with near-burst current. That combination gives about 3 W switching plus about 19 W conduction. Do not stack "80 A" and "full switching at D = 0.5": at D = 0.5 the phase current is far lower, because motor current scales with load torque, which scales roughly with RPM².
