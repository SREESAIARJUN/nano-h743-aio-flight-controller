## 0. Architecture Clarification and Critical Assumptions

The H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz by itself, and ArduPilot does not do ESC commutation. The design is therefore partitioned as follows:

| Block | Function | Representative Part |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | ArduPilot, sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM | AT32F421 / STM32G071 **[DS]** |
| Gate driver ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver, bootstrap | FD6288Q-class **[DS]** |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × N-MOSFET per ESC, 40 V | 40 V, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, PDFN5×6 **[DS]** |

**Form-factor warning.** A "nano" board (20×20 mm) cannot sustain 4 × 80 A. The copper cross-section and thermal mass are insufficient, as Section 3 shows. This model assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board**. The 80 A figure is treated as a **per-motor phase burst (≤ ~2–3 s)**, not continuous.

**Operating point:**

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, full charge |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current per motor |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | ESC PWM frequency |
| D | 0.5 (worst case for capacitors), 0.9 (worst case for FETs) | Duty cycle |
| T_amb | 40 °C | Ambient |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

Trapezoidal 6-step commutation is used, with **complementary (synchronous) PWM** as in AM32 defaults. In each 60° sector, exactly one high-side (HS) leg and one low-side (LS) leg carry I_ph:

- The PWM'd HS conducts for fraction D.
- Its complementary LS conducts during the freewheel interval (1−D).
- The opposite leg's LS stays on for the whole sector.

The current path therefore always contains **two FET channels in series**. Neglecting dead-time, which is handled in Section 2.3:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Each individual FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. This gives the per-FET average loss:

$$P_{cond,FET,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}I_{ph}^2 R\,D \qquad P_{cond,FET,LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}I_{ph}^2 R\,(1 + (1-D))$$

The LS is worst case: it carries the full sector when it is the static leg, plus freewheel when it is the PWM complement.

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence (Self-Consistent)

R_DS(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \textbf{[DS]}$$

Junction temperature in turn depends on dissipation:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta JC}$$

Solve by fixed-point iteration. The solution converges in 3–4 iterations because ∂P/∂T·R_θ ≪ 1.

### 1.3 Numbers

Inputs: R₂₅ = 1.0 mΩ, α = 0.006 K⁻¹, PCB node at 100 °C, so T_j ≈ 105 °C.

- R(105 °C) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006·80) = **1.48 mΩ**
- P_cond,ch = 2 · 6400 · 1.48 mΩ = **18.9 W per motor channel**
- Worst LS FET at D = 0.5: P = (1/3)·6400·1.48e-3·1.5 = **4.7 W average**
