# Job #56: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80A-Burst ESC

## 0. Scope and Design Assumptions

These assumptions are stated up front because every result below scales with them. Substitute real datasheet values before sign-off.

**System architecture**

- **Flight controller MCU:** STM32H743 (LQFP100), running ArduPilot.
  - SYSCLK is 400 MHz (480 MHz on rev V).
  - APB1 and APB2 timer clocks are 200 MHz.
- **ESC architecture:** four independent ESC MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32, or STM32G071), each with its own 3-phase gate driver.
  - The H743 does **not** drive the MOSFET gates directly.
  - It sends bi-directional DShot600 to each ESC MCU.
  - The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC PWM frequency**, not an H743 timer.

**Electrical operating point**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S Li-ion/LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (design point), 22.2 V nominal |
| Current | 80 A burst per motor, ≤ 5 s; 40 A continuous |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm form factor, 6-layer, 2 oz inner/outer copper, 1.6 mm |

> **Feasibility flag:** 80 A per channel × 4 on a 30.5 mm board is at the edge of what is physically achievable. A 20 × 20 "nano" footprint cannot carry it thermally, and the analysis below shows why. I've modeled 30.5 mm with 5×6 mm FETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.0\,(1+0.0045\cdot 85) = 1.38\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, one leg is PWM'd complementarily at duty D. A second leg has its low-side held on.

| Device | Conduction loss |
|---|---|
| PWM leg, high-side | $I^2 R_{DS} D$ |
| PWM leg, low-side (sync rect) | $I^2 R_{DS} (1-D)$ |
| Return leg, low-side | $I^2 R_{DS}$ |

Summing these gives a per-ESC conduction loss that is **independent of D**:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu} + I^2 R_{shunt}$$

At 80 A, with R_Cu,phase ≈ 0.3 mΩ (trace and pads) and R_shunt = 0.25 mΩ:

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 · 6400 · 1.38 mΩ | 17.66 W |
| Copper | 6400 · 0.3 mΩ | 1.92 W |
| Shunt | 6400 · 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| **Total per ESC** | | **≈ 21.2 W** |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical period:

- **Instantaneous loss while conducting:** I²R = 8.8 W.
- **Electrical-period average:** P_FET,avg ≈ 17.66
