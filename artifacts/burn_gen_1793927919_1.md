# Job #51: STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC

## Design Basis: Power Model, Thermal Model, Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation, Netlist

---

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Several constraints shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs the flight controller and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those ESC MCUs, not by the H743.
2. **"80A" is ambiguous.** It can mean phase current or battery current, where I_batt ≈ D·I_phase. I model **80A phase current per motor at D → 0.95** as the worst case.
3. **Board area and FET count conflict.** Twenty-four discrete 5×6 mm FETs need about 720 mm² of placement area, but a 20×20 nano board has 400 mm² total. You must choose one of these options:
   - Use 12 dual half-bridge DFN5×6 packages, accepting higher R_DS(on).
   - Move to a 25.5×25.5 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm
