## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate the motors in this design.** In ArduPilot systems the H743 runs the flight stack and emits DShot. Each 4-in-1 ESC has its own commutation MCUs: either four single-motor MCUs or one MCU per motor running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model below reflects that split:

```
[STM32H743 / ArduPilot] --4x bidir DShot600--> [4x ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32)]
                                                  └─> 3-phase gate driver ─> 6x N-MOSFET ─> motor
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Bridge-Level Loss

In 6-step drive, exactly two switch positions carry the phase current at every instant: one in the sourcing leg and one in the sinking leg. With synchronous rectification, the freewheel path is the complementary FET rather than the body diode, apart from dead time. Therefore:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{pk}^2\, R_{ds,hot}
$$

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W\ per\ motor}
$$

### 1.2 Per-Device Distribution (Hot-Spot Identification)

Each leg conducts during 2/3 of the electrical period:
- In one 1/3, it is the **PWM leg**. The high-side (HS) FET conducts for D and the low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1−D).
