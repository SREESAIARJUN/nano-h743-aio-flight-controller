## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings drive the rest of the document.

1. **"80A" must be qualified.** At 6S (25.2 V full charge), four channels at 80 A means about **320 A and roughly 8 kW** on the battery input.
   - A nano board (20×20 or 25×25 mm), its XT30/XT60 pads, and its input capacitors cannot sustain that.
   - The model below shows that board thermal mass limits an all-four 80 A burst to about **2 seconds** before copper reaches 110 °C.
   - **Recommended spec language:** "80 A per-channel burst (≤ 5 s, single channel, others ≤ 30 A), 45 A continuous per channel with airflow." Section 4 derives this.
2. **Partition.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate motors directly. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated low-cost MCU (baseline: STM32G071 running AM32) and a 3-phase gate driver. This is the industry-standard topology. It keeps 48 kHz PWM, zero-cross detection, and dead-time generation off the H743.
3. **Bidir DShot600 on the H743 is comfortably within DMA budget.** The H7's DMAMUX removes the fixed stream/channel conflicts of F4/F7 parts. The real constraints are timer grouping and capture-channel pairing.
