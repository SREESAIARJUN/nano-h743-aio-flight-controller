# FOC Motor Control and Sensor Fusion Design for a 3-Axis Gimbal

**Scope note:** I don't have the specification for Job #46, so this design rests on the assumptions in Section 0. Everything is parameterized, so you only need to swap in your motor, encoder and MCU values. Items marked **[VERIFY]** must be checked against your datasheets or hardware before flight.

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7, slots Ns = 12), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.06 N·m/A |
| Driver | 3-phase, inline or low-side shunts, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701-class), one per axis |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, each on its own SPI bus, gyro and accel ODR 8 kHz |
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7-class with FPU, CORDIC optional |

**Loop rates and bandwidths**

```
                 ┌──────── 1 kHz ────────┐  ┌──── 4 kHz ────┐  ┌──── 20 kHz ────┐
 q_ref (attitude)│ Attitude/angle P(+FF) │→ │ Velocity PI   │→ │ Current PI d/q │→ SVPWM
                 │ (IMU-based, Mahony)   │ω*│ + cogging FF  │iq*│ + decoupling  │
                 └──────────▲────────────┘  └──────▲────────┘  └──────▲────────┘
            dual ICM-42688 fusion (8 kHz → 1 kHz)  encoder PLL (20 kHz)  shunts (20 kHz)
```

**Bandwidth separation rule:** ω_c,cur ≥ 5·ω_c,vel and ω_c,vel ≥ 4·ω_c,ang.

Typical targets:

- Current loop: 2 kHz
- Velocity loop: 150–300 Hz
- Angle loop: 30–60 Hz

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$
