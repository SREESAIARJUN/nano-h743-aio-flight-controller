# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Architecture, Loss Model, Bidirectional DShot and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points in the brief need correcting before the model makes sense.

1. **The H743 does not drive the power stage.** In every production 4-in-1 design, and in the ArduPilot model, the H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and does the commutation. The FC only sends DShot600 and receives the bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The netlists in §6 follow this split.

2. **"Nano" and 80 A burst are physically incompatible.** At full throttle the battery current is about 4 × 80 A × D ≈ 320 A. The §3 numbers show about 100 W of stage dissipation. A 20×20 mm nano board cannot carry that current or absorb that heat. This design therefore targets a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC footprint**: 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes. The FC can still be a 20×20 stack, or it can be an AIO board.

3. **Burst definition.** "80 A burst" is taken as **per channel, 10 s maximum**, on **6S (25.2 V max)**. The continuous rating is assumed to be about 55 A.

**40 V parts on 6S:** the margin is about 37 %. This is only acceptable with ≥4×10 µF X7R per phase leg plus bulk capacitance (see §6). The switch-node ringing must be verified at **< 34 V**. Alternatively, use 60 V FETs and accept roughly 1.6× higher R_ds(on).

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

**Current path.** In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two FETs:

- during the on-time: HS(leg A) and LS(leg B);
- during the off-time: LS(leg A) and LS(leg B).

This holds regardless of duty cycle. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Per-FET share.** Each of the 6 FETs takes its share over one electrical cycle:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}$$

**Temperature coupling.** Let T_b be the local board temperature and R_jb = R_jc + R_cb. Then:

$$T_j = T_b + R_{jb}\left[P_{sw,FET} + \frac{I^2R_{25}}{3}\big(1+\alpha(T_j-25)\big)\right]$$

Define $P_{25}=I^2R_{25}/3$ and solve for T_j:

$$\boxed{T_j = \frac{T_b + R_{jb}\left[P_{sw,FET} + P_{25}(1-25\alpha)\right]}{1 - \alpha R_{jb} P_{25}}}$$
