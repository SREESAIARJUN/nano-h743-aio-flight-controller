# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #5): System Architecture & Design Model

---

## 0. Problems With the Brief

Three assumptions in the brief would cause a failed design if not addressed first.

| # | Issue | Consequence | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 / AIO design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware). The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. | Running 4× sensorless FOC/6-step plus flight control on one H7 has no firmware support and no fault isolation. | H743 = flight controller. 4× AT32F421 = ESC MCUs. DShot600 bidirectional link between them. |
| 2 | **"80A per channel" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally a burst rating measured in seconds** (see §2.6). | Four channels at 80A dissipate about 100 W into about 2 J/K of board. | The rating is treated as 80A for ≤1 s per channel. Continuous is derived separately. |
| 3 | **4 × 80A phase current at high throttle is about 300A of battery current.** | Exceeds XT60 (≈60A continuous) and XT90 limits, typical 12 AWG leads, and any practical shunt. | Battery current is budgeted separately. The "80A" is per-channel phase current, not simultaneous on all four at full duty. |

All component values below are representative engineering assumptions, chosen to make the model concrete. Every parameter is a symbol, so you can substitute your selected MOSFET's datasheet values.

### 2.2 Conduction losses (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification, complementary PWM)

At any instant two phases conduct. The PWM-ing phase alternates between its high-side FET (duty D) and its low-side FET (synchronous rectifier, 1−D). The return phase's low-side FET is held on for the full commutation sector.

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ R_{HS}\,D + R_{LS}(1-D) + R_{LS} \right] = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds,hot} \quad (R_{HS}=R_{LS})$$

Because the result is independent of D for equal FETs, burst loss is the same at any throttle that produces 80A of phase current.

$$R_{ds,hot} = k_T R_{ds,25} = 1.5 \times 1.2 = 1.8\ \text{m}\Omega$$
