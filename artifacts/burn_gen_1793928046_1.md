## 1. Motor and Current-Loop Model (FOC)

### 1.1 Electrical model in the rotor (dq) frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The electrical angle and speed follow from the encoder:

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}),\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m
$$

For a surface-mount PMSM ($L_d = L_q = L$), electromagnetic torque is:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m
$$

### 1.2 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park and inverse Park transforms:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e\\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Current PI design by pole-zero cancellation

The plant per axis is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choose the PI controller $C_i(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with:

$$
K_{p,i} = \omega_{ci} L,\qquad K_{i,i} = \omega_{ci} R
\;\Rightarrow\; T_i(s) = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}
$$

The bandwidth is chosen as $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. With $f_{pwm} = 20$ kHz, this gives $\omega_{ci} \approx 2\pi\cdot 1000$ rad/s.

The discrete form uses backward Euler with the computational delay $T_s$ accounted for; the effective phase loss at $\omega_{ci}$ is $1.5\,\omega_{ci}T_s \approx 27^\circ$.

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward are applied to the PI outputs:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
