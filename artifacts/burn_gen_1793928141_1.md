## 0. Feasibility Constraint

The thermal model in §3 shows that **80 A per channel on all four channels simultaneously cannot be sustained on a nano form factor (≤ 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm)**. It is only marginally achievable on 30.5×30.5 mm for about 1–2 s without a heatsink.

The rest of this package therefore uses the following definitions:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Supply | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst rating | 80 A per channel for ≤ 2 s, single or dual channel; 4-channel simultaneous burst limited by the thermal budget in §3.4 |
| Continuous rating | 45–50 A per channel (derived in §3.5) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), center-aligned |
| Architecture | STM32H743 FC sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32) |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation, each 60° electrical sector has one phase PWM'd, one phase held low, and one floating.

- Synchronous rectification means current flows through either the PWM phase's high-side (HS) FET during D·T_sw or its low-side (LS) FET during (1−D)·T_sw.
- The held-low LS FET conducts for the entire sector.

The instantaneous conducting resistance is therefore always 2·R_DS(on), independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

With trapezoidal current, I_rms ≈ I_dc for ripple well below the DC value. Ripple at 48 kHz on low-inductance motors adds a factor of (1 + r²/12), where r is the peak-to-peak ripple divided by I_dc:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 R_{DS}(T_j)\, I^2 \left(1+\frac{r^2}{12}\right)$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution over one electrical revolution

Each FET pair spends 120° conducting: 60° as the held-low or PWM'd device and 60° in the complementary role.

| FET | Average current share | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| HS (PWM phase) | (1/3)·D | P_HS = (1/3)·D·I²·R_DS |
| LS (PWM complement + held-low) | (1/3)(1−D) + 1/3 | P_LS = (1/3)(2−D)·I²·R_DS |

The **low-side FETs are the hotspot at partial throttle.** At D = 1 the HS and LS shares equalize at 1/3 each.

### 1.5 Self-consistent junction temperature

Conduction loss raises T_j, which raises R_DS, which raises loss again. Solve the fixed point:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta,eff}\, P(T_j), \qquad P(T_j)=k\, I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_{\theta,eff}\,k I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_{\theta,eff}\,k I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion.** The denominator must stay positive:

$$R_{\theta,eff}\, k\, I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha < 1$$

Worked example: worst-case LS FET at D → 0, so k = 2/3; I = 80 A; R_θ,eff = 15 K/W (FET to board hotspot, no heatsink).

$$15 \cdot 0.667 \cdot 6400 \cdot 0.0012 \cdot 0.0045 = 0.346$$

The result is below 1, so the design is stable. The R_θ,eff threshold at which steady state diverges at 80 A is about 43 K/W.

### 1.6 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C
