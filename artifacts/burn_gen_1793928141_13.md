## 0. Architecture and Baseline Parameters

**System partition.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which accepts bidirectional DShot600 from the H743.

```
LiPo 6S ──┬─► 4× 3-phase bridges (24 FETs) ──► M1..M4
          ├─► ESC MCU(s) (AT32F421 ×4 or similar) ◄── DShot600 (bidir) ── STM32H743
          └─► 5V/3V3 BEC ──► H743, IMU, baro, OSD, RX
```

> ⚠ **Engineering reality check (read first).** 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board means about 320 A total battery current and about 80 W of dissipation at peak (derived below). This is **only achievable as a seconds-scale burst** limited by the board's thermal mass, not as a continuous rating. Section 2.4 quantifies the burst time. Marketing an "80 A" rating on this form factor should state the burst duration and conditions explicitly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step commutation topology

In any commutation sector, two half-bridges carry current:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D), with dead time accounted for separately.
- **Static leg:** the LS FET is held ON for the full sector.

Phase current is quasi-DC across a PWM period because motor inductance is much larger than the inductance needed to keep ripple small. RMS is therefore taken as I_pk, with an optional ripple term:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[\underbrace{D}_{HS} + \underbrace{(1-D)}_{LS\ sync}] + \underbrace{I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}_{static\ LS} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

With synchronous rectification, total conduction loss is **independent of duty**. Its distribution between FETs is not, as the next table shows.

**Per-FET worst-case conduction loss:**

| FET | Loss | @ 80 A, T_j = 125 °C (R = 1.45 mΩ) |
|---|---|---|
| Static LS | I²R | **9.28 W** (hot spot) |
| PWM HS, D = 1 | I²R·D | 9.28 W |
| PWM LS, D → 0 | I²R(1−D) | 9.28 W |

**Per channel:** P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.45 mΩ = **18.6 W**

**Four channels:** **74.2 W**

### 1.4 Electro-thermal self-consistency

R_ds depends on T_j, so solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta CB})$$

$$T_j = \frac{T_b + I^2 R_{25}(1 - 25\alpha)\,R_\theta}{1 - I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_\theta}$$

With T_b = 100 °C and R_θ = 1.5 K/W:

- Numerator: 100 + 6400·0.001·0.8875·1.5 = 108.5
- Denominator: 1 − 6400·0.001·0.0045·1.5 = 0.957
- **T_j ≈ 113 °C**, so P_FET ≈ 6.4 × 1.40 = 8.96 W

**Thermal runaway criterion.** The loop is stable as long as

$$I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_\theta < 1$$

Here that product is 0.043, a large margin.
