## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three issues in the brief need resolving before the math can be trusted.

1. **The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller for four motors.** Only TIM1, TIM8 and HRTIM provide complementary outputs with dead-time, so at most two sensorless 3-phase bridges are practical. ArduPilot also has no motor-commutation firmware. The standard and recommended split is:
   - **H743**: flight controller running ArduPilot. It emits bi-directional DShot600.
   - **4× AT32F421 or STM32G071**: ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each generates its own 48 kHz complementary PWM.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is thermally implausible except as a sub-second transient.** Section 3 quantifies this.
3. **The netlist in Section 5 is a reference design.** Part numbers are representative. Verify every pin against the datasheet and the ArduPilot hwdef before layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two legs conduct at any instant:
- One leg's low-side (or high-side) FET is held fully ON for the 60° sector.
- The other leg PWMs complementarily, with HS on for D and LS on for (1−D).

With synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly **two** channels in series, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

Including inductor ripple (triangular ripple ΔI):

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}
$$

At 48 kHz with typical 5" to 7" motor inductance, ΔI/I is about 10%. The ripple term adds less than 0.1%, so it is neglected.

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per motor | Per FET (2 conducting) | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 7.7 W | 61.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.70 mΩ | **21.8 W** | **10.9 W** | **87.1 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.86 mΩ | 23.8 W | 11.9 W | 95.2 W |

### 1.3 Electro-thermal feedback (closed form)

Let P₂₅ = 2I²R_ds,25 and R_th = junction-to-ambient. Then:

$$
T_j = T_a + R_{th} P_{25}\big[1+\alpha(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

$$
\Rightarrow\quad T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{th}P_{25}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha R_{th} P_{25}}
$$

**Thermal runaway criterion (steady state):**

$$
\alpha\, R_{th}\, P_{25} \ge 1 \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; R_{th} \ge \frac{1}{0.0055 \times 15.4} = 11.8\ \text{K/W (per motor channel)}
$$

A nano board in free air exceeds this. **Continuous 80 A is mathematically unstable**, and only transient operation is possible (Section 3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The HS takes the hard transition, while the LS commutes softly through its body diode.

### 2.1 Transition times (Miller-plateau limited)

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{src}} + t_{gs} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{0.3\,\text{A}} + 10\,\text{ns} \approx 50\ \text{ns}
$$

$$
t_f \approx \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{0.6\,\text{A}} + 10\,\text{ns} \approx 30\ \text{ns}
$$

### 2.2 Loss components (per motor, at I = 80 A, V = 25.2 V)

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·80 ns·48 k | **3.87 W** |
| Dead-time diode | 2·V_f·I·t_dt·f_sw = 2·0.8·80·100 ns·48 k | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss charge | 2 × ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.05 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw = 2·60 nC·10·48 k | 0.06 W (dissipated in driver/R_g) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 4.6 W** |

### 2.3 Frequency sensitivity

Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw: about 0.096 W per kHz per motor at 80 A.

| f_sw | P_sw per motor |
|---|---|
| 24 kHz | 2.3 W |
| 48 kHz | 4.6 W |
| 96 kHz | 9.2 W |

At 80 A, conduction dominates by roughly 5:1. Dropping to 24 kHz saves only about 9% of total loss but worsens ripple and audible noise. **48 kHz is a reasonable operating point.**
