## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Standard practice, which this design follows, is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (LQFP100 / TFBGA100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM telemetry consumer |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 | Six-step / sinusoidal commutation, 48 kHz PWM, DShot slave |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | n/a | High- and low-side drive, dead time |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | n/a | Inverter |

Putting six-step commutation for four motors on the H743 would cost determinism, isolation, and firmware maintainability. It is not recommended.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path (Six-Step, Complementary PWM)

In any 60° commutation sector, current flows through exactly two legs:

- **PWM leg:**
  - High-side FET conducts for duty D.
  - Low-side FET conducts for (1−D), as a synchronous rectifier.
  - Body diode conducts only during dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100 % of the sector.

So at every instant, two channels of R_ds(on) are in series with the phase current. This holds independent of D, apart from dead time.

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1} \;\Rightarrow\; k_{T}(125^\circ C)\approx 1.6$$

### 1.3 Per-Motor Conduction Loss

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\,\left(1 - 2 t_{dt} f_{sw}\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\right) \approx 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The dead-time correction is under 0.5 % and is neglected.

| Condition | R_ds | P_cond per motor | P_cond ×4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80 A, T_j = 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2·6400·0.001 = **12.8 W** | 51.2 W |
| 80 A, T_j = 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0016 = **20.5 W** | 81.9 W |
| 30 A, T_j = 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 2·900·0.00145 = **2.6 W** | 10.4 W |

### 1.4 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET carries current for 120° electrical. Averaged over an electrical period, which is less than 1 ms at typical eRPM and therefore much shorter than the package thermal time constant:

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{P_{cond}}{6} \cdot (\text{weighting}) \;\approx\; 3.4\ \text{W at 80 A, 125}^\circ\text{C}$$

The high-side FET also carries all switching loss (Section 2). It is the hot spot.

### 1.5 Copper and Interconnect Losses (Often Forgotten)

$$R_{trace} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}\,L}{w\,t}, \quad \rho_{Cu}=1.72\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\text{m}$$

For 2 oz copper (70 µm), 15 mm path length, 6 mm width, two parallel layers:

R ≈ 1.72e-8 · 0.015 / (0.006 · 70e-6 · 2) ≈ **0.31 mΩ**

That is comparable to the FET itself, giving 0.31 mΩ·80² ≈ **2.0 W per motor**. Add the battery pad and shunt (Section 5.3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-Switching Overlap (High-Side FET)

$$P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.5 · 25.2 · 80 · 20e-9 · 48e3 = **0.968 W**

### 2.2 Reverse Recovery (Low-Side Body Diode, Dissipated in the High-Side FET)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

= 30e-9 · 25.2 · 48e3 = **0.036 W**

Q_rr scales with di/dt and current. Use the datasheet value at 80 A, which may be 2–3× higher.

### 2.3 Output Capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}\ (\text{per switching FET pair, effective})$$

≈ 0.5 · 40e-9 · 25.2 · 48e3 = **0.024 W**

### 2.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.8 · 80 · 200e-9 · 48e3 = **0.614 W**

You can cut this by lowering dead time to about 50 ns once the gate loop is validated.

### 2.5 Gate Drive (Dissipated in R_g and the FD6288Q, Not the FET)

$$P_{gate} = Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw}\times N_{switching} = 50\text{nC}\cdot 10\cdot 48\text{k}\cdot 2 = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Summary per Motor at 80 A Burst

| Loss term | W | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (T_j = 125 °C) | 20.5 | All FETs in path |
| V-I overlap | 0.97 | High-side FET |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 | Low-side FET |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.06 | High-side FET |
| PCB copper | 2.0 | Planes |
| Gate drive | 0.05 | Driver |
| **Total per motor** | **≈ 24.2 W** | |
| **Total ×4 (all motors at burst)** | **≈ 97 W** | |

Efficiency check: input ≈ 80 A × 22.2 V × D. At D ≈ 0.85, P_in ≈ 1.5 kW per motor, so the ESC loss is about 1.6 %. That is realistic.
