## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At an initial guess of T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C:

R = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.66 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current distribution (6-step, synchronous rectification)

At any instant, exactly two legs are active. In the driven leg, current flows through the high-side FET during D and through the low-side FET during 1−D. The return leg's low side conducts continuously. Therefore **two FETs always carry I<sub>ph</sub>**, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.66\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.2\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$

Each FET is active for 120° of the electrical cycle, so I<sub>rms,FET</sub> = I<sub>ph</sub>/√3 = 46.2 A.

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R}{3} = 3.54\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 DC-link capacitor ripple

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}\ \le\ 0.5\,I_{ph} = 40\ \text{A per channel (worst case at } D=0.5)$$

Four channels switching in phase would be catastrophic for a nano board. You need:
- Low-ESR MLCC banks at each bridge (≥ 4 × 10 µF/50 V X7R in 1210, with DC-bias derating applied).
- One bulk low-ESR electrolytic (≥ 470 µF/35 V) on the battery pads.
- AM32 PWM phase staggering between MCUs, if available.
