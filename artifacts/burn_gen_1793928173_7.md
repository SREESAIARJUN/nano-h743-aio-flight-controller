## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Finding

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage directly. A production AIO partitions the work as follows:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), up to 480 MHz | ArduPilot, sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (QFN28), AM32 firmware | 6-step/FOC commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class, ~1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink) | Bootstrap high-side drive |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 5×6 / 3.3×3.3 | Half-bridges for phases A/B/C |

### 0.2 Headline result

The physics below shows that an 80 A per-motor rating is a **seconds-scale transient** on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm). It is not a thermal steady state. The numbers:

- **Burst duration:** about 2–3 s (all four motors) or about 10 s (one motor) from a 50 °C board.
- **Continuous rating:** about 20 A per motor.

The burst rating must be firmware-enforced (Section 4.5). Otherwise the label is not defensible.
