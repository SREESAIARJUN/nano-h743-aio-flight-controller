# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Design Package (Job #93)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The intended architecture is:

- **FC MCU:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs (STM32G071 running AM32 is assumed). Each runs 6-step commutation at 48 kHz complementary PWM and drives one 3-phase bridge.

If you meant a single H743 driving all 12 half-bridges directly, that requires custom firmware, not ArduPilot. That design is out of scope here.

**Bottom line up front.** 80 A on a nano (20×20 or 30×30 class) board is a ~2-second burst rating, not a continuous one. The math below shows why.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, two MOSFET channels always carry the full phase current:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty D and the low-side (LS) conducts for 1−D. That is always one channel.
- **Return leg:** the LS is fully on.

Therefore conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond}(T_j) = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.95 mΩ | 25.0 W |
| 125 °C | 2.10 mΩ | 26.9 W |

Copper loss adds I²·R_cu = 6400 × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **1.9 W** per channel.
