## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Key Findings

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In every production AIO and 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421, STM32G071 or GD32 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. This document models that partition.

### 0.2 Key findings

1. **80 A per channel is a burst rating of about 2–3 s on this footprint, not 10 s.** At 80 A on all four channels the board dissipates about 135 W against roughly 5 J/K of thermal mass.
2. **A true 20×20 "nano" board cannot meet 80 A at all.** Its realistic ceiling is about 40–45 A burst. The math below uses the 30.5 mm footprint. Section 2.5 gives the 20×20 scaling.
3. **Dead-time body-diode conduction is as large as the hard-switching loss** at 48 kHz. It is the main reason 48 kHz runs hotter than 24 kHz.
