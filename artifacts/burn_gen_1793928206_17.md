**Job #64, Engineering Design Note, Rev A**

## 0. Feasibility Finding: Read This First

The model below shows that **80 A per motor cannot be sustained on a nano-format board (20×20 to 25.5×25.5 mm)** for more than about 1 s. Here is the arithmetic:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Per-ESC loss at 80 A, Tj = 125 °C | ≈ 33 W |
| 4-motor simultaneous burst | ≈ 131 W |
| Board lumped heat capacity (20×20, 6L 2oz) | ≈ 1.6 J/K |
| Adiabatic heating rate at full burst | ≈ 80 K/s |
| Usable burst time (50 → 110 °C board) | **≈ 0.7 s** |
| Continuous capability under prop-wash cooling | **≈ 13–15 A per motor** |
| Battery current at 4×80 A | 320 A (≈ 8 kW at 6S), beyond nano pad/connector capability |

**Recommendation:** Spec the board as **"15 A continuous / 30–40 A burst (≤ 5 s)"**, or move to a 30.5×30.5 mm format with dedicated FET copper pours if 80 A is a hard requirement. The full derivation follows so you can re-run it with your actual BOM.

**Assumptions are flagged with [A].** Device parameters are representative of the 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET class. Substitute datasheet values before release.
