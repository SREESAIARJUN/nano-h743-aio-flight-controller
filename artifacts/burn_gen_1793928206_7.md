# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #59): Design Model

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel has a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to those four ESC MCUs. They return eRPM telemetry over the same wire. The 48 kHz PWM is generated inside each ESC MCU. Every model below follows this partition.

**Feasibility flag.** "80 A burst" per motor on a nano-format board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting) means 320 A aggregate from the battery. An XT60 is rated for roughly 60 A continuous, and pads and copper face similar limits. I model 80 A per motor as a short burst and derive the allowable burst duration rather than assuming it is sustainable.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step conduction with synchronous rectification

The scheme is trapezoidal, with complementary PWM on the active high side. In each 60° electrical sector, three devices conduct:

- **Active high-side (HS):** conducts for fraction D.
- **Complementary low-side (LS) of the same leg:** conducts for 1 − D (sync rectification during the off-time).
- **Return LS of the opposite phase:** conducts 100% of the time.

The instantaneous bridge conduction loss is

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of duty, which is a useful property of synchronous six-step.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

- At T_j = 25 °C: P = 2 × 80² × 1.0 mΩ = **12.8 W per motor**
- At T_j = 125 °C: P = 2 × 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per motor**

### 1.3 Copper trace loss

These losses are often neglected but are significant here.

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho\, L}{w\, t}, \quad \rho_{Cu} = 1.72\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}\ (+0.393\%/K)$$

Take a phase path of L = 15 mm, w = 4 mm, two 2-oz layers in parallel (t = 140 µm):

- R_Cu = 1.72e-8 × 0.015 / (0.004 × 140e-6) = **0.46 mΩ**
- P = 80² × 0.46 mΩ ≈ **2.9 W per phase path**. Two phases conduct, giving about **5.9 W per motor**.

The board copper is roughly 30% of the MOSFET conduction loss. Use 4+ layers in parallel for the phase and bus planes, and stitch them with via arrays.

### 1.4 Per-FET loss distribution

Each device spends about 1/3 of the electrical period in one of the conducting roles:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,bridge}}{6}\cdot 1 \approx 3.2\ \text{W (hot)}$$

The peak instantaneous loss in the return LS device is I²R = 9.6 W.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg's HS/LS pair switches. Each loss mechanism is computed per motor at 80 A and 25.2 V.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched HS)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{e-}9 \times 48\text{e}3 = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode conduction during dead time

There are two dead-time intervals per switching period:

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{e-}9 \times 48\text{e}3 = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

This term is larger than the overlap loss. Reducing dead time to 100 ns halves it, but you must verify shoot-through margin against driver propagation skew.

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{e-}9 \times 25.2 \times 48\text{e}3 = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

The reverse-recovery current also adds about Q_rr/t_r of turn-on current stress. This contribution is included in the margin.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.018\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET:

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2 = 50\text{e-}9 \times 12 \times 48\text{e}3 \times 2 = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Total per motor at the 80 A burst, T_j = 125 °C

| Component | W |
|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 19.2 |
| Switching overlap | 0.97 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.12 |
| PCB copper | 5.9 |
| **Total per motor** | **≈ 27.4** |
| **4 motors** | **≈ 110 W** |
