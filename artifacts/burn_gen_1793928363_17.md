## 1. Field-Oriented Control (innermost loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here θ_off is found at startup by applying the voltage vector V_d at θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder once the rotor settles.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, three phase shunts)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

### 1.3 Park and inverse Park

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e\\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI controller (pole-zero cancellation)

The PI zero is placed on the electrical pole R/L, which makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_ci:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

$$v_{d}^{*} = \text{PI}(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad
v_{q}^{*} = \text{PI}(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e \hat L i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m$$

- Choose ω_ci ≈ 2π·1–2 kHz. Keep it at or below roughly f_PWM/10 to leave margin for the 1.5-sample computation and PWM delay.
- Set i_d* = 0. Gimbal motors operate below base speed, so field weakening is not needed.

### 1.6 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

The output is limited to the linear SVPWM circle:

$$|v_{dq}| \le V_{\max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}$$

The d-axis has priority. Then:

$$v_q^{\max}=\sqrt{V_{\max}^2-v_d^2}$$

The integrator uses back-calculation anti-windup:

$$x_I \mathrel{+}= T_s\left[K_i e + K_{aw}\,(u_{\text{sat}}-u)\right],\qquad K_{aw}\approx \frac{K_i}{K_p}$$

### 1.7 SVPWM using min-max zero-sequence injection

$$v_{a,b,c} \text{ from inverse Clarke},\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12\left[\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\right],\quad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}$$
