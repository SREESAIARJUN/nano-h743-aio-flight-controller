## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle, with encoder offset θ₀ found by d-axis alignment:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_0) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform uses the transpose. The delay-compensated angle for the inverse transform is

$$\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

The 1.5-sample term accounts for one sample of computation delay plus half a sample of PWM zero-order hold.

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The dq-frame plant is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

where the flux linkage is λ_m = Kt / (1.5 p).

Control law, with cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choose the closed-loop current bandwidth ω_c and set:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Keep ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/20. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1 kHz, so K_p = 13.2 V/A and K_i = 32 700 V/(A·s).

### 1.3 Voltage limiting and SVPWM

- **Circular limit.** If |v*| > V_dc/√3, scale v* to that magnitude, with priority given to v_d.
- **Zero-sequence injection (min-max SVPWM):**

$$v_{0} = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c) + \min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$
