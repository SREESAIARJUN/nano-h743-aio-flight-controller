# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #43): System Model and Design Review

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Feasibility Flags

Several premises in the brief need correcting before any math can be trusted.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|-------|-----------|
| F1 | **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** Bidirectional DShot is an FC→ESC protocol. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by per-channel ESC MCUs. | Architecture: H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Four ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or a single-chip 4-channel BLHeli_32 part) run 48 kHz PWM. |
| F2 | **80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) board is not physically achievable**, even as a burst. Commercial 20×20 4-in-1 ESCs are rated about 12–25 A continuous. The 24 MOSFETs alone (3.3×3.3 mm DFN) consume most of the board area. | Either (a) treat 80 A as **total** board burst (20 A per channel), or (b) move to a 30.5×30.5 mm or larger form factor. Both cases are modeled below. |
| F3 | 4 × 80 A = 320 A battery current. This exceeds XT60 (60 A continuous) and XT90, and needs roughly 8 AWG leads. | Confirms F2. |
| F4 | No MOSFET, gate driver, stackup, or burst duration was specified. | Parametric model given. The worked numbers use stated assumptions (Section 1.1) that must be replaced with datasheet values. |

## 1. Power-Stage Loss Model (per ESC channel, 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |

| I_ph | Phase current (burst) | 80 A (Case A), 20 A (Case B) |

| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse recovery charge | 50 nC |
| V_f | Body-diode forward voltage at I_ph | 0.8 V |

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

The on-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

In 6-step commutation with complementary sync rectification, current always flows through exactly **two** conducting FET-equivalents:
- the PWM leg (high side for D, low side for 1−D), and
- the static low-side leg.

Therefore:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each of the 6 FETs carries current one third of the electrical period, so:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}$$

**Case A** (80 A, T_j = 125 °C → R_ds = 1.6 mΩ):
- P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = **20.5 W per channel**
- 6.8 W per FET

**Case B** (20 A, T_j = 125 °C → R_ds = 4.0 mΩ):
- P_cond = 2 × 400 × 4.0 mΩ = **3.2 W per channel**

**Copper loss.** Add the copper resistance of the current path:

$$R_{Cu} = \rho_{Cu}\,\frac{L}{w\,t}\,[1+0.00393(T-20)]$$

For example, 2 oz (70 µm) copper, 15 mm path, 5 mm wide, 2 parallel layers gives ≈ 0.36 mΩ at 20 °C. At 80 A that is 2.3 W per channel.

### 1.3 Switching Loss (hard-switched edges, one leg switching at a time)

Transition times:

$$t_{r}\approx t_{f}\approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_g}=15\text{ ns}$$

Each loss mechanism:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f)f_{sw}$$

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I_{ph}\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus}\,Q_{rr}\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}\quad(\text{2 devices} \times \tfrac12)$$

$$P_{gate} = Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}\times 2$$

| Term | Case A (80 A) | Case B (20 A) |
|---|---|---|
| P_sw | ½·25.2·80·30n·48k = **1.45 W** | 0.36 W |
| P_dt | 0.8·80·400n·48k = **1.23 W** | 0.31 W |
| P_rr | 25.2·50n·48k = 0.06 W | 0.06 W |
| P_oss | 1.5n·635·48k = 0.05 W | 0.05 W |
| P_gate (driver dissipation) | 60n·10·48k·2 = 0.06 W | 0.06 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | **2.85 W** | **0.84 W** |

### 1.4 Totals

| | Case A (80 A/ch) | Case B (20 A/ch) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 20.5 W | 3.2 W |
| Copper | 2.3 W | 0.15 W |
| Switching | 2.85 W | 0.84 W |
| **Per channel** | **25.7 W** | **4.2 W** |
| **Board (×4)** | **≈103 W** | **≈17 W** |
| Efficiency at full duty | ≈98.7 % per channel (of ~2 kW) | ≈99.2 % |

Conduction dominates at 80 A, at roughly 80 % of losses. Lowering f_sw from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only about 1.4 W per channel. The real levers are R_ds,on and copper.

### 1.5 Bulk Capacitor Ripple

$$I_{C,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}\;\Rightarrow\;\max = I_{ph}/2$$

- **Case A:** 40 A RMS per channel, uncorrelated across 4 channels → ≈40–80 A RMS board total. This requires roughly 20+ ceramic MLCCs plus low-ESR polymer and a 1000 µF+ external low-ESR cap. It does not fit a nano board.
- **Case B:** 10 A RMS per channel. Use 4–6 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 per channel plus an external 470 µF/35 V.
