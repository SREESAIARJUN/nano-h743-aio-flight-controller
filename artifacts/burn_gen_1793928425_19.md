## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

Three issues must be settled before the math is meaningful.

### 0.1 The H743 does not commutate the motors
ArduPilot does not run BLDC/FOC commutation. "Bidirectional DShot600" means the H743 sends digital throttle to four separate ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware and generates the 48 kHz PWM.

The architecture is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 BDShot──> 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32)
                                               └─> 3-phase gate driver ─> 6× N-FET half-bridges ─> motor
```

The 48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-firmware parameter (AM32 `PWM frequency`). It is not an ArduPilot parameter.

### 0.2 "Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict
A 20×20 or 30×30 mm board with four 80 A channels dissipates more than 80 W at full burst (derived below). Section 3 shows the result: a burst rating holds for a few seconds with all four motors at 80 A, not continuously.

This board is a **burst-rated** design. The numbers below define the burst window explicitly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance
$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

At a design-point junction temperature T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.51 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step with synchronous rectification
During any commutation sector, the phase current I flows through two phase legs.

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high-side FET for duty D and the low-side FET for (1−D). With synchronous rectification, loss is I²R_DS regardless of D.
- **Return leg:** current flows through a continuously-on low-side FET, giving I²R_DS.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

**At 80 A and 1.51 mΩ:**
$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.51\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.3\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

Add the PCB copper path, roughly 0.3 mΩ for the round trip with 2 oz and short pours: 6400 × 0.3 m = **1.9 W**.

**Per-FET average:** each FET conducts about 1/3 of an electrical revolution. The electrical frequency is far above the junction thermal time constant, so averaging is valid:
$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 3.2\ \text{W}
$$
