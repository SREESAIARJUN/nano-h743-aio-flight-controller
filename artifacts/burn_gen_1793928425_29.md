## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 System partitioning

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. This design has two processing domains:

| Domain | MCU | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz | ArduPilot. Generates 4× bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 / BLHeli_32. 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM. |

The "48 kHz switching" in this document is the ESC firmware PWM frequency. It is independent of the DShot link.
