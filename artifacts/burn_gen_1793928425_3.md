## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) rectification, two phase legs carry current I at any instant:

- **Chopping leg:** HS conducts for duty D. LS conducts for (1−D) as the synchronous freewheel.
- **Return leg:** LS stays on for the whole 60° sector.

Each leg therefore always presents exactly one R_ds(on) in the current path.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds,hot} = 0.90\times1.6 = 1.44\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Add the copper path to each leg: phase pad, via fields and bus planes. For 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner copper on a 30.5 mm board, this is about 0.25 mΩ.

$$R_{leg} = R_{ds,hot} + R_{Cu} = 1.69\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{leg}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.69\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.6\ W}\ \ (\text{of which FETs: }18.4\text{ W, copper: }3.2\text{ W})$$

### 1.4 Per-FET loss (averaged over an electrical cycle)

Each phase spends 120° high, 120° low and 120° floating. Over one electrical revolution, a FET carries current for about 1/3 of the time. The electrical frequency is about 1–3 kHz, which is much faster than the junction time constant (>1 ms), so averaging is valid.

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{ds,hot}}{3} = \frac{80^2 \times 1.44\text{m}}{3} = \mathbf{3.07\ W}$$

### 1.5 Ripple correction

The phase current ripple is:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{2\,L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

For L_ph = 15 µH (typical 2207-class motor, line-to-line 30 µH) and D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{2 \times 15\mu \times 48\text{k}} = 4.4\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

The resulting RMS correction is:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} \Rightarrow +0.025\%$$

This is negligible. The 48 kHz choice is justified by ripple and noise, not by loss.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the chopping leg switches hard. Its HS FET sees hard turn-on and turn-off, and its LS FET has soft (ZVS-like) transitions plus body-diode recovery.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The LS body diode recovers when the HS turns on.

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

At 80 A, Q_rr scales roughly with di/dt and I_F. Derating by ×2 gives about 0.12 W.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot \tfrac12 = 45\text{n}\times 25.2 \times 48\text{k}\times0.5 = 0.027\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period.

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_{SD}\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2\times0.8\times80\times50\text{n}\times48\text{k} = 0.31\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

The loss below is dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g, not in the FET. Two FETs switch per channel.

$$P_{g} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 55\text{n}\times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.053\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Channel totals at 80 A burst

| Loss term | W per channel |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 18.43 |
| Conduction (copper) | 3.20 |
| V–I switching | 1.45 |
| Reverse recovery (derated) | 0.12 |
| C_oss | 0.03 |
| Dead time | 0.31 |
| Gate (driver side) | 0.05 |
| **Total** | **23.6 W** |

**4-in-1 total at an all-motor 80 A burst: 94.4 W.**

At 80 A × 4 = 320 A from 25.2 V this is roughly 8 kW in, so efficiency is about 98.8%. Power-stage loss is not the limit; the limit is heat density on a 30.5 mm board.

### 2.7 Frequency sensitivity

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial f_{sw}} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) + Q_{rr}V + \tfrac12 Q_{oss}V + 2V_{SD}I t_{dt} + 2Q_gV_{drv} \approx 40.6\ \mu\text{J}$$

Moving from 24 kHz to 48 kHz therefore costs about 0.97 W per channel at 80 A.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (Cauer form, per FET)

```
P_FET ──[Rth_jc 1.0 K/W]──┬──[Rth_c-pcb 1.5 K/W]──┬──[Rth_spread 6 K/W]── T_board node
                          │                       │
                       Cj=0.02 J/K            Cpad=0.15 J/K

T_board node (24 FETs + copper) ──[Rth_board-air]── T_amb
                                │
                          C_board ≈ 9 J/K
```

- 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz outer copper: FR4 about 4.5 g × 1.1 J/gK ≈ 5 J/K.
- Copper about 3.2 g × 0.385 J
