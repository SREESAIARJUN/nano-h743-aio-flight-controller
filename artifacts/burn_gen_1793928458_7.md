# Job #48: STM32H743 Nano FC with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC

## Design Basis, Loss Model, DShot Timer/DMA Allocation, Netlists, and Thermal Analysis

> **Scope note.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. In this design the H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32), and each ESC MCU generates its own 48 kHz complementary PWM. The loss model (Parts 2–4) covers the ESC power stage. The timer/DMA allocation (Part 5) covers the H743.

> **Key finding (read first).** An 80 A burst on all four channels of a nano-format board (about 37×37 mm, 8-layer) dissipates roughly **125 W**. Board thermal mass is about 6 J/K, so the board heats at **~20 K/s**. Starting from a 40 °C board, an all-four-motor 80 A burst is thermally limited to **~2.5–3 s**. A single-channel 80 A transient is limited to about 10 s. Continuous capability in prop-wash airflow is about **30–35 A/channel**. The "80A" rating must be specified as a burst with an explicit duration (Part 4.5).
