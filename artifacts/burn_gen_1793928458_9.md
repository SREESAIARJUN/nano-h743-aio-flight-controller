## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Engineering Flags

No MOSFET, gate-driver or board stack-up was specified, so this model is parametric. I chose representative values for a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET class (Table 0.1). **Before tape-out, replace every parameter in Table 0.1 with values from the selected part's datasheet and rerun the equations.** The equations are exact for the stated topology; the numbers are only as good as the inputs.

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 does not switch the bridges. In every ArduPilot-compatible AIO design:

- The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600.
- One or more ESC MCUs (AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per channel, or BLHeli_32) run 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM.
- Those ESC MCUs drive the gate drivers.
