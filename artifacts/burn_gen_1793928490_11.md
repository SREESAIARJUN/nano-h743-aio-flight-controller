## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors with ESC-grade timing and safety isolation. The industry-standard and recommended topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC)  ──bidir DShot600 ×4──►  4 × ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                                                     │
                                                     ├─ 3-phase gate driver (e.g., FD6288Q / 3× half-bridge)
                                                     └─ 6 × N-MOSFET bridge per motor (24 FETs total)
```

The 48 kHz PWM is a property of the ESC firmware (AM32 `PWM_FREQ`). The H743 only generates and decodes DShot.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM'd phase:** current alternates between the high-side (HS) FET (duty D) and the low-side (LS) FET (duty 1−D, synchronous rectification).
- **Static phase:** the LS FET is continuously on.

The instantaneous conduction loss per motor bridge is therefore duty-independent, ignoring dead time:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I
