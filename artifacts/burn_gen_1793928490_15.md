## 0. Findings That Constrain the Requirements

These issues change the architecture or the spec, so they come first.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---------|-------------|
| F1 | ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 runs flight control and emits DShot. Each bridge needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. | The architecture has two MCU domains (§1). The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC-firmware setting, not an H743 function. |
| F2 | An 80 A burst on all four motors at once means about 320 A at the battery. A single XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous, and a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) cannot sink about 100 W of FET loss plus about 30 W of copper loss (§5, §6). | The burst rating must be specified as: 80 A on any single channel, ≤ 2 s, with an aggregate limit (I propose 160 A total, ≤ 2 s). Otherwise the spec is not physically achievable. |
| F3 | Bidirectional DShot needs timer input capture on the output pin. Complementary outputs (TIMx_CHyN) cannot input-capture. | Motor pins must be on non-N channels (§8). |
| F4 | A single current-sense shunt on the 320 A bus at 0.25 mΩ dissipates 25 W. | Use per-ESC shunts or a Hall sensor (§7). |

The rest of this document models a **single channel at 80 A** and then aggregates.

## 1. System Architecture

```
VBAT (6S, 25.2 V max) ──┬── Bulk cap (external, ≥1000 µF low-ESR) + on-board MLCC bank
                        ├── 4× [ESC MCU → 3-phase gate driver → 6× N-MOSFET half-bridges → Motor]
                        ├── Current sense (per-ESC shunt + amplifier → H743 ADC)
                        └── Buck 25 V→5 V (3 A) → LDO 3.3 V → STM32H743 + IMU/baro/OSD

STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor)──► ESC MCU 1..4 (AM32, PWM 48 kHz)
```
