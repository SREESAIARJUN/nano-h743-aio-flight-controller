## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation geometry

Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM works as follows:

* In each 60° sector, one high-side FET (HS) of phase X is PWM-active at duty D.
* One low-side FET (LS) of phase Y is held fully on.
* The LS of phase X conducts as a synchronous rectifier during (1 − D).
* Each phase spends 120° as HS-active and 120° as LS-on per electrical cycle, so the per-device conduction fraction is 1/3.

Per-device RMS current:

$$
I_{HS,rms}=I_{pk}\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}},\qquad
I_{LS,rms}=I_{pk}\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}+\tfrac{1-D}{3}}
$$

Conduction loss per device, with R_DS(on) at junction temperature:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\left[1+k\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad k\approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}\ (\Rightarrow\times1.55\ @125^\circ\text{C})
$$

$$
P_{cond,dev}=I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Per motor channel (6 FETs), summing all devices over a cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch}=I_{pk}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[\underbrace{D}_{HS}+\underbrace{1+(1-D)}_{LS}\big]\cdot\tfrac{3}{3}=I_{pk}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,(2)
$$

Exactly two R_DS(on) are in the current path at all times (one HS or its sync-rect LS, plus one LS), independent of D. This is the useful invariant.

### 1.2 Copper / interconnect term

Add the board-level path resistance, R_cu ≈ 0.6 mΩ per channel. That covers 2 oz traces, vias, and solder pads from bus to phase pad to motor wire.

$$
P_{cond,ch}=I_{pk}^2\,[\,2R_{DS}(T_j)+R_{cu}]
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j ≈ 120 °C (factor 1.52)

| Case | R_DS(T_j) | 2R_DS + R_cu | P_cond / channel | P_cond / board (4 ch) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Typical | 1.52 mΩ | 3.64 mΩ | **23.3 W** | **93 W** |
| Worst (max R_DS) | 2.13 mΩ | 4.86 mΩ | **31.1 W** | **124 W** |

The hottest single FET is the LS of the PWM'd phase at low D. At D ≈ 1 (burst), each device carries one-third duty:

$$
P_{dev}=\tfrac{80^2\times2.13\text{ mΩ}}{3}=4.5\text{ W (worst)}
$$

> **Conclusion:** 80 A on a 30×30 board is strictly a transient rating. The thermal model in §3 bounds it.
