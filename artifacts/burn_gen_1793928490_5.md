# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Loss Model, Thermal Model, and Bi-Directional DShot DMA Allocation

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Flags

Two points shape the rest of the design.

**1. The H743 does not switch the FETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, so the switching-loss model belongs to the ESC side and the DMA model belongs to the H743 side.

**2. 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm mount, ~25×25 mm PCB) board is a marketing-grade burst figure, not a thermal capability.** The math in §3 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 95–100 W into roughly 4 J/K of thermal mass. That gives about 3 seconds before the board passes 100 °C.

I've modeled the requirement as stated and derived what is actually defensible.

**Design assumptions** (change these and re-run the equations):

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045·100) = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step current path

In any 60° commutation sector, exactly one phase is sourcing (high-side, HS) and one is sinking (low-side, LS). The third phase floats for BEMF sensing.

With complementary PWM at duty D:
- During the ON interval, current flows through HS(a) and LS(b).
- During the OFF interval, current freewheels through LS(a) (synchronous) and LS(b).

The motor current I always passes through **two conducting FETs**. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of D)}$$

Each FET in a bridge conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle. The split between FETs depends on D:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{I^2 R\,D}{3},\qquad P_{LS} = \frac{I^2 R\,(2-D)}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\ldots$$

The practical bound is simpler: averaged over rotation, each of the 6 FETs carries

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{2I^2R}{6} = \frac{I^2R}{3}$$

At D → 1, the low-sides carry slightly more than this average. Design to I²R/3 + 15%.
