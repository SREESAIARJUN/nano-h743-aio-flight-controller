# Job #60: STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Nano Engineering Model

**Scope:** Conduction and switching losses at 80 A burst, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

> **Important caveats before you build anything:**
> 1. Every device parameter below is an **assumed representative value**, not taken from a specific datasheet. Replace each one with the worst-case numbers for the exact MOSFETs, driver, and copper stack you select. The equations remain valid when you do.
> 2. The netlists are **reference-level connectivity**. They use pin *names*, not package pin numbers. They must be captured in your EDA tool and checked by ERC/DRC and a design review before layout.
> 3. **The headline result is a red flag.** As shown in §3, 80 A per channel on a nano-format board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is feasible only as a **short burst (~1–2 s)**. It is not a continuous rating. The rating should be marketed and firmware-limited accordingly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in trapezoidal 6-step with synchronous rectification

During any 60° sector, two phases are active: phase A is PWM-switched and phase B is held low.

- **During D·T<sub>sw</sub>:** current flows through Q<sub>AH</sub> → motor → Q<sub>BL</sub>.
- **During (1−D)·T<sub>sw</sub>:** current freewheels through Q<sub>AL</sub> (synchronous) → motor → Q<sub>BL</sub>.

The instantaneous conduction loss of the channel is therefore:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D)\,] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle** because two FETs are always in the path.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)] ,\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

| T<sub>j</sub> (°C) | R<sub>DS</sub> (mΩ) | P<sub>cond,ch</sub> @ 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 15.4 |
| 80 | 1.53 | 19.6 |
| 110 | 1.71 | 21.9 |
| 140 | 1.89 | 24.2 |

### 1.3 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

| Element | R (mΩ) | P @ 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| PCB copper, pad→FET→phase pad, per path | ~0.5 | 3.2 |
| Current shunt (0.25 mΩ, DC-link; ≈ phase current at D→1) | 0.25 | 1.6 |
| Solder joints, motor-wire pads | ~0.15 | 1.0 |
