## 0. Architectural Clarification (Important)

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. In every production AIO of this class:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIH6 (or VIT6), 400 MHz | ArduPilot | Sends DShot600 to the ESCs and captures bi-directional eRPM telemetry |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (one per motor) | AM32 | 6-step commutation at **48 kHz** complementary PWM |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (e.g. FD6288Q-class) | — | Drives 6 N-MOSFETs per motor |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET (6 per motor) | — | Up to 6S (25.2 V max) |

The 48 kHz switching analysis (§2) therefore applies to the ESC MCUs. The DShot/DMA analysis (§4) applies to the H743.

### 1.2 Six-Step Commutation Current Distribution

Assumptions:

- Trapezoidal commutation is used.
- The high-side switch is PWM'd with a complementary low side (synchronous rectification).
- The opposite leg's low side is held statically ON.
- Phase current I_ph is approximately constant over each 60° sector (high inductance relative to the PWM period).

Over one electrical period, each half-bridge leg spends:

- ⅓ of the period as the PWM leg,
- ⅓ as the static low-side leg,
- ⅓ floating (BEMF sensing).
