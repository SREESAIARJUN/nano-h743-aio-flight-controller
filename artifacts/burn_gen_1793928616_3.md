## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. Every loss below therefore belongs to the ESC power stage. The H743 section covers only the DShot/DMA path.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha\approx0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.4 × 1.6 = **2.24 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step, complementary (synchronous) PWM

During each 60° sector, three devices are involved:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T. The low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T, minus dead time.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is on for the whole sector.

The current is always flowing through exactly two FET channels:

$$P_{cond,ch}=I^2\,R_{DS}\,(D+(1-D)) + I^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Add the copper path (phase pour + bus, estimated 0.4 mΩ per current loop):

$$P_{cond,ch}=I^2\,(2R_{DS}+R_{Cu})=6400\,(4.48+0.4)\,\text{m}\Omega=\mathbf{31.2\ W}$$

- **Per FET:** averaged over the electrical cycle with symmetric loading, 2·I²R_DS / 6 = 28.7 / 6 ≈ **4.8 W**.
- **Instantaneous peak per FET:** I²R_DS = **14.3 W**. This value sets junction ripple at low eRPM.
