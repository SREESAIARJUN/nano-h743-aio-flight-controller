# Job #34: STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Verdict

Two points need settling before the math.

**1. Which processor does what.** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In any ArduPilot stack the work splits like this:

- **FC (STM32H743):** runs ArduPilot, generates bidirectional DShot600, and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC (4× AT32F421 / STM32G071-class MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32):** each runs one motor's 48 kHz PWM, six-step commutation, and the DShot slave.

The derivations below cover both domains.

**2. Thermal feasibility of an 80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board.** Section 2.5 shows that 4×80 A simultaneously on a 20×20 board reaches T_j(max) in **under 2 s**, and 12× PDFN5×6 FETs will not physically fit. My recommendation is a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC stacked under a 20×20 FC**, or derating the burst spec to "80 A single-channel, ≤5 s". All numbers below are parameterized so you can re-run them for your actual parts.

## 2. Thermal / Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Losses (Six-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd high-side (duty D) with complementary low-side, and a second phase's low-side is held fully on. The current path resistance is therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big] = 2I^2R_{ds,hot}$$

The result is independent of D, because synchronous rectification removes the diode term.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Per-FET breakdown.** Each FET conducts during roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle at D→1:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, I^2 R_{ds,hot} = \tfrac{1}{3}(9.6) = 3.2\ \text{W (average)}$$

### 2.2 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge hard-switches.

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·40n·48k | **1.935 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50n·25.2·48k | 0.060 W |
| C_oss | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200n·48k | 0.614 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | Q_g·V_gs·f_sw·2 | 0.048 W (mostly in driver/R_g) |
| **P_sw,ch** | | **≈ 2.69 W** |

**Sensitivity.** Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it is about 0.97 W; at 96 kHz about 3.9 W. Dead-time loss is the second-largest term, so trimming t_dt to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel.

### 2.3 Totals at 80 A

| | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| **Total** | **21.9 W** | **87.6 W** |

Per hottest FET (conduction + its share of switching):

$$P_{FET} \approx 3.2 + \tfrac{1}{3}(2.69) \approx 4.1\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Junction Rise Above Local Board

The die thermal time constant (~ms) is much longer than the electrical period at flight eRPM, so averaging is valid:

$$\Delta T_{j-pcb} = P_{FET}(R_{th,jc}+R_{th,c\text{-}pcb}) = 4.1 \times 4.5 \approx \mathbf{18.5\ K}$$

### 2.5 Board-Level Transient (Lumped Capacitance Model)

$$C_{th}\frac{dT}{dt} = P_{loss} - \frac{T - T_{amb}}{R_{th,ba}}$$

with the solution

$$T(t) = T_{amb} + P R_{th,ba}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right), \quad \tau = R_{th,ba}\,C_{th}$$

**Heat capacity for a 20×20 board, 1.6 mm, 8 layers of 2 oz copper at 50% fill:**

- FR4: 0.64 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ × 1.1 J/gK ≈ 1.30 J/K
- Copper: 0.112 cm³ × 8.96 g/cm³ × 0.385 J/gK ≈ 0.39 J/K
- FETs and other parts: ≈ 0.1 J/K
- **C_th ≈ 1.8 J/K**

**Convective resistance.** With prop wash, h ≈ 100 W/m²K over A = 8 cm² (both sides), so R_th,ba ≈ 12.5 K/W and τ ≈ 22 s. Burst events are much shorter than τ, so the adiabatic approximation holds:

$$t_{limit} \approx \frac{C_{th}\,\Delta T_{allow}}{P}$$

The allowed board rise is ΔT_allow = (125 − 18.5) − 40 °C ambient ≈ 66 K.

| Scenario | P | Time to T_j = 125 °C |
|---|---|---|
| 4×80 A, 20×20 | 87.6 W | **≈ 1.4 s** ❌ |
| 1×80 A, 20×20 | 21.9 W | ≈ 5.4 s ⚠️ |
| 4×80 A, 30.5×30.5 (C_th ≈ 4.5 J/K, + 6 g Al heatsink ≈ +5.4 J/K) | 87.6 W | ≈ 7.5 s ✅ (burst) |

**Continuous rating (steady state) on 20×20:**

$$P_{max} = \frac{66}{12.5} \approx 5.3\ \text{W total}$$

This corresponds to about 22 A per channel across all four channels.

**Conclusion:** the spec requires a 30.5 mm ESC plus heatsink, or ArduPilot-side current limiting via `BATT_CURR_LI
