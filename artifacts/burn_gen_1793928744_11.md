## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C with α = 0.006:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 0.9\ \text{mΩ}\times 1.51 = 1.36\ \text{mΩ}$$

Iterate: compute losses, then T<sub>j</sub>, then R<sub>DS(on)</sub>. The loop converges in 2–3 passes unless you are in thermal runaway (§4.3).

### 1.2 6-step conduction with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, one leg is PWM-modulated (HS on for D, LS on for 1−D) and one leg's LS is held on. Two FETs therefore always carry the phase current I:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.36\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.4\ W}$$

Per-FET average (each device conducts about one-third of the electrical period):

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2R_{DS(on)} = 2.9\ \text{W}$$

Low-side FETs run slightly hotter at low duty; high-side FETs run hotter at high duty.

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$I_{RMS}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For L<sub>ph</sub> = 15 µH, V<sub>bus</sub>−V<sub>bemf</sub> = 5 V, D = 0.8:

- ΔI<sub>pp</sub> = 5.6 A
- The correction term is less than 0.1 %, so it can be neglected at 48 kHz.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses

$$P_{Cu} = I_{bat}^2 R_{bus} + 4\cdot 2\,I^2 R_{phase\_trace} + I_{bat}^2 R_{shunt}$$

- Battery current: I<sub>bat</sub> ≈ Σ D·I<sub>ph</sub>/η = 4 × 0.8 × 80 / 0.95 ≈ **270 A**.
- With R<sub>bus</sub> = 0.25 mΩ: P<sub>Cu,bus</sub> = 18 W.
- With a 0.25 mΩ shunt: an additional 18 W.

This copper/shunt term is typically ignored and frequently dominates on small boards.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg is hard-switched at a time in 6-step operation. Per motor, use V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f = 48 kHz.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t<sub>r</sub>+t<sub>f</sub>)·f | ½·25.2·80·16 ns·48k = **0.77 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q<sub>rr</sub>·V·f | 50 nC·25.2·48k = **0.06 W** |
| C<sub>oss</sub> | ½·C<sub>oss</sub>·V²·f (per hard-switched device) | ½·1.5 nF·635·48k = **0.02 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V<sub>F</sub>·I·2t<sub>dt</sub>·f | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive | 2·Q<sub>g</sub>·V<sub>GS</sub>·f (dissipated in driver/R<sub>g</sub>) | 2·60 nC·10·48k = **0.06 W** |
| **Total per motor** | | **≈ 1.52 W** |
