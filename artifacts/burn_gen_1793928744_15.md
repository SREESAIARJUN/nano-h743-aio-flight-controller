### Architectural findings to resolve before layout

1. **The H743 should not drive the power stage directly.** ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware. The standard and correct architecture is:
   - The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600.
   - Four ESC MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) handle commutation.
   - The design assumes one ESC MCU per motor.
2. **80 A on a nano AIO is a burst figure only.** §3 shows that the continuous capability is roughly 18–25 A per motor in prop-wash cooling. This must be reflected in the product rating.
3. **Bi-directional DShot pins must be CHx, not CHxN.** Complementary outputs cannot input-capture, so they cannot receive eRPM telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.2 \times 1.45 = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Per-switch duty in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) FET carry the phase current I. Assume the PWM is applied on the HS of the leg that is driving current, with complementary LS freewheeling. Over one electrical cycle, each leg spends 1/3 of the time as PWM high-leg and 1/3 as the fixed low-leg.

| Device | Conduction fraction | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| High-side | D/3 | $P_{HS} = I^2 R_{ds}\,\frac{D}{3}$ |
| Low-side | (1 + (1 − D))/3 | $P_{LS} = I^2 R_{ds}\,\frac{2-D}{3}$ |

Check: summing over three legs gives $3\cdot\frac{D + 2 - D}{3}I^2R = 2I^2R_{ds}$. This matches two devices always in the current path. ✔
