### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.

Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071). That MCU handles the 48 kHz complementary 6-step PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection, and the eRPM telemetry reply.

This is the only architecture ArduPilot supports for bi-directional DShot. Driving 12 FETs from the H743 directly is not a supported configuration.

### 0.3 Feasibility flag

On a 20×20 or 30×30 mm "nano" stack, 80 A per channel is only a **seconds-scale burst** rating. Section 3 shows the board cannot reach thermal steady state at that current. Market it as "80 A burst / about 35–45 A continuous with airflow". Do not market it as 80 A continuous.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, complementary/synchronous PWM)

### 1.1 Commutation-state analysis

In each of the six commutation steps:
- One phase leg PWMs. Its high-side FET (HS) conducts for duty D, and its low-side FET (LS) synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- One leg has its LS fully ON.
- One leg floats and is used for BEMF sensing.
