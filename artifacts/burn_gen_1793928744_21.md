**Job #44, Architecture Baseline Rev A**

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Summary

Two points need to be settled before the math, because they change the design.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack. It does not run BLDC commutation or FOC. A production-viable "H743 4-in-1 nano" is therefore an AIO with five MCUs:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──bidir DShot600 ×4──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class)
                                               └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6× N-FET per motor
```

**2. An 80 A burst on a nano footprint is a transient rating, not a thermal operating point.** The model in §3–§5 shows:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total stage dissipation at 80 A × 4 motors | ≈ 88 W |
| Adiabatic heating rate of a 20×20 mm board | ≈ 40–45 K/s |
| Usable burst window (board-limited) | ≈ 1–1.5 s from a 50 °C start |
| Realistic sustained current per motor in prop-wash | ≈ 30–35 A |

The 80 A rating must be enforced by firmware current/temperature limiting. It cannot be guaranteed by copper alone.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant, two phase legs carry the full motor current I_m:

- **The "+" leg:** the HS FET conducts during the ON time (fraction D). The LS FET of the same leg carries freewheel current during OFF (1−D), because synchronous rectification is used.
- **The "−" leg:** the LS FET conducts 100 % of the sector.

Each leg spends 1/3 of the electrical period as "+", 1/3 as "−", and 1/3 floating.

### 2.2 Per-channel conduction loss

Two conducting FET-equivalents carry I_m at all times (dead time ignored here; it is handled in §3.4):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_m^2\, R_{DS(on),hot}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_m^2 R \cdot D,\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_m^2 R\,(1 + (1-D))$$
