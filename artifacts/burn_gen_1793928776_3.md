## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors directly. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and answers bidirectional DShot600 commands from the H743. This is the only practical split: ArduPilot has no field-oriented or 6-step commutation stack for H7-driven gate drivers.
