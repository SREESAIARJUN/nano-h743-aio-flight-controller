## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Warning

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU and runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate motors. Commutation is handled by four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32). Bi-directional DShot600 links the H743 to each ESC MCU. This is the only architecture ArduPilot supports for bidir DShot. ArduPilot does not run ESC firmware.

**Feasibility warning.** 80 A per channel on a nano-class board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mounting, roughly 30×30 mm PCB) is a *burst* rating only, measured in seconds. The math below shows the four-channel simultaneous continuous rating is thermally limited to roughly 35–45 A per channel without forced cooling. Marketing should say "80 A burst (≤ 2 s, single-channel-dominant), 40 A continuous with prop-wash airflow."

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation-aware current flow

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In any 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET conducts for 1−D (synchronous rectification).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.
- **Floating leg:** off (BEMF sensing).

Instantaneous channel conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This is independent of D, apart from dead-time intervals, which are handled in §2.
