## 0. Scope, Architecture and Assumptions

The brief did not specify the MOSFET part, battery cell count, board size or burst duration, so I fixed them as stated below. Every equation is parametric. Replace the bracketed values with your datasheet numbers and the results update directly.

**Architecture decision.** Bi-directional DShot is a link from the flight controller to the ESC. The STM32H743 therefore runs ArduPilot as the FC. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071). That ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 does not drive the gates.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current and one phase floats:

- **PWM'd phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The low-side synchronous rectifier conducts for (1−D)·T_sw, apart from dead time.
- **Return phase:** its low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

So **exactly two FET channels are in series with the motor current at all times**, independent of duty cycle. Dead time is the exception and is handled in §2.

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

The worst-case ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{pp}\,f_{sw}} \;\le\; \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{pp}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\cdot0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\cdot48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple adds only 0.06%, so it can be ignored at 80 A. It matters below about 15 A.

### 1.3 Temperature-coupled conduction loss

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2\,R_{Cu}$$

Each FET conducts for one-third of the electrical cycle on average, so the per-FET average is:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,motor} - I^2R_{Cu}}{6}$$

For copper resistance, take a phase-to-bus path of about 15 mm at 2 oz (70 µm), with paralleled layers giving about 0.3 mΩ total in the loop. Copper's tempco (0.0039/K) is close to the MOSFET's, so apply the same temperature scaling.
