## 0. Design Basis and Feasibility Check

### 0.1 Architectural correction

An STM32H743 does not commutate motors on any production 4-in-1 design. The architecture is:

- **Flight controller MCU:** STM32H743VIT6 running ArduPilot. It generates bi-directional DShot600.
- **ESC MCUs:** 4 × STM32G071 (or AT32F421) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each performs six-step BEMF commutation at 48 kHz PWM.
