## 0. Architecture Decision and Feasibility Flag

**Topology.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits 4× bidirectional DShot600 over on-board traces. It does not drive gates directly, because ArduPilot has no FOC/6-step ESC firmware. The design is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir ×4──> 4× STM32G071 (AM32) ──> 4× 3-phase gate driver ──> 24× N-MOSFET
```

> ⚠️ **Feasibility warning.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) combined with 80 A per channel means up to 320 A total and about 7.7 kW from 6S. That is a burst-only rating lasting about 1 s. It is not a continuous rating (see §2.5). Section 2 quantifies this. I recommend the design review explicitly accept "80 A ≤ 1 s burst, ~18–25 A continuous" or move to a 30.5×30.5 board with 5×6 mm FETs.
