## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation exactly two phases are active at any moment. Take phase A as the PWM-ed phase and phase B as the return:

- **PWM on-time (fraction D):** current flows through HS_A, the motor, then LS_B.
- **PWM off-time (1 − D):** current freewheels through LS_A (synchronous rectification), the motor, then LS_B.

So in both intervals the current passes through **two R_DS(on) in series**, except during dead time (§2.3).

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

Phase ripple at 48 kHz with motor inductance L_ph is

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

and the RMS current is

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line, so about 7.5 µH per phase) at D = 0.5: ΔI ≈ 5.8 A. That makes ΔI²/12 ≈ 2.8 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². **Use I_rms ≈ 80 A.**
