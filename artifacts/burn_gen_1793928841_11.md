**Loop structure per axis.** The angle error comes from the fused IMU attitude, not the encoder. The velocity feedback is the fused gyro rate projected onto the joint axis. The encoder is used only for commutation, the cogging phase, and the joint-limit and kinematic projection.

```
θ_ref ─►(Angle P + ω_ff)─► ω_ref ─►(Velocity PI)─► i_q,fb ─►(+ i_q,cog,ff + i_q,ff)─► i_q,ref ─►(Current PI dq)─► SVPWM
          ▲ θ_cam (IMU fusion)        ▲ ω_joint (gyro, projected)                        ▲ i_d,i_q (Park of i_a,i_b)
```

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform** (two-shunt measurement, i_c = −i_a − i_b):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park transform**, with θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and Torque Constant

For a surface-mounted PMSM (L_d = L_q = L):

$$
L\dot i_d = v_d - R i_d + \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
L\dot i_q = v_q - R i_q - \omega_e L i_d - \omega_e\lambda
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q,\qquad \lambda = \frac{60}{\sqrt3\,\pi\, p\, K_v[\text{rpm/V}]}
$$

### 1.3 PI Design by Pole-Zero Cancellation

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. The open loop then becomes ω_c/s, and the closed loop is first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad
\omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{\text{pwm}}}{10}\ (\text{account for the } 1.5\,T_s \text{ PWM+sampling delay})
$$

For f_pwm = 20 kHz, use ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz. Phase margin is then about 90° − ω_c·1.5T_s·(180/π) ≈ 50°.

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda
$$
