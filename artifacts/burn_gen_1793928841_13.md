# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

This design covers cascaded attitude/rate/current control, anti-cogging feedforward, and dual ICM-42688-P vibration rejection. | Loop | Rate | Period | Closed-loop bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|---|

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here θ_offset is found at calibration by aligning the d-axis with phase A, i.e. applying v_d > 0 at θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant model (SPMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.5 PI current controller with decoupling

Use pole-zero cancellation of the RL plant to get a first-order closed loop of bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;(\text{i.e. } K_i/K_p = R/L)$$

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_PWM / 10 to leave margin for the 1.5-sample computation and PWM delay.

### 1.6 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

The linear SVPWM range is a circle of radius V_bus/√3.

- Prioritize d: v_d is clamped first.
- Then v_q is limited to $\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.
- Back-calculation anti-windup integrator:

$$x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + K_i T\,e[k] + K_{aw}\,(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})$$

with $K_{aw} = 1/K_p$, or equivalently $K_i/K_p$ per unit time.

### 1.7 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

$$v_a=v_\alpha,\; v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.8 Delay compensation

The commanded voltage is applied about 1.5 Tᵢ after sampling. Advance the angle used in the inverse Park:

$$\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_i$$
