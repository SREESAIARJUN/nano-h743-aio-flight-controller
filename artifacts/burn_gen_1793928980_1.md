## 1. Field-Oriented Control (innermost loop, T_c = 50 µs)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical encoder angle and the offset found at alignment:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Delay compensation.** The PWM update lands about 1.5 T_c after sampling, so the angle used for the inverse Park transform is advanced:

$$\theta_e^{\text{pwm}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_c$$
