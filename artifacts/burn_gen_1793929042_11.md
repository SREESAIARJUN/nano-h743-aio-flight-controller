# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Design (Job #16)

## 0. Architecture Decisions

Before the math, two architectural points shape everything else.

**1. The H743 should not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated small MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) that handles commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, and bidirectional DShot decoding. Driving 24 MOSFETs at 48 kHz with ArduPilot's scheduler is not a supported or sane configuration.

**2. 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) board is only a short transient rating.** Section 3 shows it is limited to roughly 1–2 s by the thermal mass of the board, not by the silicon. If the requirement means 80 A *sustained*, it cannot be met on this footprint. The real choice is:
- (a) redefine 80 A as a ≤1 s burst with firmware-enforced limiting, or
- (b) move to 30.5×30.5 mm with paralleled FETs.

**Baseline assumptions (replace with the datasheet values of the chosen part):**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly two legs are active per channel at any instant. In the PWM leg, current alternates between the HS FET (duty D) and the LS FET (1−D). In the static leg, one FET carries current continuously. Each active leg therefore always has one FET conducting the full phase current.

**Per active leg:**

P_leg = I² · R_DS(on)(T_j) = 80² × 1.6 mΩ = **10.24 W**

**Per ESC channel (two active legs):**

P_cond,ch = 2 · I² · R_DS(on)(T_j) = **20.5 W**

**Per FET, averaged over an electrical cycle:** each FET is part of an active leg for 1/3 of the cycle.

P_cond,FET ≈ 20.5 / 6 = **3.41 W**

The instantaneous peak is 10.24 W while that FET is in its conduction window. At commutation periods well below the ~1 ms die thermal time constant, averaging is valid.

**Temperature coefficient feedback** (iterate to convergence):

R_DS(on)(T) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T − 25)], with α ≈ 0.006 /K

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. In each PWM period there is one turn-on and one turn-off transition in that leg.

### 2.1 V·I overlap

Gate current through the Miller plateau (V_pl ≈ 3 V):

- I_g,on = (10 − 3) / 4 Ω = 1.75 A → t_r ≈ (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) / I_g = 9 nC / 1.75 A = 5.1 ns
- I_g,off = 3 / 2 Ω = 1.5 A → t_f ≈ 6.0 ns

Layout and source inductance (L_s ≈ 0.5–1 nH) degrade these in practice. **Design value: t_r = t_f = 15 ns.**

P_VI = ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30e-9 × 48e3 = **1.45 W**

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (LS body diode → HS turn-on)

P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 40e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.048 W**

The induced extra HS turn-on loss is roughly the same magnitude, so budget **0.1 W**.

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

P_dt = V_SD · I · 2 · t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200e-9 × 48e3 = **0.61 W**

### 2.4 Output capacitance

P_oss ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw = **0.024 W**

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g)

P_g = Q_g · V_drv · f_sw × 2 FETs = **0.029 W**

### 2.6 Switching loss summary per channel

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 1.45 |
| Q_rr (incl. induced) | 0.10 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| C_oss | 0.02 |
| Gate drive | 0.03 |
| **Total** | **≈ 2.2** |

**Sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.1 W per channel, at the cost of more current ripple and audible noise.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total burst dissipation

- Per channel: 20.5 + 2.2 ≈ **22.7 W**
- Four channels at 80 A: **≈ 91 W**
- Add shunts (0.25 mΩ shared, ~1.6 W) and copper I²R in traces/pads (~3–5 W)
- **Total ≈ 95–98 W** into a 20×20 mm board

### 3.2 Junction rise above board (steady averaging)

Worst FET is the PWM-leg HS at high duty:

P ≈ 3.41 + 1.45/2 + 0.1 ≈ **4.2 W**

ΔT_jc = 4.2 × 1.2 ≈ **5 K**

ΔT_c-pcb (thermal via array 12×0.3 mm under drain pad, ~2 K/W) ≈ **8.5 K**

So T_j ≈ T_pcb + 14 K. The silicon is not the limiter; the board is.

### 3.3 Board thermal mass (lumped)

| Component | Heat capacity |
|---|---|
| 8-layer 2 oz, 20×20 mm, FR4 + Cu, ~3.5 g | ~2.4 J/K |
| 24 FETs + caps | ~0.6 J/K |
| **C_th** | **≈ 3 J/K** |

| Condition | R_th,board-amb |
|---|---|
| Still air | ~25 K/W |
| Prop-wash | ~8 K/W |

**Thermal time constant:** τ = R·C ≈ 24 s with prop-wash.

### 3.4 Burst duration to limit

T_pcb(t) = T_amb + P·R·(1 − e^(−t/τ))

Steady state would be 98 × 8 ≈ 780 K rise, which is impossible, so the burst is entirely capacity-limited:

t_burst ≈ C_th · ΔT_allow / P

With T_start = 45 °C and T_pcb,max = 105 °C (T_j ≈ 120 °C):

t ≈ 3 × 60 / 98 ≈ **1.8 s** (initial rise rate ≈ 33 K/s)
