# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Derivation Package (Job #27)

The design has three problems that set its limits:

1. **80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm board is a burst rating of about 2 s, not a sustained one.** The thermal capacitance of the board, not the MOSFETs, is the limit (§2.4).
2. **4 × 80 A phase current at full throttle draws roughly 250–320 A from the battery.** XT60 connectors and typical nano-class packs cannot supply this, so the battery path is usually the real limit (§2.6).
3. **40 V FETs on 6S have little margin for switching overshoot at fast edges** (§2.5). This drives the gate-resistor choice.

The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel uses its own low-cost MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) and receives bidirectional DShot600 from the H743. The architecture below follows that split.

### 2.2 Conduction losses (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

At every instant the current flows through exactly two conducting channels: one high-side or synchronous low-side FET on the PWM leg, plus the low-side FET on the return leg.

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

With peak-to-peak ripple fraction r:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right) \approx I_{ph}^2 \quad (r \lesssim 0.3)
$$

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

**Per-FET breakdown:**

- **Average power.** Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical period. The pair of devices on a leg shares the duty D and 1−D. Averaged over the six FETs this gives 19.2 / 6 ≈ **3.2 W per FET**.
- **Instantaneous power while conducting.** I²R = 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**. This raises T_j above the case by 9.6 × 1.5 = **14.4 K** during the conduction interval. This is acceptable because the electrical period (~1 ms) is much shorter than the die thermal time constant.

### 2.3 Switching-related losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg is PWM-switching at any time in 6-step. That leg contains one hard-switched transition pair.

**(a) V·I overlap**
$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

**(b) Dead-time body-diode conduction**
$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(400\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.23\ W}
$$
