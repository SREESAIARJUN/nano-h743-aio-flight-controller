## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Design Risks

Three issues affect every calculation below.

**1. The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** ArduPilot has no 4-motor commutation or FOC driver. The H743 sends DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs, which generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. This design assumes AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, which supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot with eRPM telemetry. Partition:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32) ──► 4× FD6288Q ──► 24× N-MOSFET
```

**2. "80A burst" on a nano board is thermally marginal.** Section 3 shows that four simultaneous 80 A channels dissipate about 100–120 W on roughly 3.5 J/K of board heat capacity. Bursts must stay at or below about 1 s. Treat 80 A as a per-channel transient rating, not something all four motors can do at once.

**3. Component values are representative.** Replace them with the vendor datasheets for the parts you actually procure before freezing the design.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{hot} = 1.2 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$
