# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #30): Power-Stage and DShot Architecture Model

> **Up-front engineering verdict:** On a 20×20 mm nano form factor, **80 A per channel is only achievable as a short (~1–8 s) thermal-capacitance-limited burst**, not as a continuous rating. The model below shows why and gives the time limits. Treat every number as a design-phase estimate to be validated against your actual MOSFET datasheet, stack-up, and thermal-camera measurements.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.3\,\text{mΩ} \times (1 + 0.006 \times 75) = 1.885\,\text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In every commutation sector, current flows through exactly **two** conducting MOSFETs in series:

- the PWM-ing high side, or its complementary low side during the off-time (sync rectification), plus
- the static low side of the return phase.

This gives:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.885\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.1\ W\ per\ channel}$$

$$P_{cond,4ch} = \mathbf{96.5\ W}\quad\text{(all four motors at 80 A)}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs carries the current for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The PWM-leg pair shares that interval by D and (1−D). At electrical frequencies of 1–4 kHz (e.g., 14-pole motor at 30 krpm → 3.5 kHz), the cycle is much faster than the die/PCB thermal time constant (ms-scale). Averaging is therefore valid:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx P_{ch}/6 \approx 4.0\text{–}4.8\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 FOC variant

With sinusoidal currents, peak I_pk = 80 A gives I_rms = 56.6 A per phase. All three phases conduct continuously:

$$P_{cond,FOC} = 3 \times I_{rms}^2 \times R_{DS(on)} = 3 \times 56.6^2 \times 1.885\,\text{mΩ} = 18.1\ \text{W}$$

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-current derivation

Miller plateau voltage:

$$V_{pl} \approx V_{th} + \frac{I_D}{g_{fs}} \approx 2.0 + \frac{80}{100} \approx 2.8\ \text{V}$$

Use **3.5 V** for margin. Total gate resistance R_G = 10 + 1.5 = 11.5 Ω.

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{DRV}-V_{pl}}{R_G} = \frac{10-3.5}{11.5} = 0.565\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_G} = \frac{3.5}{11.5} = 0.304\ \text{A}$$
