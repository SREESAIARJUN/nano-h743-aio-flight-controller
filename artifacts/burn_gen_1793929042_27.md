**Scope:** Job #33. Conduction and switching loss models, burst thermal analysis, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and a reference netlist.

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

These assumptions must be verified against the actual BOM before release.

| Item | Assumption | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) on the same board. | No production firmware runs FOC/6-step for 4 motors on the H743 while also running ArduPilot. ArduPilot does not support that. |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm (M2 or M3 mounting), 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner copper | Typical nano stack |
| Bus | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Worst case for switching loss |
| MOSFET | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 class. R_DS(on) = 1.6 mΩ max at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V. Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gs2 ≈ 4 nC, Q_gd = 6 nC, V_pl = 3.5 V, Q_oss ≈ 25 nC, Q_rr ≈ 30 nC, R_g,int = 1.5 Ω, R_θJC(bottom) ≈ 1.5 K/W | Representative of the class. Substitute your datasheet values. |
| Gate driver | 3-phase bootstrap driver (FD6288Q class), ~1 A source / 1.5
