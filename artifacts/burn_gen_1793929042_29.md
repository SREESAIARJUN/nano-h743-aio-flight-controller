### Architecture correction

The H743 should not drive the bridges directly. ArduPilot does not run commutation firmware. A credible 4-in-1 uses four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. The design below follows that split.
