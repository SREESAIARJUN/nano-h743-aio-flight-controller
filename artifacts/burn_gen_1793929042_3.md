Before any math, three points in the brief would cause design errors if taken literally.

| # | Issue | Resolution |

| 1 | The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no on-FC BLDC commutation. | The FC (H743) sends bi-directional DShot600 to **four ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 running AM32, one per channel). The 48 kHz PWM is an **AM32 setting** (`PWM_FREQUENCY`), not an H743 timer. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification. In any commutation sector, one phase is high (HS PWM'd), one is low (LS on), and one floats. Current always flows through exactly **two FETs in series**:

- **PWM on:** HS_A → motor → LS_B
- **PWM off (sync rect):** LS_A → motor → LS_B

Per-channel conduction loss is therefore:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{shunt} + I^2 R_{trace}
$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FETs | 80² × 2 × 1.8 mΩ | **23.0 W** |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, shared on the low-side return) | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Copper (≈0.15 mΩ path, 2 oz, 4 layers paralleled) | 80² × 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| **Total P_cond,ch** | | **≈ 25.6 W** |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical revolution:

- Each HS conducts for 1/3 of the period at duty D.
- Each LS conducts for its own 1/3 sector plus freewheel time (1−D) during its partner HS's sector.

At high throttle (D ≈ 0.9), per-FET average loss is:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{23.0}{6} \approx 3.8\text{ W}
$$

The **instantaneous** loss in a conducting FET is 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **11.5 W**. At eRPM above 20k, the sector period is below 0.5 ms, so the die never sees steady-state at 11.5 W. Junction ripple is set by Z_th(jc) at ~0.5 ms, roughly 0.15 K/W, giving about **+1.7 K of ripple**. This is negligible, so the average model is valid.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per switching event, only the PWM'd high-side hard-switches. The LS commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard switching)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 35\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.69\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}
$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

The LS body diode recovers when the HS turns on:

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.060\text{ W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2t_{dt}\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{ns}\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET.

$$
P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\cdot 60\text{n}\cdot 10 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.058\text{ W}
$$

### 2.6 Summary per channel at 80 A

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 23.0 |
| Shunt + copper | 2.6 |
| V-I switching | 1.69 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.11 |
| Gate (driver side) | 0.06 |
| **Channel total** | **≈ 28.1 W** |
| **4-in-1 total** | **≈ 112 W** |
