## 0. Architecture Constraints and Assumptions

These points determine the rest of the design and should be resolved before layout.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In an ArduPilot stack, the H743 runs the flight controller and sends DShot commands. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The design below uses **H743 FC → 4 × G071 (AM32) → FD6288Q drivers → 24 MOSFETs**.
2. **"Nano" and "80A" conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot hold 24 power FETs plus bulk capacitance at 80A. This design uses a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC** with a separate stacked 20×20 or 30.5 FC. Section 3 shows that 80A is a **burst rating of a few seconds**, not a continuous one.
3. **Component values are assumptions.** The parameters below represent a typical 40V, 5×6 PQFN MOSFET class. Replace them with the actual datasheet values once the part is chosen.
