# STM32H743 FC + 80A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture Package (Job #65)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Engineering Flags

I don't have Job #65's controlled documents: the BOM, stackup, MOSFET selection, or mechanical envelope. Every model below is parametric, and the numbers come from a representative baseline (Table 0.1). Replace the bracketed values with your datasheet values before design review.

**Architecture clarification.** On a "4-in-1 ESC" the H743 is not the commutation controller. The production-standard partition is:

- **Flight controller:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Each does six-step or FOC commutation at 48 kHz PWM.

Running four sensorless motor loops at 48 kHz on the H743 alongside the ArduPilot scheduler is not supported by ArduPilot. This document assumes the partitioned architecture.

**Flag 1: thermal claim.** As the thermal section shows, 80A per channel in a nano (20×20 mm mounting) form factor is only sustainable for about 1–2 seconds. Realistic continuous current is about 20–30A per channel. If the product spec says "80A," it must be explicitly qualified as a time-limited burst with a defined duration and starting temperature.
