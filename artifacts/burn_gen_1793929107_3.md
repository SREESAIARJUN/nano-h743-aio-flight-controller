**Scope:** loss model (conduction + switching @ 48 kHz), thermal budget for the 80 A burst claim, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

## 0. Executive Summary

The H743 is not the right place to run motor commutation. The architecture should be:

- **H743:** flight controller running ArduPilot. It generates bidir DShot600 on one advanced timer.
- **4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071):** one per motor, running AM32 firmware with bidir DShot and EDT.
- **4× FD6288Q:** three-phase gate drivers, one per motor.
- **24× 40 V N-MOSFETs:** in PDFN 3.3×3.3 packages.

**The thermal result is the key finding:**

| Scenario | Total dissipation | Board ΔT rate | Time to reach 110 °C board (from 40 °C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 motor @ 80 A | ~23 W | ~7.6 K/s | **~6–8 s** |
| 4 motors @ 80 A | ~91 W | ~30 K/s | **~1.5–2 s** |
| Continuous capability (prop-wash cooled) | ~10–12 W total | — | ≈ 25–30 A per motor |

**Recommendation:** spec the board as **"35 A continuous / 80 A burst ≤ 1 s, per motor, not simultaneous"**. Writing "80 A" without a duration and duty is not defensible on a nano (≈ 30×30 mm) footprint.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation model.** Six-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM:

- At any instant, two phase legs carry current I.
- In the PWM leg, current alternates between HS (duty D) and LS (duty 1 − D).
- With synchronous rectification, one channel is always conducting at R_DS(on), so loss is **independent of D**.

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \cdot I^2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx k_T R_{25}
$$

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.6\,\text{mΩ} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}
$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET conducts during 120° of 360° electrical:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond}}{6} \approx 3.4\ \text{W}
$$

**Instantaneous peak per FET.** During its active sector, a FET dissipates up to I²R = **10.2 W**. The electrical period at typical eRPM (e.g., 1–3 ms) is far below the FET's thermal time constant, so the average is valid for junction temperature with a small ripple term.

**Copper loss** (also a heat source):

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace}
$$

For 0.2 mΩ of power path per motor (2 oz, short pours), this adds **1.3 W per motor at 80 A**. Do not neglect it.

**Electro-thermal coupling.** Because R_DS(on) rises with T_j, solve iteratively:

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,R_{\theta j-b}
$$

The fixed point exists only if dP/dT · R_θ < 1. That condition holds here: dP/dT ≈ 0.02 W/K per FET, and 0.02 × 12 ≈ 0.24.

## 3. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per switching period, that leg has one turn-on and one turn-off hard transition. The hard-switched device is the HS FET; the LS FET is soft-switched, but its body diode recovers.

**(a) V-I overlap loss**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 35\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.69\ W}
$$

**(b) Reverse recovery loss**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

Q_rr itself rises with di/dt and I; treat this as a lower bound and multiply by ~2 for margin.

**(c) Output capacitance loss**

$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}
$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (2 edges per period)

$$
P_{dt} = V_F I \cdot 2 t_d f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 100\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}
$$

**(e) Gate drive loss** (6 FETs per motor; only 2 actively PWM, so this is conservative)

$$
P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \cdot N = 40\text{n} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\text{k} \cdot 2 = 0.04\ \text{W}
$$

Most of P_g is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET.

**Total switching-related loss per motor:** ≈ **2.1 W** (≈ 2.2 W with Q_rr margin).

**Frequency sensitivity:**

$$
\partial P_{sw}/\partial f_{sw} \approx 44\ \mu\text{W/Hz}
$$

Dropping to 24 kHz saves ~1 W per motor at 80 A. Conduction loss dominates (~90%), so **R_DS(on) and copper are the levers, not f_sw**.
