# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #59)

## 0. Feasibility Findings

The models below expose four problems that should be resolved at design review, before layout.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The H743 is the **flight controller**, not the ESC motor-control MCU. A 48 kHz commutation loop for 4 motors belongs on per-channel ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) or a dedicated FOC part. The H743 drives bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. | The architecture below assumes this split. |
| F2 | 4 × 80 A burst on a nano (20×20 or 30.5×30.5) board dissipates **≈155 W**. With prop-wash cooling only, the thermal mass supports roughly **1.5–2.5 s**, not the "10 s burst" marketing figure. | Derate the label, or enlarge the board/heatsink. |
| F3 | 320 A aggregate burst exceeds XT60 (~60 A continuous) and typical nano-board solder pads and 12 AWG leads. | Use direct-solder pads with ≥10 AWG and a 4 oz outer copper pour. |
| F4 | 3.3×3 mm 40 V MOSFETs are package- and bond-limited near 80 A pulsed. | Use PDFN5×6 or dual-FET-per-switch, or derate to ~55 A burst. |

All device parameters below are **representative assumptions**. Replace them with the chosen BOM's datasheet values and re-run the equations.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology

The topology is 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, the phase current flows through exactly **two** conducting MOSFETs:

- **ON interval (D):** high side of phase X, then the low side of phase Y.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** low side of phase X (synchronous rectification), then the low side of phase Y.

The per-channel conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

with the temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 2.2 Self-Consistent Junction Temperature

T_j depends on loss, and loss depends on T_j. Solve by iteration, converging on T_j ≈ 110 °C for burst:

$$R_{DS(on)}(110\,^\circ C) = 1.7\,\text{mΩ}\,(1 + 0.006 \cdot 85) = 2.57\ \text{mΩ}$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 2.57\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{32.9\ W\ per\ channel}$$
