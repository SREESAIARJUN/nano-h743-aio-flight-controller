## 1. Inner Current (Torque) Loop: FOC

### 1.1 Transforms

With θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset, the transforms are:

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park:**
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design

The dq voltage equations are:
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Cross-coupling and back-EMF are cancelled by feedforward:
$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\quad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$. Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole, which gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:
$$K_p^{i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}$$

At 20 kHz, ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s, so K_p = 22.6 V/A and K_i = 52 800 V/(A·s).

### 1.3 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup

$$u^* = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad u_k = \mathrm{sat}(u^*),\quad I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw} T_s (u_k - u^*),\quad K_{aw}=\frac{K_i}{K_p}$$

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and SVPWM

The voltage vector is limited to the linear-modulation circle, $|v_{dq}| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$, with d-axis priority.

Inverse Park gives $(v_\alpha, v_\beta)$. Min-max zero-sequence injection is equivalent to SVPWM:
$$v_x' = v_x - \tfrac12(\max v + \min v),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{dc}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity Control

### 2.1 Velocity estimation (PLL observer)

Differentiating a 14-bit encoder at 4 kHz gives a quantization noise of about 1.5 rad/s, which is unusable. Use a type-2 PLL instead:
$$e_\theta = \mathrm{wrap}(\theta_{meas} - \hat\theta),\quad \hat\omega_{k+1} = \hat\omega_k + K_{i}^{pll}T_s e_\theta,\quad \hat\theta_{k+1} = \hat\theta_k + T_s(\hat\omega_k + K_p^{pll}e_\theta)$$

Gains are set from a critically damped natural frequency ω_n: $K_p^{pll}=2\omega_n$, $K_i^{pll}=\omega_n^2$. Use ω_n ≈ 2π·150 rad/s.
