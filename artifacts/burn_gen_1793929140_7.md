## 0. Design Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Motors | 3× GBM2804-class hollow-shaft BLDC, **12N14P** (p = 7 pole pairs) | R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH, Kt ≈ 0.05–0.1 N·m/A |
| Encoders | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI | Used for commutation and joint angle |

| Gimbal order | Yaw (base) → Roll → Pitch (camera) | Typical handheld/drone layout |

**Loop-rate hierarchy.** Each loop needs a bandwidth separation of at least 5× from the next.

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q) PI | 20 kHz | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity PI | 4 kHz | ~150–250 Hz |
| Angle / attitude P(I) | 1 kHz | ~20–40 Hz |
| IMU sampling + filtering | 8 kHz → decimated to 4 kHz | — |
| Attitude estimator | 4 kHz (gyro), 500 Hz (accel correction) | — |

## 1. Field-Oriented Control

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

$$\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{[0,2\pi)}\!\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0})\big)$$

- θ_m is the encoder mechanical angle.
- θ_{m,0} is the offset found at calibration: apply V_d along α, wait, then read the encoder.
- Electrical speed is ω_e = p·ω_m.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform (Amplitude-Invariant)

With three phase currents (or i_c = −i_a − i_b):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park Transform and Inverse

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e\\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix}$$

The electrical angle must be **advanced by 1.5 sample periods** to compensate for the ZOH and computation delay:

$$\theta_e^{\text{pwm}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$
