## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

$\theta_{\text{off}}$ is found at commissioning: inject $v_d$ with $v_q=0$ and read the encoder once the rotor settles.

**Latency compensation.** The PWM update lands roughly 1.5 $T_{s,i}$ after sampling, so the angle is advanced:

$$\theta_e^{*} = \theta_e + \omega_e \cdot 1.5\,T_{s,i}$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 dq plant model and decoupling

$$v_d = R_s i_d + L_s \dot i_d - \omega_e L_s i_q,\qquad v_q = R_s i_q + L_s \dot i_q + \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$
