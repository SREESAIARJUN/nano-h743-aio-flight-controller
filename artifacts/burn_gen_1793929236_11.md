This is an engineering baseline, not a fab-ready release. All component parameters are representative placeholders. Before committing to layout, replace them with values from the selected MOSFET datasheet (at T_j = 125 °C) and validate with thermocouple or IR testing on prototype hardware.

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Signal chain:**
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) → bidirectional DShot600 → 4× ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32) → 4× 3-phase gate drivers → 24 N-MOSFETs → motors

The H743 does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, which closes the BEMF/commutation loop.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\text{–}0.008\ /^\circ\text{C}$$

At T_j = 125 °C with α = 0.006: R_DS ≈ 1.6 mΩ.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

Take a commutation step where phase A is PWM-high and phase B is low:

- **PWM ON:** current flows through HS_A and LS_B.
- **PWM OFF:** current flows through LS_A (synchronous rectification) and LS_B.

So exactly two FETs carry the phase current I at every instant, outside dead time:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At I = 80 A and R_DS = 1.6 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0016 = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. At typical electrical frequencies (14-pole motor at 30k rpm gives about 3.5 kHz), the die thermal time constant (~ms) averages this out:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ESC}}{6} = 3.4\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R_{DS} = 10.2\ \text{W}$$

The PWM-ing high-side FETs also carry the switching losses from Section 2.
