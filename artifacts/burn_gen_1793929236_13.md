# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage & DShot Architecture Reference

> **Scope and assumptions.** No specific MOSFET, gate driver, or stack-up was specified, so this document uses a representative 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN 40 V MOSFET class typical of nano 4-in-1 ESCs. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute datasheet values directly. All numbers marked **[A]** are assumptions to replace with your BOM data.
>
> The architecture assumes the standard split. The **H743 runs ArduPilot** and emits bidirectional DShot600. The **4-in-1 ESC runs its own per-channel MCU** (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) that performs the 48 kHz commutation PWM.
