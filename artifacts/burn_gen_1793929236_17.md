## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

Two points need settling before the derivations.

**Flag 1: Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each motor channel (or one quad-channel chip) needs an ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421, STM32G071 or GD32E230. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC PWM frequency set in that firmware. The H743 sends DShot600 frames and receives bi-directional eRPM telemetry.

**Flag 2: "Nano" plus 80 A burst.** A 20×20 mm board cannot physically carry 4 × 80 A. Section 3 shows the copper and thermal mass are an order of magnitude short. This document therefore assumes:

- a **30.5×30.5 mm board** (pads ≈ 36×36 mm),
- **8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power planes**,
- **80 A = per-motor phase current, burst ≤ 10 s.**

If a true 20×20 nano is mandatory, the realistic burst rating is about 35–40 A per motor. Section 3.6 shows how to redo the numbers.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32 and BLHeli_32 use trapezoidal six-step drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° electrical sector, phase A sources current and phase B sinks it:

- **HS_A** is PWM'd with duty D.
- **LS_A** freewheels (synchronous rectification) for (1−D).
- **LS_B** is on for the whole sector.

### 1.2 Instantaneous conduction power per motor

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{eff}\,D \;+\; I^2 R_{eff}(1-D) \;+\; I^2 R_{eff} \;=\; 2 I^2 R_{eff}
$$

Conduction loss is **independent of duty** in synchronous six-step. Only battery current scales with D (I_batt ≈ D·I_ph).

The effective resistance at temperature is:

$$
R_{eff}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + k_T (T_j - 25)] + R_{pkg+cu}, \qquad k_T \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

$$
R_{eff}(125^\circ C) = 0.70 \times 1.6 + 0.15 = 1.27\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Condition | R_eff | P_cond per motor | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold (T_j = 25 °C) | 0.85 mΩ | 2·6400·0.85e-3 = **10.9 W** | 43.5 W |
| Hot (T_j = 125 °C) | 1.27 mΩ | 2·6400·1.27e-3 = **16.3 W** | 65.0 W |

### 1.4 Per-FET average

Each FET conducts 120° of 360°, so the average duty is 1/3. Total conduction loss divides across the 6 FETs of a motor:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{eff}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{eff}}{3} = 2.71\ \text{W (hot)}
$$

This averaging is valid for thermal purposes only if the commutation period is much shorter than the junction thermal time constant (τ_jc ≈ 1–5 ms). At 80 A the motor runs at high eRPM, for example 14-pole at 25 kRPM gives 175k eRPM, about 2.9
