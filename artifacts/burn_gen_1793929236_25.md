## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It cannot also commutate four BLDC motors while running ArduPilot.

- Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
- Bi-directional DShot is the link between the H743 and those four ESC MCUs. It only makes sense with that split.
- The architecture is therefore: **H743 (ArduPilot) → 4× bi-dir DShot600 → 4× ESC MCU → 4× 3-phase bridge.**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step drive with synchronous rectification, two phases carry current at any instant:

- **Phase A, high side (PWM leg):** on for duty D. During (1−D) the A low-side FET freewheels the current.
- **Phase B, low side:** on continuously for the 60° sector.

The motor current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly **two** R_DS(on) in series:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 RMS current including PWM ripple

Line-to-line inductance for a large 6S motor is about L ≈ 10 µH. Ripple is worst at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{10\,\mu H \cdot 48\,kHz} = 13.1\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 14.3 = 6414\ \text{A}^2 \;(\text{ripple adds } 0.2\%, \text{ negligible})$$
