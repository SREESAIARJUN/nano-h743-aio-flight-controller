# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope, Architecture Clarification, and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #43 requirements package, so every parameter below is an **explicit, labeled assumption**. Swap in your datasheet values; the equations are written so you can re-run them directly.

**Partitioning.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.
