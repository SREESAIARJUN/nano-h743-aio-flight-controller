## 0. Architecture Corrections and Design Assumptions

These points determine how the rest of the analysis is structured.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and only sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The loss model (§1–3) therefore belongs to the ESC MCU domain, and the DMA model (§5) belongs to the H743 domain.
2. **"80 A burst" is treated as per channel**, at motor phase current at near-full throttle. Four channels at 80 A each would draw 320 A from the battery, which exceeds any XT60/XT90 lead and pack combination used on nano-class craft. Realistic simultaneous worst cases are lower, but I size for the stated figure.
3. **Component parameters are representative placeholders.** They are typical of 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN MOSFETs. Replace every symbol value with your selected part's datasheet number before sign-off. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are a worked example.
