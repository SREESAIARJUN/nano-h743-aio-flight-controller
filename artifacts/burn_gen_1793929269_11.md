# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #52): Design Model

**Status:** Engineering model for design review, Rev A. Every parameter marked **[A]** is an assumption. Before layout freeze, replace each [A] value with the datasheet value of the selected part and validate on a thermal test rig.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification (Read First)

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly in any production-viable 4-in-1 design. The intended architecture is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or H743IIK (BGA for nano) | ArduPilot, generates bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class [A] | AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver [A] | Bootstrap high-side, 10–12 V gate drive |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, 5×6 PDFN [A] | 6 per motor |

**Form-factor reality check.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot sustain 80 A per motor. Section 3 shows four channels at 80 A dissipate ≈70 W. This model therefore assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm mount / ~36×36 mm board**, 8-layer 2 oz copper, as the minimum. On a 20×20 board, derate to roughly 35–45 A burst.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

In any commutation sector, two phases are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for 1−D. Current flows through exactly one FET of this leg at all times, excluding dead time.
- **Fixed leg:** one LS FET conducts 100% of the sector.

So at every instant, the phase current passes through two FET channels in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{ds,hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{ds,hot} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds,hot}
$$

This result is independent of D, which is a key property of synchronous rectification.

### 2.2 Numbers at 80 A

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.44\ W}
$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET carries current for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, in varying roles:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} = 13.44 / 6 = \mathbf{2.24\ W}
$$

**Peak instantaneous FET loss** (fixed-leg LS): 6.72 W, lasting one sector.
- One sector lasts about 20–50 µs at high eRPM.
- The junction thermal time constant is on the order of milliseconds.
- So the junction follows the average, plus a small ripple of less than 1–2 K.

### 2.3 Copper (PCB) conduction loss

Assumed resistances [A]:
- Phase-output path, pad to FET: ≈ 0.15 mΩ per channel.
- Shared battery bus: ≈ 0.10 mΩ.

Phase copper loss per channel:

$$
P_{Cu,ph} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.15\times10^{-3} = 1.92\ W
$$

The bus carries the battery current, I_bat ≈ D·I_ph per motor. At D = 0.8, the four motors together draw 4 × 64 = 256 A:

$$
P_{Cu,bus} = 256^2 \times 0.10\times10^{-3} = 6.55\ W\ \text{(board total)}
$$

**Design rule:** bus copper is as significant as a whole FET channel. Use 3+ parallel internal 2 oz planes for V_bat and GND, and solder pads sized for 10–12 AWG.
