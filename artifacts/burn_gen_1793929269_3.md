# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #51): Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot/DMA Architecture

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

**Architectural correction.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz bridge PWM. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The analysis therefore splits into two domains:

| Domain | Device | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743 @ 480 MHz | ArduPilot, DShot600 TX/RX via timer + DMA |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421 (example) | 6-step/FOC commutation, 48 kHz PWM, gate drive |
| Power | 4 × (3 half-bridges) = 24 MOSFETs | Load current |

**Baseline parameters** (all values are parametric; substitute your datasheet numbers):

| Symbol | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching) |
| I_burst | 80 A per channel | Burst, not continuous |
| R_ds(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ | 40 V class, 5×6 / 3.3×3.3 DFN |
| α (R_ds temp. coeff.) | ≈ 0.0045 /K | Normalized R_ds(on) ≈ 1.45 at 125 °C |
| t_r + t_f | 20 ns | With ~2 A gate driver, R_g ≈ 2–3 Ω |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | Body diode, during dead time |
| Q_oss | 30 nC | At V_bus |
| Q_g | 30 nC @ 10 V | Total gate charge |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel (320 A aggregate) on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) footprint is a burst-only rating, limited by thermal capacitance. It is not a steady-state rating. Section 2 quantifies the time limit.

## 1. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Conduction losses

For 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification, two phase legs carry current at any instant:

- **Active high/low pair:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side (synchronous freewheel) conducts for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on for the full 60° sector.

Conduction loss per channel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This total is independent of D, which is a key insight for synchronous 6-step. The *distribution* between high-side and low-side FETs does depend on D.

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.2 m = **15.4 W** |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | **20.6 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | **22.3 W** |

**Ripple correction.** The RMS current includes the inductor ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH (line-to-line, typical 2207 motor), D = 0.5, V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

- ΔI = 13.2 × 0.5 / (15 µ × 48 k) ≈ 9.2 A
- The ripple term adds about 7 A², a 0.1 % increase, which is negligible.

At 48 kHz, ripple is not a loss driver. Prefer 48 kHz over 24 kHz for acoustic and ripple reasons.

### 1.2 Switching losses (hard-switched high-side)

Only the PWM-ing leg switches. Per channel:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 1.3 Reverse recovery and output capacitance

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ W$$

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ W$$

### 1.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 2 \times 100\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

Dead time is significant. Keep t_dt ≤ 100 ns (AM32 dead-time setting must match the driver and FET).

### 1.5 Gate drive (lands in the driver IC, not the FETs)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} \times N_{switching} = 30\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 \approx 0.03\ W$$

### 1.6 Totals at T_j = 100 °C, 80 A burst

| Loss | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 20.6 W | 82.4 W |
| Switching | 0.97 W | 3.9 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 W | 0.3 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-channel) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| PCB copper (≈0.3 mΩ path) | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 25.8 W** | **≈ 103 W** |

Conduction dominates at about 80 %. Optimization priority is therefore R_ds(on), then copper, then switching.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Thermal network

The model is a three-node Foster/Cauer reduction:

```
P ──► [Junction] ──R_jc(0.8 K/W per FET)──► [Case/Pad] ──R_cb──► [PCB Cu mass C_pcb] ──R_ba──► Ambient
            C_j (small)                                           ≈ 5 J/K               (prop wash)
```

### 2.2 Board heat capacity (25.5 × 25.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz)

| Material | Volume | Mass | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper (8 × 70 µm, ~85 % fill) | 3.1e-7 m³ | 2.8 g | 1.07 J/K |
| FR4 (1.6 mm) | 1.04e-6 m³ | 1.9 g | 2.1 J/K |
| 24 FETs + bus cap + solder | — | ~3 g | ~1.4 J/K |
| **C_total** | | | **≈ 4.6 J/K** |

### 2.3 Adiabatic burst duration

With all four channels at 80 A, P ≈ 103 W. Starting at 45 °C, the limit is 110 °C board (keeping T_j < 150 °C):

$$t_{burst} \approx \frac{C\,\Delta T}{P - \Delta T_{avg}/R_{ba}} \approx \frac{4.6 \times 65}{103 - 6} \approx \mathbf{3.1\ s}$$

The R_ba ≈ 5.5 K/W term covers prop-wash convection, which is negligible at this power level.

### 2.4 Junction rise above board

With 2 FETs conducting per channel, each dissipates about 10.3 W (conduction) plus a share of switching. Assuming R_jc + R_cb ≈ 1.5 K/W per FET:

$$\Delta T_{jb} \approx 11\text{ W} \times 1.5 \approx 17\ \text{K}$$

At the 110 °C board limit, T_j ≈ 127 °C. This sits inside a 150 °C rating with about 20 K margin.

### 2.5 Continuous rating

Steady state is limited by R_ba ≈ 5.5 K/W at 65 K rise:

- Total allowed: P_cont ≈ 12 W, so about 3 W per channel.
- Per channel: I_cont ≈ √(3 / (2 × 1.6 m)) ≈ **31 A**.

**Marketing-safe rating: 30 A continuous / 80 A burst (≤ 2 s) per channel.**

### 2.6 Firmware protection

- Enable AM32 temperature limit (set to 100 °C).
- Enable AM32 current limit.
- Set ArduPilot `MOT_BAT_CURR_MAX`.
- Implement an I²t integrator on the ESC, so that ∫(P − P_cont)dt ≤ C·ΔT ≈ 300 J.
