# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model for Job #55

**Scope:** The design has two partitions:

- **Flight controller.** An STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600.
- **4-in-1 ESC.** Four independent ESC MCUs (AM32-class) each drive a 3-phase MOSFET bridge at 48 kHz.

ArduPilot does not commutate motors directly, so the H743 never drives the gates. I have kept these domains separate throughout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step
At any instant one phase is high, one is low, and one floats. The full phase current I flows through two conducting device positions:

- **PWM on:** HS of phase A and LS of phase B. Loss = I²(R_HS + R_LS).
- **PWM off (sync rect):** LS of phase A and LS of phase B. Loss = I²(R_LS + R_LS).

With identical FETs, conduction loss is essentially independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ /K \;\Rightarrow\; R(100^\circ C) \approx 1.5\,R_{25}$$

### 1.3 Burst value per channel (I = 80 A, T_j = 100 °C)

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

This loss is shared across the two conducting device positions, so each conducting FET dissipates about 9.6 W.

### 1.4 Copper loss (do not ignore)
Assume 80 A phase and bus current over about 15 mm of path.

- Six layers in parallel of 2 oz copper (70 µm), 5 mm wide.
- Combined cross-section: 6 × 0.07 mm × 5 mm = 2.1 mm².
- Copper resistivity: ρ_Cu(100 °C) ≈ 2.2×10⁻⁸ Ω·m.

$$R = \frac{2.2\times10^{-8}\times0.015}{2.1\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.16\ \text{m}\Omega$$

- Loss: 80² × 0.16 m ≈ **1.0 W** per path.
- Budget about 2 W per channel, covering the bus feed plus two phase legs.

### 1.5 Thermal runaway check
Because R_DS(on) rises with temperature, iterate the loss model:

$$P(T) = 2I^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T-25)]$$

The system is stable only while:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{th(j-a)} < 1$$

At 80 A:
- ∂P/∂T = 2 × 6400 × 1e-3 × 0.0067 = 0.086 W/K.
- The criterion therefore requires R_th(j-a) < 11.6 K/W.
- A nano board is about 15–25 K/W per channel, which fails this check.

**Conclusion:** 80 A is valid **only as a time-limited burst**. Firmware current limiting is mandatory (see Section 3.4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step with sync rect, one half-bridge leg hard-switches per PWM period. The other active leg is static during the sector.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching, HS device)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode conduction during dead time (two dead times per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\text{n}\times635\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FET)

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\times60\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.6 Per-channel totals at 80 A, 48 kHz

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.2 |
| Copper | ~2.0 |
| V–I switching | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.11 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 23.9 W** |
| **4 channels simultaneous** | **≈ 95 W** |

Efficiency at 80 A, 25.2 V, 50% duty: the input power is about 1008 W, so η ≈ 97.6%. Efficiency looks good, but **heat density is the problem, not efficiency.**
