## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha\approx0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 0.9 × 1.5 = **1.35 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During each 60° sector, phase current I flows through two legs:
- The **PWM leg** carries the current through the HS FET during D and through the complementary LS FET during (1−D).
- The **return leg** LS FET carries it 100% of the time.

So exactly two FETs always carry I:

$$P_{cond,ESC}=2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per ESC @ 80 A | ×4 ESCs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ | 11.5 W | 46 W |
| 100 °C | 1.24 mΩ | 15.9 W | 63 W |
| 125 °C | 1.35 mΩ | **17.3 W** | **69 W** |

### 1.3 Parasitic copper and shunt

- **Shunt:** 0.25 mΩ, so I²R = 1.6 W per ESC. Use a 0.2 to 0.25 mΩ 2512 shunt, or low-side phase sensing.
- **Copper:** a 2 oz trace 5 mm wide × 10 mm long is about 0.5 mΩ, giving 3.2 W. Mitigate by stacking parallel layers and using pad-in-via.

$$P_{cond,total/ESC}\approx 17.3+1.6+(0.5\text{–}3) \approx \mathbf{20\ W}\ @125^\circ C$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each phase's HS and LS each conduct across 120° of electrical rotation. Averaged, the loss spreads over 6 FETs:

$$\bar P_{FET}\approx \frac{17.3}{6}\times\frac{6}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\approx 2.9\ \text{W}$$

(Exact split depends on D. The worst case at D→1 places about 5.8 W in the active HS FET while conducting.)

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only one FET hard-switches per PWM period. The LS FET soft-switches after dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching (V–I overlap)

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

Where t_r + t_f ≈ 20 + 20 ns with a 2 A gate driver and R_g = 3.3 Ω. Calculation: t ≈ Q_gd × R_g,total / (V_drv − V_plateau).

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt}=V_{SD}\,I\,(2t_{dead})\,f = 0.8(80)(2\times50\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f = 30\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

(Plus the induced HS turn-on current bump. This is folded into t_r margin.)

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss}\approx\tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus}f = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g)

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f = 2(40\text{n})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Summary per ESC @ 80 A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs, 125 °C) | 17.3 |
| Shunt + copper | ~2.5 |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + Q_g | 0.10 |
| **Total / ESC** | **≈ 22 W** |
| **Total, 4-in-1** | **≈ 88 W** |

**Sensitivity.** Switching loss is about 10% of the total, so conduction dominates. Raising f_sw to 96 kHz adds only about 2.3 W per ESC. The real lever is R_DS(on): consider paralleling two FETs per switch if the footprint allows, which roughly halves P_cond.
