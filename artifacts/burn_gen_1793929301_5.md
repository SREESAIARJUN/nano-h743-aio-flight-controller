## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Risks

### 0.1 Architecture Correction: the H743 Cannot Commutate Four Motors Itself

The STM32H743 has only two advanced-control timers with complementary outputs and hardware dead-time: TIM1 and TIM8. Four 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary pairs, so they cannot be driven from those timers. HRTIM is a separate block with its own limitations and is not suited to this.
