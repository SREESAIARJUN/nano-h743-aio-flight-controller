## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (e.g., STM32G474), single-precision float |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) per axis |
| Current sense | 2- or 3-shunt, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera platform, SPI, ODR = 8 kHz, shared sampling trigger |
| Gimbal kinematics | Yaw → Roll → Pitch (outer to inner), camera on pitch |

**Loop rates.** Each loop runs about 4–5× slower than the one inside it, and closes at about 1/5–1/10 of its own sample rate.

| Loop | Rate | Target closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (PWM ISR) | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | ~150–250 Hz |
| Attitude / angle | 1 kHz (IMU-decimated) | ~20–40 Hz |
| IMU acquisition + filtering | 8 kHz → decimated to 4 kHz / 1 kHz | — |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Electrical angle** (encoder mechanical angle θ_m, calibrated offset θ_0, latency compensation T_d):

$$
\theta_e = p\left(\theta_m + \omega_m T_d - \theta_0\right) \bmod 2\pi
$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\left(i_a + 2 i_b\right)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Decoupling

PMSM model in the dq frame:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Torque:**

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m
$$

**Feedforward decoupling** cancels the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 Current PI Controller

Pole-zero cancellation of the RL plant gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c \quad (\text{continuous})
$$

Discretization (forward Euler on the integrator), with back-calculation anti-windup (gain K_b ≈ 1/K_p):

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k
$$

$$
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_b\big(\mathrm{sat}(u_k) - u_k\big)
$$
