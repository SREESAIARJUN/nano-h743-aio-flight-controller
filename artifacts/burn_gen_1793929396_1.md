# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Basis Document (Job #35)

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, the thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and the reference netlist.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

Three points shape the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC commutation MCU.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 parts running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 cannot run ArduPilot and also perform four channels of sensorless FOC/6-step commutation at 48 kHz. The netlist below reflects this split.

2. **80 A burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is at the edge of physical feasibility.** The model below shows roughly 90–100 W of dissipation with all four channels at 80 A. A 20×20 board can sustain that only for a few seconds. **80 A must be specified as a time-limited burst rating per channel, not a simultaneous continuous rating.** Section 3 derives the allowable burst duration.

3. **Device parameters are assumptions.** All MOSFET, driver, and PCB values are representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN figures. Before release, re-run the model with the selected part's datasheet values, especially R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, Q_rr, and Z_th(j-c).

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.383 = **1.66 mΩ**.

**Six-step with synchronous rectification**

At any instant, the phase current flows through exactly two MOSFETs in series:

- the PWM leg, where the high side conducts for D and its complementary low side for (1−D), and
- the static low side of the return leg.

The PWM leg's path resistance is therefore R_DS independent of D. The bridge conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2(80)^2(1.66\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{21.2\ W}$$

**Per-FET average over one electrical revolution**

Each of the six FETs carries the load for 1/3 of the period:

$$\bar P_{FET} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS} = 3.54\ \text{W}$$

**Worst-case instantaneous FET**

This is the static low side during its 120° window:

$$\hat P_{FET} = I^2 R_{DS} = 10.6\ \text{W}$$

This sets the short-timescale junction ripple (Section 3.3).

**Copper and shunt**

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.4\,\text{m}\Omega = 2.56\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{shunt} \approx I^2 R_{sh} = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

The shunt figure assumes a low-side return shunt. For a DC-link shunt, multiply by D.

### 2.2 Switching at 48 kHz

Only one leg switches at a time in 6-step.

**V-I overlap (hard switching, inductive clamp)**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(30\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**Output capacitance**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Reverse recovery**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (2 edges per period)**

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot100\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = 0.307\ \text{W}$$

**Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, 2 FETs/cycle)**

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot60\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{sw,total} \approx \mathbf{1.92\ W}$$

### 2.3 Channel and Board Totals at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

| Loss | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 21.2 W | 84.8 W |
| Switching (all terms) | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| Copper | 2.6 W | 10.2 W |
| Shunt | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **27.3 W** | **109 W** |

**Interpretations**

- **Conduction dominates by a factor of about 11 over switching.** Moving from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only about 0.8 W per channel. The design lever is R_DS(on) and copper, not frequency.
- **Pick the lowest-R_DS(on) part the gate driver can drive.** Larger Q_g costs very little at 48 kHz.
- **Duty-weighted operation:** if the burst occurs at partial throttle with D < 1, conduction is unchanged under sync rectification. Only P_VI scales with current.

### 2.4 Bulk Capacitor Ripple

The DC-link capacitor RMS current per switching channel is:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\le\; 0.5I = \mathbf{40\ A_{rms}}\ \text{at } D=0.5$$

- Channels are not phase-synchronized across ESCs, so the four-channel worst case is roughly √4 × 40 = 80 A_rms (uncorrelated). A single 35 V electrolytic cannot handle this.
- **Requirements:**
  - 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 MLCCs per channel, placed at the half-bridges;
  - plus an external low-ESR 1000 µF/35 V electrolytic on the battery pigtail.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network

```
Pj(t) ── Zθ(j-c) ── Tcase ── Rθ(c-pcb) ── [C_pcb ∥ Rθ(pcb-amb)] ── Tamb
                                            ↑ lumped board node
```

- **Board thermal mass.** The board is 20×20 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz inner layers, with about 4 g of copper plus FR4 plus components:
  - C_pcb ≈ 4 g × 0.6 J/g·K ≈ 2.4 J/K (effective);
  - add about 1.5 J/K with a 2 g aluminium clip-on heatsink.
- **Board-to-ambient resistance:**
  - still air (20×20 mm): R_θ,pcb-a ≈ 25 °C/W;
  - prop-wash (~8 m/s): ≈ 8 °C/W.
