The loops are nested with bandwidth separation of at least 5× between each level:

```
 20 kHz  PWM ISR : ADC → Clarke → Park → PI(id,iq) → invPark → SVPWM
  4 kHz  task    : velocity PI  (gyro + encoder blended rate) + cogging FF
  8 kHz  IMU DRDY: dual-IMU fusion, adaptive notches, LPF
  1 kHz  task    : attitude estimator (Mahony) + angle P/PD + Jacobian mapping
```

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Motor Model (PMSM, dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

The electrical angle and speed follow from the mechanical quantities and the encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}, \qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m$$

The torque equation is:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q)i_d i_q\right] \approx K_t\, i_q,\quad K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m$$

### 1.2 Transforms (Amplitude-Invariant)

The Clarke transform uses the constraint i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

The Park transform rotates into the rotor frame:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$
