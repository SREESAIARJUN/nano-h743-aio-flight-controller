# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #18): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

Before any math, several framing issues need resolving. Otherwise the model will describe hardware that cannot exist on this board.

**1. The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.**
- The H743 has only TIM1, TIM8 (3 complementary pairs each) and HRTIM (5 pairs). That is 11 complementary pairs, not the 12 needed for 4 × 3-phase bridges.
- No maintained firmware runs four sensorless BLDC loops plus ArduPilot on one die.
- If the H743 did drive the bridges, bi-directional DShot would be meaningless, because DShot is an FC→ESC link.
- **Architecture adopted:** H743 (ArduPilot) → 4× bidirectional DShot600 → 4× AT32F421 (AM32) → 4× 3-phase gate driver → 24 MOSFETs.

**2. "80 A burst" must be bounded.**
- Four channels × 80 A × 6S is about 8 kW. No nano-class board (30.5 mm mount, ~36 × 36 mm PCB) survives that for more than about 1–2 s.
- Below, I compute the actual allowable burst duration rather than assume it.

**3. Component values are representative, not final.**
Values are for a 40 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm PQFN-class MOSFET. Replace every parameter marked ⟨DS⟩ with the selected part's datasheet value before release.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 × 1.45 = 1.74 mΩ. A conservative 1.6× factor gives **1.92 mΩ**, used below.

### 1.2 Per-FET duty weighting

In each 60° commutation sector, exactly one high-side (HS) phase is PWM'd at duty D and one low-side (LS) phase is held on. Over one electrical cycle, each phase spends:
- 1/3 of the time as the PWM'd high leg,
- 1/3 as the held low leg,
- 1/3 floating.

With complementary (synchronous) PWM, this gives:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{DS}$$

$$P_{LS} = \left[\tfrac{1}{3}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3}\right] I^2 R_{DS}$$

Exactly two FET positions conduct the full current at every instant, so the channel total is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
