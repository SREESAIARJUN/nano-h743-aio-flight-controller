## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Up-Front Findings

You did not supply a BOM for Job #24, so every number below comes from a **declared reference design**. Each equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values.

### 0.1 Architectural clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. In every production 4-in-1 design, and in ArduPilot's model:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) --bi-dir DShot600 x4--> 4x ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32, e.g. AT32F421/STM32G071)
                                          --48 kHz 6-step PWM--> gate driver --> 6 FETs --> motor
```

The DShot math (§5) applies to the H743. The switching math (§3) applies to the ESC MCUs and power stage.

### 0.2 Findings to resolve before layout

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | 4 × 80 A simultaneous = **320 A battery current**. An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous / 180 A peak. | "80 A burst" must be defined as **per-channel, non-simultaneous**, or the input connector and wiring must change (XT90 or direct solder pads). |
| F2 | The 20×20 board's thermal mass is about 2–3 J/K (§4.3). | At full 4×80 A, the board heats at about 30–55 K/s. Realistic burst duration is **1–3 s**, not the 10 s often marketed. |
| F3 | Single FET per switch position gives about 30 W per channel. | Recommend **2 FETs in parallel per position**, or derate the burst to about 55–60 A. |
