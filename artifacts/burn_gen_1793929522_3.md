# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #26): Electrothermal, Switching and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Model

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

These assumptions drive every number below. Change them and the equations still apply, but the results will differ.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

With two FETs in parallel per switch position:

$$R_{sw}(T_j) = \frac{R_{DS}(T_j)}{2} + R_{pcb}$$

Here R<sub>pcb</sub> ≈ 0.15 mΩ, the share of copper per switch path.

### 1.2 Bridge conduction (6-step, synchronous)

In any commutation sector, two legs are active:

- The **PWM leg** conducts through the high side for a fraction D of the period and the low side (synchronous freewheel) for 1−D.
- The **return leg** conducts through the low side 100 % of the time.

Therefore exactly two switch positions carry I<sub>ph</sub> at all times, independent of D:

$$\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{sw}(T_j)}$$

Over a full electrical cycle, each of the 6 positions averages one-third of the total:

$$P_{cond,pos} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I_{ph}^2 R_{sw}$$

### 1.3 Numerical result at 80 A

Assume T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C (iterated in §3.3):

- R<sub>DS</sub>(110) = 1.2 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.81 mΩ**
- R<sub>sw</sub> = 0.906 + 0.15 = **1.056 mΩ**

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.056\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{13.5\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.4 Shunt and trace loss

Assume a 0.25 mΩ low-side shared current-sense shunt. In 6-step the DC-link current flows through it:

$$P_{shunt} = I_{ph}^2 D\,R_{sh}$$

At D = 0.8 this is 6400 × 0.8 × 0.25 mΩ = **1.28 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f<sub>sw</sub> = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard. The commutating leg switches at the electrical frequency, which is negligible.

### 2.1 Transition time (gate-charge method)

$$I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{DRV}-V_{pl}}{R_{G,ext}+R_{G,int}+R_{drv,on}},\qquad t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_G}$$

Inputs: V<sub>DRV</sub> = 10 V, V<sub>pl</sub> = 3.5 V, R<sub>G,int</sub> = 1 Ω, R<sub>drv</sub> ≈ 4 Ω (FD6288 effective).

Each switch position has 2 parallel FETs, so the charge doubles: (Q<sub>gd</sub> + Q<sub>gs2</sub>) ≈ 2 × 9 nC = 18 nC.

- Turn-on: I<sub>G</sub> = 6.5 / 8.3 = 0.78 A, so t<sub>r</sub> ≈ 23 ns.
- Turn-off: I<sub>G</sub> = 3.5 / 7 = 0.5 A, so t<sub>f</sub> ≈ 36 ns.
- Add ~5 ns of commutation-loop inductance effect each way: t<sub>r</sub> + t<sub>f</sub> ≈ **65 ns**.

### 2.2 Loss components (per channel)

| Term | Equation | Value (25.2 V, 80 A, 48 kHz) |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t<sub>r</sub>+t<sub>f</sub>)·f | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 65 ns × 48k = **3.14 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q<sub>rr</sub>·V·f (×2 FETs) | 80 nC × 25.2 × 48k = **0.10 W** |
| Coss | Q<sub>oss</sub>·V·f (×2) | 60 nC × 25.2 × 48k = **0.07 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | 2·V<sub>SD</sub>·I·t<sub>dt</sub>·f | 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (in driver/LDO) | Q<sub>g</sub>·V<sub>DRV</sub>·f × 4 FETs | 160 nC × 10 × 48k = **0.08 W** |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 4.0 W** |
