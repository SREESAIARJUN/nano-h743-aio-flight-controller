## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in synchronous 6-step drive

During any 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM-switched and phase B is held low:

- **Interval D (on-state):** current flows Q_AH → motor → Q_BL. Two FETs are in series.
- **Interval (1−D) (freewheel):** current flows Q_AL (synchronous rectification) → motor → Q_BL. Two FETs are in series.

So at every instant, exactly two R_DS(on) are in the current path, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_M^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS}\right] = 2\,I_M^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) \quad (R_{HS}=R_{LS})
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]
$$

Conduction loss and junction temperature are coupled, so solve iteratively or evaluate at the design limit. At T_j = 100 °C:

$$
R_{DS}(100) = 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 75) = 2.26\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

Add PCB copper and solder parasitics, R_par ≈ 0.25 mΩ per path:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.26\,\text{m}\Omega + 80^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 28.9 + 1.6 = \mathbf{30.5\ W}
$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each phase is high-side PWM for 120°, low-side for 120°, and floating for 120°. Over a full cycle the loss is distributed fairly evenly across all six FETs:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{2 I_M^2 R_{DS}}{6} = 4.8\ \text{W (average)}
$$

**Thermal runaway check.** The loop gain is

$$
G = \frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = P\,\frac{\alpha}{1+\alpha\Delta T}\,R_{th}
$$

With 4.8 W, α/(1+αΔT) ≈ 0.0039, and R_th(j-board) ≈ 4.5 K/W, G ≈ 0.08 ≪ 1. The junction-to-board loop is stable. Ambient-referenced runaway is bounded only because the burst is time-limited (§3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-active half-bridge switches. Per motor, at V = 25.2 V and I = 80 A:

| Mechanism | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap (hard switching, HS FET) | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k | **1.45 W** |
| Body diode reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.036 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw = 0.5 × 25 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.015 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction (LS FET) | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive (mostly dissipated in driver/R_G) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2 × 40 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.038 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 1.85 W** |
