# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Analysis (Job #1)

## 0. Scope and Design Basis

### System partition

The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, such as STM32G071 or AT32F421). The H743 only originates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry.

```
VBAT ──► [Power stage: 4 × (3 half-bridges)] ──► Motors 1–4
              ▲ gate drive
         4 × ESC MCU (AM32)  ◄── DShot600 BIDIR ──► STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
                                                      ├─ SPI1: IMU1
                                                      ├─ SPI4: IMU2
                                                      ├─ I2C/SPI: Baro
                                                      └─ UARTs, ADC (Vbat, Ibat)
```
