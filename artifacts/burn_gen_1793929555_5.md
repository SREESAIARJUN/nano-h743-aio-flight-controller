## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two points in the brief need correcting before any of the math is meaningful.

**1. The STM32H743 cannot run motor commutation for ArduPilot.** ArduPilot runs the flight stack and generates DShot. Commutation needs one dedicated ESC MCU per motor. The realistic architecture is:

```
STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) ──4× BDShot600──► 4× AT32F421K8U7 / STM32G071GBU6 (AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                                              └─► 4× FD6288Q gate driver ─► 4× 6-FET bridge
```

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 150 W. With prop-wash cooling, a 20×20 board can shed roughly 10–15 W at a reasonable ΔT. The analysis below sizes 80 A as a ≤1 s per-channel burst and derives a defensible continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path (Trapezoidal 6-Step, Complementary PWM)

In any commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high side conducts for D·T_sw and the synchronous low side for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low side is fully on for the whole sector.

So, outside dead time, the current always passes through exactly two R_ds(on) in series:

$$R_{path}(T_j) = 2\,R_{ds(on),25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}$$

With α ≈ 0.006 /K, the factor at 125 °C is 1.6, giving R_ds(on) = 2.4 mΩ.

### 1.2 Total Channel Conduction Loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \left(2 R_{ds}(T_j)\right) = 80^2 \times 4.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

Copper and shunt losses add to this:

- Copper (pads, planes, motor pads): R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ.
- Shunt: 0.1 mΩ (a single low-side DC shunt per channel conducts during both sectors).

$$P_{Cu+sh} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution (Hotspot Driver)

Over one electrical revolution, each FET carries:

- the full return-leg current for 1/3 of the time (120°), and
- a PWM-shared portion of the PWM-leg current for another 1/3.

The average per FET is approximately:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}\left[1 + \underbrace{D\ \text{or}\ (1-D)}_{\le 1}\right]$$
