# Job #43: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst Design Package

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (conduction and 48 kHz switching), transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Architecture Decision and Stated Assumptions

### 0.1 The H743 should not drive the gates directly

An STM32H743 driving 12 half-bridges directly is not a viable ArduPilot architecture, for three reasons:

1. **Not enough complementary timers.** Four motors need 12 complementary PWM pairs. TIM1 and TIM8 provide 3 pairs each, which covers only 2 motors. HRTIM can supplement, but nothing in ArduPilot uses it for motor drive.
2. **No firmware support.** ArduPilot has no sensorless BEMF six-step or FOC motor-drive layer for multirotor ESCs.
3. **Wrong fault domain.** A flight-controller fault would become a shoot-through fault.
