# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80 A ESC (Job #42): Power-Stage and DShot Timing Design

This document derives the 80 A burst conduction losses, the 48 kHz switching losses, the thermal transient model, the ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and the netlists.

**Key findings:**
1. **80 A is a burst rating of about 2–3 s on a 30×30 mm board.** No continuous 80 A operation is achievable at this size.
2. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** Each motor gets its own ESC MCU running AM32, and the H743 drives them over bidirectional DShot600.
