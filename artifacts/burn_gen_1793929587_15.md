# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #61): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #61 requirements, component selections, or layout, so every parameter below is a stated assumption. The equations are general. Replace the assumed values with your datasheet values and re-run them.

**Architectural correction.** ArduPilot running on the H743 does not commutate motors. The H743 is the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071), which drive the gate drivers and FETs. The model below follows that partition.

**Main finding.** On a 20×20 mm "nano" board, 80 A per channel is only survivable as a short burst, roughly 100–200 ms. Section 3 shows why.
