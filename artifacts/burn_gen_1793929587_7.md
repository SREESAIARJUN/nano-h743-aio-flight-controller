# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

## 0. Architecture Correction and Baseline Assumptions

I have no prior record of "Job #58", so every parameter below is stated explicitly. Swap in your datasheet values and the equations still apply.

**Architectural point to resolve first:** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The design is therefore partitioned:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir ×4──> 4× AT32F421 (AM32, 48 kHz PWM) ──> 4× 3-phase bridges
```

**80A burst vs. "nano" form factor:** the math in §3 shows that 4×80A simultaneously is a seconds-scale burst on any 20×20 or 30×30 board. I assume a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board. A 20×20 board cannot carry 80A phase current through its copper and connectors without exceeding safe temperatures within about 1–2 s.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 105 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005·80) = **1.40 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step (trapezoidal) synchronous bridge

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- The PWM-ed leg has the high side on for D and the synchronous low side on for (1−D).
- The opposite leg has its low side static-on for 100%.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.40\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.9\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Per FET (each conducts 120° of 360°, averaged): ≈ 17.9/6 × (uneven split) gives roughly **3.0 W average, 9 W instantaneous** for the static low-side during its interval.
