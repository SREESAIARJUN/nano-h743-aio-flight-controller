## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: apply a d-axis vector (v_α = V_align, v_β = 0) and record θ_m once the rotor settles.

**Latency compensation.** The PWM update is applied one period after sampling, so the angle used for the inverse Park transform is advanced:

$$\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_{pwm}$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q) i_d i_q\right] \;\approx\; K_t i_q \quad(\text{SPM},\ K_t=\tfrac32 p\lambda_m)$$
