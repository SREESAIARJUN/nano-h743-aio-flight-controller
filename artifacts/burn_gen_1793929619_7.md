- **Encoder:** 14-bit magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A or MT6701).

- **MCU:** STM32G4 or H7 class with a single-precision FPU.
- **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P. IMU-C sits on the camera plate (primary feedback). IMU-B sits on the base, on the rigid side of the dampers (vibration reference and base-rate feedforward).
