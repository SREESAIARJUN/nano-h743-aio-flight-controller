# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the original Job #13 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly (§0). Change the parameters in `GimbalConfig` to match your hardware. The equations and structure carry over.

---

## 0. Assumed Platform and Timing Budget

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz (FPU, CORDIC, 3× ADC, HRTIM/TIM1/TIM8) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Encoders | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A) per axis, SPI |
| Current sense | 2-phase low-side shunts (voltage-mode fallback provided) |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, gyro ±2000 dps, accel ±16
