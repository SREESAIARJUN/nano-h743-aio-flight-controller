## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, two phases are active:

- **Phase A, the PWM leg.** The high side conducts for duty D. The low side conducts synchronously for (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **Phase B, the return leg.** The low side conducts continuously at 100%.
- **Phase C** floats for BEMF sensing.

So at every instant, exactly two MOSFET channels carry I_ph (ignoring dead time):

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS,H}
$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

For phase inductance L_ph ≈ 15 µH (typical 2207-class motor at 6S), the worst-case peak-to-peak ripple at D = 0.5 is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\times10^{-6}\cdot 48\times10^{3}} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}} = \sqrt{6400 + 6.38} = 80.04\ \text{A}
$$

Ripple adds less than 0.1%, so it is negligible.

### 1.3 Result

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.9\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{24.3\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

**Per-FET average.** Each switch carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle in total. Six FETs share the loss, so the average is about 4.05 W per FET at 80 A continuous phase current.

### 1.4 Copper conduction loss

Path length per channel (bus → FET → phase pad → return) is about 30 mm each way at 6 mm width. Four 2 oz layers are paralleled with via stitching.

$$
R_{layer} = \frac{\rho L}{wt} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot0.03}{6\times10^{-3}\cdot70\times10^{-6}} = 1.23\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
R_{path} \approx 2 \times \frac{1.23}{4} \times 1.3_{(hot)} = 0.80\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.80\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{5.1\ W}
$$
