## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in trapezoidal six-step with synchronous rectification

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct and one floats:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The complementary low side (LS) conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** the LS conducts 100 % of the sector.

Motor current I flows through exactly one PWM-leg device at a time plus the return-leg LS. Conduction loss is therefore approximately duty-independent:
