## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset).

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Current PI Design

The PMSM model in the dq frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Decoupling feedforward** (cancels the cross-coupling terms):

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to the plant G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). A PI controller with zero placed on the pole (pole-zero cancellation) is:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/20. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

Discrete form (forward Euler, sample time T_s):

$$x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + K_i T_s\, e[k] + K_{aw}\,(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})$$

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and SVPWM

**Circular voltage limit:** if √(v_d² + v_q²) > V_bus/√3, scale (v_d, v_q) down to the circle. Give d-axis priority.

**SVPWM via min/max zero-sequence injection:**

$$v_{a,b,c}\ \text{from inverse Clarke},\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\quad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Rate → Current Control

### 2.1 Gimbal Kinematics

The yaw-roll-pitch joint chain has joint angles q = [q_y, q_r, q_p]. The camera inertial rate is:

$$\boldsymbol\omega_{cam} = \boldsymbol\omega_{base}^{cam} + J(q)\,\dot q$$

For a Z-X-Y chain (yaw → roll → pitch), expressed in the camera frame:

$$J(q)=\begin{bmatrix}-\cos q_r\sin q_p & \cos q_p & 0\\ \sin q_r & 0 & 1\\ \cos q_r\cos q_p & \sin q_p & 0\end{bmatrix}\quad(\text{columns: } \hat z_{yaw},\hat x_{roll},\hat y_{pitch}\text{ in cam frame})$$

Verify the column order against your own mechanical layout. J is singular at |q_r| = 90° (gimbal lock), so clamp q_r to ±70°.
