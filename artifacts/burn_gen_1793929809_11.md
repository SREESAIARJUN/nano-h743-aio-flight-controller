**Finding 1: The architecture needs a correction.**
- The STM32H743 cannot run the ESCs directly. It runs ArduPilot as the FC.
- Each ESC channel (or a single quad-ESC MCU) runs AM32/BLHeli_32 firmware.
- Bidirectional DShot is the link between the two.

**Finding 2: 80 A burst on 20×20 mm is not thermally credible beyond about 1–3 seconds.** Section 3 shows this numerically. Treat it as a design-review blocker, not a footnote.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step commutation
In every 60° electrical sector, two phase legs carry current and one floats. With synchronous rectification:
- The **PWM leg** alternates: high-side during D·T_sw, low-side during (1−D)·T_sw.
- The **return leg** low-side (or high-side) conducts continuously for the 120° window.

So at any instant, exactly **two MOSFET channels carry I_ph in series**. Per motor channel:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot \left[2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}\right]$$

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 0.9 mΩ × 1.45 ≈ **1.31 mΩ** (use 1.44 mΩ if the datasheet shows 1.6×).

### 1.3 Per-channel numbers at 80 A (T_j = 125 °C, conservative 1.44 mΩ)

| Term | Expression | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFETs | 80² × 2 × 1.44 mΩ | **18.4 W** |
| Copper (pad→phase, 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Shunt, 0.25 mΩ (if low-side/bus sense) | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈ 21.9 W/channel** |

### 1.4 Per-device distribution
Each of the 6 FETs conducts 1/3 of the electrical cycle (120°), split between PWM and freewheel roles:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(1.44\text{ m}\Omega) \approx 3.1\ \text{W}$$

During its active window the device dissipates 9.2 W, which matters at low eRPM where the window is long relative to the device thermal time constant (~ms).

### 1.5 Partial-throttle correction
For a given battery current at duty D:

$$I_{ph} \approx I_{batt}/D$$

At D = 0.6, a 48 A battery draw is already 80 A in the phase. **Size thermals on phase current, not the advertised battery current.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The return leg changes state only at commutation (eRPM-rate, negligible).

### 2.1 V-I overlap

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} + Q_{gs2}}{I_g} \approx \frac{9 + 4\ \text{nC}}{1.5\ \text{A}} \approx 9\ \text{ns} \quad (\text{use } 15\ \text{ns with layout parasitics})$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(2 \times 100\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

Reducing dead time to 50 ns halves this, provided the driver and FET turn-off allow it without shoot-through.

### 2.3 Reverse recovery and output capacitance

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC}(25.2)(48\,\text{k}) = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = 0.030\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and gate resistors)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 60\,\text{nC}(10)(48\,\text{k}) = 0.029\ \text{W/FET}$$

Only the 2 PWM-leg FETs toggle at f_sw, so about 0.06 W per channel.

### 2.5 Per-channel switching subtotal

| Term | Loss |
|---|---|
| V-I overlap | 1.45 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 W |
| Gate (driver) | 0.06 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 2.2 W** |
