# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #1)

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

The numbers below depend on these baseline assumptions. Change any of them and re-run the equations in §1 and §2.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |

| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | 40 V-rated FETs give about 37% margin over full charge |

**Feasibility flag.** The thermal math (§3) shows that 80 A per channel on a board this size is a burst of a few seconds, not a continuous rating. Four channels at 80 A dissipate about 100 W into roughly 4 J/K of thermal mass. Validate the "80A burst" label against §3.3 before it goes on a spec sheet.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

With α ≈ 0.0059 /K (typical for trench FETs; gives about 1.5× at 110 °C):

$$R_{ds}(110^\circ C) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss in 6-step BLDC

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, current at any instant flows through exactly two FETs in series:

- During the on-time: one high-side (HS) FET plus one low-side (LS) FET.
- During the off-time: the synchronous freewheel FET plus the same LS FET.

The total therefore does not depend on duty cycle (dead time is ignored here and handled in §2):

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch}(80\,A) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each phase leg conducts for 120° electrical of every 360°. The duty split between HS and LS within that window depends on D.

- **Instantaneous worst case:** about 11.5 W in each of the two conducting FETs.
- **Commutation-averaged:** about 23.0 / 6 ≈ 3.8 W per FET, if heat spreads well across the 6 FETs.

The thermal time constant of a DFN FET (tens of ms) is longer than the commutation period (≈0.2–2 ms at typical eRPM). So use the averaged value for T_j, plus a ripple term (§3.2).

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often missed)

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase path (pad + trace, 2 oz, about 0.3 mΩ round trip) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Shunt (if low-side, 0.5 mΩ) | 0.5 mΩ (duty-weighted) | ≈1.5–3.2 W |

Planning total: P_cu,ch ≈ 3 W.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the HS FET hard-switches. The LS FET is soft-switched (ZVS through its body diode during dead time).

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r + t_f = 40 ns (gate drive of about 1–1.5 A):

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 2(100\text{n}) \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery, output capacitance, and gate charge

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | P_rr = V_bus · Q_rr · f | 25.2 × 30 nC × 48 k = 0.036 W (Q_rr rises with di/dt and I; budget 0.1 W) |
| Output capacitance | P_oss = V_bus · Q_oss · f | 0.048 W |
| Gate charge (dissipated in driver and R_g, not in the FET) | P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f | 2 × 40 nC × 10 V × 48 k = 0.038 W |

### 2.4 Switching total per channel at 80 A

$$P_{sw,ch} \approx 1.94 + 0.61 + 0.10 + 0.05 \approx \mathbf{2.7\ W}$$

The current-dependent part scales roughly linearly: P_sw,ch ≈ 0.032·I + 0.15 W.
