# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #32): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture Model

## 0. Architecture Clarification

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each motor channel on the 4-in-1 board needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. For this model that MCU is the AT32F421 or STM32G071.

I modelled this standard split. Driving 12 half-bridges directly from the H743 would require custom firmware outside ArduPilot. If that is the actual intent, tell me and I will re-scope the work.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Trapezoidal Six-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM-sourced at duty D and one phase is low-side sunk at 100%. Each leg spends 1/3 of the period sourcing, 1/3 sinking, and 1/3 floating.

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

R(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.425 = **1.71 mΩ**

**Per-FET conduction loss:**

- High side: P_HS = I² R · D / 3
- Low side: P_LS = I² R · (1 − D)/3 + I² R / 3 = I² R · (2 − D) / 3

**Per-channel totals:**

- FET conduction: Σ = 3 × (2/3) I² R = 2 I² R (two FETs are always in the current path)
- Copper: P_cu = I² · R_cu

**Results at 80 A, I² = 6400 A², with R = 1.71 mΩ:**

| Quantity | D = 1.0 | D = 0.9 |
|---|---|---|
| P_HS per FET | 3.65 W | 3.28 W |
| P_LS per FET | 3.65 W | 4.01 W |
| FET conduction per channel | 21.9 W | 21.9 W |
| Copper loss | 6.4 W | 6.4 W |

The low-side FET is the hottest device at partial throttle.

**Self-consistency:** P depends on R(T_j), and T_j depends on P. Iterate until T_j converges:

T_j,n+1 = T_board + P(T_j,n) · R_θJ-board

Use R_θJ-board ≈ 2.7 K/W (R_θJC plus solder and via array). For the low-side FET:

- 4.0 W × 2.7 K/W → ΔT_j-board ≈ 11 K
- The fixed point converges in 2–3 iterations.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches, so there is one switching event pair per period per channel.

| Loss term | Equation | Value at 80 A / 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48k = **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V · Q_rr · f_sw | **0.036 W** |
