# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture & Mathematical Model (Job #30)

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three points shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, because ArduPilot does not contain sensorless 6-step or FOC commutation for four motors. The 48 kHz PWM is therefore an ESC-MCU parameter, and the H743 only sources bidirectional DShot600.
2. **"Nano" and "80A" conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot survive 80A. This design targets a **30.5×30.5 mm two-board stack** (FC above, ESC below), with 80A as a **time-limited burst** (seconds), not a continuous rating. Section 3 derives the limit.
3. **Component values marked [P] are placeholders.** They are representative of a 40V, ~1 mΩ, 5×6/3×3 MOSFET class and must be replaced with your selected part's datasheet figures before sign-off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Paths in 6-Step Trapezoidal Commutation with Synchronous Rectification

At any instant, two phases carry I_ph. One leg is statically on; the other is PWM'd, alternating between its high-side FET and (synchronously) its low-side FET. Current therefore always flows through exactly **two channel resistances in series**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$
