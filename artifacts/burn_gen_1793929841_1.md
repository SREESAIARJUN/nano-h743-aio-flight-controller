> **Design-review flag:** Six 5×6 mm FETs × 4 channels = 24 packages (≈720 mm² of footprint) on a 36×36 mm board. That only fits double-sided, with no room for bulk capacitance near each half-bridge. ## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

R_DS(on) has a positive temperature coefficient (≈ +0.4 %/K for modern trench parts):

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.004\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS,hot} = 0.70 \times (1 + 0.004 \times 85) = 0.70 \times 1.34 \approx 0.94\ \text{m}\Omega \;\rightarrow\; \text{use } 1.0\ \text{m}\Omega$$
