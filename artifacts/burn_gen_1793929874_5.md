## 0. Architectural Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Two constraints shape the design before any detailed calculation.

### 0.1 The H743 cannot directly commutate four motors

Driving 12 half-bridges requires complementary, dead-time-inserted PWM. On the H743 only **TIM1 and TIM8** (plus HRTIM) provide this, which is not enough for four 3-phase bridges with independent BEMF/zero-cross timing.

**Decision:** Use the standard AIO topology:

- **H743** runs ArduPilot and generates **bi-directional DShot600**.
- **Four local ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32) perform commutation.

### 0.2 "80 A burst" on a nano form factor is thermally marginal

As derived in §2, 80 A **per channel, all four channels simultaneously**, dissipates about **100 W** in a board with roughly 6 J/K of heat capacity. That is survivable for about **3–4 s**, not continuously.

In addition, the battery would supply about 320 A. That exceeds any XT60/XT90 connector and any realistic 6S pack C-rating.

The model below covers both interpretations:

- **Case A:** 80 A per channel (worst case).
- **Case B:** 80 A total (20 A per channel).

## 2. Electrothermal Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM

At any instant, two phases conduct: the PWM leg and the return leg.

- **During the D interval:** current flows through HS(PWM leg), the motor, then LS(return leg).
- **During the (1−D) interval:** current flows through LS(PWM leg, synchronous rectification), the motor, then LS(return leg).

So **exactly two FETs carry I_m in series at all times**, independent of D. The battery current is:

> I_bat = D · I_m

### 2.2 Conduction loss, with self-consistent temperature dependence

> R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α_R (T_j − 25)]
>
> P_cond = 2 · I_m² · R_ds(T_j)

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.0 × 1.6 = 1.6 mΩ, giving:

> P_cond = 2 · 6400 · 0.0016 = **20.5 W per channel**
