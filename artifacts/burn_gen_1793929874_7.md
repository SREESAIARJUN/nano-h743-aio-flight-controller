## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs. The partitioning is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot. Emits 4× bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC controllers (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 [A] running AM32, or EFM8BB21 running Bluejay | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver [A] | Bootstrap high side, ~1.0 A source / 1.5 A sink |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN3.3×3.3 [A] | 24 FETs total |

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step current path

In each 60° commutation sector, current enters through leg A and returns through leg B. Leg A runs complementary PWM at duty D: the high side conducts for D and the synchronous low side for (1−D). Leg B's low side is held fully on.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = \underbrace{I^2R_{j}\,[D + (1-D)]}_{\text{leg A}} + \underbrace{I^2R_{j}}_{\text{leg B}} = 2I^2R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of duty cycle. This is the key consequence of synchronous rectification.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At an initial guess of T_j = 100 °C:

$$
R_j = 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega \times 1.4875 = 1.785\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss at 80 A

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.785\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.85\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}
$$

### 1.4 Parasitic paths

| Element | Model | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt, 0.25 mΩ, carries bus current I_bus ≈ D·I_ph (D → 1 at full throttle) | I²·R_sh | 1.60 W |
| PCB copper, 2 oz, ~0.15 mΩ per path × 2 | 2I²R_cu | 1.92 W |

Copper loss heats the board, not the junction. Track it separately.
