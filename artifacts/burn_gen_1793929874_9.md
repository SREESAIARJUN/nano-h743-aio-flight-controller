# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Map, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points in the brief would cause design errors if left as stated:

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In an ArduPilot system the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs do the 48 kHz bridge switching and return eRPM telemetry. The loss model applies to the ESC power stage. The DShot model applies to the H743.

2. **"80 A burst" on a true 20×20 mm nano board is not thermally achievable.** Section 3 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 75 W. That is roughly 2 kW per 6S burst on 400 mm² of copper. This document therefore assumes:
   - the ESC is on a 30.5 mm mounting pattern (about 36×36 mm board);
   - "80 A" is a burst rating of a few seconds per channel;
   - the continuous rating is derived below.

   A 20×20 "nano" board is realistic only at about 25–35 A burst.

3. **Bus voltage.** The model assumes 6S: 25.2 V maximum and 22.2 V nominal. 40 V MOSFETs give about 37% derating margin to spikes.

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Current waveforms (six-step / trapezoidal block commutation)

At any instant two phases conduct, and the phase current magnitude is I_m. Each phase conducts for two 60° sectors per half electrical cycle:
- one high-side position for 1/3 of the electrical period;
- one low-side position for 1/3 of the period.

With PWM duty D on the high side and synchronous rectification on the low side of the same leg:

```
I_rms,HS = I_m · sqrt(D/3)
I_rms,LS(sync) = I_m · sqrt((1−D)/3)          (freewheeling FET in the PWM leg)
I_rms,LS(static) = I_m · sqrt(1/3)            (the "on" low side of the return leg)
```

The battery current is I_bat ≈ D·I_m. Ripple is neglected because motor L/R ≫ 1/f_sw at 48 kHz.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

R_DS(on) depends on junction temperature:

```
R(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]
```

At any instant, regardless of D, exactly two conduction paths carry I_m: the PWM leg (HS or sync LS) and the static LS. Per channel:

```
P_cond,ch = 2 · I_m² · R(T_j)
```
