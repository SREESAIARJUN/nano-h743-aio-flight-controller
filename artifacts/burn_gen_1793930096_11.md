# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #25): System Architecture and Mathematical Model

## 0. Architectural Correction

The STM32H743 does not drive MOSFETs directly. The system has two separate compute domains:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Bi-directional DShot600 TX/RX, eRPM-based notch filtering |
| ESC (×4 channels) | AT32F421 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 / BLHeli_32 | 48 kHz PWM, commutation, BEMF sensing |

So the 48 kHz switching and the 80A thermal model belong to the ESC side, and the DMA allocation belongs to the H743 side. The two connect only through four DShot lines plus power, current sense and telemetry.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). In each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high-side FET for D·T and the low-side FET for (1−D)·T. The total conduction duty for that leg is therefore 1.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on continuously.

### 2.2 Equations

Hot on-resistance:

$$R_{ds,hot} = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

RMS correction for current ripple (r = ΔI_pp / I_dc):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right)$$

Conduction loss per channel:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds,hot}$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80A

- At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds,hot = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005·100) = **1.5 mΩ**
- Ripple is negligible: r ≈ 0.1 gives a factor of 1.0008.
- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per channel**

### 2.4 Distribution across the bridge

Commutation rotates the load across all three legs, so the loss averages over six FETs:

- Per FET: ≈ 19.2 / 6 = **3.2 W**
- Copper traces and phase pads: add about 10%, i.e. 2–3 mΩ of path resistance. At 80A that is roughly 1.5–2 W per channel.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Equations

Hard-switching overlap loss:

$$P_{sw,overlap} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

Reverse recovery loss:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}$$

Output capacitance loss:

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}$$

Dead-time body-diode loss:

$$P_{dt} = V_{sd}\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw}$$

Gate drive loss (mostly dissipated in the driver):

$$P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}$$

### 3.2 Numbers at 80A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Overlap | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Q_rr | 25.2 × 50 nC × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| Q_oss | 0.5 × 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.02
