## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

**Architecture clarification:** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU running ArduPilot. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation MCU per motor, or one shared MCU in some designs. The standard choice is 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 cannot do 48 kHz six-step commutation for four motors while also running EKF3.

The two link over 4× DShot600 bidirectional signals, plus current/voltage telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM, as AM32/BLHeli_32 do). In any 60° sector:

- one leg is PWM-switching (HS and LS alternate with duty D), and
- one leg's LS is held fully on.

The motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two conducting MOSFET channels**:

- During the on-time: HS(a) + LS(b).
- During the off-time: LS(a) + LS(b).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_J) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_J-25)]$$

At T_J = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS}(125) = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.005 \times 100) = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss per motor channel

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_J) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

Copper term: model R_Cu as about 0.3 mΩ path resistance (a 2 oz internal + 3 oz outer pour stack; verify by layout extraction).

**FET term:**

$$P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 0.003 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

**Copper term:**

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.0003 = 1.92\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Per-device distribution

Conduction loss is distributed across the 6 FETs by commutation sector. Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each FET carries the current 1/3 of the time on its own phase. The worst-case device is the LS FET at low duty.

For the hotspot calculation, use the instantaneous worst case: **~9.6 W in one device during its sector.** Over an electrical cycle this averages to about 19.2/6 × 2 ≈ 6.4 W per FET. That average holds as long as the eRPM is high enough that thermal mass smooths it (true above ~100 Hz electrical).

### 1.5 Self-consistency (thermal runaway check)

Because R_DS rises with T_J, solve the fixed point:

$$T_J = T_{case} + R_{\theta JC}\, I^2 R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_J-25)]\cdot k$$

Stability requires:

$$R_{\theta JC} \cdot I^2 R_{DS,25}\,\alpha\, k < 1$$

With k = 1/3 (average share per device):

$$1.5 \times 6400 \times 0.001 \times 0.005 \times 0.333 = 0.016 \ll 1$$

The device is stable. Runaway risk comes from the board-level resistance R_θ, not from R_θJC.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

All terms below are per motor channel (only the PWM leg switches).

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The LS body diode conducts during dead time, then the HS turns on into its recovery charge:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 80\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.097\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance (HS + LS)

$$P_{oss} = 2 \times \tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f = 1.5\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.307\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f = 2 \times 40\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Total per channel and per board

| Loss term | W per channel |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs, 125 °C) | 19.20 |
| Copper | 1.92 |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 |
| Q_rr | 0.10 |
| C_oss | 0.05 |
| Dead time | 0.31 |
| Gate (driver) | 0.04 |
| **Total** | **≈ 23.6 W** |

**Board total for 4 motors at 80 A: ≈ 94 W.**
