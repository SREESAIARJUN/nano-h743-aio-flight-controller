## 0. Architectural Assumptions

Some of the requested parameters conflict physically, so these assumptions frame everything that follows.

| # | Assumption / Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The **H743 does not commutate the motors**. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-compatible firmware. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. | The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC MCU's** PWM frequency, set in AM32, not in ArduPilot. ArduPilot only sees DShot. |
| A2 | "80A" is a **per-motor burst** (about 1–5 s), the hobby-industry convention. It is not continuous. | The thermal model must be **transient** (RC), not steady-state. |
| A3 | Pack is **6S** (25.2 V max). Electrical analysis uses V_bus = 25.2 V. | Use 40 V-rated MOSFETs (≥ 1.5× margin for ringing). |
| A4 | "Nano" here means a **30.5 × 30.5 mm** board. | **An 80A × 4 = 320A burst is not credible on a 20 × 20 mm board.** Even at 30.5 mm this is at the edge (see §3.5). |
| A5 | MOSFET parameters are **representative values for a 40 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm (DFN) class part**. They are not taken from a specific datasheet. | Substitute your selected part's datasheet values into the equations. |

## 2. Mathematical Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with **complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM** at duty D. Over one electrical cycle, each half-bridge leg spends:

- **120° "high"**: the high-side FET (HS) switches at duty D, and the low-side FET (LS) freewheels for 1−D.
- **120° "low"**: LS is fully on.
- **120° "floating"**: used for BEMF sensing; no conduction.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

Per-device conduction duty fractions:

$$
\delta_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}, \qquad \delta_{LS} = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}
$$

$$
P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R_{DS,hot}\,\frac{D}{3}, \qquad
P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R_{DS,hot}\,\frac{2-D}{3}
$$

Summed over 3 legs, exactly two devices carry I at any instant:

$$
P_{cond,total} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS,hot}
$$

**At 80A:**

$$
I^2 R = 6400 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = 12.29\ \text{W}
$$

$$
P_{cond,total} = 2 \times 12.29 = \boxed{24.6\ \text{W per motor}}
$$

**Per-device split at burst throttle, D = 0.9:**

- HS: 12.29 × 0.30 = **3.69 W**
- LS: 12.29 × 0.367 = **4.51 W**

### 2.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg is PWM-switching at any time, so the losses below apply once per channel and are shared across the 3 legs over a cycle.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard-switched HS):**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12 (25.2)(80)(40\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 1.94\ \text{W}
$$

**(b) Reverse recovery of the LS body diode:**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**

$$
P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot 2 = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead times per period):**

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}
$$
