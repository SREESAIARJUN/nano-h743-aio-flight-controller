# STM32H743 Flight Controller + Nano 4-in-1 80A ESC: System Model, Allocations, and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Several assumptions need to be stated before the math.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware for the H743. The working architecture is:
   - An **H743 flight controller** sends bidirectional DShot600 to
   - **4 ESC MCUs**, typically STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32, each driving its own 3-phase bridge.

   The models below follow this split. In Section 4 the H743 is the DShot master.

2. **80A in a "nano" format is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** Four channels at 80A on 6S is about 8 kW, which is roughly 320A of battery current. That cannot be sustained in a 20×20 or 30×30 form factor. Every number below is treated as a transient burst of 1 to 5 s.

3. **The MOSFET values are representative placeholders.** No part number was specified. I use a 40V DFN 3.3×3.3 class device with the parameters below. Substitute datasheet values; all equations are parametric.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S full) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.005 /K |
| Rise / fall time (at driver R_g) | t_r, t_f | 15 ns / 15 ns |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_SD | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Junction-to-PCB thermal resistance (with via array) | R_θJ-PCB | 3 K/W |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_DS = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, one high-side switch PWMs at duty D, its complementary low-side switch conducts for (1−D), and one opposite-phase low-side switch is fully on. Per phase leg, averaged over a full electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS}=\frac{I^2R_{DS}D}{3},\qquad P_{LS}=\frac{I^2R_{DS}(1-D)}{3}+\frac{I^2R_{DS}}{3}$$

$$P_{leg}=\frac{2}{3}I^2R_{DS}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \boxed{P_{cond,ch}=2\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

This is independent of D: at every instant, exactly two channel-resistances carry the full phase current.

**Numbers at 80A:**
- 25 °C: P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.0 mΩ = **12.8 W per channel**
- 125 °C: P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per channel**

**Per-FET burden:**
- Electrical-cycle average: P_cond,ch / 6 ≈ **3.2 W**
- Instantaneous while conducting: I²R = **9.6 W**

At typical electrical frequencies of 1 to 3 kHz, the electrical period is much shorter than the die thermal time constant (about 1 to 5 ms). The cycle average therefore governs, plus a small ripple term.

### 1.3 FOC / sinusoidal variant

With sinusoidal current of peak Î, the three phases share losses:

$$P_{cond,ch}=3\cdot\left(\tfrac{\hat I}{\sqrt2}\right)^2 R_{DS}=1.5\,\hat I^2R_{DS}$$

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often forgotten)

$$P_{Cu}=I^2\,\frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,L}{w\,t\,N_{layers}}$$

$$\rho_{Cu}(T) = 1.72\times10^{-8}\,[1+0.0039\,(T-20)]$$

- **Phase pours:** a 15 mm path, 4 mm wide, 3 layers of 2 oz (70 µm) gives about 0.31 mΩ, which is 2.0 W per phase path at 80A.
- **Shunt:** a 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates I²R_sh = **1.6 W** at 80A. Consider 0.15 mΩ or MOSFET R_DS sensing.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd high-side switch hard-switches. The synchronous low side commutates under near-zero voltage, so its loss is dominated by body-diode conduction and reverse recovery. Per channel, one leg is active at any time:

$$P_{sw,ch}=\underbrace{\tfrac12 V_{bus}I(t_r+t_f)f_{sw}}_{\text{V–I overlap}}+\underbrace{Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{\text{recovery}}+\underbrace{Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{\text{C}_{oss}\text{ (charge-based)}}+\underbrace{2V_{SD}I\,t_{dt}f_{sw}}_{\text{dead-time diode}}+\underbrace{2Q_gV_{drv}f_{sw}}_{\text{gate (driver side)}}$$

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k | **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 × 50 nC × 10 V × 48 k | 0.05 W |
| **Total per channel** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |
