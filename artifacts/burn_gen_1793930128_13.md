## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #2 BOM, stack-up, or enclosure data. Everything below is therefore **parametric**, with example values from representative parts. Replace the values in **Table 0** with your datasheet numbers. All the equations still hold.

### Architecture correction

ArduPilot's bidirectional DShot is a **flight-controller-to-ESC-MCU protocol**. The STM32H743 does not drive the gates. It generates DShot600 frames and captures eRPM replies. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU, which does commutation at 48 kHz.

```
[ STM32H743 FC ] --DShot600 (bidir)--> [ ESC MCU ×4: AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32 ]
                                              └─ 3-phase gate driver ─ 6× N-MOSFET ─ motor
```

### Rating interpretation

"80 A burst" is taken as **per channel, short-duration**. Four channels at 80 A would draw 320 A from the pack. A nano board, an XT60, and the battery cannot support that simultaneously. Section 3 quantifies how long a burst is actually survivable.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal commutation

The ESC uses complementary PWM (synchronous rectification). At any instant exactly **two MOSFETs carry the phase current**:

- The PWM'd leg's high-side (during duty D) or low-side (during 1−D).
- The opposite leg's low-side (fully on).

Over one electrical period, each leg spends 120° as "high", 120° as "low", and 120° floating.

Energy per leg, normalised to I²R:

$$\tfrac13\left[D + (1-D)\right] + \tfrac13(1) = \tfrac23$$

Summed over three legs:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{path}(T_j)}$$

This result is independent of D.

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected path resistance

$$R_{path}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right] + R_{cu}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{path} = 0.75(1.65) + 0.15 = 1.39\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The PWM current ripple ΔI adds RMS content:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

With a typical motor inductance of about 10 µH:

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{V(1-D)D}{L f} \approx 13\ \text{A}$$

This adds about 0.2 %, which is negligible.
