## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**, running ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own small MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), which runs 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. The H743 talks to those ESC MCUs over on-board bi-directional DShot600 traces.

Replace every parameter marked **[DS]** with the values from the datasheet of the parts you actually select. The numbers below are representative of a 40 V, PDFN3.3×3.3 class MOSFET.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S full charge | 25.2 V |
| I_ph | Burst phase current per channel | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds25 | R_DS(on) max @ V_GS = 10 V, 25 °C **[DS]** | 2.0 mΩ |
| α | R_DS(on) tempco (≈1.5× at 125 °C) | 0.005 K⁻¹ |
| Q_gs2, Q_gd | Post-threshold gate charge, Miller charge **[DS]** | 4 nC, 8 nC |
| Q_g | Total gate charge **[DS]** | 30 nC |
