## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

### What the STM32H743 does and does not do

The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. It does **not** drive MOSFETs. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and does back-EMF commutation. The thermal and switching model below applies to each ESC channel. The DMA model applies to the H743.

### Form-factor warning

A 20×20 mm "nano" board cannot sustain 4 × 80 A. Section 3 shows this. The thermal model below assumes:
- a 30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting pattern on a 36 × 36 mm PCB,
- 8 layers of 2 oz copper.

On a 20×20 board, the allowable burst time drops by roughly 2.5–3×, scaling with copper area and thermal mass.
