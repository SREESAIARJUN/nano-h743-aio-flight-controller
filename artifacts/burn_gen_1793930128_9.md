## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds ≈ 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM

At any instant, two phases are active. Current flows through:

- one high-side FET for fraction D, then
- the opposing low-side FET (sync rectification) for fraction 1−D, plus
- the steady low-side FET of the return phase.

The current path therefore always contains **two FET channels in series**. Per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

Each FET is active for a 120° window (one third of the electrical period). The average per FET, with six FETs per channel, is:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{23.0}{6} = \mathbf{3.84\ W\ (average)}$$
