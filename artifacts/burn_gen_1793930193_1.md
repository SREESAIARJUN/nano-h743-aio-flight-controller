# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #63): Design Model

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

Three issues shape the rest of this document.

**1. The H743 cannot commutate the motors directly.** ArduPilot's H743 firmware does not run sensorless BLDC commutation. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, normally an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 is the flight controller and speaks bi-directional DShot600 to those four ESC MCUs. The netlist below reflects that split.

**2. "Nano" and "80 A burst × 4" are physically incompatible.** Section 3 shows roughly 90 W of loss in the power stage at 80 A per motor.
- A 20×20 mm board cannot absorb that for more than a fraction of a second.
- A 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board with 5×6 mm PDFN MOSFETs is the minimum credible form factor.
- Even there, 80 A is a short burst rating (single digits of seconds), not a continuous one.

**3. The netlist is a reference design, not a fab-ready output.** I list nets by signal and port name. Package pin numbers, footprints, and BOM part numbers must be verified against current datasheets and your ECAD library before layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 6-step bridge with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, phase A (for example) is PWM'd high and phase B is held low. The phase current I_ph flows through:
- High-side A for a fraction D of the period, then low-side A (sync rect) for 1 − D.
- Low-side B for 100 % of the period.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

With complementary PWM the loss is independent of duty cycle, which is a useful design property.

Per FET, averaged over one electrical revolution (6 FETs share the load):

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}}{6} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}}{3}$$
