## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = \left(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0})\right) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_m,0 is found at calibration by applying a d-axis voltage and reading the encoder.

Latency compensation: the PWM update is applied about 1.5 T_s after sampling. Advance the angle used in the inverse Park transform:

$$\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,p\,\omega_m$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Electromagnetic torque (surface PMSM, i_d* = 0):

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m\,i_q = K_t\,i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

The plant per axis is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls + R}$. Choose a PI whose zero cancels the electrical pole:

$$C_i(s) = K_{p,i} + \frac{K_{i,i}}{s},\qquad K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The closed loop then becomes a first-order system with bandwidth ω_ci. Use ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10, which gives ≈ 2π·2 kHz at 20 kHz PWM.

Feedforward decoupling:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L\,i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L\,i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.6 Voltage limiting

The limit is a circle, with the q-axis given priority:

$$|v_d| \le V_{max},\qquad |v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2},\qquad V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95$$
