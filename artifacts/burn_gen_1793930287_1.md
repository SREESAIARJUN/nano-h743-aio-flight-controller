# Job #47: STM32H743 Nano FC with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst, 48 kHz, ArduPilot BDShot600)

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

The H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs running AM32 (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six N-FETs at 48 kHz complementary PWM.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (trapezoidal commutation, complementary PWM)

In each 60° commutation sector, two legs conduct:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D, the low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) for 1 − D, minus dead time. One FET in this leg always carries I.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET is held on continuously and carries I.

So at any instant, two FET channels plus the shunt and copper carry the full phase current. The conduction loss is therefore almost independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I^2\left[2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{sh} + R_{pcb}\right]\cdot(1 - \delta_{dt})$$

where δ_dt = 2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.0144 is the fraction of time the body diode conducts instead.

The FET resistance at junction temperature is:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\left[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.2 Numbers at T_j = 100 °C

- R_ds(100 °C) = 0.70 × 1.3375 = **0.936 mΩ**
- I² = 6400 A²

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FETs (2 conducting) | 6400 × 2 × 0.936 mΩ × 0.986 | **11.81 W** |
| Shunt | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | **1.60 W** |
| PCB copper | 6400 × 0.30 mΩ | **1.92 W** |
| **P_cond per ESC** | | **≈ 15.3 W** |

Per-FET split: about 5.9 W per conducting FET, rotating through all six FETs as commutation proceeds. The time-average per FET is lower (≈ 2 W), but **use the instantaneous value for the junction check at low eRPM**, such as a stalled rotor or punch-out from hover, where commutation is slow relative to the FET thermal time constant.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Voltage-current overlap (PWM leg high side; hard-switched)

The gate currents during the Miller plateau are:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{5.5}{5} = 1.1\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.9\ \text{A}$$

The resulting transition times are:

$$t_r = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 13.6\ \text{ns}, \quad t_f = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 16.7\ \text{ns}$$

The overlap loss is:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30.3\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.47\ W}$$

### 2.2 Remaining switching terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery (low-side body diode → HS turn-on) | Q_rr·V_bus·f_sw | 0.073 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss
