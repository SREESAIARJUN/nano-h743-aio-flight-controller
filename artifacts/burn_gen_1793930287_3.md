## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, the FC's H743 sends DShot600 over four signal lines. Each motor channel on the ESC board has its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32, and that MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743's real-time obligations are therefore limited to:

- DShot TX and eRPM RX timing
- DMA arbitration
- Harmonic-notch and RPM-telemetry ingestion

The power-stage math below applies to the ESC board. The DMA math applies to the FC.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path per Commutation Sector

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two phases are energised in each 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low side (LS) of the same leg conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the LS conducts for 100% of the sector.

The instantaneous conduction loss per motor channel is:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\left[R_{HS}D + R_{LS}(1-D) + R_{LS}\right] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{hot}\quad(\text{for } R_{HS}=R_{LS})
$$
