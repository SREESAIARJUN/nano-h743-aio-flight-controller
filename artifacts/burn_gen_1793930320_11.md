## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, K_t ≈ 0.07 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class), one per joint |
| Current sense | 2 or 3 low-side shunts (a voltage-mode fallback is noted in §1.6) |
| MCU | Cortex-M
