## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset).

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:** v_α = v_d cos θ_e − v_q sin θ_e, and v_β = v_d sin θ_e + v_q cos θ_e.

**Angle delay compensation.** The PWM applies the voltage roughly 1.5 samples late. Rotate the inverse-Park angle forward to correct for this:

$$\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design (pole-zero cancellation)

The PMSM in the dq frame is:

$$L\frac{di_d}{dt} = v_d - R i_d + \omega_e L i_q,\qquad L\frac{di_q}{dt} = v_q - R i_q - \omega_e L i_d - \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Use a PI controller with gains:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{c},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \quad(\text{continuous }K_i,\ \text{units V/(A·s)})$$
