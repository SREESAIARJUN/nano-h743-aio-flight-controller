# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #10): Engineering Model

## 0. Feasibility Findings

The thermal and current numbers below rule out the literal spec of "nano (20×20) form factor, 4× 80A burst, H743-driven."

| Issue | Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Who drives the FETs** | The H743 running ArduPilot does not commutate motors. Commutation needs a dedicated real-time MCU per motor. | Use 4× ESC MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32). The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to each. The 48 kHz PWM is an AM32 setting, not an H743 function. |
| **80A × 4 on 20×20 mm** | About 64W dissipated into roughly 2 J/K of board. Adiabatic rise is about 32 K/s (§3). | Use a 30.5×30.5 mm board. Alternatively rate it "80A for ≤1s per channel" and specify ~45A continuous. |
| **Input connector** | 4×80A gives 320A peak. XT60 is about 60A continuous. | Use XT90 or direct solder pads with 10 AWG. |
| **Current shunt** | A 0.25 mΩ bus shunt at 320A dissipates 25.6W. | Use per-ESC 0.5 mΩ shunts, telemetry-reported current, or a Hall sensor (e.g. ACS-series / TMCS1100-class). |

The rest of this document models the electrically valid architecture: an H743 FC plus 4 independent ESC channels on one PCB.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor, 6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In any commutation step:
- One phase is PWM-switched: HS on for D, LS on for (1−D).
- One phase has its LS held on continuously.

$$
P_{cond} = \underbrace{D\,I^2R}_{HS} + \underbrace{(1-D)\,I^2R}_{LS\ sync} + \underbrace{I^2R}_{LS\ static} = 2\,I^2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of duty, ignoring dead time.

$$
R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx 0.70\times1.5 = 1.05\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond}=2(80)^2(1.05\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{13.44\ W}
$$

Add PCB copper. For 2 oz (70 µm) copper, ρ = 1.72×10⁻⁸ Ω·m. A 15 mm path at 5 mm width on 2 layers in parallel gives:

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w t n} = \frac{1.72\text{e-}8 \cdot 0.015}{0.005\cdot 70\text{e-}6\cdot 2}=0.37\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

With two current paths per motor: P_Cu = 2·80²·0.37 mΩ ≈ **4.7 W**. Copper loss is not negligible at this current. Use 4+ internal 2 oz power planes.

### 2.2 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched HS Only)

$$
P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{e-}9)(48\text{e}3)=\mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Transition-Time Verification

$$
I_g=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g+R_{g,int}}=\frac{10-3.5}{10+1}=0.59\ \text{A},\qquad t_{Miller}=\frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g}=17\ \text{ns}
$$
