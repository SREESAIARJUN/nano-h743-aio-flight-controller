## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU running ArduPilot. The ESC section uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, one per motor (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware). The H743 does not commute motors directly. It cannot run four sensorless FOC/trapezoidal loops plus ArduPilot with adequate determinism, and doing so would forfeit standard ESC firmware.

| Parameter | Value used in the model |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (max), 22.2 V nominal |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, center-aligned, complementary (synchronous rectification) |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor, phase RMS ≈ DC-link-referred trapezoidal current |
| MOSFET (representative 40 V class, PDFN 5×6) | R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C (V_GS = 10 V); α_R = 1.5× at T_j = 125 °C; Q_g = 60 nC; Q_rr = 50 nC; Q_oss = 40 nC; V_SD = 0.8 V; t_r = t_f = 10 ns with ~2 Ω gate resistor |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver, V_DRV = 10 V (one per motor) |
| Dead time | t_dt = 100 ns per edge |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag #1:** "Nano" with **4 × 80 A burst** is physically contradictory on a 20×20 mm board. Section 3 shows the adiabatic heating rate exceeds 40 K/s. The model below is parameterized so you can re-run it for 30.5×30.5 mm. My recommendation is either to **re-rate to 4 × 45–55 A burst on 20×20** or to **move to a 30.5×30.5 form factor** for 80 A bursts limited to ≤ 2–3 s.
>
> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag #2:** All component parameters are representative. Substitute datasheet values for your selected MOSFET before freezing the design, especially R_DS(on)(T), Z_th(jc)(t), and Q_rr.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step (trapezoidal) bridge state

During any 60° commutation sector with complementary PWM:

- **Phase A:** high-side Q_AH switches at duty D; low-side Q_AL conducts synchronously for (1−D).
- **Phase B:** low-side Q_BL is statically ON.
- **Phase C:** floating (BEMF sensing).

The phase current flows through **exactly two channels at all times**, ignoring dead time:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \Big[ D\,R_{AH} + (1-D)\,R_{AL} + R_{BL} \Big] \approx 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\Big[1 + k\,(T_j - 25)\Big], \quad k \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}\ (\Rightarrow 1.5\times @ 125\,°C)
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per motor | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2·6400·0.001 = **12.8 W** | 51.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0015 = **19.2 W** | 76.8 W |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt conduction (not negligible at 80 A)

| Element | Resistance | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + 2 oz pour (est.) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W per motor |
| Current shunt (if per-ESC, 0.5 mΩ) | 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W shared; size as ≥ 3 W 2512 or use 0.25 mΩ |
| Battery pad → bus (shared) | 0.2 mΩ | at 320 A total: **20.5 W** ← dominant hidden loss |

> **Design rule:** At 4 × 80 A the DC bus carries 320 A. Bus copper must be **≥ 4 layers × 2 oz paralleled**, with stitched vias (≥ 40 × 0.3 mm vias per transition). Otherwise bus I²R exceeds a single motor's FET loss.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing half-bridge switches. The static low-side does not.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (high-side)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}
= 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery (low-side diode during dead time)

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 50\text{nC} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}
$$

The recovery current also adds to high-side turn-on overlap. Include it as an additional ~10–20 % on P_sw if the datasheet I_rrm is large.
