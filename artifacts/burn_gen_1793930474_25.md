# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (20×20 "Nano"): Design Package for Job #43

**Scope:** 80 A burst loss model, 48 kHz switching losses, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, schematic netlist excerpts, and thermal analysis.

---

## 0. Architectural Findings

Three findings shape everything below.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC controller.** Each motor channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, typically an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 sends DShot600 to those four MCUs and decodes eRPM telemetry. The netlist reflects this two-tier split.

2. **80 A per motor on a 20×20 board is a burst rating, not a continuous rating.** The model in §1–§4 predicts about 85 W of board dissipation at 4×80 A. The board can reject only a few watts continuously. The realistic rating is roughly **80 A for 2–3 s, about 25–30 A continuous with prop-wash cooling**. Marketing should not label this a continuous "80A ESC."

3. **Component values are representative, not selected parts.** I used a generic 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm MOSFET. Every number flows from the parameter table in §1.1. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values before design freeze.

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 100) = **1.50 mΩ**.

This is an electro-thermal feedback loop, solved iteratively in §4.4.

### 1.3 Commutation Topology

With six-step (trapezoidal) drive and synchronous rectification, one high-side path and one low-side path carry I_ph at every instant (ignoring dead time).

- In the PWM'd phase, HS and LS share the current with duty D and 1−D. The sum is one full-time conductor.
- The opposite phase's LS conducts continuously.

Conduction loss per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)\,\underbrace{[D + (1-D)]}_{=1} + I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

At 125 °C:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

The hottest single MOSFET is the continuously-on LS. It dissipates 9.6 W during its 120° conduction interval and averages about 3.2 W over a full electrical cycle. **Use 9.6 W for the transient junction calculation.** The electrical period is ≪ τ_board but comparable to τ_jc.
