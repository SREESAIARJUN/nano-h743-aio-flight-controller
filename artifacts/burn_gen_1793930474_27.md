# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage & DShot Architecture

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

**Topology.** The STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs directly. In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM and all switching losses therefore belong to the ESC MCUs. ArduPilot only owns the DShot timing and DMA. The model below keeps those two domains separate.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At a design junction temperature of T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}(110)=1.2\,\text{mΩ}\times(1+0.0045\times85)=1.66\ \text{mΩ}$$
