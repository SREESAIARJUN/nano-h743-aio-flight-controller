# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #38): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 sends DShot600 to those MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back. This document models both domains.

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A burst per channel on a true "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not thermally credible. The numbers below show why. They assume a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner** stack, with forced convection from prop wash. If the mechanical envelope really is 20×20 mm, de-rate to about 35–40 A burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM

This is the AM32 / BLHeli_32 default. In each 60° sector, two legs carry the phase current I:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D·T and the complementary low-side for (1−D)·T. The leg as a whole conducts current 100% of the time, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side (or high-side) conducts 100% of the sector.

The leg-level conduction loss is therefore almost independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha_R (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.2 Field-oriented / sinusoidal drive (for comparison)

$$P_{cond,FOC} = 3\,\left(\frac{I_{pk}}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2 R_{ds(on)} = 1.5\,I_{pk}^2 R_{ds(on)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, with an assumed T_j of 110 °C (iterated in §3.4)

$$R_{ds(on)}(110) = 1.0\,(1 + 0.0045 \cdot 85) = 1.38\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.38\text{e-}3 = 17.7\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{Cu} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 0.3\text{e-}3 = 3.84\ \text{W}$$

**Per-channel conduction loss ≈ 21.5 W.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The return leg is static within the sector.

**(a) V–I overlap, hard turn-on and turn-off**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{e-}9 \cdot 48\text{e}3 = 1.94\ \text{W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode at high-side turn-on**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{e-}9 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{e}3 = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr rises with di/dt and T_j. Budget 2–3× this value: **≈ 0.12 W**.

**(c) Output capacitance**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot 2 = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period)**

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{e-}9 \cdot 48\text{e}3 = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET die)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} \cdot 2 = 50\text{e-}9 \cdot 10 \cdot 48\text{e}3 \cdot 2 = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Switching subtotal ≈ 2.75 W per channel.**

**Frequency sensitivity.** Every term except P_g scales linearly with f_sw.

- At 24 kHz: ≈ 1.4 W.
- At 96 kHz: ≈ 5.5 W.

At 80 A, 48 kHz is a reasonable compromise between ripple and loss.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total dissipation during burst

| Item | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 17.7 W | 70.8 W |
| Copper | 3.8 W | 15.4 W |
| Switching + dead time | 2.75 W | 11.0 W |
| Bulk cap ESR, drivers, LDO (estimate) | 0.5 W | 2.0 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 24.8 W** | **≈ 99 W** |

**Efficiency check.** Output power is roughly 4 × 80 A × 25 V × ~0.9 effective duty ≈ 7.2 kW. The power stage is therefore about 98.6% efficient. That is fine electrically; the binding constraint is thermal.

### 3.2 Lumped two-node RC network

```
P_FET ──► [Tj] ──R_jb──► [T_board] ──R_ba──► T_amb
           │                │
          C_j             C_board
```

**Thermal capacitance of the board.** Board area is 36 × 36 mm. Copper mass is about 2.6 g (8 layers, ~70% fill average). FR4 mass is about 2.8 g. Components add about 2 g.

$$C_{board} \approx 2.6(0.385) + 2.8(0.9) + 2(0.7) \approx 4.9\ \text{J/K}$$
