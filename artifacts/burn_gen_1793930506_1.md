## 0. Architecture Corrections Before Any Math

Three assumptions in the brief would cause a failed design if taken literally.

| Issue | Problem | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **H743 cannot commutate the ESCs** | Bi-directional DShot is a link between the flight controller and *separate ESC MCUs*. Six-step sensorless commutation at 48 kHz on four motors needs 4 independent BEMF and timer subsystems. The H743 runs ArduPilot, not commutation. | Use a 2-board stack. The FC uses an STM32H743. The ESC uses 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class firmware). |
| **"Nano" (20×20) vs 80 A** | Twelve power FETs able to handle 80 A each need about 5×6 mm PQFN packages. They do not fit in a 20×20 footprint with usable copper. | ESC uses a 30.5×30.5 mount (about 36×40 mm outline). The FC can stay 20×20 if desired. The rest of this document assumes 30.5. |
| **"80 A" is a burst rating** | Section 3 shows the stack cannot sustain 80 A on all four channels. It is roughly a 5–8 s burst. | Specify 80 A / 5 s burst and about 30 A continuous at a 45 °C ambient with prop wash. |

The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC's motor PWM frequency**, which is an AM32 setting. It is unrelated to the DShot600 bit rate.
