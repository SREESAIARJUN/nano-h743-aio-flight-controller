**Architectural note.** The H743 does not drive gates. Each ESC channel, or all four, is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz switching is executed by the ESC MCU.

**Feasibility warning.** Four channels at 80 A on 6S is roughly 8 kW of bus power on a ~6.5 cm² board. Section 3 shows this is a 1–2 second all-motor capability at best, and about 7 s for a single motor. Continuous rating is about 20 A per channel. Market and spec this honestly as "80 A, ≤1 s burst, single-axis transient."
