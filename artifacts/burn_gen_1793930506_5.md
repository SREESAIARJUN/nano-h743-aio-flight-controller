# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: System Model and Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Warning

Before the math, there are three architectural facts to settle, because they determine everything downstream.

**1. Partitioning.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each motor needs a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware, for example an AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071, driving a 3-phase gate driver. The "4-in-1 ESC nano FC" is therefore an AIO board with five MCUs: one H743 and four ESC MCUs.
