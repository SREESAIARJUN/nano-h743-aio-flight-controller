# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Reference Architecture (Job #5)

**Thermal, Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 Model**

> **Scope and honesty statement**
> - **Assumed part parameters.** All MOSFET, PCB, and driver parameters below are representative assumptions, not vendor-locked values. They are flagged with ⚙. Re-run the model with your chosen parts' datasheet curves before layout freeze.
> - **80 A is a burst rating, not continuous.** On a nano form factor, the physics in §2 shows a burst window of roughly **1–2 s on all four channels**. Continuous capability lands near **30–35 A per channel**. Any "80 A continuous" claim on a 20×20 or 30×30 board is a marketing number.
> - **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** It runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AM32-class: STM32G071 or AT32F421). This is how every production AIO of this class is built.
