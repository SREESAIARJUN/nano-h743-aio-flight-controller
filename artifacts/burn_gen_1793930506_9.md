## 0. Architectural Clarification and Feasibility Summary

### 0.1 What the H743 actually does

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)**. It does not commutate motors. A "4-in-1 ESC" stage needs dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay. These MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

This design therefore uses a two-tier architecture:
