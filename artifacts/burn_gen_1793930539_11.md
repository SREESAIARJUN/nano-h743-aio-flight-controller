# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture & Analysis

**Job #57, Revision A, Architecture Baseline**

---

## 0. Scope and Critical Findings

The headline requirement, "80 A burst, nano form factor," needs to be qualified before any schematic work. This analysis concludes:

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| A true 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A. | The copper cross-section, MOSFET footprint and thermal mass are all insufficient. The baseline is therefore a 36×36 mm board (30.5 mm mounting pattern). |
| The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. | ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each motor needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 (AT32F421 class). |
| 80 A is a burst rating of about 1–3 s per channel, not a sustained rating. | The thermal model (§3) shows roughly 115 W total dissipation at 4×80 A against a board heat capacity of about 8.5 J/K. |
| Bidirectional DShot is impossible on complementary (CHxN) timer pins. | Input capture only exists on CHx pins. All four motor outputs must sit on non-N timer channels (§4). |

All component values below are **representative design values**. Final numbers must be re-run against the selected parts' datasheets (R_DS(on) max at T_j, Q_gd, Q_rr, R_θJC).
