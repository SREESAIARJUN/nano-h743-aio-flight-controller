## 0. Architecture Clarification

The H743 does not commutate the motors. In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 design the work splits like this:

| Function | Silicon | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 generation, eRPM capture | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| 6-step commutation, **48 kHz PWM**, dead-time, BEMF/eRPM telemetry | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 / BLHeli_32-class |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, DFN5×6 | n/a |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-firmware setting (for example, AM32's PWM frequency). ArduPilot only sends throttle frames and decodes eRPM replies. The loss model in sections 2–3 lives on the ESC side. The timer/DMA model in section 5 lives on the H743 side.

**Feasibility flag.** On a 20×20 mm "nano" footprint, 80 A per channel is only achievable as a short transient. Section 4 quantifies the window: about 3.3 s at 30.5×30.5 and about 1.5 s at 20×20. Treat 80 A as a *burst* rating, never continuous.
