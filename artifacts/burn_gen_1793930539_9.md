## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal control with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- **Switching leg:** HS is on for duration D, LS is on for (1 − D) minus dead time.
- **Static leg:** LS is on 100%.

The phase current therefore always flows through exactly **two channel resistances in series**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.2 Thermal runaway check (I²R positive feedback)

Write the junction temperature as a function of its own loss:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta} \cdot 2 I^2 R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

Solving for T_j gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + 2I^2R_{25}R_\theta(1-25\alpha)}{1 - 2I^2R_{25}R_\theta\,\alpha}$$

**Runaway condition:** $2 I^2 R_{25} R_\theta \alpha \ge 1$.

At 80 A: $2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 0.001 \cdot \alpha = 0.0768\,R_\theta$. Runaway therefore occurs at **R_θ ≥ 13 K/W** (effective, per channel). Any steady-state 80 A operation on a nano board is in or near this regime. 80 A is a **transient-only**
