## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Coordinate Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\left(i_a + 2 i_b\right)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**
$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 PMSM Model in the dq Frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q
$$
$$
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

Here $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$ is the electrical speed and $\lambda_m$ is the permanent-magnet flux linkage.

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

The plant after decoupling is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls + R}$. Placing the PI zero on the plant pole gives:
$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s + \omega_{ci}}
$$

Choose $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. At 20 kHz that is about $2\pi\cdot 1\,\text{kHz}$.

The discrete form uses backward Euler and is evaluated for each axis $x \in \{d, q\}$:
$$
e_x[k] = i_x^*[k] - i_x[k]
$$
$$
v_x[k] = K_{p,i}\,e_x[k] + I_x[k] + v_{x,ff}[k]
$$
$$
I_x[k+1] = I_x[k] + K_{i,i}T_s\,e_x[k] + K_{aw}\big(\text{sat}(v_x) - v_x\big)
$$

The feedforward (decoupling) terms are:
$$
v_{d,ff} = -\omega_e L\,i_q,\qquad v_{q,ff} = \omega_e\left(L\,i_d + \lambda_m\right)
$$

**Voltage limiting (circular, with d-axis priority):**
$$
|v_d| \le V_{max}
$$
$$
|v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$
$$
V_{max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt{3}}\cdot m_{max},\qquad m_{max} \approx 0.95
$$

**Computation-delay compensation.** Rotate by the angle the rotor advances in 1.5 PWM periods:
$$
\theta_e^{pwm} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$
