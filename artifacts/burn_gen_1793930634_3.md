## 0. Baseline Assumptions and Architectural Decisions

The STM32H743 cannot practically run four sensorless FOC/6-step motor loops alongside the ArduPilot flight stack. This design therefore uses a **split architecture**:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot, sensor fusion, DShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), 120 MHz | AM32 firmware, 6-step, 48 kHz PWM, bidir DShot slave |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power FETs (×24) | 40 V N-ch, PQFN 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 class | 6 per motor |

> **Design-review flag.** 80 A per channel on a nano (20 × 20 mm) board is only achievable as a **short transient**. Sections 1–3 quantify this. The sustainable continuous rating comes out at roughly **25 A per channel** under prop-wash cooling. Marketing figures should state "80 A burst (≤ 2 s, single channel) / 25 A continuous".

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During each 60° commutation sector (example: phase A high, phase B low, phase C floating):

- **Q_AH** is PWM'd at duty D.
- **Q_AL** is driven complementary, conducting for (1 − D) minus dead time.
- **Q_BL** is fully on for the whole sector.

At any instant, the phase current I_ph flows through **exactly two channels in series**: one from {Q_AH, Q_AL} plus Q_BL. Neglecting dead time:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot \left[ D\,R_{DS}(T_j) + (1-D)\,R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{DS}(T_j) \right] + I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \left( 2 R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu} \right)}
$$

The result is independent of D because the complementary FET carries the freewheel current.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]
$$

$$
R_{DS}(125\,^\circ\text{C}) = 0.80 \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 100) = 1.24\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Copper path resistance (R_Cu)

The phase-to-pad trace and the bus-to-FET pour, per channel, assume an effective path of 12 mm length × 4 mm width using 2 parallel 70 µm layers. Resistivity at 100 °C is ρ_Cu(100 °C) ≈ 2.25 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m.

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t\,n} = \frac{2.25\times10^{-8} \cdot 0.012}{0.004 \cdot 70\times10^{-6} \cdot 2} \approx 0.48\ \text{m}\Omega
$$
