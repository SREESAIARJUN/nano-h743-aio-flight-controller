# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Design Model, Job #2

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071-class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32). ArduPilot has no sensorless BLDC commutation firmware, so the H743 cannot drive the gate drivers directly.

```
H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (×4, single-wire)──► 4× ESC MCU ──► 4× 3-phase bridge (24 FETs)
```

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |
|---|---|---|

| t_r, t_f | Switching rise / fall time | 20 ns each |

### 0.3 Feasibility flag

80 A per channel on a 20×20 or 25×25 board can only be sustained for a few seconds. This is quantified in §3. Treat "80A burst" as a transient rating, not a sustainable one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, exactly **two FETs are in the current path at all times**:

- The switching leg: HS for duty D, then LS for (1−D).
- The sink leg: its LS is on continuously.

The total is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

### 1.3 Self-consistent junction temperature

Each FET dissipates I²R_ds(T_j). The junction temperature is:

$$T_j = T_c + R_{th,jc}\cdot I^2 R_{ds25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j=\frac{T_c + k(1-25\alpha)}{1-k\alpha},\quad k = R_{th,jc} I^2 R_{ds25}$$

With I = 80 A: k = 1.5 × 6400 × 0.001 = 9.6 K.

Assuming the case/board temperature T_c = 100 °C:

- T_j ≈ (100 + 9.6 × 0.8875) / (1 − 0.0432) = **113.4 °C**
- R_ds ≈ **1.40 mΩ**

### 1.4 Results

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Per FET | 6400 × 1.40 mΩ ≈ **8.96 W** |
| Per channel (2 FETs) | **17.9 W** |
| PCB copper, per channel (R_cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ: pads, vias, 2 oz pours, phase pad) | 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W** |

The copper estimate assumes 2 oz copper. For 3 oz, scale by about 0.67.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg switches at a time. Each term below is per channel.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k = **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.048 W** |
| Coss | ½ · Q_oss · V · f_sw | **0.030 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, dissipated in the driver) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw | **0.058 W** |
| **P_sw,ch** | | **≈ 2.69 W** |

### 2.1 Sensitivity

- Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 96 kHz it would be about 3.9 W.
- Dead-time loss is about 23 % of the switching loss. Cutting dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel. Verify shoot-through margin with a scope before doing this.

## 3. Thermal Budget

### 3.1 Total dissipation at full burst

$$P_{ch} = 17.9 + 3.2 + 2.69 \approx 23.8\text{ W}$$

$$P_{tot} = 4 \times 23.8 \approx \mathbf{95\ W}$$

This assumes all four channels are at 80 A. Add about 1–2 W for the H743, regulators and receiver.

### 3.2 Steady state (why 80 A cannot be continuous)

Board-to-air resistance under prop wash:

- h ≈ 50 W/m²K
- A = 2 × (0.025 m)² = 1.25×10⁻³ m²

$$R_{th,ba} = \frac{1}{hA} \approx 16\ \text{K/W}$$

With T_amb = 40 °C and T_board,max = 110 °C, the board can shed only **≈ 4.4 W** steady-state, plus whatever the power leads and frame conduct away. The realistic continuous rating is about **20–30 A per channel**.

### 3.3 Burst model (lumped capacitance)

Board thermal mass:

| Material | Mass × specific heat | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | 3 g × 0.385 J/gK | 1.16 J/K |
| FR4 | 2.5 g × 1.1 J/gK | 2.75 J/K |
| Components and solder | | ~1.0 J/K |
| **C_th** | | **≈ 4.9 J/K** |

τ = R·C ≈ 16 × 4.9 ≈ 78 s, which is much longer than the burst. The rise is therefore nearly adiabatic:

$$\frac{dT}{dt}\approx\frac{P}{C_{th}} = \frac{95}{4.9}\approx 19.4\ \text{K/s}$$
