# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Design Package, Job #10

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility

**Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. In practice an H743 does not commutate the motors directly. The ESC side uses 4 × AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32, plus a 3-phase gate driver and 6 FETs per motor. This split is the only one that ArduPilot bi-directional DShot supports.

**Assumed parameters.** Replace these with the values from your chosen parts' datasheets. All formulas are written so you can substitute directly.

| Symbol | Meaning | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Max bus voltage (6S full) | 25.2 V |
| R_ds,25 | FET on-resistance at 25 °C (40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm class) | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | Temperature coefficient of R_ds | 0.006 /K |
| Q_g | Total gate charge (at 10 V) | 40 nC |
| Q_oss | Output charge | 40 nC |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | 30 nC |
| t_r + t_f | Switching transition times (FD6288Q-class driver, R_g ≈ 4.7 Ω) | 15 + 15 ns |
| V_f | Body-diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| t_dt | Dead time | 50 ns |
| f_sw | PWM switching frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_th,jc | Junction-to-case thermal resistance | 1.5 K/W |
| Load case | 80 A phase current per motor, all four motors, burst | — |

> **Feasibility warning.** 4 × 80 A means 320 A at about 22 V, roughly 7 kW through a 30×30 mm board. This is achievable only as a burst of a few seconds. Section 3 quantifies the limit. Continuous ratings on this footprint are about 25–35 A per motor. Any "80A" rating must be labelled a burst rating with a stated duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Current path.** With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous (complementary) PWM, exactly two legs conduct at any instant:
- The PWM leg carries the phase current through its high-side FET for a fraction D of each period, and through its low-side FET for 1 − D.
- The return leg's low-side FET is on continuously.

The current therefore always flows through 2 × R_ds in series:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Phase current.** The phase current relates to battery current by I_ph ≈ I_batt / D. Including inductor ripple ΔI:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

At 80 A, ripple contributes less than 1% and is neglected below.

**Per-FET loss.** Each FET conducts for 120° electrical, which is 1/3 of the cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Results at 80 A:**

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per motor | P_cond per FET (average) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.13 W |
| 110 °C | 1.51 mΩ | **19.3 W** | **3.22 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.60 mΩ | 20.5 W | 3.41 W |

**Electro-thermal stability.** Because R_ds rises with temperature, loss and temperature feed back on each other. Iterating T_j = T_board + P_FET · R_th,jc converges when the loop gain is below 1:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{25}\, \alpha\, R_{th,jc} = 0.0192 \times 1.5 \approx 0.03 \ll 1$$

This is stable at the junction level. The real runaway path is the board level, covered in Section 3.

**Copper loss.** Phase and bus copper add loss as well. For 2 oz copper, 4 layers paralleled, with roughly 15 mm effective path length, R_cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ per motor path:
- P_cu ≈ 80² × 0.25 mΩ ≈ **1.6 W per motor**

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches. In synchronous mode the high-side FET hard-switches and the low-side FET soft-switches after dead time.

| Term | Formula | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 1.45 W |
| C_oss | Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | 0.048 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2 t_dt · f_sw | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, at the driver) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 0.04 W |
| **Total per motor** | | **≈ 1.88 W** |

**Notes on switching loss:**

- **Transition time from the gate driver.** Estimate t_r ≈ Q_gd / I_g, where I_g ≈ (V_drv − V_plateau) / (R_g + R_drv). A 30 ns total transition assumes Q_gd ≈ 8 nC and about 0.5 A plateau current. Doubling R_g roughly doubles the overlap term.
