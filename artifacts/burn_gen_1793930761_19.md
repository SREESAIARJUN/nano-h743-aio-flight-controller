## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with complementary PWM

At any commutation sector, exactly two half-bridge legs are active:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** HS conducts for D·T, LS (synchronous) for (1−D)·T.
- **Leg B (static leg):** LS (or HS) conducts 100% of the sector.

The phase current therefore always flows through **exactly two channels in series**, so per ESC channel:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.3 Ripple contribution

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\Delta I_{pp}\big|_{D=0.5} = \frac{25.2\times0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\times48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.6\text{ A}
$$

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2
$$

The ripple contribution is negligible (<0.06%). At D→1 (an 80 A burst implies near-full throttle), ripple → 0.

### 1.4 Parasitic path resistance

Copper path resistance per conduction path (pads, vias, phase pad, bus planes) is estimated at R_pcb ≈ 0.3 mΩ. This must be extracted from layout. It heats the board, not the junction.

### 1.5 Electrothermal fixed point
