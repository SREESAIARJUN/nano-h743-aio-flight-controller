## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors itself in an ArduPilot system. The validated architecture is:

```
[STM32H743 – ArduPilot] --4× bidirectional DShot600--> [4× AT32F421 – AM32] --> [4× FD6288Q gate driver] --> [4× 3-phase bridge, 24 MOSFETs]
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous bridge

In any 60° commutation sector of six-step (trapezoidal) drive, three switches carry current:

- the PWM high-side switch, for a fraction D of each cycle;
- its complementary low-side switch (synchronous rectification), for a fraction (1 − D) minus dead time;
- the commutated low-side switch of the opposite phase, for 100% of the sector.

Summing the three:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

This result is independent of duty cycle, which is a useful property for burst analysis.

Include the temperature dependence of R_ds(on):

$$
R_{hot}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At 80 A:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.25\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{28.8\ W}
$$

### 1.2 Copper and trace conduction

Assume 1.0 mΩ total for each motor current path: pads, inner-layer pours, and motor wire solder joints.

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.001 = \mathbf{6.4\ W\ per\ motor}
$$

This term is routinely forgotten, and it is about 20% of the FET conduction loss.

### 1.3 Sense shunt

Each ESC has its own shunt, R_sh = 0.25 mΩ:

$$
P_{sh} = 80^2 \times 0.25\text{ mΩ} = 1.6\ \text{W}
$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg hard-switches per sector.

**(a) V–I overlap loss**

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

**(b) Output capacitance loss**

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

**(c) Body-diode reverse recovery**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}
$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead times per cycle)

$$
P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}
$$

**(e) Gate drive** (all 6 FETs toggle at f_sw in complementary mode; this energy is dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g)

$$
P_g = 6\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 6 \times 30\text{ nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.086\ \text{W}
$$

**Switching subtotal:** approximately **2.70 W per motor**.

**Sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz, about 1.
