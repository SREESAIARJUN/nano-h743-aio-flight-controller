**Architecture.** The H743 does not drive gates. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. Each ESC MCU closes its own 48 kHz PWM loop. This split is mandatory: the H743 has no headroom to run four commutation loops alongside the EKF.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.75 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.09 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low:

- **PWM on (D):** HS_X → motor → LS_Y
- **PWM off (1−D):** LS_X (sync-rectifying) → motor → LS_Y

Exactly two FETs carry the full current at all times, independent of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\left(1+\frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12 I^2}\right)$$

The ripple term is small. With motor L ≈ 15 µH, ΔI_pp ≈ (V/2)/(L·2f) ≈ 8.7 A, so the correction is +0.1%, which is negligible.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2(80)^2(1.09\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{13.9\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution (worst-case device)

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle at full current. At full throttle (D → 0.95), the high-side FET gets the majority of the on-sector:

$$P_{HS,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\left[D\,I^2 R_{DS}\right] = \tfrac{1}{3}(0.95)(6400)(1.09\text{m}) = 2.2\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Shunt and copper

- Shunt: P = 80² × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W**
- Copper traces/planes (≈ 0.3 mΩ path): **≈ 1.9 W**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Its HS and LS are hard-switched complementary.

### 2.1 Gate currents during the Miller plateau

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{on}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{10-3}{2+2.2+1}=1.35\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{off}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{3}{1+2.2+1}=0.71\ \text{A}$$

### 2.2 Transition times

The switching charge is Q_sw ≈ Q_gs2 + Q_gd ≈ 6 + 8 = 14 nC.

$$t_{on}=\frac{14\text{n}}{1.35}=10.4\ \text{ns},\qquad t_{off}=\frac{14\text{n}}{0.71}=19.7\ \text{ns}$$
