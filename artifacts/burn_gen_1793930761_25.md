## 0. Architectural Clarifications

Three issues need resolving before the math, because they change the results.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** No production 4-in-1 runs the commutation loop on an H743. The ESC side runs AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware on a small Cortex-M0+/M4 (STM32G071, AT32F421, GD32E230). This document therefore models:
   - **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot, generating bidirectional DShot600.
   - **ESC:** 4× independent MCUs, 48 kHz complementary PWM, 3-phase bridges.
2. **"Nano" plus "80A" is thermally contradictory.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80A is a **transient (≈1–3 s) capability**, not a continuous one. Section 3 quantifies this. All 80A figures are treated as burst ratings, with continuous ratings derived separately.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Conduction topology

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with active freewheeling (complementary PWM), as AM32 does. At any instant, motor current flows through exactly two MOSFET channels:
- **On-time (D):** HS of phase A, then LS of phase B.
- **Off-time (1−D):** LS of phase A (synchronous rectification), then LS of phase B.

The current path therefore always contains two R_DS(on) elements, independent of duty cycle (dead time excepted).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110°C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.0\,(1 + 0.007 \cdot 85) = 1.595\ \text{mΩ} \approx 1.6\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.4 Distribution across the six FETs

Each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period. The HS/LS split within a phase depends on D.

**Average loss per FET:**

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{P_{cond}}{6} = 3.4\ \text{W}$$

**Instantaneous loss in the worst-case FET** during its 120° conduction window, near full duty on the HS:

$$\hat P_{FET} = I^2 R_{DS} = 6400 \cdot 1.6\times10^{-3} = 10.2\ \text{W}$$

### 1.5 Parasitic conduction (shunt + copper)

$$P_{par} = I^2 (R_{sh} + R_{cu}) = 6400 \cdot 0.7\times10^{-3} = 4.5\ \text{W}$$

### 1.6 Electrothermal runaway check

R_DS rises with T_j, which feeds back into loss:

$$P(T_j) = 2I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \qquad T_j = T_a + R_{\theta,ja}\,P$$

Solving gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{\theta}\,2I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_{\theta}\,2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha}$$

**Stability condition:** R_θ · 2I²R₂₅α < 1.

At 80A this becomes R_θ < 1/(2 · 6400 · 0.001 · 0.007) = **11.2 K/W** (effective, per channel).

A nano board in prop wash has roughly 12 K/W, which sits on the boundary. **Steady-state 80A has no stable solution.** This confirms that 80A must be treated as a transient rating.
