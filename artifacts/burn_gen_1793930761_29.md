**Architectural note (important).** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are STM32G071 or AT32F421, either four separate parts or a quad-MCU arrangement. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC-MCU side. The DMA/timer analysis applies to the H743 side.
