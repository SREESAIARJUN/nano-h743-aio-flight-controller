# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack: Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, BDShot600 DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**Architecture.** Bidirectional DShot implies two layers:

- The **STM32H743 is the flight controller.** It generates DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **Four separate ESC MCUs** commutate the bridges. A typical choice is AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32.

The H743 should not drive the 24 FETs directly. It has only two advanced complementary timers (TIM1 and TIM8) plus HRTIM, which is not enough for 4×3 half-bridges with sensorless BEMF commutation. The design below assumes this split architecture.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation model.** The bridge uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification. In every 60° sector:

- The **source phase** PWMs at duty D. Current flows through its high-side FET (HS) during D and through its low-side FET (LS) during 1−D.
- The **sink phase** keeps its LS fully on.

Therefore exactly **two FETs are always in series with the phase current**:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I^2 R_{hot}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each phase leg sources for 120° and sinks for 120° per electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{hot}, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R_{hot} + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{hot}$$

**At 80 A, D → 1:**

- I²R_hot = 6400 × 1.86 mΩ = 11.9 W
- Per FET: P_HS = P_LS ≈ **3.97 W**
- Per channel (6 FETs): P_cond ≈ **23.8 W**

**Copper path.** Allow about 0.5 mΩ of plane, via, and pad resistance per channel at 80 A: 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**.

**Electrothermal feedback.** R_hot depends on T_j, which depends on P. Iterate:

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0055/\text{K}$$

This converges in 2–3 iterations. Thermal runaway occurs only if α·I²R·Rth_ja > 1, which is not reached in burst conditions.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the source-leg FET hard-switches. The synchronous LS commutates through its body diode during dead time.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | 1.94 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 0.04 W |
| C_oss | C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.03 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_G) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 0.04 W |
| **Total switching per channel** | | **≈ 2.66 W** |

**Rise-time estimate.** t_r ≈ Q_gd / I_G. With Q_gd ≈ 8 nC and an effective ~0.4 A driver current through R_G, t_r ≈ 20 ns.

**Dead-time sensitivity.** Halving dead time to 50 ns saves 0.3 W per channel. Do not go below the driver's propagation skew plus a margin of about 30 ns.

**Frequency scaling.** Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it would be about 1.3 W.

**Linearized per-channel loss model:**

$$P_{ch}(I) \approx 2 I^2 R_{hot} + I^2 R_{cu} + k_{sw} I, \qquad k_{sw} = 2.66/80 \approx 0.033\ \text{W/A}$$

**At 80 A:** P_ch ≈ 23.8 + 3.2 + 2.7 ≈ **29.7 W per channel.**
