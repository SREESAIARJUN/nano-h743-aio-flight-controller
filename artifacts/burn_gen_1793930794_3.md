# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #25): Power-Stage Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlist and Thermal Budget

## 0. Scope Corrections and Assumptions

Four points shape everything below:

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** It runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each motor channel has its own small ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That ESC MCU does the 48 kHz PWM, the commutation and the eRPM telemetry. The H743 never touches the gate drivers.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano form factor is a transient, not a sustained capability.** The math below shows that all four channels at 80A cannot be held for more than about 2 s without exceeding safe board temperatures.
3. **All MOSFET and driver parameters are representative placeholders for a 40V PDFN3.3×3.3 class device.** They are not values from a specific datasheet. Replace them with your BOM part's values before sign-off.
4. **The DMA plan follows ArduPilot's documented approach** (timer DMA burst for output, timer channel DMA for input capture). Check exact tick constants and the BIDIR tag rules against the ArduPilot release you build. The `hwdef.h` DMA resolver output is the final authority.
