# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #21): Architecture, Loss Model, BDShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three points need to be settled before any math, because they change the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC with 48 kHz PWM runs its own MCU per channel, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. "48 kHz switching" is an ESC firmware parameter. "DShot600 DMA" is an H743 hwdef problem. These are separate subsystems, and the netlist below reflects that split.
2. **"80 A burst" must be defined.** If it means 80 A per channel on all four channels at once on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board, the thermal math in §3 shows it is only survivable for about 2–3 s. That is a valid burst spec, but the duration must be stated on the datasheet.
3. **No MOSFET was specified.** I use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN part. Every result is parametric, so substitute your real datasheet values into the equations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, complementary PWM)

AM32 and BLHeli_32 use trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification. In any 60° sector:
- The **PWM leg** alternates between the high-side FET (fraction D) and the low-side FET (fraction 1−D).
- The **return leg** low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

Current therefore always flows through exactly two channels in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$
