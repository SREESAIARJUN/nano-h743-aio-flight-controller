# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. Typical ESC MCUs are AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32 on supported parts. The 48 kHz PWM is set in the ESC firmware, not in ArduPilot. The models below are split along this boundary.

**Operating point.** The design is evaluated at 6S, with V_bus up to 25.2 V, and an 80 A burst per motor channel. The MOSFET and driver parameters are representative values for a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN-class device. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values; every equation below is parametric.

**Feasibility.** On a 20×20 mm "nano" board, 80 A is only a short burst of seconds, not a rating. The thermal section quantifies this. Expect a continuous rating closer to 20–35 A per channel, depending on airflow.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (worst case for switching losses) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current (DC-equivalent per 60° step) |
