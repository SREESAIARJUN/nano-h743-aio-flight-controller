## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back on the same wire. Everything below follows that split.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation interval two legs are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts I for duty D. The synchronous low-side (LS) FET conducts I for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the LS FET conducts I continuously.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

This result is **independent of duty**, which is a key property of synchronous rectification.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At a design point of T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{ds} = 0.75 \times 1.425 = 1.07\ \text{mΩ}
$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The inductor ripple Δi is triangular, so I_rms² = I² + Δi²/12.

- Phase-to-phase inductance of a typical 2207 motor: L ≈ 20 µH.
- Worst-case ripple: Δi ≈ V/(4·L·f) = 25.2/(4 × 20µ × 48k) ≈ 6.6 A.
