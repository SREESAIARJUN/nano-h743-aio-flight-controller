# Job #52: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC Reference Design

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction model, 48 kHz switching model, bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation for ArduPilot, reference netlist, and thermal budget.

> **Design-review flag.** A true nano (20×20 mm) 4-in-1 rated for **80 A per channel** is beyond what the board's thermal mass can sustain. Section 4 shows the copper heats at roughly 34 K/s with all four channels at 80 A. Two ways forward:
> - Treat 80 A as a **≤1–2 s single-event burst** on a 20×20 board, with a continuous rating around 35–40 A.
> - Move to a 30.5×30.5 ESC.
>
> All device parameters below are **representative assumptions**. Replace them with your selected MOSFET datasheet values before sign-off.

## 0. System Architecture

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each motor is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry back.

```
VBAT (6S) ─┬─ Shunt/Current Sense ─┬─ 4× [ESC MCU + 3× half-bridge driver + 6 MOSFETs] ─ Motors
           │                       └─ ADC → H743 (BATT_CURR)
           └─ Buck 5V/3.3V → H743, IMU, Baro, OSD
H743 TIMx_CHy ── DShot600 bidir (single wire, open-drain-ish, pulled up) ── ESC MCU TIM input
```

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path

Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM works as follows:

- In each 60° sector, two phases carry current I.
- The non-PWM phase has one FET continuously ON.
- In the PWM phase, current flows through the HS FET for duty D and through the LS FET for (1−D). Body-diode conduction occurs only during dead time.

So, independent of D, **two switch positions always carry I**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) \cdot \underbrace{(1 - 2 t_{dead} f_{sw})}_{\approx 0.981}$$

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

The model is self-consistent: loss depends on R_ds(T_j), and T_j depends on loss. Model the resistance as:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + k(T_j - 25)],\quad k \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\ (\Rightarrow 1.6\times \text{ at } 125^\circ\text{C})$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.28 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.28\times10^{-3} \cdot 0.981 = \mathbf{16.1\ W}$$

The **per-FET worst case** is a FET held ON through a full sector:

$$P_{FET,max} = 80^2 \cdot 1.28\text{ m}\Omega = 8.2\text{ W (instantaneous)}$$

Each FET conducts about 1/3 of an electrical cycle (2 of 6 sectors). At high eRPM the thermal time constant of the die (~ms) averages this out:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx 8.2 \times \tfrac{1}{3} \approx 2.7\text{ W (continuous HS/LS-weighted, at full duty)}$$

### 2.3 PCB copper loss

Assume 0.25 mΩ of phase plus bus copper per conducting path, two paths active:

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 0.25\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\text{ W}$$

## 3. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only **one leg switches** per PWM period (the PWM phase). The hard-switched transition occurs on the HS turn-on, which commutates current from the LS body diode.

### 3.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10-4.5}{3} = 1.83\text{ A},\qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 1.5\text{ A}$$

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{23\text{ nC}}{1.83} = 12.6\text{ ns},\qquad t_{off} = \frac{23\text{ nC}}{1.5} = 15.3\text{ ns}$$

### 3.2 V–I overlap loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot27.9\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.35\ W}$$

### 3.3 Other switching-related terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Output capacitance | ½·C_oss·V²·f | 0.5·2n·25.2²·48k = **0.03 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 50n·25.2·48k = **0.06 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dead·f | 0.8·80·400n·48k = **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f | 2·80n·10·48k = **0.08 W** |
