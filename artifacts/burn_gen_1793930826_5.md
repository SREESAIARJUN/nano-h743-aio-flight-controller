# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #51): Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The loss model below applies to the ESC board. The DMA model applies to the H743.
