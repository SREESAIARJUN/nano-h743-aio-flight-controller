# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #60): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 System Partition

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. The proven architecture splits the work as follows:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz | Runs ArduPilot. Issues bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | Six-step commutation with 48 kHz complementary PWM. |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Six gate outputs per channel. |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET in PQFN 5×6 | 80 A burst per phase. |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is set in the AM32 ESC firmware. The H743 is responsible only for the DShot timing.

### 0.3 Form-Factor Warning

Four channels at 80 A cannot be sustained on a "nano" 20×20 mm board. The model below assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer board with 2 oz copper. Even at that size, **80 A is a time-limited burst rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 derives the allowable burst duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

The ESC uses six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° commutation sector:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Leg B (sink leg):** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.
- **Leg C:** floating, used for back-EMF sensing.

The phase current therefore always flows through exactly two channels in series, regardless of D.

### 1.2 RMS Current Including Ripple

The ripple current is worst at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{pp}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{10\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 13.1\ \text{A}_{pk\text{-}pk}
$$

The RMS current is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 14.3 = 6414\ \text{A}^2
$$

Ripple adds only 0.2%. At 48 kHz it is negligible, which is one of the benefits of the high PWM frequency.

### 1.3 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx 0.70 \times 1.6 = 1.12\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 6414 \times 1.12\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{14.37\ W}
$$

This splits by FET within the sector:

| FET | Formula | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Leg A high side | I²R·D | 7.18·D W |
| Leg A low side | I²R·(1−D) | 7.18·(1−D) W |
| Leg B low side | I²R | **7.18 W** (worst FET) |
