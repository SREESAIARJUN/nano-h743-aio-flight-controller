# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #74): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Analysis and DShot Allocation

## 0. Executive Findings

Four findings shape the rest of this design.

1. **80 A burst on a 20×20 mm board is limited by board heat capacity, not by the MOSFETs.** With all four channels at 80 A, the board dissipates about 92 W. Its total heat capacity is about 2.3 J/K, so it heats at about 40 K/s even under ideal conditions. A realistic rating is **80 A for ≤1 s on one or two channels**. With strong prop-wash cooling, sustainable continuous current is about 25–30 A per channel. Section 4 shows the derivation.
2. **The H743 should not run motor commutation.** It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. These run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware on AT32F421 or STM32G071 parts. This is how working AIO boards are built, and it isolates the flight-control timing from the commutation timing.
3. **48 kHz switching costs about 1.5 W per channel, against about 20 W of conduction loss at 80 A.** Frequency is not the thermal problem. Rds(on) and copper are.
4. **DMA allocation is not a constraint on the H743.** DMAMUX lets any of the 16 DMA1/DMA2 streams serve any timer request. The real constraints are pin and alternate-function mapping and avoiding timer sharing with LED or PWM outputs.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega\times1.6 = 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega \quad @\,125^\circ\text{C}$$

### 2.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM

At any instant, two phases carry the current I. The PWM phase alternates its current between the high-side (HS) switch, for fraction D, and the synchronous low-side (LS) switch, for fraction 1−D. The return phase's LS switch is on continuously. Therefore exactly **two FETs carry I at every instant**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 2.3 Ripple correction

$$\Delta I_{pp}=\frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\times0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\times48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A} \quad (D=0.5,\ \text{worst case})$$
