## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

Let $\theta_{off}$ be the encoder offset found at calibration (d-axis aligned with phase A):

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, balanced, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 dq plant model

The model assumes a surface PMSM, so $L_d = L_q = L_s$:

$$v_d = R_s i_d + L_s\dot i_d - \omega_e L_s i_q$$
$$v_q = R_s i_q + L_s\dot i_q + \omega_e (L_s i_d + \lambda_m)$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling

The PI zero is placed on the electrical pole $R_s/L_s$ (pole–zero cancellation). This gives a first-order closed loop $i/i^* = \omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$:

$$K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_c, \qquad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_c$$

Feed-forward decoupling is then added:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L_s i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L_s i_d + \lambda_m)$$

Set $i_d^* = 0$; no field weakening is needed for a gimbal.
