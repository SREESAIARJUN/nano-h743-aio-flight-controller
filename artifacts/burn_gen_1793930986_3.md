# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Loop, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion

I don't have the specification for Job #48, so I designed around a typical brushless gimbal. **Verify each item in the assumptions table against your hardware before use.** Parameters are kept symbolic wherever possible.

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Joint encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| Inverter | 3-phase, center-aligned PWM, f_pwm = 24 kHz, V_bus = 12–16 V |
| Current sensing | 2 or 3 low-side shunts, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separate SPI buses, ODR 8 kHz |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU |

Each loop runs at a fixed rate, and each is at least 5–10× slower in bandwidth than the loop inside it:

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 24 kHz (PWM ISR) | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| IMU acquisition and filtering | 8 kHz (DRDY ISR) | — |
| Velocity | 8 kHz | ~150–250 Hz |
| Angle (attitude) | 8 kHz, same task | ~30–50 Hz |
| Attitude estimator | 8 kHz gyro, accelerometer correction gated | — |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

The offset θ_off is found at calibration by applying a d-axis voltage at θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform (Amplitude-Invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\qquad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

With three shunts, first reject the common mode: $i_x \leftarrow i_x - \tfrac13(i_a+i_b+i_c)$.

### 1.3 Park Transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq Frame (Surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Torque is $\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$.

### 1.5 PI Control with Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

Choosing the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$
