## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path
With complementary PWM in 6-step mode, two FETs are always in the current path:
- the PWM-phase high-side (during D) or low-side (during 1−D) switch, and
- the static low-side FET of the return phase.

The conduction loss is therefore essentially independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot \left[2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{shunt} + R_{Cu}\right]
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right),\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.4 × 1.5 = **2.1 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 2× FET (hot) | 4.2 mΩ | **26.9 W** |
| Shunt (if per-channel) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Copper (phase pads, vias, battery plane) | ~0.4 mΩ | 2.6 W |
| **Channel conduction total** | | **≈ 31.1 W** |

**Per-FET average.** Each phase leg conducts for 120° of each electrical cycle, and all 6 FETs share the 2-FET path loss:

$$
P_{FET,avg} = \frac{26.9}{6} \approx 4.5\ \text{W}
$$

### 1.4 Thermal runaway check
Because R_DS(on) rises with T_j, the loss is self-reinforcing:

$$
T_j = T_a + R_\theta\, I^2 R_{25}\left(1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right)
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_a + R_\theta I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_\theta I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}
$$

The model is stable only if **R_θ · I² · R₂₅ · α < 1**.

For a single FET's share at 80 A (I²R₂₅/3 ≈ 3.0 W at 25 °C), R_θ must stay below about 67 K/W. That condition is easily met at the die level. The real limit is the board-level aggregate (Section 3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The return-phase low-side FET is static.

### 2.1 Switching transition times

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{12-3}{10} = 0.9\ \text{A}
\qquad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.3\ \text{A}
$$

$$
Q_{sw} = Q_{gd} + \tfrac{1}{2}Q_{gs} = 10\ \text{nC}
$$

$$
t_r = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} \approx 11\ \text{ns}\ (\text{use } 15\ \text{ns})
\qquad
t_f = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} \approx 33\ \text{ns}\ (\text{use } 30\ \text{ns})
$$

Driver output impedance and source inductance add margin, which is why the rounded values are used.

### 2.2 Loss components

| Component | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 45 ns · 48 k | **2.18 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction (t_dt = 200 ns, 2 edges) | V_SD · I · 2 · t_dt · f_sw | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 0.04 W |
| C_oss (2 FETs/leg) | 2 · ½ · C_oss · V²
