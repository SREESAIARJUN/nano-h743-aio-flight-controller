## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Check

I don't have the Job #30 requirements document. Every number below rests on the stated assumptions, and each equation is written so you can substitute your actual BOM values.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (full), 22.2 V (nominal) | Worst-case switching uses 25.2 V |
| Form factor | 30.5 × 30.5 mm AIO, 8-layer, 2 oz (70 µm) all layers | A true 20 × 20 "nano" **cannot** survive 80 A/ch (see §3.5) |
| ESC MCU | 4 × AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | The H743 is the FC. It does not commutate motors. |
| Gate driver | 4 × FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap), V_DRV = 10 V | I_source ≈ 1.5 A, I_sink ≈ 1.8 A |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, 3.3 × 3.3 DFN class, 24 total | R_DS(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ @ 10 V. Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gs2 = 3.5 nC, Q_gd = 6 nC. Q_rr = 30 nC, C_oss,tr ≈ 1.5 nF. R_g,int = 1.0 Ω |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM | Standard AM32/BLHeli_32 behaviour |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz | |
| "80 A burst" | Instantaneous phase current per motor, I_ph = 80 A | Not battery current. See §1.1. |

**Key conclusion up front:** 4 × 80 A simultaneously dissipates about 140 W on roughly 9 cm² of PCB. That is a burst rating of about 1–2 s, not a continuous rating. The thermal derivation in §3 quantifies this.

## 1. Current Definitions

### 1.1 Phase vs. battery current

In 6-step drive, two phases conduct at any instant and the bus current is chopped by duty D:

$$I_{bat} = D \cdot I_{ph}, \qquad I_{ph,RMS} = I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{2}{3}}$$

At I_ph = 80 A and D = 0.85, I_bat ≈ 68 A per motor, or about 272 A for four motors. Size the XT60/XT90, battery leads, and bulk capacitance accordingly.

### 1.2 Bus capacitor ripple (per ESC)

$$I_{C,RMS} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)} \quad\Rightarrow\quad \max \text{ at } D=0.5:\ 40\ \text{A}_{RMS}$$

This requires distributed MLCCs per channel (≥ 4 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210, derated to about 3 µF each at 25 V DC bias). It also requires a low-ESR bulk electrolytic (≥ 1000 µF/35 V) at the pigtail. The board-mounted MLCCs cannot carry 40 A RMS alone.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel, at 80 A)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 2.23 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 2.48 mΩ |

Use **2.4 mΩ** for hot design.

### 2.2 Conduction losses

During one commutation sector, with complementary PWM on the switching leg:

- High-side (switching leg): conducts for D
- Low-side (switching leg, synchronous rectifier): conducts for 1 − D
- Low-side (return leg): conducts 100 %

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}$$

The total is **independent of duty**; only the distribution among FETs changes.

$$P_{cond} = 2 (80)^2 (2.4\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$
