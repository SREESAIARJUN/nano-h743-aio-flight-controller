# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Electrical, Thermal and Firmware Model (Job #48)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

I have flagged the assumptions below because they change the math. Replace the example semiconductor parameters with datasheet values before release.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each motor bridge has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 running AM32), with one tri-phase gate driver and six N-channel MOSFETs. The H743 does **not** commutate motors. It sources bidirectional DShot600. |
| Bus | 6S: 22.2 V nominal, **25.2 V worst case** for switching loss. |
| Rating | **80 A burst per motor**. Pack-level worst case is 4 × 80 = **320 A**. |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM at **48 kHz**. |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm mounting pattern, 6-layer board with 2 oz copper. |
| MOSFET, parameter set P1 (example values; verify) | 40 V device. R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ at 25 °C. Hot multiplier k_T = 1.4. Q_gs2 = 5 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, Q_g = 40 nC. Q_oss = 30 nC. Q_rr = 50 nC at 80 A. V_plateau = 4.5 V at 80 A. V_SD = 0.8 V. R_θJC = 1.5 K/W. |
| Gate drive | V_DRV = 10 V. R_on,total = 10 Ω (driver + external R_g + internal R_g). R_off,total = 8 Ω. Dead time t_d = 100 ns. |
