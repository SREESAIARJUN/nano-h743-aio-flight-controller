# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC "Nano" Stack: Architecture Package

**Job #1 | Rev A (preliminary)**

> **Read this first: assumptions and caveats**
> 1. **No vendor datasheets are attached.** Every device parameter below (R_DS(on), Q_gd, Q_rr, Z_th, and so on) is a stated assumption for a representative part class. The equations are the deliverable. Re-run the numbers against the datasheets of the parts you actually procure.
> 2. **The H743 does not switch the bridge.** In every production 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. Each 48 kHz bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32 class, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421). I have architected it that way. Doing FOC/6-step for four motors on the H743 itself is possible but not recommended for an ArduPilot target.
> 3. **"Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot sustain 4 × 80 A.** The analysis below shows 80 A per channel is a burst rating of roughly 1–3 s on 30.5×30.5 mm, and less on 20×20. I specify a 30.5×30.5 ESC with a 20×20 FC, and flag where the nano constraint breaks.
