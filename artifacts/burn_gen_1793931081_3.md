## 0. Architecture Decision and Baseline Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors.** Complementary-output timers (TIM1, TIM8, plus HRTIM) cover about two 3-phase bridges, and sensorless BEMF commutation for four motors at 48 kHz would consume the flight-control CPU budget. The standard architecture is therefore:

- **Flight controller (FC):** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.
- **ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), each with a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. That is 24 FETs total.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM. At any instant two phase legs carry current:

- **PWM leg:** HS conducts for D·T_sw and LS (synchronous rectifier) for (1−D)·T_sw. Exactly one FET of the pair is on at a time.
- **Static leg:** its LS FET is on continuously for the 60° sector.

So the current path always passes through **exactly two R_DS(on)** (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

with

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow R_{DS}(125^\circ\text{C}) \approx 1.92\ \text{m}\Omega$$
