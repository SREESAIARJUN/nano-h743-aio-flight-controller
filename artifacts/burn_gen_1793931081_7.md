**Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and does not drive gates directly. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.

**Feasibility flag.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) is physically incompatible with 4×80 A. Six FETs per channel (24 total), bulk caps, and phase pads require at least a 30.5×30.5 mm footprint. The math below uses a 30×30 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board. Section 2 shows that 80 A is a **seconds-scale burst rating**, not a continuous one.
