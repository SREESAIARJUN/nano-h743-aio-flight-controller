## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In any 60° sector:

- one high-side FET is PWM'd at duty D;
- its complementary low-side FET on the same leg conducts for (1−D);
- one low-side FET on another leg is fully on.

So at every instant the phase current I flows through **two** channel resistances: one in the active PWM leg and one in the static low side.

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

The total is:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

The per-FET average, with each FET active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, is:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\, D \qquad P_{LS,sync} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,(1-D) \qquad P_{LS,static} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R$$

**At I = 80 A, hot R_DS(on) = 1.5 mΩ:**

- FET loss: 2 × 6400 × 0.0015 = **19.2 W per channel**
- Copper path: 0.3 mΩ is assumed (power plane plus phase pad plus vias at 2 oz). That adds 6400 × 0.0003 = **1.9 W**
- Total: **P_cond ≈ 21.1 W per channel**

**Instantaneous loss in one FET while conducting:** 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**.

The junction-to-case rise at ms timescales is Z_θJC(10 ms) ≈ 0.4 K/W, giving about 4 K. That is not the limiting factor. **Board thermal mass is** (see §3).

### 1.3 Temperature-coupled form for iteration

R_DS(on) rises with temperature, so the loss and temperature must be solved together:

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Iterate until it converges. The loss is unstable (thermal runaway) if the loop gain exceeds 1:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\, R_{\theta JA} \ge 1$$
