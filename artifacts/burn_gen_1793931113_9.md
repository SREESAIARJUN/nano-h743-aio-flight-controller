### 0.1 Architecture Clarification

In any 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and issues DShot commands. Each motor is commutated by its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or EFM8 class). The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those MCUs, not the H743. The H743's job is:

- DShot600 output,
- bidirectional eRPM capture,
- current/voltage telemetry ADC.

### 0.2 Feasibility Flag: "Nano"

80 A burst per channel (320 A aggregate) does **not** fit thermally on a 20×20 mm nano board.

- Section 3 shows a 20×20 board would exceed T_j,max within about 2 to 3 s at 80 A, even with prop-wash cooling.
- I have therefore sized the power stage on a 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board.
- If 20×20 is a hard requirement, the honest rating is about **45 A burst / 30 A continuous**. Rescaled numbers are given in §3.7.
