## 0. Architectural Clarifications

Several points in the requirement need to be resolved before the math means anything.

| Item | Interpretation used in this document |
|---|---|
| "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" | The H743 is the **flight controller** running ArduPilot. The ESC section uses **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 does not commutate motors. It talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. |
| Bus voltage | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. Design limit is 30 V transient, so 40 V FETs are used. |
| "80A burst" | 80 A per motor phase-path (DC-link equivalent) for **≤ 2–3 s**, not continuous. |
| PWM | 48 kHz center-aligned complementary PWM with synchronous rectification, 6-step trapezoidal commutation. |
| Form factor | **Red flag:** a true "nano" (20×20 mm) board **cannot** survive 4×80 A, even as a burst. The numbers below assume a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** board. On 20×20 mm, derate to roughly 35–45 A burst. |
| System current | 4×80 A = **320 A battery draw**. This exceeds XT60 (~60 A cont.) and XT90 ratings. Treat "all four at 80 A simultaneously" as a thermal worst case, not an expected operating point. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths (6-step, synchronous rectification)

In any 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM-switched (high side, HS) and one phase is held low (low side, LS, fully on). The third phase floats for BEMF sensing.

- **PWM ON (fraction D):** current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **PWM OFF (fraction 1−D):** current freewheels through LS(A) (synchronous) → motor → LS(B).

In both states, **exactly two FETs** are in the current path. The total bridge conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle**:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

with the temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Per-device distribution

Each phase is HS-active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle and LS-active for 1/3. During its own PWM sector, the LS of the switched phase also conducts the (1−D) freewheel portion.

$$
P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{DS}
$$

$$
P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,(1 + (1-D))\, I^2 R_{DS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\, I^2 R_{DS}
$$

**Check:** summing over 3 HS + 3 LS gives D·I²R + (2−D)·I²R = 2I²R. ✓

The electrical frequency is f_e = (poles/2)·rpm/60. For a 14-pole motor at 30 krpm this is about 3.5 kHz, far above the FET thermal time constant (~ms). Per-device **averaging is therefore valid**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, D = 0.9, T_j = 125 °C

The resistance at temperature is R_DS(125 °C) = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.74 mΩ**.
