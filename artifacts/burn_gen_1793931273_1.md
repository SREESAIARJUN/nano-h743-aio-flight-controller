## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC/PMSM, $N_s = 12$ slots, $2p = 14$ poles ($p = 7$ pole pairs) |
| Rotor position | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (e.g. AS5048/MT6701) |
| Driver | 3-phase inverter, center-aligned PWM, 2–3 shunt current sensing |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (FPU), single-precision float |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted **symmetric about the payload rotation center** |
| Disturbance | Base vibration (vehicle or propellers), 50–400 Hz, possibly RPM-correlated |

**Rate rule:** each loop's bandwidth should be at least 4–5× below the loop inside it, and at least 10× below its own sample rate.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of the rotation above.

### 1.2 Plant model (surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\, p\, \lambda_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole $R/L$, giving a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_s/10$, for example $2\pi \cdot 1.5\,\text{kHz}$ at $f_s = 20\,\text{kHz}$.

**Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup** (forward Euler, period $T$):

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad u_k^{\text{sat}} = \text{sat}(u_k)
$$

$$
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_k^{\text{sat}} - u_k),\qquad K_{aw}\approx K_i/K_p
$$

**Voltage limiting.** Use circular limiting so that $d$-axis priority is preserved:

$$
|v_d| \le V_{max},\qquad |v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2},\qquad V_{max} = V_{dc}/\sqrt3
$$

This is the linear SVPWM limit.

### 1.4 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c) + \min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$

**Delay compensation.** Rotate the inverse Park by $\theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T$ to account for 1 sample of computation delay plus 0.5 sample of PWM delay.
