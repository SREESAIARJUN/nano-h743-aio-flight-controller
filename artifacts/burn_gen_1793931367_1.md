## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The architecture is:

```
[STM32H743 FC] --DShot600 bidir (4x)--> [4x ESC MCU, e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware]
                                         --> [4x 3-phase gate driver] --> [24x N-MOSFET]
```

All values below are design assumptions. Replace them with the datasheet values of your chosen parts before release.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per ESC | I_pk | 80 A |
| Continuous rating (target) | I_cont | 45 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, 5×6 / 3.3×3.3 class) | R_DS(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 1.6× at T_j = 125 °C |
| Rise + fall time (in-circuit) | t_r + t_f | 20 ns |
| Gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Body-diode reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Dead time | t_dt | 200 ns per edge |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_F | 0.8 V |
| Gate drive voltage | V_drv | 10 V |
| Commutation | 6-step, complementary (synchronous) PWM | |

> **Engineering flag:** 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is only achievable as a **short burst**. It is not a continuous rating. Section 3 quantifies the allowable burst duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

At any commutation state, phase A is PWM-switched (high-side Q_AH at duty D, low-side Q_AL complementary at 1−D) and phase B has its low-side Q_BL fully on.

$$
P_{cond,AH} = I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,D
$$
$$
P_{cond,AL} = I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,(1-D)
$$
$$
P_{cond,BL} = I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Summing the three terms, the duty cycle cancels:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}
$$

This result is independent of throttle when synchronous rectification is used.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad k \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.6) = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Numerical result at 80 A burst

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0010 = **12.8 W** | 51.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0016 = **20.5 W** | 82.0 W |

### 1.4 Copper and shunt contributions (do not neglect)

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A (single path) |
|---|---|---|
| Phase trace + pad, 2 oz × 6 L, ~8 mm path | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W per active phase × 2 = 3.8 W |
| Bus shunt (shared, 4 ch) | 0.25 mΩ | at 4×80 A = 320 A → **25.6 W** |

> **Shunt flag:** A 0.25 mΩ shunt at 320 A total is unacceptable. Use either a 0.1 mΩ shunt (10.2 W burst) or a hall/TMR sensor (e.g. ACS7xx-class / CT4xx-class). Otherwise the current-sense path will be the first failure point.

### 1.5 Thermal runaway check (electrothermal feedback)

$$
T_j = T_a + Z_{th}\cdot P(T_j), \qquad P(T_j) = P_0\left[1+k(T_j-25)\right]
$$

Solving gives:

$$
T_j = \frac{T_a + Z_{th}P_0(1-25k)}{1 - Z_{th}P_0 k}
$$

The system is stable only if Z_th · P_0 · k < 1. Per FET, P_0 ≈ 6.4 W (fully-on low side at 80 A, 25 °C) and k = 0.006, so the stability limit is **Z_th < 26 K/W**. Steady-state R_th,ja on a nano board is around 30–40 K/W per FET, which exceeds this limit. **80 A cannot be sustained at all; it is strictly transient-limited.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg (Q_AH / Q_AL pair) switches. The fully-on low side does not.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap loss (high side)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery loss (low-side body diode)

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

Note that Q_rr scales with di/dt and current. Measure it at 80 A, because datasheet values are often specified at lower current.

### 2.3 Output capacitance loss

$$
P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot 2 = 30\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction (significant)

$$
P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot400\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.229\ W}
$$

> At 48 kHz, dead-time loss exceeds V·I overlap loss. Reducing t_dt from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. Characterize shoot-through margin before doing so.

### 2.5 Gate drive loss (dissipated mostly in the driver IC and gate resistors)

$$
P_g = Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} \cdot 2 = 50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}\cdot2 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

### 2.6 Per-channel and total burst budget (T_j = 125 °C)

| Loss term | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 20.48 W | 81.9 W |
| Phase copper | 3.84 W | 15.4 W |
| V·I switching | 0.97 W | 3.9 W |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 W | 4.9 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + Q_g | 0.13 W | 0.5 W |
| Bus shunt (0.1 mΩ, shared) | — | 10.2 W |
| **Total** | **≈26.7 W** | **≈116.8 W** |
