# Job #33: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction model, 48 kHz switching model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, reference netlists, and thermal budget.

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three requirements conflict with physics or with how these boards are actually built. The rest of the document is designed around these corrections.

| Issue | Reality | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **"Nano" form factor + 4×80 A** | A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 320 A or dissipate the loss. Copper cross-section, MOSFET footprint and thermal mass all fail. | Use a **30.5×30.5 mm mount (≈36×36 mm board)**. Treat 80 A as a **≤2 s burst per channel**, not simultaneous continuous current. |
| **H743 switching the bridge at 48 kHz** | On an AIO, the H743 does not drive the gates. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. | H743 → DShot600 → ESC MCUs. The 48 kHz figure applies to the ESC MCUs. |
| **4×80 A at 6S** | 320 A × 25 V ≈ 8 kW. That exceeds XT60/XT90 leads and most packs. | Size the DC bus for realistic aggregate peaks (≈150–180 A). Keep the 80 A rating as a per-motor transient. |

**Baseline assumptions** (substitute your actual BOM values):
- 6S pack: V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal.
- MOSFET: 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN, 6 per channel.
  - R_DS(on) = 1.5 mΩ at 25 °C.
  - Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gd = 5 nC, Q_oss = 30 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC.
  - R_θJC ≈ 1.5 K/W.
- Gate driver: FD6288Q-class (3 half-bridges, ~1.5 A source/sink).
- Dead time: 100 ns.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification, two phases conduct at any instant:
- **On-time (D):** HS(A) → winding → LS(B).
- **Off-time (1−D):** LS(A) freewheels → winding → LS(B).

Exactly two MOSFETs are always in series with the motor current, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{hot} D + I^2 R_{hot}(1-D) + I^2 R_{hot} = 2 I^2 R_{hot}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$
R_{hot} = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{/K}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_hot = 1.5 mΩ × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Parasitic copper and shunt
- Shunt: 0.5 mΩ.
- Trace/via path: ≈0.4 mΩ (2 oz outer + 2 oz inner, 4 mm wide pour).

$$
P_{par} = I^2 (R_{shunt} + R_{trace}) = 6400 \times 0.0009 = 5.76\ \text{W}
$$

### 1.4 Results at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction (2·I²·R_hot) | 28.8 W | 115.2 W |
| Shunt + copper | 5.8 W | 23.0 W |
| **Conduction total** | **34.6 W** | **138.2 W** |

Each conducting FET dissipates 14.4 W.

The low-side FET of the "sink" phase carries 100% duty for its 60° electrical sector. Per FET averaged over a full electrical cycle (each FET conducts ~1/3 of the time):

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} \times 3 \approx 9.6\ \text{W}
$$

The **instantaneous** 14.4 W is what matters for junction transient analysis.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched half-bridge loses energy at f_sw. Values use V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A.

**1. V–I overlap (hard switching)**

Rise/fall times are driver-limited:

$$
t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{drv}} \approx \frac{5\,\text{nC}}{0.5\,\text{A}} = 10\,\text{ns}
$$

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.97\ \text{W}
$$

**2. Output capacitance**

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

**3. Body diode reverse recovery**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

**4. Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two transitions per period)

$$
P_{dt} = V_F I (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}
$$

**5. Gate drive** (dissipated in driver and gate resistors, not the FET)

$$
P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 30\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.029\ \text{W}
$$
