# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #49): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Warning

**Topology.** In any real "4-in-1 ESC" stack, the STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. Each motor channel is commutated by its **own ESC MCU** (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 never drives gates directly. The architecture below keeps that split.

**Feasibility warning.** A "nano" (20×20 or 25×25 mm) board cannot sustain 80 A per channel continuously. The math in §3 shows that 80 A must be treated as a **time-limited burst** governed by thermal capacitance, not by a steady-state thermal resistance. I derive the burst time limit explicitly.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Instantaneous conduction path
With complementary PWM in six-step commutation, current always flows through exactly **two conducting channels in series**:

- During the duty period D, the active high-side FET (phase A) and the active low-side FET (phase B) conduct.
- During 1−D, the synchronous low-side FET (phase A) and the low-side FET (phase B) conduct.

The current therefore sees 2·R_ds(on) at all times. With I_ph ≈ I_DC,phase and low ripple at 48 kHz:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

### 1.3 Self-consistent junction temperature
Conduction loss raises T_j, which raises R_ds(on). The fixed point is:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\cdot\Big(2 I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + P_{sw}\Big)
$$

Solving for T_j:

$$
T_j^* = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta\left(2I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha) + P_{sw}\right)}{1 - 2 R_\theta I^2 R_{25}\alpha}
$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion.** The denominator must stay positive:

$$
2 R_\theta I^2 R_{25}\alpha < 1
$$

At 80 A this requires R_θ < 1/(2·6400·1.2e-3·0.0045) = **14.5 K/W** (channel-level, referred to ambient). A nano board's natural-convection R_θ is roughly 20–40 K/W for the whole board. **80 A continuous is thermally unstable on this form factor.** This is the formal proof that 80 A is burst-only.

### 1.4 Numbers at T_j = 110 °C (burst design point)
- R_ds(on)(110 °C) = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.659 mΩ**
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 80² × 1.659e-3 = **21.2 W per channel**

**Per-FET averaging.** Each FET conducts for 120° electrical out of 360°. At eRPM-scale commutation frequencies (kHz), the die's thermal time constant (≈ms) averages this out:

- Average conduction loss per FET ≈ 21.2 / 6 ≈ **3.5 W**
- Peak loss during the conduction window ≈ 10.6 W

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches hard. The non-PWM low-side FET stays on for the full 60° sector.

### 2.1 Gate currents in the Miller region

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g}} = \frac{10-3}{5} = 1.4\text{ A}
$$

$$
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g}} = \frac{3}{5} = 0.6\text{ A}
