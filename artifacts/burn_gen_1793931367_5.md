# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #40): Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #40 BOM, stackup, or board outline. This document therefore gives a **parametric model with a worked reference design**. Replace the values in the assumptions table with your datasheet and layout values, and the equations still apply.

**Architectural decision:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. Commutating four motors at 48 kHz from the flight controller MCU is not supported by ArduPilot and is not recommended.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous PWM)

In each commutation sector, one phase is PWM-ed (high-side on for D, low-side on for 1−D), one phase is held low, and one phase floats. Current therefore always flows through **exactly two MOSFETs in series**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) \quad (R_{HS}=R_{LS})
$$

Temperature dependence:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}
$$

For per-device loss, each FET averages a 1/3 conduction share over an electrical cycle:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{3}
$$

### 1.2 Copper and shunt loss

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 \frac{\rho_{Cu}\,\ell}{w\,t\,n_{layers}},\qquad \rho_{Cu}(T)=1.72\times10^{-8}[1+0.0039(T-20)]\ \Omega\cdot m
$$

If a 0.25 mΩ shunt sits in the battery line, use battery current, not phase current:

$$
I_{bat} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| Phase copper (≈ 0.3 mΩ effective) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **Per channel** | | **≈ 21.1 W** |

Note the peak instantaneous loss in a single FET: 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step operation, only **one half-bridge switches** at any instant.

**Hard-switching V·I overlap:**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\,\mathrm{ns} \cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

**Output capacitance (two devices per leg):**

$$
P_{oss} = 2 \cdot \tfrac12 C_{o(er)} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\,\mathrm{nF} \cdot 635 \cdot 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.046\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Reverse recovery** (low-side body diode, after dead time):

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\mathrm{nC} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.060\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two transitions per period):

$$
P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph} \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\,\mathrm{ns} \cdot 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.61\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET):

$$
P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 60\,\mathrm{nC} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.058\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Per-channel switching total: ≈ 2.7 W.**

**Sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz you save about 1.3 W per channel but gain audible noise and current ripple. Ripple estimate: ΔI ≈ V·D(1−D)/(L_ph·f_sw). For a 20 µH motor at D = 0.5, ΔI ≈ 6.6 A pk-pk at 48 kHz.
