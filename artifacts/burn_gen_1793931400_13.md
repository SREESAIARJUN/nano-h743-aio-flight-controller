# STM32H743 AIO "Nano" Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model and Feasibility Review

Before the derivation, four architectural corrections. They change the answer.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted in this document |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. Per-motor 48 kHz PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection and bidirectional DShot decoding run on a dedicated ESC MCU. | The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Four ESC MCUs run AM32, one per motor (STM32G071 or AT32F421 class). |
| 2 | An 80 A *continuous* rating on a 20×20 mm board is physically impossible. You will see below that this is roughly 1,000 K of steady-state rise. | 80 A is treated strictly as a time-limited burst. The model computes the allowable burst duration. |
| 3 | Four simultaneous 80 A channels means 320 A through nano-sized battery
