## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths
In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two phases carry current at any instant: I enters through one leg and returns through another.

- **Non-PWM leg** (low-side held ON for the 60° sector): conducts I continuously.
- **PWM leg**: the high-side conducts for D·T_sw, the low-side (sync rect) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.

So at every instant exactly **two FET channels** carry I, apart from dead time (Section 2.4).

Ripple current adds to RMS. For L_phase ≈ 15 µH (small 2207-class motor) at 48 kHz:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L f_{sw}} \approx \frac{(25.2\times0.1)\cdot0.9}{15\,\mu\text{H}\cdot48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 3.2\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 0.85 \approx 6401\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple term is negligible at 80 A.
