# STM32H743 Nano AIO: 4-in-1 ESC Design Package (Job #50)

## 0. Architecture Note and Assumptions

**Correction to the premise.** The STM32H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors itself. An "H743 4-in-1" product is an AIO board with these parts:

- **Flight controller domain:** the STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM.
- **ESC domain:** four independent ESC MCUs, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. Each drives one 3-phase bridge at 48 kHz PWM.

The thermal and switching model below applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model applies to the H743.

**Baseline assumptions.** Replace these with your datasheet values; every equation is parametric.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, fully charged (worst case for switching) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst motor-phase current per ESC |
| R_ds,25 | 1.0 mΩ | 40 V MOSFET in PDFN5x6, V_gs = 10 V |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_ds temperature coefficient |
| t_r, t_f | 25 ns, 20 ns | At the chosen gate resistor |
| Q_rr | 50 nC | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge |
| Q_oss | 60 nC | Output charge |
| Q_g | 60 nC | Total gate charge |
| t_dead | 200 ns | Dead time |
| V_f | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward voltage |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| Topology | 6-step, complementary PWM | Synchronous rectification, AM32 default |
| Board | 36×36 mm | Nano 30.5 mm mount, 6-layer, 2 oz outer copper |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.383 = **1.38 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, two legs are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for duty D. The low side (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts 100%.

Total conduction loss per ESC:

P_cond = I²R_ds·D + I²R_ds·(1 − D) + I²R_ds = **2·I_ph²·R_ds(T_j)**

This result is independent of duty cycle, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification. It neglects the dead-time diode interval, which is handled in §2.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.38 mΩ = **17.7 W per ESC**

### 1.4 Copper loss

Per ESC, the path is about 30 mm of 2 oz pour, 6 mm wide, in two parallel layers. That gives about 0.25 mΩ effective, including the via field.

P_Cu = 2 × 80² × 0.25 mΩ ≈ **3.2 W**

### 1.5 Battery current check

I_bat = D · I_ph. At D → 1 with all four ESCs at 80 A, I_bat ≈ **320 A**.

This exceeds any XT60/XT90 and nano-board battery-pad rating. Realistic interpretation: 80 A is a **per-ESC burst** rating, with total battery current firmware-limited to about 120–160 A.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches; one switch transition pair per PWM cycle. Losses per ESC:

**(a) V-I overlap:**
P_ov = ½ · V_bus · I_ph · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 45 ns × 48 kHz = **2.18 W**

**(b) Reverse recovery:**
P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.06 W**

**(c) Output capacitance:**
P_oss ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw = **0.04 W**

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead times per cycle):
P_dt = V_f · I_ph · 2·t_dead · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 400 ns × 48 k = **1.23 W**

**(e) Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FET):
P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw = **0.06 W**

**Switching subtotal: ≈ 3.5 W per ESC.**

### Design levers

- Dead time is the second-largest switching term. Dropping from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per ESC. Do this only after verifying shoot-through margin on the scope.
- Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. Going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz halves (a) and (d), at the cost of motor current ripple and audible noise.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Network (first-order lumped plus junction stage)

```
T_j ──R_jc(0.8 K/W)── T_case ──R_c-pcb(0.7 K/W, solder+vias)── T_board ──R_ba── T_amb
                                                                  │
                                                               C_board
```

### 4.2 Board-to-air resistance

R_ba = 1 / (h · A), with two sides: A = 2 × 36² mm² = 2.59 × 10⁻³ m².

| Airflow condition | h (W/m²K) | R_ba |
|---|---|---|
| Hover, partial propwash | 50 | 7.7 K/W |
| Forward flight, direct airflow | 150 | 2.6 K/W |

### 4.3 Board heat capacity

About 7 g of copper plus FR4 plus FETs, at c_eff ≈ 0.75 J/gK: C ≈ **5.3 J/K**.

τ = R_ba · C ≈ 41 s (hover case) or 14 s (forward-flight case)

### 4.4 Transient board rise

ΔT_b(t) = P·R_ba·(1 − e^(−t/τ))

At P = 102 W, hover case:

| t | ΔT_board |
|---|---|
| 1 s | 19 K |
| 2 s | 38 K |
| 3 s | 56 K |
| 5 s | 89 K |

Early in the burst, heat flow is effectively adiabatic: dT/dt ≈ P/C ≈ 19 K/s.

### 4.5 Junction above board

The worst FET carries about 2/3 of its leg's share at peak, roughly 5 W instantaneous:

ΔT_jb = 5 × (0.8 + 0.7) ≈ **7.5 K**

### 4.6 Burst limit

Constraint: T_j ≤ 125 °C, starting from T_amb = 40 °C:

ΔT_b,max = 125 − 40 − 7.5 = 77.5 K

t_burst,max = −τ · ln(1 − ΔT/(P·R)) = −41 · ln(1 − 77.5/785) ≈ **4.3 s** (hover)

In forward flight (R = 2.6 K/W, τ = 14 s): t_burst,max ≈ 5.0 s.
