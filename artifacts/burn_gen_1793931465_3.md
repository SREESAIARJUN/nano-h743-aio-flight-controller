### Loop rates and bandwidth separation

Each loop's bandwidth is kept at least 5× below the loop inside it.

```
 20 kHz  ── Current (FOC) PI ──────────── ωc ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s
  8 kHz  ── IMU sample + notch/LPF + dual-IMU fusion
  4 kHz  ── Velocity PI (+ cogging FF) ── ωv ≈ 2π·120 rad/s
  1 kHz  ── Attitude estimator (Mahony) + Angle P/PD ── ωθ ≈ 2π·20 rad/s
```

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant in the dq frame (surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

where $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$. The torque is:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI controller with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** The PI zero cancels the electrical pole $R/L$, so the closed loop becomes first-order with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

With the assumed values ($\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot1500$): $K_p = 19.8$ V/A and $K_i = 52{,}800$ V/(A·s).

The constraint $\omega_c \le \omega_{s}/10$ (with $\omega_s = 2\pi\cdot20\text{ kHz}$) keeps the 1.5-sample PWM/ADC delay from costing more than about 20° of phase margin.

**Discretization (backward Euler, $T_s = 50\,\mu s$):**

$$
x_k = x_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k,\qquad u_k = K_p e_k + x_k
$$

**Voltage limit.** The available voltage is $|v| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$ (linear SVPWM region). Saturate the voltage vector with the d-axis given priority:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)
$$

**Anti-windup (back-calculation):**

$$
x_k \mathrel{+}= K_{aw}(u_{sat}-u_{unsat}),\qquad K_{aw} = K_i/K_p
$$
