## 1. FOC Current Loop (innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant model in the dq frame (SPM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.3 PI design by pole-zero cancellation

The plant is $G(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$ and the controller is $C(s) = K_p + \dfrac{K_i}{s}$.

Choose:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{cc},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{cc}$$

The controller zero then cancels the plant pole, and the closed loop becomes $\dfrac{\omega_{cc}}{s+\omega_{cc}}$.

Sampling delay of about $1.5\,T_s$ (computation plus PWM hold) limits the achievable bandwidth to $\omega_{cc} \le \dfrac{1}{1.5\,T_s}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}$. With $T_s = 50\,\mu s$:

$$\omega_{cc} \approx 2\pi \cdot 500\text{–}1000\ \text{rad/s}$$

### 1.4 Discrete form with decoupling and anti-windup

Error and integrator update (forward Euler integrator with back-calculation, gain $K_{aw} = 1/K_p$):

$$e_k = i^*_k - i_k$$

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s\, e_k + K_{aw}\,(v^{sat}_k - v^{unsat}_k)$$

Unsaturated outputs including cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v^{unsat}_{d} = K_p e_d + I_d - \hat\omega_e L i_q$$

$$v^{unsat}_{q} = K_p e_q + I_q + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Voltage limiting:** linear SVPWM allows a voltage circle of radius $V_{max} = V_{bus}/\sqrt3$. Use d-axis priority:

$$v_d^{sat} = \text{clip}\left(v_d,\ \pm V_{max}\right)$$

$$v_q^{sat} = \text{clip}\left(v_q,\ \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)$$
