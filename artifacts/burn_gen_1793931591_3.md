## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× BLDC gimbal motors (e.g., 12N14P), with inline or low-side current sensing |
| Encoders | 14-bit magnetic encoder per joint (e.g., AS5048/MT6701) |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (STM32G4/H7 class) |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, gyro and accel at 8 kHz ODR |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner, camera) |
