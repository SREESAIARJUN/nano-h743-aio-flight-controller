**Architectural notes before the math:**

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz switching.** In every production AIO, each motor has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 sends DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM is a setting in the ESC firmware (AM32 `PWM frequency = 48`). Driving 12 half-bridges directly from the H743 is possible in principle, but ArduPilot does not support it.
2. **An 80 A burst on a nano-class board is thermally marginal to non-viable for anything beyond about 1–3 s.** The model below quantifies this. Treat "80 A" as a short-duration burst rating, not a design point.
3. **Bidirectional DShot plus 48 kHz plus 6S** is a sound combination. The binding constraints are DMA stream allocation (§4) and copper (§3), not the protocol.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases conduct. One phase leg is PWM-switched, and the other has its low-side FET held on.

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the HS FET for duty fraction D and through the LS FET (synchronous rectification) for 1−D. That leg therefore always presents exactly one R_DS(on).
- **Static leg:** one FET, fully on.

So the motor current always sees 2·R_DS(on), independent of duty:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

Per FET, each device conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle (120° commutation):

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS(on)}}{3}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per motor | P_cond per FET | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.13 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 3.09 W | 74.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** | **3.41 W** | **81.9 W** |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses (often larger than designers expect)

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad / trace (2 oz, ~8 mm, ~4 mm wide, multilayer) | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |

| **Total parasitic per motor** | | **~4.5 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches, so one hard-switched commutation pair occurs per PWM period.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

With t_r = t_f = 10 ns (≈1.5 A gate drive into a ~45 nC device with ~8 nC Q_gd; verify on the bench):

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ ns} \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times (2\times 100\text{ ns}) \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{ nC}\times 25.2\times 48\,000 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Qrr scales roughly with di/dt and I, so treat this as ×2–3 at 80 A, about **0.12 W**.
