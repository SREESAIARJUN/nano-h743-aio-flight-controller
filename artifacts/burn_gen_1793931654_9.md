**Document:** Job #25, Rev A (engineering analysis)
**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction and switching loss model at 48 kHz PWM, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA plan, and a reference netlist.

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Warning

All results below depend on the parameters in this section. Change any of them and re-run the equations.

### 0.1 Architecture assumption

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. That is the standard topology for an ArduPilot AIO:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421, AM32/BLHeli_32-class)
                                            └─► 4× 3-phase gate driver ─► 24× N-MOSFET
```

The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC PWM frequency**. DShot600 is the FC-to-ESC command link.

### 0.3 Feasibility warning (read this first)

A "nano" AIO is 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm. As section 3 shows, **80 A per motor on four motors at once (~165 W of heat) is a burst limited to a few seconds by the board's heat capacity.** It is not a continuous rating.

Realistic continuous ratings for this footprint are about **35–45 A per motor**, and only with propwash airflow. Any datasheet claiming "80 A" on this footprint should state the burst duration. Section 3.4 gives the formula to derive it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{hot} = 2.0 \times 1.5 = 3.0\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° sector, current flows through one leg's high side and another leg's low side. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **PWM'd leg:** the high-side FET carries I for duty D and the low-side FET carries I for (1 − D), via synchronous rectification. The leg's total conduction is I²R regardless of D.
- **Static low-side leg:** carries I continuously.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{cu}$$

Ripple correction: with ripple current ΔI, replace I² with I² + ΔI²/12. At 48 kHz with typical motor inductance (~20 µH phase-to-phase), ΔI is about 6 A pk-pk. The correction is under 0.1%, so it is neglected.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| FET conduction: 2 × 80² × 3.0 mΩ | **38.4 W** |
| Copper: 2 × 80² × 0.5 mΩ | 6.4 W (spread across the board) |
| **Total per motor** | **44.8 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET average

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for one third of the electrical period on average:

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{38.4}{6} = 6.4\ \mathrm{W}$$

The instantaneous peak per FET is up to I²R = **19.2 W**. This matters for thermal ripple at low eRPM (see section 3.5).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only one leg per sector is hard-switched: the PWM'd leg.

### 2.1 Hard-switching overlap loss

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\mathrm{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.060\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-time diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = 2\, V_f I_{ph} t_{dt} f_{sw} = 2 \times 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.61\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.048\ \mathrm{W}$$
