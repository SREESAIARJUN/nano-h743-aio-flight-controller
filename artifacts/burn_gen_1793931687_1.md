## 0. Scope and Ground Rules

Three findings change the design before any numbers are run:

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every viable 4-in-1 architecture, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, plus a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 talks to these MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC MCU's PWM frequency, not an H743 timer.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board is a transient rating, not a thermal steady state.** The model in §3 shows that four simultaneous 80 A channels dissipate roughly 90 W in about 4 cm² of PCB. That is survivable for only a few seconds. This document quantifies that limit rather than hiding it.
3. **All component parameters below are design assumptions** for a representative 40 V PDFN3333 MOSFET class. Re-run the model against the datasheet of the part you actually procure, especially R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, and Q_rr.
