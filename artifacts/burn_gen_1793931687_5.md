### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 cannot run ArduPilot and also commutate 4 motors at 48 kHz with sensorless back-EMF timing. The design is therefore partitioned:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot, bi-directional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7-class running AM32 | 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
