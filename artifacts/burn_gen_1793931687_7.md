# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Electrothermal Model and Bi-directional DShot600 Architecture (Job #21)

---

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

These assumptions must be confirmed before tape-out.

| Item | Assumed value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | H743 FC running ArduPilot, plus a 4-in-1 ESC with **one MCU per channel** (AT32F421 running AM32, or STM32G071 running BLHeli_32-class firmware) | ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The H743 only sends DShot and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V full, 22.2 V nominal, ~21 V under 80 A sag | Worst-case switching losses are calculated at 25.2 V. |
| Form factor | 30.5 × 30.5 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers | A 20 × 20 mm board cannot sustain 80 A bursts (see §3.4). |
