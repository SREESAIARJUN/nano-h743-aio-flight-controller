## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Stated Assumptions

Two problems with the request as worded need resolving before any math.

**1. The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.**
- A 4-in-1 ESC needs four independent 6-step or FOC commutation loops, each with zero-cross/BEMF comparators and complementary PWM with dead time.
- The production-sane topology uses the H743 for ArduPilot. It talks bidirectional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware).
- The rest of this document assumes that split.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) form factor is not physically credible.**
- At 80 A per channel the copper alone dissipates watts.
- The math below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm mount / ~40×40 mm board**, 6-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner copper.
- **80 A is treated as a per-channel burst rating.** Section 3 derives how long that burst can last.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

- In any commutation sector, one leg is PWM'd (high side ↔ low side, complementary) and one leg's low side is held on. The third leg floats for BEMF sensing.
- With synchronous rectification, **exactly two FETs carry phase current at every instant**, independent of duty cycle D:
  - During D·T_s, current flows through HS(PWM leg) and LS(fixed leg).
  - During (1−D)·T_s, current flows through LS(PWM leg) and LS(fixed leg).
- Neglecting ripple (motor L ≫ L_crit at 48 kHz):

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

### 1.2 Electrothermal coupling

R_DS(on) depends on T_j, so the loss must be solved self-consistently:

$$
T_j = T_{PCB} + R_{\theta JP}\cdot P_{FET}(T_j)
$$

$$
P_{FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]}{3} + P_{sw,FET}
$$

The per-FET conduction term is I²R/3 because each FET conducts in 1/3 of electrical sectors on average (bridge loss ÷ 6 FETs × 2).

Closed-form solution, with P_s = switching share per FET:

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{PCB} + R_{\theta JP}\left[\frac{I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{3} + P_s\right]}{1 - R_{\theta JP}\,\frac{I^2 R_{25}\alpha}{3}}
$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion.** The denominator must stay positive:

$$
R_{\theta JP}\cdot \frac{I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}{3} < 1
$$

At 80 A this evaluates to 3.0 × (6400 × 0.0012 × 0.005)/3 = **0.0384**, so there is large margin and no local runaway.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

- R_DS(on)(125 °C) = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.80 mΩ**
- P_cond,bridge = 2 × 6400 × 0.0018 = **23.0 W per channel**
- P_cu = I² · R_cu = 6400 × 0.0005 = **3.2 W per channel**
- Average per FET ≈ 23.0 / 6 = **3.84 W**

The worst FET (the PWM-leg low side at low D) sees up to about 2× average instantaneously within its sector. This is absorbed by the FET's thermal capacitance, which has a time constant of tens of ms against an electrical period of under 1 ms at flying eRPM.
