## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in trapezoidal 6-step commutation

At any instant, two phase legs carry the current I. With synchronous rectification, the PWM'd leg conducts through the high-side FET for duty D and through the low-side FET for (1−D). The other active leg's FET conducts continuously during the 120° interval.

The bridge conduction loss is therefore (to a good approximation):

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

The share for each FET over one electrical period (each FET is active for ⅓ of the period):

$$
P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R(T_j)\cdot D_{eff},\qquad
P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R(T_j)\cdot(1-D_{eff}) \;+\; \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R(T_j)\cdot k_{cont}
$$

- D_eff → 1 at full throttle, which is the worst case for the high-side FETs.
- k_cont accounts for whichever device holds the non-PWM'd leg. It depends on firmware (AM32 complementary mode, PWM on HS).

### 1.2 Temperature-coupled solution

R depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P, so the system is solved by fixed-point iteration:

$$
T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}\!\left(R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j^{(n)}-25)]\right)\cdot R_{\theta,JB}
$$

**Evaluation at I = 80 A, T_board = 100 °C, R_θJB ≈ 1.5 K/W (FET plus via field):**

| Iteration | T_j (°C) | R_DS(on) (mΩ) | P per HS FET at D≈1 (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 1.41 | 3.01 |
| 1 | 104.5 | 1.44 | 3.07 |
| 2 | 104.6 | 1.44 | **3.07 (converged)** |

**Results:**

- P_cond per channel = 2 × 80² × 1.44 mΩ = **18.4 W**
- All four channels at simultaneous 80 A: **73.7 W conduction**

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches. The per-channel contributions are:

**1. V–I overlap (hard switching, inductive load):**

$$
P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(20\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
$$

**2. Output capacitance:**

$$
P_{oss}=Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}=30\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.036\ W}
$$
