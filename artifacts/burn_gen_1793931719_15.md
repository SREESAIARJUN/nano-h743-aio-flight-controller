# Job #63: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Architecture and Analysis Package

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Headline Finding

**Partitioning.** An STM32H743 running ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The 48 kHz switching runs on the ESC side. This package therefore assumes the following split:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM consumer |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class | AM32 (or BLHeli_32) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, bidirectional DShot replies |
| Power stage (×4) | 3-phase gate driver + 6 N-FETs | – | Inverter |

If Job #63 intends the H743 to drive the gate drivers directly, that is a custom-firmware project outside ArduPilot. Flag it at design review.

**Headline finding.** On a 20×20 mm nano board, **80 A per channel is not physically achievable, even as a burst.** The model in Sections 1–3 shows that the board reaches its junction limit in roughly 0.3–1 s at 80 A/channel. If "80 A" means **aggregate** (20 A/channel), the design is viable with roughly an 18 s burst capability. Section 3.4 gives both cases. Resolve this with the customer before layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM
At any instant, two phase legs carry the motor current I_ph:
- **PWM on:** high side of phase A plus low side of phase B.
- **PWM off (synchronous freewheel):** low side of phase A plus low side of phase B.

So **exactly two FET channels conduct I_ph at all times**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot \frac{R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}{N_p}$$

N_p is the number of paralleled FETs per switch position.
