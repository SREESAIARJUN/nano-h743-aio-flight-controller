# Job #51: STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC
## Systems Architecture, Loss Model, Bi-directional DShot Allocation, Netlists

---

## 0. Architectural Ground Truth (read first)

Three points shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 design, the H743 sends DShot600 frames. Each motor's bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071, either one per motor or one for all four. The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to that MCU's output stage.

2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is not thermally credible beyond about 1 s.** The math in §3 shows this. I have modelled it rigorously anyway, so you can choose the burst duration, board size and FET from data, not marketing.

3. **Device parameters are assumptions.** No MOSFET part number is fixed for Job #51, so I use a representative 40 V, PDFN3.3×3 trench FET (values in §1). Every equation is parameterized. Substitute your datasheet values and recompute.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 2.2 Six-step with synchronous rectification

During any commutation sector, current flows in at three places:
- the **high-side FET of phase A**, for fraction *D* of each PWM period;
- the **low-side FET of phase A** (synchronous freewheel), for fraction (1−D);
- the **low-side FET of phase B**, continuously.

So the conducting resistance in series with the motor is always two FETs:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Synchronous rectification makes conduction loss **independent of duty cycle**. This is the key simplification.

### 2.3 Copper loss

Add the board copper loss. Bus, phase and return paths for one motor are approximately R_cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ on a 2 oz (70 µm) inner/outer stack at nano trace widths:

$$P_{cu} = I_{ph}^2 R_{cu}$$

### 2.4 Numbers at T_j = 100 °C

- R_ds(100) = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.005 × 75) = **1.65 mΩ**
- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.65 m = **21.1 W per motor**
- P_cu = 80² × 0.4 m = **2.6 W per motor**

Per FET, averaged over an electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries 1/3 of the total: P_FET,cond ≈ 21.1 / 6 × 2 ≈ **3.5 W**. Per-FET RMS sharing is exactly 1/3 of the time for each of the high-side and low-side role.

### 2.5 FOC / sinusoidal variant

If the ESC runs sinusoidal drive with phase RMS current I_rms, the three phases each conduct continuously through one FET at a time:

$$P_{cond} = 3\,I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}$$

For an equivalent torque, a peak of 80 A gives I_rms = 56.6 A, so P ≈ 3 × 3200 × 1.65 m = **15.8 W**.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches. The other active phase is static for the sector.

### 3.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap (one FET turns on, its complement commutates)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 35 ns × 48 k = **1.69 W**

### 3.2 Reverse recovery of the complementary body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

= 40 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.048 W**

### 3.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

= 30 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.036 W**

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead times per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I_{ph}\,(2 t_{dt})\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k = **0.307 W**

### 3.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET)

$$P_{g} = 2\,Q_g\,V_{drv}\,f_{sw}$$

= 2 × 50 n × 10 × 48 k = **0.048 W**

### 3.6 Switching total per motor

**P_sw ≈ 1.69 + 0.05 + 0.04 + 0.31 ≈ 2.09 W.** This applies when D < 1. At 100% throttle, six-step switching losses collapse to commutation-only.

### 3.7 Frequency sensitivity

P_sw scales linearly with f_sw: about 43.5 mW/kHz at this operating point. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1 W per motor, at the cost of current ripple and acoustic noise.

### 4.1 Self-heating iteration (R_ds depends on T_j)

Iterate T_j until P and T converge, using R_θJA,eff for a FET on a small board:

$$P_{FET}(T_j) = \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]}{6} + \frac{P_{sw}}{2}$$

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET} \cdot R_{\theta JC+TIM}$$
