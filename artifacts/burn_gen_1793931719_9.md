# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #64): Architecture & Analysis Package

**Rev A: engineering model and reference design. This is not a fab-release package.**

## 0. Critical Findings

Three findings change the design before any other analysis applies.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In every ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1, each phase bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 only emits DShot600 commands and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. | The partitioning in §3 and §4 assumes this. ArduPilot has no native in-firmware ESC commutation. |
| 2 | **80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is thermally limited to roughly 1 s bursts** (§1.5). Total dissipation is about 90 W into roughly 2.2 J/K of thermal mass. | Either redefine the 80 A rating with a stated duration (recommended: "80 A / 1 s, 45 A / 10 s, 25 A continuous"), or move to a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 5×6 mm FETs. |
| 3 | **Input ripple current at 80 A is about 40 A RMS per motor** (§2.4). | Requires a low-ESR bulk electrolytic of at least 1000 µF on the pigtail plus at least 4×10 µF X7R per motor at the bridge. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Assumed power stage (per phase leg)

| Parameter | Symbol | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S nominal / max) | V_bus | 22.2 / 25.2 V | Use 25.2 V for worst case |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN | n/a | Typical of nano 4-in-1 boards |
| R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ | Datasheet typical |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K | Typical for trench Si |
| Copper/via path per phase | R_Cu | 0.3 mΩ | 2 oz, 6-layer, estimated |
| Phase current (burst) | I_ph | 80 A | Requirement |
| Current ripple ratio | r = ΔI/I | 0.3 | Low-inductance quad motor at 48 kHz |

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.45) = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.3 RMS correction for ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right) = 6400 \times 1.0075 = 6448\ \text{A}^2$$

### 1.4 Per-motor conduction loss (6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM)

In any commutation sector, current flows through two legs:
- The **PWM leg**, whose high-side FET conducts for D and low-side FET for (1 − D) with synchronous rectification.
- The **return leg**, whose low-side FET conducts 100% of the sector.

Over a full PWM period, the current therefore always sees two R_DS in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot (2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu})$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 6448 \times (2 \times 1.45 + 0.3)\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.6\ W}$$

Worst-case single FET (return-leg low side, 100% of sector):

$$P_{FET,max} = 6448 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{9.35\ W}$$

Over a full electrical revolution, each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the time. Its average loss is therefore about 3.1 W. The thermal time constant of a 3×3 die (about 1 to 5 ms) is comparable to the commutation period at low eRPM, so **use the 9.35 W sector value for junction-peak analysis at low speed and high current** (for example, punch-out from hover).
