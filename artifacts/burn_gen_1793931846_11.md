## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

These points change the design, so they come first.

1. **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each motor channel is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which owns the 48 kHz PWM and the back-EMF zero-cross detection. Examples are 4× AT32F421 or 4× STM32G071. This package therefore models two processor domains.
2. **80 A on a "nano" form factor is a burst rating only.** On 20×20 mm it is physically implausible beyond tens of milliseconds. On 30.5×30.5 mm it is achievable for about 1–3 s with heavy copper. Section 3 quantifies this. I recommend specifying **80 A / 1 s burst, 45–55 A continuous, with airflow**.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \;\Rightarrow\; R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.6 = 2.56\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant two phase legs carry current I:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side conducts for fraction D. The complementary low-side (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1−D.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side is held fully on.

The instantaneous channel conduction loss is therefore independent of duty, to first order:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2 I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.56\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{32.8\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET average loss

Each of the 6 FETs is active for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle. The electrical frequency is f_e = p·rpm/120. For a 14-pole motor at 30 krpm, f_e ≈ 3.5 kHz.

Because 1/f_e is much shorter than the die thermal time constant (about 1 ms or more), each die sees the average loss:

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3} = 5.46\ \text{W}$$

The worst case is a full-throttle punch at D → 1. Leg-A high-side and leg-B low-side then each carry the full 120° share.

### 1.4 Coupled electro-thermal solution

R_ds depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss, so the two must be solved together:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + \bar P_{FET}(T_j)\,Z_{th} \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + k\,Z_{th}\,(1-25\alpha)}{1 - k\,Z_{th}\,\alpha}, \quad k = \frac{I^2 R_{25}}{3}$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:** the solution is stable only if k·Z_th·α < 1. At 80 A, k = 3.41 W, so Z_th must stay below 48.8 K/W. Steady-state runaway is therefore not the limit. **Heat removal is the limit.**

### 1.6 Shared bus shunt

The bus current is the sum over the four channels, scaled by duty:

$$I_{bus} \approx \sum D_k I_k$$
