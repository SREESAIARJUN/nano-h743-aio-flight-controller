## 0. Scope and Architectural Correction

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot is flight-control firmware and has no in-tree sensorless BLDC commutation. The correct partition is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master, RPM-based harmonic notch |
| ESC ×4 | STM32G071GBU6 (one per motor) | AM32 | 48 kHz PWM, six-step sensorless commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | — | 10 V gate drive |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V PQFN3.3 N-MOSFETs | — | Half-bridges A/B/C |
