# Job #42: STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst Design Package

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 should be the **flight controller**, not the ESC commutation MCU. ArduPilot does not run sensorless BLDC commutation at 48 kHz for four motors. The correct partition is:

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x Bidirectional DShot600--> [4x AM32/BLHeli_32 MCU (e.g. AT32F421)] --> [4x 3-phase gate driver] --> [24x MOSFET]
```

The math below covers the ESC power stage and the H743 timer/DMA side.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Current path.** With 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** conducting channels:

- During PWM-on: HS(phase A) and LS(phase B).
- During PWM-off: LS(phase A) and LS(phase B).

So conduction loss is essentially independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right] \approx R_{25}\,k_T$$

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each low-side FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle plus its freewheel share. The hottest FET sees approximately:

$$P_{FET,max} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3}\,(1 + (1 - D)) \;\to\; \text{up to } \approx 6.4\ \text{W at low D}$$

**Copper loss.** Add the copper resistance from the pad to the phase/bus (2 oz, roughly 0.2–0.4 mΩ per path):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} \approx 6400 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 1.9\ \text{W per channel}$$
