# STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC Flight Controller (Job #27): Power Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Feasibility flag (read first):** An 80 A per-channel burst rating cannot be thermally supported on a true 20×20 mm "nano" footprint. A 20×20 board has about 4 cm² of copper per side, which leaves room for a few seconds of burst at most, at the edge of reliability. This design therefore targets a **30.5×30.5 mm stack-format ESC (36×36 mm PCB outline) paired with a compact H743 FC**. Section 5.5 gives the 20×20 derating numbers.

**Architectural correction:** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and does not commutate motors. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32) generating the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bi-directional eRPM telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd high, one phase is held low, and one floats. Within a PWM period:

| Interval | Conducting switches |
|---|---|
| Duty fraction D | HS(A) + LS(B) |
| Fraction 1−D (synchronous freewheel) | LS(A) + LS(B) |

The motor current passes through **exactly two channels at every instant**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

This result is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous 6-step. Without synchronous rectification, the (1−D) term becomes diode loss V_F·I instead.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel (80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | 20.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 22.3 W |

This creates **positive electrothermal feedback**. It is solved iteratively in Section 4.4.
