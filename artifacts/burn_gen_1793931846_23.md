# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #16): 80 A Burst Thermal Model, 48 kHz Switching Losses, and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Allocation

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings constrain everything below.

1. **80 A per channel is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The model shows about 90 W of dissipation with all four channels at 80 A. No 20×20 mm board can sink that. On a 30.5×30.5 mm board in prop-wash, the sustainable continuous current is about 25 A/channel. 80 A is a 2–3 s burst.
2. **Form factor.** Twelve DFN5×6 FETs per side cannot fit on 20×20 mm. "Nano" here therefore means the 30.5 mm stack with the FC on a separate 20×20 or 30.5 board. A true 20×20 4-in-1 with DFN3.3 FETs (R_ds about 2–3 mΩ) would be limited to roughly 40 A bursts. Re-run Section 2 with those values if 20×20 is mandatory.
3. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071) generating the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. Sections 1–3 apply to the ESC board and Section 4 to the FC.
4. **Device parameters are representative, not vendor-specific.** They describe the 40 V DFN5×6 class. Substitute exact datasheet values before release. All equations are parameterized so you can do this directly.
