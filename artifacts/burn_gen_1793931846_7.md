# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #36)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Red Flags

This package assumes a **two-board stack**:

- **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot.
- **4-in-1 ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32.

The H743 does not commutate the motors. It only generates bi-directional DShot600 frames and decodes eRPM telemetry. If you intend the H743 to commutate directly, that is a different architecture. Tell me and I'll redo Section 3.

**Red flags to address up front:**

1. **80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 or 25×25 mm) board is a burst-only rating.** It is not continuous. Section 2 quantifies the time limit (about 3 s at 40 °C ambient).
2. **40 V FETs on 6S leave about 37 % margin** before ringing is considered. A layout with tight loops and RC snubbers is mandatory. Otherwise consider 60 V FETs, which have higher R_DS(on).
3. **Bulk capacitor ripple reaches about 40 A_rms** at D = 0.5. An external low-ESR electrolytic on the pigtail is mandatory, not optional.

## 1. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Conduction loss

Assumptions:

- Trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM.
- At every instant, current flows through **exactly two FET channels**: either HS + LS (drive) or LS + LS (sync freewheel).
- I is the **phase current**. Battery current is I_batt ≈ D·I.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.56\ W}$$

The worst-case single FET is the low side that is held fully on for 120° of the electrical cycle:

- Instantaneous: I²·R = 9.28 W.
- Averaged over the electrical cycle: **3.09 W**.
