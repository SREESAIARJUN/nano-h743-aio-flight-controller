**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction model, 48 kHz switching loss model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, schematic netlist, and thermal budget.

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Design Risks

Two problems in the brief need resolving before the math.

**1. The H743 should not commutate the motors.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 firmware, each driving one FD6288Q 3‑phase gate driver. Running four sensorless FOC/6‑step loops on the same core as the EKF and scheduler would break ArduPilot's real‑time guarantees. Everything below assumes this split.

**2. 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm board is a burst rating of about 1 s, not a continuous rating.** Section 3 derives this. The total dissipation reaches about 140 W into a board with roughly 2.5 J/K of heat capacity. I recommend one of these changes:
- move to 30.5×30.5 mm, or
- specify the rating as "80 A for ≤1 s, 45 A continuous with prop wash," or
- run at 24 kHz PWM.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6‑step commutation with synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs in series:
- during PWM on‑time: high side of phase A and low side of phase B;
- during PWM off‑time: low side of phase A (synchronous) and low side of phase B.

So the per‑channel conduction loss does not depend on duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.5 × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per‑channel and total loss at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 4 \times 30.7 = \mathbf{122.9\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per‑device distribution

Each MOSFET conducts for 120° of 360° electrical, which is 1/3 of the commutation cycle. At full throttle (D → 1):

| Device | Average power | Instantaneous power during its interval |
|---|---|---|
| High side | I² R / 3 ≈ 5.1 W | I² R ≈ 15.4 W |
| Low side, at D < 1 | I²R·(1−D)/3 extra (sync‑rect share) | — |

At 80 A burst the high side is the worst case.

### 1.5 Thermal runaway check

R_DS(on) rises with temperature, so conduction loss is self‑reinforcing. The loop is stable when:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{\theta} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; I^2 R_{25}\alpha \cdot 2 \cdot R_{\theta,ch\text{-}amb} < 1$$

At 80 A: 6400 × 1.5 m × 0.006 × 2 = 0.115 W/K. Stability therefore requires R_θ,ch‑amb < 8.7 K/W. With forced prop airflow the board achieves about 3–5 K/W, so the loop is stable. In still air it is marginal.
