# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Power, Thermal and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Architectural Ground Rules

Two corrections apply before the math:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design uses an AT32F421 per channel. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. If the H743 drove the FETs directly, DShot would be unnecessary.
2. **"80A burst" on a 20×20 mm nano board is a seconds-scale transient rating, not a thermal steady state.** The model below quantifies exactly how many seconds.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

R_DS(T_j) = R₂₅·[1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C: R_hot = 1.5 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.18 mΩ** (use 2.1–2.2 mΩ).

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases carry current. One leg is PWM'd (high-side ↔ low-side complementary) and the other has its low-side held on. With synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** channel resistances, regardless of duty D:

P_cond,ch = 2 · I_rms²
