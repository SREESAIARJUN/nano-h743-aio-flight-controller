# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC: Loss, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot600 Architecture

**Document:** Job #13, Rev A (engineering estimate)
**Status:** Analytical model and reference design. It is **not fab-released.** All component parameters are representative assumptions and must be replaced with values from your selected datasheets before layout sign-off.

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings drive every later section.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The H743 cannot drive the bridge directly at a usable resolution or safety level. A 4-in-1 ESC needs **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to each one. | Architecture is H743 FC + 4× ESC MCU + 4× 3-phase gate driver + 24 MOSFETs. |
| F2 | **"Nano" (20×20 mm) with 80 A burst per channel is not physically achievable.** 3.3×3.3 mm FETs at about 2.5 mΩ give about 195 W of conduction loss at 4×80 A (§2.4). | Recommend a **30.5×30.5 mm** board with 5×6 mm FETs. On 20×20 mm, the realistic ceiling is about 35–40 A burst. |
| F3 | 80 A on all four channels at once is a **burst rating of roughly 1–2 s**, not continuous (§4). The continuous capability of a 30×30 board in prop wash is about **40 A per channel** (§4.4). | Firmware current limiting and thermal derating are mandatory. |
| F4 | A single 0.25 mΩ battery shunt at 320 A dissipates **25.6 W**. | Use per-channel low-side shunts, or a Hall sensor on the battery bus. |

The remainder assumes **30.5×30.5 mm, 6S (25.2 V max), 4-layer 2 oz copper, 5×6 mm 40 V FETs**.
