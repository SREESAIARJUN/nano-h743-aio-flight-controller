## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels in series:

- **PWM on:** HS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B)
- **PWM off (sync rect):** LS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B)

So, independent of duty cycle D:

$$R_{path}(T_j) = 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt,share}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R ≈ 1.5 × 1.2 mΩ = **1.8 mΩ**.

Because R depends on T_j, which depends on P, which depends on R, the loss calculation is a fixed-point problem:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_a + Z_{th}(t)\cdot P\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right)$$

This converges in 3–4 iterations. Thermal runaway occurs if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\, R_{th} \ge 1 \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; I^2 \cdot 2 R_{25}\,\alpha \cdot R_{th} \ge 1$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss at the 80 A burst

| Term | Expression | Value per channel |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFETs (hot) | I² · 2 · 1.8 mΩ | 6400 × 3.6 mΩ = **23.0 W** |
| Copper: phase pads + bus, ~0.25 mΩ effective [A] | I² · R_Cu | **1.6 W** |
| **Conduction subtotal** | | **≈ 24.6 W** |

**Per-FET averaging (6-step).** Each leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical period. Each FET therefore carries the current for about 1/3 of the period, split between HS and LS by D and (1−D). The average dissipation per FET is about 23 W / 6 ≈ **3.8 W**. The worst-case instantaneous single-FET dissipation during its conduction window is I²R = **11.5 W**.

### 1.4 RMS correction for ripple

Phase current is not flat DC. With inductor ripple ΔI_pp:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

The ripple is given by:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Example: 25.2 V, D = 0.5, L_ph = 15 µH, 48 kHz gives ΔI_pp ≈ 8.75 A. The correction is then +6.4 A², or 0.1 %, which is negligible at 80 A. It matters at low current with low-inductance motors.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only one leg switches at a time in complementary 6-step: one HS/LS pair, so 2 FETs.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched HS)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

The more accurate gate-plateau form for t_r is useful when choosing gate resistors:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_g + R_{drv})}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.85 \times 80 \times 100\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.33\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

This term also adds turn-on overlap in the HS. Fold it in by increasing the effective t_r if your measured waveforms show it.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC}\times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$
