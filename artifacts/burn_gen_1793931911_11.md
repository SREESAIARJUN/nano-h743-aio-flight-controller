## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Corrections

Several points in the brief need to be pinned down before the math means anything:

| Item | Interpretation used | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" | **The H743 is the flight controller MCU running ArduPilot.** Each ESC channel runs AM32 on a dedicated small MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071). | No production ESC firmware (AM32, BLHeli_32, Bluejay) runs motor commutation on an H743. A dual-role H743 would also break ArduPilot's scheduler guarantees. |
| 80 A | **80 A peak *phase* current per channel, burst ≤ 1–2 s** | The model below proves a 20×20 board cannot sustain this. Continuous rating is derived in §3.5. |
| Supply | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal, ~21 V under burst sag | Drives the 40 V MOSFET choice. |
| 48 kHz | AM32 PWM frequency, complementary (synchronous) 6-step trapezoidal | AM32's maximum standard setting. |
