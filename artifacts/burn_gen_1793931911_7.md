## 0. Architecture Decision and Feasibility Warning

The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage directly. Field-oriented or 6-step commutation for four motors at 48 kHz belongs on dedicated ESC MCUs. The partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | STM32G071GBU6 (or AT32F421K8U7) | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate driver ×4 | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power FETs ×24 | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 5×6, Rds(on) ≤ 1.0 mΩ at 10 V | Inverter legs |
| Battery | Up to 6S (25.2 V max) | 40 V FETs give about 37% margin over the 29 V transient |

**Feasibility warning:** On a true 20×20 mm nano footprint, 80 A per channel is thermally achievable only as a burst of about 1–2 s (derived in §3). A sustained 80 A rating requires a 30.5×30.5 board or larger, external heatsinking, or lower-Rds(on) FETs. The model below quantifies this so the burst rating can be set honestly.

## 2. Mathematical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM. In each 60° electrical sector:

- **Phase A high-side (Q_AH)** switches at duty D.
- **Phase A low-side (Q_AL)** is the complementary synchronous rectifier, conducting for (1−D).
- **Phase B low-side (Q_BL)** is held fully on as the return path.

So at every instant, exactly two FET channels carry I_ph.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

The on-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

Instantaneous conduction power in the commutating leg plus the return leg is:

$$P_{cond} = \underbrace{D\,I^2 R_{ds}}_{Q_{AH}} + \underbrace{(1-D)\,I^2 R_{ds}}_{Q_{AL}} + \underbrace{I^2 R_{ds}}_{Q_{BL}} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

With synchronous rectification, conduction loss is independent of duty cycle. Dead-time intervals are handled separately in §2.4.

**Self-consistent solution at T_j = 100 °C:**

- R_ds = 1.0 × [1 + 0.0045 × 75] = 1.34 mΩ
- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.34×10⁻³ = **17.15 W**

### 2.3 Switching loss (hard-switched edge on Q_AH)

Gate currents during the Miller plateau, with V_drv = 10 V and R_g,total = 5 Ω (driver plus external plus internal):

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_g} = 1.30\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.70\ \text{A}$$

Resulting transition times:

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = 8.5\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}} = 15.7\ \text{ns}$$

V–I overlap loss:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 24.2\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.17\ W}$$

Output capacitance loss:

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss(er)} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.023\ W}$$

Real layouts add 20–40% to switching loss from loop-inductance ringing. Budget **1.5 W**.
