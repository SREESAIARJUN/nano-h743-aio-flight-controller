## 0. Scope and Governing Assumptions

**System partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421, running AM32) perform the 48 kHz complementary-PWM commutation. This split is mandatory. The H7 cannot run real-time six-step control for four motors while also running EKF3.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in six-step with synchronous rectification

In each of the six sectors, two phases carry current:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high side conducts for D·T_sw. The low side synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low side is on for 100 % of the sector.

Current therefore always flows through exactly **two** MOSFET channels in series, independent of duty cycle. Dead-time diode conduction is handled separately in §2.4.

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

Peak-to-peak phase current ripple:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}}$$

The worst case is at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.56\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

The resulting RMS current is:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple adds only 0.06 %, so it is negligible at 48 kHz. That is one argument for 48 kHz over 24 kHz on low-inductance motors.

### 1.3 Channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{hot} = 2 \times 6403.6 \times 1.28\,m\Omega = \mathbf{16.39\ W}$$

Each of the six FETs carries current for roughly 1/3 of the electrical period, so the average per FET is:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{16.39}{6} = 2.73\ \text{W}$$
