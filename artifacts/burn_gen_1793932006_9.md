# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Power-Stage, Thermal and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Warning

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. Production AIO designs, and ArduPilot's supported model, use:

- **FC MCU:** STM32H743 at 480 MHz running ArduPilot. It emits bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC MCUs:** 4 × AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 firmware.
- **Each ESC channel:** a 3-phase gate driver plus 6 N-MOSFETs in a 3-phase bridge, at 48 kHz complementary PWM.

The rest of this document assumes that split.

**Feasibility warning.** An 80 A per-channel burst on a 20×20 mm nano board is thermally a transient-only rating. The math below shows the board can sustain it for only about 2–3 s from a warm start, and steady state is physically impossible. I recommend specifying the board as **"80 A / 2 s burst, 35–40 A continuous with airflow"** or moving to a 25.5/30.5 mm format.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current path

For 6-step trapezoidal or AM32 sinusoidal-like drive with synchronous rectification, two legs carry phase current at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D, the low-side for (1−D). Exactly one FET is on at all times.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on continuously.

So exactly two FETs carry I_ph at every instant, independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The R_DS(on) temperature dependence is:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Phase inductance L_ph (assume 10 µH for a 5–7" class motor) adds triangular ripple. At worst case D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\, D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{10\times10^{-6}\times 48\times10^{3}} = 13.1\,\text{A}_{pp}
$$

The RMS current including ripple is:

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}} = \sqrt{6400 + 14.3} = 80.09\,\text{A}
$$

The ripple contribution is negligible.

### 1.3 Numbers

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61.5 W |
| 100 °C | 1.65 mΩ | 21.1 W | 84.5 W |
| 125 °C | 1.80 mΩ | 23.0 W | 92.2 W |

Each FET conducts on average about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, giving about 3.8 W average per FET at 125 °C. The peak instantaneous loss is I²R = 11.5 W while that FET carries current.

**Electrothermal stability check.** Thermal runaway occurs if:

$$
\frac{dP}{dT} \cdot R_\theta > 1
$$

Here dP/dT = 2·I²·R₂₅·α = 0.077 W/K. The criterion therefore needs R_θ(board→air) < 13 K/W. Section 4 shows the board sits at about 15.6 K/W, so the design is marginally unstable at sustained 80 A. That is a second, independent reason the rating must be burst-only.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

There is one hard-switched commutation pair per PWM period per channel (the PWM leg).

**Hard-switching V–I overlap:**

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus}\, I\, (t_r+t_f)\, f_{sw} = \tfrac12 (25.2)(80)(40\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = 1.94\,\text{W}
$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.048\,\text{W}
$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead times per period):

$$
P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.61\,\text{W}
$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.06\,\text{W}
$$

This term is drawn from the driver/bus, not dissipated in the FET.

**Gate drive:**

$$
P_g = 2\, Q_g\, V_{GS}\, f_{sw} = 0.048\,\text{W}
$$

This is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors.

|---|---|---|
| Overlap | 1.94 W | 7.74 W |
| Dead time | 0.61 W | 2.46 W |
| Q_oss + Q_rr + gate | 0.16 W |
