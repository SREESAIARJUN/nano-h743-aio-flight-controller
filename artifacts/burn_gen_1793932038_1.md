# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #41): Power-Stage, Thermal and Bidirectional DShot600 Model

## 0. Scope, Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits four bidirectional DShot600 streams. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421-class running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class), which generates the 48 kHz PWM and drives a 3-phase gate driver and six switch positions.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification and complementary PWM:

- At every instant, current flows through exactly **two switch positions**.
- One position is in the PWM leg: the high-side during the on-time, the low-side (synchronous) during the off-time.
- The other position is the low-side of the return leg.

Therefore, regardless of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{sw}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Resistance of one switch position

The resistance of one switch position (n paralleled FETs) is:

$$R_{sw}(T_j) = \frac{R_{ds(on),25}}{n}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

With n = 2 and T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{sw} = 0.75\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.425 = 1.069\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 RMS current including PWM ripple

Phase current ripple is:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf}) D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

$$I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \Delta I_{pp}^2/12}$$

For typical 5-inch-class motors (L_ph ≈ 15–30 µH line-to-line), ΔI_pp is below 10 A. The ripple term is then below 0.2 % and can be neglected.

### 1.4 Result

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.069\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.7\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Thermal coupling.** P_cond depends on T_j, so solve iteratively: T_j → R → P → T_j. Three iterations converge to within 1 %. Starting at 25 °C underestimates loss by about 30 %.
