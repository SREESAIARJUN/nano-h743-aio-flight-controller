# STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, BDShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

This package is engineered from first principles, and some parameters below are assumed. Before layout freeze, replace them with the datasheet values of your selected MOSFET, gate driver, and stack-up. Section 4 contains a key conclusion that should drive the program: **80 A on a nano-class board is a seconds-scale burst capability, not a continuous rating.**

---

## 0. Architecture Decision (Must Be Fixed First)

The STM32H743 must **not** commutate the bridges directly. Four sensorless 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary PWM pairs with hardware dead-time. The H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1, TIM8) plus the HRTIM, so direct drive of four bridges is not viable alongside flight control.

**Adopted topology:**

```
6S LiPo ──► VBAT bus ──┬──► 4× [AT32F421 (AM32) → FD6288Q gate driver → 6× N-MOSFET] ──► Motors 1-4
                       │          ▲ DShot600 bidirectional (single wire, 3.3 V, half-duplex)
                       │          │
                       └──► Buck 5V/10V ──► STM32H743 FC (400 MHz) ── TIM3 CH1-4 + DMA
```

- **ESC switching frequency:** 48 kHz (AM32 supports 24/48/96 kHz). 48 kHz is a deliberate trade: it gives lower current ripple on low-inductance motors at the cost of switching loss, which is quantified below.
- **Commutation:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary PWM (synchronous rectification).

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, exactly two phases carry current at any instant: phase A is PWM'd high and phase B is held low.

- During the on-time D·T, current flows through HS_A and LS_B.
- During the off-time (1−D)·T, current flows through LS_A (synchronous rectifier) and LS_B.

Each instant therefore has **two R_DS(on) in series**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad k \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1} \;\Rightarrow\; \alpha(125\,°\text{C}) = 1.5$$

### 2.2 Phase current vs. battery current

$$I_{batt} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

The "80 A" rating is taken as **phase current**, which is the conservative interpretation for the FETs. At full throttle (D → 1) the two are equal.

### 2.3 Numbers at I_ph = 80 A

| Condition | R_DS(on) | P_FET per channel = I²·2R |
|---|---|---|
| T_j = 25 °C | 1.2 mΩ | 6400 × 2.4 mΩ = **15.4 W** |
| T_j = 125 °C | 1.8 mΩ | 6400 × 3.6 mΩ = **23.0 W** |

**Copper term.** Assume 0.3 mΩ of effective phase path per channel: the bus and phase pours plus motor pads.

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.9\ \text{W}$$

**Conduction total per channel (hot):** ≈ 24.9 W.
