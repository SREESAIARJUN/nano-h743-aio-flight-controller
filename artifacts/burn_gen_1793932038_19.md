# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Nano): Design Model, Job #20

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 (for example, AT32F421), which generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 does not drive the gates directly. The DShot math applies to the H743, and the 48 kHz PWM math applies to the ESC MCUs.

**MOSFET parameters are placeholders.** No part number was specified, so I use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values into the formulas before signing anything off.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S full) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I | 80 A |
| R_DS(on) @25 °C (max) | R₂₅ | 1.3 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temp coefficient | α | ≈0.006
