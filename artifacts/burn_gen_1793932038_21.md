# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Loss, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design Model

## 0. Architecture Constraints

These constraints shape every number in this document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own small MCU (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32) driving a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 running ArduPilot sends bidirectional DShot600 to those four MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **"Nano" form factor and 80A burst conflict thermally.** Section 3 shows that 4×80A dissipates roughly 140 W. A 20×20 board survives that for about 1 second; a 30×30 board for about 2–3 seconds. Treat 80A as a ≤1–2 s transient rating with a continuous rating near 30A per channel. Marketing an "80A" nano stack without that qualifier would be inaccurate.
3. **Component values are representative, not datasheet-verified.** The MOSFET and driver parameters below are generic 40V DFN3.3 class values. Substitute your selected part's datasheet numbers into the equations before sign-off.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

With 6-step synchronous PWM, the motor current always flows through **two** switch positions in series: a high-side FET plus a low-side FET or synchronous rectifier. Conduction loss is therefore essentially independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{hot} = 80^2 \cdot 2(2.1\,\text{m}\Omega) = \mathbf{26.9\ W}$$

Copper and shunt losses add to this:

$$P_{pcb} = I^2 R_{pcb} = 6.4\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{sh} = I^2 R_{sh} = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

**Per-FET loss.** Each high-side position conducts 120° out of every 360° electrical. The average loss per FET is:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx I^2 R_{hot}/3 = 4.5\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous loss per conducting FET is:

$$P_{FET,inst} = 13.4\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Leg, 48 kHz)

**Gate currents during the plateau:**

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = 1.4\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.6\ \text{A}$$

**Switching charge:**

$$Q_{sw} = Q_{gd} + \tfrac{1}{2}Q_{gs} = 13\ \text{nC}$$

**Transition times:**

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 9.3\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 21.7\ \text{ns}$$
