## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two points in the brief need correcting first, because they change the math.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 flight controller sends bidirectional DShot600 to **four ESC MCUs**, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.
2. **The 48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC firmware setting** (the AM32 PWM frequency). It is independent of DShot and ArduPilot.
