# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design Package

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

These items are not in the job brief, so I have fixed them explicitly. Every equation below is parametric, so you can substitute real datasheet values when the BOM is frozen.

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does **not** switch the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each of the four motor channels has its own ESC MCU running AM32, which performs the 48 kHz complementary PWM. Putting 6-step commutation for 4 motors on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not viable: timer, DMA and latency budgets all fail. Section 3 therefore covers the H743 side, and Sections 1–2 cover the ESC side.

### 0.2 Feasibility of the "80 A burst on a nano" requirement

- 4 × 80 A means **320 A** of battery current.
- The numbers below show this is only achievable as a **short burst (≈1–2 s)** on a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board.
- It is **not** achievable on a 20 × 20 mm board without exceeding Tj(max) or delaminating the copper.
- Continuous rating should be specified separately (≈ 35–45 A per channel, derived in §2.5).
