# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #23)

## 0. Scope and Feasibility Constraints

These four points shape every number that follows.

1. **Architecture.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. A production 4-in-1 ESC runs one small MCU per motor (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The **48 kHz PWM** is generated by those ESC MCUs. The **H743 (ArduPilot)** generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry.
2. **Form factor.** An 80 A burst is **not thermally viable on a 20×20 mm "nano" board** (Section 3.4). This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** stack-up. If 20×20 mm is a hard requirement, derate to roughly 40–45 A burst.
3. **Assumed operating point:** 6S (25.2 V max), 40 V MOSFETs, T_amb = 40 °C, T_j,max = 150 °C with a design limit of 125 °C.
4. **Reference only.** All component values are representative. Substitute the parameters from your actual MOSFET datasheet and re-run the equations. The netlist is a reference topology, not a DRC- or ERC-verified production file.

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

Assume 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous complementary PWM at duty D. At any instant, two legs conduct:

- The **high-side** of the active leg conducts for a fraction D of each period.
- Its **synchronous low-side** conducts for the remaining (1−D).
- The **return leg's low-side** conducts 100% of the time.

The current therefore always passes through exactly **two R_ds(on) elements**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right],\ \alpha\approx0.005\,\text{K}^{-1}$$

At 80 A with T_j = 125 °C:

$$P_{cond}=2(80)^2(1.5\,\text{m}\Omega)=\mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

**Copper term.** Add the phase and bus copper resistance R_cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ (2 oz copper, about 15 mm path, paralleled across layers):

$$P_{cu}=I^2R_{cu}=1.9\ \text{W}$$

**Per-FET split (needed for the junction calculation):**

- Hottest FET: P_HS = D·I²·R_ds. At D → 1 (full throttle burst), this reaches **9.6 W**.
- Return low-side: always **9.6 W**.

**Electrothermal coupling.** Because R_ds rises with T_j, solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,I^2R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

This converges only if:

$$R_\theta I^2 R_{ds,25}\alpha < 1$$

Here that product is 1.5 × 6400 × 1e-3 × 0.005 = 0.048, so it converges and thermal runaway is not a risk at the FET level.

### 2.2 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg is hard-switched per PWM period: the high-side turns on against the low-side's body diode or channel.

**(a) V-I overlap:**

$$P_{ov}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{n})(48\text{k})=\mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr}=V_{bus}Q_{rr}f_{sw}=25.2\cdot50\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=0.060\ W$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss}=\tfrac12 C_{oss(er)}V_{bus}^2f_{sw}=0.5\cdot1.5\text{n}\cdot635\cdot48\text{k}=0.023\ W$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt}=2\,V_{SD}\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw}=2(0.8)(80)(100\text{n})(48\text{k})=\mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive (two FETs switching):**

$$P_g=2\,Q_gV_{drv}f_{sw}=2(60\text{n})(10)(48\text{k})=0.058\ W$$

This loss is dissipated mostly in the gate resistors and driver, not the FET.

**Switching total:** P_sw ≈ **2.69 W per motor**.

### 2.3 Fixed Losses

| Item | Loss |
|---|---|
| Bulk/MLCC ESR | I_rms,cap ≈ I·√(D(1−D)), maximum 40 A at D = 0.5. With ESR_eff = 1 mΩ (low-ESR polymer plus MLCC bank), P ≈ 1.6 W, shared |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, per ESC) | 1.6 W per motor at 80 A |
| Logic | ESC MCUs, drivers, LDOs, H743 (~0.5 W), BEC: ≈ 2 W total |

### 2.4 Loss Budget at 80 A Burst

| Term | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Copper | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| Switching (all terms) | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| Shunt | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Caps + logic | — | ~3.6 W |
| **Total** | **~25.4 W** | **~105 W** |

**Efficiency:** 4 × 80 A × ~20 V average ≈ 6.4 kW delivered, so η ≈ 98.4%. The efficiency is fine; **heat density is the real problem.**

**General scaling law (per motor, hot):**

$$P(I)\approx \underbrace{3.4\text{e-}3}_{2R_{ds}+R_{cu}+R_{sh}}\,I^2 \;+\; \underbrace{0.032}_{\text{overlap+dt}}\,I \;+\; 0.15$$
