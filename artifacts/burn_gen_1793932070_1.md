## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any commutation sector, two legs are active:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is held on for the full sector.

The phase current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly **two** channels in series:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**. Because R_ds rises with temperature, the model must be iterated to convergence (Section 3.4).
