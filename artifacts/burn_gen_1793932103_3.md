## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

These points affect every number below, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 AIO, each ESC channel (or a shared dual-channel part) runs its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz six-step PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs. Section 3 covers the H743 side; Sections 1–2 cover the power stage driven by the ESC MCUs.

2. **"80 A burst" on a nano form factor is at or beyond the physical limit.** By nano I mean a 20×20 mm hole pattern, about 25–30 mm board.
   - Commercial 20×20 4-in-1s are typically rated 35–60 A burst.
   - At 80 A per channel × 4 channels × 90% duty, battery current is about **288 A** (about 7.2 kW at 6S). That exceeds any XT60/XT90 lead, any nano bus-capacitor bank, and any realistic copper cross-section on a 25 mm board
