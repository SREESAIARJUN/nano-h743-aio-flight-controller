## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle (with encoder offset θ_off found during alignment):

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design (pole-zero cancellation)

The dq voltage equations are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

With decoupling feedforward $v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q$ and $v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$, each axis reduces to $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$.

Choose $C(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{cc},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{cc}$$

The open loop then becomes $\omega_{cc}/s$, and the closed loop is first-order with bandwidth $\omega_{cc}$.
