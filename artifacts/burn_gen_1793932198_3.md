## 0. Architectural Assumptions

A "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" is two subsystems. The H743 cannot directly commutate four motors at 48 kHz with BEMF sensing while also running ArduPilot. This specification therefore assumes the following partitioning.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz, ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC MCUs | 4 × AT32
