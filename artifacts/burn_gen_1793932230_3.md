## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

Park transform, using electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d\dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q\dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque (surface PM, so L_d ≈ L_q):
$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI Design by Pole-Zero Cancellation

Plant per axis: G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

Choose the controller C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with:
$$
K_p = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

This makes the open loop ω_ci/s, so the closed loop is first-order with bandwidth ω_ci.

Bandwidth rule: ω_ci ≤ 2π f_i / 20 to keep the PWM and computation delay (≈1.5 T_s) phase loss below about 15°. At 20 kHz this gives ω_ci ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

### 1.4 Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

### 1.5 Voltage Limiting and SVPWM

Circle limit:
$$
|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3
$$
When limiting, the d-axis has priority. The integrators use back-calculation anti-windup (§2.4).

SVPWM is implemented as min-max zero-sequence injection:
$$
v_{0} = -\tfrac12(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle and Velocity Control

### 2.1 Structure

The cascade runs from the outer loop inward:

1. **Angle loop** (1 kHz), P or PI: produces ω*.
2. **Velocity loop** (4 kHz), PI: produces τ*. Velocity feedback is the IMU gyro rate mapped to the joint, blended with the encoder.
3. **Torque path:** τ* is converted to i_q* = τ*/K_t, then the anti-cogging and acceleration feedforward terms are added.

Combined current command:
$$
i_q^* = \frac{1}{K_t}\Big[\underbrace{\text{PI}_\omega(\omega^*-\hat\omega)}_{\text{feedback}} + \underbrace{J\dot\omega_{ref} + B\omega_{ref}}_{\text{inverse dynamics FF}} \Big] - \underbrace{\hat\tau_{cog}(\theta_m)/K_t}_{\text{anti-cogging}}
$$
