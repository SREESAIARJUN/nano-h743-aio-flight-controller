# Job #32: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80A Burst)
## Design Model: Losses, Thermals, Bidirectional DShot600 Timers, and Reference Netlists

---

## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

**Partitioning.** ArduPilot runs only on the STM32H743 FC. It does not commutate motors. The 4-in-1 ESC needs its own motor-control MCUs: 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or 1× EFM8/AT32 per channel running BLHeli_32/Bluejay. The FC sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM and all power-stage losses belong to the ESC side.

**Findings that change the design:**

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 80A/motor on a 20×20 board dissipates about **110–115 W total**. | The burst is limited to **about 1.5–2 s** from 40°C ambient. |
| 2 | Steady-state convective capacity of a 20×20 stack is only **about 6–15 W**. | Continuous rating is **about 25–35 A/motor**, not 80A. |
| 3 | Conduction loss dominates switching loss by about 10× at 80A. | Spend design effort on R_DS(on) and copper, not on faster edges. |
| 4 | Bidirectional DShot works cleanly on the H743 because DMAMUX allows any stream mapping. | Put all 4 motors on one timer (TIM3) with BIDIR on CH1/CH3. |

**Recommendation:** Either market this as "80A burst (≤1.5 s) / 30A continuous," or move to a 30×30 layout. A 30×30 board has about 2.25× the area and copper, which gives roughly 3–4 s burst and about 45A continuous.

## 2. Conduction-Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path with synchronous six-step rectification
During each 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd and phase B is held low:

- **ON interval (D):** current flows I → HS_A → motor → LS_B. Two FETs are in the path.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_A (sync-rect) → motor → LS_B. Two FETs are in the path.

So two FETs carry I² at all times, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{cu}$$

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A
$$P_{FET,cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.74\text{ m}\Omega = 22.3\text{ W}$$
$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.5\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\text{ W}$$
$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 25.5\text{ W}}$$

### 2.4 Electro-thermal fixed point
Because R depends on T_j, iterate:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{case} + P_{FET}(T_j^{(k)})\,Z_{th,JC}$$

With a FET carrying 80 A continuously during its sector:
- P_FET = I²R = 6400 × 1.74 mΩ = 11.1 W
- Junction-to-case rise ≈ 11.1 × 1.5 = **+17 K**

The loop converges (17 K raises R by about 8%). The model is stable, so there is no thermal-runaway risk at the FET level. Runaway risk is at the board level (§4).

## 3. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Gate-drive currents and transition times
$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{g}} = \frac{7}{5} = 1.4\text{ A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.6\text{ A}$$

$$t_r = \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{12\text{ nC}}{1.4} \approx 8.6\text{ ns} \;\rightarrow\; \text{use } 15\text{ ns (layout/ringing derate)}$$

$$t_f = \frac{12\text{ nC}}{0.6} = 20\text{ ns} \;\rightarrow\; \text{use } 25\text{ ns}$$

### 3.2 Loss components per motor
Only the PWM'd high-side is hard-switched. The sync-rect low-side switches at near-zero V_DS after dead time.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | $\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$ | 0.5·25.2·80·40n·48k = **1.94 W** |
