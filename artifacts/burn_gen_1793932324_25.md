## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

**Topology.** ArduPilot on the STM32H743 does not commutate motors directly. The working architecture is:

- **H743 flight controller:** sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry back over bidirectional DShot.
- **Four independent ESC MCUs:** AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, or the BLHeli_32 equivalent. Each runs 6-step sensorless commutation at 48 kHz PWM.
- **Gate drivers:** four FD6288Q 3-phase drivers, each driving 6 N-channel MOSFETs (24 FETs total).

**Feasibility flag: read this before committing to the form factor.**

- "80 A burst" on a nano-class board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting) cannot be sustained.
- The model below shows about 54 W of total dissipation at 4×80 A. That load reaches the junction limit in roughly 4–5 s, even with prop-wash cooling.
- I model **80 A per channel, ≤ 2 s burst** and derive the true limits. A 30.5×30.5 mm board with 5×6 mm FETs is the minimum credible platform for this rating.

**Parameter caveat.** All MOSFET parameters are design-target assumptions and are stated explicitly. Re-run the model with the datasheet values of the part you actually select.
