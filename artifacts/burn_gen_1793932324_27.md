## 0. Scope, Architecture & Feasibility

### 0.1 Architecture

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production-viable design, the stack is split as follows:

| Board | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight Controller (20×20 or 30×30) | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM consumer (harmonic notch) |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or GD32E230/STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, DShot slave, eRPM telemetry |

Driving 4×3 half-bridges plus BEMF comparators from the H743 would consume most of its advanced timers and break the ESC firmware ecosystem. This document models both boards and the interface between them.

### 0.2 The 80A Rating

The math below shows that **80A per motor on a 20×20 mm board is a sub-second burst rating at best.** A sustained 80A per motor is thermally impossible in this footprint without a heatsink.

I derive the actual limits rather than repeat a marketing number.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current Paths in 6-Step with Complementary PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, one leg is PWM'd, one leg's low side is held ON, and one leg floats.

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T_sw; the synchronous low side freewheels for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the low side carries I_ph for the full sector.

Summing the conduction losses per motor channel:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

**Key result:** conduction loss is independent of duty when synchronous rectification is used. Throttle level does not save you at a given phase current.

### 2.2 Adding Copper and Shunt

$$
P_{cond,total} = I_{ph}^2\left[2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu,path}\right] + I_{bus}^2 R_{shunt}
$$

- **Copper path:** with 2 oz copper (≈0.25 mΩ/□ at 25 °C), a realistic phase-loop path of ~2 squares gives R_Cu,path ≈ 0.5 mΩ. This also carries a +0.39%/K temperature coefficient.
- **Shunt:** a single 0.25 mΩ shunt is shared by all four channels.
- **Bus current:** I_bus = Σ D_k · I_ph,k.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80A Burst

| T_j | R_DS | 2·I²·R_DS | Cu path (I²·0.5 mΩ) | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 3.2 W | 18.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 22.3 W | ~4.1 W | 26.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.92 mΩ | 24.6 W | ~4.4 W | 29.0 W |

**Shunt loss (all four motors at 80A, D = 0.9):**

- I_bus ≈ 288 A
- P_shunt ≈ 20.7 W

That shunt loss is serious. Use a 0.15–0.2 mΩ shunt, or switch to per-phase low-side sensing.
