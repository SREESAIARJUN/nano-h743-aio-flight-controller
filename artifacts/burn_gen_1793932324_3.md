## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Finding

**The H743 is not the ESC MCU.** It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. The 4-in-1 ESC needs four motor-control MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM and return eRPM telemetry. The model below covers both sides of that link.

**80 A burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not achievable.** The physics in §2–3 shows:

| Constraint | 20×20 board | 30.5×30.5 board |
|---|---|---|
| Space for 24 power FETs | Only 3.3×3.3 DFN fits (≈1.5–2 mΩ) | 5×6 DFN fits (≈0.7–1.0 mΩ) |
| 4-motor loss at 80 A | ≈160–200 W | ≈90–100 W |
| Time to T_j = 125 °C with all 4 motors at 80 A | ≈0.4 s | ≈1.5 s |
| Realistic burst rating | 40–45 A / 10 s | 60–65 A / 10 s, 80 A / ~1 s |

**Recommendation:** pick one of two options.
- Re-scope the board to 30.5×30.5 mm and rate it "80 A / 1 s, 60 A / 10 s".
- Keep 20×20 mm and rate it "45 A burst".

Section 3 derives these numbers.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In six-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two FETs are always in the current path:
- One FET in the PWM leg: the high side for duty D, the low side for 1 − D.
- The static low-side FET of the return phase.

The duty cycle therefore cancels out of the total:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The on-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 0.9 × 1.45 = **1.305 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.305\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{16.7\ W}$$

The per-FET loss is not evenly shared. The static low-side FET carries the full 8.35 W. The busiest FET over one electrical cycle averages about 8.35 × (2/3) ≈ 5.6 W, but the instantaneous peak is 8.35 W. Use 8.35 W for junction calculations.

Copper loss in the PCB path:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 2.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

**Gate currents during the Miller plateau.**
- Turn-on: I_g,on ≈ (10 − 3.0) / (R_drv,on + R_g,ext + R_g,int) ≈ 7 / 5 Ω → 1.4 A. The driver limits this to **≈1.0 A**.
- Turn-off: I_g,off ≈ 3.0 / (R_drv,off + R_g,ext + R_g,int) ≈ 3.0 / 3.5 Ω ≈ **0.86 A**.

**Transition times.**
- t_r = (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) / I_g,on = 19 nC / 1.0 A = 19 ns
- t_f = 19 nC / 0.86 A = 22 ns

Layout inductance and ringing typically stretch these by about 1.5×, giving **t_r + t_f ≈ 62 ns**.
