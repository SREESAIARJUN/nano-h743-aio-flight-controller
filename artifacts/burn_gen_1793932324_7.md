**Critical clarifications:**

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** It generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU (or a dedicated multi-motor controller). The H743's job in this design is limited to timer and DMA allocation.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano/30.5 mm board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The math in §3 shows the continuous capability is roughly 15–20 A per channel. Marketing an 80 A figure without a time limit would be misleading.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step synchronous PWM, exactly two phase legs carry current at any instant:

- **PWM ON:** current flows through the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- **PWM OFF (synchronous rectification):** current flows through the low-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.

So **two FET channels are always in series with the motor current**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Results at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction (T_j = 125 °C) | 2 · 80² · 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** |
| FET conduction (T_j = 25 °C) | 2 · 80² · 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| PCB copper path (≈0.5 mΩ total, phase pad → bus) | 80² · 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |

### 1.4 Electrothermal fixed point

Junction temperature and R_ds(on) depend on each other, so solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_a + P\!\left(R(T_j^{(n)})\right) \cdot Z_{th}(t)$$

This loop converges only if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\, Z_{th} = 2 I^2 R_{25}\, \alpha\, Z_{th} < 1$$

At 80 A that gives 2 · 6400 · 1e-3 · 0.006 · Z_th = 0.077 · Z_th. **Thermal runaway occurs if the effective Z_th exceeds about 13 K/W.** This is safe for a burst, but sets a limit on how poor the steady-state cooling can be.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg is PWM-switching per commutation step. Use t_r + t_f = 40 ns (gate driver ≈2 A, R_g ≈ 2.2 Ω), dead time t_dt = 100 ns, and diode forward voltage V_f = 0.8 V.

| Loss mechanism | Formula | Value at 25.2 V, 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5·25.2·80·40e-9·48e3 = **1.94 W** |
| Body-diode reverse recovery | V · Q_rr · f_sw | 25.2·40e-9·48e3 = **0.048 W** |
| C_oss energy | ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw | 0.5·1.5e-9·635·48e3 = **0.023 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8·80·200e-9·48e3 = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | Q_g · V_gs · f_sw · 2 | 40e-9·10·48e3·2 = **0.038 W** |

**Design levers:**

- **Switching frequency:** f_sw scales the switching terms linearly. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per channel at burst, but raises motor current ripple.
- **Dead time:** dead-time loss matters at this current. Tune it to the minimum safe value (50–80 ns) using a measured shoot-through margin.

## 3. Total Losses and Thermal Calculations

### 3.1 Loss budget per channel at the 80 A burst

| Component | W |
|---|---|
| FET conduction (125 °C) | 20.5 |
| Copper | 3.2 |
| Switching + Q_rr + C_oss + dead time | 2.6 |
| **Total per ESC channel** | **≈ 26.3 W** |
| **4 channels simultaneously** | **≈ 105 W** |

Efficiency check: P_out ≈ 22.2 V × I_bus. At 80 A phase current, η is roughly 93–96%, depending on duty cycle.

### 3.2 Per-FET distribution

Averaged over one electrical revolution, the 21 W of conduction loss spreads across 6 FETs, giving about 3.4 W per FET. Switching loss adds about 0.4 W per FET, for **≈3.9 W average per FET**.

Instantaneous dissipation in a conducting FET is about 10 W. Over one commutation step (around 100 µs at 100k eRPM), the die-level Z_th is about 0.1 K/W, so the ripple is only about 1 K.
