## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. The standard topology, used here, is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) --DShot600 bidir x4--> 4x ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 fw)
                                                │
                                         4x FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver
                                                │
                                         4x 6 N-MOSFET half-bridges
```

The ESC MCUs produce the 48 kHz PWM. AM32 supports a configurable PWM frequency.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths (trapezoidal, complementary PWM)

During each 60° commutation sector, two legs are active:

- One leg is PWM-ed: the HS FET conducts for D, and the LS FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1−D.
- One leg holds its LS FET on continuously.

Exactly **two FETs carry I_ph at every instant**, regardless of D (ignoring dead time). The conduction loss per ESC is therefore:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 2.18\,\text{m}\Omega$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 6400 \times 2.18\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{27.9\,W}$$
