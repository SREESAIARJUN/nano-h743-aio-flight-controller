# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture Package (Job #76)

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

These points change the design, so they come first.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel needs its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which provides the 48 kHz PWM and the bidirectional DShot response. | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32-compatible) on the ESC board. The H743 runs ArduPilot. |
| A2 | "80A burst" on a nano board (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm) cannot be sustained thermally. Section 3 shows roughly 110 W of board dissipation into about 3 J/K of thermal mass. | 80 A is defined as a **≤1.5 s burst**, with 40–45 A continuous. If you need 80 A for more than 2 s, move to 30.5×30.5 mm. |
| A3 | Bus voltage was not specified. | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V maximum. 40 V MOSFETs are used, which requires a snubber and tight layout (see §5). |
| A4 | No MOSFET was specified. | Parametric model below. The worked numbers use a representative 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3-class FET. Substitute your datasheet values. |
