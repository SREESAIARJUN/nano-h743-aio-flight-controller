**Scope.** This package covers the electrothermal loss model, the transient thermal model, the ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary PWM (synchronous rectification). Consider one 60° electrical sector in which phase A is PWM'd at duty D and phase B is the low-side return:

| Interval | Conducting devices | Fraction of sector |
|---|---|---|
| PWM on | Q<sub>AH</sub> + Q<sub>BL</sub> | D |
| PWM off | Q<sub>AL</sub> (sync rect) + Q<sub>BL</sub> | 1 − D |

So exactly two R<sub>DS(on)</sub> are always in series with the motor current I:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Per-device allocation over a full electrical cycle

Each device is active in 2 of 6 sectors: once as the "PWM leg" and once as the "return leg".

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}, \qquad
P_{LS} = \frac{1}{3}I^2R_{DS} + \frac{1-D}{3}I^2R_{DS} = \frac{2-D}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}$$

The low-side FET is always the hotter conduction device.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}\ ⚠$$

Because R<sub>DS</sub> rises with temperature, the loss and the temperature must be solved together. Iterate until T<sub>j</sub> converges; this typically takes 2–3 passes. Here the analysis is seeded at T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C:

- R<sub>DS</sub> = 0.75 mΩ × 1.3825 = **1.037 mΩ**
- I²R<sub>DS</sub> = 6400 × 1.037×10⁻³ = **6.64 W**

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, D = 0.95 (full-throttle burst)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P<sub>cond,ch</sub> | 13.27 W |
| P<sub>HS</sub> per FET | 6.64 × 0.95/3 = **2.10 W** |
| P<sub>LS</sub> per FET | 6.64 × 1.05/3 = **2.32 W** |
