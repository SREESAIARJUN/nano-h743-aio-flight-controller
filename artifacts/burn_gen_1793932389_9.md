**Job #57 · Rev A (analytical baseline)**

> **Scope and assumptions.** On a 4-in-1 AIO board, the STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. It does not drive the MOSFETs directly. The ESC side uses four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. This document models:

> 1. the power stage (conduction and switching losses),

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly two phases carry current at any moment:

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high-side switch Q_H for time D·T_sw and through the complementary low-side switch for (1−D)·T_sw. Either way, one device is always in the path.
- **Return leg:** the low-side switch is on continuously during that 60° sector.

So **two R_DS(on) are always in series** with the motor current, whatever the duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS,hot} + I_{rms}^2\,R_{sh} + I_{rms}^2\,R_{Cu}
$$

### 2.2 RMS including ripple

The PWM ripple seen by the phase current is

$$
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

The worst case is D = 0.5. For a typical 5″-class motor, L_ph is about 15 µH line-to-line:

$$
\Delta I \approx \frac{12.6 \cdot 0.5}{15\,\mu\text{H} \cdot 48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 8.75\ \text{A}_{pp}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}} = \sqrt{6400 + 6.4} \approx 80.04\ \text{A}
$$
