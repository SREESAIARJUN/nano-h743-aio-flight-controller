# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #6 specification, so this design states its hardware assumptions explicitly. Every parameter is in a config struct, so you can change them without touching the algorithms.

**Check before deploying:**
1. **Register values.** Confirm all ICM-42688-P register addresses and AAF table values against your datasheet revision. Rev 1.6 was used here.
2. **Initial test.** Do the first spin with current limits at about 30% and the anti-cogging learning disabled.

---

## 0. Assumed System Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 4 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) per axis |
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7 class with FPU, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| Current sensing | 2 or 3 low-side shunts, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz
