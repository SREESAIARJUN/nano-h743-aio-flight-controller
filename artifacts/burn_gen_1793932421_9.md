## 1. Field-Oriented Current Control (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset (θ_offset is found by alignment at commissioning).

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant in the d-q frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Torque (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q):

$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Gain selection by pole-zero cancellation.** Placing the PI zero on the electrical pole R/L gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p^{i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i} = R\,\omega_c$$

Constraint: ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10 accounts for the 1.5-sample computation and PWM delay. Here 2π·1500 gives about 13° phase-margin loss from the delay, which is acceptable.

**Example numbers:** K_p = 2.1e-3 × 9425 ≈ 19.8 V/A and K_i = 5.6 × 9425 ≈ 52 780 V/(A·s).
