# STM32H743 + 4-in-1 ESC Nano AIO (Job #14): Power-Stage, Thermal & DShot Architecture

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

Two assumptions are needed to make the problem physically consistent.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** An H743 running ArduPilot cannot also run four sensorless FOC/6-step loops at 48 kHz alongside the flight stack. The architecture is therefore:
   - **H743**: ArduPilot flight controller.
   - **Bi-directional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs.
   - **ESC MCUs**: 4 × AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32 at PWM_FREQ = 48 kHz.
   - **Gate drivers**: 4 × FD6288Q 3-phase gate drivers.
   - **FETs**: 24 × 40 V N-channel FETs in 3.3 × 3.3 mm packages.
2. **"80 A burst" means per-motor phase/DC current for a short time.** It is not a continuous rating. The thermal model in §3 shows that 80 A is sustainable for roughly **1 second**, and the honest continuous rating of a nano board is about **25–30 A per motor**.
