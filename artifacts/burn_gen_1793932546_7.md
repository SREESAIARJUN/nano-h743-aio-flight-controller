## 1. Loop Architecture and Timing

```
             1 kHz                  4 kHz                 20 kHz (PWM-synchronous)
 θ_ref ──►[Angle P + FF]──ω_ref──►[Velocity PI]──i_q*──►[Current PI d/q]──►SVPWM──► Motor
              ▲                       ▲      ▲  i_q,cog                ▲
              │ attitude (camera)     │ gyro │  (anti-cogging LUT)     │ i_abc, θ_e (encoder)
         [Mahony/AHRS]◄──[Dual-IMU fusion + notch + LPF]◄── 2× ICM-42688-P @ 8 kHz
```

- **Current loop: 20 kHz.** Runs in the ADC end-of-conversion ISR, triggered at the PWM counter center.
- **Velocity loop: 4 kHz.** Uses the decimated, filtered IMU gyro rate, which is inertial. This is the key to stabilization: base disturbance is rejected at the rate loop, not the angle loop.
- **Angle loop: 1 kHz.** Uses the camera attitude from the AHRS.
- **Bandwidth separation:** each loop is ≥4–5× slower than its inner loop. Targets: ω_c,i = 2π·2000, ω_v = 2π·60, ω_θ = 2π·12 rad/s.
