## 0. Architectural Clarifications

Three points determine whether this design works. They must be settled before any numbers mean anything.

**0.1 The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack and has no FOC or 6-step commutation firmware. The 4-in-1 section therefore needs its own ESC MCU per channel, or a dual/quad-channel ESC MCU, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This document assumes **4× AT32F421 running AM32**. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600. The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM.

**0.2 80 A on a nano board is a burst rating, and it is heat-capacity limited.** On a 20×20 mm board, the steady-state thermal resistance to air is roughly 15 K/W. That supports only about 4–5 W of continuous dissipation. Four channels at 80 A dissipate about 90 W. The burst rating is therefore set by the board's thermal mass, not its steady-state resistance. Section 3 quantifies this.

**Recommendation:** use a **30.5×30.5 mm** board for 4×80 A burst. Alternatively, specify the burst as **"80 A, single channel, ≤1 s"** on the 20×20 form factor. Both options are carried through the math below.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM. Within each 60° electrical sector:

- One phase leg is PWM'd (high-side FET at duty D, low-side FET acting as synchronous rectifier for 1−D).
- One leg is held low (low-side FET on 100%).
- One leg floats (used for BEMF sensing).

The phase current flows through **exactly two conducting MOSFETs at every instant**. Those are either HS_a plus LS_b, or LS_a plus LS_b during freewheeling. This holds regardless of duty cycle, which makes the bridge loss independent of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) \qquad\text{where}\qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

### 1.2 Numerical evaluation at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | Per conducting FET (I²R) | Per bridge (×2) | Per FET, averaged over 6 FETs | All 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 6.40 W | 12.8 W | 2.13 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 8.56 W | 17.1 W | 2.85 W | 68.5 W |
| **125 °C** | **1.45 mΩ** | **9.28 W** | **18.6 W** | **3.09 W** | **74.2 W** |

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical period. At 50 k eRPM-class speeds (for example, 7 pole pairs at about 50,000 mechanical rpm gives f_e ≈ 5.8 kHz, so the period is about 172 µs), this period is much shorter than the die thermal time constant of roughly 1–10 ms. Averaging over the period is therefore valid for junction temperature.
