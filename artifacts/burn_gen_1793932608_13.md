## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_DS = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, the motor current flows through two phases:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** current flows through the high-side FET for D·T_sw and through the low-side FET for (1−D)·T_sw. Synchronous rectification means there is always exactly one FET conducting in this leg.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET conducts 100 % of the sector.

So at every instant, exactly two R_DS(on) are in series with I_ph, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

A dead-time correction is applied separately in §2.4, because the body diode conducts during dead time instead of the channel.

### 1.3 Distribution per FET

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period. The duty split between HS and LS depends on D. The averaged per-FET loss is:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot\frac{6}{6} \;\Rightarrow\; \bar P_{FET}=\tfrac{1}{6}P_{cond,ch}$$

The worst-case FET is the low-side FET at low D, at roughly 1.3× the average.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.2 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0012 = **15.4 W** | 61.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.65 mΩ | **21.1 W** | 84.5 W |
| 125 °C | 1.8 mΩ | **23.0 W** | 92.2 W |
