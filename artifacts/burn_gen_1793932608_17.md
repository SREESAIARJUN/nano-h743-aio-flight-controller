## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture clarifications

- **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware and generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. Combining flight control and inverter commutation on one MCU is not supported by ArduPilot and would be a different project.
- **"Nano" plus 80 A burst is the governing constraint.** On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board, 80 A is a **seconds-scale burst rating**, not a continuous rating. The thermal model below quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (6-step, complementary/synchronous PWM)

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The low-side FET of the same leg conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts for the whole sector.

At every instant, the current therefore flows through exactly **two** FET channels in series:

$$R_{path}(T_j) = 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$
