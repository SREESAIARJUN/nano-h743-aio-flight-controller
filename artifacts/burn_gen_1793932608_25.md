# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #19): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Basis

## 0. Architectural Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the power bridges.** A 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), each with its own 3-phase gate driver. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to those ESC MCUs. The architecture below follows that split. If you actually intend the H743 to do field commutation directly, that is a different and inadvisable design. It would put 12 complementary PWM pairs plus BEMF sensing on a flight-critical MCU.

All MOSFET and board numbers are **design assumptions**. Replace them with datasheet values for your chosen parts before you release the design.
