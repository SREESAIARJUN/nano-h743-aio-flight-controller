## 0. Architecture Decisions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot is a flight-control stack, not ESC firmware. The architecture is:

```
LiPo 6S ─┬─► 4× [ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32) → 3-ph gate driver → 6× N-FET] ─► M1..M4
         │              ▲  bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire each, inverted)
         └─► BEC ─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── TIM1 CH1..CH4 + DMA
```

- **Per motor channel:**
  - 1× ESC MCU running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM.
  - 1× 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class: VCC ≤ 20 V, bootstrap high side).
  - 6× 40 V N-MOSFETs.

### 0.2 Form-Factor Warning

80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm "nano" board cannot be supported thermally (see §3). This design assumes:

- 30.5×30.5 mm board.
- 8-layer PCB, 2 oz copper on all layers.
- Bolt-on aluminium spreader.

If 20×20 mm is a hard requirement, re-rate to roughly 35 A continuous / 50 A burst, or accept a burst window of only a few seconds.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Complementary PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct (e.g. A+ / B−):

- **Phase B low side (B_L):** ON for the whole sector and carries I.
- **Phase A:** the high side (A_H) is ON for duty D. The low side (A_L) is ON for (1 − D), minus dead time, as a synchronous rectifier.
