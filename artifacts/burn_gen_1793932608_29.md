Three design issues need resolving before schematic freeze:

1. **The H743 does not drive the gates.** ArduPilot outputs DShot only. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), or one ESC MCU for all four channels. The netlist below reflects this.
2. **80 A on 20×20 mm is a transient rating of roughly 0.5–1 s.** It is not a continuous capability. Section 3 shows why.
3. **Four channels at 80 A means 320 A from the pack.** The XT60/XT90 lead, bus copper, and battery C-rating must be included in the budget, or you must specify that 80 A is a single-channel rating.
