## 0. Architecture Correction and Sanity Flags

### 0.1 Partitioning
The STM32H743 does **not** run the 48 kHz motor PWM. In an AIO "FC + 4-in-1 ESC" design the work is split:

| Function | Silicon | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 TX, eRPM RX | STM32H743 (480 MHz, 400 MHz typical in ArduPilot) | ArduPilot |
| 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF sensing, eRPM telemetry | 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive | 4× 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288-class) or discrete | – |
| Power stage | 24× N-ch MOSFETs (6 per motor) | – |

The switching-loss section therefore models the ESC power stage. The DShot section models the H743.

### 0.2 The "80A" figure needs definition
On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm "nano" board, **80 A per motor (320 A total) is not physically sustainable** (shown in §3). Two readings are possible:

- **(a)** 80 A total, 20 A per channel.
- **(b)** 80 A per channel, short burst.

The model below uses **(b)** as the worst case and then derives the realistic continuous rating. Confirm which spec marketing or the customer actually means.
