## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 0.70 \times 1.45 = 1.015\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current Path in 6-Step Synchronous Commutation

In every commutation state, current flows through exactly **two conducting FET positions**:

- The PWM leg: the high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D, and the low-side (LS) conducts as a synchronous rectifier for fraction (1 − D).
