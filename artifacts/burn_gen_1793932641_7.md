## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification
Within any 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM-switched and phase B is held low:

- **PWM on:** current flows through HS_A, the motor, then LS_B.
- **PWM off:** current freewheels through LS_A (synchronous), the motor, then LS_B.

So exactly **two MOSFETs are always in series** with the motor current, independent of duty cycle D.

### 1.2 Channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right] \approx R_{25}\, k_T$$

**Numbers at 80 A, hot:**
- MOSFET term: 2 × 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W**
- Copper term, with R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ (pad-to-pad, 2 oz, 8 layers paralleled): 6400 × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W**

### 1.3 Per-device distribution
Over one electrical revolution, each phase leg spends 1/3 of the time as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the sink leg and 1/3 floating.

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[1 + (1-D)\right] I^2 R \;\;(\text{with } (1-D) \text{ during the PWM sector})$$

At burst (D ≈ 1), each device averages:

$$P_{dev} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, I^2 R = \tfrac{1}{3} \times 9.6\ \text{W} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

**Low-speed caveat.** If the electrical frequency is low enough that a single sector lasts longer than the junction time constant (≈ 1–5 ms for PDFN5x6), the sink FET sees the full **9.6 W** quasi-statically. This applies during startup or a stalled prop, and it is the real worst case for T_j. Firmware should enforce a stall current limit, for example AM32 "stall protection" or current limit ≤ 40 A below 1000 eRPM.
