# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #54): Reference Design Model

**Scope note.** In this architecture the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM and the 80 A power stage belong to the ESC's own MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class firmware) and its MOSFET bridges. The models below keep these two domains separate.

All device parameters are **representative values for a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 5×6 mm MOSFET class**. Substitute your actual datasheet values before committing to a layout. The equations are general; the numbers are illustrative.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Per-switch RMS current (6-step, synchronous PWM)

Each switch position owns a 120° electrical interval (1/3 of the period). Take PWM duty D applied on the high side, with the complementary low side of the same leg acting as synchronous rectifier.

- **High-side FET** conducts during D of its own 1/3 interval:
  $$I_{HS,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \frac{D}{3}$$
- **Low-side FET** conducts during (1−D) of the same leg's high interval, plus its own full 1/3 low interval:
  $$I_{LS,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \frac{(1-D) + 1}{3} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \frac{2-D}{3}$$

Summing over the 3 legs gives the total conduction loss per motor, which is **independent of D**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 3\left[\frac{D}{3} + \frac{2-D}{3}\right] I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Physically, there are always exactly two FETs in series in the current path.

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 105 °C: R_DS = 0.90 × (1 + 0.0045 × 80) = **1.22 mΩ**

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.22\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{15.6\ W}$$

### 2.3 Worst-case single FET

The worst case is the low side at low duty. As D → 0, I²_LS → (2/3)·I²:

$$P_{LS,max} = \tfrac{2}{3} \times 6400 \times 1.22\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{5.2\ W}$$

At D = 0.5: P_LS = 3.9 W and P_HS = 1.3 W, plus switching loss (see §3).

### 2.4 Copper and shunt losses (per motor, burst)

| Path | R | P = I²R |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad/trace, 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ × 2 phases in path | 0.6 mΩ | 3.8 W |
| Bus current shunt (shared, 0.25 mΩ), I_bus = D·I_ph | 0.25 mΩ | 0.4 W @ D = 0.5 (per-motor share) |

Copper loss is frequently underestimated. At 80 A it reaches roughly 25% of the FET conduction loss.
