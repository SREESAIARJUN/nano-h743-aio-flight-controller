# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #67): Engineering Model

## 0. Design Basis and Three Findings

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32-class, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421). Those MCUs run commutation and PWM at 48 kHz.

- The H743 cannot drive the gates directly under ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware.
- The ESC MCUs are therefore required, not optional.

**Finding 1: The burst current is a duration limit, not a steady rating.** "80 A burst on a nano board" is only achievable briefly. Section 3 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 85 W. On a board of roughly 30×30 mm, that heats the board at about 12–15 K/s. My recommended rating is:

- **80 A for 3 s on one or two channels at a time**, or
- **≈55–60 A for 10 s on all four channels at once.**

**Finding 2: Footprint.** A true 20×20 mm nano footprint cannot place 24 power FETs plus an H743 at this current. I assume a **30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern, ~36×36 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes**.

**Finding 3: All component values are representative.** Part numbers and parameters below come from typical 40 V, 3×3 / 5×6-class datasheets. Substitute the parameters of your chosen parts and rerun the model.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance**

R_DS(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 0.90 × 1.375 = **1.24 mΩ**.

**Current path.** With synchronous rectification (6-step or FOC), the motor current always flows through exactly one high-side and one low-side device: either the active HS plus the opposite LS, or the freewheeling LS pair. So per motor:

P_cond = I_ph² · 2 · R_DS(T_j) + I_ph² · R_cu

**Numbers at 80 A**

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 6400 × 2 × 1.24 mΩ | **15.9 W** |
| Copper | 6400 × 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |

**Distribution across the six FETs** (6-step, duty D)

- High-side FET (when its phase is active): P_HS = I² · R · D · (1/3)
- Low-side FET: P_LS = I² · R · (1 − D/… ) · (1/3), summed across the freewheel/commutation states

At burst conditions (D → 0.9), the hottest device averages about **3.5–4.5 W** over an electrical cycle. Instantaneous peaks are about 8 W during its conduction interval.

**Convergence.** The model is self-consistent: T_j feeds back into R_DS. Iterate T_j → R → P → T_j. It converges in 3–4 steps when R_th · ∂P/∂T < 1. If that loop gain reaches 1 or more, the result is thermal runaway, and that boundary sets the hard current limit.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the hard-switched device in each commutating leg contributes. Per motor:

| Term | Formula | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 35 ns × 48 kHz | **1.69 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 60 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | **0.073 W** |
| Output capacitance | ½ · Q_oss · V · f_sw | 0.5 × 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | **0.030 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_F · I · 2 t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | **0.614 W** |
| Gate drive (lost in driver, not the FET) | Q_g · V_GS · f_sw · 2 | 50 nC × 10 × 48 k × 2 | **0.048 W** |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 2.45 W** |

**Sensitivity**

- Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. Moving from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves about 1.2 W per motor at burst.
- Halving dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W.
- AM32's 48 kHz setting is chosen for low acoustic noise and smooth low-RPM behaviour, not efficiency.

## 3. Total Thermal Budget and Transient Model

**Dissipation at 80 A, all four channels**

| Item | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 15.9 W | 63.6 W |
| FET switching | 2.4 W | 9.6 W |
| PCB copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Gate drivers + ESC MCUs + LDOs | — | ~1.0 W |
| H743 + IMU + regulators (FC side) | — | ~1.2 W |
| **Total** | | **≈ 88 W** |

**Bus capacitor ripple.** Per motor, I_C,rms ≈ I · √(D(1−D)), which is up to **40 A rms at D = 0.5**.

- This is shared by the bulk electrolytic capacitor and local MLCCs.
- Each channel needs at least 4 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 MLCCs, plus a ≥470 µF low-ESR external bulk capacitor on the battery leads.

**Lumped thermal network (Cauer, 3-node)**

```
P_FET → [R_jc 1.2 K/W ÷ n_parallel] → T_case → [R_cs 0.3 K/W] → T_pcb (C_pcb) → [R_pcb-amb] → T_amb
```
