# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Constraints

I don't have access to a "Job #51" specification, so I had to assume a baseline. **Every assumed parameter below is tagged [A].** Replace these with your real datasheet values before release.

There are three architectural issues to settle before any math.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot outputs DShot. It does not run 6-step or FOC commutation. The board therefore needs four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8/STM32G071 equivalents) running the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600 on-board.
2. **"Nano" form factor vs. 80 A.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A, even as a burst. You cannot fit 24 power FETs, a TFBGA H743, and adequate bulk capacitance, and the thermal mass is far too low (see §2.5). This document sizes for **30.5×30.5 mm** as the minimum. 80 A is treated as a **time-limited burst per channel**, not a continuous rating.
3. **Battery current.** Four channels at 80 A phase current at high duty means more than 250 A from the pack. XT60/XT90 pads and 2 oz copper cannot carry this continuously. The burst rating must be defined per-motor with a defined duty cycle.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.6 × 1.5 = **2.4 mΩ**. This conservative value is used for all burst calculations below.

### 1.2 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

In 6-step operation, two phases are active at any instant. Under complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly one FET in each active leg:

- the high-side FET during the D portion of the period;
- the synchronous low-side FET during the (1−D) portion.

The series conduction path is therefore always two FETs:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0024 = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution

Each leg is active 2/3 of the electrical cycle. Averaged over that cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds} = \mathbf{5.1\ W}$$

The split between high side and low side depends on D. At low duty the low-side FET carries most of the loss, so place the thermal vias accordingly.

### 1.4 Thermal-Runaway Stability Criterion

Loss rises with temperature, and temperature rises with loss. The resulting loop gain is:

$$G = \frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = 2I^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha\,R_{th,ja}$$

With R_th,ja ≈ 4 K/W per channel (forced prop-wash, see §2):

$$G = 2(6400)(0.0016)(0.005)(4) = 0.41$$
