# Job #62: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst Design Package

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Findings

Two issues change the rest of this package, so I'm stating them first.

**1. The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) driving a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The 48 kHz PWM loss model below applies to the ESC stage. The DMA model applies to the H743.

**2. 80 A × 4 on a "nano" (20×20 mm) stack is not thermally sustainable.** The math below shows about 93 W of dissipation per board at full burst on all four channels. A 20×20 board can absorb that for only about 2 seconds before the PCB exceeds 110 °C. This package therefore defines 80 A as a time-limited burst rating with firmware enforcement. The alternative is a 25.5×25.5 or 30×30 mm ESC board.

All device parameters below are representative values for a 40 V-class 3×3 mm MOSFET. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values; every equation is given symbolically.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths per channel

During each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM-switched and one phase is held low. With synchronous rectification:

- **High-side FET (PWM leg):** conducts I for duty D
- **Low-side FET (same leg):** conducts I for (1 − D)
- **Low-side FET (return leg):** conducts I for 100 % of the sector

This gives:

```
P_cond = I² · R_ds(Tj) · [D + (1 − D) + 1] = 2 · I² · R_ds(Tj)
```

Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, which is a useful property for sizing.

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

```
R_ds(Tj) = R_ds25 · [1 + α · (Tj − 25)]
```

Iterating to converge with the thermal result in §4 (Tj ≈ 125 °C):

```
R_ds(125) = 1.0 mΩ · (1 + 0.0065 · 100) = 1.65 mΩ
P_cond    = 2 · 80² · 1.65e-3 = 21.1 W per channel
```

### 2.3 Distribution across FETs

The electrical period is much shorter than the die thermal time constant. For example, at 14-pole and 30 k RPM, the electrical frequency is 3.5 kHz, a period of about 0.29 ms; the die time constant is roughly 5–20 ms. Load therefore averages across all 6 FETs:

```
P_cond,FET(avg) ≈ 21.1 / 6 ≈ 3.5 W
```

The instantaneous worst FET is the return-leg low-side at I²·R = 10.6 W. That holds for only 1/3 of an electrical cycle and is smoothed by die capacitance.

### 2.4 Additional conduction elements

These are per channel, at 80 A.

- **Current shunt (if per-phase or per-channel), 0.25 mΩ:** I²·R_sh = 1.6 W. Many 4-in-1 boards use a single bus shunt, which carries battery current rather than phase current. In that case use I_bus = Σ(I_phase · D).
- **Copper trace and planes:** budget 0.3 mΩ effective, so about 1.9 W. This loss is spread across the board, not concentrated in the FETs.
