## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \pmod{2\pi}$$

Here $\theta_{m,0}$ is the electrical-zero offset, found by aligning with $i_d = I_{align}$ and $i_q = 0$.

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

In the dq frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

With decoupling feedforward, each axis reduces to $G(s) = 1/(Ls + R)$. Choosing the PI zero to cancel the plant pole gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_{c,i}$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{c,i}$$

Delay limit: total delay is about $1.5\,T_{pwm}$ (computation plus PWM hold). Keep $\omega_{c,i} \lesssim \dfrac{1}{4 \cdot 1.5\,T_{pwm}}$. At 20 kHz this gives roughly 2.6 kHz, hence the 1.5–2 kHz target.

Decoupled voltage commands:

$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

where $\lambda_m = \dfrac{2K_t}{3p}$.

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and SVPWM

The voltage vector is limited to the inscribed circle $|v| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. $v_d$ has priority, and $v_q$ takes the remainder:

$$v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$$

After inverse Park, $(v_\alpha, v_\beta)$ goes to SVPWM, implemented as min-max zero-sequence injection:

$$v_x = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v_x + \min v_x),\quad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$
