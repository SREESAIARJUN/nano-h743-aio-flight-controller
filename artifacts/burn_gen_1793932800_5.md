## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Two points in the brief need correcting before any numbers are meaningful.

### 0.1 The H743 does not commutate the motors

ArduPilot running on the STM32H743 is the flight controller. Bidirectional DShot is a protocol between the FC and **separate ESC MCUs**. The H743 cannot also run four sensorless 48 kHz commutation loops alongside the ArduPilot scheduler. The architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz (480 MHz capable on rev V) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 (supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot) |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) or equivalent | n/a |
