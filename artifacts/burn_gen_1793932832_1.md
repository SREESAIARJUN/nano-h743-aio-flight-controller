## 0. Design Basis and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #49 spec sheet, so the values below are typical for a 3-axis camera gimbal. Every one of them is a single `constexpr` or struct field in the code. Replace them with your measured values before tuning.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | — | 12N14P outrunner gimbal motor (GBM2804-class) |
| Pole pairs | p | 7 |
| Slots | N_s | 12 |

| Phase inductance (d≈q) | L | 2.0 mH |
| Torque constant | K_t | 0.08 N·m/A |
| Rotor plus load inertia (per axis) | J | 2–8 ×10⁻⁴ kg·m² |
| Encoder | — | 14-bit absolute (MT6701 / AS5048A) on each motor |
| MCU | — | STM32G474, Cortex-M4F at 170 MHz with FPU |
| Bus voltage | V_dc | 12–16 V |
| IMUs | — | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz |

**Loop rates (bandwidth separation of at least 5× per level):**

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (PWM center-aligned ISR) | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| IMU sampling and filtering | 8 kHz (gyro ODR) | — |
| Velocity (inertial rate) | 4 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·120 rad/s |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | ω_p ≈ 2π·20 rad/s |

**Architecture.** Each motor's encoder supplies the electrical angle for commutation and the mechanical angle for anti-cogging. The fused IMU supplies inertial rate and attitude for stabilization. This split is what lets the gimbal reject base motion: the encoder measures joint motion relative to a moving base, while the IMU measures the payload's motion in inertial space.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform (assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform at electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (PMSM in the dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$
$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q\right] \approx K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI controller with decoupling and pole-zero cancellation

Choose PI gains so the controller zero cancels the electrical pole R/L:

$$
K_{p,c} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,c} = R\,\omega_c
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

The voltage command adds feedforward decoupling and back-EMF compensation:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L_d i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

Set i_d* = 0, since a surface PMSM gains no reluctance torque.

$$
|v_{dq}| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3
$$
