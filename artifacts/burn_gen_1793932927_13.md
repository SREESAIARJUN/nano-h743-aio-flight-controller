# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Allocation, Netlist

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Design Warnings

I have no record of earlier "Job #2" requirements, so every parameter below is stated explicitly. Replace each with your real datasheet values. The equations hold regardless of the numbers you plug in.

**Architecture assumed:**
- **Flight controller:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot.
- **ESC section:** four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware) driving 3-phase bridges.
- **Link:** the FC talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600.

ArduPilot does not commutate motors itself, so the H743 cannot be the bridge controller.

> ⚠️ **Warning 1 – Thermal feasibility.** An 80 A/channel burst on a "nano" (20×20 or 30.5×30.5 mm) board is achievable only as a short burst of a few seconds. Section 3 shows that the continuous rating is roughly 30 A/channel even with prop-wash cooling. Any "80 A" claim must carry a duration qualifier.
>
> ⚠️ **Warning 2 – Netlist status.** The netlist in §5 is a reference topology, not a fab-ready release. Pin numbers must be verified against the exact package datasheets before layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega\,(1+0.0045\cdot100)=1.45\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, two legs conduct:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side switch conducts for duty D, and the synchronous low-side switch conducts for (1−D).
- **Static leg:** one switch conducts continuously.

$$P_{cond,motor}=I^2R_{DS}\,[D+(1-D)] + I^2R_{DS} = 2I^2R_{DS}$$

At 80 A this gives:

$$P_{cond}=2\cdot80^2\cdot1.45\times10^{-3}=\mathbf{18.56\ W\ per\ motor}$$

If you parallel two FETs per switch position, R_DS halves and the loss drops to 9.28 W.

### 1.3 Dead-time body-diode loss

$$P_{dt}=2\,V_{SD}\,I\,t_{dead}\,f_{sw}=2\cdot0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{1.23\ W}$$
