# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #27): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

### Architectural correction

The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. In every practical 4-in-1 design, each bridge (or the full 4-in-1) is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are four AT32F421s or an STM32G071 per channel. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The partition is:

```
[H743 / ArduPilot] --4x BDShot600--> [4x ESC MCU, AM32] --6x PWM--> [gate drivers] --> [3-phase bridge x4]
                   <--ESC telem UART (optional)--
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\Rightarrow R_{DS}(125^\circ C)=1.6\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 6-step with complementary PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, current flows through exactly two switch positions:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for duty D. The low-side conducts synchronously for 1 − D, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side is fully on.

The current path therefore always contains two R_DS(on) in series, independent of D. Per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-switch average

Each phase leg is "PWM-active" for 120° and "return-active" for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle.

$$P_{HS,avg}=\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R\,D,\qquad P_{LS,avg}=\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R\,(1-D)+\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R$$

The low-side carries the larger share. At D → 1, each high-side and low-side averages about I²R/3.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| Tj | R_DS | P_cond per channel | Average per FET (6 FETs) | Instantaneous in conducting FET |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.13 W | 6.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 3.09 W | 9.3 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** | 3.41 W | 10.2 W |

At low eRPM, use the instantaneous value. The die thermal time constant (~ms) is comparable to the commutation period, so the die temperature ripples.

### 1.5 Interconnect copper

Assume roughly 0.4 mΩ per channel path (phase pad + bus pours + vias). This adds:

$$P_{Cu}=I^2R_{Cu}=80^2\times0.4\text{ m}\Omega = 2.56\text{ W per channel}$$

The shared battery-pad and bus copper carries 4 × I_avg. Assume 0.15 mΩ, giving (320 A)² × 0.15 mΩ = **15.4 W** if all four channels burst together. This term is routinely forgotten and is why the battery pads delaminate.
