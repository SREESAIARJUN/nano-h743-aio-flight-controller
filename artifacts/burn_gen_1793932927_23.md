# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture and Feasibility

Two points shape the rest of this document.

**1. The H743 runs ArduPilot, not the ESC commutation.** The H743 cannot also run four sensorless BLDC commutation loops under ArduPilot. The standard architecture, assumed here, is:
- **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM.
- **ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Each drives a 3-phase gate driver (e.g., FD6288Q-class) and six N-MOSFETs.

**2. 80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally marginal.** The model in §1–3 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 85–90 W. The board's heat capacity limits that to roughly 1–2 seconds. Treat "80 A" as a per-channel, single-motor, sub-second rating, or move to 30×30 mm. The equations below let you defend whichever rating you choose.

All device values are **representative and parametric**. Substitute your exact MOSFET datasheet numbers before sign-off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Assumptions

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus (6S full) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Phase current (burst) | I | 80 A |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ (40 V PDFN3.3×3 class) |
| R_DS(on) temp coefficient | α | ≈ 0.006 /K |
| PCB copper + solder per current path | R_pcb | 0.3 mΩ |
| Commutation | — | 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous (complementary) PWM |

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

In 6-step drive, current always flows through exactly two conducting devices in series: the source leg (HS FET, or its synchronous LS during PWM-off) and the sink leg (LS FET).

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{pcb}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(6400)(1.45\times10^{-3}) + 6400(0.3\times10^{-3}) = 18.6 + 1.9 = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET Distribution (Hot-Spot Device)

Each phase sources current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle and sinks for 1/3.

- **Sink LS FET:** fully on for its 1/3:
  $$P_{LS,sink} = \tfrac13 I^2 R = 3.1\ \text{W}$$
- **Source HS FET** at duty D: $\tfrac13 D I^2 R$. Its complementary LS carries $\tfrac13 (1-D) I^2 R$.

The worst-case single LS FET (sink plus sync-rectification share) is about **3.1–6.2 W** averaged. Instantaneous dissipation while conducting is I²R = **9.3 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only one half-bridge per channel is PWM-switching at any time.

| Term | Equation | Values | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f | ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_F·I·2·t_dt·f | 0.8·80·2·100 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 30 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.04 W |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f (≈ C_oss·V²·f) | ~1 nF eff. | 0.03 W |
| Gate drive (in driver/regulator) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f | 2·40 nC·10·48 k | 0.04 W |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 2.66 W / channel** |

**Design notes:**
- The overlap term scales with t_r + t_f. A gate resistor change that slows edges from 40 ns to 80 ns doubles it, to about 3.9 W. Trade this against EMI and V_DS ringing. Keep peak V_DS below 32 V for 40 V parts.
- Dead-time loss is significant at 80 A. Use the minimum safe dead time; AM32 typically runs about 40–100 ns depending on the driver.
- At 100 % duty (true full throttle) the overlap and dead-time terms vanish. The 80 A, high-duty case is therefore conduction-dominated.

### 2.1 Total Electrical Loss

| Scope | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per channel | 20.5 + 2.7 | **23.2 W** |
| 4 channels @ 80 A | 4 × 23.2 | **≈ 93 W** |
| Efficiency check | P_out ≈ 25.2 × 80 × 4 ≈ 8 kW | 98.8 % electrically, but 93 W into a 4 cm² board is the binding constraint |

### 2.2 Bulk Capacitor Ripple (Often the Real Failure Mode)

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; I_{C,rms,max} = 0.5\,I = 40\ \text{A}_{rms}\ \text{per channel at } D=0.5$$

Ceramic MLCC banks (e.g., 4–6 × 10 µF/50 V X7R per channel, *derated about 60 % for DC bias*) plus an external low-ESR electrolytic of at least 470 µF on the battery leads are mandatory. The on-board MLCCs carry the high-frequency component. Check MLCC self-heating against the manufacturer's ripple curves.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Steady-State Network (Per FET)

$$T_j = T_{amb} + P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta CB}) + P_{board}\,R_{\theta BA}$$

| Element | Typical value |
|---|---|
| R_θJC (PDFN3.3) | 1.5–2.5 K/W |
| R_θ case→board (solder + thermal vias) | 1–3 K/W |
| R_θ board→ambient, 20×20, still air | 15–20 K/W |
| R_θ board→ambient, in prop wash | 4–6 K/W |
