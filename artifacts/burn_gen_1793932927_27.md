## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4‑in‑1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay. Typical parts are AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8BB51. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only emits DShot600 frames and decodes eRPM telemetry. The loss model (§1–2) therefore belongs to the ESC half, and the timer model (§3) belongs to the FC half.
2. **Placeholder component parameters.** Component parameters below are marked ⚠ **ASSUMED**. They are representative of 40 V, 5×6 mm (PDFN) trench MOSFETs. Replace them with the selected part's datasheet values (R_DS(on) vs T_j, Q_gd, Q_rr, Z_th curves) before sign-off. The equations are exact. The numbers are only as good as the inputs.
3. **"80 A" is a burst rating.** On a nano board it is not continuous. §2 quantifies exactly how long the burst can last.

### 1.2 Conduction losses

**Commutation pattern.** Under 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, two phase legs carry I_ph at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for D. The low-side (LS) synchronous rectifier conducts for (1−D).
- **Static leg:** the LS FET is on 100 %.

**Bridge conduction loss.** The PWM leg contributes I²R·[D + (1−D)] = I²R, and the static leg contributes another I²R:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.04\ W}$$

**Temperature dependence.** This term is self-heating. R_DS(on) is approximately R₂₅·(1 + α(T_j − 25)), with α ≈ 0.005 /K. Iterate with §2 if T_j falls well below 125 °C.

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs carries 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the average is about 23.04 / 6 ≈ 3.84 W.

**Instantaneous peak.** The always-on LS FET carries 11.5 W instantaneously during its 120° conduction window.
