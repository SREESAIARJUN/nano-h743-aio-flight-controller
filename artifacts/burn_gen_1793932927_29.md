## 0. Feasibility Findings and Architectural Corrections

These points change the design itself, so they come before the math.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---------|-------------|
| F1 | **80 A burst per motor is not achievable on a 20×20 mm "nano" 4-in-1.** The model in §2–§4 gives ≈100 W of total ESC dissipation at 4×80 A. A nano board's heat capacity is ~3–5 J/K, so it heats at ≈20–30 °C/s. | 80 A is only achievable as a burst of ≤1–2 s on a 30.5×30.5 mm board or larger, with 2–3 oz copper and MOSFETs in 5×6 mm PDFN packages. A true 20×20 nano layout supports roughly 25–35 A continuous and 40–45 A burst. Redefine the SKU or the rating. |
| F2 | **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In ArduPilot architecture, the H743 sends DShot commands.
