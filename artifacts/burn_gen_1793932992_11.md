## 0. Architecture Decisions (Read First)

Three decisions shape everything else, and the first two correct common misconceptions in the requirement as written.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 as the flight controller. Each of the four ESC channels has its own MCU running AM32: STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. Commutation at 48 kHz PWM with zero-cross or back-EMF detection is a hard-real-time job that ArduPilot is not designed to do. Combining both roles on one H743 is not a supported configuration.
2. **"Nano" form factor versus 80 A burst.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4 × 80 A for more than about 1.5 s (see §3.4). This baseline therefore uses **30.5×30.5 mm, 8‑layer, 2 oz copper on all layers**, with 80 A defined as a **≤ 3 s per-channel burst** rating. If a 20×20 mm board is mandatory, derate to about 45 A burst and 25 A continuous.
3. **Bus voltage.** The board supports up to 6S (25.2 V full charge) with 40 V MOSFETs. This leaves about 15 V of margin for ringing, so the layout must hold a power-loop inductance of 2 nH or less. A 60 V FET option is listed for 6S builds that need extra margin.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Sharing in 6‑Step Complementary PWM

Over one electrical period, each half-bridge leg spends:

- **⅓ of the period as the PWM leg.** The high side (HS) conducts for fraction D; the low side (LS) conducts synchronously for 1−D.
- **⅓ of the period as the sink leg.** The LS is fully on.
- **⅓ of the period floating.** This is the back-EMF sensing window.

Let I_ph be the phase (motor) current magnitude, which is what the 80 A rating refers to. Battery current is then I_bat ≈ D·I_ph.

Per-FET conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \frac{D}{3} I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,LS} = \frac{(1-D)+1}{3} I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = \frac{2-D}{3} I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Summing over the three legs gives D + (2−D) = 2. **Exactly two R_DS(on) are in the current path at every instant, independent of D:**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$
