## 0. Scope Clarifications and Assumptions

These architectural facts shape all of the math below.

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In an ArduPilot AIO, the STM32H743 sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, one per motor. Each ESC MCU (here an AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware) generates the **48 kHz** complementary PWM. Switching-loss math applies to the ESC side. DMA/timer math applies to the H743 side.
2. **"80 A burst" means per channel, transient.** A nano-class board (≈26×26 mm, 20×20 mounting) cannot dissipate 4×80 A continuously. Section 3 derives how long the burst can last.
3. **All numerical inputs are design assumptions.** Replace them with your selected MOSFET's datasheet values. Every equation is parametric.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst current per channel | I_pk | 80 A (phase current, DC-equivalent) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V N-ch, DFN3.3×3.3, one per switch position (24 total) |
