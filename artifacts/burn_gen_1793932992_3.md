# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #58): Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Warning

I have no prior documents for Job #58, so this design rests on explicit assumptions. Every number marked **[A]** must be replaced with values from your selected parts' datasheets before layout freeze.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors itself. Each of the four motor channels needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 talks to these MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. Driving 4×3 half-bridges at 48 kHz with closed-loop commutation from the H7 alongside ArduPilot is not a supported ArduPilot configuration.

**Assumed operating point.**

> ⚠ **Bottom line, stated up front:** 80 A per channel on all four channels simultaneously is ≈ 85 W of heat on a ~13 cm² board with ~5 J/K of thermal mass. The math below shows this is **not survivable for 10 s**. Either the 80 A figure is a single-channel transient rating, or the burst window must be ~2 s, or the board must grow (35×35 → 45×45 mm with 5×6 mm FETs). Section 2.6 quantifies this.

## 1. Power-Stage Topology per Channel

- Three half-bridges (phases A/B/C) form a 6-FET bridge.
- Commutation is 6-step trapezoidal with **complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM** at f_sw = 48 kHz, which is the AM32/BLHeli_32 default behaviour.
- In any commutation sector:
  - One phase's high/low pair is PWM'd complementarily: HS at duty D, LS freewheeling at (1–D).
  - A second phase's low-side is held ON.
  - The third phase floats for BEMF sensing.
- At every instant, motor current I flows through **exactly two conducting FET channels**. Body diodes conduct only during dead time.

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

The PWM'd leg contributes:

$$P_{cond,PWM} = I^2 R_{DS}\,D + I^2 R_{DS}(1-D) = I^2 R_{DS}$$

The static low-side contributes:

$$P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R_{DS}$$

So the per-channel total is **independent of duty**:

$$\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

At I = 80 A (6400 A²):

| T_j | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 17.1 W |
| 125 °C | **18.6 W** |

**PCB copper loss** must not be ignored at 80 A.
- 2 oz copper has a sheet resistance of ≈ 0.245 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, and ≈ 0.32 mΩ/□ at 100 °C.
- A realistic loop (bus plane → HS → phase pad → motor wire pad, then return through LS → GND plane) is ≈ 3 □ effective with paralleled inner layers.
- Estimated loop resistance: R_cu ≈ 0.5–0.8 mΩ.
- Resulting loss: P_cu ≈ 6400 × 0.65 mΩ ≈ **4.2 W per channel**.

### 2.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd HS switches hard. The LS turns on/off at near-zero V_DS (ZVS) after dead time.

**(a) V–I overlap, hard-switched HS:**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.97\ \text{W}$$

**(b) Output charge (C_oss):**

$$P_{oss} = V_{bus} Q_{oss} f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\text{nC} \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery of the LS body diode, dissipated mostly in the HS:**

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction**, with t_dt = 100 ns per edge, two edges per cycle:

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

**(e) Gate drive.** This loss is mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

**Switching subtotal ≈ 1.75 W per channel.**

> Sensitivity: P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it is ~0.9 W, a saving of ~0.85 W/channel. At 96 kHz it is ~3.5 W. 48 kHz is a reasonable compromise between acoustic/ripple performance and loss at this current.

### 2.4 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A, T_j ≈ 125 °C

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 18.6 W | 74.4 W |
| PCB copper | 4.2 W | 16.8 W |
| Switching + dead time + Q_rr/Q_oss | 1.75 W | 7.0 W |
| Gate drive / driver / ESC MCU / LDO | ~0.3 W | 1.2 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 24.9 W** | **≈ 99 W** |

The FC section (H743 at 480 MHz, IMU, OSD, regulators) adds ~1.5 W.

Supply-side sanity check:
- Output power is ≈ 4 × 80 A × 25.2 V × D_eff. At 4 × 80 A that is 320 A × ~22 V ≈ 7 kW.
- The battery lead (XT60/XT90) and battery cannot deliver this anyway.
- This is further evidence that "80 A burst" should be read as a per-channel rating.
