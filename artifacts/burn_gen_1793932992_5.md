# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #56): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-DShot DMA, and Netlist

## 0. Architecture Correction and Feasibility Flags

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In an ArduPilot system, the STM32H743 runs the flight stack and *sends* bidirectional DShot600 commands. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-MCU parameter, not an H743 timer. The design below is therefore:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) ──4× Bi-DShot600──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32, 48 kHz PWM) ──► 4× 3-phase gate driver ──► 24× MOSFET
```

**2. 80 A per channel cannot be sustained on a nano board.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A is not credible at all. On a 30.5×30.5 mm hole pattern with a roughly 36×36 mm board, 80 A is achievable only as a **burst of a few seconds**. Section 3 quantifies this. Everything below assumes:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max |
| Board | 36×36 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz (70 µm) copper |
| MOSFET | 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3, R_DS(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, C_oss ≈ 1.5 nF, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, V_SD ≈ 0.8 V. One FET per switch, 24 total. |
| Gate drive | V_GS = 10 V, ~1 A source/sink, dead time t_dt = 100 ns |
| Switching | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, trapezoidal 6-step |
| Ambient / start | T_amb = 40 °C, board pre-burst T_0 = 50 °C, T_j,max = 150 °C |

Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values into the equations. The model is parametric.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step synchronous operation, at any instant two phase legs carry the phase current I:
- **Non-PWM leg:** its low-side FET is on continuously, carrying I.
- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high side for fraction D and through the low side (synchronous freewheel) for fraction (1−D).

Ignoring dead time, the series path always contains **two** R_DS(on):

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Dead-time correction (body-diode interval replaces R_DS(on) during 2·t_dt·f_sw):

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\left[2 - 2t_{dt}f_{sw}\right] \approx 2I^2R_{DS}\,(1 - 0.0096)$$

The correction is negligible and is accounted for separately in §2.3.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond at 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 15.4 |
| 75 | 1.56 | 20.0 |
| 100 | 1.74 | 22.3 |
| 125 | 1.92 | **24.6** |
| 150 | 2.10 | 26.9 |

Here P_cond = 12 800 A² · R_DS(T_j).
