# Job #70: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot Allocation

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two issues in the brief change the design, so I am addressing them first.

**1. The H743 should not commutate the motors.** Four motors need 12 half-bridges, which means 24 complementary gate signals. The H743 has two advanced timers (TIM1 and TIM8, 6 complementary outputs each) plus HRTIM (10 outputs), which covers about 2–3 motors. ADC/BEMF sampling across 4 motors at 48 kHz on top of ArduPilot's scheduler is not credible either.

The architecture I am specifying instead:
- **H743 (ArduPilot):** sends bi-directional DShot600 to the ESC.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32): handle 48 kHz PWM, commutation, and eRPM telemetry.

**2. 80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is not a continuous rating.** Section 3 shows it is a burst limited to roughly 1–3 s. The 4-channel total of 320 A also exceeds what nano-format copper and connectors can carry. I model 80 A as a per-channel burst and recommend a sustained rating of about 35–45 A.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation model.** Six-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant the phase current I flows through exactly two FETs in series:
- one leg's HS (during PWM-on) or its LS (synchronous freewheel), and
- the opposite leg's LS.

Each FET carries current 1/3 of the electrical period.

**Temperature-dependent resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Per-channel and per-FET conduction loss** (ripple neglected, since ΔI_L is less than 5% of I at 48 kHz with typical motor L):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Electrothermal fixed point.** The loss and junction temperature depend on each other, so iterate:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{ref} + P_{FET}(T_j^{(n)})\,Z_{\theta}(t)$$

This converges when $\tfrac{\partial P}{\partial T}Z_\theta < 1$, i.e. $\tfrac{1}{3}I^2 R_{25}\alpha Z_\theta<1$. At 80 A and Z_θ = 1 K/W this gives 0.016, so convergence is stable.

**Results at 80 A:**

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | P per FET |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.13 W |
| 85 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 3.09 W |
| 110 °C | 1.64 mΩ | 20.9 W | 3.48 W |

**Parasitic copper and current-sense losses must be added per channel:**
- Shunt (0.25 mΩ): I²R_sh = 1.6 W
- Phase and bus copper (≈0.3 mΩ effective, 2 oz, 6 layer): ≈1.9 W

**Total conduction at 110 °C: ≈ 24.4 W per channel.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches. One hard-switched HS FET per channel, with its LS partner doing synchronous rectification (SR).

**Transition times** from gate-charge analysis, with Q_sw = Q_gd + Q_gs/2 = 14 nC:

$$t_{on}= \frac{Q_{sw}R_g}{V_{drv}-V_{pl}} = \frac{14\text{n}\cdot4}{6} = 9.3\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off}= \frac{Q_{sw}R_g}{V_{pl}} = 14\ \text{ns}$$

**Loss components:**

$$P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}$$

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss,er}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}$$

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_f\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_gV_{drv}f_{sw}$$

The gate term is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET.

**Results at 80 A, 25.2 V:**

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 0.5·25.2·80·23.3 ns·48 k | 1.13 W |
| Reverse recovery | 30 n·25.2·48 k | 0.04 W |
| C_oss | 1 n·635·48 k | 0.03 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2·0.8·80·50 n·48 k | 0.31 W |
| Gate (driver side) | 2·40 n·10·48 k | 0.04 W |
| **Ideal total** | | **≈1.55 W** |

**Layout derating.** Commutation-loop inductance of about 2–3 nH adds ringing and dv/dt-induced C_oss/Miller loss. Use a realistic multiplier k_lay ≈ 2:

**P_sw ≈ 3.1 W per channel.**

**Per-channel total at the 80 A burst:** P_ch ≈ 24.4 + 3.1 ≈ **27.5 W**. For all four channels at 80 A, P_tot ≈ **110 W**.
