## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from mechanical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant model (surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$v_d = R\,i_d + L\,\dot i_d - \omega_e L\, i_q$$

$$v_q = R\,i_q + L\,\dot i_q + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

$$\tau_m = \tfrac{3}{2}\, p\, \lambda_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 PI design by pole-zero cancellation

Each axis sees the plant $1/(Ls + R)$. Choosing a PI controller

$$C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}, \qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

places the PI zero at $R/L$. The open loop becomes $\omega_c/s$, so the closed loop is first order with bandwidth $\omega_c$.

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{\text{pwm}}/10$ to stay clear of the PWM and sampling delay ($1.5\,T_s$).

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d - \omega_e L\, i_q, \qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

The voltage vector is limited to the SVPWM linear-region circle, $|v| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$.

- The d-axis takes priority: $v_d$ is clamped to $\pm V_{\max}$.
- The q-axis gets the remainder: $|v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}$.

The integrator uses **back-calculation** anti-windup, with $K_{aw} = 1/K_p$ (per unit time):

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\,\big[K_i\, e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_{\text{sat}} - u_{\text{unsat}})\big]$$
