## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in six-step trapezoidal drive

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase X (PWM leg):** high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) FETs switch complementarily at 48 kHz. At any instant, exactly one of them carries the phase current.
- **Phase Y (return leg):** the LS FET is held on for the whole sector.

Result: **at every instant, exactly two FETs carry I_ph**, independent of duty cycle D. (This holds because synchronous rectification puts the LS-on interval of the PWM leg through the channel instead of the body diode.)

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$

The phase ripple is set by the motor inductance:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - E_{bemf})\,D}{2 L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
\;\approx\; \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2 L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$
