## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Paths in 6-Step Synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_s. The complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D)·T_s.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100 % of the sector.

So, ignoring dead time, **exactly two FET channels carry I_ph at every instant**, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,\big[1 + 0.0055\,(T_j - 25)\big]$$

Per-FET average loss: each switch participates for one third of the electrical cycle. So P_FET ≈ P_cond,ESC / 3 per FET position, with the HS/LS split depending on D.

### 1.2 Electro-Thermal Fixed Point

R_ds(on) rises with T_j, and T_j rises with loss. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P(T_j)\cdot Z_{th}(t)
\;\Rightarrow\;
P_{cond} = \frac{2I^2R_{25}(1+\alpha(T_{ref}-25))}{1-2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha\,Z_{th}}$$

Thermal runaway occurs if the denominator approaches 0, that is, if 2I²R₂₅·α·Z_th ≥ 1.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per PWM period there is one turn-on and one turn-off of the high-side under hard switching. The low-side commutates through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 V–I Overlap (Hard Switching)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot40\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse Recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.06\text{ W}$$

Q_rr grows with di/dt and current, so treat this as a lower bound.

### 2.3 Output Capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss(er)} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} \times 2 \text{ FETs} = 0.05\text{ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_f\, I_{ph}\, t_{dt}\, f_{sw} = 2\cdot0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

At 48 kHz this is significant. Cutting dead time to 100 ns halves it, but verify shoot-through margin on the scope at hot T_j.

### 2.5 Gate Drive (Dissipated in the Driver, Not the FETs)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} \times 2 \text{ switching FETs} = 50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}\cdot2 = 0.048\text{ W}$$

The driver's bootstrap and linear regulator add more: the LDO from 25 V to 10–12 V burns roughly (25−12)·I_q.

### 2.6 Frequency Sensitivity

$$P_{f}(f_{sw}) = f_{sw}\left[\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) + Q_{rr}V + C_{oss}V^2 + 2V_f I t_{dt}\right]
\approx 68\ \mu\text{J} \times f_{sw}$$

| f_sw | Frequency-dependent loss per ESC |
|---|---|
| 24 kHz | 1.6 W |
| 48 kHz | **3.3 W** |
| 96 kHz | 6.5 W |

Above ~100 kHz these terms dominate at mid-current.
