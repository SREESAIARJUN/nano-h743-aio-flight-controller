## 0. Design Basis and Architectural Corrections

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 / AIO design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8/STM32G0 equivalents). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model below reflects that split.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Calculations use 25.2 V (worst case for switching loss) |
| Burst current | **80 A per motor**, ≤ 5 s | 4 × 80 A = 320 A pack draw |
| Continuous | 55 A per motor | Target, to be validated thermally |
| f_sw | 48 kHz, center-aligned complementary | Synchronous rectification |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal | Two phase legs conduct at any instant |
| MOSFET (reference) | 40 V N-ch, PQFN 5×6, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 mΩ typ / 1.4 mΩ max @ 25 °C, Q_g ≈ 45 nC, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, Q_oss ≈ 40 nC, R_θJC ≈ 1.0 K/W | Placeholder class (e.g., Infineon/Onsemi 40 V trench). Substitute the real datasheet values. |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) or equivalent | One per ESC channel |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers | **A 20 × 20 "nano" board cannot sustain 80 A/motor.** See §3.4. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_J) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_J-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_J = 125 °C with the max-rated R_DS(on):

$$R_{DS(on),hot} = 1.4\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 \approx 2.03\ \text{m}\Omega \;\;(\text{design with } 2.1\ \text{m}\Omega)$$

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous current path

During each 60° sector, current flows through one high-side FET of phase *x* and one low-side FET of phase *y*. On the PWM-switched leg, the current alternates between the HS and the LS (synchronous) FET with duty D. On the static leg, the FET conducts continuously. Either way, two R_DS(on) are in series at all times except dead time:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\cdot 2R_{DS(on),hot}\cdot\big(1 - 2t_d f_{sw}\big)$$

With t_d = 100 ns, the factor (1 − 0.0096) is negligible:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.1\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{26.9\ W}$$

Phase current ripple adds I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12. With a typical racing motor (L_ph ≈ 10 µH), ΔI_pp ≈ 10 A, which adds about 0.1 %. This is ignored.

### 1.3 PCB copper conduction

Bus plus phase path, with an estimated 0.35 mΩ effective (2 oz, multilayer paralleled, at 100 °C):

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.35\,\text{m}\Omega = 2.2\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Current shunt

With a 0.25 mΩ shunt on the DC link per channel:

$$P_{shunt} = I_{DC}^2 R_{sh}$$

I_DC = D · I_ph. At D = 0.9:

$$P_{shunt} = (72)^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.3\ \text{W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg incurs switching loss. That is one HS/LS pair per channel at any instant.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (HS FET)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

The rise/fall times are set by gate drive. FD6288 sources about 1.5 A and sinks about 1.8 A. With R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω, Q_gd ≈ 10 nC, and V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V, the plateau current is about 1 A, giving t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 10 ns:

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr scales with di/dt and I_F. At 80 A, derate by about 2× to get **0.12 W**.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot \tfrac12 \;\;(\text{HS turn-on dissipates LS } E_{oss})$$
$$= 0.5 \times 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F I_{ph}\,(2t_d) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

The reduction lever here is t_d. AM32 dead-time = 100 ns is assumed. Going to 60 ns cuts this to 0.37 W but must be verified against shoot-through margin on the real layout.

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and R_g, not in the FET)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}\times 2 = 45\,\text{nC}\times 10\,\text{V}\times 48\,\text{k}\times 2 = 0.043\ \text{W}$$

| Mechanism | W |

| MOSFET conduction | 26.9 |
| Switching (overlap) | 0.97 |
| Reverse recovery | 0.12 |
| C_oss | 0.02 |
