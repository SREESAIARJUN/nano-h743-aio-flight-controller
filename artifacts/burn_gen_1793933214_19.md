## 0. Architectural Ground Truth and Red Flags

Three findings shape everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production FC + 4-in-1 stack, each motor channel is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU. Typical parts are the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or the EFM8BB21 running BLHeli_S. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. The ESC MCUs run 6-step synchronous commutation at 48 kHz. This document models both sides.
2. **80 A on a "nano" form factor is a burst rating only.** A true 20×20 mm board cannot fit 24 low-Rds(on) FETs with adequate copper. The model assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board** stacked under the FC. Section 3.5 shows that sustained 80 A is thermally impossible at this size. The realistic burst window is about **2 s**.
3. **Bidirectional DShot on ArduPilot requires non-complementary timer channels** (CHx, not CHxN). Each bdshot timer also consumes extra DMA streams for input capture. Pin selection is therefore constrained by DMA, not by convenience.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent Rds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

| Case | R₂₅ | T_j | R_ds(T_j) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical | 1.0 mΩ | 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ |
| Worst | 1.3 mΩ | 125 °C | 1.885 mΩ |

### 2.2 Current path in synchronous 6-step

During any commutation sector, current flows through exactly two switches:

- the PWM'd phase, through its HS FET (for duty D) or its LS FET (for 1−D, synchronous rectification);
- the return phase's LS FET (for 100 % of the period).

Because of this, conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**. Dead-time intervals are the only exception and are treated separately in §2.4.

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 2.3 Ripple contribution

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{pp}\, f_{sw}} \;\xrightarrow{D=0.5}\; \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.6\text{ A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple adds less than 0.06 %. It is negligible at this frequency, which is one argument *for* 48 kHz.

### 2.4 Results per motor channel at 80 A

| Term | Typical | Worst |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction: 2·I²·R_ds | 17.2 W | 24.1 W |
| Copper: I²·R_cu | 3.2 W | 3.2 W |
| **Total conduction** | **20.4 W** | **27.3 W** |

Per-FET loss on the continuously conducting return LS FET is I²R = **8.6 W (typ) / 12.1 W (worst)**. This is the hottest device on the board.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches. The HS device hard-switches against the LS body diode after dead time.

### 3.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{G,on}} = \frac{10 - 3.5}{7.7} = 0.84\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{G,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{G,off}} = \frac{3.5}{4.0} = 0.875\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{I_{G,on}} = \frac{15\ \text{nC}}{0.84} = 17.8\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{15\ \text{nC}}{0.875} = 17.1\ \text{ns}$$
