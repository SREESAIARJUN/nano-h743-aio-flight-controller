# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #34): Power-Stage and DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Upfront Flags

I don't have Job #34's BOM, stack-up, or layout, so this model is **parametric**. Every number comes from the assumptions below. Swap in your datasheet values and the equations still hold. Items marked **[VERIFY]** need checking against your actual parts or the current ArduPilot source.

**Flag 1: Architecture.** ArduPilot on the H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. A 4-in-1 ESC needs a per-motor ESC MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421) running 6-step commutation. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The model is therefore split:
- **ESC side:** 48 kHz switching, conduction, and thermal.
- **FC side:** H743 timer and DMA allocation for bidirectional DShot.

**Flag 2: "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board.** This is only physically defensible as a **short transient (~1–2 s)**, not a sustained rating. Section 3 shows why. Specify the burst duration explicitly in the product spec.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

In any commutation step, one phase leg is PWM'd: the high side (HS) is on for D and the low side (LS) carries the freewheel current for 1−D. A second leg's LS conducts continuously.

**Hot on-resistance:**
$$R_{hot} = k_T \cdot R_{ds(on),25} = 1.6 \times 0.90 = 1.44\ \text{m}\Omega$$

**Base conduction power per FET:**
$$P_0 = I_{ph}^2 R_{hot} = 6400 \times 1.44\times10^{-3} = 9.22\ \text{W}$$

**Per-switch conduction loss during a step:**

| Switch | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| PWM leg, HS | D·P₀ | 8.29 W |
| PWM leg, LS (sync rect.) | (1−D)·P₀ | 0.92 W |
| Return leg, LS | P₀ | 9.22 W |
| **Subtotal per motor** | | **18.43 W** |

**Copper loss** (two conduction paths in series):
$$P_{Cu} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.8\times10^{-3} = 5.12\ \text{W}$$

This term is large. Copper resistance on a nano board is not negligible: 0.4 mΩ per path is roughly 2 oz copper over about 10 mm of width-limited pour.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-leg HS hard-switches. The LS commutates softly through its body diode.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

Gate currents during the plateau:
$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{6.5}{5} = 1.30\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.70\ \text{A}$$

Switching transition times:
$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{16\ \text{nC}}{1.3\ \text{A}} = 12.3\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{16\ \text{nC}}{0.7\ \text{A}} = 22.9\ \text{ns}$$

Loss:
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(35.2\ \text{ns})(48\ \text{kHz}) = 1.70\ \text{W}$$
