# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #47): Power, Thermal, and DShot Timing Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility

**Architecture.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not drive the MOSFET gates. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The model below follows that split.

**Missing inputs.** No MOSFET, PCB stack-up, or burst duration was specified. I used the parametric placeholder device below. Replace its values with your BOM part, since every result scales directly with them.

| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current, per motor | I_ph | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| On-resistance, 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V, max | R_ds25 | 1.0 mΩ |
| On-resistance temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 0.6 %/K (about 1.6× at 125 °C) |
| Gate charges | Q_g / Q_gs2 / Q_gd | 60 / 4 / 10 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge (at high di/dt) | Q_rr | ≈ 80 nC |
| Effective output capacitance | C_oss,tr | ≈ 2 nF |
| Gate drive | V_drv, R_g,ext + R_g,int | 10 V, 4 Ω + 1 Ω |
| Plateau voltage | V_pl | 4 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |

**Feasibility warning.** Four motors at 80 A each means about 320 A drawn from the battery at full throttle, which is about 7.5 kW at 6S. A nano board (20×20 mm mount, roughly 28×28 mm PCB) cannot sustain this. The thermal section shows the burst is limited to about 2–4 seconds with all four channels loaded. Treat "80 A burst" as a transient rating, not a design point.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation pattern.** The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). Within each phase's electrical cycle:
- for 2 of 6 sectors, the high-side FET is PWM'd at duty D, and the low-side FET conducts during the off-time (1 − D);
- for 2 of 6 sectors, the low-side FET is held fully on;
- for 2 of 6 sectors, the phase floats (this is the BEMF sensing window).

**Conduction fraction per FET.**

$$k_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D \qquad k_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3}$$

**Per half-bridge.** The duty terms cancel:

$$P_{cond,HB} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,(k_{HS}+k_{LS}) = \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Per motor (three half-bridges).**

$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}, \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$
