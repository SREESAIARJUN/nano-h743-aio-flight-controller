# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #30): Architecture, Loss Model, Bi-Directional DShot Allocation, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Framing and Assumptions

These are the architectural decisions the rest of the document depends on.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has a dedicated small MCU running AM32: an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM.
   - Running 4× six-step commutation, BEMF zero-cross detection, and ArduPilot on one H743 is not a supported ArduPilot configuration.
   - It would also couple the flight-critical loop to the power-stage fault domain.
2. **"Nano" means a 20×20 mm mounting pattern**, with a board of roughly 25×25 mm. The H743 must therefore be the **TFBGA100 (8×8 mm)** package. LQFP100 (14×14 mm) leaves no room for 24 MOSFETs.
3. **Electrical baseline:**
   - Battery: 6S Li-ion/LiPo, 25.2 V max and 22.2 V nominal.
   - MOSFETs: 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN, R_DS(on),25 ≈ 1.0 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, Q_g ≈ 40 nC.
   - Gate driver: ~1 A effective.
4. **"80 A burst" means 80 A phase current per channel.**
