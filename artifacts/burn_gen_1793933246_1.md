## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In a 4-in-1 AIO:

- **Flight controller MCU (STM32H743, ArduPilot):** generates bidirectional DShot600 commands and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC MCUs (4×, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32):** run the 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation and dead-time.
- **Power stage:** 4 × 3-phase bridges, giving 24 N-channel MOSFETs and 12 half-bridge gate drivers (or 4 × 3-phase drivers).

The 48 kHz switching analysis therefore applies to the ESC side. The DMA/timer analysis applies to the H743.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is AM32's default. In each 60° sector:

- **Phase A, sourcing:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T and the low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) for (1−D)·T.
- **Phase B, sinking:** the low-side FET is fully on.
- **Phase C:** floating, used for back-EMF sensing.

Each phase leg spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle sourcing, 1/3 sinking and 1/3 floating.

### 2.2 Per-Leg Conduction Energy

$$
P_{cond,leg} = I^2 R_{DS}\left[\tfrac{1}{3}D + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3}\right] = \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{DS}
$$

With complementary PWM, conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**: current always flows through exactly two channels.

### 2.3 Per-ESC and Whole-Board Totals

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 3 \cdot \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$
