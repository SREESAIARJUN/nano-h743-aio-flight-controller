# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #9): Power-Stage and DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Design Flags

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small motor-control MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware), its own gate driver, and its own 6-MOSFET bridge. The H743 commands the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. It does not commutate the motors.
