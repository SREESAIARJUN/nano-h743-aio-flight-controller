## 0. Architectural Clarification (Read First)

A 4-in-1 ESC does **not** run its commutation on the STM32H743. The correct partition is:

| Domain | Silicon | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIT6 / VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, mixer, DShot600 bidirectional master |
| Motor control ×4 | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or equivalent Cortex-M4) | AM32 / BLHeli_32-class | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Power stage ×4 | 3-phase gate driver + 6× N-MOSFET each | — | 24 MOSFETs total |
