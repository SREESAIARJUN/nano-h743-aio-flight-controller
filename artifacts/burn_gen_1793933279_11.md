# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #59): Power-Stage Loss, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Model

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. In a 4-in-1 stack it runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071, or BLHeli_32). Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and runs its own commutation. The loss model below applies to the ESC power stage; the DMA model applies to the H743.

**Thermal reality.** "80A" on a 20×20 mm-mount board can only be a **burst** rating. Section 3 shows it is thermally bounded to about 2–3 seconds with all four channels loaded.
