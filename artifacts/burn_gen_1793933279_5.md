## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no ESC commutation firmware. The correct partition is:

```
[STM32H743 FC]--bidir DShot600 (x4, single-wire, open-drain-ish push-pull w/ pull-up)-->[4x ESC MCUs]
                                                                                       (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32,
                                                                                        or EFM8BB51 running Bluejay)
[ESC MCU] --PWM 48 kHz, 6-step--> [3-phase gate driver] --> [6x N-MOSFET half-bridges] --> motor
```

The 48 kHz switching frequency is set in the **ESC firmware** (AM32 `PWM frequency`, Bluejay `PWM 48kHz` build). The H743 only sends throttle commands and receives eRPM telemetry.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two phases are active in each 60° sector. With synchronous PWM, the current always flows through **exactly two MOSFETs in series**:

- the PWM leg's high-side FET (during *D*) or its low-side FET (during 1−*D*, synchronous freewheel), and
- the opposite leg's low-side FET (static ON for 120°).

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{ds(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow k_T \approx 1.5 \text{ at } 125\,^\circ\text{C}$$

### 2.2 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$R_{ds,hot} = 1.8 \times 1.5 = 2.7\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.0027 = \mathbf{34.6\ W\ per\ motor}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET conducts for 120° of 360°, so its duty is 1/3:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds,hot}}{3} = \frac{6400 \times 0.0027}{3} = 5.76\ \text{W}$$

Check: 6 × 5.76 = 34.6 W ✓.

At partial duty *D*, the PWM leg splits this between HS (*D*) and LS (1−*D*). The total bridge loss is unchanged.

### 2.3 PCB copper loss (frequently larger than expected)

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t\,N_{layers}}, \quad \rho_{Cu,100°C} \approx 2.2\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot\text{m}$$

Assumptions:
- Path: 8 mm bus to FET, plus 6 mm to motor pad, w = 3 mm
- 2 oz (70 µm) copper, 4 paralleled layers

$$R_{Cu} \approx \frac{2.2\times10^{-8}\times 0.014}{0.003\times70\times10^{-6}\times4} \approx 0.37\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ per path}$$

With two paths in series:

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.73\ \text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{4.7\ W\ per\ motor}$$

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switching leg incurs hard-switching loss; the static low-side FET does not.

### 3.1 Transition times from gate charge

Assume V_plateau ≈ 3.0 V, driver R_out ≈ 2 Ω source / 1 Ω sink, R_g = 2.2 Ω, V_drv = 10 V.

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_g} = \frac{7}{4.2} = 1.67\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_r = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{10\ \text{nC}}{1.67} \approx 6\ \text{ns}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{snk}+R_g} = \frac{3}{3.2} = 0.94\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_f \approx 10.7\ \text{ns}$$

FD6288-class drivers have current limits well below these ideal values, so I use a **conservative t_r + t_f
