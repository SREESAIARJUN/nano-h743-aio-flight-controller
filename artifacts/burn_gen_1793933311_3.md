## 1. Motor Model and Field-Oriented Control

### 1.1 PMSM model in the rotor (dq) frame

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R\,i_d + L_d\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q \\
v_q &= R\,i_q + L_q\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m) \\
\tau_e &= \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\big[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q) i_d i_q\big] \;\approx\; K_t\, i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m
\end{aligned}
$$

- θₑ = p·θₘ is the electrical angle and ωₑ = p·ωₘ is the electrical speed.
- Gimbal motors are surface-mount, so L_d ≈ L_q = L.
- The control law commands i_d* = 0.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

Park transform:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park, with delay compensation (see 1.4):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\hat\theta_e & -\sin\hat\theta_e\\ \sin\hat\theta_e & \cos\hat\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix},\qquad \hat\theta_e = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.3 Current PI controller (pole–zero cancellation)

The per-axis plant is G_i(s) = 1 / (Ls + R). Choose the PI controller

$$
C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$
