# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC, 20×20 mm (Job #39): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Before the math, two points in the brief would cause problems in a design review.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC's PWM frequency. AM32 lets you set it. It has no relationship to the H743 timers.
2. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm board is a few-second adiabatic rating, not a thermal operating point.** The model below shows the board reaches its limit in about 2 s at 6S. It should be specified that way, with firmware enforcement.

**Baseline assumptions.** Change these and re-run the equations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Device resistance at temperature:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\mathrm{K}$$

**Six-step drive with synchronous rectification (AM32 complementary PWM).** At any instant, two phase legs carry I_ph. The PWM'd leg alternates between its high-side and low-side FET. The other leg's low-side conducts continuously. So two FET-equivalents always carry the full current, regardless of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = \mathbf{30.7\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Copper and solder path.** Use about 0.3 mΩ of phase and bus path per channel (2 oz Cu, 20×20 layout):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 0.3\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = 1.9\ \mathrm{W}$$

**Per-FET distribution, needed for junction temperature:**
- The continuously-on low-side FET takes the worst share: 6400 × 2.4 mΩ / 3 ≈ 5.1 W averaged over a commutation cycle.
- The peak instantaneous dissipation within its 120° interval is 15.4 W. Use the peak for transient Z_th.

**Sinusoidal/FOC alternative:**

$$P_{cond} = 3\, I_{rms}^2 R_{DS},\quad I_{rms} = I_{pk}/\sqrt{2}$$
