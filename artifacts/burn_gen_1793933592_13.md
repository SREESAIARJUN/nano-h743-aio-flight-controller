**Architectural note.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 / GD32E230 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The netlist below reflects this.

**Feasibility verdict (detailed in §3):**
- **80 A on one channel:** burst-viable for about 2–5 s.
- **80 A on all four channels at once (≈ 80 W of heat on a 9 cm² board):** board copper reaches its limit in about **1.5–2 s**.
- **Continuous rating** with prop-wash cooling: about **30 A per channel**.

"80 A burst" is honest only with an explicit duration (≤ 2 s on all four channels, ≤ 5 s on a single channel) and firmware current limiting.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two of the three phases carry current. The path runs from the high-side FET of phase A, through the motor, to the low-side FET of phase B.

During PWM:
- **On-time (D):** the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.
- **Off-time (1−D):** the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectifier) and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.

So **two FETs are always in series with the phase current**, independent of D.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent evaluation (I_ph = 80 A)

| T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond per channel (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.00 | 12.8 |
| 75 | 1.23 | 15.7 |
| 100 | 1.34 | 17.1 |
| 125 | 1.45 | 18.6 |

### 1.3 Copper (PCB) conduction loss

Sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) copper:

$$
R_\square = \frac{\rho_{Cu}}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\,(1+0.0039\cdot 60)}{70\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.30\ \text{m}\Omega/\square \;\;(@85^\circ\text{C})
$$

Assume each phase path spans about 3 squares on the bus side and 3 on the phase side, with paralleling across 2 layers. That gives about 0.9 mΩ effective, or about 0.45 mΩ round-trip per channel after paralleling and via stitching.

$$
P_{Cu,ch} \approx 80^2 \cdot 0.45\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 2.9\ \text{W}
$$

The copper loss is not negligible. It is about 15% of the FET loss and is spread into the board.

### 1.4 Thermal-runaway check

Because R_DS(T) rises with temperature, the closed loop is stable only if:

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{th} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; I^2 \cdot 2R_{25}\alpha \cdot R_{th} = 6400\cdot 0.002\cdot 0.0045\cdot R_{th} = 0.0576\,R_{th} < 1
$$

This is stable for R_th < 17 K/W per channel. Section 3 gives about 22 K/W per channel to ambient in steady state. **That case is unstable, so an 80 A continuous rating is physically impossible on this board** and firmware current limiting is mandatory.
