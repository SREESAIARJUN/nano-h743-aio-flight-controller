# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Derivation (Job #1)

**Scope:** 20×20 mm (M3, 25.5 mm-class) AIO stack, 2–6S, 4× BLDC channels, ArduPilot on STM32H743, bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.

---

## 0. Architectural Correction: Two MCU Domains

An H743 running ArduPilot does **not** commutate the motors itself. ArduPilot has no 6-step or FOC commutation layer. The architecture is therefore split into two domains:

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM consumer (harmonic notch) |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 class (one per motor) | AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection, DShot slave, eRPM reply |

All loss math below concerns the ESC power stage. All DMA math concerns the H743.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D. The low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Static leg:** the LS conducts continuously for the 60° sector.

At any instant, exactly two channels carry I_m. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_m^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

This expression is **independent of D**, because synchronous rectification keeps two FETs in the path at all times.

### 2.2 Numbers at T_j = 110 °C

$$R_{ds}(110) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.006 \times 85) = 1.81\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.81\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.2\ W}$$

### 2.3 Per-device average

Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs carries an equal share:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{23.2}{6} \approx 3.9\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Electrothermal stability

Conduction loss rises with temperature, which raises R_ds, which raises loss. The loop converges only if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th,ja} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 2 I_m^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{th,ja} < 1$$

At 80 A: 2 × 6400 × 1.2e-3 × 0.006 = 0.092 W/K. This demands R_th,ja < 10.8 K/W for **the whole ESC channel**. That is achievable only transiently on a nano board. See §5.

### 2.5 PCB copper and shunt

**Phase trace, 2 oz, 3 parallel layers, 3 mm × 6 mm:**

ρ = 1.72e-8 Ω·m, t = 70 µm

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t\,n} \approx 0.16\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P = 80^2 \times 0.16\times10^{-3} \approx 1.0\ \text{W per conducting path}$$

**Shunt (0.25 mΩ, battery side, carries I_bat = D·I_m):** at D = 1, P = 1.6 W.

Include the shunt only if fitted. Most nano 4-in-1 boards use a single shared shunt.
