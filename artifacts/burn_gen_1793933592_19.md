**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not drive the gates directly. It is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which commutates its own bridge. The FC talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Instantaneous loss per channel

In six-step drive, two phase legs carry current I at any instant:
- one leg is held statically on;
- the PWM leg alternates between its high-side and low-side FET, with the complementary switch synchronously rectifying.

So two FETs are always in the current path:

$$P_{cond,ch}(T_j) = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At 80 A:

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel | Per FET (÷6, averaged over commutation) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.6 W | 2.77 W |
| 100 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 22.3 W | 3.71 W |
| 125 °C | 1.89 mΩ | 24.1 W | 4.02 W |

Peak per-FET loss while it is in the conduction path is 2× the averaged value.

### 1.2 Additional resistive losses at 80 A

| Element | Resistance | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt | 0.25 mΩ | I²R = 1.6 W |
| PCB copper, 2 oz | ρ/t = 1.72×10⁻⁸ / 70×10⁻⁶ = 0.246 mΩ/sq; ~4 squares effective per phase path across paralleled layers ≈ 0.3 mΩ | ≈ 1.9 W |

### 1.3 Electrothermal fixed point (closed form)

Let P₂₅ be the loss at 25 °C and R_θ the effective thermal resistance. Define x = T_j − 25. Then:

$$x = (T_a-25) + R_\theta P_{25}(1+\alpha x)\;\Rightarrow\; x=\frac{(T_a-25)+R_\theta P_{25}}{1-\alpha R_\theta P_{25}}$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:** α·R_θ·P₂₅ ≥ 1.

Worked example, per FET in steady state, with P₂₅ = 2.77 W, R_θJA,eff = 25 °C/W (4-layer board, prop-wash airflow) and T_a = 40 °C:
- α·R_θ·P₂₅ = 0.0045 × 25 × 2.77 = 0.31, so the design is stable.
- x = (15 + 69.3) / 0.69 = 122 → **T_j ≈ 147 °C.**

That is over the 150 °C maximum once switching loss is added. **80 A is not a continuous rating on a nano board. It must be treated as a transient (burst) rating.**

### 1.4 Burst (transient) model

The board is dominated by lumped thermal capacitance C_th:
- Board mass ≈ 6 g FR4/Cu, roughly 3 J/K for FR4 and 1.5 J/K for Cu.
- Plus FETs and heatsink pad: **C_th ≈ 5 J/K.**

Total dissipation with all four channels at 80 A and hot:

| Source | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 4 × 24.1 | 96 W |
| Switching (§2) | 4 × 2.4 | 9.6 W |
| Shunt + copper | 4 × 3.5 | 14 W |
| **Total** | | **≈ 120 W** |

Response to a burst:

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) = P R_{\theta,BA}\left(1-e^{-t/(R_{\theta,BA}C_{th})}\right),\quad R_{\theta,BA}\approx 3\ ^\circ C/W \text{ (board to air, in prop-wash)}$$

- Time constant τ = 15 s.
- Initial slope = P / C_th ≈ **24 K/s.**

Adding the junction-to-board rise of each FET:
- ΔT_jb = P_FET × R_θJB = 4 W × 2 °C/W ≈ 8 K.
- The junction tracks the board within ~1 s, because the die/package τ is tens of ms.

**Burst limit from 40 °C ambient to T_j = 125 °C:**
- Allowable board rise = 125 − 40 − 8 = 77 K.
- t = −τ·ln(1 − 77 / (120 × 3)) = −15 × ln(0.786) ≈ **3.6 s with all four motors at 80 A.**
- With one motor at 80 A and the others at hover current, the limit is about 15 s.

**Specification to publish:** "80 A burst ≤ 3 s (4 motors), 10 s (single), 35–40 A continuous."

The continuous figure follows from the fixed-point model at P such that T_j ≤ 110 °C.
