## 0. Architecture Decision

An STM32H743 does not commutate four BLDC motors in this class of product. The architecture is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, sends bidirectional DShot600 ×4 |
| ESC MCUs ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | Drives 6 FETs per channel |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | 6 per motor |

The rest of this document models that system.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology Current Paths
The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant two phase legs carry current:

- The PWM leg's high-side FET conducts for duty D.
- That leg's low-side FET synchronously rectifies for 1−D.
- The return leg's low-side FET conducts 100% of the time.

So at every instant exactly two channels (FETs) are in series with the motor current:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{cu}$$

### 2.2 Ripple Correction
For a triangular ripple with peak-to-peak value ΔI:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right),\quad r = \frac{\Delta I}{I}$$

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

With L_ph = 15 µH and D = 0.9 at full throttle, the high-throttle ripple is about 3 A, so r = 0.04. The correction is under 0.02%, so I neglect it.

### 2.3 Temperature Dependence
$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 2.4 Numbers per Channel at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds(on) | P_cond,FET (2 FETs) | P_copper | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 1.9 W | **21.1 W** |
| 100 °C | 2.06 mΩ | 26.4 W | 1.9 W | **28.3 W** |
| 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ | 28.8 W | 1.9 W | **30.7 W** |

**Per-FET average:** each of the six FETs carries the current pair-path roughly one third of the electrical cycle. At 100 °C that is about 4.4 W conduction per FET, with high-side FETs additionally taking all the switching loss.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg's high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side FET switches at near-zero voltage after its body diode commutates.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48 k = **0.968 W** |
