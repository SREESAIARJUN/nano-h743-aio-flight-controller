# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #31): Electro-Thermal Model, Bi-directional DShot DMA Plan and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to each ESC MCU, receiving eRPM telemetry in return. Everything below assumes this split.

**Key risk.** 80 A per motor on a nano-class board is a short-burst rating only. The model below shows it is thermally sustainable for seconds, not continuously. Treat every number as a design estimate to validate with thermocouple and IR testing.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery (6S, max) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 or 5×6) | R_ds(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ at V_gs = 10 V |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0060 /K |
| Rise + fall time | t_r + t_f | 20 ns (≈ 2 Ω gate drive) |
| Dead time | t_dt | 50 ns per edge |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 40 nC |
| Gate drive voltage | V_gs | 10 V |
| Board size (30.5 mm mount) | — | 36 × 36 mm |
| Stackup | — | 8 layers, 2 oz copper, 80 % fill |
| Ambient | T_a | 35 °C |

Commutation is 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification. FOC changes the conduction expressions to I_rms²-based terms per phase.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent resistance:**

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]

**Conduction path.** In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly two FETs:
- During the on-time: the PWM high side of one phase plus the static low side of another phase.
- During the off-time: the complementary low side (sync rectifier) plus the same static low side.

So per motor channel:

P_cond,ch = 2 · I² · R_ds(T_j)

**At 80 A and T_j = 100 °C:**
- R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 75) = 1.45 mΩ
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.45e-3 = **18.6 W per motor**

**Per-FET values:**
- Instantaneous, while conducting: I²R = **9.3 W**
- Electrical-period average (spread over 6 FETs): 18.6 / 6 ≈ **3.1 W**

**Thermal runaway check.** The loop is stable if:

S = (∂P/∂T_j) · R_th,ja = P_cond,25 · α · R_th,ja < 1

- Per FET: P_cond,25 · α ≈ 2.13 W × 0.006 ≈ 0.0128 W/K
- With R_th,ja,eff ≈ 25 K/W: S ≈ 0.32

The FET is stable. Solve T_j by fixed-point iteration:

T_j(k+1) = T_a + P(T_j(k)) · R_th

It converges in 3–4 iterations.
