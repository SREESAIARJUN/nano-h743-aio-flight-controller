# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Architecture, Loss Model, DMA Allocation, Netlists

## 0. Scope, Corrections, and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC commutation controller in practice. ArduPilot runs on the H743 flight controller (FC). Each ESC channel runs its own small MCU with AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs. The 48 kHz switching is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743. Everything below follows that split.
