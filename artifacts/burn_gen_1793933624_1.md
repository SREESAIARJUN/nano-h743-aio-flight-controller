## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

I have no prior project file for "Job #40", so every part number and coefficient below is a stated assumption. The model is parametric. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values and re-run Sections 1–3.

**Architecture clarification.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. The 4-in-1 ESC has four independent ESC MCUs (assumed AT32F421 running AM32). Those MCUs generate the **48 kHz** bridge PWM. The H743 never switches the FETs directly. The 48 kHz figure therefore belongs to the ESC firmware setting (AM32 `PWM_FREQ = 48 kHz`), not to H743 timers.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.005 × 85) = **1.71 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM

Within each 60° electrical sector the phase current always flows through exactly two switch positions in series. One is the PWM leg, where the high side (HS) conducts for D and the low side (LS) synchronously rectifies for 1−D. The other is the static leg, where the LS is on for the whole 120° window.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.71\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{21.9\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET position is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \frac{6400 \times 1.71\text{m}}{3} = \mathbf{3.65\ W}$$

During its active window, the instantaneous dissipation is 10.9 W. Averaging is valid when the electrical period is much shorter than the junction-to-case time constant (τ_jc ≈ 5 ms). Example: 14-pole motor at 25 kRPM gives 175k eRPM, so T_e ≈ 0.34
