# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Architecture & Mathematical Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Corrections

I have no prior context for "Job #28," so I've set baseline assumptions below. Replace them with your BOM values and the equations will carry through.

**Correction 1: the 48 kHz PWM does not come from the STM32H743.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. A working architecture uses:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► 4 × ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware)
                                              └─► 3-phase gate driver ──► 6 MOSFETs per motor
```

The H743 owns the DShot timing. Each ESC MCU owns the 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead-time, and commutation.

**Correction 2: 80 A per motor is a transient rating, not continuous, on a nano board.** The math below shows that all four channels at 80 A saturate the board thermally in about 2 seconds. Marketing an "80A" nano board without a burst duration is how boards get returned burnt.
