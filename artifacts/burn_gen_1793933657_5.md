## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology current paths
In 6-step trapezoidal commutation, two phase legs conduct at any instant:
- one **PWM leg**, where the high side (HS) is on for D and the low side (LS) is synchronous for (1−D);
- one **fixed leg**, where the LS is on 100 % of the 60° sector.

The instantaneous conduction path is therefore always **two R_ds(on) in series**:

$$P_{cond,ESC}=2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)
Evaluated at T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}(110^\circ\text{C}) = 1.3\,\text{m}\Omega\,[1+0.0055(85)] \approx 1.91\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2(80)^2(1.91\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{24.4\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-device distribution
Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET}=\frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3}= \frac{6400 \times 1.91\text{m}}{3} = 4.07\ \text{W}$$
