## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

$\theta_{off}$ is found at calibration by applying $V_d>0, V_q=0$ (the rotor aligns to $d$) and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke / Park transforms

Phase currents $i_a, i_b$ are measured; $i_c=-i_a-i_b$.

Clarke:
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park:
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant and decoupled PI current control

$dq$ dynamics (SPM machine, $L_d=L_q=L$):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

PI with feedforward decoupling, plus back-EMF feedforward ($\lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$):

$$v_d^* = K_{p,i}e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = K_{p,i}e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Pole–zero cancellation tuning** for closed-loop bandwidth $\omega_c$, constrained by $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

With $f_{pwm}=20$ kHz, choose $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1000$. This gives $K_{p,i}=13.2$ and $K_{i,i}=35{,}186$.

### 1.4 Voltage limiting (circular)

$$|v^*|\le \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}$$

Prioritize $d$. Clamp $v_q$ to $\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2}$, and back-calculate the integrators (§2.2).

### 1.5 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max injection)

Inverse Park:
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

Phase voltages:
$$v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

Zero-sequence injection and duty cycles:
$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$
