## 1. Loop Architecture and Rates

```
 Camera attitude ref ─►[Attitude P + rate FF]──► ω_cam* ──►[Inverse Jacobian]──► q̇* (joint rates)
                                ▲                                                     │
                     q̂ (Mahony, fused IMU)                                            ▼
                                │                               ┌──[Velocity PI]◄── q̇̂ (gyro→joint, filtered)
  IMU A ─┐                      │                               │        │
         ├─[align+fuse]─[dyn. notch ×2]─[LPF]──────────────────┘        ▼ i_q,fb
  IMU B ─┘                                                    + i_q,ff (anti-cogging LUT + LMS)
                                                                         │
                                                       [Current PI d/q + decoupling] ─► SVPWM
                                                                         ▲
                                                              θ_e (encoder), i_a, i_b
```

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s |
| IMU sample, fuse, filter | 8 kHz | 125 µs | — |
| Velocity (rate) | 4 kHz | 250 µs | ω_v = 2π·60 rad/s |
| Attitude (angle) | 1 kHz | 1 ms | ω_θ = 2π·12 rad/s |

The bandwidth separation is about 5× or more per stage. This is required for the cascaded design assumption that each inner loop behaves as a unity gain to the outer loop.

## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 2.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: inject i_d = I_align at θ_e = 0, wait for the rotor to settle, then record θ_m.

### 2.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 2.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$
