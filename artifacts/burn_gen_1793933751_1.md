# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #16): Electro-Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs. Each motor channel is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071, at the 48 kHz PWM rate. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. This document therefore covers two domains:

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses trapezoidal six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- At any instant, motor current flows through exactly one high-side path and one low-side path.
- In the PWM'd leg, the high-side conducts during D·T and its synchronous low-side conducts during (1−D)·T.
- The non-PWM leg's low-side conducts 100% of the time.
- The resistance seen by the current is therefore always 2·R_ds, independent of duty cycle. With synchronous rectification, duty cycle redistributes loss between devices but does not change the total.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × (1 + 0.0055 × 75) = **2.12 mΩ**
