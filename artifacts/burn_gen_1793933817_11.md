# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control & Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #59 specification, so the design below is fully parameterized. **Section 0** lists every assumed constant. Replace them with your motor and frame data, and the equations and code will remain valid.

## 0. Assumptions & Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value | Symbol |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (GBM2804 class) | $N_s=12,\ p=7$ pole pairs |
| Phase resistance / inductance | 5.6 Ω / 1.8 mH (line-neutral) | $R_s, L_s$ |
| Torque constant | 0.045 N·m/A | $K_t$ |
| Load inertia per axis (incl. camera) | $2\times10^{-4}$ kg·m² (pitch) | $J$ |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701/AS5048A) | $\theta_m$ |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on SPI @ 24 MHz, 8 kHz ODR | — |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G4/H7), single-precision FPU | — |

**Loop rates (multirate cascade, integer-divided from the PWM ISR):**

| Loop | Rate | $T_s$ | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| PWM / current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1500$ rad/s |
| Velocity + cogging FF | 4 kHz | 250 µs | $\omega_v = 2\pi\cdot 120$ rad/s |
| IMU filtering + attitude | 4 kHz (decimated from 8 kHz) | 250 µs | — |
| Angle (stabilization) | 1 kHz | 1 ms | $\omega_\theta = 2\pi\cdot 25$ rad/s |

Separation rule: each outer loop's bandwidth should be no more than about 1/4 to 1/5 of the inner loop's. The chain $1500 \to 120 \to 25$ Hz satisfies this. It also keeps the angle loop below the first structural mode of a typical carbon/aluminium gimbal arm (≈80–200 Hz).

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \pmod{2\pi}$$

Here $\theta_0$ is the encoder offset, found at commissioning by applying $V_d$ with $\theta_e = 0$ and reading the encoder after settling.

**Latency compensation.** The encoder sample precedes the PWM update by one period:

$$\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + p\,\hat\omega_m\,(1.5\,T_{pwm})$$

The factor 1.5 accounts for one sample
