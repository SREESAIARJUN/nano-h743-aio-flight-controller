## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A production 4-in-1 design needs a dedicated ESC MCU per motor, because 48 kHz complementary PWM with BEMF zero-crossing detection for four motors is a hard-real-time job that ArduPilot's ChibiOS scheduler should not own.

```
                ┌─────────────────────── FC SECTION ───────────────────────┐
 VBAT ─► 5V/3V3 │ STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot)                                 │
                │  TIM1_CH1..CH4 ──► DShot600 bidirectional (single wire)   │
                └──────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────────────┘
                       ▼          ▼          ▼          ▼
                ┌──────────────── ESC SECTION (4 identical channels) ───────┐
                │ AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 (48 kHz PWM, 6-step)    │

                │ 6× N-MOSFET 40V half-bridges, bulk cap, shunt, BEMF divider│
                └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology

The ESC uses trapezoidal 6-step commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM.

- At every instant, current I flows through two bridge "positions":
  - The PWM leg, where the high-side (HS) or low-side (LS) device conducts depending on PWM state.
  - The return leg, where the LS device is continuously on.
- With complementary PWM, the PWM leg always conducts through exactly one of its two FETs.

Total conduction loss per ESC is therefore independent of duty cycle, to first order:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The temperature dependence of R_DS(on) is:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

### 2.2 Electrothermal Fixed Point

Loss raises T_j, which raises R_DS(on), which raises loss. Solve iteratively:

$$
T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,P_{FET}\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right)
$$

Convergence requires:

$$
R_\theta \cdot \frac{\partial P}{\partial T} = R_\theta\,I^2 R_{ds25}\,\alpha < 1
$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A Burst

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{ds} = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1+0.0055 \times 85) = 1.76\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.76\,\text{m}\Omega = 22.5\ \text{W}
$$

Per-FET average: each device conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Losses are concentrated on the LS devices at low duty and on the HS devices at high duty.

| Device | Peak share | Average share |
|---|---|---|
| Worst-case single FET | ~I²R while conducting | ≈ 22.5 / 6 × (1.3–1.6 imbalance) ≈ **5–6 W** |

### 2.4 Copper Path Losses (often forgotten)

| Path | Approx. R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Battery pads to bus (2 oz, short, multi-layer) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W (shared) |
| Phase output trace + motor pad | 0.4 mΩ ×2 conducting | 5.1 W |
| Shunt (if used) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |

**Copper subtotal: about 8 W per ESC at burst.** Pour every layer under the power path and stitch it densely with vias (≥ 0.3 mm drill).

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step operation only **one leg hard-switches** at any time.

### 3.1 V-I Overlap (Hard Switching)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}
$$

$$
P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = 1.94\ \text{W}
$$

Here t_r and t_f come from the gate-drive analysis, using the Miller plateau:

$$
t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}, \quad
t_{f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{plateau}}
$$

### 3.2 Reverse Recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.3 Output Capacitance

$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = 0.015\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}
$$

Tightening dead time to 50 ns halves this, provided the driver's propagation mismatch allows it.

### 3.5 Gate Drive (charged from the driver supply, not the FET)

$$
P_g = 6\,Q_g\,V_{drv}\,f_{sw} = 6 \times 50\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.144\ \text{W}
$$

The regulator feeding the driver must supply about 15 mA per ESC.

### 3.6 Totals per ESC at 80 A Burst, T_j ≈ 110 °C

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 22.5 |
| Switching (overlap) | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.06 |
| Gate drive | 0.14 |
| Copper / shunt | ~8.0 |
| **Total per ESC** | **≈ 33 W** |
| **All 4 at burst** | **≈ 133 W** |

**Efficiency check.** Output power at 80 A, 6S, roughly 85% duty is about 1.7 kW per ESC, so η ≈ 98%. That is fine electrically, but heat **density** is the real problem.

**Frequency trade.** Switching losses scale linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz you save about 1.3 W per ESC. At 96 kHz you add about 2.6 W. 48 kHz is a reasonable balance for low-inductance quad motors and quiet operation.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Steady-State Network (per FET)

```
Tj ──[Rθjc ≈1.5 K/W]── Tcase ──[Rθ,pad+vias ≈ 4–8 K/W]── Tboard ──[Rθ,board-air]── Tamb
```

Board-to-air resistance for the 36×36 mm board, both sides, with prop wash h ≈ 80–150 W/m²K:

$$
R_{\theta,ba} = \frac{1}{h\,A} = \frac{1}{100 \times 2 \times 0.036^2} \approx 3.9\ \text{K/W (whole board)}
$$

**Steady state at full 80 A on all four motors:**

$$
\Delta T = 133 \times 3.9 \approx 520\ \text{K}
$$

This is physically impossible. **80 A is a transient-only rating.**

**Continuous capability.** For ΔT_board ≤ 50 K, total dissipation must stay at or below about 13 W, which is about 3.2 W per ESC. Inverting the loss model gives about **27 A continuous per ESC, all four simultaneously**, in prop wash. A realistic marketing pairing is 35–45 A continuous with a heatsink, or with large inner copper planes acting as a spreader.

### 4.2 Transient (Burst) Model: Lumped Thermal Capacitance

| Element | Mass | c_p | C_th |
|---|---|---|---|
| PCB (FR4 + 2 oz × 4 layers) | ~7 g | ~0.9 J/gK effective | 6.3 J/K |
| 24 MOSFETs + caps + pads | ~3 g | ~0.7 J/gK | 2.1 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 8.4 J/K** |

Board time constant:

$$
\tau = R_\theta C_{th} = 3.9 \times 8.4 \approx 33\ \text{s}
$$

$$
T_{board}(t) = T_0 + P R_\theta\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right)
