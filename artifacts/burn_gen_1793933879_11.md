# Job #28: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) System Model

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flag

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the MOSFETs. The 4-in-1 ESC section needs four dedicated ESC MCUs (an AT32F421 or STM32G071 class part running AM32/BLHeli_32-compatible firmware). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 talks to them only over bidirectional DShot600.

**Feasibility warning.** The model in Section 3 shows that 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm "nano" footprint is only a **burst rating, limited to seconds**:

- All four motors at 80 A: about 3 s.
- One motor at 80 A: about 12 s.

Treat the "80 A" figure as a transient specification, not a continuous one. Continuous capability on this footprint is roughly 25–35 A per channel with prop-wash airflow. The numbers below are first-order engineering estimates built on the stated assumptions. Validate them against the selected MOSFET datasheets and thermal-camera testing before release.

## 2. Loss Model (per channel, at 80 A)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

First, correct R_DS(on) for junction temperature:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0\,(1+0.005\cdot75) = 1.375\ \text{m}\Omega$$

With synchronous rectification in 6-step commutation, exactly two FETs carry the phase current at any instant: one in the sourcing leg (high-side or its complementary low-side during PWM-off) and one low-side in the sinking leg.

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\cdot 6400\cdot 1.375\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.6\ W}$$

Because each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, the average loss per FET is 17.6 / 6 ≈ **2.9 W**. The instantaneous loss in a conducting FET is I²R = **8.8 W**.
