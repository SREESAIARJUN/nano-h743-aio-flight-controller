# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #44): Electrothermal and DShot Design Basis

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two scope issues affect everything downstream.

1. **The H743 does not drive the power stage.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and dead-time. The switching-loss model below applies to the ESC MCU's PWM, not to an H743 timer.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano form factor is only a transient rating.** The math in §3 shows that four channels at 80 A cannot be sustained on a 30×30 mm PCB for more than about 1–2 s. The design must treat 80 A as a time-limited burst with firmware current and thermal limiting.
