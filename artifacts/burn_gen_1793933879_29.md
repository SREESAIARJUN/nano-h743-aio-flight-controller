## 1. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

**A1. Partitioning.** The STM32H743 is not the motor commutation controller. It runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600 frames. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the **48 kHz** bridge PWM and returns eRPM telemetry. Driving 12 FETs at 48 kHz with sensorless BEMF commutation directly from the H743 is possible in principle, but ArduPilot does not support it.
