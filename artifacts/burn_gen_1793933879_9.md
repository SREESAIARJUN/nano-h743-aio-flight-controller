## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

At any instant in 120° six-step commutation, two phases are active:

- One leg is PWM-switched (high-side ↔ low-side complementary).
- The other leg's low-side is held on.

The motor current I therefore always flows through exactly **two FET channels**. Synchronous rectification means the freewheel path is also a channel, not a diode, except during dead time.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 0.7 × 1.49 ≈ **1.04 mΩ**. Use 1.05 mΩ.

### 1.3 Per-motor conduction loss

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{trace} + I^2 R_{shunt}$$

| Term | Value | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 2 × R_DS(on) at 100 °C | 2.10 mΩ | **13.4 W** |
| PCB copper, phase + bus path (estimate) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Shunt (if per-motor, 0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| **Total** | | **≈ 16.9 W** |

The shunt and copper losses go into the board, not the FET junctions. They still heat the same copper mass.

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each phase is active for 2/3 of the time. Each phase's high-side and low-side split that active window depending on duty D and sector. At high throttle (D → 1), the worst-case FET is the high-side of the PWM leg.

$$P_{HS,peak} \approx I^2 R \cdot D \approx 6.7\ \mathrm{W}\ \text{(instantaneous, while in its sector)}$$

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{13.4\ \mathrm{W}}{6} \approx 2.2\ \mathrm{W}\ \text{(cycle-averaged)}$$

At 80 A and a typical eRPM of 30–60 k, the electrical period is about 1–2 ms. Junction thermal time constants are about 1–10 ms, so the junction partially follows the commutation ripple. Budget **+5–10 K** of ripple above the averaged T_j.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. Per motor, per PWM period: one turn-on and one turn-off edge.

### 2.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

The plateau voltage is V_pl ≈ 4.5 V and the driver supply is V_DRV = 12 V. With R_g,total ≈ 4 Ω (internal + external):

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{DRV}-V_{pl}}{R_g} \approx 1.5\ \mathrm{A (driver-limited)},\qquad t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{13\,nC}{1.5\,A} \approx 8.7\ \mathrm{ns}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} \approx 1.1\ \mathrm{A},\qquad t_f \approx \frac{13\,nC}{1.1\,A} \approx 11.8\ \mathrm{ns}$$

In practice, layout inductance adds ringing, so use **t_r + t_f ≈ 25 ns**.

### 2.2 Loss terms per motor at V_bus = 25.2 V, I = 80 A

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 25 ns · 48 k = **1.21 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_F · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 · 80 · 200 ns · 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 50 nC · 25.2 · 48 k = **0.06 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw (C_oss ≈ 1.5 nF) | **0.02 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs switching) | 2 · Q_g · V_DRV · f_sw | 2 · 60 nC · 12 · 48 k = **0.07 W** (mostly in driver/R_g) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 1.97 W** |
