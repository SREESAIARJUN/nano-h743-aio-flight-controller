# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #26)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Corrections

I don't have project-specific documentation for Job #26, so everything below rests on stated assumptions. Replace them with your actual BOM values and the equations will carry through.

Three corrections to the brief:

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production AIO board, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to four separate ESC MCUs (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). ArduPilot has no on-FC 6-step commutation. The architecture below reflects this.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano-format board is thermally marginal.** The math in §4 shows that 4 motors × 80A sustained is physically impossible on a 20×20 or 30×30 board. 80A is achievable only as a short per-motor transient, and you need a firmware current limit to enforce that.
3. **The netlist is a representative reference design, not a verified layout.** Pin numbers for the gate driver and MOSFETs must be checked against the datasheets of the parts you actually select. I give nets by function so pin-out errors can't propagate silently.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Paths in 6-Step Complementary PWM

At any commutation step, current flows from phase X to phase Y:

- **High-side FET of X** conducts I for fraction D of each PWM period.
- **Low-side FET of X** (synchronous rectifier) conducts I for fraction 1−D.
- **Low-side FET of Y** conducts I continuously (100 %).

Total conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds,on

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.5 mΩ.

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}
$$
