## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Flags

No MOSFET part number, board stack-up, or cell count was specified. Every number below uses the **stated placeholder parameters (§1)**. Re-run the model with datasheet values once parts are chosen. Spreadsheet-ready formulas are provided throughout.

**Flag 1: The H743 does not commutate motors.**
- In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1, each motor is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design specifies 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32.
- The H743 only emits bidirectional DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry.
- The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.

**Flag 2: "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) form factor is thermally transient-only.**
- §4 shows roughly **1 s of all-four-channel 80 A** before Tj reaches 150 °C.
- This must be stated in the product specification. Firmware current limiting is required (AM32 `CURRENT_LIMIT`, plus ArduPilot `BATT_*` limits).

**Flag 3: Interpretation of "80 A burst".**
- "80 A" is interpreted as **per channel, phase-current RMS, 6S**. This is the industry convention.
- The battery bus can therefore see up to 320 A peak, which drives the input path design (§5).
