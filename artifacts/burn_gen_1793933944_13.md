## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two premises in the request need correcting before any math is useful.

| Premise | Reality | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" | The H743 runs **ArduPilot as the flight controller**. Each ESC channel still needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 does not commutate motors under ArduPilot. | Bidirectional DShot is a link **between** the H743 and four on-board ESC MCUs. |
| "80A on a nano (20×20 mm) board" | The model below shows that a 20×20 board can sustain roughly **20–25 A/channel continuous**. 80 A is a **sub-second to ~1 s burst** only. | Firmware must enforce current and thermal limits. Marketing claims must say "burst." |

The analysis below proceeds on that basis: an all-in-one board with an H743 FC, 4× ESC MCUs, a 6S input, and 80 A transient per channel.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, exactly two phases carry I_ph:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Phase B (return leg):** the LS FET is fully on.
- **Phase C:** floating, used for back-EMF sensing.

At every instant, current passes through **exactly two channels' worth of R_DS**. Dead time is handled separately in §3.4.

**Instantaneous bridge conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS,hot}$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

R_DS rises with junction temperature T_j:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + k\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad k \approx 0.006\ /^\circ\text{C}$$

This gives α = 1.6 at 125 °C.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.24\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{28.7\ W}$$

Each FET conducts for one-third of the electrical cycle, so the per-FET average is:

$$\bar{P}_{cond,FET} = \frac{28.7}{6} \approx 4.8\ \text{W}$$
