## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7-class, FPU, 170+ MHz |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701), one per axis |
| IMU-P | ICM-42688-P on the **payload** (camera plate). Primary attitude feedback. |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park is the transpose. SVPWM uses min-max zero-sequence injection:

$$
v_{x}' = v_x - \tfrac12\left(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c) + \min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\right),\qquad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{bus}}
$$

The linear range is $|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$.

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
L\frac{di_d}{dt} = v_d - R i_d + \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
L\frac{di_q}{dt} = v_q - R i_q - \omega_e L i_d - \omega_e\lambda
$$

Torque:

$$
\tau = K_t i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda
$$

### 1.3 PI with pole-zero cancellation

Choose $C(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with:

$$
K_p = L\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\omega_c
$$

The cancellation gives an open loop of $\omega_c/s$, so each axis behaves as a first-order closed loop:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{1}{1+s/\omega_c}
$$

Account for the ZOH and computation delay $T_d \approx 1.5\,T_s$. For a phase margin of at least 60°, keep:

$$
\omega_c \le \frac{\pi/6}{1.5T_s}
$$

At 20 kHz this means $\omega_c \le 2\pi\cdot 1.1$ kHz; 1–1.5 kHz is the practical limit.
