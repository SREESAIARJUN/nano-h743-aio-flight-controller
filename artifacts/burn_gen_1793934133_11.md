## 1. FOC Inner Loop (Current Control)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform** with electrical angle θₑ = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform**:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The dq-frame motor model is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The PI controller is placed so its zero cancels the electrical pole (R/L):

$$
K_p^{(i)} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i^{(i)} = R\,\omega_{ci}
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}
$$

Choose ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_s / 13 to leave margin for the 1.5-sample PWM/computation delay. At 20 kHz that gives ω_ci ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s.
