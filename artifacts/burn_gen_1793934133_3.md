## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform (with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant model (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32\, p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI design by pole-zero cancellation

The controller is

$$
C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}, \qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

With this choice the PI zero K_i/K_p = R/L cancels the plant pole, and the closed loop becomes

$$
\frac{I}{I^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s + \omega_c}
$$

To discretize, include the 1.5·Ts computation-plus-PWM delay. Keep ω_c ≤ 1/(6·Ts) for at least 45° of phase margin. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≤ 3333 rad/s, so 2π·500 to 2π·1500 rad/s is safe.

### 1.4 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

### 1.5 Voltage limit and anti-windup

Limit the voltage vector circularly:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max}, \qquad m_{max} = 0.95
$$

Give d-axis priority: v_q,max = √(V_max² − v_d²).

Use back-calculation anti-windup on each PI integrator, with K_aw = 1/K_p:

$$
I[k+1] = I[k] + K_i T_s\, e[k] + K_{aw}\,(u_{sat} - u_{unsat})
$$
