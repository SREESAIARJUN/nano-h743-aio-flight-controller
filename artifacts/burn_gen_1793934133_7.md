## 0. Assumed System Baseline

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, single-precision FPU) |
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per joint (MT6701 / AS5048A), SPI |
| IMUs | IMU_P: ICM-42688-P on the camera payload. IMU_B: ICM-42688-P on the base/handle |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer) → Roll (middle) → Pitch (inner) |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz (PWM-synchronous). IMU 8 kHz. Velocity 4 kHz. Attitude 1 kHz |
| Inverter | 3-phase with low-side shunts, center-aligned PWM, ADC sampling at the PWM valley |

**Why two IMUs.** The second IMU is not just redundancy. It serves three purposes:

1. **Base-rate feedforward.** The controller cancels handle/airframe motion before it reaches the payload.
2. **Vibration identification.** The base IMU sees vibration without the closed-loop shaping present on the payload. This makes it the right sensor for tracking the frequency that the adaptive notches must reject.
3. **Cross-consistency.** The two IMUs check each other for fault detection.
