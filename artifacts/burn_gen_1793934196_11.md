**Assumptions** (replace with datasheet values when the BOM is frozen):

- Battery: 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max.
- FET: 40 V N-ch, PQFN 3.3×3.3, R_ds(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ at V_gs = 10 V. Q_g = 40 nC, Q_oss = 30 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 15 ns at the driver strength used.
- Body-diode V_f = 0.8 V. Dead time t_dt = 50 ns.
- PCB: 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers, 1.6 mm thick.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx0.0045/\text{K}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × 1.38 = **2.07 mΩ**.

**Current path.** In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, phase current always flows through two conducting devices:

- the high-side FET of one leg, or its synchronous low-side partner during the off-time, and
- the low-side FET of the return leg.

The loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, apart from dead-time:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

At 80 A (trapezoidal, I_rms ≈ I_dc for well-behaved 6-step):

$$P_{cond}=80^2 \times 2 \times 2.07\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{26.5\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Per device: 13.2 W while conducting. Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the per-device average is about 4.4 W. Burst junction rise is governed by **instantaneous** 13.2 W pulses lasting 1/6 of the electrical period.

**Copper loss.** Assume 0.4 mΩ effective round-trip (bus → leg → pad → return):

$$P_{Cu}=80^2\times0.4\text{ mΩ}=\mathbf{2.6\ W}$$

The temperature-dependent coefficient of copper is +0.39 %/K, so include it in the iteration.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches.

| Term | Formula | Value @ 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | 0.5·25.2·80·30 ns·48k = **1.45 W** |
| C_oss | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 30 nC·25.2·48k = **0.036 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48k = **0.048 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·100 ns·48k = **0.31 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs/leg) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | 2·40 nC·10·48k = **0.038 W** (in driver) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 1.88 W / channel** |

**Sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it falls to about 0.95 W; at 96 kHz it rises to about 3.8 W. On low-inductance motors, 48 kHz is a reasonable compromise between ripple and loss. Overlap loss dominates, so driver source/sink strength (gate resistor value) is the main lever.

## 3. Total Loss Budget

| | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 26.5 W | 106 W |
| Switching | 1.9 W | 7.5 W |
| Copper | 2.6 W | 10.4 W |
| Drivers, LDOs, MCU | 0.3 W | 1.2 W |
| **Total @ 80 A burst** | **31.3 W** | **≈125 W** |
| @ 35 A continuous (R_ds at 90 °C) | ≈5.7 W | ≈23 W |

Efficiency at the 80 A burst: 4×80×25.2 = 8064 W in, about 98.4 %. The efficiency is fine; the **power density** is the problem.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Lumped board capacity (30.5×30.5 mm, 930 mm²)

| Material | Volume | Mass | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper (8 × 70 µm × ~80 % fill) | 0.42 cm³ | 3.7 g | 1.43 J/K |
| FR4 | ~1.45 cm³ | 2.7 g | 3.0 J/K |
| FETs, caps, solder | | | ~1.0 J/K |
| **C_board** | | | **≈ 5.4 J/K** |

### 4.2 Two-node transient (junction → board → air)

$$T_j(t)=T_a+P_{FET}Z_{th,jb}(t)+P_{tot}R_{ba}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau_b}\right),\quad \tau_b=R_{ba}C_{board}$$

Parameters:

- R_ba with prop-wash airflow (~5 m/s): about 2.5 K/W, giving τ_b ≈ 13.5 s.
- Z_th,jb for a 3.3×3.3 PQFN: about 0.15 K/W at 10 ms, about 0.6 K/W at 100 ms, about 1.2 K/W at ≥1 s (steady R_th,jb ≈ 1.5 K/W).

### 4.3 80 A burst, T_a = 40 °C

The board term is P_tot·R_ba·(1−e^(−t/τ_b)), with P_tot·R_ba = 125 × 2.5 = 312 K.

| Burst | Board rise | Junction-to-board rise (13.2 W) | **T_j** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 s | 312·(1−e^(−0.037)) ≈ 11.3 K | 13.2·~1.0 ≈ 13 K | **~64 °C** |
| 1 s | 312·(1−e^(−0.074)) ≈ 22.3 K | ~16 K | **~78 °C** |
| 2 s | 312·(1−e^(−0.148)) ≈ 43 K | ~16 K | **~99 °C** |
| 4 s | 312·(1−e^(−0.296)) ≈ 80 K | ~16 K | **~136 °C** |

Notes on the table:

- R_ds rises with temperature. Iterating the 4 s case adds about 10 %, giving roughly 145 °C. That exceeds the derating limit and is near T_j,max = 150–175 °C.
- **Design limit: 80 A for ≤2 s**, then fold back. AM
