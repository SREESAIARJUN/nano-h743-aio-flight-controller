## 0. Architecture Decisions and Constraints

These points shape the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no sensorless BLDC commutation engine. The working architecture is:
   - STM32H743 FC running ArduPilot, sending **bidirectional DShot600** to
   - 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (reference: AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6) running AM32. These generate the **48 kHz** complementary PWM.
2. **"80A burst" must be defined as a time-limited rating.** The calculation in §3 shows a 20×20 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm board cannot dissipate the steady-state loss of 4×80A. The rating this document defends is **80A per channel for ≤5 s from a 40 °C start, with prop-wash airflow**. Continuous rating is derived separately in §3.4.
3. **Example component values are illustrative.** Values below come from typical 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN MOSFETs. Before freezing the BOM, replace every parameter tagged **[DS]** with the selected part's datasheet value.

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

R_ds(125 °C) = 1.0 mΩ × (1 + 0.006 × 100) = **1.60 mΩ**

This is self-consistent only if T_j is solved iteratively, which is done in §3.3.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

Each FET in a leg carries current for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle, i.e. a 1/3 share.

- High-side FET, synchronous PWM: conducts for a fraction D of its 1/3 share.
- Its complementary low-side FET (same leg) conducts the freewheel current for the remaining (1 − D) of that share.
- The opposite-phase low-side FET is fully on for its 1/3 share.

Per-leg I²R energy is therefore approximately independent of D:

P_cond,leg = (I_ph² / 3) · R_ds · [D + (1 − D)] + (I_ph² / 3) · R_ds = (2/3) · I_ph² · R_ds

P_cond,ch = 3 legs × (2/3) · I_ph² · R_ds = **2 · I_ph² · R_ds**

**Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:**

- Per channel: P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.6 × 10⁻³ = **20.48 W**
- Worst single FET (D → 1, high side): (6400 / 3) × 1.6 mΩ = **3.41 W**

**PCB copper loss** must be added. Assume a total current path of ~25 mm at an effective 0.25 mΩ per channel (4 oz paralleled planes plus phase pads):

P_Cu,ch = I_ph² · R_Cu ≈ 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W**

This dissipates in the board, not the junctions.

### 2.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side FET switches at near-zero voltage (ZVS) because the body diode is already conducting during dead time.

**(a) V-I overlap loss**

P_ov = ½ · V_bus · I_ph · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 kHz = **1.935 W**

**(b) Reverse recovery** (the low-side body diode is recovered by the high-side turn-on)

P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.060 W**

The recovery current also adds to the high-side turn-on overlap. Include it by replacing t_r with t_r + t_rr/2 if t_rr is significant.

**(c) Output capacitance loss**

P_oss ≈ Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw (combined charge/discharge of the high-side and low-side FETs)
= 60 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.073 W**

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period)

P_dt = V_f · I_ph · 2 · t_dead · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.614 W**
