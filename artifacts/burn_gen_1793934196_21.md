## 0. Architecture Review: Read This First

Three issues must be resolved before layout.

**Issue 1: The H743 should not commutate the motors.**
- 4 motors × 3 half-bridges = 12 half-bridges, which is 24 gate signals.
- The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 (6 complementary pairs) plus HRTIM (10 outputs). That totals 22, two short of 24.
- Even with 3×PWM drivers (12 PWM channels), running four sensorless BEMF commutation loops at 48 kHz alongside ArduPilot's scheduler is not supported by ArduPilot.

**Required topology:**
- H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller.
- Four dedicated ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421) run AM32 or BLHeli_32.
- The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.
- The rest of this document assumes this topology.

**Issue 2: An 80 A burst does not fit a 20×20 mm ("nano") board.**
- Commercial 20×20 4-in-1 ESCs are realistically rated 35–45 A continuous per channel.
- Section 4 quantifies the shortfall.
- Either retain the 80 A figure as a short-duration, single-motor transient spec, or move to 30.5×30.5 mm.

**Issue 3: 48 kHz PWM roughly doubles switching loss versus 24 kHz.** It is justified only for low-inductance motors (less current ripple, quieter operation). Section 3 shows the cost.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha_R (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 115 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 90) = **1.405 mΩ**

### 2.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

In every 60° electrical sector, phase current flows through exactly two conducting channels:
- The PWM leg: either the high-side FET (duty D) or the low-side synchronous FET (duty 1−D).
- The low-side FET of the return phase, which is held on.

Total conduction loss per motor, excluding dead time:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for 120° out of 360°. The average loss per FET is therefore:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = \frac{I^2R_{DS}}{3}$$

This averaging is valid when the electrical period is much shorter than the die's thermal time constant (about 1–5 ms). For example, at 20 krpm with 14 poles, f_e = 2.33 kHz.

During startup or locked rotor, use the peak value instead: one FET dissipates I²R_DS continuously.
