## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three decisions shape every downstream number, so they come first.

**1. The H743 is the flight controller, not the commutation MCU.**
ArduPilot does not perform BLDC commutation. The board needs four ESC MCUs, one per motor, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Suitable parts include AT32F421 and STM32G071. The H743 drives them over DShot600 with bi-directional telemetry. The 48 kHz figure is therefore the ESC-MCU bridge PWM frequency, which AM32 supports natively.

**2. "80 A burst" must be read as 4 × 20 A aggregate (20 A per channel).**
Section 2.4 proves that 80 A *per channel* cannot work on a 20×20 mm board. It would dissipate about 120 W and overheat the board in under a second. All primary calculations therefore use 20 A/ch (80 A total). The 80 A/ch case is retained only as a bounding analysis.

**3. Design baseline:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| MOSFET | 30–40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 class, R_DS(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V, Q_g ≈ 20 nC, Q_rr ≈ 30 nC, Q_oss ≈ 40 nC |
| Gate driver | 3-phase, FD6288Q-class (bootstrap, integrated dead time) |
| PCB | 20×20 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz on power layers |

## 2. Thermal / Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]\,, \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Using α = 0.006 K⁻¹:

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 2.18 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 2.40 mΩ |

### 2.2 Bridge Conduction Loss (6-step, synchronous PWM)

In any commutation sector, one leg is PWM-switched and one leg's low-side FET is held on:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T. The low side synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T.
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts 100 %.

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R\,D + I^2 R\,(1-D) + I^2 R = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of duty cycle. That is the key benefit of synchronous rectification over body-diode freewheeling.

If N FETs are paralleled per switch position, use R → R/N.

Include the phase copper and shunt as well:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2\,(R_{trace} + R_{shunt}), \qquad R_{trace} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t}$$

For 2 oz copper (t = 70 µm), ρ = 1.72×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, ℓ = 6 mm, w = 3 mm, two layers in parallel: R_trace ≈ 0.25 mΩ.

### 2.3 Switching Loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

**Hard-switched overlap loss (PWM leg):**
$$P_{ov} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

**Reverse recovery** (the sync-rect FET's body diode conducts during dead time):
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12\,Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period):
$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET):
$$P_g = Q_g\,V_{drv}\,f_{sw}\ \text{per switching FET}$$
