## 0. Architectural Clarification

ArduPilot on the STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz commutation PWM. In every production 4-in-1 design:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot)  ──DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor)──►  ESC MCU ×1–4 (AM32 / BLHeli_32)
                                                              │ 48 kHz 6-step / sinusoidal PWM
                                                              ▼
                                                       3-ph gate driver ─► 6 MOSFETs/motor
```

The loss model in Sections 2–4 applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA/timer model in Section 5 applies to the H743. Keeping these domains separate is a design requirement, not just documentation.

**Design-review flag:** 80 A per motor on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) footprint is achievable only as a short burst. The thermal model below quantifies the burst duration.
