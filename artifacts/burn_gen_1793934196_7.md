**Critical architecture decision: the H743 does *not* commutate the motors.** The H743 has only two advanced-control timers (TIM1 and TIM8) with complementary outputs and dead-time insertion. Four sensorless 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary pairs plus BEMF comparators, which is not viable on one chip.

The architecture is therefore:

- **H743** runs ArduPilot and emits 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- **4× ESC MCUs** run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071) and handle commutation at 48 kHz.
- **4× 3-phase gate drivers** (e.g. FD6288Q-class) drive the bridges.
- **24× MOSFETs** in total, six per motor.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous-rectified commutation

In any commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd and phase B's low side is held on:

- **PWM ON (D):** HS_A → motor → LS_B. Two FETs conduct.
- **PWM OFF (1−D):** LS_A (synchronous) → motor → LS_B. Two FETs conduct.

**Two channels always carry the full phase current, independent of duty cycle.**

### 1.2 Equations

```
R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]          α ≈ 0.55–0.65 %/K  (fit to datasheet)

P_cond,motor = 2 · I_rms² · R_ds(T_j)
```

During flat-top burst, phase-current ripple is small, so I_rms ≈ I_b. If ripple matters, use I_rms² = I_b² + ΔI_pp²/12, where:

```
ΔI_pp = (V_bus − V_bemf)·D / (L_phase · f_sw)
```

### 1.3 Numerical result at the 80 A burst

```
R_ds(110 °C) = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = 1.5 mΩ
P_cond,motor = 2 × 80² × 1.5e-3 = 19.2 W
```

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts during 1/3 of the electrical cycle (two of the six sectors).

- **Low-side FETs** conduct fully during their held-on sectors and also during the (1−D) freewheel of their PWM sectors. They run hottest at low throttle.
- **High-side FETs** run hottest at D → 1.

In the worst-case FET, peak instantaneous dissipation while conducting is:

```
80² × 1.5 mΩ = 9.6 W
```

The commutation period at high eRPM (≈1–3 ms) is much shorter than the board thermal time constant, so cycle-averaged power is valid for case and board temperature. Use the peak for junction ripple (§3.3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per motor switches per sector, so switching terms apply to a single half-bridge.

### 2.1 Loss terms

| Term | Equation | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap (hard switching) | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·30n·48k = **1.452 W** |
| Body-diode conduction (dead time) | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·600n·48k = **1.843 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50n·25.2·48k = **0.060 W** |
| C_oss (2 FETs per leg) | 2·½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 1.5n·635·48k = **0.046 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 2·60n·10·48k = **0.058 W** |
| **Total P_sw,motor** | | **≈ 3.46 W** |

### 2.2 Engineering observations

1. **Dead-time diode loss is the largest switching-related term.** Cutting t_dt from 300 ns to 150 ns saves about 0.9 W per motor. In AM32, the dead-time setting is the cheapest efficiency lever available. Verify shoot-through margin with the actual R_g and gate-driver propagation skew.
2. **48 kHz is a reasonable compromise.** Switching losses scale linearly with f_sw. At 96 kHz, P_sw rises to about 6.9 W per motor. The benefit is lower current ripple on low-inductance motors (< 20 µH).

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total burst dissipation

| Item | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 3.46 W | 13.8 W |
| Phase/bus copper (≈0.3 mΩ effective path, I²R) | 2 × 80² × 0.3m ≈ 3.8 W | 15.4 W |
| **Total (all 4 motors at 80 A simultaneously)** | **≈ 26.5 W** | **≈ 106 W** |

The copper-loss line is often omitted from vendor ratings. On a nano board it is significant: 30 mm of 2 oz trace carrying 80 A is far from negligible. Use paralleled layers, phase pads that land the wire directly beside the FETs, and stitched vias (≥ 20 × 0.3 mm per phase pad).

### 3.2 Board-level adiabatic burst model

For bursts much shorter than the convective time constant, the board behaves as a lumped thermal mass:

```
C_th,board = Σ m_i · c_p,i
           ≈ (2.5 g Cu × 0.385) + (3.0 g FR4 × 1.1) + (24 FETs ≈ 2.4 g × ~0.7)
           ≈ 0.96 + 3.3 + 1.7 ≈ 6.0 J/K

dT/dt = P_total / C_th = 106 / 6.0 ≈ 17.7 K/s     (all 4 motors at 80 A)
```

Full first-order model with convection (prop wash h ≈ 50–100 W/m²K, two-sided area ≈ 1.9e-3 m²):

```
R_th,board-amb ≈ 1/(h·A) ≈ 5.3–10.5 K/W
τ = R_th · C_th ≈ 32–63 s

T_board(t) = T_amb + P·R_th·(1 − e^(−t/τ))
```

The steady state (P·R_th ≈ 560 K or more) is physically unreachable. **The rating is purely a transient-mass rating.**
