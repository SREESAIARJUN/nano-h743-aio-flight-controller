## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Constraints

Two constraints shape the entire design, so they come first.

**1. The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation layer. Running four sensorless 6-step or FOC loops at 48 kHz alongside the flight stack is not a supportable architecture. The correct partition is:

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, emits bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz
