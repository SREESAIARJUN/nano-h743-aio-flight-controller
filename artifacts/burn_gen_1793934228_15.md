## 0. Architecture and Design Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural partition

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. In every 4-in-1 stack (AM32, BLHeli_32, Bluejay), each motor channel has its own ESC MCU that runs commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. The work divides as follows:

| Domain | Device | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 @ 480 MHz | Runs ArduPilot. Generates 4× bidirectional DShot600. Decodes eRPM telemetry. Reads the current ADC. |
| ESC logic | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, GCR telemetry reply. |
| Power stage | 4 × 6 MOSFETs + 3-phase gate drivers | Conduction and switching losses (this model). |
