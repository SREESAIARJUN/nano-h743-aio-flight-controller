## 0. Architecture Check and Key Findings

**1. The H743 cannot be the ESC controller.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. A "4-in-1 ESC" on this board therefore means one of two things:
- four small ESC MCUs running AM32 (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071), or
- one MCU per pair of motors.

The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600 on internal traces. The 48 kHz figure is the AM32 PWM frequency, not the H743's
