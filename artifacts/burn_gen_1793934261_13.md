# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model for Job #57

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

**An STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges directly.** No ArduPilot build commutates motors. Every production AIO design uses one of two topologies:

| Topology | Description | Used here |
|---|---|---|
| A | H743 (ArduPilot) sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware), which commutate the bridges | **Yes** |
| B | H743 runs FOC for 4 motors itself | No. Not supported by ArduPilot. |

The DShot model in Section 4 is therefore the FC-to-ESC-MCU link. The power model in Sections 1–3 covers the ESC power stage.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C (typ): R = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0055·85) = **1.76 mΩ**
At T_j = 110 °C (max part): **2.20 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, two legs are always active:
- **PWM leg:** current is in the HS FET for duty D and the LS FET for (1−D).
- **Grounded leg:** current is in its LS FET continuously.

So **exactly two FETs carry I_ph at every instant, independent of duty**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad I_{ph,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2\left(1+\frac{r^2}{12}\right)$$

Here r = ΔI/I_ph is the ripple ratio, with ΔI = V_bus·D(1−D) / (L_motor·f_sw).
