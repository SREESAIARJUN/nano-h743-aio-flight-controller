# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #65): System Architecture Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architecture Corrections

**This is an engineering model, not a released design.** No MOSFET, gate driver, board stackup, or form factor was specified, so I selected representative parameters and labeled them. Re-run every equation with your actual datasheet values before layout freeze. The netlists are reference-level connectivity and are not DRC/ERC-verified.

### Corrections that shape the whole design

1. **The H743 does not drive the gates.** In a bidirectional DShot system, the H743 (ArduPilot) sends DShot600 frames. A separate ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) performs commutation and 48 kHz PWM, then returns eRPM telemetry. This document models both sides.
2. **"Nano" and "80A burst" conflict.** At 20×20 mm, 80 A per channel is not thermally or electrically achievable (see §3.4). The model assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC** stacked under the FC.
3. **"80A burst" is interpreted as per channel, for 10 s.** All four channels at 80 A at once means 320 A from the pack. That case is computed below as the worst-case envelope, and it fails, as expected.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal control with complementary (synchronous) PWM, the AM32 default. During each 60° sector, phase A's high side PWMs at duty D, phase A's low side conducts in the complement (1−D), and phase B's low side stays fully on.

The instantaneous conduction loss is:

$$
P_{cond}(t) = I^2 R_{HS} D + I^2 R_{LS}(1-D) + I^2 R_{LS} = 2 I^2 R_{ds,hot}
$$

This result is duty-independent because synchronous rectification keeps one FET always in the current path.

### 1.2 Hot R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds,hot} = R_{ds,25} \cdot k_T = 1.2 \times 1.55 = 1.86\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$
P_{cond} = 2 (80)^2 (1.86\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{23.8\ W}
$$

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect loss

Assume an 8-layer board with 2 oz (70 µm) copper on outer and power planes. Each phase path (pad, plane, via array, motor pad) is about 0.35 mΩ, and two paths are in series:

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 (2 \times 0.35\,\text{m}\Omega) = 6400 \times 0.0007 = \mathbf{4.5\ W}
$$

Copper's temperature coefficient is +0.39 %/K. At a 100 K rise, this term grows to about 6.2 W.

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each FET conducts for 1/3 of the time:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{23.8}{6} \approx 4.0\ \text{W average}
$$

The peak during the FET's active sector is about 11.9 W, which matters for the transient Z_θ calculation.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches hard. The low side of the PWM'd leg turns on after dead time into near-zero voltage (body diode already conducting), so it switches softly.

### 2.1 Gate current during the Miller plateau

Turn-on:
$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{drv} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{10 - 3.5}{1 + 2 + 1.5} = 1.44\ \text{A}
$$

Turn-off:
$$
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{4.5} = 0.78\ \text{A}
$$

### 2.2 Voltage-current crossover times

$$
t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{18\,\text{nC}}{1.44} = 12.5\ \text{ns}
$$

$$
t_{off} = \frac{18\,\text{nC}}{0.78} = 23.1\ \text{ns}
$$

### 2.3 Energy per PWM cycle

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V_bus · I · (t_on + t_off) | ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 35.6 ns = **35.9 µJ** |
| Reverse recovery (low-side body diode, at HS turn-on) | Q_rr · V_bus | 80 nC · 25.2 = **2.0 µJ** |
| Output capacitance | ½ · Q_oss · V_bus | **0.5 µJ** |
| **Total** | | **38.4 µJ** |
