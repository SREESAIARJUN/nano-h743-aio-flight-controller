## 0. Corrections and Assumptions

Several items in the brief need correcting before any numbers are meaningful.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In every ArduPilot 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel (or one shared ESC MCU) runs its own firmware (AM32 / BLHeli_32 / Bluejay) and drives the gate driver. | H743 = flight controller: DShot600 out, eRPM in. ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071, AM32) = 48 kHz PWM generation. |
| 2 | **80 A per channel on a nano board is a short-burst rating only.** Section 4 shows ~135 W dissipation for 4×80 A, with a steady-state board thermal resistance around 7 K/W. | "80 A" is modelled as a burst of ≤1–1.5 s. The continuous rating is derived separately (~30–35 A per channel with airflow). |
| 3 | 4×80 A on 6S is about 7 kW from the battery. | The battery, XT60/XT90 and pad current are the system limit. That is out of scope here but flagged. |
| 4 | "Nano" is ambiguous. | Assumed 30.5×30.5 mm mounting (≈36×36 mm PCB), 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes. A 20×20 board cannot physically fit six 40 V MOSFETs per channel at this current. |

**Electrical baseline:** 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V, nominal 22.2 V. MOSFET class is 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3, with R_DS(on),25°C ≈ 1.5 mΩ (V_GS = 10 V), Q_g ≈ 30 nC, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, Q_rr ≈ 30 nC, Q_oss ≈ 25 nC. These are representative values; substitute the chosen part's datasheet.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 2.1 Per-FET RMS current

Assumptions: phase current is I (flat-top trapezoidal), PWM duty is D, and complementary PWM on the active leg (high side PWM, low side synchronous rectification). Over one electrical cycle, each phase is "high" for 1/3, "low" for 1/3, and floating for 1/3.

| Device | Conduction interval | I²_RMS |
|---|---|---|
| High-side FET | D × (1/3 cycle) | I²·D/3 |
| Low-side FET (sync-rect during PWM-off) | (1−D) × (1/3) | I²(1−D)/3 |
| Low-side FET (phase held low) | 1/3 | I²/3 |
| **Low-side total** | | **I²(2−D)/3** |

Summing over the 3 legs gives:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 3\left[\frac{I^2D}{3} + \frac{I^2(2-D)}{3}\right]R_{DS}(T_j) = \boxed{2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

Physically, two FETs are always in series with the winding.

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.5 mΩ × 1.51 ≈ **2.27 mΩ** (2.25 mΩ is used below).

### 2.3 80 A burst numbers (D = 0.9, typical at full-throttle burst)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_HS per FET | 6400 × 0.9/3 × 2.25e-3 = **4.32 W** |
| P_LS per FET | 6400 × 1.1/3 × 2.25e-3 = **5.28 W** |
