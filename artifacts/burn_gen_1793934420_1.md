## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The power-invariant form is not used. The amplitude-invariant form is used, so that i_q is the peak phase current.

**Clarke transform** (assumes i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design (Pole–Zero Cancellation)

The d-q plant is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_d = u_d - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad v_q = u_q + \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to the first-order plant 1/(Ls + R). Choose the PI as

$$
C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}, \qquad K_p = \hat L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = \hat R\,\omega_c
$$

The PI zero then cancels the plant pole, and the closed loop becomes ω_c/(s + ω_c). Use ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s, which is at most f_pwm/10 once the one-sample computational delay is accounted for.

> **Gimbal note.** Gimbal motors have high R and low L. The electrical pole R/L ≈ 3.1 krad/s is often near ω_c. The cancellation design is still valid. Some designs also run voltage-mode control (v_q = R·i_q* + ω_e·λ). The code supports both modes.

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

**Circular limit.** With SVPWM (linear region), the usable voltage magnitude is

$$
|v|_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m,\quad m\approx 0.95
$$

The d-axis has priority:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{sat}(v_d, \pm|v|_{max}),\qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{sat}\!\left(v_q,\ \pm\sqrt{|v|_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)
$$

**Back-calculation anti-windup** (applied per axis):

$$
\dot x_I = K_i\,e + K_{aw}\,(v_{sat} - v_{unsat}), \qquad K_{aw}=\frac{K_i}{K_p}
$$

### 1.4 SVPWM by Min-Max Injection

This is equivalent to sector-based SVPWM and avoids branching:

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Velocity Estimation: Encoder PLL

Differentiating a 14-bit encoder at 4 kHz produces quantization noise of roughly (2π/16384)·4000 ≈ 1.5 rad/s. A type-2 tracking PLL avoids this:

$$
e_\theta = \text{wrap}_{\pi}(\theta_{meas} - \hat\theta)
$$

\hat\omega_I[k+1] = \hat\omega_I[k] + K_{i}\,T_s\,e_\theta
$$

$$
\hat\omega = \hat\omega_I + K_{p}\,e_\theta
$$

$$
\hat\theta[k+1] = \hat\theta[k] + \hat\omega\,T_s
