## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 115 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 90) = **1.50 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous mode

In each 60° sector, current flows through two phase legs:

- **PWM'd leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D. The low side (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts 100%.

At any instant, exactly two FET channels are in series with the phase current. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{par}$$

Include ripple in the RMS current:

$$I_{ph,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example ripple: L_ph = 15 µH and D = 0.5 give ΔI = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15 µ × 48 k) = 8.75 A. The ripple term adds 6.4 A², which is less than 0.1% of 6400 A², so it is negligible at 80 A. It matters at light load with low-inductance motors.

R_par is the parasitic resistance of copper pours, vias, solder, and the shunt. Budget 0.3 mΩ per conduction path.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

- FETs: P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.50 mΩ = **19.2 W**
- Parasitics: 6400 × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W** (dissipated in copper, not the junction)

Per-FET average: each FET conducts roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the average per FET is about 19.2 / 6 ≈ **3.2 W**. The instantaneous peak while a FET is conducting is 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM'd leg switches hard: one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off per PWM period.

### 2.1 Transition times

$$I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_G}, \qquad I_{G,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_G}$$

$$t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{G,on}}, \qquad t_{off} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{G,off}}$$

With the assumed values:

- I_G,on = 5.5 / 8 = 0.69 A, so t_on = 20 nC / 0.69 A = **29 ns**
- I_G,off = 4.5 / 8 = 0.56 A, so t_off = 20 nC / 0.56 A = **36 ns**

### 2.2 V-I overlap loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_{on}+t_{off})\,f_{sw}$$

P_VI = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 65 ns × 48 kHz = **3.14 W**
