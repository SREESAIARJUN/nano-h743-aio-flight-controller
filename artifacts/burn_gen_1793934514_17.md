## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

**Trapezoidal (6-step) drive with synchronous complementary PWM.** During any 60° sector:

- **Phase X (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_s and the low-side FET for (1−D)·T_s. Dead time is excluded here.
- **Phase Y (return leg):** the low-side FET conducts for 100 % of the sector.
- **Phase Z:** floating (used for BEMF sensing).

The motor current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly **two conducting channels in series**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Including ripple, using the RMS current:

$$I_{rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I_{ph}}\right)^2}$$

**Ripple estimate.** Typical 5" to 7" class motor, L_ph ≈ 12 µH (line-to-line 24 µH):

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}}$$

At V_bus = 25.2 V, D = 0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

$$\Delta I = \frac{13.2 \times 0.5}{24\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 5.7\ \text{A}$$

The ripple correction factor is therefore √(1 + 0.0004) ≈ 1.0002, which is **negligible at 80 A**. Use I_rms ≈ I_ph.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{\alpha},\qquad \alpha \approx 2.0 \text{ (trench Si, 40 V)}$$

Add package and PCB interconnect resistance. For the DFN5×6 clip plus vias, R_pkg ≈ 0.15 mΩ.

| T_j | R_DS(on) | R_eff (incl. package) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.85 mΩ | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.33 mΩ | 1.48 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.51 mΩ | 1.66 mΩ |

### 1.3 Conduction loss at 80 A (T_j = 125 °C design point)

**Per channel (one motor):**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.66\,m\Omega = 21.2\ \text{W}$$

**Worst single FET.** This is the return-leg low-side FET at 100 % conduction during its sector:

$$P_{FET,peak} = 80^2 \times 1.66\,m\Omega = 10.6\ \text{W (instantaneous)}$$

Averaged over a full electrical revolution, each FET conducts about 1/3 of the time on the return leg plus its PWM share. The time-averaged per-FET loss is P_cond,ch / 6 × (correction). The thermal time constant of the die (~1 ms) is comparable to the electrical period at low eRPM, so **design to the instantaneous 10.6 W at low speed or high load** (e.g. punch-out from hover).

### 1.4 Copper trace and shunt losses

**Phase and power copper.** 2 oz outer + 2 oz inner ×2 (6-layer, 4 power layers in parallel), path length ≈ 15 mm, width 6 mm.

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{wt}\Big/ n = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times 0.015}{0.006 \times 70\times10^{-6}} \big/ 4 \approx 0.15\ m\Omega$$

Including vias and temperature (×1.3), R_Cu ≈ 0.2 mΩ per channel path.

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.2\,m\Omega = 1.28\ \text{W per channel}$
