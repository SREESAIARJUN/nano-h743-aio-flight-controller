# Job #45: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst)

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points change the shape of the design, so they come first.

1. **The H743 cannot commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 produces DShot frames. Each of the 4 ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class firmware). The "48 kHz switching" figure belongs to those ESC MCUs, not the H743. Here, "4-in-1 nano" means one H743 FC stacked on (or co-laid with) a board carrying 4 ESC MCUs, 4 three-phase gate drivers and 24 MOSFETs.
2. **80 A on a nano footprint is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The model below shows the full board reaches thermal limits in about **3–5 s** at 4 × 80 A. The rating must be stated as an "N-second burst" and enforced in firmware, not just written on the label.
3. **Parameterized values.** Part-specific values (R<sub>DS(on)</sub>, Q<sub>g</sub>, Q<sub>rr</sub>, R<sub>θ</sub>) are representative of the 40 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm PQFN class. Re-run the equations with your chosen MOSFET's datasheet and validate with thermocouple/IR testing.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design-point T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 85) = 1.47\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

In every commutation sector, current flows through exactly **two switch positions** in series:

- The active high-side FET or its complementary low-side, which alternate at duty D.
- The sector's fixed low-side FET.

With synchronous rectification, the freewheel path flows through a MOSFET channel, not the body diode, except during dead-time. The conducting resistance is therefore 2·R<sub>DS</sub> at every instant, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.47\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.8\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper path loss

The path includes phase pad, power plane, via fields and battery pad segment. Estimate R<sub>Cu</sub> ≈ 0.30 mΩ per motor path at 2 oz × 8 layers, with ρ<sub>Cu</sub> rising about 0.39 %/K:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.30\times10^{-3} \approx \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$
