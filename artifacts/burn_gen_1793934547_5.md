**Status:** Engineering model, Rev A. All component parameters are representative assumptions. Before layout freeze, replace them with the datasheet values of your chosen parts and validate with thermocouple and IR testing on a powered prototype.

## 0. Architecture Clarification

An STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four 3-phase bridges itself:

- That requires 24 complementary gate signals with dead time.
- It also requires per-motor BEMF zero-cross comparators.
- ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation firmware.

The design therefore uses a **split architecture**:

| Block | Device (reference) | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 class | AM32 (supports bidir DShot and EDT) |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q-class 3-phase bootstrap driver | n/a |
| MOSFETs ×24 | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | n/a |

The H743 talks to each ESC MCU over one bi-directional DShot600 line per motor. The thermal and switching math below applies to the ESC power stage; the DMA math applies to the H743.
