# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #20): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA, Netlists

## 0. Architecture Constraints and Feasibility Check

Three issues determine everything downstream.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production AIO, the FC MCU sends DShot to four local ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. "48 kHz switching" is therefore an ESC-firmware parameter, and the H743's job is the DShot link. The model below assumes this partition.

2. **80 A burst × 4 = 320 A battery current, about 7–8 kW at 6S.** On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm nano board this is a **seconds-scale transient rating**, not a continuous one. The model below quantifies how many seconds. Expect about 5 s from a 40 °C start under prop-wash cooling.

3. **All component values are assumptions.** Values marked "assumed" are representative of the class. Replace them with datasheet values for the chosen FET, driver and layout before sign-off. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are a worked example.
