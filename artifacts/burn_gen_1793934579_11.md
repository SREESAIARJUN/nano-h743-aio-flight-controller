## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. In every practical "4-in-1 AIO" design, the H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and sends DShot to four dedicated ESC MCUs that perform commutation. The design below follows that architecture.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 1.1 Conduction topology
The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM:
- **PWM ON:** high-side FET of phase A conducts, plus low-side FET of phase B.
- **PWM OFF:** low-side FET of phase A (synchronous freewheel) conducts, plus low-side FET of phase B.

So at every instant, exactly **two FETs carry the full phase current I**, regardless of duty cycle D. The duty-cycle terms cancel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{hs}D + I^2 R_{ls}(1-D) + I^2 R_{ls} = 2I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_J)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance (coupled with the thermal model)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_J) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_J-25)]$$

At T_J = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.45 mΩ**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.6\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Switching losses at f_sw = 48 kHz
Only the PWM-ed leg (one high/low pair) switches hard.

**(a) V·I overlap loss**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

**(b) Output capacitance loss**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**(c) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-times per period, t_dt = 50 ns assumed)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

**(d) Reverse recovery**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET)

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

| Loss term | W/channel @ 80 A, 6S |
|---|---|
| Conduction (T_J = 125 °C) | 18.6 |
| V·I switching | 0.97 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 |
| Coss + Qrr | 0.08 |
| **FET total** | **≈ 20.0** |
| Gate drive (driver side) | 0.05 |
| Copper: phase + bus traces (~0.3 mΩ effective) | 80² × 0.3 m ≈ 1.9 |
| **Channel total** | **≈ 22 W** |
| **All 4 channels at 80 A simultaneously** | **≈ 88 W** |

**Takeaways:**
- Conduction loss is about 90% of the total. At 48 kHz, switching loss is minor, so raising f_sw to 96 kHz costs only about 1.3 W per channel.
- Reducing R_DS(on) or paralleling FETs is the only effective way to reduce loss.
- **Efficiency check:** a motor drawing 80 A at roughly 0.85 duty corresponds to about 1.7 kW electrical input. 88 W of loss is about 5%, which is consistent with real-world 4-in-1 ESC data at burst.

### 1.4 Bus capacitor ripple
The worst case for one channel occurs at D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \text{A}_{rms}$$

Four channels at uncorrelated phase reduce this to roughly 40/√4 × √(…) ≈ 20–30 A_rms combined.

- **On-board:** use 4 × (3 × 10 µF 50 V X7R 1210) per channel, with the effective capacitance derated to about 30% at 25 V DC bias.
