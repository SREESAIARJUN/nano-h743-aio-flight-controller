## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Bridge conduction topology (six-step, complementary PWM)

In each 60° commutation sector of a trapezoidal drive, three switches are involved:

- **PWM phase, high side (HS_A):** ON for duty D
- **PWM phase, low side (LS_A):** synchronous rectifier, ON for (1 − D)
- **Return phase, low side (LS_B):** ON for 100% of the sector

With phase current I_ph flowing continuously, loss in one sector is:

$$
P_{cond,sector} = D\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds} + (1-D)\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds} + I_{ph}^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The result is **independent of duty** because synchronous rectification removes the duty-cycle dependence. Over a full electrical cycle, each of the six FETs takes an equal share:

$$
P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}}{6} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}}{3}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)] , \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

The coefficient α corresponds to a normalized R_ds of about 1.5 at 125 °C, which is typical for 40 V trench FETs ⚠.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_ds,25 (40 V, PDFN 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 class) ⚠ | 1.0 mΩ |
| T_j (iterated, see §3) | 110 °C |
| R_ds(110 °C) | 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 85) = **1.425 mΩ** |
