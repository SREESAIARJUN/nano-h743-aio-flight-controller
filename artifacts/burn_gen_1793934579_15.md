# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #65): 80 A Burst Power Stage, 48 kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Constraints

No component selections were supplied, so I have defined a **reference parameter set**. Every result below is expressed as a closed-form equation first and as a number second. When you substitute the real MOSFET datasheet values, the numbers update and the method stays the same.

**System partition.** In this architecture the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM and commutation run on the ESC's own MCU, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 never generates the 48 kHz PWM. Section 3 covers the H743 side and Sections 1–2 cover the ESC side.
