# Job #39: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

**Assumptions.** The brief did not specify hardware, so this design assumes:
- **Motors:** BLDC gimbal motors (e.g. 12N14P) with in-line current shunts.
- **Encoders:** 14-bit magnetic rotor encoder per axis (AS5048 or MT6701 class).
- **IMUs:** Two ICM-42688-P on the camera plate. They are mounted **diametrically opposite** about the payload rotation center, so `r_B ≈ −r_A`.
- **MCU:** STM32G4/H7 class with an FPU.

If your mechanics differ (for example, one IMU on the base), the sections on gyro fusion and kinematics still apply, but the lever-arm cancellation in §4.2 does not.
