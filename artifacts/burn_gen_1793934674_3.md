## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Flags

Some architectural facts constrain the rest of the document:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On a 4‑in‑1 AIO, each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. A typical choice is the AT32F421K8U7. The 48 kHz PWM frequency is therefore a setting in the ESC firmware. The H743's job is DShot600 command output and bidirectional telemetry capture.
2. **"80 A burst" is treated as a per‑channel rating** that lasts seconds, not a sustained condition. Four channels at 80 A each from 6S is about 8 kW. A nano board cannot sink that heat and cannot carry it through its battery pads. This is quantified in §3.
3. All component values below are **parametric placeholders**. Re‑run the equations with the datasheet values of the MOSFETs and driver you actually select.
