## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off})$:
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park**:
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

**Inverse Clarke**:
$$v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

**SVPWM via min-max zero-sequence injection** (equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM):
$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

The linear range is $|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$.

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The PMSM dq model is:
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

The back-EMF constant follows from the torque constant:
$$\lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$$

**Decoupling feedforward** cancels the cross terms:
$$v_d^{ff}=-\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff}=\omega_e(L i_d+\lambda_m)$$

After decoupling, each axis is $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$. Pole-zero cancellation gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_c$:
$$K_p^{i}=\omega_c L,\qquad K_i^{i}=\omega_c R \quad\Rightarrow\quad T(s)=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$ to keep the 1.5-sample PWM/ADC delay phase loss acceptable. That delay is
$$\phi_{delay} = 1.5\,\omega_c T_s,$$
about 27° at 1.5 kHz.

**Voltage limiting and anti-windup:**
- The d-axis gets voltage priority: $v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$, with $V_{max} = 0.95\,V_{bus}/\sqrt3$.
- Back-calculation anti-windup: $\dot x_I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})$, with $K_{aw}=K_i/K_p = R/L$.

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Current Control

### 2.1 Velocity Feedback

The camera's world-frame rate is directly measured by the gyro, so it is the best velocity signal for stabilization.

**Gyro rate projected onto the motor axis.** With camera rate $\boldsymbol\omega_c$ and unit axis $\hat{\mathbf a}_j$ (in the camera frame, through the joint kinematics), the joint-space rate is:
$$\omega_j^{gyro} = \hat{\mathbf a}_j^{T}\,\mathbf J_{gimbal}^{-1}\boldsymbol\omega_c$$

For the innermost axis this reduces to $\omega_j = \hat{\mathbf a}_j\cdot\boldsymbol\omega_c$.

**Encoder PLL** (used for FOC $\omega_e$, for cogging, and as a fallback velocity):
$$e_k=\mathrm{wrap}_\pi(\theta_{m,k}-\hat\theta_k),\quad \hat\omega_{k+1}=\hat\omega_k+K_{i}^{pll}T_s e_k,\quad \hat\theta_{k+1}=\hat\theta_k+T_s(\hat\omega_{k+1}+K_p^{pll}e_k)$$
