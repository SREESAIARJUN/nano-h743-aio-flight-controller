# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual-IMU Fusion

I don't have the specification for Job #17, so the design uses the assumptions below. Every value marked as a parameter should be replaced with your measured motor and mechanical data.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7-class, single-precision FPU |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC (e.g. GM4108: 24 slots / 22 poles, so p = 11 pole pairs), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 4 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701), one per axis |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 4 kHz
