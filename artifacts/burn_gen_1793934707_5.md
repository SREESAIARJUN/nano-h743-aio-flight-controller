## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz (FPU, CORDIC, HRTIM). Code is portable C++17. |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701 / AS5048A) per axis |
| Current sense | 2-shunt low-side or inline, 12-bit ADC synchronized to PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz, gyro ODR 8 kHz, ±1000 dps, accel ±8 g |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner, camera) |

The loops are nested, with bandwidth separation of at least 5× between levels:

```
PWM 40 kHz (center-aligned) ──► ADC trigger
 └─ Current loop (FOC)        20 kHz   BW ≈ 1.5 kHz
     └─ Velocity loop          4 kHz   BW ≈ 40–80 Hz   (gyro inertial rate)
         └─ Attitude loop      1 kHz   BW ≈ 8–15 Hz    (fused quaternion)
IMU pipeline (2× ICM-42688-P)  8 kHz → filters → decimate to 4 kHz
```

**Key architectural decision:** the encoder is used for commutation, the electrical angle, and decoupling. The velocity loop closes on the **inertial gyro rate**. This is what makes the camera stabilized rather than merely servoed to the frame.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Latency Compensation of θ_e

PWM updates take effect about 1.5 T_s after sampling (one computation period plus half a PWM period). Advance the angle used in the inverse Park transform by:

$$
\theta_e^{\,\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\hat\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.3 PMSM Model in the dq Frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The torque and flux-linkage relations are:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q,\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{K_t}{1.5p}
$$

### 1.4 PI with Pole-Zero Cancellation

The plant is 1/(Ls+R). Choose the controller:

$$
C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}
$$

with

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

This places the controller zero at s = −R/L, which cancels the plant pole. The closed loop then becomes first order:

$$
\frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Choose ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s. Keep ω_c T_s ≤ 0.5, which holds here: 0.47 at T_s = 50 µs.
