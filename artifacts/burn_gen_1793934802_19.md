# STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #3)
## Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists and Thermal Analysis

---

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Key Findings

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight-controller MCU and runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor has its own ESC MCU (assumed STM32G071 or AT32F421 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That ESC MCU drives a three-phase gate driver and six N-channel MOSFETs. The H743 talks to the four ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.

**Baseline parameters.** These are design assumptions. Replace them with datasheet values for the parts you actually select.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full), 30 V design max | DC link voltage |
| I_burst | 80 A per channel | Burst phase current |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_ds,25 | 1.2 mΩ @ Vgs = 10 V | 40 V FET, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 DFN, one FET per position |
| α | 0.005 /K | R_ds temperature coefficient |
| t_r / t_f | 15 ns / 10 ns | Switching edges at the chosen gate resistance |
| Q_g | 40 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_rr | 30 nC | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge |
| C_oss,eff | 1.5 nF | Energy-equivalent output capacitance |
| t_dead | 50 ns | Dead time |
| V_F | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward drop |
| Rθ_JC | 1.5 K/W | Junction to case |
| Board | 30×30 mm, 8 layers, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner | 20×20 mm mounting pattern |
| T_amb | 40 °C | Ambient (hot-day worst case) |

**Key findings.**

1. **80 A is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** At 80 A on all four channels, the board dissipates about 100 W. A 30×30 mm board in prop-wash cannot sustain that. The usable burst window is about 2–3 s with all four channels loaded, or about 8–10 s on a single channel (Section 3).
2. **Conduction loss dominates.** It accounts for about 93 % of total loss at 80 A, so R_ds(on) and copper resistance are the levers that matter. The 48 kHz switching frequency is not the primary problem.
3. **DShot timing is fully feasible.** All four motors fit on TIM1 using 5 of the H743's 16 DMA streams, thanks to the DMAMUX (Section 4).
4. **Bus capacitance is the hidden risk.** Ceramics alone cannot handle the ripple current at 80 A. A low-ESR bulk capacitor at the battery pads is mandatory (Section 2.5).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM on the active high-side leg. Within each 60° sector:

- **During D·T_sw:** current flows HS_a → motor → LS_b.
- **During (1−D)·T_sw:** current freewheels LS_a(sync) → motor → LS_b.

So exactly two FETs carry the phase current at every instant. Per channel:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The temperature dependence of R_ds is modelled linearly:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the "PWM-high" phase and 1/3 as the "steady-low" phase. This gives the following per-FET averages:

| FET | Fraction of period conducting | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| HS | D/3 | (D/3)·I²R |
| LS | (1−D)/3 + 1/3 = (2−D)/3 | ((2−D)/3)·I²R |

The low-side FET is always the hottest device. At full-throttle burst (D ≈ 0.95):

- P_HS ≈ 0.317·I²R
- P_LS ≈ 0.35·I²R

Summed over the three phases: 3·(0.317 + 0.35)·I²R ≈ 2·I²R. This matches the total in 1.1.

### 1.3 Electro-thermal fixed point

Because R_ds rises with temperature, loss and junction temperature must be solved together. Let P₀ be the loss evaluated at 25 °C and Rθ the thermal path from junction to ambient:

$$
T_j = T_a + P_0[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]R_\theta
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_a + P_0 R_\theta(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha P_0 R_\theta}
$$

Thermal runaway occurs when α·P₀·Rθ → 1. That is the hard limit for steady-state operation.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

Using 1.8 mΩ, which corresponds to T_j ≈ 125 °C:

- **Per channel:** P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **23.0 W**
- **Worst LS FET:** 0.35 × 6400 × 1.8 m = **4.0 W**
- **Copper loss:** add about 0.3 mΩ effective trace and via resistance per channel path. That adds 2 × 6400 × 0.3 m ≈ **3.8 W per channel**, which is easily forgotten. Use 4 oz-equivalent pours and via stitching under the FET tabs to keep it down.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The other two phases are static within a sector. Per channel:

| Term | Formula | Value @ 25.2 V, 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | 0.5·25.2·80·25 ns·48 k = **1.21 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 30 n·25.2·48 k = **0.036 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw (×2 FETs per leg) | **0.046 W** |
