## 0. Architecture Correction and Feasibility Flag

Two issues need to be resolved before any math is meaningful.

### 0.1 The H743 cannot be the ESC controller

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, with ADC-synchronized back-EMF zero-crossing at 48 kHz PWM. The standard approach is 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class firmware).

The architecture is therefore:

```
[STM32H743 FC] --4x bidirectional DShot600--> [4x AT32F421 ESC MCU] --> [4x 3-phase gate driver] --> [24x N-MOSFET]
```

### 0.2 80 A burst on a "nano" 20×20 mm board is not physically credible as a 4-channel simultaneous rating

Section 3 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 105 W into a board with about 2–3 J/K of thermal mass. That is roughly a 1.5 s adiabatic window before junctions exceed safe limits.

My recommendation is one of the following:
- Rate as **80 A single-channel burst ≤ 1 s** and about 45–55 A all-channel burst ≤ 10 s, on a 30.5×30.5 mm stacked board; or
- Keep 20×20 mm and derate to about 35–45 A burst.

The derivation below lets you compute exactly where your limit sits.
