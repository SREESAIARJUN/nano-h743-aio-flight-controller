## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In a 4-in-1 stack, partitioning is as follows:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 command generation, eRPM capture | STM32H743 (FC board) | ArduPilot |
| 6-step/FOC commutation, 48 kHz PWM, EDT telemetry reply | 4× ESC MCU (e.g., STM32G071 / AT32F421) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET + 4× 3-phase gate drivers | n/a |

The switching-loss model (§2) therefore applies to the ESC board. The DMA/timer model (§4) applies to the H743.

**Feasibility flag:** 80A per channel on a 20×20 / 30×30 form factor is **thermally transient-only**. §3 shows the allowable burst duration is about 1 s with all four channels loaded simultaneously.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction topology
In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly **two MOSFETs carry I_phase at all times**:
- The PWM leg: either the high-side (during on-time D) or the complementary low-side (during 1−D).
- The low-side of the return phase, held fully on.

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Temperature dependence:
$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \frac{(\alpha_R - 1)(T_j - 25)}{100}\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R = 2.4 mΩ:
$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0024 = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

At 25 °C, the same calculation gives 19.2 W. The positive tempco feedback is the dominant thermal-runaway mechanism, so the model must be solved iteratively (§3.3).

### 2.2 Hard-switching (V-I overlap)
Only the PWM-leg high-side hard-switches, once on and once off per period:
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus}\, I\,(t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

Derivation of t_r,f from the Miller plateau:
$$t_{Miller} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g},\quad I_g = \frac{V_{drive} - V_{plateau}}{R_g + R_{drv}}$$
