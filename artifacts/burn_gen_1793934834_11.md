# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Design Model, Job #18

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Warning

The specification as written, **80 A burst per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) 4-in-1**, cannot be met thermally for more than about 1 second. The numbers in §2–§4 show why. Section 4.4 gives the burst-time envelope and two alternatives.

All component values below are design-intent parameters. Before release, re-run the model with the selected MOSFET's datasheet values.

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware: an AT32F421 or STM32G071 driving one FD6288-class 3-phase driver and 6 MOSFETs. A single H743 cannot run four sensorless 48 kHz commutation loops alongside the ArduPilot scheduler with acceptable jitter. The H743 talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.
