**Architectural reality checks:**

1. **The H743 does not drive the FETs.**
   - ArduPilot on the H743 outputs DShot.
   - Each ESC channel needs its own motor-control MCU (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32, or STM32G071) plus a 3-phase gate driver.
   - The architecture below reflects this.
2. **80 A × 4 on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally sustainable.**
   - Section 3 shows the realistic burst window is about 2 seconds.
   - Rate it as "80 A burst (≤2 s, single-motor or ≤50% duty)", or move to 30.5×30.5 with 2-FET-paralleled positions.
3. **Validate DMA assignments against your ArduPilot build.**
   - Section 4 assignments must be checked against the `hwdef` DMA resolver output (`build/<board>/hwdef.h`).
   - Bidirectional DShot channel-pairing rules have changed across ArduPilot releases.
