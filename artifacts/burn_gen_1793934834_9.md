## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. On every production 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and sends bidirectional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs**. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 and drives its own gate driver and six MOSFETs.

```
 6S LiPo ─┬─ Bulk cap + TVS ─┬─ ESC ch1: G071 → FD6288Q → 6× MOSFET → Motor 1
          │                  ├─ ESC ch2 ... ch4 (identical)
          └─ 5V/3.3V/10V regulators
                             
 STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── DShot600 BIDIR ×4 ──► ESC MCUs (1 wire each, open-drain style, pulled up)
