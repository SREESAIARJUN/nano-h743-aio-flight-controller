# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #51)

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two points in the brief need correcting before any math is useful.

**1. The H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot.**
ArduPilot has no firmware path to run 6-step or FOC commutation for four motors on the flight controller MCU. The only workable topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──bidir DShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                                               └─► 4× 3-phase gate drivers ─► 24 MOSFETs
```

The H743 owns the DShot timers and DMA. The ESC MCUs own the 48 kHz PWM.

**2. "Nano" and "80A ×4" conflict.**
A 20×20 mm mount pattern cannot hold 24 power MOSFETs rated for 80A. The analysis below therefore assumes:
- a 30.5×30.5 mm mount, roughly 41×41 mm board outline, stacked FC-on-ESC;
- 80A treated as a **burst** rating per motor, not a continuous one.

The math shows exactly how long a burst can last.
