**Rev:** A (preliminary)
**Status:** Model and allocation baseline. All component values must be confirmed against final datasheets and validated on hardware.

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flag

Two points need to be fixed before the math, because they change the model.

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does **not** switch the MOSFETs. The 48 kHz PWM is produced by four ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are AT32F421 or STM32G071, either one per motor or a 4-channel part. The ESC firmware sets the 48 kHz figure, not ArduPilot. The losses below are per ESC channel, then summed.

### 0.2 "Nano" + 80 A burst is a red flag

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| 20×20 mm board, 4 × 80 A | 320 A peak from the battery pads |
| At 6S (25.2 V) | About 8 kW input |
| Result in §3 | Dissipation of about 160 W, with a burst-limited window of about 0.5–1 s |

**Recommendations:**
- Confirm whether "80 A burst" means **per motor** (assumed here) or **total board**.
- If per motor, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm stack with a separate ESC board. A shared 20×20 AIO cannot meet it thermally.
