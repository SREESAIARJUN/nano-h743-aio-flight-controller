### Architectural Correction

The STM32H743 should be the **flight controller MCU only**. ArduPilot does not run field-oriented or six-step commutation for four motors on the H7. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or Bluejay, which supports a 48 kHz PWM setting:

- AT32F421 or STM32G071 for AM32
- EFM8BB51 for Bluejay

The H743 talks to those MCUs via bi-directional DShot600. This is the only configuration in which the ArduPilot portion of the request is valid. The design below assumes it.

### Feasibility Flag

80 A burst per channel on a nano-format board is achievable only as a **seconds-long burst** with prop-wash airflow. Section 3 quantifies this. Do not publish "80 A" without a time qualifier.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path (Six-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

At any commutation step, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw, and the low-side FET conducts (sync-rect) for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Phase B (static leg):** the low-side FET is on for 100% of the step.

The motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two FET channels** in series, plus PCB copper and any shunt.

### 1.2 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \left( 2\,k_T R_{ds(on)} + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt} \right)
$$

### 1.3 Ripple Correction

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ll}\, f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$

With L_ll = 20 µH, D = 0.5, and f_sw = 48 kHz:

- ΔI_pp = 25.2·0.25 / (20e-6·48e3) = **6.6 A**
- ΔI²/12 = **3.6 A²**, which is negligible against 6400 A²

So 48 kHz is clearly sufficient for ripple purposes.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FETs (hot) | 6400 × 2 × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| Copper (pad + plane + via, ~0.5 mΩ path) | 6400 × 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |
| Shunt (if per-phase 0.25 mΩ; often omitted on 4-in-1) | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W (optional) |
| **Subtotal** | | **22.4 W** (24.0 W with shunt) |

### 1.5 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET is active for 120° of the electrical cycle.

- **Static low-side FET:** 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W** instantaneous during its window.
- **Cycle-averaged per FET:** ≈ 19.2 / 6 × 2 ≈ **6.4 W peak-window-weighted**, or **~3.2 W true average**.

At electrical frequencies above ~1 kHz, the die's thermal time constant (~ms) smooths these windows. Use the average with roughly a 1.5× hotspot margin.
