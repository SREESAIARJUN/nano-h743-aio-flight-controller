## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \mod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design

The PMSM dq model is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

With decoupling feedforward:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L\,i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L\,i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

each axis reduces to $G(s)=\frac{1}{Ls+R}$.

PI controller $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ with pole-zero cancellation, $K_i/K_p = R/L$, gives a closed loop of $\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$:

$$\boxed{K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c}$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$ to leave margin for the sampling and PWM delay ($\approx 1.5\,T_s$). At 20 kHz, $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1500$ rad/s is a good choice.

Discrete form (forward Euler on the integrator, which is exact enough at these rates):

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v^{ff}_k,\qquad I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(u^{sat}_k - u_k)$$
