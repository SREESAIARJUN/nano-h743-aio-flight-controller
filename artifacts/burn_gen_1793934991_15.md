## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The H743 therefore runs only flight control and emits DShot600. Each motor has its own ESC MCU running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM.

| Block | Device (reference) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot, 4× bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF sensing |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 1.2 A source / 1.5 A sink typical) | 6 gates per ESC |
| MOSFET ×24 | 40 V N-channel, 5×6 mm, R_DS(on) ≤ 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 3 half-bridges per ESC |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm mount, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner, 6-layer | |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** 80 A burst per channel on a "nano" 20×20 board is not physically credible. The 3×3 mm FETs that fit cannot carry it thermally. This package assumes a 30.5 mm board with 5×6 mm FETs. If 20×20 is mandatory, re-run Sections 1–2 with R_DS(on) ≈ 2.5 mΩ. The burst capability then drops to roughly 45–50 A.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector:

- one phase is PWM'd: the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D and its complementary low-side (LS) FET for 1−D;
- one phase LS is held fully ON;
- one phase floats (BEMF sense).

The current path therefore always contains exactly **two conducting FETs**, independent of D. Dead time is the exception; it is handled in Section 2.

### 1.2 Equations

Hot effective resistance:

$$R_{eff} = k_T R_{DS(on),25} + R_{cu} = 1.6(1.0) + 0.3 = 1.9\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Bridge conduction loss per ESC:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{eff} = 2(80)^2(0.0019) = \mathbf{24.3\ W}$$

Of this, the silicon-only part is 2·6400·0.0016 = 20.5 W. The remaining 3.8 W is copper loss, which heats the PCB directly.

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution

The worst case is the LS FET at high duty. Each LS FET is held ON for 1/3 of the electrical cycle and is the PWM complement for another 1/3·(1−D):

$$P_{LS} = I^2 R_{DS,hot}\left[\tfrac13 + \tfrac13(1-D)\right],\qquad P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS,hot}\cdot\tfrac13 D$$

The instantaneous loss while conducting is I²R_DS,hot = 6400 × 0.0016 = 10.24 W.

| D | P_HS | P_LS | Hottest FET |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3 | 1.02 W | 5.80 W | LS |
| 0.5 | 1.71 W | 5.12 W | LS |
| 0.9 | 3.07 W | 3.75 W | LS |

**Design driver:** P_LS,max ≈ 5.8 W at low duty and high current. This is the punch-out-from-hover case.

### 1.4 Junction Ripple Within a Commutation Period

Take 30,000 eRPM, giving f_e = 500 Hz and an ON interval of about 0.67 ms per LS FET (two sectors).

From a typical 5×6 FET curve, Z_θJC(0.67 ms) ≈ 0.15 K/W. The ripple is then:

$$\Delta T_{ripple} \approx 10.24 \times 0.15 \approx 1.5\ \text{K}$$

$$T_J = T_{case} + P_{LS}R_{\theta JC} + \Delta T_{ripple} = T_{case} + 5.8(1.2) + 1.5 = T_{case} + 8.5\ \text{K}$$
