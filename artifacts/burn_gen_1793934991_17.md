### Two architectural corrections before proceeding

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no sensorless 6-step ESC firmware. Four sensorless BEMF loops at 48 kHz would also consume most of the H743's timers and ADCs, which collides with the DShot and IMU requirements. The standard and correct partition is:
   - The H743 runs ArduPilot.
   - The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs.
   - Each ESC MCU generates its own 48 kHz PWM.
2. **"Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot sustain 4 × 80 A.** Section 3 shows that even a 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer board only survives roughly 2 s of simultaneous 4-motor 80 A burst. On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A is achievable only as a single-motor transient of a few hundred ms. All calculations below therefore use 30.5×30.5 mm. Scale heat capacity by area (×0.43) for 20×20 mm.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C: R<sub>DS</sub> = 1.0 mΩ × 1.51 = **1.51 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At every instant, two FETs carry the phase current I:

- **On-state (duration D):** high-side of the PWM phase plus low-side of the sink phase.
- **Freewheel (duration 1−D):** complementary low-side of the PWM phase plus low-side of the sink phase.

Each leg is PWM-high for 1/3 of the electrical period and sink-low for 1/3 of the period.

Per-FET average losses:

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS}\,\frac{D}{3}$$

$$P_{LS} = I^2 R_{DS}\left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}\right)$$

Per motor:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This total is independent of D.

### 1.3 Numbers (I = 80 A, D = 0.9, T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C)

- I²R = 6400 × 1.51 mΩ = 9.66 W

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P<sub>HS</sub> | 9.66 × 0.9/3 = **2.90 W** |
| P<sub>LS</sub> | 9.66 × (0.333 + 0.033) = **3.54 W** (hottest device) |
| P<sub>cond</sub> per motor | **19.3 W** |
| P<sub>cond</sub> for 4 motors | **77.3 W** |

**Electro-thermal feedback.** Because R<sub>DS</sub> rises with T<sub>j</sub>, solve for the fixed point:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P(T_j)\,R_{\theta,j\text{-}pcb}$$

With R<sub>θ,j-pcb</sub> ≈ 3 K/W (1.0 junction-to-case plus 2.0 through solder and vias), the loop gain is:

$$I^2 R_{DS,25}\,\alpha\,R_\theta / 3 \approx 0.04 \ll 1$$

The fixed point is therefore stable, with no thermal runaway at the device level. Runaway risk exists only at the board level (Section 3).
