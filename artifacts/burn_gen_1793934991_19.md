## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Topology

The bridge runs 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In any sector, current flows through one "source" leg and one "sink" leg.

- **PWM on (duration D):** current flows through the high-side of the source leg and the low-side of the sink leg.
- **PWM off (duration 1−D):** current flows through the low-side of the source leg (synchronous rectification) and the low-side of the sink leg.

Either way, **exactly two MOSFETs carry I at all times** (dead-time intervals excluded). Bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I^2\left(2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu} + R_{sh}\right), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right] \approx k_T R_{25}$$
