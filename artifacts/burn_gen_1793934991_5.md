# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #40): Reference Design Package

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

**Topology.** The STM32H743 cannot drive four BLDC motors itself in this design, and I don't recommend trying. It is the **flight controller (FC)**. It talks bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Each ESC MCU runs its own 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max | Analysis also valid for 4S |
| Burst current | 80 A **per ESC channel**, ≤10 s | Not 4×80 A simultaneously (see §2.5) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (sync rect) | |
| MOSFET (placeholder) | 40 V, R_ds(on) = 1.0 mΩ typ @ V_gs = 10 V, 25 °C | Q_g = 50 nC, Q_oss = 50 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, t_r + t_f ≈ 20 ns |
| Gate driver | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q-class), 10–12 V gate | |
| Dead time | 50 ns per transition | |
| PCB | 6-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power planes | |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board requires low-R_ds(on) FETs, typically in 5×6 mm packages. Twelve of these rarely fit a nano footprint. Treat 80 A as a **short-burst marketing rating per channel**, not a sustained or all-channel condition. The math below shows why.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, current flows through exactly two FETs in series:
- During the PWM on-time: high-side of phase A and low-side of phase B.
- During the off-time: the complementary low-side of phase A (sync rect) and low-side of phase B.

Therefore the series resistance is always 2·R_ds, independent of duty cycle (dead time excluded):

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 At 80 A burst

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per ESC |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 80² × 2.0 mΩ = **12.8 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 80² × 3.0 mΩ = **19.2 W** |

**Per-FET average.** Each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the electrical cycle, split between its high and low FETs. Averaged over a revolution, this gives ≈ 19.2 W / 6 ≈ 3.2 W per FET, with peaks near 9.6 W while a FET carries the full current.

### 1.4 Parasitic series losses (often ignored, should not be)

| Element | Value | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt | 0.25 mΩ | 80² × 0.25 m = **1.6 W** |
| Copper (pad → plane → phase pad, ~0.3 mΩ) | | **~1.9 W** |
| Total parasitic | | **≈ 3.5 W per ESC** |

### 1.5 Self-consistent junction temperature

Solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot R_{th,j\text{-}pcb}$$

With R_th,j-pcb ≈ 2 K/W (R_jc plus solder plus vias) and P_FET,peak ≈ 9.6 W:
- **ΔT_j-pcb ≈ 19 K** at the instantaneous worst case.
- The ripple is filtered thermally at eRPM rates, so the average is ≈ 6–7 K.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard. The other active leg is static for the whole commutation step.

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard switching)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery of the sync-rect body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs)

Only two FETs switch per PWM cycle:

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.6 Loss summary per ESC at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.20 |
| Switching overlap | 0.97 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 |
| Coss + Qrr | 0.11 |
| Shunt + copper | 3.50 |
| Gate drive | 0.05 |
| **Total per ESC** | **≈ 24.1 W** |
| **All four at 80 A** | **≈ 96 W** |
