# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, BDShot Timer/DMA Map, and Netlists

**Job #19 · Revision A (engineering draft)**

> **Scope note:** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. A production-viable 4-in-1 design pairs the H743 (ArduPilot) with four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32 on the same PCB, linked by on-board bidirectional DShot600 traces. Everything below assumes that split.

## 0. Executive Summary

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Per-channel loss @ 80 A, 6S, 48 kHz, Tj ≈ 115 °C | **≈ 21.9 W** (19.2 W conduction + 2.7 W switching) |
| Total ESC loss, 4 × 80 A simultaneous | **≈ 88 W** |
| Board heat capacity (30.5 mm class, 8L 2 oz, no heatsink) | ≈ 4 J/K |
| Time to Tj = 150 °C from 40 °C ambient, 4 × 80 A | **≈ 3–4 s** (≈ 7–8 s with a 5 g Al heatsink) |
| Realistic rating | 80 A **burst ≤ 2 s** per channel; 45–55 A continuous with prop-wash airflow |
| BDShot timers | TIM3 (M1/M2) and TIM5 (M3/M4). 4–6 DMA streams via DMAMUX |

**Red flag:** "80 A burst" on a 20 × 20 mm nano board is not achievable thermally or in copper. The model below assumes a **30.5 × 30.5 mm** footprint. If the board must be 20 × 20 mm, derate to about 35–40 A burst.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, as in AM32 and BLHeli_32 defaults. At any instant, phase current flows through exactly **two MOSFET channels**:

- During the PWM on-time: high-side (HS) of phase A plus low-side (LS) of phase B.
- During the off-time: LS of phase A (synchronous rectification) plus LS of phase B.

Each MOSFET is in the current path for 1/3 of the electrical period.
