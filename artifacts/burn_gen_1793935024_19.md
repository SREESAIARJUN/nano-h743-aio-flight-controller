## 0. Architectural Ground Truth and Assumptions

Several clarifications constrain the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor (or each pair) has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical parts are the AT32F421 and STM32G071. The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and commutation live on those MCUs. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to them over **bidirectional DShot600**. This split is enforced in the netlist below.
2. **80 A in a nano footprint is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal section quantifies the actual sustainable duration. With all four channels at 80 A simultaneously, the board survives **about 4 s**. Treat any "80 A" label as a figure that is only true for very short durations.
3. **Total battery current at full burst is 4 × 80 = 320 A.** An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous and about 180 A peak. The power-entry design must use pads plus 10 AWG leads or an XT90-class connector.
4. **Device parameters are representative.** They describe a 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 class N-MOSFET. Replace them with the datasheet values of the part you actually source. The equations are exact; the numbers are only as good as the inputs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology
AM32 and BLHeli_32 use six-step trapezoidal drive with **complementary (synchronous) PWM**. At any instant, exactly two phase legs conduct:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high side is on for duty D. The low side of the same leg synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low side is fully on.

Current therefore always flows through **exactly two channels**, each with resistance R_ds(on):

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \kappa (T_j - 25)\right]
$$

For typical trench FETs, κ ≈ 0.006 /K, giving α_R ≈ 1.5 at about 110 °C.

### 1.2 Numbers
$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times (1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5) = 6400 \times 0.003 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

**Ripple correction.** Motor inductance at 48 kHz gives a peak-to-peak phase ripple of about ΔI ≈ 10 A typical for a 2207-class motor on 6S:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 8.3 \;\Rightarrow\; +0.13\% \text{ (negligible)}
$$
