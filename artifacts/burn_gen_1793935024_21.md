# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #11): 80 A Burst Power-Stage Model, DShot600 Timer/DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

In this class of product, the **STM32H743 does not commutate the motors**. It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an STM32G071 or AT32F421. That MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. The models below follow that split.

Everything downstream depends on these baseline assumptions. Replace them with your BOM's datasheet values and the equations still hold.
