> **Feasibility finding (stated up front):** An 80 A per-channel rating on a 20×20 board is a **sub-second transient capability**, not a thermal operating point. Section 3 shows that the realistic continuous rating is about **35 A per channel**, and that an 80 A burst is thermally limited to roughly **0.5–0.8 s**. Specify the product as "35 A continuous / 80 A burst ≤ 0.5 s, with firmware current limiting." Do not use an unqualified "80 A" figure.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ |

### 1.2 Bridge conduction under 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **Switching leg:** the high side conducts for D·T_sw. The synchronous low side conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the low side conducts continuously.

Phase current therefore always flows through exactly **two channels in series**, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per-FET distribution, with each FET active for 120° per electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[D + \tfrac{1}{2}\right] I^2 R_{DS},\qquad
P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{2}\right] I^2 R_{DS}$$

Summed over 3 HS + 3 LS FETs, this gives 2I²R_DS. At full throttle (D → 1), the **high-side FETs are the hottest**.

### 1.3 Conduction loss at 80 A burst (T_j = 125 °C)

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.74\,\text{mΩ} = \mathbf{22.3\ W/channel}$$

The hottest FET at D = 0.95 carries (1/3)(1.45)(6400)(1.74 mΩ) = **5.4 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

Miller plateau voltage V_pl ≈ 4.5 V. Driver resistance R_g,total ≈ 4 Ω (driver plus external resistor).

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_g}{V_{drv}-V_{pl}} = \frac{5\text{nC}\cdot4}{5.5} \approx 3.6\ \text{ns (Miller only)}$$

Including Q_gs2, the driver current limit, and the effect of loop inductance (L_loop ≈ 3 nH, di/dt limit), use the conservative values **t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 15 ns**.
