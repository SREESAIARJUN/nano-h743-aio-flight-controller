## 0. Assumptions and Corrections to the Brief

No prior design data for Job #42 was provided. Everything below is derived from first principles with **stated placeholder parameters**. Each value tagged **[DS]** must be replaced with the selected part's datasheet value before release.

### Architectural correction: the STM32H743 does not run the ESC

In every production AIO design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 cannot run ArduPilot and four sensorless six-step commutation loops at once with acceptable jitter. The design is therefore partitioned as follows:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (QFN) | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF commutation |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver, bootstrap) | 6 gate outputs per motor |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | Three half-bridges per motor |

### Reality check on "80 A burst" in a nano form factor

The model below shows that **80 A per channel is a seconds-scale transient rating**, not a sustainable one. For a 26×26 mm board, the time limit at 80 A on all four channels is about 2 s. The rating must be specified with a duration, for example "80 A / 2 s, 4 channels simultaneous".
