# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #71)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Several premises in the request need clarifying before any math is meaningful.

| Item | Assumption / Correction |
|---|---|
| H743 role | The H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) on AM32 firmware. A single H743 cannot run ArduPilot plus 4× sensored/sensorless FOC/trapezoidal control at 48 kHz robustly. |
| "80A burst" | Interpreted as **80 A per channel, battery-side, short duration (≤ a few seconds)**. |
| Supply | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max. |
| Form factor | 30.5 mm mount, ~36×36 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power layers. |
| MOSFETs | 40 V N-ch in 3.3×3.3 DFN class, R_DS(on) = 1.5 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, C_oss ≈ 1 nF, R_θJC = 1.5 K/W. **Substitute your actual datasheet values. Every result below scales directly.** |
| Control | Trapezoidal six-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM at f_sw = 48 kHz. |

The thermal conclusion (§3) shows that **80 A is a burst rating measured in seconds, not a continuous rating**. Every nano AIO behaves this way, and the marketing number should be qualified accordingly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.5 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.175 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-FET RMS current (six-step, synchronous)

At duty D, the phase current relates to battery current by I_ph = I_bat / D. In each 120° conduction sector, one high-side and one low-side FET carry I_ph. With synchronous rectification, the current always flows through exactly two FET channels.

- High-side, PWM-ing leg: $I_{rms,HS}^2 = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \frac{D}{3}$
- Complementary low-side, same leg: $I_{rms,LS'}^2 = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \frac{1-D}{3}$
- Static low-side, return leg: $I_{rms,LS}^2 = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \frac{1}{3}$

Summed over all six FETs:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Numbers at the 80 A burst (D ≈ 0.95, I_ph ≈ 84 A)

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 84^2 \times 2.175\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\ W/channel}$$

- The worst single FET (static low-side) dissipates 84²/3 × 2.175 mΩ = **5.1 W**.
- **4 channels: 123 W conduction.**

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect I²R

Assume a pad-to-pad path of ~0.4 mΩ per phase (2 oz, short pours) plus 0.3 mΩ in the shared battery bus:

- Per channel: 84² × 0.4 m × 2 ≈ **5.6 W**
- Shared bus: (320 A)² × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **31 W**. This term is critical. It requires heavy pours, stitched layers, and directly soldered XT60 pads.
