# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

**Revision:** A (Architecture Baseline)
**Status:** Engineering derivation. All component parameters are reference-design assumptions and must be replaced with datasheet values for the parts actually procured.

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Two points in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful.

**0.1 The H743 cannot be the commutation controller for 4 motors.**
- Four 3-phase bridges need 4 × 3 complementary PWM pairs with hardware dead-time. The H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1, TIM8). HRTIM could add more, but it is not supported by ArduPilot or AM32 for this role.
- Bi-directional DShot is by definition an FC↔ESC-MCU protocol. If the H743 drove the FETs directly, there would be no DShot.

**Baseline architecture:**

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x BiDir DShot600--> [4x AT32F421 (AM32)] --> [4x FD6288Q gate driver] --> [4x 6-FET bridge]
```

**0.2 "Nano" form factor vs. 80 A.**
- An 80 A × 4 burst at 6S is about 8 kW of motor power and about 100 W of ESC dissipation (§2.6).
- That is not survivable on a 20 × 20 mm board.
- This design assumes a **30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** ESC with the FC stacked above it.
- 80 A is treated as a **time-limited burst**. The burst duration is derived in §3.
