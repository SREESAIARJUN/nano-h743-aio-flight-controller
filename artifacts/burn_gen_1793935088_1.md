## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These points affect every calculation that follows, so they come first.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC commutation MCU.** ArduPilot runs on the H743. Each motor channel is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU. This design uses 4× AT32F421 running AM32. The H743 sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry over the same wire. The H743 never drives gates.
