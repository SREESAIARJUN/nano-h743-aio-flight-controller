### Architectural clarification

The H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, which drive the gate drivers and FETs. The thermal and switching models (§1–3) apply to the ESC power stage. The DMA model (§4) applies to the H743.

### Headline findings

1. **The 80A rating is a transient rating only.**
   - At 80A on all four channels, the power stage dissipates about **67 W**.
   - A 30×30 mm board with prop-wash cooling can reject about **8 W continuously** at ΔT = 60 K.
   - Realistic burst duration from a 50 °C start is about **5–8 s**.
   - Honest continuous rating is about **25–28 A per channel** (all four loaded) without a heatsink.
2. **Conduction loss dominates.** It is about 87% of loss at 80A. FET R_DS(on) and copper resistance are the primary design levers; 48 kHz switching loss is secondary.
3. **The DShot DMA plan fits easily on the H743.** With DMAMUX, all four motors on TIM1 consume 1 burst stream plus capture streams, leaving at least 9 streams for SPI, UART and ADC.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct (for example, A+ and B−):

- **Phase A (PWM leg):**
  - HS_A conducts for the fraction D.
  - LS_A conducts for (1 − D − 2t_dt·f_sw) as a synchronous rectifier.
  - LS_A's body diode conducts for 2t_dt·f_sw.
- **Phase B (static leg):** LS_B is fully on for the whole sector.

The motor current I therefore always flows through **two channels in series**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\left[\underbrace{D + (1-D-2t_{dt}f_{sw})}_{\text{PWM leg}} + \underbrace{1}_{\text{static leg}}\right] \approx 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

R_DS(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This α gives about 1.6× at 150 °C; take the exact curve from the datasheet. At a design point of T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{DS}(110)=0.80(1+0.005\cdot85)=1.14\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80A

$$
P_{cond,ch}=2\cdot 80^2\cdot1.14\times10^{-3}=\mathbf{14.6\ W}
$$

**Thermal runaway check.** Because P depends on T_j, the system is a fixed-point iteration:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th}\,P(T_j)
$$

It is stable only if R_th · ∂P/∂T_j < 1. With ∂P/∂T = 2I²R₂₅α = 0.051 W/K per channel and per-FET R_th,j-board ≈ 9 K/W, the loop gain is about 0.08. **The system is stable.**

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect losses (often forgotten)

The phase current path runs: motor pad → phase trace → FET → GND/VBAT plane → battery pad. Assume an effective series copper resistance of 0.4 mΩ per channel [A]:

- Copper conduction: P_Cu = 80² · 0.4 mΩ = **2.6 W per channel**.
- Current-sense shunt (if a low-side 0.25 mΩ shunt is used on the common return): it carries Σ|I_dc|, not phase current.
  - Battery current is about I_ph · D, roughly 40A per channel at D = 0.5.
  - Combined: 160 A² · 0.25 mΩ = 6.4 W. **This is a significant loss.**
  - Use a 0.1 mΩ shunt or a Hall sensor, giving about 2.6 W.
