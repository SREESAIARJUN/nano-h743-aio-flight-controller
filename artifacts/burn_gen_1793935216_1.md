## 1. Plant Model

### 1.1 Electrical (rotor dq frame)

$$
v_d = R_s i_d + L_s\frac{di_d}{dt} - p\,\omega_m L_s i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R_s i_q + L_s\frac{di_q}{dt} + p\,\omega_m L_s i_d + p\,\omega_m \lambda_m
$$

The flux linkage follows from the torque constant: $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}$ (amplitude-invariant Clarke).

### 1.2 Mechanical (one axis)

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - b\,\omega_m - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}
$$

Here $\tau_{dist}$ lumps together cable drag, imbalance $m g\, r \sin(\cdot)$, and base-motion coupling.

### 1.3 Transforms

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p(\theta_m - \theta_{off})$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose. The angle used must be delay-compensated:

$$
\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + p\,\omega_m\,(1.5\,T_c)
$$

The $1.5\,T_c$ accounts for one sample of computation delay plus half a sample of PWM zero-order hold.

## 2. Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 2.1 PI with pole–zero cancellation

The per-axis plant is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{L_s s + R_s}$. Choose the PI controller

$$
C_i(s) = K_{p,i} + \frac{K_{i,i}}{s}
$$

with

$$
K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_{ci}
$$

The PI zero then cancels the electrical pole, and the closed loop becomes first order: $\dfrac{\omega_{ci}}{s + \omega_{ci}}$.

The total loop delay $T_d \approx 1.5\,T_c$ limits the bandwidth. For phase margin ≥ 60°:

$$
\omega_{ci} \le \frac{\pi/6}{T_d} \approx \frac{0.52}{75\,\mu s} \approx 7000\ \text{rad/s}
$$

**Choose $\omega_{ci} = 2\pi\cdot 800\ \text{Hz} \approx 5000\ \text{rad/s}$.**

### 2.2 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - p\,\hat\omega_m L_s i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + p\,\hat\omega_m (L_s i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
