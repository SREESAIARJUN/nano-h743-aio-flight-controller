## 0. Architecture Corrections Before Starting

The brief has two problems that must be resolved before layout.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. It emits bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, and those MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 commutation firmware.
   - Baseline ESC MCU: 4× AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32, AM32-supported), one per motor.
   - Alternative: an STM32G071 per channel.
2. **80A per motor is not achievable on a 20×20 mm nano board.**
   - The burst-thermal model in §3 shows a 20×20 board saturates in under 1 s.
   - **Baseline changed to a 30.5×30.5 mm stack:** FC board on top, 4-in-1 ESC board below.
   - "80A burst" is defined as **≤ 2 s at 6S, 4 channels simultaneously, 40 °C ambient, with prop-wash airflow.** Continuous rating is 45–50A per channel.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

Temperature-adjusted on-resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] = 1.0 \times 1.45 = 1.45\ \text{m}\Omega$$

With synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two FETs in series:
- the high side (or its complementary low side during PWM-off) of the driven phase, and
- the low side of the return phase.

This holds independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.6\ W}$$

Copper path loss, assuming R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ for pad → phase → bus with 2 oz × 4 layers paralleled:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

Per-FET distribution:
- Over a full electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries one-third of the conduction duty, so P_FET,avg = 18.6 / 6 = **3.1 W**.
- **Worst case (stall or low eRPM, commutation frozen):** two FETs carry it all, so P_FET,peak = **9.3 W each**.

### 2.2 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Edge Only)

Gate currents during the Miller plateau:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{6.5}{5} = 1.3\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = 0.7\ \text{A}$$

Resulting transition times:

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 9.2\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 17.1\ \text{ns}$$

Switching loss:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 26.3\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{1.27\ W}$$

### 2.3 Secondary Switching-Related Losses

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8·80·100 ns·48 k = **0.31 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | **0.06 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw | **0.05 W** |
| Gate driver quiescent + LDO | — | ~0.05 W |

### 2.4 Totals

| Scope | Loss |
|---|---|
| Per channel, FET only | 18.6 + 1.27 + 0.47 = **20.3 W** |
| Per channel, including copper | **22.2 W** |
| 4-in-1, all channels at 80A | **≈ 89 W** |
| Bus capacitor ESR loss (I_rip,rms ≈ 0.5·I at D = 0.5 → ~40A over 4×(2×1000 µF + 2×10 µF MLCC), ESR_eff ≈ 3 mΩ) | +~4.8 W |
| **Design total** | **≈ 94 W** |

Efficiency at the burst point:

$$P_{out} \approx 4 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times D_{eff}(\approx 0.9) = 7.26\ \text{kW}$$

$$\eta_{ESC} \approx 98.7\ \%$$

The efficiency is fine; the absolute heat flux is the limit.

### 2.5 Design Sensitivity

- **Conduction dominates (~90%).** Halving R_ds saves about 9 W per channel. Lowering f_sw from 48 to 24 kHz saves only about 0.8 W per channel and increases motor ripple.
- **Gate resistor choice:** going from R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω to 4.7 Ω roughly doubles P_sw, but lowers dv/dt and ringing. Keep R_g,ext ≈ 3.3 Ω on turn-on, with an anti-parallel diode + 1 Ω for fast turn-off.
