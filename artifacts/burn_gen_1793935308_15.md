## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Architectural Flags

**Flag 1: Partitioning.** The H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel is driven by its own ESC MCU running AM32 (assumed here: AT32F421). The H743 talks to the ESCs only through bidirectional DShot600. Accordingly:

- Section 1 and Section 2 (losses and thermal) apply to the ESC power stage.
- Section 3 (DMA and timers) applies to the H743.

**Flag 2: Form factor.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) and "80 A burst ×4" are physically incompatible at reasonable duty cycles. Section 2.3 shows that even a 30.5×30.5 mm board is limited to a burst of a few seconds at 4×80 A. The math below is therefore written for a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz inner / 3 oz outer** stack. Treat 20×20 mm as out of spec for 80 A unless the burst definition is ≤1 s.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 1.1 Six-step conduction pattern

Each phase spends its electrical cycle as follows:

| Interval | Duration | FET behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| PWM leg | 120° | High-side conducts for fraction D; low-side conducts for 1−D (sync rectification) |
| Return leg | 120° | Low-side conducts 100% |
| Floating | 2 × 60° | No conduction |

So at every instant exactly **two FET channels** carry I_ph. Motor inductance makes I_ph quasi-DC within a PWM period, so I_rms ≈ I_ph.

### 1.2 Conduction losses

The hot channel resistance is:

$$R_{DS,hot} = k_T \cdot R_{DS(on),25} = 1.40 \times 0.80\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.12\,\text{m}\Omega$$

**Total FET conduction loss per channel:**

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS,hot} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.12\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{14.34\,W}$$

**Per-device time-averaged loss**, where each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{DS,hot} = \tfrac{1}{3}(0.9)(7.168) = 2.15\,\text{W}$$

$$P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[(1-D) + 1\right] I^2 R_{DS,hot} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1.1)(7.168) = 2.63\,\text{W}$$

Check: 3 × (2.15 + 2.63) = 14.34 W. ✓

**Copper path loss:**

$$P_{Cu} = 2\, I^2 R_{path}/2 = I^2 R_{path} = 6400 \times 0.4\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{2.56\,W}$$

This assumes R_path is the full round-trip conducting path.

### 1.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The high-side hard-switches. The low-side turns on after the body diode has already clamped, so it is near-ZVS and its V·I overlap is negligible.

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap (HS) | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·35 ns·48 k | **1.69 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | 2·V_SD·I·t_dead·f_sw = 2·0.8·80·100 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2·60 nC·48 k | **0.07 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw = 0.5·2 nF·635·48 k | **0.03 W** |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw = 2·60 nC·10 V·48 k | 0.06 W |
| **Subtotal (FET-dissipated)** | | **≈ 2.40 W** |
