# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8): Systems Architecture Package

**Rev A.** Parametric model; every numeric result traces back to the assumptions in §0.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Statement

The design rests on four architecture assumptions:

1. **Topology.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Motor commutation runs on **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AM32-class, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421). The H743 does **not** commutate motors. It emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
2. **Battery.** 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V maximum and 22.2 V nominal. Analysis uses 25.2 V as the worst case.
3. **Board.** A 30.5 × 30.5 mm ESC stack, 6-layer, with 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes.
4. **Burst current.** "80 A burst" means 80 A RMS phase current per motor, sustained for a duration we will *derive*.

> **Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel on a true 20 × 20 mm "nano" footprint is not thermally credible. The FET area, copper cross-section and capacitor volume do not fit. Section 3.5 shows that even at 30.5 mm, the 4 × 80 A case is limited to about **2 s**. If the mechanical envelope is hard-locked at 20 × 20, the burst rating must be re-derived. Expect roughly 35 to 45 A.

### Reference MOSFET

The reference part is a 40 V N-channel in a 5 × 6 DFN with these parameters:

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_ds25 | 1.2 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC |
| Gate-drain (Miller) charge | Q_gd | 8 nC |
| Gate-source charge after threshold | Q_gs2 | 4 nC |
| Plateau voltage | V_pl | 3.0 V |
| Internal gate resistance | R_g,int | 1.0 Ω |
| Output charge @ 25 V | Q_oss | 45 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.0 K/W |

The gate driver is an FD6288Q-class 3-phase bootstrap driver with V_drv = 10 V. External gate resistance is 5 Ω for turn-on and 2 Ω for turn-off (diode-bypassed). Dead time is 100 ns.

> **Margin note.** 40 V FETs on 6S leave about 15 V of avalanche margin against switch-node ringing. This is acceptable **only** with the snubber and decoupling layout in §5. A 60 V FET costs roughly 1.6× in R_ds and therefore in conduction loss.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with **complementary (synchronous) PWM**. At every instant, the phase current flows through exactly two conducting FETs:

- one high-side FET, in phase A, and
- one low-side FET, in phase B,

or through the synchronous low-side FET of phase A in place of its high-side FET during the PWM off-time.

The per-FET conduction losses are:

$$P_{cond,HS} = I^2 \, R_{ds}(T_j)\, D$$

$$P_{cond,LS,sync} = I^2 \, R_{ds}(T_j)\,(1-D)$$

$$P_{cond,LS,return} = I^2 \, R_{ds}(T_j)$
