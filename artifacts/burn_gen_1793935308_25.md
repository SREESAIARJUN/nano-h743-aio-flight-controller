## 0. Architecture Decisions and Scope

Two issues in the requirement need to be settled before the math.

**1. The STM32H743 cannot be the commutation controller.** ArduPilot has no BLDC/FOC commutation layer. The production architecture is:

- **FC domain:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It emits DShot600 and receives bi-directional eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC domain:** 4 × AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Each MCU commutates one motor and generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

The "48 kHz switching" analysis below therefore applies to the ESC MCUs, not the H743.

**2. "Nano" plus 80 A per motor is thermally a burst-only rating.** The math in §3 shows this. I assume a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board and 6S (25.2 V maximum). A 20 × 20 mm board roughly halves the burst duration.

**Assumed MOSFET parameters.** These are representative of a 40 V, ~1 mΩ-class PQFN. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values.
