## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C with R_DS,25 = 0.95 mΩ (max): R_DS = 0.95 × 1.38 ≈ **1.31 mΩ**. A design value of **1.2–1.3 mΩ** is used below.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous PWM

At any instant two legs are active:

- **PWM leg:** current alternates between the high-side FET (duty D) and the low-side FET acting as synchronous rectifier (1−D).
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

The phase current I_ph therefore always passes through exactly two R_DS in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per-FET averages follow from the 1/3 commutation share:

- Each HS FET: I²R·D/3
- Each LS FET: I²R·(1+(1−D))/3

The worst FET is a low-side device at low D.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, R_DS = 1.2 mΩ

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Instantaneous loss in one conducting FET | 80² × 1.2 mΩ = **7.68 W** |
| Total conduction loss per motor | 2 × 7.68 = **15.36 W** |
| Average per FET (6 FETs) | 2.56 W |
| Worst-case LS FET (D → 0) | 7.68 × 2/3 = **5.1 W** |

Trapezoidal ripple adds roughly 2–5 % to I_rms (ΔI_pp/I ≈ 20 % → factor 1 + r²/12). PCB copper and solder add about 0.3 mΩ per path, which is about **+3.8 W per motor**. Do not neglect this on a 2 oz nano board.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per PWM period there is one turn-on and one turn-off of the hard-switched HS FET. The LS FET switches near zero voltage after dead time.

### 2.1 Loss components

| Term | Formula |
|---|---|
| V-I overlap | P_VI = ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw |
| Edge time | t_r,f ≈ (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) / I_g |
| Reverse recovery | P_rr = Q_rr · V · f_sw |
| Output capacitance | P_oss = Q_oss · V · f_sw |
| Dead-time body diode | P_dt = V_SD · I · 2 t_dt · f_sw |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver/R_g) | P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw |

### 2.2 Numbers (V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, t_r = 20 ns, t_f = 15 ns)

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| P_VI = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 35 ns × 48 kHz | **1.69 W** |
| P_rr = 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| P_oss = 60 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.07 W |
| P_dt = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| P_g = 2 × 60 nC × 10 V × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| **Total switching per motor** | **≈ 2.5 W** |

**Sensitivity:**
- Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw and t_sw. At 96 kHz it would be about 5 W.
- Dead time is the second-largest term. Trimming t_dt from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor, but verify shoot-through margin against driver propagation skew.

### 2.3 Total electrical loss at 80 A burst

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Per motor (incl. copper) | 15.4 + 2.5 + 3.8 ≈ **21.7 W** |
| **4 motors** | **≈ 87 W** |
