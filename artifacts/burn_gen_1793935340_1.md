## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

I don't have prior Job #31 documents, so everything below is built on explicit assumptions. Replace the bracketed parameters with your BOM's datasheet values and the equations will carry through.

### 0.1 Partition the commutation onto separate ESC MCUs

Bi-directional DShot is a link *between* a flight controller and ESC MCUs. If the H743 drove the 48 kHz bridges directly, DShot would have no purpose. The plan therefore uses this partition:

| Function | Device |
|---|---|
| Flight control, ArduPilot, DShot600 master | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) |
| Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry | 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 |
| Gate drive | 4 × 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q-class) |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN3.3×3.3 |

### 0.2 Form-factor reality check

Twenty-four 80 A-class FETs and an H743 will not fit on a true 20×20 mm "nano" board at 80 A. Section 3 quantifies why. I therefore model **30.5×30.5 mm as the baseline** and show 20×20 mm as the stretch case.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation two phases conduct at any instant, so exactly two FET channels are in series with the motor current at every moment:

- **Commutating-PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T. The low side conducts for (1−D)·T as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts the full sector.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\,/\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 0.70 × 1.6 = **1.12 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Total bridge conduction loss per ESC

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.12\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{14.3\ W}$$

Note that this result is independent of duty D, because synchronous rectification always closes the path through a channel rather than a diode.

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each phase is active for 4 of the 6 sectors.

- **Worst-case instantaneous FET (return-leg low side):** I²R = **7.17 W**. This value drives junction ΔT during a sector, though the sector is short.
- **Average per FET:** 14.3 / 6 = **2.39 W**. This value drives board heating.

### 1.5 Parasitic copper and shunt losses (per ESC)

| Element | Assumption | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt | 0.25 mΩ, I_dc ≈ D·I_ph; at D = 0.8, I_dc = 64 A | 1.02 W |
| Phase/bus copper | 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ effective | ≈ 1.9 W |

These copper and shunt losses are **often omitted and they are not negligible**.
