# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #1): Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Check

I'll state assumptions up front so every number can be re-run with real datasheet values.

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It does not commutate the motors. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs.

The 48 kHz PWM, gate drive and losses live on the **ESC side**. Assumed ESC side:
- 4× AM32-class ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421), one per motor.
- 1× 3-phase gate driver per motor (FD6288Q-class, bootstrap).
- 6× N-MOSFETs per motor, 24 in total.

### 0.2 Feasibility problem: 80 A per motor on a 20×20 board

A 20×20 mm board fits DFN3×3 FETs at best. As shown below:
- 80 A per motor generates roughly **40 W per motor, about 160 W per board**.
- That is survivable only as a **sub-second to ~1 s burst**.

Commercial 20×20 4-in-1 boards honestly rate around 35–55 A burst. Before layout, the requirement must be pinned down:
- Is "80 A burst" per-motor phase current, or total battery current?
- What is the burst duration?

The model below assumes the worst case: **80 A per motor phase current, 6S**.

- the static low leg.
