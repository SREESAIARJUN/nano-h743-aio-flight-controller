## 0. Architecture Clarification and Scope Flags

Before the derivations, two architectural points constrain everything else.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each motor channel (or a shared dual-channel part) has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and handles BEMF commutation. The model below treats these as two separate domains:
- **Power domain:** ESC MCU, gate driver, MOSFETs. Covers conduction and switching losses at 48 kHz.
- **Control domain:** H743 timers, DMA, and DShot signal integrity.
