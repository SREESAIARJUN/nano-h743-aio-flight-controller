# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Reference Design Model

## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

I don't have access to the specific schematics or BOM for Job #54. Everything below is a **parametric reference model** built on stated assumptions. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values and board stackup into the equations before release.

Before the math, three issues change the design:

1. **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each motor (or the whole bridge) is driven by dedicated ESC MCUs, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S. The H743 only emits DShot600 and captures bi-directional telemetry. The 48 kHz switching analysis therefore applies to the ESC MCU and gate-driver domain.
2. **80 A burst on a nano-format board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is thermally limited to seconds**, as shown in §3. Specify it as a time-limited burst rating, never as a continuous rating.
3. **All four motors at 80 A simultaneously means about 320 A of battery current.** That exceeds any XT30/XT60 harness and most nano-board copper. The realistic burst case is about 80 A peak per motor at partial overlap. I compute the worst case anyway.
