# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4×80A-Burst ESC (Job #72): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Timing, Netlists, Thermal Analysis

**Assumed design basis:** 6S (25.2 V max), 25.5 × 25.5 mm "nano" AIO, 8-layer 2 oz PCB, 48 kHz complementary PWM, ArduPilot on the H743, AM32 firmware on per-channel ESC MCUs.

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These results drive every later decision, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs a dedicated ESC MCU (one per motor, or one 4-channel MCU) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 only generates DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry. The architecture below uses 4× AT32F421K8U7 (QFN-32) running AM32.
2. **80 A per channel on a 25.5 mm board is a short burst rating only.** The model in §1 gives about 32 W of loss per channel at 80 A. With all four channels loaded, the board heats at roughly 30 K/s adiabatically, so the realistic window is **≈1.5–2 s for all four channels**, longer if only one channel bursts. Continuous rating should be specified at about 35–40 A.
3. **Battery-side current exceeds the connector rating.** At high duty, 4 × 80 A produces 250–320 A of battery current. XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous and XT90 about 90 A. The "80 A" figure must be marketed as a per-channel, short-duration burst, not a simultaneous one.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Topology assumption

The model assumes trapezoidal six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active leg. At any instant, current flows through:
- the PWM leg, alternating between its high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) FET at duty *D*, and
- the low-side FET of the return leg, which is held on continuously.

So exactly **two FET channels carry the phase current at all times**, independent of D, and no body-diode conduction occurs except during dead time.

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[ D\,R_{HS}(T_j) + (1-D)\,R_{LS}(T_j) + R_{LS,ret}(T_j) \right] \approx 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

The ripple contribution comes from the motor phase inductance *L*:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L\,f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$

Example: L = 15 µH (typical 2207 motor, phase-to-phase), V_bus = 25.2 V, D = 0.9, V_bemf ≈ 21 V:

- ΔI_pp ≈ (4.2 × 0.9) / (15 µH × 48 kHz) ≈ **5.3 A**
- Ripple term ≈ 2.3 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\text{–}0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

**FET selection** is a 40 V N-channel in 3.3 × 3.3 DFN (e.g. the AON7xxx or BSZ0xxNS class), chosen because it fits 12 FETs on 25.5 mm. Values assumed (verify against the chosen part's datasheet):

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_ds(on) @ 10 V, 25 °C | 1.6 mΩ |
| Q_g | 45 nC |
| Q_gd | 8 nC |
| Q_gs | 10 nC |
| V_plateau | 3.2 V |
| R_g,int | 1.0 Ω |
| C_oss(er) | 1.2 nF |
| Q_rr | 35 nC |
| V_SD | 0.8 V |
| R_θJC (bottom) | 1.5 K/W |

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.6 × (1 + 0.005 × 85) = **2.28 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Conduction loss at 80 A

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.28\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{29.2\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

This is about 14.6 W per conducting FET. The model is self-consistent: T_j must be iterated, as shown in §4.3.

**Parasitic copper and shunt losses** (per channel):

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Low-side shunt | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Phase/bus copper (≈ 4 mm effective, 8 layers parallel) | ≈ 0.15 mΩ | ≈ 1.0 W |

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-drive transition times

The gate driver is an FD6288Q (3-phase, 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink, 12 V VCC from the BEC). External gate resistors:
- R_g,on = 10 Ω
- R_g,off = 2.2 Ω, via a fast-off diode (BAT54) in parallel

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv}} = \frac{12-3.2}{10+1+4} \approx 0.59\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{2.2+1+2} \approx 0.62\ \text{A}
$$

$$
t_{sw,on} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{(5+8)\,\text{nC}}{0.59} \approx 22\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{sw,off} \approx \frac{13\,\text{nC}}{0.62} \approx 21\ \text{ns}
$$
