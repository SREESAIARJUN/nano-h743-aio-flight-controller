## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical Angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning by driving V_d along α (θ_e = 0) and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park Transforms
Using amplitude-invariant form and assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 PMSM Model in the dq Frame
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.4 Current PI Controller with Decoupling
Choose the zero to cancel the electrical pole, and set the closed-loop bandwidth to ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

With current at 20 kHz and about 1.5 samples of delay, ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s gives about 60° phase margin.

The voltage commands add decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon-inscribed circle:

$$|v^*| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$$

The q-axis gets priority when limiting.

### 1.5 Inverse Park and SVPWM (Min–Max Injection)
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

$$v_a=v_\alpha,\; v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.6 Angle Compensation for Computation Delay
The voltage is applied about 1.5 T_s after sampling, so use a predicted angle in the inverse Park transform:

$$\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

## 2. Gimbal Kinematics and Cascaded Angle/Velocity Control

### 2.1 Kinematics
Let q = [ψ, φ, θ]ᵀ be the joint angles (yaw, roll, pitch).

Joint axes, expressed in the camera frame for a yaw→roll→pitch chain:
- **ẑ** for yaw (outer)
- **x̂** for roll
- **ŷ** for pitch (inner)

The camera angular velocity is the base rate rotated into the camera frame plus the joint contributions:

$$\boldsymbol\omega_{cam} = R_{cb}(q)\,\boldsymbol\omega_{base} + J(q)\,\dot q$$

The Jacobian is:

$$J(q) = \begin{bmatrix} -\cos\varphi\sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0\\ \sin\varphi & 0 & 1\\ \cos\varphi\cos\theta & \sin\theta & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$
