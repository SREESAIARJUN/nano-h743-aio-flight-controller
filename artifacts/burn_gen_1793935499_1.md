Three constraints in the brief conflict with physics or with ArduPilot's architecture. Each needs a decision before layout begins.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted in this document |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ArduPilot does not commutate motors.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and outputs DShot. Each motor needs its own ESC MCU running commutation firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32) at 48 kHz PWM. | H743 flight controller plus four ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7, AM32 firmware). | 2 | **80A × 4 on a 20×20 mm board.** Peak battery current approaches 320A. Section 2.6 shows the board alone heats at about 50 K/s. | "80A" is treated as a **burst rating of 1 s or less per motor**. The sustainable continuous rating is derived in §2.7. |

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation conduction topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, consider the state where phase A is PWM'd and phase B is held low:

- **High-side A** conducts for fraction D.
- **Low-side A** (synchronous rectifier) conducts for fraction (1−D).
- **Low-side B** conducts 100% of the interval.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor is therefore:

$$
P_{cond,inst} = I^2 R_{hot}\,D + I^2 R_{hot}(1-D) + I^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**, because the synchronous rectifier carries the current during the off-time.

### 2.2 Hot R_DS(on)

$$
R_{hot} = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha_R (T_j - 25)\right]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R_{hot} = 1.5\,\text{mΩ} \times (1 + 0.0065 \times 100) = 2.48\,\text{mΩ}
$$

### 2.3 Conduction loss at 80A burst

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.48\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{31.7\ W\ per\ motor}
$$

Per-FET average: each FET is active for 120° of the electrical cycle. The worst case is a low-side FET during its non-PWM 120° segment:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\, I^2 R_{hot} = \tfrac{1}{3}\times 15.9 = 5.3\ \text{W}
$$

This averaging is valid because the electrical period (about 0.6 ms at 100k eRPM) is shorter than the junction-to-pad thermal time constant (about 1–5 ms).

### 2.4 Switching losses at 48 kHz (per motor, one switching leg active)

**(a) Hard-switching V-I overlap**

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz}
= 1.45\ \text{W}
$$

**(b) Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

**(c) Output capacitance**

$$
P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.024\ \text{W}
$$
