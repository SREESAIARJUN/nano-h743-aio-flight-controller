## 2. Field-Oriented Control: Inner Current Loop

### 2.1 Coordinate transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

where p is the number of pole pairs (7 for 12N14P).

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 2.2 Plant model (SPMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 2.3 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

The plant per axis is $G(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Use $\omega_c = 2\pi f_c$ with $f_c \approx 2$ kHz. To account for the one-sample PWM transport delay $T_d \approx 1.5\,T_s$, back off until the phase margin $\phi_m = 90^\circ - \omega_c T_d\cdot\frac{180}{\pi} \ge 45^\circ$.

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Voltage limiting:** apply a circular limit at $|v| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$, giving d-axis priority. Use clamp-based anti-windup on each integrator.

### 2.4 Space-vector PWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{InvClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta)$$
$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\left[\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\right]$$
$$d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$

This is mathematically equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM.
