## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 System partitioning
- **Flight controller.** STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **4-in-1 ESC.** Four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) run AM32 or BLHeli_32-compatible firmware with 48 kHz center-aligned complementary PWM.
- The H743 does not commutate the motors. Combining both roles in a single MCU is not a supported ArduPilot configuration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
In 6-step trapezoidal drive, two phase legs carry current at any instant: one sources and one sinks. With synchronous rectification (complementary PWM):

- The **PWM leg** conducts through the high side (HS) for a fraction D of each period and through the low side (LS) for 1−D.
- The **static leg** conducts through its LS continuously.

So each conducting leg presents exactly one R_DS(on) in the current path at every instant, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Battery-side current is I_bat = D·I_ph. Phase current, not battery current, sets FET dissipation. This is why a high-pitch prop at partial throttle is the worst case.

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution
Each FET is active for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle.

- **HS FET:** conducts for D of each PWM period while active, plus it carries all of the switching loss.
- **LS FET:** conducts for 1−D while its leg is the PWM leg (60°), and 100% while its leg is the static leg (60°).

Averaged over one electrical cycle:

$$
P_{HS,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[ I^2 R\, D \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} \right] \cdot 2 + \tfrac{1}{3}P_{sw}
\qquad
P_{LS,avg} = \tfrac{1}{6}I^2 R(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{6}I^2R
$$

The die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) is comparable to the electrical period at low eRPM. At low RPM, use the **instantaneous** active-window loss for T_j peak, not the average.

### 1.3 Temperature-coupled solution
R_DS(on) depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss. Iterate:

$$
T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}\!\left(R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j^{(n)}-25)]\right)\cdot(R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta C\text{-}B})
$$
