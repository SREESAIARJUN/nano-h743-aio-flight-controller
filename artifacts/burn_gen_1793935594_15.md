## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd and phase B is held low:
- **ON interval (D):** current flows through Q_AH, the motor, then Q_BL.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** current freewheels through Q_AL (synchronous), the motor, then Q_BL.

So at every instant **exactly two FETs carry the full phase current I**, independent of duty (neglecting dead time). Per motor:

$$P_{cond,FET} = I^2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \cdot 2$$

Loss is distributed unevenly within a sector: Q_BL takes I²R for 100% of the time, Q_AH takes it for D, and Q_AL for 1−D. Rotating through the 6 sectors, each FET averages:

$$P_{per\,FET,avg} = \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = \frac{I^2 R}{3}$$

**The worst-case instantaneous FET is the static low-side**, which carries I²R for a full 1/3 electrical period. At low eRPM this matters, because the thermal time constant of a die is about 1–10 ms.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on), self-consistent

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Because T_j depends on P, which depends on R(T_j), solve by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{ref} + P\big(R(T_j^{(k)})\big)\cdot R_{\theta,eff}$$

Thermal runaway occurs if the loop gain is ≥ 1:

$$\frac{\partial T_j}{\partial T_j} = I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta,eff}\cdot k_{share} \ge 1$$

At 80 A on one FET with R_θ,eff = 10 K/W (FET to board-ambient): 6400 × 0.9e-3 × 0.0045 × 10 = **0.26**. This is stable, but it amplifies temperature rise by 1/(1−0.26) ≈ 1.35×.

### 1.3 Numbers at T_j = 125 °C (design corner)

R(125) = 0.9 × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.305 mΩ**

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per motor (2 FETs) | 80² × 1.305e-3 × 2 = **16.70 W** |
| Static low-side FET (instantaneous) | 80² × 1.305e-3 = **8.35 W** |
| Average per FET | 16.70 / 6 = 2.78 W |

### 1.4 Copper loss (often ignored, and it's large)

Path per motor: bus plane → high-side drain pad → phase pad → motor wire → return phase → low-side → GND plane. Assume R_Cu,path ≈ 0.4 mΩ at 25 °C, with α_Cu = 0.00393/K, giving ≈ 0.51 mΩ at 95 °C:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.51\text{e-}3 = \mathbf{3.26\ W}\ \text{per motor}$$

Planar copper resistance estimate:

$$R = \rho_{Cu}(T)\cdot\frac{L}{W\cdot t\cdot N_{layers}}$$

with ρ = 1.72e-8 Ω·m and t = 70 µm for 2 oz. For example, 10 mm path, 6 mm width, 4 paralleled 2 oz layers gives 0.10 mΩ per segment. Via fields are the usual bottleneck: target ≥ 30 vias of 0.3 mm per 80 A transition, at about 0.5 mΩ/via in parallel.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg switches per PWM period: the PWM'd phase (Q_AH hard-switches, Q_AL soft/ZVS-ish via the body diode).

### 2.1 Transition (V–I overlap) loss in Q_AH

$$t_{sw} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g},\quad I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv}}$$

With 15 nC / 1.0 A, t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 15 ns:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{e-}9 \times 48\text{e}3 = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$
