# STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC Nano FC (Job #11): Power Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture

**Revision:** A (pre-layout)
**Scope:** Per-channel 80 A burst power stage, 48 kHz switching model, bidirectional DShot600 DMA plan for ArduPilot, reference netlists, and the thermal budget.

---

## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These points shape everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-controller firmware. It does not run FOC or six-step commutation. A 4-in-1 ESC therefore needs its own commutation MCUs:
   - **Option A (recommended):** 4× independent ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32). This gives better fault isolation and suits a stacked FC + ESC design.
   - **Option B:** an AIO board with the H743 FC section plus 4× ESC MCUs on a shared PCB.

   In both cases the H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. Section 4 describes that link.

2. **"Nano" vs. 80 A burst.** 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm board is not thermally credible beyond about 1 s. This design assumes:
   - a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC** (36×36 mm outline), 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power layers;
   - a 20×20 FC stacked above it, or a 30.5 AIO.

   The math in Sections 2–3 lets you rerun the numbers for 20×20 if the product requirement is firm.

3. **Representative component parameters.** No MOSFET was specified. All numbers use the representative 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 device below. **Replace these with values from your selected part's datasheet before release.**

## 1. Operating Model

The model assumes six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous complementary PWM, which is AM32's default. At any instant within a 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET switches at duty D. The low-side FET of the same leg conducts synchronously during 1−D.
- **Fixed leg:** the low-side FET is held ON for the whole sector.
- **Floating leg:** both FETs are off and the BEMF is sensed.

Phase current is assumed continuous with average I and peak-to-peak ripple ΔI. Two cases follow from this:

- Conduction loss involves exactly **two FET R_ds paths in series at all times**.
- Switching loss is incurred by **one leg** per PWM cycle.

## 2. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 2.1 RMS correction for ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right),\quad r = \frac{\Delta I}{I}$$

Phase inductance and ripple:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Using L_ph = 8 µH (line-to-line), V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 3 V at full throttle, D ≈ 0.95, and f_sw = 48 kHz:

- ΔI ≈ 3.7 A, so r ≈ 0.046.
- The ripple correction factor is ≈ 1.0002, which is negligible at 80 A.
- Ripple matters only at low throttle and high current, i.e. during stall or desync. That case is handled by the ESC current limit.

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

| T_j | Multiplier k_T | R_ds |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.34 | 1.34 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.45 | 1.45 mΩ |

Design value: **k_T = 1.45** (125 °C). This is conservative for the end of a burst.

### 2.3 Bridge conduction loss

PWM leg (high-side for D, low-side for 1−D) plus fixed leg (low-side for 100 %):

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.6\ W}\ \text{per channel}$$

This loss is independent of D under synchronous rectification. That is a key property: burst loss does not drop at partial throttle if current is held.

### 2.4 Per-FET instantaneous loss

The fixed-leg low-side FET dissipates I²R_ds = **9.3 W** for its 120° electrical window, and each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle.

The electrical period is short compared with the die's thermal time constant:
- At 30 k mech RPM with 14 poles, f_e = 3.5 kHz and T_e = 286 µs.
- The die/package time constant is τ_die ≈ 1–3 ms.

The die therefore sees close to the average loss with a small superimposed ripple:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond}}{6} \approx 3.1\ \text{W},\qquad \Delta T_{ripple} \approx (9.3 - 3.1)\cdot Z_{th}(100\,\mu s) \approx 6.2 \times 0.15 \approx 1\ \text{K}$$

### 2.5 Shunt and copper losses

| Source | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt | I² R_sh = 80² × 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| PCB copper (bus + phase, ≈ 0.4 mΩ path) | 80² × 0.4 mΩ | 2.6 W |

> **Recommendation:** use a single bus shunt per channel, 0.5 mΩ, 2512 (3 W). Keeping it at 0.5 mΩ preserves current-sense resolution at low current while bounding I²R.
