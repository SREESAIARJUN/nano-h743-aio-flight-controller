## 1. Loss Model (per motor, one 3-phase bridge)

### 1.1 Conduction loss

With synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs carry the phase current I at any instant: one high-side and one low-side, in different phases. During PWM-off, the low-side of the "high" phase replaces the high-side. So, independent of duty D:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 135 °C this gives R ≈ 1.5 mΩ. Add the shunt and copper in the path:

$$P_{path} = I^2 (R_{shunt} + R_{Cu}),\quad R_{shunt}=0.2\,\mathrm{m\Omega},\ R_{Cu}\approx 0.3\,\mathrm{m\Omega}$$

**At 80 A:**
- P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 0.0015 = **19.2 W**
- P_path = 6400 × 0.0005 = **3.2 W** (PCB and shunt, not junction)

### 1.2 Switching loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ed leg hard-switches. The low-side is a soft (ZVS-like) turn-on after dead time.

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

The transition time is set by Miller charge and gate drive:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\, R_g}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}$$

With R_g,total ≈ 4 Ω, V_drv = 10 V and V_pl ≈ 3.5 V, each edge takes about 6 ns. Ringing and di/dt limits make 15 ns per edge (t_r + t_f = 30 ns) a realistic figure.

P_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30e-9 × 48e3 = **1.45 W**

Secondary terms:

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f = 50n × 25.2 × 48k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | Q_oss · V · f / 2 ≈ 40n × 25.2 × 48k / 2 | 0.02 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_F · I · 2t_dt · f = 0.8 × 80 × 200n × 48k | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (in driver, not FET) | 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f = 2 × 40n × 10 × 48k | 0.04 W |

### 1.3 Totals at 80 A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz

| | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching + Q_rr + C_oss | 1.53 W | 6.1 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| **Semiconductor total** | **21.3 W** | **85.3 W** |
| Shunt + copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| **Board total** | **24.5 W** | **~98 W** |

**Design insight:** conduction loss is about 90% of the total. Raising f_sw from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs only about 0.8 W per motor. Lowering R_DS(on) or paralleling FETs is the lever that matters, not the switching frequency.

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Averaged over an electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs takes about P_total/6 ≈ 3.6 W. During its 120° conduction window, the instantaneous FET loss is roughly I²R ≈ 9.6 W. This is short relative to the junction-to-case time constant (~ms) at typical eRPM, so use the average for T_j and check the peak against Z_th(jc) at low eRPM, where conduction windows are long.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Network

```
 P_FET ──[R_jc=1.5 K/W]──┬──[R_cb≈1.0 K/W (pad+vias)]──┬── Board node (C_b)
   (each of 24 FETs)     │                              │
                         C_j (≈0.01 J/K)                ├──[R_ba]── T_amb
                                                        │
                                                   P_path, P_drv, P_MCU
```

### 2.2 Board lumped parameters

- **Board heat capacity.** Mass is about 9 g, including FETs, copper, FR4 and caps. With effective c_p ≈ 0.6 J/g·K, **C_b ≈ 5.5 J/K**.
- **Board-to-ambient resistance under prop wash.** Take h ≈ 100 W/m²K forced. Area for both sides is 1.86e-3 m², plus ~20% for components.

$$R_{ba} = \frac{1}{h A} \approx \frac{1}{100 \times 2.2\times10^{-3}} \approx 4.5\ \mathrm{K/W}$$
