# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture Package for Job #41

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction model, 48 kHz switching-loss model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, netlists, and thermal budget.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Design Flags

Job #41 has no attached spec, so this package uses the baseline below. Correct any line and the equations can be re-evaluated.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| ESC topology | 4 × 3-phase half-bridge (24 MOSFETs), 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous (complementary) PWM |
| ESC MCUs | 4 × AT32F421 running AM32 (a single-MCU 4-in-1 variant is also possible) |
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100), 400 MHz (ArduPilot default H7 clock) |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| Burst | 80 A per motor, 2–5 s duration |
| Continuous rating | 45 A per motor |
| Motor | 14-pole (7 pole pairs), phase-phase L ≈ 20 µH |
| MOSFET (representative) | 40 V, R_DS(on) = 1.5 mΩ @ 25 °C (V_GS = 10 V), 3.3 × 3.3 mm PQFN, Q_g ≈ 50 nC, C_oss ≈ 1.5 nF, Q_rr ≈ 60 nC |

> ⚠️ **Flag 1: Form factor.** "Nano" (20 × 20 mm mount) cannot physically host 24 MOSFETs rated for 80 A burst, plus bulk capacitance and an H743. The math in §3 shows that even a 30.5 × 30.5 mm 8-layer board is thermally marginal. **Recommendation:** a stacked FC (20 × 20) on an ESC (30.5 × 30.5), or relabel the product as an 80 A single-motor burst, not 4 × 80
