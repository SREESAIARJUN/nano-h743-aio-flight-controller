## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The STM32H743 runs flight control and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each motor needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This document assumes the following:

```
                 ┌──────────────────────── 20×20 / 25.5×25.5 stack-in-one ─────────────────────┐
 VBAT 6S ──┬────►│ Bulk caps ─► 4× [ESC MCU ─► 3φ gate driver ─► 6× N-FET half-bridges] ─► M1..M4│
           │     │                    ▲ DShot600 bidir (1 wire each, 3.3 V)                     │
           └────►│ Buck 5V/3A ─► LDO 3.3V ─► STM32H743VIT6 (TIM1 CH1-4) + IMU/baro/OSD        │
                 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Conduction Losses

### 1.1 Current path (six-step trapezoidal, synchronous/complementary PWM)

At any instant, exactly two legs carry the phase current:

- **PWM leg:** high-side FET for duty D, then the synchronous low-side FET for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** low-side FET held on for the full 60° sector.

Neglecting dead time, the current always flows through exactly two channels in series. The total conduction loss is therefore:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[\,2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\,\right]
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

At T_j = 115 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 90) ≈ **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH (line-to-line) and D = 0.5 give ΔI ≈ 8.75 A. The correction adds only about 0.1 %, so it is negligible at 80 A.

### 1.4 Per-motor conduction loss at 80 A

$$
P_{cond} = 6400 \times (2 \times 0.0015 + 0.0005) = 6400 \times 0.0035 = \mathbf{22.4\ W}
$$

This splits into 19.2 W in the silicon and 3.2 W in the copper.

### 1.5 Worst single FET

The return-leg low-side FET carries the full current for the whole sector:

$$
P_{FET,max} = I^2 R_{ds} = 6400 \times 0.0015 = \mathbf{9.6\ W}
$$

The resulting junction-to-case rise is ΔT_JC = 9.6 × 1.5 = **14.4 K**.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The terms below are per motor.

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw = 30 n × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.036 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40 n × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.048 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | 2·V_f·I·t_dt·f_sw = 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 n × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs; dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 0.048 W |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈ 2.68 W** |

**Totals:**

- **Per motor:** P_tot ≈ 22.4 + 2.68 ≈ **25.1 W** (silicon ≈ 21.9 W)
- **Board total, 4 motors:** P_board ≈ **100 W** during an all-motor 80 A burst

### 2.1 Frequency trade-off

The switching term scales linearly with f_sw:

$$
\frac{\partial P_{sw}}{\partial f_{sw}} \approx 55.8\ \mu\text{W/Hz}
$$

Moving from 24 kHz to 48 kHz therefore costs about 1.3 W per motor. In exchange, you get lower acoustic noise and finer throttle resolution.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped RC network

```
P_j ──[R_θJC 1.5 K/W]──┬──[R_θCB ≈ 1.0 K/W (vias + TIM)]──┬──[R_θBA]── T_amb
                       C_j (≈0.05 J/K per FET)            C_board
```

### 3.2 Board thermal capacity

The board is 6–8 layer, 2 oz inner copper, about 6 g including FETs and capacitors. With c_eff ≈ 0.6 J/(g·K):

$$
C_{board} \approx 3.5\ \text{J/K}
$$

### 3.3 Board-to-air resistance

With prop-wash convection: R_θBA ≈ 8 K/W. This gives a time constant of τ = R·C ≈ 28 s.

### 3.4 Steady state is impossible

At 100 W, the steady-state rise would be ΔT_ss = 100 × 8 = 800 K. The design is therefore strictly burst-limited.

### 3.5 Transient response

$$
T_{board}(t) = T_{amb} + P\,R_{\theta BA}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right)
$$

At early times this approximates a linear ramp:

$$
\frac{dT}{dt} \approx \frac{P}{C} = 28.6\ \text{K/s}
$$
