## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

Several architectural facts constrain everything that follows.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each of the four bridges is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This baseline uses an AT32F421 per motor, which AM32 supports. The **48 kHz PWM is an ESC-firmware parameter**, not an ArduPilot parameter.
2. **Form factor.** The board has a 20×20 mm mounting pattern on a ~26×26 mm outline, so the H743 must be the **TFBGA100 (8×8 mm)**. LQFP100 (14×14) will not fit alongside four bridges.
3. **"80 A burst" means per-motor phase current, short duration.** Four motors at 80 A and near-full duty draw ≈ 320 A from the battery. That exceeds any XT60 and any nano-class trace or pad budget. The model below shows burst is limited to roughly **2–3 s** by board thermal mass. The continuous rating is far lower.
4. **MOSFET parameters are placeholders** representative of a 40 V, ~1.2 mΩ PQFN 3.3×3.3 device. Replace them with the selected part's datasheet values and re-run the equations. All formulas are given symbolically for this purpose.
