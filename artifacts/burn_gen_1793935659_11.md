# Job #61: STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC
## 80A Burst Thermal, 48kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 Architecture

> **Scope and assumptions.** No MOSFET part number, board size, or stack-up was specified. I used a representative 40V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET and a 30.5×30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz board. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute your datasheet values. Section 2.6 shows that 80A burst on a nano-class board is only valid for a few seconds.

---

## 0. Architecture

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four 3-phase bridges. That would need 24 PWM outputs (12 complementary pairs) with dead-time and break inputs. The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 as advanced timers, and HRTIM gives 10 outputs. The correct partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6, 480 MHz | ArduPilot, sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q-class, about 10V drive) | High/low-side gate drive with bootstrap |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 N-MOSFETs per motor (24 total) | Inverter |
| Current sense | One shunt per ESC plus amplifier, or a total-current shunt | Current/telemetry |

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction loss

The ESC uses 120° trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At every instant the motor current flows through exactly two channels:

- During the PWM **on**-time: HS(A) and LS(B).
- During the PWM **off**-time: LS(A) and LS(B), via synchronous rectification.
