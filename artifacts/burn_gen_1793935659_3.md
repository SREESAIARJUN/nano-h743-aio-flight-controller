# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Design Package (Job #56)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four BLDC motors and run ArduPilot at the same time. The standard and correct partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, emits bi-directional DShot600 ×4 |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | STM32G071 / AT32F421 (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class) | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) or equivalent | Drive 24 MOSFETs |
| Power stage | 24 × 40 V N-MOSFET, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN | Synchronous half-bridges |

**Design assumptions** (replace these with datasheet values for your exact BOM):

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_pk | 80 A (≤ 10 s) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 0.70 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 0.0045 /K (×1.45 at 125 °C) |
| Rise / fall time (with chosen R_g) | t_r, t_f | 10 ns / 10 ns |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_SD | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Gate drive voltage | V_drv | 10 V |
| Board size (ESC) | — | 40×40 mm, 8-layer, 3 oz outer / 2 oz inner |

> **Nano form-factor caveat:** an 80 A burst rating is not credible on a 20×20 mm board. The copper cross-section and thermal mass do not support it. The math below assumes a 30.5/40 mm-class ESC stacked with a 20×20 or 30.5 FC.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS}(125) = 0.70 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.015\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

At any instant in each 60° sector, two phases carry current:

- One phase is PWM'd. Its high-side FET conducts for duty D, and its synchronous low-side FET conducts for 1 − D.
- The return phase has its low-side FET held on.

The current therefore always flows through exactly two channels. The per-motor conduction loss is independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.015\times10^{-3} = 13.0\ \text{W}$$

Each FET conducts for about one third of the electrical period, so the average per-FET loss is:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = 2.17\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and shunt conduction

| Element | R (assumed) | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace (3 oz, short) | 0.20 mΩ × 2 paths | 2.56 W |
| Low-side shunt (if per-motor) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| Shared bus plane (320 A burst on all 4) | 0.05 mΩ | 5.12 W (whole board) |

**Copper total per motor:** ≈ 4.2 W, plus 1.3 W of shared bus loss allocated to each motor.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches. The loss terms per motor are:

**(a) Hard-switching V·I overlap**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.968\ \text{W}$$

**(b) Output capacitance**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery** (low-side body diode is commutated by high-side turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per cycle)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.614\ \text{W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (switching pair, dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g)

$$P_{g} = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

| Term | W / motor |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 0.968 |
| Q_oss | 0.036 |
| Q_rr | 0.048 |
| Dead time | 0.614 |
| Gate | 0.048 |
| **Σ switching** | **1.71** |

**Optimization note:** dead time is the second-largest switching term. Cutting t_dt from 100 ns to 50 ns (AM32 dead-time setting, verify against your shoot-through margin) saves about 0.3 W per motor. Raising f_sw to 96 kHz doubles every term to ≈ 3.4 W.

## 3. Total Burst Loss Budget

| Component | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction (125 °C) | 13.0 W | 52.0 W |
| MOSFET switching | 1.7 W | 6.8 W |
| Phase copper + shunt | 4.2 W | 16.6 W |
| Shared bus | — | 5.1 W |
| Gate drivers, LDOs, MCUs | 0.15 W | 0.6 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 19.0 W** | **≈ 81 W** |

**Efficiency at full burst:** P_out ≈ 4 × 25.2 × 80 × η_motor-side. The ESC itself is about 98.9 % efficient (81 W of ≈ 8 kW).
