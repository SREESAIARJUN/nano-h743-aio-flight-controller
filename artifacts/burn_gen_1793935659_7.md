## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Several points in the brief need to be settled before the model is meaningful.

**1. The H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot.**
ArduPilot is flight-control firmware. It has no sensorless BLDC commutation, and it cannot generate the 24 complementary gate signals needed for four bridges. The architecture must therefore be:

- **H743**: runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 on 4 lines.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These produce the 48 kHz complementary PWM and return eRPM telemetry.

The 48 kHz figure is an ESC-firmware setting (AM32 `PWM frequency = 48`). It is not an H743 parameter.

**2. "Nano" and "80 A burst" are in tension.**
The model below quantifies this. On a 20×20 board, 80 A per motor cannot be thermally survived for more than a fraction of a second. I sized this design for a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner** stack and give a burst-duration limit rather than a continuous rating.
