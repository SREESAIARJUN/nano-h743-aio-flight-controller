I don't have the specification for Job #5, so this design uses a common reference configuration. Every constant is a parameter, so you can substitute your real values.

|---|---|

## 1. FOC Current Loop Equations

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

$\theta_{\text{off}}$ is found at startup. Apply $V_d>0$, $V_q=0$ at $\theta_e=0$, let the rotor settle, and record $\theta_m$.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (power-invariant amplitude form, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Motor model (surface PMSM, $L_d=L_q=L$)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Torque is $\tau = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$.

### 1.5 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

Choose the zero to cancel the electrical pole $R/L$, with crossover $\omega_c$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

The closed loop is then first order, $i/i^* = \omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

Keep $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_s/20$ so that the 1.5-sample PWM and computation delay costs less than about 25° of phase. At 20 kHz this gives $f_c \lesssim 1$–$2$ kHz.
