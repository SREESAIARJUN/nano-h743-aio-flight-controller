# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual ICM-42688-P Sensor Fusion: Design Specification

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

I don't have the Job #11 specifics, so this design states its plant assumptions explicitly. Every gain below is derived from these parameters, so substitute your measured values.

### 0.1 Plant parameters (per axis)

| Parameter | Symbol | Default | How to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motor topology | — | 12N14P gimbal BLDC (2804/4108 class) | Datasheet |
| Pole pairs | p | 7 | Datasheet |
| Phase resistance | R | 5.0 Ω (line-to-line / 2) | 4-wire ohmmeter |
| Phase inductance | L | 2.0 mH | LCR meter at 1 kHz |
| Flux linkage | λ | 0.012 Wb | Back-EMF test, see below |
| Torque constant | K_t = 1.5·p·λ | 0.126 N·m/A | Derived |
| Axis inertia (with payload) | J | 2.0×10⁻⁴ kg·m² | Step-response fit |
| Bus voltage | V_bus | 12 V | — |
| Encoder | — | 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI | — |
| MCU | — | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz with FPU and CORDIC | — |

Measure λ by back-driving the motor: λ = V_peak,phase / ω_e.
