## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In trapezoidal six-step control, two phase legs are active at any instant. Exactly one switch in each active leg carries the current:

- **High-side leg (PWM-ing):** the HS FET conducts during D·T_sw. The LS FET conducts during (1−D)·T_sw as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Low-side leg:** the LS FET conducts for the full 120° commutation interval.

As a result, the current always flows through exactly two channel resistances, independent of duty cycle (dead time excluded):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

At 48 kHz with typical L_ph ≈ 10–30 µH, ΔI_pp is a few amps. The correction is under 0.5% and is neglected below.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | Per-FET loss (I²R) | Per motor (×2) | All 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 7.68 W | 15.4 W | 61.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | 10.3 W | 20.6 W | 82.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 11.1 W | 22.3 W | 89.1 W |

### 1.5 Electro-thermal fixed point

Let P₀ = I²R₂₅ and let R_θ be the junction-to-board thermal resistance. Then:

$$T_j = T_b + R_\theta P_0[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_b + R_\theta P_0(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_\theta P_0 \alpha}$$

**Stability criterion:** R_θ·P₀·α < 1. If this fails, the FET goes into thermal runaway.

With R_θ = 4 K/W (1 K/W junction-to-case plus about 3 K/W for pad, vias, and spreading):

- R_θ·P₀·α = 4 × 7.68 × 0.0045 = 0.138, so the design is stable.
- At T_b = 100 °C: T_j = (100 + 27.3) / 0.862 ≈ **148 °C**, before adding switching loss.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the high-side FET of the PWM-ing leg hard-switches. Its partner low-side FET commutates through its body diode during dead time.

| Mechanism | Equation | Per motor value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap (HS FET) | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48 k = **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time body diode (LS FET) | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.05 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ≈ ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | **0.02 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, mostly lost in driver and R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | **0.04 W** |
| **Total switching, per motor** | | **≈ 2.17 W** |

### 2.1 Frequency sensitivity

$$P_{sw} \propto f_{sw}$$

| f_sw | Switching loss per motor |
|---|---|
| 24 kHz | ≈ 1.1 W |
| 48 kHz | ≈ 2.2 W |
| 96 kHz | ≈ 4.3 W |

At 80 A, conduction loss dominates (about 90% of the total). Raising f_sw buys smoother current and quieter motors at modest thermal cost.
