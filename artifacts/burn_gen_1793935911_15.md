## 0. Architecture Clarification

In any 4-in-1 stack, the **STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET gates**. The partitioning is:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 master, eRPM telemetry decode | STM32H743 (400/480 MHz) | ArduPilot |
| Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead-time, BEMF/ZC | 4× ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive | 4× 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288-class) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor) | n/a |

The 48 kHz switching-loss model therefore constrains the **ESC firmware configuration**. The DMA model constrains the **ArduPilot hwdef**.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Topology Reasoning

With 6-step trapezoidal drive and synchronous rectification, two phase legs carry the phase current at every instant. Each conducting leg passes current through exactly **one** FET: the high-side during t_on, or the low-side during freewheel. Because the freewheel FET is actively enhanced, the result is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\left[R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu}\right]
$$

### 2.2 Temperature Dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_FET per motor | P_Cu per motor | **Total per motor** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 3.84 W | 16.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W | 3.84 W | 21.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.50 mΩ | **19.2 W** | 3.84 W | **23.0 W** |

The per-FET peak is I²·R_DS = 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W** in a 3.3×3.3 mm package. ⚠

### 2.3 Electro-Thermal Fixed Point

R_DS depends on T_j, which depends on P. The model is a fixed-point iteration:

$$
T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{board} + R_{\theta JB}\cdot I^2 R_0\left[1+\alpha(T_j^{(k)}-25)\right]
$$

The iteration converges only if R_θJB · I² · R₀ · α < 1:

- At 80 A: 3.0 × 6400 × 1e-3 × 0.005 = **0.096**. Stable, with about 10% loss amplification.
- Thermal runaway threshold: I_crit = √(1 / (R_θJB · R₀ · α)) ≈ **258 A**. This gives comfortable margin.
