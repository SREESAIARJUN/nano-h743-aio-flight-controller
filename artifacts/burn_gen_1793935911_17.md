## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

**Topology.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 (an AT32F421 is assumed here). The H743 drives four bi-directional DShot600 lines to those MCUs, and eRPM telemetry returns on the same wires.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

The design point is T_j = 115 °C:

$$R_{DS}(115) = 0.75\,(1 + 0.54) = 1.155\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step (trapezoidal) conduction with synchronous rectification

During any 60° sector, two phases carry the phase current I:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T and the low side (sync-rect) for (1−D)·T, less the dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low side is fully on.

Per sector, per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\,\big[D + (1-D) - 2\tfrac{t_{dt}}{T}\big] + I^2 R_{DS} \approx 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each individual FET conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{2}{6}\,I^2 R_{DS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

At the 80 A burst:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.155\times10^{-3} = 14.8\ \text{W}$$
$$P_{cond,FET} \approx 4.9\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and shunt I²R (often forgotten)

| Path | R | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pads/pours, 3 oz, about 8 mm long × 6 mm wide, 2 paths | ≈ 0.10 mΩ each | 1.3 W |
| Low-side current shunt (0.25 mΩ, battery-path return) | 0.25 mΩ × duty-weighted | ≈ 1.0 W |
| V_bat bus share | ≈ 0.05 mΩ | 0.3 W |
| **Subtotal per channel** | | **≈ 2.6 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Hard switching occurs on the PWM leg only: one high-side turn-on and one turn-off per period. The worst case is taken at V = 25.2 V and I = 80 A, which in practice occurs at high-but-not-100% duty. At D = 100% the switching loss vanishes.

### 2.1 Gate current during the transitions

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{drv,src} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{10 - 3}{3.5 + 4.7 + 1.0} = 0.76\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv,snk} + (4.7\parallel 2.2) + R_{g,int}} = \frac{3}{2.0 + 1.5 + 1.0} = 0.67\ \text{A}$$

### 2.2 Transition times

$$t_{ri} = \frac{Q_{gs2}}{I_g},\qquad t_{fv} = \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g}$$

- **Turn-on:** t_ri = 5/0.76 = 6.6 ns and t_fv = 8/0.76 = 10.5 ns, so **t_on ≈ 17.1 ns**.
- **Turn-off:** t_rv = 8/0.67 = 11.9 ns and t_fi = 5/0.67 = 7.5 ns, so **t_off ≈ 19.4 ns**.
