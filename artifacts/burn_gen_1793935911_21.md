## 0. Architectural Clarification and Reality Check

Two points affect every calculation below.

**1. The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC commutation MCU.**
Each 4-in-1 ESC runs its own motor-control MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The H743 generates DShot600 and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz switching frequency is a parameter of the ESC firmware (in AM32, PWM frequency = 48 kHz), not of the H743.

**2. On a nano (20×20 mm) footprint, "80 A burst" is a short-duration transient, not a capability.**
The math in §3 shows:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total dissipation at 4 × 80 A | ≈ 112 W |
| Board heat capacity | ≈ 2 J/K |
| Temperature rise rate | ≈ 55 K/s |
| Realistic continuous rating | ≈ 15–16 A per channel |
| Realistic burst rating | 80 A for ≤ 0.5–1 s |

The datasheet rating should state the burst duration explicitly, for example "80 A / 0.5 s."

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current Path

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs conduct at any instant. With synchronous rectification, current flows through **exactly one MOSFET in each of the two active legs**:

- During the PWM on-time: high-side (HS) of leg A plus low-side (LS) of leg B.
- During the off-time: LS of leg A (synchronous freewheel) plus LS of leg B.

The path therefore always contains two R_DS(on) in series, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 2.06 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ |

### 2.3 Burst Numbers (I_ph = 80 A, T_j = 100 °C)

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.06\times10^{-3} = 26.4\ \text{W per channel}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 4 \times 26.4 = 105.6\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Per-Device Distribution

| Device | Conducts for | Current | Average conduction loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| HS FET | 120° of electrical cycle, duty D | — | (1/3)·D·I²R |
| LS FET | Its own 120° sector, plus (1−D) of the opposite sector | — | — |
| **Worst case** (D → 1) | — | — | (1/3)(80²)(2.06 m)
