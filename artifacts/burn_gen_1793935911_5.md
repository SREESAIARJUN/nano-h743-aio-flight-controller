# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #46): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Verdict

Two findings shape the rest of this document.

**1. The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable AIO design:
- The **H743 runs ArduPilot** and emits bidirectional DShot600.
- **Four ESC MCUs** run the commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. Typical choices are the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware.

So "48 kHz switching" is an ESC-firmware parameter, and the H743's job ends at the DShot/telemetry interface. Section 4 and the netlist follow this split.

**2. 80 A on a 20×20 mm (nano) board is a burst-only rating, and a short one.**
- On the conservative single-FET reference design (§2.3), the math below shows about **19 W per channel** at 80 A and 6S.
- That is about **77 W per board** with all four channels at 80 A, against roughly **2.5 J/K** of board heat capacity.
- Result: about **2.6 s** to reach Tj = 110 °C from a 40 °C start, with no airflow.

Any "80 A" claim must be specified with:
- a duration (≤ 2 s recommended),
- the number of simultaneous channels,
- the cooling condition.

All device parameters below are representative of 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3 MOSFETs (R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 mΩ). **Substitute your selected part's datasheet values before release.**

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Hot-die resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0\,(1+0.0045\cdot85) = 1.38\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Current path under 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

During any commutation sector:
- **The PWM leg** conducts through the high-side FET for D·T and the low-side FET for (1−D)·T. One FET is always in path.
- **The fixed leg** conducts through one low-side FET continuously.

So exactly **two FET positions carry I_ph at all times**, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\left(\frac{R_{ds}(T_j)}{n_p} + R_{cu}\right)$$

### 2.3 Numbers per channel

| Configuration | FET loss | Copper loss | **Total** |
|---|---|---|---|
| n_p = 1 | 2·6400·1.38 mΩ = 17.66 W | 2·6400·0.3 mΩ = 3.84 W* | **17.7 W FET + 3.8 W PCB** |
| n_p = 2 | 8.83 W | 3.84 W | **8.8 W FET + 3.8 W PCB** |

\*Copper loss is board heating, not die heating. It still counts toward total board heat in §5.

For loss per individual MOSFET, divide by the sector duty. Each FET is in path about 1/3 of the electrical cycle on average. This sets the per-die steady-state limit; it is not part of the board total.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-leg high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side FET switches at roughly zero voltage after dead time.

### 3.1 Hard-switching overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot24\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = 1.16\ \text{W}$$

Cross-check transition time against gate drive:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_g}, \qquad I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}$$

Example: (V_drv − V_plateau) = 10 − 4.5 V across 2.2 Ω external + 1 Ω internal gives I_g ≈ 1.7 A. With Q_sw ≈ 20 nC, t ≈ 12 ns, consistent with the assumption. Weaker integrated drivers (≈ 1 A source / 1.5 A sink) will roughly double t_r and therefore P_sw.

### 3.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 3.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 0.030\ \text{W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (often underestimated)

$$P_{dt} = 2\,t_{dt}V_fI_{ph}f_{sw} = 2\cdot200\text{n}\cdot0.8\cdot80\cdot48\text{k} = 1.23\ \text{W}$$

Cutting dead time to 100 ns halves this loss. That is the cheapest efficiency gain available, provided shoot-through margin is verified on the scope.

### 3.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors)

$$P_g = 2\,Q_gV_{GS}f_{sw} = 2\cdot60\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 3.6 Per-channel total at 80 A, n_p = 1

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 17.66 |
| Hard switching | 1.16 |
| Dead time | 1.23 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.14 |
| **FET/driver subtotal** | **20.2** |
| PCB copper | 3.84 |
| **Channel total** | **≈ 24 W** |
