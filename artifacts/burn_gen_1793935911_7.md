**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. That runs on each ESC channel's own MCU, which runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware at a 48 kHz PWM setting. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC channels over **bidirectional DShot600**, one wire per motor. The model is split along that boundary.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max | Worst case for switching loss |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor channel | 320 A total pack burst |
| PWM frequency (f_sw) | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) | Set in ESC firmware |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal | 2 phases active, 1 phase PWMs at a time |
| MOSFET | 40 V, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, PDFN 5×6 (class of BSC010N04LS) | **Representative**; substitute your BOM datasheet values |
| Gate driver | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class), V_drv = 10 V | |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm ESC, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power planes | |

All formulas are parametric. Swap in your actual datasheet values and the numbers follow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.55) \approx 1.6\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.2 Six-step conduction topology

Two legs carry the phase current I_ph at any instant:

- **The PWM leg.** The HS FET conducts for duty D. The LS FET conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier, minus dead time.
- **The return leg.** The LS FET is held fully on.

So the conducting path always contains exactly two FET channels in series, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Plugging in I_ph = 80 A, R_DS = 1.6 mΩ, and R_Cu (planes plus phase pad) ≈ 0.3 mΩ:

$$P_{FET,cond} = 6400 \times 3.2\times10^{-3} = 20.5\ \mathrm{W}$$
$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = 1.9\ \mathrm{W}$$

The phase-current ripple correction is small and is neglected:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \Delta I^2/12$$

With ΔI ≈ 4 A at 48 kHz for a typical 5–10 µH motor at mid-duty, the added term is 1.3 A², which is under 0.03 %.

### 1.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_F\,I_{ph}\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(200\,\mathrm{ns})(48\,\mathrm{kHz}) = 1.23\ \mathrm{W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

### 2.1 Gate-drive-limited transition times

Gate-loop parameters:

- R_g,total = R_drv (3 Ω) + R_ext (2 Ω) + R_g,int (1 Ω) = 6 Ω
- V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V
- Q_gd ≈ 15 nC, Q_gs2 ≈ 6 nC

**Turn-on.** Gate current is (10 − 3.5)/6 = 1.08 A.

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{21\,\mathrm{nC}}{1.08\,\mathrm{A}} \approx 19\ \mathrm{ns}$$

**Turn-off.** Gate current is 3.5/6 = 0.58 A.

$$t_{f} \approx \frac{21\,\mathrm{nC}}{0.58\,\mathrm{A}} \approx 36\ \mathrm{ns}$$

### 2.2 Hard-switching V–I overlap

Only the HS FET of the PWM leg hard-switches. The LS FET soft-switches through its body diode.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(55\,\mathrm{ns})(48\,\mathrm{kHz}) = 2.66\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery, output capacitance, and gate charge

| Term | Formula | Inputs | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f | Q_rr ≈ 50 nC | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss,eff·V²·f | C_oss ≈ 2 nF | 0.03 W |
| Gate (driver dissipation) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f | Q_g = 95 nC | 0.09 W |

### 2.4 Per-channel and total loss budget at 80 A

| Loss term | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction (hot) | 20.5 W | 82.0 W |
| Copper | 1.9 W | 7.6 W |
