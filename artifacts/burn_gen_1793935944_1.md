## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. In every production AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) handle commutation, back-EMF sensing, and the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 cannot commutate the motors itself: four 6-step drivers would need 12 complementary PWM outputs, 12 comparator or ADC channels, and commutation timing jitter below 1 µs, all while it is also running the EKF. This document models that split architecture.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation, exactly two phases conduct at any instant: one leg sources current and one leg sinks it.

- With complementary PWM, the PWM-ed leg alternates between its high-side FET (for duty D) and its low-side FET in synchronous rectification (for 1−D).
- The sinking leg's low-side FET stays on continuously.
- Therefore, at every instant, the full phase current I_ph flows through exactly two FET channels in series, independent of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]$$

R_DS(on) depends on temperature, and temperature depends on loss, so the two must be solved together. With P = k·R(T) and T = T_ref + R_θ·P:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta k R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_\theta k R_{25}\alpha}, \qquad k = 2I_{ph}^2$$

The solution diverges (thermal runaway) when $R_\theta \, k \, R_{25}\, \alpha \ge 1$.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

k = 2·80² = 12,800 A²

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per motor | Average per FET (÷6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 2.56 W |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | 20.6 W | 3.43 W |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 22.3 W | 3.72 W |

**Per-FET distribution.** Each leg conducts 2/3 of the electrical period. Within the PWM sector, current is split D : (1−D) between the high-side and low-side FETs. Under burst conditions D approaches 1, so the high-side FETs carry most of the loss. Do not treat the six FETs as thermally identical: the high-side die run hotter during punch-outs.

**Copper loss** must be added. A 2 oz, 4 mm wide, 15 mm long trace is about 1.0 mΩ.

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace} \approx 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 3.2\ \text{W per motor}$$

This assumes the current paths are paralleled across multiple layers.
