# STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with Integrated 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Constraints

### 0.1 Which MCU switches the FETs

The STM32H743 does not drive the 48 kHz bridge PWM. In every viable 4-in-1 design, the switching MCU is a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are the STM32G071 or AT32F421: either one per motor, or a single MCU per 4-in-1. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

```
6S Battery ─┬─► 4× 3-phase bridges (24 FETs) ◄─ 4× gate drivers ◄─ 4× ESC MCU (AM32, TIM1 complementary PWM, 48 kHz)
            │                                                          ▲  (1-wire bidir DShot600)
            └─► BEC 10V/5V/3V3 ─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ───────────────┘
```

### 0.2 80 A burst is a transient rating, not a steady-state one

On a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm), "80 A burst" can only be met for a limited time. Section 3 quantifies that limit. The math is parameterized so you can substitute your real FET datasheet values.
