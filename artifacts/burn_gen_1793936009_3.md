## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform**, with $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m-\theta_{0})$, where $\theta_0$ is the electrical offset found at calibration:

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

**SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection).** This is equivalent to 7-segment SVM and gives a linear range up to $|v| = V_{dc}/\sqrt3$:

$$v_a=v_\alpha,\; v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}$$
