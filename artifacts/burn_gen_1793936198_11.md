## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architectural Correction

### 0.1 The H743 should not commutate the motors

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It should **not** drive the gate drivers directly.

- ArduPilot has no sensorless BLDC commutation stack.
- A 480 MHz flight-control loop should not carry 4× 48 kHz FOC/6-step ISRs.

**Baseline topology:**

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──4× bidir DShot600──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware)
                                              └─► 4× 3-phase gate driver ──► 24× N-MOSFET
```

All loss and DMA math below follows from this split.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, phase current I flows through:

- one high-side FET for duty D,
- its complementary low-side FET for (1 − D), during synchronous freewheel,
- the opposite-phase low-side FET for 100% of the sector.

Summing the conduction intervals:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

**Key result:** this is independent of duty cycle when synchronous rectification is used. At any instant, exactly two channels conduct.

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Phase-current ripple is set by the PWM:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
\;\xrightarrow{\;V_{bemf}\approx DV_{bus},\;D=0.5\;}\;
\frac{25.2\cdot0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\cdot48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.6\ \text{A}_{pp}
$$

The resulting RMS current is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2
$$

The ripple contribution is **0.06%**, so it is negligible at 48 kHz. That is a benefit of the high f_sw.

### 1.3 Temperature-coupled conduction loss (closed form)

Start with:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \qquad T_j = T_{ref} + P\,R_\theta
$$

Define the reference loss at 25 °C:

$$
P_0 = 2 I^2 R_{25}
$$

Solving the two equations self-consistently gives:

$$
\boxed{T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + P_0 R_\theta\,(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha P_0 R_\theta}}
$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:**

$$
\alpha\, P_0\, R_\theta \ge 1
$$

The PCB layout must keep α·P₀·R_θ ≤ 0.3 to stay well clear of runaway.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

- Reference loss: P₀ = 2 × 6404 × 1.2 mΩ = **15.4 W per motor**.
- Hot resistance at T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.81 mΩ**.
- Hot conduction loss: P_cond = 2 × 6404 × 1.81 mΩ = **23.2 W per motor**.
- Per-FET average (6 FETs share the work over a full electrical cycle): **3.9 W**.
