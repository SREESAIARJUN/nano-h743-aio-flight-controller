## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Flag

I don't have prior project documents for Job #18, so this document states every assumption explicitly. Replace the placeholder device parameters (marked ⟨param⟩) with datasheet values for your selected parts and the equations carry through unchanged.

**Assumed architecture.** The H743 does not commutate the motors directly. Ripping out the ESC MCUs would mean writing a custom ESC firmware stack inside ArduPilot, which is not supported. The architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──bidir DShot600 ×4──► 4 × AT32F421 (AM32 firmware)
                                              ──► 4 × FD6288Q 3-phase gate drivers
                                              ──► 24 × N-MOSFET (6 per motor)
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS,hot} = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha\approx0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS,hot}=1.2\,\text{m}\Omega\times(1+0.0045\times85)=1.66\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM-switched (complementary HS/LS) and one phase is held on (LS, or HS depending on the PWM scheme). Because synchronous rectification is used, **exactly two FETs carry the phase current at every instant, independent of duty cycle**, apart from dead time.

$$P_{cond,motor}=I_{rms}^2\cdot 2R_{DS,hot},\qquad I_{rms}^2=I_{DC}^2+\frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

### 1.3 Ripple current

$$\Delta I_{pp}=\frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{\phi\phi}\,f_{sw}}$$
