## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs in series:
- The active phase's high-side FET (during D), or its low-side FET (during 1−D).
- The return phase's low-side FET.

The duty cycle therefore cancels for the switched leg:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_0\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]^{\,\approx}\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\,\text{K}^{-1}\Rightarrow R(125\,°C)\approx 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega$$

This creates a positive feedback loop: higher temperature raises R_DS(on), which raises loss. It is solved iteratively in §3.4.
