## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Several requirements interact, so these decisions are fixed first. All later numbers depend on them.

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot) plus **4× AT32F421 ESC MCUs (AM32)** on the same PCB | Bidirectional DShot is an FC-to-ESC-MCU protocol. If the H743 drove the gates directly, DShot would have no purpose, and ArduPilot has no FOC/6-step ESC firmware. |
| 48 kHz | AM32 PWM frequency setting (24/48/96 kHz supported) | Sets the switching loss term. |
| Bus voltage | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Calculations use 25.2 V as the worst case. |
| MOSFET | 40 V, Rds(on) ≈ 0.7 mΩ @25 °C, 5×6 mm SuperSO8/PQFN class | **Representative values. Substitute the selected part's datasheet values into every equation.** |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver, 12 V VCC, approx. 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink | Common 4-in-1 practice. |
| Form factor | **30.5×30.5 mm mounting (≈36×36 mm board), 8-layer, 2 oz all layers** | See the warning below. |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm mount. **80 A per channel on 20×20 mm is not physically credible.** There is no room for 24 power FETs of the size required or for the copper cross-section needed. The model below uses 30.5×30.5 mm. Even there, 80 A is a **short burst rating (seconds)**, not continuous. Section 3 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Commutation with Synchronous Rectification

In each 60° electrical sector, current flows through one high-side FET of phase *x* and one low-side FET of phase *y*. With complementary PWM at duty *D*:

- **ON interval (D):** current flows through HS_x and LS_y.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** current flows through LS_x (synchronous rectifier) and LS_y.

Exactly two channels are always in series with the motor current:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{m}^2 \, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \quad (\text{independent of } D)
$$
