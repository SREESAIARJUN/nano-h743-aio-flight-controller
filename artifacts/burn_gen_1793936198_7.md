**Architectural correction.** The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. It is the flight controller. It emits bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, which own commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. The 80 A and 48 kHz analysis therefore applies to the ESC half of the board. The DMA analysis applies to the H743.

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel on a 30 × 30 mm board is a **burst rating of seconds, not continuous**. Section 3 quantifies this. Treat any "80 A continuous" marketing claim on this form factor as physically unsupportable without a heatsink.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (Six-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Current path

In 120° trapezoidal commutation, two phases are active at any instant:
- One leg is PWM-modulated: the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the synchronous low-side FET conducts for 1 − D.
- The opposite leg's low-side FET is held on.

With synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** channels in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature and ripple corrections

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\, D}{2 L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" or 7" motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-to-line 30 µH):
- At D = 0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 12 V, ΔI_pp ≈ (13.2 × 0.5)/(30e-6 × 48e3) ≈ **4.6 A**.
- The ripple term is therefore 4.6²/12 = 1.8 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

$$R_{ds}(110) = 0.70 \times (1 + 0.005 \times 85) = 1.00\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6402 \times 1.00\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{12.8\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Additional series copper must be counted as well:
- Shunt: 0.5 mΩ, adding I²R = 3.2 W. Consider removing the shunt and using R_ds sensing, or using 0.2 mΩ.
- Phase pads and traces: ≈ 0.3 mΩ, adding 1.9 W.
