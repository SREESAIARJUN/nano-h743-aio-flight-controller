# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Electrothermal Model and Bidirectional DShot600 Design

## 0. Scope and Architectural Assumptions

The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. In every viable 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any 60° sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low:

- **PWM on (duty D):** current flows through HS_X → motor → LS_Y.
- **PWM off (1 − D):** current freewheels through LS_X (synchronous rectification) → motor → LS_Y.

So exactly two MOSFETs carry the full phase current at every instant, regardless of duty. The per-channel conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

With L_ph = 10 µH (typical line-to-line for a 5" motor) and V_bus = 25.2 V at D = 0.5 near V_bemf ≈ 12.6 V:

$$\Delta I_{pp}=\frac{12.6\times0.5}{10\,\mu H\times48\,kHz}=13.1\,A$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = 6400+14.3 = 6414\ A^2$$

The ripple contribution is about 0.2%, so it is negligible at 48 kHz. That is one of the arguments *for* 48 kHz.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha\approx0.0045\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel (2 FETs) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.83 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.16 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.60 W |
| 140 °C | 1.52 mΩ | 19.47 W |

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses

**PCB copper.** Phase pad → FET → bus is about 0.3 mΩ round trip per conducting path:

$$P_{Cu}=I^2R_{Cu}=6400\times0.3\ m\Omega=1.92\ W$$

**Bus current shunt.** A 0.2 mΩ shunt sees I_bus,rms = I·√D, so worst case at D → 1:

$$P_{shunt}\le 6400\times0.2\ m\Omega = 1.28\ W$$
