## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Critical Findings

### 0.1 Architectural clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the power stage directly. The architecture is split into two parts:

```
LiPo 6S ──► 4-in-1 ESC board (4× ESC MCU + 4× 3-phase gate driver + 24 MOSFETs)
                ▲  DShot600 bidirectional (1 wire per motor, half-duplex)
                │
           FC board: STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── TIM3/TIM5 + DMA ── M1..M4
```

- The 48 kHz PWM and the commutation run on each ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32).
- The H743 only generates DShot600 frames and decodes the eRPM telemetry.
- Loss and thermal analysis applies to the ESC board. Timer and DMA analysis applies to the FC.
