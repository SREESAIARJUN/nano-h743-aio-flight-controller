# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture (Job #41)

## 0. Feasibility Findings

**Finding 1: Topology.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate motors. Each of the four motor channels needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. The 48 kHz switching frequency is set in the ESC firmware, not in ArduPilot. The model below follows this split.

**Finding 2: The 80 A rating.** The analysis interprets "80 A burst" as **80 A per channel, short duration, on 6S**.
- **All four channels at 80 A means 320 A at the battery.** That exceeds any nano-format connector, the battery leads, and the board copper by a wide margin. Section 4 shows the board survives that case for only about 1–2 s.
- **A single channel at 80 A is realistic only as a burst of a few seconds.**
- **The marketing rating should state duration and conditions.** For example: "80 A / 5 s, single channel, 25 °C ambient, prop-wash airflow."

**Finding 3: Component values.** No specific MOSFET part number was given. The calculations use parameters typical of a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN N-channel part. **Substitute datasheet values for the selected FET before release.**
