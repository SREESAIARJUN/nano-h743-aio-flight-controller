## 0. Architecture Clarification and Stated Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. In every production AIO design the system splits into two domains:

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 / VIT6 (480 MHz max; ArduPilot runs it at 400 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control. Emits bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC. |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071, or 1× quad-capable MCU | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | 6-step sensorless commutation. Generates 48 kHz complementary PWM. Returns eRPM telemetry over DShot. |

The power math in §1–§3 therefore describes the ESC half. The DMA math in §4 describes the H743 half.

> **Engineering flag:** a 40 V device on 6S leaves about 15 V of headroom for ringing. This is common in the industry, but it requires the snubber and bulk-capacitance measures in §5. For a conservative design, use 4S, or use 60 V FETs and accept roughly 1.6× higher R_DS(on).
