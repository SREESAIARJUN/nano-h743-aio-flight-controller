# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Mathematical Model

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

I don't have access to prior "Job #44" documents, so this design rests on stated assumptions. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your actual datasheet values.

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly. The H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 (or BLHeli_32), giving four ESC MCUs in total. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600 on short internal traces.
