### Critical architecture flags

| # | Flag | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each 4-in-1 ESC runs its own MCU per channel (e.g., STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). | The H743 only generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM lives in the ESC MCUs. |
| F2 | "Nano" (20×20 mm) and 80 A burst are physically incompatible for sustained operation. | Minimum realistic footprint is 30.5×30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz or heavier copper. |
| F3 | 4 × 80 A simultaneously at full throttle gives roughly 320 A battery current. | XT60 connectors (~60 A continuous) and typical shunts cannot support this. "80 A burst" must be defined as **per channel, short duration, not all four simultaneously**. |
| F4 | Board heat capacity is only about 6 J/K. | A burst of 80 A on all four channels lasts less than 4 s before limits (§3.4). |
