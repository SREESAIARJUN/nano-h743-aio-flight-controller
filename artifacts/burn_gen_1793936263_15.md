## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Flags

Two points need settling before any math.

**1. The H743 belongs on the flight controller, not the ESC.**
- ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. It emits DShot and consumes eRPM telemetry.
- Each 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 (one per motor, or a 4-core arrangement).
- This document therefore treats the system as:

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x BDShot600--> [4-in-1 ESC: 4x MCU + 4x 3-phase driver + 24 MOSFETs]
```

**2. "80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm nano board is thermally implausible as a sustained or multi-second rating on all four channels.**
- The derivation in §3 shows roughly **130 W of loss** with all four channels at 80 A.
- That yields only about **1.3 s** of headroom.
- Treat 80 A as a single-channel, short-transient rating, or move to a 30.5×30.5 mm format.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, two legs are active at any instant:
- **PWM leg:** current alternates between the high-side (HS) FET during D and the low-side (LS) FET during 1–D. Either way, exactly one FET conducts I_ph.
- **Return leg:** its LS FET stays on continuously during the 60° sector.
