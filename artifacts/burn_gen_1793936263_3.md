# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model and Reference Design (Job #56)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and One Critical Finding

**System partition.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU running ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel uses its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware with 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bi-directional DShot600.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |

| Burst phase current per channel | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET class | — | 40 V, PDFN 5×6, R_ds(on) 1.0 mΩ typ @ 25 °C, V_gs = 10 V |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | k_T | ≈ 1.6 @ T_j = 125 °C |
| Rise + fall time (driver-limited) | t_r + t_f | 15 + 15 ns |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns per edge |
| Body diode V_f / Q_rr | — | 0.8 V / 40 nC |
| Output capacitance | C_oss | 1.5 nF (effective) |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Board ("nano", stacked) | — | 20 × 20 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz all layers |
| Ambient | T_a | 40 °C |

> **Critical finding (stated up front).** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A is only survivable as a **single-channel, seconds-scale burst**. All four channels at 80 A will reach a 125 °C board limit in about **1.8 s**. The math is in §3. Either:
> - specify the 80 A as a per-channel burst with firmware current limiting at roughly 35–40 A continuous, or
> - move to a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step (trapezoidal) topology with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for fraction D. The complementary low-side conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

So the current always passes through exactly **two** R_ds(on) elements, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At 80 A with a hot junction (1.6 mΩ):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.2 Copper path loss

Assume about 0.3 mΩ of total path resistance per channel (bus planes, phase pads, battery pads):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{path} = 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle.

- **Instantaneous peak:** 80² × 1.6 mΩ = 10.2 W.
- **Cycle-averaged:** about (20.5 + switching)/6 ≈ 3.8 W per FET (see §2).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per channel:

$$P_{sw} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}}_{\text{V–I overlap}} + \underbrace{Q_{rr} V f_{sw}}_{\text{recovery}} + \underbrace{2\cdot\tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f_{sw}}_{\text{C}_{oss}} + \underbrace{2 V_f I\, t_{dt} f_{sw}}_{\text{dead time}} + \underbrace{2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}}_{\text{gate}}$$

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k | 1.45 W |
| Reverse recovery | 25.2 × 40 nC × 48 k | 0.05 W |
| C_oss (2 FETs) | 1.5 nF × 25.2² × 48 k | 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver) | 2 × 50 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.05 W |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |
