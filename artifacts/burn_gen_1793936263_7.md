## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal control with synchronous rectification

During any commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd (high side Q_H for duty D, low side Q_L for 1 − D, complementary) and one phase's low side is held fully on. The current therefore always flows through exactly **two channels in series**:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{hot}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

$$
R_{hot} = k_T R_{25} = 1.6 \times 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.92\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W\ per\ channel}
$$
