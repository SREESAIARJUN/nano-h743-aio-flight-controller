## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel (or the whole 4-in-1) runs its own commutation MCU, such as AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU owns the 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and gate drive. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

| Domain | Owner | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot TX/RX, logging | STM32H743 (ArduPilot) | Timer/DMA, eRPM decode, harmonic notch |
| Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead-time | ESC MCU ×4 (AM32 / BLHeli_32) | Six-step or FOC, current limit |
| Power stage | 3-phase gate driver + 6 FETs per motor | 80 A burst conduction and switching |

**Parameterized values.** All device values below are representative of a 40 V, 5×6 mm, sub-1 mΩ FET class. Before release, substitute the values from the actual selected FET's datasheet: R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, Q_gs2, Q_rr, Q_oss and Z_th(jc).

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current Path Topology

In six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, motor current always traverses exactly two FET positions:

- the PWM-ing phase leg (high-side FET during D, synchronous low-side FET during 1−D), and
- the static low-side FET of the return phase.

The duty cycle redistributes current between the two FETs of the PWM leg, but one of them always conducts. The conduction loss per motor is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu,path}$$

### 2.2 Temperature Dependence

R_DS(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad k \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This creates an electro-thermal feedback loop, which must be iterated (Section 4.4).
