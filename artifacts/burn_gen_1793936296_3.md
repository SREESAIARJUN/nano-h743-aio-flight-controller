## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
θ_e is the electrical angle: θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset.

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**
- i_α = i_a
- i_β = (i_a + 2 i_b)/√3

**Park:**
- i_d = i_α cos θ_e + i_β sin θ_e
- i_q = −i_α sin θ_e + i_β cos θ_e

**Inverse Park:**
- v_α = v_d cos θ_e − v_q sin θ_e
- v_β = v_d sin θ_e + v_q cos θ_e

### 1.2 PMSM model in the dq frame
- v_d = R i_d + L di_d/dt − ω_e L i_q
- v_q = R i_q + L di_q/dt + ω_e L i_d + ω_e λ

Electromagnetic torque (surface PM, L_d = L_q):

τ_e = (3/2) p λ i_q ≡ K_t i_q

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling
Each axis plant is G_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole. The closed loop then becomes a first-order lag with bandwidth ω_c.

C_i(s) = K_p + K_i/s, with K_p = L ω_c and K_i = R ω_c, giving i/i* = ω_c/(s + ω_c).
