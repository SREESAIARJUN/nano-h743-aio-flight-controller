**Architecture.** The encoder is used only for commutation and the cogging phase. The gimbal's stabilization loops (velocity and attitude) close on the **inertial** camera rate and attitude measured by the fused IMUs. This is what rejects base motion.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

$$\theta_e = \mathrm{wrap}_{2\pi}\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})\big)$$

$\theta_{\text{off}}$ comes from an alignment routine: inject $V_d$ at $\theta_e=0$ and read the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform (amplitude-invariant, two shunts, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park Transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq Frame (surface PMSM, $L_d=L_q=L$)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

where $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$ and $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}$ (amplitude-invariant convention).
