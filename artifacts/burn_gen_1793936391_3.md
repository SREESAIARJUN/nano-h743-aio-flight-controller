# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Flight Controller: Power-Stage & BDShot Architecture Model

**Scope:** This document models conduction loss, switching loss, and transient thermal behavior for an 80 A/motor burst at 48 kHz PWM. It also allocates timers and DMA for ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 on the STM32H743, and provides reference netlists.

> **Important note on inputs.** I don't have the Job #3 BOM, stack-up, or MOSFET selection. Every numeric result below comes from the **explicitly declared baseline parameters** in §0. All equations are parametric, so substitute your actual datasheet values and the numbers update directly. Treat the worked figures as engineering estimates. They are not a substitute for bench validation with thermocouples, a thermal camera, and current probes.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous PWM

At any instant, exactly two phases conduct: one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS). Each phase spends 120° electrical as the high leg, 120° as the low leg, and 120° floating.

Inside its 120° "high" window, the PWM leg alternates:
- the HS FET conducts for D,
- its complementary LS FET conducts for (1 − D), which is synchronous rectification.

Inside its 120° "low" window, the LS FET is held fully on.

This gives the per-device duty-weighted conduction factors:

$$
k_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}, \qquad k_{LS} = \frac{(1-D) + 1}{3} = \frac{2-D}{3}
$$

The check sums correctly: 3·(k_HS + k_LS) = 3·(2/3) = 2. This confirms that two devices are conducting at all times.
