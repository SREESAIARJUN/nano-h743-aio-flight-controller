## 0. Feasibility Finding

**An 80 A per-channel burst on a nano-class board is thermally limited to about 1–2 seconds, not tens of seconds.** At 80 A, each channel dissipates about 28 W, about 110 W total. The board's heat capacity of about 4.5 J/K absorbs this only briefly before junction limits are reached.

The 80 A figure is defensible only as a short-duration burst rating with firmware enforcement. Section 4.5 covers the details. If the requirement means sustained 80 A, the form factor must grow to 30.5 × 30.5 mm or larger with paralleled FETs. That is a requirements change, not a design tweak.
