## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle and speed observer

The electrical angle is

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Mechanical speed comes from a type-2 PLL on the encoder, which avoids differentiating quantization noise:

$$
\varepsilon_k = \operatorname{wrap}_{\pi}(\theta_{m,k}-\hat\theta_k),\quad
\hat\omega_{k+1}=\hat\omega_k + T_s k_i\,\varepsilon_k,\quad
\hat\theta_{k+1}=\hat\theta_k + T_s(\hat\omega_k + k_p\varepsilon_k)
$$

The gains are $k_p = 2\zeta\omega_n$ and $k_i=\omega_n^2$. Use $\zeta=0.707$ and $\omega_n = 2\pi\cdot 300\,\text{rad/s}$.

### 1.2 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, assuming $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$:
$$i_\alpha=i_a,\qquad i_\beta=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a+2i_b)$$

Park transform:
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e&\sin\theta_e\\-\sin\theta_e&\cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose.

### 1.3 Plant and PI design (pole–zero cancellation)

The machine equations are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e(L i_d+\lambda_m)$$

Apply decoupling feed-forward:

$$v_d^\star = \text{PI}_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^\star = \text{PI}_q + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

Each axis then reduces to $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$. Choose PI gains so the controller zero cancels the plant pole:

$$K_p^{i}= L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i}= R\,\omega_c\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{I}{I^\star}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Use $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1.5\,\text{kHz}$. This is below $f_{pwm}/10$, which leaves margin for the 1.5-sample PWM/computation delay.

### 1.4 Voltage limit and SVPWM

Apply a circular limit on the voltage vector:

$$|v_{dq}|\le V_{max}=\tfrac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95$$

The q-axis gets priority: clamp $v_d$ to $\pm V_{max}$ first. Then clamp $|v_q|\le\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2}$.
