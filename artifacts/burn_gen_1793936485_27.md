### 0.2 Key finding: the spec cannot hold 80 A continuously

80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm 4-in-1 is a **sub-2-second burst rating only**. Section 3 shows the board accumulates heat at about 40 K/s with all four channels at 80 A. Most of this document is about bounding that burst. If the program requires sustained 80 A, the form factor must change to a 30.5×30.5 mm layout with paralleled 5×6 FETs.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

Each 60° commutation sector has one phase PWM'd high, one phase held low, and one phase floating for BEMF sensing. Over a full electrical cycle, each phase leg spends:

- **120° sourcing.** The high-side (HS) FET conducts for fraction D. The synchronous low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1−D).
- **120° sinking.** The LS FET conducts at 100%.
- **120° floating.**

### 1.2 Per-FET average conduction loss

With R_hot = k_T · R_DS(on),25:

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{hot}\cdot\frac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{LS} = I^2 R_{hot}\cdot\frac{1 + (1-D)}{3}$$

Summing one leg:

$$P_{leg} = I^2 R_{hot}\cdot\frac{2}{3}$$

Summing three legs per ESC:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}}$$

This is independent of D, which is consistent with exactly two FET channels always being in series with the motor current.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

R_hot = 1.6 mΩ.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per ESC | 2 × 80² × 1.6×10⁻³ = **20.5 W** |
| P_HS (D = 0.95) | 6400 × 1.6 m × 0.317 = 3.24 W |
| P_LS | 6400 × 1.6 m × 0.35 = 3.58 W |
| Hottest FET | LS, ≈ 3.6 W average |

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often forgotten)

- **Trace/plane loss.** Assume ~0.5 mΩ effective per phase path, inner 2 oz, two phases in series. P = 80² × 1.0 m = **6.4 W per ESC**.
- **Shunt.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt on the shared DC return gives 80² × 0.25 m × D ≈ **1.5 W**. Using a 4-in-1 aggregate current sense instead of per-channel shunts reduces the count but not the loss density.

### 1.5 Temperature coefficient feedback

Model R_DS(on) as:

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\mathrm{K}$$

Loss and temperature are coupled through P = f(R(T_j)) and T_j = T_board + R_θjc·P. This converges only if α · R_θ,total · P_25 < 1. That condition holds per FET (R_θjc ≈ 1.5 K/W), but the board-level loop (Section 3) does not converge in steady state. This is formally why the rating is burst-only.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches at any instant. Its HS FET switches hard, and its LS FET recovers through the body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 Overlap (V–I crossover)

$$E_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\,\text{ns} = 30.2\ \mu\text{J}$$

$$P_{sw} = E_{sw} f = 30.2\,\mu \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

The overlap times come from gate charge: t ≈ Q_gd / I_gate = 10 nC / ~0.7 A at the Miller plateau ≈ 15 ns.

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\,\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f = 40\,\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

The Q_rr-induced turn-on overlap adds roughly 2× this amount in the HS FET, about 0.1 W.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f = 0.5 \times 50\,\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.03\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate charge (dissipated in the driver, not the FET)

$$P_g = 2 \cdot Q_g V_{GS} f = 2 \times 50\,\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W per ESC}$$

The bootstrap and LDO overhead brings the driver total to about 0.1 W.

### 2.6 Switching total

$$P_{sw,ESC} \approx 1.45 + 0.31 + 0.15 + 0.03 \approx \mathbf{1.95\ W}\ (\text{6S, 80 A})$$

On 4S (16.8 V), the total scales roughly linearly with V_bus, giving about 1.35 W.
