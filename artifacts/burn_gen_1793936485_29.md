# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #24)

## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

This document is a **parametric engineering model** built on representative component values. These values are listed in §1. Replace them with the datasheet values of your selected parts before release. Every equation is written so you can substitute directly.

**Architecture assumed.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. Putting closed-loop FOC or 6-step commutation for 4 motors on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not supported by ArduPilot and is not recommended.

**Key findings:**

1. **80 A per channel is a burst rating only, about 1 to 2 s, on a nano board.** Natural plus prop-wash cooling sustains only about 7 to 15 W total board dissipation. 80 A on all four channels produces about 130 W.
2. **Conduction losses dominate.** They are about 15× the switching loss at 48 kHz. MOSFET Rds(on) and copper resistance drive the design, not gate drive speed.
3. **Commutation overshoot can exceed a 40 V FET rating** with 2 nH of loop inductance at fast edges. Layout and gate-resistor tuning are mandatory, not optional.
4. **Bidirectional DShot600 on the H743 needs 3 DMA streams per 4-motor timer.** That is 1 UP-burst stream plus 2 capture streams. DMAMUX makes this easy, but all buffers must sit outside DTCM.
