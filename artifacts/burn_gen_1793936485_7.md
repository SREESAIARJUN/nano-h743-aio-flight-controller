# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #47): Reference Architecture & Math Model

I don't have Job #47's actual MOSFET part numbers, stack-up, board outline, or airframe data. Every number below comes from a stated, adjustable assumption. Treat the netlists as a reference design to adapt and verify. They have not been signed off for fabrication.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the **STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs**. The partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), ArduPilot | Sends bi-directional DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs, receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC MCU ×4 | e.g. AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (AM32 / BLHeli_32) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, back-EMF sensing |
| Gate driver ×4 | e.g. FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge, bootstrap) | Level-shift and drive 6 FETs per motor |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3 or 5×6 | Phases A/B/C |

So the losses (§1–3) live on the ESC board, and the DMA/timer allocation (§4) lives on the FC.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor, 6-step with synchronous rectification)

In 6-step commutation, one high-side FET (PWM) and one low-side FET (on for the 120° sector) conduct. During PWM off-time the complementary low-side FET conducts synchronously. **At any instant, two FETs carry I in series.**

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot \left(2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\right)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{ds,25}\,[1 + k(T_j - 25)],\quad k \approx 0.005\,/\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds ≈ 1.5 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \cdot (2\cdot1.5 + 0.5)\times10^{-3} = 6400 \cdot 3.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.4\ W}$$

(FETs: 19.2 W, copper: 3.2 W.)

Per-FET average: each FET conducts roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle, but the PWM high-side and sync low-side split by duty D. A good worst-case per-FET figure is ~19.2 W / 6 × 2 ≈ **6.4 W peak-sector average**. Use 9.6 W (I²·R_ds) as the instantaneous figure for transient Z_th.

### 2.2 Hard-Switching Loss (one switching FET per motor at a time)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dead} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.4 Reverse Recovery, C_oss, Gate Charge

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \cdot 50\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 1\text{n} \cdot 635 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.015\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2\cdot40\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

(Gate loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g, not the FET.)

### 2.5 Total

| Term | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Conduction (Cu) | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching | 1.94 W | 7.8 W |
| Dead-time | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.11 W | 0.45 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 25.1 W** | **≈ 100 W** |

**Insight:** at 80 A, conduction loss is about 88% of the total. Raising f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz costs only ~1.3 W per motor. R_ds(on) and copper are the levers that matter, not frequency.

**Efficiency check:** P_out ≈ 25.2 × 80 × 4 ≈ 8.06 kW, so η ≈ 98.8% electrically. The heat is still the problem.

### 2.6 DC-Link Capacitor Ripple (often the real failure point)

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; I_{C,rms,max} = 0.5\,I = \mathbf{40\ A\ rms\ per\ motor}\ (D = 0.5)$$

Mitigations:
- Place ≥ 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 MLCCs per half-bridge group, directly at the FET drains/sources.
- Add a shared low-ESR bulk capacitor (e.g. 35 V 1000 µF + external 470 µF at the pads).
- MLCC derating at 25 V bias is about −60%, so plan with ~4 µF effective each.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Steady-State Path (per FET)

$$T_j = T_a + P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta CB} + R_{\theta BA,\text{share}})$$

For a nano board with no heatsink, R_θBA for the whole board in prop-wash is about 4–6 K/W total. That gives:

$$\Delta T_{board,ss} = 100\ \text{W} \times 5\ \text{K/W} = 500\ \text{K}$$

**80 A continuous is impossible.** Sustainable continuous current at ΔT_board = 60 K:

$$P_{allow} = 60/5 = 12\ \text{W total} \Rightarrow I_{cont} \approx \sqrt{\frac{12/4}{3.5\text{m}}} \approx \mathbf{29\ A/motor}$$

A slightly higher figure is achievable with lower R_ds and a thermal pad to the frame.

### 3.2 Burst (Lumped Transient) Model

$$C_{th}\frac{dT}{dt} = P - \frac{T - T_a}{R_{\theta BA}} \;\Rightarrow\; T(t) = T_a + P R_{\theta BA}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right),\quad \tau = R_{\theta BA} C_{th}$$

Estimate C_th: 10 g board (FR4 + Cu + FETs), c ≈ 0.9 J/(g·K), so C_th ≈ 9 J/K and τ ≈ 45 s.

For t ≪ τ the response is nearly adiabatic: ΔT ≈ P·t / C_th = 100·t/9 ≈ **11 K/s**.

| Burst duration | ΔT_board |
|---|---|
| 2 s | ~22 K |
| 5 s | ~54 K |
| 10 s | ~105 K → exceeds limits from T_a = 40 °C |
