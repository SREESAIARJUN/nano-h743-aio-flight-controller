# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Design Model (Job #45)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly. Four 3-phase bridges need 24 gate signals with dead-time, plus fast, synchronized ADC and back-EMF sensing. The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 as complementary advanced timers (plus HRTIM). Running four motor-control loops alongside ArduPilot's EKF3 is a timing-risk anti-pattern.

**Adopted architecture:**

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz in this design | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), running AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V-class) or equivalent | Bootstrap high-side drive |
| MOSFET ×24 | 40 V, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.5 mΩ @ 25 °C, 3.3×3.3 mm (e.g., ON NTTFS / Infineon ISZ-class) | Power stage |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max | — |

### 0.2 Interpretation of the 80 A rating

"80 A burst" is modeled as **80 A per motor, DC-equivalent phase current, for ≤1 s**. This is evaluated for two cases:

- **Worst case:** all four channels at 80 A simultaneously.
- **Single-channel case:** one channel at 80 A.

Section 2.5 shows that a nano-class (20×20 or 30×30 mm) board cannot sustain this. Treat 80 A as a marketing-grade burst figure. The engineered continuous rating comes out near 35–40 A per channel.

### 0.3 Parameter values

All component parameters (R_DS(on), Q_g, Q_rr, C_oss, rise/fall times, R_θ) are representative values for this MOSFET class. Substitute the datasheet values of the part you actually choose.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step conduction with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector:

- **Phase A (PWM phase):** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The complementary low side (LS) conducts for (1 − D).
- **Phase B (return phase):** the LS conducts 100% of the time.
- **Phase C:** floating; used for back-EMF zero-crossing detection.

Loss in the PWM phase leg:

$$P_{A} = D\,I^2R_{HS} + (1-D)\,I^2R_{LS} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\quad(\text{independent of }D)$$

Loss in the return leg:

$$P_B = I^2 R_{ds,hot}$$

Total conduction loss per channel:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha\approx 0.006\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}(110) = 1.5\,\mathrm{m\Omega} \times 1.51 = 2.27\,\mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

The path runs from bus pad, through the HS FET, the phase pad, the motor, the return phase pad, and the LS FET, back to the GND plane. With 4 layers × 2 oz copper, plus vias, plus the shunt:

$$R_{Cu}\approx 0.35\,\mathrm{m\Omega}\ (\text{traces/vias}) + 0.25\,\mathrm{m\Omega}\ (\text{shunt}) = 0.60\,\mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.4 Results at 80 A

With I² = 6400 A²:

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 6400 × 2.27 mΩ | **29.1 W** |
| Copper + shunt | 6400 × 0.60 mΩ | **3.8 W** |
| **Per channel** | | **32.9 W** |
| **4 channels** | | **131.6 W** |

### 1.5 Per-device average

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle (HS or LS, depending on sector).

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{29.1}{6}\approx 4.85\ \mathrm{W}$$

This is a cycle average. At low eRPM, the instantaneous peak is 14.5 W while a FET conducts. Below roughly 50 Hz electrical frequency, the junction tracks that instantaneous peak rather than the average.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-phase leg switches: one HS/LS pair per channel per sector. Operating point: V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r = t_f = 20 ns (driver sourcing about 1.5 A into a Q_gd of about 10 nC):

$$P_{VI} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot40\,\mathrm{ns}\cdot48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{DT} = V_F\, I\,(2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\,\mathrm{ns}\cdot48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

This assumes t_dt = 100 ns per edge. AM32 dead-time must be tuned to the driver and FET.

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 40\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss,er} V^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\,\mathrm{nF}\cdot635\cdot48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and gate resistors, not in the FET)

$$P_G = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot45\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot10\cdot48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.04\ W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total

| Scope | Value |
|---|---|
| Per channel | **2.69 W** |
| 4 channels | **10.8 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz, about 1.35 W per channel would be saved, at the cost of more current ripple and audible noise. At 96 kHz, switching losses double to 5.4 W per channel.
