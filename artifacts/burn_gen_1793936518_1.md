## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

This design uses the industry-standard partition. The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM.

Driving 24 FETs directly from the H743 is possible, but ArduPilot has no native motor-commutation stack. That approach would also put sub-microsecond commutation interrupts on the flight-control core.
