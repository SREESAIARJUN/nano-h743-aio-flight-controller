## 0. Architecture Decisions and Scope

Three decisions set the scope of everything that follows.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which performs commutation, 48 kHz PWM and eRPM telemetry. ArduPilot has no native FOC or 6-step motor control. The H743-to-ESC link is bidirectional DShot600.
   - Topology: an all-in-one (AIO) board, H743 FC plus a 4-in-1 power stage, with one ESC MCU per channel. A single 4-channel ESC MCU is not used.

2. **80 A is a burst rating, not continuous, on any "nano" footprint.** Section 3 shows that a 30.5×30.5 mm board can sustain about 25–30 A per channel continuously under prop wash. 80 A is achievable for about 3 s from a 25 °C start. A 20×20 mm board cannot credibly claim 80 A burst; its copper heat capacity is roughly half that of 30.5×30.5. **Baseline: 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers.**

3. **The rating convention is 80 A per-channel phase (motor) current**, the worst-case definition. Battery-side current is D·I_ph, which is smaller.
