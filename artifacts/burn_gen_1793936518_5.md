# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst): Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and ArduPilot Bi-directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Several facts about this spec should be settled before layout.

1. **80 A per channel on a nano board is a short burst only.** The model below shows about 115–120 W of dissipation on a board with roughly 5 J/K of heat capacity. That gives a sustainable burst of about **1–2 s**, not a continuous rating. Firmware current limiting must enforce this.
2. **4 × 80 A means 320 A at the battery.** XT60 connectors, battery leads, the bus planes and the bulk capacitors must be sized for that. A 30.5 × 30.5 mm board is the practical minimum, not 20 × 20.
3. **The 48 kHz PWM is set by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.** The ESC MCUs (AT32F421/STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) generate the 48 kHz. ArduPilot only sends DShot frames and receives eRPM telemetry.
