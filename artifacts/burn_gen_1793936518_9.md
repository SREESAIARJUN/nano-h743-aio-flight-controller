# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design Package

## 0. Architecture Scope and Assumptions

In a 4-in-1 ESC, the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot on the flight-controller side and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM, does commutation, and returns eRPM telemetry. The model below covers both halves.
