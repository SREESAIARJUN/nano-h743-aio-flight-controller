**Feasibility verdict (detailed in §3):** 80 A per motor is survivable only as a short transient (≈ 2–3 s) on a nano/30.5 board. Realistic continuous rating is about 35–45 A per motor with airflow. Any "80 A" label should be specified with a duration and an ambient temperature.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Use the datasheet normalized R_DS(on) vs. T_j curve if available. At T_j = 125 °C this gives a factor of 1.5, so R_DS ≈ 1.8 mΩ.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

In every 60° commutation sector, one phase is the PWM leg (high side HS, complementary low side LS), one phase is the return (LS held on), and one phase floats.

- **During t_on:** current flows through HS_a and LS_b, so 2 × R_DS.
- **During t_off (synchronous):** current flows through LS_a and LS_b, so 2 × R_DS.

Therefore, independent of duty, the motor current always flows through **two** channels (excluding dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{Cu}$$

For trapezoidal drive, I_ph,rms ≈ I_DC,motor (flat-top phase current during its 120° conduction). Here R_Cu is the PCB plus pad plus solder path: ~0.3–0.6 mΩ per phase loop on 2 oz copper.

### 1.3 Per-FET average loss

Each LS FET carries current about 1/3 of the electrical period (as return) plus its synchronous freewheel share. Each HS FET carries D/3. A practical worst-case design value is:

$$P_{cond,FET,max} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{3}\cdot (1 + (1-D)) \quad\text{(LS FET, worst at low D)}$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.6\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.4\,\text{m}\Omega = 2.6\ \text{W (dissipated in the PCB, which helps spread heat)}$$

### 1.5 Self-consistent junction temperature (fixed-point iteration)

R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. Iterate:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{ref} + R_{\theta,j\text{-}ref}\,P\big(R_{DS}(T_j^{(k)})\big)$$

Converge when |ΔT| < 0.5 K. Thermal runaway occurs if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_{\theta} = I^2 R_{DS,25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta} \ge 1$$

Check per FET at 80 A: 6400 × 1.2 mΩ × 0.005 × R_θ(≈3 K/W) / (share ≈ 1/3 to 2/3) ≈ 0.08–0.15. This is well below 1, so there is no runaway at the device level. The limit is board heat capacity, not device stability.

## 2. Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Per motor, only the **PWM leg** hard-switches (one HS turn-on/turn-off per period). The return leg is static within a sector.

### 2.1 Hard-switching (V–I overlap)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

Rise/fall time is estimated from gate charge:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{g,src}},\quad t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{g,sink}}$$

With Q_gd + Q_gs2 ≈ 10 nC, 0.5 A / 1 A drive, plus layout inductance: t_r ≈ 20 ns and t_f ≈ 15–20 ns, so use t_r + t_f = 40 ns.

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the complementary LS body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

(Q_rr rises with di/dt and I_F; derate ×2 at 80 A: **0.12 W**.)
