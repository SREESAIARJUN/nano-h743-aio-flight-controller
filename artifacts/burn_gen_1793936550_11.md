# Job #62: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst) Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

**Topology.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071), a 3-phase gate driver, and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. The switching-loss model below therefore applies to the ESC power stages, and the DMA model applies to the H743.

**Key assumptions.** Change these and re-run the equations.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) | Worst case is used for switching loss |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 DFN | Representative class, not a validated part |
| R_DS(on) | 1.2 mΩ typ at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | |
| Q_g / Q_rr / C_oss(er) | 50 nC / 60 nC / 1 nF | |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class | Approx. 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink |
| t_r + t_f (effective V–I overlap) | 30 ns at 80 A | |
| Dead time | 100 ns per edge; body diode V_f = 0.8 V | |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step | |
| Burst current | 80 A phase current per channel | |

**⚠ Engineering flags.** These need to be resolved before the 80 A figure is used anywhere.

1. **Dissipation.** 4 × 80 A burst means about 100 W of dissipation (derived below) on a nano board (20×20 to 30×30 mm). The board can only survive this for about 1–2 s. It cannot sustain it. "80 A" must be specified as a time-limited burst, for example 80 A for ≤1 s with a ≥10 s recovery.
2. **Battery current.** Total battery current can reach about 320 A. An XT60 connector (about 60 A continuous) and typical 12 AWG leads are not compatible with this. Battery-side current will be lower in practice because I_bat ≈ D·I_phase, but the connector rating still needs review.
3. **Netlist scope.** The netlists below are representative reference netlists: one ESC channel plus the H743 motor/telemetry interface. A full production netlist, with every decoupling cap, the IMU, the OSD and so on, comes from the EDA tool. It should not be hand-written in a document.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, at any instant there are three conduction paths:

- **PWM leg, high-side:** conducts for D·T. Loss = I²·R·D
- **PWM leg, low-side (synchronous):** conducts for (1−D)·T. Loss = I²·R·(1−D)
- **Return leg, low-side:** conducts for the full period. Loss = I²·R

These sum to:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of duty cycle, which is a useful design property.

**Temperature dependence of R_DS(on):**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\,/^\circ C$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

**Ripple correction.** I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12, where

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}}$$

With L_LL = 20 µH (phase-to-phase), D = 0.9 and V = 25.2 V:

- ΔI = 25.2 × 0.09 / (20 µH × 48 kHz) = **2.36 A**
- ΔI²/12 = **0.46 A²**, which is negligible against 6400 A².

**Result at 80 A:**

| T_j | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|
| 125 °C (R = 1.8 mΩ) | 2 × 6400.5 × 1.8 mΩ = **23.0 W** |
| 25 °C (R = 1.2 mΩ) | **15.4 W** |

**Per-FET peak.** The return low-side FET carries 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **11.5 W** instantaneous. Commutation rotates this duty across the 6 FETs, giving a **time-averaged ≈ 3.85 W per FET** at 125 °C. At typical eRPM (electrical frequency of hundreds of Hz to kHz), the die's thermal time constant (ms) only partially averages this. Design to the 11.5 W peak for junction ripple.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz (Per Channel)

Only the PWM leg switches. The loss terms are:

$$P_{sw} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f}_{V\text{-}I\ overlap} + \underbrace{Q_{rr} V f}_{recovery} + \underbrace{2\cdot\tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f}_{C_{oss}} + \underbrace{V_f\, I\, (2t_{dt})\, f}_{dead\text{-}time\ diode} + \underbrace{2\,Q_g V_{GS} f}_{gate\ (driver)}$$

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k | 1.45 W |
| Q_rr | 60 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.073 W |
| C_oss | 1 nF × 25.2² × 48 k | 0.030 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | 0.61 W |
| Gate (in driver) | 2 × 50 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.048 W |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |

**Totals:**

- **Per channel:** P_cond + P_sw ≈ **25.2 W** (hot)
- **Board, 4 channels:** ≈ **101 W**, plus shunts, traces and regulators, so roughly **105 W**.

**Design levers:**

- **Dead time.** Reducing it to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel. Verify with a shoot-through margin test.
