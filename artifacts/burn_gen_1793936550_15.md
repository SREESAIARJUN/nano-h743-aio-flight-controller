## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Corrections

Three constraints shape the rest of the design.

**1. ArduPilot does not commutate motors.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which handles commutation, 48 kHz PWM and eRPM telemetry. This design uses an STM32G071 per channel, a well-supported AM32 target. A "single H743 drives 12 gates" design would need custom firmware outside ArduPilot.

**2. 80 A per channel will not fit on a 20×20 mm board.** At 6S that is roughly 2 kW per motor and 320 A aggregate. The thermal model below supports a 30.5×30.5 mm stack:
- the FC board,
- an ESC board with 2 FETs in parallel per switch position (24 FETs total),
- a bonded aluminium heat spreader.

"Nano" here refers to the FC board only.
