# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Timing Design (Job #59)

## 0. Scope and Feasibility

Three facts constrain this design:

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller only.** Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC's PWM frequency, not anything the H743 generates.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mounting) is thermally marginal.** The math in §3 shows about 80 W of dissipation at full burst on all four channels. That is survivable only for seconds, and only with airflow and heavy copper. Treat 80 A as a per-channel, ≤1–2 s, single-motor transient rating. Plan on roughly 35–45 A continuous.
3. **Datasheet values are placeholders.** The MOSFET and driver parameters below are representative. Re-run the model with the actual datasheet values of the parts you select.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 VBAT (6S, 25.2V max) ──┬── Bulk Cap ── 4× Power Stage (3 half-bridges each)
                        ├── 5V/10V BEC (gate drive + ESC MCU 3V3)
                        └── FC 5V BEC → 3V3 LDO → STM32H743

 STM32H743 ── TIM1 CH1..4 (DShot600, bidirectional) ──► ESC MCU 1..4 (AM32)
 ESC MCU n ── 3× PWM (complementary, 48 kHz) ──► FD6288Q ──► 6× N-MOSFET ──► Motor n
 ESC MCU n ◄── BEMF dividers (3) + virtual neutral, shunt → op-amp → ADC
 ESC current sum ──► FC ADC (CURR), VBAT divider ──► FC ADC (VOLT)
```

|---|---|---|

| R_ds(25) | MOSFET on-resistance, 25 °C, V_gs = 10 V | 0.9 mΩ (40 V, DFN 3.3×3.3 class) |
| k_T | R_ds multiplier at T_j = 125 °C | 1.6 |
| t_r + t_f | Switching transition time (driver-limited) | 20 ns |
| Q_oss | Output charge | 40 nC |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | 50 nC |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 60 nC |
| V_f | Body-diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| t_dt | Dead time per transition | 100 ns |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| L_ph | Motor phase-to-phase inductance | 15 µH (typical 5" motor) |

### 2.2 Conduction losses

**Commutation and synchronous rectification.** In 6-step trapezoidal commutation, two phases conduct at any instant. With synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels:

- the high-side FET of one phase during the ON portion, or its complementary low-side FET during OFF;
- plus the low-side FET of the return phase.

**Hot on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds(hot)} = R_{ds(25)}\,k_T = 0.9\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.6 = 1.44\,\text{m}\Omega$$

**Ripple-corrected RMS current.** Worst-case ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{in}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\times10^{-6}\times 48\times10^{3}} = 8.75\,\text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 6.4 \approx 6406\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple contribution is about 0.1%. Treat the phase current as DC at 80 A.

**Per-channel conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(hot)} = 6406 \times 2.88\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.45\ W}$$

**Per-FET conduction loss.** Each FET conducts for roughly 1/3 of the electrical period, split between ON-state and synchronous-rectification state:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond}}{6}\cdot 2 \approx 3.1\,\text{W (average over a mechanical revolution)}$$

**PCB copper loss.** Add trace and plane resistance between the battery pads, FETs, and motor pads. Assume 0.3 mΩ per channel on 2 oz outer layers plus internal power planes:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = 1.92\,\text{W}$$

**Shunt loss.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt per channel adds:

$$P_{sh} = 80^2 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = 1.6\,\text{W}$$

If you use a single summed-current shunt instead, compute the loss on the summed current.

### 2.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz

The switching node hard-switches once per PWM edge on the high-side FET. The low-side FET switches softly, since its body diode is already conducting.

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V_in·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 k | **0.968 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V_in·f_sw = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.048 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V_in·f_sw = 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.060 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | 0.614 W |
| Gate drive (6 FETs, lost in driver/R_g) | 6·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw = 6 × 60 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.173 W |

$$P_{sw,total} \approx \mathbf{1.86\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Frequency sensitivity.** Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw:

- 24 kHz: about 0.93 W
- 96 kHz: about 3.7 W

At 80 A, 48 kHz is a reasonable compromise between switching loss and acoustic/ripple behaviour.

### 2.4 Per-channel and board totals at full burst

| Loss | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 18.45 W | 73.8 W |
| Switching | 1.86 W | 7.4 W |
| Copper | 1.92 W | 7.7 W |
| Shunt | 1.60 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **23.8 W** | **95.3 W** |

**Efficiency check.** Electrical input at 80 A × 4 channels is up to about 8 kW. Even so, roughly 95 W of heat on a ~6–10 cm² board is the real limit.
