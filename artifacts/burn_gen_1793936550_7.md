# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #66): Reference Design Analysis

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Scope

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. In every production "FC + 4-in-1" stack, the architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (×4)──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                                                 └─► 4× 3-phase gate driver ─► 24× MOSFET ─► motors
```

The H743 owns the DShot/DMA timing (§4). The ESC MCUs own the 48 kHz PWM and commutation (§2–3).

**Feasibility.** 80 A *per channel* on a nano (20×20 mm) form factor cannot be sustained. The thermal model below shows it is a **~2–3 second burst rating at best**. Treat that limit as a design constraint, not a margin.

**Assumptions.** These are typical values. Replace them with your BOM datasheet values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current | I | 80 A |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) @25 °C, V_GS=10 V | R₂₅ | 0.9 mΩ (40 V, 3.3×3.3 dual-cool class) |
| R_DS(on) tempco | α | 0.006 /K |
| Gate–drain charge | Q_gd | 10 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Body diode V_f | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Switching frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Current shunt | R_sh | 0.5 mΩ |
| Gate drive | V_drv | 12 V, ~1 A effective plateau current |

## 1. Electrical Operating Model (6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM)

In any commutation sector, two phase legs are active:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) synchronously rectifies for 1−D.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the LS is on continuously.
- **Leg C:** floating (BEMF zero-cross sensing).

Phase current is ≈ constant at I within the PWM period, since L_motor ≫ 1/f_sw ripple scale.

## 2. Conduction Losses

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 0.9 mΩ × (1 + 0.006·85) = 0.9 × 1.51 = **1.36 mΩ**

### 2.2 Per-channel MOSFET conduction

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[\underbrace{D}_{HS_A} + \underbrace{(1-D)}_{LS_A\,(sync)} + \underbrace{1}_{LS_B}] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is **independent of duty** with synchronous rectification. That makes it a robust worst-case bound.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.36\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.4\ W}$$

### 2.3 Shunt and copper

$$P_{sh} = I^2 R_{sh} = 6400 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

Copper loss is estimated by assuming ~0.3 mΩ effective path resistance (2 oz inner/outer + vias, two phase paths):

$$P_{Cu} \approx 2 \times 6400 \times 0.15\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 1.9\ \text{W}$$

**Recommendation:** use a single DC-link shunt per channel, or sense via R_DS(on) to remove the 3.2 W shunt term.

## 3. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

These losses occur on the PWM leg only.

### 3.1 V–I overlap

Switching times come from the gate charge: t_r ≈ t_f ≈ Q_gd/I_g ≈ 10 ns. Use **20 ns each** to allow for layout inductance and Miller plateau stretch.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse recovery (LS body diode, at HS turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

### 3.3 Dead-time diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

### 3.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FETs)

$$P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\times50\text{nC}\times12\times48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 3.6 Switching total

$$P_{sw} \approx 1.94 + 0.06 + 0.61 + 0.02 + 0.06 \approx \mathbf{2.7\ W/channel}$$

**Caveat:** at true full throttle (D → 100 %), AM32/BLHeli stop chopping, so P_sw → 0. The worst case for combined loss is high current at D ≈ 0.85–0.95, which occurs during punch-outs with voltage sag.

### 3.7 Loss budget

| Term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 17.4 W | 69.6 W |
| Shunt | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Copper | 1.9 W | 7.6 W |
| Switching | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| **Total** | **25.2 W** | **≈101 W** |

Efficiency at burst: P_out ≈ 4 × 25.2 × 80 × 0.9 ≈ 7.3 kW, giving η ≈ 98.6 %. The efficiency is fine. The power *density* is the problem.

### 3.8 Bulk capacitor ripple

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \le 0.5\,I = 40\ \text{A}_{rms}\ \text{per channel (at } D=0.5)$$

Phase-staggering is uncontrolled across independent ESCs, so worst case approaches ~80 A_rms total. Size the design as follows:

- **Distributed MLCCs:** ≥ 4 × 10 µF/50 V X7R per channel, placed at each half-bridge.
- **Low-ESR bulk capacitor:** ≥ 1000 µF/35 V polymer, plus an external capacitor on the battery leads.
