## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
With electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park:**
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.3 Decoupled PI with Pole-Zero Cancellation
The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are cancelled by feedforward:
$$v_d = PI_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = PI_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The plant then reduces to 1/(Ls + R). Choosing
$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c\quad(\text{so } K_i/K_p = R/L)$$
places the PI zero on the plant pole. The closed loop becomes the first-order system ω_c/(s + ω_c).

Practical limit: ω_c ≤ 2π·f_PWM/10, because the effective plant delay is about 1.5·T_s.
