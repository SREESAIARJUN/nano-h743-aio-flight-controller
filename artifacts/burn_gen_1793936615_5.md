# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion

I have no specification for "Job #22," so this design uses the assumptions below. Every assumption is a parameter in the code. Before you build, check the ICM-42688-P register values (§6.2) against TDK datasheet DS-000347 rev 1.x. Also check the motor sign and mounting conventions (§1, §4) on the real hardware.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× BLDC gimbal motors (e.g., GBM2804, 7 pole pairs, 12 slots). Low-side shunt current sensing. A voltage-mode fallback is provided. |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic absolute encoder per joint (AS5048A / MT6701 class), read over SPI at the current-loop
