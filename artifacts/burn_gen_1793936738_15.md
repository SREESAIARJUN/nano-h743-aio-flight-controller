# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #25): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, DMA Allocation, and Netlist

## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Corrections

Several points in the brief need clarifying, because they change the results.

| Item | Assumption / Correction |
|---|---|
| Role of the H743 | It runs **ArduPilot as the flight controller**. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running **AM32**), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs. |
| "80 A burst" | Interpreted as **80 A phase current per channel** for ≤5 s. The math below shows that a nano-class board **cannot sustain 80 A continuously**. The honest continuous rating is about 30 A per channel. |
| Board size | 36 × 36 mm PCB on a 30.5 mm mounting pattern, 8 layers with 2 oz copper on power layers. A 20 × 20 board cannot carry 80 A bursts; its copper thermal mass is too small. |
| Battery | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V maximum. Use 40 V-rated FETs. |
| MOSFET basis | 40 V, 5 × 6 mm DFN class: R_ds(on),25 = 1.0 mΩ, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, V_SD = 0.8 V, R_θJC = 0.8 K/W. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses trapezoidal BLDC drive with synchronous (complementary) rectification. At any instant, two phase legs carry the motor current I_ph:

- The PWM leg conducts through its high-side FET for duty D and its low-side FET for 1−D.
- The return leg conducts through its low-side FET for 100% of the commutation interval.

As a result, **exactly two R_ds(on) are always in series with I_ph**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Copper path

The copper path runs from the phase pad, through the FET drains, to the bus. It consists of 2 oz outer layers plus 2 × 2 oz inner layers, about 8 mm long and 6 mm wide per segment.

$$R_{Cu} \approx \frac{\rho L}{A_{eff}} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.008}{(4\times 70\mu m)\cdot 6mm}\approx 0.08\ \text{m}\Omega \ (\text{per segment}),\ \sim0.25\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ total path, hot}$$

### 1.4 Result at 80 A

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.5\text{m}) + (80)^2(0.25\text{m}) = 19.2 + 1.6 = \mathbf{20.8\ W/channel}$$

The per-FET split is worst case for the return-leg low side (100% conduction): **9.6 W** in a single device.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. Gate drive is about 1.5 A source and 2 A sink, with the Miller plateau near 4.5 V.

**Transition times**

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{10\text{nC}}{\approx0.8\text{A}} \approx 12\text{ ns},\quad t_f \approx 10\text{ ns}$$

**Loss terms**

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ V I (t_r+t_f) f_sw = ½·25.2·80·22n·48k | **1.06 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8·80·2·100n·48k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 50n·25.2·48k | 0.06 W |
| Coss charge | Q_oss · V · f_sw | 0.05 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw = 2·50n·10·48k | 0.05 W (in driver) |
| **P_sw total** | | **≈ 1.83 W/channel** |

**Sensitivity**

- P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it is about 0.9 W; at 96 kHz about 3.7 W.
- Dead time is the largest lever after edge rate. AM32 dead-time tuning to about 50 ns saves roughly 0.3 W.

## 3. Total Loss Budget

| | Per channel | 4 channels (all bursting) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Copper | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Switching | 1.83 W | 7.3 W |
| Driver, MCU, LDO | ~0.3 W | 1.2 W |
| **Total** | **~22.9 W** | **~91.7 W** |

**Efficiency at burst.** Assume D ≈ 0.85, so I_bat ≈ 68 A per channel and P_in ≈ 1714 W. Then η ≈ 98.7%. Efficiency is fine; **heat density is the constraint.**
