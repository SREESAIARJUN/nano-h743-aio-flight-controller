## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step commutation, exactly two phase legs carry the motor current I at any instant:
- The PWM leg alternates between its high-side FET (duty D) and its low-side FET (duty 1−D, synchronous rectification).
- The return leg's low-side FET conducts continuously during that 60° sector.

The motor current therefore always flows through exactly **two** channels in series:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

with the temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\left[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045/\text{K} \;\Rightarrow\; k_T \approx 1.4 \text{ at } 110^\circ\text{C}
$$

### 1.2 Per-FET average
Each leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical period. Averaged over a full commutation cycle, each of the 6 FETs dissipates:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)}{3}
$$

The split between high side and low side depends on D. At low throttle the low-side FETs carry the most loss.

### 1.3 Worked values at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| R_ds(on) hot | 2.0 × 1.4 | 2.8 mΩ |
| Silicon loss per motor | 80² × 2 × 2.8 mΩ | **35.8 W** |
| PCB copper (≈0.3 mΩ phase + bus path) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Per-FET average | 80² × 2.8 mΩ / 3 | 6.0 W |
| **4 motors, conduction total** | | **≈151 W** |

**Thermal runaway check.** Since dR/dT > 0, the effective thermal resistance is

$$
R_{\theta,eff}=\frac{R_\theta}{1-\alpha P R_\theta}
$$

This must stay positive and finite, so α·P·R_θ < 1 is required. With R_θ ≈ 15.6 K/W (Section 4) and P = 151 W, α·P·R_θ ≈ 10.6. The board is **unconditionally runaway-unstable at sustained 80 A** and survives only through thermal capacitance and time-limiting.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Its high-side FET hard-switches against the low-side FET's body diode.

### 2.1 Edge timing from gate charge
Approximating the Miller plateau as about 4.5 V:

$$
t_{r}\approx\frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{g,on}},\quad I_{g,on}\approx\frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_g+R_{drv}} \approx 1\text{ A}
$$

With Q_gd + Q_gs2 ≈ 20 nC, t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 20 ns.

### 2.2 Loss components (per motor, at 80 A)

| Component | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f = ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 40 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.048 W |
| C_oss energy | ½·C_oss·V²·f | 0.023 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD·I·2·t_dead·f = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs switching) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f | 0.038 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈2.66 W** |

### 2.3 Combined loss budget

| | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (Si + Cu) | 37.7 W | 150.8 W |
| Switching | 2.7 W | 10.6 W |
| Driver/LDO/MCU overhead | ~0.3 W | 1.2 W |
| **Total** | **40.7 W** | **≈163 W** |

The ESC stage is about 98 % efficient (2016 W output per motor), but 163 W in 4 cm² is the problem.
