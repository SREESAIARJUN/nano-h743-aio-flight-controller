## 0. Assumptions and Three Design Risks

I don't have context on "Job #5," so everything below rests on stated assumptions. Each one is parameterized so you can substitute real datasheet values.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It cannot also run four sensorless BLDC commutation loops, so each ESC channel uses its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The example uses an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over on-board bidirectional DShot600 traces.

| Risk | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| R1 – Form factor | "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm mount. Four channels × 6 FETs × 80 A burst does not fit thermally on 20×20. | This package sizes for a **30.5×30.5 mm mount on a ~36×36 mm PCB**. On 20×20, derate to ~40 A burst and ~20 A continuous. |
| R2 – "80 A" definition | This is taken as **per-channel phase current, burst (≤5 s)**, not continuous. | The DC bus current is D·I_phase, which is lower at partial throttle. |
| R3 – Continuous rating | The math below shows ~30 A/channel continuous at best on this board size. | The 80 A figure is a transient, heat-capacity-limited rating and must be marketed as such. |

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two phases are active at any instant:

- **PWM on-time:** current flows through HS(phase x) → motor → LS(phase y).
- **PWM off-time:** current freewheels through LS(phase x) → motor → LS(phase y), with the complementary LS on.

So the motor current always flows through **two FETs in series**, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

For trapezoidal drive with a flat-top current, I_rms ≈ I_ph. For FOC/sinusoidal drive, replace with I_pk²/2 per phase across three phases. The result is the same order of magnitude.

### 1.2 Temperature coupling and self-consistent junction temperature

The on-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th,j\text{-}ref}\, P_{FET}(T_j)
$$

Solving the fixed point for one FET carrying conduction loss P = k·R_ds(T_j), where k is the effective I²·duty for that FET:

$$
T_j^* = \frac{T_{ref} + R_{th}\, k\, R_{25}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - R_{th}\, k\, R_{25}\, \alpha}
$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion.** The loop is stable only while the denominator stays positive:

$$
R_{th}\, k\, R_{25}\, \alpha < 1
$$

This is the hard design constraint. Check it at maximum ambient.

| Tj | R_ds | P_cond per channel | Per conducting FET (instantaneous) |

| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W |

| **125 °C (design point)** | **1.50 mΩ** | **19.2 W** | **9.6 W** |

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}
