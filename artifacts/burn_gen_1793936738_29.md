# Job #49: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst)

## Architecture Note

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 commands to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Those ESC MCUs drive the 3-phase gate drivers at the 48 kHz switching frequency.

The loss model below applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA and timer model applies to the H743.

> **Size reality check:** A true 20×20 "nano" board cannot carry 80 A per channel beyond a fraction of a second. The copper cross-section and surface area are too small. The figures below assume a 30.5 mm board. For 20×20, scale thermal capacitance by about 0.43 and surface area by about 0.43, and expect correspondingly shorter burst times.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

The ESC uses 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous complementary PWM. At any instant, exactly two MOSFETs plus the board copper sit in series with the motor current:

$$R_{path}(T_j) = 2\,R_{ds(on),25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)] + R_{cu}, \quad \alpha \approx 0.0047/\text{K}$$

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{path} = 80^2 \times (2 \times 1.68 + 0.5)\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.86\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{24.7\ W\ per\ channel}$$

This splits into 21.5 W in the MOSFETs and 3.2 W in the copper.

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET conducts for about one third of the electrical cycle. The electrical frequency is a few kHz (for example, 14 poles at 30 krpm gives 3.5 kHz). That is far faster than the thermal time constants, so averaged power is valid:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{21.5}{6} \approx 3.6\ \text{W}$$

---

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge hard-switches. The terms are:

$$P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 1.45\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{dt} = 2 V_f I\, t_{dt} f_{sw} = 2 \times 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{gate} = 2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 0.06\ \text{W} \quad \text{(dissipated in the driver)}$$

$$P_{sw,total} \approx \mathbf{2.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

The per-channel total is therefore:

$$P_{ch} = 24.7 + 2.2 \approx \mathbf{27\ W}$$

For all four channels at 80 A, plus about 1.5 W for the H743
