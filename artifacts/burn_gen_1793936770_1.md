### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 cannot directly drive the 4-in-1 power stage under ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no native gate-drive or commutation firmware. The correct partition is:

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x bi-dir DShot600--> [4x ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071), AM32 or BLHeli_32]
                                                     └─> 3-phase gate driver ─> 6x N-MOSFET per motor
```

The 48 kHz switching frequency belongs to the ESC MCUs. The DShot/DMA allocation belongs to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs:
- During PWM-on, the path is HS(phase A) and LS(phase B).
- During PWM-off, the path is LS(phase A) (synchronous freewheel) and LS(phase B).

So the per-motor conduction loss is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Self-Consistent Junction Temperature

Loss raises temperature, and temperature raises R_DS. This feedback loop must be solved iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + Z_{th}\,P_{FET}(T_j)\;\Rightarrow\; P_{FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{25}(1+\alpha(T_{ref}-25))\,k}{1 - I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,Z_{th}\,k}$$

Here k is the conduction share of that FET.

**Thermal runaway condition:** the solution exists only if

$$I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\, Z_{th}\,k < 1$$

At 80 A: 6400 × 1.2e-3 × 0.0055 = 0.0422 /(K/W). For a FET carrying the full share (k = 1), Z_th must therefore be below 23.7 K/W. You are safe here, but the margin narrows quickly on poor copper.
