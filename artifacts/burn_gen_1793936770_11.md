## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges. The architecture splits into two tiers:

| Tier | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400–480 MHz | ArduPilot, sensor fusion, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive (×4) | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q-class), bootstrap |
