# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #22): Power-Stage, Thermal & Bidirectional DShot Architecture

## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Constraints

**Partitioning**
- The H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600.
- Four ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz six-step PWM. Suitable parts are AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. A single-MCU 4-in-1 design such as a G4 running AM32 multi is an alternative.

**Parameter basis**
- No MOSFET part was specified. All numbers use a representative 40 V N-channel MOSFET in a 5×6 mm PDFN package. Parameters are listed in §1.1.
- Substitute your datasheet values. The model is fully parametric.

**Bus voltage**
- 6S LiPo at full charge: V_bus = 25.2 V, which is the worst case for switching loss.

**Form factor**
- 30.5 or 25.5 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz copper.
- "80 A" is treated as a **burst** rating. §3 shows it cannot be sustained continuously on a nano-class board.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 MOSFET parameters (representative)

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| R_ds(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ (max, V_gs = 10 V) | On-resistance at 25 °C |
| α | 0.005 /K | Linearized R_ds temperature coefficient |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd = Q_sw | 15 nC | Miller switching charge |
| Q_g | 60 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_rr | 80 nC | Body-diode reverse recovery charge (at high di/dt) |
| Q_oss | 40 nC | Output charge |
| V_plateau | 3.5 V | Miller plateau voltage |
| R_g,int | 1.0 Ω | Internal gate resistance |
| V_f (body diode) | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward voltage |
| R_θJC | 1.2 K/W | Junction-to-case thermal resistance |

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

The commonly used power-law form, $R_{ds,25}(T/298)^{2.3}$, gives results within about 5 % over 25–150 °C.

### 1.2 Six-step commutation current paths

Assumptions: trapezoidal (120°) drive, high-side PWM, synchronous rectification (complementary low-side).

| Interval | Conducting devices |
|---|---|
| PWM ON (duty D) | HS_A + LS_B |
| PWM OFF (1 − D) | LS_A (sync rect.) + LS_B |

At every instant, exactly **two FETs** carry the phase current I_ph, independent of D. Per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j),\qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

**Ripple current:**

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2 L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

The factor of 2 accounts for two phase windings in series. For a typical 5" motor, L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-to-line 30 µH). At full throttle (D → 1, V_bemf ≈ 0.9 V_bus):

$$\Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{2.5 \cdot 1}{30\mu \cdot 48k} \approx 1.7\,A$$

This is negligible against 80 A: it contributes +0.03 % to I²R.
