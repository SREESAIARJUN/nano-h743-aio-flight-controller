## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Reality Check

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In every production ArduPilot AIO design, the architecture splits into two layers:

- **H743 (flight controller):** runs ArduPilot and emits **bidirectional DShot600** on 4 timer channels.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32): each runs 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM and drives a 3-phase gate driver plus 6 MOSFETs.

The model below assumes that split. Component values are representative candidates. **Verify every value against the actual datasheets you select.**
