| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 / VIT6, 400 MHz SYSCLK (ArduPilot default), timer kernel clock 200 MHz | |
| Board | 4-in-1 on 20×20 mm mounting (≈ 28×28 mm outline), 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power planes | |

**Flag A: the 80 A figure.** 80 A *per channel* is outside the steady-state capability of any 20×20 mm form factor. The analysis below shows roughly 86 W dissipated at 4×80 A. The design is therefore only defensible as an **80 A, ≤ 1–2 s burst rating** with a 30–45 A continuous rating. If "80 A" was meant as the *total* board rating (4×20 A), every loss term drops by about 16×, and the thermal problem goes away. Confirm which interpretation applies with the program owner.

**Flag B: architecture.** The H743 is the flight controller. The 48 kHz bridges are driven by four independent ESC MCUs. Do not attempt to drive gates from the H743. It has neither the timer count nor the latency budget alongside ArduPilot.

**Correction to the table:** 4 channels × 3 phases × 2 FETs = **24 MOSFETs**.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification
At any commutation step, one phase is PWM'd (HS/LS alternating) and one phase has its low-side held ON. Current therefore always flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels in series**:

- During PWM-on (duty D): HS_x → motor → LS_y
- During PWM-off (1−D): LS_x (sync rectifying) → motor → LS_y

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,x} + R_{LS,y} \right] \approx 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

This simplification holds for identical FETs, independent of D. That independence is a useful property of sync rectification.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left(1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right),\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /\mathrm{K}\ (\text{40 V trench, typical})
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) (from 1.0 mΩ max) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ |

Use **1.45 mΩ** (worst case at T_j = 125 °C). Note that this term is electro-thermally coupled: loss raises temperature, which raises resistance, which raises loss. §3 iterates it.

### 1.3 Copper / interconnect resistance
Phase pad → motor wire pad, plus a share of the bus plane and solder joints. The estimate for 2 oz inner/outer paralleled, ~8 mm path, ~4 mm wide, with vias:

$$
R_{Cu} \approx \rho_{Cu}(T)\frac{L}{W\,t_{eff}} \approx 0.4\ \mathrm{m\Omega\ per\ channel\ (incl.\ joints)}
$$

### 1.4 Shunt (if per-channel sensing is used)
R_sh = 0.25 mΩ. With a single shared bus shunt, allocate ¼ of the bus loss to each channel. The bus carries the average current, not the phase current; see §1.5.

### 1.5 Results, per channel at I_ph = 80 A

$$
P_{FET,cond} = 2(80)^2(1.45\times10^{-3}) = 18.6\ \mathrm{W}
$$
$$
P_{Cu} = (80)^2(0.4\times10^{-3}) = 2.6\ \mathrm{W}
$$

Bus shunt at full-throttle burst (D ≈ 0.9, I_bus ≈ D·I_ph ≈ 72 A per channel, so 288 A total):
$$
P_{sh,total} = (288)^2(0.25\times10^{-3}) = 20.7\ \mathrm{W}
$$

The bus-shunt figure is unacceptable. **Recommendation:** use a 0.1 mΩ shunt (8.3 W for ≤ 2 s, still marginal) or a Hall-effect/phase-reconstruction scheme. Per-ESC current sensing via low-side phase shunts is preferable. Alternatively, accept a **0.1 mΩ, 4-terminal shunt rated ≥ 5 W with 2 s pulse derating** (check the shunt's pulse-energy curve: ≈ 17 J).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Each PWM period has one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off of the HS FET, with LS sync-rectifier dead-time intervals.

### 2.1 Hard-switching overlap loss (V-I crossover)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}
$$

With gate-driver-limited edges for FD6288Q (≈ 1.0 A/0.6 A drive) into Q_gd ≈ 12 nC:

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{G,on}} \approx \frac{12\,\mathrm{nC}}{0.6\,\mathrm{A}}\ (\text{eff., incl. }R_G) \approx 20\ \mathrm{ns},\qquad t_f \approx 15\ \mathrm{ns}
$$

$$
P_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(35\times10^{-9})(48\times10^3) = 1.69\ \mathrm{W}
$$
