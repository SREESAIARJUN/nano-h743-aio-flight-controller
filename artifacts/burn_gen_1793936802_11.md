## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The H743 does not drive the power stage. It is the flight controller. Each of the four bridge channels has its own ESC MCU running AM32 (BLHeli_32-class firmware) at 48 kHz PWM. The two are linked by bidirectional DShot600. All math below follows this split.

```
 STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir x4──► 4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32, 48 kHz)
                                                 └─► FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver
                                                       └─► 6× N-MOSFET bridge per motor
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

With 6-step trapezoidal drive and complementary (synchronous) PWM, each phase leg spends 120° in the "high" state and 120° in the "low" state:

- **High state:** the high-side FET is PWMed at duty D. The low-side FET of the same leg freewheels synchronously for (1−D).
- **Low state:** the low-side FET is held fully on.

This gives the per-FET conduction duty factors:

$$
\delta_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}, \qquad \delta_{LS} = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3} = \frac{2-D}{3}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.4675 = **1.76 mΩ**.

$$
