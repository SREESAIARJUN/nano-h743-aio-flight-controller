## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

### 0.1 The H743 does not commutate the motors

On every production 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot frames. Commutation (6-step, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF zero-cross, eRPM telemetry) runs on separate ESC MCUs, either one per motor or one per pair. The H743 has no hardware path for 4× closed-loop sensorless commutation alongside a 1–8 kHz attitude loop.

**Two consequences:**
- The **48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-firmware setting** (for example, AM32 `PWM frequency`), not an ArduPilot parameter.
- **ArduPilot only owns the DShot600 timer/DMA side.**

This document uses:
- **FC side:** STM32H743VIT6 running ArduPilot.
- **ESC side:** 4× AT32F421K8 (or STM32G071) running AM32, each driving one FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-MOSFETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low:

- **PWM on-time (D):** HS_X → winding → LS_Y. Two FETs in series.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** LS_X (synchronous) → winding → LS_Y. Two FETs in series.

The current always flows through exactly two channels in series, independent of duty cycle. Duty cycle only changes which FET in leg X carries the current.

### 1.2 Equations

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx R_{DS,25}\cdot k_T,\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ /K$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2\,\big[\,2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu}\,\big]$$

Per-device duty-weighted losses within a sector:

$$P_{HS,X} = I^2 R_{DS} D,\quad P_{LS,X} = I^2 R_{DS}(1-D),\quad P_{LS,Y} = I^2 R_{DS}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

- R_DS(110 °C) = 1.0 × 1.55 = 1.55 mΩ
- FET conduction: 80² × 2 × 1.55 mΩ = **19.8 W**
- Copper: 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**
- **P_cond,ch ≈ 23.0 W per motor**

### 1.4 Distribution across the 6 FETs

**At high eRPM:** the electrical period is much shorter than the junction thermal time constant (a few ms). The loss therefore averages over the 6 FETs:

- P_FET,avg ≈ 19.8 / 6 = **3.3 W**

**At low eRPM or stall (worst case):** the LS_Y device carries the full current for an entire sector.

- P_LS,Y = 80² × 1.55 mΩ = **9.9 W instantaneous**
- ΔT_JC ≈ 9.9 W × Z_θJC(sector duration).
  - Sector ≈ 1 ms: ≈ 1.5
