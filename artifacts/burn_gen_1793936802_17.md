**Scope:** Mathematical models for 80 A burst conduction loss, 48 kHz switching loss, and transient thermal behavior; ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation; reference netlists.

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot cannot drive four 3-phase bridges directly. The architecture is therefore:

```
 [STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x bidir DShot600--> [4x ESC MCU, AM32] --> [4x FD6288-class driver] --> [4x 3-phase bridge, 24 FETs]
```
