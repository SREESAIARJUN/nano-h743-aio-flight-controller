# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Design Model, Job #58

## 0. Architecture and Feasibility

**Architecture.** The H743 does not switch the bridges directly. ArduPilot cannot run motor commutation, and no fielded design generates 12 complementary 48 kHz gate signals from the FC MCU. The defensible architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600──▶ 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32, PWM 48 kHz)
                                          └─▶ 3-phase gate driver ─▶ 6× N-MOSFET per motor
```

The switching-loss model below applies to the ESC MCUs' 48 kHz PWM. The DShot/DMA model applies to the H743.

**Feasibility.** On a "nano" (20×20 mm) footprint, 80 A per channel is physically viable only as a ~1–2 s burst. Section 2 shows ~22 W of loss per channel (~90 W board-wide) against roughly 2 J/K of thermal mass. I've built the model around a hard burst limit rather than a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

Assume 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (AM32 complementary PWM). At any instant, phase current flows through exactly two conducting MOSFETs:

- the active-leg HS (duty D) or its LS (duty 1−D), and
- the return-leg LS (always on).

The current path resistance is therefore 2·R_ds regardless of D.

**Junction-dependent resistance:**

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.0 · (1 + 0.0067·75) = **1.50 mΩ**

**Per-channel conduction loss:**

P_cond = I_ph² · 2 · R_ds(T_j) · (1 + r²/12)

The ripple factor r = ΔI/I_ph is negligible at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance.

P_cond(80 A, 100 °C) = 6400 · 2 · 1.5e-3 = **19.2 W per channel**

**Per-FET loss.** Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs carries its share for 120°, so the average is 19.2/6 ≈ **3.2 W per FET**. At low eRPM (stall, punch-out), instantaneous per-FET dissipation is I²R = **9.6 W** for 1/3 of the period. FET thermal time constants (~1–10 ms) can track this, so evaluate peak T_j at minimum eRPM.

**Copper and shunt (per channel).** Assume a 2 oz, ~10 mm-wide path of about 15 mm per segment, giving ≈ 0.4 mΩ round-trip:

P_Cu ≈ 6400 · 0.4e-3 = **2.6 W**

A 0.25 mΩ DC-bus shunt carries I_bus ≈ D·I_ph. At D ≈ 0.9:

P_shunt ≈ (72)² · 0.25e-3 = **1.3 W**

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM'ing leg (one HS/LS pair) switches each cycle. The hard-switched device is the HS; the LS sees body-diode conduction plus reverse recovery.

**Gate current** (check against driver limits):

- Turn-on: I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl)/(R_g,int + R_g,ext) = 7.5/11 = **0.68 A** (below 1.5 A source, OK)
- Turn-off: I_g,off = V_pl/R_g = 4.5/11 = **0.41 A**

**Transition times:**

- t_r = (Q_gs2 + Q_gd)/I_g,on = 12 nC/0.68 A = **17.6 ns**
- t_f = 12 nC/0.41 A = **29.3 ns**
