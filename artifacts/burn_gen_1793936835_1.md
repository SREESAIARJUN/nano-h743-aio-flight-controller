## 1. Control Architecture

```
          ┌───────────── 1 kHz ─────────────┐ ┌──── 4 kHz ────┐ ┌──── 20 kHz ────┐
q_ref ──► Attitude P ──► ω_ref(cam) ─► J⁻¹(q) ─► Rate PI ─► τ* ─► ÷Kt ─► i_q* ─► Current PI ─► SVPWM
             ▲                                      ▲   (+ cogging FF + rate FF)       ▲
       Mahony (dual IMU)                     notched/LPF gyro (dual-fused)       encoder θe
```

Three design decisions shape the structure:

- **Inertial rate loop.** The rate loop is closed on the IMU gyro, not on encoder velocity. This is what rejects base motion.
- **Encoder for commutation only.** The encoder supplies the electrical angle θe and the joint angles q. The joint angles are used for the Jacobian and for the cogging compensation phase.
- **Joint-space mapping.** Camera-frame rate errors are mapped into joint space through the gimbal Jacobian.

## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop, Ts = 50 µs)

### 2.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle:

$$\theta_e = p(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.2 Machine model and decoupled PI control

The PMSM model in the rotor (dq) frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

The electromagnetic torque is:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t i_q$$

The PI current law adds decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = K_{p,i}e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d\,dt - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = K_{p,i}e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q\,dt + \hat\omega_e \hat L i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m$$

**Gain selection.** Placing the PI zero on the electrical pole (pole-zero cancellation) gives:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The closed loop is then the first-order system:

$$\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s + \omega_{ci}}$$
