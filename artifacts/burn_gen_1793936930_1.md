# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC Stack: Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot Allocation

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class, AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware). The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. Everything below follows that split.

**Assumed component class.** These are representative values, not a specific BOM. Replace them with datasheet values before sign-off.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_ph | 80 A |
| MOSFET: 40 V, 5×6 PDFN, single device per switch | — | — |
| R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 0.70 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient (≈110 °C) | k_T | 1.6 |
| Effective rise + fall time (with gate driver) | t_r + t_f | 35 ns |
| Reverse-recovery charge of body diode | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Effective output capacitance | C_oss,er | 1.5 nF |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time per edge | t_dt | 100 ns |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| PCB copper path resistance per channel (two legs) | R_cu | 0.5 mΩ |

**Warning:** "80A" on a nano-form-factor (20×20 or 30×30 mm) 4-in-1 is a **burst rating only**. The math below shows sustained operation is physically impossible without forced cooling well beyond prop wash.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation model.** In six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification, two half-bridge legs carry I_ph at any instant. Within each leg, the high-side (HS) conducts for D and the low-side (LS) for (1−D). The leg's total conduction loss is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{leg} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right] \approx k_T R_{25}$$

**Numbers:**
- R_DS(hot) = 0.70 × 1.6 = **1.12 mΩ**
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 80² × 1.12×10⁻³ = **14.3 W**
- Copper: P_cu = 80² × 0.5×10⁻³ = **3.2 W**
- **Per-channel conduction plus copper ≈ 17.5 W**

**Phase current ripple.** The RMS correction is small at 80 A. Inductor ripple is ΔI = V_bus·D(1−D)/(L·f_sw). With L ≈ 15 µH (typical 5-inch-class motor, phase-to-phase):

- ΔI ≈ 25.2 × 0.25 / (15×10⁻⁶ × 48×10³) ≈ 8.75 A peak-to-peak
- I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12 → adds about 0.1 %, which is negligible.

**Thermal runaway check.** The loop gain is ∂P/∂T × R_th. It must stay below 1:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j} = I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha \approx 6400 \times 0.7\text{m}\Omega \times 0.006 = 0.027\ \text{W/K per device}$$

Multiplied by R_th,j-a,effective of about 25 K/W, the loop gain is 0.67. It is stable but significantly amplifying, so rated R_DS must use the hot value.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per PWM period, one device per active leg is hard-switched (the HS in this model). The complementary LS sees body-diode conduction during dead time and reverse recovery.

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·35 ns·48k = **1.69 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss,er·V²·f_sw | ½·1.5 nF·635·48k = **0.023 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48k = **0.048 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (in driver, not FET) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·60 nC·10·48k = 0.058 W |

**Switching total per channel ≈ 2.4 W** (FET dissipation), plus 0.06 W in the gate driver.

**Design levers:**
- At 24 kHz, the f_sw-proportional terms roughly halve (−1.2 W/ch). The cost is audible noise and higher ripple.
- Dead-time loss is about 25 % of the switching budget. Trimming 100 → 60 ns saves about 0.25 W/ch if shoot-through margin allows. Verify with a double-pulse test.

## 3. Total Burst Loss Budget

| Item | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction (hot) | 14.3 W | 57.2 W |
| MOSFET switching | 2.4 W | 9.6 W |
| PCB copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Current shunt (see §6) | — | 10.2 W |
| Gate drivers, LDOs, MCUs | ~0.3 W | ~1.2 W |
| **Total** | **~20 W** | **~91 W** |

Electrical efficiency at full burst: P_out ≈ 4 × 80 × 25.2 × D_eff. At throttle-limited D ≈ 0.9, P_out ≈ 7.3 kW, so efficiency is about 98.8 %. The efficiency is fine; the problem is power density.
