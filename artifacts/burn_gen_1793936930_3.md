# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package

**Job #12, Rev A (engineering draft)**

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Constraints

Several architectural constraints shape everything below.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It is not the ESC controller.** ArduPilot does not perform motor commutation. The 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32). This allows 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot telemetry, and keeps the H743 free for ArduPilot. The H743 talks to the ESCs over 4× BDShot600.
2. **80 A burst on a "nano" form factor is thermally marginal.** The math in §3 shows it. This design uses a **30.5 × 30.5 mm** stack (the smallest practical size for 12× 5×6 mm FETs is effectively a 30×30 ESC). A 20×20 nano board cannot dissipate 80 A loads; at that size the realistic rating is about 35–45 A burst.
3. **"80 A burst" means per channel for ≤ 5 s, one channel at a time at peak.** All four channels at 80 A simultaneously is evaluated separately (§3.4) and is limited to about 2 s.
4. **Device parameters below are representative values for a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, DFN5×6 N-MOSFET.** Substitute the actual datasheet values for the selected part before release.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |

| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (worst case for switching loss) |
