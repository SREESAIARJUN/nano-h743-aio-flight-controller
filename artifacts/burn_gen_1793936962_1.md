## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of the same rotation matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupled PI

The PMSM voltage equations in the rotor frame are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are cancelled with a feedforward voltage:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

After cancellation, each axis behaves as $G(s) = 1/(Ls + R)$.

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choosing a target closed-loop current bandwidth $\omega_c$ gives:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

Recommended values:
- $\omega_c \approx 2\pi \cdot 1.5\ \text{kHz}$, which must be ≤ $f_{\text{pwm}}/10$.
- Use $\omega_c$ below $2\pi f_s/10$ to cover the one-sample PWM delay. For tighter loops, add delay compensation: $\theta_e^{\text{pwm}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$.

**Discrete PI with clamping anti-windup:**

$$e_k = i^{*}_k - i_k$$
$$I_k = I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k \quad (\text{frozen if saturated and } e_k \cdot u_k > 0)$$
$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v^{ff}$$

**Voltage circle limit.** The maximum linear SVPWM voltage is $V_{\max} = V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. Priority goes to $v_d$:

$$v_q^{\lim} = \sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}$$
