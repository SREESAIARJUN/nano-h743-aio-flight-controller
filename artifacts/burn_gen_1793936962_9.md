# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #64 specification, so every hardware number below is a **named, overridable parameter**. Substitute your real motor, IMU-mount and MCU data before tuning.

The design assumes:

- **MCU:** STM32G4-class Cortex-M4F with FPU.
- **Motor:** a GBM2804-class gimbal BLDC.
- **Encoder:** 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A or MT6701).
- **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, both on SPI and sharing CLKIN for synchronous sampling.
