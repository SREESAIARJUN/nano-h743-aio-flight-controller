**Architecture note.** In a standard AIO design the STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. Four dedicated ESC MCUs do that. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to each ESC MCU. The model below follows that split.

**Feasibility warnings. Raise these at design review.**
1. **80 A on a "nano" (20×20 mm) AIO is not thermally credible.** It is aggressive even on 30.5×30.5 mm. Section 3 shows that 80 A is a transient, Z_th-limited burst rating of roughly 1–2 s. The continuous rating will be about 25–35 A per channel, depending on airflow.
2. **A single common current shunt for 4×80 A is not viable.** At 320 A, a 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates 25.6 W. Use per-channel low-side sensing or a Hall-effect sensor such as an ACS7xx or MCA1101-class part.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 1.1 Current paths

In each 60° electrical sector, one phase high-side (HS) PWMs against its complementary low-side (LS). One other phase's LS is held fully on.

- **ON interval** (fraction D): current flows HS_A → motor → LS_B.
- **Freewheel interval** (fraction 1−D, synchronous): current flows LS_A → motor → LS_B.
