## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two points need to be settled before the math.

**1. The H743 should not commutate the motors.** On every production H743 "AIO" design, the H743 runs ArduPilot only. Each motor gets a dedicated ESC MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Reasons:

- Sensorless BEMF commutation at 48 kHz PWM on 4 motors would consume most of the H743's timer and ADC resources.
- It would also break ArduPilot's scheduling guarantees.

This document therefore uses: **H743 → bi-directional DShot600 → 4× ESC MCU → 3-phase gate driver → 6 MOSFETs per motor.**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification (AM32 complementary PWM)

At any instant two phases carry the motor current I:

- **PWM'd phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Grounded phase:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

There are always exactly two FET channels in series with the winding, so:
