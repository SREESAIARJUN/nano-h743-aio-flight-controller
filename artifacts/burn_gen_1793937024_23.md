# STM32H743 FC + 80A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot/DMA Allocation, Netlists

## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot be the commutation controller for four motors.** It has only two advanced-control timers (TIM1 and TIM8) with complementary outputs and dead-time insertion. Driving 4 × 3 half-bridges with dead time needs 12 complementary pairs. ArduPilot also does not run ESC commutation firmware.

The architecture is therefore a stack:

| Board | Function | Key ICs |
|---|---|---|
| FC (top) | ArduPilot, IMU, DShot600 bidirectional out | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm, the only H743 package that fits nano layouts) |
| ESC (bottom) | 4 independent channels | 4× AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (AM32 firmware), 4× FD6288Q 3-phase gate drivers, 24× N-MOSFETs |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The topology is trapezoidal 6-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM, as used by AM32. During each 60° sector:

- One phase leg is PWM-switched, with the high side (HS) on for duty D and the low side (LS) synchronously rectifying for 1−D.
- A second leg's LS is on continuously.

The current therefore always flows through exactly **two conducting channels in series**, ignoring dead time (handled in §2).

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot R_{ds,hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds,hot} = 2 I^2 R_{ds,hot}
$$

$$
R_{ds,hot} = k_T R_{ds,25} = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

**Validity.** This is independent of D only if the 80 A is the DC-link-referred phase current during conduction, which is valid for sustained high-throttle bursts.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, either as switch or synchronous rectifier. The average loss per FET is:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{19.2}{6} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}
$$

The LS devices carry slightly more because the sync-rectification share is added at low D.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often forgotten, significant at 80 A)

| Element | R (est.) | P @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace, 2 oz, ~8 mm, 3 mm wide, both layers | ≈0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Low-side current shunt (0.25 mΩ; often omitted on 4-in-1s) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| DC bus plane share | ≈0.1 mΩ | 0.64 W |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈3.8 W per channel** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (HS device)

Gate current during the Miller plateau:

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10-3}{5.7} = 1.23\ \text{A}, \quad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{3}{5.7} = 0.53\ \text{A}
$$
