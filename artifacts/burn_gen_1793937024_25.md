## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C with α = 0.0047: R_DS ≈ 1.5 × 1.40 = **2.1 mΩ**.

### 1.2 RMS current per switch

Each leg spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the PWM'd high side, 1/3 as the static low side, and 1/3 floating. With synchronous rectification, the low side of the PWM'd leg conducts during (1−D):

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2\frac{D}{3},\qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2\left[\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}\right] = I_{ph}^2\frac{2-D}{3}$$

$$P_{cond,leg} = R_{DS}(T_j)\, I_{ph}^2\,\frac{D + (2-D)}{3} = \frac{2}{3}\,R_{DS}\,I_{ph}^2$$
