## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Paths in 6-Step with Synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D. The low-side (LS) synchronously rectifies for fraction (1−D).
- **Phase B (return leg):** the LS conducts 100% of the sector.
- **Phase C:** floating (used for BEMF sensing).

Conduction loss per motor:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

It is independent of duty cycle. This is the key property of synchronous 6-step.

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.45 mΩ.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.56\ W\ /\ motor}$$

### 1.2 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period in the switching leg:

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_f\,I_{ph}\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(200\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper/Interconnect Loss (Often Ignored, Significant at 80A)

Assume about 0.25 mΩ per phase path (pad + 2 oz pour + motor wire solder joint). Two phases conduct at any time:

$$P_{Cu} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{Cu} = 2(6400)(0.25\text{ m}\Omega) = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

This heat goes directly into the PCB, not into the FET junctions.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The LS FET turns on after dead time with its body diode already conducting (ZVS-like), so its switching loss is negligible. The LS reverse recovery is charged to the HS turn-on.

### 2.1 Transition Times from Gate Charge

Assume a Miller plateau V_pl ≈ 3.5 V.

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{6.5}{11} \approx 0.59\text{ A}, \qquad t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} \approx 25\text{ ns}$$

With a turn-off diode on the gate resistor (R_off ≈ 5.5 Ω):

$$t_{off} \approx \frac{15\text{ nC}}{3.5/5.5} \approx 24\text{ ns}$$

Use 25 ns for both.

### 2.2 V·I Overlap Loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(50\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{2.42\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse Recovery, Coss, and Gate Drive

| Loss term | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 80n × 25.2 × 48k | 0.097 W |
| Coss energy | P_Coss = ½ · C_oss,er · V_bus² · f_sw | 0.023 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs active, burned in R_g and driver) | P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw | 0.058 W |
