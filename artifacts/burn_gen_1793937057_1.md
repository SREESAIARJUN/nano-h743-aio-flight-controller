# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Thermal Model, Switching Losses, and ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Design Review Flags

The calculations below depend on the following assumptions. Replace the bracketed parameters with values from your chosen MOSFET datasheet and rerun the math.

**The H743 does not commutate motors.** ArduPilot sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32). Those MCUs run the 48 kHz PWM and six-step commutation. The H743's job is DShot generation and eRPM telemetry capture.

**Package choice.** On a 20×20 mm board, use the STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm). The LQFP100 (14×14 mm) does not fit alongside 24 MOSFETs.

**Definition of "80 A burst".** I treat it as 80 A phase/DC-link current per motor, applied to all four motors at once. That is the worst case. As shown below, it is achievable only for about 1 second on a nano board. That result is the main design finding.

**Bus voltage.** 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V maximum. This requires 40 V-rated MOSFETs.

**Assumed MOSFET:** 40 V N-channel, PDFN 3.3×3.3 mm.

| Parameter | Value |
