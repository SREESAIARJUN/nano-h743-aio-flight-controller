## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, two FETs are always in series with the motor current at any instant:

- **During the PWM on-time:** the PWM leg's high-side FET conducts, in series with the opposite leg's low-side FET.
- **During the PWM off-time:** the PWM leg's low-side FET conducts (synchronous rectification), in series with the same opposite-leg low-side FET.

Because of this, conduction loss per motor is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for about 1/3 of an electrical cycle, so averaged over rotation:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}{3}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \text{[ASSUMED, read from the datasheet's normalized R}_{DS(on)}\text{ vs T}_j\text{ curve]}$$

Assumed FET: 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3, R₂₅ = 1.5 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V **[ASSUMED]**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

I² = 6400 A².

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P per motor (2 FETs) | P per FET (average) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 3.20 W |
| 100 °C | 2.18 mΩ | 27.9 W | 4.65 W |
| 132 °C | 2.46 mΩ | 31.5 W | 5.25 W |

### 1.4 PCB copper loss (often forgotten)

- 2 oz copper has a sheet resistance of about 0.25 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, and roughly +0.39 %/K.
- A phase path of about 3 squares across 2 parallel layers gives roughly 0.4 mΩ.

$$P_{Cu} \approx 6400 \times 0.4\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 2.6\ \text{W per motor}$$

This is significant. Stack phase and bus copper on at least 3 layers and use via arrays: at about 1.5 A burst per 0.3 mm via, 80 A needs **≥ 50 vias per transition**.
