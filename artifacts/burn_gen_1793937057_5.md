# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller with Integrated 80 A 4-in-1 ESC

**Job #15: System Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, DShot Timer/DMA Plan, and Netlists**

> **Note on architecture:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) that drives a 3-phase bridge. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. This document treats both layers.
>
> **Main finding:** The thermal analysis shows that 80 A per channel on a nano (≈25.5 × 25.5 mm) board is a burst rating of about 2–3 s. It is not a continuous rating. Section 3 quantifies this.
