## 0. Architecture Decisions and Two Findings

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM, commutation and eRPM telemetry run on four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32). The H743 does not drive the gates directly. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware, and the timing domains should stay isolated.

**Finding 1: "80 A" can only be a burst rating at nano scale.** Section 3 shows the board can carry roughly 80 A per motor for about 3–5 s. It can sustain only about 25–30 A per motor continuously. Write the spec as "80 A burst (≤3 s, T_board,start ≤ 50 °C)."

**Finding 2: The DC bus is the real system limit.** Four motors at 80 A on 6S draw 320 A, or about 7.5 kW. The battery connector, pads, and bus capacitor ripple will fail before the FETs if all four burst together (see §3.5).
