## 0. Executive Summary

**1. The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor's 48 kHz PWM and commutation is run by a dedicated ESC MCU. This design uses four STM32G071 MCUs running AM32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those ESC MCUs. Putting 48 kHz commutation for 12 half-bridges on the flight-control MCU would compromise both functions.

**2. 80 A is a burst rating only, and a short one.** With all four channels at 80 A, the board dissipates about 105 W. A 30×30 mm board in prop wash can reject only about 10–12 W continuously.
- Calculated burst duration: **about 1.1 s** with all four motors at 80 A from an 85 °C junction-limited start condition.
- Calculated continuous rating: **about 28–30 A per channel**.
- The marketing number must be stated as "80 A, 1 s burst."
