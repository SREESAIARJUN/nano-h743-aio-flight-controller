| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm board (25.5 mm mount), 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power planes |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.75 × (1 + 0.55) = **1.16 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, two FETs carry the phase current at every instant:
- the high-side FET of the active phase or its synchronous low-side partner, and
- the low-side FET of the return phase.

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) \cdot (1 + \tfrac{1}{12}r^2)$$

Here r is the ripple ratio ΔI/I. With motor inductance around 20 µH, ripple is about 15%, so the ripple term is negligible (≈ 0.2%).

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.16\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{14.85\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Copper (PCB) conduction

Assume a 2 oz plane, about 8 squares of path from the battery pad to the phase pad, with 2 layers in parallel:

R_sq(2 oz, 25 °C) ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□ → (8 × 0.25 / 2) = 1.0 mΩ, rising to 1.3 mΩ at 100 °C.

Battery current share ≈ 80 A × duty. At full throttle (D ≈ 1), P_Cu ≈ 80² × 1.3 m ≈ **8.3 W per channel path**.

This is often the dominant overlooked loss. Mitigations:
- Stitch all power layers.
- Place the battery pads centrally.
- Add solder-bump bus bars.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Transition times from gate charge

| Edge | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Turn-on | I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl)/(R_g + R_g,int) = (10 − 3.5)/(3.3 + 1.7) | 1.3 A |
| | t_r = Q_sw / I_g,on = 12 nC / 1.3 A | **9.2 ns** |
| Turn-off | I_g,off = V_pl / (R_g,off + R_g,int), using a 1 Ω sink diode path | ≈ 1.2 A |
| | t_f | ≈ **10 ns** |

### 2.2 Loss components (per channel, one hard-switching FET pair)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ V I (t_r + t_f) f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 19.2 n × 48 k | **0.93 W** |
| Output capacitance | ½ C_oss,tr V² f = 0.5 × 1.5 nF × 635 × 48 k | 0.02 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr V f = 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.04 W |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f I · 2t_dt · f = 0.8 × 80 × 2 × 100 ns × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive | 2 × Q_g V_drv f = 2 × 50 n × 10 × 48 k | 0.05 W (dissipated in driver/R_g) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 1.65 W** |

**Design lever:** Dead-time diode loss is about two-thirds of switching loss. Trimming t_dt from 100 ns to 50 ns (AM32 dead-time setting, verified with a scope for no shoot-through) saves about 0.3 W per channel.

### 2.3 Per-channel and board totals (80 A burst, T_j = 125 °C)

| | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 14.85 W | 59.4 W |
| Switching + dead time | 1.65 W | 6.6 W |
| PCB copper | 8.3 W | 33.2 W |
| Shunts (0.25 mΩ) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 26.4 W** | **≈ 106 W** |

Electrical efficiency at this point is 1 − 106 / (4 × 80 × 25.2 × D). At D = 1 that is about 98.7%. It looks good, but 106 W into a 30×30 mm board is not sustainable, as the next section shows.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped RC network

```
P_FET ─► R_jc (1.2 K/W per FET) ─► Case ─► R_cb (0.8 K/W via array) ─► Board node [C_b] ─► R_ba ─► Ambient
```

**Junction rise above board** (one FET, ~7.4 W conduction + share of switching ≈ 8.2 W):
ΔT_jb = 8.2 × (1.2 + 0.8) = **16.4 K**

**Board thermal capacitance:**

| Element | Calculation | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|
| FR4 | 1.44 cm³ × 1.9 g/cm³ × 1.1 J/gK | 3.0 J/K |
| Copper | ~0.4 cm³ × 8.96 × 0.385 | 1.4 J/K |
| FETs, caps, connectors, solder | | ~1.6 J/K |
| **C_b** | | **≈ 6 J/K** |

**Board-to-air resistance:** Prop-wash forced convection gives h ≈ 100 W/m²K. Area is 2 × 9 cm² = 1.8×10⁻³ m².
R_ba ≈ 1/(hA) ≈ **5.5 K/W**, so τ = R_ba C_b ≈ **33 s**.

### 3.2 Burst duration

$$T_b(t) = T_a + P R_{ba}\,(1-e^{-t/\tau})$$

With P = 106 W, adiabatic slope = P / C_b = **17.7 K/s**.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| T_a | 40 °C |
| Board limit | 105 °C (T_j ≈ 121 °C, under the 150 °C max with margin) |
| ΔT budget | 65 K |

$$t_{burst} = -\tau \ln\left(1 - \frac{\Delta T}{P R_{ba}}\right) = -33 \ln(1 - 65/583) \approx \mathbf{3.9\ s}$$

### 3.3 Continuous rating

The board can shed 65 K / 5.5 K/W ≈ **11.8 W** continuously. Total loss scales as k·I², with k ≈ 106 / 80² per 4 channels. Solving gives I_cont ≈ 80 × √(11.8/106) ≈ **27 A per chann
