## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Three constraints govern the rest of the design.

**A. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
- The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits four bidirectional DShot600 streams.
- Four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.
- Commutating 4 motors from the H743 is not supported by ArduPilot and would destroy loop determinism.

**B. 80 A burst is per channel, not simultaneous.**
- 4 × 80 A at full duty is about 320 A of battery current.
- An XT60, a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board, and the battery leads cannot carry that.
- I model a single channel at 80 A and also the all-four worst case. The result shows the all-four case is limited by thermal mass to a few seconds.

**C. All numeric device parameters are placeholders.**
- They are representative of 40 V, ~1 mΩ PQFN 3.3×3.3 MOSFETs.
- Re-run every equation with the final datasheet values before freezing the BOM.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha\approx0.0067\,\text{K}^{-1}\;\Rightarrow\;R_{ds}(100^\circ\text{C})\approx1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current Paths in 6-Step Commutation with Complementary PWM

In each 60° electrical sector:
- **HS_x (PWM'd high phase):** conducts I for a fraction D of the sector.
- **LS_x (synchronous rectifier):** conducts I for a fraction (1−D) of the sector.
- **LS_y (low phase):** conducts I for the whole sector.

Each leg spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the "high" phase and 1/3 as the "low" phase. Exactly two channels are in the current path at all times, so:

$$P_{cond,leg}=\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}\,[D+(1-D)]+\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}=\tfrac{2}{3}I^2R_{ds}$$

$$P_{cond,ch}=3\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}I^2R_{ds}=2I^2R_{ds}$$
