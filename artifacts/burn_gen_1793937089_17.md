> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag:** 4 × 80 A burst means **up to 320 A** at the battery pads. That exceeds what an XT60 (~60 A continuous, ~180 A pulse) or a 30×30 board's input copper can carry. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, two phases conduct:
- **PWM leg (e.g., phase A):** the high-side FET Q_AH is ON for duty D, and the low-side Q_AL is ON for (1–D) through synchronous rectification.
- **Static leg (e.g., phase B):** Q_BL is ON for the full sector.
- **Floating phase:** phase C carries no current and is used for BEMF zero-cross detection.

The current therefore always flows through exactly **two** channel resistances:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Phase inductance L_ph is typically 5–20 µH for 5" class motors. The ripple is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}D - E_{bemf})(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}
$$

Example: with ΔI ≈ 8 A pk-pk at 48 kHz, the ripple term adds 5.3 A² to 6400 A², i.e. **0.08 %**. It is negligible, so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent resistance (iterated)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.8 mΩ.

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}
$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET is active in only 2 of the 6 sectors per electrical cycle:
- Average per-FET conduction loss ≈ 23.0 / 6 = **3.84 W**.
- The instantaneous peak (static low-side FET) is I²R = **11.5 W**.

The electrical frequency is f_e = RPM × pole-pairs / 60. For a 7-pole-pair motor at 25k RPM, that is about 2.9 kHz, a 345 µs period. The die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) is much longer, so **the die sees the average**, with a small ripple of:

$$
\Delta T_{j,ripple} \approx P_{pk} \cdot Z_{th,jc}(t_{on}\!\approx\!115\,\mu s) \approx 11.5 \times 0.05 \approx 0.6\ \text{K}
$$
