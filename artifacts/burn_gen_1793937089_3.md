# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #68): Power-Stage Thermal Model, Switching Losses, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

This document gives the conduction and switching loss models for an 80 A burst, the bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA mapping for ArduPilot, a reference netlist, and a thermal budget. Assumed component values are labeled. Replace them with datasheet values for your selected parts before release.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. In every production 4-in-1 stack:

```
[STM32H743 FC]  --DShot600 bidir (4 lines)-->  [4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071)]
                                                     |  6× PWM (complementary)
                                                [4× 3-phase gate driver]
                                                     |
                                                [24× N-MOSFET, 3 half-bridges/motor]
```

The H743 runs ArduPilot. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generates the 48 kHz PWM. The loss model below therefore applies to the ESC board, and the DMA model applies to the FC.

**Specification check:** "80 A burst" must be defined. **80 A per motor on a 20×20 / 25×25 nano board is not physically sustainable beyond about 1 s** (see §3). **80 A total (20 A/motor)** is realistic. Both cases are computed.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Assumed MOSFET (3×3 DFN, 40 V class)

| Parameter | Symbol | Value (assumed) |
|---|---|---|
| R<sub>DS(on)</sub> @ 25 °C, V<sub>GS</sub> = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.2 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.0055 /K |
| Gate charge | Q<sub>g</sub> | 45 nC |
| Output charge | Q<sub>oss</sub> | 35 nC |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q<sub>rr</sub> | 40 nC |
| Body diode V<sub>f</sub> | V<sub>f</sub> | 0.8 V |
| R<sub>θJC</sub> (bottom) | | 1.5 K/W |

Temperature-dependent resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C: R = 1.2 × 1.4675 ≈ **1.76 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-Step Trapezoidal, Complementary (Synchronous) PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **Active (PWM) leg:** high-side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) synchronous switch conducts for 1 − D. Together they carry I for 100 % of the period.
- **Return leg:** LS fully on, carrying I for 100 % of the period.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This is independent of D under synchronous rectification.

Each FET conducts for 120° per electrical revolution, so the average per FET is P<sub>cond,motor</sub>/6. The instantaneous peak in a return-leg LS FET is I²R.

## 2. Switching Losses at f<sub>sw</sub> = 48 kHz, V<sub>bus</sub> = 25.2 V (6S full)

Only the PWM leg switches. Its loss terms are:

| Term | Equation |
|---|---|
| V–I overlap | P<sub>VI</sub> = ½ · V · I · (t<sub>r</sub> + t<sub>f</sub>) · f<sub>sw</sub> |
| Reverse recovery | P<sub>rr</sub> = Q<sub>rr</sub> · V · f<sub>sw</sub> |
| Output capacitance | P<sub>oss</sub> = Q<sub>oss</sub> · V · f<sub>sw</sub> |
| Dead-time body diode | P<sub>dt</sub> = 2 · V<sub>f</sub> · I · t<sub>dt</sub> · f<sub>sw</sub> |
| Gate drive (HS + LS) | P<sub>g</sub> = 2 · Q<sub>g</sub> · V<sub>drv</sub> · f<sub>sw</sub> |

Assumed timing: t<sub>r</sub> + t<sub>f</sub> = 20 ns, t<sub>dt</sub> = 100 ns, V<sub>drv</sub> = 10 V.

The gate-drive loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

### 2.1 Results per Motor

| Term | 80 A/motor (T<sub>j</sub> 110 °C) | 20 A/motor (T<sub>j</sub> 70 °C, R = 1.50 mΩ) |
|---|---|---|
| P<sub>cond</sub> = 2I²R | 2·6400·1.76 m = **22.5 W** | 2·400·1.50 m = **1.20 W** |
| P<sub>VI</sub> | 0.5·25.2·80·20 n·48 k = **0.97 W** | **0.24 W** |
| P<sub>rr</sub> | 40 n·25.2·48 k = **0.048 W** | 0.048 W |
| P<sub>oss</sub> | 35 n·25.2·48 k = **0.042 W** | 0.042 W |
| P<sub>dt</sub> | 2·0.8·80·100 n·48 k = **0.61 W** | **0.15 W** |
| P<sub>g</sub> (driver) | 2·45 n·10·48 k = **0.043 W** | 0.043 W |
| **Total/motor** | **≈ 24.2 W** | **≈ 1.72 W** |
| **Total, 4 motors** | **≈ 97 W** | **≈ 6.9 W** |

Conduction dominates (93 %) at 80 A. Raising f<sub>sw</sub> to 96 kHz adds only about 1.7 W per motor. Switching frequency is not the thermal lever; R<sub>DS(on)</sub> and copper are.

**Bus capacitor ripple (per motor, worst case at D = 0.5):**

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

This gives 40 A<sub>rms</sub> at 80 A and 10 A<sub>rms</sub> at 20 A. Size the low-ESR bulk capacitance and on-board MLCCs accordingly. Their ESR loss adds to the board heat.
