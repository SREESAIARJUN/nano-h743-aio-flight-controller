## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs carry the phase current at every instant:

- **On-time (D):** high-side of phase A + low-side of phase B
- **Off-time (1−D):** low-side of phase A (sync rectifier) + low-side of phase B

The conduction loss is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

**Temperature correction** (typical 40 V trench FET, ≈ +0.6 %/K):

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25} \cdot \left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: k_T = 1.6, so R = 1.92 mΩ.

**Ripple correction.** Phase inductance L_ph ≈ 10 µH (typical 2207-class motor) gives the peak-to-peak ripple:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Worst case is V_bemf ≈ V_bus/2 and D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{12.6 \cdot 0.5}{10\mu \cdot 48k} = 13.1\ \text{A}$$

The RMS current then follows:

$$I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}} = \sqrt{6400 + 14.3} = 80.09\ \text{A}$$

The ripple contribution is negligible (+0.2 %).

**Result at 80 A:**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot (80.09)^2 \cdot 1.92\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

### 1.2 Switching Loss (hard-switched PWM leg)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The sync-rectifier FET turns on at near-zero V_ds (after dead time), so its switching loss is ignored.

**Transition times from gate charge.** The Miller plateau is V_pl ≈ 4.5 V, and total gate resistance is R_g,tot = 4.7 Ω (external) + 1 Ω (internal) + ≈ 3 Ω (driver output) ≈ 8.7 Ω.

Turn-on gate current:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{12 - 4.5}{8.7} = 0.86\ \text{A}$$

The driver's source current limit (~0.6 A) governs, so I_g,on = 0.6 A:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{0.6\,\text{A}} = 16.7\ \text{ns}$$

Turn-off gate current:

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{4.5}{8.7 - 3 + 1} = 0.67\ \text{A}$$

$$t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{0.67\,\text{A}} = 15\ \text{ns}$$

Adding the current-commutation interval, Q_gs2 (~3 nC):

$$t_{sw,total} \approx 40\ \text{ns}$$

**V–I overlap loss:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I\,(t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (body diode of the sync FET, recovering at turn-on of the PWM FET):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 50\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.030\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period, V_F ≈ 0.8 V at 80 A):

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$
