## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Measured phase currents are $i_a, i_b$, with $i_c = -i_a - i_b$.

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:** with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$,
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Electrical model and decoupling
For a surface PMSM ($L_d = L_q = L$):
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \psi_m$$

The controller output therefore includes feedforward decoupling terms:
$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \psi_m)$$

### 1.3 Pole-zero cancellation tuning
The plant is $G_i(s) = \frac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choose the PI gains so the PI zero cancels the electrical pole:
$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The closed loop then becomes $\frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}$. Choose $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_s/20$, which is about 2π·1 kHz at 20 kHz.

### 1.4 Delay compensation
The PWM update happens 1.5 sample periods after the angle is sampled. Advance the angle used in the inverse Park transform accordingly:
$$\theta_e^{\text{pwm}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

### 1.5 Voltage limiting and SVPWM
The voltage vector is limited to the inscribed circle of the SVPWM hexagon:
$$|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}$$
Prioritize $v_d$, then give $v_q$ the remainder: $v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.

**SVPWM via min-max zero-sequence injection:**
$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\quad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.6 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup
At sample $k$, with error $e_k$:
$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad \bar u_k = \text{sat}(u_k),\quad I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\big[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(\bar u_k - u_k)\big]$$
Use $K_{aw} \approx K_i/K_p$, which is the reciprocal of the integral time.
