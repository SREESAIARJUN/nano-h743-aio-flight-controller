## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset), wrapped to [0, 2π).

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of the Park matrix applied to (v_d, v_q).

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The PMSM model in the dq frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to 1/(Ls + R). A PI controller with pole-zero cancellation gives a first-order closed loop of bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p^i = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^i = R\,\omega_c$$

With the PWM transport delay (≈ 1.5·T_s), the bandwidth is limited by:

$$\omega_c \le \frac{1}{3\cdot1.5\,T_s}\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz at } 20\text{ kHz}$$
