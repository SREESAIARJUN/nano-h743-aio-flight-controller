## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Finding

**System partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does **not** commutate the bridges. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 at 48 kHz PWM. The 48 kHz figure applies to the ESC MCUs. The H743 timer and DMA design concerns only DShot.

**Key finding.** A sustained or simultaneous 80 A per-channel burst is **not thermally credible on a 20×20 mm nano footprint**:

- Conduction plus switching losses come to about **28 W per channel at 80 A** and about **112 W board-wide**.
- The PCB's bulk heat capacity is only a few J/K, so the board reaches its limit in about **1 second**.

Section 3 quantifies this. Section 7 lists the design changes needed to make an "80 A burst" rating defensible.

All device parameters below are **stated assumptions** representative of 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs. Replace them with the selected part's datasheet values and rerun the equations.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel, 6 FETs)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

Assume six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. Within any 60° electrical sector:

- **PWM phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T, and the complementary low-side conducts for (1−D)·T. Exactly one of the two carries I_ph at any instant.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET is held ON for the full sector.

At any instant, therefore, **two R_DS(on) are in series with I_ph, independent of duty**:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on),hot} = 2(80)^2(2.08\,\text{m}\Omega) = \mathbf{26.6\ W}$$

Each FET occupies the "return low-side" role for 120° and the PWM role for 120° per electrical cycle. Losses therefore average across the six FETs:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx 26.6/6 = 4.4\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{cond,FET,peak} = I^2R = 13.3\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous peak matters for junction ripple at low eRPM, where the 120° conduction window is long relative to the die thermal time constant.

**Copper conduction (add to the budget).** For an 80 A path on 2 oz outer layers plus 2×1 oz inner layers in parallel, with roughly 0.5 mΩ effective per channel path including the phase pad and current shunt:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W per channel}$$

### 2.2 Hard-Switching Loss (PWM high-side)

Gate current during the Miller plateau:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext,on}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{7}{5.7} = 1.23\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_r = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 6.5\ \text{ns}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext,off}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{3}{3.2} = 0.94\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_f = 8.5\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(15\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{0.73\ W}$$

Real-world loop inductance (2–4 nH) adds overshoot and lengthens the transitions. Apply a layout factor of **1.5×**, giving **≈1.1 W**.

### 2.3 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction (synchronous low-side)

$$P_{dt} = V_F I_{ph}(2t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(200\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.4 Reverse Recovery, Output Capacitance, Gate Drive

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}(25.2)(48\text{k}) = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.015\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2(30\text{n})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.029\ \text{W}$$

The factor of 2 in the gate term covers the complementary PWM pair. This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs.

### 2.5 Per-Channel and Board Totals at 80 A

| Loss term | W / channel |
|---|---|
| FET conduction | 26.6 |
| Switching (with 1.5× layout factor) | 1.1 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.06 |
| Gate drive | 0.03 |
| Copper / shunt | 3.2 |
| **Total** | **≈ 31.6 W** |
| **4 channels simultaneous** | **≈ 126 W** |

**Efficiency check.** Assuming roughly 80% duty at punch-out, motor input power is about 25.2 × 80 × 0.8 ≈ 1.6 kW per channel. A 31.6 W loss gives about 98% efficiency. This is electrically fine. **The problem is purely thermal density.**

**Sensitivity.** Conduction dominates, and losses scale as I² and R_DS(on):

- At 60 A: P_cond = 15 W.
- At 40 A: P_cond = 6.7 W.
- Halving R_DS(on) by moving to 5×6 PDFN parts at 0.6–0.7 mΩ roughly halves the dominant term.
