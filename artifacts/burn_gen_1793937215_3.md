## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning: who generates what

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. A production "4-in-1 ESC nano FC" is two subsystems on one board:

| Subsystem | MCU | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot. Generates bi-directional DShot600 and captures eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC ×4 | 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (or 4× EFM8BB21 running BLHeli_S) | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, and the 3-phase gate driver per channel |

Running four 6-step commutation loops on the H743 is possible in principle. However, ArduPilot does not support it, and it would make the autopilot's hard-real-time scheduler compete with commutation ISRs. **Recommendation: keep the partition.** The rest of this document assumes it.

### 0.3 Engineering flags (read before committing to the spec)

1. **"80 A burst" on a nano-class board is a few-second rating, not a continuous one.** The math in §3 gives about 3.7 s to thermal limit and roughly 26 A/channel continuous under prop wash. Specify both numbers on the datasheet.
2. **Total bus current is 4 × 80 = 320 A.** This is beyond any XT60 or XT90 connector and most 6S packs (≈ 7 kW). In realistic flight, all four channels rarely peak together. Size the connector, wiring, and shunt for a declared aggregate burst, e.g. 200 A.
3. **The shunt is a hidden heater.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt at 320 A dissipates 25.6 W. Use ≤ 0.1 mΩ, or Hall/per-channel sensing.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phase legs are active:

- **Leg A** is PWM-switched. The high side conducts for duty D; the low side (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Leg B** has its low side held ON.

The phase current I flows through exactly **two** conducting MOSFET channels at every instant, ignoring dead time. The instantaneous conduction power per ESC channel is therefore:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left(2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\right)
$$
