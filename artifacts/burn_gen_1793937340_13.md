## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

During any commutation sector, current flows through **two MOSFETs in series**:

- **Static leg:** one low-side FET stays on for the whole sector and conducts I for 100% of the time.
- **Switching leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1 − D).

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Under FOC/sinusoidal drive with phase current amplitude I_pk, the equivalent result is P ≈ 3·(I_pk/√2)²·R_DS. That works out to 1.5·I_pk²·R_DS, about 25% lower than the trapezoidal worst case. The design below uses the trapezoidal case.

### 1.2 Ripple correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

**Example:** L_ph = 15 µH (typical 5" motor phase-to-phase / 2), V_bus − V_bemf = 12 V, D = 0.5.

- ΔI = 12 × 0.5 / (15 µH × 48 kHz) = **8.3 A p-p**
- ΔI²/12 = 5.8 A² against 6400 A², a 0.09% correction. This is negligible at 80 A.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on): electrothermal fixed point

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,R_{\theta JB}$$

Solving self-consistently with R_θJB = R_θJC + R_solder/vias ≈ 1.5 K/W:

$$T_j = \frac{T_b + R_{\theta JB}\,I^2 R_{25}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - R_{\theta JB}\, I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}$$

**Thermal runaway condition:** the denominator must stay positive.

$$I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\, R_{\theta JB} < 1$$

At 80 A: 6400 × 0.001 × 0.005 × 1.5 = **0.048**, which is stable with large margin. The FET is not the runaway limit; the board heat capacity is (§3).
