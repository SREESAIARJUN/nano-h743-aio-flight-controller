## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

In a production 4-in-1 stack, the **STM32H743 runs ArduPilot on the FC**. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) that generates the **48 kHz** gate PWM. The H743 does not commutate motors. It sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, synchronous rectification)

In each 60° commutation sector, two legs are active:
- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D. The complementary low side conducts for 1−D (synchronous rectification).
- **Return leg:** the low side is held on.

At every instant, exactly **two MOSFET channels** carry I_ph. The loss is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2(80)^2(1.2\,\text{m}\Omega) = \mathbf{15.36\ W}$$

Each switch conducts over 120° of the 360° electrical cycle. Averaged per device:

$$P_{cond,FET}=\tfrac{1}{6}P_{cond,motor} = 2.56\ \text{W}$$

**Temperature coupling.** On-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Iterate T_j until it converges. Two to three passes are sufficient. The 1.2 mΩ value corresponds to T_j ≈ 100 °C.
