## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Warning

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs a dedicated ESC MCU per channel running BLHeli_32, AM32 or Bluejay. This design uses an **AT32F421 running AM32**. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Trapezoidal 6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Per-phase conduction states
Over one electrical cycle, each phase spends:
- **1/3 driven high (PWM leg).** The high-side FET conducts for fraction D. The low-side FET of the same leg conducts for fraction (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **1/3 driven low.** The low-side FET is on continuously.
- **1/3 floating**, used for BEMF zero-cross sensing.

The conduction losses for each device are:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\, D$$

$$P_{LS} = \frac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,(1-D) + \frac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$P_{phase} = \frac{2}{3} I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The per-phase sum is **independent of duty cycle**, which is a key result of synchronous rectification.

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 3 \cdot \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R = 2\, I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R = 1.5 mΩ.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\ \mathrm{m\Omega} = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$

**Worst-case single FET:**
- At D → 0 (low throttle, high current, e.g. during a stall or punch-out start), the low-side FET reaches (2/3)·I²R = **6.4 W**.
- At D = 1, the high-side and low-side FETs each dissipate **3.2 W**.

### 1.4 PCB copper loss (frequently ignored, significant at 80 A)
Assume 4 layers of 2 oz copper in parallel for the phase and bus paths, effective R ≈ 0.25 mΩ per channel path:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.25\,\mathrm{m\Omega} \approx 1.6\ \mathrm{W\ per\ channel}$$

Battery pads and the main bus at 320 A, 0.05 mΩ:

$$320^2 \times 0.05\,\mathrm{m\Omega} \approx 5.1\ \mathrm{W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. At any instant, one high-side FET is switching and its complementary low-side FET is commutating via the body diode.

### 2.1 Hard-switching (V–I overlap)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{PWM} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
