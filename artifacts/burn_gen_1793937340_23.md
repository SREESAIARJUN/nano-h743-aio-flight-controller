## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous PWM)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, exactly two phase legs conduct during each 60° sector:

- One high-side switch is PWM'd with duty D. Its complementary low-side switch synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- One low-side switch on another leg is held on for the full sector.

At any instant, the motor current I therefore flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels in series**. That holds for both PWM states because of synchronous rectification.
