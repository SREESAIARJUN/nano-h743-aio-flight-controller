## 0. Architectural Correction and Assumptions

**The H743 cannot run the 48 kHz power stage.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack. It does not commutate motors. A real 4-in-1 needs:

| Domain | Silicon | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC ×4 | 4× AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 (sets 48 kHz PWM, bidirectional DShot, eRPM telemetry) |
| Gate drive | 4× three-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class, ~1.2 A/1.5 A) | — |
| Power | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | — |

The H743 talks DShot600 to the ESC MCUs. The 48 kHz switching is an AM32 parameter (`PWM_FREQ`).

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification:
- Two phases carry current at any instant.
- The loop is HS(x) → motor winding → LS(y).
- The PWM duty D switches the HS. The complementary LS of the same leg freewheels during (1−D).

Path resistance per motor:

$$R_{path} = 2\,R_{DS,hot} + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}$$

PCB copper is 2 oz on outer layers plus 2 oz inner pour, with a phase-pad to bus run of about 8 mm:
- R_Cu ≈ 0.40 mΩ (round-trip, including vias)
- R_shunt = 0.25 mΩ (common low-side shunt, per ESC)

$$R_{path} = 2(1.09) + 0.40 + 0.25 = 2.83\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Burst conduction (I = 80 A, phase-current RMS ≈ DC in 6-step)

| Element | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FETs (both conducting) | I²·2R_DS,hot = 6400·2.18 mΩ | **13.95 W** |
| Copper | I²·R_Cu | 2.56 W |
| Shunt | I²·R_shunt | 1.60 W |
| **Total per motor** | | **18.1 W** |

**Per-FET average.** Each switch conducts one third of the electrical cycle. During that time it shares the HS position with its complement in proportion to D (at full burst D → ~1):

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS,hot}}{3} = \frac{6400 \times 1.09\text{m}}{3} = 2.33\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Temperature feedback
R_DS rises with T_j:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + 0.0042\,(T_j - 25)]$$

Iterating the loss and T_j equations to a fixed point converges at T_j ≈ 105–110 °C under burst, which is consistent with the α = 1.45 assumption. If T_j exceeds about 135 °C the loop runs away. AM32's temperature limit must cut before that (set `TEMP_LIMIT` = 110 °C).

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

These are per motor, with one hard-switched leg active at a time.

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard turn-on/off of the HS)
The driver has a source/sink of 1.2/1.5 A into R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω, with a plateau around 3 V:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{9\text{nC}}{(10-3)/(2.2+5.8)} \approx 10\ \text{ns},\qquad t_f \approx 9\text{nC}/\left(3/8\right) \approx 24\ \text{ns}$$

(5.8 Ω is the driver's internal output resistance.)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 34\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.65\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS, two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, 2 t_{dt}\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

Reducing t_dt to 100 ns halves this. Only do so after scope verification of shoot-through margin.

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \cdot 40\text{nC} \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

At 80 A, Q_rr scales up roughly 1.5–2×, so budget **0.09 W**.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f_{sw}\cdot 2 = 0.5 \cdot 1.4\text{n} \cdot 635 \cdot 48\text{k} \cdot 2 = 0.043\ \text{W}$$
