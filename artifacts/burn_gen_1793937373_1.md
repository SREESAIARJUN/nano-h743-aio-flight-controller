## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Three points need to be settled before the math:

1. **The H743 runs the flight controller only, not the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and never drives the power stage directly. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU, such as an STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
   - The 48 kHz PWM is an **ESC firmware setting**.
   - DShot600 is the **FC→ESC command link**.
   - These are independent clock domains.
