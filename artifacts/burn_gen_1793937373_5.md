## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C with α = 0.005, the normalization factor k_T = 1.5. So R_hot = 1.5 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° sector, one high-side (HS) device chops at duty D. The same-leg low-side (LS) device freewheels for (1−D). The opposite leg's LS device conducts continuously. Over one electrical cycle, each leg is active for 1/3 of the time as "source" and 1/3 as "sink."

Per-device energy-averaged power:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{I^2 R_{hot}\,D}{3}$$

$$P_{LS} = \frac{I^2 R_{hot}}{3}\big[1 + (1-D)\big]$$

At every instant, exactly two channels conduct I. The bridge total is therefore independent of D:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}}$$
