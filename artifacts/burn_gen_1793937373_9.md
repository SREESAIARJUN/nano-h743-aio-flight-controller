## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology-derived current flow

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous (complementary) PWM, at any instant in a 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and its complementary low-side FET conducts for (1–D) after the dead time. One FET in this leg always carries I.
- **Static leg:** one low-side FET conducts I for the full sector.
- **Floating leg:** used for BEMF sensing and carries no current.

Therefore the instantaneous conduction loss per channel is independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$
