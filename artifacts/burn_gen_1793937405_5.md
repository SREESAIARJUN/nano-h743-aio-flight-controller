## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

An STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz with the sensorless BEMF control that 4-in-1 ESCs expect. The standard partitioning is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot, sends bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), 120 MHz | AM32 firmware, 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM reply |
| Gate drivers (×4) | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | Drive 6 N-FETs per channel |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET, 3.3 × 3.3 mm DFN | 24 FETs total |

All numbers below use these design assumptions. Replace them with datasheet values for your BOM.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_ph | 80 A (DC-equivalent during conduction) |
| FET R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V, max | R_25 | 1.0 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature multiplier at T_j ≈ 125 °C | k_T | 1.5 |
| Rise / fall time at the gate drive used | t_r, t_f | 10 ns / 10 ns |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC @ 10 V |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_F | 0.8 V |
| Dead time (each edge) | t_dt | 100 ns |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Board | — | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz all layers |

> **Feasibility flag:** 80 A per channel on a nano/30.5 mm AIO is a **burst rating only**, on the order of seconds. The math in §3 shows why. Do not publish it as a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology
Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM:
- At any instant, two phases conduct. One leg is held on and one leg is PWM-modulated.
- With complementary PWM, the current path always passes through exactly **two FET channels**: one high-side and one low-side equivalent. This holds during both the PWM-on interval and the freewheel interval, excluding dead time.

### 1.2 Hot R_DS(on)
$$R_{hot} = R_{25}\,k_T = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega$$

A more general form is $R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$ with α ≈ 0.005 K⁻¹, which gives k_T = 1.5 at 125 °C.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss
$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{hot} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution
Each FET conducts for 120° of each 360° electrical cycle, either directly or as the synchronous freewheel partner. Averaged over the cycle:
$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

Commutation frequency check: at 100 k eRPM, the 6-step rate is 10 kHz, a 100 µs period. That is far shorter than the die thermal time constant (ms range), so cycle-averaging is valid.

### 1.5 Copper and connector losses (not negligible)
- Shared bus path (pads to bus bar to FETs) ≈ 0.3 mΩ per channel.
- With all four channels at full throttle, battery current ≈ 4 × 80 × D. At D ≈ 1, that is 320 A.

$$P_{Cu,bus} \approx (320)^2 \times 0.15\,\text{m}\Omega_{(shared\ XT60/pad\ path)} \approx 15\ \text{W}$$
