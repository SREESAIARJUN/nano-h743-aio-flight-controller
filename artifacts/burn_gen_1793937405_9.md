**Job #65: Thermal, switching-loss, and DMA/timer architecture**

## 0. Design Corrections

These four issues change the design, so they come first.

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no sensorless 6-step BLDC commutation path, and putting 4× commutation ISRs beside the EKF would break both. | Architecture: **H743 (ArduPilot FC) → 4× AT32F421 (AM32) → FD6288Q gate drivers → 24× MOSFETs**. Bidirectional DShot terminates at the AT32s. |
| 2 | **"80A burst" on a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is a few seconds, not a rating.** The model below shows 4-motor simultaneous 80A heats the board from 40°C to 110°C in about **1.6 s**. | Spec as "80A × 1 s per channel, one channel at a time". Continuous is about **20–25A per channel** under prop wash. |
| 3 | **4 × 80A = 320A from the battery** (about 7 kW at 6S). | XT60 (about 60A continuous) and 12 AWG leads cannot support this. The battery-side burst budget is the real system limit. |
| 4 | **Bulk capacitance cannot fit on-board.** The math in §3 shows ≥ 420 µF is needed. | An external low-ESR electrolytic of at least 470 µF / 35 V is a **mandatory** part of the design. |

All component values below are **representative**. Substitute the datasheet values of your chosen MOSFET into the equations; the models are what matter.
