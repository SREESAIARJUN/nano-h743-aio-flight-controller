## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

Here θ_off is found at calibration by aligning the rotor to a d-axis voltage vector.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (power-variant, 2 shunts, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The flux linkage is λ_m = K_t / (1.5 p).

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

Pole-zero cancellation places the closed loop at a first-order bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/10. At f_s = 20 kHz, ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s.

The control law is:

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q
$$
$$
v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e\lambda_m
$$

with i_d^* = 0.
