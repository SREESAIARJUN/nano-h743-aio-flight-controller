## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{e,0}$$

where θ_{e,0} is the electrical offset found at calibration: apply a voltage along the d-axis, let the rotor settle, and record the encoder reading.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform (Amplitude-Invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park Transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant Model (Surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \psi_m)$$

with ω_e = p·ω_m. The back-EMF constant is λ = ψ_m, and k_t = (3/2)·p·ψ_m.
