## 2. FOC Current Loop

### 2.1 Transforms

**Electrical angle:**
θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset)

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant):
- i_α = i_a
- i_β = (i_a + 2i_b)/√3
- This form assumes i_a + i_b + i_c = 0.

**Park transform:**
- i_d = i_α cos θₑ + i_β sin θₑ
- i_q = −i_α sin θₑ + i_β cos θₑ

**Inverse Park transform**, with delay compensation:
- θₑ* = θₑ + 1.5·ωₑ·T_s. This compensates one sample of computation delay plus half a sample of PWM zero-order hold.
- v_α = v_d cos θₑ* − v_q sin θₑ*
- v_β = v_d sin θₑ* + v_q cos θₑ*

### 2.2 Plant and PI Design

The dq-frame motor model is:
- v_d = R·i_d + L·di_d/dt − ωₑ·L·i_q
- v_q = R·i_q + L·di_q/dt + ωₑ·L·i_d + ωₑ·λ_m

**Decoupling:** add the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms as feedforward:
- v_d,ff = −ωₑ·L·i_q
- v_q,ff = ωₑ·L·i_d + ωₑ·λ_m

What remains is a first-order plant per axis: G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning,** with target bandwidth ω_c (rad/s):
- K_p = L·ω_c
- K_i = R·ω_c
- The closed loop then becomes ω_c/(s + ω_c).
- Keep ω_c ≤ 2π·
