## 0. Architecture Scope and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. In this stack:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 master, bidirectional eRPM capture | STM32H743VIT6 (FC board) | ArduPilot |
| 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF sensing | 4 × ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071) | AM32 |
| Gate drive | 4 × FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET (6 per channel) | n/a |

The loss model (Sections 1–3) therefore applies to the ESC board. The DMA model (Section 4) applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current distribution: 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous (complementary) PWM

In each 60° electrical sector, three switches are involved:
- One high-side FET is PWM-modulated at duty D.
- Its complementary low-side FET conducts during (1−D).
- One low-side FET of another phase is fully on.

Over one electrical period, each leg spends 1/3 of the time as the PWM leg and 1/3 as the return leg. This gives per-FET RMS currents of:

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{D}{3} I^2, \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{1}{3}I^2 + \frac{1-D}{3}I^2 \cdot k_{sync}$$

where k_sync = 1 for synchronous rectification. Diode freewheel is treated separately in Section 2.

At the worst burst point (D → 1), I_HS,rms² = I_LS,rms² = I²/3.

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.38 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Losses at 80 A

Per FET:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2}{3} R_{ds}(T_j) = \frac{6400}{3}\times 1.38\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{2.94\ W}$$

Per channel: two FETs are always in series in the current path, so

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.38\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.7\ W}$$

Four channels: **70.7 W**.

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

**Per-channel shunt.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates 80² × 0.25 m = **1.6 W**. Use a 2512 metal-element shunt rated at least 3 W.

**Shared battery copper.** Assume pad-to-bus resistance R_bus ≈ 0.15 mΩ. Use 2 oz outer layers plus 2 oz inner, stitched.

$$P_{bus} = (4 \times 80)^2 \times 0.15\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{15.4\ W}$$

This term is dominant and frequently ignored. Each 0.05 mΩ of extra copper costs about 5 W at full burst.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing high-side FET hard-switches. Its complement switches near zero voltage but incurs dead-time diode conduction and reverse recovery. At any instant only **one leg per channel** is switching.

### 2.1 Loss components (hard-switched FET, linear V-I overlap approximation)

| Component | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | P_VI = ½ V_bus I (t_r + t_f) f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k | **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery (lands in the hard-switched FET) | P_rr = Q_rr V_bus f_sw = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | **0.048 W** |
| Output capacitance | P_oss = ½ Q_oss V_bus f_sw = 0.5 × 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | **0.030 W** |
