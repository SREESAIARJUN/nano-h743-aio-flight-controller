## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology

With six-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous complementary PWM, exactly two FETs carry the phase current I at any instant, outside dead time:

- the high-side FET of the driving phase (duty D), or the low-side FET of the same leg during the off-time (synchronous freewheel), and
- the low-side FET of the sinking phase (100 % of the commutation interval).

Summed over the PWM period, the conduction energy equals two FETs conducting I continuously:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 115 °C:

$$R = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 90) = 1.794\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Evaluation at 80 A

$$P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \times 1.794\times10^{-3} = 11.48\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 22.96\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_f\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(100\times10^{-9})(48\,000) = 0.614\ \text{W}$$

### 1.5 Electrothermal Fixed Point

Conduction loss and junction temperature depend on each other:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + Z_{th}\cdot I^2 R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + Z_{th}I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha\,Z_{th}\,I^2R_{25}}$$

**Stability criterion (no thermal runaway):**

$$\alpha\,Z_{th}\,I^2R_{25} < 1$$

At 80 A with R_θjc = 1.5 K/W this gives 0.0055 × 1.5 × 6400 × 0.0012 = 0.063, which is stable. The binding constraint is therefore the board's thermal mass, not the junction (see §3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-Switching V–I Overlap

Only the PWM'd high-side FET hard-switches. The synchronous low-side FET switches at near-zero voltage (ZVS) after the body diode conducts.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\times10^{-9})(48\,000) = 1.935\ \text{W}$$

**Refinement for gate-plateau behavior.** The transition time depends on the Miller charge and the gate drive current:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}, \qquad t_{f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{plateau}}$$

Use these to size R_g. Faster edges reduce P_VI but raise dV/dt-induced EMI on the gyro.

### 2.2 Reverse Recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output Capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12\,C_{oss}\,V_{bus}^2\,f_{sw} = 0.5(2\text{n})(635)(48\text{k}) = 0.030\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Gate Drive (Dissipated in the Driver and R_g)

$$P_{g,FET} = Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} = 60\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.029\ \text{W}$$

With two FETs switching per channel at any instant, P_g ≈ 0.058 W, rising to about 0.17 W if all six gates are counted conservatively.
