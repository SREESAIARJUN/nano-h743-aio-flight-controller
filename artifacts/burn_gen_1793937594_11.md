# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80 A ESC (Job #17): Design Package Rev A

## 0. Design Review Flags

Four issues change the shape of the design. Resolve them before layout.

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **"Nano" vs. 80 A.** A 20×20 mm (nano) board cannot carry 4 × 80 A. Copper cross-section and thermal mass are too small. | The model below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm mount, roughly 41×41 mm board**. The numbers show that even this board supports 80 A only as a burst of seconds. |
| F2 | **The H743 does not switch the FETs.** The 48 kHz commutation PWM runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class). The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. | Switching losses belong to the ESC MCU domain. DMA and timer planning belongs to the H743 domain. |
| F3 | **"80 A" must be defined.** | Here it means **80 A phase (motor) current per channel at a duty cycle near 1**, so battery current ≈ phase current. That is the worst case for FET heating. |
| F4 | **Parameter values are representative, not from a specific part.** | Every equation is parametric. Replace Rds(on), Qg and Rθ with values from the selected datasheet before sign-off. |

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

- The high-side FET (HS) conducts for a fraction D of each PWM period.
