# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #44): Systems Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. A production stack partitions as follows:

| Board | MCU | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VI (400 MHz, ArduPilot) | Attitude control; DShot600 bidirectional master |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32) or 4× EFM8BB51 (Bluejay) | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz; eRPM telemetry |

Everything below models that partition.
