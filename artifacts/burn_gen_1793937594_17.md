# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer and DMA Architecture

**Document class:** Architecture derivation (pre-layout)

**Status of numbers:** Every component parameter below is an explicit assumption, marked **[A]**. The equations are general. Replace the [A] values with your selected datasheet values before release. I have not verified any specific vendor part against these numbers.

---

## 0. System Partition and Key Up-Front Findings

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (ArduPilot) | Attitude control. Emits bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4 channels, or 1 MCU per channel) | AM32-class ESC MCU, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 **[A]** | Commutation and 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drive | 3-phase half-bridge driver, FD6288Q-class **[A]** | Bootstrap high-side drive with integrated dead-time |
| Power stage | 12× N-MOSFET, 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm **[A]** | 3 half-bridges per motor |

**Important: the 48 kHz switching does not run on the H743.** It runs on the ESC MCUs. The H743 only sends DShot frames and receives eRPM. The two domains share a PCB, copper, and a thermal mass, so the thermal analysis must be done jointly.

**Headline findings (Sections 2–4 give the derivations):**

1. **Per-channel loss at 80 A is about 25 W.** This includes conduction, switching, dead-time diode, and copper losses.
2. **Four channels at 80 A dissipate about 100 W.** A nano board (~3 J/K thermal mass) heats at roughly **33 K/s** under that load. The "80 A burst" is therefore physically a **~2 s burst** on one or two channels, not a sustained or simultaneous rating.
3. **Continuous rating with prop-wash cooling is about 20–22 A per channel.** This is the honest datasheet number.
4. **Do not overlook the dead-time body-diode loss.** It is comparable to the V·I switching loss at 48 kHz.
5. **Bulk-capacitor ripple current (~40 A RMS at 50 % duty) is a real thermal and reliability hazard.** An off-board low-ESR electrolytic is mandatory.
