## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. That job belongs to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, for example the AT32F421). The H743 runs ArduPilot and drives four bi-directional DShot600 lines.

| Burst current (per motor) | I_pk | 80 A, ≤ 5 s target |

| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 0.90 × (1 + 0.0067 × 85) = **1.41 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases carry the full phase current I_ph.

- **PWM leg:** current flows in the HS switch during D·T and in the LS switch (sync-rect) during (1−D)·T. The total is I²R for the whole period, independent of D.
- **Static leg:** the opposite LS (or HS) switch conducts I²R continuously.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Phase current vs. battery current

$$I_{ph} \approx \frac{I_{batt}}{D}\quad(\text{at } D\to 0.95\text{–}1.0 \text{ in burst, } I_{ph}\approx I_{batt})$$

### 1.4 80 A burst result

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.41\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.0\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.5 Copper and shunt contributions

| Contribution | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper, 2 oz, ~0.25 mΩ effective path per motor | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Shared bus shunt, 0.1 mΩ, at 4×80 A = 320 A | 320² × 0.1 mΩ | **10.2 W** |

The shunt figure is a burst-only design hazard. **Recommendation:** use a 0.05 mΩ shunt or Hall/magnetic sensing (for example a TMCS1100-class device is insufficient at this current; a CT4xx-class sensor is a candidate).

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. At D ≈ 0.95, use I_sw = 80 A and V = 25.2 V.

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·35n·48k = **1.69 W** |
| Coss | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 40n·25.2·48k = 0.048 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50n·25.2·48k = 0.060 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200n·48k = 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 2·40n·10·48k = 0.038 W (dissipated in R_g/driver) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.45 W per motor** |

**Sensitivity:**
- Moving to 24 kHz halves the switching term to about 1.2 W.
- At 96 kHz the term reaches about 4.9 W.
- f_sw = 48 kHz is a reasonable trade-off against motor current ripple on low-inductance (<20 µH phase-phase) motors.

### Total per-motor and board loss (80 A burst, T_j = 110 °C)

$$P_{motor} = 18.0 + 2.45 + 1.6 \approx \mathbf{22.0\ W}$$

$$P_{board} = 4 \times 22.0 + 10.2_{\text{(shunt)}} \approx \mathbf{98\ W}$$
