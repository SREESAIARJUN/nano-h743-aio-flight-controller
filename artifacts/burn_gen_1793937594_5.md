## 0. Architectural Clarifications

Three points affect every calculation that follows:

1. **The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** It is the flight controller. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. This design uses an AT32F421K8U7, one per channel. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC MCU's PWM frequency. The H743 is responsible only for the bidirectional DShot600 link.
2. **The 80 A burst is treated as per-motor, phase/battery-referred, for ≤ 5 s.** On a nano (20×20 / 25.5 mm) footprint this is thermally marginal. Section 3 shows it is time-limited, not steady-state. A sustainable continuous rating is derived in §3.5.
3. **The bus is 6S**, with V_bus,max = 25.2 V. MOSFETs are 40 V class.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation structure

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) rectification. In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM-driven high, one phase is held low, and one phase floats.

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the high-side phase, 1/3 as the low-side phase, and 1/3 floating.
