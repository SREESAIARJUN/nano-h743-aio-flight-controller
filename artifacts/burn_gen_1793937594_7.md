## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Basis

An important architectural point first. In every practical "H743 + 4-in-1" AIO design, **the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors**. The partitioning is:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz max, run at 400 MHz) | ArduPilot, sensor fusion, DShot600 master, bi-dir eRPM capture |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 4 × AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (or 1 MCU per motor in BLHeli_32) | Six-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, DShot slave, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive | 4 × FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver) | High/low-side gate drive with bootstrap |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET (6 per motor) | Inverter bridges |

The 48 kHz switching loss model therefore applies to the ESC MCUs' PWM. The DShot/DMA model applies to the H743.
