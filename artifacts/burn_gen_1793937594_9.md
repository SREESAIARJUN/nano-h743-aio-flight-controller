# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC: Electrothermal Model, DShot600 Bidirectional DMA Plan, and Netlist

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Three points affect everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production AIO board, each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600. This design follows that split, which is what ArduPilot supports.

2. **80 A per channel is a burst rating, not continuous.** The model shows that a nano-class board (30×30 mm) cannot dissipate 4×80 A continuously. Section 3 derives the burst duration limit.

3. **Component values are placeholders.** I use a representative 40 V MOSFET in a 3.3×3.3 mm package. Substitute the datasheet values for your chosen part.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous PWM)

In any commutation sector, current I_ph flows through one high-side FET of the sourcing phase and one low-side FET of the sinking phase. With complementary (synchronous) PWM on the sourcing leg:

| FET | Fraction of electrical period conducting |
|---|---|
| HS, sourcing phase | D/3 |
| LS, same leg (synchronous rectifier) | (1−D)/3 |
| LS, sinking phase | 1/3 |
