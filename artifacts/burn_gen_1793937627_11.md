## 0. Scope and Architectural Assumptions

Before any math, three assumptions need to be stated. Several of them correct the literal wording of the request.

1. **Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7, AM32 firmware), one per channel. ArduPilot has no native FOC or 6-step ESC firmware for H7. Merging FC and ESC onto one MCU would also forfeit ArduPilot's scheduler guarantees.
2. **"80 A burst" means a time-limited rating, not a continuous one.** In a nano-class footprint (≤ 36 × 36 mm board, 30.5 mm mounting), the model below shows:
   - **80 A per channel is thermally sustainable for about 2–4 s.**
   - **The continuous rating is about 30–35 A per channel** with prop-wash cooling.
   
   Any marketing sheet should reflect this.
3. **Switching frequency.** f_sw = 48 kHz is the AM32 PWM frequency, set as a fixed value rather than variable PWM. The bus is 6S (V_bus,max = 25.2 V, nominal 22.2 V).

All component values below are design-class parameters. Before release, substitute datasheet values for the chosen MOSFET and driver. The equations are written symbolically for that reason.
