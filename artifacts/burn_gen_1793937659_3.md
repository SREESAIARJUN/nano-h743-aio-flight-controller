## 0. Architectural Reality Check

Several constraints shape every later section, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no native 6-step BLDC commutation. The board therefore uses the following split:
   - **FC domain:** the STM32H743 running ArduPilot.
   - **ESC domain:** 4× dedicated ESC MCUs. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 are typical choices; BLHeli_32 is end-of-life.
   - **Link:** the two domains talk over on-board bidirectional DShot600 traces.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano form factor is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** Section 4 shows that a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board supports roughly **2 s at 4×80A** and **~20–28A per channel continuous** under prop-wash cooling. Marketing must not print "80A" without a time qualifier.
3. **Total battery current at 4×80A is 320A.** That exceeds any XT60 (60A continuous, ~180A short burst) and most nano-class power leads. Realistically, the per-channel burst is never simultaneous across all four channels at full duty. Section 4 nonetheless models the worst case.
