## 1. Field‑Oriented Control (innermost loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude‑invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform maps (v_d, v_q) back to (v_α, v_β) using the transposed matrix.

### 1.2 Plant model and decoupling

In the rotor (d–q) frame, with electrical speed ω_e:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Feeding the cross‑coupling and back‑EMF terms forward removes them from the loop:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$$

What remains for each axis is a first‑order plant:

$$G_i(s) = \frac{1}{Ls + R}$$
