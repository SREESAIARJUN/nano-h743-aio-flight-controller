# Job #46: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC and Dual-IMU Stabilization Design

## 0. Assumptions

I have no prior context for Job #46, so this design rests on the baseline below. Change the constants in `config.hpp` (§7.1) if your hardware differs. The structure of the design does not depend on these values.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (MT6701 / AS5047P) over SPI |
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, single-precision FPU, 170 MHz) |
| Power stage | 3-phase driver, center-aligned PWM 20 kHz, 3-shunt current sensing |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mirrored about the plate's rotation center, SPI at 24 MHz |
| Bus | 12–16 V |

### Loop rate hierarchy

| Loop | Rate | Feedback | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | Phase shunts plus encoder angle | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| Rate (velocity) | 4 kHz | Fused gyro, projected on joint axis | ω_v ≈ 2π·60 rad/s |
| Attitude (angle) | 1 kHz | Fused quaternion | ω_θ ≈ 2π·12 rad/s |
| IMU sampling | 8 kHz ODR | 2× ICM-42688-P | — |

The bandwidth ratio between nested loops should be at least 4–5. That keeps each inner loop approximately unity-gain from the outer loop's point of view.
