## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Power-invariant amplitude scaling is used, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0.

**Clarke:**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Delay compensation.** The PWM update is applied one period after sampling. Use $\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$ for the inverse Park transform.

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupled PI
Surface PMSM (L_d = L_q = L):
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The control law below combines PI, cross-decoupling, and back-EMF feedforward:
$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)$$
