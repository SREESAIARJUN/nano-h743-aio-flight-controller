## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

I have no prior specification for Job #56, so the design uses the typical brushless-gimbal assumptions below. Every number is a parameter in the code. Change the `MotorParams`, `LoopRates` and IMU configuration to match your actual hardware.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class Cortex-M4F/M7, single-precision FPU |
| Motor | 12N14P gimbal BLDC (GBM2804-class), pole pairs `p = 7`, R ≈ 5.6 Ω, L ≈ 2.0 mH (phase), Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A/MT6701) |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P rigidly mounted on the camera (payload) plate, SPI + DMA, ODR 8 kHz |
| Bus | 12–16 V, 3-phase driver with low-side shunt current sensing |
| Loop rates | Current/FOC 20 kHz · Rate (velocity) 4 kHz · Attitude (angle) 1 kHz |

**Signal flow, per axis**

```
            ┌────────── 1 kHz ─────────┐ ┌──────── 4 kHz ───────────┐ ┌──────── 20 kHz ─────────┐
θ_ref ──►(Σ)─► P/PD angle ─►(+ω_ff)─►(Σ)─► PI rate ─►(+i_ff,cog)─►(Σ)─► PI i_d/i_q ─► SVPWM ─► motor
          ▲                            ▲                              ▲
          │ attitude (Mahony on        │ inertial rate projected      │ Clarke/Park of shunt currents
          │ fused dual-IMU data)       │ onto joint axis (filtered)   │ θ_e from encoder
```

The two loops use different sensors:

* **Stabilization (rate + attitude)** uses the payload IMUs. This gives an inertial reference.
* **Commutation and cogging compensation** use the joint encoder, which gives the rotor position.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Motor model in the rotor (dq) frame

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R\,i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q \\
v_q &= R\,i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m \\
\tau_e &= \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\big[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q\big] \;\approx\; K_t\, i_q, \qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m
\end{aligned}
$$

Definitions:

* Electrical angle: $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}})$
* Electrical speed: $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$
* Gimbal motors are surface-PM, so $L_d \approx L_q = L$.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

Park:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$
