## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Angle-latency compensation: the PWM update is applied about 1.5 sampling periods after the current sample, so use

$$\theta_e^{*} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design by pole-zero cancellation
The PMSM in the dq frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

With feedforward decoupling

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda,$$

each axis reduces to $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$.

Choose the PI controller $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ with:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The PI zero cancels the plant pole, leaving the closed loop $1/(s/\omega_c+1)$. Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$, e.g. $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1000$ rad/s.

### 1.3 Discretization and anti-windup
Backward-Euler integrator with back-calculation:

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad \bar u_k = \operatorname{sat}(u_k)$$
$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(\bar u_k - u_k),\qquad K_{aw}\approx K_i/K_p$$
