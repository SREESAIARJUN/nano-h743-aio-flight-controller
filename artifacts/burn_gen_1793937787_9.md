# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specification for Job #39, so this design uses explicit placeholder parameters. They are collected in one `Config` struct so you can replace them with your hardware's values. The equations hold in general. The numbers are typical for a 3-axis gimbal with GBM-class hollow-shaft motors.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | 12N14P (12 slots, 14 poles, 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic, absolute (MT6701/AS5048 class) |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M33 with FPU (STM32G4 class) |
| Current sensing | 2 low-side shunts. A voltage-mode fallback is provided. |
| Loop rates | Current/FOC 20 kHz, velocity 4 kHz, angle/attitude 1 kHz |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P at 8 kHz ODR on the camera payload, mounted symmetrically about the payload CoG |
