## 0. Architectural Clarification

On a 4-in-1 ESC, the **STM32H743 does not drive the gates directly.** The partition is:

| Domain | Silicon | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 command and eRPM capture |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | 48 kHz PWM, commutation, BEMF sensing |
| Power stage (×4) | 3-phase gate driver + 6 N-FETs | none | Power conversion |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is configured **in ESC firmware**. The H743 only handles the DShot link. Both sides are modeled below.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current distribution in six-step trapezoidal drive
