# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #2): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan and Netlists

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

No prior specification for Job #2 was provided, so the baseline below is stated explicitly. Replace the placeholder values with your selected parts' datasheet values before release.

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 running ArduPilot does not commutate motors. ArduPilot has no in-tree BLDC commutation or FOC. The correct architecture is:

- **H743 (ArduPilot):** sends bidirectional DShot600 commands to 4 ESC MCUs.
- **4× ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class):** e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor. Each drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFET positions.

**Form-factor warning.** 80 A per motor is not thermally credible on a 20×20 mm nano stack. The model below assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm two-board stack (FC on top, ESC below) and treats 80 A strictly as a timed burst. Section 3 quantifies the burst duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design junction temperature of T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.425 = **1.425 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases conduct and the phase current I_ph flows through:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET for duty D, then the low-side FET (sync rectification) for 1−D. The leg is therefore always conducting through exactly one FET.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET, fully on.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\,[D+(1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of throttle duty, which is the useful property of sync rectification. The bus current is I_bus ≈ D·I_ph.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.425\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{18.24\ W\ per\ motor}$$

| Configuration | Per motor | 4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| 1 FET per position | 18.24 W | 72.9 W |
| 2 FETs paralleled per position (R/2) | 9.12 W | 36.5 W |

**Recommendation:** use two FETs in parallel per position (12 FETs per motor, 48 total) if the board area allows. Otherwise use a lower-R_DS(on) part.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

In 6-step operation, only one leg hard-switches per commutation sector.

### 2.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{10-3}{3.2} = 2.19\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_{on} = \frac{12\text{ nC}}{2.19} = 5.5\ \text{ns}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{3}{3.2} = 0.94\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_{off} = 12.8\ \text{ns}$$

Real layouts with 1–2 nH of common-source inductance typically produce 2–3× these times. Validate on the bench with a double-pulse test.

### 2.2 Loss terms per motor

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on+t_off)·f = ½·25.2·80·18.3 ns·48 k | 0.885 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 40 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.048 W |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f (≈E_oss·f) | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | 0.614 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f | 0.058 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈1.64 W** |

### 2.3 Totals at 80 A burst

$$P_{ch} = 18.24 + 1.64 \approx \mathbf{19.9\ W/motor},\qquad P_{ESC} \approx \mathbf{79.5\ W}$$

With paralleled FETs, the total is ≈ 9.1 + 1.9 ≈ 11.0 W per motor, or **≈44 W** for the ESC. Switching loss rises slightly because the parallel pair has twice the capacitance and charge.
