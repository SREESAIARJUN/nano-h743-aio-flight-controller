## 0. Assumptions and Three Architectural Findings

| Parameter | Value |

**Finding 1: The H743 cannot be the ESC controller.**
- Driving 4 motors needs 12 complementary half-bridge pairs with hardware dead time.
- The H743 offers only TIM1 and TIM8 (3 complementary pairs each, so 2 motors), plus HRTIM (5 units × 2 outputs = 10).
- The correct partition is:
  - **H743 = flight controller.** It outputs bidirectional DShot600.
  - **Four ESC MCUs** (STM32G071 or AT32F421, one per motor) generate the 48 kHz PWM.
- The model below follows that partition.

**Finding 2: Switching-node overshoot will destroy 40 V FETs at the "natural" switching speed.**
- This is resolved in §2.3 by deliberately slowing di/dt with gate resistance.

**Finding 3: 80 A on all four motors at once is a ~1-second capability on a 20×20 board, not a rating.**
- Total loss is ≈140 W.
- See §3 for the transient proof and the recommended spec wording.

## 1. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design-point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}(110) = 1.6\,(1+0.0065\cdot85) = 2.48\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Conducting devices in 6-step synchronous mode

In any commutation sector:
- The active high-side carries I for fraction D of each PWM period.
- Its complementary low-side carries I for the remaining (1−D).
- The opposite phase's low-side is fully on and carries I.

So exactly two R_ds paths always carry the phase current I, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond} = 2\cdot 80^2\cdot 2.48\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{31.8\ W/motor}$$

### 1.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = 2\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw}\,V_f\,I = 2\cdot100\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k}\cdot0.8\cdot80 = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
