# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #7): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility

**System partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage. Each motor channel uses its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically 4× AT32F421 or 4× STM32G071. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.

**Baseline parameters.** No MOSFET part number was specified, so the analysis uses the parametric baseline below. Every equation is written so you can substitute real datasheet values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_pk | 80 A, 10 s rated |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET | – | 40 V, PDFN 5×6, R_ds(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V |
| On-resistance tempco | α | 0.0045 /°C |
| Thermal resistance, junction to case | R_θjc | 1.5 K/W |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Gate charge | Q_g | 40 nC |
| Effective output capacitance | C_oss,eff | 1.5 nF |
| Rise / fall time | t_r / t_f | 15 ns / 15 ns (≈2 A gate driver) |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Board | – | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz power layers |

**Feasibility warnings:**
- **Board size.** A true 20×20 mm "nano" cannot carry 80 A/channel, even in bursts. The 5×6 MOSFETs (24 total) and the 2 oz copper planes do not fit. Either the board grows to 30.5×30.5 mm, or the burst rating drops to about 40–45 A.
- **Burst definition.** 80 A burst is per channel. All four channels at 80 A means 320 A and about 8 kW from the battery, which no AIO connector or pad set can carry. Rate the system at roughly 4×55 A continuous-equivalent with 80 A single-channel bursts.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

During any 60° commutation sector, exactly two phase legs carry the full phase current I:
- **PWM on-time (D):** high-side of phase A, then the winding, then low-side of phase B.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through the low-side of phase A (synchronous rectification, so the channel conducts, not the body diode), then the winding, then low-side of phase B.

At all times two channels are in series, so:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) \quad \text{(independent of } D\text{)}$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Per-FET average over one electrical revolution:
- Each high-side FET conducts for 1/3 of the revolution, weighted by D.
- Each low-side FET conducts for 1/3 (as the return leg) plus 1/3 × (1−D) (freewheeling).

With D = 0.5, the per-FET losses are nearly equal, about P_cond,motor / 6 × 2 ÷ 2. For layout, use the simpler figure P_FET ≈ P_cond,motor / 3 per conducting pair.

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

$$R_{ds}(110) = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 85) = 2.07\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.07\text{m} = 26.5\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Parasitic conduction terms (do not ignore at 80 A)

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + 2 oz plane spreading, per phase (≈0.25 mΩ, ×2 in path) | 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| Current shunt (shared, 0.25 mΩ, sees battery current I_bat ≈ D·I) | – | ≈0.4 W at D = 0.5 |
| Battery plane / XT60 pads (shared) | ≈0.3 mΩ | I_bat² R |

### 1.4 Thermal runaway check

Conduction loss rises with temperature: dP/dT_j = P·α/(1+α ΔT). Stability requires:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{\theta,total} < 1$$

With 26.5 W, α = 0.0045, and R_θ ≈ 3 K/W (FET to air), the loop gain is about 0.26. The design is stable, but the self-consistent temperature must be solved by iteration (Section 4.3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg hard-switches. Its low-side partner commutates after the dead time, at near zero voltage. Per motor:

**(a) Hard-switching V·I overlap (high-side)**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 1.45\ \text{W}$$

**(b) Output capacitance**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,eff} V^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.023\ \text{W}$$

**(c) Body-diode reverse recovery (low-side diode recovers when the high-side turns on)**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$
