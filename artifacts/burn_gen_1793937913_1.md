## 0. Architectural Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

The H743 does not commutate the motors. In any practical 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware) driving a 3-phase gate driver. Responsibilities therefore split as follows:

- **H743 (ArduPilot):** generates bidirectional DShot600 frames and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC MCUs:** run 48 kHz complementary PWM and handle commutation.

The 48 kHz switching loss analysis below applies to the ESC power stage, configured in ESC firmware, not in ArduPilot.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,(1+\alpha)^{(T_j-25)}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.006¹⁰⁰ = **1.82 mΩ**. A linear approximation of 1.6× is often quoted; use the exponential form for burst modelling.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM

In any 60° sector, current flows through exactly two FET channels:

- one high-side (PWM'd, alternating with its complementary low-side), and
- one low-side held ON.

Because the complementary FET carries current during the PWM off-time, the conducting path always contains two R_DS elements, independent of D. Ignore the dead-time diode intervals here; they are handled in §2.
