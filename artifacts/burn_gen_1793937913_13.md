# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

**Architecture clarification.** On a 4-in-1 AIO board, the STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel (or a shared quad ESC) has its own commutation MCU, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry.

**80 A on a nano board.** Treat this as a short-burst figure. Section 3 shows it is thermally capacity-limited to seconds. The board can only meet it with paralleled FETs, heavy copper, and airflow.
