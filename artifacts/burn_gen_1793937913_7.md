## 0. Feasibility Summary

**A 20×20 mm nano board cannot sustain 80A on four channels at once.** The analysis below shows:

| Scenario | Total loss | Time to T_j = 125 °C (from 50 °C board) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 motor at 80A, 3 at 20A | ≈ 30 W | **≈ 5 s** |
| 4 motors at 80A | ≈ 100 W | **≈ 1.5–2 s** |
| Continuous, prop-wash cooled | — | ≈ 25–30 A/ch sustainable |

**Recommendations:**
- Specify the product as **"80A burst, ≤ 2 s, per channel"** with a firmware current limit (AM32 current-limit plus ArduPilot `BATT_*` and `MOT_*` limits).
- Use a 30.5×30.5 mm board if a 10 s burst is a hard requirement.

**Architecture assumption:**
- The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**.
- Each ESC channel runs its own motor-control MCU (AT32F421 running AM32).
- Commutation, BEMF sensing and closed-loop timing do not belong on the H7 when it is also running ArduPilot's EKF3 at 400 Hz+.
- The FC↔ESC interface is bi-directional DShot600.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths
- In six-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant.
- With complementary PWM, the current always flows through exactly one FET in each of the two active legs:
  - **ON state:** HS(A) → motor → LS(B)
  - **OFF state:** LS(A) → motor → LS(B)
- So **two R_DS(on) are always in series** with the phase current.

The conduction loss per channel is:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction
The current ripple in the active phase pair is:

$$\Delta i = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta i^2}{12}$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH, D = 0.6, V_bemf = 14 V gives Δi ≈ 4.7 A. The correction to I_rms² is about 0.03%, so it is negligible at 80A.

### 2.3 At 80A
- Evaluated at T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.383 = **1.66 mΩ**
- P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.66 mΩ = **21.2 W per channel**
- This is about **10.6 W per conducting FET**, which is the critical number for the thermal analysis.

### 2.4 Additional conduction elements
| Element | Formula | At 80A |
|---|---|---|
| Copper path, 2 oz, ≈ 0.25 mΩ total | I²R_cu | 1.6 W |
| Current shunt, 0.25 mΩ (if per-channel) | I²R_sh | 1.6 W |
| Battery-side current (bulk-cap path) | I_bat = D·I_ph | — |

### 2.5 Temperature feedback (self-consistent solution)
Losses rise with temperature, and temperature rises with losses. With an effective thermal resistance R_θ from junction to ambient, the steady-state solution is:

$$T_j - T_a = \frac{R_\theta\,P_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_a-25)]}{1-\alpha\,R_\theta\,P_{25}}$$

The **thermal runaway criterion** is α·R_θ·P_25 ≥ 1.

- For one FET: P_25 ≈ 7.7 W and α = 0.0045.
- Runaway therefore occurs at R_θ ≥ 29 K/W.
- The nano board with airflow sits near **15–20 K/W**, so it is stable, but the gain term reaches 1.5–2.4×. Temperature feedback is a first-order effect and must not be ignored.
