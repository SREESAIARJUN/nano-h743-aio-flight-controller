## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Finding

Three points need to be settled before the math.

**1. Architectural boundary.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not switch MOSFETs. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AT32F421-class running AM32, or a BLHeli_32 target), which generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 and the ESCs talk over bidirectional DShot600. The loss model therefore applies to the ESC power stage, and the DMA model applies to the H743.
