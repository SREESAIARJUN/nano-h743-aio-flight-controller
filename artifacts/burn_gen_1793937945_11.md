# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Design Model for Job #66

**Scope:** 80 A burst thermal model, 48 kHz switching losses, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, reference netlist.

---

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. That would require 24 gate signals, 12 comparator or ADC channels for back-EMF, and hard real-time 48 kHz PWM, all competing with the EKF.

The design is an **AIO stack**:

- **H743**: runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **4 × AT32F421K8U7** (or STM32G071): run AM32 at 48 kHz complementary PWM, one MCU per ESC channel.
- **4 × FD6288Q** 3-phase gate drivers (250 V, about 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink, bootstrap HS).
- **24 × N-MOSFETs**, 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3.
