## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

Two points in the request need correcting before the math is meaningful.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a DShot system the H743 runs ArduPilot and *emits* DShot. Each motor's commutation is done by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 4-in-1 board therefore carries four ESC MCUs (or one multi-motor MCU), and the H743 belongs on a separate stacked FC board. Placing a 400 MHz H743 on the same copper as about 80 W of FET dissipation would push it past its 85 °C (industrial) or 125 °C (automotive) ambient rating within seconds during a burst.
2. **"Nano" and 80 A burst are in tension.** As Section 3 shows, a 20×20 mm board cannot survive this. The thermal analysis assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern on a roughly 36×36 mm, 8-layer board. A 20×20 mm design should be derated to about 35–45 A burst.
