# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano) Design Package, Job #89

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model for 80 A burst, switching losses at 48 kHz, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

> **Engineering note before the math:** On a 20×20 mm nano board, 80 A per motor can only be a short **transient burst**. The model below shows it is limited to about **1.5 s** before the junction exceeds 125 °C. Sustained current on this form factor lands around 15–20 A per motor. The numbers here are derived so you can re-run them with your actual MOSFET datasheet.
