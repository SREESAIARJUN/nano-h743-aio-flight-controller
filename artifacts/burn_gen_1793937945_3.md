## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Verdict

**The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production "4-in-1 ESC + FC" design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU, and the 48 kHz figure applies to those MCUs:

```
                 ┌──────────── FC domain (3V3) ────────────┐
 IMU/Baro/OSD ── │ STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz (ArduPilot)      │
                 │ TIM1 CH1..CH4 → bidirectional DShot600   │
                 └──────┬──────┬──────┬──────┬──────────────┘
                        │M1    │M2    │M3    │M4   (single-wire, bidir)
                 ┌──────▼──────▼──────▼──────▼──────────────┐
                 │ 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32)  │ ← 48 kHz PWM generated here
                 │ 4× 3-phase gate driver                   │
                 │ 24× N-MOSFET (40 V)                      │
                 └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Design verdict on "nano" plus 80A:** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80A. It lacks the copper cross-section, the MOSFET footprint area, and the thermal mass. The math below shows that even a 30.5×30.5 mm (36×36 mm outline) board supports 80A per channel only as a seconds-scale burst. I have therefore modeled:

- **Form factor:** 36×36 mm, 30.5 mm mounting, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 3 oz inner power planes.
- **Rating:** 80A per channel burst (≤ 5 s), 50A per channel continuous with airflow.
- **Supply:** 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal.

All component parameters below are representative values. Substitute the datasheet numbers of your selected parts into the equations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Paths

**Trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with synchronous rectification.** At any instant two phases are active (for example, A high and B low):

| PWM state | Conducting devices | Count in series |
|---|---|---|
| ON (duty D) | Q_AH + Q_BL | 2 |
| OFF (1−D, sync rect.) | Q_AL + Q_BL | 2 |

The phase current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly two R_DS(on) in series:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{Cu}$$

**FOC/sinusoidal (AM32 sine mode).** The result has the same form, with $I_{rms} = I_{pk}/\sqrt{2}$ per phase, summed over three legs:

$$P_{cond} = 3\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}$$

For R_DS,25 = 1.2 mΩ (representative 40 V, 5×6 PDFN, V_GS = 10 V):

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.65 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.80 mΩ |

### 1.3 Phase Current Versus Battery Current

$$I_{batt} = D \cdot I_{ph} \quad\text{(ignoring ripple)}$$

At part throttle the MOSFETs carry far more current than the battery meter shows. An "80A burst" must be specified as phase current.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80A Phase Current, T_j = 125 °C

The copper path is R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ (planes, vias, and pads, per channel round trip).

$$P_{cond} = 2(80^2)(1.8\,\mathrm{m\Omega}) + 80^2(0.25\,\mathrm{m\Omega}) = 23.04 + 1.60 = \mathbf{24.6\ W/ch}$$
