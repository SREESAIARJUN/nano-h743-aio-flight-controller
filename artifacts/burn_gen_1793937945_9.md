# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage Thermal Model, Switching Losses, and Bidirectional DShot DMA Architecture

**Document:** Job #60 (Architecture Rev A)

**Status:** Engineering model. Values marked *[A]* are assumptions that must be replaced with datasheet values for the selected parts before layout freeze.

---

## 0. Architectural Ground Truth and Feasibility Flag

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs**. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver plus 6 FETs. The 48 kHz PWM is the ESC MCU's switching frequency, not an H743 timer.
2. **A sustained 80 A per channel is not thermally achievable on a 20×20 mm nano board.** The model below shows that 80 A is a **sub-second to ~3 s burst rating**. Continuous capability is roughly 25 A per channel. The datasheet must state the burst duration and starting temperature, or the "80 A" claim is not defensible.
3. **Package constraint.** Twelve FET positions per two channels do not fit in DFN5×6 on a nano board (24 × ~30 mm² ≈ 720 mm² > 400 mm² of board area). The model therefore assumes **DFN3.3×3.3, one FET per switch position**.
