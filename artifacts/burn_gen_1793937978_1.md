## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (Innermost)

### 1.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform, assuming $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform at electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant (Surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q\\
v_q &= R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m\\
\tau_e &= \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
\end{aligned}
$$

### 1.3 PI Controller with Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

The current PI uses pole-zero cancellation. Setting $K_i/K_p = R/L$ cancels the electrical pole, which gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_{c}$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

With $f_{pwm}=20$ kHz, choose $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot1500$ rad/s. This gives $K_p = 20.7$ V/A and $K_i = 52\,800$ V/(A·s).

Control law, including decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d^* &= \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q\\
v_q^* &= \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
\end{aligned}
$$

Set $i_d^* = 0$; field weakening is not needed for a gimbal.

### 1.4 Voltage Limiting and SVPWM

The linear SVPWM region is a circle of radius $V_{max} = V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. Limiting is d-axis prioritized:

$$
v_d^* \leftarrow \text{sat}(v_d^*, \pm V_{max}),\qquad
v_q^* \leftarrow \text{sat}\!\left(v_q^*, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}}\right)
$$

SVPWM is implemented by min-max (common-mode) injection, which is mathematically identical to sector-based SVPWM:

$$
v_{abc} = T_{Clarke}^{-1}\, v_{\alpha\beta},\quad
v_{cm} = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\quad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_{cm}}{V_{dc}}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Velocity / Angle Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}
$$

Because $\omega_c$ is much larger than $\omega_v$, the current loop is treated as unity gain, $i_q \approx i_q^*$.

### 2.2 Velocity PI (Symmetric-Optimum Placement)

The open loop is $L(s) = \dfrac{K_t}{Js}\cdot K_{p,v}\dfrac{s+\omega_i}{s}$. Placing crossover at $\omega_v$ with integrator zero $\omega_i=\omega_v/a$ (where $a=4$ gives about 62° phase margin before delays):

$$
K_{p,v} = \frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_{i,v} = K_{p,v}\frac{\omega_v}{a}
$$

Constraints:
- $\omega_v \le \omega_c/5$
- $\omega_v$ must stay below the first structural resonance of the gimbal arm (typically 150–400 Hz), with margin.

Choose $\omega_v = 2\pi\cdot 80$ rad/s. This gives $K_{p,v}=2.01$ A·s/rad and $K_{i,v}=252$ A/rad.
