# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Fusion

I don't have the specifics of Job #46, so the design is parameterized. It assumes:

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Hollow-shaft gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A/MT6701 class), one per joint |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class with FPU, center-aligned PWM at 20–40 kHz |
| Loop rates | Current loop 20 kHz, velocity loop 4 kHz, attitude/angle loop 1 kHz, IMU ODR 8 kHz |
| IMUs | Two ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted point-symmetric (r_B = −r_A) with B rotated 180° about z |
| Axis order | Base → Yaw → Roll → Pitch → Camera |

Change the constants in `GimbalConfig` to match your hardware. Flagged datasheet values must be verified before flight.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle with delay compensation

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) + \tfrac{3}{2}\,T_s\,\omega_e,\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m$$

The 1.5·Ts term compensates one sample of computation delay plus half a sample of PWM zero-order-hold delay.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$
