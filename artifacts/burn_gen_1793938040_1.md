## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

An STM32H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors directly at 48 kHz with sensorless BEMF zero-crossing detection. It lacks enough advanced-timer complementary channel sets and comparator/ADC resources for 12 half-bridges. The production-proven partitioning is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | STM32G071GBU6 or AT32F421G8U7 (QFN28) | AM32 / BLHeli_32-class firmware, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, 250 V / 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink) | One per ESC |
| MOSFETs (×24) | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 class | 6 per ESC |

**Design assumptions (change these and the math scales):**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per ESC | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) @25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_25 | 1.5 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient (T_j = 125 °C) | k_T | 1.5 |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous complementary PWM, two phases conduct during each 60° electrical sector:

- **Phase X (PWM phase):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Phase Y (return phase):** the low-side FET is fully on.
- **Phase Z:** floating, used for BEMF sensing.

### 1.2 Per-sector conduction loss

$$P_{cond,sector} = I^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}$$

This loss is **independent of duty cycle** when synchronous rectification is used.

$$R_{hot} = k_T \cdot R_{25} = 1.5 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 2.25\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.25\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{28.8\ W\ per\ ESC}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET averaging

At burst, the electrical frequency is roughly 3.5 kHz (14-pole motor at 30,000 rpm, so f_e = 7 × 500 Hz). The FET die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms for the die, more for the package) is much longer than one commutation period (286 µs). Each FET therefore sees its average loss. The six FETs share the load equally over a full electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{28.8}{6} = 4.8\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect conduction

Assume an effective path resistance of 0.5 mΩ per ESC (pads, vias, 2 oz pours, motor solder pads):

$$P_{Cu,ESC} = 80^2 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

The shared battery pads and bus add 0.3 mΩ for the total 320 A burst current (4 × 80 A):

$$P_{bus} = 320^2 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = 30.7\ \text{W}$$

This is a major and often-ignored term. It drives the requirement for a 2 oz outer copper plus 4 inner power-plane stack (see §6).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM phase's high-side and low-side pair hard-switches. The model below is per ESC.

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard switching)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = 1.45\ \text{W}$$

**Timing check.** The FD6288Q drives about 1.5 A into a typical Q_gd of about 10 nC, giving t ≈ 7 ns. Adding gate resistance R_g = 4.7 Ω and the Miller plateau brings this to a realistic 15 ns per edge.

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = 0.31\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ kHz} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss,eff} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\text{ nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{ kHz} = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g)

$$P_{gate} = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{ nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{ kHz} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Switching subtotal

| Term | W per ESC |
|---|---|
| V-I overlap | 1.45 |
| Dead time | 0.31 |
| Q_rr | 0.04 |
| C_oss | 0.05
