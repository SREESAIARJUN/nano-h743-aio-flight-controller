## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset (electrical angle).

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant (SPM motor, L_d ≈ L_q = L)
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.3 Decoupled PI controller (pole-zero cancellation)
The controller is
$$v_{d}^* = \underbrace{K_p e_d + K_i\!\int e_d}_{PI} - \hat\omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = PI(e_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

Set the PI zero at the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:
$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

Discretization delay (about 1.5 T_s) limits the achievable bandwidth to ω_c ≲ 1/(10 T_s).

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and anti-windup
The linear SVPWM limit is |v| ≤ V_bus/√3.
- Give priority to v_d, and limit v_q to $\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.
- Use back-calculation anti-windup: $\dot I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})$, with K_aw = 1/K_p·K_i (i.e. T_t = T_i).
