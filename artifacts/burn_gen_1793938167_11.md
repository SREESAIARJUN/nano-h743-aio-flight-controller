**Architecture assumption.** This is a standard stacked design:
- The H743 flight controller sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware).
- Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q-class) and 6 MOSFETs.
- Commutation is 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous (complementary) rectification.

The 48 kHz PWM belongs to the ESC MCU. The H743 never generates motor PWM directly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 135 °C: R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.0045 × 110) = **1.79 mΩ**. Use 1.8 mΩ.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step commutation with sync rectification

- During PWM on-time: the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.
- During PWM off-time: the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectifier) and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.

So **exactly two FETs carry I_ph at all times**, independent of duty D.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET's average share depends on duty. Over one electrical cycle:

- **High-side FET (phase x):** conducts during 120° × D, so P_HS = I²R × D/3.
- **Low-side FET (phase x):** conducts during 120° as the return path plus 120° × (1−D) as the sync rectifier, so P_LS = I²R × (2−D)/3.

| Duty D | P_HS per FET | P_LS per FET | Hottest FET |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 1.92 W | 5.76 W | LS |
| 0.9 | 3.46 W | 4.22 W | LS |
| 1.0 | 3.84 W | 3.84 W | Equal |

**Design rule:** at partial throttle with high current (e.g., prop stall or hard punch-out at mid-throttle), the **low-side FETs are the thermal limit**. Place them on the best-cooled copper and give them the most thermal vias.

### 1.4 Battery-side and copper losses

- Battery current per channel: I_bat = D × I_ph = 72 A at D = 0.9.
