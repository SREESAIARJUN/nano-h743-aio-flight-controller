## 1. Power Stage Topology (per motor)

- **Bridge:** 3-phase, 6 N-MOSFETs, driven with trapezoidal 6-step commutation.
- **PWM:** complementary (synchronous rectification), which is the AM32 default. Switching frequency f_sw = 48 kHz.
- **Gate driver:** 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class), with bootstrap high-side supply.

**Reference MOSFET** (40 V, 3.3×3.3 PQFN class, typical for nano boards):

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance at 25 °C | R_ds(on),25 | 1.6 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient (normalized) | k(T_j) | 1 + 0.0055·(T_j − 25) |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 45 nC @ 10 V |
| Gate-drain charge | Q_gd | 10 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 35 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC @ 20 V |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_SD | 0.8 V |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |

At T_j = 125 °C, k = 1.55, so **R_ds(hot) = 2.48 mΩ**.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In each 60° electrical sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low. The current path then alternates as follows:

- **ON interval** (duty D): current flows through HS_X, the motor, and LS_Y.
- **OFF interval** (1 − D): current flows through LS_X (synchronous rectification), the motor, and LS_Y.

**Two FETs are always in series with the phase current, independent of D.**

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

The RMS current includes PWM ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example ripple with L_ph = 15 µH (low-kV 2–3″ motor), V_bus = 25.2 V, D = 0.5, E ≈ 12.6 V:

ΔI_pp = (12.6 × 0.5)/(15e-6 × 48e3) ≈ **8.75 A**. Adding ΔI²/12 = 6.4 A² to 6400 A² is negligible (+0.1%).

### 2.2 Per-FET distribution

Each FET is active in one-third of the electrical cycle:

- **Low-side "anchor" FET** (LS_Y): conducts the full sector, so its share is (1/3)·I²R.
- **High-side PWM FET**: conducts D·(1/3)·I²R.
- **Low-side synchronous rectification:** carries the remaining (1−D)·(1/3)·I²R, and falls on the same physical LS FET in a different sector.
