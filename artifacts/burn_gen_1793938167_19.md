## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step commutation

In any 60° sector, two phases conduct and one floats. Take sector A+ / B−:

- **Phase A high-side (HS):** on for duty D.
- **Phase A low-side (LS):** on for (1−D), synchronously rectifying the freewheel current.
- **Phase B LS:** on for 100% of the sector.

So at every instant, exactly **two channels conduct** the full motor current I. The total bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

With peak-to-peak inductor ripple ΔI and ripple ratio r = ΔI/I:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right)$$

For a typical low-inductance racing motor, r ≈ 0.3 at 48 kHz. That gives a factor of 1.0075, which is negligible here.

The ripple ratio follows from:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Note the 2L term: two phases are in series.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A (per ESC channel)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction @ 125 °C | 2 × 80² × 1.8 mΩ | **23.04 W** |
| FET conduction @ 25 °C | 2 × 80² × 1.2 mΩ | 15.36 W |
| Copper path | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | **3.20 W** |
| Ripple correction | × 1.0075 | +0.17 W |
| **Total conduction (hot)** | | **≈ 26.4 W** |

### 1.5 Per-device distribution

Over a full electrical cycle, each FET's average loss is:

- Low-side: (1/3)·I²R (1/6 of the cycle as the full-sector LS, plus the (1−D) freewheel share during its own PWM sector)
- High-side: (D/6)·I²R

At 80 A, D is near 1, so the **worst device dissipates ≈ 3.8–4.2 W averaged.**

During a single sector it sees **I²R = 11.5 W**. At electrical frequencies above ~200 Hz, the die thermal time constant (~ms) averages this out. At low eRPM under high current (stall or a hard punch-out from idle), it does not, and the instantaneous junction temperature rises sharply. **Design for the stall case** with the ESC's current limiting enabled.

### 1.6 Electrothermal stability

Thermal runaway is avoided only when:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\, R_{\theta,JA,eff} < 1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 2 I^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha\, R_{\theta} < 1$$

At 80 A:

$$2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 1.2\text{m}\Omega \cdot 0.005 = 0.077\ \text{W/K}$$

This requires R_θ < 13 K/W. That holds at the device level (5.5 K/W junction-to-board). It **fails at the board-to-ambient level** (§3), which is exactly why 80 A is a burst rating and not a continuous one.
