> **Engineering honesty flag:** "80 A burst per channel" on a 25.5 mm board is physically achievable only for seconds, and simultaneous 4 × 80 A (≈ 320 A battery at full duty) exceeds what any nano-class connector, battery lead, or LiPo can deliver.
