## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction losses: trapezoidal 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, two phases conduct.

- **PWM phase:** high-side (HS) on for duty D, low-side (LS) on for 1 − D (complementary).
- **Return phase:** LS on for 100 % of the sector.

Temperature-dependent resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

Per-sector conduction loss, which is independent of D because HS and LS alternate:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At I = 80 A and T_j = 100 °C, R_DS = 1.0 × 1.3375 = 1.34 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.34\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.1\ W}$$

Distribution per FET (time-averaged over one electrical revolution): each phase is in the PWM role 1/3 of the time, the return role 1/3, and floating 1/3.

- The worst single device is the return-path LS while it is active: 6400 × 1.34 m = **8.6 W instantaneous**.
- Its average over the revolution is ≈ 2.9 W plus its PWM-role share.
- The six FETs share the 17.1 W roughly equally, about 2.85 W each on average.

### 2.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only one half-bridge switches at a time (the PWM phase).

**(a) V–I overlap, hard-switched:**
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}$$

**(b) Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery.** The LS body diode recovers when the HS turns on:
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$
Additional Q_rr-induced loss in the HS is ≈ Q_rr·V·f again, so budget **0.1 W** total.

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period):
$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{n}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.614\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive.** Dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g, not the FET:
$$P_{g} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\times50\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.3 Passive path losses

$$P_{shunt} = I^2 R_{sh} = 6400\times0.5\text{m} = \mathbf{3.2\ W},\qquad P_{Cu} = 6400\times0.4\text{m} = \mathbf{2.56\ W}$$
