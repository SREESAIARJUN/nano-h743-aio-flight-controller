> **Feasibility verdict.** 80 A per channel on a 20×20 board is a **sub-second burst rating only**. The math below shows about 100 W of total dissipation against roughly 2 J/K of board thermal mass. That is about +50 K/s adiabatic rise, so continuous ratings on this footprint will land around 25–35 A per channel. If 80 A must be sustained for more than about 1 s, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm form factor with paralleled FETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in synchronous 6-step drive

During any 60° commutation sector, two phase legs are active:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D. The complementary low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D), with synchronous rectification.
- **Return leg:** the LS conducts 100% of the sector.

With synchronous rectification and an ideal flat-top phase current I, **exactly two MOSFET channels carry I at every instant, independent of duty**. Dead time is handled separately in §2.4.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.74 mΩ | **22.3 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.92 mΩ | 24.6 W |
| 150 °C | 2.10 mΩ | 26.9 W |

This creates **electro-thermal positive feedback**. The self-consistent junction temperature is

$$T_j = T_a + R_{th}\,P_0\big[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\big] \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{th}P_0(1-25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha R_{th} P_0}$$

where P₀ is the loss at 25 °C. Thermal runaway occurs when **α·R_th·P₀ ≥ 1**. For P₀ = 61.6 W (4 channels, all FETs, 25 °C) and α = 0.006, this gives R_th,crit = 2.7 K/W. A 25×25 mm board in prop-wash typically sits at 6–10 K/W. **Steady-state 80 A on all four channels is therefore thermally unstable** and is only permissible as a time-bounded burst (§4).

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Averaged over one electrical revolution, each leg is the PWM leg for 1/3 of the time, the return leg for 1/3, and floating for 1/3:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R,\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2R + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\,I^2R$$

The instantaneous dissipation while conducting is I²R = 6400 × 1.74 mΩ = **11.1 W per FET**.
