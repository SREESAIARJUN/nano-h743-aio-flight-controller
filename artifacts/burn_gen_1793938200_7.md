# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Flight Controller: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #15 specification, so the design rests on the stated assumptions below. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute real datasheet values.

**Architecture.** An STM32H743 cannot directly commutate 4 BLDC motors in a sensible ArduPilot design. The standard topology is:
- The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.
- Each ESC MCU (AT32F421 running AM32) drives a 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q) and 6 N-MOSFETs.
- 48 kHz is the ESC PWM frequency.
