## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### Partitioning

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. It does not commutate the motors.

Each motor channel (or one MCU for all four, as in some AM32 4-in-1 designs) has its own ESC MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the **48 kHz** complementary PWM and handles commutation. The 48 kHz figure is an ESC firmware setting. ArduPilot never sees it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Topology During One 60° Sector

In a 6-step drive, two phases conduct and one floats:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET is on for fraction D. The complementary low-side (LS) FET carries freewheel current for (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Static leg:** the LS FET is on for the entire sector.

The phase current I_ph flows continuously, assuming ripple is small relative to I_ph (checked in 1.4).

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result does not depend on duty cycle, because synchronous rectification gives the freewheel path the same resistance as the active path. Add the copper path loss:

$$P_{Cu} = I_{ph}^2\,(2R_{trace} + R_{pad,motor})$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence and Self-Consistent Tj

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P(T_j)\,Z_{th}$$

**Stability condition (no thermal runaway):**

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}Z_{th} = 2I^2R_{DS,25}\,\alpha\,Z_{th} < 1$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

Assume T_j ≈ 125 °C, which gives R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

- P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.74 mΩ = **22.3 W per channel**
- Copper (assume 0.15 mΩ of total path): 6400 × 0.15 mΩ = **0.96 W**

Per-FET average, since each of the 6 FETs takes about 1/6 of the conduction duty over an electrical cycle:

- ≈ 22.3 / 6 = **3.7 W per FET**

The electrical period at burst rpm is in the tens to hundreds of µs, which is much shorter than the die thermal time constant of about 1 ms. Averaging is therefore valid.

Runaway check per FET: 2·I²·R₂₅·α/6 × Z_th(j-board) = 6400 × 1.2e-3 × 0.0045 / 3 × 2 K/W ≈ 0.023. This is well below 1, so the design is **stable**.
