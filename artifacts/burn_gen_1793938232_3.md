# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

**Scope note:** I have no prior project files for Job #52, so the parameters below are stated assumptions. Swap in your BOM values. The equations are structured for that, and every number traces back to a listed input.

## 0. Executive Findings

1. **An 80 A per-channel burst is thermally transient-only on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) footprint.** With all four channels at 80 A, the board dissipates about 120 W. Adiabatic headroom is roughly **3–4 s** before FET junctions reach a 125 °C derated limit. Continuous ratings for this form factor realistically sit at **35–45 A per channel**.
2. **Conduction loss, not switching loss, dominates at 80 A.** At 6S and 48 kHz, switching adds about 10% to total FET loss.
3. **The PCB copper is a first-order loss term.** At 80 A, about 1 mΩ of phase and bus copper costs as much as the FETs. It must be modeled, not ignored.
4. **Architectural clarification:** the H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM. In a 4-in-1 stack, the ESC runs its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421/STM32G071 with AM32 or BLHeli_32), which owns the 48 kHz switching. The H743 (ArduPilot) only emits DShot600 frames and captures the bi-directional eRPM telemetry. These are two independent timing domains.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation structure
In 6-step trapezoidal drive, at any instant one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) path carry the phase current. The ESC uses complementary PWM with synchronous rectification, so the off-time current freewheels through the LS FET channel rather than its body diode.

Over one PWM period with duty D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,\big[\underbrace{D}_{HS} + \underbrace{(1-D)}_{LS\ sync} + \underbrace{1}_{return\ LS}\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

This simplifies because the commutating leg alternates HS/LS while the return leg conducts continuously. The result is **independent of D**, which is useful because worst case doesn't depend on throttle mapping.

### 2.2 Temperature-corrected R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K} \Rightarrow R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.8\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 2.3 Numbers (per channel, 80 A, hot)

$$
P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = 23.0\ \text{W}
$$

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 1.0\times10^{-3} = 6.4\ \text{W}
$$

Copper tempco is +0.393%/K. At a 100 °C copper temperature, P_Cu ≈ 8.3 W.
