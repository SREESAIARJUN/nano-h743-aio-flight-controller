### Design flags (read before committing to layout)

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight stack, not ESC firmware. The architecture is H743 (ArduPilot), sending bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Do not try to run 24 gates from the H743.
2. **80 A × 4 on a "nano" board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal model in Section 3 shows roughly **3 s** of simultaneous all-motor burst from a warm start before the board reaches its limit. A single motor bursting while the others cruise is far more forgiving. Publish the rating honestly, for example "80 A burst per channel, 1 channel at a time, 10 s; 4-channel simultaneous 3 s".
3. **About 300 A of pack current** requires XT90-class wiring and solder pads, and the battery pad copper must be treated as a heat source in its own right (Section 2.4).

## 1. Electrical Operating Model

For a 6-step drive with synchronous rectification, during each 60° commutation sector:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET (Q_AH) conducts for duty D. The low-side FET (Q_AL) conducts for 1−D, minus dead time.
- **Leg B (fixed leg):** the low-side FET (Q_BL) conducts 100% of the sector.
- **Leg C** floats and is used for back-EMF sensing.

So the motor current *I* always passes through exactly **two conducting channels**, plus body-diode intervals during dead time.

DC link current: I_DC ≈ D · I_ph
