# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Package

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Finding

### 0.1 Architecture

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly in any production ArduPilot design. The validated architecture is:

| Block | Device (reference) | Firmware | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, emits bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (QFN32) | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q-class 3-phase bootstrap driver | n/a | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| MOSFETs ×24 | 40 V, ~1.0 mΩ, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | n/a | Power bridges |

All 48 kHz switching math applies to the ESC MCUs. All DShot/DMA math applies to the H743.

### 0.2 Feasibility Finding

**80 A on all four channels simultaneously is a seconds-scale burst on a nano-class board, not a sustained rating.** Section 3 derives this. The numbers below are reference calculations built on stated assumptions. Replace them with your selected MOSFET's datasheet values and validate on hardware with a thermocouple or IR camera.
