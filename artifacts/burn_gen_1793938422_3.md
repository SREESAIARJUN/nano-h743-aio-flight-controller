## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Angles

The encoder gives the mechanical angle $\theta_m$. The electrical angle is

$$
\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\big(p\,\theta_m - \theta_{e,0}\big)
$$

$\theta_{e,0}$ is found at calibration: apply $V_d$ at $\theta_e = 0$, let the rotor settle, and record $p\,\theta_m$.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform

This is the amplitude-invariant form, assuming $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park Transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Motor Model (dq frame, surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The electromagnetic torque is

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with Decoupling

After decoupling, the plant becomes $G_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R)$. Pole–zero cancellation for a closed-loop bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$ gives:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
\qquad(\omega_{ci}\le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10)
$$

The control law is:

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

The reference is $i_d^* = 0$ (no field weakening is needed for a gimbal).

**Voltage limit.** The voltage vector is clamped to the linear SVPWM circle, with d-axis priority:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95
$$

$$
v_d \leftarrow \operatorname{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad
v_q \leftarrow \operatorname{clamp}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)
$$

**Discretization and anti-windup.** Use forward Euler with back-calculation anti-windup:

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_k^{sat} - u_k),\qquad
K_{aw} \approx \frac{1}{K_p}\cdot\frac{K_i T_s}{1}
$$
