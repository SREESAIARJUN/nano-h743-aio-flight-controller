**Architecture assumption.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example, AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware), and the H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600. The H743 does not commutate motors directly. This is the only architecture ArduPilot supports for bi-directional DShot.

**Feasibility warning.** 80 A per motor in a "nano" (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) 4-in-1 is beyond what the copper and thermal mass can sustain for more than a couple of seconds, as Section 3 quantifies. Treat 80 A as a short-burst rating, and enforce it in firmware with current limiting and thermal derating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha = \frac{k_T - 1}{100} = 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.55 = **1.86 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct. Phase A's high side is PWM'd with duty D, and phase B's low side is held on.

- **ON interval (D):** current flows through HS_A and LS_B.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_A (synchronous rectification) and LS_B.

Current therefore always flows through exactly two FETs, so the bridge conduction loss is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The loss splits across FETs within one commutation as follows:

| FET | Conduction fraction | Loss while sector active |
|---|---|---|
| HS (PWM'd) | D | D·I²·R |
| LS (complementary, same leg) | 1−D | (1−D)·I²·R |
| LS (opposite leg, static) | 1 | I²·R |

Each FET is active for 120° of the electrical cycle, so averaged over a cycle:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = \frac{I^2 R}{3}$$

**At 80 A and T_j = 125 °C:**

- P_cond,bridge = 2 × 6400 × 1.86 mΩ = **23.8 W per motor**
- Peak instantaneous FET loss (static LS) = I²R = **11.9 W**
- Average per FET ≈ **3.97 W**

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses (do not ignore at 80 A)

- **Phase path.** Assume a 15 mm path, 2 mm wide, on 2 oz copper (70 µm) over 2 parallel layers:
  - R_Cu = ρL/(w·t·n) = 1.72e-8 × 0.015 / (0.002 × 70e-6 × 2) ≈ 0.92 mΩ
  - Loss across two current paths ≈ 2 × 6400 × 0.92e-3 ≈ **11.8 W per motor**
  - This is comparable to the FET loss. Use 6 layers of 2 oz for the power planes, and tie phase pads to all layers with via arrays.
- **Shunt.** 0.25 mΩ in the DC link gives 6400 × 0.25e-3 = **1.6 W** (shared bus, scaled by duty).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches; one HS/LS pair per motor at any instant. Switching occurs at V_bus with load current I.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched turn-on and turn-off)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

Switching times derived from gate current:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g},\qquad I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int} + R_{drv}}$$
