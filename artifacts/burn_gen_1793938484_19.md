## 0. Architecture and Feasibility Issues

The brief contains three problems that would cause a design review failure if left unstated.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC runs one commutation MCU per motor, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design uses an AT32F421K8U7 running AM32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESCs only over bidirectional DShot600.
