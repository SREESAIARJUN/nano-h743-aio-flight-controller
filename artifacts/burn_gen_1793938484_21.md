## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Summary

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 commands to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

| Domain | Owner | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Attitude/rate loop, DShot TX, eRPM RX | STM32H743 @ 480 MHz | 1–2 kHz loop |
| Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead-time | 4× AT32F421 (AM32) | 48 kHz |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V-class) | — |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN3.3×3.3 | — |

**Feasibility verdict.** The thermal model below shows the following:

- **80 A on one channel** is feasible as a **burst of 1–2 s** in a 30.5×30.5 mm "nano-class" stack.
- **80 A on all four channels at once** is limited by heat capacity to roughly **1 s**. It **cannot** be a continuous rating.
- The honest continuous rating is about **35–40 A per channel** with propwash airflow.

The firmware derating scheme in §5.5 is mandatory.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current-Path Topology

The ESC uses trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with synchronous rectification. In any 60° sector:

- **Leg A** (PWM leg): the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side FET conducts for (1 − D). Phase current flows continuously through one FET of the leg.
- **Leg B** (return leg): the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.
- **Leg C:** floating (used for BEMF sensing).

So at any instant, exactly **two FET channels** carry the full phase current I.

### 2.2 Per-Channel Conduction Power

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

The temperature dependence is:

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + 0.005\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

The temperature dependence makes this a positive-feedback equation. Solve it iteratively with the thermal network in §4.

**At T_j = 125 °C:** R = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.80 mΩ**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

**At T_j = 75 °C:** R = 1.50 mΩ, so P = **19.2 W**

### 2.3 Per-FET Distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs carries current for 2 of 6 sectors as the "main" conductor. Synchronous low-side conduction during PWM-off is shared on top of that. Time-averaged:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\times 2 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\ \approx\ \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{3}\cdot\tfrac{1}{1}\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}... $$
