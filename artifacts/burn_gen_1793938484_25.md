**Flags to raise at design review:**

- **80 A per channel on a 30.5 mm board is aggressive.** The math in §1–§3 shows it is burst-only and thermal-capacity-limited, not steady-state.
- **Read "80 A" as phase current, not battery current.** Battery current ≈ D·I_phase. Confirm which one the marketing spec means.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 105 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0075 × 80) = **1.60 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-phase current distribution (six-step, synchronous)

Each half-bridge spends one-third of the electrical period in each state:

| State (⅓ period each) | HS FET | LS FET |
|---|---|---|
| PWM-high leg | conducts I for fraction D | conducts I for (1−D) (sync rect.) |
| Low leg | off | conducts I continuously |
| Floating | off | off |

**Per-FET RMS-squared duty factors:**

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R \cdot \tfrac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{LS} = I^2 R \cdot \tfrac{1}{3}\left[(1-D) + 1\right]$$

**Total per motor channel** (two series conduction paths at all times):

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers (I = 80 A, D = 0.95, T_j = 105 °C)

- I²R = 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = 10.24 W
- P_HS = 10.24 × 0.317 = **3.24 W**
- P_LS = 10.24 × 0.350 = **3.58 W** (hottest FET)
- P_cond,ch = **20.5 W**
- Board (4 ch) = **81.9 W**

### 1.4 Copper losses (do not ignore)

Each phase path is roughly 10 mm of 3 mm wide trace in parallel across 3 layers of 2 oz copper:

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{w t n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8} \cdot 0.01}{0.003 \cdot 70\times10^{-6} \cdot 3} \approx 0.27\text{ mΩ}$$

Counting the phase, return, and bus share gives about 0.5 mΩ effective per path, so 2 × 6400 × 0.5 mΩ ≈ **6.4 W per channel**. This is a significant hidden term. Use polygon pours, via stitching, and solder-reinforced phase pads.
