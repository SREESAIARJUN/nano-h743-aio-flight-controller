## 0. Architecture and Scope

### 0.1 Partitioning

ArduPilot on the STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The H743 is the flight controller. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. Each ESC channel uses its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The model therefore splits into two domains:

| Domain | Silicon | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz | Attitude control, DShot600 TX, GCR eRPM RX, harmonic notch |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 + FD6288Q + 6× N-MOSFET | Six-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF sensing, current limit |

### 0.2 Design Basis

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S Li-ion/LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | 40 V FETs give 59% voltage margin |
| Phase current | 80 A burst, 45 A continuous (target) | Per channel |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary, active freewheel | AM32 default range |
| Dead time | 50 ns | |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm ESC, 20×20 mm or 30.5 mm FC | |
| Assumed MOSFET | 40 V, R_DS(on),25 = 0.70 mΩ, Qg = 60 nC, Qgd = 12 nC, Qrr = 60 nC, Qoss = 50 nC, R_θJC = 0.8 K/W, 5×6 mm PQFN | Class-representative. **Substitute the selected part's datasheet values before release.** |

> **Feasibility flag.** "80 A burst" on a 30.5 mm 4-in-1 board is a **time-limited rating**, not a thermal steady state. Section 3 derives the actual burst duration. The result is a few seconds, and the label should state it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-Step Conduction Topology

At any commutation step, two half-bridges are active:

- **Phase A (PWM leg).** The high-side (HS) FET conducts for a fraction D of the period. The complementary low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Phase B (return leg).** The LS FET conducts continuously for the 60° electrical step.
- **Phase C.** This phase floats and is used for BEMF zero-cross detection.

Phase current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly **two** channels in series, whatever the duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,A} + R_{LS,B} \right] \approx 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The approximation holds for identical FETs, which gives a duty-independent result.

### 1.2 Temperature Coefficient

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design-point T_j = 120 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 0.70 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 95) = 0.70 \times 1.43 = 1.00\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Burst Conduction Loss per Channel

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.00\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{12.8\ W}$$

Per-FET worst case is the Phase-B LS FET at 100% of the step: 80² × 1.0 mΩ = **6.4 W**.

### 1.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

During dead time, current commutates through the body diode (V_F ≈ 0.8 V at 80 A). There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times (100\times10^{-9}) \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 1.5 Copper and Interconnect

Assume a phase path of about 0.15 mΩ (pad + 2 oz inner planes + solder joint) and a battery-pad path shared by all channels of about 0.10 mΩ:

- Per-channel phase copper: 80² × 0.15 mΩ = **0.96 W**
- Bus copper at full throttle: I_bus ≈ 4 × 80 × D. At D → 1, this is 320 A, giving 320² × 0.10 mΩ = **10.2 W**, shared across the board.

> Bus copper is the hidden loss term. Use 4 oz outer + 2 oz inner ×4, and fuse the battery pads into a solid pour. Do not route battery current through vias in single file. Use ≥ 40 vias of 0.3 mm per pad (≈ 1.5–2 A each for burst).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-Switching V–I Overlap

Only the PWM leg's HS FET hard-switches. The LS sync-rectifier switches at near-zero voltage, so it is ZVS-ish because the body diode clamps first.

Transition times come from gate charge. The FD6288Q sources about 1.0 A and sinks about 1.5 A. With R_G = 4.7 Ω external and a Miller plateau V_pl ≈ 3.0 V at V_G
