# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #43): Architecture, Loss Models, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Architecture Correction

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. The stack has two levels:

| Layer | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control. Sends bidirectional DShot600 and reads eRPM telemetry. |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | Commutation. Runs 48 kHz complementary PWM with 6 MOSFETs per motor. |

The loss models below apply to the ESC power stage. The DMA plan applies to the H743.

**Feasibility warning.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) and "80A ×4" are physically incompatible except for sub-second bursts. This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board** (40×40 mm outline), 8-layer, 2 oz copper. Section 3 shows why.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

The motor uses six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification. At any instant, current flows through exactly two MOSFETs in series:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1−D) via synchronous rectification.
- **Fixed leg:** the low-side FET is on for the whole 60° sector.

Each FET is active for one-third of the electrical cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-adjusted R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.007\ /\text{K}$$

Reference FET: a 40 V PDFN5×6 part, R_DS,25 = 1.0 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V. At T_j = 125 °C, k_T ≈ 1.5, so R_DS,hot = 1.5 mΩ.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS}\right] + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

With R_HS = R_LS = R:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2I^2R + I^2R_{Cu}$$

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| 2 · 80² · 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| R_Cu (pads + planes + vias ≈ 0.15 mΩ per path) | 0.96 W |
| **P_cond per channel** | **≈ 20.2 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET average loss

The worst-case FET carries I²R for one-third of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R = \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot 6400 \cdot 1.5\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\text{ W}$$

At 80A, eRPM is in the kHz range, so the die thermal time constant (~ms) averages this ripple.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Assumed operating point: V_bus = 25.2 V (6S full charge), I = 80 A, gate driver ≈ 1.5 A source/sink (e.g., FD6288Q).

| Mechanism | Equation | Parameters | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | t_r + t_f = 40 ns | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | Q_rr = 50 nC | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw × 2 FETs | C_oss = 1 nF | 0.03 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_F · I · 2 · t_dt · f_sw | V_F = 0.8 V, t_dt = 200 ns | **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | Q_g · V_GS · f_sw × 2 | Q_g = 60 nC, V_GS = 10 V | 0.06 W |
| **P_sw per channel** | | | **≈ 3.3 W** |

**Optimization levers:**
- **Dead time:** cutting it from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. Verify against shoot-through margin at T_j,max.
- **Switching frequency:** 24 kHz halves P_sw but increases current ripple and acoustic noise.

### Total power-stage loss

$$P_{ch} \approx 20.2 + 3.3 = 23.5\text{ W}, \qquad P_{4\times} \approx \mathbf{94\text{ W}}\ \text{(all four motors at 80A)}$$

Efficiency check: P_out ≈ 25.2 × 80 × 0.9 ≈ 1.8 kW per channel, so η ≈ 98.7%. The efficiency is fine. **Heat density is the real constraint.**

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped thermal network

```
P_FET → [R_jc ≈ 0.8 K/W] → case → [R_cb ≈ 0.7 K/W, via array] → board (C_th) → [R_ba] → ambient
```

### 3.2 Board thermal capacitance (40×40 mm, 1.6 mm, 8L/2 oz)

| Component | Calculation | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| FR4 | 2.56 cm³ × 1.9 g/cm³ = 4.9 g × 1.1 J/g·K | 5.4 J/K |
| Copper | 8 × 70 µm × 16 cm² × 0.7 fill = 0.63 cm³ → 5.6 g × 0.385 J/g·K | 2.2 J/K |
| Components + solder | | ≈ 0.8 J/K |
| **Total C_th** | | **≈ 8.4 J/K** |

### 3.3 Burst transient

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) = P\,R_{ba}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right),\qquad \tau = R_{ba}\,C_{th}$$

With prop-wash airflow, R_ba ≈ 3 K/W, so τ ≈ 25 s. For bursts much shorter than τ, the board heats almost adiabatically: dT/dt ≈ P / C_th.

| Scenario | P | dT/dt | Time to ΔT = 60 K (board 85 °C at 25 °C ambient) |
|---|---|---|---|
| All 4 motors at 80A | 94 W | 11.2 K/s | **≈ 5.5 s** |
| 1 motor at 80A, 3 at 30A | ≈ 34 W | 4.0 K/s | ≈ 15 s |
| Steady 40A, all 4 | ≈ 26 W | Steady state ΔT = 78 K | Marginal |

### 3.4 Junction margin

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(R_{jc}+R_{cb}) = 85 + (3.2 + 0.6)(1.5) \approx 91\ ^\circ\text{C}$$

This is well below the 150 °C limit. **The limits are the board and solder, not the die.**

### 3.5 Rating and design conclusions

- **Specification:** 80A burst for ≤ 5 s, all channels. 45A continuous with airflow.
- **Firmware protection:** an NTC on the ESC with AM32 thermal derating starting at 100 °C board temperature is mandatory.
- **Bulk capacitance ripple:** I_rms = I·√(D(1−D)), which reaches **40 A_rms per channel** at D = 0.5. Fit 4 × 470 µF low-ESR polymer capacitors
