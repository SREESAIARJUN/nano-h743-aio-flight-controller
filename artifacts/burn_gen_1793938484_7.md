## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Assumptions

Several points need to be fixed before deriving anything, because they change the results.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot sends throttle commands. It has no sensorless BLDC/FOC commutation stack. The board therefore needs **four dedicated ESC MCUs**. This design uses 4× AT32F421K8U7 (QFN-32, 120 MHz) running AM32 firmware. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600.
2. **No MOSFET part number was specified.** All numbers below use a representative 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 device. Substitute your datasheet values into the equations.
3. **"80 A burst" is interpreted as 80 A per motor, phase-current peak, for a few seconds.** Section 3 shows this is not survivable as a continuous rating on a nano (20×20 mm) board. The derivation states this explicitly rather than hiding it.
4. **Supply voltage:** 6S LiPo, worst case V_bus = 25.2 V.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Topology argument

This is 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) rectification. At every instant the phase current flows through **exactly two conducting MOSFET channels**:

- **During the PWM ON period (fraction D):** the high-side switch of phase A and the low-side switch of phase B conduct.
- **During the PWM OFF period (fraction 1−D):** the low-side switch of phase A (synchronous freewheel) and the low-side switch of phase B conduct.

Two channels conduct regardless of D, so the bridge conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**. Dead time is the exception and is treated in §2.

### 1.2 Equations

On-resistance at junction temperature:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big] \approx R_{DS(on),25}\cdot k_T$$

Conduction loss per motor (one bridge):

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Each of the 6 FETs is active for a 120° window, so averaged over an electrical cycle:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,bridge}}{6} \quad\text{(average)}, \qquad P_{cond,FET,peak} \le I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}$$

The low-side FET of the PWM leg carries more loss when D is low (freewheel-dominant). Use the peak value for worst-case junction checks.

### 1.3 Numbers at I_ph = 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond per bridge | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| Average per FET | 19.2 / 6 | 3.2 W |
| Instantaneous worst-case per FET | 80² × 1.5 mΩ | 9.6 W |
| P_cond, all 4 motors | 4 × 19.2 | **76.8 W** |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

**Phase copper** (2 oz outer + 2 oz inner paralleled, about 8 mm path, about 0.25 mΩ effective per phase path, two phases in the current loop):

$$P_{Cu} = 2\,I^2 R_{trace} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.25\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\text{ W per motor}$$

**Current shunt.** One bus shunt per ESC (0.25 mΩ, 2512 package): P = 80² × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W per motor**.

> Recommendation: use the 0.25 mΩ shunt with a ×50 INA-style amplifier, or a Hall sensor on the main bus only. At 0.5 mΩ the shunt alone would dissipate 3.2 W per motor.
