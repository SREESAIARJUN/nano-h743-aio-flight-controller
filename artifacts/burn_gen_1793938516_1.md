## 0. Basis of Design and Assumptions

I don't have a datasheet set or prior design files for "Job #42," so this document uses **parameterized models with stated reference values**. Every equation is written so you can substitute your actual MOSFET, driver and PCB stack-up data. Values marked **[A]** are assumptions that must be checked against vendor datasheets before layout freeze.

### Architectural correction

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no native sensorless BLDC/FOC commutation for four motors. The standard and supportable architecture is:

```
LiPo ──► Power stage (4 × 3-phase bridges)
            ▲ gate drive
   4 × ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware)
            ▲ bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire per motor)
   STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
```

The H743 handles flight control, DShot generation, EDT/RPM capture (used for the harmonic notch), and battery voltage/current ADC.
