## 0. Architectural Decisions and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 cannot practically run the FOC/6-step commutation for four motors while also running ArduPilot. The architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (rev V, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot, emits bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | STM32G071GBU6 (or AT32F421) | AM32 firmware, 6-step with complementary PWM at 48 kHz |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, ~12 V gate) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 5×6 | 24 FETs total |
| Form factor | **30.5 × 30.5 mm**, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers | |

### 0.2 Feasibility Note: Form Factor

A 20 × 20 mm "nano" outline cannot place 24 PDFN 5×6 FETs. 3×3 mm FETs at roughly 2.5 mΩ would push conduction loss past 40 W per channel at 80 A. The 80 A target therefore forces the 30.5 mm footprint. Even then, **80 A is a time-limited burst rating, not a continuous one** (derived in §3).
