# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #32): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two premises in the request need fixing before the math means anything.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware. It has no BLDC commutation, current-loop, or gate-drive code. In a correct "H743 4-in-1" design:

- The **FC MCU** is the STM32H743VIT6. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600.
- The **ESC MCUs** are four small Cortex-M0+/M4 parts running AM32 (or one per pair with BLHeli_32-class firmware). They generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

Everything below assumes this split. The netlist reflects it.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano board must be defined.** 4 × 80 A simultaneously is 320 A from the battery. That is about 7 kW at 6S, through an XT60 rated for 60 A continuous, on a board with roughly 4–5 J/K of heat capacity. This package therefore defines:

- **80 A = per-channel burst.** It is a single motor, or an asymmetric transient, lasting ≤ 2–5 s.
- **Battery-side worst case = 4 × 80 A**, analyzed as a bounding case to show that it is time-limited to about 2 s.
