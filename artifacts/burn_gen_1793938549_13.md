# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #68): Power-Stage Loss, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Upfront Corrections

Two points about the brief change the analysis, so they come first.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each motor is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421, STM32G071 or GD32E230. The H743 owns the DShot600 links and bidirectional telemetry. The ESC MCUs own the 48 kHz PWM, the gate drive and the commutation. The design below follows that partitioning.

**2. "80 A burst" must be defined.** The two readings differ by a factor of 16 in loss:

| **A: 80 A per channel** | 80 A | ~320 A | Only for roughly 1–2 s, even with ideal FETs |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$R_{ds}(110^\circ C) = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega \times [1 + 0.0045 \times 85] = 1.5 \times 1.3825 = \mathbf{2.07\ m\Omega}$$

### 1.2 Bridge conduction topology

The ESC uses trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At every instant the current path runs through two series MOSFETs:
- During the on-time: high-side of phase X plus low-side of phase Y.
- During the off-time: low-side of phase X (synchronous freewheel) plus low-side of phase Y.

So the per-motor conduction loss is essentially independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts during 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The loss is split between the high-side and low-side devices of a phase by D and (1–D):

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\, I^2 R_{ds},\qquad P_{cond,LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2R_{ds} + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}$$

The second term in P_cond,LS is the "always-on" low-side return leg. **The low-side FETs are the thermal worst case at low duty.**

### 1.3 Numbers

| | Case A (80 A) | Case B (20 A) |
|---|---|---|
| I² | 6400 A² | 400 A² |
| P_cond per motor | 2 × 6400 × 2.07 mΩ = **26.5 W** | 2 × 400 × 2.07 mΩ = **1.66 W** |
| Worst low-side FET (D → 0) | (2/3) × I²R = **8.8 W** | **0.55 W** |

PCB copper adds loss on top of this. A 2 oz trace of 10 mm × 3 mm is about 0.55 mΩ per layer. With 3 parallel layers, about 0.2 mΩ adds roughly 10% to the figures above.
