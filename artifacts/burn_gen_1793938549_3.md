## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step drive, the motor current always flows through exactly **two switch positions**:
- During the on-time: the PWM high side plus the opposite leg's low side.
- During the off-time: the synchronous-rectifying low side plus the opposite low side.

The two-position path holds regardless of duty cycle D, so the bridge conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I^2 \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{k_T R_{ds,25}}{N_p}$$

Variant A (N_p = 1): R_hot = 1.6 × 1.55 = **2.48 mΩ**

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.48\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 4.96\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{31.7\ W}$$

Each of the 6 switch positions carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period. Averaged over a rotation (eRPM is in the kHz range, far faster than package thermal time constants
