## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no in-tree BLDC commutation firmware for this use, and running 4× sensorless 6-step loops at 48 kHz alongside EKF3 is not a sound partition. The architecture used here is:

```
┌──────────────── FC section ────────────────┐      ┌──────── ESC section (×4) ────────┐
│ STM32H743VIT6 @480 MHz, ArduPilot           │ BDShot600 │ AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32)    │
│ IMU (SPI), Baro (I2C), OSD, UARTs, SD (SDMMC)│◄────────►│ 3-ph gate driver (FD6288Q-class)│
│ TIM3 CH3/CH4, TIM5 CH1/CH2 → M1..M4         │  1 wire   │ 6× N-MOSFET, 3.3×3.3 mm         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘           └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct during each 60° sector:

- **Phase A (PWM'd):** current flows through HS_A during D·T and through LS_A (synchronous) during (1−D)·T.
- **Phase B:** current flows through LS_B continuously during the sector.

So at any instant exactly **two FETs carry I** (neglecting dead time). The instantaneous conduction loss per ESC is therefore:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of D)}$$

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow k_T(100^\circ\text{C}) = 1.5$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Condition | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Cold (25 °C) | 2 × 80² × 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W per ESC** |
| Hot (100 °C) | 2 × 80² × 2.4 mΩ | **30.7 W per ESC** |

### 2.4 Copper loss

Assume 15 mm of 3 mm-wide trace on 4 layers in parallel (2+3+3+2 oz).

- Per-layer sheet resistance: ~0.25 mΩ/sq for 2 oz, ~0.17 mΩ/sq for 3 oz.
- 5 squares per layer → ~0.17 mΩ effective per conductor (battery and phase paths, ×2).
- P_Cu ≈ 80² × 0.35 mΩ ≈ **2.2 W per ESC**.

### 2.5 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{FET,avg} = \frac{30.7}{6} \approx 5.1\ \text{W (hot)}, \qquad P_{FET,inst} = 15.4\ \text{W while conducting}$$

The PWM'd HS/LS pair splits its share by D. At full throttle (D → 1), the HS carries nearly all of it.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Gate currents and transition times

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10-3}{5} = 1.4\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.6\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 5.7\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 13.3\ \text{ns}$$

Adding layout and di/dt-limited stretching gives **t_on + t_off ≈ 25 ns** (design value).

### 3.2 Loss components

Per ESC, one hard-switched leg is active per sector:

**V–I overlap loss**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 25\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.21\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (LS body diode conducts in dead time, then HS turns on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

**Output capacitance**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.030\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (2 edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} I \cdot 2 t_{dead} f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
