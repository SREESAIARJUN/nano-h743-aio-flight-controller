# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #64): Power-Stage Thermal Model, 48 kHz Switching Model, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Allocation

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Three architectural points shape everything below:

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the commutation MCU.** ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which generate the 48 kHz PWM. AT32F421 and STM32G071 are common choices. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
2. **The 48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-firmware parameter.** AM32 supports a 48 kHz PWM setting. The H743 never sees it.
3. **"80 A burst" is extremely aggressive for a nano form factor (≈20×20 to 30×30 mm).** The model in §3 shows this is a transient rating of a few seconds, set by board heat capacity. It is not a steady-state capability. Treat it as a design constraint to verify, not a marketing number to inherit.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step commutation conduction pattern

In trapezoidal six-step drive, two phase legs carry current I at any instant. Each leg:

- is high-side active for 120°,
- is low-side active for 120°,
- floats for 120°.

Assume complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM on the active leg at duty D.

**Per-FET RMS-squared current, averaged over one electrical cycle:**

- High-side FET: I²_HS = (1/3)·D·I²
- Low-side FET (sync-rect during the leg's high interval, plus full-on during its low interval): I²_LS = (1/3)·(1 − D)·I² + (1/3)·I²

At burst (D → ~0.95–1), the worst FET approaches **(1/3)·I²**, and both FETs in a leg together carry **(2/3)·I²**.

**Per-channel conduction loss.** At every instant two FETs conduct the full current, so:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature coupling (self-consistent solution)

The resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

For a single FET with thermal resistance R_θ to a reference temperature T_ref, define k = I²_FET · R_25. Then:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_\theta\, k\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j=\frac{T_{ref}+R_\theta k(1-25\alpha)}{1-R_\theta k\alpha}$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion:** R_θ · k · α ≥ 1. The design must keep R_θ · k · α ≪ 1. Target < 0.3.
