**Rate hierarchy.** Each loop runs at least 5× slower than the loop inside it, and each crossover sits at least 5× below the inner loop's crossover.

```
 20 kHz  ISR: ADC → Clarke/Park → current PI (d,q) → inv-Park → SVPWM
  4 kHz        Encoder read, velocity PI + cogging FF + gyro-rate feedback
  8 kHz        IMU FIFO drain → notch/LPF → dual-IMU fusion (decimate to 1 kHz)
  1 kHz        Attitude estimator (Mahony) → angle P(D) → joint-rate setpoints
```

Target crossover frequencies:

- Current loop: ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s
- Velocity loop: ω_v = 2π·150 rad/s
- Angle loop: ω_θ = 2π·20 rad/s

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset).

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

In the rotor frame the motor obeys:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Here λ_m = K_t / (1.5·p) is the flux linkage. The controller adds feedforward to cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choosing

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

cancels the plant pole, giving a closed loop of exactly ω_c/(s + ω_c).

**Discretization.** Use backward Euler on the integrator, I[k] = I[k−1] + K_i·T_s·e[k]. This is valid when ω_c·T_s ≪ 1; here 9425 × 50 µs = 0.47, which is acceptable.

Add a one-sample delay compensation to the angle used in inverse Park: θ_e + 1.5·ω_e·T_s.
