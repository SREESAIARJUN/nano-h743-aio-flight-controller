# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model and Allocation Package (Job #12)

The calculations below use stated assumptions, so you can substitute your actual BOM values and re-run them.

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Two points shape every number that follows.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** Bi-directional DShot means each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design assumes 4× AT32F421 running AM32, each driving one 3-phase bridge. The H743 generates DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
2. **"Nano" vs. 80 A.** An 80 A per-motor burst on a 20×20 mm board is not thermally credible (see §2.5). The baseline here is a **30.5×30.5 mm mount on a ~37×37 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board**. A 20×20 variant is evaluated for comparison.

**Baseline assumptions**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V (full), 22.2 V nominal |
| MOSFET (×24) | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on) = 0.70 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g = 55 nC, Q_rr = 45 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, R_θJC = 1.2 K/W |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase, 1.5 A sink / 1.2 A source), V_GS = 10 V |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal (AM32 default), 120° conduction |
| Burst current | 80 A phase current per motor, all 4 motors simultaneously (worst case) |

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At the design limit T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 0.70 \times 1.49 = 1.04\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

Add the copper trace and via resistance per leg, estimated at R_cu ≈ 0.15 mΩ (2 oz, short pours, via arrays).

### 1.2 Conduction loss

In 6-step operation, phase current I flows through one high-side leg and one low-side leg at all times. With complementary PWM, the "off" interval is carried by the synchronous FET of the same leg, so the leg always presents ≈ R_DS.

$$P_{cond} = I^2\,(2R_{DS} + 2R_{cu}) = 80^2 \times (2.08 + 0.30)\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.2\ W}$$

Silicon only: 80² × 2.08 mΩ = **13.3 W**. Copper: **1.9 W**, which is spread into the board.

**Dead-time body-diode loss** (t_dt = 50 ns, V_F ≈ 0.8 V, two transitions per period):

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_F\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(50\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 1.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-modulated leg switches. Transition times come from the gate charge and driver current, with R_G,total ≈ 5 Ω giving ~1.2 A effective:

$$t_{r}\approx\frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_G}\approx\frac{14\text{ nC}}{1.2\text{ A}}\approx 12\text{ ns},\qquad t_f\approx 10\text{ ns}$$

**V–I overlap loss:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(22\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.06\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (hard turn-on into the conducting body diode of the sync FET):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 45\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.054\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and R_G, not the FET). Two FETs per switching event, one leg active:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2(55\text{n})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.053\text{ W}$$

Total switching loss per channel: **P_sw ≈ 1.16 W**.

> **Ringing caveat:** A 25.2 V bus with a ~3 nH loop gives di/dt overshoot V = L·di/dt ≈ 3n × 80/10n = 24 V. Without a tight decoupling loop, the FET sees ~49 V on a 40 V part.
> - Place ≥ 3× 1 µF/50 V X7R 0603 per bridge, within 2 mm of the high-side drain and low-side source.
> - Provide bulk capacitance at the pad: 470 µF/35 V low-ESR plus a TVS (SMBJ28A).

### 1.4 Shunt loss

AM32 typically uses a per-ESC low-side shunt. With R_sh = 0.25 mΩ:

$$P_{sh} = I^2 R_{sh} \times \tfrac{2}{3} \approx 80^2(0.25\text{m})(0.67) = \mathbf{1.07\ W}$$

The 2/3 factor reflects the average shunt occupancy in 6-step operation with a common low-side return. Use a 2512 shunt with ≥ 3 W rating.
