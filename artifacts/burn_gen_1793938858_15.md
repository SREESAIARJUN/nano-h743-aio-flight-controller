## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Red Flags

The project files for Job #46 were not available, so every value below is parameterized. Wherever this document says "assumed," substitute the real datasheet number.

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly in any production 4-in-1 design. The real architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC) --4x bidirectional DShot600--> 4x ESC MCUs (AM32 / BLHeli_32)
                                                        --> 3-phase gate drivers --> 24 MOSFETs
```

The 48 kHz PWM is therefore generated by the ESC MCUs. The H743 timer and DMA work concerns DShot only.

### 0.2 Feasibility warning

"80 A burst per motor" on a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is not physically credible as a sustained or multi-second rating. The model in §3 shows why:

- About 100 W of dissipation must go into roughly 4 J/K of thermal mass.
- That allows only about 2 seconds at 80 A on all four motors.
- It implies about 320 A of battery current, which exceeds any connector used on nano-class aircraft.

Treat 80 A as a **≤1–2 s, single-motor-dominant transient rating**, or re-scope to a 30.5×30.5 board.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct and one floats during every 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for the duty fraction D. Its complementary low-side (LS) FET conducts for 1 − D (synchronous rectification).
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is held on for the whole sector.

Neglecting ripple, the motor current always passes through exactly two R_ds(on) in series:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds,hot}\quad,\quad R_{ds,hot}=k_T R_{ds,25}$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

The peak-to-peak current ripple is:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

The RMS current over the flat top then becomes:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

Example: L_ph = 10 µH, D = 0.9, and V_bus − E = 5 V give ΔI ≈ 9.4 A. The ripple term is ΔI²/12 ≈ 7.4 A², which is negligible against I² = 6400 A². It is ignored below.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

- R_ds,hot = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**
- P_cond = 80² × 2 × 1.8 mΩ = **23.0 W per motor**

Per-FET load: each FET conducts during 120° of every 360° electrical cycle.

- Instantaneous worst case: 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **11.5 W**
- Cycle average: **≈ 3.8 W**
