# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Engineering Model & Reference Design

> **Scope and status.** This is an engineering derivation with a reference netlist. Every device parameter marked **[A]** is an assumption you must replace with the selected part's datasheet values. The netlist is a design-intent reference, not a fab-ready ECAD export. It must pass ERC/DRC and peer review before layout.

## 0. Design-Critical Finding

**An 80 A per-channel burst on all four channels at once is not thermally sustainable on a 20×20 mm nano board for more than about 1–2 s.** Section 3 derives this. You have three options:

1. Define "80 A burst" as ≤1 s, or as non-simultaneous across channels.
2. Move to a 30.5×30.5 mm board, which gives about 3–4 s of burst capacity.
3. Derate the burst to about 55 A per channel.

The rest of this document models the full 80 A case so the limits are explicit.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo (18.0–25.2 V)
   │
   ├─► 4-in-1 ESC power stage (24× N-MOSFET, 4× 3-ph gate driver)
   │      ▲ gate signals @ 48 kHz (complementary, dead-time inserted)
   │   4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class, AM32 firmware)
   │      ▲ Bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire per motor, 3.3 V)
   │
   └─► Buck 5 V / 3.3 V ─► STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) ─ IMU, baro, OSD, SD, UARTs
```

- The H743 does not drive the gates. The 48 kHz switching and commutation run on the ESC MCUs.
- The H743 only generates DShot frames and decodes eRPM telemetry.

| R_ds temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |

| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns per edge |

| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |

| Gate drive voltage | V_gs | 10 V |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |

### 2.2 Conduction Losses (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In each 60° commutation sector, current flows through exactly two device positions. These are the high side of phase X (or its complementary low side during PWM off-time) and the low side of phase Y.

**Hot resistance:**

R_ds(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)] = 1.0 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**

**Instantaneous conduction loss per channel:**

P_cond,ch = I_ph² · 2 · R_ds(T_j) = 80² × 2 × 1.45 mΩ = **18.56 W**

This loss is independent of D under synchronous rectification. The PWM'd leg alternates HS/LS but always presents one R_ds.

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle:

P_cond,FET = I_ph² · R_ds · (1/3) = **3.09 W**

Check: 6 × 3.09 W = 18.56 W ✓

**Battery current:** I_batt ≈ D · I_ph = 76 A per channel, so about **304 A** for the whole pack when all four channels burst.
