## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Verdict

### 0.1 Signal chain (important)

ArduPilot on the H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz commutation PWM. The topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (1-wire/motor)──> 4× ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class)
                                                          │ 48 kHz complementary PWM, 6-step
                                                          ▼
                                                    3-ph gate driver ──> 6× MOSFET per motor
```

- The 48 kHz switching-loss model applies to the ESC power stage, which is driven by the ESC MCUs.
- The DMA/timer model applies to the H743.

### 0.2 Feasibility verdict

A 20×20 mm nano board cannot sustain 80 A/channel. The math below shows:

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Total dissipation at 80 A × 4 channels | ≈ 106 W |
| Safe 4-channel simultaneous burst duration | ≈ 1.0–1.4 s |
| Continuous per-channel rating (no heatsink, prop-wash cooling) | ≈ 14–20 A |

Treat "80 A burst" as a sub-2-second, single-event rating, enforced by firmware current limiting (see §6). If 80 A for 5–10 s is a hard requirement, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 5×6 mm PQFN FETs and a heatsink.
