# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power Stage Losses, Thermal Model, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. An AIO FC/ESC uses:

- **FC domain:** the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600.
- **ESC domain:** four dedicated ESC MCUs (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071) run AM32 or BLHeli_32. They generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM and drive 3 half-bridges each.

The two domains share only the DShot signal lines, GND, and the battery and current-sense nets. The math below is split accordingly.

The 30.5 mm board is a deliberate choice: a true 20×20 nano layout cannot physically sustain 80 A × 4. Section 3 shows why.

## 1. Conduction Losses (80 A Burst)

With synchronous rectification and 6-step trapezoidal commutation, the load current always flows through **two** MOSFETs in series (one HS, one LS). This holds regardless of duty cycle, because the complementary LS carries the freewheel current during the PWM off-time.

**Hot on-resistance:**

R_hot = R_25 · k_T = 1.5 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ**

**Conduction loss per ESC channel:**

P_cond = I² · 2R_hot = 80² × 2 × 2.4 mΩ = **30.7 W per motor**

**Per-FET distribution:**

- Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle.
- At a typical electrical frequency (≈3.5 kHz for 14 poles at 30 k rpm), the electrical period is ≈0.29 ms. This is much shorter than the die/package thermal time constant of ~1–10 ms.
- The die therefore sees **time-averaged** power: P_FET,avg ≈ 30.7 / 6 ≈ **5.1 W**.
- The instantaneous loss is 15.4 W while a FET is conducting.

**Copper loss:** add PCB copper loss. Each 2 oz layer is ~0.25 mΩ/sq. A realistic phase path with 4 paralleled layers is ~0.3 mΩ:

P_Cu ≈ 80² × 0.3 mΩ × 2 ≈ **3.8 W per motor**

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg hard-switches; the LS commutates softly via the body diode.

| Term | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | 1.94 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 400 ns × 48 k | 1.23 W |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2 × 50 nC × 48 k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.03 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2 × 40 nC × 10 V × 48 k | 0.04 W (dissipated in the driver/gate resistor) |
| **P_sw total** | | **≈3.3 W per motor** |

**Sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw.

- At 24 kHz: ≈1.65 W.
- At 96 kHz: ≈6.6 W.

At 80 A, 48 kHz is a reasonable compromise: switching loss is about 10% of conduction loss.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped board heat capacity (adiabatic burst)

| Element | Mass | c_p | C_th |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper (8 × 70 µm, ~80% fill, 9.3 cm²) | ~3.7 g | 385 J/kg·K | 1.43 J/K |
| FR-4 (1.6 mm) | ~2.75 g | 1100 J/kg·K | 3.0 J/K |
| FETs, caps, connectors | — | — | ~1.0 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **≈5.4 J/K** |

**Adiabatic heating rate:**

dT/dt = 153 W / 5.4 J/K ≈ **28 K/s**

### 3.2 Steady-state convection (prop wash)

- Convection coefficient: h ≈ 100 W/m²K.
- Area: A = 2 × 9.3 cm² = 1.86×10⁻³ m².
- Board-to-air resistance: R_ba = 1/(hA) ≈ **5.4 K/W**.
- Thermal time constant: τ = R_ba · C_th ≈ 29 s.

**Board temperature:**

T_board(t) = T_amb + P·R_ba·(1 − e^(−t/τ))

### 3.3 Junction stack (per FET)

**Thermal path:** Z_jc ≈ 1.5 K/W, plus the via array/solder ≈ 1.5 K/W, giving **R_jb ≈ 3 K/W**. The package reaches steady state within ~50 ms, so it is treated as steady state for bursts.

**Hottest FET** (the PWM-leg HS, which takes the conduction share plus most of the switching loss):

P ≈ 5.1 + 3.3/3 ≈ **6.2 W**, so ΔT_jb = 6.2 × 3 ≈ **19 K**
