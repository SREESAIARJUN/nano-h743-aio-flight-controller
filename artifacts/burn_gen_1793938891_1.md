# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #5): Design Basis, Loss Models, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning
The STM32H743 is the flight controller and runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an STM32G071 or AT32F421. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM.

The design therefore has two coupled problems:
- **Power stage:** ESC MOSFET losses and thermals.
- **Signal link:** bidirectional DShot600 from H743 timers via DMA, with eRPM telemetry returned.

### 0.2 Feasibility flag
Treat "80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm nano stack as **seconds-scale only**. Section 3 shows the board reaches its thermal limit in about 2 s at full 80 A per channel. All component values below are representative and must be replaced with your selected parts' datasheet values.
