## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. Each ESC channel runs its own commutation MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The analysis is partitioned that way.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | Worst case for switching loss |
| Burst current | 80 A per channel (phase/DC-link, 6-step) | |
