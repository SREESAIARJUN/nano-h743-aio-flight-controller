### Architectural clarification

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600.

Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU that:
- generates the 48 kHz six-step or FOC PWM,
- decodes DShot,
- returns eRPM telemetry.

Typical choices are an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 does **not** drive the gates. This split is what makes 48 kHz PWM and bi-directional DShot coexist cleanly.

### Headline finding

**80 A per channel on a nano-format board is a ~3-second burst rating at best, not a continuous rating.** The math below shows:
- about **88 W** total dissipation at burst,
- about **25 A per channel** realistic continuous current under prop wash.

Commercial "nano 80 A" labels are marketing peaks. Specify this explicitly in the product datasheet.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.0 \times 1.5 = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in six-step synchronous commutation

In any commutation interval, two phase legs conduct the motor current I:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_s. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T_s, apart from dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously for the whole interval.

So exactly one FET in each of the two legs carries I at every instant, outside dead time:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

At I = 80 A and 125 °C:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0015 = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET breakdown (worst device)

The return-leg low-side FET conducts 100 % of its interval:

$$P = I^2 R_{ds} = 9.6\ \text{W}$$

It conducts during one of every three commutation sectors per electrical revolution. Its thermal mass smooths that duty only if:

$$f_{elec} \gg \frac{1}{\tau_{jc}} \;(\sim 1\text{–}10\ \text{ms})$$

This holds at flight eRPM. At startup or stall it does not. **A locked-rotor condition puts the full 9.6 W or more on a single FET.**

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase trace + pad, 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ per path × 2 paths | 0.6 mΩ total | 3.84 W |
| Low-side shunt (if used), 0.5 mΩ | 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W (scaled by duty when shared) |

Recommendation: use **phase-current sensing via FET R_ds or Hall sensing**, not series shunts, on a nano board.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Each switching node incurs the following losses.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus}\, I\, (t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction (2 edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.614\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (two FETs per switching leg)

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

Most of this is dissipated in the driver and the gate resistor, not in the FET.

### 2.6 Channel and board totals

| Loss term | W per channel | W, 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.20 | 76.8 |
| V–I overlap | 1.94 | 7.76 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 | 2.46 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.11 | 0.43 |
| Gate drive | 0.06 | 0.23 |
| **Semiconductor subtotal** | **21.9** | **87.7** |
| Copper traces | 3.84 | 15.4 |
| **Grand total** | **~25.8** | **~103 W** |

### 2.7 Frequency sensitivity

Frequency-dependent loss is about 2.7 W per channel at 48 kHz:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial f_{sw}} \approx 56\ \mu\text{W/Hz per channel}$$

Effect of lowering the frequency:
- **24 kHz:** saves about 1.35 W per channel.
- **96 kHz:** costs about 2.7 W per channel. Avoid it at this current level.

Conduction dominates, so **FET R_ds(on) and copper are the levers**, not frequency.

### 2.8 Bus capacitor ripple

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; 40\ \text{A}_{rms}\ \text{per channel at } D = 0.5$$

Channels are not phase-interleaved, so this is worst-case additive.

Requirements:
- Local MLCC bank of at least 4 × 10 µF / 50 V X7R per channel, placed at the half-bridge
