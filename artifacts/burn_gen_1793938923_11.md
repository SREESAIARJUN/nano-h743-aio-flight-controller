# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

These choices determine every number below, so they come first.

| Item | Assumption | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Motor control | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 running **AM32**, one per motor | ArduPilot does not do sensorless motor commutation on the H743. The H743 runs ArduPilot only. |
| FC↔ESC link | Bidirectional DShot600, on-board traces | Native ArduPilot BDShot support |
| Battery | 6S LiPo (25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal) | |
| MOSFETs | 40 V N-ch, PDFN3.3×3. Representative values: R_DS(on) = 1.2 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, Q_rr ≈ 30 nC | Typical of nano-ESC parts |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase bootstrap driver, V_DRV ≈ 10 V | |
