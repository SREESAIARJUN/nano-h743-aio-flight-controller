## 0. Architecture Correction (Read First)

An STM32H743 cannot practically run the 48 kHz commutation for four motors itself:

- Four motors need 12 complementary half-bridges, which means 24 gate signals with dead-time.
- The H743 has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs: TIM1 and TIM8, giving 3 complementary pairs each. That is 6 half-bridges, or 2 motors.
- HRTIM adds 10 outputs, but sensorless back-EMF zero-cross for 4 motors at 48 kHz on one core is a poor engineering trade.

**The design therefore uses this architecture:**

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x bidir DShot600--> [4x ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 / AM32 or BLHeli_32-class)]
                                                         |-- 48 kHz complementary PWM --> 3-phase gate driver --> 6 MOSFETs (per motor)
```

The 48 kHz switching-loss model applies to the ESC power stage. The DShot/DMA model applies to the H743.
