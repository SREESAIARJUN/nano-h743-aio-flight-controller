## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Stated Assumptions

Two points in the brief need clarifying before the math, because they change the model.

1. **The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each of the four motor channels has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. A typical ESC MCU is the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743's job is limited to DShot600 command output and bi-directional telemetry capture. Sections
