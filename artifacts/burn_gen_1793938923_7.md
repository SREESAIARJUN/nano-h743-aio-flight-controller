**Architecture decision.** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Running 4×3-phase 48 kHz PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection, and ArduPilot on one core is not viable. The design therefore uses:

- **FC side:** STM32H743VIH6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz) running ArduPilot. It emits bi-directional DShot600.
- **ESC side:** 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071), each running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM.
- **Gate drive:** 4× FD6288Q 3-phase bootstrap gate drivers.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 105 °C: R_DS = 1.8 × 1.40 = **2.52 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, current I flows through two legs:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D; the complementary low side conducts for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the low side is on for 100% of the sector.

Instantaneous bridge loss:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This is independent of duty cycle, which is the key result.

Adding copper path resistance R_Cu (phase pour, bus, vias, solder; estimated at 0.4 mΩ round trip):

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2\,[2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu}]$$

| I (A) | 2·R_DS (105 °C) | R_Cu | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 5.04 mΩ | 0.4 mΩ | 4.9 W | 19.6 W |
| 50 | 5.04 mΩ | 0.4 mΩ | 13.6 W | 54.4 W |
| **80** | 5.04 mΩ | 0.4 mΩ | **34.8 W** | **139 W** |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each leg sources current for 1/3 of the electrical period and sinks for 1/3. At burst throttle (D → 1):

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(80^2)(2.52\text{ mΩ}) = 5.4\text{ W}$$
