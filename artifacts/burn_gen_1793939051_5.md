## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg (phase X):** the high-side conducts for duty D. The low-side (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1 − D).
- **Return leg (phase Y):** the low-side is held ON for the whole sector at full current.

Including the PWM ripple ΔI, the RMS current is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$

For low-inductance racing motors (L_ph ≈ 10–20 µH), ΔI is a few amps. The ΔI²/12 term is then <1 % of I_ph² at 80 A, so it is dropped below.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS,ret} \right] \;\xrightarrow{R_{HS}=R_{LS}=R}\; P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Two FETs are always in series with the phase current, independent of D. That is the key result of synchronous rectification.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond per channel (W) | Hottest single FET (return LS) (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.50 | 19.2 | 9.6 |
| 100 | 2.18 | 27.8 | 13.9 |
| 125 | 2.40 | 30.7 | 15.4 |

Copper and trace losses add on top. A 25 mm path of 2 oz Cu at about 0.5 mΩ, plus solder and pads, gives roughly **+3–6 W per channel** at 80 A. Include this in the thermal model.
