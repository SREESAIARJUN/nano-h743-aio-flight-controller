# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #6)

## 0. Architectural Ground Truths and Assumptions

Four constraints shape the whole design:

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the motor commutator.** ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. Bi-directional DShot is a link *between* the FC and four dedicated ESC MCUs. The architecture is therefore H743 (FC) plus 4× ESC MCUs running AM32 (AT32F421K8U7 baseline, STM32G071 alternate), 4× 3-phase gate drivers and 24 MOSFETs.
