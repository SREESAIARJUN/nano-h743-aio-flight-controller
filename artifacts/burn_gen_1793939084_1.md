# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the Job #13 specification, so this design uses the stated assumptions below. Every parameter is a named `constexpr` or config field, so you can substitute your real values.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Hollow-shaft gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (GBM2804-class), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.06 N·m/A |
| Encoders | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A) per joint |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G474 / H7), center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, FIFO or DRDY |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer, z) → Roll (middle, x) → Pitch (inner, y) → camera |

```
             1 kHz                     4 kHz                      20 kHz
q_ref ─►[Angle P + FF]─ω_ref─►[Velocity PI + FF]─iq_ref─►(+)─►[Current PI d/q]─►SVPWM─► Motor
  ▲   (IMU attitude)     ▲   (filtered gyro → joint) ▲      │ iq_cog(θ_m)        ▲
  │                      │                           └─[Anti-cogging FF]◄─θ_m──┘ encoder
  └── Dual-IMU fusion ◄──┴── notch bank / ANF / consistency check ◄── 2× ICM-42688-P @ 8 kHz
```

**Rate separation rule.** Each outer loop's crossover is at most 1/4 to 1/5 of the inner loop's crossover. Each inner loop's crossover is at most 1/10 of its sample rate, because of the 1.5·Ts transport delay.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle with encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad p = 7 \text{ pole pairs}$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$
