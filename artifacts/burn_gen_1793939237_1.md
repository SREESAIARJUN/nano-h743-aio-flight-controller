## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 cannot run four sensorless FOC/6-step ESC loops while also running ArduPilot. The realistic architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC)  ──DShot600 bidir ×4──►  4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class ESC MCUs)
                                                     └─► 4× 3-phase bridges (24 MOSFETs)
```

The rest of this document uses that partition.

**Key findings:**

1. **Thermal capacity, not silicon, limits the 80 A burst.** In a 30.5×30.5 mm board, 80 A on all four channels produces about **126 W**. The board's thermal mass of about 5.5 J/K heats at roughly **23 K/s**. The realistic burst window is therefore **about 1.5–2 s**, not "sustained burst."
2. **Continuous capability is about 30 A per channel** with prop-wash cooling. Any "80 A" label must state the burst duration.
3. **Bus capacitor ripple (about 40 A RMS per channel at D=0.5) is the hidden failure mode**, more than the MOSFETs themselves.

All numbers below are parametric. They use the stated assumed MOSFET parameters, so you should substitute your selected part's datasheet values.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

With complementary PWM in 6-step commutation, the phase current always flows through exactly two channels: the active high-side FET or the synchronous low-side FET of the PWM leg, plus the continuously-on low-side FET of the return leg.

**Per-FET conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R_{DS,hot} \cdot D \qquad P_{cond,LS,sync} = I^2 R_{DS,hot}(1-D) \qquad P_{cond,LS,ret} = I^2 R_{DS,hot}$$

**Per channel (independent of D):**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 I^2 R_{DS,hot}$$

**Numbers:**

$$R_{DS,hot} = 1.5\,\text{mΩ} \times 1.5 = 2.25\,\text{mΩ}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.00225 = \mathbf{28.8\ W}$$

The hottest single FET is the return-leg low side at 100 % conduction during its 120° interval:

$$80^2 \times 0.00225 = 14.4\ \text{W (instantaneous)}$$

Averaged over an electrical cycle, each FET conducts 1/3 of the time, giving a per-FET average of about 4.8–9.6 W, depending on D. Use the 14.4 W instantaneous value for the junction transient when the eRPM is low (stall or punch-out start).

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap (PWM leg, one hard-switched FET per edge pair)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse recovery and output capacitance

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

### 3.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f I (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

### 3.4 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver/MCU rail, not the FET)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2 = 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 3.5 Per-channel and board totals

| Loss | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 28.80 W | 115.2 W |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 W | 7.76 W |
| Dead time | 0.61 W | 2.44 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 W | 0.34 W |
| Gate drive | 0.06 W | 0.23 W |
| **Total** | **≈31.5 W** | **≈126 W** |

**Observations:**

- Conduction dominates at about 91 %. Raising f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz costs only about 2.5 W per board.
- Paralleling FETs or choosing a lower R_DS(on) is the only effective lever for reducing losses.
- With dead time at 100 ns, the dead-time loss is about one third of the switching loss. Tightening dead time to 50 ns (if the driver's propagation matching allows) saves about 1.2 W on the board.
