## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Early Findings

Some assumptions change the numbers, so they come first.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | H743 runs **ArduPilot on the FC**. The 4-in-1 ESC uses a **separate ESC MCU** (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). ArduPilot does not commutate motors on the FC MCU. |
| Bus | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. Calculations use 25.2 V as worst case. |
| Rating | **80 A burst per channel**, ≤10 s, 48 kHz complementary (synchronous) PWM, trapezoidal/6-step. |
| Form factor | "Nano" = 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting, 6-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers. |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 DFN. R<sub>DS(on)</sub> = 1.5 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q<sub>g</sub> = 30 nC, Q<sub>rr</sub> = 30 nC, Q<sub>oss</sub> = 40 nC, R<sub>θJC</sub> = 1.5 K/W. |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap). Mid-Miller switching t<sub>r</sub> ≈ t<sub>f</sub> ≈ 20 ns. |

**Early findings:**

1. **4 × 80 A simultaneously is not physically credible on a nano board.** That is about 8 kW from the battery, with roughly 130 W dissipated in under 7 cm² of board. The 80 A figure is only defensible as a single-channel, short-duration burst. Section 2.4 quantifies the limit.
2. **Input ripple cannot be handled by on-board ceramics.** An external low-ESR bulk electrolytic is mandatory (§2.6).
3. **DShot600 on a 200 MHz timer clock gives a non-integer prescaler.** The resulting ≈0.8 % bit-rate error is acceptable (§3.2).

## 2. Power-Stage Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Loss with Temperature Coupling

**Temperature dependence of on-resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

**Current path.** In 6-step operation with complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two FETs in series:

- During t<sub>on</sub>: the high-side of phase A and the low-side of phase B.
- During t<sub>off</sub>: the low-side of phase A (synchronous rectifier) and the low-side of phase B.

**Per-channel conduction loss** (dead time ignored here; added in §2.3):

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This loss is independent of duty cycle, which is a property of synchronous rectification.

**Per-FET loss.** Each FET conducts for 120° of each 360° electrical cycle. The high-side carries D of that window and its synchronous partner carries 1−D. Instantaneous loss in a conducting FET is I²·R<sub>DS</sub>.

**Numbers at 80 A** (T<sub>j</sub> assumed 110 °C, so R<sub>DS</sub> = 1.5 × 1.38 = 2.07 mΩ):

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.07\text{ mΩ} = 26.5\text{ W per channel}$$

Peak instantaneous loss in one conducting FET: 6400 × 2.07 mΩ = **13.2 W**.
