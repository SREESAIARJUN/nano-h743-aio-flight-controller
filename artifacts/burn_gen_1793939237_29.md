# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 80A 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Reference Design Analysis (Job #16)

This document derives three things: the 80A burst thermal and loss model, switching losses at 48 kHz PWM, and the ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation. Reference netlists follow.

---

## 0. Architecture Interpretation and Assumptions

### Architecture

The STM32H743 serves as the **flight controller** MCU. It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. The 4-in-1 ESC uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, one AT32F421K8U7 per motor, running AM32 firmware.

The H743 is not suitable as the commutation MCU for four motors. It also has no AM32 or BLHeli target. If the requirement is literally one H743 driving 24 FETs, that is a separate custom-firmware project and needs a scope change.

The thermal analysis below still models the simultaneous worst case, because it bounds the design.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in Six-Step Commutation

In any commutation sector, current flows through exactly two conducting devices:
- the PWM'd high side (or its complementary low side during the off-time), and
- the statically-on low side of the return phase.

Per-motor conduction loss is therefore:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

### 1
