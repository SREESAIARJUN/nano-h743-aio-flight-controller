## 1. System Block Architecture

```
 VBAT (3–6S, ≤25.2V)
   │
   ├─► Bulk + MLCC bank ──► 4× ESC power stages (3 half-bridges each = 24 MOSFETs)
   │                          ▲ gate drive (3-ph driver ×4)
   │                          │
   │                      4× ESC MCU (AM32, 48 kHz center-aligned PWM, complementary w/ dead time)
   │                          ▲ DShot600 bidirectional (1 wire per motor, open-drain-ish w/ pull-up)
   │                          │
   ├─► 5V/3.3V buck ──► STM32H743 (400/480 MHz) ── IMU (SPI), Baro (I2C), OSD, Flash, UARTs
   └─► Current shunt + amp ──► H743 ADC (battery current)
```

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 2.1 Operating mode
The design assumes six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with **complementary (synchronous) PWM**, which is the AM32 default. In any commutation sector:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty $D$, and the low-side (LS) conducts for $(1-D)$ as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the LS conducts 100% of the sector.
- **Leg C:** floating, used for BEMF sensing.

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

**Reference device:** 40V N-channel, PQFN 3.3×3.3, $R_{DS,25}=1.2\ \text{m}\Omega$ (max, $V_{GS}=10$ V), $\alpha = 0.005$.

At $T_j = 110^\circ$C:

$$
R_{DS} = 1.2\,(1+0.005\cdot 85) = 1.71\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 2.3 Per-ESC conduction loss
The loss can be written as the sum of the HS, LS-sync and return-leg terms:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}
$$

The first two terms sum to one full $I^2R$ term, so:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of $D$ (dead time neglected here; it is added in §3).

At $I = 80$ A:

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 1.71\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.9\ W\ per\ ESC}
$$

### 2.4 Per-FET distribution
Over one electrical revolution each FET carries current for 1/3 of the time. The instantaneous peak is $I^2R = 10.9$ W.

$$
\bar P_{FET,cond} = \frac{21.9}{6} = \mathbf{3.65\ W}
$$

The electrical frequency (hundreds of Hz to kHz) is far faster than the junction thermal time constant (ms), so the **average** value applies.

### 2.5 PCB copper loss (frequently underestimated)
Assume an effective bus + phase path of $R_{Cu} \approx 0.4\ \text{m}\Omega$ per ESC (2 oz outer layers, plus via arrays).

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} \approx 6400 \cdot 0.4\times10^{-3} = 2.6\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.6 Electrothermal convergence
Iterate: $T_j \rightarrow R_{DS} \rightarrow P \rightarrow T_j$. The loop gain is

$$
G = \alpha \cdot P_{FET} \cdot R_{th,ja}
$$

- If $G \ge 1$, the device is in **thermal runaway** (no stable solution).
- With $R_{th,ja} \approx 25$ K/W (nano board, no heatsink): $G = 0.005\cdot3.65\cdot25 \approx 0.46$. The loop converges in steady state, but only with a **very high** $T_j$ (see §4).

## 3. Switching Losses at $f_{sw} = 48$ kHz

### 3.1 Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| $V_{bus}$ | 25.2 V (6S full) |
| $I$ | 80 A |
| $t_r + t_f$ (at 80 A, ~2 A driver) | 30 ns |
| $Q_{rr}$ | 40 nC |
| $Q_{oss}$ | 30 nC |
| $Q_g$ | 40 nC @ 10 V |
| Dead time $t_{dt}$ | 200 ns |
| Body-diode $V_f$ | 0.8 V |

### 3.2 Loss terms per ESC (one PWM leg switching)

| Mechanism | Equation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | $\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$ | $0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot30\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = $ **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | $Q_{rr} V f_{sw}$ | **0.05 W** |
| Output capacitance | $\tfrac12 Q_{oss} V f_{sw}$ | **0.02 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | $2 V_f I t_{dt} f_{sw}$ | $2\cdot0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=$ **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (driver dissipation) | $2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}$ | **0.04 W** |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.8 W** |

**Design lever:** reducing dead time to ~100 ns, if driver propagation matching allows, saves about 0.6 W per ESC. The 48 kHz choice costs about 2.8 W versus about 1.4 W at 24 kHz. In exchange it gives lower current ripple, lower motor iron and copper ripple losses, and quieter operation.

### 3.3 Total loss budget at 80 A

| Item | Per ESC | ×4 (all motors at 80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 21.9 W | 87.6 W |
| Switching | 2.8 W | 11.2 W |
| Copper | 2.6 W | 10.4 W |
| **Total** | **27.3 W** | **109 W** |

Efficiency at 80 A × 25.2 V (2016 W per ESC) is ≈ 98.6%. Efficiency is fine; **the problem is power density.**

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Steady-state junction temperature

$$
T_j = T_a + P_{FET}\,(R_{th,jc} + R_{th,cb} + R_{th,ba,share})
$$

Typical values:
- $R_{th,jc} \approx 1.5$ K/W
- Solder/via interface ≈ 2 K/W
- Board-to-air share per FET at nano size with prop wash ≈ 20–30 K/W

With $P_{FET} = 3.65 + 2.8/2 \approx 5.0$ W (the switching loss lands on the PWM-leg pair):

$$
T_j \approx 25 + 5.0\cdot 28 \approx \mathbf{165^\circ C}
$$

This **exceeds the 150°C absolute maximum**. **Conclusion: 80 A is a burst rating only.** Sustained capability
