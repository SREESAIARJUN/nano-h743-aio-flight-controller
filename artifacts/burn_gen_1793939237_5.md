### Feasibility flags

1. **"Nano" vs. 80 A.** A 20×20 mm 4-in-1 cannot carry 4 × 80 A. The copper cross-section and FET count do not fit. This design assumes a 30.5 mm stack. If "nano" means 20×20 is mandatory, the realistic burst ceiling is about 35–45 A per motor.
2. **Battery-side current.** At full throttle (duty ≈ 1), 4 × 80 A = **320 A at the battery**. That is far beyond XT60 (60 A continuous) and beyond most pack C-ratings. Battery current is I<sub>batt</sub> ≈ Σ D·I<sub>phase</sub>. Specify AS150/XT90 and 10 AWG leads, or derate the burst.
3. **Integrated FC + ESC.** If the H743 shares the board with the power stage, the IMU must be isolated both thermally and in vibration (see §5.4). Otherwise use a stacked FC with a separate ESC.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C: R<sub>DS</sub> = 1.0 × 1.51 = **1.51 mΩ** per FET.

For each switch position with N<sub>p</sub> = 2 paralleled FETs:

$$R_{pos} = \frac{R_{DS}(T_j)}{N_p} \cdot k_{share}, \qquad k_{share}\approx 1.1\ \text{(layout/V}_{th}\text{ mismatch)}$$

R<sub>pos</sub> = 1.51 / 2 × 1.1 = **0.83 mΩ**

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right), \quad r = \frac{\Delta I_{pp}}{I_{DC}}$$

Ripple is ΔI = V<sub>bus</sub>·D(1−D)/(L<sub>ph</sub>·f<sub>sw</sub>). For a typical 5-inch to 7-inch motor (L<sub>ph</sub> ≈ 15 µH line-to-line) at D = 0.5:

ΔI = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15e-6 × 48e3) ≈ **8.75 A**, so r ≈ 0.11 and the ripple factor is 1.001. Ripple is negligible at 80 A, but it matters at low current.
