## 0. Scope, Architecture Assumptions, and Early Findings

I don't have a controlling spec for Job #11, so this design rests on the explicit assumptions below. Every number derives from them, so changing an assumption (MOSFET part, gate drive, burst duration) means re-running the equations. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers illustrate them.

| Parameter | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Topology | Stacked or AIO: STM32H743 FC running ArduPilot, plus **4 independent ESC MCUs** (AM32-class, e.g. STM32G071/AT32F421), each running 6-step trapezoidal commutation |
| PWM | 48 kHz complementary (synchronous rectification), PWM on the high side |
| "80 A burst" | 80 A **phase** current per channel, the worst case for conduction loss |
| MOSFET (per switch position, ×24) | Generic 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3: R_DS(on)=1.2 mΩ @25 °C/10 V; Q_g=30 nC; Q_gd=5 nC; Q_gs2≈3 nC; Q_rr≈30 nC; Q_oss≈25 nC; V_SD=0.8 V; R_θJC≈1.5 K/W; T_j,max=175 °C |
