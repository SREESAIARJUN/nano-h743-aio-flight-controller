## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

Several premises need to be stated or corrected first, because they change the math.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run the 48 kHz complementary PWM and 6-step commutation. Typical choices are the STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The architecture below reflects that.
2. **"80A burst" must be per channel and time-bounded.** As Section 3 shows, 4 × 80 A simultaneously on a 30×30 board without a heatsink is thermally impossible for more than about 2–3 s. Treat 80 A as a single-channel, ≤10 s rating at ≤50% of channels.
3. **Battery and switching assumptions.** 6S nominal (Vbus = 25.2 V max), synchronous 6-step trapezoidal drive, center-aligned complementary PWM at 48 kHz.
4. **Representative MOSFET (40 V, 5×6 PowerPAK class).** Treat these as placeholders and replace them with your selected datasheet values.
