## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 ESC that runs ArduPilot bi-directional DShot, the architecture splits into two layers:

| Layer | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz, run at 400 MHz in ArduPilot) | Attitude control; generates DShot600 frames and decodes eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (or EFM8BB51/STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, dead-time |
| Gate drive | FD6288Q (3-phase bootstrap driver), one per motor | Level shifting, approx. 1.0/1.5 A source/sink |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 4 × 3-phase bridges |

So "switching losses at 48 kHz" belong to the ESC MCUs. The H743 owns only the DShot/telemetry timing domain. I model them separately and then couple them thermally.
