## 1. Commutation Model (Trapezoidal 6-Step, Synchronous PWM)

In each 60° electrical sector, exactly **two half-bridges conduct**:

- **PWM leg:** the HS and LS FETs alternate at 48 kHz. During the duty cycle D, current flows through the HS FET. During 1−D, it flows through the LS FET (synchronous rectification). Either way, exactly one FET carries I at every instant.
- **Static leg:** one FET is held on for the whole sector.

So at every instant, **two FET channels are in the current path**, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

Each FET conducts for roughly 1/3 of the electrical period, so the per-FET average is:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot\frac{6}{6}=\frac{2I^2R_{ds}}{6}\ \text{(averaged over a full electrical cycle)}
$$

### 1.1 Temperature dependence of Rds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

| T_j | Multiplier | Rds (typ) | Rds (max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.00 mΩ | 1.30 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.34 | 1.34 mΩ | 1.74 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.45 | 1.45 mΩ | 1.89 mΩ |

### 1.2 Conduction loss at 80 A

PCB copper: one phase path has about 8 mm × 4 mm across 8 layers of 2 oz copper, giving R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ (including vias), shared with neighbors.

| Case | 2·Rds + R_Cu | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| T_j=100 °C, typ | 2.93 mΩ | **18.8 W** | 75 W |
| T_j=125 °C, typ | 3.15 mΩ | 20.2 W | 81 W |
| T_j=125 °C, max | 4.03 mΩ | 25.8 W | 103 W |
