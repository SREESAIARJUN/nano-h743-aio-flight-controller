## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx 0.0045\,/\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS}(125) = 1.3\,\mathrm{m\Omega}\times 1.45 \approx 1.9\,\mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.2 Per-leg conduction in 6-step synchronous mode

During any commutation sector, current flows through one high-side FET (HS) and one low-side FET (LS) of the complementary leg. Within the PWM leg, the HS conducts for a fraction D of the period and the LS synchronous-rectifies for (1−D). The other leg's LS conducts continuously.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}$$

The result is independent of D, which is the key property of synchronous rectification. Ripple adds a small correction:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

This is under 1 % at 48 kHz for typical 10–20 µH phase inductance. It is neglected below.
