## 0. Design Basis and Assumptions

I don't have the specifics of "Job #1," so the design is parameterized around a typical brushless gimbal. **Replace the bracketed values with your hardware.**

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 170+ MHz |

Bandwidth separation of at least 5× between nested loops is enforced throughout:

$$
\omega_{c,i}\;(\approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}) \;\ge\; 5\,\omega_{c,v}\;(\approx 2\pi\cdot 150\text{ Hz}) \;\ge\; 5\,\omega_{c,\theta}\;(\approx 2\pi\cdot 25\text{ Hz})
$$

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

Here θ_off is found at commissioning: apply a d-axis current vector at θ_e = 0, let the rotor settle, then read the encoder.

**Latency compensation.** The encoder is read roughly one PWM period before the new duty takes effect, so the angle is extrapolated:

$$
\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + p\,\omega_m\,(T_{\text{enc}} + 1.5\,T_{\text{pwm}})
$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

For a 3-shunt measurement with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant and current PI design

The PMSM in the rotor (dq) frame:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Decoupling plus back-EMF feedforward.** Adding these terms makes each axis a pure first-order plant 1/(Ls+R):

$$
v_d^{*} = u_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{*} = u_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation.** Choose C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with K_i/K_p = R/L. The open loop becomes ω_ci/s, which gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_ci:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$
