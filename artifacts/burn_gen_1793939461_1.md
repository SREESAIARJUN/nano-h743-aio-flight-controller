## 1. Field-Oriented Current Control (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: inject a d-axis current vector at θ_e = 0, let the rotor settle, then record θ_m.

**Latency compensation.** Extrapolate the angle by 1.5 PWM periods: one period for the computation delay plus half a period for the ZOH effect.

$$\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_{pwm}$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}\qquad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

With three shunts, discard the phase that has the shortest low-side on-time and reconstruct it as $-(i_x+i_y)$.

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant and decoupled PI design

The dq-frame model is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

**Feedforward decoupling.** Add these terms to the PI outputs:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L\,i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda)$$

After decoupling, each axis is the first-order plant $G(s)=\frac{1}{Ls+R}$.

**Pole-zero cancellation PI.** Choose $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ with:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{c},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{c}\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; T(s)=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1\,\text{kHz}$.

**Integrator discretization.** Use forward Euler:

$$x_i[k] = x_i[k-1] + K_i T_s\, e[k]$$
