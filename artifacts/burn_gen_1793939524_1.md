**Architecture note:** In a standard FC + 4-in-1 stack, the H743 does not switch the FETs. Each ESC channel, or a shared ESC MCU (e.g., STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32/BLHeli_32), generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The model below follows that partitioning.

**Feasibility flag:** 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) 4-in-1 is beyond what that form factor can sustain thermally. Section 3 shows this quantitatively. Expect 30.5×30.5 mm or larger, with paralleled FETs, for an honest 80 A burst rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous rectification (complementary PWM), phase current at any instant flows through:

- one high-side FET (or its complementary low-side FET in the same leg during PWM-off), and
- one low-side FET in the returning phase.

So two FET channels always carry I_phase, regardless of duty cycle. Only the dead time is excluded, and that is handled in §2.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx \frac{k_T-1}{100}=0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \left[\,2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{trace}\right]$$

For trapezoidal drive with flat-top current, I_rms ≈ I_burst (conservative).

| T_j | R_ds | P_FETs (2 × I²R) | P_trace | **P_cond,ch** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 1.92 W | 14.7 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W | 1.92 W | **21.1 W** |

**Per FET (worst case):** 9.6 W at 125 °C.
**Four channels at 80 A simultaneously:** about 84 W of conduction loss alone.

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point

Because R_ds depends on T_j and T_j depends on P, solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,R_{th,j\text{-}pcb},\qquad P_{FET}=I^2R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

This has a closed form:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{pcb} + I^2 R_{ds,25}R_{th}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - I^2R_{ds,25}R_{th}\alpha}$$

**Thermal runaway condition:** if $I^2 R_{ds,25}\, R_{th}\, \alpha \ge 1$, there is no stable solution. With 80 A, 1 mΩ, and α = 0.005, runaway occurs at R_th ≥ 31 K/W. Your design is far from that limit, but the margin shrinks if FETs are poorly soldered (voids).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step drive, only one leg is hard-switched per commutation sector. The complementary FET soft-switches (ZVS after dead time).

**(a) V-I overlap (hard-switched FET):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = C_{oss(er)} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\,\text{nF}\cdot 635\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery** (charged into the hard-switched FET):
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction:**
$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot100\,\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = 0.31\ \text{W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (2 switching FETs, dissipated mostly in driver and R_g):
$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot60\,\text{nC}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

| Component | Per channel |
|---|---|
| V-I overlap | 1.94 W |
| C_oss | 0.05 W |
| Q_rr | 0.05 W |
| Dead time | 0.31 W |
| Gate | 0.06 W |
| **P_sw,ch** | **≈ 2.4 W** |

**Frequency scaling:** P_sw is linear in f_sw. Halving to 24 kHz saves about 1.2 W per channel but increases current ripple:

$$\Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

For low-inductance (~10 µH phase-to-phase) racing motors at 48 kHz and D = 0.5, ΔI_pp ≈ 13 A. That ripple adds conduction loss:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \Delta I_{pp}^2/12$$

which adds about 14 A², or roughly +0.2 %. So 48 kHz is a sensible trade-off.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total dissipation (burst, T_j hot)

$$P_{ch} = 21.1 + 2.4 \approx 23.5\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{ESC,4ch} \approx \mathbf{94\ W}$$

Add about 1 W for the BEC/LDOs and H743 (~0.5 W at 480 MHz). **Total ≈ 95 W.**

### 3.2 Two-node transient network

```
P_FET ──► [R_th,j-pcb] ──► T_pcb ──► [R_th,pcb-air] ──► T_amb
   (C_j small, τ_j ~ 10 ms)    │
                                C_pcb (dominant)
```

**Per-FET junction rise above board:**

R_th,j-pcb = R_th,jc (0.8 K/W) + solder/via array (≈ 1.5 K/W). This totals about 2.3 K/W.

The hard-switched FET dissipates 9.6 + 2.4 ≈ 12 W, so:

$$\Delta T_{j\text{-}pcb} \approx 12 \cdot 2.3 = \mathbf{27.6\ K}$$
