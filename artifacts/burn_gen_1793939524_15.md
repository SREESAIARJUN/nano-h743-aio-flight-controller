## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

These issues shape everything that follows.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation, FOC, or zero-cross detection. A 48 kHz PWM sensorless six-step loop needs a dedicated MCU per motor. | Use **4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32** (each channel can be reflashed). The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. |
| 2 | **80 A on a "nano" footprint is a burst rating only.** The thermal model in §3 shows a ~30×30 mm board sustains about 25–32 A per channel continuously with prop-wash cooling. | Specify the rating as **80 A for ≤1 s, 4 channels simultaneously**. Firmware (AM32 current limit plus ArduPilot `BATT_*` limits) must enforce this. |
| 3 | The MOSFET part is not fixed yet. | The models are parametric. Numbers use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN part. **Re-run the calculations with the chosen part's datasheet values.** |
| 4 | ArduPilot DMA timing constants vary between versions. | Values are given with tolerance. **Verify against the `AP_HAL_ChibiOS` source and `dma_resolver.py` output for your branch.** |

**Assumed board:** 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern (≈36×36 mm outline), 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers, 6S (25.2 V max), FR-4 Tg170.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Current definitions

In six-step drive with synchronous rectification, the **phase current** is not the battery current:

$$I_{ph} = \frac{I_{batt}}{D}$$

"80 A burst" is defined here as **I_ph = 80 A**, which is the worst case for the FETs. At full throttle (D ≈ 1), phase current equals battery current.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

At every instant, two FETs carry I_ph:

- During t_on: high-side of phase X plus low-side of phase Y.
- During t_off: low-side of X (synchronous) plus low-side of Y.

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + k_T(T_j - 25)\right]$$

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At T_j = 115 °C, R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 90) = 1.405 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.405\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.98\ W}$$

Because R_DS depends on T_j, the loss and temperature must be solved together. Iterate T_j until it converges (§3.4).

### 2.3 Switching loss (one switching leg at a time)

**Hard-switching V–I overlap:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (2 transitions per cycle):**

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} I_{ph} (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g, not the FETs):

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$

### 2.4 Copper and shunt loss

These are assumptions: the per-phase trace from battery pad to FET is about 0.3 mΩ for 2 oz across 4 paralleled layers, and the shunt is 0.25 mΩ, or 0 with AM32 low-side sensing disabled.

$$P_{Cu} = I_{ph}^2 (2 \times 0.3\text{m}) + I_{batt}^2 R_{shunt} \approx 3.84 + 1.6 = \mathbf{5.4\ W}$$
