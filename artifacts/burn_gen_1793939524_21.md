# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano Form Factor): Electro-Thermal & Timing Model

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three issues shape everything that follows:

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no in-tree firmware for an H7 to run four sensorless BLDC FOC/6-step loops while also flying. The correct architecture is an **H743 flight controller plus four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071, or BLHeli_32-class), linked by bidirectional DShot600. This document models that architecture.
2. **80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is a transient-only rating.** The math in §3 shows it is survivable for about 1–3 s, not continuously. The firmware must enforce an I²t limit.
3. **Device values here are representative**, not specific to a vendor part. Before release, re-run every equation with the chosen MOSFET's datasheet curves.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2 \times 1.55 \approx 1.86\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Use **2.0 mΩ** for design margin.

### 2.2 Conduction losses

In 6-step operation, two phase legs conduct at a time:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D, and the synchronous low-side conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side is held ON for the full 60° sector.

Neglecting dead time:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}$$

The result is independent of D, which is a key property of synchronous rectification.

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.002 = \mathbf{25.6\ W}$$

Ripple correction: I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12. With ΔI ≈ 4 A p-p (typical motor inductance at 48 kHz), the correction is under 0.03 %, so it is ignored.

### 2.3 Switching losses

Only the PWM leg switches. Each loss term is computed below.

**Hard-switching V·I overlap:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.015\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistor, not the FET):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

**Total switching:**

$$P_{sw} \approx \mathbf{2.34\ W}$$

### 2.4 Per-channel and board totals

| Loss | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 25.6 W | 102.4 W |
| Switching | 2.3 W | 9.4 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, I²R, return-leg duty) | ≈1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| PCB copper (≈0.3 mΩ effective path) | ≈1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| Bulk cap ESR (see below) | — | ≈2–5 W |
| **Total** | **≈31 W** | **≈125 W** |

Input capacitor RMS current per channel:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

This peaks at 40 A when D = 0.5. Partial phase interleaving across the four ESC MCUs reduces the combined value.
