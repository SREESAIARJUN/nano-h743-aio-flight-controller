# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Electro-Thermal Model, Bi-Directional DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

An STM32H743 cannot run ArduPilot and also commutate 12 half-bridges in real time. The proven architecture splits the work:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 400 MHz in ArduPilot) | ArduPilot, bi-directional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (QFN32) | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3× half-bridge, bootstrap) | 12 V gate drive |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× N-MOSFET 40 V, PDFN 5×6 per ESC | 24 FETs total |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | |

**Representative MOSFET parameters.** Substitute your actual datasheet values. The model is fully parametric.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on) max @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.3 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.006 /K (gives ×1.6 at 125 °C) |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Gate-source charge after threshold | Q_gs2 | 7 nC |
| Gate-drain charge | Q_gd | 10 nC |
| Plateau voltage | V_pl | 3.0 V |
| Reverse recovery charge (hot, high di/dt) | Q_rr | 60 nC |
| Output charge @ 25 V | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Body diode V_SD (hot) | V_SD | 0.75 V |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.2 K/W |

**Operating point for "80 A burst":** I_ph = 80 A per ESC, V_bus = 25.2 V (worst case for switching), f_sw = 48 kHz, motor L_LL ≈ 20 µH.
