## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Sanity Flags

The scope needs several clarifications before any math is meaningful.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It does not commutate motors. 4-in-1 ESCs run a dedicated MCU per channel (AT32F421 / STM32G071 / GD32E230) on AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware. | Two-domain design. H743 is the FC and emits BDShot600. 4× AT32F421 run AM32 at 48 kHz PWM. |
| A2 | **80 A on a "nano" (20×20 mm) 4-in-1 is not physically sustainable.** Commercial 20×20 boards are typically rated 35–45 A continuous and 45–55 A burst. Below I show that 80 A on all four channels dissipates about 108 W. | I model 80 A as a **transient burst** and derive the allowable burst duration. I recommend 25.5×25.5 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm if 80 A is a hard requirement. |
| A3 | No MOSFET part number was specified. | I use a parametric model with a representative 40 V, 1.0 mΩ PDFN3.3 device. Replace these values with datasheet values at layout freeze. |
| A4 | Bus voltage | 6S. Burst analysis at V_bus = 24 V (sag under load from 25.2 V). |
