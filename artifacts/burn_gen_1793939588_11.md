## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology Reasoning

In 6-step trapezoidal drive, two phase legs conduct at any instant:

- The **PWM leg** alternates between its high-side switch (duty D) and its low-side synchronous rectifier (duty 1−D).
- The **return leg** low-side is fully on.

So at every instant, exactly **two switch positions** carry phase current I. This is independent of D, which is why synchronous rectification makes conduction loss duty-independent.

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent Rds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ** per FET.

With 2 FETs in parallel, and a current-sharing imbalance factor k_s ≈ 1.1:

$$
R_{sw} = \frac{R_{ds}(T_j)}{2}\,k_s = 0.80\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Channel Conduction Loss

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{sw} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.80\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{10.2\ W}
$$

### 1.4 PCB Copper Loss

The current path is the bus plane, the phase pad and the return plane. Assume about 25 mm of path at ~4 mm effective width on 2 oz (70 µm) copper, with two layers paralleled per net.

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho\, l}{w\, t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times 0.025}{0.004\times 70\times10^{-6}} \approx 1.54\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ per layer}
$$

With 2 layers in parallel per net and 2 nets in series:

$$
R_{Cu,eff} \approx 1.54\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu,eff} \approx 9.8\ \text{W}
$$

**This is as large as the MOSFET loss.** It drives the stackup rule:

- Use 6–8 layers with at least 4 layers on V_bus/GND.
- Use 3 oz outer copper where possible.
- Target R_Cu,eff ≤ 0.4 mΩ, which gives **P_Cu ≈ 2.6 W**. That target is used from here on.

### 1.5 Current-Sense Shunt

A 0.25 mΩ shunt per channel at 80 A dissipates 1.6 W. Alternatives:

- Use a single board-level 0.1 mΩ shunt.
- Use 0.2 mΩ shunts with the INA amplifier gain set accordingly.

**Baseline:** 0.2 mΩ shunt, **P_sh = 1.28 W** per channel.
