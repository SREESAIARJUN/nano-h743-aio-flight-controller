# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #69)

**Scope:** 80A burst conduction loss, 48 kHz switching loss, the thermal transient model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and a reference netlist.

---

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Flags

Three issues in the brief need to be settled before the math, because they change the results.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The H743 cannot commutate the ESC.** ArduPilot does not run motor commutation. A 4-in-1 needs either one ESC MCU per motor or a single ESC MCU driving four bridges. | Use 4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32, one per bridge. The H743 sends DShot600 to each. |
| 2 | **"80A burst" × 4 = 320A battery current.** No nano (20×20 mm) board, XT60 connector (60A continuous) or pigtail can carry this. | Treat 80A as a **per-motor, single-channel, ≤1 s** burst. The thermal model below shows ~1 s is the real ceiling even before connector limits. |
| 3 | **"Nano" thermal mass is tiny.** | The model gives about **20A continuous per channel** with prop-wash cooling. Any "80A" marketing figure must be stated with its duration. |
