**Feasibility warning up front:** 4 × 80 A means **320 A drawn from the battery**. That is far beyond any nano-class power connector (XT30 ≈ 30 A continuous, XT60 ≈ 60 A continuous) and beyond the board's thermal capacity. The math below shows that "80 A" on a nano board can only be a **sub-2-second burst rating**.

## 1. Electrical Topology (per ESC channel)

- Six-step trapezoidal (or sinusoidal) drive uses three half-bridges.
- At any instant in six-step operation:
  - One phase is PWM-switched (HS/LS complementary).
  - One phase has its LS held fully ON.
  - One phase floats, for back-EMF zero-crossing detection.
- Motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels in series**:
  - the PWM leg (HS during D, LS during 1−D), plus
  - the static LS.

## 2. Mathematical Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction loss

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \beta\,(T_j - 25)],\quad \beta \approx 0.0045\ /\text{K} \Rightarrow R_{ds}(125^\circ C) \approx 2.2\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Per channel (two devices always in path, ripple neglected since I_ripple ≪ I_DC for a motor inductance in the 10s of µH):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0022 = \mathbf{28.2\ W}$$

Per device, averaged over an electrical revolution (each FET carries the full current about 1/3 of the time):

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3} = \mathbf{4.7\ W}$$

If you add ripple, use $I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \Delta I_{pp}^2/12$.

### 2.2 Hard-switching loss (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-time (body diode) conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{dt} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.4 Reverse recovery, C_oss, and gate charge

| Loss term | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | $V\,Q_{rr}\,f = 25.2 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k}$ | 0.048 W |
| Output capacitance | $\approx Q_{oss} V f = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k}$ | 0.036 W (split HS/LS) |
| Gate drive | $2\,Q_g V_{GS} f = 2 \times 30\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k}$ | 0.029 W (in driver and R_g, not FET) |
