# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #56): Power-Stage and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture and Feasibility Notes

These points shape every calculation below.

1. **Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. Motor commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, each driving one FD6288-class 3-phase gate driver). The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. Running FOC/6-step for four motors on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not supported by ArduPilot and is not proposed.
2. **80 A is a burst rating, not continuous.** On a 30.5×30.5 mm board, the thermal model in §3 shows about 2–4 s of 80 A per channel from a warm start, and roughly 28–32 A continuous with prop-wash cooling. A 20×20 "nano" footprint cannot carry 24 power FETs at the Rds(on) assumed here, so a 30.5 mm (or 2-board stacked) ESC section is assumed.
3. **Parameters are placeholders.** All component values are design parameters. Substitute datasheet values for the selected MOSFET and driver before sign-off. The model is written parametrically so you can do this directly.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel, 6-step synchronous, high-side PWM)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step drive, two phases conduct at every instant. With synchronous rectification, the current path always passes through exactly **two** MOSFET channels:
- the PWM leg uses either the HS (for duty D) or the complementary LS (for 1−D);
