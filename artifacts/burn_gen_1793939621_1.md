## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical Angle and Frame Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count and a calibrated offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform** to the rotor frame:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park** back to the stationary frame:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant Model (SPM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 Delay Compensation

Two delays separate measurement from actuation:
- **PWM update delay** (one sample), so commands apply one sample late.
- **Zero-order hold** of half a sample.

Rotate the inverse Park with an advanced angle to compensate:

$$\theta_e^{cmd} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

### 1.4 Current PI Controllers with Decoupling

The controller combines a PI term with the cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward terms:

$$v_d^* = \underbrace{K_{p,i}e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d}_{PI} \;-\; \omega_e L\,i_q$$

$$v_q^* = K_{p,i}e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q \;+\; \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda)$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order, ω_c/(s + ω_c):

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

Keep ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s / 15 because of the 1.5-sample delay.
