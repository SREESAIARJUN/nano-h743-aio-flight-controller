## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Critical Findings

Before the derivations, three findings shape the whole design.

### 0.1 The H743 does not drive the power stage

ArduPilot on the H743 emits bidirectional DShot600 commands. Each motor channel needs its own commutation controller to run six-step/BEMF control at 48 kHz PWM. The reference architecture is:

| Function | Device |
|---|---|
| Flight control (ArduPilot) | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm), sized for a nano footprint |
| ESC control | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver, QFN24) |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFETs, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN |

### 0.2 "80A burst" is thermally a ~1–3 second rating on a nano board

The math below shows:

- 80 A on all four channels simultaneously dissipates about **120–145 W** in roughly 6 cm² of PCB.
- Adiabatic heating at that load is about **35–40 K/s**.
- Realistic continuous capability with prop-wash cooling is about **20–25 A per channel**.

This should be specified as **"80A burst (≤2 s, single-channel-dominant), 25A continuous"**, or the board will fail in field use.

### 0.3 The single shared current shunt is a hidden failure point

At 4×80 A = 320 A, even a 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates **25.6 W**. Section 2.7 addresses this.
