## 0. Architecture Feasibility Review

Before the derivations, the following issues determine how the rest of this document should be read.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The H743 does not commutate motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits DShot. Each motor channel needs a commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. A single quad-ESC MCU running AM32 is possible, but a 4×AT32F421 arrangement is the proven path. | The netlist below uses FC MCU → 4× ESC MCU → gate driver → MOSFETs. |
| 2 | **80 A burst per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not thermally credible.** The derivation in §3 shows about 84 W of dissipation against about 3 J/K of heat capacity. | Use a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC footprint, or derate to about 45–55 A burst. The math is parameterised so you can re-run it. |
| 3 | **48 kHz PWM is high for 80 A.** It is legal in AM32, but switching and dead-time losses scale linearly with it. | Shown explicitly in §2.3. 24 kHz with dynamic PWM is the recommended trade. |
| 4 | **Assumed component values must be checked against the actual BOM.** Rds(on), Qg, Qrr, and package thermal values below are representative of 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 5×6 mm (or 3.3×3.3 mm) MOSFETs. | Re-substitute datasheet values before sign-off. |

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (AM32 default "complementary PWM"). At any instant the current path is:

- **PWM leg:** high-side FET for duty D, low-side FET (synchronous) for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** low-side FET fully on.

Therefore two R_ds are always in series, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds,25}\,\alpha_T
$$

$$
P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

**Temperature feedback.** Using R_ds(T) ≈ R_ds,25·[1 + 0.005(T_j − 25)], the system has a fixed point:

$$
T_j = T_a + R_{th}\cdot P(T_j)
$$

Thermal runaway occurs when ∂P/∂T · R_th ≥ 1, that is, when 0.005·P_cond,25·R_th ≥ 1. With P_cond,25 = 12.8 W, the runaway threshold is R_th ≥ 15.6 K/W per channel. A 20×20 mm board in prop wash sits around 4–8 K/W per channel, so it is stable but close to the threshold. This is a further argument for derating.

### 2.2 Copper Loss

$$
P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W}
$$

This term is frequently ignored. On a nano board it is about 15% of the total, which is why 2 oz copper on the outer and power layers is mandatory.

### 2.3 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches (one edge pair per period).

**V–I overlap:**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
$$

$$
P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}
$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}
$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}
$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD} I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

**Gate drive** (two FETs switching per period, taken from the driver/BEC, not the FETs):

$$
P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\,\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}
$$

Total frequency-dependent loss:

$$
P_{sw}(f) \approx (P_{VI}+P_{oss}+P_{rr}+P_{dt}) = 1.69\ \text{W at 48 kHz} \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; 35.2\ \mu\text{J/cycle}
$$

### 2.4 Per-Channel and Board Totals at 80 A Burst

| Term | W/channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (hot) | 19.2 | 76.8 |
| Copper | 3.2 | 12.8 |
| Switching + dead time | 1.69 | 6.8 |
| Gate drive | 0.05 | 0.2 |
| **Total** | **24.1** | **96.6 W** |

At 24 kHz, the switching term halves to about 0.85 W per channel, saving about 3.4 W across the board. Conduction dominates by roughly 11:1, so the effective levers are lower R_ds, more copper, and paralleled FETs, not lower f_sw.

**Efficiency check.** Assume D = 0.8 at burst, giving an output of about 25.2 × 0.8 × 80 ≈ 1.61 kW per motor. Then η ≈ 1 − 24.1/1613 ≈ **98.5%**.

### 2.5 DC-Link Capacitor Ripple

The worst case is at D = 0.5:

$$
I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80 \times 0.5 = 40\ \text{A}_{rms}\ \text{per channel}
$$

Because the four channels are not phase-locked, the shared bulk capacitance sees roughly √4 × 40 ≈ 80 A_rms.

Per-channel local MLCCs: about 6 × 10 µF/50 V X7R in 1206. Allow for DC-bias derating to roughly 3 µF effective each
