```
6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──┬── Bulk/MLCC bank ──┬── ESC ch1..ch4  (each: MCU + 3-ph gate driver + 6 FETs)

                       └── 5V/3V3 BEC ──────┴── STM32H743 FC ── DShot600 (bidir) ──► ESC MCUs
```

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-Step, Complementary (Synchronous) PWM

In any commutation sector, two legs are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET is on for duty D; the low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is on 100 % of the sector.

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$
