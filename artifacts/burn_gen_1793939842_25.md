### Architectural correction: the H743 does not drive the bridges

An STM32H743 cannot practically run four sensorless BLDC commutation loops, and ArduPilot has no ESC firmware. The correct partition is:

- **STM32H743**: ArduPilot. Emits 4× bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32): commutation, PWM at 48 kHz, BEMF sensing.
- **4× 3-phase gate drivers and 24 MOSFETs.**

The netlist in §5 reflects this partition.

### Feasibility red flags (go/no-go items for design review)

1. **4 × 80 A = 320 A battery current.** This exceeds an XT60 (≈60 A continuous, ~180 A burst), typical 12 AWG leads, and any nano-scale input pad. Either "80 A" is a per-channel non-simultaneous burst, or the spec is unrealistic. My recommendation is to specify it as **80 A single-channel / 160 A aggregate burst**, and to confirm this with the customer.
2. **Burst duration on this footprint is limited to ≈1.3 s** (derived in §3). Marketing must not quote "80 A" without a duration qualifier.
3. **A current-sense shunt sized for 320 A dissipates watts.** See §5.4.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At any instant in a sector, one phase is the **PWM leg** (HS and LS alternate at duty D), one phase is the **return leg** (LS held on), and the third phase floats for BEMF sensing. Exactly **two FET channels carry the full phase current I** at any instant:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot \big(R_{DS,HS}\,D + R_{DS,LS}(1-D)\big) + I^2 R_{DS,LS}
\;\approx\; 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

The temperature-corrected resistance is:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow k_T(125^\circ\text{C})\approx 1.6
$$

Copper and interconnect losses add to this. Each channel has the bridge loop plus phase pad, approximately R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ for 2 oz with vias, giving:

$$
P_{Cu,ch} = I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on) hot | 1.4 mΩ × 1.6 | 2.24 mΩ |
| P_cond per channel | 2 × 80² × 2.24 mΩ | **28.7 W** |
| P_Cu per channel | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | **1.9 W** |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts during 2 of the 6 sectors (120° of 360° electrical). At electrical frequencies above ~200 Hz the die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) partially averages this, so:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 4.8\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{FET,peak} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{2} \approx 14.3\ \text{W (low-eRPM worst case)}
$$

**Design rule:** at low eRPM with high current (stall, punch-out from hover), the junction tracks the 14.3 W peak, not the average. Check the transient thermal impedance curve Z_θJC(t) at t = one sector duration.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The return-leg LS is static within a sector.

### 2.1 Transition times from gate charge

Assume a Miller plateau V_pl ≈ 3.5 V and driver voltage V_dr = 10 V.

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{dr}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{6.5}{5} = 1.3\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.7\ \text{A}
$$

$$
t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{1.3} \approx 6.9\ \text{ns}, \qquad
t_{f} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}} = \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{0.7} \approx 12.9\ \text{ns}
$$

With layout parasitics, round up to t_r + t_f ≈ **20 ns**.
