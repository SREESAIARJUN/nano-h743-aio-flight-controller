## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Six-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

During any commutation sector, two phase legs carry current:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high side conducts for D; the low side (synchronous) conducts for 1−D.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low side conducts for 100% of the sector.

$$P_{cond,path} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of duty cycle, which is the key insight for burst sizing.

### 1.1 Electro-thermal self-consistency (worst-case FET = return-leg LS)

The return-leg low side conducts continuously. Its loss and junction temperature depend on each other:

$$P_{FET} = I^2 R_{ds25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \qquad T_j = T_b + P_{FET}R_{th,jb}$$

Solving for T_j:

$$\boxed{T_j = \frac{T_b + I^2R_{ds25}(1-25\alpha)R_{th,jb}}{1 - I^2R_{ds25}\,\alpha\,R_{th,jb}}}$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:** the denominator must stay positive, i.e. $I^2R_{ds25}\alpha R_{th,jb} < 1$.

Numbers at T_b = 70 °C (board temperature at the end of the burst; see §3):
- I²R_ds25 = 6400 × 0.7 mΩ = 4.48 W
- Runaway factor = 4.48 × 0.006 × 8 = **0.215** (margin of 4.6×; safe)
- T_j = (70 + 4.48 × 0.85 × 8) / 0.785 = **128 °C**
- R_ds(128 °C) = 0.70 × 1.618 = **1.13 mΩ**
- P_FET,worst = **7.24 W**

Conduction loss per motor:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.13\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{14.5\ W}$$

### 1.2 Bus-side copper and shunt

The bus current is I_bus = D·I_phase. At full-throttle burst (D → 1), I_bus,total ≈ 320 A peak across four motors.

- A 0.25 mΩ total-current shunt dissipates 0.25 mΩ × 320² = **25.6 W**, which is unacceptable. Use per-channel shunts or a sensorless/ADC-estimated current.
- With per-channel shunts at 80 A, each dissipates **1.6 W**.
- Copper: a 2 oz pour has about 0.25 mΩ/□. Budget **≈ 1 W/motor** for the trace from bus to phase pad on a 20×20 board.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per motor, only the PWM leg switches hard; the synchronous FET switches at near-zero voltage.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·30 ns·48k | 1.452 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50 nC·25.2·48k | 0.060 W |
| Coss | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48k | 0.614 W |
| Gate (mostly in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 0.048 W |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 2.21 W/motor** |

- Dead-time diode loss is about 28% of switching loss. Halving t_dt to 50 ns saves 0.3 W/motor, if the driver propagation-delay matching allows it.
