# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Electrothermal Model and ArduPilot BDShot600 Allocation (Job #21)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

Three points shape the rest of this document.

1. **Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, an AT32F421 running AM32 (BLHeli_32 is discontinued). The interface between them is 4× bidirectional DShot600.
2. **No MOSFET or gate driver was specified.** I modeled a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN device and an FD6288Q-class driver. All numbers scale with the parameters in §1. Substitute your datasheet values before freezing the design.
3. **80 A on a true 20×20 mm "nano" board is only a short burst rating.** The model below gives about **1 s** of all-motor 80 A before reaching the junction design limit. That is a burst rating, not a continuous one. I recommend relabeling it as "80 A / 1 s burst, ~35–40 A continuous with airflow", or moving to a 25.5/30.5 mm form factor.
