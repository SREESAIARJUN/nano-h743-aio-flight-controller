## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly in a sensible design. The partition is:

| Block | Device (reference) | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS HAL) | Attitude control; sends bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 | AM32 | 6-step commutation with complementary PWM at 48 kHz; returns eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver | n/a | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power FETs (×24) | 40 V N-channel, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | n/a | 6 per motor |

**Assumptions used throughout:**
- Battery: 6S, V_DC = 25.2 V (full charge, worst case for switching loss).
- Burst current: **80 A phase current per motor**. At about 95 % duty this is roughly equal to DC current, giving 320 A total from the pack.
- Switching frequency: 48 kHz.
- Modulation: 6-step trapezoidal (AM32). One leg PWMs its high-side FET with complementary low-side synchronous rectification, and one leg's low-side FET is held on statically.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Per-FET RMS current in 6-step operation

Over one electrical period, each leg spends:
- one third as the PWM leg (high side on for duty D, low side on for 1 − D),
- one third as the sink leg (low side statically on),
- one third floating (used for back-EMF zero-cross detection).

The per-FET RMS currents squared are:

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = I^2\frac{D}{3}, \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = I^2\left(\frac{1-D}{3}+\frac{1}{3}\right)$$

Summed over one leg:

$$\sum = I^2 \cdot \tfrac{2}{3}$$

Summed over three legs this gives 2I². This matches the physical picture: current always flows through exactly two FETs in series.

### 2.2 Conduction loss equations

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on),hot}$$

$$P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R\frac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R\frac{2-D}{3}$$

### 2.3 Numbers at I = 80 A, D = 0.95, R = 1.92 mΩ

Base term: I²R = 6400 × 1.92×10⁻³ = **12.29 W**

| Element | Result |
|---|---|
| High-side FET: 12.29 × 0.95/3 | **3.89 W** |
| Low-side FET: 12.29 × 1.05/3 | **4.30 W** (hottest device) |
| Per motor: 2 × 12.29 | **24.6 W** |
| 4-in-1 total | **98.3 W** |
