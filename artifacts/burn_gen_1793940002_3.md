## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate motors. Each of the four motor channels has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32), plus a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 speaks bidirectional DShot600 to those four MCUs.

```
VBAT ─┬─ Bulk/MLCC bank ─┬─ ESC1: MCU + FD6288Q + 6×MOSFET ── Motor 1
      │                  ├─ ESC2 ... ESC4
      ├─ 10V/5V/3V3 regulators
      └─ STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── 4× bidir DShot600 ──> ESC MCUs
```
