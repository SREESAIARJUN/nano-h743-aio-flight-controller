**Loop architecture.** The encoder handles commutation and joint-angle limits. The rate and angle loops close on the inertial camera attitude, i.e. fused gyro projected onto each joint axis. This is what makes the gimbal *stabilize* rather than just hold joint position.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control: Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, assuming balanced currents (iₐ + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

The PMSM model in the rotating frame (surface magnets, L_d = L_q = L) is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Add the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms as feedforward:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

With these, each axis reduces to the first-order plant 1/(Ls + R).

### 1.3 PI Gains by Pole-Zero Cancellation

Choose the PI as C(s) = K_p + K_i/s. Set the PI zero equal to the plant pole (K_i/K_p = R/L). The open loop then becomes K_p/(Ls), so the closed loop is first order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$
