## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 2.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi, \qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m$$

`θ_off` is found at calibration by applying the voltage vector `v_d = V_align`, `v_q = 0` with θe = 0 and reading θm.

### 2.2 Clarke transform (power-invariant amplitude form, balanced currents)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

### 2.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.4 Plant model (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
