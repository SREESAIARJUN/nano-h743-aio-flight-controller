I don't have the Job #19 hardware sheet, so the plant parameters below are **explicit assumptions**. Every gain is derived from them in closed form, so you can substitute your measured values directly.

| Symbol | Meaning | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| $p$ | Pole pairs | 7 (gimbal motor, 12N14P) |
| $N_s$ | Stator slots | 12 |
| $R_s,\ L_s$ | Phase resistance and inductance | 5.6 Ω, 2.2 mH |
| $K_t$ | Torque constant | 0.11 N·m/A |
| $J_{\text{pitch}},\ J_{\text{roll}},\ J_{\text{yaw}}$ | Reflected inertia | 2.5e-4, 4.0e-4, 7.0e-4 kg·m² |
| $V_{bus}$ | Bus voltage | 12–16.8 V (4S) |
| Encoder | Absolute magnetic encoder | 14-bit (e.g., AS5047P / MT6701) |
| IMUs | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate | 2 units, separate SPI buses |
