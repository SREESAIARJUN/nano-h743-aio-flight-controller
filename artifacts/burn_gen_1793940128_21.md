# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #36): System Architecture & Mathematical Model

## 0. Critical Corrections Before Design

Three findings change the architecture. They should be resolved before layout.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In every ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel (or a shared ESC MCU) runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware on a small MCU such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only sends DShot600 commands and receives bidirectional (eRPM) telemetry. | The switching-loss model below belongs to the ESC power stage. The DMA model belongs to the H743. They are decoupled through the DShot link. |
| 2 | **"Nano" (20×20 mm) with 80 A burst on all four channels is thermally impossible.** Section 3 shows ~130 W of dissipation into ~5 J/K of board thermal mass. Junctions reach their limit in ≈3.5 s, even with optimistic assumptions. | 80 A should be specified as a **single-channel, ≤10 s transient** with the other channels at cruise. Alternatively, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm footprint with 5×6 mm FETs. |
| 3 | **All device parameters below are representative assumptions.** They are not datasheet-verified values for a specific BOM. | Re-run the model (Section 3.6) with the actual MOSFET datasheet values: R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, Q_rr, Z_th(jc). |

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

**Temperature-dependent resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.5 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ**.

**Current path.** In six-step with complementary PWM, each 60° sector conducts through exactly two FET channels in series at every instant:
- PWM phase: the high-side FET during D·T, then the low-side synchronous FET during (1−D)·T.
- Sink phase: the low-side FET for 100% of the sector.
