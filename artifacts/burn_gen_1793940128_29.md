### 0.1 Architecture Clarification

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors in this class of product.

- **ESC side:** Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware.
- **FC side:** The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to those four ESC MCUs.

The DMA analysis in §4 therefore applies to the H743-to-ESC link.

### 0.2 Headline Result

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Loss per channel at 80 A | ≈ 26.6 W |
| Loss with all 4 channels at 80 A | ≈ 106 W |
| Adiabatic board heating rate | ≈ 24 K/s |
| Usable burst duration (from a 40 °C board) | ≈ 1.5–2.5 s |

80 A is a **burst** rating, not a continuous rating. Firmware current limiting and temperature derating are mandatory.

### 0.3 Form-Factor Warning

A true 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4 × 80 A for longer than about 1 s:

- It has roughly half the thermal mass of the 30.5 mm board.
- DFN3.3 copper fan-out limits it.

For a 20×20 product, I recommend marketing it as 40–45 A continuous / 55 A burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.383 = **1.66 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-Step Current Path

At any instant, phase current I flows through exactly two half-bridge legs (one sourcing, one sinking). The third leg floats for BEMF zero-cross sensing.

With synchronous rectification, each active leg always presents exactly one conducting FET
