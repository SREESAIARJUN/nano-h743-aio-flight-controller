# STM32H743 FC + Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Power Stage, Thermal, and BDShot Architecture

This document assumes a 6S nano (20×20 mm mount) stack. MOSFET and driver values are representative placeholders. Replace every parameter marked **[DS]** with your actual BOM datasheet values before sign-off.

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo: V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Topology | 3-phase full bridge × 4, synchronous (complementary) PWM, 6-step trapezoidal |
| ESC firmware/MCU | AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071 (one per motor, or one per two motors). The **H743 does not drive gates.** It sends DShot to the ESC MCUs. |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz |
| MOSFET **[DS]** | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, Q_oss ≈ 40 nC |
| Gate driver **[DS]** | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver, about 1.5 A source/sink. Gives t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 20 ns at the switch node. |
| Dead time | 200 ns per edge |
| Rating | 80 A **per motor, phase current, burst** |

**Feasibility warning.** 80 A × 4 on a 20×20 board is not thermally sustainable. The math in §3 shows it is a burst rating measured in seconds. Treat "80 A" as a transient spec with a firmware-enforced time limit, not a continuous one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \textbf{[DS]}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

At any instant, exactly two phases conduct. One FET in each active leg carries the phase current I. With synchronous rectification, the PWMing leg alternates between HS and LS, but one FET is always in the path. Each leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle.

Per leg:
$$P_{cond,leg} = \tfrac{2}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per motor (3 legs):
$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per motor | Average per FET (÷6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.13 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W | 2.93 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** | **3.2 W** |

**Peak-FET caveat.** The LS FET of the non-PWM leg conducts continuously for its 1/3-cycle. Over the full cycle its average loss is about I²R/3, which equals the table value. However, its instantaneous dissipation is 9.6 W, so check Z_th(j-c) at the commutation period:

- At 30,000 eRPM: t_elec = 2 ms, so each FET sees roughly 0.67 ms pulses.
- Z_th at 1 ms is typically about 0.2 K/W for this package class. This gives ΔT_ripple ≈ 2 K, which is negligible.

### 1.4 Other copper losses (per motor, 80 A)

| Element | R | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace (2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ) **[layout]** | 0.3 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 0.3 m ≈ 3.8 W |
| Bus shunt (shared, 0.25 mΩ) | carries I_bus, not I_phase | see §3.1 |

### 1.5 Self-consistent junction temperature

R_DS depends on T_j, so iterate:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}\big(T_j^{(n)}\big)\cdot R_{th,j-board}$$

With R_th,j-board ≈ 2 K/W **[DS + via design]**, the iteration converges in 3–4 steps.

**Thermal runaway condition.** Runaway occurs if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} \geq 1$$

Here ∂P/∂T = I²·R₂₅·α·(2/6) per FET, which is about 0.0107 W/K. Multiplied by 2 K/W this gives 0.02, so the FET itself is stable. The real limit is the board heat sink (§3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only **one leg per motor is hard-switching** at a time (the PWM leg). V = 25.2 V (worst case) and I = 80 A.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss (hard-switched HS turn-on and turn-off)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{DT} = V_F\,I\,(2\,t_{dead})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = V\,Q_{rr}\,f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\text{ nC} \times 48\text{ k} = 0.06\text{ W}$$

Q_rr scales with di/dt and I_F; at 80 A, budget 2–3× the datasheet value, so about 0.15 W.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\,V\,f_{sw} \approx 40\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.05\text{ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate charge

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{ n} \times 10 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.048\text{ W per motor}$$
