# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #43): System Model

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The numbers that follow depend on these choices. Every parameter is labeled so you can substitute your actual BOM values.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | **Stacked pair.** The FC runs ArduPilot on an STM32H743. The 4-in-1 ESC has **four independent ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 does *not* commutate the motors. It sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm mounting, about 25×25 mm board outline. Both boards use 6-layer stackups with 2 oz copper on the outer layers. |
| Battery | 4S (16.8 V max). 6S is covered as a variant in §1.5. |
| MOSFET (4S) | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN. R<sub>DS(on),25°C</sub> = 1.0 mΩ, Q<sub>g</sub> = 60 nC, Q<sub>gd</sub> = 8 nC, Q<sub>rr</sub> ≈ 40 nC, R<sub>θJC</sub> = 1.0 K/W. These are representative values; replace them with your datasheet's. |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class three-phase bootstrap driver, one per channel. Peak drive is about 1–1.5 A. |
| Drive scheme | 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM at f<sub>sw</sub> = 48 kHz. Dead time is 100 ns. |
| Burst | 80 A per channel. §1.4 covers both 1 channel and all 4 channels at 80 A. |

> **Feasibility flag:** 4 × 80 A is 320 A of battery current. That exceeds any XT60/XT30 lead, nano-board copper pour, or most 4S packs. Treat "80 A burst" as a **per-channel, seconds-scale** rating. Don't treat it as a simultaneous rating. The thermal model below quantifies exactly how many seconds.

## 1. Power-Stage Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step drive with synchronous rectification, phase current flows through exactly **two** conducting FETs at any instant: one in the PWM-ing leg (HS or LS, depending on PWM state) and one in the static low-side leg. Ripple current at 48 kHz with motor inductance of 10 µH or more is small, so I<sub>rms</sub> ≈ I<sub>dc</sub>.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Temperature dependence of R<sub>DS(on)</sub>:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C the multiplier is k<sub>T</sub> ≈ 1.6, so R<sub>DS(on)</sub> = 1.6 mΩ.

$$P_{cond,ch}(80\text{ A}) = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.6\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

Averaged over one electrical cycle, this loss is shared across 6 FETs, so each FET dissipates about **3.4 W average**. The instantaneous loss in a conducting FET is **10.2 W**.
