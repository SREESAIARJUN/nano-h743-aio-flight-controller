**Rev A: architecture baseline**

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Caveats

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four per-channel ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU is assumed to be an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. They drive the gate drivers and MOSFETs. Every loss figure below belongs to the ESC power stage. The DShot and DMA section belongs to the H743.

All device parameters are **design assumptions** for a typical 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values into the equations.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S, full charge | 25.2 V |
| I_ph | Burst phase current, per channel | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds,25 | R_DS(on) max at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | 0.0056 /K |
| Q_g / Q_gs2 / Q_gd | Gate charges | 50 / 5 / 10 nC |
| Q_rr | Body-diode recovery charge (high di/dt) | 60 nC |
| Q_oss | Output charge at 25 V | 40 nC |
| V_f | Body-diode forward voltage at 80 A | 0.8 V |
| V_plateau | Miller plateau voltage | 3.5 V |
| V_drv | Gate-drive voltage | 10 V |
| R_g,total | Driver + external + internal gate resistance | 5 Ω |
| t_dt | Dead time | 150 ns |
| R_θJC | Junction-to-case thermal resistance, per FET | 1.2 K/W |
| Topology | One FET per switch position; 6 FETs × 4 = 24 FETs | — |

> **Headline finding:** a nano-class board with a single FET per position **cannot sustain 80 A**. "80 A burst" on this platform physically means about 3 s with all four motors at 80 A, or about 10–12 s with one motor at 80 A. Section 3 proves this. Firmware current limiting is mandatory.
