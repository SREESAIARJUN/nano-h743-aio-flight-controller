# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #28): System Architecture & Mathematical Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges directly. In this architecture:

- **The FC (H743, ArduPilot)** generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **The 4-in-1 ESC** has four local ESC MCUs (e.g., STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32). They generate 48 kHz complementary PWM through 3-phase gate drivers into 24 MOSFETs.

All component values below are **reference parameters**. Replace them with your selected MOSFET datasheet values and re-run the model. Any formula marked ⚙ is parametric.
