# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #12): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-directional DShot Engineering Model

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Design Flags

Before deriving anything, here are the architectural assumptions. If any are wrong, the results change.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | AIO board: STM32H743 runs ArduPilot (flight control). It drives **four independent ESC MCUs** (one per motor, e.g. AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32 class firmware) over bi-directional DShot600. The H743 does **not** commutate MOSFETs directly. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V maximum. MOSFETs rated 40 V. |
| "80A burst" | **Per motor**, about 10 s. If 80 A is the *total* board rating, divide all I² terms by 16. |
| Form factor | "Nano" means a 20×20 mm mounting pattern, roughly 25×25 mm board, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs. |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal commutation. |
| Device parameters | Representative values only. Substitute datasheet values for the selected MOSFET before release. |

**Design flag.** The thermal results in Section 3 show that 80 A per motor on a 20×20 board is a **sub-3-second transient rating**, not a 10-second one. Sections 3.4 and 7 cover this in detail.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

During any commutation sector, three MOSFETs are involved:
- **PWM phase:** high-side (HS) conducts for duty D, low-side (LS) for (1−D). The LS conducts as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return phase:** LS conducts continuously.
- **Floating phase:** off, used for BEMF sensing.

Phase current I_ph flows through exactly two MOSFET channels at every instant:

```
P_cond,motor = I_ph,rms² · R_DS(on)(T_j) · [ D + (1−D) + 1 ] = 2 · I_ph,rms² · R_DS(on)(T_j)
```
