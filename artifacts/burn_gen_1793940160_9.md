## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Stated Assumptions

**Who generates the 48 kHz PWM.** On a 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an STM32G071 or AT32F421. That ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only sends bidirectional DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry back. The model is partitioned the same way:

- **ESC power stage:** conduction losses, switching losses, thermal behaviour.
- **H743:** DShot timers and DMA.

**"Nano" and 80 A are in tension.** The thermal math below shows that 80 A per motor is a **burst** rating of roughly 1.5–4 s, depending on board size. It is not a continuous rating. I state the realistic continuous figure explicitly so the spec sheet does not overclaim.
