# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #67): System Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Allocation and Netlist

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Findings

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate motors. A production-grade split is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 at 400 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, bi-directional DShot600 master |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421 / STM32G071-class MCU | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Power stage (×4) | 3-phase gate driver plus 6 N-MOSFETs | none | Bridge |

**Finding 1: "Nano" and "80 A burst" are incompatible.** The model below shows ~23 W of dissipation per channel at 80 A. The four channels together produce ~90 W, and the junctions reach their limit in roughly 2–3 s, even on a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board with heavy copper.

- **On a 20 × 20 mm board,** a realistic rating is 25–35 A burst and 15–20 A continuous.
- **Recommendation:** either enlarge the board to 30.5 × 30.5 mm and specify "80 A / 2 s," or keep the nano size and derate.

The numbers below make this quantitative.
