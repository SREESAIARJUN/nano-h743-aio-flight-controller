## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active leg. At any instant, current flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels**:

- **Active interval (duty D):** HS of phase A and LS of phase B.
- **Freewheel interval (1−D):** LS of phase A (synchronous rectification) and LS of phase B.

The series resistance is therefore 2·R_DS(on) regardless of duty cycle. This is why ESC conduction loss is nearly duty-independent once synchronous rectification is enabled.

### 2.2 Equations

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)] \approx R_{25}\cdot k(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{pcb}$$

The distribution across the six FETs over one electrical revolution:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{P_{cond,ch} - I^2R_{pcb}}{6}\ \ \text{(average)},\qquad P_{cond,FET,peak} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\ \ \text{(during its conduction window)}$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

- R_DS(110 °C) = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**
- MOSFET conduction: 2 × 80² × 1.8×10⁻³ = **23.04 W**
- PCB copper: 80² × 0.5×10⁻³ = **3.20 W**
- **P_cond,ch = 26.24 W per channel**

Peak dissipation in a single conducting FET is 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **11.5 W** while it conducts. This sets the junction-to-case rise in §3.3.
