## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Phase current versus DC current

In 6-step drive, two phases conduct per 60° sector. The DC-link current relates to phase current through duty cycle D:

$$I_{DC} = D \cdot I_{ph} \quad\Rightarrow\quad I_{ph} = \frac{I_{DC}}{D}$$

This is the most commonly missed effect. **At partial throttle the phase current exceeds the battery current.** "80 A DC" at D = 0.8 means 100 A in the FETs.

### 1.2 Per-sector conduction with synchronous rectification

Within one sector, current flows through three paths:
- The high-side FET of leg A for a fraction D of the time.
- The low-side FET of leg A for the remaining 1−D (synchronous freewheel).
- The low-side FET of leg B for 100% of the time.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The temperature dependence of R_DS(on) is:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0059\ /\mathrm{K}\ \text{(gives ×1.5 at 110 °C)}$$

### 1.3 Numerical results (R_DS = 1.5 mΩ hot)

| Case | I_ph | P_cond per channel | Per FET (÷6, averaged over rotation) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80 A DC, D ≈ 1.0 | 80 A | 2·6400·1.5e-3 = **19.2 W** | 3.2 W |
| 80 A DC, D = 0.8 | 100 A | **30.0 W** | 5.0 W |
| 25 A DC, D ≈ 1.0 | 25 A | 1.9 W | 0.31 W |

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect losses

These are not negligible on a nano board. Assume about 0.5 mΩ of effective series resistance from pad, trace, via and solder paths per phase path, with two paths in series:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 2 \cdot I_{ph}^2 \cdot 0.5\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = 6.4\ \mathrm{W}\ \text{at 80 A}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches. The terms below are per channel, using V = 25.2 V and I = 80 A.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f = 0.5·25.2·80·40e-9·48e3 | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_F·I·2·t_dt·f = 0.8·80·2·200e-9·48e3 | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 50e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f (approx.) | 0.04 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g, 2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f = 2·60e-9·12·48e3 | 0.07 W |
| **Total P_sw per channel** | | **≈ 3.3 W** |

**Design levers:**
- **Dead time.** Losses here are larger than most designers expect. Trimming from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel, provided you verify there is no shoot-through.
- **Switching frequency.** Moving from 48 kHz to 24 kHz halves P_sw. The cost is more audible noise and higher current ripple in low-inductance motors.
