## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = N_p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

Here θ_off is found at commissioning: apply a d-axis voltage vector at θ_e = 0 and read the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Torque for a surface PMSM:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 N_p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

Use the controller

$$
C_i(s)=K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}, \qquad K_p = L\,\omega_{c},\quad K_i = R\,\omega_{c}
$$

With these gains the PI zero cancels the plant pole R/L. The closed loop becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c.

- Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10. For example, ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s.
- Discretize with forward Euler: I_k = I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k. This is adequate because ω_c T_s ≪ 1.

### 1.6 Decoupling feedforward

$$
v_d^{*} = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e \hat L\, i_q,\qquad
v_q^{*} = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e \hat L\, i_d + \omega_e \hat\lambda_m
$$

### 1.7 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

The inscribed circle of the SVPWM hexagon gives the voltage limit:

$$
V_{\max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{\max},\qquad m_{\max}\approx 0.95
$$

- d-axis priority: |v_d| ≤ V_max.
- q-axis limit: |v_q| ≤ √(V_max² − v_d²).
- Integrators use back-calculation: I_k += K_aw (v_sat − v_unsat).

### 1.8 Inverse Park and SVPWM

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

Inverse Clarke:

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\;\; v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\;\; v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

SVPWM by min-max zero-sequence injection (equivalent to classical 7-segment SVPWM):

$$
