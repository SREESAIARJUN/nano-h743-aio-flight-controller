## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, current flows through one high-side FET (phase A) and one low-side FET (phase B).

With complementary PWM at duty D:
- **HS_A** conducts for D·T.
- **LS_A** (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D)·T, minus dead time.
- **LS_B** conducts for the full sector.

So at every instant, about **two R_DS(on) channels are in series**, independent of D. Burst current means near-full throttle, where D → 1.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.3 mΩ × 1.375 = **1.79 mΩ**.

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.3 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.95 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I^2\left(2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\right)$$

Here R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ covers PCB copper, vias, and the solder pads from phase pad to bus.

| T_j | 2R_DS + R_Cu | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 2.9 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74 W |
| 100 °C | 3.88 mΩ | **24.8 W** | **99 W** |
| 125 °C | 4.2 mΩ | 26.9 W | 108 W |

### 1.4 Positive feedback

P_cond rises with T_j, and T_j rises with P_cond. The stability condition for the junction-to-sink loop is:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{25}\alpha \cdot R_{th} < 1$$

At 80 A: I² · 2R₂₅α = 6400 × 2.6e-3 × 0.005 = 0.083 W/K.

- For junction-to-board (R_th ≈ 2 K/W) the loop gain is 0.17. This is stable.
- For board-to-air (R_th ≫ 12 K/W) the loop gain exceeds 1. **Thermal runaway in steady state.** This confirms the 80 A condition is burst-only.
