## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

The physical claims and architecture need to be settled before deriving anything.

**Assumed architecture (standard stack topology):**
- **FC board:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It sends DShot600 with bidirectional telemetry (BDShot).
- **ESC board:** a 4-in-1 with **four independent ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, or EFM8/BLHeli_S-class). Each MCU drives one 3-phase bridge via a 3-phase gate driver (e.g., FD6288Q).
- The H743 does **not** commutate motors. If you intend the H743 to commutate motors directly (single-MCU FC+ESC), DShot disappears entirely. That would require a separate design built on 3× advanced timers plus ADC injection, so tell me if that is the intent.

**Feasibility flags:**

| Claim | Reality check |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step (trapezoidal) with synchronous rectification
During each 60° commutation sector, current flows through **one high-side and one low-side FET** in different legs. With complementary PWM:
- **PWM on (duty D):** HS_a + LS_b conduct.
- **PWM off (1−D):** LS_a (synchronous rectifier) + LS_b conduct.

So **exactly two channels are always in conduction** (ignoring dead time). This gives a total conduction loss per ESC that is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 RMS with ripple
For a triangular ripple on a DC level:

$$I_{rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{1 + \frac{r^2}{12}} = 80\sqrt{1 + \frac{0.04}{12}} = 80.13\ \text{A}$$

### 1.3 Temperature-coupled solution
$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per ESC | P_cond, 4 ESC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.84 W | 51.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.2 W | 68.8 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.5 W |

### 1.4 Per-device distribution
The sector rotates every 60°e, so each leg's HS conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period and LS for 1/3 plus the freewheel share. The worst single device is the **low-side of the "return" leg** during its sector:

$$P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R_{ds} = 80^2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = 9.3\ \text{W (instantaneous, during its 120° interval)}$$

The time-averaged per-device loss is about P_cond,ESC / 6 ≈ 3.1 W. However, at low eRPM (e.g., stall or punch-out from zero) the sector period approaches the FET's thermal time constants, so **design to the 9.3 W instantaneous figure**.

### 1.5 Copper / interconnect conduction
For 2 oz (70 µm) copper, sheet resistance is R_□ = ρ/t = 1.72e-8 / 70e-6 = 0.246 mΩ/□.

A 4-layer stack with 3 paralleled power layers gives ≈0.082 mΩ/□. A typical phase path is about 6 squares, giving ≈0.5 mΩ. Phase copper loss is therefore 80² × 0.5 mΩ ≈ **3.2 W per ESC**.

This is not negligible: it adds about 17% to device conduction loss. Mitigations:
- Use 3 oz outer layers.
- Stitch each power plane with more than 30 vias (0.3 mm) per amp-heavy transition. Each via is about 1 mΩ, so you need many in parallel.
