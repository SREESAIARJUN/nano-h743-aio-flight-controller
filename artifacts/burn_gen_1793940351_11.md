# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the Job #35 hardware specification, so I've used the baseline below. All values are parameters in the code. Replace them with your measured values.

|---|---|

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle and transforms

Electrical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here θ_{m,0} is the encoder offset found during alignment, by injecting d-axis current with θ_e = 0.
