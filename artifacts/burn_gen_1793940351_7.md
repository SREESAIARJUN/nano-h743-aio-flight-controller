### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count, and a calibrated encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (three-shunt measurement, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$
