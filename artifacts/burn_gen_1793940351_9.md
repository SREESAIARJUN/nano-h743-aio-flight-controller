# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

**Assumptions (Job #46 parameters were not provided, so adjust these to your hardware):**

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A or MT6701 class), one per axis |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted symmetric about the plate centroid, SPI at 24 MHz |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G4/H7 class) with single-precision FPU |
| Loop rates | Current 32 kHz, velocity 8 kHz, angle/attitude 1 kHz |
