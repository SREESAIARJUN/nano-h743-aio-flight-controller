# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A Burst 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Models, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlist

## 0. Architecture Correction

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four 6-step bridges at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. That would require 12 complementary PWM outputs, 4× back-EMF comparators, and microsecond-level commutation latency alongside the EKF.

The sound partition is a two-board stack:

| Board | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| FC (20×20 mm, M3 mount or 25.5 whoop) | STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot | Attitude control, EKF, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 | Per-motor 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF commutation, eRPM telemetry |

Everything below assumes this split.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in six-step with synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, one phase is PWM-driven and one is the return phase:

- **PWM phase:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low side (LS) synchronously freewheels for 1−D.
- **Return phase:** the LS conducts 100%.

The instantaneous conduction loss per motor is therefore duty-independent:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1+\frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I}\right)^2\right),\qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

Take L_ph = 15 µH (low-kV 5" motor), V_bemf ≈ 0.85·V_bus, and D ≈ 0.95:

$$
\Delta I \approx \frac{3.8 \times 0.95}{15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 5\,\text{A}
$$

The ripple factor is about 1.0003, which is negligible at 80 A. It matters only at low current with low-inductance motors.

### 2.3 Temperature-coupled solution

Conduction loss and junction temperature are coupled:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,Z_{th,jc}(t)
$$

Solve by fixed-point iteration. At T_j = 110 °C, R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005·85) = **1.425 mΩ**:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.425\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.2\ W}
$$

### 2.4 Per-FET distribution over one electrical cycle

Each phase is PWM-driven for 1/3 of the cycle, return-low for 1/3, and floating for 1/3:

$$
P_{HS}=\frac{I^2R\,D}{3},\qquad P_{LS}=\frac{I^2R\,[(1-D)+1]}{3}
$$

| D | P_HS | P_LS | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.00 | 3.04 W | 3.04 W | Full-throttle punch |
| 0.50 | 1.52 W | 4.56 W | **LS is the hot device** at partial throttle and high current |

**Design rule:** size LS copper and vias for 1.5× the HS heat load.
