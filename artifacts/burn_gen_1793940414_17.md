## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, motor phase current I_ph always flows through exactly two conducting MOSFET channels:

- one in the "source" leg: HS during the on-time D, or LS synchronous rectification during 1−D;
- one LS in the "sink" leg.

Because phase current is continuous through the inductive winding, **conduction loss depends on I_ph, not on the battery current I_bat = D·I_ph.** This is the most common error in ESC thermal budgets. A 30 A battery reading at 40 % throttle can mean 75 A in the FETs.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.60 = **1.92 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \left( 2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu} \right)$$

R_Cu is the copper path from battery pad → bus → FET → phase pad → return, estimated at 0.6 mΩ for 2 oz × 8 layers with via stitching.

| Term | Formula | @ 80 A, T_j = 125 °C |
|---|---|---|
| FET channels | 80² × 2 × 1.92 mΩ | **24.6 W** |
| Copper | 80² × 0.6 mΩ | **3.8 W** |
| **Channel total** | | **28.4 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each phase conducts for 2/3 of the electrical period. The two-FET load is therefore spread across 6 devices with a time-weighting of 1/3 each:

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = \frac{24.6}{3} \approx 8.2\ \text{W (average)}$$

**Commutation ripple.** At low eRPM, a single FET holds the full I²R for the whole 60° conduction interval. Is this significant for the thermal model? It depends on how the sector duration compares with the device's thermal time constant (τ_jc ≈ 1–5 ms for PDFN 3.3):

- Example at 40,000 eRPM: sector ≈ 0.25 ms, well below τ_jc, so the average holds.
- At startup or stall (< 2,000 eRPM), the sector duration approaches τ_jc. The peak per-FET loss then tends toward I²R ≈ 12.3 W for the LS sink device.

**Stall/desync at 80 A is the worst case.** AM32 stall protection must be enabled.

### 1.5 Junction temperature during the burst (transient model)

**Steady state is unattainable at 80 A,** so the burst is modelled as a two-node lumped RC network.

```
P_FET ──► [T_j] ──R_θJB──► [T_board] ──R_θBA──► T_amb
           C_j (small)       C_board (dominant)
```

**Junction-to-board rise** (R_θJB = R_θJC 1.2 K/W + solder/via array ≈ 1.8 K/W, total 3.0 K/W):

$$\Delta T_{jb} = (8.2 + 1.0_{sw\ share}) \times 3.0 \approx 28\ \text{K}$$

**Board thermal capacitance** (30.5 × 30.5 mm):

| Element | Volume | c_v (J/cm³·K) | C (J/K) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper (8 × 70 µm × 930 mm²) | 0.52 cm³ | 3.45 | 1.80 |
| FR4 (1.6 mm) | 1.49 cm³ | 1.9 | 2.83 |
| FETs, caps, solder (est.) | | | 0.6 |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 5.2 J/K** |

**Board-to-ambient** in prop wash (h ≈ 150 W/m²K, A ≈ 2 × 930 mm² = 0.00186 m²):

$$R_{\theta BA} = \frac{1}{hA} \approx 3.6\ \text{K/W}$$
