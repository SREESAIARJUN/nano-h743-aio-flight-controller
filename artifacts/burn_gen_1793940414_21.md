# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Electro-Thermal Model and DShot600 Bidirectional DMA Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

No MOSFET part, board size or stack-up was specified, so the numbers below rest on the stated baseline parameters. The equations are general. Swap in datasheet values and re-run them before committing to layout.

**Architectural correction.** In a 4-in-1 ESC the commutation and the 48 kHz PWM run on the ESC's own MCU, not the H743. The H743 only sends DShot and decodes eRPM telemetry. The baseline is therefore:

- **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot.
- **ESC:** AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware, with an FD6288Q gate driver and 8 × N-MOSFET per motor (4 motors).

**Board size.** An 80A-per-motor burst on a true 20×20 "nano" footprint is not thermally credible. The baseline is a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC under a 20×20 or 30×30 FC. A 20×20 variant is evaluated in §2.5.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths
The model assumes trapezoidal six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM. At any instant, two phase legs carry I_ph:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D·T. The low-side FET conducts for (1 − D)·T as the synchronous rectifier.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

Per FET:

$$P_{HS,A} = D\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j),\quad P_{LS,A} = (1-D)\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j),\quad P_{LS,B} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Total per motor, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Numerical evaluation (T_j = 100 °C, so k = 1.30)
- R_ds(100 °C) = 1.2 × 1.30 = **1.56 mΩ**
- I_ph² = 6400 A²
- FET conduction per motor = 2 × 6400 × 1.56 m = **19.97 W**
- Copper path (phase trace + battery planes + vias, estimated 0.4 mΩ effective): 6400 × 0.4 m = **2.56 W**
- **P_cond,motor ≈ 22.5 W**
- **Four motors at 80A simultaneously: ≈ 90 W**

**Hottest single FET:** the continuously conducting return-leg low side, at 6400 × 1.56 m = **10.0 W**. Commutation rotates this role through all six FETs. Averaged over an electrical revolution, each FET sees 1/3 of the total, about 6.7 W. The thermal analysis below still uses 10 W as the peak, because the commutation period (about 1 ms at 10k eRPM-equivalent) is shorter than the die thermal time constant but not much shorter.

### 1.3 Battery-side current check
I_bat ≈ D·I_ph. At D = 0.9 that is 72 A per motor, or **288 A pack current** for four motors. The XT60 and pack must be specified accordingly, which in practice means XT90 or an AS150 class connector. Most "80A" ESC ratings assume this burst never happens on all four motors at once. Flag this in the requirements.
