# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #29): Power Stage, Thermal Model and Bi-directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Architecture and Key Findings

### 0.1 System partition
In an AIO "FC + 4-in-1 ESC" design, the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It generates bi-directional DShot600 frames. Four ESC MCUs (one per motor, AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) perform 6-step commutation at the 48 kHz PWM frequency. The model below is therefore split into two domains:

- **Power domain:** per-motor 3-phase bridge, gate driver and ESC MCU.
- **Control domain:** H743 timers, DMA, bi-directional DShot600 and telemetry.

### 0.2 Key findings
1. **The 80 A rating is a time-limited burst, not a continuous rating.** Burst duration and continuous current are quantified in §3.4–§3.6. At realistic nano and 30×30 board heat capacity:
   - 80 A on all four channels is sustainable for about 2 s.
   - 80 A on a single channel is sustainable for about 9 s.
   - Continuous current with prop-wash cooling is roughly 20 A per channel.
2. **Bulk-capacitor ripple current** (up to about 40 A RMS per channel) is as significant a design constraint as MOSFET heating.
3. **The H743 DMA budget comfortably supports 4× bi-directional DShot600** with no stream sharing.
