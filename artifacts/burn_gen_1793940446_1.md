# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #25): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and DShot DMA Plan

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Assumptions

Two points determine the shape of every calculation below.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs four independent commutation controllers. Each controller performs BEMF zero-cross detection, 48 kHz complementary PWM, and dead-time control. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel uses its own small MCU. The baseline is AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, with a 3-phase gate driver. This partitioning follows standard industry practice. Running four sensorless FOC/6-step loops on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not credible.

2. **"Nano" plus "80 A burst" is thermally hostile.** The math below shows that 80 A is achievable only as a short transient of around 1 s with all four motors loaded. It is not a 10 s rating. I state the real envelope rather than a marketing number.

**Design baseline (change these and re-run the equations):**

The cost is about 1.6–2× higher R_DS(on), which roughly doubles the conduction numbers below. The 40 V case is calculated as the optimistic bound; 4S is the natural home for 40 V parts.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM
At any instant two phases are active and one floats.

- During the **on-time D**, current flows through HS_A, the motor, and LS_B.
- During the **off-time (1−D)**, current freewheels through LS_A (synchronous) and LS_B.

LS_B conducts 100 % of the time. The A-leg shares the current between HS_A (D) and LS_A (1−D). Therefore, independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D) + 1\,] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence
$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /\text{K}
$$
- At T_j = 125 °C, typical part: R = 1.4 × 1.45 = **2.03 mΩ**
- At T_j = 125 °C, max part: R = 1.8 × 1.45 = **2.61 mΩ**

### 1.3 Ripple correction
Phase inductance L_ph is about 15 µH (typical 2207-class motor, line-line ≈ 30 µH). The current ripple is:
$$
