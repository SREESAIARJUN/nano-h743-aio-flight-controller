# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #2)

**Scope:** 80 A burst loss model, 48 kHz switching model, thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Findings

Several issues need resolving before layout. Each one changes the numbers below.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no ESC firmware. The ESC side needs dedicated MCUs, as assumed here: 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32. | The H743 role is limited to DShot600 out and eRPM telemetry in. |
| A2 | **80 A per motor on a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is burst-only, about 2 s.** The model below shows roughly 85 W of dissipation at 4×80 A, against a board that sheds about 15–20 W continuously. | "80 A" must be specified as a burst of at most 1–2 s, or as an aggregate. |
| A3 | **4×80 A equals 320 A of battery current.** An XT30 (about 30 A continuous) and 14–16 AWG leads cannot supply this. | Confirm whether 80 A is per motor or total. If total, all losses drop by about 16× (I² scaling at 20 A per motor) and the design is comfortable. |
| A4 | **6S maximum is assumed: V_bus = 25.2 V.** | This requires 40 V FETs and 35 V bulk caps at minimum. 50 V caps are preferred because of the ringing margin. |

Every equation below is parametric, so changing the assumptions only changes the inputs.
