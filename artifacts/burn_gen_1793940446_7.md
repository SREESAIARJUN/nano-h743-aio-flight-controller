# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #46): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel or the whole 4-in-1 board runs its own commutation firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32) on a small MCU such as the STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC over bi-directional DShot600. The model below follows that split:

```
[FC board 20x20: STM32H743 + IMU + baro + OSD]  --8-pin harness-->  [ESC board 20x20: ESC MCU + 3ph gate driver + 12 MOSFETs]
```

**Flag up front:** "80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm stack is a transient rating only. The math below shows it holds for roughly 1 s with all four motors loaded, not continuously. The thermal section quantifies this so the rating can be defended or derated.
