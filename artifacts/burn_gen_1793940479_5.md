**Scope:** 6S input, 20×20 mm stack, ArduPilot firmware, bi-directional DShot600, 48 kHz ESC PWM.

**Status:** Engineering model and reference netlist. Every device parameter marked *[A]* is an assumption. Replace it with datasheet values for the parts you actually choose before layout freeze.

## 0. Feasibility Finding

The model below shows that **80 A burst per motor is not achievable in a 20×20 mm 4-in-1 format.** That load dissipates about 39 W per motor and about 156 W per board, which drives the board temperature up at roughly 100 K/s.

The design is closed and validated for these two interpretations instead:

| Interpretation | Per-motor current | Board loss | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: 80 A per motor burst | 80 A | ~156 W | Not feasible at 20×20. Needs 30.5×30.5 or larger with 5×6 FETs. |
| B: 80 A total pack burst | 20 A | ~11 W | Feasible. Design baseline. |

The equations are general, so you can substitute any current.

## 2. Conduction-Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 2.0 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.9 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM

In any 60° sector, phase X has its high-side FET PWM'd at duty D, and phase Y has its low-side FET held on.

- During the on-time (fraction D), current flows through HS_X and LS_Y.
- During the off-time (fraction 1−D), current freewheels through LS_X and LS_Y, because synchronous rectification replaces the body diode.

The motor current therefore always passes through exactly two channels:

$$P_{cond,motor}=I_m^2\cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)\quad\text{(independent of D)}$$

Averaged over a full electrical cycle, this loss spreads across the 6 FETs. Each FET conducts for 120° of the cycle, split by duty between the high-side and low-side devices of its leg:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET}\approx \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6}$$

### 2.3 Numbers at T_j = 125 °C

| | Case A (80 A) | Case B (20 A) |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond per motor = I²·5.8 mΩ | **37.1 W** | **2.32 W** |
| Average per FET | 6.2 W | 0.39 W |

### 2.4 Electro-thermal iteration

Loss raises temperature, which raises R_ds, which raises loss. Solve:

$$T_j = T_{pcb}+R_{\theta JC}\cdot\bar P_{FET}(T_j)$$

$$\bar P_{FET}(T_j)=\tfrac{1}{6}\,2I^2R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Rearranged in closed form:

$$T_j=\frac{T_{pcb}+k\,(1-25\alpha)}{1-k\alpha},\qquad k=\tfrac{R_{\theta JC}}{3}I^2R_{ds,25}$$
