## 1. Field-Oriented Control: the inner current loop

### 1.1 Transforms
With the electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant model and decoupling

The surface-mounted PMSM in the dq frame (L_d ≈ L_q = L) is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Feedforward decoupling cancels the cross terms and the back-EMF:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

### 1.3 Current PI (pole-zero cancellation)

The controller is C(s) = K_p + K_i/s. Choose:

$$K_p^{i}= L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i}= R\,\omega_c$$

The open loop then becomes ω_c/s, giving a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c.

Size ω_c with two limits in mind:
- Keep ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/20, because of the computation delay plus the zero-order hold (≈1.5 sample periods).
- A practical value is f_c ≈ 1–1.5 kHz at f_pwm = 20 kHz.

### 1.4 Torque constant and voltage limit

$$\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

The voltage vector is limited to the SVPWM linear region, |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3. Apply the limit circularly, with priority to the d-axis.

### 1.5 Inverse Park and SVPWM

Inverse Park gives v_α and v_β. SVPWM is then implemented by min-max zero-sequence injection:

$$v_x' = v_x - \tfrac12(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{bus}}$$
