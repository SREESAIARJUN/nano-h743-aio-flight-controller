## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs P = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Rotor position | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per joint (e.g., AS5048/MT6701) |
| Inverter | 3-phase, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz, inline or low-side shunts |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera stage over SPI, sampled at 8 kHz ODR |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer) → Roll (middle) → Pit
