## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I have no Job #26 parameter sheet, so this model uses the baseline below. Every equation is written symbolically, so you can substitute your real BOM values.

**Architecture.** ArduPilot does not commutate MOSFETs directly. The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. If Job #26 intends the H743 to drive the bridges directly, the DShot section no longer applies and a FOC firmware stack is needed instead.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Paths per Commutation Sector

In any 60° electrical sector:
- one phase is PWM'd: the high side (HS) conducts during D, and the synchronous low side (LS) conducts during (1−D);
- one phase's LS is continuously on (the return path);
- the third phase floats (BEMF sensing).

### 1.2 Equations

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

Channel conduction loss:

$$P_{cond}= I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[\underbrace{D}_{HS}+\underbrace{(1-D)}_{LS\ sync}+\underbrace{1}_{LS\ return}\big]=2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of D, because synchronous rectification keeps two FETs in the path at all times. The exception is dead time (§2.4).

Optional copper term:

$$P_{Cu}=I^2 R_{trace},\qquad R_{trace}=\frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t\,N_{layers}}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS}=1.1\,\text{m}\Omega\times1.375=1.51\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond}=2(80)^2(1.51\times10^{-3})=\mathbf{19.4\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Copper term: phase and bus copper of about 0.3 mΩ round-trip gives P_Cu ≈ 1.9 W per channel. This heat lands in the board, not the dies.

Per-FET average loss: each FET carries roughly 1/6 of the channel load averaged over an electrical revolution, so P_FET ≈ 3.2 W. At low eRPM (<~200 Hz electrical, e.g. during a stall or a punch from hover), the instantaneous per-FET loss approaches **9.7 W**. That case sets the die ΔT, not the average.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate Current During the Miller Plateau

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{drv}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{10-4.5}{10}=0.55\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{tot}}=0.45\ \text{A}$$

### 2.2 Transition Times

$$t_{on}\approx\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}}=\frac{14\,\text{nC}}{0.55}=25.5\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{off}\approx\frac{14\,\text{nC}}{0.45}=31.1\ \text{ns}$$

### 2.3 Hard-Switched V–I Overlap (HS of the PWM phase only)

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus}\,I\,(t_{on}+t_{off})\,f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(56.6\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz})=\mathbf{2.74\ W}$$

The LS turns on after dead time with its body diode already conducting (ZVS), so its overlap loss is approximately 0.
