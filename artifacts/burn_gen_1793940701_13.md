## 0. Architecture Corrections

The brief contains two problems that affect everything downstream.

1. **The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors.** Sensorless BLDC control at 48 kHz PWM needs a dedicated per-motor MCU with comparator-based BEMF zero-cross detection. Neither ArduPilot nor AM32 runs 3-phase commutation on the H743. The correct partition is:
   - **H743:** flight controller running ArduPilot.
   - **4× AT32F421K8U7:** one per motor, running AM32, with 48 kHz PWM.
   - The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to each ESC MCU.
2. **"80 A" on a nano footprint is a burst rating of roughly 1–4 s, not a continuous rating.** Section 3 proves this quantitatively. Marketing and the datasheet must state it as a timed burst.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

The commutation scheme is 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At every instant, the motor current flows through exactly two MOSFETs:
- one device in the PWM leg (the high side during on-time, the low side during off-time), and
- the low side of the return leg.

The on-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{hot}=R_{ds,25}\left(\tfrac{T_j+273}{298}\right)^{2.3}\approx 1.4\times1.6=2.24\ \text{m}\Omega$$
