## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

Several points need to be settled before the math.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and sends DShot600 commands. Each motor channel is driven by an ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 class firmware, which handles 48 kHz PWM, commutation, and the bidirectional eRPM reply.
   - Typical ESC MCUs are the STM32G071 and AT32F421.
   - The usual implementation is one MCU per motor (4 total). Dual-motor-per-MCU designs exist but are less common.

2. **80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm) footprint is far outside normal practice.** Commercial 20×20 AIOs are typically rated 12–25 A continuous. The model below shows that 80 A is achievable only as a **sub-second to ~2 s burst** with adiabatic heat-soak. I recommend either:
   - 30.5×30.5 mm with 8 FETs per phase-pair, or
   - redefining the requirement as "80 A for ≤1 s, 45 A for 10 s."

3. **System current scales hard.** 4 × 80 A = **320 A pack draw** at full burst. The design then needs:
   - an XT90 or larger connector,
   - 10 AWG or heavier battery leads,
   - a 6S pack rated for 320 A.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path
In 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation, two phases conduct at any instant.

- **Active-PWM leg:** The high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The synchronous low-side FET of the same leg conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Return leg:** The low-side FET conducts continuously during the 60° sector.

The motor current therefore always passes through **exactly two FET channels in series**:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 \cdot 2\,k_T R_{DS(on)} = I^2 R_{path}$$

### 2.2 Per-channel result at 80 A

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.0\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution
Each FET is active for 120° electrical (1/3 of the time).

- **Instantaneous dissipation while active:** I²·k_T·R = **9.6 W**.
- **Time-averaged dissipation per FET:** 19.2 W / 6 = **3.2 W**, assuming symmetric utilization.

The thermal time constants of the die and pad (ms range) are much longer than the commutation period at hover-to-full eRPM (100 µs–1 ms). The average value is therefore valid for junction estimation. A ±10–15 % ripple term is superimposed.

### 2.4 Shunt and copper
- **Shunt:** 80² × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W**.
- **Copper:** Assume 4 mm wide 2 oz traces on 3 parallel layers, about 15 mm path length, giving ~0.4 mΩ. That adds 80² × 0.4 mΩ = **2.6 W**.

$$P_{cond,total/ESC} \approx 19.2 + 1.6 + 2.6 = \mathbf{23.4\ W}$$

## 3. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM-ing leg switches. Hard switching occurs on the high-side FET. The low-side FET soft-switches through its body diode during dead time.
