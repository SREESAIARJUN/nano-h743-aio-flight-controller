## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

On a 4-in-1 AIO, **the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not drive MOSFETs.** It sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware) generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM through a 3-phase gate driver. The loss model below applies to the ESC power stage. The DShot/DMA model applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During each 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM-high and phase B is the low-side return:

- **PWM on (duty D):** current flows through HS_A, the motor, then LS_B. That is 2 FETs.
- **PWM off (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_A (synchronous, not the body diode), the motor, then LS_B. That is still 2 FETs.

So the bridge always has exactly two channels in series:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the PWM phase and 1/3 as the return phase:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

I²R = 6400 × 1.45e-3 = 9.28 W.

| Case | P_HS per FET | P_LS per FET | Bridge total |
|---|---|---|---|
| D = 1.0 (full-throttle burst) | 3.09 W | 3.09 W | **18.56 W** |
| D = 0.5 | 1.55 W | **4.64 W** (worst FET) | 18.56 W |

**Design rule:** the low-side FETs are the thermal limiters at partial throttle and high current, for example during hard deceleration or prop-wash recovery.

### 1.5 Copper and interconnect conduction loss

Assume the effective path is 0.5 mΩ per bridge (phase pads, internal planes, solder, bus bars):

P_Cu = I² × R_path = 6400 × 0.5e-3 = **3.2 W per motor**.
