## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

The model rests on one architectural point. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 48 kHz six-step or FOC drive for four motors needs dedicated commutation MCUs, one per motor. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs.

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32) ──► 4× FD6288Q ──► 4× 6-FET bridge ──► Motors
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C with the 1.3 mΩ max value:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.3\,(1+0.0055\cdot85) = \mathbf{1.91\ m\Omega}$$

Because R_DS rises with temperature, losses and temperature feed each other. Section 3.4 solves that loop iteratively.

### 1.2 Six-step current paths

In six-step (trapezoidal) drive, two legs always carry phase current I_ph: one high-side FET and one low-side FET. Each FET is active for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle.

With complementary PWM at duty D on the high side:

| Device | Conduction fraction of electrical period |
|---|---|
| High-side FET (PWM'd) | D/3 |
| Low-side FET, same leg (synchronous rectification during off-time) | (1−D)/3 |
| Low-side FET, return leg (held on) | 1/3 |

Total conduction loss per ESC channel (two FETs in series at every instant):

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\cdot80^2\cdot1.91\text{m} = \mathbf{24.4\ W}$$

Worst-case loss per FET (one FET for its full 1/3 share):

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac13 I_{ph}^2 R_{DS} = \mathbf{4.07\ W}$$

**Interconnect copper.** A 30 mm path on 2 oz copper, 6 mm wide, across 2 paralleled layers gives about 0.35 mΩ:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.35\text{m}\cdot 2 \approx \mathbf{4.5\ W}\ \text{per ESC}$$

This loss spreads into the board rather than concentrating in the FETs, so it is often overlooked.
