# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Electro-Thermal Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlists

**Design reference: Job #36, Rev A (engineering draft)**

---

## 0. Scope and Key Findings

### 0.1 Architecture

"STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" is interpreted in its only practical form. An STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32).

- The ESC MCUs do the 48 kHz FET switching.
- The H743 never drives gates directly. ArduPilot has no 6-step or FOC motor-commutation path.

### 0.2 Thermal Reality Check

The model shows that **"80 A" can only be a transient rating on a nano/30.5 mm board.**

| Rating | Value | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Burst | 80 A | ≈ 4 s with all four channels simultaneously; ≈ 10–15 s with one channel |
| Continuous | ≈ 30–35 A per channel | Prop-wash convection only |

Anything advertised beyond this needs a heatsink or must be derated.
