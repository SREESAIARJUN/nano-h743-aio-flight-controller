## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The following decisions frame the whole model:

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the gates.** It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each motor has a dedicated ESC MCU that performs commutation and 48 kHz PWM. This document uses an AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, which supports bidir DShot, EDT, and 48 kHz PWM.
2. **Form factor:** 20×20 mm mounting pattern on a 25×25 mm board outline, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner copper.
3. **Bus:** 6S LiPo, analyzed at the worst case of 25.2 V.
4. **Assumed component parameters.** Replace these with your selected part's datasheet values. The model is fully parametric.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |

**Feasibility warning up front.** The model below shows that 80 A per motor on a 20×20 board is achievable **only as a burst of roughly 1 to 2 seconds**. It is not achievable as a continuous rating. Any "80 A" label must carry this qualification.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation-aware per-FET conduction

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM. In each 60° sector two phases conduct:

- **The PWM'd high side.** The HS FET conducts for fraction D. Its complementary LS FET conducts for fraction (1−D).
- **The return low side.** This LS FET conducts 100 % of the sector.
