## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At any instant, phase current flows through **exactly two MOSFETs in series**:

- the active high-side (or its synchronous low-side complement during PWM-off), and
- the low-side of the return leg.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At an assumed T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.45\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.3 RMS current including PWM ripple

The worst-case ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$
