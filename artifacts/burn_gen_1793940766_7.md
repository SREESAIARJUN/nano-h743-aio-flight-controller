## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I have no Job #57 documentation such as BOM, stack-up, or MOSFET part numbers. Every number below comes from the stated assumptions, so substitute your actual datasheet values into the equations. The parameterized equations are the deliverable, and the numbers are worked examples.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.5 × 1.45 ≈ **2.18 mΩ**. Use 2.25 mΩ to add margin for spread.

### 1.2 Current path accounting (6-step, synchronous PWM)

At any instant, the motor current flows through **exactly two FETs**:
- **On-time (D):** the high-side FET of the PWM leg and the static low-side FET of the return leg.
- **Off-time (1−D):** the complementary low-side FET of the PWM leg and the static low-side FET.

The conduction path therefore always contains 2 × R_DS, independent of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.25\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{28.8\ W}$$

Each FET holds a role for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Averaged over the cycle, the six FETs share the load roughly equally:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{28.8}{6} \approx \mathbf{4.8\ W}$$

The **instantaneous** dissipation per FET while conducting is 80² × 2.25 mΩ = 14.4 W. This value sets the junction ripple at low eRPM, where the electrical period approaches the FET's thermal time constant. This matters at stall or punch-out from hover.

Per-switch RMS current while the switch is active:
- HS: $I_{rms,HS} = I\sqrt{D/3}$
- Complementary LS: $I\sqrt{(1-D)/3}$
- Static LS: $I/\sqrt{3} = 46.2$ A

### 1.3 Parasitic copper path

Assume 0.5 mΩ for bus planes, phase pads, and vias per motor:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\text{ W/motor}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The other legs commutate at the eRPM rate, which is negligible by comparison.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (complementary FET body diode)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

$$P_{oss} = 2 \times \tfrac12 C_{oss,eff} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.030\text{ W}$$
