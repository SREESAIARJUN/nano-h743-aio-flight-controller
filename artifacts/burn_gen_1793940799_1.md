## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Three findings shape the rest of this document.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---------|-------------|
| F1 | The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors. Running four sensorless BLDC loops at 48 kHz alongside the ArduPilot scheduler, EKF3 and IMU sampling is not viable on one core. | Use a split architecture: the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, or STM32G071). The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs. |
