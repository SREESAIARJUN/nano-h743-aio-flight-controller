## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller MCU**. It does not commutate motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class firmware). ArduPilot runs on the H743 and sends **bi-directional DShot600** to those four ESC MCUs.

| Parameter | Value | Notes |

| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm stack (FC + ESC) | A true 20×20 board cannot hold 80 A. See §3.5. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | The model also covers 4S. |
| Burst current | **80 A per channel, ≤ 2 s** (DC-link) | 4 × 80 A simultaneously is the worst case. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step trapezoidal commutation

At any instant, two of the three phases conduct. With complementary PWM:

- **PWM leg:** during the on-time D, current flows through the high-side (HS) FET. During the off-time (1 − D), it flows through the synchronous low-side (LS) FET.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is held on for the full 60° sector.

As a result, **exactly two FET channels carry I_ph at every instant**, regardless of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At the design hotspot T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 85) = 1.38\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 RMS current with PWM ripple

The phase current has a triangular ripple of peak-to-peak value ΔI:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right),\qquad r = \frac{\Delta I}{I}$$

The ripple is:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph,ll}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_ll ≈ 20 µH), D = 0.9, V_bemf ≈ 0.85·V_bus, f_sw = 48 kHz:

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{3.8 \times 0.9}{20\times10^{-6}\times 48\,000} \approx 3.6\ \text{A},\qquad r = 0.045$$

The ripple correction (≈ 1.0002) is negligible at full current.

### 1.4 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

The copper term uses R_Cu, the phase trace, pad, and via resistance. Take R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ for each conducting path pair on 2 oz copper.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.38\,\text{m}) + 80^2(0.25\,\text{m}) = 17.7 + 1.6 = \mathbf{19.3\ W}$$

### 1.5 Per-FET split

Each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical period, so the time-averaged loss is:

$$\bar{P}_{FET} \approx \frac{2\, I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = 2.95\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous loss while conducting is 8.8 W. Electrical frequency exceeds 1 kHz at flight eRPM, which is fast compared with the die thermal time constant of about 1 ms. Using the average is therefore valid. Add 10–15 % margin for the return-leg LS FET, which sees more on-time at low duty.
