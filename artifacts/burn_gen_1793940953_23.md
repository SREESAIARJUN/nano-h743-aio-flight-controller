## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path topology
In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs carry the phase current at every instant:
- the high-side (or its complementary low-side, during PWM-off) of the PWM leg, and
- the low-side of the return leg.

Each individual FET conducts for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle, so its conduction duty is 1/3.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]^{\,}, \quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.
