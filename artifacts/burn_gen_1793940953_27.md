## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits **bi-directional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs, which close the 48 kHz commutation loop. The ESC MCUs are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. All switching-loss math below applies to the ESC power stage.

```
 6S LiPo ──┬── 4-in-1 ESC board (4× MCU, 4× 3-ph driver, 24× MOSFET)
           │        ▲ DShot600 bidir (×4)   │ VBAT / CURR / GND
           │        │                       ▼
           └── STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot) ◄── 8-pin JST-SH stack connector
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current distribution under 6-step commutation with complementary PWM

Over one electrical period, each phase leg spends:

- **1/3 of the period** as the PWM phase. The high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **1/3 of the period** as the sink phase, with the low-side FET fully on.
- **1/3 of the period** floating, which is when BEMF is sensed.

Per-FET RMS currents:

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{I_{ph}^2 \, D}{3}, \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{I_{ph}^2\,(1-D)}{3} + \frac{I_{ph}^2}{3} = \frac{I_{ph}^2\,(2-D)}{3}$$

The hot on-resistance follows a linear temperature approximation:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) \approx R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At 110 °C this gives R_ds ≈ 1.4 mΩ × 1.5 = **2.1 mΩ**.
