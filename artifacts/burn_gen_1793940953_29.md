# Job #48: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80A ESC

## Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Directional DShot600 Architecture Model

---

## 0. Architecture Corrections Before Modeling

Three points shape everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not run the ESCs.** ArduPilot on the STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each motor channel (or one MCU for all four) needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. The DMA model in §5 covers the **H743 → ESC-MCU** link.

2. **80A burst on a nano (20×20 mm) board is not thermally achievable.** §4 shows that four channels at 80A dissipate about **120 W**. That cannot be removed from a 20×20 mm or even 30.5×30.5 mm board for more than a few seconds. The model below therefore assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm board with a bonded heatsink**. It derives the **maximum burst duration** rather than assuming 80A is continuous.

3. **Component parameters are placeholders.** MOSFET and driver values are representative of a 40V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN class. Every number marked **[DS]** must be replaced with the selected part's datasheet value before sign-off.
