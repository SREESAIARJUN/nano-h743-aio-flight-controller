## 1. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

### 1.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. In every viable 4-in-1 design the partitioning is as follows:

```
[ FC: STM32H743 @480 MHz, ArduPilot ] --4x bidir DShot600 (1-wire, half-duplex)-->
[ 4-in-1 ESC: 4x AT32F421 (AM32) or 1x MCU per channel ]
      -> 4x 3-phase gate driver (e.g. FD6288Q class)
      -> 4x 6 N-ch MOSFET bridges -> motors
```

The H743 owns DShot generation, eRPM capture, the harmonic notch, current and voltage ADC, and logging. Each ESC MCU owns commutation, the 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and BEMF sensing.
