# Job #33: STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), 80 A Burst Design Package

**Rev A, Architecture Baseline**

---

## 0. Architectural Clarifications

Three issues shape the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel (or a shared ESC MCU) runs its own firmware (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class) on a small Cortex-M0+/M4 such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. Therefore:
   - The 48 kHz PWM and its switching losses live in the ESC domain.
   - Timer and DMA allocation live in the H743 domain.

   I keep these as separate models below.

2. **"Nano" vs 80 A.** A 20×20 mm board cannot sink 4×80 A continuously. The thermal model below shows 80 A is a time-limited burst (about 5–6 s under prop wash). It is not a rating. The power stage assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC layer with a 20×20 FC stacked or co-located. If a true 20×20 single board is mandatory, burst current must be derated to roughly 45–55 A.

3. **Component values are engineering placeholders.** They are representative of the 40 V PDFN5×6 MOSFET class and the FD6288-class 3-phase gate driver. Every number marked † must be replaced with the qualified part's datasheet value before release.
