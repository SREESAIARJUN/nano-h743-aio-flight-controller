# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Job #29: Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Design Package

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Several assumptions need confirming before release, because the conclusions depend on them.

| Item | Assumption | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | H743 is the **flight controller** only. Each motor has a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32) driving a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. | The H743 cannot run 4× sensorless commutation and the flight stack at once. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max. 40 V MOSFETs. | Sets switching V and FET rating |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm board on 20×20 mount holes ("nano-mount"), 6-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes | 80 A burst on a true 20×20 PCB is not thermally credible (see §3.5). |
| MOSFET | Generic 40 V DFN3.3×3.3. R_DS(on),max = 1.6 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V. Q_g = 38 nC, Q_rr = 35 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, R_θJC = 1.3
