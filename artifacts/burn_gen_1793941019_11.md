## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

Two facts shape everything below.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the commutation engine.** Running four sensorless 6-step or FOC ESCs on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not a supported ArduPilot configuration. It would also wreck scheduling determinism. The architecture is therefore:
   - **STM32H743VIT6** (FC, ArduPilot), which sends bidirectional DShot600 to
   - **4× AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071** ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware), each driving one 3-phase bridge.
2. **"80 A burst per channel" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally and electrically marginal.** The derivation shows this quantitatively. Design limits are expressed as **burst duration**, not as a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology
Assume 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active leg. At any instant, two legs conduct:

- **PWM leg:** high-side (HS) conducts for D·T_s. The complementary low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D)·T_s, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** LS fully ON.

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,HS} = D\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad P_{cond,LS,sync} = (1-D)\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$
$$P_{cond,LS,return} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$
$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)} \quad\text{(independent of D)}$$

**Ripple correction.** The phase inductance is L_ph ≈ 20 µH for a typical 5" motor. Worst ripple occurs at D = 0.5:
$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$
$$I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{ph}^2 + \Delta I_{pp}^2/12} = \sqrt{6400 + 3.59} = 80.02\ \text{A} \;\;(\text{negligible})$$

**Temperature dependence.** R_ds(T_j) = R_25·[1 + α(T_j − 25)] with α ≈ 0.0053 /K, which gives k_T ≈ 1.40 at 100 °C:
$$R_{ds}(100^\circ C) = 1.2 \times 1.40 = 1.68\ \text{m}\Omega$$
$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.68\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.5\ W}$$

**Electrothermal feedback.** Since R_ds rises with T_j, iterate T_j → R_ds → P → T_j. Runaway occurs if
$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_{\theta} = 2I^2R_{25}\alpha\,R_\theta \geq 1$$
For one FET carrying I²R (not 2I²R): 6400 × 1.2 mΩ × 0.0053 × R_θ ≥ 1, so R_θ ≥ 24.6 K/W. A properly heatsunk 3×3 DFN sits well below this, so the system is stable. On a bare nano board with no airflow it starts to approach this limit.
