# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Architecture

**Document:** Job #56, Systems Architecture Rev A (draft for design review)

---

## 0. Scope and Critical Findings

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In any production 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. This document models that architecture.

### 0.2 Findings to resolve before layout

| # | Finding | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | An "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board is thermally limited to roughly **1.5–2.5 s** on all four channels simultaneously. It is not a continuous rating. | **High** |
| 2 | DC-link capacitor RMS ripple reaches **~40 A_rms per channel** at D = 0.5. The bulk electrolytic cannot carry this alone. A distributed MLCC bank is mandatory. | **High** |
| 3 | DShot DMA buffers on the H7 must sit in DMA-accessible, non-cacheable SRAM, not DTCM. | Medium |
| 4 | All numeric results depend on MOSFET parameters assumed in §1. They must be re-run against the selected part's datasheet and validated on hardware. | Process |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.51 mΩ**

### 2.2 Current path per commutation step

During each 60° step there are two phases in play:

- **Phase A (PWM-switched leg):** high-side conducts for D, synchronous low-side conducts for (1 − D).
- **Phase B (return leg):** low-side is fully on.

Total conduction resistance seen by I_ph at every instant is therefore

$$R_{path} = \underbrace{[D + (1-D)]R_{ds}}_{\text{PWM leg}} + \underbrace{R_{ds}}_{\text{return leg}} = 2R_{ds}$$
