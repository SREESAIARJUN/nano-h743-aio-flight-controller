## 0. Scope and Assumptions

This document gives the analytical derivations and reference design. Every assumed device parameter is stated explicitly. You must re-run the numbers with the datasheet values of the parts you actually procure before layout freeze.

**System partitioning (important):** The STM32H743 is the flight-controller MCU running ArduPilot. It generates DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry. It does not commutate the motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (reference: AT32F421 running AM32) that executes 6-step commutation with 48 kHz complementary PWM.
