## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology
In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification, at any instant the motor current flows through exactly two conducting devices:

- **PWM-on interval (D):** HS_x + LS_y
- **PWM-off interval (1−D):** LS_x (sync rect) + LS_y

Each conduction path is two R_DS(on) in series, so channel conduction loss is independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Ripple contribution
Peak-to-peak phase ripple is worst at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}
$$

The RMS of the flat-top current including ripple is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{+0.06\%, negligible}
$$
