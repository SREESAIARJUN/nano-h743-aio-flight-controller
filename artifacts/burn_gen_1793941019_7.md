## 0. Architecture Findings (Read First)

These two findings change the design. They should be resolved before any copper is laid.

### 0.1 The H743 does not commutate the motors

In every ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 design, the motors are driven by **dedicated ESC MCUs**, one per motor or one per pair. Typical choices are the AT32F421, STM32G071 or STM32F051, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

The H743 flight controller runs ArduPilot and only sends **bidirectional DShot600** commands to the ESC MCUs. Driving 24 gates at 48 kHz with ArduPilot's scheduler is neither supported nor sensible.

The partition used throughout this package is:

```
[H743 FC] --DShot600 bidir x4--> [4x AT32F421 ESC MCU] --> [4x 3-ph gate driver] --> [24x MOSFET]
```

### 0.2 80 A burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally viable

As shown in §3, four motors at 80 A dissipate about **85 W**. A 20×20 mm stack has roughly 3 J/K of heat capacity, so the board heats at about **28 K/s**.

| Interpretation | Viability |
|---|---|
| 80 A *per motor*, ≤1–2 s burst, 30×30 mm board with heatsink | Feasible |
| 80 A per motor on 20×20 mm | Not feasible: FET pad area and bus capacitance won't fit |
| 80 A *total* (20 A/motor) on 20×20 mm | Feasible |

**Recommendation:** use a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC footprint, or redefine the spec as 80 A total / 40 A per motor burst. The math below uses the **worst case: 80 A per motor**.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per Motor Bridge)

During trapezoidal or sinusoidal 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, current always flows through **exactly two conducting FET positions**:
- one high-side FET for duty D,
- the complementary low-side for (1−D),
- plus the return-phase low-side.

### 2.1 Conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \cdot 2$$

Ripple correction for a phase inductance L and electrical period:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L\, f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor (L ≈ 15 µH, line-to-line) near full throttle, ΔI ≈ 3–5 A. That adds less than 0.5% to I_rms², so it is neglected here.

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 1.44\,\text{m}\Omega \times 2 = \mathbf{18.43\ W}$$

The temperature dependence must be iterated, because R_ds rises with T_j:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 2.2 Hard-switching loss (48 kHz)

Transition times from Miller charge (driver plateau V_pl ≈ 4.5 V, V_drv = 10 V):

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}(R_g + R_{drv,src})}{V_{drv} - V_{pl}} = \frac{9\text{n}\,(4.7 + 10)}{5.5} \approx 24\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}(R_g + R_{drv,snk})}{V_{pl}} = \frac{9\text{n}\,(4.7 + 6.7)}{4.5} \approx 23\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 47\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{2.27\ W}$$

### 2.3 Secondary switching terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Body-diode dead-time conduction | V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8·80·400 n·48 k | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 50 n·25.2·48 k | **0.06 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss,er·V²·f_sw ·2 = 0.5·1.5 n·635·48 k·2 | **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (switching pair) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw = 2·55 n·10·48 k | **0.05 W** (mostly in R_g/driver) |

### 2.4 Bridge total

$$P_{bridge} = 18.43 + 2.27 + 1.23 + 0.06 + 0.05 + 0.05 \approx \mathbf{22.1\ W\ per\ motor}$$

$$P_{4\text{-in-}1} \approx \mathbf{88\ W}\quad(\text{plus copper loss, below})$$

### 2.5 Worst single FET

At D ≈ 1, the high-side of the driven phase carries almost all of the conduction loss plus all of the switching loss:

$$P_{FET,max} = 80^2 \times 1.44\text{m} + 2.27 + 0.03 \approx \mathbf{11.5\ W}$$
