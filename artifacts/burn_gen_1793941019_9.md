## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors.

- **ArduPilot is flight-control firmware.** It *transmits* DShot600 and *receives* bidirectional eRPM telemetry. It contains no sensorless BLDC commutation code.
- **Each ESC channel therefore needs its own MCU.** The correct topology is H743 (ArduPilot) → 4 × ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) → 4 × three-phase bridges.
- **Sharing one H743 is also a timing problem.** Driving 12 half-bridges with sub-µs zero-crossing detection on the same MCU as the EKF is not viable.

The rest of this document assumes that topology.

## 1. Conduction Losses (80 A Burst)

With synchronous complementary PWM, current always flows through either the high-side FET (duty D) or the low-side FET (duty 1−D) of the switching phase. Per-leg conduction loss is therefore **independent of D**:

$$P_{cond,leg} = I^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] = I^2 R_{hot}$$

In 6-step commutation two legs carry current at any instant (one switching, one held on):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot} = 2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

**Worst single FET** (high side at D → 1): 6400 × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**.

**PCB copper loss.** For a 2 oz trace (R_sq ≈ 0.25 mΩ/sq) with roughly 4 squares of effective path per phase, ×2 phases:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 2(80)^2(1.0\times10^{-3}) \approx 12.8\ W\ \text{(single-layer path)}$$

Paralleling 4 power layers brings this to ≈ **3.2 W**. This term is frequently ignored and should not be.

**With FOC / sinusoidal drive:** substitute I_rms = I_pk/√2 for all three legs:

$$P = 3\,(I_{pk}/\sqrt2)^2 R_{hot} = 1.5\,I_{pk}^2 R_{hot}$$

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches in 6-step.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 40 ns · 48 kHz | 1.94 W |
| C_oss charge/discharge | 2 × ½ · C_oss · V² · f = 1.5 nF · 635 · 48 kHz | 0.046 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f = 50 nC · 25.2 · 48 kHz | 0.060 W |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f = 0.8 · 80 · 200 ns · 48 kHz | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (lost in driver/R_g) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f = 2 · 60 nC · 10 · 48 kHz | 0.058 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

**Sensitivity.** Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 96 kHz it is about 3.9 W, which is why 48 kHz is a sensible compromise against current ripple. Cutting dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W.

## 3. Total Loss Budget at Burst

| Item | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| PCB copper (4-layer parallel) | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-channel) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 26.7 W** | **≈ 107 W** |

**Efficiency at 80 A.** Output power is roughly 25 V × 80 A × D. At D ≈ 0.9 that is about 1.8 kW per channel, so efficiency is about 98.5%. The percentage is fine. The problem is power **density**: about 107 W dissipated into roughly 9 cm² of board.
