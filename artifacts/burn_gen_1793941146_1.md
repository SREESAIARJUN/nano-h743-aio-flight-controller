## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant, two phases are active: one PWM leg and one return leg (low-side held ON).

- PWM leg, ON interval (D): current flows through the high-side (HS) FET.
- PWM leg, OFF interval (1−D): current freewheels through the low-side (LS) FET in synchronous mode.
- Return leg: the LS FET is ON for the entire 60° sector.

Exactly **two FET channels always conduct the phase current in series**, independent of duty cycle. That makes the channel conduction loss nearly duty-independent:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Ripple contribution

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\, D(1-D)}{L_{ph\text{-}ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$

Worst case is D = 0.5. With V = 25.2 V and L_ph-ph = 30 µH:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{30\times10^{-6} \times 48\times10^{3}} = 4.38\,\text{A}
\;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 1.6\,\text{A}^2 \ll 6400\,\text{A}^2
$$

Ripple adds only about 0.025 % to the loss, so it is negligible at 48 kHz. It matters only for very-low-inductance motors (< 5 µH).

### 1.3 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.0 × 1.55 ≈ **1.55 mΩ** (datasheet curves typically give 1.5–1.7×).

The PCB copper path (bus, phase pads, motor pad) adds to this:

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\, \ell}{w\, t}, \qquad \rho_{Cu}(T)=1.72\times10^{-8}[1+0.00393(T-20)]
$$

Budget about 0.3 mΩ of effective series copper per channel for a nano board.

### 1.4 Numerical result at 80 A burst

| Condition | R_DS per FET | P_cond FETs | P_Cu | **P_cond,ch** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T_j = 25 °C (start of burst) | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 1.9 W | **14.7 W** |
| T_j = 125 °C (end of burst) | 1.55 mΩ | 19.8 W | 2.3 W | **22.1 W** |

**Per-FET average:** over an electrical cycle each leg is active 2/3 of the time, giving ≈ 19.8 W / 6 ≈ **3.3 W per FET** (hot). The instantaneous peak is 9.9 W in the conducting FET.

### 1.5 Electro-thermal stability criterion

Because R_DS rises with temperature, positive feedback exists. The steady state is stable only if:

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\, R_{th,ja} < 1
\;\Rightarrow\;
2 I^2 R_{DS,25}\,\alpha\, R_{th,ja} < 1
$$

At 80 A: 2·6400·0.001·0.0055 = 0.0704 W/K. Runaway therefore occurs if R_th,ja > **14.2 K/W per channel**. A nano board in still air is above this (§3), so **80 A is inherently burst-only** and requires firmware current/temperature limiting.
