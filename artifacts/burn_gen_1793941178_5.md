## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset).

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 PMSM model and decoupled PI

The motor model in the rotating frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

Here ω_e is the electrical speed and λ_m is the PM flux linkage (= 2Kt/(3p) in the amplitude-invariant convention).

**Control law** (PI plus cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward):

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d+\lambda_m)$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choose current bandwidth ω_c ≈ 2π·2 kHz, at most f_pwm/10:

$$K_p^{i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i} = R\,\omega_c$$

This gives a closed-loop transfer function i/i* = ω_c/(s+ω_c).
