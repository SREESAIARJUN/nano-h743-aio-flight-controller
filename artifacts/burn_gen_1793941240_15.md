## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Up-Front Design Risks

The physics forces the following decisions. Verify them before layout.

| # | Item | Decision / Finding |
|---|------|------|
| A1 | ESC topology | The H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs. Each motor channel is commutated by a local ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. |
| A2 | Bus voltage | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. Design to 25.2 V, with 40 V FETs for ringing margin. |
| A3 | "80 A burst" | Interpreted as 80 A per motor, all four motors simultaneously, as the worst case. |
| A4 | Form factor | **Risk.** 80 A per motor is not thermally viable on a true 20×20 mm ESC. The math in §3 is done on a 30×30 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board. The FC can stay 20×20 nano. A 20×20 ESC should be rated about 35 A continuous / 45 A burst. |
| A5 | PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal commutation. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct. The PWM leg alternates between HS-on and LS-on (synchronous freewheel). The other leg's LS is held on.

So at every instant, the motor current $I$ passes through exactly **two conducting MOSFET channels** in series, ignoring dead time (handled in §2). This holds regardless of duty cycle $D$.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2\,R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Reference FET class: 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6, $R_{DS(on),25}=1.0\,\text{m}\Omega$ typ at $V_{GS}=10$ V (1.2 mΩ max).

| $T_j$ | $R_{DS(on)}$ typ | $R_{DS(on)}$ max |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 1.20 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 1.65 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 1.80 mΩ |

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

Phase pad to FET, plus the bus plane share. Each layer is 2 oz copper ($t=70\,\mu\text{m}$, $\rho=1.72\times10^{-8}\,\Omega\text{m}$). The path is 12 mm long, 6 mm wide, paralleled over 3 layers:

$$R_{Cu}=\frac{\rho L}{w t N}=\frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.012}{0.006\cdot70\times10^{-6}\cdot3}=0.164\,\text{m}\Omega$$

Applying a hot-copper factor (+0.39 %/K at ΔT=60 K, about 1.23×), adding vias and the bus return, gives $R_{Cu}\approx 0.45$ mΩ per motor path.

### 1.4 Result at 80 A, $T_j = 100$ °C (typ)

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.375\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.6\ W}$$
$$P_{Cu} = 80^2\cdot0.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{2.9\ W}$$
