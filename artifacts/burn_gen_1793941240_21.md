# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #1)

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly in any practical 4-in-1 design. The standard partition, used here, is:

- **STM32H743 (FC):** runs ArduPilot and generates 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. These perform commutation at 48 kHz PWM.
- **Gate drivers:** 1× 3-phase driver per motor (FD6288Q-class shown).
- **Power stage:** 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor).

**Design inputs.** Change any of these and the equations below still hold.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.3375 = **1.34 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary (synchronous) PWM

At every instant two phases conduct. Current flows through one high-side path and one low-side path:

- The **PWM leg** alternates between its high-side FET (duty D) and its synchronous low-side FET (1 − D).
- The **return leg** holds its low-side FET on.

Two R_DS(on) are therefore always in series. Including current ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

The ripple term is under 1 % at 80 A for typical L_ph of 5 to 20 µH at 48 kHz, so it is neglected below.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\left(1 - \frac{2t_d f_{sw}}{1}\right) \approx 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A and T_j = 100 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.34\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.15\ W\ per\ motor}$$

Average per FET (6 FETs, each conducting about 1/3 of the electrical cycle as a series element): **≈ 2.86 W**.

### 1.4 Electro-thermal stability

Conduction loss rises with temperature, so check for thermal runaway:

$$\frac{\partial P_{cond}}{\partial T_j} = 2 I_{ph}^2 R_{25}\alpha = 2(6400)(10^{-3})(0.0045) = 0.0576\ \text{W/K}$$

The stability criterion is (∂P/∂T)·R_th < 1. With per-channel effective R_th ≈ 7 K/W (§3): 0.0576 × 7 = **0.40 < 1**. The loop is stable and converges; there is no runaway. The loop gain does amplify the rise by 1/(1 − 0.40) ≈ 1.67×, so the temperature must be computed iteratively, as in §3.4.
