## 0. Architectural Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Flight MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz in ArduPilot | ArduPilot's standard H743 clocking. It gives a 200 MHz timer kernel clock. |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | ArduPilot cannot commutate motors. Each motor needs a dedicated ESC MCU. AM32 supports bidirectional DShot and a selectable 24/48 kHz PWM. |
| Gate driver | 4× FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver (VCC 12 V from a buck) | Common in 4-in-1 designs, with integrated bootstrap. Check the dead-time and VCC limits for your part. |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, 5×6 mm or 3.3×3.3 mm | 6S max is 25.2 V. A 40 V rating gives 1.6× margin for ringing. |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Assumption |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active leg | AM32 default |

**Reality check on "80 A burst."** Four motors at 80 A each is 320 A, or about 7 kW from 6S. No nano-class pack or XT60/XT90 lead supports that. The model therefore treats two cases:
- **Case A:** a single-motor 80 A transient (realistic, e.g. yaw/punch asymmetry).
- **Case B:** all four motors at 80 A (worst-case bound, used for the burst-duration limit).

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2\,(1+0.005\cdot85) = 1.71\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Copper trace and via resistance per current path (R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ, 2 oz, 6 layers) is added separately. This loss heats the board, not the junction.

### 1.3 Current-path topology in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During any 60° sector, two phases conduct:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of the time; its complementary low-side FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the time.

So the instantaneous conduction loss per ESC is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}$$
