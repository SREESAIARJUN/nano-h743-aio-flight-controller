## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Feasibility Findings

These findings affect the rest of the design, so they come first.

| Item | Assumption / Decision |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max. Design for 30 V transients. 40 V MOSFETs required. |
| "80A burst" | **Phase current per channel**, 4 channels simultaneously, duration-limited (see §3) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal (AM32/BLHeli_32 style) |
| Architecture | H743 runs ArduPilot only. The motor commutation runs on **separate ESC MCUs** (1× AT32F421 or STM32G071 per channel). The H743 does **not** drive gates. |
| MOSFET (reference) | 40 V, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ @
