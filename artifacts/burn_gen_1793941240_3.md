## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Derivation (6-step, synchronous rectification)

In 6-step commutation, exactly two phases carry the phase current I at any instant: one leg sourcing and one sinking.

With complementary PWM at duty D:
- The PWM leg conducts through the high-side for D·T and the low-side for (1−D)·T.
- The return leg's low-side conducts continuously.

In every sub-interval, therefore, **exactly two FETs are in series with I**, regardless of D (dead time aside):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Per leg, each phase is active 2/3 of the electrical period:

$$I_{leg,RMS} = I_{ph}\sqrt{2/3}$$

The RMS split between a leg's high and low FET depends on D and on the PWM scheme (PWM on high-side only, or chopped on both). At D → 1, the losses spread evenly over all 6 FETs.

### 1.2 Including copper and the shunt

$$P_{cond,total} = I_{ph}^2\,\big[2R_{DS(on)} + 2R_{Cu,phase} + R_{shunt}\cdot k_{shunt}\big]$$

- Assume R_Cu,phase ≈ 0.25 mΩ per path (2 oz, multi-layer stitched pours on 20×20 mm).
- Assume a 0.5 mΩ shunt on the low-side DC return. It carries the motor DC current, ≈ D·I_ph.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, D ≈ 1 (burst), T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.65 mΩ | **21.1 W** |
| Phase copper | 2 × 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| Shunt | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| **Total conduction, per motor** | | **27.5 W** |
| Per FET, averaged over 6 | 21.1 / 6 | ≈ 3.5 W |

At a cold start (T_j = 25 °C, 1.1 mΩ), FET conduction is 14.1 W. The gap between 14 W and 21 W is the **positive thermal feedback**. Solve it iteratively:

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per switching event pair (on + off) on that leg, the components are as follows.

**(a) Hard-switching V·I overlap.** Use t_sw ≈ Q_gd / I_g to estimate the transition times.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With Q_gd = 8 nC and I_g ≈ 0.8 A effective, t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 10 ns:

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery of the opposing body diode**

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\,\text{nC} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

**(c) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (2 dead-times per period)

$$P_{dt} = 2 V_F I_{ph} t_{dt} f_{sw} = 2 \times 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(d) C_oss charge/discharge** (C_oss,eff ≈ 1.5 nF)

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f_{sw} = 0.023\ \text{W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (2 FETs per period, dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors)

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = 0.04\ \text{W}$$
