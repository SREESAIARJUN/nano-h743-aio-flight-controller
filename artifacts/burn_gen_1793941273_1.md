# Job #48: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst, 48 kHz PWM, ArduPilot Bidirectional DShot600)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Design Basis

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Typical ESC MCUs are the STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, or the STM32F051 running BLHeli_32. Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM with hardware dead-time.

This split is required for three reasons:
- The H743 cannot run four sensorless BEMF commutation loops at 48 kHz alongside the EKF3 attitude loop.
- AM32 and BLHeli_32 depend on comparator and timer peripherals that are tightly mapped to each phase.
- Bidirectional DShot is the protocol designed for exactly this FC-to-ESC split.

**Design basis.** All numbers below follow from these assumptions. Change one and re-run the equations.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst current | 80 A per channel, phase current, ≤ 2 s |
| PWM | 48 kHz, synchronous (complementary), 6-step trapezoidal |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner (see the §4 note on 20 × 20 mm) |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch PDFN5×6, one per switch position, 24 total |
| Motor | ~40 µH line-to-line, 14-pole |
