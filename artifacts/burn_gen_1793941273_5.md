## 0. Design Review Flags

Four issues shape every calculation below.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted in this document |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not run the ESC.** In every production 4-in-1, each motor bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. | Architecture: the H743 is the flight controller. A 4-in-1 power stage uses one AT32F421 (AM32) per channel. |
| 2 | **"80A burst" on a nano form factor (20×20 mm) is not thermally achievable.** The MOSFET footprint alone exceeds the board area. | Board is a 30.5×30.5 mm stack (≈36×36 mm outline). The math below quantifies what "80A burst" actually means: ~3 s with all four motors loaded. |
| 3 | **A 48 kHz PWM rate costs efficiency.** Switching loss scales linearly with frequency. | 48 kHz is kept as specified (AM32 supports it). The 24 kHz delta is shown in §2.6. |
| 4 | **The netlist is a reference design, not a release.** Pin assignments are checked against STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) alternate-function tables. | MOSFET and driver parameters are representative datasheet values. Re-verify against final BOM part numbers before layout freeze. |

**System assumptions**

- Battery: 6S LiPo. V_nom = 22.2 V, V_max = 25.2 V. Worst case V_bus = 25.2 V is used for switching.
- Commutation: trapezoidal 6-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM.
- MOSFET (per switch position): 40 V N-ch, 5×6 mm PQFN, representative parameters:
  - R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ max at 25 °C
