> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag (address at design review):** 80 A burst per channel on a "nano" (20×20 or 25.5) board with single 3.3×3.3 FETs is beyond what the package, copper, and thermal mass realistically support. The model below shows about 77 W total dissipation at 4×80 A, which is roughly a 5 K/s adiabatic heating rate. **Recommendation:** use a 30.5×30.5 board with 5×6 mm FETs, or parallel 3.3×3.3 FETs (2 per switch). Alternatively, de-rate the burst spec to 55–60 A. The math below quantifies this trade-off.
