# Job #54: STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC Design Package

**Doc rev:** A0 (pre-layout)
**Scope:** Electro-thermal loss model, 48 kHz switching analysis, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, schematic netlists, and thermal budget.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

Two points need to be settled before this package is accepted.

**0.1 Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, here an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, which natively supports 48 kHz PWM and bi-directional DShot. This document therefore covers two boards:

- **FC board:** H743, DShot600 bidir outputs.
- **ESC board:** 4× (MCU + 3-phase gate driver + 6 MOSFETs).

**0.2 The 80 A burst on a 20×20 mm nano stack is physics-limited.** The model in §2–§4 shows:

- **Continuous rating:** about 4 × 13 A thermally sustainable.
- **80 A on one channel:** sustainable for about 9 s.
- **80 A on all four channels at once:** sustainable for about 1.4 s.

Above those durations the PCB copper, not the MOSFETs, is the limiting factor. The datasheet must specify "80 A burst" as **≤1 s on all four channels, 10% duty**, enforced in firmware (§4.5). If marketing requires 80 A per channel continuous, the form factor must change to 30.5×30.5 mm minimum, with paralleled FETs and a heatsink.

All MOSFET and PCB parameters below are **stated assumptions** for a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN part. Re-run the model with the final datasheet values and calibrate it against IR thermography (§4.6).
