## 0. Feasibility Check

Three issues must be settled before detailed design:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 at 48 kHz. Options are an AT32F421 per channel or one dual-channel MCU per two channels. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600, which matches the protocol requirement in the brief.
2. **80 A per channel × 4 = 320 A peak battery current.** That is not a "nano" (20×20) power level. The math below shows that even a 30.5×30.5 board with 8-layer, 2 oz copper sustains 80 A on all four channels for only about **2 seconds**. I therefore define the spec as:
   - **80 A burst:** ≤ 2 s, per channel, phase current.
   - **Continuous:** about 35–40 A per channel with prop-wash airflow.
3. **Form factor:** 30.5×30.5 mm mounting, 36×36 mm board outline, 8 layers, 2 oz on all layers. 20×20 is not viable at this current. The derivation shows why.
