# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

## 0. Assumptions

I don't have the Job #13 specification, so the design rests on the assumptions below. Every one of them is a named constant in the code.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Sensing | 2-shunt (or inline) phase current sensing per axis, 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per joint |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (e.g. STM32G4/H7) |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the **camera plate**, rigidly mounted at known positions r_A and r_B. This is used for noise averaging, fault isolation and lever-arm cancellation. |
| Platform | Multirotor, so the dominant vibration comes from propeller harmonics (≈80–400 Hz, varying) |
