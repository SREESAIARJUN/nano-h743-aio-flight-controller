## 0. Architecture, Assumptions, and Ground Rules

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, which generate the 48 kHz commutation PWM. The ESC MCUs are AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This split is standard, and the timing math in §5 depends on it.

No MOSFET part number was specified, so I modelled a **representative 40 V N-channel FET in a PDFN 3.3×3.3 package**. Substitute your datasheet values into the equations; the structure of the model does not change.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value used | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching loss) |
| Phase current (burst) | I_ph | 80 A | Per channel, "80A burst" rating |
| R_DS(on) @25 °C, max | R₂₅ | 1.5 mΩ | Datasheet max, not typical |
| R_DS(on) tempco | α | 0.0045 /K | Gives ≈1.34× at 100 °C |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In six-step commutation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through **exactly two FETs in series**:
- the high-side FET, or its complementary low-side FET during PWM off-time, on the driven phase, and
- one low-side FET on the return phase.

Ignoring dead time (handled in §2), the instantaneous conduction loss per channel is:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

with temperature dependence:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

### 1.2 Numbers

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$
R_{DS(on)} = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 75) = 2.006\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.006\times10^{-3} = 25.7\,\text{W}
$$

**Per-FET average.** The six FETs share this loss over an electrical revolution, so each dissipates about 25.7 / 6 ≈ **4.3 W**. The peak during its conduction interval is higher.

**Copper loss** in the PCB phase loop:

$$
P_{cu,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = 3.2\,\text{W}
$$
