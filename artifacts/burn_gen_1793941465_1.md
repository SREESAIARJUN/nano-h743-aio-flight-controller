## 0. Assumed Platform and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, FPU, CORDIC), one per gimbal or one per axis |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5.6 Ω, L ≈ 2.1 mH, Kt ≈ 0.12 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) over SPI |
| Current sense | 2-shunt low-side with inline amplifier, sampled at PWM center |
| PWM | 20 kHz center-aligned. Current loop runs at 20 kHz |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted symmetrically about the payload rotation center (r₂ = −r₁), ODR 8 kHz, SPI @ 24 MHz |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz → velocity 4 kHz → angle/attitude 1 kHz |

**Loop hierarchy (per axis):**

```
Attitude ref ─► [Angle P+FF] ─► ω_ref ─► [Velocity PI] ─► i_q,fb ─┐
     ▲              1 kHz        ▲          4 kHz                 ├─► [Current PI dq] ─► SVPWM
     │                           │                    i_q,cog(θ) ─┘        20 kHz
  Mahony attitude ◄── dual-IMU fused/filtered gyro + accel (8 kHz in, 1 kHz out)
```

**Key gimbal-specific choice:** velocity feedback is the camera's *inertial* rate projected onto the motor axis (from the gyro), blended with encoder rate. This choice makes the loop reject base motion directly.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Coordinate Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}
$$

**Park transform**, using electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d \cos\theta_e - v_q \sin\theta_e, \qquad
v_\beta = v_d \sin\theta_e + v_q \cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 SVPWM via Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection

This is equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM and yields a linear range of |v| ≤ V_bus/√3.

$$
v_a = v_\alpha, \quad
v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2} v_\beta, \quad
v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2} v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big), \qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

### 1.3 Current-Loop Design (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

The dq plant for a surface PMSM (L_d = L_q = L) is:

$$
L\frac{di_d}{dt} = v_d - R i_d + \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
L\frac{di_q}{dt} = v_q - R i_q - \omega_e L i_d - \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Use a PI controller with feedforward decoupling:

$$
v_d = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

With C(s) = K_p + K_i/s, choose K_i/K_p = R/L to cancel the electrical pole. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c, \qquad
\frac{i_q}{i_q^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s + \omega_c}
$$
