## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder reading:

$$
\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0})\big)
$$

θ_{m,0} is the encoder offset captured during alignment, by applying a d-axis voltage with θ_e = 0.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 2-shunt)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
