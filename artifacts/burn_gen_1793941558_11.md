**Architectural clarification.** In an AIO with a 4-in-1 ESC, the STM32H743 does not drive the gates. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (typically STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) plus a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry. Sections 1–3 concern the ESC power stage; Section 4 concerns the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths
Assume 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM, AM32 default).

- At any instant, two phases carry I_ph.
- During the PWM on-time, current flows through HS(a) and LS(b).
- During the off-time, current flows through LS(a), synchronously, and LS(b).

So exactly two MOSFET channels conduct I_ph at all times, apart from dead time.

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple
The worst-case ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.6\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple is negligible (0.06 %) at this inductance. It becomes significant only with low-inductance motors (below about 5 µH).

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.38 mΩ**.

### 1.4 Conduction loss per ESC channel

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 6404 \times 1.38\,m\Omega = \mathbf{17.7\ W}$$

### 1.5 Copper loss
Include this term; it is often forgotten. Model the phase trace plus pad as roughly 0.3 mΩ per conducting path, giving two paths in series:

$$P_{Cu} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.3\,m\Omega \approx 3.8\ \text{W}$$

### 1.6 Thermal runaway check
The electro-thermal loop is stable if

$$\frac{\partial P_{cond}}{\partial T_j}\, R_{th,ja} < 1$$

Here ∂P/∂T = 2 I² R_25 α = 0.058 W/K. With R_th,ja ≈ 10 K/W, the loop gain is 0.58, so the loop is **stable but with low margin**. It would go unstable above roughly 17 K/W, for example with a poor solder void fraction or no inner-layer copper under the FETs.
