# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack (Job #22): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot/DMA Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Headline Findings

### Architecture

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In this stack:

| Layer | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 at 480 MHz | ArduPilot | Attitude control. Generates bidirectional DShot600 and captures eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 4× AT32F421 (or equivalent), one per motor | AM32 / BLHeli_32 | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM, gate drive |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET with 3-phase gate drivers | n/a | 80 A burst per channel |

"48 kHz switching" is an ESC firmware parameter (AM32 `PWM_FREQUENCY`). "DShot600 DMA" is an H743/ArduPilot concern. The model treats them separately and joins them at the signal interface.

### Headline findings

1. **80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mm mount, roughly 30×30 mm board) form factor is thermally transient-only.**
   - All four motors at 80 A simultaneously gives about **3 s** before the board reaches 110 °C.
   - The sustainable continuous rating is about **26 A per motor**.
   - Marketing "80 A burst" must be qualified as "≤ 3 s, single-event, with propwash."
2. **Conduction loss dominates** (about 87% of power-stage loss at 80 A). Switching loss at 48 kHz is secondary but not negligible.
3. **DShot600 bidirectional on the H743 is cleanly allocatable**, because the H7 DMAMUX removes the F4/F7 fixed-stream conflicts. It needs 1 UP stream plus 2 capture streams per timer.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

During each 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd and phase B is held low:

- **PWM ON (duty D):** HS_A → motor → LS_B. Two FETs in the path.
- **PWM OFF (1−D), complementary:** LS_A (synchronous) → motor → LS_B. Two FETs in the path.

Two FETs conduct at all times, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds,hot}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = I^2\,(2R_{ds,hot} + R_{cu} + R_{sh})$$

### 2.2 Numerical result at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Per motor |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFETs | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| Copper | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| Shunt | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| **Total conduction** | | **24.0 W** |

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical period, plus synchronous-rectification share for the LS devices. A uniform-spread estimate is:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

**Note on hot-spotting:** the LS FETs carry more freewheel current at low D. At D < 0.3, expect the LS devices to run about 20–30% hotter than the HS devices.
