## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification, at any instant:
- one leg is PWM'd: the high-side FET conducts during D, and its low-side sync FET conducts during 1−D;
- one low-side FET of the return leg is held on.

So **two FET-equivalents always carry I_ph**. Each of the 6 FETs per motor carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period.
