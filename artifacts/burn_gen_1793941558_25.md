## 0. Architecture Assumptions and a Feasibility Warning

Some choices below are assumptions I made where the brief was silent. Replace them with your selected parts before release.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 (AT32F421 class). The H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V maximum (worst case for switching loss) |
| Form factor | "Nano" board: 20×20 mm mounting, about 26×26 mm PCB, 6-layer, 2 oz outer and inner copper |
| MOSFETs | 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 package, one per switch (6 per channel, 24 in total) |
| MOSFET parameters | R_D
