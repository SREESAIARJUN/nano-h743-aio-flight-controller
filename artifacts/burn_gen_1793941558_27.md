## 0. Architecture Corrections (Read First)

Three issues in the brief affect everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** ArduPilot outputs DShot commands only. Each motor's 6-step/FOC commutation and 48 kHz PWM runs on a dedicated ESC MCU, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
   - The 4-in-1 therefore has four ESC MCUs.
   - The H743 talks to them over four bi-directional DShot600 lines.
   - The switching-loss model below lives on the ESC side. The DMA model lives on the FC side.

2. **"80A" must be defined.** In this document, 80A means **phase current per motor, burst**. Battery current is lower: I_bat ≈ D·I_ph.

3. **80A on a nano (20×20 mm) stack is thermally burst-only.** The math in §3 shows:
   - All four motors at 80A: about **1.3 s** before T_j reaches 125°C.
   - Continuous capability: about **18A per motor** in typical prop-wash airflow.

   Marketing ratings that claim more assume heat-sinking or airflow that a nano stack does not have.
