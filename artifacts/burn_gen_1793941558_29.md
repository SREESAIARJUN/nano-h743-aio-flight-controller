## 0. Architectural Ground Truth (Read First)

Two findings shape the rest of this document.

**Finding 1: The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
In a production 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and only *commands* the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. Commutation at 48 kHz runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32.
