## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

Several points need to be settled before the math.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs a per-channel ESC MCU running BLHeli_32 or AM32. | H743 = flight controller (ArduPilot). 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 = ESC MCUs (AM32). They are linked by bi-directional DShot600. |
| 2 | "80A burst" is interpreted as **80A per motor channel**. | With all four channels at burst, that is **320A** from the pack. This drives the bus capacitor, shunt and battery-connector sizing. |
| 3 | Form factor. 80A per channel on a true 20×20mm board is not thermally credible beyond ~1 s. | Analysis assumes a **30.5×30.5mm, 8-layer, 2oz** board. A 20×20 variant should be derated to ~45–55A burst. |
| 4 | Supply. | 6S LiPo: V_bus,max = 25.2V, V_nom = 22.2V. MOSFETs rated 40V. |
| 5 | PWM. | 48 kHz, synchronous rectification (complementary PWM), trapezoidal 6-step. |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

At T_j = 100°C: R_ds = 1.2 × 1.3375 ≈ **1.6 mΩ**. This is the design point used below.
