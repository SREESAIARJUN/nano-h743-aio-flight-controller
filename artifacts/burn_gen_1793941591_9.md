## 0. Architecture Summary and Feasibility Statement

The "80A burst" figure is the dominant risk on a nano-format board. Section 3 shows that it is thermally achievable only as a **time-limited burst** of about 1.3 s with all four motors loaded, or about 5 s with one motor loaded. Sustained capability is closer to **20–28 A per motor** with prop-wash cooling. Marketing copy should not state 80A without the burst duration.
