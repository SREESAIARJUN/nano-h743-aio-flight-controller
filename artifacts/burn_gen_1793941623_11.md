**Architecture note.** The H743 does not commutate motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to four ESC MCUs. Driving 24 FETs at 48 kHz with sensorless BEMF timing from the H743 is not supported by ArduPilot and is not recommended.

**Design flags (stated up front):**
1. **80 A × 4 channels on 20×20 mm is a ~1–2 s transient rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 quantifies this.
2. **Battery current.** At D ≈ 1, I_bat ≈ 4 × 80 = 320 A. The pads, plane and connector cannot carry this continuously on a nano board.
3. **Current sensing.** A shunt sized for 320 A dissipates heavily. A Hall sensor or per-channel phase sense is recommended (Section 5).

```
