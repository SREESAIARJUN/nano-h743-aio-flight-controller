## 0. Architecture Clarification

An STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four sensorless BLDC motors itself. It has only two advanced timers (TIM1/TIM8) with hardware complementary outputs and dead-time, and ArduPilot has no on-FC ESC firmware. The design therefore uses this split:
