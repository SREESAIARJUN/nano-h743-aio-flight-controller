# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #66)
## System Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, DShot/DMA Allocation, and Reference Netlist

> **Principal Architect's summary:** The math below shows that **80 A per channel is achievable only as a short burst on a nano-format board.** It is not a continuous rating. With all four channels at 80 A simultaneously, the board reaches its thermal limit in about 2 s with no heatsink. The analysis also shows that the realistic continuous rating is about 25 A per channel, which matches what the industry ships in this form factor. The spec sheet must state burst duration and conditions explicitly. Everything here is a reference design and must be validated on hardware.
