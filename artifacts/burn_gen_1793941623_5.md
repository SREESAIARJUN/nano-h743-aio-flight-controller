## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421, STM32G071, or EFM8 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 is the flight controller. It generates bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The partition used here is:

```
[H743 FC] --4x BDShot600 (single-wire, open-drain-ish, pulled up)--> [4x ESC MCU] --> [Gate drv] --> [3-phase bridge] x4
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation, two phase legs carry current at any instant. With complementary (synchronous) PWM, the current is carried by either the high-side or the low-side switch of the PWM-ed leg. It never flows through both, apart from dead time.

The series path therefore **always contains two MOSFETs**, independent of duty cycle D.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = k_T \cdot R_{ds(on),25}
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times (1.5 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega) = 6400 \times 3.0\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

### 1.2 Distribution per switch

Each leg is active for 2/3 of an electrical cycle. Within that window the conduction is split between the high side (D) and the low side (1−D) of the PWM leg, while the opposite leg's switch conducts fully.

Averaged over a commutation cycle, the six switches share the loss roughly equally:

$$
\bar P_{cond,sw} \approx \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}
$$

Peak instantaneous dissipation in the static (non-PWM) switch during its 1/3-cycle window is I²R = 9.6 W.

The die thermal time constant (τ ≈ 1–5 ms) exceeds the electrical period above about 1,000 eRPM-Hz, so the average value governs.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses (often ignored, not negligible)

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt (if fitted) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Phase pad / trace (2 oz, 8 L, ~0.3 mΩ path) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Battery pad → bridge (shared, 4 ch at 320 A pk) | 0.1 mΩ | 10.2 W total |

The shared DC-link copper at 4×80 A is a major hidden heater. Use solid planes on L1, L2, L7, and L8 for V_bus and GND.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ed leg switches (2 MOSFETs per channel at any time).

**(a) V-I overlap (hard switching)**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
$$

**(b) Dead-time body-diode conduction**

$$
P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.307\ W}
$$

**(c) Reverse recovery**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}
$$
