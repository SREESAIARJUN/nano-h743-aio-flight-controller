| IMU sampling | 8 kHz | - | ≥ 2× highest vibration harmonic to notch |
| Attitude fusion | 1 kHz | - | - |

**Key gimbal-specific choices:**
- The encoder is used for **commutation** (electrical angle).
- The **inertial stabilization** loops use the camera-side IMU. The gyro rate is projected onto each motor axis, and attitude comes from fusion.
- For stabilization, the gyro loop beats encoder velocity because it directly rejects base motion.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b) \quad(\text{assuming } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset) and p = pole pairs:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

**Torque** (surface PM, L_d ≈ L_q):

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

The plant is $\frac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choose PI $K_p + K_i/s$ with zero at $R/L$:

$$
K_{p,c}= L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,c}= R\,\omega_c \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$
