## 0. Feasibility Finding (Read First)

Before the derivation, here is a result that drives the rest of the architecture:

> **80 A burst on all four channels at once is not thermally or electrically achievable on a nano (20×20 mm) AIO board.** With 6S power, 4 × 80 A ≈ 320 A, which is about 8 kW. The model below puts total board dissipation near **78 W**. The adiabatic thermal budget of a 20×20 board would be exhausted in about **1 second**.

**Recommended interpretation of the requirement (Assumption A0):**
- **80 A burst** is a per-channel, single-motor rating for **≤ 5 s**, at a **30.5×30.5 mm** board size.
- **4-channel simultaneous burst** is derated to **55 A for ≤ 5 s** and **40 A continuous**.

All equations are parametric, so you can substitute any other burst definition. Every numeric value below is an engineering estimate based on stated assumptions. Validate it against the selected MOSFET datasheet and against IR thermography on prototype boards.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two switch positions carry phase current at every instant:

- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is fully on, so it conducts for 100% of the commutation interval.
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for fraction D, and its complementary low-side FET conducts for fraction (1 − D).

Each FET in a leg conducts for one third of the electrical cycle on average. Per channel, exactly two R_ds(on) are always in the current path.

### 2.2 RMS current including ripple

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

Assume a typical 5-inch motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line) at D = 0.9, with V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 2.5 V at full load:

- ΔI ≈ 2.5 × 0.9 / (15 µH × 48 kHz) ≈ 3.1 A
- Ripple term = 0.8 A², which is negligible relative to 6400 A².
