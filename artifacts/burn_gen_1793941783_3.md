|---|---|---|

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform, using electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling
For an SPMSM (L_d = L_q = L):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

The controller output is the PI term plus decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

with λ_m = K_t / (1.5 p).

### 1.3 Gains by pole-zero cancellation
The PI zero cancels the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \quad(\text{continuous}),\qquad K_i^{disc} = K_i T_s$$

Choose ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s. This is under f_s/10, leaving margin for the 1.5-sample PWM/computation delay.

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and SVPWM
1. **Circular limit:** cap the voltage vector at |v| ≤ V_bus/√3, giving d-axis priority:
   $$v_{q,max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}}$$
2. **Min-max (zero-sequence) injection SVPWM.** This is equivalent to sector-based SVPWM:
   $$v_{x}' = v_x - \tfrac12(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.5 Anti-windup
Use back-calculation with gain K_aw ≈ 1/K_p (per-sample form):

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s\, e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_{sat} - u_{unsat})$$
