# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

**Assumptions.** I don't have the Job #52 spec sheet, so the design is parameterized. Where I needed concrete numbers, I used a typical brushless gimbal stack:

- **Motor:** 12N14P gimbal BLDC (GBM2804-class), with pole pairs p = 7.
- **Commutation sensor:** 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701).
- **MCU:** STM32G4 / H7-class with FPU.
- **Current sensing:** inline or low-side shunts.
- **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P on the camera plate.

Replace the values in `Config` with your measured R, L, λ, J, and Kt.
