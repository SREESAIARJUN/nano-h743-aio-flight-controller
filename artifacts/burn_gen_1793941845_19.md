## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Finding

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate 4 BLDC motors with sensorless BEMF control in a production-viable way. The standard partitioning, used here, is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or VIH6 (TFBGA100) for nano | ArduPilot, 4× bi-dir DShot600 out |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 / BLHeli_32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, 20 V bootstrap) | 6 FETs per channel |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 6S max (25.2 V) |

**Key finding.** On a 20×20 mm (nano) board, **80 A per channel is a transient rating of about 1.5 to 2 s, not a continuous one.** At 80 A on all four channels the system is thermally unstable: no steady state exists. Section 2.6 shows the math. Marketing should specify "80 A burst ≤ 1 s, 10% duty." The continuous rating is roughly 20 to 30 A per channel, depending on airflow.

All parameters marked ⟨P⟩ are representative placeholder values. Substitute the final MOSFET datasheet values before release. The equations are general.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the high side. In any 60° sector:

- one phase's high-side switches at duty *D*, with its low-side complementary (1−D);
- one phase's low-side is fully ON;
- one phase floats (BEMF sensing).

Over one electrical cycle, each phase spends ⅓ in PWM-high, ⅓ in full low, and ⅓ floating.

### 2.2 Per-FET Conduction Loss

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,\frac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{LS} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\left[\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right]$$

### 2.3 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

Summing over 3 legs gives:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 3\cdot I^2R_{ds}\left(\tfrac{D}{3}+\tfrac{1-D}{3}+\tfrac{1}{3}\right) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This is independent of D, because two FETs are always in series with the phase current. The **worst single FET** is the low-side, at D → 0 (low throttle, high current, such as a stalled or locked prop):

$$P_{LS,max} = \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{ds}$$
