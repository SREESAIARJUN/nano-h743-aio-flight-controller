# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and One Architectural Correction

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 should be the **flight controller MCU only**. Each ESC channel, or the 4-in-1 as a whole, needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32/Bluejay. Typical choices are the AT32F421, STM32G071, or GD32E230.

The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. Running 4× sensorless FOC or 6-step commutation plus ArduPilot's EKF on one H743 is not supported by ArduPilot and would break the timing model below.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low side (LS) conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the LS stays on for the whole sector.

So the bridge always has exactly two channel resistances in series with I_ph, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D R_{HS} + (1-D) R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] \approx 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each switch conducts about 1/3 of the electrical period. The electrical period is less than 1 ms at flight eRPM, which is far below the die thermal time constant of roughly 5–10 ms. Each FET therefore sees the time-average:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,bridge}}{6}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-coupled solution (fixed point)

R_DS(on) and T_j depend on each other. Junction temperature is:

$$T_j = T_B + P_{FET}(T_j)\,(R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta CB}), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Substituting the loss equation (with per-FET switching loss written as P_sw,FET):

$$T_j = T_B + \left[\tfrac{2I^2R_{25}(1+\alpha(T_j-25))}{6} + P_{sw,FET}\right] R_{\theta,JB}$$

Solving for T_j in closed form:

$$T_j=\frac{T_B + R_{\theta,JB}\left[\frac{I^2R_{25}}{3}(1-25\alpha) + P_{sw,FET}\right]}{1-\frac{I^2R_{25}\,\alpha}{3}R_{\theta,JB}}$$

**Runaway criterion.** The denominator must stay positive:

$$\frac{I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta,JB}}{3} < 1$$

With our values: 6400 × 0.0016 × 0.0055 × 7.8 / 3 = **0.146**. There is no electrothermal runaway; the loop gain is 0.146.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

Two cases, using P_sw,FET ≈ 0.35 W (from §2, allocated to the two switching FETs and averaged over six):

| T_B (board) | T_j (solved) | R_DS(T_j) | P_cond,bridge | P_cond,FET |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 °C | 97 °C | 2.23 mΩ | 28.6 W | 4.76 W |
| 90 °C | 156 °C ⚠ | 2.75 mΩ | 35.2 W | 5.87 W |

**Takeaway:** the junction sits roughly 57–66 K above the local board. This is set by R_θCB, i.e., the via array under the pad. That makes the via array your single most important layout parameter.
