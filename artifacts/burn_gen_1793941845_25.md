> **Architectural note:** The H743 should not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot is not a motor-control firmware, and 4 × 48 kHz commutation with BEMF zero-cross detection would compete with the EKF and scheduler. The industry-standard AIO split is H743 → bidirectional DShot → 4 × ESC MCU. The rest of this document assumes that split.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent Rds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

| Tj | Rds(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.60 mΩ |

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, current flows through:

- **PWM leg:** either the HS FET (for time D·T) or the synchronous LS FET (for time (1−D)·T). Current always flows through exactly one Rds in this leg, except during dead time, which is handled in Section 2.
- **Return leg:** the static LS FET, on for the whole sector.
- **Floating leg:** no current (BEMF sensing).

The result is independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2\,R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.3 Copper loss

Assume a phase path of 2 oz outer layer plus 2 oz internal layer in parallel, about 15 mm long, 4 mm wide, with vias. The estimated path resistance is R_Cu ≈ 0.6 mΩ total, including the battery-pad share and pad-to-FET runs.

### 1.4 Results at I_ph = 80 A

| Tj | 2·I²·Rds | I²·R_Cu | **P_cond per channel** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 12.8 W | 3.8 W | 16.6 W |
| 100 °C | 18.6 W | 3.8 W (rises with Cu tempco, ~+0.39 %/K) | **≈ 22.4 W** (≈ 23.5 W with hot Cu) |
