**Architecture clarification.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-sector conduction topology

In each 60° commutation sector, three FETs carry current:

- The **high-side FET of phase A** (PWM, duty D) conducts I for D·T.
- The **complementary low-side FET of phase A** conducts I for (1−D)·T (synchronous freewheel).
- The **low-side FET of phase B** (return path) conducts I for 100% of T.

The total instantaneous I²R in the bridge is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = D I^2R + (1-D)I^2R + I^2R = 2I^2R_{ds}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET averages over one electrical cycle

Each role is held for 1/3 of the cycle.

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)I^2R + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R$$

The **worst FET is the low side at low D**, which approaches (2/3)·I²R.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

The common factor is I²R = 6400 × 1.45e-3 = **9.28 W**.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per channel (2I²R) | **18.56 W** |
| Worst low-side FET (D→0) | 6.19 W |
| High-side FET (D = 1) | 3.09 W |
| 4 channels | **74.2 W** |

### 1.5 Copper and shunt losses

| Path | Resistance | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Copper per channel (phase pad + bus pour, ≈0.5 mΩ) | 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W/ch → 12.8 W total |
| Common bus shunt (0.25 mΩ) carrying ΣI ≈ 4×80×D_avg | 0.25 mΩ | ≈ 1.6 W at ~80 A average bus current (D ≈ 0.25) |

**Mitigation.** Paralleling two FETs per position cuts conduction loss by 2× but doubles Q_g. It is usually not physically possible on a 20×20 board.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the **PWMing half-bridge** hard-switches. Per channel, one turn-on and one turn-off occur each PWM period.

### 2.1 Loss components

**V·I overlap (hard switching):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\times25.2\times80\times30\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.048\ W$$
