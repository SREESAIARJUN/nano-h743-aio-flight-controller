# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

**Job #20, Rev A (Engineering Baseline)**

## 0. Architectural Corrections Before Modeling

Three issues in the requirement change the model.

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a FC + 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model is therefore split between the FC (DShot/DMA) and the ESC (losses/thermal).
2. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm nano board is not thermally achievable for more than about 1 s.** The math in §3 proves this. The rating only holds as a short per-motor transient. I give the derated continuous figure and the board size needed for a true 80 A burst.
3. **Battery-side current is not survivable at 4 × 80 A.** At full duty that is about 320 A through an XT60 (rated around 60 A) and 20 mm-wide copper. Treat 80 A as a single-motor transient while the other three motors run at lower current.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

In six-step operation with synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs carry the full current I_m at every instant:

- the high side of the sourcing phase during PWM-on, or the low side of the same leg during PWM-off;
- the low side of the sinking phase.

Total conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_m^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period (120°). Within that window the high-side and low-side FETs of the PWM leg split time by D and (1−D):

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_m^2 R_{DS} \cdot D,\qquad P_{cond,LS(sync)} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_m^2 R_{DS}(1-D),\qquad P_{cond,LS(sink)} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_m^2 R_{DS}$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.003 = \mathbf{38.4\ W}$$
