## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Assumptions

Several items in the brief need correcting before the models apply.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 as the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC-side setting (AM32 supports 24–48 kHz). The H743 only generates bidirectional DShot600 and consumes eRPM telemetry. The design below uses **4× Artery AT32F421K8U7 (AM32)**, one per channel. One MCU per channel is more robust and has better firmware
