# Job #42: STM32H743 Nano AIO with 80 A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC
## System Model: Conduction Losses, 48 kHz Switching, Thermal Transients, and ArduPilot Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

---

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 System Partition

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It should not commutate the motors directly. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay with bidirectional DShot (BDShot) support. The two domains communicate over four DShot lines.

```
 6S LiPo ──┬── Bulk/MLCC bank ── 4× 3-phase bridges (24 FETs) ── Motors 1-4
           │                        ▲ gate drivers ×4
           │                 ESC MCU ×4 (AM32 / BLHeli_32 / Bluejay)
           │                        ▲ DShot600 (bidirectional, single wire)
           └── 5V/3V3 BECs ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── IMU, baro, OSD, UARTs
```

### 0.3 Feasibility Flags

Two items need confirmation before layout begins.

1. **Burst-only rating.** An 80 A burst on a 20×20 or 30×30 nano board is a transient rating, not a continuous one. The model below quantifies how long the burst can last.
2. **Package current limit.** Many 3.3×3.3 mm FETs specify 60–100 A package limits under ideal heatsinking. Confirm the bond-wire/clip pulse rating (I_DM and the SOA curve) for an 80 A, roughly 2 s pulse.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path

This model assumes six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the default in AM32/BLHeli_32. In every commutation sector, exactly two FETs are in series with the motor current:

- **PWM on-time (D):** high-side (HS) FET of phase A plus low-side (LS) FET of phase B.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** LS FET of phase A (synchronous rectification) plus LS FET of phase B.

So, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$
