## 0. Architectural Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation stack, and an H743 running a 400 Hz+ attitude loop should not also run four sensorless FOC/6-step loops. The correct partitioning is:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, sensors, bidir DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver, 20 V boot) | 6 gate outputs per channel |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 3 half-bridges per motor |

> **Form-factor caveat.** 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm "nano" footprint is not physically realizable with a single PDFN3.3 per switch. Everything below assumes a 30.5/36 mm board. Section 3 shows that even at that size, 80 A is a **time-limited burst rating**, not a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **PWM phase:** the high-side (HS) switch conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) switch conducts for 1−D (synchronous rectification), excluding dead time.
- **Return phase:** the LS switch is held on for 100% of the sector.

The total channel conduction loss is therefore approximately independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I_{rms}^2 R_{hot}
$$
