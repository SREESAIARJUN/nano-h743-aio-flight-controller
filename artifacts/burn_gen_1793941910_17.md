## 0. Scope Corrections

Three requirements, as literally stated, would produce a board that fails. The design below keeps your targets but bounds them.

| Requirement | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| **H743 + 4-in-1 ESC** | The H743 cannot run ArduPilot *and* 4× sensorless commutation at 48 kHz with bidirectional DShot to itself. That is architecturally circular. | **Two-board stack.** The H743 runs the FC. The ESC board has 4× AM32-class MCUs (STM32G071 / AT32F421) and gate drivers
