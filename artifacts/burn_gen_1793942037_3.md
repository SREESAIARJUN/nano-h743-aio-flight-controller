> **⚠ Feasibility flag.** "80 A burst" on a 20×20 nano form factor is not physically credible. You cannot place 24 FETs, the bulk capacitance and 80 A-rated copper in 400 mm². These calculations assume 30.5×30.5 mm. Even at that size, 4 × 80 A simultaneous is a few-second capability, as derived in §1.5.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation
At every instant, two phases carry the motor current I:

- **PWM on:** high-side FET of phase A and low-side FET of phase B.
- **PWM off:** freewheel through the low-side of A (synchronous rectification) and the low-side of B.

Two R_DS(on) are always in series, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\cdot 80^2\cdot 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ ESC}$$

The parasitic copper and shunt losses add to this:

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper (2 oz, 8 layers paralleled, about 0.3 mΩ effective path) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Current-sense shunt (if per-ESC) | 0.2 mΩ | 1.3 W |
| Motor-pad solder joints | about 0.1 mΩ | 0.6 W |

**Total conduction loss: about 23 W per ESC at 80 A, or about 92 W for 4 channels.**

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution
Each FET conducts roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The low-side FETs carry the extra freewheel share at low duty. The worst case is the low-side FET at D → 0:

$$P_{LS,max} \approx \tfrac{2}{3}\,I^2 R \approx 6.4\ \mathrm{W}, \qquad P_{avg/FET} \approx 19.2/6 = 3.2\ \mathrm{W}$$

Junction rise above the board:
$$\Delta T_{j\text{-}pcb} = P\cdot(R_{thJC}+R_{th,solder}) \approx 6.4\cdot(1.5+0.5) \approx \mathbf{13\ K\ worst\ case}$$

### 1.5 Burst thermal transient (board-level lumped model)

Heat capacity:
- FR4: 1.44 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ × 1.1 J/(g·K) ≈ 2.9 J/K
- Copper: 8 × 70 µm × 9.3 cm² ≈ 0.52 cm³ → 4.7 g × 0.385 J/(g·K) ≈ 1.8 J/K
- FETs, capacitors, solder: ≈ 0.5 J/K

So **C_th ≈ 5.2 J/K**.

With prop-wash cooling at h ≈ 60 W/(m²·K) over about 2 × 9.3 cm² (two faces):
$$R_{th,amb} \approx \frac{1}{60\cdot 1.86\times10^{-3}} \approx 9\ \mathrm{K/W}$$
$$\tau = R C \approx 47\ \mathrm{s}$$

Since the burst is much shorter than τ, the board heats approximately adiabatically:
$$\frac{dT}{dt}\approx\frac{P_{tot}}{C_{th}}$$
