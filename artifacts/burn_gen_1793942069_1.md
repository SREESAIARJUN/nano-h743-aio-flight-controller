## 1. System Architecture

```
                     ┌───────────────── 1 kHz ─────────────────┐   ┌──── 4 kHz ────┐  ┌──── 20 kHz ─────┐
 θ_ref ──►(+)─► Angle P/PI ─►(+)─► ω_ref ─►(+)─► Velocity PI ─►(+)─► i_q* ─►(+)─► Current PI ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
          ▲                  ▲ ω_ff         ▲                   ▲ i_cog,ff     ▲
          │                  │              │                   │              │ i_d,i_q (Clarke/Park)
   θ_cam (attitude est.)  trajectory   ω_meas (gyro ⊕ encoder)  Anti-cogging(θ_m)
          ▲                                 ▲
   Dual ICM-42688-P ─► AAF ─► calibration ─► fusion (lever-arm cancel + inverse variance) ─► notch bank ─► Mahony
```

**Design principles:**

- The **outer loop closes on the camera attitude** from the IMU. It does not close on motor angle.
- The **velocity loop uses the IMU gyro rate**, which is low-latency and relative to inertial space. It is blended with the encoder-derived rate for stiffness against structural modes.
- The **current loop uses the encoder angle** for commutation.
- **Bandwidth separation** of at least 5× is kept between loops:
  - current ≈ 1.5 kHz
  - velocity ≈ 150 Hz
  - angle ≈ 20–30 Hz

## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 2.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad p = 7
$$

### 2.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, two-shunt measurement)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 2.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.4 Plant in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Torque: $\tau = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$, so $\lambda_m = \dfrac{2K_t}{3p}$.

### 2.5 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

The plant is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choose the PI zero at $R/L$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}
$$

- **Bandwidth:** $\omega_{ci} = 2\pi\cdot 1500$ rad/s. Keep $\omega_{ci} \le \omega_{\text{PWM}}/10$ to account for the 1.5-sample delay (computation plus PWM update).
- **Decoupling feedforward:**
$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
- **Voltage limit:** clamp to $|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$ (the SVPWM linear region). Prioritize $v_d$ to retain flux control.
