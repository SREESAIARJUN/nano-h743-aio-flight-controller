**Two-sensor control architecture.**
- The encoder gives the electrical angle for commutation. It also gives the joint angle, used for soft limits and axis-coupling kinematics.
- The fused IMU gives inertial camera rate and attitude. These close the stabilization loops, because the goal is an inertially stable camera, not a fixed joint angle.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and current PI controllers

The dq-frame plant, with ω_e = p·ω_m and λ the flux linkage:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

Torque:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

Control law:
- Pole-zero cancellation PI.
- Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward.
- Back-calculation anti-windup.

$$
v_q^* = K_p e_q + \int\!\big(K_i e_q + K_{aw}(v_q^{sat}-v_q^{*})\big)dt + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

The d-axis is identical, with feedforward −ω_e L i_q and reference i_d* = 0.

Gains for a closed-loop current bandwidth ω_c (choose ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, i.e. at most f_pwm/10):

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

The closed loop then reduces to a first-order lag:

$$
\frac{i_q}{i_q^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

**Voltage limit.** Saturate (v_d, v_q) to a circle, giving d-axis priority:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max}
$$

### 1.3 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

This is mathematically equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM.

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\qquad
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v_x + \min v_x)
$$

$$
d_x = \frac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$
