## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**PMSM model in the dq frame:**

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

**PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation.** Choose the closed-loop current bandwidth ω_c (for example 2π·1.5 kHz, below f_s/10):

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m),\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}
$$

**Voltage limiting.** The vector is circle-limited to |v| ≤ V_bus/√3, with priority given to v_d. The integrators use back-calculation anti-windup (Section 2).
