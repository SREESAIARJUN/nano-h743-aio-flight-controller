## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

ArduPilot does not run field-oriented control or 6-step commutation on the flight controller. A "4-in-1 ESC nano FC" therefore uses this partitioning:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (400 MHz, 2 MB flash) | ArduPilot, generates 4× bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | STM32G071 / AT32F421 (AM32) or EFM8BB51 (Bluejay) | Commutation, BEMF, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET per motor | 3-phase inverter |

All numbers below depend on the following assumptions. Replace them with your BOM values and the equations still hold.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full) | V_bus | 25.2 V | Worst case for switching loss |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_pk | 80 A | Treated as DC-equivalent during burst |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz | |
| MOSFET | – | 40 V, R_DS(on) = 0.70 mΩ @ 25 °C, 5×6 / 3.3×3.3 class | e.g., BSC007N04LS6-class |
| R_DS(on) temperature factor at T_j = 125 °C | k_T | 1.5 | Iterated in §2.5 |
| Q_g / Q_gd / Q_rr / Q_oss | – | 90 nC / 15 nC / 60 nC / 80 nC | Datasheet-class values |
| Gate drive voltage | V_dr | 12 V (FD6288 V_CC) | |
| Dead time | t_dt | 200 ns | Typical AM32 setting range |
| Body-diode V_f @ 80 A | V_f | 0.85 V | |

> **Feasibility flag:** 80 A/motor (320 A aggregate) on a nano-format board (20×20 or 30×30 mm) is a **burst-only** rating. §3 shows it is thermally limited to seconds, not minutes. The marketing number should say "80 A burst (≤ 5 s), 45–55 A continuous with airflow" unless you have validated otherwise.

## 1. Inverter Conduction Topology

For 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, which AM32 and Bluejay use:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is fully on for the entire 60° sector.
- **Floating leg:** off; BEMF is sensed here.

The current path therefore always passes through **two** R_DS(on) in series, independent of D (ignoring dead time).

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \cdot \underbrace{[D + (1-D)]}_{\text{PWM leg}} + I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\cdot \underbrace{1}_{\text{return leg}} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K} \Rightarrow k_T(125^\circ\text{C}) \approx 1.5
$$

Numerically, at 80 A:

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times (0.70\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5) = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.44\ W}
$$

**Copper/PCB conduction must be added.** Assume a phase path of 2 oz outer layers plus 2 oz inner layers in parallel, roughly 0.25 mΩ total trace and via resistance per current loop at temperature:

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.60\ W}
$$

**Per-FET hotspot:** the return-leg low-side FET dissipates I²R for the full sector:

$$
P_{FET,peak} = 6400 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = 6.72\ \text{W}
$$

Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Above ~50 Hz electrical (≈ 430 RPM on a 14-pole motor), the die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) averages this:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond}}{6} = 2.24\ \text{W}
$$

**Locked-rotor / low-RPM desync is the true worst case:** one FET sees the full 6.72 W continuously. The ESC firmware must enforce stall protection (AM32 `stall protection` / current limit).

### 2.2 Hard-Switching Loss (PWM Leg High-Side)

Switching time from gate charge:

$$
I_g = \frac{V_{dr} - V_{plateau}}{R_g + R_{g,int}} = \frac{12 - 3.0}{10 + 1.2} \approx 0.80\ \text{A}
$$

$$
t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} = \frac{15\ \text{nC}}{0.80\ \text{A}} \approx 19\ \text{ns}
$$

The FD6288 sink current is stronger than its source current, so t_f is slightly faster. 19 ns is used conservatively for both edges.

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 38\times10^{-9} \times 48\times10^3 = \mathbf{1.84\ W}
$$
