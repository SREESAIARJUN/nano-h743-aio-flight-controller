# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture Package (Job #48)

## 0. Architectural Premise and Scope

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. Running four sensorless FOC/6-step loops at 48 kHz on the FC MCU would collide with ArduPilot's scheduler and offers no benefit. The architecture used here is:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), AM32 firmware | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 5×6 | 24 FETs total |

**Form factor.** A true 20×20 "nano" layout cannot carry 80 A per channel. This package assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner-power** stack. The 80 A figure is treated strictly as a **burst rating**, and Section 2 quantifies how long that burst can last.

**Placeholder values.** All device parameters below (R_DS(on), Q_g, rise/fall times, R_th) are design placeholders typical of 40 V, ~1 mΩ trench FETs. Substitute the datasheet values of your chosen part and re-run the model. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers illustrate them.

## 1. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Operating Point and Symbols

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, full charge (worst case for switching loss) |
| I | 80 A | Phase current, burst |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_DS25 | 1.2 mΩ | R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient |
| t_r, t_f | 15 ns each | Effective V–I overlap times at the chosen R_g |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward drop at 80 A |
| Q_rr | 30 nC | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge |
| C_oss(er) | 1.5 nF | Energy-related output capacitance |
| Q_g | 50 nC | Total gate charge |
| V_drv | 10 V | Gate-drive voltage |

The R_DS(on) temperature dependence is:

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

In 6-step operation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, the current path always passes through **exactly two conducting FETs**:

- the PWM leg, where either the high-side or low-side FET is on (duty D vs 1−D), and
- the static low-side FET of the return phase.

The loss is therefore duty-independent:

$$P_{cond}=2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

| Condition | Value |
|---|---|
| At 25 °C | 2 × 6400 × 1.2 mΩ = **15.4 W** |
| At T_j = 125 °C (R_DS = 1.74 mΩ) | **22.3 W** |

Copper loss must be added on top. For a phase path plus bus segment of ≈0.5 mΩ:

$$P_{Cu}=I^2R_{Cu}=6400 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega=3.2\ \text{W}$$

This loss heats the board, not the junctions, but it raises the effective ambient seen by the FETs.

### 1.3 Switching Losses (only the PWM leg switches)

**Hard-switching V–I overlap:**

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz})=\mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt}=V_{SD}\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot200\,\text{ns}\cdot48\,\text{kHz}=\mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr}=V_{bus}\,Q_{rr}\,f_{sw}=25.2\cdot30\,\text{nC}\cdot48\,\text{kHz}=0.036\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss}=\tfrac12 C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}=0.023\ \text{W}$$
