## 0. Architecture Correction (Read First)

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. A production-grade AIO uses:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC MCUs (×4, or 2× dual) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 ⟨P⟩ | AM32 (supports bidirectional DShot + EDT) |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q-class 3-phase bootstrap driver ⟨P⟩ | — |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 ⟨P⟩ | — |

The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over DShot600. That link is the DMA/timer problem in §4.

**Feasibility flag:** 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 or 25.5 mm) board is a burst-only rating of roughly 1 second, as §3 shows. Do not let marketing quote it as continuous.
