# STM32H743 FC + 80A Burst Nano 4-in-1 ESC: Reference Design Package (Job #4)

**Scope:** loss model, thermal model, switching analysis, ArduPilot BDShot600 timer and DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

> **Engineering caveats**
> 1. **Placeholder silicon.** No MOSFET or driver part was specified. All numbers use a representative 40V 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET and an FD6288-class 3-phase driver. Every equation is parametric, so substitute your BOM datasheet values and the model re-solves.
> 2. **The H743 is not the ESC MCU.** In a 4-in-1 stack the STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel runs a dedicated low-cost MCU (e.g., AT32F421 with AM32, or a BLHeli_32-class part) that performs commutation at 48 kHz. The H743 only generates DShot and decodes eRPM telemetry. The design is partitioned that way below.
> 3. **"80A burst" on a nano board is thermally bounded in seconds, not minutes.** This is derived quantitatively in Section 3.

- **Phase Y (return leg):** the low side is on for 100% of the sector.
