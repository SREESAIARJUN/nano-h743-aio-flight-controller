# STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Analysis (Job #13)

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Scope

Three issues shape every number below.

1. **The H743 cannot be the ESC controller.** ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The architecture is:
   - **STM32H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bidirectional DShot600 to
   - **4 × ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Each drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.
   - The 48 kHz PWM is an AM32 parameter, not an H743 function.
2. **"80 A burst" must mean per channel, phase current, ≤1 s.** On a nano footprint (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm), Section 3 shows continuous capability is about 20 A per channel. Peak battery current also needs a hard system limit. Four channels at 80 A and high duty means more than 250 A from the pack, which no nano power-entry path survives.
3. **All device parameters are representative.** They are not taken from a specific datasheet. Replace them with your selected MOSFET, driver, and stack-up data before sign-off. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are a worked baseline.
