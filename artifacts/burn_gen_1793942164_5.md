## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

```
 6S LiPo ──► XT60 ──► 4-in-1 ESC board (4 × [MCU + 3-ph driver + 6 FETs])
                         │  8-pin JST-SH (VBAT, GND, CUR, TLM, M1–M4)
                         ▼
               STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot) ── bidirectional DShot600 ×4
```

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743's job is DShot
