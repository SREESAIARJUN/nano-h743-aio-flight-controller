## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)** MCU. It does not commutate motors. Commercial 4-in-1 ESCs, and this design, use one dedicated ESC MCU per motor. Here that is an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 firmware.

The interface between the two layers is **bi-directional DShot600**: the H743 sends throttle frames, and each ESC replies with eRPM. The design therefore splits into two halves:

| Domain | Owner MCU | Covered in |
|---|---|---|
| Power stage: 24 MOSFETs, 4 gate drivers, current sense | 4× ESC MCU (AM32) | §1–§4, §6.2 |
| Motor command and telemetry: DShot600 BDShot timers and DMA | STM32H743 (ArduPilot) | §5, §6.1 |
