# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture & Mathematical Model (Job #56)

## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

Three corrections shape every model below.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In this architecture the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 commands. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AM32 on STM32G071/AT32F421, or BLHeli_32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. This document therefore models two separate silicon domains connected by the DShot link.
2. **"80A" must be treated as a short burst rating, not a continuous one.** At a nano form factor (≈30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting, single MOSFET per switch), the thermal model shows:
   - about **5 s** of full-burst capacity,
   - about **30 A per channel** continuous with all four channels loaded simultaneously.
3. **Total bus current must be checked.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery-side demand exceeds
