## 0. Architecture Baseline and Feasibility Flags

The numbers below rest on a few architectural decisions and engineering judgements.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Flight MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (rev V, 480 MHz), ArduPilot |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32. Each motor needs its own commutation MCU. The H743 only generates DShot. |
| Gate driver | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge, bootstrap, 250 V) |
| MOSFETs | 24× 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3. Model parameters are listed in §1.1. |
| Supply | 2–6S. V_bus,max = 25.2 V, design point V = 25.2 V |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal |
| Form factor | 25.5×25.5 mm board, 20×20 mounting, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes |

**Flag 1: 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.** The H743 timer budget (§5) covers only DShot.

**Flag 2: "80A burst" on a nano board is only valid as a short, single-channel, airflow-dependent rating.** The maths in §3 shows all four channels at 80 A simultaneously gives roughly 3 s before thermal limits. Spec it on the datasheet as "80A burst, ≤2 s, 1 channel, ≥8 m/s airflow" or the product will fail field returns.

**Flag 3: 40 V FETs on 6S leave about 37% margin over V_bus.** Phase-node ringing must be held below about 32 V. This requires a snubber and tight layout (§6). It is acceptable but not generous.

**All MOSFET and driver parameters are representative values. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet maximums before release.**
