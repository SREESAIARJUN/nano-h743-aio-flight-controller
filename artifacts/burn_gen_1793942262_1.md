## 1. Field-Oriented Control: Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning by applying a d-axis current vector and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

With $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.4 PI gains by pole-zero cancellation

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

$$\frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. At 20 kHz this gives about 2π·1000 rad/s.

### 1.5 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

$$|v_{dq}| \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max}$$
