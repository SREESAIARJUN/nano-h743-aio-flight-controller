## 0. Architecture Decision and Stated Assumptions

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly.** ArduPilot's scheduler, EKF and logging jitter are incompatible with sensorless 6-step commutation for four motors at up to ~40 kHz electrical. The production-proven partition is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, run at 400 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM consumer |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (120 MHz) | AM32 | Sensorless commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, bidirectional DShot slave |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase bootstrap driver) | — | HS/LS drive, about 10–12 V gate |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET (PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6) | — | Three half-bridges per motor |

**Form-factor caveat.** An "80 A nano" board is a burst rating only. Section 3 shows that a 20×20 mm board cannot sustain 4×80 A for more than a few seconds. I specify a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board with a stacked 20×20 FC**. If you need a single-board 20×20 design, derate to about 35–45 A burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM
In any 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM-switched (HS active for D, sync-rectifying LS active for 1−D) and phase B's low side is held on.

- During the D interval the path is HS_A → motor → LS_B.
- During the 1−D interval the path is LS_A → motor → LS_B (synchronous freewheel).

So **exactly two channels conduct at all times**, apart from dead time:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$
