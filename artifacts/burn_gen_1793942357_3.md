# FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specification for Job #29. This design uses the assumptions below, and every one of them is a constant you can change in the code. Verify the ICM-42688-P register values against your datasheet revision before flashing.

## 0. Assumptions and Rate Plan

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH, current sensing on 2 phases |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701/AS5048), SPI |
| MCU | Cortex-M7 or M4F with single-precision FPU (STM32G4/H7 class) |
| IMU-A | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate. It is the stabilization feedback. |
| IMU-B | ICM-42688-P on the base/handle. It provides disturbance feedforward and a vibration spectrum reference. |
| Kinematic chain | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with balanced currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$
