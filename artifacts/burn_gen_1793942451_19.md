## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Findings

The rest of the document depends on these assumptions, so they come first.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | The STM32H743 is the **flight controller only**. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 does not commutate motors. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max / 22.2 V nominal |
| "80 A burst" | Interpreted as **80 A per-channel phase current**, all four channels simultaneously (worst case) |
| Form factor | "Nano" = 20×20 mm mounting, board ≈ 26×26 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz copper |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) switching, 120° trapezoidal commutation |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 class: R_DS(on) = 1.6 mΩ @ 25 °C/10 V, t_r = 15 ns, t_f = 12 ns, Q_g = 30 nC, Q_rr = 30 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, R_θJC = 2.5 K/W |

### Key Findings

1. **80 A × 4 in a nano format is a burst-only rating of about 1 second.** The model below shows continuous capability around **20–30 A/channel** with prop-wash cooling.
2. **320 A total battery current exceeds what the power entry can carry.** The board pads, XT30/XT60 connectors, and wire gauge that fit a nano board cannot support it. The realistic requirement is probably "80 A total burst" or "80 A/ch on a 30×30 board". Please confirm with the customer before layout.
3. All numbers below are **design-intent estimates**. Validate them with the selected MOSFET datasheet, a thermal-camera test, and a SPICE/FEA run before release.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | Multiplier | R_DS |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.60 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.45 | 2.32 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.60 | 2.56 mΩ (design value) |

### 1.2 Current paths in 120° commutation

Two phases conduct at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high-side FET for duty D. With complementary switching, it flows through the low-side FET for (1−D).
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is fully on.

Instantaneous conduction loss per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}$$

Ripple correction for inductor current ripple ΔI (triangular):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

This adds less than 1% at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance, so it is neglected.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.56\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{32.8\ W}$$

Per FET, each device conducts 120° of each 360° electrical cycle:

$$P_{FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \frac{6400 \times 2.56\text{ m}}{3} = 5.46\ \text{W (average)},\quad 16.4\ \text{W (instantaneous)}$$

The electrical period is about 0.3–2 ms, which is much shorter than the die thermal time constant (tens of ms). Averaging is therefore valid for the board model. For die-level peak checks, use the Z_θJC(t) curve at t_p = one-third of the commutation period.
