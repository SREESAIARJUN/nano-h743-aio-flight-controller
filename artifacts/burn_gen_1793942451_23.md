# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #41): 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching Loss, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**Partitioning.** On a "4-in-1 ESC" stack, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends DShot600 commands and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The math below is split along that boundary.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Trapezoidal 6-Step, Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation two phases are always active. One high-side FET (HS) and one low-side FET (LS) carry I_ph through the motor.

- During the PWM off-time with synchronous rectification, the complementary LS of the switching leg carries the current instead of the HS.
- Either way, the current path always contains exactly **two FETs in series**.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2 \times (1 + 0.006 \times 100) = 1.92\ \text{mΩ} \;\rightarrow\; \text{use } 1.8\text{–}1.9\ \text{mΩ (iterate, §3.5)}$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss per motor

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.4 Distribution per FET

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for one-third of the electrical cycle, with HS and LS sharing the PWM interval.

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} \approx 23.0/6 = \mathbf{3.84\ W}$$

The electrical frequency is typically above 1 kHz, which is far faster than the FET's thermal time constant. Average power is therefore valid for junction rise.
