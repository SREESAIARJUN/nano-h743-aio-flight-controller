**Architectural clarification.** The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz gate PWM. Each ESC MCU does. The H743 only produces bidirectional DShot600 and consumes eRPM telemetry. These are two separate timing domains.

**Feasibility warning.** The math below shows that 80 A on all four channels at once is a transient capability lasting about 2 s on a 20×20 board. It is not a continuous rating. The realistic continuous rating is about 20–25 A per channel. Publishing "80A" without a duration qualifier would be misleading.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.4 × 1.5 = **2.1 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In every commutation sector, current flows through exactly two conducting devices in series:

- the high-side (HS) of the PWM leg, or its complementary low-side (LS) during the off-time;
- the LS of the return leg.

Ignoring dead time, the instantaneous conduction loss is independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period at D → 1:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}}{3}$$
