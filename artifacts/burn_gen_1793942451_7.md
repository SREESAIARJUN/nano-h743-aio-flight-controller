# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #31)

## Thermal, Switching-Loss and DShot/DMA Architecture Package

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings affect the rest of the design.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each motor needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. | The architecture is **H743 → 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421) → gate drivers → 24 MOSFETs**. |
| F2 | "80 A burst" on a nano-class board (20×20 mm mounting, about 30×30 mm PCB) is achievable only as a **seconds-scale transient**. The total loss at 4×80 A is about 110–120 W into a board with about 5 J/K of heat capacity. | Rate it as **80 A per motor for ≤2 s, 1 % duty**, and about 35–40 A continuous with airflow. |
| F3 | 48 kHz PWM roughly doubles the switching loss compared with 24 kHz. At 80 A it adds about 2.4 W per motor. | Use the AM32 variable-PWM option: 48 kHz at low throttle for smoothness, 24 kHz above about 60 % duty. |
| F4 | "80 A" is interpreted here as **phase (motor) current per channel**. If it means battery current, the phase current at partial duty is higher and the conduction loss rises. | Confirm the definition with the customer. |

**Assumptions used throughout:** 6S LiPo (25.2 V max), 40 V MOSFETs in PQFN 3.3×3.3 packages, six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 MOSFET parameters (representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 PQFN)

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_ds25 | 1.2 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 0.0045 /K (normalized-R_ds curve) |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 2.0 K/W |
| Total gate charge (10 V) | Q_g | 30 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Output capacitance (25 V) | C_oss | 1.0 nF |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |

The on-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 ≈ 1.74 mΩ. Add about 0.15 mΩ of package and solder parasitics, so R_eff ≈ 1.9 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step bridge with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, phase current I flows through exactly two conducting devices:

- one high-side or low-side FET in the PWM phase (alternating with duty D), and
- one FET in the fixed phase.

**Bridge conduction loss per motor**

$$P_{cond,bridge}=2\,I^2R_{eff}$$

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so averaged over the cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET}=\frac{2I^2R_{eff}}{6}=\frac{I^2R_{eff}}{3}$$

**At I = 80 A, R_eff = 1.9 mΩ:**

- I²R = 6400 × 0.0019 = 12.2 W
- P_cond,bridge = **24.3 W per motor**
- P_cond,FET (average) = **4.05 W**
- Peak instantaneous dissipation in a conducting FET = **12.2 W**

**Ripple correction.** With 48 kHz PWM and a low-inductance motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH):

$$\Delta I_{pp}\approx\frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}}=\frac{25.2\cdot0.25}{15\mu\cdot48k}\approx 8.75\,\text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2=I^2+\frac{\Delta I^2}{12}\;\Rightarrow\;\text{adds }0.1\%\text{ (negligible)}$$

### 1.3 Other copper losses per motor channel

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase traces and pad (2 oz, 4 layers paralleled) | 0.15 mΩ × 2 | 1.9 W |
| Current shunt (shared, bus side, 0.25 mΩ, D = 0.5 average) | 0.25 mΩ | 0.8 W |
| Bus planes / XT60 pigtail share | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W (board share about 1 W) |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing FET hard-switches. The synchronous partner is commutated through its body diode during dead time.

**1. Hard-switching overlap (V-I crossover)**

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

With a FD6288Q-class driver (about 1.0 A source / 1.5 A sink) and R_g = 0 Ω, the switching edges are t_r ≈ 22 ns and t_f ≈ 18 ns:

$$P_{VI}=0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot40\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**2. Body-diode reverse recovery**

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=30n\cdot25.2\cdot48k=\mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

The diode recovery current also adds turn-on overlap in the HS. Include a +15 % factor on P_VI, giving **+0.29 W**.

**3. Output capacitance (C_oss) discharge**

$$P_{oss}=\tfrac12 C_{oss}V^2 f\cdot 2=1\text{n}\cdot635\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.03\ W}$$

**4. Dead-time diode conduction** (t_dt = 50 ns per edge, 2 edges per period)

$$P_{dt}=V_f\,I\,2t_{dt}f=0.8\cdot80\cdot100\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

**5. Gate drive** (2 FETs switching, dissipated in the driver and R_g)

$$P_g=2Q_gV_{gs}f=2\cdot30n\cdot10\cdot48k=\mathbf{0.029\ W}$$

**Switching total per motor at 80 A: P_sw ≈ 2.6 W**
