## 0. Architecture Correction and Stated Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each of the four ESC channels runs its own small MCU (for example STM32G071/AT32F421 on AM32, or STM32F051 on BLHeli_32). These MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to them and receives eRPM telemetry back. The loss model (sections 1–3) applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA plan (section 4) applies to the H743.

**Physics check on "80 A nano".** A 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board cannot dissipate 80 A × 4 continuously. Section 3 shows that 80 A is a **heat-capacity-limited transient**, roughly 2–3 s, and not a thermal steady state. The spec sheet should say "80 A burst (≤2 s, 40 °C start)", not "80 A".

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Per-FET duty accounting

In 120° trapezoidal commutation, at any instant one leg sources (PWM), one leg sinks (low-side held on), and one leg floats. With synchronous (complementary) PWM at duty D, averaged over one electrical revolution:

| Device | Conducting fraction |
|---|---|
| High-side FET, leg *k* | D/3 |
| Low-side FET, leg *k* | (1−D)/3 + 1/3 = (2−D)/3 |

So for each FET:

$$P_{cond,HS}=\frac{D}{3}I^2R_{ds}(T_j)\qquad P_{cond,LS}=\frac{2-D}{3}I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Per motor

Summing over 3 legs gives the following. Exactly two R_ds are always in series in the current path, which is a useful sanity check.

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\,I^2R_{ds}(T_j),\qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$
