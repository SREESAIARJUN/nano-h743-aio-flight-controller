### 0.2 Architectural correction

The H743 does **not** commutate the MOSFETs. ArduPilot has no ESC commutation firmware. Each ESC channel needs its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

```
6S ──► Shunt ──► Bulk caps ──► 4× [ESC MCU (AT32F421) ─► FD6288Q ─► 6× MOSFET ─► Motor]
                                         ▲ BDShot600 (1 wire each)
                                 STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ◄─ IMU/Baro/OSD/SD
```

### 0.3 Up-front feasibility verdict

Read this before the math. The numbers below show that a **sustained** 80 A on all four channels of a nano board is not physically achievable.

- An 80 A rating on this format is only defensible as a **duty-limited burst**: ≤ 1–2 s, single or dual channel, at ≤ 10 % duty.
- Reaching even that likely requires **paralleled MOSFETs** (12 per ESC).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.3 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.89 mΩ** (max, hot).

### 1.2 Current path

**6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification.** At any instant, phase current flows through exactly two conducting devices:

- the PWM leg's HS (for D) or LS (for 1−D), plus
- the static LS of the return phase.

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

**FOC / sinusoidal variant.** Use I_rms = I_pk/√2 per phase:

$$P_{cond,FOC} = 3\, I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}$$

For equal phase-current magnitude, this comes out comparable to the 6-step result.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Case | R_DS | P_FET (2 devices) | P_Cu (0.5 mΩ path) | **Total per ESC** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold, typ | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 3.2 W | 16.0 W |
| Cold, max | 1.3 mΩ | 16.6 W | 3.2 W | 19.8 W |
| **Hot, max (design)** | 1.89 mΩ | **24.2 W** | 3.2 W | **27.4 W** |
| Hot, 2× parallel FETs | 0.945 mΩ | 12.1 W | 3.2 W | 15.3 W |

The worst single device is the static return-phase LS FET, which carries the full current at 100 % duty:

$$P_{LS,max} = 80^2 \times 1.89\text{ mΩ} = 12.1\text{ W}$$

### 1.4 Electrothermal convergence

R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. Iterate until stable:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{case} + R_{\theta JC}\, I^2 R_{DS,25}[1+\alpha(T_j^{(n)}-25)]$$
