## 0. Architecture Decisions and Stated Assumptions

Two issues must be settled before any numbers mean anything.

**Decision 1: The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
ArduPilot has no field-grade BLDC commutation loop. The correct architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──4× bidir DShot600──► 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32 firmware)
                                                   │
                                                   ├─► 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q class)
                                                   └─► 6× N-MOSFET half-bridges per motor
```
